# u3.12.01 Modellings DKT - DST - Q4G - DKTG - Q4GG

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings DKT - DST - Q4G - DKTG - Q4GG:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear possibilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings.

They can be used for three-dimensional plate problems [R3.07.03](doc:r3.07.03) in linear mechanical analysis for all the modellings and in material non-linearity for the modellings DKT, DKTG and Q4GG only. Their use for shell problems is commonly accepted, considering that the plane of the finite element can be likened to a facet tangent to the middle surface of the shell (caution: only plane facets are permitted).

The thermomechanical analyses are chained from the thermal shell finite elements (see [U3.22.01](doc:u3.22.01)).

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

For the three three-dimensional plate modellings, the discretisation degrees of freedom are, at each node of the support mesh cell, the six displacement components (three translations and three rotations) at the vertex nodes of the support mesh cell. These nodes are assumed to describe a facet tangent to the middle surface of the shell.

| **Finite element** | **Degrees of freedom (at each vertex node)** |
| --- | --- |

| DKT / DKQ / DKTG | DX | DY | DZ | DRX | DRY | DRZ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DST / DSQ | DX | DY | DZ | DRX | DRY | DRZ |
| Q4G (Q4 $\gamma$ )/ Q4GG | DX | DY | DZ | DRX | DRY | DRZ |

### 1.2 Support mesh cell of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the finite elements, in displacement formulation, may be triangles or quadrilaterals. In the latter case, the mesh cells are assumed to be plane (4 coplanar vertex nodes):

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Finite element** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| DKT | TRIA3 QUAD4 | MEDKTR3 MEDKQU4 |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DKTG | TRIA3 QUAD4 | MEDKTG3 MEDKQG4 |  |
| DST | TRIA3 QUAD4 | MEDSTR3 MEDSQU4 |  |
| Q4G (Q4 $\gamma$ ) | QUAD4 TRIA3 | MEQ4QU4 MET3TR3 |  |
| Q4GG | TRIA3 QUAD4 | MET3GG3 MEQ4GG4 |  |

### 1.3 Support mesh cell of the loadings

All the loadings applicable to the facets of the plate elements are treated by direct discretisation on the support mesh cell of the element in displacement formulation.

*No loading support mesh cell is therefore necessary for the faces of the plate elements.*

For the loadings applicable on the edges of the plate elements, a support mesh cell of type SEG2 can be used:

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Finite element** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| DKT | SEG2 | MEBODKT |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DST | SEG2 | MEBODST |  |
| DKTG | SEG2 | MEBODKT |  |
| Q4G (Q4 $\gamma$ ) | SEG2 | MEBOQ4G |  |
| Q4GG | SEG2 | MEBOQ4G |  |

## 2 Assignment of the characteristics

For these plate or shell elements, it is necessary to assign geometrical characteristics which are complementary to the mesh data. The definition of these data is carried out with the command AFFE_CARA_ELEM associated with the following factor keyword:

**COQUE**

Makes it possible to define and assign to the mesh cells the thickness, the shear coefficient, the eccentricity, ...

For the study of structures comprising multilayer materials, it is necessary to assign the characteristics of each of the layers (thickness, type of material) and their stacking (fibre orientation). The definition of these data is carried out with the command DEFI_COMPOSITE.

## 3 Supported loadings

The available loadings are the following:

**FORCE_ARETE**

Makes it possible to apply linear forces.

Supported modellings: DKT, DST, Q4G

**FORCE_COQUE**

Makes it possible to apply surface forces.

Supported modellings: DKT, DST, Q4G, DKTG, Q4GG

**PESANTEUR**

Makes it possible to define the acceleration and the direction of gravity.

Supported modellings: DKT, DST, Q4G, DKTG, Q4GG

**PRES_REP**

Makes it possible to apply surface forces.

Supported modellings: DKT, DST, Q4G, DKTG, Q4GG

**PRE_EPSI**

Makes it possible to apply an initial strain loading.

Supported modellings: DKT, DST, Q4G, DKTG, Q4GG

The application of a thermal expansion loading is carried out by defining the factor keyword AFFE_VARC under AFFE_MATERIAU [U4.43.03](doc:u4.43.03).

## 4 Non-linear possibilities

For the modelling DKT only.

### 4.1 Constitutive laws

Only the modellings 'DKT' and 'DKTG' have non-linear possibilities. The constitutive laws specific to this modelling, usable under COMPORTEMENT in STAT_NON_LINE and DYNA_NON_LINE, are the plane-stress constitutive relations available with the modellings 'AXIS' and 'C_PLAN' (*cf.* [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).

### 4.2 Strains

The available strains, used in the constitutive relations under the keyword DEFORMATION for the operators STAT_NON_LINE and DYNA_NON_LINE, are (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)):

```text
/    'PETIT'
```

The strains used for the constitutive relation are the linearised strains.

```text
/    'PETIT_REAC'
```

The strain increments used in the incremental constitutive relation are the linearised strains of the displacement increment in the updated geometry.

**Note:**

*Warning: the analysis of the strains using* *PETIT_REAC is only an approximation of the large-displacement assumptions. It requires very small loading increments to be performed. In order to take large displacements, and especially large rotations, into account correctly, it is recommended to use the modelling* *COQUE_3D, with* *DEFORMATION=’GROT_GDEP’* .
