# v7.03.100 HPLV100 - Parallelepiped with temperature-dependent Young's modulus

**Summary**

This thermo-elastic calculation compares the solution provided by *Code_Aster* to an analytical solution when the Young's modulus varies in a nonlinear way from temperature.

The Modelling is not physical and is described in [V7.90.01](doc:v7.90.01).
