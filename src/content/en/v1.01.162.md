# v1.01.162 ZZZZ162 – Validating the field writing and rereading in MED format

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to validate the writing and rereading of the MED-format fields for thermal and mechanical analysis.

For each analysis, the results are printed in the MED format which is then reread and tested. Validation concerns:

- keywords :

  - GROUP_NO, GROUP_MA, node, MAILLEDE The IMPR_RESU OPERATOR,
- Operators

  - LIRE_RESU('FORMAT='MED')POUR UNE LISTE OF INSTANTS AND/OR NUM EROS OF ORDRE,
  - CREA_TABLE/RESU: CHAM_NO et CHAM_ELEM,
  - IMPR_RESU/FORMAT MED: NOM_RESU_MED,
  - IMPR_RESUET LIRE_RESUAU FORMAT MEDPOUR UN EVOL_VARC.

We test the temperature, the movements and the constraints.

Two modellingss are proposed:

- Modelization A: AXIS thermal and mechanical analysis,
- Modelization B: thermal and mechanical analysis in 3d.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
