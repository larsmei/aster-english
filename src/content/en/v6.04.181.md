# v6.04.181 SSNV181 - Checking the correct account of shear in BETON_UMLV and BETON_BURGER models

**Summary:**

This test validates the correct consideration of shear in constitutive laws BETON_UMLV and BETON_BURGER. The results of this test are compared with an analytical solution.

- Modelling A: Long-term shear test with BETON_UMLV.
- Modelling B: Long-term shear test with BETON_BURGER.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

In this test, a uniform and constant drying field is created in the structure.

The mechanical loading corresponds to a shear in the $xz$ plane; its intensity is $\mathrm{10 }[\mathrm{MPa}]$. The load is applied in $\mathrm{1s}$ and is kept constant for 750 days.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The beginning of the calculation is assumed to be the moment –1. At this moment there is no drying field, no mechanical stress.

At moment 0, a drying field corresponding to $\text{100 \%}$ d-hygrometry is applied.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The analytical solution is based on the resolution of the two differential equations that govern the deviatoric part of the behavior (see [R7.01.06](doc:r7.01.06) and [R7.01.35](doc:r7.01.35)). The choice of the $\kappa$ parameter at a very large value ensures equivalence between the two models for the applied load.

The deviatoric stresses are the cause of a slipping mechanism (or quasi-dislocation mechanism) of the CSH sheets in the nanoporosity. Under deviatoric stress, the creeping ss is carried out at constant volume. Furthermore, the UMLV creep law assumes the isotropy of the deviatoric creep.
