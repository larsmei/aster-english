# v3.01.013 SSLL13 – Calculation of beam fittings

**Summary:**

This test is for the analytical verification of the ferrage density calculated using the CALC_FERRAILLAGE operator.

Several loading cases are studied at ELU for Modelling A, at l-ELS (Characteristic) for Modelling B and l-ELS QP for Modelling C. ## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The densities of longitudinal steels are calculated according to the 3 Pivots (A, B, C) method.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

#### 2.2.1 Calculations at l-ELU

Details of the calculation for the default case are given (Configuration 1 + Configuration 2 for cases where compression steel is required).

##### 2.2.1.1 Loading case 1

The beam is subjected to $N=-1000000\text{N}$ compression and shear force ${V}_{z}=100000\text{N}$ .

**L longitudinal closing:**

The strength of unrefined concrete to compression is given by:

${N}_{\mathit{Rd}}={A}_{c}\times {f}_{\mathit{cd}}=0,30m\times 0,50m\times (\frac{35\mathit{MPa}}{1,5})=3,5\text{MN}>1\text{MN}$

Therefore, the section is in Pivot C (Cas of uniform deformation) such that the concrete can stand alone against the stress

${A}_{\mathit{SYS}}={A}_{\mathit{SYI}}={A}_{\mathit{SZS}}={A}_{\mathit{SZI}}=0{\mathit{cm}}^{2}$

**Transverse lock:**

In Eurocode 2, the calculation of the transverse railing may take account of the normal compression effort and the first step is to check the strength of the non-ferral concrete transversally to the shear force, given by the formula below:

${V}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=[max({C}_{\mathit{Rd},c}k{(100{\rho}_{l}{f}_{\mathit{ck}})}^{1/3};{v}_{min})+{k}_{1}{\sigma}_{\mathit{cp}}]\times {b}_{w}d$

Either, if the impact of the normal effort is taken into account:

${V}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=[max(\frac{0,18}{1,5}\cdot (1+\sqrt{\frac{200}{460}})\cdot {(100\times 0\times 35)}^{1/3};\frac{0,053}{1,5}\cdot (1+\sqrt{\frac{200}{460}})\cdot {35}^{0,5})+0,15\times (\frac{1,0}{0,3\times 0,5})]\times 0,3\times 0,46$

${V}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=185868N$

This term is higher than the value of the shear force requesting the section, which is worth 100,000 N.

Therefore, no cross-ferrals are required.

If the benefit of normal effort is not taken into account, then:

${V}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=69884N$

This then becomes less than the shear force requesting the section, i.e. ${V}_{\mathit{Ed}}=100000N$; then we go through the Modelling of Ritter-Mosche lattice given at the level of Eurocode 2, considering:

- ${\theta}_{b}=21,8°$ - angle of inclination of concrete compression rods
- ${V}_{\mathit{Rd},max}=\frac{{\alpha}_{\mathit{cw}}{b}_{w}z{\nu}_{1}{f}_{\mathit{cd}}}{\tan({\theta}_{b})+\mathrm{cot}({\theta}_{b})}=\frac{1,0\times 0,3\times (0,9\times 0,46)\times 0,6\times (35/1,5)}{\tan(21,8°)+\mathrm{cot}(21,8°)}=564798N>100000N$ - verification of non-crush of the rods
- ${V}_{\mathit{Rd},s}=(\frac{{A}_{\mathit{sw}}}{s})z{f}_{\mathit{yd}}/\tan({\theta}_{b})$ - resistance of the transverse railing frames to be predicted. To verify the stability of the mesh, the dimensioning then leads to the installation: ${V}_{\mathit{Rd},s}={V}_{\mathit{Ed}}$; i.e.: $\frac{{A}_{\mathit{sw}}}{s}=\frac{{V}_{\mathit{Ed}}}{z{f}_{\mathit{yd}}/\tan({\theta}_{b})}=3,93{\mathit{cm}}^{2}/m$

##### 2.2.1.2 Loading case2

The beam is subjected to a normal traction force of $N=1000000\text{N}$ and a shear force ${V}_{z}=-100000\text{N}$ .

**L longitudinal closing:**

It is a section that is fully symmetrically tense.

The steel cross section is therefore equal to ${A}_{S}=\frac{N}{{f}_{\mathit{yd}}}=\frac{N}{({f}_{\mathit{yk}}/{\gamma}_{s})}$ (Pivot A – Case of uniform deformation).

Each frame bed therefore takes over the fourfold effort:

${A}_{\mathit{SYS}}={A}_{\mathit{SYI}}={A}_{\mathit{SZS}}={A}_{\mathit{SZI}}=\frac{{A}_{S}}{4}=5,75{\mathit{cm}}^{2}$

**Transverse lock:**

Same as in the previous case, in the absence of a compression effort:

$\frac{{A}_{\mathit{sw}}}{s}=3,93{\mathit{cm}}^{2}/m$

##### 2.2.1.3 Loading case3

The beam is subjected to a normal traction force of $N=1000000\text{N}$ , a shear force ${V}_{z}=-100000\text{N}$ and a ${M}_{T}=-10000\text{Nm}$ twist moment

**L longitudinal closing:**

Same as previous case: ${A}_{\mathit{SYS}}={A}_{\mathit{SYI}}={A}_{\mathit{SZS}}={A}_{\mathit{SZI}}=5,75{\mathit{cm}}^{2}$

**Transverse lock:**

At l-Eurocode 2, the first step is to check the strength of the sheared concrete to the coupling shear force and twist moment:

- For l-shear force:

${V}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=[max({C}_{\mathit{Rd},c}k{(100{\rho}_{l}{f}_{\mathit{ck}})}^{1/3};{v}_{min})+{k}_{1}{\sigma}_{\mathit{cp}}]\times {b}_{w}d$

Either:

${V}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=[max(\frac{0,18}{1,5}\cdot (1+\sqrt{\frac{200}{460}})\cdot {(100\times 8,846\times {10}^{-3}\times 35)}^{1/3};\frac{0,053}{1,5}\cdot (1+\sqrt{\frac{200}{460}})\cdot {35}^{0,5})]\times 0,3\times 0,46$

${V}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=91948N$

- For twisting:

${T}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=2{f}_{\mathit{ctd}}{t}_{k}{a}_{k}$

Either:

${T}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=2\times (\frac{2,25}{1,5})\times (9,375\times {10}^{-2})\times (8,379\times {10}^{-2})$

${T}_{\mathit{Rd},c}=23534\mathit{Nm}$

- Either the strength of the sheared concrete is calculated as follows:

$\frac{{V}_{\mathit{Ed}}}{{V}_{\mathit{Rd},c}}+\frac{{T}_{\mathit{Ed}}}{{T}_{\mathit{Rd},c}}=\frac{100000}{91948}+\frac{10000}{23534}=1,512>1,0$

Therefore, a transverse railing is required; then the Ritter-Mosche mesh model given at Eurocode 2 level is used, considering:

- ${\theta}_{b}=21,8°$ - angle of inclination of concrete compression rods
