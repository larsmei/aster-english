# v1.01.382 ZZZZ382 - Validation of CALC_CHAMP on beams with complex fields

**Summary:**

This test has no physical meaning, it is essentially a computer test.

In the presence of complex modes on a modal basis (mode_meca_c), CALC_CHAMP does not allow calculation. However, if the modal base is returned on a physical basis, then CALC_CHAMP accepts complex modes.

The purpose of this test is therefore to validate CALC_CHAMP on beams in the presence of complex fields.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
