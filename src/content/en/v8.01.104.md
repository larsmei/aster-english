# v8.01.104 FDLV104 - mass-added calculation on 3D model

**Summary:**

This fluid field test (fluid coupling/structure) validates the mass calculation added to a generalised 3D model, i.e. two spheres coupled by an incompressible fluid.

By modal analysis performed by dynamic substructure, the coupled modes of both spheres are obtained because of the presence of the fluid. The eigenfrequencies are compared to those determined by direct calculation.

The frequencies are obtained within 3%.

Fluid side: One imposes a pressure (i.e. temperature) zero in a node of the fluid mesh.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

A direct modal calculation is used to obtain the fluid-modified frequencies around the spheres.

The two spheres are deduced by a translation of the vector of $(2,0.,0.)$ components and by a rotation of $(90.,0.,90.)$ nautical analyses, which allows us to envisage the construction of a generalized model on the 1st sphere. The same modal calculation with fluid-structure coupling can be performed using dynamic substructure. The **direct** calculation with added mass account leads to the following frequencies of the immersion system:

| ${f}_{1}=$ | $3.1172\mathrm{Hz}$ |
| --- | --- |
| ${f}_{2}=$ | $3.1183\mathrm{Hz}$ |
| ${f}_{3}=$ | $9.2727\mathrm{Hz}$ |
| ${f}_{4}=$ | $9.8267\mathrm{Hz}$ |
| ${f}_{5}=$ | $22.4400\mathrm{Hz}$ |
| ${f}_{6}=$ | $30.3295\mathrm{Hz}$ |

### 2.2 Baseline

Direct modal calculation by *Code_Aster* .

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

The uncertainties on the solution are related to the discretization of the fluid/structure interface and the invariance of the mesh by rotation of $(90°,0°,90°)$ nautical angles.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Modelling includes:

- structure side:

336 elements hulls DKT Modelling sphere 1,

6 Discreet elements type K_TR_L (DIS_TR Modelling) Modelling the six springs connecting sphere 1 to the solid,

- fluid side:

672 THER_FACE3 elements thermal Modelling the fluid/structure interface,

39 445 THER_TETRA4 elements thermal fluid Modelling.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of mesh cells and types: 672 mesh cells TRIA3, 39 445 TETRA4, 6 SEG2

### 3.4 Remarks

The difference on the last two eigenfrequencies is due to a non-symmetry of the mesh of the fluid/structure (thermal) interface with that of the first. The **mesh** of the fluid/structure interface (thermal elements) of the second sphere is not deduced exactly from that of the first by rotation of nautical angles of $90°,0°,90°$.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results obtained by dynamic substructure calculation show a perfect concordance with direct calculation. Nevertheless, to achieve this agreement, it is necessary to ensure that the grids of the fluid/structure interfaces (elements thermal) of the substructures that repeat within the fluid mesh are perfectly deduced by the nautical angle rotations defined in DEFI_MODELE_GENE.
