# v3.01.112 SSLL112 - Circular vault under uniform pressure

**Summary:**

This test allows to verify the internal forces of elements POU_D_T for Modelling a circular vault.

It allows us to see the refinement needed to obtain correct results on the VY and VZ components of the field of effort.

## 2 Reference solutions

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solutions

The beam in Figure 1.1.1-a checks the equilibrium equations (plane problem).

${V}_{y}=\frac{\mathrm{dN}}{d\theta }$ , $N+\frac{{\mathrm{dV}}_{y}}{d\theta }=-pR$ , $\frac{\mathrm{dM}}{d\theta }+{\mathrm{RV}}_{y}=0$

($p$: normal constant load distributed in all point of the beam).

$N(\theta )$ *,* ${V}_{y}(\theta )$ *,* ${M}_{z}(\theta )$ refers to the forces (normal, sharp, and bending moment) in a point vault expressed in the local marker.

Their integration with the boundary conditions:

${V}_{y}(0)=0$ , ${M}_{z}(0)=0$

give:

${V}_{y}(\theta )=0$ , $M(\theta )=0$ , $N(\theta )=-\mathrm{pR}$

### 2.2 Baseline

Interior efforts for $\theta =0°,6°,42°$ and $60°$ .

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.

### 3.4 Variation in mesh refinement

Initially, the mesh of this test included 30 mesh cells modeled in POU_C_T. With this refinement the results with elements POU_D_T are very good except for ${V}_{y}(0°)$:

| **Type of effort** | **Reference** | **Ecart (%)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ${V}_{y}(0°)$ | 0.0000 | 5.23 (absolute) |
| ${V}_{y}(6°)$ | 0.0000 | 1E-11 (absolute) |
| $N(60°)$ | –1.000E+02 | 1.3E-3 (relative) |
| $\mathit{MFZ}(42°)$ | 0.0000 | 1E-12 (absolute) |

With 450 mesh cells, you only get a 0,34 difference on ${V}_{y}(0°)$.

## 4 Summary of the results

The normal force in the vault (only non-zero effort) is calculated with good precision ($\text{0,1\%}$) for the Modelling adopted. However, it is noted that the value of ${V}_{y}(0°)$ remains excluded from the reference despite the significant refinement.
