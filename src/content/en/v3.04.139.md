# v3.04.139 SSLV139 - Flaming of a circular plate with a compression force evenly distributed over its contour

**Summary:**

This test represents a stability calculation of a circular plate subjected to a compression force evenly distributed over its contour. We determine the critical load leading to the elastic flaming d-Euler and the associated modal deformity.

This test validates AXIS_FOURIER Modelling for linear flashing with mesh cells QUAD8 and TRIA6 (circonferential mode equal to zero), and AXIS Modelling with mesh cells QUAD8.

The critical load obtained is compared to an analytical reference solution.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.139/10000000000005A30000029DF428B595553132FB.png)

- Plate thickness: $h=0.0005m$
- Plaque radius: $R=0.115m$

### 1.2 Material properties

The Material properties component of the plate is:

| $E=2.1{10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$ | Young's modulus |
| --- | --- |
| $\nu =0.3$ | Poisson's ratio |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

boundary conditions:

- on the contour of the plate (point $B$ ) : following displacement $Y=0$ and rotation around $Z=0$

Loading: A uniformly distributed compression force $F$ is applied to the outer contour of the plate.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Not applicable.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The critical load value is given in [bib1] by the following expression:

${F}_{\mathrm{cr}}=\frac{14.68D}{{R}^{2}}$

with: $D$ the flexural rigidity of the plate (in $\mathrm{N.m}$ ) defined by the following expression:

$D=\frac{E{h}^{3}}{12(1-{\nu}^{2})}$

This critical load is associated with a circumferential mode equal to 0.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

For the given characteristics, the critical load is:

${F}_{\mathrm{cr}}=2668.315N/m$

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

analytical solution

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

1. S.P. TIMOSHENKO, J.M. GERE: Theory of Elastic Stability, Second Edition, DUNOD (1966)

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Limit conditions:

- mesh-cell group $\mathrm{AD}$ : $\mathrm{DX}=0,\mathrm{DZ}=0$
- mesh-cell group $\mathrm{BC}$ : $\mathrm{DY}=0,\mathrm{DZ}=0$

Characteristics of discretization

- $\mathrm{AB}$ and $\mathrm{CD}$ sides: 460 elements
- $\mathrm{AD}$ and $\mathrm{BC}$ sides: 4 elements

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of nodes: 6449

Number of mesh cells: 1840 (QUAD8)

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

| **Identification** | **Reference** |
| --- | --- |
| Critical pressure ($n=0$) | $8.4935{10}^{5}\mathrm{Pa}$ |
| Moving $\mathrm{DY}$ to node $D$ | 1 |

### 3.4 Remarks

- The reference ${P}_{\mathrm{cr}}$ critical pressure used in the control file was obtained from the critical load referred to in paragraph [§ 2.2 ]:

${P}_{\mathrm{cr}}=\frac{{F}_{\mathrm{cr}}}{2\pi h}=849350.94N/{m}^{2}$

- Normalization of the eigenmode for the largest translation components implies a reference value equal to 1 for $\mathrm{DY}$ displacement to node $D$.

## 6 Summary of the results

The results obtained are very satisfactory for mesh cells QUAD8 regardless of the Modelling used (AXIS or AXIS_FOURIER): the uncertainties on critical pressure do not exceed 0.14%.

However, it should be noted that AXIS_FOURIER Modelling is much less accurate with mesh cells TRIA6 than with mesh cells QUAD8.

This test allowed testing and comparison of modellings AXIS and AXIS_FOURIER linearly flaming d-Euler of a thin circular structure subjected to an external compression force evenly distributed over its contour.
