# v6.04.170 SSNV170 - Elasto-viscoplastic single tensile cube (constant deformation speed)

**Summary:**

This test case in elasto-viscoplasticity allows to implement an axial creep test. The first two modellings (non-linear) simulate a simple tensile test with a constant imposed deformation speed. Modelling C is based on B Modelling with different materials and loads enabling the pseudo-discharge of the VISC_TAHERI. In all three modellings, traction is performed on a 3D modeled cube with a mesh cell HEXA8.

- Modelling A

  - Use of Lemaître [R5.03.08](doc:r5.03.08) nonlinear viscoplasticity behavior relationship.
- Modelling B:

  - Use of Taheri [R5.03.05](doc:r5.03.05)'s elasto-plasticity behavior relationship.
- Modelization C:

  - Use of Taheri's elasto-plasticity behavior relationship with pseudo-discharge activation.
