# v4.23.301 TTLP301 - Heat transfer in perforated plate

**Summary:**

This test, industrial, is based on independent validation of version 3 in linear transient thermal analysis.

This is a 2D plane problem represented by two modellings, one plane, the other volume.

The functions tested are as follows:

- element thermal plane,
- element thermal volumetric,
- transient thermal analysis algorithm,
- boundary conditions of exchange and flow.

### 1.1 Geometry

- $[\mathrm{AC}]$ Flow Density $q=1W/{\mathrm{cm}}^{2}$ ,
- $[\mathrm{AB}]$ , $[\mathrm{BE}]$ , $[\mathrm{DC}]$ $\varphi =0.$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

$T(t=0)=0$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is a numerical solution obtained by the elements finite method. This solution is based on a linear triangular mesh presented in the reference [1] . The calculations were made by considering an increment of time $\Delta t=0.01s$ .

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

Point $C$ temperature for $t=0.1,0.2,\mathrm{...},0.9,1.0,1.1,1.2s$ .

### 2.3 References

  1. Donea, "On the accuracy of finish element solutions to the transitient heat-conduction equation", Int. J. num. Meth. Engng, vol 8, n°1, pp 103-110, 1974.

## 5 Summary of the results

Both modellings give good results in agreement with reference solution, with a relative difference below 2% except locally on the smallest temperature value and for the lowest time t (start of problem).

A finer mesh in the point $C$ area should improve the quality of the results that are considered acceptable based on the modellings performed.
