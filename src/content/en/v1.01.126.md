# v1.01.126 ZZZZ126 - Validation of CREA_CHAMP OPERATION command: 'ASSE'

**Summary:**

This test validates 'ASSE' operation of the CREA_CHAMP command, which allows the production of a field (nodes or elements) by "assembly" of existing field tips.

The test consists of assigning the assigned fields to geometric entities (mesh cells and nodes), quantities (movements, constraints or internal variables), and then combining the 'ASSE' operation with the CREA_CHAMP command and checking that the result field contains the correct values.
