# v4.03.102 TPLS102 – Thick beam in flat stresses – linear temperature variation according to width

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the stress calculation in a thick beam of flat stresses subject to a temperature variation depending on the width.

modellings :

- $A$ Modelling: DKT with mesh cells TRIA3
- $B$ Modelling: DKT with mesh cells QUAD4

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v4.03.102/Shape1.gif)

Thickness = $\mathrm{0.1m}$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=20000\mathit{Pa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\alpha ={10}^{-5}/°C$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

boundary conditions :

- On the $\mathit{AB}$ and $\mathit{CD}$ edges: $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DZ}=0$
- On the $\mathit{BC}$ edge: $\mathit{DY}=0$

Loading

- The load applied is a temperature load

  - Constant following $X$ and $Z$
  - Variable on $Y$ axis: $T(Y)=\mathrm{50Y}-50$

![Figure](/figures/v4.03.102/Shape2.gif)

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None

## 5 Summary of the results

For ${\sigma}_{xx}$, the maximum difference is:

- 2.0% with mesh cells TRIA3
- 1.5% with mesh cells QUAD4.

A finer mesh in the direction of temperature variation would yield better results.
