# v3.01.106 SSLL106 - Right hose

**Summary:**

This test allows simple verification of elements straight pipe mechanically of static linear structures. The model is linear.

For each model, 6 types of loading are applied at the end: traction, 2 shear forces, 2 bending moments and torsion. In addition, internal pressure, distributed linear force and thermal expansion are applied.

The values tested are displacements, nodes forces, and stresses and deformations at Gauss points. The reference solution is analytical (RDM).

- Two modellings (A and B) test the element TUYAU 3-mode Fourier (TUYAU_3M Modelling): Modelling A uses MECA_STATIQUE, Modelling B uses STAT_NON_LINE (elastic behaviour).
- Two modellings (C and D) test the element TUYAU with 6 Fourier modes (TUYAU_6M Modelling).
- Two modellings (E and F) test the element TUYAU with 3 modes of Fourier and 4 nodes (TUYAU_3M Modelling).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Analytical solution [V3.01.102](doc:v3.01.102) (Timoshenko cinematic):
$B$ displacements in the $(\mathit{Oxyz})$ beam-related marker.
The analytical solution for pressure loading in bib2.

$$
\text{Traction simple : }
& {u}_{x}=\frac{{F}_{x}L}{ES}
&

\text{Flexion simple : }
& {u}_{y}=\frac{{F}_{y}{L}^{3}(4+\Phi_y)}{12E{I}_{z}}
&  {\theta}_{z}=\frac{{L}^{2}{F}_{y}}{2E{I}_{z}}

\text{Flexion simple : }
& {u}_{z}=\frac{{F}_{z}{L}^{3}(4+\Phi_z)}{12E{I}_{y}}
&  {\theta}_{y}=\frac{-{L}^{2}{F}_{z}}{2E{I}_{y}}

\text{Torsion : }
& {\theta}_{x}=\frac{{M}_{x}L}{G{J}_{x}}
&

\text{Flexion pure : }
& {u}_{z}=\frac{-{M}_{y}{L}^{2}}{2E{I}_{y}}
&  {\theta}_{y}=\frac{{M}_{y}L}{E{I}_{y}}

\text{Flexion pure : }
& {u}_{y}=\frac{{M}_{z}{L}^{2}}{2E{I}_{z}}
&  {\theta}_{z}=\frac{{M}_{z}L}{E{I}_{z}}

\text{Pression : }
& {u}_{r}=\frac{P{b}^{2}r}{E({b}^{2}-{a}^{2})}\left[(1-\nu )+(1+\nu )\frac{{a}^{2}}{{r}^{2}}\right]
$$

With $u_r$ calculated in $r=\frac{a+b}{2}$.

The shear coefficients are given by:

$$
\Phi_z = \frac{12EI_y}{L^2 G A'_z}
$$

Here, the values are obtained with $S = A'_y = A'_z$.

For generalized beam deformations, constitutive law shall be used:

$$
\text{Traction simple : }
& {\epsilon}_{x}=\frac{{F}_{x}}{ES}
&

\text{Flexion simple : }
& {\gamma}_{xy}=\frac{{F}_{y}}{GA'_y}
& {\kappa}_{z}=\frac{{F}_{y}(L-x)}{E{I}_{z}}

\text{Flexion simple : }
& {\gamma}_{xz}=\frac{{F}_{z}}{GA'_z}
& {\kappa}_{y}=\frac{{F}_{z}(L-x)}{E{I}_{y}}

\text{Torsion : }
&
& {\kappa}_{x}=\frac{{M}_{x}}{G{J}_{x}}

\text{Flexion pure : }
&
& {\kappa}_{y}=\frac{{M}_{y}}{E{I}_{y}}

\text{Flexion pure : }
&
& {\kappa}_{z}=\frac{{M}_{z}}{E{I}_{z}}

\text{Pression : }
& \epsilon_{\theta\theta} = P \frac{b}{E e}
&
$$

### 3.5 Remarks

The shear values for the shear force are not accurate for this model. This is due to the order 2 interpolation functions of this element, for beam displacements and beam rotations. As the beam transverse shears are obtained by: ${\gamma}_{xy}={\theta}_{z}-\frac{{\mathit{du}}_{y}}{\mathit{dx}}$ , and for single bending, rotations vary as order 2 polynomials, but displacements, like order 3 polynomials, which is misapproached by the interpolation functions. The displacement derivative is therefore not accurate.

## 9 Summary of the results

This test allows you to check the correct functioning of the element TUYAU (3 modes and 6 modes of Fourier) in linear elasticity, with MECA_STATIQUE and STAT_NON_LINE operators, for all the loads applicable to this element.

Differences compared to reference solution analysis (supplied beam solution) is very low for displacements (0.04% to 0.06%), except for the pressure load where the difference 3% is due to the fact that $\mathrm{Wo}$ represents an average radial displacement. In reality this radial displacement varies in thickness. the difference on deformations and stresses $(\approx 1\text{\%})$ is more important than that on displacements but remains acceptable given that these values are calculated at integration points located in the thickness of the pipe.
