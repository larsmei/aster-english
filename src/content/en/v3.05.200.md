# v3.05.200 SSLX200 – 3D_POU connection: Simple and pure bending of a clamped-free beam

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the 3D_POU (AFFE_CHAR_MECA) connection. This connection allows to establish an restraint between a beam model with volume Modelling. The test case represents a beam:

- One part of which is modelled with elements density and the other part modelled with elements beams,
- Clamped has one end and free at the other end,
- Subject to tensile and bending forces.

Two types of analyses are performed:

- Linear static analysis: the displacements and stresses are tested in the case of traction and bending loading,
- Dynamic analysis: the first two bending modes are tested.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

#### 2.1.1 Static

Movements in $B$

- Simple Traction ${u}_{x}=\frac{{F}_{x}L}{ES}$
- Pure Flexion ${u}_{z}=-\frac{{M}_{y}{L}^{2}}{2E{I}_{y}}$ ${\theta}_{y}=\frac{{M}_{y}L}{E{I}_{y}}$
- Pure Flexion ${u}_{y}=\frac{{M}_{z}{L}^{2}}{2E{I}_{z}}$ ${\theta}_{z}=\frac{{M}_{z}L}{E{I}_{z}}$

Maximum stress in $A$

- Simple Traction ${\sigma}_{x}=\frac{{F}_{x}}{S}$
- Pure flexion ${\sigma}_{x}=-\frac{{M}_{y}}{\frac{2{I}_{y}}{h}}$
- Pure flexion ${\sigma}_{x}=-\frac{{M}_{z}}{\frac{{\mathrm{2I}}_{z}}{b}}$

#### 2.1.2 Bending frequencies

Mode 1: ${f}_{1}=\frac{3.516}{2{L}^{2}\pi }\sqrt{\frac{\mathit{EI}}{\rho S}}$

Mode 2: ${f}_{2}=\frac{22.0345}{2{L}^{2}\pi }\sqrt{\frac{\mathit{EI}}{\rho S}}$

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

We use a:

- 3D and POU_D_E Modelling for beam,
- Element DIS_TR POI point C,
- Restraint 3D_POU to point A for connecting the beam and the face of the volume,
- Restraint 3D_POU to point C to connect the element DIS_TR and the volume face.
