# v2.01.024 SDLD24- Two independent spring mass system under forced harmonic pressure

**Summary**

This test case consists of calculating the amortized transient response of a clamped-free system of two linear spring masses, subject to a mono-support required acceleration.

Its objective is to validate the method of calculating modes by blocking one and the other of the two masses. A single modal base is created from these two modes. A dynamic calculation is then made using a modal damping derived from these modes.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The response of a linear system consisting of two independent masses and springs is calculated to an acceleration imposed on their point anchor ${P}_{1}$:

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.024/10000201000000E7000000E9536EB9CCA0C3DA44.png)

We have a distance ${P}_{1}{P}_{2}={P}_{1}{P}_{3}=1m$.

### 1.2 Material properties

- restraint stiffeners: ${k}_{1}=100N/m$;
- point masses: $m=m({P}_{2})=m({P}_{3})=100\mathit{kg}$ .
- $\text{5 \%}$ modal damping for all modes

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The unknown displacement $u$ , $\dot{u}$ speed and $\ddot{u}$ accelerations referred to in this document are defined in the drive motion reference.

**boundary conditions**

The only travels allowed are $x$-axis translations.

In the required displacement marker, the point ${P}_{1}$ is clamped: ${u}_{x}={u}_{y}={u}_{z}=0$ .

**Charge**

In the global marker, the point anchor ${P}_{1}$ is subjected to a required harmonic displacement of frequency ${f}_{\mathrm{ex}}$. The calculation is made from $0$ to $20s$ in the associated relative benchmark.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The system is initially assumed to rest in the relative marker: $t=0$ , ${u}_{x}(0)=0$ and $\dot{{u}_{x}}(0)=0$ in all point.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The springs and point weights are modelled by discrete elements at 3 degrees of freedom DIS_T. The nodes $N2$ and $N3$ are slightly shifted to $Y$ , respectively $Y=0,01m$ and $Y=-0,01m$ , which does not alter the results since only the $X$ problem is solved.

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.024/100002010000017D000000D21A3CFE6296669D24.png)

The node $N1$ is clamped and is subject to an acceleration imposed $\gamma (t)$ .

The mode of the first spring mass system is first calculated by imposing ${u}_{x}({P}_{3})=0$. Then the mode of the second, by imposing ${u}_{x}({P}_{2})=0$. A single modal base is created from these two modes, using the DEFI_BASE_MODALE command with the NUME_REF option. A dynamic calculation is then performed with the DYNA_NON_LINE command using a modal damping derived from these modes.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results are in agreement with the analytical solution, which validates:

- implementation of the NEWMARK schema in time in DYNA_NON_LINE
- the methodology of calculating modes by blocking one and the other of two subsystems, and then creating a single modal base used to define modal damping.
