# v2.03.503 SDLS503 - Bending vibrations of a sandwich beam

**Summary:**

This test represents a modal analysis calculation of a sandwich beam that is simply supported. This test validates:

- elements finite plate modelingDKT with mesh cells QUAD4 and TRIA3,
- elements finite DST plate Modelling with mesh cells QUAD4 and TRIA3,
- account shall be taken of the transverse shear stiffness,
- the inclusion of composite material.

The frequencies and modes obtained are compared to an analytical reference solution. Note that DKT Modelling, which does not take into account transverse shear, is not suitable for Modelling this test case. Thus, the errors are very important for modellings A and B. The results obtained with DST are satisfactory.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The calculation shall be based on the following dynamic equilibrium and behavioral [bib2] relationships:

| $\frac{\partial {M}_{y}}{\partial x}+{V}_{z}=⟨\rho I⟩\frac{{\partial}^{2}{\theta}_{y}}{\partial {t}^{2}}$ | $\frac{\partial {V}_{z}}{\partial x}=⟨\rho S⟩\frac{{\partial}^{2}{v}_{z}}{\partial {t}^{2}}$ |
| --- | --- |
| ${M}_{y}=⟨EI⟩\frac{\partial {\theta}_{y}}{\partial x}$ | ${V}_{z}=k⟨GS⟩\left(\frac{\partial {v}_{z}}{\partial x}-{\theta}_{y}\right)$ |

These relationships allow to write the $v(x,t)$ transverse dynamic bending motion equation. The equation is obtained at the proper frequencies after having associated the boundary conditions.

The equation at the eigenfrequencies shall be written:

$\sin({X}_{2})=0$ with ${X}_{2}={\left[{\stackrel{ˉ}{\omega}}^{2}\frac{(1+a)}{2}+\sqrt{{\stackrel{ˉ}{\omega}}^{2}({\stackrel{ˉ}{\omega}}^{2}{(\frac{1-a}{2})}^{2}+\frac{1}{{r}^{2}})}\right]}^{1/2}$

et

${\overline{\omega}}^{2}=\frac{⟨\rho I⟩{\omega}^{2}{L}^{2}}{⟨EI⟩}$ ; ${\overline{r}}^{2}=\frac{⟨\rho I⟩}{⟨\rho S⟩{L}^{2}}$  ; $a=\frac{⟨\rho S⟩⟨EI⟩}{k⟨\rho I⟩⟨GS⟩}$

The equation solutions at the eigenfrequencies then write: ${X}_{2}=n\pi (n=1,2,3,\mathrm{...})$

## 7 Summary of the results

DKT Modelling is not suitable for Modelling this test case, errors are very important. DKT formulation does not take into account transverse shear unlike DST Modelling. For this type of example, where the structure is made of a composite and relatively thick material ($h/L=0.1$), it is preferable to use DST Modelling.

The results obtained with DST are:

- satisfactory for the first 3 frequencies with mesh cell TRIA3 and for the first 5 frequencies for mesh cell QUAD4 with better accuracy for mesh cell QUAD4,
- $\text{10\%}$ error for the 4th and 5th frequency with mesh cell TRIA3 is significant. A finer mesh should improve the results by having a better representation of the latest modes.
