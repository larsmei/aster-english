# v6.04.207 SSNV207 – Cyclic shear test including micro-discharges

*Summary*

A stress-controlled cyclic shear test is conducted
for three behavioural models: Hujeux (modelization A), Iwan (modelization B) and CSSM (modelization C).
During the load-discharge cycles, micro-discharges are carried out.
The calculated solutions are compared with results from Code_Aster for an identical cyclic loading path without micro-discharges.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The cyclic shear test is performed on a single Gauss point.
The test is therefore fully checked in terms of the stresses and deformations imposed.

Using the SIMU_POINT_MAT command allows you to directly impose a deformation and/or stress field.

- ${\mathrm{d}\sigma}_{xx}={\mathrm{d}\sigma}_{yy}={\mathrm{d}\sigma}_{zz}=0$
- ${\mathrm{d}\varepsilon}_{yz}={\mathrm{d}\varepsilon}_{zx}=0$

Differences between GEFDYN and Code_Aster suggest a suspicious GEFDYN management of ring deviatory ring hardening
While the model predicts an elastic micro-discharge, GEFDYN offers a complete hardening of the cyclic deviatory circle
until perfect plasticity, before defining a cyclic circle with elastic radius. The behaviour of the Code_Aster respects the elastic character
The discharge and the discharge shall be carried out at the exit of this micro-discharge with a hardening slope identical to that obtained before.

GEFDYN development team is notified of the discrepancies between the two calculation codes.
