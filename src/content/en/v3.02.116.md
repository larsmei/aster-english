# v3.02.116 SSLP116 – Square flexion plate – constant temperature gradient

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the calculation of displacements, moments and stresses in a square plate, simply supported, in bending subject to a constant temperature gradient.

modellings :

- $A$ Modelling: DKT with mesh cells TRIA3
- $B$ Modelling: DKT with mesh cells QUAD4
- $C$ Modelling: COQUE_SOLIDE with mesh cells HEXA9

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.116/geometrie.jpg)

Thickness = $\mathrm{0.25cm}$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=30.\times {10}^{6}N/{\mathit{cm}}^{2}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\alpha =6.5\times {10}^{-6}/°C$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

boundary conditions :

- On the $\mathit{AB}$ edge: $\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DRX}=0$
- On the $\mathit{AD}$ edge: $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DRY}=0$
- On the $\mathit{BC}$ and $\mathit{CD}$ edges: $\mathit{DZ}=0$

Loading

- The load applied is a temperature load that varies as follows:

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.116/Shape17.gif)

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None
