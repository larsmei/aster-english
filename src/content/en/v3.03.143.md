# v3.03.143 SSLS143 - Cantilever beam with excreted core

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the calculation of EPSI_ELGA and DEGE_ELNO options for multifibre beams of Euler-Bernoulli POU_D_EM, including if the reference axis is not confused with the elastic centre line.

The test case also validates the calculation of the elastic matrix (RIGI_MECA) when the reference axis is not confused with the elastic centre line (excenter).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A $L=1m$ length console beam (see Figure 1).

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.143/1000020100000197000000D78F05AB9371C20FFA.png)

Figure 1: Poutre console

### 1.2 Material properties

The right section is rectangular $b\times h=0.4\times 1{m}^{2}$ , and homogeneous with a material of Young's modulus $E=3\times {10}^{10}\mathit{Pa}$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The beam is clamped at one end and charged by $F={10}^{6}N$ force at its other end (see Figure 1)

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Analytical expressions

Starting from the clamped end, the expression of the bending moment is: $M(x)=F(x-L)$

The deflection at the loaded end of the beam is $f=\frac{{\mathit{FL}}^{3}}{{\mathit{EI}}_{{G}_{0}}}$ where ${I}_{{G}_{0}}$ is the quadratic moment calculated at the barycentre of ${G}_{0}$ section: ${I}_{{G}_{0}}={\int}_{S}{(y-{y}_{{G}_{0}})}^{2}\mathit{dS}$ .

The curvature in a point located at a distance $x$ from the clamped support is ${\chi }_{s}(x)=-\frac{M(x)}{{\mathit{EI}}_{{G}_{0}}}$. Due to the excentre of the reference axis, the lengthening of the beam (at the level of this axis) is:

${ϵ}_{s}(x)=-\frac{{A}_{G}}{S}{\chi }_{s}(x)$ where $S$ is the section's l'aire and ${A}_{G}$ the section's static moment in relation to an axis passing through $G$: ${A}_{G}={\int}_{S}\mathit{zdS}$ .

The point $(x,y,z)$ coordinate deformation is: $ϵ={ϵ}_{s}(x)-{\chi }_{s}(x)z$ , and the stress at the same point is: $\sigma =Eϵ$

### 2.2 Calculation of the characteristics of the right section

To eliminate uncertainty in the numerical calculation of the geometrical characteristics of the right section (low number of fibres), the values used in the reference solution are calculated as in the numerical calculation:

$S={\sum}_{\mathit{fibres}}{S}_{i}$ ${A}_{G}={\sum}_{\mathit{fibres}}{z}_{i}{S}_{i}$ ${I}_{G}={\sum}_{\mathit{fibres}}{z}_{i}^{2}{S}_{i}$ ${I}_{{G}_{0}}={\sum}_{\mathit{fibres}}{({z}_{i}-{z}_{{G}_{0}})}^{2}{S}_{i}$

where ${z}_{i}$ is the central control of $i$ and ${S}_{i}$ the central control of $i$.
