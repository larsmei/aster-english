# v3.04.301 SSLV301 - Cylindrical beam console under linearly distributed load

**Summary:**

The purpose of the test is to validate a linearly distributed load, based on a 2D analysis with Fourier series decomposition of the load.

Here we make two calculations:

1. a calculation with the first 2 modes (0 and 1),
2. a calculation with the first 10 modes.
