# v8.21.100 ADLV100 - Piston coupled to a fluid column

**Summary:**

Calculation of the first mode acoustic fluid-structure coupling of a fluid system1 - fluid piston 2.

The stiffness and mass matrices of the fluid and fluid-structure coupling elements are tested.

The free surface boundary condition is also tested.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v8.21.100/1000246E0000267300000F4C2D8EFD6EA689E23E.svg)

${L}_{a}=0.075$ , ${L}_{s}=0.025$ , ${L}_{e}=0.05m$

constant square section, $a=0.05m$ or circular section (in the case axisymmetric) constant radius $R=a=0.05m$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

| Solid: $E={2.10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$ | ${\rho}_{s}=7800\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ | $\nu =0.3$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| air : ${c}_{a}=340m/s$ | ${\rho}_{a}=1.2\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ |  |
| water : ${c}_{e}=1400m/s$ | ${\rho}_{e}=1000\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ | $c=\mathrm{célérité}\mathrm{du}\mathrm{son}$ |

$c=$ speed of sound in the fluid

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

- For all $M$ points on the $(\mathrm{M1}\mathrm{M2}\mathrm{M3}\mathrm{M4})$ side the pressure and displacement potential are zero (free surface condition),
- For the solid $P$ points, all degrees of freedom points are blocked except translation into $x$ so that this solid behaves like a piston on the $x$ axis.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

At low frequencies, the acoustic wavelengths of the movements envisaged are large compared to the characteristic dimension of the $(\omega L/c\ll 1)$ fluid volume. The problem is therefore one-dimensional according to the $x$ axis.

It is shown [bib1] that a light fluid like air acts essentially as an added stiffness while a heavy fluid behaves only as an added mass. Therefore, the first specific frequency of the system can be calculated:

$$
\omega =\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}
$$

With:

$$
k={k}_{\text{air}}={\rho}_{a}{c}_{a}^{2}\frac{S}{{L}_{a}}
$$

Thus, we find:

$$
\omega =\sqrt{\frac{{\rho}_{a}{c}_{a}^{2}}{{L}_{a}\left({\rho}_{s}{L}_{s}+{\rho}_{e}{L}_{e}\right)}}
$$

**Note:**

*The first clean pulse* $\omega$ *of the system checks well* $(\omega L/c\ll 1)$ *.*

### 2.2 Bibliographical references

1. GIBERT - Vibration of Structures . Interaction with fluids. Sources of random excitement - Collection of the EDF Research and Studies Directorate.
