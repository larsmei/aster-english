# v6.02.119 SSNL119 - Static response of a reinforced concrete beam (rectangular section) with nonlinear behaviour

**Summary:**

The problem is to analyze the response of a reinforced concrete beam in 3 points bending until the failure through [R3.08.08](doc:r3.08.08) multifibre beam Modelling or plate Modelling using homogenized reinforced concrete law. This test corresponds to a static analysis of a beam with nonlinear behavior. The modellings of the test are:

- Modelling A: Concrete is modelled with the softening damaging law of Mazars behavior in its $\mathrm{1D}$ [R7.01.08](doc:r7.01.08) version and steel is elastoplastic, the type d-elements used is POU_D_EM;
- Modelling B: the reinforced concrete beam is modelled with the homogenized GLRC_DM law and a steel grid according to an elasto-plastic law representing the lower steels;
- Modelling C: the reinforced concrete beam is modelled with homogenized GLRC_DM law with a medium-sized reinforcement section;
- Modelling D: reinforced concrete beam is modelled with homogenized DHRC law.
- Modelling E: copy of Modelling A with elements POU_D_TGM to validate EPSI_ELGA and EPSP_ELGA options for this type d

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Simple support in $B$: $\mathit{DY}=0$ .

$A$ dual support: $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DZ}=0$ as well as $\mathit{DRX}=\mathit{DRY}=0$ .

Virtually static loading: $\mathit{DY}$ monotone downward displacement applied at mid-trace $C$ (3 point bending test), according to a linear time function:

| $t$ | $\mathit{DY}$ |
| --- | --- |
| $0,0$ | $0,0\mathrm{cm}$ |
| $3,0$ | $-3,0\mathit{cm}$ |
| $5,0$ | $-5,0\mathit{cm}$ |

The final displacement applied for Modelling A is $-3,0\mathit{cm}$ .

For Modelling D, the displacement is imposed according to $\mathit{DZ}$ .

**Note:** transverse frames are not included in the calculations.

## 8 Summary of the results

The tests are performed in non-regression because there is no reference with the Mazars constitutive law or GLRC_DM. The Mazars Law coefficients are set to suit the best of a concrete behavior curve obtained with the Law of La Borderie (which is no longer available in *Code_Aster*).

For modellings B, C and D, the results are also compared with experimental measurements.
