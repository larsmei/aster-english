# v1.01.101 ZZZZ101 - Validation of AFFE_CARA_ELEM and POST_ELEM Operators

**Summary:**

Validation of AFFE_CARA_ELEM and POST_ELEM operators.

This test is for the calculation of mass, centre of gravity and centre of gravity inertia tensor for the following modellings:

- Discreet elements: DIS_TR and DIS_T,
- elements bar : BARRE,
- elements beam: POU_D_E, POU_D_T,
- elements of hulls : DKT, DST, Q4G,
- elements density: 3D.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

Mass and centre of gravity:

$\begin{array}{}m=\rho \underset{v}{\int}\mathrm{dv}=\rho \underset{v}{\int}\mathrm{dx.dy.dz}\\ {x}_{G}=\frac{\underset{v}{\int}\mathrm{x.dv}}{m}{y}_{G}=\frac{\underset{v}{\int}\mathrm{y.dv}}{m}{z}_{G}=\frac{\underset{v}{\int}\mathrm{z.dv}}{m}\end{array}$

Inertial tensor:

$\begin{array}{cc}{I}_{xx}=\rho \underset{v}{\int}(\mathrm{y²}+\mathrm{z²}).\mathrm{dv}& {I}_{xy}=\rho \underset{v}{\int}\mathrm{x.y.dv}\\ {I}_{yy}=\rho \underset{v}{\int}(\mathrm{x²}+\mathrm{z²}).\mathrm{dv}& {I}_{xz}=\rho \underset{v}{\int}\mathrm{x.z.dv}\\ {I}_{zz}=\rho \underset{v}{\int}(\mathrm{x²}+\mathrm{y²}).\mathrm{dv}& {I}_{yz}=\rho \underset{v}{\int}\mathrm{y.z.dv}\end{array}$

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

Masses and inertias for the different modellings.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

**Note:**

For one of the inshell modeled meshes, the solution is digital (non-regression).
