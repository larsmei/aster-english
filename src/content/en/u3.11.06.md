# u3.11.06 Modellings TUYAU_3M and TUYAU_6M

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings TUYAU_3M and TUYAU_6M:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear capabilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings.

The modellings TUYAU_3M and TUYAU_6M correspond to a formulation of line elements for straight or curved piping, which rely on a Timoshenko beam kinematics for the displacements and the rotations of the mean fibre and on a shell kinematics for the strains of the cross-section (ovalisation, warping, swelling). These transverse strains are decomposed into Fourier series. The TUYAU_3M modelling takes into account 3 modes at most, whereas the TUYAU_6M modelling takes into account 6 Fourier modes.

These modellings can be used for problems of three-dimensional piping in linear or non-linear mechanical analysis and in small displacements.

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

The degrees of freedom are, at each node of the support mesh cell:

- the six displacement components of the mean fibre (three translations and three rotations),
- three degrees of freedom corresponding to modes 0 and 1,
- for each Fourier mode, 6 degrees of freedom ($U$ corresponds to the warping, $V$ to the circumferential displacement, $W$ to the radial displacement).

|  | UI2 | VI2 | WI2 | UO2 | VO2 | WO2 | mode 2 |
|  | UI3 | VI3 | WI3 | UO3 | VO3 | WO3 | mode 3 |
| TUYAU_6M | DX | DY | DZ | DRX | DRY | DRZ |  |
|  | W0 | WI1 | WO1 |  |  |  |  |

### 1.2 Support mesh cell of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the finite elements, in a displacement formulation, are segments with 3 or 4 nodes.

## 2 Assignment of the characteristics

For these 1D structural elements, it is necessary to assign geometric characteristics which are complementary to the mesh data. The definition of these data is carried out with the command AFFE_CARA_ELEM associated with the following factor keywords:

- POUTRE

Makes it possible to define and to assign the characteristics of the cross-section.

Supported modellings: TUYAU_3M, TUYAU_6M

- ORIENTATION

Makes it possible to define and to assign a generatrix.

Supported modellings: TUYAU_3M, TUYAU_6M

- MASSIF

Optional, makes it possible to define and to assign a growth direction (necessary only in the case of a constitutive law ASSE_COMBU).

Supported modellings: TUYAU_3M, TUYAU_6M

## 3 Supported loadings

The loadings available are the following:

- 'FORCE_POUTRE'

Makes it possible to apply line forces

Supported modellings: TUYAU_3M, TUYAU_6M

- 'FORCE_TUYAU'

Makes it possible to apply a pressure in the pipe.

Supported modellings: TUYAU_3M, TUYAU_6M

- 'PESANTEUR'

Makes it possible to apply a gravity-type loading.

Supported modellings: TUYAU_3M, TUYAU_6M

The application of a thermal-expansion loading is carried out by defining the factor keyword AFFE_VARC under AFFE_MATERIAU [U4.43.03](doc:u4.43.03).

## 4 Non-linear capabilities

### 4.1 Constitutive law

All the constitutive laws available in C_PLAN are usable under COMPORTEMENT in STAT_NON_LINE and DYNA_NON_LINE (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).

### 4.2 Strains

Only the linearised strains, keyword 'PETIT' under DEFORMATION, are available in the constitutive relations (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).
