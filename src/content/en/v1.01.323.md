# v1.01.323 ZZZZ323 – Validation of local marker printing by IMPR_RESU/CONCEPT

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the MED file printing of local elements markers by the AFFE_CARA_ELEM operator.

5 modellings are made:

- A: beams, elements discreet
- B: hulls, grids and membranes on linear mesh cells
- C: hulls, grids and membranes on mesh cells quadratics
- D: 3D solid elements
- E: Solid elements 2D

The 5 modellings have different geometries, so the "Geometry" and "Reference Results" paragraphs will be treated in each model.

To test the MED file, it is reread by LIRE_CHAMP after being created by IMPR_RESU.

## 1 Modelling A

### 1.1 Geometry and modelling

The mesh consists of:

- 9 mesh cells SEG2 with 6 beam types and 2 discrete two-nodes types (there are two elements POU_D_T types).
- 2 mesh cells SEG3 with 2 types of 3 nodes hose modeled.
- 1 mesh cells SEG4 with 4 nodes pipe modeled.
- 2 mesh cells POI1 with 2 types of d的elements discrete to a node modeled.

All mesh cells with a length are oriented according to the $(1,1,0)$ vector.

### 1.2 Local marker orientation

To define the local benchmark of these elements are used the ANGL_VRIL keywords for beams and elements discrete two-nodes, GENE_TUYAU for pipes and ANGL_NAUT for the elements discrete node from factor keyword ORIENTATION from AFFE_CARA_ELEM operator (see U4.42.01).

The above table gives the chosen orientations for each element:

| Beams | ANGL_VRIL | $90$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discreet to two nodes | ANGL_VRIL | $-90$ |
| Discreet to a node | ANGL_NAUT | $(90,-90.0,90.0)$ |
| Pipes | GENE_TUYAU | $(0.,0.,1.)$ |

### 1.3 Calculation of local benchmarks

Local markers are formed by $x$, $y$ and $z$ vectors.

#### 1.3.1 Beams

The $x$ vector is defined by geometry and is therefore equal to $\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0\right)$. The $90$ value of ANGL_VRIL rotates the default $90°$ marker, which gives $y=(0,0,1)$ and $z=\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0\right)$.

#### 1.3.2 Discreet to two nodes

As with $x=\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0\right)$ beams, but this time we rotate in the other direction which gives $y=(0,0,-1)$ and $z=\left(\frac{-\sqrt{2}}{2},\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0\right)$.

#### 1.3.3 Pipes

No change for $x$. $(0.,0.,1.)$ was given to GENE_TUYAU, the $y$ vector is then the $(0.,0.,1.)$ projection on the orthogonal plane to $x$ , i.e. $(0.,0.,1.)$ itself.

We have $z=\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0\right)$ then.

But a different treatment of GAMMA1 angle in Code_Aster induces an additional 90° rotation around $x$, which finally gives:

$y=\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0\right)$ et $z=(0.,0.,-1.)$

**Note:**

The mesh cells SEG4 pipes are not compatible with those worn by mesh cells SEG3, so they are treated separately.

#### 1.3.4 Discreet to a node

In this case the local marker is only defined by the ANGL_NAUT value. The second component of the given vector gives $x=(0.,0.,1.)$. From the three components it is determined that $y=(0.,-1.,0.)$ and $z=(1.,0.,0.)$ .

### 4.2 Local marker orientation

| HEXA1 | ANGL_REP | $(\mathrm{45.0,45}\mathrm{.0,90}.0)$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HEXA2 | ORIG_AXE/ANGL_AXE | $(\mathrm{100.0,0}\mathrm{.5,0}.5)$ / $(\mathrm{0.0,}-45.0)$ |

### 4.3 Calculation of local benchmarks

Local markers are formed by $x$, $y$ and $z$ vectors.

The values given in ANGL_REP define the following benchmarks:

$x=\left(\mathrm{0.5,0}\mathrm{.5,}-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$ , $y=\left(\mathrm{0.5,0}\mathrm{.5,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$ et $z=\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0\right)$ .

The ANGL_AXE/ORIG_AXE torque is used in the case of a cylindrical geometry model. They define an ${e}_{z}$ axis as the axis of the cylindrical marker.

$x$ corresponds to the ${e}_{z}$ vector of this cylindrical marker, the point reference being the mesh cell barycentre here $(1.5,0.5,0.5)$. $y$ corresponds to the $-{e}_{\theta}$ vector and $z$ eu ${e}_{r}$ vector.

In this example $x=\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0,\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$ , $y=(0,1,0)$ and $z=\left(\frac{-\sqrt{2}}{2},0,\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$ .
