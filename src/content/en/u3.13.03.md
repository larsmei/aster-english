# u3.13.03 Modellings 2D_FLUIDE, 2D_FLUI_STRU, AXIS_FLUIDE, AXIS_FLUI_STRU

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings 2D_FLUIDE, 2D_FLUI_STRU, AXIS_FLUIDE, AXIS_FLUI_STRU:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear possibilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings.

The modellings 2D_FLUIDE (elements in a plane) and 2D_FLUI_STRU (1D fluid-structure interaction elements) correspond to the 2D formulation, under the linear assumption, of the coupled problem allowing the study of the vibratory behaviour of a structure in the presence of a non-viscous, compressible fluid [R4.02.01](doc:r4.02.01). At present, taking the free surface into account is not developed.

The modellings AXIS_FLUIDE (elements in a plane) and AXIS_FLUI_STRU (1D fluid-structure interaction elements) correspond to the axisymmetric formulation, under the linear assumption, of the coupled problem allowing the study of the vibratory behaviour of a structure in the presence of a non-viscous, compressible fluid [R4.02.01](doc:r4.02.01). At present, taking the free surface into account is not developed.

## 2 Supported loadings

The supported loadings are the following:

1. **VITE_FACE**

Makes it possible to specify the real vibratory normal-velocity field imposed as a loading on boundary elements.

Supported modellings: 2D_FLUIDE, AXIS_FLUIDE

1. **IMPE_FACE**

Makes it possible to specify the real impedance map imposed as a boundary condition on boundary elements.

Supported modellings: 2D_FLUIDE, AXIS_FLUIDE

1. **ONDE_FLUI**

Makes it possible to specify a real sinusoidal incident-wave pressure amplitude arriving normally to a face.

Supported modellings: 2D_FLUIDE, AXIS_FLUIDE

## 3 Non-linear possibilities

### 3.1 Constitutive laws

The only constitutive relation available in DYNA_NON_LINE, for the modellings **2D_FLUI_STRU** and AXIS_FLUI_STRU under COMPORTEMENT, is the RELATION 'ELAS' (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).

### 3.2 Strains

Only the linearised strains, keyword 'PETIT' under DEFORMATION, are available in the constitutive relations (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).
