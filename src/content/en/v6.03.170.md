# v6.03.170 SSNP170 – Taylor Patch Test

**Summary:**

This test consists of the quasi-static analysis of a problem with frictionless contact of which the analytical solution is known. It is particularly interested here in the integration of contact terms ( *patch-test* ) by the method *Mortar* *Local Average Contact* (LAC).It is a question of studying two identical blocks gridded differently and subjected to compression.

This test has 25 modellings. The objective is to test all possible pairing combinations with mesh incompatible for the type of pairing *Mortar* with LAC cutting in $2D$ and $3D$.

Thus, this test has ten modellings in $2D$:

- Modelling A: TRIA3 - TRIA3

- Modelling B: TRIA6 - TRIA6

- Modelling C: QUAD4 - QUAD4

- Modelling D: QUAD8 - QUAD8

- Modelling E: TRIA3 - TRIA6

- Modelling F: TRIA3 - QUAD4

- Modelling G: TRIA3 - QUAD8

- Modelling H: TRIA6 - QUAD4

- Modelling I: TRIA6 - QUAD8

- Modelling J: QUAD4 - QUAD8

And fifteen modellings in $3D$:

- Modelling K: TETRA4 - TETRA4

- Modelling L: TETRA10 - TETRA10

- Model M: HEXA8 - HEXA8

- Modelling N: HEXA20 - HEXA20

- Modelling O: HEXA27 - HEXA27

- Modelling P: TETRA4 - TETRA10

- Modelling Q: TETRA4 - HEXA8

- Modelling R: TETRA4 - HEXA20

- Modelling S: TETRA4 – HEXA27

- Modelling T: TETRA10 - HEXA8

- U: TETRA10 Modelling - HEXA20

- Modelling V: TETRA10 - HEXA27

- Modelling W: HEXA8 - HEXA20

- X: HEXA8 Modelling - HEXA27

- Modelling Y: HEXA20 – HEXA27

- Modelling Z: Cut PENTA6 , PENTA15, PYRA5, PYRA13

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.170/10000000000004D60000031ECE154F1D38C1AF9B.png)

Length $a=100\mathit{mm}$ .

Width $b=50\mathit{mm}$ .

$O$ point mid segment $\mathit{AB}$ (mark origin).

### 1.2 Material properties

**Schedules 1 and 2:**

Poisson's ratio: $0.3$

Young's modulus: $2000\mathit{MPa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Plate 1 is blocked in $Y$:

- On $\mathit{HG}$ $\mathit{DY}=0$ .

Plate 2 is subjected to a pressure imposed:

- On $\mathit{CD}$ : $\mathit{PRES}=25\mathit{MPa}$

> Symmetric conditions are imposed in $X$ to model only half of the domain.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Without friction, the contact pressure at the interface is equal to the pressure imposed.

### 2.2 Baseline

The contact pressure is constant and equal to $25\mathit{MPa}$ over the entire contact surface.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.

### 2.4 Bibliographic reference

  1. EL-ABBASI and K.J. BATHE: " *Stability and Patch Test Performance of Contact Discretion and a New Solution Algorithm* ", Computers & Structures, 79, 1473-1486, 2001

## 29 Summary of the results

This very simple example is designed to test a new technique for sub-elements subdivision-based contact terms integration. This method is enabled when MORTAR is selected for a contact area in CONTINUE formulation. All possible CREA_MAILLAGE/DECOUPE_LAC matches are valid.

This technique allows to obtain contact pressures very close to the analytical solution. Even on highly incompatible meshes, residues are obtained which are almost zero. Here, the pressure is constant throughout the contact surface, unlike the more conventional methods which usually generate oscillations on this type of case.
