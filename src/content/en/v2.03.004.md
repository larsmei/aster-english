# v2.03.004 SDLS04 - Cyclical substructure: clamped ring thin plate in its hub

**Summary:**

The scope of this test concerns structural dynamics, and in particular modal calculation by dynamic cyclic substructure.

The purpose is to calculate the proper modes of a axisymmetric structure (the clamped ring plate in its hub) as a cyclic repeater structure.

The model consists of an angular sector of $\text{20°}$ of the ring, meshed into triangles to which elements of plate type are assigned: DKT. Two calculation methods are tested:

- Craig-Bampton cyclic dynamic substructure
- Dynamic cyclic substructure of Mac Neal

The reference results are derived from an analytical calculation. They validate the modal computing tools by cyclic dynamic substructure implemented in *Code_Aster* .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in the SDLS04/89 form of the VPCS guide which presents the analytical solution as follows:

The solution of the frequency determinant established from the Bessel functions leads to the formula:

${f}_{ij}=\frac{1}{2\pi {R}_{e}^{2}}{\lambda}_{ij}^{2}\sqrt{\frac{E{t}^{2}}{12\rho (1-{\nu}^{2})}}$

With:

- $i$ = number of nodal diameters
- $j$ = number of nodal circles

and ${\lambda}_{ij}^{2}$ such as:

## 5 Summary of the results

The frequencies calculated by cyclic substructure, whether by Craig-Bampton or Mac Neal method, different from the direct modal calculation of less than $\text{1 \%}$.

This test validates both methods of sub-structure.
