# v7.31.100 WTNV100 - Undrained triaxial test with CJS (Level 1)

**Summary**

This test validates the CJS model's level 1. It is a triaxial test in undrained condition.

In the first two modellings calculations are performed only on the solid part of the soil, the undrained aspect being modelled by a zero volume deformation of the skeleton, modellings $\mathrm{3D}$ which differ from one another only by the mesh.

In the third model, hydraulic coupling is taken into account, the sample is fully saturated, the skeleton and fluid are assumed to be incompressible.

Because of symmetry, only one of the eighth samples tested is considered to be triaxial.

The containment level is $100\mathit{kPa}$ .

The results obtained with the CJS1 model are compared with an analytical solution.

## 6 Summary of the results

The values of *Code_Aster* are in perfect agreement with the reference values. Concerning coupling with hydraulics, this test proves that computerically, the CJS/THM coupling works and that the hydraulic equations are at least able to restore the zero volume variation when the water is incompressible.
