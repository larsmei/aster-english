DEFI_BASE_REDUITE, DEFI_DOMAINE_REDUIT and REST_REDUIT_COMPLET models.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference results are either values of non-regression (empirical mode values, reduction coefficients) or values (movements, temperatures, stresses and fluxes) calculated on a **complete** model.

We also test the reduced coordinates. It is recalled that the empirical modes ${\Psi}_{m}(x)$ are such that:

$$
u(x,t)\approx \sum_{m=1}^{\mathit{nbmode}}{\Psi}_{m}(x)\times {a}_{m}(t)
$$

For a given $u(x,t)$ field (temperature, displacement, thermal flux or stress) with ${a}_{m}$ the reduced coordinates.
