# v1.04.417 PYNL04 – Validation of CALCUL command with elements plates and shells

**Summary:**

This test is intended to validate the CALCUL command for elements DKT and COQUE_3D by comparing with the STAT_NON_LINE command.

## 1 Description

### 1.1 Geometry

The study model is a square plate on the side $c=\mathrm{1m}$ and $\mathit{ep}=\mathrm{0.05m}$ thickness.

![Figure](/figures/v1.04.417/10000201000002AB00000202745849D5D71BD1B2.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The properties of the steel for the plate are given in the following table.

| **Material** | **Storage** |
| --- | --- |
| **Young's modulus** | $2\times {10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$ |
| **Poisson's ratio** | $0.2$ |
| **Volume size** | $7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$ |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 boundary conditions

The side defined by the $\mathit{AB}$ segment of the plate is blocked at 0 for $\mathit{DX}$, $\mathit{DZ}$ and $\mathit{DRY}$ components, and the $\mathit{DY}$ component is blocked in $A$.

#### 1.3.2 Loading:

The load consists of applying a linear force according to $Z$ on both points $C$ and $D$ , going from $0$ to $50000N/m$ in one step (1.0 s).

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is obtained by numerical problem resolution (behaviour integration) using the STAT_NON_LINE command. The stress field, the nodal force vector, is obtained for comparison.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The mesh is composed of a single mesh cell QUAD4 modeled in DKT.

In modelling, one single iteration is required in the STAT_NON_LINE command to compare with the CALCUL results. The Modelling is under the linear elastic behavior relationship with DEFORMATION = ‘PETIT', as well as DEFORMATION = ‘GROT_GDEP'.

### 3.2 Comparisons and results

The difference between the stress field and the nodal force vector calculated by STAT_NON_LINE is tested and those calculated by the CALCUL command.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

The mesh is composed of a single mesh cell QUAD9 modeled in COQUE_3D.

In modelling, a single iteration is required in the STAT_NON_LINE command under the linear elastic behavior relationship only with DEFORMATION = ‘PETIT

### 4.2 Comparisons and results

The difference between the stress field and the nodal force vector calculated by STAT_NON_LINE is tested and those calculated by the CALCUL command.

Note: A significant difference is observed under the DEFORMATION = ‘GROT_GDEP' behavior for COQUE_3D. This difference is due to the non-taking into account of the change in normal in the CALCUL command.

## 5 Executive summary

This test validated the CALCUL command for the integration of the constitutive law elastic for elements plates and shells.
