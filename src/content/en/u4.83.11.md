# u4.83.11 Operator POST_RCCM

## 1 Purpose

Check criteria of the RCC-M. This concerns in particular:

- certain fatigue criteria of ZE200 in the post-processing of analyses on piping (with or without environmental effect),
- the fatigue criteria of B3600 in the post-processing of piping analyses,
- the equivalent-moment criterion of B3600 in the post-processing of piping analyses,
- the level 0 and level A criteria of B3200 (with or without environmental effect) in the post-processing of analyses on 2D or 3D structures.

The level 0 criteria aim to protect the equipment against the damage of excessive deformation and of elastic, elastoplastic or plastic instability. The level A criteria aim to protect the equipment against the damage of progressive deformation and of fatigue. The fatigue damage may also be computed by taking into account the effects of the PWR environment (recent modification of the RCC-M by the AFCEN).

As input to the command POST_RCCM, one must specify:

- either tables of stresses on an analysis segment coming from finite-element analyses ( TYPE_RESU_MECA=’EVOLUTION’ and TYPE_RESU_MECA=’B3200’ ) ;
- or the result of analyses on a piping line (TYPE_RESU_MECA=’B3600’).
- or the result of analyses on a piping line *and* tables of stresses coming from finite-element analyses (TYPE_RESU_MECA=’ZE200a’ and TYPE_RESU_MECA=’ZE200b’) ;

Produces a data structure of type table.

Before a first use, it is strongly recommended to refer to the reference document [R7.04.03](doc:r7.04.03) and the guidance document [U2.09.03](doc:u2.09.03).

## 3 Operands common to all the options

### 3.1 Operand TYPE_RESU

```text
TYPE_RESU=    /    'DETAILS',
              /    'VALE_MAX',
              /    ‘SYSTUS’
```

Type of values contained in the table produced as a result:

- DETAILS: the values computed at each time instant are supplied.
- SYSTUS: the values computed for each situation or combination of situations entering into the usage factor are supplied. This option is available only for TYPE_RESU_MECA= B3200, ZE200a or ZE200b
- VALE_MAX: only the maximum values are given; these maxima are not, however, necessarily reached at the same analysis time instant.

### 3.2 Operand TITRE

```text
♦    TITRE    =    titre
```

Character string describing the title of the table of values created, which appears in the printout of this table by IMPR_TABLE [U4.91.03](doc:u4.91.03).

### 3.3 Operand INFO

```text
♦    INFO    =    /1
                  /2
```

Allows a more or less detailed display in the message file.

### 3.4 Result types: keyword TYPE_RESU_MECA

Five types of post-processing are available in POST_RCCM: these post-processings follow the rules of different paragraphs of the RCC-M: B3600, B3200, ZE200.

*Results of RCC-M type B3600 for piping:* 'B3600'

With the option FATIGUE, this type of result is dedicated to the post-processing of mechanical analyses ( MECA_STATIQUE , STAT_NON_LINE , COMB_SISM_MODAL ) on a piping line. The fatigue analysis is carried out according to the rules of paragraph B3600.

Several situations may be defined, but in a single group. Seismic situations may be taken into account.

With the option MOMENT_EQUIVALENT, this type of result is dedicated to the post-processing of mechanical analyses ( MECA_STATIQUE , STAT_NON_LINE , DYNA_VIBRA, DYNA_NON_LINE ) on a piping line. This option allows the analysis of the maximum equivalent moments on the line. According to the formula of paragraph B3600:

$MEQ=\sqrt{({MT}^{2}+{MFY}^{2}+{MFZ}^{2})}$

The table produced contains the temporal maxima of the components MT, MFY, MFZ and MEQ as well as the value of the curvilinear abscissa of the node in question.

*Results of RCC-M type B3200:* 'EVOLUTION' and 'B3200'

'EVOLUTION' is dedicated to the post-processing of one or more thermomechanical analyses (MECA_STATIQUE, STAT_NON_LINE) on a 2D or 3D modelling. The results are transmitted via stress tables, extracted on the analysis segment or segments. The accessible criteria are:

- for the current zones (away from a geometric singularity): the level 0 criteria (option PM_PB), the level A criteria excluding fatigue (option SN) and the fatigue criteria (option FATIGUE_ZH210) on segments;
- for the geometric singularities: analysis of the crack-initiation factor (option AMORCAGE) on a circular cutting line around the singularity.

This option is well suited to cases where there are few situations to study, or to cases where it is necessary to determine the crack-initiation factor.

'B3200' is, by contrast, well suited to analyses on a component subjected to numerous situations, possibly distributed among several operating groups. An earthquake may be taken into account, as well as passage situations and sharing groups. One may also assess the environmental fatigue resistance with this option.

The loadings may be entered in several forms.

This type of result may therefore require the prior analysis:

- of the stresses associated with the thermal transients considered, which must be extracted on the analysis segment ( RESU_THER ) ,
- of the stresses associated with the pressure loadings: in the form of a transient ( RESU_PRES ) or in the form of a unit loading with two pressures for the stabilised states ( RESU_MECA_UNIT, PRES_A and PRES_B ),
- of the stresses associated with the loadings due to the forces and to the moments:

  - in the form of a transient ( RESU_MECA )
  - for unit loadings (unit global forces and moments applied at the boundaries of the model) with two torsors for the stabilised states (CHAR_ETAT_A and CHAR_ETAT_B). These forces may either be computed with code_aster, or come from the OAR database,
  - for unit loadings to which one applies a torsor, this torsor is computed by interpolation between two torsors ( CHAR_ETAT_A and CHAR_ETAT_B ) which correspond to the temperatures TEMP_A , and TEMP_B and by means of the temperature during the situation TABL_TEMP.

The accessible criteria are the level 0 criteria (option PM_PB ), the level A criteria excluding fatigue (option SN ), the fatigue criteria (option FATIGUE ) and the environmental fatigue criteria (option EFAT) on segments.

*Results of RCC-M type ZE200:* 'ZE200a' and 'ZE200b'

The ZE200 method of the RCC-M is a mixed method which combines B3200 and B3600. This type of result is therefore dedicated to the post-processing of mechanical analyses on a piping line according to B3600, combined with the thermal transients, for 'ZE200a'. The method 'ZE200b' in addition treats the stresses due to the pressure in the form of a transient.

These options are well suited to analyses on piping subjected to numerous situations, possibly distributed among several operating groups. An earthquake may be taken into account, as well as passage situations and sharing groups. One may also assess the environmental fatigue resistance with this option.

### 3.5 Table produced

The command POST_RCCM generates a concept of type table. The command IMPR_TABLE [U4.91.03](doc:u4.91.03) makes it possible to print the content of the table. For further information, one may refer to the document [U2.09.03](doc:u2.09.03).

### 3.6 Operand AXIS

```text
◊     AXIS    = / ‘NON’ [DEFAUT]
                / ‘OUI’
```

Indicator making it possible to declare the axisymmetry of the modelling.
For TYPE_RESU_MECA='EVOLUTION', if this operand takes the value ‘OUI’, the analysis of the equivalent membrane stresses Pm and bending stresses Pb will be carried out by an algorithm specially designed for axisymmetric models. Given the inhomogeneous distribution of the mass in the radial direction, this algorithm introduces a corrective shift of the mean line into the stress linearisation formulation.
For the other values of TYPE_RESU_MECA, if this operand takes the value ‘OUI’, the analysis will stop with an error message because no specific treatment has been implemented.

## 4 Operands specific to results of type B3200

### 4.1 Preliminaries

From several results of mechanical analyses on a component, one computes level 0 and level A criteria of the RCC-M.

The loadings may be entered in several forms.

This type of result may therefore require the prior analysis:

- of the stresses associated with the thermal transients considered, which must be extracted on the analysis segment ( RESU_THER ) ,
- of the stresses associated with the pressure loadings: in the form of a transient ( RESU_PRES ) or in the form of a unit loading with two pressures for the stabilised states ( RESU_MECA_UNIT, PRES_A and PRES_B ),
- of the stresses associated with the loadings due to the forces and to the moments:

  - in the form of a transient ( RESU_MECA )
  - for unit loadings (unit global forces and moments applied at the boundaries of the model) with two torsors for the stabilised states (CHAR_ETAT_A and CHAR_ETAT_B). These forces may either be computed with code_aster, or come from the OAR database. **The frame used for the 2D or 3D analysis must be consistent with the one in which the global forces coming from the beam analysis are expressed.**
  - for unit loadings to which one applies a torsor, this torsor is computed by interpolation between two torsors ( CHAR_ETAT_A and CHAR_ETAT_B ) which correspond to the temperatures TEMP_A , and TEMP_B and by means of the temperature during the situation TABL_TEMP.

#### 4.1.1 Option PM_PB

Option making it possible to compute the level 0 criteria, which aim to protect the equipment against the damage of excessive deformation, of plastic instability and of elastic and elastoplastic instability. These criteria require the analysis of the equivalent stresses of membrane *Pm*, of local membrane *Pl*, of bending *Pb* and of membrane plus bending *Pm+Pb*.

#### 4.1.2 Option SN

Option making it possible to compute the level A criteria (excluding fatigue), which aim to protect the equipment against the damage of progressive deformation. They require the analysis of the range of variation of the linearised stress at a point, denoted *Sn*. Under certain conditions, this option also allows the analysis of *Sn\** and of the thermal ratcheting (if the factor keyword RESU_THER is present).

**Note:**

*With the option ‘SN’, the analysis is carried out without combination between the situations defined: each situation will be processed in succession. In order to have the quantities (grandeurs) with combination between each situation, one must use the option ‘FATIGUE’.*

#### 4.1.3 Option FATIGUE

The fatigue analyses are carried out in the sense of the RCCM B3200 on the analysis segment. Two fictitious transients are thus identified in order to combine two situations with each other [R7.04.03](doc:r7.04.03).

#### 4.1.4 Option EFAT

The environmental fatigue analyses (option ‘EFAT’) are carried out in the sense of the RCCM B3200 on the analysis segment. Two fictitious transients are thus identified in order to combine two situations with each other. The effects of the PWR environment on the usage factor are then taken into account. ### 4.17 Table produced and example

The table produced by POST_RCCM depends on the analysis OPTION and on the type of result requested under the operand TYPE_RESU:

- if TYPE_RESU=’VALE_MAX’ (default option): the table is simple and contains only the maximum parameters of the various quantities (grandeurs) (Pm, Pb, Pmpb, Sn, Sn*, Sp, Salt, Fu, Fen, integrated Fen, Fu_environnement and the quantities (grandeurs) related to ratcheting) at the two ends
- if TYPE_RESU=’DETAILS’: the table is much richer. It contains the maximum parameters mentioned previously (row 'MAXI') plus all the parameters computed, for each situation (rows of type 'SITU') and each combination of situations (rows of type 'COMB'), with or without earthquake, and the combinations which actually entered into the analysis of the usage factor (row 'FACT'). It is then strongly recommended to print the table with successive filters so that it is easy to use.
- If TYPE_RESU=’SYSTUS’  : the table contains all the parameters computed for all the situations and combinations which actually entered into the analysis of the usage factor (row ' FACT ').
- **Printout of the maxima:** if TYPE_RESU=’VALE_MAX’ or with a filter in IMPR_TABLE: FILTRE=_F(NOM_PARA='TYPE', VALE_K='MAXI'), the table produced may contain, depending on the option requested, the following parameters: PM_MAX, PB_MAX, PMPB_MAX, SN_MAX, SN*_MAX, SIGM_M_PRES, SN_THER_MAX, CRIT_LINE_MAX , SP_THER_MAX, CRIT_PARA_MAX, SP_MAX, SALT_MAX, FU_TOTAL, FEN_TOTAL, FEN_INTEGRE and FUEN_TOTAL.

```text
FILTRE=(_F(NOM_PARA='TYPE', VALE_K='SITU' ),
_F(NOM_PARA='SEISME', VALE_K='AVEC' ou ‘ SANS’) ),
```

the table produced contains, depending on the option requested, the following parameters for each of the situations (with or without earthquake): PM, PB, PMPB, SN, INST_SN_1, INST_SN_2, SN*, INST_SN*_1,INST_SN*_2, SIG_PRES_MOY, SN_THER, CRIT_LINE, SP_THER, CRIT_PARAB, SP1(MECA), INST_SALT_1, INST_SALT_2, SALT and FU_UNIT .

FILTRE=(    _F(NOM_PARA='TYPE', VALE_K='COMB' ),
_F(NOM_PARA='SEISME', VALE_K='AVEC' ou ‘ SANS’) ),

the table produced contains, depending on the option requested, the following parameters for each of the combinations of situations (with or without earthquake): SN, INST_SN_1, INST_SN_2, SN*, INST_SN*_1,INST_SN*_2, SIG_PRES_MOY, SN_THER, CRIT_LINE, SP_THER, CRIT_PARAB, SP1(MECA), INST_SALT_1, INST_SALT_2, SALT and FU_UNIT .

## 6 Operands specific to the option FATIGUE of type B3600

### 6.1 Preliminary notes concerning the steps prior to this post-processing

From several results of mechanical analyses (MECA_STATIQUE, STAT_NON_LINE, COMB_SISM_MODAL) on a piping line, one computes fatigue criteria by the option FATIGUE.

The data required for the post-processing are summarised here (and detailed in the following paragraph):

- The geometry of the piping line.
- The material field: it is the map of the materials assigned to the mesh-cell groups of the mesh by AFFE_MATERIAU, to which one must add the fatigue curve, E_REFE, M_KE and N_KE (keywords RCCM).
- AFFE_CARA_ELEM makes it possible to assign the elementary characteristics.
- Stress indices (at each node of the mesh).
- The operating scenario containing the list of situations:
- For each situation:

1. Numbers of occurrences of each situation (therefore of each stabilised state).
2. Pressure and mean temperature of each stabilised state.
3. List of the mechanical loadings of each stabilised state.
4. The group to which the situation belongs.
5. The associated thermal transient.

- The results of the analyses for each mechanical loading (including the earthquake), identified by its number, with, for information, the name of the load case: element field at nodes of generalised forces, for each loading (EFGE_ELNO, or SIEF_ELNO).
- For each node, a reference to a thermal result defined below.
- Results of the thermal analyses: the 2D or 3D finite-element analyses which give this information depend both on the geometry and on the transient. One therefore has one thermal analysis per type of junction, and per type of transient. In practice one carries out two POST_RELEVE_T per transient and per different type of thickness or of geometry: one POST_RELEVE_T with the option EXTRACTION, and a second with the option MOYENNE

The preliminary analyses to be carried out are therefore:

- Beam-type analyses (elastic analysis) for each loading (one uses only the moments, expressed in a frame local to each element, a frame assumed identical for all the results) making up each of the two stabilised states of each situation.
- A seismic analysis (inertial response and anchor displacements) (a single type of earthquake taken into account).
- The analysis of each thermal transient, on as many 2D or 3D meshes as there are different thicknesses or components.

The operands and keywords of the option FATIGUE have been chosen so as to allow a later use in connection with the OAR tool. They are therefore inspired by the specifications of the OAR database [3].

### 6.2 Operand CHAM_MATER

```text
♦    CHAM_MATER    =    chmat
```

It is the material field containing, for all the mesh cells of the model, the material characteristics useful to FATIGUE and defined under the keywords ELAS_FO, FATIGUE and RCCM of DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01) (E, NU, ALPHA, WOHLER, E_REFE, M_KE, N_KE, SM).

**Note on the fatigue curves:**

*For small stress amplitudes, the problem of extending the fatigue curve may arise: for example, for the RCCM fatigue curves beyond 106 cycles, the corresponding stress, 180 MPa, is regarded as an endurance limit, that is, any stress lower than 180 MPa must produce a zero usage factor, or an infinite allowable number of cycles.*

*The method adopted here corresponds to this notion of endurance limit: if the stress amplitude is lower than the first abscissa of the fatigue curve, a zero usage factor is taken.*

## 8 Operands specific to results of type ZE200a and ZE200b

### 8.1 Preliminaries

From several results of mechanical analyses on a piping line, one computes only level A criteria of the RCC-M (therefore no option 'PM_PB'). The methods 'ZE200a' and 'ZE200b' are very similar. Indeed only the name ZE200 exists in the RCC-M (and not ZE200a and ZE200b). The difference between the two methods lies in the definition of the pressure loading. In 'ZE200b', the pressure loading is in the form of a transient and therefore requires an analysis on the 2D or 3D mesh in order to extract on the segment the stresses due to the pressure, whereas in 'ZE200a' the pressure enters into the equations of type B3600.

Preliminary analyses to be carried out in code_aster or to be extracted from the OAR database (if available):

- Analysis of each thermal transient, on the 2D or 3D mesh.
- Beam-type analyses (elastic analysis) for each loading (one uses only the moments, expressed in a frame local to each element, a frame assumed identical for all the results) making up each of the two stabilised states of each situation.

The data required for the post-processing are summarised here:

- The material (assumed unique at first) which the segment under study passes through: isotropic elastic material to which one must add the fatigue curve, E_REFE, M_KE and N_KE.
- The geometry of the piping line.
- Stress indices
- The operating scenario (available in OAR) containing the list of situations:
- For each situation:
- Numbers of occurrences of each situation.
- Pressure and mean temperature of each stabilised state if 'ZE200a'.
- Associated pressure transient if 'ZE200b'.
- List of the mechanical loadings of each stabilised state.
- The group to which the situation belongs.
- The associated thermal transient. The temperature during the situation if one wishes to assess the environmental fatigue resistance.
- The definition of each mechanical loading (including the earthquake), identified by its number, with, for information, the name of the load case, and the torsor of generalised moments corresponding to this loading, to be applied at the boundaries of the model.
- The results of the thermal analyses: extraction of the stresses on a segment of the 2D or 3D finite-element model. One therefore has one thermal analysis per transient.

#### 8.1.1 Option SN

Option making it possible to compute the level A criteria (excluding fatigue), which aim to protect the equipment against the damage of progressive deformation. They require the analysis of the range of variation of the linearised stress at a point, denoted Sn, and of the amplitude Sn*.

**Note:**

*With the option ‘SN’, the analysis is carried out without combination between the situations defined: each situation will be processed in succession. In order to have the quantities (grandeurs) with combination between each situation, one must use the option ‘FATIGUE’ or 'EFAT'.*

#### 8.1.2 Option FATIGUE

The fatigue analyses (option ‘FATIGUE’) are carried out in the sense of the RCCM ZE200 on the analysis segment. Two fictitious transients are thus identified in order to combine two situations with each other [R7.04.03](doc:r7.04.03).

#### 8.1.3 Option EFAT

The environmental fatigue analyses (option ‘EFAT’) are carried out on the analysis segment. Two fictitious transients are thus identified in order to combine two situations with each other. The effects of the PWR environment on the usage factor are then taken into account. One must add the factor keyword 'ENVIRONNEMENT' and the keywords 'O_ETOILE' and 'TABL_TEMP' under each occurrence of the factor keyword 'SITUATION' [R7.04.03](doc:r7.04.03).

*The temperature must imperatively be defined on the nodes of the analysis segment considered (a column ABSC_CURV is therefore required).*

### 8.15 Table produced and example

The table produced by POST_RCCM depends on the analysis OPTION and on the type of result requested under the operand TYPE_RESU:

- if TYPE_RESU=’VALE_MAX’ (default option): the table is simple and contains only the maximum parameters of the various quantities (grandeurs) (Sn, Sn*, Sp, Salt, Fu, Fu_environnement and the quantities (grandeurs) related to ratcheting) at the two ends
- if TYPE_RESU=’DETAILS’: the table is much richer. It contains the maximum parameters mentioned previously (row 'MAXI') plus all the parameters computed, for each situation (rows of type 'SITU') and each combination of situations (rows of type 'COMB'), with or without earthquake, and the combinations which actually entered into the analysis of the usage factor (row 'FACT'). It is then strongly recommended to print the table with successive filters so that it is easy to use.
- If TYPE_RESU=’SYSTUS’  : the table contains all the parameters computed for all the situations and combinations which actually entered into the analysis of the usage factor (row ' FACT ').
- **Printout of the maxima:** if TYPE_RESU=’VALE_MAX’ or with a filter in IMPR_TABLE: FILTRE=_F(NOM_PARA='TYPE', VALE_K='MAXI'), the table produced may contain, depending on the option requested, the following parameters: SN_MAX, SN*_MAX, SIGM_M_PRES, SN_THER_MAX, CRIT_LINE_MAX , SP_THER_MAX, CRIT_PARA_MAX, SP_MAX, SALT_MAX, FU_TOTAL , FEN_TOTAL, FEN_INTEGRE and FUEN_TOTAL.
- **Printout of the quantities (grandeurs) of each situation:** with a filter in IMPR_TABLE:

```text
FILTRE=(_F(NOM_PARA='TYPE', VALE_K='SITU' ),
_F(NOM_PARA='SEISME', VALE_K='AVEC' ou ‘ SANS’) ),
```

the table produced contains, depending on the option requested, the following parameters for each of the situations (with or without earthquake): SN, INST_SN_1, INST_SN_2, SN*, INST_SN*_1,INST_SN*_2, SIG_PRES_MOY, SN_THER, CRIT_LINE, SP_THER, CRIT_PARAB, SP1(MECA), INST_SALT_1, INST_SALT_2, SALT and FU_UNIT .

- **Printout of the quantities (grandeurs) of each combination of situations:** with a filter in IMPR_TABLE:

```text
FILTRE=(    _F(NOM_PARA='TYPE', VALE_K='COMB' ),
_F(NOM_PARA='SEISME', VALE_K='AVEC' ou ‘ SANS’) ),
```

the table produced contains, depending on the option requested, the following parameters for each of the combinations of situations (with or without earthquake): SN, INST_SN_1, INST_SN_2, SN*, INST_SN*_1,INST_SN*_2, SIG_PRES_MOY, SN_THER, CRIT_LINE, SP_THER, CRIT_PARAB, SP1(MECA), INST_SALT_1, INST_SALT_2, SALT and FU_UNIT .

- **Printout of the combinations of situations which entered into the analysis of the usage factor:** with a filter in IMPR_TABLE:

```text
FILTRE=(    _F(NOM_PARA='TYPE', VALE_K='FACT' ), ),
```

the table produced contains, depending on the option requested, the following parameters for each of the combinations of situations which entered into the analysis of the usage factor FU_TOTAL : SN, INST_SN_1, INST_SN_2, SN*, INST_SN*_1,INST_SN*_2, INST_SALT_1, INST_SALT_2, SALT, FU_UNIT, NOCC_PRIS, FU_PARTIEL.

If the option ‘ EFAT ’ is called, the following quantities (grandeurs) are added to the maxima relative to the option 'FATIGUE': FEN_TOTAL, FEN_INTEGRE and FUEN_TOTAL. In addition, if TYPE_RESU=’DETAILS’ , two quantities (grandeurs) are added to the rows of type 'FACT': FEN and FUEN_PARTIEL.

For monitoring the analyses, additional information may be found in the message file, in particular for the option FATIGUE. One can thus follow, at the origin and then at the end of the segment: the processing of each of the situations and then of the combinations of situations, and the display of FU_TOTAL (including the part related to the sub-cycles and the part related to the seismic sub-cycles)

The test cases **rccm13a** ('ZE200a') and **rccm13b** ('ZE200b') provide complete examples of use. For further information, one may also refer to the document [U2.09.03](doc:u2.09.03).

## 9 Operands specific to results of type EVOLUTION

For a precise description of the analyses carried out by these options, one may consult the document [R7.04.03](doc:r7.04.03) and the user's guide [U2.09.03](doc:u2.09.03).

The material characteristics required for the analysis of the criteria are to be defined by the command DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01). The computed values and the limit values are stored in the table tabl_post_rccm, which one prints by means of the command IMPR_TABLE [U4.91.03](doc:u4.91.03).

**Note on the use of the material characteristics as a function of the temperature:**

*For the post-processing, the RCC-M B rules require the use of the material characteristics, such as Sm, at the maximum temperature of the transients.*

*The operator DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01) nevertheless allows the definition of the material characteristics as a function of the temperature (factor keyword RCCM_FO).*

*With the option 'EVOLUTION', the user must define the material characteristics, for the post-processing, only by the factor keyword RCCM of the command DEFI_MATERIAU. Indeed, in order to use this option 'EVOLUTION', the user has to supply as input data only stresses, the thermal evolution being ignored even in the case where it exists in the previous analysis steps.*

*With the option 'EVOLUTION', the use of the characteristics as a function of the temperature therefore leads to an execution error.*

The analysis is carried out in post-processing of one or more thermomechanical analyses (MECA_STATIQUE, STAT_NON_LINE) on a 2D or 3D modelling. The results are transmitted via stress tables, extracted on the analysis segment or segments. These stress tables may be created by the commands POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE. The possible analysis options are:

1. level 0 criteria by the option PM_PB;
2. level A criteria (excluding fatigue) by the option SN;
3. fatigue criteria (also of level A) by the option FATIGUE_ZH210;
4. crack-initiation criteria in singular zones by the option AMORCAGE.

The first three options may be called alone or simultaneously. The last option may be called only alone: it indeed requires a record of the stresses on a circular cutting line around the geometric singularity, whereas the other options are dedicated to segments crossing the structure.

This option is well suited to cases where there are few situations to study. It is not possible to take seismic situations into account.

### 9.1 Operand MATER

```text
♦    MATER    =    mat
```

It is the material containing the characteristics useful to POST_RCCM and defined under the keyword RCCM of DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01).

**Note on the fatigue curves:**

*For small stress amplitudes, the problem of extending the fatigue curve may arise: for example, for the RCCM fatigue curves beyond 106 cycles, the corresponding stress, 180 MPa, is regarded as an endurance limit, that is, any stress lower than 180 MPa must produce a zero usage factor, or an infinite allowable number of cycles.*

*One recalls that the fatigue curve is defined in the material properties (keyword FATIGUE / WOHLER) as being the number of cycles to failure as a function of the half-amplitude of the stress Salt. The small stress amplitudes therefore correspond to the extension to the left of the curve. Several situations may arise, according to the type of extension adopted in DEFI_FONCTION:*

- if PROL_GAUCHE = 'EXCLU' or 'CONSTANT', the analysis is carried out by assuming that the first value of the Wöhler curve supplied is the endurance limit of the material. In other words, any alternating stress smaller than the first value indicated in the DEFI_FONCTION will correspond to a zero usage factor. The method adopted here therefore does correspond to the notion of endurance limit;
- if PROL_GAUCHE = 'LINEAIRE', the curve is extended in a linear manner. Warning: the extension is not carried out in logarithmic coordinates, so one may have non-negligible usage factors even for low values of Salt. In order to return to the notion of endurance limit, it is then recommended to add, in the definition of the curve, the allowable number of cycles for a very low value of Salt (computed by hand, for example by interpolation with a power law).

### 9.2 Operand SY_MAX

```text
◊     SY_MAX    =    symax,
```

Conventional yield limit for the maximum temperature reached during the cycle. This operand is used only for the analysis of the thermal ratcheting (cf. § 9.1.4.2). If the yield limit SY_MAX is not defined, one takes the value defined under the operand SY_02 of the keyword RCCM in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01). If this operand is not defined either, the analysis of the thermal ratcheting is impossible.

### 9.3 Operand RAYON_MOYEN

```text
◊     RHO_MOYEN    = rho, (défaut : -1.)
```

Topological curvature of the mean line at the intersection with the extraction segment in the XOY plane of the axisymmetric model. The default value -1 implies that the segment being processed is locally straight. Otherwise, the curvature must be strictly positive.

This operand will be taken into account if the operand AXIS = ‘OUI’.

### 9.4 Option PM_PB

Option making it possible to compute the level 0 criteria, which aim to protect the equipment against the damage of excessive deformation, of plastic instability and of elastic and elastoplastic instability. These criteria require the analysis of the equivalent stresses of membrane *Pm*, of local membrane *Pl*, of bending *Pb* and of membrane plus bending *Pm+Pb*.

The necessary operands are MATER, the stress table TABL_RESU_MECA (built by POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE after the mechanical analysis at the post-processing location) and possibly the stress table TABL_SIGM_THER built from an analysis with the thermal loading alone.

The analysis points are the two ends of the analysis segment. If several extraction segments have been used to define one and the same stress table, the analysis is carried out in succession for each of them. This option makes it possible to obtain the peak stress $F$ in the output table of the operator, which is useful for the fatigue and progressive-deformation analyses.

The limit values are Sm and 1.5*Sm, Sm being the allowable stress as a function of the material and of the temperature, given by the keyword SM of the keyword RCCM in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01).

**Notes:**

*The analysis of PM and PMPB is carried out from the primary stresses only, therefore excluding stresses of thermal origin. If TABL_SIGM_THER is supplied, one assumes that the result indicated in TABL_RESU_MECA corresponds to a thermomechanical analysis and one therefore subtracts the thermal stresses from it. If only TABL_RESU_MECA is supplied, the analysis is carried out directly from the stresses indicated in the table.*

**Note:**

*For the option ``PM_PB`` in ``EVOLUTION``, one recovers in the output table of the operator the peak stresses* $F$ *which are defined by* $F = \sigma_{\text{totale}} - (\text{Pm_Pb} +  Q)$ *where* $Q$ *is the thermal secondary stress defined by* $Q = Pm_{\text{thermique}} + Pb_{\text{thermique}}$.*

### 9.5 Option SN

Option making it possible to compute the level A criteria (excluding fatigue), which aim to protect the equipment against the damage of progressive deformation. They require the analysis of the range of variation of the linearised stress at a point, denoted *Sn*.

If the user requests it (presence of the operand TABL_SIGM_THER) one also carries out the analysis of Sn*.

If the user requests it (presence of the operands TABL_SIGM_THER and TABL_RESU_PRES) one also carries out the analysis of the thermal ratcheting.

The necessary operands are MATER and the stress table TABL_RESU_MECA (built by POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE after the mechanical analysis at the post-processing location) to be supplied in the keyword TRANSITOIRE.

The analysis points are the two ends of the analysis segment. If several extraction segments have been used to define one and the same stress table, the analysis is carried out in succession for each of them.

The limit value of *Sn* is 3*Sm, Sm being the allowable stress as a function of the material and of the temperature, given by the keyword SM of the factor keyword RCCM in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01).

**Note:**

*The keyword TABL_RESU_MECA may be repeated several times under a single keyword TRANSITOIRE. For the analysis of Sn and Sn\*, there will nevertheless be no combination between the situations thus defined: each stress table will be processed in succession.*

#### 9.5.1 Computation of Sn*

If the operand TABL_SIGM_THER of the factor keyword TRANSITOIRE is present, one also carries out the analysis of *Sn\**.

In order for the analysis to be consistent and in accordance with the RCC-M, the stresses supplied in TABL_SIGM_THER must have been obtained with a thermal loading alone, it being known that the result given by TABL_RESU_MECA may be due to a combination of this thermal loading with other loadings. The time instants of the table TABL_SIGM_THER must therefore correspond to those of the table TABL_RESU_MECA.

#### 9.5.2 Computation of the thermal ratcheting

If the operands TABL_SIGM_THER and TABL_RESU_PRES of the factor keyword TRANSITOIRE are present, one also carries out the analysis of the thermal ratcheting. For this, one must also have previously defined the conventional yield limit for the maximum temperature reached during the cycle, either by the operand SY_MAX of POST_RCCM, or by the operand SY_02 of the keyword RCCM in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01). If no yield limit is defined, the analysis of the thermal ratcheting is impossible.

In order for the analysis to be consistent and in accordance with the RCC-M, the stresses supplied in TABL_RESU_PRES must have been obtained with a pressure loading alone.

In the result table there appear, for each end of each analysis segment, the yield limit SY, the range of variation of the stress of thermal origin SP_THER, the maximum of the general membrane stress due to the pressure SIGM_M_PRES and two allowable maximum values of the range of variation of the thermal stress, computed either by assuming a linear temperature variation in the wall (VALE_MAXI_LINE), or by assuming a parabolic temperature variation in the wall (VALE_MAXI_PARAB).

### 9.6 Option FATIGUE_ZH210

Option making it possible to compute the usage factor resulting from the combination of one or more transients, according to the method of RCC-M appendix ZH210.

The range of variation of the stress at each end of the analysis segment is computed from the stress tables TABL_RESU_MECA, for each combination of time instants belonging to the transient or transients defined by the user. One then applies a combination and cumulation method in order to obtain the total usage factor, cf. [R7.04.03](doc:r7.04.03).

The time instants corresponding to the extreme states must be specified by the user by the operands NUME_ORDRE, INST or LIST_INST.

**Notes:**

*The keyword TABL_RESU_MECA may be repeated several times under a single keyword TRANSITOIRE. For the fatigue analysis, the results contained in each stress table will be combined with one another.*

*For the operands NUME_ORDRE, INST or LIST_INST, a possible confusion is the use of the list of time instants of the thermomechanical analyses (MECA_STATIQUE, STAT_NON_LINE) instead of the list of time instants corresponding to the extreme states expected by the operator POST_RCCM. In the first case, all the analysis time instants are then regarded as stress extrema and lead to run times which may be large.*

### 9.7 Option AMORCAGE

Option making it possible to compute the crack-initiation factor at a singular zone. For this option, the stresses are to be supplied as input in the table TABL_SIGM_THETA and must correspond to the extraction of the stresses, in the local frame, on a circular cutting line of diameter D_AMORC (material parameter defined in the RCC-M) around the geometric singularity. The stress tables may be created by using the operator MACR_LIGN_COUPE(TYPE=’ARC’).

For this option, it is also mandatory to define in the material properties (keyword RCCM) the coefficients of the crack-initiation law (A_AMORC and B_AMORC), the diameter of the circle on which the stresses are extracted (D_AMORC) and the coefficient between stress and effective stress (R_AMORC).

### 9.8 Operand TYPE_KE

```text
◊     TYPE_KE    =    / ‘KE_MECA’,            [DEFAUT]
                      / ‘KE_MIXTE’
```

The elastoplastic correction factor Ke may be computed in two ways [R7.04.03](doc:r7.04.03):

- KE_MECA: this is the original method, the only one available in versions earlier than version 7.2;
- KE_MIXTE: this method decomposes the range of variation of the alternating stresses into a thermal part and a mechanical part. It has been authorised since the 1997 modification of the RCC-M.

### 9.9 Keyword TRANSITOIRE

This factor keyword makes it possible to define the transient or transients to be studied.

#### 9.9.1 Operand TABL_RESU_MECA

```text
◊     TABL_RESU_MECA    =    tabmeca
```

Table of the stresses on the analysis segment, built for example by POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE from mechanical results of type evol_elas and evol_noli.

#### 9.9.2 Operand TABL_SIGM_THER

```text
◊      TABL_SIGM_THER= tabth
```

Table of the stresses on the analysis segment, built for example by POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE on a result obtained with a thermal loading alone. This keyword allows in particular the analysis of Sn* [§9.1.4.1].

#### 9.9.3 Operand TABL_SIGM_THETA

```text
◊    TABL_SIGM_THETA= sigt
```

This operand is to be used only in the case of the option AMORCAGE [§9.1.6]. It corresponds to the table of the stresses, in the local frame, on a circular cutting line around the geometric singularity. The table must necessarily contain the columns ANGLE, ABSC_CURV, INST and SIZZ, where SIZZ corresponds to the stress sigma_theta_theta in the local frame of the cutting line. The diameter of the circle on which the stresses are extracted is a material parameter (operand D_AMORC of DEFI_MATERIAU / RCCM)

Such a table may be built by means of the operator MACR_LIGN_COUPE (TYPE='ARC', REPERE='CYLINDRIQUE').

#### 9.9.4 Operand TABL_RESU_PRES

```text
◊    TABL_RESU_PRES= tabpres
```

Table of the stresses on the analysis segment, built for example by POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE on a result obtained with the pressure loading alone. This keyword allows the analysis of the thermal ratcheting [§9.1.4.2].

#### 9.9.5 Operand NB_OCCUR

```text
◊    NB_OCCUR        =    /    nocc,
/    1,            [DEFAUT]
```

Number of occurrences for the analysis of the usage factor.

### 9.10 Operands TOUT_ORDRE / TOUT_INST / INST / LIST_INST / PRECISION / CRITERE

```text
◊    TOUT_ORDRE, TOUT_INST, INST, LIST_INST
```

These keywords allow the selection of the time instants corresponding to the fields grouped in the stress tables tabmeca, tabth and/or sigp under the symbolic names previously specified.

```text
◊    PRECISION, CRITERE
```

Keywords (optional) defining the precision (10-6 by default in relative terms) and the search criterion (RELATIF by default) of an order number from a time-instant value.

#### 9.10.1 Warning on the list of analysis time instants

In the post-processing of the stress analyses in order to check the criteria of RCC-M B3200, one must distinguish two lists of analyses.

In the stress analysis, it is the user's responsibility to supply a first list of analysis time instants with a time-step subdivision fine enough to ensure that all the extreme values of the stresses during the transient are computed.

For the post-processing with the operator POST_RCCM, a second list of time instants to be supplied by the user, via the operands TOUT_ORDRE, TOUT_INST, INST or LIST_INST and distinct from the previous list of computed time instants, indicates the **time instants corresponding to the extreme states** expected by the operator POST_RCCM.

The confusion of these two lists is a frequent error. By supplying the same list of time instants, all the analysis time instants are then regarded as stress *extrema*. This leads to run times which may be large and to excessive values of usage factors.

### 9.11 Execution phase

One checks that the analysis time instants of the table tabth are identical to those of the table tabmeca. On the other hand, one cannot check that the thermal transients which contributed to the mechanical results tabth and tabmeca are identical. It is up to the user to ensure consistency (including the material data).

> **Note:**

*If the various tables supplied as input contain the coordinates of the points, consistency checks are carried out: alignment of the points (for the options PM_PB, SN and FATIGUE_ZH210) or diameter of the circular cutting line (option AMORCAGE); check of the consistency between the various situations.*

*It is therefore strongly recommended not to delete the coordinates of the points in the stress-extraction tables.*

### 9.12 Example of use

An example of use of the operator POST_RCCM with results of type EVOLUTION may be found in the test case **rccm01**.

An analysis of the RCCM criteria proceeds in the following manner:

1. definition of the material parameters and of the fatigue curve,
2. definitions of the mechanical and thermal loadings,
3. linear or non-linear thermomechanical analysis,
4. (if analysis of Sn*) mechanical analysis with the thermal loading alone,
5. (if analysis of the thermal ratcheting) mechanical analysis with the pressure alone,
6. definition of the analysis segment and extraction of the results with POST_RELEVE_T or MACR_LIGN_COUPE,

then (possibly in restart):

```text
SN1=POST_RCCM(MATER=MAT,

TYPE_RESU='VALE_MAX',

TYPE_RESU_MECA='EVOLUTION',

OPTION='SN',

TITRE='SN, RESULTAT: RESU2b AVEC RESUTH',

TRANSITOIRE=_F(TABL_RESU_MECA = T_RESU2b,

TABL_SIGM_THER = T_RESUTHb, )

)

IMPR_TABLE ( TABLE = sn1 )
```

An example of use of the operator POST_RCCM with results of type EVOLUTION for the option AMORCAGE may be found in the test case **rccm09**.

For further information, one may refer to the documents [U2.09.03](doc:u2.09.03) and [R7.04.03](doc:r7.04.03).
