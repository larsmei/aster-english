# v1.01.410 PYNL03 – THER_NON_LINE in split commands

**Summary:**

This test case is designed to validate the method of solving a nonlinear thermal problem in burst commands. To do this, it reproduces the test case TTNL303 identically without using the THER_NON_LINE command.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution was obtained with the elements finite calculation software "IVOHEAT" [bib2] cited in the reference [bib1]. This solution is based on mesh consisting of 20 elements isoparametric at 4 nodes of identical size, using a modified Crank-Nicolson method with ${10}^{-6}$ accuracy.

### 2.2 Baseline

Temperature at:

- $t=10s$ for $x=\mathrm{0.01,}\mathrm{0.02,}\mathrm{0.04,}\mathrm{0.06,}0.08$ and $0.1$ ,
- $t=13s$ for $x=\mathrm{0.01,}\mathrm{0.02,}\mathrm{0.04,}\mathrm{0.06,}0.08$ and $0.1$ .

### 2.3 Bibliographical references

  1. Orivuori, "Efficient method for solution of nonlinear heat conduction problems", Int. J. num. Meth. Engng, vol. 14, No. 10, pp. 1461-1476, 1979

  1. Orivuori, "A finite element method applied to the solution of the transient heat conduction problem', License Thesis, Tech. Univ., Helsinki (1977), in Finnish.

### 3.3 Remarks

The time step discretization is as follows:

10 steps for $[0.,1.D-3]$ or $\Delta t=1.{D}^{-4}$

9 steps for $[1.D-3,1.D-2]$ i.e. $\Delta t=1.{D}^{-3}$

9 steps for $[1.D-1,1.D0]$ i.e. $\Delta t=1.{D}^{-1}$

9 steps for $[1.D0,10.D0]$ i.e. $\Delta t=1.0$

3 steps for $[10.D0,13.D0]$ or $\Delta t=1.0$

The results are in good agreement with the reference solution and are considered acceptable. This test case has been used to verify the realization of a nonlinear thermal calculation in burst controls, taking into account variable thermal conductivity and a variable boundary condition over time.
