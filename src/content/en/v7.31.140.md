# v7.31.140 WTNV140 - Anisotropic drained elastic triaxial test

**Summary:**

This test validates the mechanical part of the transverse anisotropy into THM. This is a triaxial test with zero pressure. This test can therefore be compared with a case of pure mechanics.

The anisotropy marker will be different from the main marker. The different geometries (3D, 2D, AXI) are tested.

Modelling A is an elastic case in 3D isotropy transverse treated in pure mechanics and then in HM.

Best Modelling an elastic case in orthotropic 2D treated in pure mechanics then in HM.

Modelling This is an elastic case in axisymmetry treated in pure mechanics and then in HM.

### 1.2 Material properties

- General case 3D (transverse isotropy)

Specific settings for ELAS_ISTR:

${\%}_{L}=9\mathit{GPa}$ ${\%}_{N}=18\mathit{GPa}$ ${N}_{L}=0.24$ ${N}_{L}=0.48$ ${G}_{L}=8.88\mathrm{GPa}$

The transverse anisotropy marker is defined by the nautical angles $\alpha =30°$ and $\beta =-60°$ .

- General case 2D (orthotropy)

Specific settings for ELAS_ORTH:

Modellings C and D, which are D_PLAN, will be made, a variant considering that the 2D plane corresponds to the anisotropy plan.

${\%}_{L}=9\mathit{GPa}$ ${\%}_{T}=18\mathit{GPa}$ et ${\%}_{N}=9\mathit{GPa}$

${N}_{L}=0.48$ ${N}_{L}=0.24$ ${{\rm N}}_{\%}=0.48$ ${G}_{L}=8.88\mathrm{GPa}$

The transverse anisotropy marker is defined by the nautical angle $\alpha =40°$ .

- THM-related parameters(here no impact since pressure is held zero:

$\text{PORO}=0.14$ ,the coefficients of Biot ${B}_{L}=0.3$ and ${B}_{N}=0.6$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On the $x=\mathrm{1m}$ plane edge: $P=\mathrm{25MPa}$ containment application

On the $z=\mathrm{1m}$ plane edge: $P=\mathrm{29MPa}$ containment application (case 3D)

On the $y=\mathrm{1m}$ plane edge: application of a $0,01m$ displacement following a $\mathrm{1s}$ ramp.

Symmetric conditions are applied to the other edges and the pressure is maintained zero everywhere (total draining).

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The initial constraints are anisotropic (in the overall plan), i.e.:

${\sigma}_{xx}=-\mathrm{25MPa}$ ; ${\sigma}_{yy}=-\mathrm{22MPa}$ ; ${\sigma}_{zz}=-\mathrm{29MPa}$
