# v2.01.109 SDLD109 – Calculation of spectra with consideration of floor-material interaction

**Summary:**

This test case gives an example of the use of CALC_SPECTRE_IPM[U4.32.12] macro-command that allows for the interaction of heavy equipment in the calculation of floor spectra. The relative or absolute reference processing and the correction of the initial state are validated.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.109/10000200000003550000011B7F5AF3F301FBC860.png)

Model A, representing the bending mode of a floor with heavy equipment, is modelled.

### 1.2 Model properties

The floor has the following properties:

- mass ${m}_{1}=1$ ,
- stiffness vertical ${k}_{1}=4000$ ,
- ${\xi}_{1}=0.07$ damping .

The proper frequency of the floor is ${f}_{1}^{\mathrm{\ast }}=10.07$ .

The equipment has the following properties:

- ${m}_{2-1}=0.05$ ${m}_{2-2}=0.025$ ${m}_{2-3}=0.025$ mass ,
- stiffness vertical ${k}_{2-1}=200$ ${k}_{2-2}=800$ ${k}_{2-3}=50$ ,
- ${\xi}_{2}=0.05$ damping .

The eigenfrequencies of each equipment are: ${f}_{2-1}=10.07$ , ${f}_{2-2}=28.47$ and ${f}_{2-3}=7.12$ .

In the case studied, the ratio of the masses is $\lambda =\frac{{m}_{2}}{{m}_{1}}=0.1$ . The frequency of a device being identical to that of the support, one is in a case where the interaction is maximum.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Boundary conditions: the node N1 is blocked.

Loading: Sine acceleration is imposed on $f(t)=\sin(2\pi 20t)$ spring mass system

The system's transient response is calculated with the DYNA_VIBRA operator on a physical basis (BASE_CALCUL='PHYS') as a transient (TYPE_CALCUL='TRAN'), with a 0,1 ms time step.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution corresponds to model B.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Model A is modeled with elements DIS_T

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of nodes : 2

Number of mesh cells: 3 DIS_T for mass modelling (M_T_D_N), stiffness (K_T_D_L) and shock absorber (A_T_D_L)

### 3.3 Results

The vertical response spectrum obtained from CALC_SPECTRE_IPM macro-command from the vertical acceleration of node N02 is compared to that obtained from model B. In one case, the relative reference resolution (single-set loading) is used.

A verification of pseudo_acceleration values is performed for several acceleration values.

The case is also validated as an absolute benchmark, compared to the relative result with a zero drive signal.

Finally, the initial correction (CORR_INIT='OUI') for which an initial non-null acceleration is required is validated. To do this, the drive signal will be shifted slightly in time: $f(t)=\sin(2\pi 20.(t+0.00001))$ . The response obtained will therefore remain very close to the initial calculation without this offset.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results showed a very good match with reference solution. The differences are below 3% and could be further reduced by decreasing the time step resolution, at the expense of the calculation time.
