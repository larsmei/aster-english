# v2.04.134 SDLV134 - Cyclic substructure: Cantilever beam – free nodes on the axis

**Summary:**

The scope of this test concerns structural dynamics, and in particular modal calculation by dynamic cyclic substructure.

The purpose is to calculate the proper modes of a axisymmetric structure (circular section beam, clamped at its base) as a cyclically repeatable structure.

The model consists of an angular $\text{60°}$ section of the beam, meshed in elements hexahedric density to which elements 3D are assigned. Only the dynamic cyclic substructure method of Craig-Bampton is tested. The Mc Neal method, as implemented, does not allow for free nodes on the axis.

The correct calculation is checked by studying the relative displacements of the nodes coincide with the squellet on which the complete result is returned.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

$\vec{z}$ vertical axis circular cross section beam:

- Length: $L=0.1m$
- Rayon: $R=0.01m$

### 1.2 Material properties

$E={2.10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$

$\nu =0.3$

$\rho =7\mathrm{800kg}/{m}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Clamped support of the face in $z=0$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Not applicable for modal analysis.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

In this test, it is checked that the nodes of the axis have the same displacement at the end of the beam. Indeed, the movement of the entire beam is reconstructed by rotation of the movements of a single sector of 60°. It is checked that the 6 nodes coincide on the z=0.1m axis have the same displacement.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The first compression tensile mode and the first two bending modes are checked. The test is performed on the relative displacement, which must therefore be zero.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

None.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

This cyclic repeatability structure is studied using the dynamic cyclic substructure method of CRAIG-BAMPTON.

A basic sector, consisting of a 60° angular sector, is meshed in hexahedrons.

The modal base used for the sector is composed of 15 modes and ** modes that are forced** associated with interfaces.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of nodes: 77.

Number of mesh cells and types : 30 mesh cells HEXA8

## 4 Summary of the results

Relative displacements obtained by restitution on a skeleton of the modes calculated by cyclic substructure using the Craig-Bampton method differ from the reference calculation of less than 1e-3%. This test validates the cyclic substructure method in the presence of nodes free axis.
