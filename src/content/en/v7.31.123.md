# v7.31.123 WTNV123 - Triaxial suction test fixed with Barcelona model

**Summary:**

This test validates the integration of the Barcelona [r7.01.17](doc:r7.01.17) model. This test in model A, in hydro-mechanical coupling (modelling HHM), includes two mechanical loading routes, the capillary pressure being fixed to a non-zero value (unsaturated test conditions):

- An isotropic compression path.
- A triaxial compression path.

The Modelling is performed on a hexahedral element 3D.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The capillary pressure remains constant at the $\mathrm{PRE1}={200}$ kPa value.
Similarly, the gas pressure is fixed to $\mathrm{PRE2}={1}$ Pa. In the first phase of the load, the isotropic compression is performed until the average stress $\sigma_m=-200$ kPa is reached. In the second phase, a triaxial compression is performed, during which the normal stress at the side faces remains equal to $-200$ kPa, while increasing the displacement on the upper face in the compression direction (v7.31.123-cube).

The boundary conditions symmetry are:

- On mesh cells *DEVANT* and *DERRIERE*: $DX=0$.
- On mesh cells *GAUCHE* and *DROITE*: $DY=0$.
- On the mesh cell *BAS* : $DZ=0$.

During the isotropic compression phase, the same normal stress is imposed on the three mesh cells *HAUT* , *DROITE* and *DEVANT*. During the triaxial compression phase, this normal stress remains imposed on *DROITE* and *DEVANT*. At the same time, the vertical displacement is imposed increasing in compression on the mesh cell *HAUT*.

It is recalled that during these two loads, neither the capillary pressure nor the gas pressure varies, and they remain equal to $\mathrm{PRE1}={200}$ kPa and $\mathrm{PRE2}={1}$ Pa respectively.
