# v3.03.146 SSLS146 - Calculation of railing on a portal

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the COMBINAISON_FERRAILLAGE macrocontrol, which allows the calculation of the dimensioning ferrication density and the identification of the associated loading cases. The calculation is carried out on a portal composed of a slab (plate) based on four poles.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The structure used is described in the following:

- a slab of dimensions dx = 10m and dy = 6m in the plane (x,y), and d ez thickness = 30cm.
- 4 posts on which the slab rests, 5m high, and with a rectangular cross section of dimensions: hy = 50cm and hz = 20cm. Local axis ‘y' of poles coincides with the overall X axis of geometry, while the local axis ‘z' coincides with the global Y axis of geometry.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The 4 poles are clamped in their bases.

Three cases of loadings will also be considered:

- $G$ clean weight of the structure
- ${P}_{r}=15000N$ over-fact pressure applied to the slab
- A horizontal translation force following ‘X

The ferrailing calculation will then be conducted in relation to 7 combinations of these 3 forces, corresponding to different limit states of calculation:

- Comb 1 - ELU FONDAMENTAL – ${P}_{r}$ dominant: $1,35G+1,5{P}_{r}+1,5\times 0,6{F}_{t}$
- Comb 2 - ELU FONDAMENTAL – ${F}_{t}$ dominant: $1,35G+1,5\times 0,7{P}_{r}+1,5{F}_{t}$
- Comb 3 - ELU ACCIDENTEL : $G+{P}_{r}+{F}_{t}$
- Comb 4 - ELSCARACTERISTIQUE – ${P}_{r}$ dominant: $G+{P}_{r}+0,6{F}_{t}$
- Comb 5 - ELSCARACTERISTIQUE – ${F}_{t}$ dominant: $G+0,7{P}_{r}+{F}_{t}$
- Comb 6 - ELSQUASI PERMANENT – ${P}_{r}$ dominant: $G+{P}_{r}+0,15{F}_{t}$
- Comb 7 - ELS QUASI PERMANENT – ${F}_{t}$ dominant: $G+0,3{P}_{r}+{F}_{t}$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The structure is initially at rest.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The railing is calculated using the Capra-Maury method (command CALC_FERRAILLAGE called by the COMBINAISON_FERRAILLAGE command).

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

Once the field of generalized forces is calculated, the ferrage field is calculated according to the Capra Maury method (for elements 2D), as well as the specifications of Eurocode 2 and the proposed calculation algorithms for the different calculation limit states (ELU/ELS CARA/ELS QP).

For further understanding, refer to the basic test cases reserved for CALC_FERRAILLAGE operator at the level of ssls134 and ssll13 test cases.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

RAS.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

This Modelling is similar to **modelization A**, but it is intended to cover previously untested keywords,
to ensure the proper functioning of the macro-command for all use cases.

It is important to note that `COMBINAISON_FERRAILLAGE` does not perform any direct calculation**: it simply iterates on the combinations and calls the `CALC_FERRAILLAGE` command.
As a result, some values associated with keywords do not necessarily have realistic physical meaning, and do not correspond to concrete engineering cases.

The **geometric and mesh** characteristics and the **quantities tested** are identical to those of Modelling A, with results evaluated at the same locations: in the centre of the slab and in support of pole A.

In this Modelling, two calculations are made:

1. **Calcul 1**: based on two combinations, one at the ELU Fundamental and the other at the ELS Characteristic, according to the BAEL 91.
The following parameters are enabled:

  - For **dalle**:

```
FERR_COMP = "OUI"
EPURE_CISA = "OUI"
FERR_MIN = "BAEL91"
```
  - For **pots**:

```
FERR_COMP = "OUI"
EPURE_CISA = "OUI"
FERR_MIN = "VALE_MIN"
FERR_SYME = "OUI"
SEUIL_SYME = 1.0e-03
RHO_LONGI_MIN = 1.0e-05
RHO_TRNSV_MIN = 1.0e-05
```
2. **Calculum 2**: also with two combinations, one at the ELU Fundamental and the other at the ELS Characteristic, but according to the EC2. This calculation is intended to test the steel classes not tested in Modelling A. One **A steel class for slab** and one **C class for poles** are used.
