# u3.11.05 Modelling POU_D_T_GD

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modelling POU_D_T_GD:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear capabilities,
- the test cases implementing the modelling.

The POU_D_T_GD modelling corresponds to a formulation of Timoshenko beam elements taking into account large displacements and large rotations (cf. [R5.03.40](doc:r5.03.40)).

It can be used for problems of three-dimensional beams in isotropic mechanical analysis and in linear elastic constitutive behaviour.

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

The degrees of freedom are, at each node of the support mesh cell, the six displacement components (three translations and three rotations).

| **Finite element** | **Degrees of freedom (at each vertex node)** |  |  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| POU_D_T_GD | DX | DY | DZ | DRX | DRY | DRZ |  |

### 1.2 Support mesh cell of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the finite elements, in a displacement formulation, are two-node segments SEG2:

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Finite element** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| POU_D_T_GD | SEG2 | MECA_POU_D_T_GD |  |

## 2 Assignment of the characteristics

For these 1D structural elements, it is necessary to assign geometric characteristics which are complementary to the mesh data. The definition of these data is carried out with the command AFFE_CARA_ELEM associated with the following factor keywords:

- **POUTRE**

Makes it possible to define and to assign the characteristics of the cross-section.

- **ORIENTATION**

Makes it possible to define and to assign the principal axes of the cross-sections of beam-type elements.

## 3 Supported loadings

The specific loadings, available in AFFE_CHAR_MECA, are the following:

- **'EPSI_INIT'**

Makes it possible to apply an initial-strain loading.

- **'FORCE_POUTRE'**

Makes it possible to apply line forces.

- **'PESANTEUR'**

Makes it possible to apply a gravity-type loading.

- **'INTE_ELEC'**

Makes it possible to apply the LAPLACE force acting on a principal conductor, due to the presence of a secondary conductor which is not necessarily straight with respect to this principal conductor.

## 4 Non-linear capabilities

### 4.1 Constitutive law

The constitutive law specific to this modelling, usable under COMPORTEMENT in STAT_NON_LINE and DYNA_NON_LINE, is the relation ELAS_POUTRE_GR (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).

### 4.2 Strains

Only the strain 'GROT_GDEP', which makes it possible to treat beams in large displacements and in large rotations, is available (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)). The strains used in the constitutive relation are the GREEN-LAGRANGE strains.

## 5 Examples implementing the modelling: test case

- Non-linear statics
- SSNL103A [V6.02.103](doc:v6.02.103): Analysis of the static deflected shape, in large displacements and in large rotations, of a beam clamped at one end and subjected to a bending moment at the other end.
- Non-linear dynamics
- SDNL103A [V5.02.103](doc:v5.02.103): Analysis of the response of a portal frame clamped at its feet and subjected to a dynamic force applied at the middle of its span and perpendicular to its plane.
