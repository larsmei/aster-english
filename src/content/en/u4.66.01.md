# u4.66.01 Operator CALC_MATR_AJOU

## 1 Purpose

Compute added mass [R4.07.03](doc:r4.07.03), damping or stiffness matrices.

The matrices are induced by a perfect, incompressible fluid, at rest or in potential flow, on a structure in 2D, axisymmetric 2D or in 3D.

The terms of these matrices are computed on the modal basis of the structure in vacuum.

The computation of the terms of the matrices is carried out by solving the LAPLACE equations within the framework of a **thermal analogy**. The computed temperature plays the role of a pressure in the fluid domain. One must therefore take care to define the fluid material by **thermal** characteristics, and to assign a **thermal** model to the part of the mesh which represents the fluid.

Thanks to this operator, one can determine, for example, the added matrices of a bundle of tubes, considered as a single structure immersed in one and the same fluid.

Within an analysis by dynamic substructuring, it is also possible to evaluate the added mass matrix on a generalised model.

For systematic uses of this command, and in order to simplify the writing of the command file, one will efficiently use the macro-command MACRO_MATR_AJOU [U4.66.11](doc:u4.66.11).

Produces a data structure of type matr_asse_gene_R.

## 3 Operands

### 3.1 Operand MODELE_FLUIDE

```text
♦    MODELE_FLUIDE = fluide
```

**Thermal** model that one assigns to the part of the mesh which corresponds to the fluid. The boundary elements at the fluid/structure interface must be present in the fluid model. It is on this model that one solves the Laplace equation with a boundary condition of "fluid flux" type, in order to have the pressure field throughout the fluid and *a fortiori* the pressure field at the fluid/structure interface.

The nodes of the elements of the fluid mesh must all be numbered in such a way that the Jacobian of the element is of positive sign over the whole fluid mesh.

### 3.2 Operand MODELE_INTERFACE

```text
♦    MODELE_INTERFACE = interf
```

**Thermal** interface model gathering all the boundary elements defining the fluid/structure interface. It is on this model that one computes the terms of the added matrices.

The computation depends on the **orientation** of the normal of these interface elements. One must take care that this normal is oriented, on **all** these elements, from the structure towards the fluid (adopted convention).

If the fluids bathing the structure are of different densities, one must define the interface model by as many mesh-cell groups, not geometrically coincident, as there are fluids in contact with the structure. For example, a pipe may have its inner surface in contact with a fluid of density 1, and its outer surface in contact with a fluid of density 2. The interface model is therefore built on two mesh-cell groups that are not geometrically coincident, one in contact with the fluid of density 1 (interfa1) and the other in contact with the fluid of density 2 (interfa2).

![Figure](/figures/u4.66.01/100004D20000223600001741958A414D1B56A3D8.svg)

### 3.3 Operands CHAM_MATER / CHARGE

```text
♦    CHAM_MATER = matflui
```

Material field defining the fluid immersing the structure. This material is defined by equivalent **thermal** characteristics in the operator DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01). The thermal conductivity (keyword LAMBDA) is **always taken equal to 1.** The specific heat (keyword RHO_CP) plays the role of **density of the fluid.**

```text
♦    CHARGE = charge
```

Thermal load of type [char_ther] necessary for the solution of the linear system resulting from the Laplace equation in the fluid. One must impose an **arbitrary** temperature (which in fact plays the role of a pressure) on an **arbitrary** node of the fluid mesh, in order to make the system non-singular. This operation is carried out with the operator AFFE_CHAR_THER [U4.44.02].

### 3.4 Operands MODE_MECA / CHAM_NO / MODELE_GENE

```text
♦    /    MODE_MECA = modes
```

Dynamic modes computed on the structural model. If one has several non-connected structures immersed in one and the same fluid, for which one wants to determine the added matrices comprising the coupling terms through the fluid, the structural model that one defines gathers the whole of the immersed structures. The modes used by the operator are the modes computed for the global structure.

```text
/    CHAM_NO = chamno
```

Nodal field of displacements assigned on node groups defining the fluid/structure interface. By assigning such a displacement field on the interface node group(s) by means of the operator CREA_CHAMP, one can, for example, easily simulate rigid-body modes of the structures. This operand is to be used if one wants to estimate the terms of the added matrices and of the coupling matrices for a plane geometry **without first carrying out a modal analysis** of the structure in vacuum, and if one wants to assign these terms in discrete elements (modelling DIS_T). The operand INFO must imperatively be active in order to print the computed terms.

**Warning:**

*The use of this operand* **excludes** *that of the operand* *NUME_DDL_GENE. One therefore cannot carry out a modal, transient or harmonic analysis using the operand* *CHAM_NO.*

*Indeed, in order to carry out a modal analysis in water, one must have a generalised mass matrix and a generalised stiffness matrix of the structure. This supposes having carried out a modal analysis on the structure in vacuum (which is precisely what one wants to avoid).*

*Ultimately, this operand is used only in order to* **evaluate the added mass matrix**, *the added stiffness and the added damping, and to use them in order to couple beam-type structures by discrete mass elements, for example.*

```text
/    MODELE_GENE = modgen
```

Generalised model built by the operator DEFI_MODELE_GENE [U4.65.02](doc:u4.65.02). This keyword is to be used when one carries out an analysis by dynamic substructuring, and one wants to compute the added mass matrix coupling the whole set of the substructures. In this case, the substructures may be in different mesh files, these files themselves possibly being distinct from the fluid mesh file. The substructures which present a repetitiveness within the fluid need be meshed only once, but one will take care, at the level of the fluid mesh, to mesh all the fluid/structure interfaces. Moreover, one must take care that the nodes of the fluid interface coincide as well as possible with the nodes of the structure interface, in order to be able to copy, on the basis of a geometric proximity criterion, the values of the structural displacement field onto the nodes of the fluid interface (see operand DIST_REFE).

**Warning:**

*The use of this operand* **makes mandatory** *that of the operand* *NUME_DDL_GENE.*

### 3.5 Operand OPTION

This keyword specifies the added mass, stiffness or damping computation that one wants to carry out, using respectively 'MASS_AJOU', 'RIGI_AJOU', or 'AMOR_AJOU'. The last two computation options require the presence of a stationary (thermal) potential (see operand POTENTIEL).

### 3.6 Operand DIST_REFE

Reference distance to be supplied when one carries out an **added mass** computation on a generalised model. This distance is an absolute geometric criterion intended for copying values of structural displacements into a thermal fluid model, in order to solve there the Laplace equation of the unsteady pressure field. By default, it is equal to 10–2 m.

### 3.7 Operand NOEUD_DOUBLE

```text
◊    NOEUD_DOUBLE = 'OUI'
```

This operand is to be used when one carries out an **added mass** computation from a generalised model which comprises a substructure meshed by a wireframe or surface mesh (*i.e.* without thickness, such as a beam or a shell) and surrounded by two fluids. One must in this case, at the mesh level, duplicate the nodes of the fluid interfaces with respect to those of the structure, in order to be able to compute the jump in hydrodynamic pressure on either side of the structure (*cf.* figure below).

![Figure](/figures/u4.66.01/1000150400002461000010050A66C58130DE2C89.svg)

### 3.8 Operand POTENTIEL

```text
◊    POTENTIEL = phi
```

Stationary (thermal) potential necessary for the computation of the added damping and of the added stiffness of the structure subjected to a potential flow. This potential is produced by the operator THER_LINEAIRE [U4.54.01](doc:u4.54.01).

### 3.9 Operand NUME_DDL_GENE

◊    NUME_DDL_GENE = numgen

Generalised numbering based on the mechanical modes of the global structure. The presence of this operand makes it possible to compute an added mass matrix of type matr_asse_gene_R. It must necessarily be present if one subsequently wants to carry out a modal, harmonic or transient analysis.

### 3.10 Operand INFO

```text
◊    INFO =
```

Indicates the level of printout of the results of the operator on the file MESSAGE.

| 1 : | no printout, |
| --- | --- |
| 2 : | printout of the upper triangular part of the added mass, added damping or added stiffness matrices. |

Column presentation.

### 3.11 Factor keyword SOLVEUR

See [U4.50.01](doc:u4.50.01).

### 3.12 Operand AVEC_MODE_STAT

Makes it possible to switch off the computation of the added-mass terms on the static modes contained in the modal basis of the substructures in the case of an analysis with a generalised model (*cf.* [§3.4]).

### 3.13 Operand NUME_MODE_MECA

Makes it possible to specify a list of numbers of modes retained for the computation.

## 4 Execution phase

One checks that the fluid and interface models are indeed thermal.

One checks that the storage of the added matrices is done on a **full** profile.

One checks that the added self-mass coefficients (diagonal terms of the matrix) are indeed all positive. Otherwise, an alarm message is emitted so that the user checks the orientation of the normals.

One checks that, for a computation of added damping or of added stiffness, the keyword POTENTIEL is indeed supplied.

In the case of an added-mass computation on a generalised model, one informs the user about the copies of structural displacement fields into the fluid (number of values copied) and one indicates how to change the reference distance (DIST_REFE).

## 5 Example: Computation of the added mass matrix of a structure constituted of two concentric cylinders separated by an incompressible fluid

The cylinders are each supported by springs. The added mass matrix is computed for the first two modes of the structure, namely the rigid-body modes of each of the cylinders in the direction Ox (*cf.* diagram). One will present here only the steps of the command file, because this example is inspired by the test case FDLV101 [V8.01.101](doc:v8.01.101). For the detail of the syntax of the other operators, the user may therefore refer to the command file of this test case.

![Figure](/figures/u4.66.01/10000F2600001DC4000017FA64ACE706587A5FD4.svg)

1. Definition of the fluid material by equivalent thermal characteristics:
2. Assignment of this fluid material onto the fluid mesh, including the mesh-cell group defining the fluid/structure interface.
3. Assignment of the thermal fluid model (also comprising the interface)
4. Assignment of the thermal interface model
5. Assignment of the global structural model (comprising the two cylinders and their support springs)
6. Assignment of an imposed temperature in the fluid mesh
7. Computation of the eigenmodes of the structure in vacuum
8. Generalised numbering based on the computed mechanical modes
9. Computation of the added mass matrix of type matr_asse_gene_r (operand NUME_DDL_GENE present). As a result, one can then compute the eigenmodes of the "wetted" structure

```text
matraj = CALC_MATR_AJOU( MODELE_FLUIDE = fluide ,

MODELE_INTERFACE = interf ,

CHAM_MATER = chammat1 ,

CHARGE = charge ,

OPTION = 'MASS_AJOU' ,

MODE_MECA = modes ,

NUME_DDL_GENE = numgen ,

INFO =1 )
```

1. Computation of the generalised mass and stiffness matrices on the generalised numbering based on the computed mechanical modes numgen. These matrices are of type [matr_asse_gene_r]
2. Computation of the total mass matrix of the structure (generalised mass matrix plus added mass matrix)
3. Computation of the modes of the immersed structure.
4. Recovery of the "wetted" modes onto the physical basis.
