# v2.03.115 SDLS115 – Comparison with analytical solution of a traction plate

**Summary:**

This test validates the basic operation of DKT Modelling for a transient calculation with an explicit schematic of digital integration by the DYNA_NON_LINE operator. The plate is submitted to boundary conditions corresponding to a single traction, allowing to find the calculated answer analytically.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.115/10000200000001E4000001A6EC7B0AFDC6CE4BE3.png)

Square plate **:**

Length: $l=1.0m$

Thickness: $e=0.1m$

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus, $E=4.388{10}^{10}N/{m}^{2}$

Poisson's ratio, $\nu =0.0$

Density, $\rho =2500.0\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

**boundary conditions:**

| **L** ocalization | **Clocked components** |
| --- | --- |
| A1 | DX, DY, DZ, DRX, DRY, DRZ |
| A | DX, DZ, DRX, DRY, DRZ |
| C | DZ, DRX, DRY, DRZ |

**Charges:**

The linear force is applied on side B in the direction $x$ , which depends on time as,

$F(t)={Q}_{0}EKe\cos(\mathrm{Kl})\sin(\omega t)$ ,

where the following parameters are used:

- ${Q}_{0}$ ($={10}^{-4}m$ ) - loading amplitude
- $E$ – Young's modulus defined above (in $N/{m}^{2}$ )
- $e$ – thickness defined above (in $m$)
- $l$ – the size of the plate defined above (in $m$)
- $K$ ($=\frac{\pi}{\mathrm{8l}}$ ) the number of waves of the analytical solution (${m}^{-1}$ )
- $\omega$ – frequency (time $2\pi$), related to the number of wave $K$ , $K=\omega /c$ , $c$ being the speed of waves in the structure, $c=\sqrt{\frac{E}{\rho}}$

The introduced parameterization allows you to apply the load just to get the analytical solution, determined simply by the parameters ${Q}_{0}$ and $K$, and then by other parameters of the dimensions and material properties of the structure.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Initially, the movements are zero everywhere and the speeds are according to the following spatial distribution,

${v}_{0}(x,y)=\omega {Q}_{0}\sin(\mathrm{K.x})$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

Here we treat a problem of structure (quasi)-unidimensional subject to an edge force, $F(t)$, where the analytical solution can write as,

$u(x,t)={Q}_{0}\cos(Kx)\sin(\omega t)$

To obtain this solution the force and initial conditions specified above must be applied. The parameters are also commented on.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

This is the $x$ displacement to node $A2$ and at the moment, ${t}_{max}=0.0012s$, which must be equal to

$u({x}_{A2,t})={Q}_{0}\cos(K{x}_{A2})\sin(\omega {t}_{max})$

the value being calculated in the data file from the selected values of the parameters.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Exactly.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

1. Timoshenko, *Theory of Vibration* , 1939
