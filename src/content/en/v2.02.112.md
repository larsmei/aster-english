# v2.02.112 SDLL112 – Seismic analysis of a multi-supported beam (spectral response)

**Summary**

This test concerns seismic analysis by the spectral method of a vertical beam, clamped at its base and articulated at two points of different altitude. The structure is subjected to an excitation provided in the form of a pseudo-acceleration oscillator spectrum.

Through this problem, the CQC operator's COMB_SISM_MODAL [U4.84.01](doc:u4.84.01) modal combination is tested, with or without consideration of neglected modes.

In addition, CALC_MODES [U4.52.02](doc:u4.52.02), NORM_MODE [U4.52.11](doc:u4.52.11), MODE_STATIQUE [U4.52.14](doc:u4.52.14), DEFI_FONCTION [U4.31.02](doc:u4.31.02) and DEFI_NAPPE [U4.31.03](doc:u4.31.03) are tested.

Validation consists of:

  - compare the results with those obtained with CASTEM 2000 (modelization A (3D): single-support combination CQC, multi-support excitation identical to CQC combination supports);
  - find the mono-support response by the multi-support path, in the case of excitations identical to the supports(modellings A (3D) and B ($\mathrm{2D}$ ), combination CQC);
  - check the non-regression of the response in case there are no reference data (different excitations at the supports).

The results obtained are consistent with those obtained from CASTEM 2000. In the case of excitation equal to the supports, the multi-support response calculated via the ‘CORRELE' option is identical to the one-support response calculated.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The Modelling is three-dimensional. The beam is decomposed into 10 elements Modelling POU_D_E.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of nodes : 11

Number of mesh cells and types: 10 mesh cells of type SEG2 (POU_D_E)

The node group $\mathit{encastre}$ contains the node $\mathit{N1}$ ($\mathit{dx}=\mathit{dy}=\mathit{dz}=\mathit{drx}=\mathit{dry}=\mathit{drz}=0$ ).

The node group $\mathit{attache}$ contains the nodes $\mathit{N5}$ and $\mathit{N9}$ ($\mathit{dx}=\mathit{dy}=0$ ).

### 3.3 Modelling parameters

SISM_MO1 calculation: Single-press response on the first 10 modes, $x$ excitation, without static correction (combination of CQC modal responses)

SISM_MO2 calculation: Single-press response on the first 10 modes, $x$ excitation, with static correction (combination of CQC modal responses)

SISM_MU1 calculation: Multi-press response on the first 10 modes with identical excitation on $x$ at all supports, without static correction and without anchor displacement (combination of CQC modal responses)

SISM_MU2 calculation: Multi-press response on the first 10 modes with different $x$ and $y$ excitations and associated with support, with static correction (combination of CQC modal responses), with differential displacements to support. Quadratic combination of DDS responses.

SISM_MU6 calculation: Multi-press response on the first 10 modes with identical excitation on all supports, with static correction and no anchor displacement (combination of CQC modal responses)

Quadratic combination of directional responses (QUAD)
