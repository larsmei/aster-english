# v6.04.205 SSNV205 – Constant isotropic pressure drained cyclic shear test

**Summary:**

The constant isotropic pressure drained cyclic shear test simulates the loss of stiffness from a soil as a function of the amplitude of the applied cyclic deformations, and also determines the dissipation introduced during loading cycles.

In this case, the constitutive laws of Hudeux (modelization A) and Iwan (modelization B) are first examined.
Two laws currently available in Code_Aster for the cyclic behaviour of granular materials. Next, the CSSM model (modelization C) designed around the general representation of granular geomaterials under monotonous and cyclic loadings is interested.

In model A, the conduct of these tests also validates the introduction of the cyclic deviatory thresholds of the Hujeux behavior model. The results of the Code_Aster are compared with the results obtained with the GEFD yn software for the same behavior model.

In model B, the results obtained with the Iwan model are compared with those of Modelling with the Hujeux model, considering similar behavioural curves for both models.

In model C, the results obtained using model CSSM are compared with those obtained from material point calculations by Mtest.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A hardware element point is considered.

### 1.2 Material properties

#### 1.2.1 Hujeux and Iwan's behavioural models

- ${c}_{\mathit{cyc}}=1\times10^{-1}$, ${c}_{\mathit{mon}}=2\times10^{-1}$
- ${r}_{\mathit{ela}}^{d}=5\times10^{-3}$, ${r}_{\mathit{ela}}^{i}=1\times10^{-3}$, ${r}_{\mathit{ela}}^{d,c}=5\times10^{-3}$, ${r}_{\mathit{ela}}^{i,c}=1\times10^{-3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

As a reminder, using the SIMU_POINT_MAT command allows you to directly impose a deformation and/or stress field.

A zero evolution during loading is required for the following components of stress and deformation tensors:

- ${\mathrm{d}\sigma}_{xx}={\mathrm{d}\sigma}_{yy}={\mathrm{d}\sigma}_{zz}=0$
- ${\mathrm{d}\varepsilon}_{yz}={\mathrm{d}\varepsilon}_{zx}=0$

The v6.04.205-image_loading evolution is required for $\varepsilon_{xy}$ shear deformations:

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.205/image_chargement.png)

Several calculations are performed by varying the amplitude of deformations according to the $\left\lbrace 2\times10^{-5},2\times10^{-4},2\times10^{-3}\right\rbrace$ values.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The initial stress state is isotropic and corresponds to a pressure equal to $50$ kPa.

## 2 Reference solution for Modelling A

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution is obtained from the equivalent simulations with the Hujeux model performed
with the software of calculation by the method of elements Finis, GEFD yn, developed at the École Centrale Paris.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The quantities and reference results provided by GEFD yn are as follows:

- Evolution of shear stress during loading.
- Evolution of plastic density deformation.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

The uncertainties on the solution are directly related to the accuracy required to achieve the convergence of the numerical calculation r
with GEFD yn, i.e. $1\times10^{-6}$ as a relative displacement standard.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

1. D.Aubry, A.Modaressi. GEFD yn, Manuel Scientifique. École Centrale Paris, LMSS-Mat, 1996.

## 6 Summary of the results

This test compares the results of two behavioural models for the cyclic shear response of granular materials.

Model A validated the behaviour of the cyclic loading Hujeux model compared to the results of the reference code of this behavior model.

Model B verified that the Iwan model provides results similar to the Hudeux model (at an difference of about 5%), but with an easy calibration (2 parameters).

Model C illustrated the CSSM model's cyclic-loaded hardening.
