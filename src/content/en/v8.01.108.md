# v8.01.108 FDLV108 - Calculation of an additional depreciation in ring flow (variable volume mass)

**Summary:**

This fluid-structure domain test implements the mass and damping calculation added on a cylindrical structure subject to an annular flow that is assumed to be potential. Mass and damping added by flow on the structure for an upstream speed of $4{\mathrm{m.s}}^{-1}$ , on a 3D model for the fluid and hull for the structure. The structure has a rotational displacement around a pivot located at the downstream end of the cylinder relative to the flow. The interest of the test lies in the consideration of a fluid domain of density ** not homogeneous** .

## 4 Summary of the results

The flow damping calculation tool (potential hypothesis) was validated on the rotational mode of a cylindrical structure subject to an annular flow with variable density. However, it should be noted [bib1] that the very good concordance between the proposed semi-analytical model for comparison and the numerical calculation is only obtained if the cylinder is sufficiently long, the semi-analytical model being in fact only a solution close to the problem.
