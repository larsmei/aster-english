# r7.01.17 Barcelona model

**Summary:**

This document presents the formulation and the numerical integration of the elastoplastic Barcelona constitutive model [bib2]__, designed to describe the mechanical behaviour of unsaturated soils, in particular clays. This model is an extension of the modified Cam-Clay model [bib1]__ taking into account, in a schematic manner, the effects of the capillary pressure on the elasticity, the reversibility domain, and the hardening mechanisms observed experimentally on unsaturated soils. The Barcelona model comprises a total of sixteen parameters. The constitutive equations are solved by means of a time-implicit integration method, implemented in the tool MFront [bib5]__. Use of the model is necessarily linked to the HHM environment [r7.01.11](doc:r7.01.11).

## 2 Introduction to the Barcelona model: behaviour under isotropic loading

The isotropic loading paths are visualised by varying the net pressure $p''=-\sigma_m''=-(\sigma_m+p_g)$ and the capillary pressure $p_c=p_g-p_l$ in order to characterise the behaviour of the soil in its elastic and plastic phases. For this purpose one denotes by $e=d\Omega^p/d\Omega^s$ the void ratio (ratio between the pore volume $d\Omega^p$ and the volume of the solid skeleton $d\Omega^s$ in the *REV*), for which the additive decomposition between elastic and plastic parts $e^e$ and $e^p$, respectively, is admitted.
