# v2.03.129 SDLS129 - CALCUL MODAL D'UNE ETOILE A 3 BRANCHES

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the correct consideration of the substructure orientations in DEFI_MODELE_GENE, as well as the restitution of complex generalised modes on a physical basis. A three-branch star plane model, consisting of two distinct elements macros: a element macro for the central part of the star, and a element macro for the star branch, is constructed.

Two modellings are made:

•Model A: calculation of actual modes, without depreciation;

•Model B: introduction of a Rayleigh damping system for complex mode calculations.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A three-branched star as shown in Figure 1.1-1 is considered. The figure also gives the dimensions (in metres) of the structure. The thickness is homogeneous, and is 5mm.

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.129/1000000000000339000002D043405A5301B5B1BA.png)

Figure 1.1-1: Géométrie de l'étoile.

### 1.2 Material properties

The constituent material of each substructure is a standard, elastic isotropic steel. Its properties are:

- Young's modulus $E=210000\mathit{MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0,3$
- density $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$

For model B, Rayleigh coefficients are introduced to obtain a 15% modal damping for frequencies at 10 Hz and 50 Hz, i.e.

$\lbrace \begin{array}{ccc}\alpha & =& 2({\xi}_{1}{\omega}_{1}-{\xi}_{2}{\omega}_{2})/({\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}_{2}^{2})\\ \beta & =& 2{\xi}_{1}{\omega}_{1}-\alpha {\omega}_{1}^{2}\end{array},$ with $\lbrace \begin{array}{ccccc}{\xi}_{1}=0.15& \text{/}& {f}_{1}=10\mathit{Hz}& \text{/}& {\omega}_{1}=2\pi {f}_{1}\\ {\xi}_{2}=0.15& \text{/}& {f}_{2}=50\mathit{Hz}& \text{/}& {\omega}_{2}=2\pi {f}_{2}\end{array}.$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The structure is clamped on the stops shown in red in Figure 1.1-1, and is not loaded (modal calculation without prestress).

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Geometry and mesh

The geometry of Figure 1.1-1 is represented by two substructures. A substructure representing the central part, and a substructure representing one of the branches of the star. The central part is meshed with 45 triangular elements, and the arm is meshed with 104 elements quadrangular. The meshes are compatible at the interfaces between the centre and each arm.

### 2.2 Material properties

For Modelling A, the Rayleigh damping is not considered. $\alpha =\beta =0.$ is installed

### 2.3 Tested quantities and results

In this test, the relative displacement of nodes in screws, one belonging to the central substructure and the other belonging to the arm, is compared for the first three modes of the assembly. The three nodes for which the tests are performed are represented by green dots in Figure 1.1-1. This relative displacement must be zero for each node.

## 3 Modelling B

### 3.1 Geometry and mesh

The geometry and mesh are identical to those of model A.

### 3.2 Material properties

For model B, depreciation is introduced through the Rayleigh coefficients defined in section 1.2.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

In this test, the phase shift between the real and imaginary parts of the displacements of the three nodes represented by green dots in Figure 1.1-1 is compared for the proper modes 2 and 3 of the assembly. The value of the ratio of the imaginary and real parts of the displacement is tested. The results are summarized in Table 3.3-1.
