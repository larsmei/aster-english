# v3.04.318 SSLV318 – Validation of the X-FEM Tridimental Crack Catalogue

**Summary:**

This document is intended to validate the definition of a three-dimensional X-FEM crack *via* the catalogue of predefined shapes.

## 1 Reference problems

### 1.1 Geometry of the crack of the " rectangle" shape

The side cube of $1m$ is considered. This cube has a rectangle-shaped crack with rounded angles. The crack is located in the normal plane $y$ mid-distance from the front and rear side of the cube (see ). The crack is actually a half-rectangle. The complete rectangle has for $\mathrm{2a}$ length and $\mathrm{2b}$ width. Moreover, the corners of the rectangle are rounded, with a $r$ radius. The center of the complete rectangle is therefore the point coordinates $(0,5;0,5;1)$. The centre of the marker is the point $\mathrm{P4}$ . In the continuation, we will take $a=0,3$ , $b=0,15$ and $r=0,05$ .

### 1.2 Cylinder-shaped crack geometry

The same cube is considered as before, but with a cylinder-shaped crack. The cylinder axis is parallel to the $y$ axis and passes through the point $C$ coordinates $(1;0,5;1)$. The cylinder radius is $r=0,5$. The crack front is therefore a quarter of a circle. The crack lips are located in the $y\le 0,5$ half-space (see ).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Rectangle-shaped crack

The purpose of this test is to validate the definition of this crack by calculating the associated level sets. The test is in fact only about the value of $\mathrm{lst}$ at $\mathrm{P4}$ and $\mathrm{P5}$ points. A quick calculation gives $\mathrm{lst}(\mathrm{P4})=\sqrt{({(\frac{1}{2}-(a-r))}^{2}+{(1-(b-r))}^{2})}-r$ and $\mathit{lst}(\mathit{P5})=0,2$ .

### 2.2 Cylinder-shaped crack

The purpose of this test is to validate the definition of this crack by calculating the associated level sets. The test is based on the values of $\mathit{lsn}$ and $\mathrm{lst}$ at $\mathrm{P4}$ and $\mathrm{P5}$ points. A quick calculation gives:

$\mathit{lsn}(\mathit{P4})=\sqrt{2}-r$ et $\mathit{lst}(\mathit{P5})=1-r$ ,

$\mathit{lst}(\mathit{P4})=-0,5$ et $\mathit{lst}(\mathit{P5})=-0,5$ .
