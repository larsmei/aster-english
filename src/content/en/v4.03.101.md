# v4.03.101 TPLS101 - Infinite plate subjected to a symmetrical heat exchange with the outside

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to test the three-field linear thermal hull model compared to the analytical solution, for an infinite plate subjected to a torque of heat exchange conditions with the outside, symmetrical with respect to the average sheet. The heat equation is solved by stationary, with linear, isotropic, homogeneous conduction.

The results are presented for the elements finished available thermal overfacial hull triangles and quadrangles.

Compared to the TPLS100 [v4.03.100](doc:v4.03.100) test, this test allows to verify the contribution of the exchange coefficients to the thermal rigidity, as well as various methods of assigning the boundary conditions. Moreover, the solution is such that the temperature is uniform in thickness.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v4.03.101/1000096A00001CF000000EE23159C12A02E3E90E.svg)

Length: $L=\mathrm{20mm}$

Width: $b=\mathrm{2mm}$

Thickness: $h=\mathrm{4mm}$

### 1.2 Material properties

$\lambda =1000W/\mathrm{mm}/°C$ conductivity

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- Temperature zero to point $O$ , over the entire thickness.
- On the top ${(\mathrm{ABEF})}^{+}$ and bottom ${(\mathrm{ABEF})}^{\text{–}}$:
exchange coefficient: $h=10W/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}/°C$
outside temperature : $\mathrm{Text}=50°C$
- On the top ${(\mathrm{BCDE})}^{+}$ and bottom ${(\mathrm{BCDE})}^{\text{–}}$:
exchange coefficient: $h=10W/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}/°C$
outside temperature : ${T}_{\mathrm{ext}}=–50°C$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

**Analytic**

For more details, see [r3.11.01](doc:r3.11.01) and bib1.

### 2.2 Baseline

Upper, lower skin temperature and medium leaf.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.
