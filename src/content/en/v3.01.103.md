# v3.01.103 SSLL103 - Elastical flame of a corneal

**Summary:**

A two-articulated straight beam (gender with equal wings) is subjected to normal (excentrated or unexcented) or bending moment stress.

We're looking for critical loads of elastic flaming.

- linear elastic mechanics,
- a beam flashing,
- the centre of twisting,
- interest of the test: calculation of the geometric rigidity matrix of the elements POU_D_TG and POU_D_T,
- 2 modellings.

Uncertainty persists on the number of reference solution flashing modes [§5].

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

With the result of the waching, the calculations made by V. De Ville De Goyet [bib1] give:

either:

${I}_{y}={\int}_{A}{z}^{2}\mathrm{dA}$     ${I}_{y}={\int}_{A}{y}^{2}\mathrm{dA}$     ${I}_{{\mathrm{yr}}^{2}}={\int}_{A}y({y}^{2}+{z}^{2})\mathrm{dA}$     ${I}_{{\mathrm{yr}}^{2}}={\int}_{A}z({y}^{2}+{z}^{2})\mathrm{dA}$

${P}_{\mathrm{cry}}=\frac{{\pi}^{2}E{I}_{z}}{{L}^{2}}$     ${P}_{\mathrm{crz}}=\frac{{\pi}^{2}E{I}_{y}}{{L}^{2}}$     ${P}_{\mathrm{crx}}=(\frac{GJ+{\pi}^{2}E{I}_{\omega}}{{L}^{2}}){\mathrm{Ar}}_{a}$

${\mathrm{Ar}}_{c}=\frac{({I}_{y}+{I}_{z})}{A}+{y}_{c}^{2}+{z}_{c}^{2}+{y}_{c}(\frac{{I}_{\mathrm{yrz}}}{\mathrm{Iz}}-2{y}_{c})+{z}_{c}(\frac{{I}_{{\mathrm{zr}}^{2}}}{\mathrm{Iz}}-2{z}_{c})$

${\mathrm{Ar}}_{a}=\frac{({I}_{y}+{I}_{z})}{A}+{y}_{c}^{2}+{z}_{c}^{2}+{y}_{a}(\frac{{I}_{\mathrm{yrz}}}{\mathrm{Iz}}-2{y}_{c})+{z}_{a}(\frac{{I}_{{\mathrm{zr}}^{2}}}{\mathrm{Iz}}-2{z}_{c})$

with:

$({y}_{a},{z}_{a})$: point force application coordinates

$({y}_{c},{z}_{c})$: torsion center coordinates

Case 1, 2, 3:

Three critical loads are obtained by solving the 3° degree equation in $P$:

${\mathrm{Ar}}_{a}({P}_{\mathrm{cry}}-P)({P}_{\mathrm{crz}}-P)({P}_{\mathrm{crx}}-P)-{P}^{2}({P}_{\mathrm{crz}}-P){({z}_{c}-{z}_{a})}^{2}-{P}^{2}({P}_{\mathrm{cry}}-P){({y}_{c}-{y}_{a})}^{2}=0$

Case 4:

The $\mathrm{Mcr}$ critical moment (around the $y$ axis) is:

$\mathrm{Mcr}=\pm {((GJ+\frac{{\pi}^{2}E{I}_{\omega}}{{L}^{2}}){P}_{\mathrm{cry}})}^{1/2}$

By neglecting the spin: the reference analytical solution is given in [bib2] [bib3].

### 2.2 Baseline

## 5 Summary of the results

The analytical solution gives us 3 flaming modes whose critical loads are the root of a 3° degree equation.

Are there any other critical loads intersected between the 3 values found?

*Aster* finds the right critical loads, but in the middle of many others ... for example in case 3, the 3 critical loads sought correspond to the NUME_MODE: 1, 10 and 19.

This is true for both modellings.
