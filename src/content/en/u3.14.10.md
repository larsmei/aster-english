# u3.14.10 Modelling 3D_FLUI_ABSO

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modelling 3D_FLUI_ABSO:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the associated support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear possibilities,
- the test cases implementing the modelling.

The modelling 3D_FLUI_ABSO (Phenomenon: mechanics) corresponds to finite elements whose support mesh cells are surface mesh cells. They make it possible to take into account the absorbing-boundary condition of fluid volumes.

## 3 Non-linear possibilities

### 3.1 Constitutive laws

The only constitutive relation available for this modelling is the RELATION 'ELAS' (under COMPORTEMENT) in DYNA_NON_LINE (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).

### 3.2 Strains

Only the linearised strains, keyword 'PETIT' under DEFORMATION, are available in the constitutive relations (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).
