# v6.05.115 SSNS115 - Swelling of a flexible membrane

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the operation of element MEMBRANE in large deformations for two hyperelastic constitutive laws and different types of mesh cells (linear, quadratic and bi-quadratic). Thus, swelling of a disc subjected to subsequent pressure is considered and the results are compared with solutions from the literature.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

$1000\mathit{mm}$ ray disc is considered in the $(0,X,Y)$ plane.

![Figure](/figures/v6.05.115/100000000000020A00000148FB600BF392088E2D.png)

The thickness of the membrane is indicated in AFFE_CARA_ELEM via the keyword EPAIS and is $1\mathit{mm}$.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is isotropic hyperelastic with properties of:

- $E=2\mathit{Mpa}$
- $\nu =0,3$

Two constitutive lawss are valid, the law of Saint Venant Kirchhoff and the New Hookean law.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The outer edge of the membrane is embedded and the entire disc is pressured evenly.

For the law of Saint Venant Kirchhoff the pressure is $25\mathit{kPa}$. It is applied in 2 increments.

For New-Hookean law, the pressure is controlled via point $O$ displacement according to $Z$, noted $W$, in order to continue the calculation beyond the limit load. The calculation is then continued after the maximum pressure is reached. The steering is performed in 10 increments, up to $W=2500\mathit{mm}$. The controlled reference pressure is $1\mathit{Pa}$. The quantity ETA_PILO then corresponds to the pressure value in $N/{m}^{2}$.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

An initial $1\mathit{Pa}$ voltage is reported in AFFE_CARA_ELEM via the N_INIT keyword. This voltage disappears after the first increment of Newton.

Some differences on the solutions obtained can be explained by differences in mesh sizes. In addition, the reading of values was done via the software *G3D ata Graph Analyzer* on a scan of a graph contained in the reference book. Therefore, uncertainty is directly related to the quality and accuracy of the print of the work, as well as the accuracy of the points made.

### 6.4 Remarks

The moving steering (SUIV_PILO) was used to achieve convergence. The neo-Hookean constitutive law shows a *snap-through* at about 1.4kPa. This results in strong non-linearities around this value that can be overcome by controlling the following pressure.

The mesh is about twice as coarse as the reference and the mesh cells are linear and non-quadratic. This explains the differences observed in the reference solution for this Modelling.

## 9 Summary of the results

This document validates element's MEMBRANE in large deformations, for:

- the constitutive laws of Saint Venant Kirchhoff and neo-Hookean,
- linear, quadratic and bi-quadratic elements.

This validation is based on comparison with literature results and is supplemented by several non-regression tests. The elements of the reference literature was also used to implant the element membrane in large deformations in code_aster, so the correct placement of the element in the screw code against the sources used is checked.

Given the uncertainty of the reference results, it is not possible to conclude that elements of higher order is appropriate to obtain more accurate results at mesh identical. However, it can be concluded that all elements of the elements of the constitutive law used are satisfactory here, regardless of the constitutive law used.

The element of membranes in large deformations is found to be highly nonlinear and requires a number of Newton iterations (sometimes more than 100) to reach the initial equilibrium.
