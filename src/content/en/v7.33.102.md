# v7.33.102 WTNA102 - Dissolved air diffusion (axi)

**Summary:**

This is considered a problem at constant temperature and saturation. Appropriate boundary conditionss impose constant water pressure and vapour pressure. A gas pressure is imposed on one edge of the domain (zero flows on the other side). Only dry air and dissolved air pressures connected by Henry's law evolve. This problem is reduced to an equation for dry air pressure of the type "heat equation". The reference solution will then be a thermal calculation of Code_Aster.

${p}_{w}=\mathrm{cte}={p}_{w}^{0}$

${p}_{\mathrm{vp}}=\mathrm{cte}={p}_{\mathrm{vp}}^{0}$

${F}_{\mathrm{vp}}=0$

$S({p}_{c})=\mathrm{cte}={S}_{0}$

$T=\mathrm{cte}={T}_{0}$

$\phi =1$

${M}_{\mathrm{as}}^{\mathrm{ol}}={M}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{\mathrm{ol}}={M}_{\mathrm{vp}}^{\mathrm{ol}}$

On all edges: No hydraulic and thermal fluxes.

We will now linearize ${p}_{\mathrm{vp}}$ based on ${p}_{w}$ .

**Writing** ${p}_{\mathrm{vp}}$ **linear function of** ${p}_{w}$ **:**

Section 4.2.3 of the Code_Aster [R7.01.11](doc:r7.01.11) reference document gives us the relationship: $\frac{{\mathrm{dp}}_{\mathrm{vp}}}{{p}_{\mathrm{vp}}}=\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{vp}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{\mathrm{RT}}\frac{{\mathrm{dp}}_{w}}{{\rho}_{w}}$ . If we linearize this expression we get: ${p}_{\mathrm{vp}}=\frac{{p}_{\mathrm{vp}}^{0}}{\mathrm{RT}}\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{vp}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{{\rho}_{w}^{0}}{p}_{w}+({p}_{\mathrm{vp}}^{0}-\frac{{p}_{\mathrm{vp}}^{0}}{\mathrm{RT}}\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{vp}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{{\rho}_{w}^{0}}{p}_{w}^{0})$ which we can write in the form:

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

#### 2.1.1 Calculation of air mass conservation

The storage of the mass of gas shall be written:

> $\frac{{\mathrm{dm}}_{\mathrm{air}}}{\mathrm{dt}}+d({M}_{\mathrm{as}}+{M}_{\mathrm{ad}})$                 éq 2.1.1-1

It is written that the total water mass and the total air mass are kept (because there is no water flow or gas at the edge) and it is obtained:

${m}_{\mathrm{air}}={m}_{\mathrm{as}}+{m}_{\mathrm{ad}}={S}_{0}({\rho}_{\mathrm{ad}}-{\rho}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{0})+(1-{S}_{0})({\rho}_{\mathrm{as}}-{\rho}_{\mathrm{as}}^{0})$

So

> $d({m}_{\mathrm{as}}+{m}_{\mathrm{ad}})={S}_{0}d{\rho}_{\mathrm{ad}}+(1-{S}_{0})d{\rho}_{\mathrm{as}}$             éq 2.1.1-2
> $d{\rho}_{\mathrm{as}}=\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{as}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{\mathrm{RT}}{\mathrm{dp}}_{\mathrm{as}}$ et $d{\rho}_{\mathrm{ad}}=\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{{K}_{H}}{\mathrm{dp}}_{\mathrm{as}}$
> $\frac{{\mathrm{dm}}_{\mathrm{air}}}{\mathrm{dt}}=d(\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{{K}_{H}}{S}_{0}+(1-{S}_{0})\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{as}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{\mathrm{RT}}{\mathrm{dp}}_{\mathrm{as}})$

Calculation of speeds:

> $\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{as}}}{{\rho}_{\mathrm{as}}}={\lambda}_{\mathrm{gz}}(-\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{as}})$                                 éq 2.1.1-3

since ${F}_{\mathrm{vp}}=0$ and $\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{vp}}=0$

et

${M}_{\mathrm{ad}}={\rho}_{\mathrm{ad}}{\lambda}_{\mathrm{lq}}(-\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{lq}})-{F}_{\mathrm{ad}}\nabla {C}_{\mathrm{ad}}$ with ${C}_{\mathrm{ad}}={\rho}_{\mathrm{ad}}$

Like $\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{lq}}=\nabla {p}_{w}+\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{ad}}=\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{ad}}=\frac{\mathrm{RT}}{{K}_{H}}\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{as}}$

> ${M}_{\mathrm{ad}}={\rho}_{\mathrm{ad}}{\lambda}_{\mathrm{lq}}\frac{\mathrm{RT}}{{K}_{H}}(-\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{as}})-\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{{K}_{H}}{F}_{\mathrm{ad}}\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{as}}$

[eq 2.1.1-1] may then be simplified as follows:

$C\frac{{\mathrm{dp}}_{\mathrm{as}}}{\mathrm{dt}}=Ld(\nabla {p}_{\mathrm{as}})$

with

$C=\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{{K}_{H}}{S}_{0}+(1-{S}_{0})\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{as}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{\mathrm{RT}}$

et

$L={\rho}_{\mathrm{as}}^{0}{\lambda}_{\mathrm{gz}}+\frac{\mathrm{RT}}{{K}_{H}}{\rho}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{0}{\lambda}_{\mathrm{lq}}+\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{as}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{{K}_{H}}{F}_{\mathrm{ad}}$

Equation of the heat with the result known.

### 2.2 Baseline

With the previous numerical values, we find:

${p}_{\mathrm{as}}={10}^{5}\Rightarrow {p}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{0}=\frac{\mathrm{RT}}{{K}_{H}}{p}_{\mathrm{as}}^{0}=4992$

${\rho}_{\mathrm{as}}^{0}=\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{as}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{\mathrm{RT}}{p}_{\mathrm{as}}^{0}=0.4$ et ${\rho}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{0}=\frac{{M}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{\mathrm{ol}}}{\mathrm{RT}}{p}_{\mathrm{ad}}^{0}=0.02$

${\rho}_{\mathrm{vp}}^{0}={\rho}_{\mathrm{vp}}={4.10}^{-3}$

The constants of the heat equation are then:

> $C=2.48{10}^{-6}$
> $L=1.4{10}^{-16}$

### 2.3 Uncertainties

The uncertainties are quite large because analytical solution is an approach solution due to linearization of equations.
