# u3.13.02 Mechanical modelling AXIS_FOURIER

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the mechanical modelling AXIS_FOURIER:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear possibilities,
- the test cases implementing the modelling.

The modelling AXIS_FOURIER (Phenomenon: mechanical) corresponds to finite elements whose support mesh cells are plane and make it possible to model the longitudinal section of solids of revolution about the axis $\mathit{OY}$ , (in cylindrical coordinates $(r,z,\theta )$ ) and subjected to loadings whose spatial distribution is decomposed into a Fourier series about this axis.

## 2 Supported loadings

The available loadings are the following:

- 'FORCE_CONTOUR'

Makes it possible to apply linear forces on the edge of a 2D domain.

- 'FORCE_INTERNE'

Makes it possible to apply body forces.

- 'PESANTEUR'

Makes it possible to apply a gravity-type loading.

- 'PRES_REP'

Makes it possible to apply a pressure to a continuum domain.

- 'ROTATION'

Makes it possible to define a rotation speed and the direction of the rotation vector.

## 3 Non-linear possibilities

No non-linear possibility exists; this modelling can be used only with MECA_STATIQUE or by a manual assembly.

## 4 Examples of implementation: test cases
