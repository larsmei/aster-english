# u4.82.07 Operator POST_KCP correction β

## 1 Purpose

Harmfulness assessment of a defect by the Kcp β-correction method for a semi-elliptical defect located under the cladding of pressurised-water reactor vessels.

The objective of this method is to evaluate, on a sound mesh (without a meshed defect) and in post-processing of a mechanical study (axisymmetric or 3D) in linear elasticity, the elastic-plastic stress intensity factor at each of the two tips of the defect, that is at the tip on the cladding side and at the tip on the base-metal side. The quantities obtained as output of this method can then be used to determine subsequently the margin factors with respect to fracture, by comparison with the critical toughness of the fracture mode considered at the defect tip under consideration.

POST_KCP first computes the elastic stress intensity factors at the two tips of the defect, using the stresses at the nodes obtained from the mechanical solution and the residual stresses given by the user. Then, in a second step, the operator computes the plastically corrected stress intensity factor from its elastic equivalent by the so-called “β correction” method. This method is specific to underclad defects stuck to the interface and makes it possible to take into account the influence on the defect of the plastification phenomenon occurring at the tip on the cladding side and at the tip on the base-metal side [R7.02.10](doc:r7.02.10).

Produces a concept of type table_sdaster.

## 2 Syntax

```text
table = POST_KCP(
    ◆ MODELE = modele,
    ◆ MATER_REV = mater,
    ◆ EPAIS_REV = float,
    ◆ MATER_MDB = mater,
    ◆ EPAIS_MDB = float,
    ◆ UNITE_LONGUEUR = / "M" (par défaut),
                       / "MM",
    ◆ FISSURE = _F(
         ◇ FORM_FISS = / "SEMI_ELLIPTIQUE",
                       / "BANDE",
         # Si: equal_to("FORM_FISS", 'SEMI_ELLIPTIQUE')
             ◆ LONGUEUR = float,
             ◆ CORRECTION = / "BETA_3D" (par défaut),
                           / "BETA_2D",
         ◆ PROFONDEUR = float,
         ◆ ORIENTATION = / "CIRC",
                         / "LONGI",
      ),
    ◆ K1D = _F(
         ◆ TABL_MECA= table,
         ◆ TABL_THER = table,
      ),
)

◆ : obligatoire
◇ : optionnel
⟐ : présent par défaut
& : ensemble
/ : un seul parmi
| : plusieurs choix possibles
```

## 3 Operands

The operator POST_KCP may be used within axisymmetric or three-dimensional modellings.

In the figure below we present a schematic of a vessel slice with a semi-elliptical defect present.

Semi-elliptical defect located under the cladding

### 3.1 Operand MODELE

Concept of type modele.

### 3.2 Operand MATER_REV

Name of the concept of type materiau defining the material that constitutes the cladding. Required in order to recover:

- Young's modulus, for the computation of the influence coefficients (semi-elliptical defect),
- the elastic limits, for the plastic correction of the stress intensity factors.

### 3.3 Operand EPAIS_REV

Thickness of the cladding. Required for:

- the computation of the stress intensity factors in the case of the influence-coefficient method (semi-elliptical defect)
- the plastic correction.

### 3.4 Operand MATER_MDB

Name of the concept of type materiau defining the material that constitutes the base metal. Required in order to recover Young's modulus, for the computation of the influence coefficients (semi-elliptical defect).

### 3.5 Operand EPAIS_MDB

Thickness of the base metal, required for the correction of the stress intensity factors by the influence-coefficient method (semi-elliptical defect).

### 3.6 Operand UNITE_LONGUEUR

Makes it possible to use the millimetre as the unit of length.

If the user specifies UNITE_LONGUEUR = “MM” in the command POST_KCP, this means that the user wishes to handle length units in millimetres, and the command POST_KCP carries out the transformation of the units.

### 3.7 Keyword FISSURE

Factor keyword for the geometric characterisation of the defect. It can be used only once.

#### 3.7.1 Operand FORM_FISS

| Shape of the defect: | 'BANDE' for a band defect a/c = 0, |
| --- | --- |
|  | 'SEMI_ELLIPTIQUE' for a semi-elliptical defect, |

#### 3.7.2 Operand CORRECTION

| CORRECTION: | 'beta_2d' for a band defect, |
| --- | --- |
|  | 'beta_3d' for a semi-elliptical defect, |

#### 3.7.3 Operand LONGUEUR

Second dimension of the defect (axial or circumferential according to the orientation of the defect) in SI units.

#### 3.7.4 Operand PROFONDEUR

Radial dimension of the defect in SI units.

#### 3.7.5 Operand ORIENTATION

| Characterisation of the orientation of the defect: | 'CIRC' for a circumferential defect, |
| --- | --- |
|  | 'LONGI' for a longitudinal defect. |

### 3.8 Keyword K1D

Factor keyword for the characterisation of the thermomechanical transient. This keyword may be repeated.

#### 3.8.1 Operands TABL_MECA

The table TABL_MECA is mandatory. It supplies the transient of the stresses undergone by the vessel during the loading history, respectively on the cladding side (of tip A of the defect: interface between the cladding and the base metal) and on the base-metal side (at tip B of the defect).

The parameters common to these tables that are required are:

- 'INST'[R],
- 'ABSC_CURV'[R]
- 'COOR_X'[R],
- 'COOR_Y'[R]
- 'COOR_Z'[R] (in 3D)
- 'SIXX'[R] (in 3D with 'LONGI'),
- 'SIYY'[R] (in 3D with 'LONGI' or in 2D with 'CIRC'),
- 'SIXY'[R] (in 3D with 'LONGI'),
- 'SIZZ'[R] (in 2D with 'LONGI' or in 3D with 'CIRC').

The reader is invited to consult the documentation of the commands POST_RELEVE_T [U4.81.21](doc:u4.81.21) and MACR_LIGN_COUPE [U4.81,13] for further information on the meaning of the parameters.

The macro-command MACR_LIGN_COUPE must be used with the keywords TYPE='SEGMENT' and NB_POINTS=5. The influence-coefficient method requires the evaluation of a polynomial of degree 5 representing the evolution of the stress normal to the segment representing the crack [R7.02.10](doc:r7.02.10). The macro-command MACR_LIGN_COUPE allows the user to define the locations at which the stress is recorded, independently of the mesh used during the thermomechanical study.

#### 3.8.2 Operand TABL_THER

Table supplying the thermal transient in the vessel during the loading history from tip A to tip B.

The required parameters of this table are:

- 'INST'[R],
- 'ABSC_CURV'[R],
- 'TEMP'[R].

The reader is invited to consult the documentation of the commands POST_RELEVE_T [U4.81.21](doc:u4.81.21) and MACR_LIGN_COUPE [U4.81.13](doc:u4.81.13) for further information on the meaning of the parameters.

## 5 Examples

Examples of use of the command POST_KCP are provided in the test case EPICU03.

Before the command POST_KCP is used, it is necessary to record the stresses and the temperatures along the defect.

The tables TABL_MECA and TABL_THER are obtained directly by using the macro-command MACR_LIGN_COUPE on the support segment of the defect going from tip A of the defect on the cladding side to tip B on the base-metal side.

```text
S1G = MACR_LIGN_COUPE(RESULTAT=SIG,
                      VIS_A_VIS=_F(GROUP_MA_1='M'),
                      NOM_CHAM='SIGM ELNO',
                      LIGN_COUPE=_F(TYPE='SEGMENT',
                                    NB_POINTS=5,
                                    COOR_ORIG=(Pointe_A, 0., 0.),
                                    COOR_EXTR=(Pointe_B, 0., 0.)))

TEMP_G = MACR_LIGN_COUPE(RESULTAT=TEMP,
                         NOM_CHAM='TEMP',
                         LIST_INST=INST_TH,
                         LIGN_COUPE=_F(TYPE='SEGMENT',
                                       NB_POINTS=5,
                                       COOR_ORIG=(Pointe_A, 0., 0.),
                                       COOR_EXTR=(Pointe_B, 0., 0.)))
```

After these various recordings have been carried out, the computation of the stress intensity factor can indeed be performed with the command POST_KCP.

```text
KBETA = POST_KCP(MODELE=MO,
                    MATER_REV=MAME_RE2,
                    MATER_MDB=MAME_MET,
                    EPAIS_REV=EPREV,
                    EPAIS_MDB=EPMDB,
                    UNITE_LONGUEUR='M',
                    FISSURE=_F(FORM_FISS='SEMI_ELLIPTIQUE',
                               PROFONDEUR=0.005,
                               LONGUEUR=0.025,
                               ORIENTATION='LONGI'),
                    K1D=_F(TABL_MECA=S1G,
                           TABL_THER=TEMP_G),)
```
