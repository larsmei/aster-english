# v3.02.103 SSLP103 - Calculation of stress intensity coefficients KI and KII for a linear elasticity cracked circular plate

**Summary**

This is a static linear elasticity fracture mechanical test for a two-dimensional problem. A cracked circular plate (with a crack inclined 30 degrees from the abscissa axis) is considered for which the following is calculated:

- stress intensity coefficients ${K}_{I}$ and ${K}_{\mathrm{II}}$ ,
- the $G$ energy return rate from the d-IRWIN formula.

The advantage of the test is to know the analytical solution which gives the stress intensity coefficients and to have an inclined crack.

Two modellings are:

- Modelling A: FEM for elements C_PLAN, D_PLAN
- Modelling B: FEM for elements D_PLAN_INCO_UPG

This test includes a Modelling process that successively treats flat deformations and flat stresses (elements of continuous media).

The numerical results do not differ more than $\text{1 \%}$ to $\text{2 \%}$ from the reference values.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a circular plate of $\mathit{OA}=100\mathit{mm}$ radius, with a crack inclined 30 degrees from the axis of the abscisses.

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.103/10001E9A00001E2E00001E98587B639E42EB891B.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

The material characteristics are as follows:

$E=200000\mathit{MPa}$

$\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The movements are imposed on the contour of the plate. They are derived from the analytical solution singular in mixed mode (with ${K}_{I}=2.$ and ${K}_{\mathrm{II}}=1.$).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.103/10000CA8000016A200000EE22445279D7C236AB8.svg)

In flat deformations or flat stresses, the distribution of displacements is given in this $(0,{x}_{1,}{x}_{2})$ by:

$\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{u}_{1}=\frac{1+\nu }{E}\sqrt{\frac{r}{2\pi }}({K}_{I}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}(k-\cos\theta )+{K}_{\mathit{II}}\sin(\frac{\theta}{2})(k-\cos\theta +2))\\ {u}_{2}=\frac{1+\nu }{E}\sqrt{\frac{r}{2\pi }}({K}_{I}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}(k-\cos\theta )-{K}_{\mathit{II}}\cos(\frac{\theta}{2})(k+\cos\theta -2))\end{array}$

with $k=3-4\nu$ flat deformations

$k=\frac{3-\nu }{1+\nu }$ in flat constraints

or in the $(O,X,Y)$ marker by: $\lbrace \begin{array}{}{u}_{x}=\cos\alpha {u}_{1}-\sin\alpha {u}_{2}\\ {u}_{y}=\sin\alpha {u}_{1}+\cos\alpha {u}_{2}\end{array}$

On the contour of the plate, we have: $r=\mathrm{OA}=100\mathrm{mm}$ .

It is chosen to take ${K}_{I}=2.$ and ${K}_{\mathrm{II}}=1.$ and to impose the displacements on the circular plate contour.

### 3.4 Remarks

The formula of IRWIN gives: $G=\frac{(1-{\nu}^{2})}{E}({K}_{I}^{2}+{K}_{\mathrm{II}}^{2})$ in flat deformations

and $G=\frac{1}{E}({K}_{I}^{2}+{K}_{\mathrm{II}}^{2})$ in flat constraints.

The calculations are made with a lower $10.0$ and higher $20.0$ beam integration crown.
