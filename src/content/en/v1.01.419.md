## 1 Reference problem

The restraint is made between 2 LIAISON_MAILLE-like hulls of the AFFE_CHAR_MECA command. This restraint is made on 2 different meshes and models:

- In the first case, one of the hulls is out of the box. The restraint will take this into account.
- In the second case, no hulls will be excreted. However, they will be arranged so that their average plane is placed in the same place as for the first case. In this case the restraint will be done classically.

Both cases must have the same kinematic relationships.
