# v1.04.116 PETSC01 - Validation of the PETS c solver in linear elasticity 3D

**Summary:**

This test case allows you to validate the PETSC solver in linear elasticity $\mathrm{3D}$ in different configurations:

- Splintered orders or global operators
- Dualization and elimination of boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_CINE/MECA)
- Using PETSC with NEWTON_KRYLOV method in the nonlinear dynamic operator

## 4 Modelling A

MECA_STATIQUE resolution operator.

Dualization and elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA and AFFE_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, CR and CG algorithms (with incomplete LDLT pre-conditioning with 0 filling level and RCMK renumbering).

## 5 Modelling B

STAT_NON_LINE resolution operator.

Dualization and elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA and AFFE_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, CR algorithm (with incomplete LDLT pre-conditioning with 0 filling level and RCMK renumbering).

## 6 Modelling C

CALC_MATR_ELEM, FACTORISER and RESOUDRE split commands.

Dualization and elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA and CALC_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, CR algorithm (with incomplete LDLT pre-conditioning with 0 filling level and RCMK renumbering).

## 7 Modelling D

MECA_STATIQUE resolution operator.

Dualization and elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA and AFFE_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, CR algorithm (pre-conditioning LDLT_SP factoring simple precision and without renumbering).

## 8 Modelling E

STAT_NON_LINE resolution operator.

Dualization and elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA and AFFE_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, CR algorithm (pre-conditioning LDLT_SP factoring simple precision and without renumbering).

## 9 Modelling F

CALC_MATR_ELEM, FACTORISER and RESOUDRE split commands.

Dualization and elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA and CALC_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, CR algorithm (pre-conditioning LDLT_SP factoring simple precision and without renumbering).

Export and import of PETS c vectors in a nodes field using toPetsc and fromPetsc methods is verified.

## 10 Modelling G

MECA_STATIQUE resolution operator.

Elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, CR algorithms and GCR (5 resolutions without renumbering with JACOBI pre-packaging, SOR pre-packaging, SANS pre-packaging, ML preconditioning and BOOMER pre-packaging respectively).

## 11 Modelling H

DYNA_NON_LINE resolution operator.

Dualization and elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA and AFFE_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, GMRES algorithm (LDLT_SP pre-conditioning by simple factoring precision).

## 12 Modelling I

DYNA_NON_LINE resolution operator.

Dualization and elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA and AFFE_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, GMRES algorithm (LDLT_SP pre-conditioning by simple factoring precision).

Using the NEWTON_KRYLOV method instead of the NEWTON method.

## 13 Modelling J

MECA_STATIQUE resolution operator.

Dualization and elimination of kinematic boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA and AFFE_CHAR_CINE).

PETSC Solver, CG and GGRES algorithms with pre-conditioning of the Lagrangian type increased BLOC_LAGR.

## 14 Summary of the results

This test case shows the correct functioning of the PETSC solver in the different cases studied.
