# v3.04.316 SSLV316 – Spread crack imposed with X-FEM

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to verify that the simplex, upwind and geometrical methods of the PROPA_FISS operator correctly calculate the position of the bottom of a 3D crack that propagates in mixed mode.

Several propagations of a crack are simulated by imposing a given advance and propagation direction. The position of the crack after each propagation is therefore known and it can be verified whether the bottom position calculated by the operator PROPA_FISS is correct.

### 1.1 Geometry

**Figure** 1.1-a **: geometry of cracked plate**

| --- | --- |
| thickness | $E=\mathrm{1m}$ |
| height | $H=\mathrm{18m}$ |

Initial length of flat crack: ${a}_{0}=\mathrm{2m}$

The crack is positioned in the middle of the plate height ($H/2$ ).

### 1.2 Material properties

Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.316/100002010000032A000005E65ABF88483B3279A7.png)

**Figure** 1.3-a **: boundary conditions and loads**

boundary conditions:

> Point $P$ : $\Delta X=\Delta Y=\Delta Z=0$
> Points on the $\mathrm{AB}$ segment: $\Delta X=\Delta Z=0$
> Surface points $\text{INF}$ : $\Delta Z=0$

Loading:

> Surface pressure $\text{SUP}$: $P=-1\mathrm{MPa}$

The load is constant during propagation. Three calls to the operator PROPA_FISS are made to simulate a propagation of the initial crack already present in the structure. At each call the advance and the propagation direction of **each point** of the crack bottom are imposed:

> Point forward from background: $\Delta a=0.4m$
> Transmission angle: $\beta =30°$

The positive direction of the $\beta$ angle is visible on the .

The bottom of the crack remains **always straight** throughout the spread. The motivation for this choice will be explained in paragraph 4.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

It is intended to verify that the position of the bottom after propagation, calculated by the operator PROPA_FISS, is correct. Therefore, the theoretical position determined by the advance and the propagation direction imposed must be calculated.

As already noted, the crack bottom remains straight throughout the spread. The bottom is always perpendicular to the two surfaces of the plate parallel to the $\mathit{YZ}$ plane, as shown. The bottom position can therefore be indicated using only the $Y$ and $Z$ coordinates.

The initial position is as follows:

> ${y}_{0}={a}_{0}=2.0$
> ${z}_{0}=9.0$

After propagation $i$ , the new background position can be calculated as follows:

> ${y}_{i}={y}_{i-1}+\Delta a\cdot \cos(i\cdot \beta )$
> ${z}_{i}={z}_{i-1}+\Delta a\cdot \sin(i\cdot \beta )$

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

For the three propagations calculated in the tests, the background position is as follows:

| **Propagation** | **Coord** ${y}_{i}$ | **Coord** ${z}_{i}$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2.34641 | 9.19999 |
| 2 | 2.54642 | 9.54640 |
| 3 | 2.54644 | 9.94640 |

**Table** 2.1

In the current version of Code_Aster, the coordinates of the crack bottom points are only available in the .mess file and therefore they cannot be verified directly in the command file.

However, for this test case, we know the theoretical position and the shape (a segment) of the crack bottom. In fact the bottom is always coincide with the edge that connects the two $(0,{y}_{i},{z}_{i})$ and $(1,{y}_{i},{z}_{i})$ points. Using MACR_LIGN_COUPE, we can record the values of the level set at the intersection points between this edge and the faces of the elements of the mesh. If the position of the bottom after propagation is correctly calculated by PROPA_FISS, the value of the two level set must be equal to zero for all intersection points found because, by definition, the crack bottom is formed by all points where the level set tangent and normal are equal to zero.

This explains why it was decided to give the same advance and direction of propagation at all points of the crack bottom. Even if the crack bottom is located by only two coordinates, the propagation is 3D and the algorithms implemented in PROPA_FISS calculate a 3D propagation.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The **upwind** method is used by PROPA_FISS to solve crack propagation equations.

**No auxiliary grid** is used. This is possible because the mesh of the structure is very regular. The calculation field is **located** around the bottom of the crack.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a mesh composed of 6720 elements HEXA8 (see ).

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.316/10000201000001230000031A91CF748FC10B399D.png)

**Figure** 3.2-a **: mesh of the structure**

The mesh is not very refined to reduce the calculation time. The size of the elements is uniform and equal to $0.29x0.33x0.25m$.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The intersection points between the edge giving the theoretical background position and the faces of elements mesh are calculated using MACR_LIGN_COUPE (see ). For each of these points, the value of the normal ($\mathrm{LSN}$) and tangent ($\mathrm{LST}$) level set is calculated using the POST_RELEVE_T operator and the maximum and minimum values are verified to be almost zero:

| **Propag.** $i$ | **Max** ${\mathit{LSN}}_{i}$ | **Min** ${\mathit{LSN}}_{i}$ | **Max LST_** i | **Min** ${\mathit{LST}}_{i}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | -2.64E-16 | -8.40E-16 | 9,71E-16 | -4,16E-17 |
| 2 | -3.30E-04 | -3.30E-04 | -2.23E-06 | -2.25E-06 |
| 3 | -3.44E-03 | -3.53E-03 | -2.20E-04 | -2.22E-04 |

The values obtained are calculated from the nodes values of mesh using the elements shape functions. Therefore, these values are expected to be affected by an error that depends on the size of the elements of mesh. Indeed, the level set representation accuracy is itself related to the size of the elements. Therefore, a tolerance is used to check if the calculated level sets are almost zero. Considering that the mesh is coarse, a tolerance equal to 15% of the length of the largest mesh edge in the propagation zone is affected:

Tolerance used = $0.15\times 0.33=0.05m$

### 3.4 Remarks

All tested values respect the tolerance used. This means that the crack bottom position calculated by the upwind method is correct and that the domain location works well for the upwind method without auxiliary grid.

It should be noted that after the three simulated propagations, the crack deviated from $90$. However, the total advance is small. Therefore, a very severe mixed mode propagation was simulated. The conditions used are more severe than the conditions normally found for real structures. However, the upwind method did calculate the position of the crack and thus its robustness was verified.

## 12 Summary of the results

All the methods used (upwind, upwind+adjustable griler, simplex and geometric) allowed the position of a crack propagating in mixed mode under severe conditions to be properly calculated. This also allowed the implementation of these methods in the PROPA_FISS operator to be validated, including the possibility of locating the level-set update area.

If the mesh used in the test case is considered to be coarse, it can be said that the methods calculate the position of the crack bottom in a sufficiently precise manner.
