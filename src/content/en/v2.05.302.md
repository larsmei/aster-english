# v2.05.302 SDLX302 - clamped beam and concentrated mass subjected to transverse random force

**Summary:**

A clamped beam with a concentrated mass is subjected to random strain in the transverse direction.

This test is valid, using a code comparison, for calculating the proper bending modes and the displacement modes in a stochastic approach.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.05.302/Object_1.svg)

| Length of beam | $L=20.0m$ |
| --- | --- |
| Inner diameter | $d=0.388m$ |
| Outer diameter | $D=0.400m$ |
| Mass concentrated at the top | $M=300.\mathrm{kg}$ |
| Mass inertia moment | $J=200.\mathrm{kg}{m}^{2}$ |

### 1.2 Material properties

| Volumetric mass of the tube | $\rho =7850{\mathrm{kg.m}}^{-3}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Young's modulus | $E=210.E+9{\mathrm{N.m}}^{-2}$ |
| Poisson's ratio | $\nu =0.$ |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

The tube is clamped at the base. The mass is free. Movement is allowed in a vertical plane $(\mathrm{DX},\mathrm{DRZ})$ .

A random $F(t)$ effort applied to the concentrated mass is assimilated to a Gaussian stationary, centered, white noise-limited-banded process from $\mathrm{1.Hz}$ to $\mathrm{101.Hz}$. It is characterized by an difference type ${\sigma}_{F}=1\mathrm{kN}$, and a unilateral spectral density in ${S}_{F}(f)$ frequency such as:

$\begin{array}{}\forall f\in [1\mathrm{Hz},101z]\\ {S}_{F}(f)=\frac{{\sigma}_{F}^{2}}{100}={10}^{4}{N}^{2}s\end{array}$
