# v9.01.110 SZLZ110 - Generalized Lemaître damage in post-processing

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to calculate the damage of Lemaître generalized "LEMAIT_S" from the stress tensor data and the plastic deformation accumulated at all times ${t}_{i}$ (provided by the user).

The material characteristics $E$ (Young's modulus), $\nu$ (Poisson's ratio), $S$ and ${p}_{d}$ (material parameters) may depend on the temperature, which must therefore be provided by the user at the same times as the constants and plastic deformation.

## 1 Reference problem

The $D(t)$ damage is calculated from the $\sigma (t)$ stress tensor data and the $p(t)$ cumulative plastic deformation resulting from a thermomechanical calculation. The kinetics of damage are given by:

| $\dot{D}=\frac{1}{{(1-D)}^{\mathrm{2s}}}{\left[\frac{1}{\mathrm{3ES}}(1+\nu ){\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{2}+\frac{3}{\mathrm{2ES}}(1-2\nu ){\sigma}_{H}^{2}\right]}^{s}\dot{p}$ | si $p>{p}_{d}$ |
| --- | --- |
| $D=0$ | if not |

${\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}$ is the equivalent constraint of von Mises

${\sigma}_{H}$ is the hydrostatic stress

${p}_{d}$ represents the damage threshold

$S$ is a material characteristic ($\mathrm{MPa}$ )

$s$ is a material characteristic

### 1.2 Loading

The load corresponds to a tensile test at constant temperature and at the required deformation speed, as defined in paragraph [§2.2].

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is generated from the POST_FATIGUE option. The methodology adopted is to define a load history and recover the evolution of the combined plastic deformation associated with a $\mathrm{3D}$ tensile test in thermo-viscoplasticity.

The $\sigma (t)$ and $p(t)$ loading history is then used in an POST_FATIGUE calculation with the material parameters presented in paragraph [§1.1] to define an reference solution.
