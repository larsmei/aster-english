# v2.05.001 SDLX01 - Flexion of a symmetrical portal

**Summary:**

This plan problem consists of searching for vibration frequencies of a mechanical structure composed of a rectangular-sectioned beam assembly (symmetric portal). This structural mechanical test corresponds to a dynamic analysis of an assembly structure having linear behavior. It includes a single model.

Through this problem, the Timoshenko beam element is tested and vibration frequencies are calculated by the reverse iteration method.

The results obtained are in very good agreement with those of the VPCS guide. The error on the first thirteen vibration frequencies is less than 0.2%.
