# v2.05.002 SDLX02 - Pipe: Hovgaard problem. Spectral analysis

**Summary:**

The three-dimensional problem consists of *primo* , looking for the vibration modes of a mechanical structure composed of a curved beam clamped-clamped (Hovgaard problem), *secundo* , to analyze the response of this structure subjected to an acceleration spectrum. This mechanical test of the structures corresponds to a dynamic analysis of a linear model (structure assembly) with linear behavior. It comprises three modellings.

Through this problem, the Timoshenko beam element (straight beam) is tested in bending, the calculation of eigenmodes by the Lanczos method, the calculation of static modes and the calculation of a spectral response of a structure subject to an acceleration spectrum (also tested spectrum interpolation).

The results obtained are in good agreement with the reference results (compilation of results obtained by other software packages).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Averages of results obtained with the calculation software : Poux, ADL, TITUS-T.
See also bib1: Validation Guide for Structure Calculation Software- AFNOR- 1990 (for modal calculation).
The reference values of the eigenfrequencies provided in the form are underestimated and were subsequently corrected in 1992.
However, they were retained for diagonal mass matrix calculations, which are known to produce a default approximation of the eigenfrequencies, contrary to the coherent mass matrix.

### 2.2 Baseline

| Modal calculation: | First nine eigenfrequencies. |
| --- | --- |
|  | nodes displacement: $\mathrm{N3}$ $\mathrm{N5}$ and $\mathrm{N7}$ , $\mathrm{N9}$ , $\mathrm{N11}$ |
| Spectral modal response: | Nodes support reaction: $\mathrm{N1}$ , $\mathrm{N15}$ |
|  | Generalised nodes efforts: $\mathrm{N3}$ , $\mathrm{N7}$ , $\mathrm{N11}$ |

### 2.3 Uncertainty on the solution in eigenfrequencies

In the order of ${1\%}$ on the first five modes.

Between ${1\%}$ and ${2,5\%}$ for modes 6 to 9.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references
