# v2.01.025 SDLD25 - Mass-spring system with proportional viscous shock absorber (spectral response)

**Summary**

This unidirectional problem consists in performing a spectral seismic analysis of a mechanical structure consisting of a set of spring masses with viscous shock absorbers subjected to seismic stress provided in the form of a response spectrum of pseudo acceleration oscillators.

Through this problem, we test the SRSS operator's COMB_SISM_MODAL [U4.54.04](doc:u4.54.04) modal combination. In addition, we test several pre-processing operators; DEFI_FONCTION and DEFI_NAPPE.

This test is also a POUX resorption test. There is no differences between the Code_Aster results and the POUX results.
