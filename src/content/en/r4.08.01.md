# r4.08.01 Computation of the thermal strain

**Summary**

This document is devoted to the presentation of the computation of the thermal strain. It indicates the information required for the computation of the thermal strain and the various possibilities for the definition of this information by the user.

## 1 Introduction

The values of the expansion coefficients are determined by dilatometry tests which take place starting from the ambient temperature ($0°C$ or more generally $20°C$). Consequently, one generally has the values of the expansion coefficient defined with respect to $20°C$ (the temperature at which the thermal strain is assumed to be zero).

Certain studies require a reference temperature different from the ambient temperature (zero thermal strain for a temperature other than the ambient temperature). A change of reference must then be carried out in the computation of the thermal strain (equation and figure below).

![Figure](/figures/r4.08.01/100034F0000069BB0000374BB075F41294B1EA27.svg)

$$
{\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}\left(T\right)={\epsilon}_{m}^{\mathit{th}}\left(T\right)-{\epsilon}_{m}^{\mathit{th}}\left({T}_{\mathit{ref}}\right)
$$

Where ${\epsilon}_{m}^{\mathit{th}}$ is the measured thermal strain (defined with respect to the ambient temperature) and ${\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}$ is the computed thermal strain (defined with respect to a reference temperature).

In *code_aster*, the thermal strain is computed by the following expression:

$$
{\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}\left(T\right)=\widehat{\alpha}\left(T\right)\left(T-{T}_{\mathit{ref}}\right)
$$

where $\widehat{\alpha}\left(T\right)$ is the mean expansion coefficient (in the RCC_M sense) at the temperature $T$ determined with respect to the temperature ${T}_{\mathit{ref}}$ (${T}_{\mathit{ref}}$ being the temperature at which one considers that ${\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}\left({T}_{\mathit{ref}}\right)=0$ ).

In THM, the computation of the thermal strain is different. The thermal strain is evaluated by the following formula:

$$
{\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}\left(T\right)=\alpha \left(T-{T}_{\mathit{ini}}\right)
$$

The thermal expansion coefficient $\alpha$ is necessarily a constant. The curve cannot be shifted. The thermal expansion coefficient must therefore have been evaluated at the ambient temperature. In addition, the reference temperature is given by THM_INIT/TEMP in DEFI_MATERIAU.

## 2 Computation of the thermal expansion coefficient

### 2.1 From the reference temperature

The values of the thermal expansion coefficient have been determined by dilatometry tests carried out starting from the temperature ${T}_{\mathit{ref}}$ . In this case, the keyword TEMP_DEF_ALPHA must not be specified in the command DEFI_MATERIAU [u4.23.01](doc:u4.23.01). The equation is simplified, since ${\epsilon}_{m}^{\mathit{th}}\left({T}_{\mathit{ref}}\right)=0$ . Hence:

$$
{\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}\left(T\right)=\widehat{\alpha}\left(T\right)\left(T-{T}_{\mathit{ref}}\right)
$$

Where the values of the expansion coefficient $\widehat{\alpha}\left(T\right)$ are entered under the keyword ALPHA (or ALPHA_* ) in DEFI_MATERIAU.

### 2.2 From a temperature different from the reference temperature

The values of the thermal expansion coefficient have been determined by dilatometry tests which took place starting from a temperature ${T}_{\text{def}}$ different from the reference temperature ${T}_{\mathit{ref}}$ .

Indeed, in general one has the values of the expansion coefficient defined with respect to the ambient temperature, $0°C$ or more generally $20°C$ , and certain studies require a reference temperature different from the ambient temperature.

A change of reference must then be carried out (by the equation ). In this case, the user enters, under the keyword TEMP_DEF_ALPHA of the command DEFI_MATERIAU, the value of the temperature ${T}_{\text{def}}$ , and under the keyword ALPHA (or ALPHA_* ) the values of the expansion coefficient $\alpha \left(T\right)$ (defined with respect to the temperature ${T}_{\text{def}}$ ). In the command AFFE_MATERIAU, under the keyword TEMP_REF, the user indicates the value of the reference temperature ${T}_{\mathit{ref}}$ . The computation of ${\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}\left(T\right)$ is carried out using the formula:

$$
{\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}\left(T\right)=\alpha \left(T\right)\left(T-{T}_{\text{def}}\right)-\alpha \left({T}_{\mathit{ref}}\right)\left({T}_{\mathit{ref}}-{T}_{\text{def}}\right)=\widehat{\alpha}\left(T\right)\left(T-{T}_{\mathit{ref}}\right)
$$

The computation of ${\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}\left(T\right)$ requires the prior computation of the values of the function $\widehat{\alpha}\left(T\right)$ .

The function $\widehat{\alpha}\left(T\right)$ remains defined (or entered) for the same values of $T$ as $\alpha \left(T\right)$ and keeps the same attributes (same type of interpolation ('LIN', 'LOG') and same type of prolongation ('CONSTANT', 'LINEAIRE', 'EXCLUS').

#### 2.2.1 Computation at temperatures different from the reference (to within a precision)

The expression of $\widehat{\alpha}({T}_{i})$ is obtained using the equation .

$$
\widehat{\alpha}\left(T_{i}\right) = \frac{\alpha\left(T_{i}\right)\left(T_{i} - T_{\text{def}}\right) - \alpha\left(T_{\mathit{ref}}\right)\left(T_{\mathit{ref}} - T_{\text{def}}\right)}{T_{i} - T_{\mathit{ref}}}   \quad \forall i \text{ tel que } T_{i} - T_{\mathit{ref}} \ge \text{Prec}
$$

The value of the precision is:

- either specified by the user under the keyword PRECISION of the factor keyword ELAS_FO (command DEFI_MATERIAU [u4.23.01](doc:u4.23.01)),
- or equal to $1.$ : default value.

#### 2.2.2 Computation at temperatures close to the reference (to within a precision)

The equation cannot be used directly. The equation is differentiated with respect to the temperature:

$$
\frac{\partial {\epsilon}^{\mathit{th}}}{\partial T}=\alpha '(T)(T-{T}_{\text{def}})+\alpha (T)=\widehat{\alpha}'(T)(T-{T}_{\mathit{ref}})+\widehat{\alpha}(T)
$$

Taking the value of the derivative at the temperature ${T}_{\mathit{ref}}$ , one obtains:

$$
\widehat{\alpha}({T}_{\mathit{ref}})=\alpha '({T}_{\mathit{ref}})({T}_{\mathit{ref}}-{T}_{\text{def}})+\alpha ({T}_{\mathit{ref}})
$$

It is considered that:

$$
\widehat{\alpha}(T_{i}) = \widehat{\alpha}(T_{\mathit{ref}}) \quad \forall i \text{ tel que } T_{i} - T_{\mathit{ref}} \ge \text{Prec}
$$

The value of the precision is:

- either specified by the user under the keyword PRECISION of the factor keyword ELAS_FO (command DEFI_MATERIAU [u4.23.01](doc:u4.23.01)),
- or equal to $1.$ : default value.

Also, in order to compute $\widehat{\alpha}({T}_{i})$ one must first compute $\alpha '({T}_{\mathit{ref}})$ .

#### 2.2.3 Computation of the derivative of the thermal expansion coefficient

The computation of the derivative of the thermal expansion coefficient is carried out by an algorithm that treats three cases.

First case:

$$
\forall i\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\text{tel que}\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}|{T}_{i}-{T}_{\mathit{ref}}|\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}<\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\text{Prec}\text{avec}i\ne 1,i\ne N
\alpha '({T}_{\mathit{ref}})=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\alpha ({T}_{i+1})-\alpha ({T}_{\mathit{ref}})}{{T}_{i+1}-{T}_{\mathit{ref}}}+\frac{\alpha ({T}_{\mathit{ref}})-\alpha ({T}_{i-1})}{{T}_{\mathit{ref}}-{T}_{i-1}}\right)
$$

Second case:

$$
\forall i\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\text{tel que}\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}|{T}_{i}-{T}_{\mathit{ref}}|\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}<\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\text{Prec}\text{avec}i=N
\alpha '({T}_{\mathit{ref}})=\frac{\alpha ({T}_{\mathit{ref}})-\alpha ({T}_{i-1})}{{T}_{\mathit{ref}}-{T}_{i-1}}
$$

Third case:

$$
\forall i \quad \text{tel que} \quad \left| T_{i} - T_{\mathit{ref}} \right| < \text{Prec} \quad \text{avec } i = 1
\alpha'(T_{\mathit{ref}}) = \frac{\alpha(T_{i+1}) - \alpha(T_{\mathit{ref}})}{T_{i+1} - T_{\mathit{ref}}}
$$
