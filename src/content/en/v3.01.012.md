# v3.01.012 SSLL12 - Three-switched bar trim

**Summary:**

Static linear mechanical response of structures of a triangulated system of articulated bars (ground trellis) under 3 solicitations:

- support displacement,
- point forces,
- expansion effect.

This test validates the element BARRE in different loading cases. It also validates the LIAISON_OBLIQUE option of the AFFE_CHAR_MECA command.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.01.012/100000000000034000000172A7F2065165B3FC70.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

Isotropic linear elastic material: $E=2.1E+11\mathrm{Pa}$

Lineic expansion coefficient: $\alpha =1.E–05°{C}^{-1}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

$A$ (${u}_{A}={v}_{A}=0$ ) articulation.

Roller support in $B$ and $C$ (${v}_{B}=v{’}_{C}=0$ ).

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Cumulation of 3 solicitations:

- support displacement: ${v}_{A}=–0.02m$ , ${v}_{B}=–0.03m$ , $v{'}_{C}=–0.015m$
- Point forces: ${F}_{E}=–\mathrm{150 }\mathrm{kN}$ , ${F}_{F}=–\mathrm{100 }\mathrm{kN}$
- expansion effect of all bars for difference temperature of $30°C$ relative to the mounting temperature (reference geometry).

The differences relative to the references are less than $\text{0.18\%}$ for the demands (thermal dilation, point face, forced displacement) separated and less than $\text{0.34\%}$ when these stresses are combined.
