# v4.61.101 MTLP101 - Metallurgical calculation for a zircaloy

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to calculate the nodes of metallurgical evolution associated with a thermal history in the case of a zircaloy.

It is involved in the validation of the CALC_META command.

## 1 Reference problem

![Figure](/figures/v4.61.101/Shape1.gif)

### 1.1 Geometry

**Figure 1.1-a: Geometry of the problem**

It is a cylinder of $0.05m$ radius and $0.05m$ height.

The square in bold corresponds to the axisymmetric model used in [§3].

### 1.2 Material properties

The material properties are described by the following parameters:

(Zirconium)

$\rho {C}_{P}=2000000{\mathrm{J.m}}^{-3}.°{C}^{-1}$

$\lambda =9999.9{\mathrm{W.m}}^{-1}.°{C}^{-1}$

Metallurgy coefficients:

$\mathrm{teqd}=809°C$ , $K=1.135E-2$ , $n=2.187$

$\mathrm{t1c}=831°C$ , $\mathrm{t2C}=0.$ , $\mathrm{qsr}=14614$ , $\mathrm{Ac}=1.58E-4$

$m=4.7$ , $\mathrm{t1r}=949,1°C$ , $\mathrm{t2r}=0$ , $\mathrm{Ar}=-5.725$ , $\mathrm{Br}=0.05$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The temperature is imposed on the entire cylinder at the times $t=\mathrm{0s},\mathrm{120s}$ and $\mathrm{240s}$.

$T(x,y,t=0)=20°C$

$T(x,y,t=120)=1200°C$

$T(x,y,t=240)=20°C$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The following variables are initialized to the following values:

$\mathrm{V1}(x,y,t=0)=1.0$

$\mathrm{V2}(x,y,t=0)=0.0$

$\mathrm{V3}(x,y,t=0)=0.0$

$\mathrm{V4}(x,y,t=0)=0.0$

$\mathrm{V1}$ *: proportion of cold phase* $\alpha$

$\mathrm{V2}$ *: proportion of cold phase* $\alpha$ *, mixed with phase* $\beta$

$\mathrm{V3}$ *: nodes temperatures*

$\mathrm{V4}$ *: time corresponding to the temperature at which the equilibrium transformation begins or ends*

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Baseline

The reference results were obtained with an earlier version of Aster.

The tests performed are non-regression tests.

### 2.2 Uncertainty about the solution compared to the non-regression result

The uncertainty criterion is in absolute value. It is $[\mathrm{1E-4,}1E-2]$ .
