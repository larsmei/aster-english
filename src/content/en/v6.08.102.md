# v6.08.102 SSND102 – constitutive law non-linear kinematic for discreet elements

**Summary:**

The problem is quasi-static non-linear in structural mechanics. It is a law with non-linear kinematic hardening. We analyze the response of discrete elements supporting a non-linear constitutive law kinematic under a cyclic load.

The discrete elements tested are:

1. $\mathrm{3D}$ (K_T_D_L, K_TR_D_L, K_T_D_N, K_TR_D_N);
2. $\mathrm{2D}$ (K_T_D_L, K_TR_D_L, K_T_D_N, K_TR_D_N).

All degrees of freedom are tested, the parameters of the loading on the move and those of the kinematic law are different for all degrees of freedom so as to cover different situations.

Reference solutions are obtained in an analytical way or by comparison with an external application.
