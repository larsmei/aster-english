# 1 **v3.03.116** SSLS116 - Membrane loading of an excreted plate

**Summary:**

This test concerns the excentre of a plate in relation to the plane of the mesh or plane of the purity. The load is purely membrane.

The reference is given by a first resolution where one models a non-extracted plate. It validates the second calculation where one models a plate out of the mesh plane.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.116/Forme1.gif)

The plate excreted from the depurate plane (which is here confused with the lower plane of the excreted plate) was represented here.

To avoid overloading the diagram, the nonextracted reference plate is not drawn, for which the depurate plane is also the average plane.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material component plate is characterised by the following data:

| $\mathrm{EL}=\mathrm{20000.Pa}$ | $\mathrm{ET}=\mathrm{20000.Pa}$ | $\mathrm{EN}=\mathrm{20000.Pa}$ |
| --- | --- | --- |

$\nu LN=0.$ $\nu LT=0$ $\nu TN=0.$ $\mathrm{GLT}=\mathrm{2000.Pa}$ $\mathrm{GLN}=0.$ $\mathrm{GTN}=0.$

$\rho =\mathrm{1000.kg}/{m}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The mesh cell $\mathrm{L14}$ is clamped $\mathrm{DX}=\mathrm{DY}=\mathrm{DZ}=0.$

$\mathrm{DRX}=\mathrm{DRY}=\mathrm{DRZ}=0.$

The forces are applied to the mesh cell $\mathrm{L12}$

| $\mathrm{FX}=\mathrm{1000.N}$ $\mathrm{MY}=\mathrm{100.N.m}$ | on the excreted plate |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathrm{FX}=\mathrm{1000.N}$ | on the nonextracted plate |

These loads are applied using FORCE_ARETE of AFFE_CHAR_MECA in the depurate plane.

**Note:**

*The application of a force* $\mathrm{FX}$ *on the plate excreted at the plane of the depuration generates a moment* $\mathrm{MY}$ *which must be compensated to be in purely membrane conditions at the level of the excretion plate.*

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The membrane type calculation with the nonextracted plate is used as a reference. The non-regression in relation to the results obtained by this first calculation is verified.

### 2.2 Baseline

They consist of the $\mathrm{DX},\mathrm{DY},\mathrm{DZ},\mathrm{DRX},\mathrm{DRY}$ displacement field values to nodes $\mathrm{N66}$ ( for DKT) and nodes $\mathrm{N1}$ (for DKQ) and the calculation of frequencies for the first 4 modes.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

The uncertainty is zero since it is the same calculation made by two different ways.

- [R3.07.03](doc:r3.07.03): DKT,DST,DKQ,DSQ and Q4G plate elements.
- [R3.07.06](doc:r3.07.06): Treatment of the elements DKT,DST,DKQ,DSQ and Q4G plate.
