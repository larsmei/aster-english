# v1.01.363 ZZZZ363 – Dynamic damage to AXIS-cut test piece

**Summary:**

This test represents a dynamic damage calculation of a cut-out test piece. It allows the validation of GRAD_VARI Modelling to axisymmetric, which allows for the calculation of damage regulated by the gradient of damage, taking into account degrees of freedom displacement and damage to nodes.

GRAD_VARI Modelling performs a local resolution, Gauss points by Gauss points. AXIS_GRAD_VARI Modelling is valid for constitutive law ENDO_SCALAIRE.

Modelling and constitutive laws Endoming Tested:

- **Model A**: AXIS_GRAD_VARI Modelling with constitutive law ENDO_SCALAIRE

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A $10\mathit{dm}$ height cut test piece is considered. $3\mathit{dm}$ width and $1\mathit{dm}$ radius of ENT.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.363/10000000000001B00000027DE55D1F6471B68862.png)

**Figure 1:** **Promotion of the problem**

### 1.2 Common properties of the material

The material is assimilated to concrete (decipemetric scale is used):

Elastic characteristics:

> $E=3.E10\text{Pa}=3.E8{\text{N/dm}}^{2}$
> $\nu =0.2$

Tensile strength of the damage law:

> $\mathit{SY}=3.E4{\text{N/dm}}^{2}=3.E6\text{Pa}$

Energy restitution rate per crack surface (Griffith constant):

> ${G}_{f}=10\text{N/dm}=100{\text{J/m}}^{2}$

Density:

> $\mathit{Rho}=2300\mathit{Kg}/{m}^{3}=2.3\mathit{Kg}/{\mathit{dm}}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

**Block**: $\mathrm{DX}=0$ on nodes that belong to the symmetry axis ($x=0.$ ), to ensure the structure is maintained (in red on Figure 1).

**Loading**: The upper and lower parts of the cut test piece are moved (in green on Figure 1).

## 2 reference solution

This test case is a case of non-regression. This is a study, which quickly changes from a weakly damaged state to a state of rupture (damage equal to 1).

The loads are not analytical. The values of the imposed displacements are simply empirical. Similarly, associated damage values are found which are not exact values. With no reference value for this type of Modelling, this test case is checked only for non-regression of the code.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

A model of damage GRAD_VARI, which is a mixed Lagrangian formulation of damage regulated by the gradient of damage, is considered. It takes into account in addition the degrees of freedom of displacement and damage to the nodes, the coeficients of Lagrange.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 1034 elements TRIA6 and 462 elements QUAD8. The mesh at the centre of the test piece is oriented out of symmetry.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.363/10000000000001F9000003044A9724E6DB505A33.png)

**Figure 2**: Representation of mesh

### 3.3 Damage law: material ENDO_SCALAIRE

Standard concrete characteristics are defined above.

Characteristics related to the non-local damage law:

$c=1.875\text{N};p=1.5;m=10$ which corresponds to the damage area $\mathrm{1D}$ equal to $D=0.5\text{dm}$

The correspondence with physical parameters is as follows:

$c=3/8D{G}_{f};m=\frac{3E{G}_{f}}{2D\cdot {\mathit{SY}}^{2}};p=m/4-1;$

### 3.4 Boundary conditions and loads

** Charge**:

A normal $-5\mathit{SY}/E=-5E-04$ displacement speed is imposed on the upper part and $+5\mathit{SY}/E=5e-04$ on the lower part of the cut-out swab so that the test piece works in compression.

The load on the required displacement is applied during $1.2\mathit{sec}$ .

### 3.5 Tested quantities and results

This test case is only validated in non-regression:

- Non-regression on the point $\text{P\_HAUT}$ move test.
- Non-regression test on point stress field of gauss 1 of mesh cell $\mathit{M160}$ .
- Non-regression test on the point forced field of gauss 1 of the mesh cell $\mathit{M1324}$ .
- Non-regression test on maximum stress in test piece.
- Non-regression test on the nodal reaction to point $\text{P\_HAUT}$ .

## 4 Summary of the results

This test allows to check in non-regression:

- AXIS_GRAD_VARI Modelling with constitutive law ENDO_SCALAIRE dynamic.
