# v1.01.402 ZZZZ402 - Validation of POST_NEWMARK

**Summary:**

This test validates the use of the POST_NEWMARK command.

Modelization A: simulation of seismic response and calculation of residual displacements of a work in embankment in elasticity with DYNA_VIBRA in transient time calculation.

Modelling B: simulation of seismic response and calculation of residual displacements of a embankment work with the Iwan law via DYNA_NON_LINE.

Modelization C: simulation of seismic response and calculation of the dynamic safety factor of a work in embankment in elasticity with DYNA_VIBRA in transient time calculation.

Modelling D: simulation of seismic response and calculation of residual displacements of a remplacement structure in elasticity with DYNA_VIBRA in transient time calculation, with verification of the calculation of the mass of the sliding circle with keyword VERI_MASSE.

Modelling E: simulation of seismic response and calculation of residual displacements of a work in embankment in elasticity with DYNA_VIBRA in transient time calculation, and estimation of critical acceleration and the ky seismic coefficient.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution of Modelling A is a non-regression test.

Model A is taken into account as the calculation reference for model B.

Model C performs the calculation of the safety factor for a dynamic calculation.

Modelling D compares two mesh results with different refinements to verify the impact of the mesh elements Finis mass estimate.

Modelling E performs critical acceleration calculation and seismic coefficient.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Model A uses a behavior model for the linear dam, the calculation is performed from the DYNA_VIBRA operator on a physical temporal basis, with a non-depreciated Newmark time integration scheme ($\beta =0,25$ and $\gamma =0,5$) and a reduced modal damping of 0.01%.

The A circle definition model is used from the RAYON, CENTRE_X, and CENTRE_Y keywords and from a sufficiently refined 2D auxiliary mesh from the same circle using the keyword MAILLAGE_GLIS.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

The value of irreversible displacement is tested at different times estimated by the Newmark method.

| t=0,9 s | 'NON_DEFINI' | 0.001% |
