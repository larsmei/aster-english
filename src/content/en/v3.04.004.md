# v3.04.004 SSLV04 - Hollow, flat-stressed cylinder

**Summary:**

This test is taken from the VPCS Guide (test SSLV04/89) and has as its object a hollow cylinder charged with internal pressure.

This three-dimensional problem is dealt with with with different modellings:

- $3D$: 9 modellings (Pentahedra, hexahedron, tetrahedron and pyramids, degrees 1 and 2),
- $2D$ flat stress: 4 modellings (diangles and quadrangles degrees 1 and 2, quadrangles at 9 nodes),
- $2D$ axisymmetric: 3 modellings (diangles and quadrangles degrees 1 and 2, quadrangles at 9 nodes).

The tested features are:

- distributed pressure,
- background effect (with fixed or variable pressure),
- travel requirements,
- rigidity matrices,
- deformations and stresses of nodes,
- nodal reactions (modelization K),
- use of MACR_LIGN_COUP on a multi_elas concept and a comb_fourier concept (modelization I)

Il y a 16 modellings.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Flat stress (cylinder with free edges at the ends)

$\sigma_{zz} = 0$

$\sigma_{rr} = P \frac{a^2}{b^2 - a^2} \left( 1 - \frac{b^2}{r^2} \right)$

$\sigma_{\theta\theta} = P \frac{a^2}{b^2 - a^2} \left( 1 + \frac{b^2}{r^2} \right)$

$\sigma_{r\theta} = 0$

$u_r = \frac{P}{E} \frac{a^2}{b^2 - a^2}  \left( (1 - \nu) + (1 + \nu)\frac{b^2}{r^2} \right) r$

$\varepsilon_{rr} = \frac{P}{E} \frac{a^2}{b^2 - a^2}    \left( (1 - \nu) - (1 + \nu)\frac{b^2}{r^2} \right)$

$\varepsilon_{\theta\theta} = \frac{u_r}{r}$

We get:

$$
\text{pour } r = 0.1 :
\begin{cases}
u_r = 59.10^{-6} \\
\sigma_{rr} = -60 \\
\sigma_{\theta\theta} = 100 \\
\sigma_{zz} = \sigma_{r\theta} = 0 \\
\varepsilon_{rr} = -45.10^{-5} \\
\varepsilon_{\theta\theta} = 59.10^{-5}
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\text{pour } r = 0.2 :
\begin{cases}
u_r = 40.10^{-6} \\
\sigma_{rr} = 0 \\
\sigma_{\theta\theta} = 40 \\
\sigma_{zz} = \sigma_{r\theta} = 0 \\
\varepsilon_{rr} = -6.10^{-5} \\
\varepsilon_{\theta\theta} = 2.10^{-4}
\end{cases}
$$

Crossing into the Cartesian axis system:

$\sigma_{xx} = \sigma_{rr}\cos^2\theta + \sigma_{\theta\theta}\sin^2\theta - 2\sigma_{r\theta}\sin\theta\cos\theta$

$\sigma_{yy} = \sigma_{rr}\sin^2\theta + \sigma_{\theta\theta}\cos^2\theta + 2\sigma_{r\theta}\sin\theta\cos\theta$

$\sigma_{xy} = \sigma_{rr}\sin\theta\cos\theta - \sigma_{\theta\theta}\sin\theta\cos\theta - 2\sigma_{r\theta}(\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta)$

with:

- $\theta = 0^\circ$ at $A \text{ et } B$ points,
- $\theta = 22.5^\circ$ at $C \text{ et } D$ points,
- $\theta = 45^\circ$ at $E \text{ et } F$ points.

### 2.2 Baseline

$(u, v)$ displacements and $(\sigma_{xx}, \sigma_{yy}, \sigma_{zz}, \sigma_{xy})$ constraints to $A, B, C, D, E, F$ points.
