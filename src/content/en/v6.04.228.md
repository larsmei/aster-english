# v6.04.228 SSNV228 – Pre-voltage of a stud

**Summary:**

This test illustrates the pre-voltage of a stud in Code_Aster. The methodology used is to apply a relative displacement between a d-group of the stud and a d-group of the nut (between the two faces that are normally threaded). Two modellings are proposed:

- **Model A**: Manual displacement determination (use of `DEFI_CONTACT`)
- **Model B**: Automated determination of displacement using `MACR_RECAL` (use of `DEFI_CONTACT`).
- **Model C**: Manual displacement determination (use of `DEFI_CONT`)

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Methodology

The pre-voltage setting consists of imposing a relative displacement between nodes of the nut and nodes of the stud. This relative displacement is imposed in the axis of the stud and must correspond to the desired tightening effort. This relative displacement is imposed via the keyword `LIAISON_GROUP` of the `AFFE_CHAR_MECA_F` command.

For the user, it is therefore a question of determining the displacement to be imposed in order to find the desired clamping effort. To do so, the study must be replayed several times by changing the value of the relative displacement until reaching the desired clamping effort.

This test case offers three modellings:

- **Modelling A**: a model to illustrate a manual calibration (with `DEFI_CONTACT`).
- **Model B**: a model to illustrate an automated re-calibration (with `DEFI_CONT`),
- **Modelling C**: a model to illustrate a manual calibration (with `DEFI_CONT`).

*Note:*

*This method applies only to small movements.*

*It assumes that the difference in movement between the nut and the stud is constant throughout the area.*

### 1.4 Materials

The material is elastic and its properties are: $E=200000\mathit{MPa}$ and $\nu =0.3$ .

### 1.5 Boundary conditions, contact and loading

The boundary conditions blockings are as follows:

- blocking of the degree of freedom DZ on the sides of the underside of the stud and the joint,
- symmetry conditions via blocking of normal lateral surface displacement with bore,
- symmetry conditions via blocking of normal lateral side movement without bore,
- flatness condition of the cutting face of the tube in the $\mathit{XY}$ plane by imposing an identical value for the degree of freedom DZ on all nodes on the face,
- clamping condition via a zero difference between degrees of freedom DX and DY of the screw screw screw and nut nodes
- $1.0E-06\mathit{Pa}$ pressure inside the tube.
- contact condition between the sides of the joint and flange.

The load corresponds to a relative displacement imposed on the nodes of the nut and stud. It is zero at the moment and is `DEPL_R` at the moment $1.0$.

*Note:

The displacement is named `DEPL_R` in order to reuse the A Modelling control file directly with `MACR_RECAL` in the B Modelling. Indeed, the macro-command requires the presence of '__' at the end of the parameters to be re-calved.

### 1.6 Modelling

The modelling is 3D.

### 1.7 Seeking effort

An `DEPL_R` displacement corresponding to an average $\mathrm{30 }\mathit{MPa}$ stress in the stud stem is applied, which is an effort resulting from:

${F}^{\mathit{résultant}}={\sigma}^{\mathit{pré}-\mathit{tension}}.\pi .{R}_{\mathit{goujon}}^{2}/2=\mathrm{1695.6N}$ . Division by 2 takes symmetry into account.

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics

This model illustrates the method of clamping a stud. It is to impose a relative shift between the nodes of the nut and stud and to calculate the resulting stress. The calculation is made by changing the value of `DEPL_R` until the resulting stress corresponds to the desired effort.

The contact is modelled using `DEFI_CONTACT` and using the continuous method (`FORMULATION` = `CONTINUE`).

### 2.2 Tested quantities and results

The recalibration is considered satisfactory when `DEPL_R` is $8.0E-3\mathit{mm}$ . This value allows to pre-tension the stud with an average stress of $30\mathit{MPa}$ in the stud.

The resulting effort is compared to the effort sought.

| **Result** | **Reference** | **Reference value** | **Tolerance** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resulting effort on the nut | ANALYTIQUE | $-1695.6N$ | $1.0E-02$ |

Non-regression tests with $1.0E-6$ tolerance are performed on the resulting forces of the following surfaces:

- face of the nut in contact with the stud,
- face of the stud in contact with the nut,
- face of the underside of the stud,
- face of the joint in contact with the flange,
- face of flange in contact with joint.

*Note: the resulting stress calculated on each side is identical.*

## 3 Modelling B

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

This Modelling illustrates the method of tensioning a stud by performing an automatic recalibration of the relative displacement value between the nodes of the nut and stud. To do this, the `MACR_RECAL` macro-command is used in a master file and Modelling A as a slave file.

The function of the effort to be achieved is:

```text
CIBLE =DEFI_FONCTION(

NOM_PARA='INST' ,

NOM_RESU='DZ' ,

VALE= (0.0 , 0.0 ,

1.0 , F_RESULT ,),

)
```

Call to the re-arrangement control:

```text
RECAL =MACR_RECAL(

PARA_OPTI = _F(NOM_PARA='DEPL_R__' ,

VALE_INI= 0.004, VALE_MIN= 0.004, VALE_MAX= 0.012, ) ,

COURBE = _F(FONC_EXP= CIBLE, NOM_FONC_CALC='REACF' ,

PARA_X='INST' , PARA_Y='DZ') ,

)
```

In the slave file the calculation of reacf:

```text
REACF =POST_RELEVE_T(

ACTION=_F(INTITULE='FZ_CEG' ,

OPERATION='EXTRACTION' ,

RESULTAT= RESU,

NOM_CHAM='REAC_NODA' ,

RESULTANTE='DZ' ,

GROUP_NO='N_SCEG' , ) ,

)
```

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

The recalibration is used to determine a value of `DEPL_R` close to $8.0E-3\mathit{mm}$ . This value allows the stud to be energized with an average stress of $30\mathit{MPa}$ in its rod.

The only quantity that can be recovered in the `MACR_RECAL` concept is the parameter value. The `TEST_RESU` is therefore performed on this value, and it is a test of non-regression.

| **Result** | **Reference** | **Reference value** | **Tolerance** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resulting effort on the nut | Non-regression | $0.0079595$ | $5.0E-06$ |

*Note: in the slave file a print of *REACF is made. The value of the effort corresponding to the parameter is* $-1.69560E+03$ *, which corresponds to the value of the target effort.*

## 4 Modelling C

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

This model illustrates the method of clamping a stud. It is to impose a relative shift between the nodes of the nut and stud and to calculate the resulting stress. The calculation is made by changing the value of `DEPL_R` until the resulting stress corresponds to the desired effort.

## 5 Summary of the results

With `DEFI_CONTACT`, both methods allow the same result, i.e. a relative displacement to force close to $8.0E-3\mathit{mm}$ to obtain an average stress of $30\mathit{MPa}$ in the stud.

The behaviour of the nonlinear calculation is similar between modellings A and C (passing from the operator `DEFI_CONTACT` to `DEFI_CONT`), despite a difference of a few percents.
