# v7.22.123 HSNV123 - Thermo-metal-mechanical EDGAR

**Summary:**

This test illustrates a mechanical calculation on a material (Zircaloy) undergoing metallurgical transformations.

In concrete terms, the CALC_META operator initially calculates the metallurgical evolution associated with a given thermal history. This metallurgical evolution is then provided to STAT_NON_LINE which will perform a mechanical calculation by taking into account the metallurgical phases (in addition to mechanical loadings). The mechanical calculation material is defined with ELAS_META_FO, META_ECRO_LINE and META_VISC_FO.

This test case non-regression allows you to check the consistency of *Code_Aster* from one version to the next with regard to metallurgy.
