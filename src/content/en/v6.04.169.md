# v6.04.169 SSNV169 - Fluid coupling – damage

**Summary:**

This is an elementary non-regression test to validate the coupling between the BETON_UMLV_FP clean creep model and the ENDO_ISOT_BETON and MAZARS damage models. The test is to maintain a constant effort on an element and to let it flutter.

It is assumed that drying does not evolve over time, so the sorption function is chosen arbitrarily.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

In this test, drying and temperature are assumed to be uniform and invariant. L-m2 is 100% and temperature $20°C$.

The mechanical load corresponds to a unidirectional tensile on the upper side of the test piece ($\mathit{P5P6P7P8}$ ) imposed in 1 second and then kept constant until (quasily) the material ruin by tertiary creep. The load intensity is equal to 0.6 times the instantaneous peak load in the case of coupling with ENDO_ISOT_BETON and 0.8 times the instantaneous peak load for coupling with MAZARS.

## 9 Executive summary

All these tests are non-regression tests, which validate the implantation of point models of computer and not physical view. Thus, on speaker tests, it was noted that the coupling of ENDO_ISOT_BETON model with BETON_UMLV_FP in the current version greatly overestimates damage.

In addition, the user is warned that the current MAZARS model coupling with BETON_UMLV_FP is explicit and therefore sensitive to the size of the time step used. A convergence study is therefore essential.
