# v1.01.221 ZZZZ221 – Validation of a thermo-hydration chaining calculation

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case axisymmetric is to verify the calculation of a thermo-hydration chaining. The concrete structure studied is composed of two levees.

The main steps in the chaining are as follows:

- Step 1: Thermo-Hydratation calculation on the first lift with operators:

  - DEFI_MATERIAU(THER_HYDR),
  - THER_NON_LINE(COMPORTEMENT='THER_HYDR'),
- Step 2: Defnition of initial conditions on both lifts from the first lift results and initial values for the second lift.

  - CREA_CHAMP(OPERATION='AFFE',NOM_CMP='TEMP')
  - CREA_CHAMP(OPERATION='AFFE',NOM_CMP='HYDR_NOEU')
- Step 3: Thermo-Hydratation calculation on both lifts with operators:

  - DEFI_MATERIAU(THER_HYDR),
  - THER_NON_LINE(COMPORTEMENT='THER_HYDR'),

Non-regression tests are performed on temperature and hydration.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
