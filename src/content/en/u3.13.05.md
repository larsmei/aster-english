# u3.13.05 Modellings AXIS_SI, D_PLAN_SI, C_PLAN_SI

**Summary:**

The modellings AXIS_SI, D_PLAN_SI, C_PLAN_SI (Phenomenon: mechanical) correspond to finite elements whose support mesh cells are surface mesh cells.

The suffix _SI means: Under-integrated: the integration of the terms relating to the constitutive laws is carried out in a reduced manner (Gauss-point scheme of lower order than the modelling with full integration).

The modelling assumptions are the following:

- AXIS_SI for axisymmetry (Fourier mode 0) about the $Y$ axis,
- D_PLAN_SI for the plane strains,
- C_PLAN_SI for the plane stresses.

This document describes, for the modellings:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear possibilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings.

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

| **Modelling** | **Degrees of freedom** **(at each vertex node)** |
| --- | --- |
| AXIS_SI | DX: corresponds to the radial displacement DY: corresponds to the longitudinal displacement |
| D_PLAN_SI | DX: displacement along $X$ DY: displacement along $Y$ |
| C_PLAN_SI | DX: displacement along $X$ DY: displacement along $Y$ |

### 1.2 Support mesh cell of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the finite elements may be triangles or quadrilaterals. The elements are isoparametric. Only the element which is based on the mesh cell QUAD8 is under-integrated.

| **Modellings** | **Mesh cell** | **Interpolation** | **integration** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AXIS_SI D_PLAN_SI C_PLAN_SI | TRIA3 QUAD4 TRIA6 QUAD8 QUAD9 | Linear Bi-linear Quadratic Serendipity Bi-quadratic | full reduced full reduced full |

### 1.3 Support mesh cell of the loadings

| **Modellings** | **Mesh cell** | **Interpolation** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AXIS_SI D_PLAN_SI C_PLAN_SI | SEG2 SEG3 | Linear or Quadratic |

## 2 Supported loadings

The available loadings are the following:

- **EPSI_INIT**

Makes it possible to apply an initial strain loading.

Supported modellings: AXIS_SI, C_PLAN_SI, D_PLAN_SI

- **PESANTEUR**

Makes it possible to define the acceleration and the direction of gravity.

Supported modellings: AXIS_SI, C_PLAN_SI, D_PLAN_SI

- **ROTATION**

Makes it possible to define a rotation speed and the direction of the rotation vector.

Supported modellings: AXIS_SI, C_PLAN_SI, D_PLAN_SI

- **CONTACT**

Makes it possible to define the zones subjected to contact conditions.

Supported modellings: AXIS_SI, C_PLAN_SI, D_PLAN_SI

- **FORCE_CONTOUR**

Makes it possible to define linear forces on the edge of a domain.

Supported modellings: AXIS_SI, C_PLAN_SI, D_PLAN_SI

- **FORCE_INTERNE**

Makes it possible to define body forces.

Supported modellings: AXIS_SI, C_PLAN_SI, D_PLAN_SI

- **PRES_REP**

Makes it possible to apply a pressure.

Supported modellings: AXIS_SI, C_PLAN_SI, D_PLAN_SI
