# v2.02.155 SDLL155 - Simplified seismic response Rock of a straight pipe with variable cross-section

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the POST_ROCHE command on an elementary case of a
straight beam composed of 2 mesh cells. The section is variable.

This test also validates (excluding POST_ROCHE) two features used in
POST_ROCHE macro-command:

- POST_ELEM/INTEGRALE on elements POU_D_T for generalized effort fields,
- CALC_CHAM_ELEM dedicated ROCH_ELNO option.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A hollow pipe of cross-section varying homothetically, with external radius ranging from $10\mathit{mm}$ to $5\mathit{mm}$, thickness varying from $5\mathit{mm}$ to $2.5\mathit{mm}$ and length $2m$ is considered.

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.155/100002010000020200000072517CB7DC8A612B8C.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=200\mathit{GPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho=7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$
- $\alpha =1.2E-5{K}^{-1}$

The law of Ramberg Osgood used for post-processings ssss:

${ϵ}_{p}=K{(\frac{\sigma}{E})}^{1/n}$

With:

- $K=0.01$
- $n=2$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

An elastic set of calculations is performed independently on the beam structure.

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.155/100002010000019C000000785C63C96ABAD54C29.png)

#### 1.3.1 Own weight

A $g=9.81m/{s}^{2}$ gravity field is imposed in the $-Z$ direction. The point $A$ is clamped.

#### 1.3.2 Movement imposed

The point $A$ is clamped. The structure is twisted to point $B$ via an angle imposed $\theta =0.02\mathit{rad}$ on the $X$ axis.

#### 1.3.3 Thermal dilation

The point $A$ is clamped. The point $B$ is blocked on the $X$ axis. A change in the $\Delta T=100K$ temperature field generates a compression in the beam.

#### 1.3.4 Pressure imposed

The pressure inside the $P=1\mathit{MPa}$ pipe is taken into account.

#### 1.3.5 Spectral seismic

No inertial seismic loads taken into account in this test case.

Only DDS loading is taken into account.

The point $A$ is clamped. The point $B$ is blocked on the $Z$ axis. A spectral acceleration is imposed on the 3 $X$ , $Y$ and $Z$ axes with the respective $1.$ , $1.$ and $0.5$ ratios:

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.155/1000020100000280000001E0D438E8C97734A253.png)

The first 4 dynamic eigenmodes and static correction via pseudo-modes on the 3 axes are taken into account. The reduced damping is set at 2%.
