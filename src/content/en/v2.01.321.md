# v2.01.321 SDLD321 - Dynamic transient response of a harmonic oscillator with variable damping

**Summary:**

The system is a 1 degree of freedom harmonic oscillator under resonance harmonic excitation. Different dampings will be considered:

- critical depreciation,
- average depreciation,
- very low depreciation.

Through this problem, the various algorithms of the DYNA_VIBRA [U4.54.03](doc:u4.54.03) command are tested and their ability to deal with extreme damping problems is tested. The results are compared with the exact analytical solutions.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The system consists of a mass, spring and shock absorber. It allows a single degree of freedom translation.

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.321/Object_1.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

stiffness de restraint : $k={25.10}^{3}{\mathit{N.m}}^{-1}$

Point mass: $m=10\mathit{kg}$

Viscous damping:

$c={c}_{\mathit{critique}}$ ; $c=0,01{c}_{\mathit{critique}}$ ; $c={10}^{-5}{c}_{\mathit{critique}}$

with ${c}_{\mathit{critique}}=1000{\mathit{kg.s}}^{-1}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

End A clamped.

The following harmonic force x at the point $B$ resonance frequency:

$F(t)={F}_{0}\sin(\omega t)$ for $t\ge 0$ with ${F}_{0}=5N$ and $\omega =\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}=50{\mathit{rad.s}}^{-1}$ .

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The system is at rest at $t=0$: $u(0)=0$ and $\frac{\mathit{du}}{\mathit{dt}}(0)=0$ .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Simple oscillator checks the following equation:

$m\ddot{u}+c\dot{u}+ku={F}_{0}\sin(\omega t)$

with $u(0)=0$ and $\dot{u}(0)=0$

$\omega$: clean pulse of the $\omega =\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$ oscillator

The critical depreciation is ${c}_{\mathit{critique}}=\mathrm{2m}\omega$ .

The solution for $c={c}_{\mathrm{critique}}$ is:

$u(t)=\frac{{F}_{0}}{\mathrm{2k}}\left[{e}^{-\omega t}(1+\omega t)-\cos(\omega t)\right]$

The solution for subcritical damping such as $\frac{c}{{c}_{\mathit{critique}}}=\xi$ is:

$u(t)={e}^{-\xi \omega t}(\frac{{F}_{0}}{2\xi k}\cos({\omega}_{D}t)+\frac{{F}_{0}\omega }{2k{\omega}_{D}}\sin({\omega}_{D}t))-\frac{{F}_{0}}{2\xi k}\cos(\omega t)$

with ${\omega}_{D}=\omega \sqrt{1-{\xi}^{2}}$

### 2.2 Baseline

Moving and speed of point $B$ .

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Analytical solution exact.

## 6 Summary of the results

For model A, both displacement and velocity results have an absolute error well below $\text{1 \%}$ compared to analytical solution.

Newmark's integration scheme is more precise than Euler's.

At $\text{1 \%}$ of critical damping (modelization B), the Fu-Devogelaere integration scheme is of dreadful accuracy (no error compared to the reference solution).

The time step adaptive order 2 diagram also gives very low error percentage results.

For very low damping (modelization C), we will note a better accuracy for the Euler integration scheme than for a Newmark scheme. For the latter, the error increases with time but remains still less than $\text{1 \%}$ .
