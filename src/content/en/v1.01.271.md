# v1.01.271 ZZZZ271 - Validation of PROJ_CHAMP in 2D and 3D for Gauss dot fields

**Summary:**

This test validates the projection of internal stress fields and variables using the PROJ_CHAMP ('ECLA_PG') command for an mesh $\mathrm{2D}$ and an mesh $\mathrm{3D}$ .

## 1 Test principle

Two areas are being studied.

*First domain*

The first domain studied is a square $[-1,1]\times [-1,1]$

It is meshed in two different ways:

MA1 : Cut the square into 76 TRIA6

MA2 : We cut the square into 25 QUAD4

On the mesh MA1, the first component of the stress field and the first component of the internal variable field, on each Gauss point, is defined by the $1+x+y$ formula where $x$ and $y$ represent the coordinates of the Gauss point.

These fields are then projected on the mesh MA2.

The projection value at two points of any Gauss is tested.

*Second domain*

The second domain studied is a cube $[0,1]\times [0,1]\times [0,1]$

It is meshed in two different ways:

MA3 : The cube is cut into 38 TETRA4

MA4 : The square is cut into 64 HEXA8

On the mesh MA3, the first component of the stress field and the first component of the internal variable field, on each Gauss point, is defined by the $1+x$ formula where $x$ represents the apsis of the Gauss point.

These fields are then projected on the mesh MA4.

The projection value on any Gauss point is tested.
