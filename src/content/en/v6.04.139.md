# v6.04.139 SSNV139 - Bias plate

**Summary:**

Almost static calculation of a horizontal clamped elastic plate on one side and subjected to a vertical concentrated force. The results are compared with those of SAMCEF software.

The advantage is to test COQUE_3D's finite element in non-linear geometric COQUE_3D using the GROT_GDEP large 3D rotation update algorithm in STAT_NON_LINE.

Rotations are 3D and slightly above 1 radian.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.139/Object_1.svg)

Clamped ${P}_{1}{P}_{2}$-shaped and $P$-concentrated vertical force:

$f=-F{e}_{z}$ ; $F>0$

### 1.2 Material properties

Elastic behaviour:

$E=2100000$ ; $\nu =\frac{1}{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Clamped support in ${P}_{1}{P}_{2}$ . The successive states of equilibrium under load consisting of force are sought:

$F(t)=t$

$P$ , $t$ being the pseudo time.

Particular attention is paid to horizontal and vertical $P$ displacements.

## 2 reference solution

This [bib4] solution is the one obtained with SAMCEF [bib1] software. The Modelling is based on a resulting stress hull theory with a corotational [bib3] formulation and DSQ [bib2] discretization.

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.139/100007FC000069BB00002F70F3B7DD3F6641FCBF.svg)

The mesh is considered to be $20\times 4$ elements quadrilateral to 4 nodes each.

## 5 Summary of the results

The model used for reference solution is based on a resultant stress theory with corotational formulation [bib3], while that of *Code_Aster* uses a volumetric approach with a total Lagrangian formulation [R3.07.04](doc:r3.07.04).

For this thin shell, the triangle and quadrangle give good results.
