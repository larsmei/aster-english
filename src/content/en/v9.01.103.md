# v9.01.103 SZLZ103 - RAINFLOW Method

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to test the RAINFLOW cycle counting method from a load history in constraints.

The filtering functionality of the load history is also tested, and the consideration of a stress concentration coefficient ${K}_{T}$ is taken into account.

This example is a validation test of POSTDAM software developed by the REME Department, provided in the Validation Manual of version 1.0 of this software.

The results provided by the POST_FATIGUE operator are quite identical to those provided by POSTDAM software.

## 1 Reference problem

The analysis consists of extracting the elementary cycles by the RAINFLOW [R7.04.01](doc:r7.04.01) cycle counting method.

First, the load history is filtered in strain with a filter level of 0.9.

Then a stress concentration coefficient ${K}_{T}=1$ is applied.
