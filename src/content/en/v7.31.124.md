# v7.31.124 WTNV124 - Deaturation-Consolidation Test with Barcelona Model

**Summary:**

This test validates the integration of the Barcelona [r7.01.17](doc:r7.01.17) model. This test in Modelling A, in hydro-mechanical coupling (modelling HHM), includes two loading routes:

- A deaturation journey.
- An isotropic compression path.

The Modelling is performed on a hexahedral element 3D.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The gas pressure remains constant at $\mathrm{PRE2}={1}$ Pa. In the first loading phase, a desaturation is performed, during which the capillary pressure increases to $\mathrm{PRE1}={400}$ kPa. In the second phase, by maintaining this capillary pressure, an isotropic compression is performed until the average stress $\sigma_m=-600$ kPa is reached.
