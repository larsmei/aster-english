# v5.01.108 SDND108 - constitutive law DIS_CONTACT in dynamic

**Summary:**

This test validates the use of constitutive law DIS_CONTACT for contact and frangling on discrete dynamically.

### 1.1 Geometry

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Modelling A

This case models a mass that bounces over an elastic surface (modelization A)

#### 2.1.1 Baseline

The mass is released with an initial speed ${V}_{0}$ (configuration a), as long as it has not touched the ground, it only undergoes the gravity. When it touches the elastic ground, it is subjected in addition to the gravity to an effort of the type $k.\delta x$ (configuration b). Once the rebound is complete, it is no longer subjected to the gravity (configuration a) with an initial speed ${V}_{1}$.

![Figure](/figures/v5.01.108/10000000000006280000044A084203840137B87C.png)

**Table** 2.1.1-1 **:** Behavioural model

Equation during free fall (configuration a):

$$
m.\ddot{x}=-m.g
$$

And so, by integration:

$$
x=-g.\frac{{t}^{2}}{2}+{V}_{0}.t
$$

The time of the fall is obtained by solving $x=h$:

$$
{t}_{\mathit{chute}}=\frac{1}{g}\left[{V}_{0}+\sqrt{{V}_{0}^{2}-2.g.h}\right]
$$

Equation during rebound (configuration b):

$$
m.\ddot{x}=-m.g+k.(h-z)
$$

And, therefore:

$$
x={C}_{1}\sin(w.\stackrel{~}{t})+{C}_{2}\cos(w.\stackrel{~}{t})+(h-g/{w}^{2})
$$

Both constants are:

$$
{C}_{2}=g/{w}^{2};{C}_{1}=\frac{-1}{w}\sqrt{{V}_{O}^{2}-2.g.h}
$$

The time corresponding to the de-lamination is obtained by solving $x=h$:

$$
\tan\left(w.\frac{{\stackrel{~}{t}}_{\mathit{decol}}}{2}\right)={w}^{2}.\frac{{C}_{1}}{g}
$$

### 2.2 Modelling B

This case models a mass that bounces over an absorbent surface.

#### 2.2.1 Baseline

The mass is released with an initial speed ${V}_{0}$ (configuration a), as long as it has not touched the surface, it only undergoes the gravity. When it touches the absorbent surface, it is subjected in addition to the gravity to an effort of the type $k.\delta x+c.\dot{x}$ (configuration c). Once the rebound is complete, it is no longer subject to gravity (configuration a) with an initial speed ${V}_{1}$.

![Figure](/figures/v5.01.108/1000000000000628000003FDA7D229F2BC03F93C.png)

**Table** 2.2.1-1 **:** Behavioural Model

Equation during free fall(configuration a)

$$
m.\ddot{x}=-m.g
$$

And so, by integration:

$$
x=-g.\frac{{t}^{2}}{2}+{V}_{0}.t
$$

The time of the fall is obtained by solving $x=h$:

$$
{t}_{\mathit{chute}}=\frac{1}{g}\left[{V}_{0}+\sqrt{{V}_{0}^{2}-2.g.h}\right]
$$

Equation during rebound (configuration c):

$$
m.\ddot{x}=-m.g+k.(h-z)-c.\dot{x}
$$

And so:

$$
x=\exp\left(\frac{-c.\stackrel{~}{t}}{2.m}\right)\left[{C}_{1}\sin\left(\frac{\mathit{Rd}.\stackrel{~}{t}}{2}\right)+{C}_{2}\cos\left(\frac{\mathit{Rd}.\stackrel{~}{t}}{2}\right)\right]+(h-g/{w}^{2})
\textrm{avec}
\stackrel{~}{t}=t-{t}_{\mathit{chute}}
$$

Constants are:

$$
{C}_{2}=g/{w}^{2};{C}_{1}=\frac{c.g-2.k.\sqrt{{V}_{O}^{2}-2.g.h}}{k.\sqrt{4.k.m-{c}^{2}/{m}^{2}}}
$$

The time corresponding to the de-lamination is obtained by solving $x=h$ numerically.

#### 2.2.2 Uncertainty about solutions

No uncertainty (analytical solution).
