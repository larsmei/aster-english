# v3.04.121 SSLV121 - Etirage of a transverse isotropic parallelepiped under its own weight

**Summary:**

This structural mechanical test allows the evaluation of the displacements and stresses of a parallelepiped deforming under its own weight. The material is elastic isotropic transverse linear. The model is three-dimensional. The model is similar to the VPCS SSLV07 test (but in this case the material is isotropic) and to the SSLV120 test (in this case the material is orthotropic.).

The differences of the results obtained by *Aster* are between $\text{0,00\%}$ and $\text{0,4\%}$ of the analytically calculated reference.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is derived from the SSLV07/89 plug in the VPCS guide (considering in addition a transverse isotropic elastic matrix). The analytical expression of the solution is as follows:

Travel:

$u=-\frac{{\nu}_{\text{NL}}\rho gxz}{{E}_{N}}$

$v=-\frac{{\nu}_{\text{NL}}\rho gyz}{{E}_{N}}$

$w=\frac{\rho g{z}^{2}}{2{E}_{N}}+\frac{\rho g{\nu}_{\text{NL}}}{2{E}_{N}}({x}^{2}+{y}^{2})-\frac{\rho g{L}^{2}}{2{E}_{N}}$

Constraints:

${\sigma}_{zz}=\rho gz$ ${\sigma}_{zz}={\sigma}_{yy}={\sigma}_{xy}={\sigma}_{yz}={\sigma}_{\mathrm{zx}}=0$

## 4 Summary of the results

The displacement and stress results are very close to the analytical solution with the adopted model ($\text{< 0.2\%}$ for displacement, $\text{< 0.5\%}$ for stress).

The fact that there is only one stress component (${\sigma}_{zz}$ ) in the problem allows you to test only 2 elastic coefficients (${E}_{N}$ and ${\nu}_{\text{LN}}$ ).

Although these coefficients are constant, they were introduced as functions to test the ELAS_ISTR_FO functionality.

The elastic coefficients in the $\mathrm{XY}$ plane and the $Z$ direction were selected to obtain the same values of displacements at $B$ , $C$ , $D$ and $E$ as those calculated for an isotropic material (test SSLV07) or orthotropic material (test SSLV120). Digitally, these values are very similar to those of these tests at the points considered (order ${10}^{-6}$ ) the difference resulting from the construction mode of the stiffness matrices in the different cases. At point $X$ , these values differ but correspond well to the reference solution.
