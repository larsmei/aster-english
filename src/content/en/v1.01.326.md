# v1.01.326 ZZZZ326 – Validation of Subpoint Printing in MED Format

**Summary:**

This test is used to validate the printing of sub-points in MED format. It consists of 2 modellings:

- 'DKT' Modelling,
- 'TUYAU_3M' Modelling.

Each modellings works with and without CARA_ELEM to validate both modes of operation.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
