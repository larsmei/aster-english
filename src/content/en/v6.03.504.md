# v6.03.504 SSNP504 – Large sliding contact for compliant slashes

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to test contact in large slips for slashes in accordance with mesh.

A 2D structure is considered, a rectangle with two oblique cracks passing through it completely (we will then speak of interfaces). The blocking of horizontal displacements is imposed on the four corners of the rectangle, vertical displacements are imposed on the lower and upper edges of the rectangle in order to tighten the middle block which is imposed a horizontal displacement to drag it up along the interfaces. Following the compression stress thus created, contact pressures appear on the areas in contact, with an evolution of their values according to the progress of the middle block.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The structure is a healthy rectangle in which two oblique interfaces are introduced. The interfaces are parallel and placed as shown on the []. The dimensions of the structure as well as those concerning the geometry of the interfaces are:

$H=9m$ ;

$L=4m$ ;

$B=4m$ ;

$h=2.125m$ ;

$\alpha =13.49$

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.504/10000000000000E100000163909A5EB5CE99F1AC.png)

Figure 1.1-1: Géométrie de la structure et positionnement des **interface** s.

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus : $E=100\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio : $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Horizontal displacement is blocked at all corners of the structure (see [Figure 1.3-1-o]. On the lower and upper edges of the rectangle, movement is imposed along the $Y$ axis which will close the interfaces to generate contact pressure. The middle block is subjected to a significant slip by applying a controlled displacement along the $X$ axis on its left edge.

The numerical values of the required displacements are:

> ${\mathrm{Depl}}_{X}=2.00m$ ;
> ${\mathrm{Depl}}_{Y}=1.0E-2m$ .

Their application is done according to a classic ramp function, in 4 no load.

The friction coefficient of Coulomb is taken equal to $0,5$ .

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.504/1000000000000105000001871547BC985F5A145D.png)

**Figure 1.3-1: Illustration of boundary conditions and loadings.**

## 2 reference solution

non-regression.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

This is FEM Modelling, in flat deformations. The three blocks are gridded with compliant mesh cells and the contact conditions are imposed on the stops of these blocks. Master edges on the middle block and slave edges are declared on the lower and upper blocks in order to comply with [Figure 1.3-1].

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh is set (see []) and has 3 mesh cells blocks of QUAD4 type. The 3 blocks each have 256 mesh cells.
