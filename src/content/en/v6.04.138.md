# v6.04.138 SSNV138 - Cantilever plate in large rotations subject to one moment

**Summary:**

Almost static calculation of an clamped elastic plate on one side and subjected to bending moment on the other side, leading to large rotations of the plate.

**Interest:**

Test the elements non-linear geometric finished COQUE_3D (modellings A and C), DKT (modellings E and F), DKTG (modellings G and H) and POU_D_T_GD (modelization B) using the $\mathrm{3D}$ GROT_GDEP large rotation update algorithm in STAT_NON_LINE.

Also compare the results to POU_D_TGM multifibre beam POU_D_TGM which allows to process large rotations under the hypothesis of small loading increments.

**Note:**

*This test is the test case plate version of the SSNL103 beam*. The mechanical characteristics have been modified to support superfacial modelling.*

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.138/Object_1.svg)

Clamped rectangular plate in ${P}_{1}{P}_{4}$ and ${P}_{1}{P}_{4}$ subjected to a linear torque in ${P}_{2}{P}_{3}$:

$m=-{\mathrm{me}}_{y};m>0$

### 1.2 Material properties and section characteristics

Elastic behaviour:

$E=12\times {10}^{6}\text{Pa};\nu =0$

The fact that the Poisson's ratio ($\nu$ ) is zero makes the plate solution identical to the beam solution.

${I}_{y}$ is the inertia of the section with a beam model:

${I}_{y}=\frac{b{h}^{3}}{12}=\frac{1}{12}\times {10}^{-3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

Clamped support ${P}_{1}{P}_{4}$ . The successive states of equilibrium under the load of the ${P}_{2}{P}_{3}$ linear torque are investigated:

$m(t)=100t$; $t$ pseudo-time.

Particular attention is paid to horizontal and vertical displacements and rotation of the ${P}_{2}{P}_{3}$ line.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

With a beam kinematic and a model in resulting effort, the curvature (in large rotations) of the cantilever submitted to bending moment $M=\text{mb}$ is, with the previous numerical data:

$\frac{d\theta }{\text{dx}}=\frac{\text{mb}}{{\text{EI}}_{y}}=\frac{t}{L}$

This is the solution of Euler.

### 2.2 Baseline

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.138/Object_15.svg)

Depending on the solution d-Euler, the deformed is a circle arc. At ${P}_{2}{P}_{3}$ $(x=L)$ section, the rotation is:

$\theta (x=L)=t.$

In the absence of normal effort, the average surface area remains inextensible and the radius of curvature is given by:

$R={(\frac{d\theta }{\text{dx}})}^{-1}=\frac{L}{t}$

The horizontal displacement is then

$u=R\sin\theta -L=L(\frac{\sint}{t}-1)$

and vertical displacement is

$v=R(1-\cos\theta )=\frac{L}{t}(1-\cost)$

## 12 Summary of the results

- **modellings** COQUE_3D, POU_D_T_GD et POU_D_TGM

There are convergence difficulties which disappear by multiplying the thickness by 3 or 4.

It is necessary to increase the value of the COEF_RIGI_DRZ which attributes stiffness around the normal elements shell which is by default ${10}^{-5}$ (the smallest bending rigidity around the directions in the hull plane) so that it can increase the value of the angle of rotation that can be reached. Values of this coefficient up to ${10}^{-3}$ remain lawful.

During Newton iterations, membrane deformations appear and cancel at convergence.

The convergence speeds of NEWTON algorithms are comparable for modellings POU_D_T_GD and COQUE_3D.

The convergence rate of NEWTON algorithm in the case of POU_D_TGM Modelling is much lower than the other two because this Modelling requires in this case very small loading increments to describe the geometric transformation well and remain in the hypothesis of small deformations. The time cost $\text{CPU}$ sss is felt since the calculation is about 10 times longer than that of POU_D_T_GD Modelling. Of course the multifibre element has the advantage of being able to treat several types of behavior and not only an elastic behavior like the element POU_D_T_GD. If the required precision is not of the order of 1%, one can afford to use less time step.

Moreover, it is important for a problem like this, where the inertia of the section plays a leading role, to ensure that the section is discretized with enough fibers when POU_D_TGM is used, to obtain inertia as close to the theoretical value as possible (which is why we have meshed with nearly 40 fibers in thickness).

- **modellings** DKT et DKTG

DKT Modelling allows to reach a large $300°$ rotation angle , more important than that obtained with DKTG $86°$ Modelling .

Compared to the analytical solution, the difference maximum on the move is 2% for modellings DKT and DKTG
