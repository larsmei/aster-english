# v1.03.129 MFRON04 – Code_Aster-MFront interface test for anisotropic constitutive laws

**Summary:**

This test validates the implementation of constitutive laws elastic and orthotropic elasto-plastics defined using MFront on uniaxial loads.

Modellings A and B consider an orthotropic linear elastic model, in modellings 3D and AXIS respectively.

Modellings C and D consider an elastoplastic model associated with linear elasticity isotropic and Hill's orthotropic plasticity criterion, modellings 3D and AXIS respectively.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

#### 1.1.1 modellings A et C

It is a cube of unitary dimension.

![Figure](/figures/v1.03.129/Object_1.svg)

*Height: $h=1$ m, width: $l=1$ m, thickness: $e=1$ m.*

#### 1.1.2 modellings B et D

It is a square of unitary dimension.

![Figure](/figures/v1.03.129/Object_2.svg)

*Height: $h=1$ m, width: $l=1$ m.*

### 1.2 boundary conditions

#### 1.2.1 modellings A et C

- On the $x=e$ side, the displacement is blocked in $DX$.
- On the $y=0$ side, the displacement is blocked in $DY$.
- On the $z=0$ side, the displacement is blocked in $DZ$.
- On the $z=h$ side, the displacement is driven in $DZ$ from $0$ to $0.01$ m in a growing way.

#### 1.2.2 modellings B et D

- On the $x=0$ edge, the displacement is blocked in $DX$.
- On the $y=0$ edge, the displacement is blocked in $DY$.
- On the $y=h$ edge, the displacement is driven in $DY$ from $0$ to $0.01$ m in a growing way.

### 1.3 Material properties

#### 1.3.1 modellings A et B

These modellings consider an orthotropic linear elastic model. In the orthotropy marker, the stress-deformation relationship can be written:

with $\poissonCoef_{ij}/\youngModulus_i=\poissonCoef_{ji}/\youngModulus_j$.

The relationship depends on nine elastic constants:

- Young's modulus $\youngModulus_1$ in direction 1.
- The Young's modulus $\youngModulus_2$ in direction 2.
- Young's modulus $\youngModulus_3$ in direction 3.
- Poisson's ratio $\poissonCoef_{12}$ in direction 2 under loading in direction 1.
- Poisson's ratio $\poissonCoef_{23}$ in direction 3 under loading in direction 2.
- Poisson's ratio $\poissonCoef_{13}$ in direction 3 under loading in direction 1.
- $\shearModulus_{12}$ shear module in plane 1-2.
- The $\shearModulus_{23}$ shear module in plane 2-3.
- The $\shearModulus_{13}$ shear module in plane 1-3.

#### 1.3.2 modellings C et D

These modellings consider an associated elasto-plastic model, with linear elasticity isotropic and Hill's orthotropic plasticity criterion.

The elasticity depends on two parameters, the Young's modulus $\youngModulus$ and the Poisson's ratio $\poissonCoef$ (or equivalently the $\bulkModulus$ compressibility module and the $\shearModulus$ shear module).

Hill's orthotropic plasticity criterion is based on seven constants (six anisotropic parameters and a limit of elasticity).

When $F=G=H$ and $L=M=N=3F$, Hill's orthotropic criterion is reduced to von Mises' isotropic criterion with $\sigma_0$ as the yield strength.
