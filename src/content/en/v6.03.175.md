# v6.03.175 SSNP175 – Taylor Patch Test - Solution Connection

**Summary:**

This test consists of quasi-static analysis of a problem with connection of solutions
The analytical solution is known to be a particular interest here in integration.
the terms of connection ( *patch-test* ) by different methods.
The aim is to study two identical blocks, which are gridded differently and are subject to compression.

This test is a contactless version of the ssnp170 test.

This test has 25 modellings. The objective is to test all possible pairing combinations with mesh incompatible for the different massive fittings.

Thus this test includes the following modellings:

- Modelling A: TRIA3 - TRIA7; D_PLAN - D_PLAN_HHO1; Penalization
- Modelling B: TRIA3 - TRIA6; D_PLAN - D_PLAN_INCO_UPG; Penalization
- Modelling C: QUAD4 - QUAD4; D_PLAN - D_PLAN; Penalization
- Modelling D: QUAD8 - QUAD9; D_PLAN - D_PLAN_HHO2; Penalization
- Modelling E: TETRA4 - TETRA4; 3D - 3D; Penalization
- Modelling F: TETRA4 - HEXA20; 3D - 3D_INCO_UPG; Penalization
- Modelling G: TETRA4 - TETRA15; 3D - 3D_HHO1; Penalization
- Modelling H: HEXA27 - HEXA27; 3D - 3D_HHO1; Penalization
- Modelling I: TRIA3 - TRIA6; D_PLAN - D_PLAN_INCO_UPG; Lagrangien augmented
- Modelling J: TETRA4 - TETRA4; 3D - 3D; Lagrangian augmented
- K Modelling: TETRA4 - HEXA20; 3D - 3D_INCO_UPG; Lagrangien augmented
- Modelling L: TETRA20 - TETRA20; 3D - 3D; Lagrangien augmented
- Modelling M: TRIA3 - TRIA7; D_PLAN - D_PLAN_HHO1; Nitsche
- Modelling N: TETRA4 - TETRA15; 3D - 3D_HHO2; Penalization
- Modelling O: TETRA4 - TETRA4; 3D - 3D; Lagrangien - node Orphan
- Modelling P: as Modelling B with 1 MPI HPC
- Q Modelling: as K Modelling with 1 MPI HPC

Width $b=50\mathit{mm}$ .

$O$ point mid segment $\mathit{AB}$ (mark origin).

### 1.2 Material properties

**Schedules 1 and 2:**

Poisson's ratio : $0.3$

Young's modulus : $2000\mathit{MPa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Plate 1 is blocked in $Y$:

- On $\mathit{HG}$: $\mathit{DY}=0$.

Plate 2 is subjected to a pressure imposed:

- On $\mathit{CD}$ : $\mathit{PRES}=25\mathit{MPa}$

> Symmetric conditions are imposed in $X$ to model only half of the domain.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The pressure at the interface is equal to the pressure at the top.

### 2.2 Baseline

The pressure is constant and equal to $25\mathit{MPa}$ over the entire contact surface.

The displacement and normal stress shall be continuous at the interface

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.

## 20 Summary of the results

This is a very simple example of testing the new massive fittings.

This technique allows to obtain pressures very close to the analytical solution.
Even on highly incompatible meshes, residues are obtained which are almost zero for the Lagragien.
Here, the pressure is constant over the whole contact surface, unlike the penalized method
which usually generates oscillations on this type of case.
