# v1.02.013 PERFE03 – non-regression aggregate design of the PERFECT platform

**Summary:**

This test validates the controls used by the AGREGAT module of the PERFECT platform, which simulates the radiation effects on the reactor component. Here we are interested in the steel tank.

A volume element is considered to be applied to which a deformity is imposed. The material consists of an aggregate consisting of 300 single-crystal grains, in a representative elemental volume.

Model A tests the average stresses and deformations obtained for an imposed deformation of $\text{0.18\%}$ .

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v1.02.013/100000000000039C000003586B407D8329B35E0C.png)

L-Aggregate is represented by a hexahedral controlled mesh with a volume of side 1.

It has 300 grains, defined by the belonging of each mesh cells to a particular mesh-cell group centered around a germ randomly positioned in the cube.

## 2 reference solution

The purpose of this test is to check the validity of the command file used in PERFECT. The tests are therefore non-regression.

The values tested are the mean stresses and deformations following $Z$ at 1.8.
