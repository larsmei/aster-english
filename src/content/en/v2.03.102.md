# v2.03.102 SDLS102 - Free vibrations of a compression blade

**Summary:**

This test validates the calculation of the eigenfrequencies of a compression blade using the CALC_MODES command.

The modellings correspond to the use of elements COQUE_3D MEC3QU9H (modelization A), MEC3TR7H (modelization B) and COQUE_SOLIDE (modelization C).

The reference solutions are experimental results. the difference between the numerical results and the experimental values does not exceed $\text{4,5\%}$ for the three modellings.

## 6 Summary of the results

The results are satisfactory. But the mesh with elements MEC3TR7H must be fine to have the same level of error as that with elements MEC3QU9H.

The element COQUE_SOLIDE gives excellent results with less than degrees of freedom.
