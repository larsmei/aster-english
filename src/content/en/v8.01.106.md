# v8.01.106 FDLV106 - Calculation of depreciation added in ring flow

**Summary:**

This fluid/structure domain test implements the mass and damping calculation added on a cylindrical structure subject to an annular flow that is assumed to be potential. Initially, mass and damping added by flow on the structure for different upstream speeds ($4m/s$ , $4.24m/s$ and $6m/s$ ) are calculated on a 3D model for the fluid and hull for the structure. The structure has a rotational displacement around a pivot located at the downstream end of the cylinder relative to the flow.

The coefficients determined are assigned to a discrete model equivalent to 1 ddl mass-responsible-absorbent, on which modal analysis is performed, to determine the system's own complex frequencies for the different flow speeds:

$4m/s$: damping,

$4.24m/s$: critical speed, zero damping,

$6m/s$: negative damping, floating.

For each flow rate, the modal calculation (quadra problem QEP) is checked for proper operation by varying the modal solvers (SORENSEN, TRI_DIAG and QZ) and the blocking factor: DDL_IMPO alone, MECA_IMPO alone or a paning of both.

## 4 Summary of the results

The flow damping calculation tool (potential hypothesis) was validated on the rotational mode of a cylindrical structure subject to annular flow. However, it should be noted [bib1] that the very good concordance between the proposed semi-analytic model for comparison and the numerical calculation is obtained only if the cylinder is sufficiently long.

Indeed, the semi-analytical model is in fact only a solution to the problem.
