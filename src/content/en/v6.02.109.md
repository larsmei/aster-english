# v6.02.109 SSNL109 – VMIS_ISOT_NL 1D Law on finite element Bar

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry is a slash in the $(Oxy)$ plane, the original coordinates of which are $(2,1,0)$ and $(4,2,0)$ end. Its section is $S=5 mm^2$.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastoplastic with linear isotropic hardening, with the following characteristics:

$$
E=2  \times 10^{5}\ \mathrm{MPa}\ ;\ R_0=400\ \mathrm{MPa}\ ;\ R_H=2 \times 10^{3}\ \mathrm{MPa}
$$

Where $E$, $R_0$ and $R_H$ respectively designates the Young's modulus, the yield limit and the $d \sigma / d{\varepsilon}^{p}$ hardening slope in uniaxial traction.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

All nodes on the bar are controlled while moving, with:

The load intensity $\varepsilon$ is monotonous in the range 0 to 5.25%.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The displacement imposed leads to uniform $\varepsilon$ elongation deformation in the bar.
The yield limit is reached for $\varepsilon=\varepsilon^y=R_0/E$ deformation.
Beyond that, the answer is plastic. Because of monotonous uniaxial behavior, it simply writes:

$$
\sigma = E(\varepsilon-\varepsilon^p)=R_0+R_H\varepsilon^p \Rightarrow \sigma = R_0 + \frac{E R_H}{E+R_H}(\varepsilon-\varepsilon_y)
$$

The normal stress is then deduced from the stress via $N=S\sigma$.

### 2.2 Baseline

At the end of the load, $\varepsilon=5.25×10^{-2}$ and therefore $S=2 500 N$.
