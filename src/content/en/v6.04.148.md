# v6.04.148 SSNV148 - 3D and landfill models of Weibull and Rice-Tracey

**Summary:**

This quasi-static non-linear mechanical test validates Weibull and Rice and Tracey models in 3D for non-monoton mechanical loadings (see POST_ELEM and POST_BEREMIN).

At the $–50°C$ temperature, a smooth cylindrical test piece is initially deformed up to 10%. After slightly unloading, the level of deformation is maintained constant while reducing the test piece temperature to $–150°C$ in a uniform manner. At this new temperature, additional deformation is applied to reach 15% in total. The probability of fracture by cleavage and the rate of growth of the test piece cavities are calculated for the entire loading route.

The Modelling of the test piece is done with elements 3D (HEXA20, PENTA15).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A smooth cylindrical half test piece is considered.

### 1.2 Material properties

A Von elasto-plastic constitutive law is adopted Linear isotropic hardening ‘VMIS_ISOT_LINE. The deformations used in the behavioral relationship are linearized deformations.

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.148/1000214E000069BB00004D4F16B6534DE5FFFC59.svg)

The Young's modulus $E$ , the ${E}_{t}$ tangential module and the Poisson's ratio are temperature-neutral. It is taken: $E=200\mathit{GPa}$ , ${E}_{t}=2000\mathit{MPa}$ and $\nu =0,3$ .

The evolution of the temperature yield limit is given in the following table:

| **Temperature** $[°C]$ | – **150** | – **100** | – **50** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ${\sigma}_{Y}[\mathit{MPa}]$ | 750 | 700 | 650 |

Finally, thermal expansion (heat expansion coefficient taken equal to 0) is neglected.
