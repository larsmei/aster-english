# v1.01.346 ZZZZ346 – Bifurcation of a right XFEM interface with quadratic elements 2D

**Summary**

The purpose of this test is to validate the XFEM sub-cut algorithm with quadratic elements 2D. The general sub-cut algorithm is described in the [R7.02.12](doc:r7.02.12) reference documentation. Moreover, the sub-cut is mainly used when calling the MODI_MODELE_XFEM [U4.41.11](doc:u4.41.11) operator.

In this test, one is interested in a particular cutting situation: the bifurcation of an interface along a ridge, within the element. Depending on the position of the point fork on the edge, two [R7.02.12](doc:r7.02.12) cutting configurations are available. These configurations correspond to two regularizations of the level-set shape.

Four modellings are envisaged:

1. $A$ Modelling: $\text{X-FEM}\mathrm{2D}$ with elements TRIA6, the interface fork before the mid point on the bifurcation edge.
2. $B$ Modelling: $\text{X-FEM}\mathrm{2D}$ with elements QUAD8, the interface fork before the mid point on the bifurcation edge.
3. $C$ Modelling: $\text{X-FEM}\mathrm{2D}$ with elements TRIA6, the interface fork after the mid point on the bifurcation edge.
4. $D$ Modelling: $\text{X-FEM}\mathrm{2D}$ with elements QUAD8, the interface fork after the mid point on the bifurcation edge.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The $\mathrm{2D}$ structure is a square unit plate ($\mathit{LX}=0,1$ , $\mathit{LY}=0,1$ ). A "right" interface cuts the domain into 2 subdomains.

The interface bifurcates near the centre of the $(0,0.5)$ square with an $\theta =67,5°$ angle relative to the horizontal.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.346/10000201000000EE000000E060973FFD7F104D23.png)

**Figure** 1.1-1 **: Geometry of cracked plate**

The interface is defined analytically by the union of 2 segments on the right, the respective equations of which are:

- $X=0$ for the vertical segment.
- $Y=2x+0.55$ for the oblique segment.

The point intersection of the 2 segments on the right is calculated to be located on the edge where the bifurcated interface:

- $(0,0.55)$ for modellings $A$ and $B$ , or
- $(0,0.6)$ for modellings $C$ and $D$ , or

Figure 1.1-2: «zoom» au voisinage du point de bifurcation

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.346/10000201000003600000020958070A9972EF1609.png)

Figure 1.1-3: «zoom» au voisinage du point de bifurcation

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus:            $E=210{10}^{9}\mathrm{Pa}$

Poisson's ratio:            $\nu =0$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

*Modelling* $A$:

The interface breaks the domain into 2 subdomains. The moving solution is imposed on each subdomain, thanks to the conditions of Dirichlet on the edges:

- on the $\mathit{LIG2}$ edge: $\mathit{DX}=-1$ and $\mathit{DY}=0$
- on the $\mathit{LIG4}$ edge: $\mathit{DX}=+1$ and $\mathit{DY}=0$

There is no contact at the interface level. We are just interested in the movements of the 2 blocks, which can interpenetrate.

The imposed Dirichlet conditions also block the rigid body movements of the 2 subdomains.

*modellings* $B$ , $C$ et $D$ :

Same load as in $A$ Modelling.

### 1.4 reference solution

*Modelling* $A$:

By construction, the displacement field is uniform on each sub-block.

- On the subdomain on the right, we have: $\mathit{DX}=-1$ and $\mathit{DY}=0$
- On the left-hand subdomain, we have: $\mathit{DX}=+1$ and $\mathit{DY}=0$

*Modelling* $B$:

Same analytical solution as $A$ Modelling

*Modelling* $C$:

Same analytical solution as $A$ Modelling

*Modelling* $D$:

Same analytical solution as $A$ Modelling

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a regular mesh composed of $8\times 8$ TRIA6, respectively, along the $x,y$ axes. The interface is not meshed.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.346/10000000000002EE000002F4832CAD5C0E318EE3.png)

**Figure** 2.1-1 **: mesh plate (TRI6)**

### 2.2 Tested quantities and results

The bifurcation is located along a ridge, on the nodes $(0,0.5)$ summit segment, $(0,0.625)$ and node $(0,0.5625)$ middle segment. The bifurcation takes place precisely at point $(0,0.55)$ and the interface does not pass through the mid node.

The displacement on the node mid position in $(0,0.5625)$ is tested to check that it is positioned on the "good" side of the interface.
