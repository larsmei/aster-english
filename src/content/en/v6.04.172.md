# v6.04.172 SSNV172 – Single crystal viscoplastic behaviors

**Summary:**

This test validates monocrystalline behaviors in a uniaxial situation. The geometry treated is a cube, the state of stress and deformation is homogeneous. In order to test the constitutive lawss at best, regardless of the definition of the sliding systems, a particular nonphysical slip system is used here, which represents a slide in one direction. This allows to compare two of the monocrystalline viscoplastic behaviors, (with kinematic hardening defined by a recall variable) with the macroscopic viscoplastic behavior of Chaboche.

For other behaviors (especially KOCKS_RAUCH) the non-regression of the results is checked only.

All these tests are performed in Modelling A (3D).

Modelling B allows the validation of single crystallin behaviors in $\mathrm{2D}$ (C_PLAN) by intercomparison with Shaboche's law.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

| node $\mathit{P4}$ | : $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=0$ |
| --- | --- |

The force load imposed is increasing from $\mathit{FX}=0$ to $\mathit{FX}=25\times 0.755N$, in one increment, leading to a single-axial stress state of $75.5\mathit{MPa}$ (linearity limit)

The load then increases to $\mathit{FX}=25\times 0.955N$ in $n$ increments. The reference calculation is obtained with $n=100$ . The single crystallized calculations are performed with $n=20$ .

With respect to the MONO_VISC2 and MONO_DD_KR behaviors, the load is a load displacement ranging from 0, at the initial moment, to 0.001 at instant 2, in $m$ increments.

For implicit resolutions, $m=20$, and for explicit resolutions, $m=100$.

For model B the load is a required displacement varying from 0, at the initial moment, to 0.001 at moment 3, in 20 increments.
