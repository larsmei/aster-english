# v2.03.109 SDLS109 - eigenfrequencies of a thick cylindrical ring

**Summary:**

This test is based on a vibrational study of the VVP collector of N4 slices. This collector is thick and has a maximum ratio of thickness to mean radius of 0.13, which can be typical of an industrial structure, is slightly higher than the usual validity limit value for plates and hulls.In this study, the Modelling of the inshell collector is then evaluated by comparison with a volume model on a ring.

This test allows the CALC_MODES [U4.52.02](doc:u4.52.02) clean value search operator to be evaluated with the following modellings rigidity and mass matrices:

1. DKQ (finite element MEDKQU4) and DSQ (finite element MEDSQU4) plates,
2. DKT (finite element MEDKTR3) and DST (finite element MEDSTR3) plates with mesh in ears and mesh in stars,
3. COQUE_3D three-dimensional hulls (elements finished MEC3QU9H and MEC3TR7H),
4. elements beam kinematic wires and Fourier TUYAU_3M (finite element MET3SEG3) and TUYAU_6M (finite element MET6SEG3) modes,
5. elements solid plate type COQUE_SOLIDE

The results are compared with the solution from a ring volume model (finite element MECA_HEXA8) showing Fourier modes of order 2 (ovalization) and 3 (trifolio) as well as 2 off-plane modes.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.109/10003FF600001F0F00001EAD1B549C2267BB15C4.svg)

It is a cylindrical ring, medium radius ${R}_{m}=0.369m$, thickness $t=0.048m$ and length $L=0.05m$.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is homogeneous, isotropic, linear elastic. The elastic coefficients are:

$E=185000\mathrm{MPa}$ et $\nu =0.3$ .

The density is constant and is $\rho =7800{\mathrm{kg.m}}^{-3}$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The structure is free in space.

### 1.4 Order of quantity of eigenfrequencies

The search for eigenmodes corresponds to Fourier modes of order 2 and 3 of the ring. The frequencies of a ring can be estimated from an analytical model of curve beam d-Euler [bib1]. For Fourier mode of order $n$, the frequency is:

${f}_{n}=\frac{n({n}^{2}-1)}{2\pi {R}_{m}^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{E{I}_{y}}{m({n}^{2}+1)}}$

où:    ${I}_{y}=\frac{L{t}^{3}}{12}$ et $m=\rho Lt$

For the modes of ovalization ($n=2$) and trifoliation ($n=3$), the corresponding frequencies are $211.65\mathrm{Hz}$ and $598.64\mathrm{Hz}$ respectively. The search for eigenmodes is performed on the $200-800\mathrm{Hz}$ band to capture both modes of Fourier.

### 1.5 Bibliographic reference

1. Blevins R.D., Formulas for natural frequency and mode shape, N.Y.: Van Nostrand Reynhold Company, 1979, 492 pp.

## 10 Summary of the results

- Although the results are honourable, DKQ, DSQ, DKT, DST, TUYAU and TUYAU_6M models do not allow the out-of-plane mode estimates of the ring to be made. Only COQUE_3D with mesh in QUAD9 and elements COQUE_SOLIDE provide a good frequent estimate of these modes.
- DSQ and COQUE_3D models in QUAD9 give an error of about 0.3% for the ovalization and trifoliation modes. For COQUE_3D models in TRIA7, TUYAU and TUYAU_6M, this error is around 0.7% and is more than 1% for DKQ, DKT and DST.
- The best results of DSQ compared to DKQ confirm that the effect of transverse shear is not negligible in the hulls of noticeable thickness.
- On a developable geometry like that of the cylinder studied here, the elements quadrangular finished elementss provide better results than the triangular finished elementss. The DSTs, tested on an mesh in ears and on a mesh symmetrical in stars, are much worse than the DSQs. The same applies to COQUE_3D in TRIA7s compared to COQUE_3D in QUAD9s.
- The performance of COQUE_3D is good in quantitative terms and in calculation time. The richness of interpolation, the inclusion of the cylinder curvature in elements and the metric correction in the shell thickness seem to explain these good results.
- The element COQUE_SOLIDE gives excellent results despite a plate approach (and thus a plane-faceted approximation). Unlike DKQ and DST, the out-of-plane modes are correctly represented.
