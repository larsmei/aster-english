# v5.02.139 SDNL139 - 1D-3D non-intrusive switch of a two-screwed beam

**Summary:**

This test validates the non-intrusive switch between 1D and 3D models, developed in [1].

This is a mixed 1D-3D model of a slender beam on two supports, subject to static loading.

The results of the calculations are compared with those obtained by Code_Aster with a 3D reference mono-model. The results coincide perfectly with the reference solution.

## 1 Reference problem

The objective of this test case is to validate the non-intrusive switch from a beam model to a mixed 3-D beam model in *Code_Aster*.

The results obtained are compared with those obtained from a complete 3D model in *Code_Aster* .

### 1.1 Geometry

A thin slender structure of length $0,25m$ is considered to be Z-axis and circular cross-sectional, and is supported on both ends at positions $-0,1m$ (support 1) and $0,15m$ (support 2) respectively. The section radius is $0,005m$.

![Figure](/figures/v5.02.139/100002000000043800000160B5065B2DDF76972C.png)

Image 1.1-1: Rotor Geometry

### 1.2 Material properties

The two-wired beam has a density of $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$.

The Young's modulus is $E=2.{10}^{11}N{m}^{-2}$ and the Poisson's ratio is $\nu =0,3$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The beam is based on two infinitely rigid supports:

- $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DZ}=0$ at support level 1
- $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DZ}=0$ at support level 2

Also, the rigid rotational body movement according to the beam axis is blocked ($\mathit{DRZ}=0$) over the entire structure.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is a complete 3D calculation with *Code_Aster* (see model B).

1. Mr. Tannous, Development and Evaluation of 1D and 3D coupled numerical modelling approaches to rotor-stator contact, Thesis of the Nantes Central School.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The $0,25m$-length, two-sided structure, extending between $Z=-0,1m$ and $Z=0,15m$ , is connected to the nodes levels at the $0,0m$ and $0,05m$ positions to a 3D model by the AFFE_CHAR_MECA AFFE_CHAR_MECA's 3D_POU option.

It is modelled by elements of Timoshenko beam (POU_D_T) and elements quadratic density (PENTA15 and HEXA20).

![Figure](/figures/v5.02.139/10000200000004B5000001D8F3159758216CC258.png)

Image 3.1-1: mesh of the 3D volumetric model

DYNA_VIBRA calculates the dynamic response of the structure for 3 s, due to a nodal force equal to 100 N on the node of the mesh 3D located at $Z=0,02m$. The shift from the 1D model to the mixed 1D-3D model is done at the moment $\mathit{Tb}=2s$.

## 5 Summary of the results

The test case implements a non-intrusive dynamic shift in *Code_Aster* from a 1D beam model to a 1D-3D mixed model on the basis of a two-supported structure. The results of the resulting mixed model are compared with the results obtained with the complete 3D equivalent model in *Code_Aster* .
