# v3.03.125 SSLS125 - Flaming of a free cylinder under external pressure

**Summary:**

This test represents a stability calculation of a free cylindrical thin envelope at its ends subjected to external pressure. Critical loads leading to elastic flaming d-Euler are calculated. The geometric rigidity matrix used in solving the problem to the specific values is that due to the initial stresses.

It allows to validate elements COQUE_SOLIDE finished Modelling.

The resulting critical load and eigenmode are compared to an analytical reference solution.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.125/Object_1.svg)

The symmetry of the problem allows to model a quarter of cylinder length $L$ , with symmetry conditions specific to the lower edge.

$L=\mathrm{2m}$

Average radius $R=\mathrm{2m}$

Thickness $e=\mathrm{0.02m}$

### 1.2 Material properties

The Material properties component of the plate is:

| $E={2.10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$ | Young's modulus |
| --- | --- |
| $\nu =0.3$ | Poisson's ratio |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- Loading:
- uniformly distributed pressure of ${p}_{\mathrm{cr}}=1.\mathrm{Pa}$ on the cylindrical part.
- Symmetry conditions:
- on $\mathrm{AB}$: DZ = 0
- on $\mathrm{BC}$: DX=0
- on $\mathrm{DA}$: DY=0

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Not applicable

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The critical pressure is given in [bib1] or [bib2] by the following expression:

$$
{P}_{\mathrm{CR}}=\frac{E}{12.(1-{\nu}^{2})}.{n}^{2}.{(\frac{e}{R})}^{3}
$$

with $n$ the mode number (here $n=2,4,6$ )

### 2.2 Baseline

The critical pressures (in $\mathrm{Pa}$ ) are:

| ** Mode (n)** | **Reference** |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 73260 |
| 4 | 293040 |
| 6 | 659340 |
