# v5.01.107 SDND107 - Seismic release and excitation of a system with non-linear "Zener" viscous shock absorber

**Summary:**

The response of the nonlinear viscous shock absorber DIS_VISC, of generalised

The aim is to validate the viscous law in linear and nonlinear dynamics. The following tests are performed:

  1. linear dynamic (operator DYNA_NON_LINE) in one case release, using a discrete linear d-axis as the basis for comparison for the generalised constitutive law 的Zener.

  1. in nonlinear dynamics (operator DYNA_NON_LINE) with a sinusoidal acceleration and then zero acceleration.

  1. In transient nonlinear dynamics (operator DYNA_VIBRA with a time pattern of Euler type) with a sinusoidal acceleration and then zero acceleration. It is the same study as B. The continuation is also validated.
- D, E) in transient nonlinear dynamics (operator DYNA_VIBRA with two Runge-Kutta-type time diagrams of order 5 and order 3, adaptive stepwise) with sinusoidal acceleration and zero acceleration. It is the same study as B. The continuation is also validated.

This behavior is also validated with the STAT_NON_LINE command in SSND101 [V6.08.101](doc:v6.08.101).

$\Delta D={C}_{3}.{∣\frac{{F}_{1}}{{C}_{3}}\left(1+\frac{{E}_{2}}{{E}_{1}}\right)-\frac{{E}_{2}}{{C}_{3}}\left({U}_{2}-{U}_{1}\right)∣}^{1+1/{\alpha}_{3}}$
