# v1.01.384 ZZZZ384 – Validation of MASS_REP key word for AFFE_CARA_ELEM command

**Summary:**

The objective is to test and validate the different uses of the MASS_REP keyword of the affe_cara_elem command. This key word allows to distribute masses over mesh cells types POI1 in proportion to the mesh cells area connected to the node.

The test case validates several assignment methods:

- distribution of a mass over a surface;
- distribution of an over-facial mass over a surface;
- distribution of a mass on a line;
- distribution of a linear mass on a line;
- distribution of a mass, weighted by a space-dependent function, over a surface.

For a mass to be distributed over a surface, mesh cells type POI1 is affected by the mass:

${\mathit{Masse}}_{\mathit{POI1}}=\frac{{\mathit{Masse}}_{\mathit{Totale}}\ast {\mathit{Surface}}_{\mathit{Noeud}}}{{\mathit{Surface}}_{\mathit{Totale}}}$

For an over-facility to be distributed over a surface, mesh cells type POI1 is affected by the mass:

${\mathit{Masse}}_{\mathit{POI1}}={\mathit{Masse}}_{\mathit{Surfacique}}\ast {\mathit{Surface}}_{\mathit{Noeud}}$

If a distribution function is given, the mass (surfacial or linear) is weighted by the value of the function calculated at the centre of gravity of the mesh cell (surfacial or linear) and then distributed to mesh cells type POI1.
