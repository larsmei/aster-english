# v6.03.129 SSNP129 - Validation of the constitutive law regulatory BETON_REGLE_PR

**Summary:**

This test case is designed to validate the constitutive law regulatory BETON_REGLE_PR.

This is an elementary test on an mesh cell where you can display an analytical solution.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A size 1 plate is considered.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.129/100002000000018E00000182449AE8230842E889.png)

### 1.2 Boundary conditions and loads

The problem is considered to remain plan $\mathit{DZ}=\mathit{DRX}=\mathit{DRY}=\mathit{DRZ}=0$ .

A symmetry condition is applied to the $\mathit{N1N2}$ and $\mathit{N1N3}$ sides.

A uniform $y$ displacement equal to $-0,002$ is applied to $\mathit{N2N3}$.

A uniform $x$ displacement equal to $0,0002$ is applied to $\mathit{N3N4}$.

### 1.3 Material properties

$E=2,1\cdot {10}^{6}\mathit{MPa}$

$\nu =0,2$

${E}_{T}=-{10}^{4}\mathit{MPa}$

${\sigma}_{y}^{t}=3\mathit{MPa}$

${\sigma}_{y}^{c}=30\mathit{MPa}$

${\varepsilon}_{c}={10}^{-3}$
