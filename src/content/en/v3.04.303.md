# v3.04.303 SSLV303 - clamped Cylinder under clean weight and pressure

**Summary:**

The purpose of the test is to validate a gravity load and pressure, from a 2D analysis with Fourier mass decomposition of the load.

Two modellings are adopted for this analysis; they differ from the keyword used to define gravity:

1. Modelling A: to validate the keyword gravity,
2. Modelling B: to validate the keyword FORCE_INTERNE.

The gravity is calculated in mode 1, and the applied pressure is given in mode 1.

Both load cases are combined and compared with a numerical calculation in 3D model (model C).

A fourth model (D) is built to validate the keyword FORCE_INTERNE defined from a function.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

For gravity load alone:

The value of the radial displacement field, depending on $z$ , is given by:

$ur = \dfrac{q}{12\,EI} \Bigg\| \dfrac{Z^{4}}{2} - Lz^{3} + \dfrac{L^{2}}{2}z^{2} \Bigg\|$

The maximum displacement in the median section is:

$ur_{(E)} = \dfrac{PL^{3}}{384\,EI}, \quad P : \text{poids propre du cylindre}$

For the pressure load case, a comparison with the C Modelling results is made.

### 2.2 Baseline

1. Move to the middle section, $\mathrm{ur}(E)=0.3566\times {10}^{-6}m$
2. Constraints of clamped support to point B: ${\sigma}_{zz}=–0.2496\times {10}^{6}\mathrm{Pa}$

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Analytical solution for gravity.

boundary conditions:

1. clamped ($u=v=w=0$ ) end section
2. symmetry conditions in the $xz$ plane: $v=0$

**Pression on circumference (field $\mathrm{Up}$)**

The cylinder surface is divided into 8 rows d-elements according to the circumference (1 row d-elements represents a radian $\pi /8$ sector.
The pressure being in $\cos\theta$ , it is assumed to be uniform on each row. For all point of the $\theta$ angle surface , (included between ${\theta}_{1}$ and ${\theta}_{2}$ , ${\theta}_{1}=(n–1)\frac{\pi}{8}$ , ${\theta}_{2}=n\frac{\pi}{8}$ , $1\le n\le 8$ , the value of the pressure assigned to the d-row of elements containing this point is taken equal to $\frac{\mathrm{p0}}{2}(\cos{\theta}_{1}+\cos{\theta}_{2})$

## 7 Summary of the results

Max. deviations (%) between modellings AXIS_FOURIER and 3D Modelling, observed at $E,F,B$ points (in $\theta =0°$ plane), on combined load cases.

|  | **Location** | **Ecart** **AXIS_FOURIER/3D** **In (%)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $U$ displacements:= $u$ in 3D= ${u}_{r}$ in AXI | POINT $F$ | 1.5 |
| Constraints ${\sigma}_{zz}$ | POINT $B$ | 2.8 |
| Constraints ${\sigma}_{xx}(\mathrm{3D})$= ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}(\mathrm{AXI})$ | POINT $B$ | –14.1 |
| Constraints ${\sigma}_{yy}(\mathrm{3D})$= ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }(\mathrm{AXI})$ | POINT $B$ | 14.6 |

- The results between modellings 3D on the one hand and AXIS_FOURIER on the other hand are consistent in terms of displacement (difference of 1.5%) and ${\sigma}_{zz}$ bending stress (difference of 2.8%).
- At l-clamped support, the ${\sigma}_{xx}={\sigma}_{yy}=0$ relationship results in:

${\sigma}_{xx}={\sigma}_{yy}=\frac{\nu}{1-\nu }{\sigma}_{zz}$

The d-d-clamped support relationship is well verified at point $B$ , in 3D Modelling.

- In addition, at point $B$ we have:

${\sigma}_{xx}={\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}$

${\sigma}_{yy}={\sigma}_{\theta \theta }$

In AXIS_FOURIER Modelling, the difference between the two stresses is about 25%.

- A second calculation on the AXIS_FOURIER model was made with a finer mesh: 4 elements in thickness instead of 2, mesh denser in the vicinity of the clamped support $\mathrm{AB}$ (total 800 TRIA6).

The difference observed on ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}$ and ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }$ stresses at point $\mathrm{AB}$ remains: ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}=1.51\times {10}^{6}$ , ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }=2.08\times 106$ (combined load case).

The relationship d的clamped support ${\sigma}_{xx}={\sigma}_{yy}$ is therefore much better verified on the 3D model, with a mesh in the rough thickness.
