# v6.04.118 SSNV118 - Tensile shear test with Shaboche viscoplastic model

**Summary:**

Non-linear quasi-static mechanical problem of transient structures.

Analysis of the response of a volume element to a shear load that imposes a uniform stress-deformation state.

On an identical problem, we perform 4 modellings:

- the first test the VISCOCHAB behavior with implicit integration and constant material coefficients with a coherent tangent matrix at each iteration,
- the second test the VISCOCHAB behavior with implicit integration and material coefficients dependent on temperature and integration with an elastic matrix,
- the third test the VISCOCHAB behavior with explicit integration and constant coefficients,
- the fourth compares the VISCOCHAB and VISC_CIN2_MEMO behaviors, with implicit integration and coherent tangent matrix.

This test therefore validates in particular the numerical integration of Shaboche's elastoviscoplastic behavior model taking into account the phenomenon of memory of the nutwork, for both VISCOCHAB and VISC_CIN2_MEMO models.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Reference solution software is used to establish SIDOLO, which allows simulation and identification of constitutive laws.

The equations of the model are written by the Fortran user as a system of first-order differential equations, solved by a 4-step, adaptive Runge Kutta method.

### 2.2 Baseline

${\sigma}_{xx},{\sigma}_{xy},{\varepsilon}_{xx},{\varepsilon}_{xy},{\mathrm{X1}}_{xx},{\mathrm{X2}}_{xx},p,R,q,{\xi}_{xx}$ at the moment $P(t=10s)$ where $\mathrm{X1}$ and $\mathrm{X2}$ are the kinematic nutring variables, $p$ the cumulative plastic deformation, $R(p,q)$ the isotropic nutring variable and $\xi$ the internal variable allowing for the consideration of the nutring memory.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Uncertainty of SIDOLO.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

1. SIDOLO, version 2.3, Notice of use, Ecole Nationale Supérieure des Mines de Paris, Centre des Matériaux, September 1995.

## 7 Summary of the results

Because the model equations are highly nonlinear, small time increments are necessary to obtain a precise solution.

On this test with simple geometry and boundary conditions, the local time step redistribution improves the accuracy of the results without increasing the calculation time.
