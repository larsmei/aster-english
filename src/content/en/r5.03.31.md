# r5.03.31 Constitutive law FONDATION

**Summary:**

This document describes the non-linear elastoplastic behaviour of a rectangular shallow foundation subjected to a static or seismic three-dimensional loading. This constitutive law is assigned to 3D discrete elements made up of a single node in translation/rotation (DIS_TR) assigned a diagonal stiffness matrix (K_TR_D_N) by means of the relation FONDATION called by the operators for solving non-linear problems STAT_NON_LINE [R5.03.01](doc:r5.03.01) or DYNA_NON_LINE [R5.05.05](doc:r5.05.05). The keyword for defining the characteristics of the material in DEFI_MATERIAU is FONDA_SUPERFI.

The law represents both the sliding mechanism, the mechanism of loss of bearing capacity and the mechanism of uplift of the foundation. Before one of the previously mentioned mechanisms comes into play, all the loading directions present a linear elastic behaviour described by classical stiffness characteristics.

Three mechanisms, which can combine, are distinguished in the constitutive law:

- the first representing the sliding between the underside of the foundation and the soil (treated as a plastic mechanism with hardening);
- the second representing the phenomenon of loss of bearing capacity under the effect of too large a vertical load (treated, here too, as a plastic mechanism with hardening);
- the third representing the phenomenon of uplift–re-contact of the foundation under the effect of a large moment (treated as a non-linear elastic mechanism).

### 1.2 Sliding mechanism

The expression of the yield surface of this mechanism is based on the Coulomb friction model. The frictional strength is the sum of a friction term, a function of the vertical force, and of a cohesion term computed on a reduced area:

${f}_{s}=\sqrt{{({F}_{x}-{q}_{{h}_{x}})}^{2}+{({F}_{y}-{q}_{{h}_{y}})}^{2}}+{F}_{z}\tan({\varphi}_{inter})-{c}_{inter}{A}^{'}$

Where the reduced area is written in the following manner, with the division by 0 taken into account:

${A}^{'}={L}_{x}{L}_{y}\times \lbrace \begin{array}{cc}\hfill \left(1+\frac{2|{M}_{x}|}{{L}_{y}{F}_{z}}\right)\left(1+\frac{2|{M}_{y}|}{{L}_{x}{F}_{z}}\right)& si\text{ }{F}_{z}<0\hfill \\ \hfill 1& si\text{ }\left({F}_{z}=0\right)et\text{ }({M}_{x}=0)et({M}_{y}=0)\hfill \\ \hfill 0& si\text{ }\left({F}_{z}>0\right)ou\text{ }\left(({F}_{z}=0)et({M}_{y},{M}_{x})\ne (0,0)\right)\hfill \end{array}$

In the plane of the forces $(-{F}_{z},{F}_{x})$ , this surface is given in the figure below:

![Figure](/figures/r5.03.31/100002010000040F000002A72C75DF98199F10E9.png)

Figure 1.2-1: Evolution of the surface of the sliding mechanism

With this failure criterion is associated a specific plastic displacement denoted $\underline{{U}_{p}^{sl}}$ with the following coordinates:

$\underline{{U}_{sl}^{pl}}={}^{T}({U}_{x,s}^{pl},{U}_{y,s}^{pl},{U}_{z,s}^{pl},{\theta}_{x,s}^{pl},{\theta}_{y,s}^{pl},{\theta}_{z,s}^{pl}=0)$

The sliding hardening variable ${Q}_{\mathit{sl}}={}^{T}({q}_{{h}_{x}},{q}_{{h}_{y}})$ are added in order to take the cyclic effects into account. These variables evolve when the point representing the force wrench at the foundation goes beyond the surface $>$ . Their evolution is described by the following hardening law:

$\begin{array}{cc}{\mathrm{d}q}_{{h}_{x}}={H}_{h}\cdot {dU}_{x,s}^{pl}\text{ }\exp(-\gamma {v}_{x,s}^{pl})& \mathrm{où}\phantom{0}{U}_{x,s}^{pl}=\sum{dU}_{x,s}^{pl}\text{ }\mathrm{et}\text{ }{v}_{x,s}^{pl}=\sum|{dU}_{x,s}^{pl}|\\ {\mathrm{d}q}_{{h}_{y}}={H}_{h}\cdot {dU}_{y,s}^{pl}\text{ }\exp(-\gamma {v}_{y,s}^{pl})& \mathrm{où}\phantom{0}{U}_{y,s}^{pl}=\sum{dU}_{y,s}^{pl}\text{ }\mathrm{et}\text{ }{v}_{y,s}^{pl}=\sum|{dU}_{y,s}^{pl}|\end{array}$

Which can be written with the following matrix relation:

${\mathrm{d}Q}_{sl}={}^{T}({\mathrm{d}q}_{{h}_{x}},{\mathrm{d}q}_{{h}_{y}}),\text{ }\text{ }\mathrm{d}{U}_{sl}^{pl}=\text{ }{}^{T}\left(\mathrm{d}{U}_{x,s}^{pl},\mathrm{d}{U}_{y,s}^{pl},\mathrm{d}{U}_{z,s}^{pl},\mathrm{d}{\theta}_{x,s}^{pl},\mathrm{d}{\theta}_{y,s}^{pl},\mathrm{d}{\theta}_{z,s}^{pl}=0\right)$

${\mathrm{d}Q}_{sl}={H}_{sl}\cdot \mathrm{d}{U}_{sl}^{pl}\text{}\mathrm{avec}\text{ }{H}_{sl}=\left(\begin{array}{cccccc}{H}_{h}\exp(-\gamma {v}_{x,s}^{pl})& 0& 0& 0& 0& 0\\ 0& {H}_{h}\exp(-\gamma {v}_{y,s}^{pl})& 0& 0& 0& 0\end{array}\right)\text{ }$

$>$ and $>$ are two parameters characteristic of this law, defined as follows:

- $>$ is the hardening modulus when the mechanism is activated for the first time $>$ . In order to ensure continuity with the elastic behaviour, it is recommended to choose ${H}_{h}$ of the same order of magnitude as the stiffness in horizontal translation.
- the coefficient $>$ , homogeneous to the inverse of a displacement, is computed so as to have ${\mathrm{d}Q}_{sl}\approx 0$ when $>$ or $>$ approaches a limit value $>$ defined beforehand. From the properties of the exponential function, one can estimate that $\gamma =\frac{5}{{U}_{max,s}^{pl}}$ .

### 1.3 Bearing-capacity loss mechanism

The expression of the yield surface of this mechanism (abbreviated “CP”) is inspired by the usual criteria. It is based on the justification of the vertical force with respect to a given elastic limit ${V}_{lim}$ . This elastic limit is reduced as a function of the inclination and of the eccentricity of the load, respective functions of the horizontal force and of the moment:

${f}_{\mathit{CP}}=-{F}_{z}-{V}_{lim}{\left(1+\frac{\sqrt{{F}_{x}^{2}+{F}_{y}^{2}}}{{F}_{z}}\right)}^{3}\left(1+\frac{2\left|{M}_{x}\right|}{{L}_{y}{F}_{z}}\right)\left(1+\frac{2\left|{M}_{y}\right|}{{L}_{x}{F}_{z}}\right)$

With this failure criterion is associated a specific plastic displacement denoted ${U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{pl}$ with the following coordinates:

${U}_{CP}^{pl}={}^{T}({U}_{x,CP}^{pl},{U}_{y,CP}^{pl},{U}_{z,CP}^{pl},{\theta}_{x,CP}^{pl},{\theta}_{y,CP}^{pl},{\theta}_{z,CP}^{pl}=0)$

When the point representing the force wrench of the foundation goes beyond the loading surface, the surface evolves as a function of the irreversible vertical settlement by means of 4 kinematic hardening variables ${R}_{h,x},\text{}{R}_{h,y},\text{}{R}_{m,x},\text{}{R}_{m,y}$ respectively for the translation along x, the translation along y, the rotation about x and the rotation about y, and of an isotropic hardening variable $R$ with the following vector notation ${Q}_{\mathit{CP}}={}^{T}({R}_{h,x},{R}_{h,y},R,{R}_{m,x},{R}_{m,y})$ . The expression of this surface becomes:

${f}_{\mathit{CP}}=-{F}_{z}-({V}_{lim}+R)\left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\left(1+\frac{\sqrt{{\left({F}_{x}-{R}_{h,x}\right)}^{2}+{\left({F}_{y}-{R}_{h,y}\right)}^{2}}}{{F}_{z}}\right)}^{3}\left(1+\frac{2\left|{M}_{x}-{R}_{m,x}\right|}{{L}_{y}{F}_{z}}\right)\left(1+\frac{2\left|{M}_{y}-{R}_{m,y}\right|}{{L}_{x}{F}_{z}}\right)\text{}\mathrm{si}\text{ }{F}_{z}<0\\ 1\phantom{0}\mathrm{si}\text{}\left(\left({F}_{x}-{R}_{h,x}\right),\left({F}_{y}-{R}_{h,y}\right),{F}_{z},\left({M}_{x}-{R}_{m,x}\right),\left({M}_{y}-{R}_{m,x}\right)\right)=\left(0,\text{ }0,\text{ }0,\text{ }0,\text{ }0\right)\hfill \\ 0\text{}\mathrm{sinon}\hfill \end{array}\right)$

The following figures show the evolution of the surface of the criterion for the isotropic hardening variable and for the kinematic hardening variables:

![Figure](/figures/r5.03.31/1000020100000525000003266BEE631C6AB24BE8.png)

Figure 1.3-1: Evolution of the surface of the CP mechanism with isotropic hardening

The isotropic hardening variable is deduced directly from the plastic settlement by the relation:

$\begin{array}{c}R=\left({V}_{ult}-{V}_{lim}\right)\left(\frac{{v}_{z,CP}^{pl}}{{v}_{z,CP}^{pl}+{v}_{r,CP}^{pl}}\right)\end{array}\text{}\mathrm{avec}\text{ }{U}_{z,CP}^{pl}=\sum{dU}_{z,CP}^{pl}\text{ }\mathrm{et}\text{ }{v}_{z,CP}^{pl}=\sum\left|{dU}_{z,CP}^{pl}\right|$

${\mathrm{D}}^{'}\mathit{où}\frac{\partial R}{\partial {U}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}}=\frac{{\delta}_{z}^{\mathit{CP}}\left({V}_{ult}-{V}_{lim}\right){v}_{r,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}}{{\left({v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}+{v}_{r,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\right)}^{2}}\text{ }\mathrm{avec}\text{}\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}=\sum\left|{\mathrm{d}U}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\right|\hfill \\ \hfill {\delta}_{z}^{CP}=\lbrace \begin{array}{cc}\hfill 1& \mathrm{si}\text{ }\mathrm{d}{U}_{z,CP}^{pl}>0\hfill \\ \hfill -1& \mathrm{sinon}\hfill \end{array}\end{array}$

$>$ and ${v}_{r,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}$ are two parameters characteristic of this hardening law, defined as follows:

- $>$ is the bearing capacity of the ground under the foundation, defined from in situ tests or from finite-element simulations.
- The value of ${v}_{r,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}$ denotes a reference settlement; this value can be of the order of 10% of the small dimension of the foundation.

![Figure](/figures/r5.03.31/10000201000004E40000031AC8429C3CDC9CBB04.png)

Figure 1.3-2: Evolution of the surface of the CP mechanism with kinematic hardening alone

The evolution of the hardening variables is similar to that of sliding (for the kinematic hardening):

$\left(\begin{array}{c}\mathrm{d}{R}_{h,x}={I}_{h,x}\exp\left(-{\gamma}_{h,x}{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\right)\mathrm{d}{U}_{x,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\\ \mathrm{d}{R}_{h,y}={I}_{h,y}\exp\left(-{\gamma}_{h,y}{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\right)\mathrm{d}{U}_{y,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\\ \mathrm{d}R=\frac{\partial R}{\partial {U}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}}\mathrm{d}{U}_{z,CP}^{pl}\text{ }\hfill \\ \mathrm{d}{R}_{m,x}={I}_{m,x}\exp\left(-{\gamma}_{m,x}{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\right)\mathrm{d}{\theta}_{x,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\\ \mathrm{d}{R}_{m,y}={I}_{m,y}\exp\left(-{\gamma}_{m,y}{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\right)\mathrm{d}{\theta}_{y,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\end{array}\right)$

Which can be written with the following matrix relation:

$\mathrm{d}{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}={I}_{\mathit{CP}}\cdot \mathrm{d}{U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\text{}\mathrm{avec}\text{ }\lbrace \begin{array}{c}\mathrm{d}{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}={}^{T}\left(\mathrm{d}{R}_{h,x}\text{ },\text{ }\mathrm{d}{R}_{h,y}\text{ },\text{ }\mathrm{d}R\text{ },\text{ }\mathrm{d}{R}_{m,x}\text{ },\text{ }\mathrm{d}{R}_{m,y}\right)\hfill \\ \mathrm{d}{U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{pl}={}^{T}\left(\mathrm{d}{U}_{x,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\text{ },\mathrm{d}{U}_{y,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\text{ },\text{ }\mathrm{d}{U}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\text{ },\text{ }\mathrm{d}{\theta}_{x,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\text{ },\text{ }\mathrm{d}{\theta}_{y,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\right)\end{array}\mathrm{et}$

${I}_{\mathit{CP}}=\left(\begin{array}{ccccc}{I}_{h,x}{e}^{-{\gamma}_{h,x}{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}}& 0& 0& 0& 0\\ 0& {I}_{h,y}{e}^{-{\gamma}_{h,y}{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}}& 0& 0& 0\\ 0& 0& \frac{\partial R}{\partial {U}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}}& 0& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& {I}_{m,x}{e}^{-{\gamma}_{m,x}{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}}& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& 0& {I}_{m,y}{e}^{-{\gamma}_{m,y}{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}}\end{array}\right)$

For each kinematic hardening law, ${I}_{h\text{ }ou\text{ }m,x\text{ }ou\text{ }y}$ and ${\gamma}_{h\text{ }ou\text{ }m,x\text{ }ou\text{ }y}$ are two parameters characteristic of this law, defined as follows:

- $>$ is the hardening modulus when the mechanism is activated for the first time $\left({U}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}={v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}=0\right)$ In order to ensure continuity with the elastic behaviour, it is recommended to choose ${I}_{\mathit{CP}}$ of the same order of magnitude as the stiffness of the degree of freedom considered.
- the coefficient $>$ , homogeneous to the inverse of a displacement, is computed so as to have ${\mathrm{d}Q}_{\mathit{CP}}\approx 0$ when ${v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}$ approaches a limit value ${v}_{max,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}$ defined beforehand. From the properties of the exponential function, one can estimate that ${\gamma}_{\mathit{CP}}=\frac{5}{{v}_{max,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}}$ .

### 1.4 Uplift mechanism

The uplift mechanism (activated only if the parameter DECOLLEMENT is equal to ‘OUI’), considered as being a purely geometric mechanism, is described by a degradation of the rotational stiffness. This degradation is assumed to be reversible. Different expressions can be used according to whether one considers the secant stiffness or the tangent stiffness. In practice, the rotational stiffness ${K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}\text{ }$ or ${K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}$ varies as a function of the eccentricity ${e}_{x,y}=-|{M}_{x,y}|/{F}_{z}$ . Below, the value of the secant rotational stiffness:

${K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}^{séc}=\frac{{M}_{x}}{{\theta}_{x}}={K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}^{0}\lbrace \begin{array}{cc}1& \mathrm{si}|{M}_{x}|\le -\frac{{F}_{z}{L}_{y}}{6}\\ \hfill -\frac{27}{4}\cdot \frac{2\left|{M}_{x}\right|}{{F}_{z}{L}_{y}}\cdot {\left(1+\frac{2\left|{M}_{x}\right|}{{F}_{z}{L}_{y}}\right)}^{2}\text{ }& \mathrm{sinon}\hfill \end{array}$

${K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}^{séc}=\frac{{M}_{y}}{{\theta}_{y}}={K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}^{0}\lbrace \begin{array}{cc}1& \mathrm{si}|{M}_{y}|\le -\frac{{F}_{z}{L}_{x}}{6}\\ -\frac{27}{4}\cdot \frac{2\left({M}_{y}\right)}{{F}_{z}{L}_{x}}\cdot {\left(1+\frac{2\left({M}_{y}\right)}{{F}_{z}{L}_{x}}\right)}^{2}\text{ }& \mathrm{sinon}\hfill \end{array}$

Where ${K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}^{0}$ and ${K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}^{0}$ are the rotational stiffnesses without uplift.

This uplift is implemented in code_aster only in the form of a tangent stiffness:

${K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}^{tan}=\frac{\partial {M}_{x}}{\partial {\theta}_{x}}={K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}^{0}\lbrace \begin{array}{cc}1& \mathrm{si}|{M}_{x}|\le -\frac{{F}_{z}{L}_{y}}{6}\\ \frac{27}{8}{\left(1+\frac{2\left|{M}_{x}\right|}{{F}_{z}{L}_{y}}\right)}^{3}& \mathrm{sinon}\hfill \end{array}$

${K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}^{tan}=\frac{{M}_{y}}{{\theta}_{y}}={K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}^{0}\lbrace \begin{array}{cc}1& \mathrm{si}|{M}_{y}|\le -\frac{{F}_{z}{L}_{x}}{6}\\ \frac{27}{8}{\left(1+\frac{2\left|{M}_{y}\right|}{{F}_{z}{L}_{x}}\right)}^{3}\text{ }& \mathrm{sinon}\hfill \end{array}$

Thus the moment cannot exceed the ultimate moment:

${M}_{x}^{ult}=\left|\frac{{F}_{z}{L}_{y}}{2}\right|\text{ }et\text{ }{M}_{y}^{ult}=\left|\frac{{F}_{z}{L}_{x}}{2}\right|$

The variation of the secant and tangent stiffnesses as a function of the moment is given in the figure below.

### 2.1 Determination of the loading increment

#### 2.1.1 Input data

In addition to the parameters of the constitutive law one has as input the variables at the beginning of the time step (O):

- the global force: ${F}^{O}={}^{T}\left({F}_{x}^{o},{F}_{y}^{o},{F}_{z}^{o},{M}_{x}^{o},{M}_{y}^{o},{M}_{z}^{o}\right)\text{ }$ ;
- the displacement: ${U}^{O}={}^{T}({u}_{x}^{o},{u}_{y}^{o},{u}_{z}^{o},{\theta}_{x}^{o},{\theta}_{y}^{o},{\theta}_{z}^{o})\text{ }$ ;
- the internal sliding-hardening variables: ${Q}_{sl}^{o}={}^{T}({q}_{{h}_{x}}^{O},{q}_{{h}_{y}}^{O})\text{ }$ ;
- the plastic sliding displacement: ${U}_{sl}^{O}={}^{T}({u}_{x,s}^{pl,O},{u}_{y,s}^{pl,O},{u}_{z,s}^{pl,O},{\theta}_{x,s}^{pl,O},{\theta}_{y,s}^{pl,O})\text{ }$ ;
- the bearing-capacity hardening variables: ${Q}_{sl}^{o}={}^{T}({R}_{h,x}^{O}\text{ },{R}_{h,y}^{O},{R}^{O},{R}_{m,x}^{O},{R}_{m,y}^{O})\text{ }$ ;
- the plastic displacement in CP: ${U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}={}^{T}({u}_{x,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,O},{u}_{y,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,O},{u}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,O},{\theta}_{x,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,O},{\theta}_{y,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,O})\text{ }$ ;
- the intermediate internal variables in sliding and CP: ${v}_{x,s}^{pl,O}$ , ${v}_{y,s}^{pl,O}$  and ${v}_{z,CP}^{pl,O}$ ;

As well as the final displacement at the end of the time step: ${U}^{F}={}^{T}({u}_{x}^{F},{u}_{y}^{F},{u}_{z}^{F},{\theta}_{x}^{F},{\theta}_{y}^{F},{\theta}_{z}^{F})$

The whole set of the variables at the end of the time step is required (notation “F” instead of “O”)

#### 2.1.2 Construction of the tangent elastic matrix

An initial stiffness matrix ${K}^{ini}$ must be assigned to the discrete element. This matrix must be symmetric and diagonal in its local frame (the non-diagonal terms are ignored).

The first stage consists, starting from the global force ${F}^{O}$ at the beginning of the time step, in computing the tangent elastic rotational stiffness along x ${K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}^{tan,o}$ and y ${K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}^{tan,o}$ with the formulation seen in [§ 1.4 ]; one thus obtains the tangent elastic stiffness matrix:

${K}_{élas}^{o,tan}=\left(\begin{array}{cccccc}{K}_{xx}^{ini}& 0& 0& 0& 0& 0\\ 0& {K}_{yy}^{ini}& 0& 0& 0& 0\\ 0& 0& {K}_{zz}^{ini}& 0& 0& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& {K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}^{tan,o}& 0& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& 0& {K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}^{tan,o}& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& 0& 0& {K}_{\mathit{rz},\mathit{rz}}^{ini}\end{array}\right)$

#### 2.1.3 Elastic trial

From the tangent elastic stiffness matrix, one computes the force

${F}^{élas}={}^{T}\left({F}_{x}^{élas},{F}_{y}^{élas},{F}_{z}^{élas},{M}_{x}^{élas},{M}_{y}^{élas},{M}_{z}^{élas}\right)$ which would be obtained if no plasticity mechanism is activated (also called “elastic trial”). That is:

${F}^{élas}={F}^{O}+{K}_{élas}^{O,tan}\cdot \left({U}^{F}-{U}^{O}\right)$

#### 2.1.4 Computation of the plastic flow

One now computes ${f}_{\mathit{CP}}\left({F}^{élas},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}\right)$ and ${f}_{s}\left({F}^{élas},{Q}_{sl}^{O}\right)$ , see § 1.3 and § 1.2 .

##### 2.1.4.1 If ${f}_{\mathit{CP}}\le 0`et $>$

Then no plasticity mechanism is reached, the foundation is in the elastic domain, the elastic trial corresponds to the force at the end of the time step: ${F}^{F}={F}^{élas}$  and no plasticity variable changes:

${Q}_{sl}^{F}={Q}_{sl}^{O}\text{ },{U}_{sl}^{F}={U}_{sl}^{O},{v}_{x,s}^{pl,F}={v}_{x,s}^{pl,O},{v}_{y,s}^{pl,F}={v}_{y,s}^{pl,O},\text{ }{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{F}={Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}\text{ },{U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{F}={U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}\text{ }et\text{ }{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,F}={v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,O}$

![Figure](/figures/r5.03.31/100002010000037B000001E6899CF6748E8A5069.png)

Figure 2.1-1: Illustration of the elastic trial without a failure mechanism being reached

##### 2.1.4.2 If ${f}_{\mathit{CP}}>0`et $>$

Only the bearing-capacity loss mechanism is reached; no plasticity variable linked to sliding changes:

${Q}_{sl}^{F}={Q}_{sl}^{O}\text{ },{U}_{sl}^{F}={U}_{sl}^{O},{v}_{x,s}^{pl,F}={v}_{x,s}^{pl,O},{v}_{y,s}^{pl,F}={v}_{y,s}^{pl,O}$

One then seeks the loading correction ${dF}^{cor}\text{ }$ and the evolution of the hardening variables ${\mathrm{d}Q}_{\mathit{CP}}$ necessary in order to return onto the criterion, namely:

${f}_{\mathit{CP}}({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}+\mathrm{d}{Q}_{\mathit{CP}})=0$

This equation is solved by an iterative loop until convergence, by writing at iteration k the limited expansion of this equation about the point $({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k})$ :

${f}_{\mathit{CP}}\left({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}+\mathrm{d}{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}\right)\approx \left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{f}_{\mathit{CP}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\\ +{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor}-{F}^{ité,k}\right)\\ +{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial {Q}_{\mathit{CP}}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}+{\mathrm{d}Q}_{\mathit{CP}}-{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\end{array}\right\rbrace =0$

With, to start the iterative loop: $({F}^{ité,0},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,0})=({F}^{élas},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O})$

The loading correction and the evolution of the hardening can be related to the plastic displacement increment ${\mathrm{d}U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}$ by the following relations:

${\mathrm{d}F}^{cor}={K}_{élas}^{O,tan}\cdot {\mathrm{d}U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}$ and ${\mathrm{d}Q}_{\mathit{CP}}={I}_{CP}^{O}\cdot {\mathrm{d}U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}$

Where ${I}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}\text{ }$ is computed with the parameters at the beginning of the time step (see § 1.3 ).

One makes the hypothesis that the plastic flow is normal to the yield surface; thus, one relates the plastic increment to the plastic flow variable $\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}$ :

${\mathrm{d}U}_{CP}^{k}=\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}$

Thus one can write and obtain an approximation of the plastic flow:

$\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}=\frac{{f}_{\mathit{CP}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)+{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({F}^{élas}-{F}^{ité,k}\right)+{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial {Q}_{\mathit{CP}}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}-{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)}{\left({}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right){K}_{élas}^{O,tan}-{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial {Q}_{\mathit{CP}}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right){I}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}\right)\times \left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right)}$

From the plastic flow $\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}$ , one deduces ${\mathrm{d}U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}$ , ${\mathrm{d}F}^{cor}$ and ${\mathrm{d}Q}_{\mathit{CP}}$ , and one re-evaluates the failure criterion:

- if $\left|{f}_{\mathit{CP}}\left({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}+{\mathrm{d}Q}_{\mathit{CP}}\right)\right|\le \mathrm{error}$ then the convergence of the correction has been reached;
- otherwise one runs the loop again with:

$\left({F}^{ité,k+1},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k+1}\right)=\left({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}+{\mathrm{d}Q}_{\mathit{CP}}\right)$

After convergence of the loop at iteration “m”, the plasticity parameters linked to the bearing capacity are updated:

${Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{F}={Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,m+1},{U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{F}={U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}+{\mathrm{d}U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{m},{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,F}={v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,O}+\left|\mathrm{d}{U}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,m+1}\right|$

One also recovers the force at the end of the time step:

${F}^{F}={F}^{ité,m+1}$

##### 2.1.4.3 If ${f}_{\mathit{CP}}\le 0`et :math:`{f}_{s}>0$

Only the bearing-capacity loss mechanism is reached; no plasticity variable linked to sliding changes:

${Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{F}={Q}_{CP}^{O}\text{ },{U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{F}={U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O},{v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,F}={v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl,O}$

The situation is solved in the same way as in § 2.1.4.2 ; one then seeks the loading correction ${\mathrm{d}F}^{cor}\text{ }$ and the evolution of the hardening variables ${\mathrm{d}Q}_{sl}$ necessary in order to return onto the criterion, namely:

${f}_{sl}({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor},{Q}_{sl}^{O}+\mathrm{d}{Q}_{sl})=0$

This equation is solved by an iterative loop until convergence, by writing at iteration k the limited expansion of this equation about the point $\text{ }({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k})$ :

${f}_{s}\left({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor},{Q}_{sl}^{O}+\mathrm{d}{Q}_{sl}\right)\approx \left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{f}_{s}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\\ +{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor}-{F}^{ité,k}\right)\\ +{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial {Q}_{sl}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({Q}_{sl}^{O}+{\mathrm{d}Q}_{sl}-{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\end{array}\right\rbrace =0$

With, to start the iterative loop: $({F}^{ité,0},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,0})=({F}^{élas},{Q}_{sl}^{O})$

The loading correction and the evolution of the hardening can be related to the plastic displacement increment ${\mathrm{d}U}_{sl}^{k}$ by the following relations:

${\mathrm{d}F}^{cor}={K}_{élas}^{O,tan}\cdot {\mathrm{d}U}_{sl}^{k}$ and ${\mathrm{d}Q}_{sl}={H}_{sl}^{O}\cdot {\mathrm{d}U}_{sl}^{k}$

Where ${H}_{sl}^{O}\text{ }$ is computed with the parameters at the beginning of the time step (see §1.2).

One makes the hypothesis that the plastic flow is normal to the yield surface; thus, one relates the plastic increment to the plastic flow variable $d{\lambda}_{sl}^{k}$ :

${\mathrm{d}U}_{sl}^{k}=\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{sl}^{k}$

Thus one can write and obtain an approximation of the plastic flow:

$\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{sl}^{k}=\frac{{f}_{s}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)+{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({F}^{élas}-{F}^{ité,k}\right)+{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial {Q}_{sl}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}-{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)}{\left({}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right){K}_{élas}^{O,tan}-{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial {Q}_{sl}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right){H}_{sl}^{O}\right)\times \left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right)}$

From the plastic flow $\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}$ , one deduces ${\mathrm{d}U}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}$ , ${\mathrm{d}F}^{cor}$ and ${\mathrm{d}Q}_{\mathit{CP}}$ , and one re-evaluates the failure criterion:

- if $\left|{f}_{s}\left({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor},{Q}_{sl}^{O}+{\mathrm{d}Q}_{sl}\right)\right|\le \mathrm{error}$ then the convergence of the correction has been reached;
- otherwise one runs the loop again with: $\left({F}^{ité,k+1},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k+1}\right)=\left({F}^{élas}-{\mathrm{d}F}^{cor},{Q}_{sl}^{O}+{\mathrm{d}Q}_{sl}\right)$

After convergence of the loop at iteration “m”, the plasticity parameters linked to sliding are updated:

${Q}_{sl}^{F}={Q}_{sl}^{ité,m+1},{U}_{sl}^{F}={U}_{sl}^{O}+{\mathrm{d}U}_{sl}^{m},{v}_{x,s}^{pl,F}={v}_{x,s}^{pl,O}+\left|\mathrm{d}{U}_{x,s}^{pl,m+1}\right|\text{ },{v}_{y,s}^{pl,F}={v}_{y,s}^{pl,O}+\left|\mathrm{d}{U}_{y,s}^{pl,m+1}\right|$

One also recovers the force at the end of the time step:

${F}^{F}={F}^{ité,m+1}$

##### 2.1.4.4 If ${f}_{\mathit{CP}}>0`et $>$

Then both plasticity mechanisms are reached. The situation is then solved in the same way as in § 2.1.4.2 and § 2.1.4.3 but by writing the two criteria instead of one, by writing at iteration k the limited expansion of this equation about the point $\text{ }\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)$ :

- ${m}_{\mathit{CP},\mathit{CP}}=\left({}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right){K}_{élas}^{O,tan}-{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial {Q}_{\mathit{CP}}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right){I}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}\right)\times \left(\frac{\partial {f}_{CP}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{CP}^{ité,k}\right)\right)$
- ${m}_{s,s}=\left({}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right){K}_{élas}^{O,tan}-{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial {Q}_{sl}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right){H}_{sl}^{O}\right)\times \left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right)$
- ${m}_{s,CP}={}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right){K}_{élas}^{O,tan}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{sl}^{ité,k}\right)\right)$
- $\tilde{{f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{k}}={f}_{\mathit{CP}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)+{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}}{\partial F}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({F}^{élas}-{F}^{ité,k}\right)+{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{CP}}{\partial {Q}_{\mathit{CP}}}\left({F}^{ité,k},{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)\right)\left({Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{O}-{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}^{ité,k}\right)$

##### 2.1.4.6 Handling of the inflection points

The bearing-capacity criterion presents two inflection points and the sliding criterion presents one inflection point. At these inflection points, the solution methods of the previous paragraphs are not sufficient; the criterion must then be separated into several sub-criteria in order to be able to converge on the inflection points, as the figure below shows:

![Figure](/figures/r5.03.31/100002010000044800000424B1721E2E0E2746DB.png)

Figure 2.1-5: Illustration of the decomposition into two sub-criteria at the inflection points

Thus, in order to handle the bearing-capacity criterion, 4 sub-criteria should be considered:

- The normal criterion:

${f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{+++\rbrace =-{F}_{z}-\overline{V}{\left(1+\frac{\sqrt{{\overline{{F}_{x}}}^{2}+{\overline{{F}_{y}}}^{2}}}{{F}_{z}}\right)}^{3}\left(1+\delta \left(\overline{{M}_{x}^{O}}\right)\frac{2\overline{{M}_{x}}}{{L}_{y}{F}_{z}}\right)\left(1+\delta \left(\overline{{M}_{y}^{O}}\right)\frac{2\overline{{M}_{y}}}{{L}_{x}{F}_{z}}\right)}$

$\delta \left(\overline{{M}_{y}^{O}}\right)=\lbrace \begin{array}{c}+1\text{ }\mathrm{si}\text{ }\overline{{M}_{y}^{O}}={M}_{y}^{O}-{R}_{r,y}^{O}>0\text{ }\text{au début du pas de temps}\\ -1\text{ }\mathrm{si}\text{ }\overline{{M}_{y}^{O}}={M}_{y}^{O}-{R}_{r,y}^{O}<0\text{ }\text{au début du pas de temps}\end{array}$

- For the criterion linearised along the moment about y:

${f}_{\mathit{CP},lin}^{My+\rbrace =\left(1-\frac{{l}_{in}}{{\left(1-\frac{2\sqrt{{\left({F}_{x}-{R}_{h,x}\right)}^{2}+{\left({F}_{y}-{R}_{h,y}\right)}^{2}}}{{l}_{in}{L}_{y}\left({V}_{lim}+R\right)}\right)}^{3}\left(1-\frac{2\left({M}_{x}-{R}_{m,x}\right)}{{l}_{in}{L}_{y}\left({V}_{lim}+R\right)}\right)}\right){L}_{x}{F}_{z}+2\delta \left(\overline{{M}_{y}^{O}}\right)\left({M}_{y}-{R}_{m,y}\right)}$

$\mathrm{et}\text{ }{f}_{\mathit{CP},lin}^{My-\rbrace =\left(1-\frac{{l}_{in}}{{\left(1-\frac{2\sqrt{{\left({F}_{x}-{R}_{h,x}\right)}^{2}+{\left({F}_{y}-{R}_{h,y}\right)}^{2}}}{{l}_{in}{L}_{y}\left({V}_{lim}+R\right)}\right)}^{3}\left(1-\frac{2\left({M}_{x}-{R}_{m,x}\right)}{{l}_{in}{L}_{y}\left({V}_{lim}+R\right)}\right)}\right){L}_{x}{F}_{z}-2\delta \left(\overline{{M}_{y}^{O}}\right)\left({M}_{y}-{R}_{m,y}\right)}$

$\delta \left(\overline{{M}_{y}^{O}}\right)=\lbrace \begin{array}{c}+1\text{ }\mathrm{si}\text{ }\overline{{M}_{y}^{O}}={M}_{y}^{O}-{R}_{r,y}^{O}>0\text{ }\text{au début du pas de temps}\\ -1\text{ }\mathrm{si}\text{ }\overline{{M}_{y}^{O}}={M}_{y}^{O}-{R}_{r,y}^{O}<0\text{ }\text{au début du pas de temps}\end{array}$

The system is then solved as a multi-criterion in the same way as in § 2.1.4.4 .

### 2.2 Determination of the tangent matrix

#### 2.2.1 Input data

In addition to the parameters of the constitutive law, one has as input the variables obtained at the end or at the beginning of the time step (the notation F or O will therefore not be used), whose computation is presented in § 2.1 :

- the global force: $F={}^{T}\left({F}_{x},{F}_{y},{F}_{z},{M}_{x},{M}_{y},{M}_{z}\right)$
- the sliding hardening variables: ${Q}_{sl}={}^{T}\left({q}_{hx},{q}_{hy}\right)$
- the plastic sliding displacement: ${U}_{sl}={}^{T}\left({U}_{x,s}^{pl},{U}_{y,s}^{pl},{U}_{z,s}^{pl},{\theta}_{x,s}^{pl},{\theta}_{y,s}^{pl}\right)$
- the bearing-capacity hardening variables: ${Q}_{CP}={}^{T}\left({R}_{h,x},{R}_{h,y},R\text{ },{R}_{m,x},\text{ }{R}_{m,y}\right)$
- the plastic displacement in bearing capacity: ${U}_{\mathit{CP}}={}^{T}\left({U}_{x,\mathit{CP}}^{pl},{U}_{y,\mathit{CP}}^{pl},{U}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl},{\theta}_{x,\mathit{CP}}^{pl},{\theta}_{y,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}\right)$
- the internal variables in sliding and in bearing capacity: ${v}_{x,s}^{pl}$ , ${v}_{y,s}^{pl}$ and ${v}_{z,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}$
- As well as all their associated differentials $\mathrm{d}F,\text{}\mathrm{d}{Q}_{sl},\text{ }\mathrm{d}{Q}_{\mathit{CP}},\mathrm{d}{U}_{sl},\text{ }\mathrm{d}{U}_{\mathit{CP}}$
- the internal variable ${d}^{état}$ which indicates which criteria have been reached during the computation of the loading increment, which makes it possible to have all the criteria reached taking into account the handling of the convergence at the origin (§ 2.1.4.7 ) and the sub-criteria for the handling of the inflection points (§ 2.1.4.6 ).

#### 2.2.2 Construction of the tangent matrix

##### 2.2.2.1 If $>$

Then no plasticity mechanism is reached, which corresponds to § 2.1.4.1 ; the foundation is in the elastic domain. From the global force $F$ , the tangent elastic rotational stiffnesses along x ${K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}^{tan}$ and y ${K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}^{tan}$ are computed with the formulation seen in [§ 1.4 ]; one thus obtains the tangent elastic stiffness matrix which, in the case of $>$ , is the tangent matrix:

$K={K}_{élas}^{tan}=\left(\begin{array}{cccccc}{K}_{xx}^{ini}& 0& 0& 0& 0& 0\\ 0& {K}_{yy}^{ini}& 0& 0& 0& 0\\ 0& 0& {K}_{zz}^{ini}& 0& 0& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& {K}_{\mathit{rx},\mathit{rx}}^{tan}& 0& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& 0& {K}_{\mathit{ry},\mathit{ry}}^{tan}& 0\\ 0& 0& 0& 0& 0& {K}_{\mathit{rz},\mathit{rz}}^{ini}\end{array}\right)$

##### 2.2.2.2 If $>$

Reading ${d}^{état}\text{ }$ makes it possible to know which failure criteria have been reached during the previous loading phase. It is noted here that there have been ${n}_{s}$ sliding-failure sub-criteria reached (taking into account the possible inflection point presented in § 2.1.4.6 ), denoted ${f}_{s}^{i\in ⟦1,ns⟧}$ (with ${\lambda}_{s}^{i}$ the associated plastic multiplier) and ${n}_{\mathit{CP}}$ bearing-capacity failure sub-criteria reached (taking into account the inflection points presented in § 2.1.4.6 as well as the handling of the convergence at the origin presented in § 2.1.4.7 ), denoted ${f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j\in ⟦1,{n}_{\mathit{CP}}⟧}$ (with ${\lambda}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}$ the associated plastic multiplier).

For each sub-criterion, one has:

${f}_{s}^{i}=0,\text{ }\mathrm{d}{f}_{s}^{i}={}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{i}}{\partial F}\right)\mathrm{d}F+{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{i}}{\partial {Q}_{sl}}\right)\mathrm{d}{Q}_{sl}=0\text{ }\mathrm{et}\text{ }{f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}=0,\text{}\mathrm{d}{f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}={}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}}{\partial F}\right)\mathrm{d}F+{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}}{\partial {Q}_{\mathit{CP}}}\right)\mathrm{d}{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}=0$

By setting the following relations:

$\mathrm{d}F={K}_{élas}^{tan}\cdot \mathrm{d}{U}_{élas}={K}_{élas}^{tan}\left(\mathrm{d}U-\mathrm{d}{U}_{s}-\mathrm{d}{U}_{\mathit{CP}}\right),\text{ }\mathrm{d}{Q}_{sl}={H}_{sl}\cdot \mathrm{d}{U}_{sl},\text{ }\mathrm{d}{Q}_{\mathit{CP}}={I}_{\mathit{CP}}\cdot \mathrm{d}{U}_{\mathit{CP}}$

The plastic flows are denoted:

$\mathrm{d}{U}_{sl}=\sum_{i=1}^{i={n}_{s}}\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{i}}{\partial F}\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{sl}^{i}\text{ }\mathrm{et}\text{ }\mathrm{d}{U}_{CP}=\sum_{j=1}^{j={n}_{CP}}\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}}{\partial F}\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}$

One arrives at the following system:

$\left(\begin{array}{cc}{M}_{sl,sl}^{K}& {M}_{sl,\mathit{CP}}^{K}\\ {}^{T}\left({M}_{sl,\mathit{CP}}^{K}\right)& {M}_{\mathit{CP},\mathit{CP}}^{K}\end{array}\right)\left(\begin{array}{c}{\mathrm{d}\lambda }_{\mathrm{s}l}^{i\in \left(1,\mathrm{n}\mathrm{s}\right)}\text{ }\\ {\mathrm{d}\lambda }_{CP}^{j\in \left(1,{n}_{CP}\right)}\text{ }\end{array}\right)=\left(\begin{array}{c}{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{i\in \left(1,ns\right)}}{\partial F}\right)\\ {}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j\in \left(1,{n}_{\mathit{CP}}\right)}}{\partial F}\right)\end{array}\right){K}_{élas}^{tan}\mathrm{d}U\text{ }\mathrm{ou}\text{ }{M}^{K}\mathrm{d}\lambda ={}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial F}\right){K}_{élas}^{tan}\mathrm{d}U$

With:

- ${\left({M}_{sl,sl}^{K}\right)}_{(i,j)\in {⟦1,ns⟧}^{2}}={}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{i}}{\partial F}\right){K}_{élas}^{tan}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{j}}{\partial F}\right)-{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{i}}{\partial {Q}_{sl}}\right){H}_{sl}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{j}}{\partial F}\right)$
- ${\left({M}_{sl,\mathit{CP}}^{K}\right)}_{(i,j)\in \left(1,{n}_{s}\right)\times \left(1,{n}_{\mathit{CP}}\right)}={}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{i}}{\partial F}\right){K}_{élas}^{tan}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}}{\partial F}\right)$
- ${\left({M}_{\mathit{CP},\mathit{CP}}^{K}\right)}_{(i,j)\in {\left(1,{n}_{\mathit{CP}}\right)}^{2}}={}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{i}}{\partial F}\right){K}_{élas}^{tan}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}}{\partial F}\right)-{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{i}}{\partial {Q}_{\mathit{CP}}}\right){I}_{\mathit{CP}}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{CP}^{j}}{\partial F}\right)$
- By simplification $\left(\begin{array}{c}{\mathrm{d}\lambda }_{sl}^{i\in \left(1,ns\right)}\text{ }\\ {\mathrm{d}\lambda }_{\mathit{CP}}^{j\in \left(1,{n}_{\mathit{CP}}\right)}\text{ }\end{array}\right)=\mathrm{d}\lambda$  and $\left(\begin{array}{c}{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{i\in \left(1,ns\right)}}{\partial F}\right)\\ {}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j\in \left(1,{n}_{\mathit{CP}}\right)}}{\partial F}\right)\end{array}\right)={}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial F}\right)$

This system is subsequently inverted:

${\left({M}^{K}\right)}^{-1}={\left(\begin{array}{cc}{M}_{sl,sl}^{K}& {M}_{sl,\mathit{CP}}^{K}\\ {}^{T}\left({M}_{sl,\mathit{CP}}^{K}\right)& {M}_{\mathit{CP},\mathit{CP}}^{K}\end{array}\right)}^{-1}=\left(\begin{array}{cc}{S}_{sl,sl}^{K}& {S}_{sl,\mathit{CP}}^{K}\\ {}^{T}\left({S}_{sl,\mathit{CP}}^{K}\right)& {S}_{\mathit{CP},\mathit{CP}}^{K}\end{array}\right)={S}^{K}$

One thus obtains the expression of the plastic flow:

$\mathrm{d}\lambda ={S}^{K}{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial F}\right){K}_{élas}^{tan}\mathrm{d}U$

Starting again from the relation between force and elastic displacement:

$\mathrm{d}F={K}_{élas}^{tan}\mathrm{d}{U}_{élas}={K}_{élas}^{tan}\left(\mathrm{d}U-\sum_{i=1}^{i=ns}\frac{\partial {f}_{s}^{i}}{\partial F}\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{sl}^{i}-\sum_{j=1}^{j={n}_{\mathit{CP}}}\frac{\partial {f}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}}{\partial F}\mathrm{d}{\lambda}_{\mathit{CP}}^{j}\right)={K}_{élas}^{tan}\left(\mathrm{d}U-\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial F}\right)\mathrm{d}\lambda \right)$

Thus one can write:

$\mathrm{d}F=\left({K}_{élas}^{tan}-{K}_{élas}^{tan}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial F}\right){S}^{K}{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial F}\right){K}_{élas}^{tan}\right)\mathrm{d}U$

After inversion of ${M}^{K}$ , the program therefore returns the tangent plastic matrix:

${K}^{tan}={K}_{élas}^{tan}-{K}_{élas}^{tan}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial F}\right){S}^{K}{}^{T}\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial F}\right){K}_{élas}^{tan}$

## 3 Summary of the parameters of the constitutive law

The parameters of the constitutive law are entered as data under the keyword FONDA_SUPERFI of the command DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01):

- LONG_X: Corresponds to the length $>$ along the local axis x of the foundation (mandatory);
- LONG_Y: Corresponds to the length $>$ along the local axis y of the foundation (mandatory);
- PHI: Corresponds to the friction angle $>$ in degrees at the soil/foundation interface (mandatory);
- COHESION: Corresponds to the cohesion $>$ at the soil/foundation interface (homogeneous to a translational stiffness, optional);
- RAID_GLIS: Corresponds to the stiffness of the sliding hardening $>$ (optional);
- GAMMA_REFE: Parameter $>$ of the kinematic hardening (homogeneous to the inverse of a distance, optional);
- CP_SERVICE: Corresponds to the elastic limit force $>$ of the foundation (mandatory);
- CP_ULTIME: Corresponds to the ultimate limit force $>$ of the foundation (optional);
- DEPL_REFE: Reference settlement ${v}_{r,\mathit{CP}}^{pl}$ for the isotropic hardening of the bearing-capacity criterion (optional);
- RAID_CP_X: Corresponds to the stiffness of the kinematic hardening in translation along x of the bearing-capacity criterion $>$ (homogeneous to a translational stiffness, optional);
- GAMMA_CP_X: Parameter $>$ of the kinematic hardening in translation along x of the bearing-capacity criterion (homogeneous to the inverse of a distance, optional);
- RAID_CP_Y, GAMMA_CP_Y: Stiffness $>$ and parameter $>$ of the kinematic hardening in translation along y (optional);
- RAID_CP_RX, GAMMA_CP_RX: Stiffness $>$ and parameter $>$ of the kinematic hardening in rotation about x (optional);
- RAID_CP_RY, GAMMA_CP_RY: Stiffness $>$ and parameter $>$ of the kinematic hardening in rotation about y (optional);
- DECOLLEMENT: Parameter for activation of the uplift mechanism if equal to ‘OUI’. (Optional and by default ‘NON’)

Except for DECOLLEMENT, all the optional parameters are by default equal to 0.

Note: To these parameters is added the initial stiffness matrix associated with the discrete element, which must be defined symmetric and diagonal in the local frame of the discrete element (K_TR_D_N).
