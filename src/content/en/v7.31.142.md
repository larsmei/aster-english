# v7.31.142 WTNV142 - Triaxial undrained test with Mohr-Coulomb law

**Summary**

A triaxial calculation is performed under undrained conditions with Mohr-Coulomb law. Solutions calculated with an HM model are compared to those given by SIMU_POINT_MAT. This test has two modellings:

- 3D_HM_SI (STAT_NON_LINE) Modelling;
- 3D_HM_SI_DIL(STAT_NON_LINE) Modelling;

### 1.2 Material properties

The elastic properties are:

- isotropic compressibility module: $K=516,2\mathit{MPa}$
- shear module: $\mu =238,2\mathit{MPa}$

The hydraulic properties are:

- Biot coefficient: $b=1$
- fluid compressibility module: ${K}_{l}=1000\mathit{GPa}$

The Mohr-Coulomb Law parameters are:

- friction angle: $\varphi =33°$
- Dilatance angle: $\psi =27°$
- cohesion: ${c}_{0}=1\mathit{kPa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

A triaxial test consists of imposing a vertical load variation on the test piece while maintaining the constant lateral pressure. It can be drained (the fluid pore pressure does not vary during the test) or undrained (the valve is closed: the fluid pore pressure changes in the sample).

In the model under consideration, the cubic element represents one eighth of the sample, so the limit conditions are as follows:

- The symmetry conditions:
- ${u}_{z}=0$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{BAS}$ group
- ${u}_{x}=0$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$ group
- ${u}_{y}=0$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{DEVANT}$ group
- Side pressure conditions:
- ${P}_{n}=1$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{COTE}$ group
- The loading conditions:
- ${P}_{n}=1$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{HAUT}$ Group (phase 1)
- ${u}_{z}=-1$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{HAUT}$ Group (Phase 2)

The load is carried out in two phases:

- *Initiation* . Isotropic loading between $t\in \left[-2;0\right]\mathit{secondes}$: the pressure $P$ on the mesh cells $\mathit{COTE}$ and $\mathit{HAUT}$ groups varies from $0$ to $P={P}_{0}=50\mathit{kPa}$ , the isotropic preconsolidation pressure in the intial state;

| *Triaxial test proper*: displacement imposed on mesh-cell group $\mathrm{HAUT}$ with $t$ varying between $t\in \left[0-12\right]\mathit{secondes}$ and ${u}_{z}$ varying between ${u}_{z}\in \left[0;-0,12\right]\mathit{mm}$. The total vertical ${\epsilon}_{zz}$ deformation is $0,012\text{\%}$; | ${\epsilon}_{1}$![Figure](/figures/v7.31.142/Forme1.gif)${\Sigma}_{3}=P\left(t\right)$ ${\Sigma}_{2}=P\left(t\right)$ |

### 1.4 Results

The solutions given by a real coupled hydromechanical calculation are compared to those given by SIMU_POINT_MAT, which solves the following *purely mechanical* problem:

$\Sigma =\sigma +bp=C:\epsilon -\frac{b{K}_{l}}{3}\mathit{trace}\left(\epsilon \right)=P\left(t\right)$

The solutions are post-treated with point $C$ for the terms ${\sigma}_{xx}$ horizontal effective stress, $p$ hydraulic pressure, ${\epsilon}_{v}^{p}$ plastic density deformation and $\mid {\epsilon}_{d}^{p}\mid =\sqrt{\frac{3}{2}\left(\epsilon -\frac{{\epsilon}_{v}^{p}}{3}I\right):\left(\epsilon -\frac{{\epsilon}_{v}^{p}}{3}I\right)}$ plastic deviatoric deformation.

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics

Modelling A is performed in 3D sub-integrated (3D_HM_SI), using STAT_NON_LINE.

The time step is from $\Delta t=0,4\mathit{sec}$ to $t=9,6\mathit{sec}$ (a purely elastic phase), then from $\Delta t=0,025\mathit{sec}$ to $t=12\mathit{sec}$ (a plastic phase). The automatic time step redistribution is activated.
