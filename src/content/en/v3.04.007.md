# v3.04.007 SSLV07 - Stretching a parallelepiped under its own weight

**Summary:**

This static 3D test validates the following features:

- load by own weight (gravity or internal force) and uniform pressure,
- calculation of the potential energy of the structure,
- Residue error estimator (modelizationB)
- elements incompressible (modelizationD).
- use of MACR_LIGN_COUP on a mult_elas concept (modelization A)

It includes 4 models. Its interest lies in the identification of the effect of the Poisson's ratio (cross-sectional contract).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in the SSLV07/89 form of the VPCS guide which presents the calculation method as follows:

Travel:

$\begin{array}{c}u=-\frac{\nu \rho gxz}{E}\\ v=-\frac{\nu \rho gyz}{E}\\ w=\frac{\rho g{z}^{2}}{2E}+\frac{\nu \rho g}{2E}({x}^{2}+{y}^{2})-\frac{\rho g{L}^{2}}{2E}\end{array}$

Constraints:

$\begin{array}{c}{\sigma}_{zz}=\rho gz\\ {\sigma}_{xx}={\sigma}_{yy}={\sigma}_{xy}={\sigma}_{yz}={\sigma}_{\mathit{zx}}=0\end{array}$

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.007/10001E60000011FC000013A345A9480F67F3F1C4.svg)

### 2.2 Baseline

Moving points $B$ , $C$ , $D$ and $E$ .

${\sigma}_{zz}$ constraints in $A$ and $E$ .

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution
