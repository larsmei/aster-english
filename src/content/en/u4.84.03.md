# u4.84.03 Operator POST_FATI_ALEA

## 1 Purpose

Compute the fatigue damage undergone by a structure subjected to a random loading.

To this end POST_FATI_ALEA makes it possible, from the spectral moments which fully characterise the random signal:

- to extract, by a statistical cycle-counting method (stress-peak counting method or level-crossing counting method), the number of elementary loading cycles undergone by the structure,
- to determine the elementary damage associated with each elementary cycle using the Wöhler curve of the material,
- to determine the mean damage over the duration of the signal.

It is also possible to take into account the influence of the elasto-plastic concentration coefficient.

The mean damage is stored in a table of type table_sdaster.

## 2 Syntax

```text
table = POST_FATI_ALEA(
    ◆ / MOMENT_SPEC_0 = float,
    ◇ & MOMENT_SPEC_2 = float,
    ◇ & MOMENT_SPEC_4 = float,
      / TABL_POST_ALEA = table,
    ◆ COMPTAGE = / "PIC",
                 / "NIVEAU",
    ◇ DUREE = float (défaut: 1.0),
    ◇ CORR_KE = "RCCM" (ou non renseigné),
    ◆ DOMMAGE = "WOHLER" (ou non renseigné),
    ◆ MATER = mater,
    ◇ TITRE = text,
)

◆ : obligatoire
◇ : optionnel
⟐ : présent par défaut
& : ensemble
/ : un seul parmi
| : plusieurs choix possibles
```

## 3 Operands

### 3.1 Operands TABL_POST_ALEA / MOMENT_SPEC_0 / MOMENT_SPEC_2 / MOMENT_SPEC_4

These operands make it possible to introduce the value of the three spectral moments of orders 0, 2 and 4, which may have been determined by the command POST_DYNA_ALEA [U4.84.04](doc:u4.84.04).

These values fully characterise the random signal for the statistical cycle-counting methods:

- stress-peak counting method, which uses 0, 2 and 4,
- level-crossing counting method, which requires only 0 and 2.

```text
♦    /    ♦    MOMENT_SPEC_0        =    0    ,                            [R]
♦    MOMENT_SPEC_2        =    2    ,                            [R]
◊    MOMENT_SPEC_4        =    4    ,                            [R]
```

The value of the spectral moment is supplied after the corresponding operand.

```text
♦    /        TABL_POST_ALEA    =    table    ,                        [tabl_post_alea]
```

Makes it possible to specify the name of a table created by POST_DYNA_ALEA [U4.84.04](doc:u4.84.04), in which values of the spectral moments 0, 2, 4) are stored, for various modes or various nodes.

The values are read back and a mean-damage value is computed for each triplet of spectral moments encountered in the table.

However, since the method for computing the mean damage is valid only for loadings homogeneous with stresses, an alarm is issued when the computation of the mean damage does not correspond to power spectral densities homogeneous with stresses (DSP_CONT).

### 3.2 Operand COMPTAGE

```text
♦    COMPTAGE    =
```

In order to compute the damage undergone by a structure, the elementary cycles of the loading history must first be extracted.

/    'PIC'    ,

Makes it possible to choose the stress-peak counting method to determine the elementary cycles of the random loading [R7.04.02](doc:r7.04.02).

/    'NIVEAU'    ,

Makes it possible to choose the level-crossing counting method to determine the elementary cycles of the random loading [R7.04.02](doc:r7.04.02).

### 3.3 Operand DUREE

```text
◊    DUREE            =        /    duree    ,                            [R]
/    1.        ,                            [DEFAUT]
```

Makes it possible to introduce the duration of the signal, which appears in the expression of the mean damage [R7.04.02](doc:r7.04.02).

### 3.4 Operand CORR_KE

```text
◊    CORR_KE    = 'RCCM'    ,
```

This operand makes it possible to take into account an elasto-plastic concentration coefficient ${K}_{e}$ , which is defined by the RCC-M as the ratio of the real strain amplitude to the strain amplitude determined by an elastic analysis.

$\lbrace \begin{array}{ccccccccc}{K}_{e}& =& 1& \text{si}& & & \Delta \sigma & \text{<}& 3{S}_{m}\\ {K}_{e}& =& 1+\frac{(1-n)(\Delta \sigma /3{S}_{m}-1)}{n(m-1)}& \text{si}& 3{S}_{m}& \text{<}& \Delta \sigma & \text{<}& 3m{S}_{m}\\ {K}_{e}& =& 1& \text{si}& 3m{S}_{m}& \text{<}& \Delta \sigma & & \end{array}$

where ${S}_{m}$ is the maximum allowable stress, and $n$ and $m$ are two constants depending on the material.

The values of ${S}_{m}$ , $n$ and $m$ are introduced in the operator DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01) under the keyword FATIGUE and the operands SM_KE_RCCM, N_KE_RCCM and M_KE_RCCM.

### 3.5 Operand DOMMAGE

```text
♦    DOMMAGE = 'WOHLER'    ,
```

This operand makes it possible to specify the damage-computation method, which, for a random loading, is the Wöhler method.

To compute the damage, the user must introduce in the operator DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01) the Wöhler curve of the material, which may be given in three distinct mathematical forms [R7.04.02](doc:r7.04.02):

- function discretised point by point (keyword FATIGUE, operand WOHLER),
- analytical Basquin form (keyword FATIGUE, operands A_BASQUIN and BETA_BASQUIN),
- “current-zone” form (keyword FATIGUE, operands E_REFE, A0, A1, A2, A3 and SL, and keyword ELAS, operand E).

**Note on the fatigue curves:**

*For small amplitudes, the problem of extending the fatigue curve may arise: for example, for the RCC-M fatigue curves beyond 106 cycles, the corresponding stress, 180 MPa, is regarded as an endurance limit, that is, any stress lower than 180 MPa must produce a zero usage factor or an infinite allowable number of cycles.*

*The method adopted here corresponds to this notion of endurance limit: if the stress amplitude is lower than the first abscissa of the fatigue curve, a zero usage factor is taken, that is, an infinite allowable number of cycles.*

### 3.6 Operand MATER

```text
♦    MATER = mater ,
```

Makes it possible to specify the name of the material mater created by DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01).

The material mater must contain at least the definition of the Wöhler curve of the material [R7.04.02](doc:r7.04.02).

If one wishes to take into account an elasto-plastic concentration coefficient ${K}_{e}$ , the material data (N, M and SM) required for the computation of ${K}_{e}$ must also have been specified.

### 3.7 Operand TITRE

```text
◊    TITRE= titre ,
```

Title associated with the table.

### 3.8 Table produced

The operator POST_FATI_ALEA creates a table, tabl_post_f_alea, which comprises 1 parameter: DOMMAGE: value of the mean damage over the duration of the signal.

**Note:**

*In the case where the operand* *TABL_POST_ALEA has been used to introduce the values of the spectral moments, the value of the mean damage over the duration of the signal is stored in the table, for each triplet of spectral moments present in the table.*

The command IMPR_TABLE [U4.91.03](doc:u4.91.03) makes it possible to print the table produced.

## 4 Example


```text
mate = DEFI_MATERIAU    (
FATIGUE = _F    (A_BASQUIN        = 1.001730939 E–14 ,
BETA_BASQUIN        = 4.065                    )
)

table = POST_FATI_ALEA    (
MOMENT_SPEC_0    =    182.5984664        ,
MOMENT_SPEC_2    =    96098024.76        ,
MOMENT_SPEC_4    =    6.346193569E+13    ,
COMPTAGE        =    'PIC'                ,
DOMMAGE        =    'WOHLER'            ,
MATER            =    mate                ,
)
```
