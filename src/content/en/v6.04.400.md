# v6.04.400 SSNV400 – constitutive law BETON_RAG Check: Damageable mechanical

**Summary:**

These tests validate the constitutive law BETON_RAG on a strained cube in traction or compression, with a radial loading path. The model's response is compared to solutions obtained by analytically resolving the equations that govern the [R7.01.26](doc:r7.01.26) behavior.

Four modellings are proposed:

- Modelization a: stress test in strain imposed, on a cube free to deform in the direction perpendicular to the load.
- Modelization b: stress test in strain imposed on a cube free to deform in the direction perpendicular to the load.

- Modelization c: Tensile test in strain imposed, on a confined cube (blocked transverse deformations).
- Modelling d: stress-deficit test imposed on a confined cube (blocked transverse deformations).
