# v2.03.122 SDLS122 - Modal analysis of a plate pressed on its corners – Under structuring

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to validate the CALC_CORR_SSD command and present a typical example of its use. Here we compare the first 5 eigenfrequencies of a rectangular steel plate of $\mathrm{1m}\times \mathrm{2m}\times \mathrm{1cm}$ based on its 4 corners. To compare, we consider on the one hand a complete mesh of the rectangular plate, and on the other two square meshes, of different finesses. A first model is constructed generalized by a method of substructure, then we evaluate the quality of the results provided by this model by means of the CALC_CORR_SSD command. The calculated enrichment terms are then used to construct a new generalized model, and to verify the relevance of the enrichment.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A rectangular steel plate of $\mathrm{2m}\times \mathrm{1m}\times \mathrm{1cm}$ is considered.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=210000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The plate is simply supported on its 4 corners: the translations are blocked, the rotations are free.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Not applicable for modal analysis.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

Here, a finite element model of the rectangular plate is used to construct the reference solution. This Modelling is based on square-hulled elements. The plate is discretized using $20\times 40$ elements.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The first 5 frequencies of the plate supported are considered as follows:

- $5,806\mathit{Hz}$
- $17,175\mathit{Hz}$
- $20,516\mathit{Hz}$
- $32,422\mathit{Hz}$
- $39,845\mathit{Hz}$

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

The proposed solution is slightly dependent on the choices made and the evolution of the solvers.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

DKT Modelling is used on the basis of squared d-elements QUAD4. The calculation method is based on dynamic substructure, using fixed interface modes, and interface modes. Before enrichment, each elements macro is built using

- 1 dynamic mode with fixed interface
- 6 interface modes

For enrichment, we add

- 4 interface modes
- 6 modes with fixed interface

Details of the methods of calculation of these enrichments are given in the U2 documentation.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh of the first plate contains 15x15 elements of type QUAD4.

The mesh of the first plate contains 14x14 elements of type QUAD4.

## 4 Summary of the results

The test case shows the use and relevance of the CALC_CORR_SSD command. Starting from a rough generalized model, it is shown that the enrichment terms offered by CALC_CORR_SSD significantly improve the quality of the reduced model. Starting from a model with up to 70% error, iteration results in less than 3% error.
