# v1.01.115 ZZZZ115 - Validation of the CALC_COUPURE command

**Summary:**

This test case is used to validate the CALC_COUPURE command.

For more information see U4.81.24

> Contents

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry and mesh

A rectangular plate is considered at the L=12 m and H=3 m height. L-thickness is B=0.3 m. The mesh is set, each element is square on the 0.3 m side. The geometry in the Figure is tested, but also the same geometry that has rotated 150°.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.115/1000020100000504000001BA5CE7484B2ED0BB4D.png)

Figure 1.1-1: Géométrie et maillage de la plaque testée.

### 1.2 Cuts made

Three cuts are made:

- The first at the clamped support level and is expressed in the global benchmark.
- The second at 3m to the right of the boundary condition and is expressed in the global marker.
- The third is identical to the first but is expressed in the local reference as described in the figure above.

The torsos are evaluated at the middle of the cut.

### 1.3 Material properties

The plate is made of concrete, characterised by the following elastic properties:

- Young's modulus: 30 GPA
- Poisson's ratio: 0,2

The calculation is linear elastic.

### 1.4 modellings

CALC_COUPURE applies to plate elements. Modelling of type DKT (modelization A) and type DST (modelization B) will be used.

### 1.5 Boundary conditions and loads

The plate is loaded statically or dynamically (spectral method) according to the model.

#### 1.5.1 Static loadings

The plate is clamped on the left side.

Two loads are tested:

- **CH1**: shear on the right side of the plate and normal force. Both are applied as forces distributed on the right side of the plate.
- **CH2**: Off-plane shear applied as a load distributed on the right side of the plate.

The forces applied are:

- ${F}_{X}=100\mathit{kN}$
- ${F}_{Y}=6000\mathit{kN}$
- ${F}_{Z}=600\mathit{kN}$

#### 1.5.2 Dynamic loadings

The plate is in simple support on the left and right sides.

A constant response spectrum is provided with reduced frequency and damping.

The acceleration spectrum is 5000 mm/s in the x direction.

### 1.6 Quantities and reference results

The quantities extracted by the CALC_COUPURE command is tested for the 3 cuts performed. The tests are analytical.

#### 1.6.1 Static loadings

If the plate is represented as an clamped beam at its end, it is simple to calculate the internal forces in the static case.

The results shall be looked at at the $d$ distance from the clamped support, for a cut parallel to the right and left sides. In the case where the torso is expressed in the global marker, the analytical results shall be:

**CH** 1:

- ${R}_{1}=0$
- ${R}_{2}={F}_{Y}$
- ${R}_{3}={F}_{Z}$
- ${M}_{1}={F}_{Z}(L-d)$
- ${M}_{2}={M}_{3}=0$

**CH** 2 :

- ${R}_{1}={F}_{X}$
- ${R}_{2}={R}_{3}={M}_{1}={M}_{2}={M}_{3}=0$

In the local benchmark, the results are:

**CH** 1:

- ${R}_{1}={F}_{Y}$
- ${R}_{2}={F}_{Z}$
- ${R}_{3}=0$
- ${M}_{1}={M}_{2}=0$
- ${M}_{3}={F}_{Z}(L-d)$

**CH** 2 :

- ${R}_{3}={F}_{X}$
- ${R}_{1}={R}_{2}={M}_{1}={M}_{2}={M}_{3}=0$

#### 1.6.2 Dynamic loadings

The acceleration response spectrum refers to an earthquake in the X direction, normal at the plate plane, which is therefore required to flex and shear out of the plane.

The objective of the tests carried out is to check the proper functioning of the modal combinations. Several configurations are studied:

- Calculation of the torso associated with the modal contribution of the first mode,
- Calculation of the torso taking into account the contribution of the first 2 modes (combination CQC).
- Signature of the contribution of the first modal on unit acceleration.
- Calculation of the quadratic combination of the contribution of the 3 earthquake directions,
- Calculation of Newmark combinations related to the 3 earthquake directions.

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Type of finite element

Elements DKT will be used for this modelling.

### 2.2 Boundary conditions and loading

The static loads are those described in paragraph 1.5.1.

### 2.3 Quantities and reference results

The analytical results are checked with an accuracy of less than ${10}^{-5}$, except for certain theoretically zero moments for which a tolerance of 1N.m, corresponding to a lever arm shift of less than 1mm is accepted.

## 3 Modelling B

### 3.1 Type of finite element

Elements DST will be used for this modelling. The geometry is the same as model A but has been rotated by 150°.

### 3.2 Boundary conditions and loading

The static loads are those described in § 1.5.1 , after 150° rotation.

### 3.3 Quantities and reference results

The analytical results are checked with an accuracy of less than ${10}^{-5}$, except for certain theoretically zero moments for which a tolerance of 1N.m, corresponding to a lever arm shift of less than 1mm is accepted.

## 4 Modelling C

### 4.1 Type of finite element

Elements DKT will be used for this modelling.

### 4.2 Boundary conditions and loading

The seismic loads with response spectrum are those described in § 1.5.2.

### 4.3 Quantities and reference results

The analytical results are verified with an accuracy less than the ${10}^{-2}$.
