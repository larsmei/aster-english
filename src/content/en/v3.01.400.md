# v3.01.400 SSLL400 - Variable section beam, subject to one-off or distributed forces

**Summary:**

This test is based on the independent validation of version 4 of the beam models.

This test allows the verification of straight beams calculations in the static linear domain.(modelling with elements beams POU_D_E, straight beam d的EULER).

At the same time, three beams are calculated from different sections: circle section, rectangle section, and general section. These beams are subjected to one-off or distributed forces.

The values tested are displacements and rotations, generalized forces, and stresses.

## 2 Reference solutions

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solutions

#### 2.1.1 Circular section

##### 2.1.1.1 $\mathit{Fx}$ point-driven beam

The equilibrium equation is:

$\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\mathrm{EA}(x)\frac{\partial u}{\mathrm{dx}})=0$ with $A(x)={A}_{1}(1+c\frac{x}{L})$ and $c=\sqrt{\frac{{A}_{2}}{{A}_{1}}}-1$ , $N(L)={F}_{x}$

By integrating [R3.08.01](doc:r3.08.01) twice, we obtain the displacements according to the applied force, i.e.:

$u(x)=\frac{{\mathrm{LF}}_{x}}{{\mathrm{EA}}_{1}}(\frac{x}{L+\mathrm{cx}})$

and thus at the end of the beam L:

$u(L)=\frac{L}{E\sqrt{{A}_{1}{A}_{2}}}{F}_{x}$

Internal efforts are being made by:

$N(x)=\mathrm{EA}(x)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(x)={F}_{x}$

and constraints by:

${\sigma}_{xx}=\frac{N(x)}{A(x)}$

##### 2.1.1.2 Beam subjected to a punctual bending force $\mathit{Fy}$

The equilibrium equation, under the hypothesis of Euler, is given by the equation:

$\frac{{\partial}^{2}}{\partial {x}^{2}}\left[{\mathrm{EI}}_{z}(x)\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {x}^{2}}\right]=0$ with ${I}_{z}(x)={I}_{{z}_{1}}{(1+c\frac{x}{L})}^{4}$ and $c={(\frac{{I}_{{z}_{2}}}{{I}_{{z}_{1}}})}^{\frac{1}{4}}-1$ , ${V}_{y}(L)={F}_{y}$

We resolve the integration equation by taking into account the constitutive law modified ${\mathrm{MF}}_{z}={\mathrm{EI}}_{z}\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {x}^{2}}$ and the $\frac{\partial {\mathrm{MF}}_{z}}{\partial x}+{V}_{y}=0$ equilibrium equation

The four successive integrations, taking into account for the calculation of the integration constants that:

$\begin{array}{}\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left[{\mathrm{EI}}_{z}(x)\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {x}^{2}}\right](L)=-{V}_{y}(L)=-{F}_{y}\\ ({\mathrm{EI}}_{z}(L)\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {x}^{2}})(L)=0\\ \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}(0)=0\\ v(0)=0\end{array}$

lead to the expression:

$v(x)=+\frac{{F}_{y}{L}^{2}}{6E{I}_{{z}_{1}}}\frac{{x}^{2}(3L-x+2\mathrm{cx})}{{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{2}}$

and expression of ${\theta}_{z}(x)$

${\theta}_{z}(x)=+\frac{{F}_{y}\mathrm{L²}}{6E{I}_{{z}_{1}}}\frac{x(6\mathrm{L²}-3\mathrm{Lx}+6L\mathrm{cx}-\mathrm{cx²}+2\mathrm{c²}\mathrm{x²})}{{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{3}}$

Internal efforts are being made by:

$\begin{array}{}{V}_{y}(x)={F}_{y}\\ {\mathrm{MF}}_{z}(x)={F}_{y}(L-x)\end{array}$

and constraints by:

${\sigma}_{xx}(x)=∣{\mathrm{MF}}_{z}(x)∣\frac{R(x)}{{I}_{z}(x)}$

${\sigma}_{xy}=\frac{{V}_{y}(x)}{A(x)}$ no shear correction coefficient in the hypothesis d

##### 2.1.1.3 Beam subjected to a point twist moment $\mathrm{Mx}$

The movement is given by the equation:

$\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left[G{I}_{p}(x)\frac{\partial {\theta}_{x}}{\partial x}\right]=0$ with ${I}_{p}(x)={I}_{{p}_{1}}{(1+c\frac{x}{L})}^{4}$ and $c={(\frac{{I}_{{p}_{2}}}{{I}_{{p}_{1}}})}^{\frac{1}{4}}-1$ , ${M}_{x}(L)={M}_{x}$

After integration, and taking into account that:

$G{I}_{p}(L)=\frac{\partial {\theta}_{x}}{\partial x}(L)={M}_{x}$ , et ${\theta}_{x}(0)=0$

we get the expression of ${\theta}_{x}(x)$:

${\theta}_{x}(x)=\frac{L{M}_{x}}{3G{I}_{{p}_{1}}}\frac{x(3{L}^{2}+3L\mathrm{cx}+\mathrm{c²}\mathrm{x²})}{{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{3}}$

We must also have for internal efforts and constraints:

$\begin{array}{}{M}_{x}(x)={M}_{x}\\ {\sigma}_{xy}(x)=\frac{{M}_{x}(x)}{{I}_{p}(x)}{R}_{T}(x)\\ {\sigma}_{xz}(x)=\frac{{M}_{x}(x)}{{I}_{p}(x)}{R}_{T}(x)\end{array}$

##### 2.1.1.4 Bending moment-pointed beam $\mathit{My}$

The reasoning for finding the analytical solution is the same as before. We use the constitutive law ${M}_{y}(x)=-{\mathrm{EI}}_{y}(x)\frac{{\partial}^{2}w}{\partial {x}^{2}}$ and the $\frac{\partial {\mathrm{MF}}_{y}}{\partial x}-{V}_{z}=0$ equilibrium equation The calculation of the integration constants differs: we have ${V}_{z}(L)=0$ and ${M}_{{F}_{y}}(L)={M}_{y}$.

The expression of $w(x)$ is obtained:

$w(x)=\frac{L{M}_{y}}{6E{I}_{{y}_{1}}}\frac{\mathrm{x²}(3L+2\mathrm{cx})}{{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{2}}$ ,

and expression of ${\theta}_{y}(x)$:

${\sigma}_{y}(x)=\frac{L{M}_{y}}{3E{I}_{\mathrm{y1}}}\frac{x(3{L}^{2}+3L\mathrm{cx}+{c}^{2}{x}^{2})}{{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{3}}$

Internal efforts and constraints must also be addressed:

$\begin{array}{}{V}_{z}(x)=0\\ {\mathrm{MF}}_{y}(x)=\mathrm{My}\\ {\sigma}_{xx}(x)=∣{\mathrm{MF}}_{y}(x)∣\frac{R(x)}{{I}_{y}(x)}\end{array}$

##### 2.1.1.5 $\mathrm{fx}$ constant-distributed tensile force beam

The equilibrium is described by the equation

$\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\mathrm{EA}(x)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x})=-{f}_{x}$ with $A(x)={A}_{1}{(1+c\frac{x}{L})}^{2}$ and $c={(\frac{{A}_{2}}{{A}_{1}})}^{\frac{1}{2}}-1$

By incorporating this equation for the first time, we obtain:

$EA(x)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=-{f}_{x}x+{c}_{1}$

The $N(L)=0$ boundary condition involves ${c}_{1}={f}_{x}L$. We have:

$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=-{f}_{x}\frac{(L-x)}{EA(x)}$

either:

$u(x)={f}_{x}\int\frac{(L-x)}{EA(x)}\mathrm{dx}+{c}_{2}$

${c}_{2}$ is determined for $u(0)=0$

All calculations made, we have:

$u(x)=\frac{{L}^{2}{f}_{x}}{E{A}_{1}{c}^{2}}\frac{cx+{c}^{2}x+(L+cx)\log\frac{L}{L+cx}}{L+cx}$ .

Internal forces are derived from the constitutive law $N(x)=EA(x)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}$:

$N(x)={f}_{x}(L-x)$

and constraints are given by:

${\sigma}_{xx}(x)=\frac{N(x)}{A(x)}=\frac{{f}_{x}(L-x)}{{\left[\sqrt{{A}_{1}}+(\sqrt{{A}_{2}-\sqrt{{A}_{1}}})\frac{x}{L}\right]}^{2}}$

##### 2.1.1.6 Beam subjected to a flexive force distributed consistently $\mathit{fy}$

From the equilibrium equation:

$\frac{{\partial}^{2}}{\partial {x}^{2}}\left[E{I}_{z}(x)\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {x}^{2}}\right]=-{f}_{y}$ with ${I}_{z}(x)={I}_{{z}_{1}}{(1+c\frac{x}{L})}^{4}$ and $c={(\frac{{I}_{{z}_{2}}}{{I}_{{z}_{1}}})}^{\frac{1}{4}}-1$

we perform four successive integrations. The determination of the integration constants is made from the following boundary conditions:

$\begin{array}{}{V}_{y}(L)=0\\ {M}_{z}(L)=0\\ \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}(0)=0\\ v(0)=0\end{array}$

The analytical expression for $v(x)$ and $\theta (z)$ in the presence of a distributed load is, any calculation made:

$\begin{array}{}v(x)=\frac{-{f}_{y}{L}^{3}}{12{\mathrm{EI}}_{{z}_{1}}{c}^{4}{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{2}}\left[\begin{array}{c}-6{L}^{2}\mathrm{cx}+{x}^{2}(-9{\mathrm{Lc}}^{2}-3{\mathrm{Lc}}^{4})+{x}^{3}(-2{c}^{3}+2{c}^{4}-2{c}^{5})\\ +\log(1+c\frac{x}{L})(6{L}^{3}+12{L}^{2}\mathrm{cx}+6L{c}^{2}{x}^{2})\end{array}\right]\\ {\theta}_{z}(x)=\frac{+{L}^{3}{f}_{y}x}{6E{I}_{{z}_{1}}{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{3}}\left[3{L}^{2}-3\mathrm{Lx}+3\mathrm{Lcx}+{x}^{2}(1-c+{c}^{2})\right]\end{array}$

Internal efforts are being made by:

${V}_{y}(x)={f}_{y}(L-x)$ et ${\mathrm{Mf}}_{z}(x)=\frac{1}{2}{f}_{y}{(L-x)}^{2}$

constraints by:

$\begin{array}{}{\sigma}_{xy}(x)=\frac{{V}_{y}(x)}{A(x)}\\ {\sigma}_{xx}(x)=∣{\mathrm{Mf}}_{z}(x)∣\frac{R(x)}{{I}_{z}(x)}\end{array}$

#### 2.1.2 Rectangular section

##### 2.1.2.1 $\mathit{Fx}$ point-driven beam

The equilibrium equation is:

$\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\mathrm{EA}(x)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x})=0$ with $A(x)={A}_{1}+({A}_{2}-{A}_{1})\frac{x}{L}$ , $N(L)={F}_{x}$

By incorporating twice, and taking into account the fact that:

$EA(L)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}(L)={F}_{x}$ , $u(0)=0$

For the determination of the integration constants, we obtain the analytical expression of $u(x)$ , i.e.:

$u(x)=\frac{{F}_{x}L}{{A}_{1}\mathrm{Ec}}\log(1+c\frac{x}{L})$

For internal efforts and constraints, we have:

$\begin{array}{}N(x)={F}_{x}\\ {\sigma}_{xx}=\frac{N(x)}{A(x)}\end{array}$

##### 2.1.2.2 Beam subjected to a punctual bending force $\mathit{Fy}$

The movement is given by the equation:

$\frac{{\partial}^{2}}{\partial {x}^{2}}\left[E{I}_{z}(x)\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {x}^{2}}\right]=0$ with ${I}_{z}(x)={I}_{{z}_{1}}{(1+c\frac{x}{L})}^{3}$ and $c={(\frac{{I}_{{z}_{2}}}{{I}_{{z}_{1}}})}^{\frac{1}{3}}-1$ , ${V}_{y}(L)={F}_{y}$

The same reasoning as for the circular section leads to the following result:

$v(x)=-\frac{{F}_{y}{L}^{2}}{2E{I}_{{z}_{1}}{c}^{3}}\frac{\left[2\mathrm{Lcx}+{c}^{2}{x}^{2}-{c}^{3}{x}^{2}+2L(L+\mathrm{cx})\log(\frac{L}{L+\mathrm{cx}})\right]}{(L+\mathrm{cx})}$

${\theta}_{z}(x)=\frac{{F}_{y}{L}^{2}}{2{\mathrm{EI}}_{{z}_{1}}}\frac{x(2L-x+\mathrm{cx})}{{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{2}}$

We must have for internal efforts and constraints:

$\begin{array}{}{V}_{y}(x)={F}_{y}\\ \\ {M}_{{F}_{z}}(x)={F}_{y}(L-x)\\ \\ {\sigma}_{xx}(x)=\frac{{H}_{y}(x){M}_{{F}_{x}}(x)}{{\mathrm{2I}}_{z}(x)}\\ \\ {\sigma}_{xy}(x)=\frac{{V}_{y}(x)}{A(x)}\end{array}$

##### 2.1.2.3 Beam subjected to a point twist moment $\mathit{Mx}$

The movement is given by the equation:

$\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left[G{I}_{p}(x)\frac{\partial {\theta}_{x}}{\partial x}\right]=0$ with ${I}_{p}(x)={I}_{{p}_{1}}{(1+c\frac{x}{L})}^{3}$ and $c={(\frac{{I}_{{p}_{2}}}{{I}_{{p}_{1}}})}^{\frac{1}{3}}-1$ , ${M}_{x}(L)={M}_{x}$

By the same reasoning as the circular beam, we obtain the analytical expression of ${\theta}_{x}(x)$:

${\theta}_{x}(x)=\frac{L{M}_{x}x(2L+\mathrm{cx})}{2{I}_{{p}_{1}}G{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{2}}$

${I}_{{p}_{1}}$ and ${I}_{{p}_{2}}$ are calculated according to the formulae given in the [R3.08.01](doc:r3.08.01) reference documentation.

Internal efforts and constraints are given by:

$\begin{array}{}{M}_{x}(x)={M}_{x}\\ {\sigma}_{xy}(x)=\frac{{M}_{x}(x)}{{I}_{p}(x)}{R}_{T}(x)={\sigma}_{xz}\end{array}$

##### 2.1.2.4 Bending moment-pointed beam $\mathit{My}$

The same reasoning is used as before, and the following analytical expressions are obtained for $w(x)$ and ${\theta}_{y}(x)$:

$\begin{array}{}w(x)=-\frac{L{M}_{y}{x}^{2}}{2{\mathrm{EI}}_{{y}_{1}}(L+\mathrm{cx})}\\ \\ {\theta}_{y}(x)=\frac{L{M}_{y}x(2L+\mathrm{cx})}{2E{I}_{{y}_{1}}{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{2}}\end{array}$

for the efforts:

$\begin{array}{}{V}_{z}(x)=0\\ {\mathrm{MF}}_{y}(x)={M}_{y}\end{array}$

and constraints:

${\sigma}_{xx}(x)=\frac{{H}_{z}(x){\mathrm{MF}}_{y}(x)}{2{I}_{y}(x)}$

##### 2.1.2.5 $\mathrm{Fx}$ constant-distributed tensile force beam

The equilibrium equation is:

$\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left[\mathrm{EA}(x)\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right]=-{f}_{x}$ with $A(x)={A}_{1}(1+c\frac{x}{L})$ and $c=(\frac{{A}_{2}}{{A}_{1}})-1$ .

After two integrations and taking into account the fact that $N(L)=0$ for determining the first d-integration constant, and $u(0)=0$ for determining the second, we obtain the analytical expression of $u(x)$:

$u(x)=\frac{-L{f}_{x}}{E{A}_{1}{c}^{2}}\left[cx+(L+{L}_{c})\log(\frac{L}{L+cx})\right]$

Internal efforts are known by the following expression:

$N(x)={f}_{x}(L-x)$

and constraints by:

${\sigma}_{xx}(x)=\frac{{f}_{x}(L-x)}{A(x)}$

##### 2.1.2.6 Beam subjected to a flexive force distributed consistently $\mathrm{Fy}$

The equilibrium equation is:

$\frac{{\partial}^{2}}{\partial {x}^{2}}\left[{\mathrm{EI}}_{z}(x)\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {x}^{2}}\right]=-{f}_{y}$ with ${I}_{z}(x)={I}_{{z}_{1}}{(1+c\frac{x}{L})}^{3}$ and $c={(\frac{{I}_{{z}_{2}}}{{I}_{{z}_{1}}})}^{\frac{1}{3}}-1$

We integrate this equation four times in succession. The integration constants are calculated taking into account the fact that:

$\begin{array}{}{V}_{y}(L)=0\\ {\mathrm{MF}}_{z}(L)=0\\ \frac{\partial v}{\partial x}(0)=0\\ v(0)=0\end{array}$

The analytical result for deflection and $L$ rotation is as follows:

$\begin{array}{}v(x)=\frac{{L}^{3}{f}_{y}}{4E{I}_{{z}_{1}}{c}^{4}(L+cx)}\left[x(6\mathrm{Lc}+4{\mathrm{Lc}}^{2})+{x}^{2}({\mathrm{5c}}^{2}+{\mathrm{2c}}^{3}-{c}^{4})\right]\\ +({\mathrm{6L}}^{2}+{\mathrm{4L}}^{2}c+\mathrm{8Lcx}+4{\mathrm{Lc}}^{2}x+{\mathrm{2c}}^{2}{x}^{2})\log(\frac{L}{L+\mathrm{cx}})\end{array}$

$\begin{array}{}{\theta}_{z}(x)=\frac{{L}^{3}{f}_{y}}{4{\mathrm{EI}}_{{z}_{1}}{c}^{3}{(L+\mathrm{cx})}^{2}}\left[x(2\mathrm{Lc}+2{\mathrm{Lc}}^{3})+{x}^{2}(3{c}^{2}+2{c}^{3}-{c}^{4})\right]\\ +(2{L}^{2}+\mathrm{Lcx}+2{c}^{2}{x}^{2})\log(\frac{L}{L+\mathrm{cx}})\end{array}$

Internal efforts are expressed by:

${V}_{y}(x)={f}_{y}(L-x)$ , ${\mathrm{Mf}}_{z}(x)=\frac{1}{2}{f}_{y}{(L-x)}^{2}$

constraints by:

$\begin{array}{}{\sigma}_{xy}(x)=\frac{{V}_{y}(x)}{A(x)}\\ {\sigma}_{xx}(x)=∣\frac{{\mathrm{Mf}}_{z}(x){h}_{y}}{{I}_{z}(x)2}∣\end{array}$

#### 2.1.3 General Section

##### 2.1.3.1 Beam subjected to gravity forces

The forces of gravity are applied according to the $z$ axis. The beam movement induced by these forces is therefore a bending movement in the $(xoz)$ plane.

The equilibrium equation is given by the expression:

$\frac{{\partial}^{2}}{\partial {x}^{2}}(E{I}_{y}(x)\frac{{\partial}^{2}w}{\partial {x}^{2}})=\underset{\mathrm{poids}\mathrm{linéique}}{\rho A(x)g}$

with $A(x)={A}_{1}{(1+c\frac{x}{L})}^{2}$ , $c={(\frac{{A}_{2}}{{A}_{1}})}^{\frac{1}{2}}-1$ and ${I}_{y}(x)={I}_{\mathrm{y1}}{(1+d\frac{x}{L})}^{4}$ $d={(\frac{{I}_{\mathrm{y2}}}{{I}_{\mathrm{y1}}})}^{\frac{1}{4}}-1$

By integrating a first time, we get internal l的shear force:

${V}_{z}(x)=-\int\rho A(x)g\mathrm{dx}+{C}_{1}$

${C}_{1}$ is determined so that ${V}_{z}(L)=0$ .

We get:

${V}_{z}(x)=\frac{L{A}_{1}\rho g}{3c}\left[-{(1+c\frac{x}{L})}^{3}+{(1+c)}^{3}\right]$ .

By integrating a second time, we get the internal bending moment:

${M}_{y}(x)=\int{V}_{z}(x)\mathrm{dx}+{c}_{2}$

${C}_{2}$ is calculated so that ${M}_{y}(L)=0$

We get:

${M}_{y}(x)=\frac{{A}_{1}\rho g}{12{L}^{2}}{(L-x)}^{2}(6{L}^{2}+8{L}^{2}c+3{L}^{2}{c}^{2}+4\mathrm{Lcx}+2\mathrm{Lcx}+\frac{2{\mathrm{Lc}}^{2}x}{+{c}^{2}{x}^{2}})$

We then calculate the rotation from the constitutive law ${M}_{y}(x)=E{I}_{y}(x)\frac{\partial {\theta}_{y}}{\partial x}$

So we have ${\theta}_{y}(x)=\int\frac{{M}_{y}(x)}{E{I}_{y}(x)}\mathrm{dx}+{C}_{3}$ with ${\theta}_{y}(0)=0$

The deflection $w(x)$ is determined from the relationship d

We calculate $w(x)$ by integration of ${\theta}_{y}(x)$: $w(x)=-\int{\theta}_{y}(x)+{C}_{4}$

with ${C}_{4}$ such as $w(0)=0$ .

The analytical expressions of ${\theta}_{y}(x)$ and $w(x)$ are not transcribed here because they are far too heavy. They were calculated, as in previous ones, by the formal MATHEMATICA calculation software.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results confirm that elements POU_D_E with variable section has a good degree of reliability.

For the circular section, all results are accurate to nodes (the properties of the constant section element are found) except for the distributed forces where the effect of the discretization finesse is felt.
