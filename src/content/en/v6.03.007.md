# v6.03.007 SSNP007 - Stability analysis of an undrained slope with low layer by Morgenstern-Price method

**Summary:**

This test case analyses the stability of an undrained slope with a low layer.
safety of slope by Morgenstern-Price method based on the theory of limit d-balance in
using CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command.

The test case is from the article by D.V. Griffiths [1] published in 1999.
Due to the presence of the low layer crossing the slope body, the fracture surface develops
along the low layer and therefore has the typically noncircular form.

**By comparing the result with the reference value, the result of CALC_STAB_PENTE presents an error of 3.2%,
which allows scientific validation of the Morgenstern-Price method and the improved fireworks algorithm (EFWA)
Implemented in CALC_STAB_PENTE.**

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The MSDS is calculated and the noncircular fracture surface is located using
Morgenstern-Price method of CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command.
Knowing that the breaking surface passes through the low layer
si $\lambda ={c}_{2}/{c}_{1}\ll 1$,
the search areas of the points are defined
d-end of the breaking surface as
the extremities of the low layer on the slope profile,
either `X1_MINI = 1,8`, `X1_MAXI = 2,4`, `X2_MINI = 15,6` and `X2_MAXI = 16,2`.

Based on the pre-tests, EFWA algorithm finds the optimum in 10 iterations
So, we're taking a margin with `ITER_MAXI = 30`.

The result of the critical surface divided into 5 slices is shown in Fig3-modelA.

The result of the FS from the CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command gives the difference 3.7%
