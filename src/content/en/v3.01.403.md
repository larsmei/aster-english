# v3.01.403 SSLL403 - Flaming of a beam under the effect of its own weight

**Summary:**

This test allows linear elasticity to validate the load due to gravity forces for straight beam Modelling of Euler (POU_D_E). It also allows the implementation and validation of the geometric stiffness matrix calculation.

The reference solution is analytical and the results are considered satisfactory.

## 2 Reference solutions

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solutions

As a local reference, $x$ along the $\mathit{OA}$ axis of the beam, the bending moment, at the $x$ abscisse, is expressed as follows:

${M}_{{F}_{y}}(x)=p{\int}_{x}^{L}\left[v(\xi )-v(x)\right]d\xi$ .

The deflection $v(x)$ therefore satisfies the equation:

$E{I}_{z}=\frac{{d}^{2}v}{{\mathrm{dx}}^{2}}=p{\int}_{x}^{L}\left[v(\xi )-v(x)\right]d\xi =-p\left[{\int}_{x}^{L}v(\xi )d\xi +(L-x)v(x)\right]$

By drifting the two members, the differential equation is obtained:

$\frac{{d}^{3}v}{{\mathrm{dx}}^{3}}+\frac{p}{E{I}_{z}}(L-x)\frac{\mathrm{dv}}{\mathrm{dx}}=0$

The $v'(x)=\frac{\mathrm{dv}}{\mathrm{dx}}$ function satisfies the linear and homogeneous differential equation of the second order:

$\frac{{d}^{\mathrm{2v}}'}{{\mathrm{dx}}^{2}}+\frac{p}{E{I}_{z}}(L-x)v'=0$ ,

which can be solved using the functions of Bessel. The value of the critical linear weight is then equal to:

${p}_{c}=7,837\frac{E{I}_{z}}{{L}^{3}}$ .

The analytical solution gives numerically:

${p}_{c}=7,8372{10}^{11}\cdot \frac{{10}^{-8}}{12}=1,3061667{10}^{3}$ .

## 4 Summary of the results

The results are very close to the analytical solution (difference: $\text{1\%}$ for 10 elements). This difference is a function of the discretization finesse being given the assumptions used for geometric rigidity (cf.[R3.08.01]). This therefore validates this type of load for the d-Euler flaming.
