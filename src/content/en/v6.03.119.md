# v6.03.119 FORMA20 - mesh mechanical adaptive on a bending beam

**Summary:**

In this test case, it is a matter of ensuring **the non-regression of TP #1 associated with the courses "Indicators of error and adaptation of mesh; State of art and implantation in** **Code_Aster"** of the training "Nonlinear static analysis with *Code_Aster*".

In fact, an elastic **calculation is distorted on a metal bending beam** in flat stress Modelling. It is done **converging uniformly** via the HOMARD refinement-derefining tool encapsulated in MACR_ADAP_MAIL, then **freely** by connecting the process to a localized spatial error map on each finite element.

From the point view of the **computer validation** , this test case allows of course to test the non-regression of different mapping couplings of d-errors/process refinement-derefining in mechanics, but also the options of "pre and post-processings" of these calculations (smoothing of stresses to nodes, switching from an error by element to an error at nodes by element).

Each Modelling is associated with a TP question and the main part of the correction elements** has been recorded. The entire text of the TP is available on the [http://www.code-aster.com/utilisation/formations](http://www.code-aster.com/utilisation/formations) website.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solutions

In such a case, it is not possible to exhume an analytical solution! The reference solution used for error calculations on the deflection and on the potential energy of deformation is in fact an approximate solution obtained after a series of four uniform refinements (on the same mesh but in TRIA6).

This uniform refinement procedure can be controlled by an PYTHON loop and MACR_ADAP_MAIL option UNIFORME operator. The first two modellings are just an illustration of this feature.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The mesh is made with elements type TRIA3. The calculation is made in linear elasticity with STAT_NON_LINE operator.

Spatial error maps of the Zhu-Zienkiewicz indicator version 1 (ERZ1_ELEM) and the indicator are calculated as pure residue (ERME_ELGA). Before calculating the stress field at nodes (SIGM_ELNO) and, to post-treat the error card (via GIBI), it must be converted from CHAM_ELEM by element to CHAM_ELEM at nodes by element. The value of deflection (POST_RELEVE_T) and potential deformation energy (POST_ELEM) is also determined.

The whole is placed in a PYTHON loop allowing the setting up of a uniform refinement procedure in nb_calc=4 levels (via MACR_ADAP_MAIL option UNIFORME=

This can be seen in the convergence of the values of deflection and d-energy, the increase in their relative errors compared to the errors provided by the indicators (the same in relative terms and over the whole structure), the variations in the indicators' efficiency indices and their correct verification of the saturation hypothesis.

To illustrate "good practice" tips for the quality of studies, on the mesh geometry, mesh proper and d-type finished elements, the adhoc options of LIRE_MAILLAGE, MACR_ADAP_MAIL and MACR_INFO_MAIL are used.

### 3.4 What should be retained from this part of the TP...

**MACR_INFO_MAIL is therefore complementary to** **LIRE_MAILLAGE(VERI_MAIL** **and** **INFO) and** **POST_ELEM. Their combined "efforts" can therefore:**

- check the mesh's alignment with the initial geometry (by mass, size, surface and volume),
- list GROUP_MA and GROUP_NO, which are the primary models for good boundary conditions Modelling,
- Diagnosis of possible problems (symeticization or connection, elements d-draft still present in the model, boundary conditions on surfaces or lines of bad dimensions, interpenetration of elements),
- evaluate the quality of mesh d'un point strictly finite element view.

### 4.4 What should be retained from this part of the TP...

Elements ${P}_{1}$ are not recommended in mechanical. **The good practice is rather:** ${P}_{1}$ **heating lamp and** ${P}_{2}$ (possibly sub-integrated) **mechanical** (not to artificially favour the thermal component of the deformation field and try to avoid spatio-temporal oscillations of the temperature field and its violation of the principle of maximum).

The choice of **type d的finite element takes precedence over the quality of the mesh cells** on which this element is based.
