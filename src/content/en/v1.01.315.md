## 1 Test principle

The purpose of the test is to validate the programming of the PROD_MATR_CHAM command for the real matrices (mrmult.f routine).

Three cases of figures are checked for homogeneous boundary conditions *non*:

1. Boundary conditions eliminated (AFFE_CHAR_CINE)
2. Dualised boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA + DDL_IMPO + LIAISON_DDL)
3. mixture between dualised and eliminated boundary conditions

For each case:

- The FACTORISER + RESOUDRE command is resolved by a linear $K\times U=F$ system with known $K$ and $F$.
- The $\mathit{FF}=K\times U$ product is then performed with the PROD_MATR_CHAM command and $\mathit{FF}$ is checked for $F$.

## 2 Validation

When all boundary conditions are dualized, $\mathit{FF}$ is checked to be $F$ on all degrees of freedom.

However, when boundary conditions is eliminated, $\mathit{FF}$ is equal to $F$ only on the undisposed degrees of freedom. On the eliminated degrees of freedom, $\mathit{FF}$ is null.
