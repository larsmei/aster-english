# v6.04.519 SSNV519 - Modelling a creep test with visco-hyper-elastic law (ELAS_HYPER_VISC)

**Summary:**

This test consists of simulating the creep of a single element volume-finished, uniaxially tractioned, with the purpose of validating the constitutive law ELAS_HYPER_VISC.
The results obtained are compared and validated with results provided by the Abaqus software.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a test on a cubic mesh cell of $1$ mm side.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material obeys the visco-hyperelastic constitutive law. The hyperelastic behavior is of the Neo-Hookean type. The visco-elastic behavior is given by a series of Prony order $3$.
The parameters of the law are:

| ${\nu}=0.495$ | Poisson's ratio |
| --- | --- |
| ${\mathrm{C_{10}}}=0.30,{\mathrm{C_{01}}}=\mathrm{C_{02}}=\mathrm{C_{20}}=0$ | Long-term Mooney-Rivlin material parameters, i.e., for infinite behavior (MPa) |
| ${\mathrm{G}}=[1.33, 0.66, 0.33]$ | Long-term shear module list of Prony Series 3 (MPa) |
| ${\tau}=[100, 10000, 1000000]$ | List of values of the relaxation time of the Prony series of order 3 (s) |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Symmetric conditions are imposed on the $3$ orthogonal faces in the $3$ direction of the marker.

An effort load of $3.45$ N is imposed and maintained constant from $1$ s.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The test is based on the results of the Abaqus calculation code. It is noted that the viscous flow in the Abaqus calculation code is modelled by introducing a decomposed coefficient in Prony series on the
The isochore and volumetric parts of the second Piola Kirchoff tensor. In the case of the ELAS_HYPER_VISC law, the viscous scoop only concerns the isochore part of the tensor of the stresses.
This implementation difference should not impact the results of the two codes given the very low compressibility of the material.

### 2.2 Baseline

The equivalent deformation and stress of Von Mises is ensured to correspond well with the values obtained from the calculation to within $1\%$ at two different moments.

### 2.3 Bibliographical references

EC2 Modelling Report, RAP-EC2-02112-2-A.pdf (06/02/24) - Simulation of mechanical behaviour of an elastomer joint - Study of integration of a viscoelastic constitutive law
