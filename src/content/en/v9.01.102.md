# v9.01.102 SZLZ102 - Fatigue with different counting methods

**Summary:**

This test concerns the methods of cycle counting (RAINFLOW, RCCM) from a load history in constraints.

From a simple load history defined by DEFI_FONCTION, the elementary cycles are extracted by the RAINFLOW [R7.04.01](doc:r7.04.01) cycle counting method and then by the RCCM [R7.04.01](doc:r7.04.01) cycle counting method.

The ${K}_{T}$ stress concentration coefficient is also tested.

This example is a validation test of POSTDAM software developed by the REME Department, provided in the Validation Manual of version 1.0 of this software.

The results provided by the POST_FATIGUE operator are quite identical to those provided by POSTDAM software.

## 1 Reference problem

The analysis consists of extracting the elementary cycles from a load-in-contrain history.

- First call to POST_FATIGUE:

The elementary cycles are extracted by the RAINFLOW cycle counting method, on the ${\sigma}_{1}(t)$ load history.

- Second call to POST_FATIGUE:

The elementary cycles are extracted by the RCCM cycle counting method, on the ${\sigma}_{1}(t)$ load history.

- Third call to POST_FATIGUE:

The elementary cycles are extracted by the RAINFLOW cycle counting method, on the ${\sigma}_{2}(t)$ load history, and a ${K}_{T}=2$ stress concentration coefficient is used.

- Fourth call to POST_FATIGUE:

The elementary cycles are extracted by the RCCM cycle counting method, on the ${\sigma}_{2}(t)$ load history, and a ${K}_{T}=2$ stress concentration coefficient is used.
