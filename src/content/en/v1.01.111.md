# v1.01.111 ZZZZ111 - Validation of the DEFI_CABLE_BP operator

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test case is to validate the DEFI_CABLE_BP operator, which calculates the voltage profiles in prestressing cables of a concrete structure, in accordance with BPEL rules: these allow for the loss of tension by contact between cables and concrete, by recoiling at anchors, by removing and creeping concrete and by relaxing steel, the material constituting the cables.

For modellings A and B, the structure is a semi-cylindrical veil, containing four prestressed cables in its thickness. The cables each describe a half circle in a horizontal plane, and thus travel the sail along its length. Two cables have an eccentricity compared to the average radius of the sail.

The results obtained are validated by comparison with those theoretically expected. The voltage profiles can be explained analytically outside the areas where the losses by recoil apply to the anchors. This calculation is made for the modellings DKT (modelization A) and Q4GG (modelization B).

Modellings C and D can validate the operation of the PROJKM routine in cases where the projection can only be made on an node.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.111/10000000000003F4000001BA94DE6E4356733B05.jpg)

The concrete veil has a semi-cylindrical shape: the height is $H=10m$ and the average radius is $R=\mathrm{10 }m$.

The thickness of the sail is $e=0,6m$. The "equivalent average radius", in the sense of BPEL on the vertical section of the sail is therefore ${r}_{m}=0,283m$ , knowing that:

${r}_{m}=\frac{\mathit{eH}}{2(e+H)}$ (reference BPEL 2.1.5)

The cables each describe a half circle in a horizontal plane, and thus travel the sail along its length. The dimensions of the planes containing the cables are:

- for cable number 1: ${z}_{1}=1m$;
- for cable 2: ${z}_{2}=3,5m$;
- for cable 3: ${z}_{3}=6m$;
- for cable #4: ${z}_{4}=8,5m$ .

Cables 3 and 4 are eccentric to the average beam of the sail, respectively:

- ${\mathrm{ex}}_{3}=0,05m$ ;
- ${\mathrm{ex}}_{4}=0,1m$ .

The right section of each cable is ${S}_{a}=1,5{.10}^{-4}{m}^{2}$.

### 1.3 Loading

A normal tensile force is applied to both ends of each cable. The applied voltage value is ${F}_{0}={2.10}^{5}N$ .

The following relationships are used to assess the loss of tension by relaxation of cables over time:

| $\Delta {\sigma}_{\mathit{pj}}=\Delta {\sigma}_{p}(x)r(j)$ | (reference BPEL 3.3.24) |
| --- | --- |
| $\Delta {\sigma}_{p}=\frac{6}{100}{\rho}_{1000}(\frac{{\sigma}_{pi}(x)}{{f}_{\mathit{prg}}}-{\mu}_{0}){\sigma}_{pi}(x)$ | (reference BPEL 3.3.23) |
| $r(j)=\frac{j}{j+9\times {r}_{m}}$ | (reference BPEL 3.3.24 and 2.1.51) |

| ${\sigma}_{pi}(x)$ | called initial voltage, the point voltage d-abscisse $x$ , after instant voltage loss. |
| --- | --- |
| $j$ | assessment moment, day |
| ${r}_{m}$ | equivalent average radius, $\mathit{cm}$ |

The characteristics are evaluated on the $j=10$ day.

The test results shall be carried out in accordance with the requirements of the Annex to Regulation (EU) No 1303/2013.

**Note:**

*This problem ignores the equilibrium resolution of the complete steel-beton structure and is limited to the determination of prestress in the cables according to BPEL.*

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Curvilinear abscess and cumulative angular deviation

The cables each describe a semi-circle path in a horizontal plane. As a result, the curvilinear abscisse $s$ and the cumulative angular deviation $\alpha$ very simply express:

$\left\{  \begin{array}{c}s={R}_{c}\theta \\ \alpha =\theta \end{array}\right.$

where ${R}_{c}$ is the radius of the half circle described by the cable, and $\theta$ l-azimut is the radius of the half circle described by the cable, and $\theta$ l-azimut is the radius of the half circle described by the cable.

The reference values for the tests are estimated using these expressions.

### 2.2 Normal effort in cables

A cable describing a horizontal half circle of ${R}_{c}$ radius is considered. ${\theta}_{0}$ l-azimut is noted to be the end of the area where the voltage losses by reverse apply to the first anchorage; $\pi -{\theta}_{0}$ is the beginning of the area where the voltage losses by reverse apply to the second anchorage.

Taking into account the previous expressions of curvilinear abscisse and the cumulative angular deviation, the tension profile along the cable can be set by the $\theta$ l-azimut, outside areas where the losses by recoil apply to anchors:

$F(\theta )=-{F}_{0}({x}_{\mathit{flu}}+{x}_{\mathit{ret}})$

$+{F}_{0}\left[1+r(j)\times \frac{5}{100}{\rho}_{1000}\times {\mu}_{0}\right]\exp(-(f+\varphi {R}_{c})\theta )$

$-{F}_{0}\times r(j)\times \frac{5}{100}{\rho}_{1000}\times \frac{{F}_{0}}{{S}_{a}{f}_{\mathit{prg}}}\times \exp(-2(f+\varphi {R}_{c})\theta )$ on the $\left[{\theta}_{0};\frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ interval

$F(\theta )=-{F}_{0}({x}_{\mathit{flu}}+{x}_{\mathit{ret}})$

$+{F}_{0}\left[1+r(j)\times \frac{5}{100}{\rho}_{1000}\times {\mu}_{0}\right]\exp(-(f+\varphi {R}_{c})(\pi -\theta ))$

$-{F}_{0}\times r(j)\times \frac{5}{100}{\rho}_{1000}\times \frac{{F}_{0}}{{S}_{a}{f}_{\mathit{prg}}}\times \exp(-2(f+\varphi {R}_{c})(\pi -\theta ))$ on the $\left[\frac{\pi}{2};\pi -{\theta}_{0}\right]$ interval

The reference values for the tests are estimated using these expressions, which define a voltage profile symmetrical to the central node.

### 2.3 Projection index

The projection of node from one of the cables on a mesh cell of the concrete veil results in the assignment of a $\mathit{IPROJ}$ projection index according to the following rule:

**Projection on mesh cell triangle** nodes $\mathit{N1}$ peaks , $\mathit{N2}$ and $\mathit{N3}$ :

| $\mathit{IPROJ}=0$ | the point projected is within the triangle; |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathit{IPROJ}=11,12$ ou $13$ | if the point projected belongs to the $[\mathit{N1};\mathit{N2}]$ , $[\mathrm{N2};\mathrm{N3}]$ or $[\mathrm{N3};\mathrm{N1}]$ edge respectively; |
| $\mathit{IPROJ}=2$ | if there is coincidence of point projected with a node summit. |

**Projection on mesh cell quadrangle** nodes $\mathit{N1},\mathit{N2}\mathit{N3}$ and $\mathrm{N4}$ peaks:

| $\mathit{IPROJ}=0$ | if the point projected is within the quadrangle; |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathit{IPROJ}=11,12,13$ ou $14$ | if the point projected belongs respectively to the $[\mathrm{N1};\mathrm{N2}]$ , $[\mathrm{N2};\mathrm{N3}]$ , $[\mathrm{N3};\mathrm{N4}]$ or $[\mathrm{N4};\mathrm{N1}]$ edge; |
| $\mathrm{IPROJ}=2$ | if there is coincidence of point projected with a node summit. |

The reference values for the tests are estimated by predicting the locations of the cable projection, taking into account their position with respect to the sail and the arrangement of the mesh cells on the sail.

### 2.4 Excentricity

The eccentricity of an node belonging to one of the cables is defined as the distance from this node to the mesh cell of the concrete veil on which it is projected.

Viewed in a horizontal plane, the mesh cell trace is a rope on the $R$ radius half circle. $\alpha$ is noted as the angular sector covered by the mesh cell. The node of the cable, noted $\mathit{NC}$, is located on the ${R}_{c}$ radius half circle. Its relative position relative to the mesh cell is spotted by the $\beta$ zilimut.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.111/Object_31.svg)

The $(\cos\frac{\alpha}{2};\sin\frac{\alpha}{2})$ vector is normal to the rope, which passes through the point $(R;0)$ .

The equation of the rope is therefore $\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}x+\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}y-R\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}=0$

The distance from point to one right in the plane is given by:

$d=\frac{∣a{x}_{0}+b{x}_{0}+c∣}{\sqrt{{a}^{2}+{b}^{2}}}$

where $({x}_{\mathrm{0 }};{y}_{0})$ is the coordinates of point and $\mathit{ax}+\mathit{by}+c=0$ is the equation of the right.

The node $\mathit{NC}$ of the cable has $({R}_{c}\cos\beta ;{R}_{c}\sin\beta )$ coordinates. Its eccentricity compared to the mesh cell of the sail on which it is projected is therefore worth:

${\mathit{ex}}_{c}=∣{R}_{c}\cos(\frac{\alpha}{2}-\beta )-R\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}∣$

The reference values for the tests are estimated using this expression.

## 3 Modelization A and B

### 3.1 Features of modellings

The concrete veil is represented by elements DKT for Modelling A and elements Q4GG for Modelling B, supported in both cases by mesh cells quadrangles at 4 nodes: there are 10 mesh cells on a vertical generator and 32 on a horizontal half circle. With this arrangement, the mesh cells have dimensions close to those of a square of $1m$ on the side.

All of the following is common to modellings A and B.

$e=0,6m$ thickness is assigned to all mesh cells of the sail, as well as a concrete material for which ELAS and BPEL_BETON behaviors are defined: the parameters take the values given previously in v1.01.111-materiaau-beton-voile.

Each cable is represented by 128 elements MECA_BARRE, supported by mesh cells segments at 2 nodes. On a horizontal half circle, there are 4 times more mesh cells on a cable than on the concrete veil.

A straight section area ${S}_{a}=1,5{.10}^{-4}{m}^{2}$ is assigned to all mesh cells cables, as well as a steel material for which the ELAS and BPEL_ACIER behaviors are defined: the parameters take the values given previously in v1.01.111-material-steel-cables.

The ${F}_{0}={2.10}^{5}N$ voltage is applied to the two nodes ends of each cable. The value of this voltage is consistent with the values of section and elastic limit stress, for twisted prestress cables. The evaluation of the tension losses by relaxation and recoil at anchors is carried out according to the BPEL rules.

Since the same voltage is applied to both ends of each cable, and since the anchors are placed on the same step, the voltage profiles obtained must be symmetrical with respect to the nodes cable media.

Given the geometrical characteristics and the mesh, the nodes of cables 1 and 2 project on the top nodes and edges of the mesh cells of the concrete veil. For the nodes of these two cables, the projection indices obtained must be in accordance with the following sequence: 2 for the first node, then $13-13-13-2$ until the last node.

The nodes of cables 3 and 4 are projected on the edges and inside the mesh cells of the concrete veil. For the nodes of these two cables, the projection indices obtained must be in accordance with the following sequence: 14 for the first node, then $0-0-0-12$ until the last node.

The projection index allocation rule is defined in v1.01.111-projection index.

Based on Mesh characteristics, nodes eccentricities of the cables are evaluated using v1.01.111-eccentricite expression with $\alpha =\frac{\pi}{32}$ and $\beta =0,\frac{\alpha}{4},\frac{\alpha}{2},\frac{3\alpha }{4}$.

### 3.3 Remarks

Relative differences are obtained for the cumulative angular deviation of an order of quantity significantly higher than those obtained for curvilinear abscisse, which is calculated with very good accuracy.

The cause is inherent in the cubic spline interpolation method used to interpolate the cable trajectories. This method consists of interpolating the trajectory over each sub-range by a polynomial of order 3, ensuring continuity of the first and second derivatives at the sub-interval terminals. The interpolation of a circular trajectory by order 3 polynomials is an approximation, the effects of which are more sensitive on the second derivatives than on the first derivatives.

Therefore, the accuracy obtained for the curvilinear abscisse, calculated using the first derivative of the trajectory, is significantly better than that obtained for the cumulative angular deviation, calculated using cross products between the first and second derivatives. To improve the accuracy on the cumulative angular deviation, the mesh should be significantly refined: this approach is not valid a priori, as it would lead to very substantial additional calculation costs for a small gain in accuracy.

The differences by default on the curvilinear abscisse and the cumulative angular deviation induce differences by excess on the calculated voltage values, but the accuracy remains satisfactory: this validates the calculation method.

The projection indices obtained are consistent with those expected, and the eccentricities are calculated with very good accuracy: this validates the projection operations.
