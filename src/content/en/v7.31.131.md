# v7.31.131 WTNV131 - Dispersal of dissolved air in water (3D)

**Summary:**

This is considered a problem at constant temperature and saturation. Appropriate boundary conditionss impose constant water pressure and vapour pressure. A gas pressure is imposed on one edge of the domain (zero flows on the other side). Only the pressures of dry air and dissolved air connected by Henry's law evolve. This problem is reduced to an equation for dry air pressure of the type "heat equation". The reference solution will then be a thermal calculation ASTER. This test case is the extension $\mathrm{3D}$ of the test case WTNP103.

The results are in very good agreement with the semi-analytic solution.

For modellings A and C, the convergence criterion is set to RESI_GLOB_RELA = 1E-14 to minimize the error in the solution (non-regression tests) associated with the mixed formulation of the problem.
