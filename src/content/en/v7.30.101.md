# v7.30.101 WTNL101 – Coupled Saturated THM Problem

**Summary:**

This is a single dimensional problem of saturated THM. Thermal loading is a constant heat flow at one end of the domain. The spread of pressure and temperature in the bar is observed. This test is a test of non-regression.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A one-dimensional problem in Cartesian coordinates is placed, corresponding to a hypothesis of flat deformations in the $y$ direction.

The "structure" considered, is finally a segment of length $L=20m$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

We're heating up a vertical bar.

With ${\lambda}_{T}\frac{\partial T}{\partial x}={\Psi}_{T}$ time independent $t$

Which corresponds to:

- $x=0$: zero displacement, zero hydraulic flow, constant time constant thermal flow ${\Psi}_{T}=100{\mathrm{W.m}}^{-2}$
- $x=L$: zero displacement, zero hydraulic flow, zero thermal flow.

$u(x)=P(x)=0$ $T(x)={T}_{0}=20°C$ everywhere.
