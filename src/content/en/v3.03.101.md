# v3.03.101 SSLS101 - Circular plate placed under uniform pressure

**Summary:**

The case of a circular plate placed on the edge in linear elasticity under 3 loads (clean weight, pressure, constant distributed effort) giving the same deformed.

The first two modellingss are used to evaluate the influence of mesh.

The test consists of 8 modellings (models of Love-Kirchhoff, Mindlin-Reissner and COQUE_3D) plus 3 modellings of "connection" between hulls or between hulls and 3D.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Two reference solutions are available for the calculation of the deformed material, according to the plate theory used:

- Love-Kirchhoff theory, commonly used for so-called "thin" plates, which will be retained for modellings $A$ , $B$ and $E$ ,
- Reissner's theory, including shear effects for so-called "shard" plates, which will be retained for modellings $F$ , $G$ and $H$ .

In all point remote from $r$ from the center of the $r\le R$ plate, we have for the calculation of the deflection:

$w(r)=-P\frac{{R}^{4}}{64D}(1-\frac{{r}^{2}}{{R}^{2}})(1+\frac{{r}^{2}}{{R}^{2}}-\frac{2(3+\nu )}{1+\nu }-\varphi )$ with $D=\frac{E{t}^{3}}{12(1-{\nu}^{2})}$

et $\varphi =0(\text{Love-Kirchhoff})$ ou $\varphi =\frac{16}{5}{(\frac{t}{R})}^{2}\frac{1}{1-\nu }(\text{Reissner})$

For the calculation of moments, the two theories lead to the same expressions:

${M}_{\mathit{rr}}(r)=\frac{{\mathit{PR}}^{2}}{16}(3+\nu )\left[{(\frac{r}{R})}^{2}-1\right]{M}_{\theta \theta }(r)=\frac{{\mathit{PR}}^{2}}{16}(3+\nu )\left[1-\frac{1+3\nu }{3+\nu }{(\frac{r}{R})}^{2}\right]$

In the centre of the plate:

$\begin{array}{c}w(0)=-\frac{{\mathit{PR}}^{4}}{64D}(\frac{5+\nu }{1+\nu })(\text{Love}-\text{Kirchhoff})\text{ou}w(0)=-\frac{{\mathit{PR}}^{4}}{64D}(\frac{5+\nu }{1+\nu }+\varphi )(\text{Reissner})\\ {M}_{\mathit{rr}}(0)={M}_{\theta \theta }(0)=-\frac{{\mathit{PR}}^{2}}{16}(3+\nu )\end{array}$

**Note:**

*Code_Aster calculates the nodes moments of each finite element in the reference reference mark defined by the outside normal and the reference axes defined on the hull (see* *AFFE_CARA_ELEM in the documentation of use).*

*The value of the moment* ${M}_{xx}$ *(or* ${M}_{yy}$ *), extracted from the field* *'EFGE_ELNO', in a node belonging to several finished elements can be considered as the average of the values calculated on the elements which have this node in common. This average can be obtained by the procedure* *POST_RELEVE* [U4.74.03](doc:u4.74.03).

For each node, we have: $({M}_{\text{rr}}+{M}_{\theta \theta })=({M}_{xx}+{M}_{yy})=\text{Sm}$

> for point $O$ ${M}_{xx}={M}_{yy}={M}_{\text{rr}}={M}_{\theta \theta }$
> for $A\mathit{et}D$ ${M}_{xx}={M}_{\text{rr}}\mathit{et}{M}_{yy}={M}_{\theta \theta }$ points
> for $C\mathit{et}E$ ${M}_{xx}={M}_{\theta \theta }\mathit{et}{M}_{yy}={M}_{\mathit{rr}}$ points
> for $B\mathit{et}F$ ${M}_{xx}={M}_{yy}=({M}_{\text{rr}}+{M}_{\theta \theta })/2$ points

In relation to efforts:

- On the pressed edge, there are significant errors (up to 20%) compared to analytical solutions. The error is most marked on $H$ (Q4G) Modelling.
- By refining the mesh of each model, the convergence of effort is observed, i.e. the error tends to 0.
