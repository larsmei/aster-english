# v3.01.118 SSLL118 – clamped beam subject to displacements defined in a local marker

**Summary:**

This test case validates the DDL_POUTRE option of the AFFE_ char_meca command, which allows you to impose defined moves in a local beam-related marker.

The conditions on the degrees of freedom $(\mathrm{dx},\mathrm{dy},\mathrm{dz},\mathrm{drx},\mathrm{dry},\mathrm{drz})$ expressed in the local benchmark are translated into conditions on the degrees of freedom $(\mathrm{DX},\mathrm{DY},\mathrm{DZ},\mathrm{DRX},\mathrm{DRY},\mathrm{DRZ})$ expressed in the global benchmark.

This test case allows for the validation of the fact that imposing a zero displacement in a local marker is correctly translated into the global benchmark.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution uses the following results:

![Figure](/figures/v3.01.118/10000000000001570000007CC194C973192E55E6.png)

$\delta c$ and $\delta b$ represent the displacement at $C$ and $B$ points.

> $\mathrm{EI.}\delta c=\frac{\mathrm{Fc}.L}{3}+\frac{5.\mathrm{Fb}.{L}^{3}}{48}$     [1]
> $\mathrm{EI.}\delta b=\frac{5.\mathrm{Fc}.{L}^{3}}{47}+\frac{\mathrm{Fb.}{L}^{3}}{24}$     [2]

La support reaction $\mathrm{Fa}=-\mathrm{Fc}-\mathrm{Fb}$     [3]

The boundary conditions that are used during validation are: a $C$ displacement: $\delta c$ and a possible $B$ effort: $\mathrm{Fb}$. The resolution of equations [], [], [] give the following expressions:

$\begin{array}{}\delta b=\frac{7.\mathrm{Fb.}{L}^{3}}{768.\mathrm{EI}}+\frac{5.\delta c}{16}\\ \mathrm{Fc}=\frac{3.\delta \mathrm{c.}\mathrm{EI}}{{L}^{3}}-\frac{5.\mathrm{Fb}}{16}\\ \mathrm{Fa}=-\frac{3.\delta \mathrm{c.}\mathrm{EI}}{{L}^{3}}-\frac{\mathrm{11.Fb}}{16}\end{array}$
