# v3.04.162 RCCM14 – POST_RCCM B3200 analysis: sharing groups, passing situations

**Summary:**

This test is an elementary validation test of the POST_RCCM command with the TYPE_RESU_MECA='B3200'.

The analytical solution is simple, and allows testing the post-processing in the sense of the RCC-M. The constraints are not calculated but extracted from tables.

More specifically, the A Modelling allows testing the FATIGUE option for B3200 results with consideration of sharing groups, passing situations, non-combinable situations. Subcycles are also tested for fatigue calculation.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Material properties

The material properties and characteristics of the RCC-M calculation are as follows:

1. Young's modulus: $E=2.E+05\mathit{MPa}$ ;
2. material constants for $\mathrm{Ke}$ calculation: $n=0.2$ , $m=\mathrm{2 }$ ;
3. Young's modulus reference: ${E}_{\mathit{REFE}}=2.E+05\mathit{MPa}$;
4. permissible constraint : $\mathit{Sm}=200\mathit{MPa}$ .

The Wöhler curve is defined analytically: ${N}_{\mathrm{adm}}=\frac{{5.10}^{5}}{{S}_{\mathrm{alt}}}$

### 1.2 Changes in constraints

The stresses on the d-analysis segment are not calculated but read directly in a table. The only non-zero component of the stress tensor is ${\sigma}_{yy}$. Several situations are considered. These situations are not intended to represent a specific real transient, but to cover all possible stresses (constant, linear or nonlinear evolution of the stress in thickness).

* Combination of situations 2 and 3*

Since situation 1 can only be combined with itself, the combination of situations 2 and 3 is calculated.

We're calculating the Sn of the situation.

The game is maximized on moments and pressure, there are 4 possibilities:

- Status A of situation 2 and status A of situation 3.

${\sigma}_{\mathit{LIN},0}=(1-0,4)\ast 37,5+(1-0,4)\ast 37,5+(-1-0)\ast 25+(-1,5--0,6)\ast -25=42,5$

- Status A of situation 2 and status B of situation 3.

${\sigma}_{\mathit{LIN},0}=(1-1)\ast 37,5+(1-1)\ast 37,5+(-1--1)\ast 25+(-1,5--1,5)\ast -25=0$

- Statement B of situation 2 and statement B of situation 3.

${\sigma}_{\mathit{LIN},0}=(10-1)\ast 37,5+(-10-1)\ast 37,5+(1--1)\ast 25+(0,1--1,5)\ast -25=-65$

- Statement B of situation 2 and status A of situation 3.

${\sigma}_{\mathit{LIN},0}=(10-0,4)\ast 37,5+(-10-0,4)\ast 37,5+(1-0)\ast 25+(0,1--0,6)\ast -25=12,5$

In the end we have ${\mathit{Sn}}_{0}^{\mathit{SITUS}2,3}=-65\pm \Vert 50--12,5\Vert =127,5$.

The Sp of the combination is calculated in a similar way.The part is maximized on moments and pressure, there are 4 possibilities:

- Status A of situation 2 and status A of situation 3.

${\sigma}_{\mathit{LIN},0}=(1-0,4)\ast 50+(1-0,4)\ast 50+(-1-0)\ast 0+(-1,5--0,6)\ast 0=60$

- Status A of situation 2 and status B of situation 3.

${\sigma}_{\mathit{LIN},0}=(1-1)\ast 50+(1-1)\ast 50+(-1--1)\ast 0+(-1,5--1,5)\ast 0=0$

${\sigma}_{\mathit{LIN},0}=(10-1)\ast 50+(-10-1)\ast 50+(1--1)\ast 0+(0,1--1,5)\ast 0=-100$

${\sigma}_{\mathit{LIN},0}=(10-0,4)\ast 50+(-10-0,4)\ast 50+(1-0)\ast 0+(0,1--0,6)\ast 0=-40$

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

No thermal or mechanical calculations are performed in this test: the stress reading tables are provided directly to the POST_RCCM operator. The B3200 results are analyzed for the FATIGUE option.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

On this simple test case, all the tested results are in agreement with the reference solution with an accuracy of ${10}^{-4}$ %

- for the calculation of $\mathit{Sn}$ , $\mathit{Sp}$ , $\mathit{Salt}$ and the use factor,
- with passing situations
- with sharing groups
- with sub-cycles taken into account.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results are accurate and show that the POST_RCCM operator correctly selects the quantities to be processed and correctly calculates the integrals (means on segments) for the B3200 results.
