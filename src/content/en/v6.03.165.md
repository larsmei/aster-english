# v6.03.165 SSNP165 – Ring on block

**Summary:**

This test consists of crushing a half-ring on a block with a contact condition between the two. There are two flat-deformed modellings, with and without friction and a friction-free Modelling in Mortar, ALGO_CONT=LAC pairing and LAC cutting. The validation is in non-regression.

This test is very discriminating on advanced methods Lagrangien type increased with Newton generalized.

It is inspired by the digital example 'Elastic ring and block' of the publication [1] .

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The half ring consists of two inner and outer parts, of the same thickness $5\mathit{mm}$ . The radius of the neutral fiber is $95\mathit{mm}$ . The block is a rectangle of dimensions $50\mathit{mm}$ by $250\mathit{mm}$ . The initial distance between the block and the half ring is $20\mathit{mm}$ .

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.165/100000000000052B000002F21EB7CE532FC65A88.jpg)

### 1.2 Material properties

The constitutive law used is elastoplastic with linear isotropic hardening (VMIS_ISOT_LINE).

The material of the inner part of the ring is characterised by:

- $E=100000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =2.7E-5{\mathit{kg.mm}}^{-3}$
- $\text{D\_SIGM\_EPSI}=9.0E4\mathit{MPa}$
- $\text{SY}=10.E100\mathit{MPa}$

The material of the outer part of the ring is characterised by:

- $E=1000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =2.7E-5{\mathit{kg.mm}}^{-3}$
- $\text{D\_SIGM\_EPSI}=9.0E2\mathit{MPa}$
- $\text{SY}=10.E100\mathit{MPa}$

> The material of the block is characterised by:

- $E=300\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =2.7E-5{\mathit{kg.mm}}^{-3}$
- $\text{D\_SIGM\_EPSI}=10.\mathit{MPa}$
- $\text{SY}=10.E100\mathit{MPa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The lower edge of the block is blocked such as $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=0$ .

$\mathit{DX}=0$ and $\mathit{DY}=-90\mathit{mm}$ displacement is imposed on both ends of the half-ring in the $\mathit{XZ}$ plane.

### 1.4 Other calculation parameters

The calculation is done with the DYNA_NON_LINE operator. The HHT (SCHEMA=的HHT) schema is used with the ALPHA parameter at -0.3 (the default value of this parameter is -0.1 and it leads to a calculation time too large for Modelling B).

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

D_PLAN modelling is used.

The contact between the half ring and the block is free of friction.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 240 elements of type QUAD4.
