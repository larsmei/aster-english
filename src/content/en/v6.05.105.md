# v6.05.105 SSNS105 – Non-linear behaviour of a rebar table

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the modellings grid_membrane and grid_excentre for reinforcements. The reinforced concrete model is subjected to a load of displacements imposed: ddl_impo. The nonlinear behavior of the rebar arrays is modelled by grid_isot_line for isotropic plasticity.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A reinforced concrete cube is considered $L=1m$ side.

![Figure](/figures/v6.05.105/10000200000001CC0000015FEE937DCCD077ECF4.png)

The rebar arrays belong to the plane defined by the four nodes $\mathit{N05}-\mathit{N02}-\mathit{N03}-\mathit{N08}$ .

Two rebar panels are defined: one according to the $X$ local direction and one according to the $Y$ local direction.

The rate of ironing for each rebar is $0.1{m}^{2}/\mathit{ml}$ (Section per linear metre).

### 1.2 Features of modellings

The mesh cell concrete is modeled with a element hexa8.

The modellings considered for reinforcing sheeting are:

- Modelling A (§3): use of Membrane_grid with mesh cells tria3 support
- Modelling B (§4): use of Membrane_Grate with quad4 support mesh cells
- Modelling C (§5): use of excentre grid with mesh cells tria3 support

### 1.3 Material properties

Concrete material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- ${E}_{b}=20000\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.2$

The constitutive law of the frame follows an elastoplastic model with properties:

- ${E}_{a}=200000\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\nu =0$
- ${E}_{\mathrm{ecr}}^{\mathrm{acier}}=20000\mathrm{MPa}$
- ${\sigma}_{e}^{\mathrm{acier}}=200000\mathrm{MPa}$

The grid_isot_line model for isotropic hardening plasticity is used in stat_non_line.

### 1.4 Boundary conditions and loads

![Figure](/figures/v6.05.105/10000200000001A5000001425BD1DA55CB9AE19C.png)

boundary conditions:

- clamped support en $\mathit{A1}$
- $\mathit{DX}=0$ on the $\mathit{A1}-\mathit{A4}$ edge
- $\mathit{DY}=0$ on the $\mathit{A1}-\mathit{A2}$ edge
- $\mathrm{DZ}=0$ on the lower surface of the cube (N01-N02-N03-N04)

Loading by required travel:

- $\mathrm{DX}=1$ on the $\mathit{A2}-\mathit{A3}$ edge
- $\mathit{DY}=1t$ on the $\mathit{A3}-\mathit{A4}$ edge

where $t$ is the pseudo-time parameter.

### 1.5 Initial conditions

Initially, displacement and stress are zero everywhere.
