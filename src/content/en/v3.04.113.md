# v3.04.113 SSLV113 - Error estimate on a two-material hollow cylinder

**Summary:**

This test validates the pure residue error estimator applied to the linear elasticity 2D, static. A hollow cylinder consisting of two materials and subjected to internal and external pressures is considered.

The 2 models are axisymmetric, on 8-node quadrangles.

The value of the test lies in the comparison between the exact and calculated stresses, on the one hand, the estimated error and the exact error, on the other hand. This test also allows to show the validity of the residual estimator on a bimaterial structure, unlike the Zhu-Zienkiewicz estimator which is not applicable on structures with discontinuities in the stress field (here at the material interface).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

$\begin{array}{ccc}{\mu}_{i}& =& \frac{{E}_{i}}{2(1+\nu )}\\ {\lambda}_{i}& =& \frac{\nu {E}_{i}}{(1-2\nu )(1+\nu )}\end{array}$

$$
\begin{array}{ccccc}{a}_{1}& =& -0.98097& {b}_{1}& -1.11741\\ {a}_{2}& =& -1.34405& {b}_{2}& -0.30048\end{array}\rbrace
$$

For material $i$ , one has:

$\begin{array}{ccc}{u}_{r}& =& {a}_{i}r+\frac{{b}_{i}}{r}\\ {u}_{z}& =& A\end{array}$

$\lbrace \begin{array}{ccc}{\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}& =& {\lambda}_{i}(2{a}_{i}+A)+2{\mu}_{i}({a}_{i}-\frac{{b}_{i}}{{r}^{2}})\\ {\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}& =& {\lambda}_{i}(2{a}_{i}+A)+2{\mu}_{i}({a}_{i}+\frac{{b}_{i}}{{r}^{2}})\\ {\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}& =& 2{\lambda}_{i}{a}_{i}+({\lambda}_{i}+2{\mu}_{i})A\end{array}$

### 2.2 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.

## 5 Summary of the results

The ERRE_ELEM_SIGM Residual Error Estimator gives good results on bi-material problems.

**Note:**

*The error estimator of Zhu-Zienkiewicz does not give correct results. Indeed, at the interface it detects a strong error because it performs continuous smoothing of the constraints while there is a jump for* ${\sigma}_{zz}$ *and* ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }$ *.*
