# v1.01.312 PLEXU03 – Validation of prestress cables in CALC_EUROPLEXUS

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the linkage of a prestress cable voltage calculation in Code_Aster with a fast dynamic calculation in Europlexus via Code_Aster CALC_EUROPLEXUS macro-command.

Specifically, it validates the following points in CALC_EUROPLEXUS:

- use of elements BARRE
- the consideration of kinematic relationships from DEFI_CABLE_BP
- Q4GG Modelling with mesh-cell group containing triangles and quadrangles
- the consideration of an initial state (movements and constraints)
- the calculation of the stresses from the initial displacement made by Europlexus
- use of keyword EQUILIBRE ETAT_INIT

Modelling B is also used to validate the calculation of FORC_NODA to DEFORMATION = 'PETIT_REAC' for elements BARRE and DEFORMATION = 'GROT_GDEP' for elements Q4GG from Europlexus.

## 1 Description

### 1.1 Geometry

The concrete plate consists of a square of $2m$ side and an isocele triangle of a base of $2m$ and a height of $0,5m$.

The thickness of the plate is $e=0,6m$.

A cable, located on the $[\mathit{AB}]$ segment, crosses the square part of the plate horizontally, mid-height, without eccentricity in the thickness. L-section right of the cable is ${S}_{a}=1,5{.10}^{-4}{m}^{2}$.

The plate is made of concrete and the steel cable.

### 1.3 boundary conditions

Nodes $O$ and $C$ are clamped: all translation and rotation degrees of freedom are blocked.

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics

The concrete plate is modelled by two elements Q4GG, the first is supported by a quadrangular mesh cell and the second by a triangular mesh cell.

The cable is represented by 4 elements BARRE, supported by 4 mesh cells segments at 2 nodes.

### 2.2 Loading

The plate is subjected to a pressure that varies with time:

| Instant | 0,0 | 1.00E-005 | 2.00E-004 | 5.00E-004 | 1.00E-003 | 2.00E-003 | 3.00E-003 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pressure in $\mathit{Pa}$ | 0,0 | 2.00E+003 | 1.00E+005 | 1.00E+006 | 2.50E+006 | 7.00E+006 | 0,0 |

No tension is initially imposed in the cable.

### 2.3 Test steps

After defining the model, materials and loads, DEFI_CABLE_BP macro-command is used to obtain kinematic relationships between the plate and the cable.

CALC_EUROPLEXUS and DYNA_NON_LINE commands are then launched with the same models, materials, loads, etc. The movements resulting from the results of these two commands are compared to validate the correct consideration of the kinematic relationships between plate and cable in Europlexus.

The IMPR_RESU command is called to ensure that the recovery of the results from Europlexus is performed correctly.

Modelling Identical A

### 3.2 Loading

An initial voltage is imposed in the cable with a value of $\mathrm{2,0E5}N$ .

### 3.3 Test steps

After defining the model, materials and loads, DEFI_CABLE_BP macro-command is used to obtain kinematic relationships between the plate and the cable and to determine the initial voltage in each element cable. The keyword RELAXATION is not specified, so all elements cables have a voltage of $\mathrm{2,0E5}N$.

CALC_PRECONT macro-command is used to tension concrete from the DEFI_CABLE_BP cable_precont concept.

The result from CALC_PRECONT macro-command is then given as the initial state to CALC_EUROPLEXUS. The displacements and stresses (CONTRAINTE ='OUI') are transmitted to Europlexus. No additional load is given, the purpose being to check the balance of the system. Therefore, the balance is not forced in Europlexus (EQUILIBRE = 'NON').

After several time step, it is checked that the displacements and stresses have not changed.

## 4 Modelling C

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

Modelling Identical A

### 4.2 Loading

The plate is subjected to a static pressure load which varies linearly with time.

| Instant | $0.0$ | $1.0$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pa pressure | $0.0$ | $10.0$ |

No tension is initially imposed in the cable.

### 4.3 Test steps

After defining the model, materials and loads, DEFI_CABLE_BP macro-command is used to obtain kinematic relationships between the plate and the cable.

A static calculation is made with the pressure loading using the STAT_NON_LINE operator to build an initial state.

The result from the STAT_NON_LINE operator is then given as the initial state to CALC_EUROPLEXUS. Only the movements are transmitted to Europlexus (CONTRAINTE ='NON'). No additional load is given, the purpose being to verify that the stresses calculated by Europlexus are the same as those calculated by Code_Aster.

Note: To recover the same constraints the static calculation in Code_Aster must be done on large shifts as it is the kinematics used by Europlexus. Moreover, you must give the NITER operand the number of time step that Code_Aster performed to arrive at its final state during the static calculation.

CALC_EUROPLEXUS is called 3 times:

- CONTRAINTE ='NON'et EQUILIBRE ='OUI':

The initial and final moments of the test are checked that the stresses found are equal to those calculated by Code_Aster. Since the equilibrium has been forced there must be no differences between the initial and final states.

- CONTRAINTE ='NON'et EQUILIBRE ='OUI':

This time the static calculation load is not given to CALC_EUROPLEXUS. This should not change the results from the previous case. The only difference is that the external fictitious forces added by Europlexus to be at equilibrium will be more important.It is checked at the initial moment and at the final moment that the stresses found are equal to those calculated by Code_Aster.

- CONTRAINTE ='NON'et EQUILIBRE ='NON':

The initial stress is verified that the stress found is equal to that calculated by Code_Aster. Since the equilibrium is not forced, slight changes in displacement and constraints between the initial and final state can be observed.

## 5 Executive summary

The calculation done with Europlexus via CALC_EUROPLEXUS took the elements BARRE into account.

The displacement test shows that kinematic relationships have been correctly taken into account. The processing of elements quadrangles and triangles in the same mesh-cell group has been well done.

It was also found that the initial state is taken into account correctly, as well as the stress calculation from the Europlexus displacements.
