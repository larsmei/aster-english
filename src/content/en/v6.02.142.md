# v6.02.142 FORMA43 - Practical work of the Civil Engineering training: 3 point flexion of a reinforced concrete beam.

**Summary:**

The problem is to analyze the response of a reinforced concrete beam in 3-point bending until the ruin with a [R3.08.08](doc:r3.08.08) multifibre beam model. This test corresponds to a static analysis of a beam with nonlinear behavior. The concrete is modelled with the constitutive law softening damageable Mazars version $1D$ [R7.01.08](doc:r7.01.08). The steel is modeled with an elasto-plastic law.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Simple support in $B$: $\mathit{DY}=0$ .

$A$ dual support: $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DZ}=0$ as well as $\mathit{DRX}=\mathit{DRY}=0$ .

Virtually static loading: $\mathit{DY}$ monotone downward displacement applied at mid-trace $C$ (3 point bending test), according to a linear time function:

| $t$ | $\mathit{DY}$ |
| --- | --- |
| $0,0$ | $0,0\mathrm{cm}$ |
| $3,0$ | $-3,0\mathit{cm}$ |
| $5,0$ | $-5,0\mathit{cm}$ |
