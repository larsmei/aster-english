# v6.01.114 SSNA114 - Drained triaxial test with VISC_MAXWELL

**Summary**

This test validates the VISC_MAXWELL model. It is a triaxial pure mechanical test (drained condition). The calculations are applied only to the solid part and hydraulic coupling is not taken into account. Due to symmetry, one only cares about the eighth of a sample subjected to a triaxial test.

The containment level is 5 bars.

### 1.3 Initial conditions, boundary conditions, and loading

**Phase 1:**

The sample is taken to a homogeneous state of stress: ${\sigma}_{xx}^{0}={\sigma}_{yy}^{0}={\sigma}_{zz}^{0}=0,5\mathit{MPa}$ in 1000 s, by imposing the corresponding total pressure on the front, right lateral and upper sides. The movements are blocked on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$), left lateral (${u}_{y}=0$) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$) sides.

**Phase 2:**

The blocked movements are maintained on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$), left (${u}_{y}=0$) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$) sides.

A displacement is applied to the upper face to obtain ${\epsilon}_{zz}=-0,01$ deformation (in 1000 s counted from the beginning of phase 2). On the front and right side faces, boundary conditions are maintained as total stress:

$\sigma .n={\sigma}_{0}(=0.5\mathit{MPa})$
