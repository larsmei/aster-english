# v2.05.400 SDLX400 – Articulated beam on elastic support

**Summary:**

This test consists of calculating the frequencies of a beam with one of the ends articulated and the other is either free or supported by a spring.

The reference solution of this problem is analytical.

The originality of this test is to model this beam using elements volume and beams.

This test consists of three modellings:

- Modelling A: POU_D_E;
- Modelling B: 3D and POU_D_E;
- Modelization C: POU_D_TG.

The 3D-beam connection is performed by LIAISON_ELEM in the AFFE_CHAR_MECA operator.
For Modelling A, the modal calculation (generalised problem GEP with real modes) is checked by varying the modal solvers (SORENSEN, QZ and Jacobi) and the blockings: DDL_IMPO alone, MECA_IMPO alone or a paning of both.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

#### 2.1.1 Case 1: Free end

The beam equation is written:

$$
\frac{{d}^{4}v}{{\mathit{dx}}^{4}}=\frac{-\rho S}{\mathit{EI}}\frac{{d}^{2}v}{{\mathit{dt}}^{2}}
$$

We're looking for a solution of the form:

$$
v(x)=\sin(\omega t)\left[A\cos(\mathit{kx})+B\sin(\mathit{kx})+C\cosh(\mathit{kx})+D\sinh(\mathit{kx})\right]
$$

with $\omega =\sqrt{\frac{\mathit{EI}}{\rho S}}{k}^{2}$

The boundary conditions are:

En $x=0$ , $v=0$ et $\frac{{d}^{2}v}{{\mathit{dx}}^{2}}=0$

En $x=L$ , $\frac{{d}^{2}v}{{\mathit{dx}}^{2}}=0$ et $\frac{{d}^{3}v}{{\mathit{dx}}^{3}}=0$

By replacing $v$ with the expression (), we obtain:

- En $x=0$ , $A=C=0$ ;
- En $x=L$ , $\sin(\mathit{kL})\cosh(\mathit{kL})=\cos(\mathit{kL})\sinh(\mathit{kL})$         (3)

The first six roots of () are given in the following table:

| $\mathit{kL}$ |
| --- |
| $0$ |
| $3.9266$ |
| $7.06858$ |
| $10.2102$ |
| $13.3518$ |
| $16.4934$ |

The values of ${\omega}_{i}$ , $i=1,\mathrm{...},6$ are derived.

#### 2.1.2 Case 2: Elastic support end

The equation with the values is as follows:

$$
\sin(\mathit{kL})\left({k}^{3}{L}^{3}\frac{\mathit{EI}}{{L}^{3}}\cosh(\mathit{kL})-K\sinh(\mathit{kL})\right)-\sinh(\mathit{kL})\left({k}^{3}{L}^{3}\frac{\mathit{EI}}{{L}^{3}}\cos(\mathit{kL})+K\sin(\mathit{kL})\right)=0
$$

The clean pulses are given by:

$$
\omega =\frac{1}{{L}^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{\mathit{EI}}{\rho S}}{k}^{2}{L}^{2}
$$

The first six roots of () are given in the following table:

| $\mathit{kL}$ |
| --- |
| $2.7880886$ |
| $4.5619625$ |
| $7.1895724$ |
| $10.249031$ |
| $13.368916$ |
| $16.502406$ |

The values of ${\omega}_{i}$ , $i=1,\mathrm{...},6$ are then derived.

## 6 Summary of the results

The results obtained with elements beam POU_D_E and POU_D_TG are in very good agreement with analytical solution and are less than $0.46$ % and $0.61$ % respectively. The results obtained by mixing two types of elements (beam and volume) and then connecting degrees of freedom with LIAISON_ELEM are less accurate (but the difference remains less than $3.61$ %).

Note that the calculated eigenfrequencies are identical regardless of the method used in the modal solver (SORENSEN or JACOBI).
