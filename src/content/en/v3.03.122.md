**Summary:**

This test case allows testing the eccentricity of the plates with modellings DKT and DST.

### 1.1 Geometry

Elastic

- $E=2.1\times {10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$         Young's modulus
- $\nu =0.3$                 Poisson's ratio

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Forced displacement $(m)$:

- $\mathrm{AF},\mathrm{CD}$ segment: $\mathrm{DZ}=0$
- $\mathrm{FA}$ segment: $\mathrm{DX}=0$
- point $A$      : $\mathrm{DX}=\mathrm{DY}=\mathrm{DRZ}=0$

Loading

- Pressure on $\mathrm{BE}$: $p=2.\times {10}^{5}N/m$ $P=\mathrm{pl}$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Travel:

- Points $B$ and $E$

$f=\frac{{\mathrm{Pl}}^{3}}{96{\mathrm{EI}}_{1}}+\frac{{\mathrm{Pl}}^{3}}{96{\mathrm{EI}}_{2}}$ with ${I}_{i}=\frac{b\times {e}_{i}^{3}}{12}$

- Points $G$ and $H$

$f=-\frac{{\mathrm{Pl}}^{3}}{192{\mathrm{EI}}_{1}}(\frac{{I}_{1}}{{I}_{2}}+\frac{7}{2})$

- bending moment :

$M(x)=\frac{\mathrm{P.x}}{2}$

### 2.2 Reference quantity

- $\mathrm{DZ}$ moving following $z$ at $B$ and $G$ points.
- $\mathrm{MXX}$ bending moment for $x=\frac{L}{2}$ and $x=\frac{L}{4}$
