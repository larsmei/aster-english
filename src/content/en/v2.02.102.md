# v2.02.102 SDLL102 - Portico subjected to electrodynamic forces

**Summary:**

This test is a three-dimensional problem of direct transient dynamic calculation with distributed forces of electrodynamic origin applied to a portal (bar on 3 insulating columns of a transformation station).

This test was provided by the Transport Network Study Centre (EDF-DEPT) and has since been supplemented by an international benchmark based on experimental measurements (results of several foreign codes): CIGRE-structure D test.

It allows comparison of displacement results compared to those obtained by other industrial codes using a finite elements method or finite differences.

This test contains Modelling with elements type SEG2.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Modelling POU_D_E

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.102/1000000000000149000000B3D9269AE321D154A0.jpg)

Discretization:

- elements $\mathrm{AB}$ , $\mathrm{EF}$ , $\mathrm{IJ}$ : 10 mesh cells : SEG2
- elements $\mathrm{BC}$ , $\mathrm{FG}$ , $\mathrm{JK}$ : 10 mesh cells : SEG2
- elements $\mathrm{CD1}$ , $\mathrm{GH1}$ , $\mathrm{KL1}$ : 1 mesh cell : SEG2
- elements $\mathrm{D2H1}$ , $\mathrm{H2L1}$ : 30 mesh cells : SEG2

Dynamic $\mathrm{1s}$ evolution discretized to time step $5.{10}^{-4}s$ with NEWMARK $(a=0.25,d=0.5)$ algorithm.

Storage of results every 20 time step is ${10}^{-2}s$ .

The results obtained by Code_aster are satisfactory compared to other codes. They are almost always lower than the measurements (effects of $\mathrm{AB}$ , $\mathrm{EF}$ , $\mathrm{IJ}$ overvalued). The maxima are trimmed due to periodic storage.
