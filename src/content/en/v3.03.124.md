# v3.03.124 SSLS124 - Internal pressure sphere in perfect plasticity

**Summary:**

An interest is being made in a study on a half-sphere subjected to internal pressure, modelled in two parts: half in elements 3D density, the other in elements 2D hulls. The objective is to test the implementation of the restraint `COQ_3D` in `AFFE_CHAR_MECA`, in the context of a non-linear calculation with perfect plasticity according to the von Mises criterion. The simulation allows to validate the mechanical continuity between the two domains and to analyse the propagation of the plasticization under increasing pressure.

## 1 Reference problem

Representation of a sphere under internal pressure:

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.124/sphere_pression.png)

Characteristics:

| Average area radius | $\mathrm{1}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Thickness | $\mathrm{0.1}$ |

The objective is to apply internal pressure so that the plastic zone stops at the average surface, i.e. $r_c = 1$ radius.
This calculation is based on a perfect constitutive law elasto-plastic, with a Von Mises plasticity criterion, where the threshold stress is fixed to $\sigma_y = 50000$.

Considering that the internal radius is $a$ and the external radius is $b$,
The internal pressure expression, noted $p$, to achieve this configuration is given by:

$$
p = \frac{2}{3} \sigma_y \left[ 1 + 3 \ln\left( \frac{r_c}{a} \right) - \frac{r_c^3}{b^3} \right]
$$

The radial displacement in a point located at a $r$ radius in the plastic area is given by:

$$
u_r = \frac{\sigma_y}{E} \, r \left[
    (1 - \nu) \frac{r_c^3}{r^3}
    - \frac{2}{3}(1 - 2\nu)
    \left( 1 + 3 \ln\left( \frac{r_c}{r} \right) - \frac{r_c^3}{b^3} \right)
\right]
$$

where $E=1.e06$ is the Young's modulus, and $\nu=0.3$ the Poisson's ratio.

For comparison, a point $A$ located on the half-sphere 3D, $(0, 0, -0{,}95)$ coordinates is selected.
