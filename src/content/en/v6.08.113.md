# v6.08.113 SSND113 - SIMU_POINT_MAT in large deformations, required transformation gradient

**Summary:**

A small point hardware problem is compared for VISC_ISOT_TRAC behaviour in large SIMO_MIEHE deformations, the solution obtained by imposing a deformation tensor with that obtained by imposing the corresponding transformation gradient. This test validates this feature in SIMU_POINT_MAT

Modelling A: This Modelling serves as a reference, deformations imposed with SIMU_POINT_MAT.

Modelling B: This model uses a transformation gradient imposed with SIMU_POINT_MAT.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

This is a material point, representative of a homogeneous state of stress and deformation.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The load is in deformations imposed on the $y$ axis , corresponding to $\dot{\varepsilon}$ speeds of ${10}^{-3}{s}^{-1}$

${\varepsilon}_{yy}=0.2$ for $T=2000s$ , in 100 increments.

All other components of the deformation are zero.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Constraints and deformations zero to $t=0$ .

## 2 Baseline

Intercomparison between the two modellings A, and B, the VISC_ISOT_TRAC behavior in large deformations being validated elsewhere (SSNL129 for example).

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Defective Tensor imposed on the point hardware via SIMU_POINT_MAT command.

${\varepsilon}_{yy}=0.2\frac{t}{2000}$ , in 100 increments. ${\varepsilon}_{xx}={\varepsilon}_{zz}=0$ , ${\varepsilon}_{xy}={\varepsilon}_{xz}={\varepsilon}_{yz}=0$
