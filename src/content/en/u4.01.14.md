# u4.01.14 New features and modifications of version 18

**Summary:**

The purpose of this document is to provide an overall view of the syntax modifications and of the new possibilities of the code_aster commands introduced during the development of version 18, that is, since version 17.4.

For further details, one should consult the documentation of the commands and the histor files of the sub-versions.

## 1 Mechanics

- New stabilised mixed finite elements (#35072 - 18.1)
  A new type of stabilised mixed linear elements is introduced (formulation u, epsilon). These are the modellings **3D_MIX_STA** and **D_PLAN_MIX_STA**. The additional degrees of freedom are the strain components (EPXX, EPYY ... etc).
The detail of the formulations is given in [R3.06.15](doc:r3.06.15).
- New isoparametric cubic element in 2D (#35515 - 18.1)
  The following isoparametric cubic elements are added: TRIA10, QUAD12 and TETRA20
- New pile-foundation finite element **3D_INTERF_POU** (#33213 - 18.1)
  A new finite element is added representing a non-linear phenomenological interaction between a pile segment (model POU_D_T) and a small volume of soil around it (3D model — HEXA8). It may be described as an “interface” element.
This new finite element must be associated with the material **INTERF_POU_ELAS** or **INTERF_POU_CINE**.
The detail of the formulations is given in [R3.06.15](doc:r3.06.15).
- Improvement of the HHO elements and extension of the features.
  Several issues deal with these improvements:

  - Addition of the modelling AXIS_HHO. (#32760 - 18.1)
  - Addition of the use of the line search in statics (#35433 - 18.1)
  - Addition of the use of piloting (#35432 - 18.1)
  - Addition of the prism and penta geometric supports (#35409 - 18.1)
  - Addition of post-processings (#35293 - 18.1)
  - Addition of static condensation (#35447 - 18.1)
  - Addition of the modellings AXIS_HHO_GRAD_VARI (#35287 - 18.1)
  - Addition of the VARC stresses for HHO (#35438 - 18.1)
  - Addition of the HHO elements with MECA_STATIQUE (#35439 - 18.1)

## 2 Constitutive laws

- Constitutive law **BETON_BURGER** (#35484 - 18.1)
  The names of the internal variables are renamed (in order to shorten them) in the MFRONT file.
- Addition of hardening recovery for the constitutive law **VMIS_ISOT_NL** (#35545 - 18.1)
  The hardening-recovery feature **REST_ECRO** is added for the constitutive law VMIS_ISOT_NL.
- Cohesive law equivalent to **JOINT_MECA_FROT** (#35543 - 18.1)
  A cohesive law equivalent to JOINT_MECA_FROT is added, based on a mixed formulation carried by the interface elements.
- Modification of the **creep** law in the constitutive law **BETON_RAG** (#35499 - 18.1)
  This development concerns the rewriting of the creep part with a formulation of BURGER type _R7.01.35. As in the former formulation, the creep law involves spherical and deviatoric creep strains. The essential difference between the new law and the former one comes from the description of reversible and irreversible creep.
- Implementation of the constitutive law **DIS_ECRO_CINE** in DYNA_VIBRA (#35738 - 18.1)
  The possibility is added of using the constitutive law DIS_ECRO_CINE in the operator DYNA_VIBRA, so that this law can be used in the framework of dynamic analyses on a modal basis.

## 3 Contact

- So-called “element-based” method (#35413 - 18.1)
  Implementation of an alternative method for numerical integration in frictional contact with a mortar approach (with ray casting/raytracing). In this classical so-called “element-based” method, all the integration points of the slave mesh cell are projected.
This method is developed for the operator `DEFI_CONT` usable with MECA_NON_LINE.

## 4 Loadings and pre-processing

- New linear relation on a generalised modal basis `AFFE_CHAR_GENE`. (#34773 - 18.1)
  A new operator is added in order to define loadings on the modellings on a modal basis. Via the factor keyword `LIAISON`, one can impose a non-homogeneous linear relation between degrees of freedom of the problem in a context of solution on a modal basis.
- New computation for **RIGI_PARASOL** via the keyword `METHODE` (#35216 - 18.1)
  For AFFE_CARA_ELEM, options RIGI_PARASOL and RIGI_GRILLE, the computation of the area assigned to the node has until now been carried out by dividing the area of the mesh cell by the number of nodes of the mesh cell.
This computation is exact for all the TR3 and for the QU4 whose angles are right angles (comparison PRES_REP), but not for the other supports.
A new method is proposed, based on the same methodology as PRES_REP and FORCE_COQUE. A new keyword `METHODE='UNIFORME'/'DIAG'` is introduced for the choice of the distribution method, with `METHODE = 'DIAG'` by default (new distribution method; the former method remains accessible via 'UNIFORME').

## 5 Post-processings

- Computation of **SIEF_ELGA** and **STRX_ELGA** (#35440 - 18.1)
  The computation of SIEF_ELGA and STRX_ELGA is added in `MECA_STATIQUE`.
- Addition of account taken of complex matrices for **PROJ_BASE** (#35707 - 18.1)
  The operator **PROJ_BASE** can carry out projections with complex matrices, as was already possible with PROJ_MATR_BASE.
- Med output for checking the cut lines (#35079 - 18.1)
  In the command MACR_LIGN_COUPE, the output of a med file is allowed, presenting the results obtained at each node of the cut, in order to facilitate the checking and the exploitation of the results produced during the cut. In addition, a paravis/paraview macro has been developed in order to automate the visualisation of this med file in paravis.
This macro is present in the code_aster sources (repo src->data->paraview->macros).
- Addition of a MED attribute for the description of the fields (#34432 - 18.1)
  This description is used in the aster to medcoupling conversion and in IMPR_RESU.
- code-aster / code-saturne coupling (#35741 , #35740 - 18.1)
  The possibility is added of projecting the P0 fluid-force field with the nodal projection.

## 6 Performance

- Improvement of the command **CALC_FERRAILLAGE** (#35021 - 18.1)
  Thanks to a refactoring of the code, the performance of the reinforcement computation at ELS_QP is improved.
A speed-up by a factor of 8 is observed on certain runs.

## 7 Solution and solvers

- Change to **DEFI_LIST_INST** and addition of the first time step `PAS_INIT` (#34657 -18.1)
  The keyword `PAS_INIT` is added, alongside PAS_MINI and PAS_MAXI. It makes it possible to set the starting value of the time step.
This change is available only in MECA_NON_LINE.
- Optimise the acceleration of the time step (#35652 - 18.1)
  In the case of automatic adaptation of the time step, one may be obliged to take a very small time step in order to pass over the points of the list of time instants.
The optimum consists in keeping the time step as large as possible before the compulsory passage instant, so that the time step following that instant is itself also as large as possible.
This change is available only in MECA_NON_LINE.

## 8 Syntax modifications and removals

- Removal of the command `CALC_BT` (#32638 - 18.1)
- Removal of the constitutive laws **PINTO_MENEGOTTO** and **GRILLE_PINTO_MEM** (#33840 - 18.1)
- Removal of the option **PETIT_REAC** for DKT and COQUE_3D (#35530 - 18.1)
- Removal of **FORCE_ELEC** and **DEFI_FONC_ELEC** (#32742 - 18.1)
- Modification of the computation mode of **RIGI_PARASOL** and **RIGI_GRILLE** with the use of a new keyword METHODE='UNIFORME'/'DIAG' ('DIAG' by default).
- Renaming of the internal variables of the constitutive law BETON_BURGER (#35484 - 18.1)
