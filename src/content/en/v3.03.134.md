### 2.1 Method of calculation

The density of longitudinal steels is calculated using the Capra and Maury method. Given the direction of effort, the "size" facet is obvious. The analytical calculation is therefore a section calculation to determine the forces to which the 2 steel beds (upper and lower) are subjected.
