# v5.03.100 SDNV100 - Impact of a beam on a rigid wall

**Summary**

This problem corresponds to a direct transient analysis of a nonlinear system modelled in elements density. A first slender structure (beam) of square cross section is animated by an initial velocity and strikes a rigid wall. Nonlinearity comes from the contact conditions between the structure and the wall.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The problem is unidirectional according to $z$ .

A quarter of the beam is considered with symmetry conditions: movement is blocked

$x$ on $x=0$ plane and $y$ on $y=0$ plane.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

All nodes of the mesh cell of the beam are charged on the $z$ axis:

- the initial displacement: ${u}_{0}=2\mathrm{mm}$
- initial speed: ${v}_{0}=-100m/s$

## 17 Summary of the results

The accuracy of the calculation is relatively average, which is due to the choice of the penalty coefficients used to model the contact. The increase in the contact stiffness greatly improves the displacement field but generates the large oscillations of the speed field around the analytical solution.
