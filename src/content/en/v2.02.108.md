# v2.02.108 SDLL108 - "Coffee Table" by NEUBERT

**Summary**

This multi-directional problem consists in performing a spectral seismic analysis of a structure composed of elements of non-mass beams and discrete masses at nodes. It includes modelling.

Seismic excitation is provided as three acceleration oscillator response spectra to supports on $X$ , $Y$ and $Z$ axes.

Through this problem, we test the MODE_STATIQUE command and the quadratic mode combination and quadratic combination options and Newmark combination of COMB_SISM_MODAL excitation directions.

The results obtained are in good agreement with the reference results obtained with the HERCULE code.

## 5 Summary of the results

A relatively good agreement is obtained between the solution calculated with *Code_Aster* and the solution calculated by HERCULE:

- Frequency: The differences observed for the first 18 frequencies is low, below $\text{0.2\%}$.
- Displacement: The observed differences is less than $\text{1\%}$ if a quadratic combination of excitation directions is used, and less than $\text{2 \%}$ if a combination of Newmark excitation directions is used.
- Efforts: the observed differences are less than $\text{2\%}$.
