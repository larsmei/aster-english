# v2.05.106 SDLX106 – Soil impedances under a rectangular foundation embedded in a homogeneous medium

**Summary:**

This test is used to validate the *Code_Aster* -MISS3D chaining. It is a typical case of soil impedance calculation under a rectangular foundation embedded in a homogeneous medium. The reverse transfer functions of impedances obtained by the chaining between *Code_Aster* and MISS3D are compared with those obtained by another method of calculation, using an exclusive Modelling by *Code_Aster* , where the infinite medium condition is represented by absorbent elementss assigned to the boundaries of a homogeneous soil volume surrounding the foundation. differencess are obtained by about 10% for 2 frequency values chosen respectively from the low and high range.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

MISS3D software uses the frequent coupling method to account for soil-structure interaction. This method, based on dynamic substructure, consists of cutting the field of study into three sub-domains:

- soil (which is discretized to elements borders),
- the foundation (which is mesh in EF),
- the structure (which is mesh in EF and represents the building and/or a ground bounded domain).

**The soil**

The soil corresponds to a layer of homogeneous semi-infinite medium.

**The Foundation**

The rectangular foundation is shown on [Figure 1.1-a] below. The dimensions of the foundation are $24m$ in the $X$ direction, $12m$ in the $Y$ direction, and $8m$ in the $Y$ direction. The $8m$ is then modelled by 108 elements in the $X$ superfacial direction. To speed up the calculations, the mesh cells in the $Y$ direction has been deliberately derefined in height: in practice, mesh cells in the square form should be used and therefore twice as much mesh cells in height.

### 1.2 Boundary conditions and mechanical loadings

To calculate the 6 static rigid body modes of the foundation and the eigenmodes, the 6 degrees of freedom translation and rotation of the central node at the foundation base is blocked by imposing a solid restraint relationship. This node central 6 load Nodal Force Module 1.E6 is also applied to each of the 6 translation and rotation components.

The reverse transfer functions of impedances by two harmonic calculation paths are then obtained as responses to these 6 stresses: either a modal calculation based on static foundation modes by directly reversing impedances obtained by the bonding between *Code_Aster* and MISS3D on a model reduced to the foundation, or a physical calculation based on another method using exclusive Modelling by *Code_Aster* d-a large homogeneous soil volume surrounding the foundation: with this Modelling, the infinite medium condition is represented by absorbent superfacial elements assigned to the boundaries of this volume.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference results are soil impedances obtained from the incident displacement fields and induced stresses calculated by MISS3D software at various depth levels of a stratified soil. The incident field at the ground surface is considered unitary in each direction of space and for any frequency of stress. The variation of this field in depth of soil, or deconvolution, is obtained from functions of Green. These functions form a basis of elementary solutions, representing the response, at various receptor levels, to unit stresses at various source levels of the soil [bib1].

### 2.2 Baseline

The reverse transfer functions of impedances obtained by the chaining between *Code_Aster* and MISS3D are compared with those obtained by the other calculation method, using an exclusive Modelling by *Code_Aster* , where the infinite medium condition is represented by absorbent elementss assigned to the boundaries of a large homogeneous soil volume surrounding the foundation.

The impedance values for each of the 6 rigid body directions obtained by inversion of the previous transfer functions can also be compared with those given by the reference [bib2], which synthesizes several numerical or semi-analytic studies performed by various authors.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The characteristics used and the mesh are those derived from the data of [§1]. Two calculation frequencies corresponding to a low range frequency and a high range frequency are used respectively for the respective $a0=\omega R/\mathit{Vs}$ adimensional frequency parameter values of $0.4$ and $1.2$ $8.5$ and $25.5\mathit{Hz}$ respectively if the $6m$ value for $R$ equals the foundation half width.

The automated calculation mode of the parameters of MISS3D is used in CALC_MISS.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh supplied to *Code_Aster* contains mesh cells types of QUAD4 to model the rectangular foundation modeled by elements DST. It is important to have oriented the elements of the foundation surface with normal fit back into the ground. A total of 108 mesh cells is obtained for the foundation with a size of mesh cell of approximately $2m$ in horizontal and $4m$ in vertical (we de-refined vertically to decrease the calculation time but the recommended value is $2m$).

The mesh also contains 23040 mesh cells HEXA8 type to model the homogeneous soil volume surrounding the foundation with 3D solid elements. The outer surface of this soil volume is discretized by 3888 mesh cells QUAD4 affected by elements 3D_ABSO to represent the absorbent boundary condition.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The inverse transfer functions of impedances obtained by the chaining between *Code_Aster* and MISS3D are compared with those obtained by another calculation method, using an exclusive Modelling by *Code_Aster* , where the infinite media condition is represented by absorbent elementss assigned to the homogeneous soil volume boundaries surrounding the foundation.

## 4 Modelling B

Similar to Modelling A, assuming that there are no nodes PB and PB2 groups for the node of the foundation centre of gravity.

A solution without re-mailing is shown in this model. The methodology allows you to find this node by DEFI_GROUP, if it exists in the mesh, or to create it by POST_ELEM.

## 5 Modelling C

Similar to Modelling B, in the sense that an node fictitious restraint for macro-element is added by the new GROUP_NO key word of MACR_ELEM_DYNA to calculate the frequent ground impedances under the foundation.

The static interface modes of rigid restraint are replaced by the first 6 zero frequency modes of the ibre foundation structure. A new mixed model consisting of the foundation structure and super-mesh cell is made using the ground macro-element. The MATR_IMPE_INIT option is also tested to extract soil behavior from two frequencies.

Frequent responses are equivalent to those obtained directly by the chaining of *Code_Aster* to MISS3D as the 'AUTRE_ASTER' reference.

## 6 Summary of the results

Comparison of the inverse transfer functions of impedances obtained by the chaining between *Code_Aster* and MISS3D and those obtained by a physical calculation of a model, exclusively by *Code_Aster* , of the homogeneous soil volume surrounding the foundation, gives relatively large differencess of 18% on average and up to 35%. This is explained by the following points in the Code_Aster calculation alone:

- frequency independence in the formulation of absorbent elementss assigned to the volume boundaries representing the infinite medium condition.
- a volume of soil modelled not sufficiently large
- a ground mesh not sufficiently fine

On the other hand, the comparison of the chain calculation with the results of the bibliographic reference are good. The differences are on average around 2% with the low value at 0.7% and the highest value at 7%.
