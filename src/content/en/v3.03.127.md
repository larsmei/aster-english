# v3.03.127 SSLS127 – Flexion of a reinforced concrete slab (model GLRC_DAMAGE) pressed on 4 sides: elastic plate speed

**Summary:**

This test represents the calculation of a reinforced concrete slab, in bending, subjected to pressure. It allows to validate the DKTG Modelling with the GLRC_DAMAGE model for linear elastic behaviour and Q4GG Modelling with the ELAS model. The slab is in simple support on its four sides.

Four modellings are performed:

1. Modelling A allows you to test the DKTG model with TRIA3
2. Modelling B allows you to test the DKTG model with QUAD4
3. Modelling C allows testing the Q4GG model with TRIA3
4. Modelling D allows testing the Q4GG model with QUAD4

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Square slab, of length $l=1.8m$ , thickness $h=0.12m$ , in single support on the four edges. The bending rail is parallel to the edges; it is identical on each of the two sides and in both directions ($\mathrm{dx}$ , $\mathrm{dy}$ being the spacing of the irons in the directions $x$ and $y$ ). The coating of the longitudinal irons closest to the faces is $22\mathrm{mm}$ . The coating of the irons in relation to the lateral edges of the slab of $2\mathrm{cm}$ is neglected. The following table summarizes the data of the ferrage. The geometric percentage of steel $\mu$ is given for one side in a direction.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The elastic relationships, linking the $N$ and $M$ bending forces to the $\varepsilon$ membrane deformations and $\kappa$ curvatures, and taking into account two symmetrical grids, describe:

$N=(\frac{{E}_{b}h}{1-{\nu}_{b}^{2}}\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1& {\nu}_{b}& 0\\ {\nu}_{b}& 1& 0\\ 0& 0& \frac{1-{\nu}_{b}}{2}\end{array}\right]+2{E}_{a}\left[\begin{array}{ccc}{a}_{x}& 0& 0\\ 0& {a}_{y}& 0\\ 0& 0& 0\end{array}\right])\epsilon$

$M=(\frac{{E}_{b}{h}^{3}}{12(1-{\nu}_{b}^{2})}\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1& {\nu}_{b}& 0\\ {\nu}_{b}& 1& 0\\ 0& 0& \frac{1-{\nu}_{b}}{2}\end{array}\right]+2{E}_{a}{e}_{s}^{2}\left[\begin{array}{ccc}{a}_{x}& 0& 0\\ 0& {a}_{y}& 0\\ 0& 0& 0\end{array}\right])\kappa$

As a slab configuration, the usual Poisson's ratio ${\nu}_{b}=0,22$ is assigned to concrete. The slab is simply pressed on the four edges:

| ![Figure](/figures/v3.03.127/100013D6000069BB00005245D61C86F33C5274F5.svg) | The equivalent stiffness bending is:${D}_{\mathit{éq}}=\frac{{E}_{b}{h}^{3}}{12(1-{\nu}_{b}^{2})}+2{E}_{a}{e}_{s}^{2}{a}_{x}$ , either here: ${D}_{\mathrm{éq}}=5,8786\text{MNm}$ ; Also: ${\nu}_{\mathrm{éq}}=\frac{{\nu}_{b}{E}_{b}{h}^{3}}{12(1-{\nu}_{b}^{2}){D}_{\mathrm{éq}}}$ , or: ${\nu}_{\mathrm{éq}}=0,2022$ |
| --- | --- |

| quantity | Expression |
| --- | --- |
| Deflection at the pressure centre [2] | $w=\frac{0,0464{\mathit{pl}}^{4}}{12(1-{\nu}_{\mathit{éq}}^{2}){D}_{\mathit{éq}}}$ |
| Center curvature [2] | ${\kappa}_{xx}={\kappa}_{yy}=\frac{0,04784{\mathit{pl}}^{2}}{(1+{\nu}_{\mathit{éq}}){D}_{\mathit{éq}}}$ |
| Global central time [2] | ${M}_{xx}={M}_{yy}=0,04784{\mathrm{pl}}^{2}$ |

### 2.2 Baseline

For modellings A and B where GLRC_DAMA is valid with elements DKTG:

- Deflection at the pressure centre: $w=6,926{.10}^{-5}\text{m}$
- Center curvature: ${\kappa}_{xx}={\kappa}_{yy}=2,193{.10}^{-4}{\text{m}}^{-1}$
- Global time at centre: ${M}_{xx}={M}_{yy}=1550\text{Nm/ml}$

For modellings C and D where ELAS is valid with elements DKTG:

- Deflection at the pressure centre: $w=7,895{.10}^{-5}\text{m}$
- Center curvature: ${\kappa}_{xx}={\kappa}_{yy}=2,351{.10}^{-4}{\text{m}}^{-1}$
- Global time at centre: ${M}_{xx}={M}_{yy}=1550\text{Nm/ml}$

### 3.4 Remarks

The coefficients of the following elasticity matrices used in the calculations were calculated with ${\nu}_{b}=0,22$:

1. Membrane elasticity matrix: $\left[\begin{array}{ccc}4832& 990,4& 0\\ 990,4& 4832& 0\\ 0& 0& 1756\end{array}\right]{10}^{6}\text{N/m}$
2. Flexibility flexural matrix: $\left[\begin{array}{ccc}5,879& 1,188& 0\\ 1,188& 5,879& 0\\ 0& 0& 2,107\end{array}\right]{10}^{6}\text{N/m}$

To be sure to remain in the elastic domain, the elastic limits expressed in the orthotropy benchmark are arbitrarily fixed at a very high value:

- Elastic limits in positive bending:

1. Direction x: ${1.10}^{10}\text{MNm/ml}$
Direction y: ${1.10}^{10}\text{MNm/ml}$

- Elastic limits in negative bending:

1. Direction x: $-{1.10}^{10}\text{MNm/ml}$
Direction y: $-{1.10}^{10}\text{MNm/ml}$

As the structure remains in the elastic domain, the kinematic recall coefficient (Prager constant) can take any value.

## 7 Summary of the results

Comparing the results of the four modellings with the analytical solution, we can see:

- DKTG: $\text{5 \%}$ maximum for displacement, and $\text{8\%}$ for moment and curvature.
- Q4GG: $8\text{\%}$ maximum for displacement, and $3\text{\%}$ for moment and curvature.

It can therefore be estimated that these modellings validate DKTG Modelling and GLRC Modelling in elastic behavior, Q4GG Modelling and ELAS Modelling.
