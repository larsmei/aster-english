# u3.13.12 Modelling D_PLAN_ABSO

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modelling D_PLAN_ABSO:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the associated support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear capabilities,
- the test cases implementing the modelling.

The modelling D_PLAN_ABSO (Phenomenon: mechanics) corresponds to finite elements whose support mesh cells are line mesh cells. They make it possible to take into account the absorbing boundary condition of sections of solids, studied in plane strain.

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

| **Modelling** | **Degrees of freedom (at each vertex node)** |
| --- | --- |

| D_PLAN_ABSO | DX: displacement along $X$ |
| --- | --- |
|  | DY: displacement along $Y$ |

### 1.2 Support mesh cells of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the finite elements are segments. The elements are isoparametric.

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Interpolation** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| D_PLAN_ABSO | SEG2 | linear |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | SEG3 | quadratic |  |

### 1.3 Support mesh cells of the loadings

The same as previously.

## 2 Supported loadings

The available loading is the following:

- **'ONDE_PLANE'**

Makes it possible to impose a seismic loading by a plane wave.

## 3 Non-linear capabilities

### 3.1 Constitutive laws

The only constitutive relation available under DYNA_NON_LINE, for this modelling, under COMPORTEMENT is the relation 'ELAS'.

### 3.2 Strains

Only the linearised strains, keyword 'PETIT' under DEFORMATION, are available.

## 4 Implementation example: test case

- Non-linear dynamics

SDLV120B [V2.04.120](doc:v2.04.120): Analysis of the propagation of a compression wave in an infinite elastic bar (absorption of the wave at the boundary of the mesh).
