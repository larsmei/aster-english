# v4.23.101 TTLP101 - Frusted plate at prescribed temperatures with exchange condition through crack lips

**Summary**

This test involves a square plate with a straight crack opening, subjected to a temperature gradient. A thermal exchange takes place between the lips of the crack (factor keyword ECHANGE_PAROI of the operators AFFE_CHAR_THER and AFFE_CHAR_THER_F [U4.44.02](doc:u4.44.02)).

Three modellings are considered:

- $A$ Modelling: $\text{FEM}\mathrm{2D}$ (mesh crack)
- $B$ Modelling: $\text{X-FEM}\mathrm{2D}$ , crack in the middle of elements
- $C$ Modelling: $\text{X-FEM}\mathrm{3D}$ , crack in the middle of elements

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The $\mathrm{2d}$ structure is a unit square ($\mathit{LX}=1m$ , $\mathit{LY}=1m$ ), with a straight crack opening to the right, located at mid-height. (Figure ) The left line is called the $x=0$ line , the right line is called the $x=\mathit{LX}$ line and the bottom line is called the $y=0$ line .

![Figure](/figures/v4.23.101/100000000000020E000001C38B9C795AD10E9DE2.png)

**Figure** 1.1-a **: Geometry of the cracked square plate**

${P}^{+}$ is the point coordinates $\left(\mathit{LX},{\mathit{LY}}^{+}/2\right)$ (located on the upper lip), ${P}^{-}$ the point coordinates $(\mathit{LX},{\mathit{LY}}^{-}/2)$ (located on the lower lip), and $Q$ the point coordinates $\left(\mathit{LX}/2,\mathit{LY}/2\right)$ (located on the crack tip).

### 1.2 Material properties

Thermal conductivity: $\lambda =1{\mathit{W.m}}^{\text{-1}}.{K}^{\text{-1}}$

Volumetric heating capacity: $\rho {C}_{p}=2\mathit{J.m}-{\mathrm{3.K}}^{-1}$

Exchange coefficient between the lips of the $h=2{\mathit{W.m}}^{\text{-2}}.{K}^{\text{-1}}$ crack

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The problem is solved on the time interval $\left[0.s,1.s\right]$ discretized in 5 time step equal (of duration $\Delta t=0.2s$). The default value is taken in THER_LINEAIRE of the parameter of the theta-schema: $\theta =0.57$ .

The following temperature ramp shall be imposed on nodes segment $\mathit{AB}$ (see Figure ):

à $t=0.s$ , $\text{}\stackrel{̄}{T}{\rbrace ^{\mathit{AB}}=10°C$ ;            à $t=1.s$ , $\text{}\stackrel{ˉ}{T}{\rbrace ^{\mathit{AB}}=20°C$

The following temperature ramp shall be imposed on nodes segment $\mathit{CD}$ (see Figure ):

à $t=0.s$ , $\text{}\stackrel{̄}{T}{\rbrace ^{\mathit{CD}}=20°C$ ;            à $t=1.s$ , $\text{}\stackrel{̄}{T}{\rbrace ^{\mathit{CD}}=40°C$

Finally, on the lips of the crack, a Neumann condition of type exchange condition is imposed.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The initial state is determined by solving the stationary problem at $t=0.s$ (with the boundary conditions given in paragraph 1.3.)

The reference solution is obtained by refining the mesh Modelling A (elements classic with mesh crack): mesh regular compound of $500\times 500$ QUAD4 (instead of $100\times 100$ QUAD4 for mesh A)

## 3 Modelling A

In this Modelling, the $\mathrm{2D}$ structure is considered. The classic elements method is used. The crack is meshed, so the exchange condition between the crack lips is applied using the GROUP_MA1/GROUP_MA2 keywords of the factor keyword ECHANGE_PAROI operators AFFE_CHAR_THER and AFFE_CHAR_THER [U4.44.02](doc:u4.44.02). This Modelling serves as a reference for the next model.

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

PLAN modelling of the THERMIQUE phenomenon is used.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a regular mesh composed of $100\times 100$ QUAD8, respectively on the $x$ and $y$ axes. The crack is meshed.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The temperature at the end of the last time step ($t=1.s$ ) is tested at ${P}^{+}$ , ${P}^{-}$ and $Q$ (see Figure ).

## 4 Modelization B: dimension 2 non-grinded crack

In this Modelling, the $\mathrm{2D}$ structure is considered. The extended elements finite method (X-FEM) is used. The crack is not meshed, so the exchange condition between the crack lips is applied using keyword FISSURE factor keyword ECHANGE_PAROI operator AFFE_CHAR_THER [U4.44.02](doc:u4.44.02).

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

PLAN modelling of the THERMIQUE phenomenon is used.

### 4.2 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a regular mesh composed of $101\times 101$ QUAD4, respectively, along the $x$ , $y$ axes. The crack is not meshed.

### 4.3 Tested quantities and results

The temperature at the end of the last time step ($t=1.s$) is tested at ${P}^{+}$ , ${P}^{-}$ and $Q$ (see Figure ). This is done by testing the temperature field after using POST_MAIL_XFEM and POST_CHAM_XFEM operators.

## 5 Modelization C: 3D non-mesh crack

In this Modelling, the $\mathrm{3D}$ structure is considered. The extended elements ($\text{X-FEM}$) method is used. The crack is not meshed, so the exchange condition between the crack lips is applied using keyword FISSURE factor keyword ECHANGE_PAROI operator AFFE_CHAR_THER_F [U4.44.02](doc:u4.44.02).

### 5.1 Modelling characteristics

3D modelling of the THERMIQUE phenomenon is used.

### 5.2 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a regular mesh composed of $11\times 11\times 1$ HEXA8, respectively, along the $x$ , $y$ and $z$ axes. The crack is not meshed.
