# v2.02.110 SDLL110 - Long beam in a false-to-fold beam under fluid-elastic and turbulent excitation: ANL tests

**Summary:**

This test case is part of the FLUSTRU [bib1] [bib2] software resorption [bib2], based on two sets of test results obtained from a campaign conducted by the ANL (Argonne National Laboratory - USA) [bib3] laboratory. Based on the configuration of this campaign, the calculation-test comparison validates the calculation by a frequent method of the linear vibration response of a tubular structure of beam-type on supports and in a beam-to-beam structure, subject to transverse external flow over a part of its length. The first step is to determine the effects of fluid-elastic coupling on the dynamic behaviour of the beam. These results in a variation of the values of the eigenfrequencies, modal damping and the vibration response level of the beam, depending on the rate of flow. The calculation-test comparison is for the vibration response level. This test case is also intended to prevent the regression of the work put into operation in the form of a possible FLUSTRU regression.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The tube is modelled using Timoshenko's straight beam elements: POU_D_T. It is broken down into 270 elements over four sections. $\mathrm{AB}$ and $\mathrm{CD}$ sections each comprise 61 elements, $\mathrm{BC}$ section, 60 elements and $\mathrm{DE}$ section, 88. At $A$ and $D$ points, degrees of freedom is blocked in $x$, $y$ and $z$ translation directions, and $z$ rotational direction. According to the results of the first series of tests, the rest fluid damping is taken at 1.3% and the added mass coefficient is 2.0711.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The total number of nodes used for this mesh is 271.

The mesh cells, of type SEG2, are 270.

The mesh file is in ASTER format.

### 3.3 Steps of calculation

The flow velocity profile and parameters taking into account fluid-structure coupling are defined using DEFI_FONC_FLUI and DEFI_FLUI_STRU operators. Modal parameters of the structure are calculated by taking into account the fluid-elastic forces using CALC_FLUI_STRU operator. The definition of random excitation is performed using DEFI_SPEC_TURB operator. The excitation is projected on the modal basis using PROJ_SPEC_BASE operator. Modal response interspectres are calculated using DYNA_SPEC_MODAL operator. The nodes self-sensing spectrospheres are derived from this using REST_SPEC_PHYS operator.

The spectral response calculation can only be performed for one flow speed at a time, so these last three steps (PROJ_SPEC_BASE, DYNA_SPEC_MODAL, REST_SPEC_PHYS) are performed in a loop, in the control file, where the flow speed list is searched.

### 3.4 Test values

[Figure 5.1-a] compares RMS displacement to point $E$ , expressed as a percentage of the tube's outer diameter, between:

- d-one part of the first ANL series of tests which covered 3 of the tubes of the tube beam in the same way as a steam generator beam,
- and on the other hand simulation using *Code_Aster* .

These results are presented in terms of the intertube rate of the incident flow, expressed in $m/s$ . The experimental results were obtained for discrete values of flow speeds. The calculation was conducted for 201 equally distributed velocity values over the $[0.5‑2.5m/s]$ interval , the calculated displacement appears as a continuous curve in the explored range of speeds. Experimentally as in the calculation, there is a strong increase in RMS displacement. This increase is associated with vibrational instability of the dynamic system, combined with the cancellation of its damping. The flow rate at which this increase occurs is the rate of instability of the system. The calculation allows the estimation of this speed at $2m/s$ while the experimental prognostic observation a value greater than $1,8m/s$ (instability tests are often stopped before instability is reached to avoid structural damage). In this configuration, the difference calculates the measurement of this essential result for the maintenance of steam generator tubes is therefore of the order of 10%.

### 3.6 Non-regression Tests

To ensure the non-regression of the code, TEST_FONCTION and TEST_TABLE operators are used in the command file. These two operators are able to test, on the one hand, the values of the eigenfrequencies and reduced damping, and on the other hand the RMS displacement. The tolerance is set at 1E–03 %.

## 5 Conclusion

This test case helps to verify the validity of the calculation by the frequency method available in *Code_Aster* of the linear vibratory response of a tubular structure of the beam type simply supported in a cross-flowing external flow over a part of its length. It is based on two sets of experimental results obtained during a test campaign carried out by the ANL (USA) laboratory. It appears that for this configuration, the dynamic system's instability speed, a very important quantity in optimizing the maintenance of steam generator tubes, is approached by 5 to 10%. Furthermore, the test case results in results that will be used as reference values to avoid regression of the main operators employed in the vibrational calculation of steam generator tubes.
