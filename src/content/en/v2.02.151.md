# v2.02.151 SDLL151 - Eigenmodes of a viscoelastic beam clamped - free

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the calculation of complex modes of a beam with both standard elastic material and viscoelastic material whose properties depend on frequency.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Rectangular section beam consisting of two layers of different materials:

Width: 0.01 m

Length: 0.15 m

Thickness: Elastic material (layer 1 below): 0.001 m

> Viscoelastic material (layer No. 2 above): 0.002 m

### 1.2 Material properties

The material of layer 1 of the bottom is elastic isotropic (steel); its properties are constant:

- Young's modulus $E=210000\mathit{MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0,3$
- density $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$
- hysteretic damping $\eta =0,001$

The material of the layer 2 of the top is viscoelastic (elastomer); some of its properties are dependent on the frequency:

- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0,45$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Clamped support on a steel edge.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The solution is calculated in a mixed analytical and numerical manner.

The dynamic rigidity (complex) of the viscoelastic beam is calculated by the equation:

${\mathit{EI}}^{\text{*}}={E}_{1}{I}_{1}\left(1+{e}_{2}{h}_{2}^{3}+\frac{3{(1+{h}_{2})}^{2}({e}_{2}\times {h}_{2})}{1+{e}_{2}\times {h}_{2}}\right)$         (1)

The meaning of the terms of the formula is given in the list below:

${E}^{\text{*}}$: Young's modulus of viscoelastic beam

$I$: second moment of inertia of the cross-section of the viscoelastic beam

${I}_{1}$: second moment of inertia of cross-section of material of layer 1

${E}_{j}^{\text{*}}$: Young's modulus of the material of the layer n°j

${e}_{2}$: ${E}_{2}^{\text{*}}/{E}_{1}$ module ratio (material damping is considered negligible)

${H}_{j}$: thickness of material of layer n° *j*

${h}_{2}$: ${H}_{2}/{H}_{1}$ thickness ratio

$\eta$ dampings of complex modes are then calculated as the ratio of the imaginary part of dynamic stiffness to its actual part:

$\eta =\frac{\Im (\mathit{EI})}{\Re (\mathit{EI})}$         (2)

Finally, the ${f}_{i}$ eigenfrequencies are calculated after [2] by the following equation:

${f}_{i}=\frac{{\lambda}_{i}^{2}}{{\mathrm{2\pi L}}^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{\Re (\mathit{EI})}{m}}$         (3)

With ${\lambda}_{i}$: modal coefficient (given in [2]) associated with the eigenfrequency ${f}_{i}$

$L$: length of the viscoelastic beam

> $m$: linear mass of viscoelastic beam

To take into account the frequency dependence of the mechanical properties of the viscoelastic material, the own damping and frequencies are calculated by an iterative method.
