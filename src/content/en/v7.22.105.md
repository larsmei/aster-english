# v7.22.105 HSNV105 - Traction-slicing plate: elasto-viscoplasticity with metallurgy

**Summary:**

This non-linear quasi-static mechanical test consists of loading a square plate into a tensile shear. It is largely inspired by SSNP14 [V6.03.014](doc:v6.03.014) and SSNP15 [V6.03.015](doc:v6.03.015) tests from the VPCS guide. The behavior relationship that we validate here is an elasto-viscoplastic behavior relationship that we introduced in *Code_Aster* for mechanical analyses that take into account metallurgy. It is an isotropic law of type Norton-Hoff with additive nutrification that can be restored.

The temperature and metallurgical state are constant, so no plasticity of transformation or restoration of metallurgical hardening is considered. Nor is there any viscous restoration of hardening.

The plate is modelled by a element volume (HEXA8).

The results obtained by *Code_Aster* are very close to the reference analytical solution (difference $\text{< 0.04\%}$).
