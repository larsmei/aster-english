# v1.01.116 ZZZZ116 – Validation of the theta field calculation in CALC_G

**Summary:**

This test is used to validate the calculation of the theta field used in the calculation of the energy return rate. It consists of two modellings, in 2D and 3D, having analytical solutions that can be calculated at certain points.

This test has no physical meaning (there is no displacement, deformation, stress) and is purely computer validation. Comparisons of the theta field with analytical solution are performed, as well as non-regression tests.

## 1 Summary of the results

This test case enabled the calculation and export of the theta field to be validated in CALC_G. The results are extremely close to the analytical solution.
