# u4.01.09 New features and modifications of version 13

**Summary:**

The purpose of this document is to provide an overall view of the syntax modifications and of the new possibilities of the **code_aster** commands introduced during the development of version 13, that is, since version 12.3.24.

For further details, one should consult the documentation of the commands and the histor files of the sub-versions.

## 1 Linear and non-linear dynamics

### 1.1 Operator DYNA_VIBRA

> The overhaul of the operator DYNA_VIBRA gave rise to the following changes:

- Reprogramming of the DEVOGE integration scheme and generalisation to all analyses.
- Availability of the ITMI integration scheme for any transient analysis and not only IFS.
- Introduction of a new method for the treatment of shocks, drawing on the particular features of IFS, with the keyword TRAITEMENT_NONL = 'EXPLICITE'/'IMPLICITE'.
- Change to archiving in order to allow the user to force the computation and the archiving of the time instants of interest.
- Treatment of localised non-linear forces (options TRAN / GENE).
- Introduction of unidirectional friction for the constitutive law DIS_CHOC via the keyword UNIDIRECTIONNEL=/'OUI'/'NON'.

### 1.2 MISS3D coupling

- Improvement of CALC_MISS / DEFI_SOL_MISS with more extensive automation of the generation of the MISS3D data
- Improvements of DEFI_SOL_EQUI, including the introduction of an ONDE_PLANE loading of several types, with or without periodicity boundary conditions
- Modification of CALC_MISS / IMPEDANCE_TEMPORELLE for the output of a binary file

### 1.3 Tools

**Dynamic analysis on a modal basis:**

- Check of the physical meaning of the damping computed by the macro-command CALC_AMOR_MODAL

> **Post-processings of signals:**

- Computation of coherence functions from time signals via CALC_FONCTION/COHERENCE
- Interpolation of accelerograms via CALC_FONCTION/INTERPOL_FFT
- Introduction of the macro-command CALC_SPECTRE_IPM for the modelling of floor–equipment interaction
- Introduction of the macro-command LISS_SPECTRE in order to automate the smoothing of spectra.

## 2 Thermo-hydro-mechanics

### 2.1 Modellings

Membrane, plate and shell modelling:

- Introduction of a membrane modelling in large transformations
- Improvement of the DKT element with account taken of the third rotation in the plate elements
- Refactoring of the SHB elements

### 2.2 Loadings

> **Improvements:**

- Account taken of large displacements in the rigid links AFFE_CHAR_MECA/LIAISON_SOLIDE
- Account taken of the reflected wave in the plane-wave loading AFFE_CHAR_MECA_F/ONDE_PLANE/DIST_REFLECHI

### 2.3 Constitutive laws

- Improvement of the constitutive law ENDO_FISS_EXP with the modelling of crack closure.
- Introduction of the Iwan law for the modelling of the cyclic behaviour of soils
- Introduction of a Rankine model for the modelling of the behaviour of joints.
- Introduction of the LKR law (improvement of LETK by modifications of the hardening functions and introduction of the thermal part) for thermo-elasto(-visco)-plastic modelling applicable to rocks and to soils (dilatant materials).
- Modification of the constitutive law GRAN_IRRA_LOG for the modellings of guide tubes.

### 2.4 Post-processings

**Engineering post-processings:**

- Introduction of the macro-command POST_LIQUEFACTION for the post-processing of a criterion for detection of soil liquefaction.
- Changes to the operator POST_RCCM.

**Visualisations:**

- Addition of features for the visualisation of the generalised forces of structural elements in the form of diagrams on the mesh.
- Addition of features for the visualisation of the parameters of ELAS_FO.

## 3 Fracture mechanics

> **X-FEM elements:**

- Optimisation of the cutting into sub-elements in the quadratic case for the X-FEM elements.
- Introduction of Lagrange multipliers for the X-FEM hydro-mechanical models.

## 4 Numerical methods

### 4.1 Model reduction (ROM)

- Introduction of the ROM ( *Reduced Order Model* )
- Introduction of the post-processing operator for the ROM: REST_REDUIT_COMPLET
- Application of model reduction in vibro-acoustics

### 4.2 Contact

**New contact method:**

- Introduction of the LAC method and improvement of segment–segment pairing;
- Parallelisation of the “loop” over the slave mesh cells.

## 5 Architecture, ergonomics, performance

### 5.1 Ergonomics

**Simplified programming**

- Factorisation of the finite-element catalogues
- Improvement of the performance of reading the mesh in *.med format

> **Simplification of the post-processings:**

- Naming of the internal variables according to the mechanical terminology

**Removal:**

- Removal of the command DEFI_PARTITION
- Removal of printout in CASTEM format
- Removal of direct access to the keywords NOEUD and MAILLE

### 5.2 Performance

**Version upgrade for the tools:**

- MUMPS version 5.1
- Version of the METIS renumberer: from 4.0 (Sept 1998) to 5.1.0 (March 2013).
- Version of PETSc (3.7.3)
- MED version 3.2.1

> **Addition of new features related to the MUMPS and PETSc solvers:**

- Introduction of a new value of the keyword SOLVEUR/POSTTRAITEMENTS='MINI'
- Introduction of the LowRank method via the new keywords SOLVEUR/LOW_RANK_TAILLE and SOLVEUR/LOW_RANK_SEUIL.
- Automatic activation of threads
- Parallel analysis phase in MUMPS
- Second-level preconditioner LMP.

Improvement of parallelism:

- Authorisation of the parallelism of elementary computations for macro-elements
- Introduction of a hybrid distribution mode for elementary computations (SOUS_DOMAINE)
