# v6.04.214 SSNV214 - constitutive law BETON_RAG: cyclic loading of a concrete test piece

**Summary:**

This document presents a test to validate the capabilities of the BETON_RAG behavior model, used to estimate the long-term behaviour of structures affected by the alkali-granulat reaction. The behaviour of a test piece under cyclic loading in single tensile mode is simulated.

## 1 Reference problem

This test is intended to validate the damage part of the BETON_RAG behavior model.

### 1.1 Geometry

The test is based on a single cubic finite element (1m x 1m).

### 1.2 Ownership of constitutive law

Young's modulus:        $E=32000\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio:        $\nu =0.2$

Fragility parameter of tensile concrete: $\mathit{MT}=1,7$

Fragility parameter of compression concrete: $\mathit{MC}=1,5$

Equivalent tensile force of concrete: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{ft}}=5.66\mathit{MPa}$

Equivalent stress of compression concrete: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{fc}}=38.3\mathit{MPa}$

Angle of Drucker Prager's criterion: $\alpha =0,15\mathit{rad}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The load applied to nodes of the X=1m plane consists of two tensile loading cycles and then two compression loading cycles:

## 2 reference solution

This test validates the model's ability to reproduce uniaxial cyclic loading. The imposed deformation steering allows you to traverse the entire curve, including the softening part. No irreversible deformation is visible since the part of the model tested is elastic and can be damaged. The results of the model are compared with the results of the Benchmark cited in the bibliographic reference.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Characteristic of modelling

The problem is modelled in 3D.

To converge model A uses the elastic matrix throughout the simulation. Using the tangent matrix, throughout the simulation, leads to non-convergence when damage becomes too large.

This test case allows the change from the tangent matrix to the elastic matrix to be validated as soon as the time step (after subdivision) becomes below a given threshold. So we start with the tangent matrix and then when the subdivision we switch to an elastic matrix.
