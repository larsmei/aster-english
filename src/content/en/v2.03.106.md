# v2.03.106 SDLS106 - Modal calculation of a rectangular plate simply pressed on all its edges

**Summary:**

This modal analysis field test implements the calculation of clean bending modes of a rectangular plate simply pressed on all its edges.

The calculation is done

- under-structured on Ritz modal basis, with a Craig-Bampton or Mac Neal interface;
- either directly.

The reference solution is analytical, both for eigenfrequencies and modal deformities.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 reference solution

After [bib1], the clean vibration frequencies of a rectangular plate supported on all its edges are given by the analytical formula:

${f}_{ij}=\frac{{\lambda}_{ij}^{2}}{2\pi {L}^{2}}{\left[\frac{E{h}^{3}}{12{\rho}_{s}(1-{\nu}^{2})}\right]}^{\frac{1}{2}}$

with

${\lambda}_{ij}^{2}={\pi}^{2}\left[{i}^{2}+{\left(\frac{L}{l}\right)}^{2}{j}^{2}\right]$

où

$i$ and $j$ are the number of half waves of the modal deformed along the large axis and small axis of the plate. ${\rho}_{s}$ is the mass per unit surface.

Either

$\begin{array}{c}{f}_{11}=17,13\mathit{Hz}\\ {f}_{21}=35,63\mathit{Hz}\\ {f}_{12}=50,01\mathit{Hz}\\ {f}_{31}=66,46\mathit{Hz}\\ {f}_{22}=68,51\mathit{Hz}\end{array}$ .

Modal deformities are also calculated analytically: the ${z}_{ij}$ modal shift perpendicular to the plate, for $(i,j)$ mode, depending on point coordinates $(x,y)$ , is given by:

${z}_{ij}(x,y)=\sin\left(\frac{i\pi x}{L}\right)\sin\left(\frac{j\pi y}{l}\right)$ .

### 2.2 Case of substructure calculations: reference solution of each substructure

Each substructure is a $l=1,5m$ long and $\frac{L}{2}=1m$ wide plate, pressed on three sides and clamped on a long side, vibrant in bending.

[bib1] It is shown that eigenfrequencies are:

${f}_{ij}=\frac{{\lambda}_{ij}^{2}}{2\pi {l}^{2}}{\left[\frac{E{h}^{3}}{12{\rho}_{s}(1-{\nu}^{2})}\right]}^{\frac{1}{2}}$

with ${\lambda}_{11}^{2}=42,53$ , ${\lambda}_{21}^{2}=69,00$ , ${\lambda}_{31}^{2}=116,30$ , ${\lambda}_{12}^{2}=121,00$

what gives for the first frequencies:

$\begin{array}{}{f}_{11}=47,26\mathrm{Hz}\\ {f}_{21}=76,57\mathrm{Hz}\\ {f}_{31}=129,24\mathrm{Hz}\\ {f}_{12}=134,47\mathrm{Hz}\end{array}$

### 2.3 Bibliographic reference

1. BLEVINS R.D : Formulas for natural frequency and mode shape. Ed. Krieger 1984.

## 11 Summary of the results

The modal calculation was validated on the proper bending modes of a plate simply pressed on its four edges, for both the eigenfrequencies and the modal deformities.

The results are satisfactory, whether the calculation is done directly or by substructure with modal base type "Ritz".
