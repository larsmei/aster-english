# v6.02.122 SSNL122 - Multifibre cantilever beam under stress

**Summary:**

This test concerns multifibre beam validation with POU_D_TGM Modelling.

This problem allows testing:

- elements finished linear beams type with POU_D_TGM Modelling,
- the consideration of guidance,
- the calculation of SIEF_ELGA and EFGE_ELNO.

### 1.2 Material properties

Elasto-plastic behavior material with linear nutring, for linear element:

Elasticity:

- Young's modulus $E=2.1E+\mathrm{11 }\mathrm{Pa}$

Plasticity:

- slope of the traction curve in the plastic domain $\text{d\_sigm\_epsi}=1.0E+08\mathrm{Pa}$
- $\mathrm{sy}=400.0E+\mathrm{06 }\mathrm{Pa}$ elastic limit

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Point $A$ , clamped support perfect (locking of movements and rotations),

- degrees of freedom blocking: $\mathrm{DX},\mathrm{DY},\mathrm{DZ},\mathrm{DRX},\mathrm{DRY},\mathrm{DRZ}$ .
- Loading to point $B$: $F=({F}_{x},{F}_{y},{F}_{z})$ .

### 3.2 Numbering of fibres

The main $(\mathrm{Y0},\mathrm{Z0})$ inertia marker of the beam must be turned 90° so that the strongest $\mathrm{IZ}$ inertia is on the $Y$ axis of the global marker. The goal is to test the ORIENTATION keyword of the AFFE_CARA_ELEM command.

| **In the local beam marker** | **In the global benchmark of study** |
| --- | --- |
| ![Figure](/figures/v6.02.122/Object_9.svg) | ![Figure](/figures/v6.02.122/Object_10.svg) |

Several types of fibres are used:

- fibres with numbers ranging from 1 to 32. They are assigned a non-zero area in the command file,
- The fibers are located at the 4 corners of the section. These fibers, in the control file, are assigned a section equal to zero. They are used only in post-processing,
- The fibers are located on the outer edge of the section. These fibres, in the control file, are assigned a section equal to zero. They are only used in post-processing.
