# v6.04.147 SSNV147 - Traction of a damaged bar: validation of the steering

**Summary:**

The use of softening laws can lead to rough *snap-back* that makes it difficult to run the calculation. To track these instabilitys, two types of steering are available in *Code_Aster*: elastic prediction (PRED_ELAS), which depends on the generic constitutive law and deformation steering (DEFORMATION). To validate these techniques, a tensile test is carried out on a square section bar whose section has been weakened to cause the localization of damage.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry and boundary conditions

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.147/Object_1.svg)

**Figure** 1.1-a **: geometry of the structure studied**

## 2 reference solution

The values tested are non-regression, except for modellings K and L.

For the latter, the PRED_ELAS control method allows the maximum damage to be controlled, so that the reference value available (the sum of the expected increments) at the end of the loading process is available. Typically, after 2 increments with target steering of 0.1, the damage must be equal to 0.1 + 0.1 = 0.2 when the maximum damage occurs at the same place for both increments (wherein the choice of an Poisson's ratio of 0 for the modellings K and L).
