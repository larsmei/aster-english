# v3.06.310 SSLA310 – Radial crack in a bimaterial subjected to internal pressure

**Summary:**

This test case deals with a axisymmetric problem, in the finished domain, with a crack located at the interface between the two materials. The materials are isotropic linear elastic. The model is subjected to a pressure loading on the lips and has a punctual load in the center of the lips of the crack.

The objective of this test case is to validate G integration for bi-materials for different Young's modulus ratios, and different Poisson's ratio ratios.

The reference solution is calculated by element finished with ABAQUS.

The calculation of $G$ is performed on different crowns surrounding the crack bottom.

The first crown with a zero radius gives poor results compared to the other crowns whose results are excellent since they lead to variations lower than $7$ %.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results for the $G$ energy return rate are correct because the difference is less than $7$ % (excluding the calculation on the first crown with a zero radius).

The stability of the $G$ calculation on the other crowns is excellent as it leads to variations below $0,05$ %.

The use of crowns with a lower radius of zero is prohibited.
