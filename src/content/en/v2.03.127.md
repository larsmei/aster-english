# v2.03.127 SDLS127 - Harmonic response of a viscoelastic sandwich plate clamped on one edge

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the calculation of the harmonic response of a structure with both standard elastic materials and viscoelastic materials whose properties depend on frequency.

Three modellings are made:

- Modelling A: direct calculation on a physical basis, by calculating frequency assemblies by frequency; this Modelling serves as a reference;
- Modelling B: preliminary calculation of actual eigenmodes, then harmonic calculation on modal basis;
- Modelling C: Prior calculation of improved real eigenmodes ("beta-modes"), then harmonic calculation on modal basis.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.127/Cadre3.gif)

Image 1.1-1: Geometry of sandwich plate.

Rectangular sandwich plate consisting of three layers of different materials:

> Side 0.05 m x 0, 15 m

> Thickness: aluminium (top): 0.5 mm

> viscoelastic material (centre): 1 mm
> steel (below): 1 mm

### 1.2 Material properties

The material of the upper layer is aluminium (isotropic elastic); its properties are constant:

- Young's modulus $E=70000\mathit{MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0,3$
- density $\rho =2700\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$
- hysteretic damping $\eta =0,001$

The material of the bottom layer is steel (isotropic elastic); its properties are constant:

- Young's modulus $E=210000\mathit{MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0,3$
- density $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$
- hysteretic damping $\eta =0,002$

The material of the central layer is viscoelastic (elastomer); some of its properties are dependent on frequency:

| Frequency (Hz) | Real part of Young's modulus $E$ (MPa) | Loss factor $\eta$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 23,2 | 1,1 |
| 10 | 58 | 0,85 |
| 50 | 145 | 0,7 |
| 100 | 203 | 0,6 |
| 500 | 348 | 0,4 |
| 1000 | 435 | 0,35 |
| 1500 | 464 | 0,34 |

Table 1.2-1: Propriétés dépendantes de la fréquence du matériau viscoélastique.

The rest are constant:

- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0,45$
- density $\rho =1200\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

boundary conditions:

- clamped support on one edge of the steel layer.

Loading:

- Nodal force at point A: FZ=1.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Not applicable (harmonic calculation).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The co-located harmonic response to point A is calculated by a direct frequency method.

- a viscoelastic material is defined whose properties are interpolated from data of the
- The mass and hysteretic rigidity assemblies are calculated;
- The equation of dynamics in physical basis (operator DYNA_VIBRA [U4.53.03](doc:u4.53.03)) is solved.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The tests are performed on the frequency response co-located with point A at 1 Hz, 100 Hz and 500 Hz.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Digital solution.
