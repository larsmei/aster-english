# u3.24.01 Thermal modellings 3D and 3D_DIAG

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the thermal modellings 3D and 3D_DIAG:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the associated support mesh cells,
- the supported materials and loadings,
- the non-linear possibilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings

The modelling 3D (phenomenon THERMIQUE) corresponds to finite elements whose support mesh cells are volume mesh cells.

The modelling 3D_DIAG covers the same possibilities as 3D and differs from it only for a thermal analysis in which the thermal mass matrix is then diagonalised before solution.

## 2 Supported loadings

The loadings available are the following:

- **SOURCE**

Makes it possible to apply volumetric sources to a 3D domain.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_DIAG

- **FLUX_REP**

Makes it possible to apply normal fluxes to faces of volume elements.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_DIAG

- **ECHANGE**

Makes it possible to apply exchange conditions with an external temperature to faces of volume elements.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_DIAG

- **ECHANGE_PAROI**

Makes it possible to apply exchange conditions between two walls.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_DIAG

- **GRAD_TEMP_INIT**

Makes it possible to impose a temperature gradient assumed to be uniform in an element

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_DIAG

- **CONVECTION**

Makes it possible to take into account the heat-transport terms by convection, for THER_NON_LINE_MO only.

Supported modelling: 3D, 3D_DIAG

- **RAYONNEMENT**

Makes it possible to take into account the flux radiated to infinity on faces of volume elements

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_DIAG

## 3 Non-linear possibilities

Two operators are available for the study of non-linear behaviours:

- THER_NON_LINE [U4.54.02](doc:u4.54.02): this operator makes it possible, in steady state or in transient regime, to solve the problems of:
- Standard non-linear thermal analysis: material depending on the temperature, boundary conditions (radiation and non-linear imposed flux),
- Non-linear thermal analysis with analysis of the hydration of the concrete,
- Drying of the concrete.
- THER_NON_LINE_MO [U4.54.03](doc:u4.54.03): this operator makes it possible to solve the steady heat equation in a moving reference frame linked to a loading and moving in a given direction and at a given speed.

## 4 Examples of implementation: test cases

### 4.1 Standard thermal

- **3D**
- Linear steady thermal analysis

TPLL100A [V4.02.100](doc:v4.02.100): Thermal analysis of an anisotropic plane wall subjected to an imposed temperature and to a flux.

- Linear transient thermal analysis

TTLL01C [V4.21.001](doc:v4.21.001): Linear transient thermal analysis of an infinite wall subjected to a thermal shock.

- Non-linear steady thermal analysis

TPNA01A [V4.41.001](doc:v4.41.001): Thermal analysis of a hollow cylinder whose inner wall is subjected to radiation and whose outer wall is subjected to an exchange by convection.

- Non-linear transient thermal analysis

TTNL03A [V4.22.003](doc:v4.22.003): simulation of an adiabatic test: analysis of the thermo-hydrating behaviour of a sample of fresh concrete immersed in a calorimeter, the setting taking place with release of heat.

- Non-linear steady thermal analysis with a moving load

TPLV105A [V4.04.105](doc:v4.04.105): This test presents the thermal simulation by finite elements of the Varestraint test. This weldability test is employed to characterise the resistance to hot cracking of the materials.

- **3D_DIAG**
- Linear transient thermal analysis

TTLL01J [V4.21.001](doc:v4.21.001): Linear transient thermal analysis of an infinite wall subjected to a thermal shock.

### 4.2 Hydration

- **3D**

TTNL03A [V4.22.003](doc:v4.22.003): simulation of an adiabatic test: analysis of the thermo-hydrating behaviour of a sample of fresh concrete immersed in a calorimeter, the setting taking place with release of heat.

- **3D**
