# v6.01.120 SSNA120 – axisymmetric tester with elements seal and interface

**Summary:**

Modelling A ensures that non-regression features Code_Aster in breaking mechanics and compares it with a local approach (Rousselier). The tested feature is the cohesive law for ductile breakage: CZM_TRA_MIX [R7.02.11](doc:r7.02.11).

A cut axisymmetric test piece is pressed into tensile. The evolution of the force and the diametrel contraction during the propagation of ductile rupture is calculated. The Modelling of the test piece is performed with elements $\mathrm{2D}$ (QUA8).

Moreover, Modelling B allows to validate elements joints in xis (AXIS_JOINT) with the CZM_LIN_REG law.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry and loading

A axisymmetric type cut-out test piece is considered. The cohesive area represented by elements interface (modelling A) or elements joint (Modelling B) is positioned on the $\mathrm{AB}$ line.

![Figure](/figures/v6.01.120/10000200000001200000024E7BC4E8374B2298D7.png)

**Figure 1**: $\mathrm{AE4}$ test piece geometry.

### 1.2 Material properties

The behaviour of the axisymmetric test piece material (densal material) is described by using an elastoplastic constitutive law with an isotropic hardening (law VMIS_ISOT_TRAC).

Take: $E=207\mathit{GPa}$ and $\nu =0.3$ and the selected nutring curve is given below:

![Figure](/figures/v6.01.120/10000200000001C100000134E1017A238A1EAC7A.png)

**Figure 2**: isotropic hardening curve of the volume material.

The crack is represented with two different laws for modellings A and B:

**Model A:**

For interface elements the following parameters are used in CZM_TRA_MIX law:

${\sigma}_{c}=1200\mathit{MPa}$ , ${G}_{c}=130\mathit{MPa}\cdot \mathit{mm}$ , ${\delta}_{e}=0.01\mathit{mm}$ , ${\delta}_{p}=0.07\mathit{mm}$ , ${\delta}_{c}=0.157\mathit{mm}$ ,

The resulting law is schematized below.

![Figure](/figures/v6.01.120/10000200000001600000010008F7320B67E65E25.png)

**Figure 3**: constitutive law of the elements interface.

NB: Only half of the crack is modelled due to symmetry of the problem, the tenacity of the material is ${\mathrm{2G}}_{c}$.

**Model B:**

For the elements joint the following parameters are used in the CZM_LIN_REG law:

${\sigma}_{c}=1200\mathit{MPa}$ , ${G}_{c}=130\mathit{MPa}\cdot \mathit{mm}$ , $\mathit{pena}\mathit{adherence}=1.E-5$

NB: Only half of the crack is modelled due to symmetry of the problem, the tenacity of the material is ${\mathrm{2G}}_{c}$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

See Figure 1 for the boundary conditions:

- $X$ motion blocked on line $\mathrm{AD}$,
- $l$ forced displacement following $Y$ direction on line $\mathrm{DC}$ .

The evolution of the $l$ displacement over time is given in the following table:

| Time $[s]$ | 0 | 1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moving $l$ $[\mathit{mm}]$ | 0 | 0,5 |

The cohesive area is represented by the elements interface on the $\mathrm{AB}$ line. The top lip of the elements interface is called $\mathrm{AB}$ and the lower lip is called $A'B'$. The boundary conditions on the elements interface are:

- $X$ displacement imposed on $\mathrm{AB}$ and $A'B'$ lips: ${\mathrm{DX}}_{\mathrm{AB}}={\mathrm{DX}}_{A'B'}$
- $Y$ motion blocked on line $A'B'$ .

## 2 reference solution

The node $B$ displacement following $X$ (DEPL) and the force applied to the test piece (REAC_NODA) were calculated.

**For modelling A:**

A comparison is made with two different calculations:

- an earlier Code_Aster execution with CZM_TRA_MIX law, it is an test case non-regression;
- an earlier Code_Aster execution where ductile rupture is modelled with the Rousselier model by ROUSS_PR law.

In the case of Modelling with the Rousselier model, the parameters selected for this model are: $D=2$ , ${\mathit{SIGM}}_{1}=460$ and $\mathit{f0}=0,0005$

**For Modelling B:** only non-regression tests are performed.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Modelling of ductile rupture is done with AXIS_INTERFACE Modelling and CZM_TRA_MIX law. Volume elements are modeled with AXIS Modelling.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The input mesh is linear. It is converted into a quadratic mesh by LINE_QUAD in CREA_MAILLAGE.

After transformation its characteristics are as follows:

Number of nodes: 962

Number of elements: 280 QUAD8.

Number of elements interface: 15 QUAD8.
