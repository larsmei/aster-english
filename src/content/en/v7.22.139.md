# v7.22.139 HSNV139 - Traction-slicing plate: elastic-plasticity with metallurgy

**Summary:**

This non-linear quasi-static mechanical test consists of loading a square plate into a tensile shear. It is largely inspired by SSNP14 [V6.03.014](doc:v6.03.014) and SSNP15 [V6.03.015](doc:v6.03.015) VPCS tests.

The objective of this test case is the validation of two elasto-plastic constitutive lawss with metallurgy, in the case of a material of type **Zircaloy**:

- constitutive law elastoplastics from VonLinear kinematic nutring machines,
- constitutive law elasto-plastic Von isotropic linear hardening devices, in large deformations.

The temperature and metallurgical state are constant. The elasto-plastic behavior with metallurgy is compared to the equivalent elasto-plastic behavior without metallurgy.

The plate is modelled by a element volume (HEXA8).

### 1.2 Material properties

The material properties are described by the following parameters:

** Thermo-metallurgical parameters:**

- Zircaloy:

  - $\rho {C}_{p}={2.0E}^{-3}{\mathit{J.mm}}^{-3.}°{C}^{-1}$
  - $\lambda =9.9999{\mathit{W.mm}}^{-1.}°{C}^{-1}$
- Metallurgy coefficients:

  - $\mathit{teqd}=809°C$ , $K=1.135{E}^{-2}$ , $n=2.187$
  - $\mathit{t1c}=831°C$ , $\mathit{t2c}=0.$ , $\mathit{qsr}=14614$ , $\mathit{Ac}=1.58E-4$
  - $m=4.7$ , $\mathit{t1r}=949,1°C$ , $\mathit{t2r}=0.$ , $\mathit{Ar}=-5.725$ , $\mathit{Br}=0.05$

** Thermo-metal-mechanical parameters:**

- Thermo-metal-elastic parameters:

  - Young's modulus : $E=195000\mathit{MPa}$
  - Poisson's ratio : $\nu =0.3$
  - Mean thermal expansion coefficient of cold phases: ${\alpha}_{f}=15E-6$
  - Average heat expansion coefficient of the hot phases: ${\alpha}_{\gamma}=23E-6$
  - Definition temperature of the expansion coefficient: ${T}_{\mathit{ref}}=600°C$
  - Selection of the reference metallurgical phase : $\mathit{Froide}$
  - Deformation of the non-reference phase from the reference phase, at ${T}_{\mathit{ref}}$: $\Delta {\epsilon}_{f\gamma }^{{T}_{\mathit{ref}}}={2.52E}^{-3}$
  - Cold phase yield limit 1: ${\sigma}_{y,1}=181\mathit{MPa}$
  - Cold phase yield limit 2: ${\sigma}_{y,2}=181\mathit{MPa}$
  - Hot phase elasticity limit: ${\sigma}_{y,\gamma }=0\mathit{MPa}$
  - Mixing function (calculation of the yield limit of the multiphase material): $\mathit{fonction}\mathit{identité}$
- Thermo-metal-plastic parameters (law with linear nutring)

  - Cold phase 1 traction curve penetrating : ${E}_{T,1}=1930\mathit{MPa}$
  - Cold phase 2 traction curve penetrating : ${E}_{T,2}=1930\mathit{MPa}$
  - Hot phase traction curve penetrating : ${E}_{T,\gamma }=0\mathit{MPa}$

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

![Figure](/figures/v7.22.139/1000068E00001B63000017AB3D137CA7BDA6AF2A.svg)

The loading and boundary conditions are modelled by:

On the node N04, DX=DY=0

On the node N08, DX=DY=DZ=0

On the node N02, DX=0.

On the node N06, DX=0.

Nodal force is imposed on:

(N01 N03 N05 N07), $\mathit{FX}=-\text{}\frac{1}{4}{\sigma}_{d}(t)$ , $\mathit{FY}=-\text{}\frac{1}{4}{\tau}_{d}(t)$

(N03 N04 N07 N08), $\mathit{FX}=-\text{}\frac{1}{4}{\tau}_{d}(t)$

(N02 N04 N06 N08), $\mathit{FY}=\frac{1}{4}{\tau}_{d}(t)$

(N01 N02 N05 N06), $\mathit{FX}=\frac{1}{4}{\tau}_{d}(t)$

The mechanical calculation is done with the constitutive law elasto-plastic from VonMises with linear kinematic nutring (keyword'RELATION = META_P_CL').
