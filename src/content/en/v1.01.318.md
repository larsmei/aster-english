# v1.01.318 ZZZZ318 – Validation of SOUS_POINT method of the PROJ_CHAMP command

**Summary:**

The objective is to validate the SOUS_POINT method of the PROJ_CHAMP command. To do this, you must check that:

- The subpoint coordinates on the families of the MATER list calculated by PROJ_CHAMP are accurate for elements POU_D_EM, POU_D_TGM and DKT, GRILLE_EXCENTREE (TRIA3 and QUAD4).
- The thermal field projection (TEMP, HYDR_ELNO) on a 3D model is correct on the corresponding subpoint mechanical model.
- The projection works for an isolated field and for a EVOL_THER result.

Note: the fields that are output from this command are ELGA fields.
