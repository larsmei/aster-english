# v3.04.001 SSLV01 – Traction / compression of an oblique interface with CZM_ELAS_MIX behavior

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the constitutive law CZM_ELAS_MIX integration algorithm. The problem studied is the uniaxial tensile and compression of an interface, positioned obliquely in relation to the direction of the load. There is an analytical solution for this problem.

The problem is implemented:

- in 3D in Modelling A
- in C_PLAN in Modelling B

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is an elastic cube (or squared in 2D), 1,000 mm side and crossed by an oblique interface at 45°. The geometry is carried forward in figure, with the yellow interface and the associated marker at the bottom left. The cube is positioned so that $0\le x\le 1000$, $0\le y\le 1000$ and $0\le z\le 1000$.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material of the volume follows an elastic law and interface to the CZM_ELAS_MIX law. The characteristics are reported below:

| $E=30000\text{MPa}$ | Young's modulus |
| --- | --- |
| $\nu =0.2$ | Poisson's ratio |
| ${k}_{n}=20\text{MPa/mm}$ | Stiffness of the interface in the normal direction |
| ${k}_{t}=40\text{MPa/mm}$ | Stiffness of the interface in the tangent direction |

Furthermore, the increase coefficient of the Lagrangian (PENA_LAGR_ABSO), without impact here because the solution is homogeneous, is fixed to $r=600\text{MPa/mm}$ .

Finally, we examine the six possible adhesion combinations: normal elastic, unilateral or perfect adhesion and tangential elastic or perfect adhesion.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The cube is subjected to $\sigma ={\sigma}_{0}\text{}{e}_{x}\otimes {e}_{x}$ constraint. More precisely:

- $x=0$ face is blocked in ${u}_{x}=0$
- $y=0$ face is blocked in ${u}_{y}=0$
- $z=0$ face is blocked in ${u}_{z}=0$
- The $x=1000\text{mm}$ face is subjected to $T={\sigma}_{0}{e}_{x}$ overfacial force

These conditions also allow to block rigid body movements. The stress intensity ${\sigma}_{0}$ successively takes the values +2 MPa and -2 MPa.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

As this is an elastic problem, there is no need for initial conditions.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

This problem admits an analytical solution that is exposed in this part.

The load is such that the solution is homogeneous.The constraint that is installed in the cube is $\sigma ={\sigma}_{0}\text{}{e}_{x}\otimes {e}_{x}$. And the stress vector on the interface has as normal and tangential components ${\sigma}_{n}={\sigma}_{0}/2$ and ${\sigma}_{t}=-{\sigma}_{0}/2$ , where the local $(n,t,{e}_{z})$ marker is direct.

The result is a deformation in the volume equal to:

$\epsilon =\frac{{\sigma}_{0}}{E}({e}_{x}\otimes {e}_{x}-\nu {e}_{y}\otimes {e}_{y}-\nu {e}_{z}\otimes {e}_{z})$

By noting $\delta$ the displacement jump through the interface, it is deduced that the displacement in a point $x$ cube is:

$u(x)=\epsilon \cdot x+\delta H(x)$

where $H$ is the Heaviside function which is 0 to the left of the interface and 1 to the right.

Finally, the displacement jump through the interface depends on the choice of adhesion conditions. The following table gives the values of its normal and tangential components according to the combination of adhesion conditions chosen. The vertical component ${\delta}_{z}$ is zero in all cases.

|  | Normal elastic adhesion | Unilateral normal adherence | Normal adherenceperfect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Elastic tangential adhesion | ${\delta}_{n}={\sigma}_{n}/{k}_{n}$ ${\delta}_{t}={\sigma}_{t}/{k}_{t}$ | ${\delta}_{n}=⟨{\sigma}_{n}⟩/{k}_{n}$ ${\delta}_{t}={\sigma}_{t}/{k}_{t}$ | ${\delta}_{n}=0$ ${\delta}_{t}={\sigma}_{t}/{k}_{t}$ |
| Perfect tangential adherence | ${\delta}_{n}={\sigma}_{n}/{k}_{n}$ ${\delta}_{t}=0$ | ${\delta}_{n}=⟨{\sigma}_{n}⟩/{k}_{n}$ ${\delta}_{t}=0$ | ${\delta}_{n}=0$ ${\delta}_{t}=0$ |

### 2.2 Baseline

The displacement is evaluated at point $x=y=z=1000$ in 3D and $x=y=1000$ in 2D, depending on the analytical solution above.

It will be ensured that the calculation returns the expected displacement for all combinations of adhesion conditions and for both (traction and compression) of the stress.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

None.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

This is a 3D volume model and 3D_INTERFACE_S for the interface.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh volume is composed of 80 mesh cells TETRA10, the interface is composed of 10 mesh cells PENTA15.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results of Modelling A

The three DX, DY and DZ components of the $x=y=z=1000$ coordinate cube top displacement are tested for both +2 MPa and -2 MPa of the applied ${\sigma}_{0}$ force and for each of the six adhesion combinations (i.e. $3\times 2\times 6=36$ values tested). The accuracy is the default.

All results are in line with expected results.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

This is a C_PLAN model for volume and PLAN_INTERFACE_S for interface.

### 4.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh of the volume is composed of 10 TRIA6 mails, the interface is composed of 3 QUAD8 mails.

### 4.3 Tested quantities and results of Modelling A

The two components DX and DY of the displacement at the top of the $x=y=1000$ coordinate square for both +2 MPa and -2 MPa of the applied ${\sigma}_{0}$ force and for each of the six combinations of adhesion conditions (i.e. $2\times 2\times 6=24$ tested values) are tested. The accuracy chosen is that of the default.

All results are in line with expected results.

## 5 Summary of the results

There is an excellent agreement between Modelling and reference solution.
