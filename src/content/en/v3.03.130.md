# v3.03.130 SSLS130 - Structural Zoom: hole plate subjected to normal stress on a edge

**Summary:**

This test represents the static calculation of a free clamped hole plate bending by a normal effort applied to its free edge.

This is a process of applying the so-called structural zoom method, which improves the quality of a result obtained on an mesh which is too coarse or insufficiently detailed. The particular feature of the method is that the solution is calculated on an mesh located around the area of interest (patch), by applying as a load on the contour of the patch the movements (or forces) from the original model.

In this test, solutions obtained on a fine mesh and a coarse mesh after using structural zoom are compared.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a rectangular plate, holed in the centre:

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.130/Shape1.gif)

Thickness: $\mathrm{0.001m}$

### 1.2 Material properties

Classic homogeneous material on the whole structure: $E=7.1{10}^{10}$ , $\nu =0.3$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Clamped support on the 6 degrees of freedom on the left side of the plate.

Nodal force on right side of $\mathrm{100N}$ amplitude plate in $X$ and $\mathrm{1N}$ direction in $Z$ .

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is obtained by Modelling A. This is the same calculation obtained on an mesh with a degree of refinement similar to that of Modelling B after using the structural modification method.
