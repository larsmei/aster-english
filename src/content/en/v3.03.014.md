# v3.03.014 SSLS14 - Combination of static and seismic loads on the SMART model

**Summary**

The purpose of this test is to validate the operation of the POST_COMBINAISON command. The POST_COMBINAISON command allows the combination of different physical quantitiess following calculations to the elements finished upstream. This command will only perform post-processings by manipulating results in the form of fields from previous calculations.

The test uses the modal analysis of the reinforced concrete building model conducted during the SMART2013 (Seismic design and best-estimate Methods Assessment of Reinforced concrete buildings subject to Torsion and non-linear effects) campaign conducted by the CEA and EDF. This campaign focuses on the seismic study of irregular reinforced concrete buildings whose response is strongly affected by the torsion movements of the whole. The model represents an electrical reinforced concrete building of three floors on a scale of 1/4; it is carried out in the law of similarity of Cauchy-Froude (i.e. the eigenfrequencies increased by a ratio of 2).

The test checks the results obtained during calls to the POST_COMBINAISON command in cases where data structures of the sd_result type are to be combined.
