# v6.04.211 SSNV211 - Drained triaxial test with VISC_DRUC_PRAG

**Summary**

This test validates the VISC_DRUC_PRAG model based on Drucker-Prager elastoplastiqe model. The creep is taken into account by a power law of type Perzyna. It is a triaxial test in pure mechanics or drained condition. Calculations are performed only on the solid part of the soil without hydromechanical coupling. A containment level of $5\mathit{MPa}$ is applied. Due to symmetry, one only cares about the eighth d.a. sample subjected to a triaxial test. The Modelling is axisymmetric.

This is a non-regression test.

### 1.3 Initial conditions, boundary conditions, and loading

**Phase 1:**

The sample is taken to a homogeneous state: ${\sigma}_{xx}^{0}={\sigma}_{yy}^{0}={\sigma}_{zz}^{0}$ , by imposing the corresponding containment pressure on the front, right side and upper side. The movements are blocked on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$ ), left side (${u}_{y}=0$ ) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$ ) faces.

**Phase 2:**

The blocked movements on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$), left (${u}_{y}=0$) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$) sides are maintained, as well as the containment pressure on the front and right side. A displacement is applied to the upper face: ${u}_{z}(t)$ , so as to obtain a deformation ${\varepsilon}_{zz}=-6\text{}$ .
