# v6.03.501 SSNP501 - Crushing of a polyurethane ring between two non-frictionable plates

**Summary:**

The test consists of simulating the flat stress crushing of a circular polyurethane elastic ring by two unformable symmetrical plates. The objective is to test the contact-related features. This test involves a re-collage on a contact area of significant length with the presence of large elastic deformations.

A symmetrically imposed displacement is applied to both plates; the resulting force and contact pressure for different points in contact are compared with the results obtained in the reference article.

In the six modellingss proposed, the ring is modeled with mesh cells QUAD4 in flat constraints:

- **modelization A** , a contact nodes-mesh cells (`DEFI_CONTACT`) **without friction** treated with the active stress method was defined between the plate and the ring,
- **modelization B** , a contact nodes-mesh cells (`DEFI_CONTACT`) **without friction** treated with the penalised method was defined between the plate and the ring,
- **modelization C** , a contact nodes-mesh cells (`DEFI_CONTACT`) **without friction** treated with the continuous method was defined between the plate and the ring,
- **D** Modelling , a contact nodes-mesh cells (`DEFI_CONTACT`) **without friction** treated with the GCP method was defined between the plate and the ring,
- **modelization E** , a contact nodes-mesh cells (`DEFI_CONT`) **without friction** treated with the increased Lagrangian method was defined between the plate and the ring,
- **modelling F** , a contact nodes-mesh cells (`DEFI_CONT`) **without friction** treated with the penalised method was defined between the plate and the ring.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.501/10000000000001A90000011820715F75C39257AC.jpg)

| outer radius of the ring | $6,35\mathrm{cm}$ |
| --- | --- |
| inner radius of the ring | $4,15\mathrm{cm}$ |
| displacement imposed | $4,45\mathrm{cm}$ |

### 1.2 Material properties

Ring: polyurethane, constitutive law elastic.

| Young's modulus: | $E=407N/{\mathrm{cm}}^{2}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Poisson's ratio: | $\nu =0,48$ |
| Friction coefficient: | $\mu =0$ |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The constraints are flat.

An incremental displacement imposed from $0$ to $4,45\mathrm{cm}$ is applied to the nodes of the unformable plates.

**Note on units:**

The dimensions and movements are in centimetres, so to remain homogeneous, the pressures must be entered in $N/{\mathrm{cm}}^{2}$ .

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The solution is derived from a calculation code and an experimental test.

From the reference solution valid for whole plate modelling, the normal result must be divided by two to obtain a reference valid for a half-plate.

### 2.2 Baseline

The normal reaction force is as follows:

| Dutyed displacement ($\mathrm{cm}$ ) | Reaction force ($N$ ) |
| --- | --- |
| 1,1125 | 8,0083 |
| 2,2250 | 16,0166 |
| 3,3375 | 24,0250 |
| 4,4500 | 32,0333 |

The contact force is given as a reference. It is used to define validation tests, which are accompanied by non-regression tests with more stringent tolerances.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

These results are relatively approximate because they are measured directly on the paper curve.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

- A.F.SALEEB, K.CHEN, and T.Y.P.CHANG: "An effective two dimensional frictional contact model for arbitrary curved geometry" - Int.J.Num.Meth.Eng.37 (1994) p.1297‐1321.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Modelling testing the nodes-mesh cells (`DEFI_CONTACT`) contact features without friction treated with the active stress method has been implemented. Given the symmetry of the problem, it includes a quarter of the ring and the mesh of an unformable plate.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.501/1000000000000170000001C015F9DD4B7346FD34.png)

**boundary condition:**

| Symmetry conditions: | nodes of the $\mathrm{LAB}$ group located in the $X=0$ plane are blocked in the $X$ direction (DX=0), nodes of the $\mathrm{LCD}$ group located in the $Y=0$ plane are blocked in the $Y$ direction (DY=0), all nodes of the mesh cell $«\mathrm{Plaque}»$ group are blocked in the $X$ direction (DX=0) |
| --- | --- |

To avoid rigid body movements, the nodes $A$ and $\mathrm{P1}$ have the same vertical displacement.

**Charges:**

The $Y$ is the $Y$-imposed move on all nodes plates: $\mathrm{DY}$ varies from $0$ to $2,225\mathrm{cm}$.

(the value of $4,45\mathrm{cm}$ is the vertical approximation of the two symmetrical plates).

**Notes:**

*The mesh was made in * $\mathrm{cm}$ *.*

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The ring and plate are gridded in elements QUAD4, and the plate is rigidized because all these nodes have the same displacement.

Number of nodes: 290.

Number of mesh cells and type: 241 QUAD4 and 96 SEG2.

## 9 Summary of the results

Whatever the type of Modelling of the contact area, the results obtained are satisfactory. The differences observed on the reaction force are small. But the reference values are very approximate because they are extracted from a paper curve.

The mesh of the calculation code taken as a reference and the mesh used in the Modelling are different. Furthermore, it is not explained in the reference how the normal contact pressure is extracted. Thus, no reference tests on this pressure have been performed. However, non-regression tests are performed on the contact pressure ($\mathrm{SIYY}$ to the node in contact). The appearance of this pressure and the contact area are identical between the two calculation codes.
