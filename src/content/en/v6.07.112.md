# v6.07.112 COMP012 – Validation of CALC_ESSAI_GEOMECA macro-command with multiple constitutive laws

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the CALC_ESSAI_GEOMECA [U4.90.21] macro-command, which simulates eight types of load paths characteristic of geomechanical tests at the point hardware:

- Triaxial monotone drained test with required displacement.
- Undrained monotonous triaxial test with required displacement.
- Undrained force force cyclic triaxial test.
- Triaxial cyclic alternating drained test with required displacement.
- Non-alternate cyclic triaxial test drained at required displacement.
- Test of drained cyclic shear with required deformation.
- Cyclical oedometer test drained with force imposed.
- Isotropic cyclic pressure test drained at force imposed.
- Undrained cyclic triaxial test with required displacement.

In model A, these nine tests are simulated with Hujeux's law. For the first three tests, the calculated solutions are compared with results.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Six typical load paths for geomechanical testing are automatically defined by the macro-command **CALC_ESSAI_GEOMECA** [U4.90.21](doc:u4.90.21).

Following the redesign of the macro-command in Code_Aster version 14.2, the input stresses and deformations are in line with the ground mechanics convention, namely that the compression is positive.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

Simulation with point hardware. The objective is to validate the compatibility of CALC_ESSAI_GEOMECA with MOHR_COULOMB law.
