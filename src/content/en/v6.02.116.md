# v6.02.116 SSNL116 - Gas-insulated cable strand

**Summary:**

The problem is quasi-static non-linear in structural mechanics.

The behaviour of a segment of gas-insulated cable, buried at a low depth modelled by bars, is analyzed. The interaction with the ground is taken into account by nonlinear elements bar. In the vertical direction, this behavior is asymmetric.

Only one model implements this gas-insulated cable (GIC), the mesh of which is obtained by a fortran program associated with the test.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A section of C.I.G (corresponding to an experiment carried out on the Renardières site). The cable is modelled by elements of Euler's multifibre beam. To model the ground behavior, each mesh cell of the line is combined with 6 bars: 3 in each node of the mesh cell. In each node, a bar is oriented in the same direction as the C.I.G, and allows to take into account the axial forces of the ground on the C.I.G. A bar is oriented vertically, and allows to take into account the (asymmetric) action of the ground following the vertical. The third is oriented to complete the trihedron.
