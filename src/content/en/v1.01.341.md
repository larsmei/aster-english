# v1.01.341 ZZZZ341 - Validation of the following pressure space function

**Summary:**

This test validates the application of a follower pressure whose intensity is a function of the updated space variables ($\mathit{XF}$ , $\mathit{YF}$ , $\mathit{ZF}$ ) in 2D and 3D:

- A cube, with the Poisson's ratio zero, is initially placed in uniform compression following $Y$ by a space-dependent following pressure;
- Then a rigid rotation is applied to the cube around one of its edges. The multiplicative function of the pressure loading is zero at this moment, so the cube is released.
- Finally at the last moment of calculation, the following pressure load is again applied. This load is a function of space so that the new position of the cube makes the pressure applied now follows $X$ .

The support reaction is raised to the clamped face. The solution is analytical.

The special feature of the treatment of the following pressure loading depends on the space:

- initially, geometry is updated to take account of normal change
- in a second step, the pressure intensity is calculated **from the updated space variables** ($\mathit{XF}$ parameters , $\mathit{YF}$ , $\mathit{ZF}$ in DEFI_FONCTION)

This test consists of three modellings:

- Modelling A: D_PLAN;
- Model B: 3D;
- Modelling C: D_PLAN with SYME='OUI' symetrication option.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
