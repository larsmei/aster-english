# v2.02.001 SDLL01 - Short beam on single supports

**Summary:**

This two-dimensional problem consists in searching for vibration frequencies of a mechanical structure composed of a beam in single support at both ends. This test case of Structure Mechanics corresponds to a dynamic analysis of a linear model with linear behavior. The influence of the position of the points considered as support points (points on neutral fiber or points excentrated at the base of the beam) in relation to the neutral fiber of a thick beam is studied.

This test allows testing some of the features that concern Timoshenko beams, rigid restraintss and searching for eigenfrequencies by inverse iterations.

The results obtained with the support points on the neutral fibre, i.e. with the support points excentric, are compared with the analytical calculations on the beams of TImoshenko. The results obtained with the support points excentric are compared with the results of non-regression.

When the support points are eccentric, there is a coupling between the different traction-compression and bending modes.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in Timoshenko's book on the theory of vibrations of beams and plates ([1]):

**Problem 1:** Analytical calculation

The bending equation of the unslender beams gives the Timoshenko formulation, superimposing the effects of simple bending, shear force deformations and rotation inertia.

The eigenfrequencies in traction-compression are given according to this theory by:

${f}_{i}=\frac{{\lambda}_{i}}{2\pi L}\sqrt{\frac{E}{\rho}}$ with ${\lambda}_{i}=\frac{(\mathrm{2i}-1)}{2}\pi$ $i=1,2,\mathrm{...}$

> The reference [1] contains an equivalent formula for bending modes.

**Problem 2:**

Since the problem does not have analytical solution, the solution is established by non-regression results.

The bending and traction-compression modes are coupled.
