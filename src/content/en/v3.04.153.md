# v3.04.153 SSLV153 - Error indicators – mesh cells volumetrics and functions

**Summary:**

This case tests the calculation of error indicators per residue and in amount of interest on a very simple mesh: 4 mesh cells volumetrics of each possible type, tetrahedron, pentahedron, hexahedron and pyramid. Compared to other test cases, this one allows to test the non-regression on two functions:

- the consideration of all types of edges for mesh cells 3D,
- the load expressed by functions.

This case is not a validation of error indicators, but it can be used as an example for their use.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The field studied is a volume with facets, the only interest of which is to be an assembly of mesh cells different. The starting point is a cube on the side 100. The front side of this cube is the base of a pyramid; the contiguous side is the base of a pentahedron. Between the pentahedron and the pyramid, there is a tetrahedron.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is defined with:

Young's modulus $=400000$

Poisson's ratio $=0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The upper side of the block is subjected to a pressure of 13, 14, 15 and 16 respectively, for the upper side of the tetrahedron, pyramid, pentahedron and hexahedron, respectively.

The outer triangular side of the pentahedron and the rear side of the hexahedron are clamped.

The other external sides are free. A zero normal stress value is voluntarily imposed to ensure correct calculation of error indicators.

## 2 reference solution

There is no analytical solution.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The calculation is a linear mechanical resolution. A residual error indicator is then calculated. The surfacial terms are then tested. The following dual calculation is defined to optimize the $\mathrm{DZ}$ component of the displacement field. This modifies the load by cancelling external pressures and adding an internal, unitary volume force according to $Z$.

The same calculation is done twice. The first calculation is performed with loads expressed as real constants. The second calculation is done with loads in the form of functions. The results are strictly identical.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The original mesh was created "hand-made", in MED format. It is in degree 1. It is passed in degree 2 by the MODI_MAILLAGE command.

Each volume form corresponds to a unique mesh cell 3D. The hexahedron is in contact on one of its sides with the pentahedron and on one side close to the pyramid. The tetrahedron is located between the pentahedron and the pyramid. Thus, each mesh cell volume is in contact with two other mesh cells of different type, on the one hand, and with the outside, on the other hand. The only unrepresented contact is that between the pentahedron and the pyramid. The mesh cells 2D bordering the domain are all created.
