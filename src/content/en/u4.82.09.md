# u4.82.09 Macro-command CALC_GP

## 1 Purpose

The purpose of this macro-command is to compute, in post-processing of a non-linear elastoplastic mechanical analysis, the energetic fracture-mechanics parameter called ${G}_{P}$. The energy approach based on this criterion is detailed in the document [R7.02.16](doc:r7.02.16).

This macro-command can be used in 2D and in 3D.

In 2D, it makes it possible to use the approach with a specific mesh dedicated to the approach (presence of groups of elements forming particular zones called chips) or a sufficiently fine free mesh in which the zones are built by the macro-command. It is important to point out that

In 3D, only a specific mesh dedicated to the approach, comprising groups of elements forming slices of several chips, can be used.

The macro-command returns a table containing, for each requested analysis time instant:

- the name of the analysis zone (chip) ZONE
- the elastic energy, modified or not, in the zone (ENER_ELTR for a chip mesh

or ENER_ELAS for a free mesh),

- the length of the zone (or the area in 3D) DELTA_L
- the parameter GP
- an indicator specifying the location of the maximum at each time instant MAX_INST

The user may also request a reduced table in which only the rows corresponding to the maximum at each time instant are given. In the case where the user has used the automatic construction of the chip zones, a field may be requested as output, making it possible to visualise the zones and to check that they are correctly defined.

## 3 Energetic elastoplastic fracture model Gp

The energetic model for predicting fracture in elastoplasticity is described in more detail in the reference documentation [R7.02.16](doc:r7.02.16).

The aim of this model is to estimate the critical loading for cleavage initiation in an elastoplastic structure; it therefore represents a deterministic alternative to the models based on principal-stress levels, namely Beremin or Bordet (also available in *Code_Aster*).

The model is based on a representation of the defect by a notch; the principle of minimising the potential energy of the structure with respect to the advance of the defect alone finally leads to a criterion on the mean elastic energy present in particular zones $C(l)$ downstream of the notch and commonly called chips. The Figure presents a definition of these zones, which are therefore measured from the notch root up to a distance $l$; ${L}_{C}$ here represents the diameter of the notch.

![Figure](/figures/u4.82.09/1000000000000441000001757E397CBBB489C2F5.png)

Figure 3-1- Definition of the zone of interest.

The cleavage criterion is then written:

$(\exists l>0)\tilde{{G}_{P}}(l)\ge {G}_{\mathit{PC}}$ with $\stackrel{̃}{{G}_{P}}\left(l\right)=\frac{{\int}_{C\left(l\right)}{\Phi}_{\mathit{el}}^{\mathit{traction}}d\Omega }{l}$ ,

where ${\Phi}_{\mathit{el}}^{\mathit{traction}}=\frac{\lambda}{2}H(\mathit{tr}(ϵ))\mathit{tr}{(ϵ)}^{2}+\mu \sum_{i=1}^{3}H({ϵ}_{i}){ϵ}_{i}^{2}$ , $H$ represents the Heaviside function, $ϵ$ represents the elastic strain tensor, ${ϵ}_{i}$ represents the principal elastic strains.and ${G}_{\mathrm{PC}}$ is a material parameter to be determined. In 3D, the distance $l$ is replaced by the area of the chip in the propagation plane of the notch.

In order to carry out the computation of the elastic energy in these zones called chips, two solutions exist in 2D:

- having defined these zones in the mesh ${\Phi}_{\mathit{el}}^{\mathit{traction}}=\frac{\lambda}{2}H(\mathit{tr}(ϵ))\mathit{tr}{(ϵ)}^{2}+\mu \sum_{i=1}^{3}H({ϵ}_{i}){ϵ}_{i}^{2}$
- defining these zones a posteriori in the free mesh ${\Phi}_{\mathit{el}}=\frac{1}{2}\sigma {A}^{-1}\sigma$

![Figure](/figures/u4.82.09/100000000000045E0000011EEF037B8DDE2B2B2A.png)

Figure 3-2- Classical mesh with definition of the chips.

In the first case, the Figure presents a view of the mesh to be used. This mesh comprises 95 chips of small size (each meshed by 8 quadrilateral finite elements), then a coarsening zone. A mesh-cell group must then be associated with each chip.

In the second case, the Figure presents one possible mesh around the notch. This mesh must be sufficiently fine in this zone in order to allow a reliable computation of the parameter of the energy method.

![Figure](/figures/u4.82.09/1000000000000534000003600620F6357A2570DF.png)

Figure 3-3- Free mesh without definition of the chips.

In 3D, the notion of a slice is introduced, each slice containing several chips. The slices follow one another along the defect front, which is a line. In 2D, there is only one slice, the defect tip being reduced to 1 point. The chips of the 1st slice are defined in the same way as in 2D, with the difference that the mesh cells are solid and must be hexahedral; the list is continued by adding the chips of the 2nd slice in the same way; one finally obtains a list of ${\mathrm{nb}}_{\mathrm{copeaux}}\times {\mathrm{nb}}_{\mathrm{tranches}}$ mesh-cell groups.

## 4 Operands

CALC_GP is a macro-command and therefore calls other commands of *Code_Aster* internally. Most of the keywords are passed as they are to the other commands. It will be indicated below in which command(s) the keywords are used.

### 4.1 Operand RESULTAT

> ♦ RESULTAT    =    resumeca,    [resultat]

Denotes the result of the thermomechanical analysis for which the parameter ${G}_{P}$ is computed.

Used by POST_ELEM and CALC_CHAMP.

### 4.2 Operand LIST_INST

> ♦ LIST_INST    =    instant,    [l_R]

List of time instants at which the parameter will be computed.

**Warning**: the quantities from which the parameter ${G}_{P}$ is computed being non-linear, no temporal extrapolation is allowed; if a time instant is specified, it must belong to the list of archiving time instants of the thermomechanical analysis.

Used by POST_ELEM, CREA_CHAMP and CALC_CHAMP.

### 4.3 Operand PRECISION

> ◊ PRECISION    =    /prec,    [R]

[DEFAUT]

Accuracy with which the list of time instants must be considered.

### 4.4 Operand CRITERE

> ◊ CRITERE    =    /'ABSOLU'    [DEFAUT]

> /'RELATIF'

Denotes the type of accuracy for the determination of the list of time instants.

### 4.5 Operand GP_MAX

> ◊ GPMAX    =    CO('TABGPMAX')    [CO]

Indicates whether the user wishes to obtain as a result a second table, a restriction of the mandatory complete table containing only the row of the location of the maximum of the parameter ${G}_{P}$ for each analysis time instant.

### 4.6 Operand TRANCHE_2D

> ◊ TRANCHE_2D    =_F(

> ♦ ZONE_MAIL        =    /'NON'

> =    /'OUI'

> #Si ZONE_MAIL = 'OUI' :

> ♦ GROUP_MA        = l_group    [l_group_ma]
> ♦ TAILLE        =    l_taille    [l_R]

> #Si ZONE_MAIL = 'NON' :

> ♦ TAILLE        = taille    [R]
> ♦ CENTRE        = centre    [R,R,R]
> ♦ RAYON            = $R$     [R]
> ♦ ANGLE            = $\theta$     [R]
> ♦ NB_ZONE        = n    [I]
> ◊ CHAMP_VISU    = CO('CHAMP')    [CO]

Denotes the set of geometric parameters required for the computation of the energy parameter.

#### 4.6.1 Keyword ZONE_MAIL

> ♦ ZONE_MAIL        =    /'NON'

> =    /'OUI'

Indicates whether the mesh represents the geometry of the chip zones.

If 'OUI', one is in the case of a mesh such as that in the Figure.

#### 4.6.2 Case ZONE_MAIL = 'OUI'

##### 4.6.2.1 Keyword GROUP_MA

> ♦ GROUP_MA        = l_group    [l_group_ma]

List of the mesh-cell groups on which the computations will be carried out. Each mesh-cell group must correspond to a chip zone.

Used by POST_ELEM.

##### 4.6.2.2 Keyword TAILLE

> ♦ TAILLE        = l_taille    [l_R]

List of the sizes of the zones. This list must be of the same size as the list of mesh-cell groups.

#### 4.6.3 Case ZONE_MAIL = 'NON'

In this case, a geometric analysis zone is built by the macro-command. The following parameters make it possible to define it. The Figure presents the parameters defining these zones; it represents a notch of centre ${C}_{\mathit{ent}}$, of radius $R$; the third zone ${C}_{3}(l)$ of length $3\times {l}_{C}$ is hatched.

![Figure](/figures/u4.82.09/100000000000033D0000023C9E3EED43B08090B4.png)

Figure 4-1- Geometric definition of the zones in the unmeshed case.

##### 4.6.3.1 Keyword TAILLE

> ♦ TAILLE        = ${l}_{C}$         [R]

Increment of the size of the zones. Zone n is therefore of size n* ${l}_{C}$.

Used by FORMULE.

##### 4.6.3.2 Keyword CENTRE

> ♦ CENTRE        = centre    [R,R,R]

Denotes the coordinates of the centre of the notch ${C}_{\mathrm{ent}}$ in the global frame.

Used by FORMULE.

##### 4.6.3.3 Keyword RAYON

> ♦ RAYON        = $R$         [R]

Denotes the radius of the notch.

Used by FORMULE.

##### 4.6.3.4 Keyword ANGLE

> ♦ ANGLE        = $\theta$         [R]

Denotes the angle formed between the direction of the notch and the axis $\overrightarrow{X}$ of the global frame.

The angle must be given in degrees and measured in the trigonometric sense.

Used by FORMULE

##### 4.6.3.5 Keyword NB_ZONE

> ♦ NB_ZONE        = n            [I]

Denotes the number of zones (chips) considered in the analysis.

##### 4.6.3.6 Keyword CHAMP_VISU

> ◊ CHAMP_VISU        = CO('CHAMP')    [CO]

If the user so wishes, the output of a field at the Gauss points representing the chips may be requested. The value of this field is $1$ in the chip zone and $0$ elsewhere. The Figure presents a visualisation of this field for the mesh presented in the Figure.

### 4.7 Operand TRANCHE_3D

> ◊ TRANCHE_3D    =_F(

> ♦ GROUP_MA         = l_group    [l_group_ma]

> )

#### 4.7.1 Keyword GROUP_MA

> ♦ GROUP_MA        = l_group    [l_group_ma]

List of the mesh-cell groups on which the computations will be carried out.

Each mesh-cell group must correspond to a chip zone.

Each list of mesh-cell groups corresponds to a given slice; the mesh-cell groups inside each list must correspond to the chips and be ordered from the closest to the defect front to the furthest.

Used by POST_ELEM.

### 4.8 Operand SYME

> #Dans le cas 2D
> ♦ SYME    =    /'OUI'

> /'NON'

States whether a symmetry of the structure with respect to the notch has made it possible to mesh only half of the structure. In figures and , only the part of the structure above the notch is defined; in this case, the user will supply SYME='OUI' and the result indicated in the table will take the symmetry into account (multiplied by two).

### 4.9 Operand FOND_FISS

> #Si TRANCHE_3D :
> ♦ FOND_FISS    =    fond    [fond]

In 3D, the user must previously define a crack front, with separated lips (since the defect is represented by a notch).

This crack front makes it possible in particular to compute the areas (denominator of the computation of ${G}_{p}$).
