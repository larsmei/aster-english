# v1.01.287 ZZZZ287 – Basic POST_RUPTURE Operator Validation

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the POST_RUPTURE operator in an elementary way. This test has no physical meaning, it is essentially a computer test.

Different operations are tested:

- normalisation of curvilinear abscisse,
- calculation of the bifurcation angle,
- calculation of the crack-failure progression increment fatigue with the Paris law with and without threshold,
- calculation of the equivalent stress intensity factor,
- calculation of the ${(\Delta K)}_{\mathit{eq}}$ used for variable amplitude propagation,
- setting negative ${K}_{1}$ stress intensity factor values to zero and calculating the new G yield rate by the Irwin formula.

This test contains two modellings:

- Model A considers a crack with a bottom.
- Model B has a crack with two bottoms.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
