# v4.25.001 TTLV01 – Sphere: heat exchange by convection

**Summary:**

This test is based on independent validation of version 3 in linear transient thermal analysis.

This is a three-dimensional problem represented by four modellings, one volume, the other three axisymmetric.

The functions tested are as follows:

- elements thermal volumetric,
- elements thermal axisymmetric,
- transient thermal analysis algorithm,
- limit conditions: convection.

The results are compared with those provided by VPCS.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v4.25.001/Object_1.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

| $\lambda =48.822W/m°C$ | thermal conductivity |
| --- | --- |
| ${c}_{p}=669.0J/\mathrm{kg}°C$ | specific heat |
| $\rho =7200\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ | density |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Convection on external surface with air:

- ${h}_{e}=232.5W/{m}^{2}°C$ ,
- ${T}_{e}=1000°C$ .

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Initial temperature: $T(t=0)=20°C$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in the TTLV01/89 plug in the VPCS guide.

- calculation of coefficients,
- reading on abacus by Gurney-Lurie.

### 2.2 Baseline

Surface and center temperature of the sphere for $t$ between $400s$ and $2400s$

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

< 2%

Below $600s$, uncertainty increases (hard reading of abacus).

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

- Guide de validation des computaux de computation de structures. Société Française des Mécaniciens, AFNOR 1990 ISBN 2-12-486611-7

## 11 Summary of the results

The results obtained are satisfactory. Maximum the difference obtained (3%) is on the outer surface of the sphere for the lowest moment t. This difference decreases when the moment t increases.

Whatever the Modelling, the results are always more accurate at the centre than at the outer surface of the sphere.

The results between linear and quadratic elements are substantially the same.
