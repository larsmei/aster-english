# v7.22.102 HSNV102 - Thermo-metallo-plasticity coupled in single traction

**Summary:**

The mechanical evolution of a cylindrical bar subjected to known and uniform $T(t)$ and $Z(t)$ thermal evolutions is examined (the metallurgical transformation is of a martensitic type).

The elements used are elements axisymmetric.

For modellings A and B, the behavior relationship is VonMises plasticity with isotropic hardening. For modelingB, transformation plasticity is also taken into account.

In model C, a viscoplastic behavior with linear isotropic hardening is considered and the transformation plasticity is not taken into account.

The elastic limit and slope of the tensile curve depend on temperature and metallurgical composition.

The expansion coefficient $\alpha$ depends on the metallurgical composition.

Metallurgical transformations take place at ${\dot{\epsilon}}^{p}\ne 0$ (this is in the sense that the **couple** test is the plasticity of transformation of classical plasticity).

> **Metallurgical parameters:**

TRC to model a metallurgical evolution of the martensitic type of the shape:

${Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}=\lbrace \begin{array}{ccc}0.& \mathit{si}t\le {\tau}_{1}& {\tau}_{1}=25s\\ 1-{e}^{\varphi \lambda (t-{\tau}_{1})}& \mathit{si}{\tau}_{1}\le t<{\tau}_{2}& {\tau}_{2}=40s\\ 1.& \mathit{si}t\ge {\tau}_{2}& \end{array}$

with $\varphi =0.03$ and $\lambda =-10°{\mathit{C.s}}^{-1}$

**Thermal parameters:**

Heat capacity: $\rho {C}_{p}=2.{10}^{6}{\mathit{J.m}}^{-3}.°{C}^{-1}$

Conductivity: $\lambda =9999.9{\mathit{W.m}}^{-1}.°{C}^{-1}$

** Thermomechanical parameters:**

- Thermo-elastic parameters:

Young's modulus $E=200000{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$

Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$

Thermal expansion coefficients

${\alpha}_{\mathit{fbm}}=15.{10}^{-6}°{C}^{-1}$ ${\alpha}_{\mathit{aust}}=23.5{10}^{-6}°{C}^{-1}$

Definition temperature of the expansion coefficient: ${T}_{\mathit{ref}}=900°C$

Reference thermal deformation state: $\Delta {\epsilon}_{f\gamma }^{{T}_{\mathit{ref}}}=2.52{10}^{-3}$

Limit of elasticity:

${\sigma}_{y}^{\mathit{fbm}}={\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}+{s}^{\mathit{fbm}}(T-{T}^{0})$ with ${\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}=50.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$ and ${s}^{\mathit{fbm}}=1.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa.}°{C}^{-1}$

${\sigma}_{y}^{\mathit{aust}}={\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}+{s}^{\mathit{aust}}(T-{T}^{0})$ with ${\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}=50.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$ and ${s}^{\mathit{aust}}=1.3{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa.}°{C}^{-1}$

- Thermoplastic parameters (law with linear nutring)

Tangential modules: ${E}_{T}^{\mathit{fbm}}$ and ${E}_{T}^{\mathit{aust}}$ are selected as:

${H}^{\mathit{fbm}}={H}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}+{\lambda}^{\mathit{fbm}}(T-{T}^{0})$ with ${H}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}=0.\mathit{Pa}$ and ${\lambda}^{\mathit{fbm}}=-6.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa.}°{C}^{-1}$

${H}^{\mathit{aust}}={H}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}+{\lambda}^{\mathit{aust}}(T-{T}^{0})$ with ${H}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}=0.\mathit{Pa}$ and ${\lambda}^{\mathit{aust}}=-1.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa.}°{C}^{-1}$

It's recalled that $H=\frac{{\mathit{EE}}_{T}}{E-{E}_{T}}$

- Parameters for transform plasticity:

Background:

| In the case of a metallurgical evolution of the martensitic type, the model of the plasticity transformation is as follows: ${\dot{\epsilon}}^{\mathit{pt}}=\frac{3}{2}\stackrel{̃}{\sigma}{k}^{\mathit{fbm}}{F}^{'}({Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}){\dot{Z}}_{\mathit{fbm}}$ |
| --- |

Model settings: ${k}^{\mathit{fbm}}=1.{10}^{-10}{\mathit{Pa}}^{-1}$ and ${F}^{'}({Z}_{\mathit{fbm}})=2(1-{Z}_{\mathit{fbm}})$

**Additional viscous parameters of Modelling C:**

> Both $\eta$ and C parameters are taken null so that the viscoplastic model tends to a plastic model independent of time.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- ${u}_{Z}=0$ on the $\mathit{AB}$ side (symmetry condition).
- traction imposed on the side $\mathit{CD}$ , $p(t)={p}_{0}t$ , ${p}_{0}={15.10}^{\mathrm{6 }}\mathit{Pa}$ .
- $T(t)={T}^{0}+\mu t$ , $\mu =–10°\mathit{C.}{s}^{-1}$ on the whole structure.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

${T}^{0}=900°C$ .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

**Before transformation** , thermo-elastic solution for $t<{\tau}_{1}$ (no metallurgical transformation $\dot{Z}=0$ ).

$\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\epsilon}_{zz}(t)={\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)\\ {\sigma}_{zz}(t)={p}_{0}t\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)}{E}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)={\alpha}_{\mathit{aust}}(T-{T}^{0})\end{array}$

The elastic limit is reached for:

${\tau}_{1}=\frac{{\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}}{{p}_{0}-{s}^{\mathit{aust}}\mu }$

**During transformation** , thermo-metallo-elastic-plastic solution, for ${\tau}_{1}\le t\le {\tau}_{2}$ , ${\tau}_{\mathrm{2 }}=\mathrm{40 }s$ .

$\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\epsilon}_{zz}(t)={\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{pt}}(t)\\ {\sigma}_{zz}(t)={p}_{0}t\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)}{E}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)={Z}_{\mathit{aust}}{\alpha}_{\mathit{aust}}(T-{T}^{0})+{Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}\left({\alpha}_{\mathit{fbm}}(T-{T}^{0})+\Delta {\epsilon}_{f\gamma }^{{T}_{\mathit{ref}}}\right)\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)-({Z}_{\mathit{aust}}{\sigma}_{y}^{\mathit{aust}}(T)+{Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}{\sigma}_{y}^{\mathit{fbm}}(T))}{{Z}_{\mathit{aust}}{H}^{\mathit{aust}}(T)+{Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}{H}^{\mathit{fbm}}(T)}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{pt}}(t)={k}^{\mathit{fbm}}\left({\sigma}_{zz}({\tau}_{1})-\frac{{p}_{0}}{2\lambda \varphi }\right)-{k}^{\mathit{fbm}}\left({\sigma}_{zz}(t)-\frac{{p}_{0}}{2\lambda \varphi }\right){(1-{Z}_{\mathit{fbm}})}^{2}\end{array}$

**After transformation** , thermo-elasto-plastic solution, for $t\ge {\tau}_{2}$

$\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\epsilon}_{zz}(t)={\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{pt}}({\tau}_{2})\\ {\sigma}_{zz}(t)={p}_{0}t\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)}{E}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)={\alpha}_{\mathit{fbm}}(T-{T}^{0})+\Delta {\epsilon}_{f\gamma }^{{T}_{\mathit{ref}}}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)-({\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}+{s}^{\mathit{fbm}}\mu t)}{{H}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}+{\lambda}^{\mathit{fbm}}\mu t}\end{array}$

### 2.2 Baseline

${\sigma}_{zz}^{}$ , ${\epsilon}_{zz}^{}$ , ${\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}$ , ${\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}$ , ${\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{meca}}$ , ${\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{plas}}$ et $\chi$ à $\mathrm{24 }s$ , $\mathrm{26 }s$ , $\mathrm{40 }s$ et $\mathrm{90 }s$ .

with:

${\epsilon}^{\mathit{meca}}$: mechanical deformations

${\epsilon}^{\mathit{plas}}$: plastic deformations (including transform plasticity)
