# v4.41.300 TPNA300 - Heat generator tube with variable conductivity

**Summary:**

This test is based on the independent validation of version 3 in nonlinear steady-state thermal analysis.

This is a 2D axisymmetric problem represented by four modellings, two axisymmetric, one plane and the last volume.

The functions tested are as follows:

- element thermal axisymmetric,
- element thermal plane,
- element thermal volumetric,
- variable properties,
- limit conditions (heat source, temperature imposed).

The value of the test lies in the consideration of variable properties and nonlinear behaviour.

## 12 Summary of the results

The four modellings give results with values exceeding the tolerance initially set (5%):

- for Modelling A (AXIS: QUAD4,TRIA3), maximum the difference is 15.3% and 4 out of 16 exceed tolerance,
- for Modelling B (PLAN: QUAD8,TRIA6), maximum the difference is 13.8% and 3 out of 24 exceed tolerance,
- for Modelling C (3D: HEXA8, PENTA6), the difference maximum is 15% and 5 out of 24 values exceed tolerance,
- For Modelling D (AXIS: QUAD9), maximum the difference is 13.7% and 4 out of 16 exceed tolerance.

These tolerance exceedances are observed for values close to 0.

The calculations were performed in °C. The determination of the difference, considering temperatures in °F (as in reference solution), gives a maximum difference very different from that obtained in°C (3% instead of 15%).

In addition, the original reference (Kreith's book) cited in the ANSYS verification manual was not available. The reference solution method of obtaining (graphic estimate) and its uncertainty are therefore unknown.

The results are considered acceptable in the light of the above points.

This test tested the consideration of variable thermal conductivity within several modellings. The main controls tested are as follows:

- DEFI_MATERIAU associated with the THER_NL keyword, allowing to define the characteristics of a material whose characteristics vary according to temperature,
- THER_NON_LINE controls allowing resolution of a non-linear steady-state thermal analysis problem or not.
