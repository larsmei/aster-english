# v1.01.108 ZZZZ108 - Code_Aster-MISS3D Interface Test

**Summary:**

This test allows the Modelling of a structure of model-type building skewer (elements discrete and beams) in linear harmonic and transient dynamics, supplemented at its elements density and superfacial base.

Its interest is to test the *Code_Aster* commands specific to the soil-structure interaction for interfacing with MISS3D software (ECP-LMSSM at).

In this test, post-processing also tests the displacement of the control points located in the ground and added to the structure Modelling by *Code_Aster*.

Finally, it is verified that the accelerograms can be given directly on a frequency basis by reproducing the calculation for which they are given on a time basis.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A skewer model of the building is used with a radier that is modelled by a layer of 136 mesh cells TRIA3 superfacial and 6 degrees of freedom is assigned to its lower side.

The Modelling presented is a simplified model for which the building is represented by a flat structure. Four substructures are represented by four non-heavy vertical beams, of variable bending inertia and bearing nodal masses and inertias representing civil engineering and equipment. Discreet elastic restraintss connect these beams at different levels. The four beams are clamped on a general radier of great bending inertia.

### 1.2 Material properties

$E=4.0{10}^{10}\mathit{Pa}$

$\rho =2500\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$ (elements heavy only)

$\nu =0.15$

- features of point weights ('M_TR_D_N') and restraints node-node ('K_TR_D_L').

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Restraints solid on the top and bottom of the radier (LIAISON_SOLIDE on the nodes $\mathit{HRADIER}$ and $\mathit{SRADIER}$ groups).

- Blocking of the node centre ($\mathit{N98}$ ) of the lower side (6 degrees of freedom) to calculate the rigid body modes,
- ${10}^{4}\mathit{Newtons}$ nodal force applied to node $\mathit{N1500}$ ,
- Or transient acceleration $\mathit{EUR}$ spectrum in $X$ direction of amplitude $0.25g$ ,
- Or unit harmonic acceleration in the $X$ and $Y$ directions

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Zero displacements, speeds and acceleration.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The resolution in the projection modal base (constituted by eigenmodes with blocked interface and static modes forced calculated by the *Code_Aster*) performed by MISS3D in the frequency range on the elements border finished. There is also the possibility to perform this resolution by *Code_Aster* using the harmonic resolution operator after recovering the ground impedances and seismic forces variable according to frequency from MISS3D.

MISS3D post-processed time return by Fourier reverse transformation.

Return to the physical basis of a harmonic or transient evolution resulting from *Code_Aster* .

### 2.2 Baseline

Harmonic responses to the upper nodes (N800, N1500 and N2700) at frequencies close to resonances.

Maximum transient accelerations at these same points for EUR spectrum of $0.25g$ .

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Digital solution.

## 3 Modelling C

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

25 elements beam POU_D_T,

5 elements de restraint node-node (DIS_TR_L),

26 elements POI1 point mass (DIS_TR_N),

2 elements POI1 point zero mass (DIS_T) for modelling control points in the post-treating ground,

136 elements density ('3D' model) for radiating and 136 elements DST for its lower face.

1st harmonic calculation: in the $(0,10\mathit{Hz})$ step by step interval of $0.1\mathit{Hz}$,

2nd harmonic calculation: in the $(0,10\mathit{Hz})$ step by step interval of $0.1\mathit{Hz}$,

3rd transient calculation: in the $(0,10s)$ interval by step of ${10}^{-2}s$ .

This Modelling is characterized by the presence of soil control points to model the deconvolution of a vertical sinusoidal signal imposed on the soil surface.

3 elements POI1 point zero mass (DIS_T) are introduced to model control points in the soil to be treated.

They correspond to 3 levels in depth of homogeneous soil: surface and shifted respectively from one quarter and half a flat wave of pressure with vertical impact.

This soil is a homogeneous soil, the characteristics of which are summarized in the following table:

These values induce a pressure wave speed ${V}_{P}=250m/s$ , which gives a pressure wave length of 50 meters for an excitation frequency of $5\mathit{Hz}$ . The second and third control points are therefore depressed from $12.5m$ and $25m$ respectively in the vertical direction.

A transient $(0,4s)$ stepwise calculation of ${10}^{-2}s$ is performed with a sinusoidal $5\mathit{Hz}$ frequency acceleration imposed on the ground surface in the vertical direction $Z$.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of nodes : 190

Number of mesh cells and type: 136 PENTA6, 136 TRIA3, 30 SEG2, 28 POI1

### 3.3 reference solution

For sinusoidal frequency $f$ stress in $C$ wave speed and $\beta$ hysteretic damping ground, analytically, the $Z$ depth amplitude factor is:

$\mathit{Az}=\mathit{sh}(\beta \pi fZ/C)\sin(2\pi fZ/C)+\mathit{ch}(\beta \pi fZ/C)\sin(2\pi fZ/C)$

The corresponding amplitude in speed will then be:

$\mathit{Vz}=\mathit{Az}/(2\pi f)$

And the corresponding amplitude on the move will be:

$\mathit{Dz}=-\mathit{Az}/{(2\pi f)}^{2}$

It can only be seen from comparison that the peaks of harmonic responses correspond well to the resonance frequencies obtained with an equivalent soil spring model.

On the other hand, for the resolution in the frequency domain of the harmonic problem projected on a modal basis consisting of eigenmodes with blocked interface and static modes forced, the same results are obtained by *Code_Aster* and by MISS3D.

It is also found that the results are the same with accelerograms on a temporal or frequency basis.
