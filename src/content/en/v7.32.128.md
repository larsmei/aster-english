# v7.32.128 WTNP128 – Fluid-pressure concrete-coupling test

**Summary:**

The test presented here allows to check the correct functioning of the elements joints with hydromechanical coupling and use of cohesive laws.

The modeled test is a corner crack test with fluid pressure in the crack.

Comparisons with experimental and numerical tests are available for this trial.

## 1 Reference problem

The "wedge splitting test" was initially proposed by Brühwiler and Wittmann to obtain a stable crack propagation and thus determine the fracture properties of the concrete. Slowik and Saouma then used this test to study the effect of fluid pressure in the crack. Segura and Carol modelled this fluid-pressure cracking test. The trial by Slowik and Saouma is repeated here.

A device, known as a "coin", allows a force to be applied to the $B$ and $B'$ points (see figure ) to separate the sample in two. The experiment is controlled by the CMOD (Crack Mouth Opening Displacement), i.e. the displacement imposed between $B$ and $B'$.

### 1.2 Material properties

- Properties of the interstitial fluid (liquid water):

| Density | ${1.10}^{-6}{\mathrm{kg.mm}}^{-3}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Viscosity | ${1.10}^{-9}\mathrm{MPa.s}$ |

Water is considered incompressible.

- Properties of the massif :

The concrete massif is elastic and has the following properties:

| Young's modulus | $27500\mathrm{MPa}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Poisson's ratio | $0,2$ |

Given the time scales considered and the permeability of the crack very high compared, concrete is assumed to be waterproof. It is therefore modelled by classic elements D_PLAN.

- Discontinuity properties:

The discontinuity is broken down into three parts:

- The rubber membrane that ensures the seal of the notch;
- The notch in which constant pressure is imposed;
- The crack path along which the crack spreads.

The membrane has linear elastic behavior. The constitutive law JOINT_BANDIS is used with the $\gamma =0$ parameter to make it linear. The membrane properties are as follows:

| Initial normal rigidity ${K}_{ni}$ | $20{\mathrm{MPa.mm}}^{-1}$ |
| --- | --- |
| $\gamma$ coefficient | 0 |

For notch, CZM_LIN_REG law is used by initializing internal variables so that elementss are initially broken.

For cracking, the CZM_EXP_REG law is used with the following parameters:

| ${G}_{c}$ cracking energy | $0,150\mathrm{MPa.mm}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Critical stress ${\sigma}_{c}$ | $3,25\mathrm{MPa}$ |

The numerical parameters of the elements joints with cohesive law are

| PENA_ADHERENCE | ${1.10}^{-3}$ |
| --- | --- |
| PENA_CONTACT | $1$ |

- Crack tip properties

| Biot $N$ module | $0,3{.10}^{-3}{\mathrm{MPa.mm}}^{-1}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Fictive OUV_FICT Opening | 10 $\mathrm{mm}$ |

### 1.3 Initial conditions

The fluid pressure is initially $0,21\mathrm{MPa}$ in the notch and $0,0\mathrm{MPa}$ in the future crack.

### 1.4 boundary conditions

The mechanical and hydraulic boundary conditions are:

- $F$: blocked movements in all directions and zero pressure imposed;
- In the $[\mathrm{GH}]$ notch: pressure imposed from $0,21\mathrm{MPa}$;
- In the $[\mathrm{HI}]$ membrane: pressure imposed by $0,0\mathrm{MPa}$;
- In $B$ and $B'$, the movements are symmetrical;
- In $B'$ , a force is imposed in the $(\begin{array}{c}\cos(\alpha )\\ -\sin(\alpha )\end{array})$ direction , the intensity of which is controlled by the $\mathrm{DX}$ component of the point $B'$ movement . At each $t$ moment , the horizontal component of the $B'$ displacement must be equal to ${u}_{x}=-\frac{q}{2}t$ , where $q$ is the loading speed . So the DDL_IMPO option is used.

![Figure](/figures/v7.32.128/10000000000003A000000406E2F8A0286FCEC124.jpg)

The solid is also waterproof and the fluid exchange between cracks and solids is blocked. The fluid pressures on the edges of the joint element and the hydraulic Lagrange multipliers are blocked at zero.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics A

The Modelling is performed in flat deformations with 3644 elements TRI3 for the solid and 201 elements QU4 for discontinuity. The adopted cohesive law is CZM_EXP_REG. The mechanical loading is slow: the loading speed ${q}_{t}$ is $0,002\mu {\mathrm{m.s}}^{-1}$.

Time Discretization: 29 time step from $10s$.

## 6 Modelling D

### 6.1 Modelling characteristics

$D$ Modelling is almost identical to $A$ Modelling. Only elements D_PLAN of the massif is modified. They are replaced by elements D_PLAN_HMS. Thus, rather than a totally waterproof medium, one considers a medium with low permeability:

Exchanges between cracks and massives are permitted for this Modelling.

## 7 Summary of the results

The results obtained validate the correct functioning of the elements joints with hydro-mechanical coupling with the regulated cohesive laws.

Modellings $A$ and $B$ test the compatibility of the element joint with CZM_EXP_REG law. The results are compared with experimental results. The overall mechanical response of the structure and the effect of the loading speed on the crack flow are reproduced correctly numerically.

$C$ Modelling, by non-regression testing only, validates the use of CZM_LIN_REG law. The results are fully in line with what is expected.

Finally, $D$ Modelling allows to test compatibility with cohesive laws in the presence of elements HM_DPQ8S superfacials that are very little permeable in the massif.
