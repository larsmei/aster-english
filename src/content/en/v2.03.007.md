# v2.03.007 SDLS07 - Eigenmodes of a thin spherical envelope

**Summary:**

This VPCS guide test validates the CALC_MODES own values search operator algorithms with the following modellings rigidity and mass matrices:

1. Three-dimensional shells: elements finished DKT (mailing a 1/8 sphere),
2. $\mathrm{2D}$ axisymmetric TRIA6 and QUAD8 (mesh of a section),
3. Three-dimensional shells: elements finished axisymmetric isoparametric SEG3 (mesh linear section),
4. COQUE_3D three-dimensional shells: finite element MEC3QU9H (mesh 1/8 sphere),
5. COQUE_3D three-dimensional shells: finite element MEC3TR7H.

The results obtained are compared with analytical solution (HAYEK) and show for the first six modes differences less than:

- $\text{0,45\%}$ for elements continuous media axisymmetric,
- $\text{0,20\%}$ for elements DKT shell,
- $\text{0,17\%}$ for elements isoparametric axisymmetric hulls,
- $\text{0,17\%}$ for elements COQUE_3D.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is homogeneous, isotropic, linear elastic. The elastic coefficients are: $E=\mathrm{200 000 }\mathrm{MPa}$ and $\nu =0.3$ .

The density is constant and is $\rho =7800\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The structure is free in space.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

For thin spheres ($\mathrm{i.t}\ll R$ with $i$ , mode order), the eigenmodes with radial and tangential displacement established by membrane theory are given by [bib1] and [bib2]:

${f}_{i}=\frac{{\lambda}_{i}}{2\pi R}\sqrt{\frac{E}{\rho (1-{\nu}^{2})}}$

with ${\lambda}_{i}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\sqrt{b\pm \sqrt{{b}^{2}-4(1-{\nu}^{2})({i}^{2}+i-2)}}$ and $b={i}^{2}+i+1+3\nu$

The theory presented by Hayek allows to introduce a correction of the bending effect (approximation of the general theory of Wilkinson) which leads to values of ${\lambda}_{i}$ function of the

$\begin{array}{}a={t}^{2}/12{R}^{2}\\ b=i(i+1)\end{array}$

and solution of:

${\lambda}_{i}^{4}-{\lambda}_{i}^{2}[1+3\nu -a(1-\nu )+b(1+a\nu +\mathrm{ab})]+\mathrm{ab}[{b}^{2}-\mathrm{4b}+5-{\nu}^{2}]+(1-{\nu}^{2})[b-2(1+a)]=0$

## 8 Summary of the results

- DKT hull Modelling, restricted here to modes with 3symmetries compared to $\text{x = 0}$ , $\text{y = 0}$ , $z=0$ , provides eigenfrequencies with an error of less than $\text{0.4\%}$ on the first 20 modes.
- $\mathrm{2D}$ axisymmetric continuous media Modelling provides eigenfrequencies with an error below $\text{2\%}$.
- COQUE_AXIS (elements isoparametric quadratic) Modelling provides eigenfrequencies with an error of less than $\text{0.2\%}$ on the first 5 modes (space discretization identical to the trace of mesh $\mathrm{2D}$ axisymmetric).
- Degenerate COQUE_3D Modelling (elements thick shell MEC3QU9H, MEC3TR7H) provides eigenfrequencies with an error of less than $\text{1.2\%}$ on the first 10 modes.
