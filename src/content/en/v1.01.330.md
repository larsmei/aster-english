# v1.01.330 ZZZZ330 – Validation of the calculation of potential energy for elements beams

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the calculation of potential energy for the following elements beams: POU_D_EM, POU_D_TG and POU_D_TGM.

The calculation of potential energy is done after a static calculation and a modal calculation.

**Note:**

*The validation is already done for elements* *POU_D_E* and *POU_D_T.*

## 2 Reference Solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

**In the static linear case:**

${E}_{\mathit{pot}}={W}^{\mathit{ext}}=\frac{1}{2}{\sum}_{i\in N}{D}_{i}{F}_{i}^{\mathit{ext}}$ where $N$ is all the nodes of the model.

For a small model it is therefore easy to calculate potential energy from displacements.

**In the case of modal calculation:**

If $\Phi$ is a problem-specific mode, eigenfrequency $f=\frac{\omega}{2\pi }$, with $K$ rigidity matrix of $M$ mass matrix then $(K-{\omega}^{2}M)\Phi =0$, from where ${\Phi}^{T}(K-{\omega}^{2}M)\Phi =0$.

If we standard modes relative to the $M$ mass matrix then we have ${\Phi}^{T}K\Phi ={\omega}^{2}={(2\pi f)}^{2}$ .

Or ${E}_{\mathit{pot}}=\frac{1}{2}{\Phi}^{T}K\Phi$ . So just check that ${E}_{\mathit{pot}}=2{(\pi f)}^{2}$ .
