# v4.02.101 TPLL101 - Heating by effect Joule of a hollow cylinder

**Summary:**

Electrical currents are imposed inside and outside a hollow cylinder of finished length, and then the temperature established by a Joule-induced heat source is calculated. The reference solution is analytical.

The field of application is the stationary linear thermal.

The model is axisymmetric.

The test is performed on elements QUAD8 and TRIA6 for the first Modelling, axisymmetric (AXIS). With elements TETRA4 for the second Modelling, 3D.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

- Elastostatic problem $V$ electric potential

In volume $\Delta V=0.$

boundary conditions NEUMANN $\lbrace \begin{array}{cc}\mathrm{j.n}=0.& \text{sur}\mathrm{CD}\text{et}\mathrm{AB}\\ \mathrm{j.n}=-10.& \text{sur}\mathrm{AD}\\ \mathrm{j.n}=3.6787944& \text{sur}\mathrm{BC}\end{array}$

$\sigma =1.$ electric conductivity

$j.n=-\sigma \nabla V$

Axisymmetric Solution

$\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(r\frac{\partial V}{\partial r})=0\Rightarrow V={V}_{0}\log\frac{r}{A}$

Boundary conditions on $\mathrm{AD}$ and $\mathrm{BC}$ require:

${V}_{0}=10.$

Note:

*Knowledge of $A$ is not required for thermal calculation.*

- Thermal problem $T$ temperature

$-\lambda \Delta T=s$ with $s=\sigma {(\nabla V)}^{2}$ volumetric source

Boundary conditions: $T=0.$ on $\mathrm{DA}$ and $\mathrm{BC}$

> $-\lambda \nabla T.n=0$ on $\mathrm{DC}$ and $\mathrm{AB}$

Axisymmetric solution:

$\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(r\frac{\partial T}{\partial r})=-\frac{\sigma}{1}\frac{{v}_{0}^{2}}{{r}^{2}}$ $\Rightarrow$ with boundary conditions

$T(r)=-\frac{1}{2}\sigma \frac{{v}_{0}^{2}}{\lambda}\log(\frac{r}{{R}_{0}})\log(\frac{r}{{R}_{1}})$

### 2.2 Baseline

$T=588.9313°C$ (temperature at point $M$).

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.
