# v3.03.311 SSLA311 – Circular fissure subject to an annular charge

**Summary:**

This test case deals with the case of an internal crack located in the centre of a circular bar, in axisymmetric mode. The particularity of the load is that the linear load applies to a ring very close to the crack bottom. The fixed parameter is the radius of the $a$ crack, and the parameter to be explored is the application radius of the $c$ charge. The medium is elastic, linear, homogeneous and isotropic. The geometric domain is assumed to be unlimited in $r$ and $z$.

The meshes must therefore take into account the proximity of the load in such a way as to respect the load application deck.

Since the load is normal at the crack, we calculate the characteristic coefficient of the $K$ breaker mechanics in pure $I$ mode. Then we use the Irwin formula in flat deformation to deduce $G$ from it.

This is of course an approximation.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The point $\mathit{PB}$ is used for the application of ${F}_{Y}$ force.

The position of the point $\mathit{PB}$ is different depending on the modellings, and is managed by the $C$ parameter.

The distance $a$ separating the $\mathit{P8}$ and $\mathit{P0}$ points is a constant parameter ($a=0,1$ ).

In the different models, the $c/a$ ratio is modified.

The lip of the crack is the $\mathit{P8P0}$ straight. $\mathit{P0}$ is therefore the crack front.

For all modellings (A, B, C and D):

Movement required:

| Clamped support on the $\mathit{P8P9}$ side | $\mathit{DX}=0$ |
| --- | --- |
| Clamped support on the $\mathit{P0P11}$ side | $\mathit{DY}=0$ |

Charge imposed:

$$
\textrm{Force Nodale sur le point}
\mathit{PB}
$$

Only the point $\mathit{PB}$ position and the resulting mesh position are different between the modellings (the mesh radius size radiating at the bottom of the crack is different depending on the modellings).

## 7 Summary of the results

On these 4 modellings, we find the solution displayed by Y. MURAKAMI numerically.

The difference maximum between reference solution and the solution calculated by CODE_ASTER is $0,02$ %.

This test validates the use of RUPTURE operators on modellings axisymmetric.

## 8 Remarks

It is necessary to take precautions for the use of the FORCE_NODALE key word of the CALC_G command. The load creates a singular stress field in the vicinity of its point application. In the particular case we process, where the load is close to the crack bottom, therefore crowns, the upper radius of the $G$ calculation crown must not reach the singular field created by the load (at the risk of having false results).
