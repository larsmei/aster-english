# v7.03.101 HPLV101 - Uniformization of homogeneous material

**Summary:**

This test, in a trivial situation where the material is homogeneous, has the solution of stationary thermal and mechanical problems, with loads corresponding to a temperature gradient and a required deformation, similar to those corresponding to the elementary problems of the periodic homogenization method.
