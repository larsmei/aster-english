# v2.02.015 SDLL15 - slender beam, clamped-free, with excreted mass or inertia

**Summary:**

This three-dimensional problem consists in calculating the frequencies and vibration modes of a mechanical structure composed of a slender straight beam, clamped-free, tubular cross section and an excreted mass attached to the free end of the beam. This Structures Mechanics test corresponds to a dynamic analysis of a linear model with linear behavior. It consists of a single model.

This problem allows testing the Euler Bernouilli beam element, the point mass model and the modal calculation by the Lanczos method.

The results obtained are in good agreement with those of the VPCS guide. The two calculations performed (excentricity of the point mass zero or different from zero) allow to highlight the coupling of the different modes when the point mass is excreted.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in the SDLL15/89 form of the VPCS guide which presents the calculation method as follows:

The problem with non-excise mass leads to decoupled modes:

- traction-compression (mass-only effect),
- torsion (effect of inertia around the neutral fibre),
- bending in planes $x,y$ and $x,z$ (mass effect).

The different frequencies are determined with a model by elements finished beam d-Euler (slender beam).

For the first mode with an excreted mass, a Rayleigh method gives the approach formula:

${f}_{1}=\frac{1}{2\pi }\sqrt{\frac{3E{I}_{z}}{{L}^{3}({m}_{c}+0.24M)}}$

with $M$ = total beam mass.

When the mass is out of reach, $(x,z)$ bending and twist modes are coupled, as well as $(x,y)$ bending and traction-compression modes.

For the proper mode, point $B$ components are used to calculate the components at the centre of gravity of the mass (point $C$ ) by:

$\left[\begin{array}{c}{u}_{c}\\ {v}_{c}\\ {w}_{c}\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{c}{u}_{B}\\ {v}_{B}\\ {w}_{B}\end{array}\right]+\left[\begin{array}{ccc}0& {z}_{c}& -{y}_{c}\\ -{z}_{c}& 0& +{x}_{c}\\ +{y}_{c}& -{x}_{c}& 0\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c}{\theta}_{\mathit{xB}}\\ {\theta}_{\mathit{yB}}\\ {\theta}_{\mathit{zB}}\end{array}\right]$

${u}_{c}={u}_{B}=-{\theta}_{\mathit{zB}}$

For this test:

$\begin{array}{c}{v}_{c}={v}_{B}\\ {w}_{c}={w}_{B}+{\theta}_{\mathit{xB}}\end{array}$

Calculations by:

```text
CALC_MODES
OPTION = 'PLUS_PETITE'
CALC_FREQ=_F(NMAX_FREQ = n)    Cas 1: n=10, Cas 2: n=8
SOLVEUR_MODAL=_F(METHODE = 'TRI_DIAG')
```

In the test, the $\frac{{u}_{C}}{{v}_{B}}$ ratio values for modes 2 and 3 (except manually) cannot be checked. As regards the $\frac{{w}_{C}}{{w}_{B}}$ values, the technique is as follows: if you impose ${w}_{B}=1$ (NORM_MODE command), then you have $\frac{{w}_{C}}{{w}_{B}}=1+{\theta}_{\mathit{xB}}$ and you can check the ${\theta}_{\mathit{xB}}$ values.
