# v6.04.218 SSNV218 – Calculation of the energy parameter Gp in 3D

**Summary**

This nonlinear static mechanical test presents the calculation of the ${G}_{p}$ parameter in $\mathrm{3D}$ for a quarter-shaped ellipse block structure using the post-processing CALC_GP macro. The structure is subjected to traction in $I$ mode at constant temperature.

The upstream calculation is a nonlinear calculation with isotropic hardening (VMIS_ISOT_TRAC) in large deformations.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The face in $x=0$ has a displacement imposed according to the $x$ zero axis.

The face in $y=\mathrm{Ly}$ has a displacement imposed according to the $y$ zero axis.

The face in $z=0$ has a displacement imposed according to the $z$ zero axis.

A time linear growth force is applied to the face at an altitude of $\mathrm{Lz}$ .

This force is $\mathrm{2000N}$ at the moment $\text{INST}=\mathrm{20s}$.

## 2 reference solution

This test case is a case of non-regression. The solution to which we compare here is obtained using a python version of the command, which allowed to put the point approach ${G}_{p}$ .

The bottom of the shell is elliptical in shape, described by slices. Each slice consists of chips. This total area of chips corresponds to the virtual propagation of the shell.

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.218/10000200000000C1000000959059F6BE534A4658.png)

Each chip shall be determined and at each moment the ${G}_{p}(\Delta S)$ quantity defined by:

${G}_{p}(\Delta S)=2[{W}_{\mathit{elas}}^{\mathit{traction}}(\Delta S)]/\Delta S$

where ${W}_{\mathit{elas}}^{\mathit{traction}}(\Delta S)$ is the elastic energy of traction calculated on the cumulated area of shaving on a slice.
