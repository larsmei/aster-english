# v6.05.112 SSNS112 – Compression test and alternating traction of a reinforced concrete pole

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the alternating uniaxial response of a reinforced concrete beam, based on experimental tests, modelled by laws:

- GLRC_DM, [R7.01.32](doc:r7.01.32), generalised reinforced concrete law used with elements DKTG
- MAZARS_UNIL,[R5.03.09], 1D Concrete Law, combined with a nonlinear law for VMIS_CINE_GC steel on a POU_D_EM Multifibre beam model
- DHRC, [R7.01.37](doc:r7.01.37), homogenized reinforced concrete law used with elements DKTG.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A reinforced concrete pole of $0,7m$ length , on $\mathrm{Ox}$ axis , of square cross-section of height and width equal to $0,15m$ is considered.

![Figure](/figures/v6.05.112/10000201000004370000032A7BF090CEAEEEB4A9.png)

Figure 1: section du poteau en béton armé.

The longitudinal frames are four $\mathit{HA14}$ .

Cross-sectional frames are not included in the modellings below.

Two $X$ and $Y$ reinforcing beds are used for Modelling A.

For Modelling B, one steel fibre of $6,15{\mathit{mm}}^{2}$ section is used.

### 1.2 Material properties

GLRC_DM constitutive law has the following parameters for concrete:

- Young's modulus: $E=28500\mathit{MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.2$
- Maximum compression stress: ${\sigma}_{c}=25\mathrm{MPa}$
- Compression peak deformation: ${ϵ}_{c}=2,25.\text{}{10}^{-3}$
- Maximum tensile stress: ${\sigma}_{t}=2,94\mathit{MPa}$

The parameters for steel are:

- Young's modulus: $E=195000\mathit{MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.3$
- Elasticity limit: ${\sigma}_{y}=610\mathrm{MPa}$
- Tangent module (plastics): ${E}_{t}=19,5\mathit{MPa}$

DEFI_GLRC is used to obtain the parameters of GLRC_DM law. The stress of the concrete has been reduced to ${\sigma}_{t}=1,6\mathit{MPa}$. In addition, the parameters ${\gamma}_{c}=0,35$ and ${\alpha}_{c}=60$ are also fixed for nonlinear compression behavior.

The equivalent membrane elastic module, cf.[R7.01.32], is valid with this material data: ${E}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{m}=34021.0\mathrm{MPa}$ , i.e. a stiffness membrane according to the $\mathrm{Ox}$ direction: ${E}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{m}\ast S=765.393\mathrm{MN}$ .

The DEFI_MATER_GC operator was used to determine the parameters of the MAZARS_UNIL and VMIS_CINE_GC laws.

One end of the beam, edge $A$, is blocked and the other end, edge $B$, is imposed a distributed effort of result $\mathit{FX}=1\mathit{kN}$ in the direction $X$.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is given by experimental results, acquired from the QJ5TC test named test, provided in [1] . Based on the force cycles – deformation measured on average on the pole, we identify, see Figure 1:

- a range of deformations between -0.002 and 0.003, i.e. not causing general plastic deformations of steels in view of steel characteristics, except potentially locally at cracks;
- an stiffness elastic equivalent axial $743,7\mathit{MN}$ ,
- an stiffness axial equivalent post-damage traction $120,1\mathit{MN}$ , i.e. a ratio between the two $0,161$ .

### 2.1 Bibliographical references

1. BENMANSOUR M.B. Modelling of alternating cyclic behaviour of reinforced concrete. Application to various static pole tests.Thesis of Doctorate of the National School of Bridges and Chaussés. 6 January 1997.

## 6 Summary of the results

The results of the different modellings are compared with the experimental measurements [1] . All models allow a satisfactory representation of the dissymmetric behaviour of the beam in tensile and compression. Elastic and post-elastic stiffeners are correctly reproduced. The d-hysteresis loops are less extensive because the behavioral models do not represent all the dissipative mechanisms of the reinforced concrete material.
