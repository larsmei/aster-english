## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The displacement field following the $y$ axis at the end of the plate ($\mathit{BC}$ segment) is given in the beam theory hypothesis by:

${u}_{y}^{\mathit{BC}}=\frac{{\mathit{PL}}^{3}}{{\mathrm{3EI}}_{z}}(1+0.98\frac{{l}^{2}}{{L}^{2}})$ (solution with shear force in Timoshenko beam)

d'où ${u}_{y}^{\mathit{BC}}=0.00121m$

The normal stress field ${\sigma}_{xx}$ due to bending is given by:

${\sigma}_{xx}=\frac{\mathit{Pl}}{{\mathrm{2I}}_{z}}(L-x)$ on the $\mathit{AB}$ edge

either ${\sigma}_{xx}=37.8\times {10}^{6}(L-x)$

- ${\sigma}_{xx}$ constraints of nodes $A$ , $B$ , $E$
