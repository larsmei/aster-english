# v6.01.123 SSNA123 – constitutive law validation of axisymmetric-irradiated steels

**Summary:**

This elemental test is intended to validate the constitutive law IRRAD3M of the steels under irradiation. Three modellings are present to validate each aspect of the law separately:

- modelling (a) focuses on the plastic part of the law,
- modelling (b) on the irradiation part,
- modelling (c) on the swelling part.
