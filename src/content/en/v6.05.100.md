# v6.05.100 SSNS100 - Non-linear behaviour of a thermally loaded rebar

**Summary:**

A non-extracted rebar sheet relative to the average sheet and clamped on its four sides is subjected to thermal loading. The orientations of the rebar are confused with the $(X,Y)$ axes of the global reference.

The main interest of this test is to validate the numerical integration of GRILLE_ISOT_LINE and GRILLE_CINE_LINE elasto-plastic behavior models of a rebar array associated with finite element GRILLE_EXCENTRE (plate with excentered against reference plane), in the general STAT_NON_LINE algorithm.

To obtain reference solutions, analytical solutions have been established for the two elasto-plastic behaviors with isotropic hardening and linear kinematics.
