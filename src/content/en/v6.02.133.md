# v6.02.133 SSNL133 – Post-elastically-flooded L-shaped structure

**Summary:**

A L-shaped structure composed of two slender beams thin rectangular cross-section is clamped at one of its ends and subjected to a force at the other end. The post-flood behavior associated with positive force values is sought. The field of testing is:

- nonlinear elastic mechanics (large displacements, large rotations),
- beam-blazing (instability),
- the modellings tested are POU_D_TGM,POU_D_T_GD,POU_D_TG,POU_D_T,POU_D_E andPOU_D_EM.

It allows you to test the kinematics in large movements and large rotations activated via the keyword GROT_GDEP.

Note: reference can be made to test case SSLL105 for determining critical flaming loads for this structure.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.02.133/100000000000032C000002D81C7F36758CE94D54.png)

Disruption

### 1.2 Material properties

$E=71240\mathit{MPa}$

$\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

boundary condition: clamped support en $A$

Loading in C depending on the $t\in [0;5]$ pseudo-time:

${F}_{x}=1.0\times t[N]$

${F}_{z}=0.001\times t[N]$ (disruption to initiate post-slambing behaviour)

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The post-flood response obtained with POU_D_T_GD Modelling (modelization A) is taken as reference solution, which will provide non-regression values.

### 2.2 Baseline

Non-regression tests at a few points of force-moving curves representative of the structure's behavior (Force applied to point $C$ based on the displacement of point $C$ following $X$ and $Z$ ).

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Other Aster modelling.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

  1. DEVESA: Treatment of large displacements in the element 7ddl corneal implanted in the *Code_Aster* validation by a classic test case (HM‐77/94/079).

  1. Shakourzadeh. Modelling of three-dimensional thin-walled beams and simulation of nonlinear geometric and elastoplastic behavior. Ph.D. Thesis, University of Compiègne, Compiègne (1994).

### 3.4 Remarks

The strategy adopted to obtain the force-movement response of the structure is to pilot effort.

The time step automatic subdivision is also required. Indeed, the element POU_D_T_GD does not converge uniformly into a residue and it is therefore difficult to complete a calculation with a uniform time cut unless taking very small time step which is suboptimal.
