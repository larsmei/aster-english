# v3.04.306 SSLV306 - 3D beam on required travel

**Summary:**

The test is designed to validate the forced displacements on the face (FACE_IMPO), their values being variable in space. These values are imposed on the end of a 3D beam, Modelling a bending load.

## 5 Modelling B

The characteristics are identical to those of model A, only the load is different.

After applying the force according to Y as for model A, the displacement field on the upper side of the beam is extracted.

This displacement field is then applied with CHAMNO_IMPO (with a multiplicative coefficient that has no physical meaning and allows computer verification).

This technique can be used to make a structural zoom.

## 6 Modelling results B

It is verified that the displacement at the end of the beam is the same as for model A with the multiplicative coefficient.

## 7 Summary of the results

The function "impaired displacement function" provides the expected results; the values of bending constraints are satisfactory, considering that the problem being dealt with is a bending problem.

Comparison of the displacement fields obtained by CHAMNO_IMPO with a multiplicative coefficient and reference solution shows no difference.
