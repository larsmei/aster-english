# v1.01.237 ZZZZ237 – LIAISON_UNIL and TOLE_APPA elemental validation

**Summary:**

This test is an analytical and non-regression test. Analytical tests are performed on movements only. These tests are supplemented with non-regression on nodal force values. The objective is to validate the following features of the DEFI_CONTACT operator:

- nodes exclusion by TOLE_APPA in discrete and continuous formulation
- restraint's LIAISON_UNIL unilateral displacement condition

Modelization A:

- 'CONTRAINTE' method reference contact calculation, TOLE_APPA validation

Modelling B:

- Validation of LIAISON_UNIL compared to the results of Modelling A
