### 7.1 Equations introduced

The command THER_LINEAIRE [U4.33.01](doc:u4.33.01) makes it possible to treat the equation in the transient case as it is described above, but it also makes it possible to solve the steady problem, which reduces to the following equation:

and the following boundary conditions:

${t}_{s}$ being the time instant taken in order to evaluate the boundary conditions of the equation.

In the transient case, an initial state must be supplied. This initial state (temperature field) may be chosen from among the following:

- a field which may be uniform or arbitrary, created by the command CREA_CHAMP,
- a result field of a steady problem described by the equations above; the computation time instant is taken at the first time instant defined in the list of reals describing the time discretisation defined by the user,
- a field extracted from the result of a transient problem.

The time discretisation (value of $\Delta t$) must be supplied in the form of one or more lists of time instants. These lists are created by the user by the command DEFI_LIST_REEL [U4.21.04](doc:u4.21.04).

A thermal transient may be computed by making several calls to the command THER_LINEAIRE [U4.33.01](doc:u4.33.01), enriching the same concept of type evol_ther and supplying, from the second call onwards, the initial time instant of the restart of the analysis (in order to obtain ${T}^{-}$) and possibly the final time instant.

The temperature fields resulting from an analysis contain both the value at the nodes of the mesh and at the Lagrange nodes. On a restart of the analysis, it is possible to vary the type of the boundary conditions; the field used internally to initiate the new analysis is then reduced to the nodes of the mesh alone. The result concept of type evol_ther will then contain nodal fields based on different numberings. The operators of *Code_Aster* then interpolate only at the nodes of the mesh when the numbering differs.
