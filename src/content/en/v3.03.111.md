# v3.03.111 SSLS111 - Single plate excentering

**Summary:**

This test allows the validation of single plates (i.e., it is neither multilayered nor homogenized behaviour).

The modellings used are:

> Modelling $A$ - DST, DSTQ(QUAD4)+MECA_STATIQUE
> Modelling $B$ - DKT, DKTG(TRIA3)+MECA_STATIQUE
> $D$ Modelling - Q4G , Q4G(QUAD4)+MECA_STATIQUE
> $E$ Modelling - Q4G , Q4G(QUAD4)+MECA_STATIQUE
> Modelling $F$ - DKT, DKTG(QUAD4)+MECA_STATIQUE
> Modelling $G$ - DST,DSQ(QUAD4)+MECA_STATIQUE
> Modelling $H$ - DKTG,DKTG(QUAD4)+MECA_STATIQUE
> Modelling $I$ - Q4G, Q4G(QUAD4)+MECA_STATIQUE
> Modelling $J$ - DKT, DKT(QUAD4)+STAT_NON_LINE
> Modelling $K$ - DKTG, DKTG(QUAD4)+STAT_NON_LINE
> Modelling $M$ - Q4GG, Q4GG(QUAD4)+ STAT_NON_LINE
> $N$ Modelling - DKTG, DKTG(QUAD4)+STAT_NON_LINE – GLRC_DAMAGE
> $O$ Modelling - DKT. DKT(QUAD4)+STAT_NON_LINE

Modellings $A$ and $B$: Two plates are modelled out of the average plane and the results are compared to an analytical solution.

$D$ Modelling , $E$ , $F$ , $G$ , $H$ , $I$ , $J$ , $K$ , $M$ , $N$ :

A single plate is modelled out of the average plane and the results are compared to an analytical solution. Between $D$ and $E$ Modelling, the fineness of the mesh is changed.

$O$ Modelling:

A single plate is modelled off the average plane to validate the calculation of the SIXZ and SIYZ stress components. Two calculations are performed, the first in monolayer, which is used as a reference to the second in multilayer.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 modellings A et B

The $A1A4$ edge is clamped.The plate is $0,8m$ thick. The plate is excreted from $e=0,4m$.

A nodal force $\mathit{Fz}=–1000\text{N}$ is applied to the $A1A2$ edge.

#### 1.3.2 Modelization D, E, F, G , H, I, J, K, M and N

The $A1A4$ edge is clamped. The plate is $0,8m$ thick. The plate is excreted from $e=0,4m$.

A ${F}_{z}=–1000N/m$ transverse distributed nodal force is applied to the $A2A3$ edge and a distributed nodal force of ${F}_{x}=4000N/m$ traction is applied to the same $A2A3$ edge.

#### 1.3.3 Modelling O

The $A1A4$ edge is clamped. The plate is $0,8m$ thick. The plate is excreted from $e=0,4m$.

A nodal force $\mathit{Fz}=–1000\text{N}$ is applied to the $A1A2$ edge.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

#### 2.1.1 modellings A et B

The deflection $\mathrm{fl}$ is given by the formula: $\mathrm{fl}={\mathrm{FlL}}^{3}/\mathrm{3EI}$

where $l$ is the width, $L$ the length of the plate, and $I={\mathrm{lh}}^{3}/12$ , $h$ being the thickness.

### 2.2 Baseline

#### 2.2.1 modellings A et B

They consist of the $\mathrm{DZ}$ displacement field values at $\mathrm{A3}$ and point $\mathrm{A1}$ forces. On the other hand, the 4 smallest frequencies of the structure are calculated.

#### 2.2.2 Modelization C to N

In this case, only one ex-entry plate is represented (ex-center $e=0,4m$ , $h/2=0,4m$ thickness ). The deflection $w$ at the free end is given by the expression: $w=\left(2{F}_{z}L-3{F}_{x}e\right)\ell {L}^{2}/6\mathit{EI}+{F}_{z}L/(6\mathit{Gh5}/6)$ . The overall stresses on the clamped $\mathrm{A1A4}$ edge , of $\ell =5m$ length are: ${N}_{x}={F}_{x}$ and ${V}_{z}=-{F}_{z}$ .

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

For modellings $A$ and $B$ , the reference solution is analytical, so there is no uncertainty.

For other modellings, another solution is used as reference solution for the eigenfrequency from an unexccentric calculation.

For Modelling O, two calculations are performed, the first in monolayer, which is used as a reference for the second in multilayer.

## 16 Summary of the results

The results of the 3 modellings are very good, except for shear forces modellings $B$ ($15\text{\%}$ error). modellings $D,E,F,G$ also give good results. However, it should be noted that the Q4G Modelling depends on mesh (because of the isoparametric interpolation of the rotation-translations). In other words, with refinement of mesh one reduces the errors made with the D Modelling.

For $O$ Modelling, the results are consistent with what is expected for an excreted plate.
