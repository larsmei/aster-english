# v6.04.503 SSNV503 - slider slippery on a rigid plane

**Summary:**

This test represents a contact calculation of a slider slippery on a rigid plane. The objective of this test is to enable the calculation of the Coulomb criterion and the correct transmission of pressure to be validated in a certain way.

The different modellings of the contact area tested are:

- **Modelization B** (2D): contact node-mesh cell with penalised method and function-defined game.
- **Modelling C** (2D): contact node-mesh cell, continuous method for contact and friction, set defined by a function.
- **Modelization D** (2D): contact node-mesh cell, penalized method for contact and friction, geometrical set.
- **Modelling E** (3D): contact node-mesh cell, continuous method for contact and friction, set defined by a function.
- **Modelling F** (3D): contact node-mesh cell, penalised method for contact and friction, set defined by a function.
- **G** (3D) Modelling: contact node-mesh cell, penalized method for contact and friction, geometrical set.
- **Modelling H** (2D): contact node-mesh cell, continuous method for contact and friction, geometrical set.
- **Modelling** **I** (3D): contact node-mesh cell, continuous method for contact and friction, geometrical set.
- **Modelling** **J** (2D): contact by elements joint, JOINT_MECA_FROT law

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.503/Object_5.svg)

Calculation hypothesis: We neglect the deformation of slider.

Loading 1 – Normal pressure ${P}_{n}:\mathrm{300N}/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}$

Check:

1. The correct transmission of normal forces at the contact area: the normal pressure at the contact area is equal to the pressure applied

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.503/Object_87.svg)

2. That the vertical displacement of the slider at the $\mathrm{AB}$ contact area is equal to the game.

Loading 2 – Normal pressure ${P}_{n}:\mathrm{300N}/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}$ and tangential pressure ${P}_{t}=\mathrm{178.2N}/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}$

It is a grip situation. It is checked that nodes of the slider located in the slip zone ($\mathrm{AB}$ ) do not move tangentially:

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.503/Object_7.svg)

Loading 3 – Normal pressure ${P}_{n}:\mathrm{300N}/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}$ and tangential pressure ${P}_{t}=\mathrm{181.8N}/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}$

It is a slip situation. It is checked that nodes of the slider located in the slip zone ($\mathrm{AB}$ ) moves from $\mathrm{9mm}$ to $X$:

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.503/Object_97.svg)

Determination of the spring stiffness $k$: the spring stiffness is determined according to the desired displacement. At the time of the slip, the force in the spring is:

${F}_{r}={F}_{t}–\mu$ ${F}_{n}=0,01\mu {F}_{n}$ with (${F}_{t}=181.8\times 20$ , ${F}_{n}=300\times 40$ )

| ${F}_{r}=K{U}_{t}$ | : | force in spring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ${F}_{t}={P}_{t}\times {S}_{\mathrm{AD}}$ | : | tangential force |
| ${F}_{n}={P}_{n}\times {S}_{\mathrm{CD}}$ | : | normal force |
| ${U}_{t}$ | : | tangential displacement |
| ${S}_{\mathrm{AD}}$ | : | surface |
| ${S}_{\mathrm{DC}}$ | : | surface |

For $9.\mathrm{mm}$ displacement the $k$ spring stiffness must be $0.01\mu {F}_{n}/9=4N/\mathrm{mm}$

### 2.2 Baseline

- **Charge 1(Normal Pressure** ${P}_{n}$ **):**

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.503/Object_9.svg)

- **Charge 2 (** ${P}_{n}:\mathrm{300N}/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}$ **and** ${P}_{t}=\mathrm{178.2N}/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}$ **):** Check that there is at least one node of the contact surface that does not slip. It is tested that at least one of the nodes on the opposite side of the side load application does not slip.
- **Load 3 (** ${P}_{n}:\mathrm{300N}/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}$ **and** ${P}_{t}=181.8/{\mathrm{mm}}^{2}$ **):** All nodes of the contact surface is checked for slippage. All nodes on the opposite side of the side load application is tested for slippage.

Less than 0.1%
