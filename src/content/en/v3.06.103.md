# v3.06.103 SSLA103 - Calculation of desiccation removal and endogenous removal on a cylinder

**Summary:**

This test case is designed to validate the calculation of desiccation removal and endogenous withdrawal. It also tests the possibility of making dependent the characteristics of hydration and drying materials (in the case of the Mazars model). It is a cylinder that undergoes a uniform drying and hydration. The temperature also varies.

The cylinder is modeled by four elements quadrangles at 8 nodes for modellings $A$ , $C$ , $E$ , $F$ , $H$ and by a element HEXA20 for $B$ Modelling . For modellings:

- $A$ and $B$ , the behavior is assumed to be elastic, which allows to validate the withdrawal calculation both with STAT_NON_LINE and with MECA_STATIQUE.
- $C$ and $H$ enable the removal calculation to be validated with the law of MAZARS and MAZARS_UNIL (without damage activation).
- $E$ validates the removal calculation with ENDO_ISOT_BETON law (with NEWTON method and IMPLEX method).
- $F$ coupling ENDO_ISOT_BETON / BETON_UMLV_FP

The results obtained by c *ode_aster* are identical to the reference analytical solution.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On the side $\mathrm{AB}$ : ${u}_{z}=0$

The structure varies evenly:

1. the temperature of $T=20°C$ at the initial time up to $T=120°C$ at the final time
2. the moisture content of $100l/{m}^{3}$ at the initial time up to $80l/{m}^{3}$ at the final time
3. hydration varies from 0. at the initial time to 1. at the final time.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Given the nature of the stresses, total deformation is only due to the withdrawal and thermal expansion. Consequently, we have:

![Figure](/figures/v3.06.103/Object_9.svg)

with:

- $T$ , time temperature $t$
- ${T}_{\mathrm{ref}}$ , reference temperature
- ${C}_{0}$ , the initial moisture content ($\text{HR=100\%}$ moisture content).
- $C$ , the water content at time $t$
- $h$ , the degree of hydration at time $t$
- $\alpha$ , the expansion coefficient
- $\kappa$ , the removal coefficient of desiccation
- $\beta$ , endogenous withdrawal coefficient

Since elastic deformations are zero in this problem, the stresses are zero, as well as damage in the case of modellings with the law of MAZARS and ENDO_ISOT_BETON.

### 2.2 Baseline

The value of the deformation is checked after 3600 days, as well as the stress. It is also checked that the plastic deformation is zero, as well as the damage for the modellings concerned. The results are tested with STAT_NON_LINE and with MECA_STATIQUE (for modellings $A$ and $B$).

## 8 Summary of the results

The results obtained with the *Code_Aster* are identical to the analytical solution. The thermal expansion and endogenous removals and desiccation calculations for the elastic model, whether with STAT_NON_LINE or MECA_STATIQUE, as well as for the Mazars law, for the ENDO_ISOT_BETON law and for the BETON_UMLV_FP/ENDO_ISOT_BETON coupling case, have been validated. Note that the modellings $A$ and $B$ also validate the withdrawal calculations for the VMIS_ISOT_TRAC and VMIS_ISOT_LINE laws that use the same routine as ELAS.
