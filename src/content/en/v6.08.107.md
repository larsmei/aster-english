# v6.08.107 SSND107: Multiple rotations in large deformations, kinematic and mixed hardening

**Summary:**

This test modeled an element subjected to four $45°$ rigid body tensile-rotation cycles, with constitutive laws elasto-plastics with kinematic and mixed nutring in large hypoelastic deformations (GDEF_LOG). On the one hand, the invariance of the equivalent stress of von Mises during the rotation phases is checked and the values obtained with the different constitutive laws are identical. This test validates the treatment of kinematic nutring made in the context of the large hypoelastic deformations.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.08.107/10000200000004530000015FCDEB098627D618FE.png)

*Reference problem (for $90°$ rotation)*

A element of $1000\mathrm{mm}$ cubic material is considered to be on the side, subject alternately to a traction force and then to an overall rotation of $45°$. It undergoes a total of 4 traction/rotation cycles.

## 2 Baseline

This test does not have a reference result as such.

The solutions provided by each of the laws are compared (they are supposed to be equivalent).

In addition, the constantness of the equivalent stress of Von Mises is checked during the rotation phases.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results are satisfactory. All constitutive laws results are identical and the rotation of rigid bodies does not give rise to any additional stress. The model of large deformations tested is therefore objective.
