# v6.02.105 SSNL105 - Hooped frame

**Summary:**

Mechanical test of structures in static nonlinear elasticity.

The frame is made of articulated bars. The bays are made of two cables. Only one model is built.

This test allows to check the traction-compression behaviour of the elements cable (possible pull, zero compression) and the elastic behaviour of the bars.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Square frame with two cabled bays on both diagonals.

![Figure](/figures/v6.02.105/1000166400002059000017AB4A6EB7C015B95C10.svg)

Cables are not connected in their environments.

### 1.2 Material properties

$E=2.1{10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$

$\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

| $\mathrm{DZ}=0.$ | nodes 1, 2, 3, 4 |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathrm{DY}=0.$ | nodes 1, 4 |
| $\mathrm{DX}=0.$ | nœud 1 |
| $\mathrm{FX}=1000.N$ | nœud 3 |

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The solution is obtained by the elementary static.

| In the $1-3$ cable | $\begin{array}{cc}N& =1000\sqrt{2}N\\ & \simeq 1414.2N\end{array}$ |
| --- | --- |
| In the $3-4$ bar | $\begin{array}{cc}N& =-1000N\end{array}$ |

$N=0$ anywhere else (and especially in cable 2-4).

### 2.2 Uncertainty about the solution

The reference solution is accurate.

### 2.3 Bibliographical references

Simple problem designed to contrast the behavior of two cables: one at traction, the other at compression.
