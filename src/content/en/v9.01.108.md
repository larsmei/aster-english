# v9.01.108 SZLZ108 - Damage by TAHERI(TAHERI_MANSON and TAHERI_MIXTE) methods

**Summary:**

There are two modellingss:

- Modelling A: This test is for the purpose of calculating damage from a purely uniaxial loading history in deformations by TAHERI_MANSON or TAHERI_MIXTE methods.
- Modelling B: This test is for the purpose of calculating damage from a constant loading history by the three methods of counting RAIN_FLOW, NATUREL and RCCM.

Taheri methods apply only to deformation loadings and do not allow the Manson-Coffin method to take into account the order of stress cycle occurrence.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Modelling A

The analysis is to determine the damage to a structure subjected to a deformation loading history. The Rainflow method is used to determine the number of elementary cycles and the half amplitude of each cycle.

For the loads considered, the Rainflow method determines 5 cycles of half amplitude:

$\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{1}}{2}=0.25$ , $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{2}}{2}=0.25$ , $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{3}}{2}=0.75$ , $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{4}}{2}=0.25$ et $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{5}}{2}=1.75$ .

Then the structural damage is calculated using the TAHERI_MANSON method and the TAHERI_MIXTE method.

As long as the deformation amplitude of the various cycles applied to the structure remains increasing $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{1}}{2}\le \frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{2}}{2}\le \mathrm{...}\le \frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{n}}{2}$ , the TAHERI_MANSON and TAHERI_MIXTE methods are identical to the MANSON_COFFIN method (calculation of the number of cycles at break, ${N}_{\mathrm{rupt}}$ , by interpolation on the Manson-Coffin curve and calculation of the damage by $1/{N}_{\mathrm{rupt}}$ ).

However, if a $i$ cycle has a half-amplitude $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{i}}{2}$ less than $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{i-1}}{2}$ , Taheri methods differ from the Manson-Coffin method.

- The TAHERI_MANSON method is to determine a stress amplitude $\frac{\Delta {\sigma}_{i}}{2}$ from $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{i}}{2}$ and ${\varepsilon}_{\max}$ (maximum value of the deformation half amplitude encountered before the $i$ cycle).

To do this, the user must provide an $\frac{\Delta \sigma }{2}(\frac{\Delta \varepsilon }{2,}{\varepsilon}_{\max})$ tablecloth under the TAHERI_NAPPE operand.

From $\frac{\Delta {\sigma}_{i}}{2}$, a half amplitude of $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{i}^{\text{*}}}{2}$ deformations is determined using a function introduced under the TAHERI_FONC operand. The value of the elemental damage of the $i$ cycle is determined by interpolation of $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}_{i}^{\text{*}}}{2}$ on the Manson_Coffin curve.

- For the 'TAHERI_MIXTE' method, the same procedure is followed for the determination of the $\frac{\Delta {\sigma}_{i}}{2}$ stress half amplitude , and the value of the $i$ cycle elemental damage is determined by interpolation of $\frac{\Delta {\sigma}_{i}}{2}$ on the Wöhler curve.

This method therefore requires the data of the curves of Wöhler and Manson_Coffin.

The total damage is determined by linear accumulation of the elementary damage.

#### 1.1.1 Material properties

For the calculation of the damage of TAHERI_MANSON, the Manson_Coffin curve is required, a table sheet for calculating $\frac{\Delta \sigma }{2}$ from ( $\frac{\Delta \varepsilon }{2}$ and ${\varepsilon}_{\max}$ ) and a function for calculating $\frac{\Delta {\varepsilon}^{\text{*}}}{2}$ from $\frac{\Delta \sigma }{2}$. The table sheet (cyclic nutring curve with pre-lunching) is introduced under the TAHERI_NAPPE operand and the function (cyclic nutting curve) under the TAHERI_FONC operand. The Manson_Coffin curve is introduced into DEFI_MATERIAU.

For the calculation of the damage of TAHERI_MIXTE, the Manson_Coffin curve, the Wöhler curve and a sheet (cyclic nutring curve with pre-nutting) are required to calculate $\frac{\Delta \sigma }{2}$ from ($\frac{\Delta \varepsilon }{2}$ and ${\varepsilon}_{\max}$). The sheet is introduced under the TAHERI_NAPPE operand. The Manson_Coffin curve and the Wöhler curve are introduced into DEFI_MATERIAU.
