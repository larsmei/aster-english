# v3.01.107 SSLL107 - Validation of MACR_CARA_POUTRE

**Summary:**

All modellings of this test validates the macro_command for calculating beam section characteristics, MACR_CARA_POUTRE [U4.42.02](doc:u4.42.02), for all the options proposed. The sections studied are different according to the modellings.

The different modellings:

- A: calculation of the characteristics of a corneal section.
- B: calculation of the characteristics of a circular section.
- C: calculation of the characteristics of a rectangular section.
- D: calculation of the characteristics of a rectangular section of the cell.
- E: calculation of the characteristics of an octagonal section.
- F: calculation of the characteristics of a circular section with a chain on a beam calculation.
- G: calculation of a network of 2 rectangular cross-section beams.
- H: calculation of the characteristics of a thin U-section.
- I: calculation of the torsion constant for a holed section.
- J: calculation of shear coefficients for a circular cross-section.
- K: calculation of shear coefficients for a rectangular hole cross section.
- L: change of quadrant characteristics, based on model A.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry of the different sections is provided via a mesh plane. They are different for each model, and will therefore be described in the corresponding paragraphs.

Modelling F also implements chained calculation of the characteristics of a circular cross-section, and the use of these characteristics in a calculation of straight beam, $L=1m$ length in pure traction.

### 1.2 Material properties

Not applicable, except for Modelling F, where the treated beam has an Young's modulus of $2.E11 \ Pa$ and an Poisson's ratio of $0.3$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Not applicable, except for Modelling F, the straight beam is clamped at one end and subjected to $F = 1000 \ N$ traction at the other end.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Since the solutions are specific to each model, they are described in the corresponding paragraphs, and are mainly derived from bib1 and bib2.

### 2.2 Baseline

The calculations by MACR_CARA_POUTRE [U4.42.02](doc:u4.42.02) are described here.

- Geometrical characteristics:

  - In the OYZ description marker of mesh $2D$ for the mesh supplied by the user
*   Area: A_M
*   centre of gravity position: CDG_Y_M,CDG_Z_M
*   moment and area inertia product, at the centre of gravity G in the GYZ marker: IY_G_M, IZ_G_M, IYZ_G_M
  - In the same global benchmark, for mesh obtained by symetricization if SYME_Y or SYME_Z:
*   Area: A
*   centre of gravity position: CDG_Y,CDG_Z
*   moment and area inertia product, at the centre of gravity G in the GYZ: IY_G, IZ_G, IYZ_G
  - In the Gyz. Main Inertiality Marker of the right section, the name of which corresponds to that used in the description of the elements neutral fibre beam GX [U4.24.01](doc:u4.24.01).
*   Main inertia moments of the Gyz reference area, usable for calculating the bending stiffness of the beam: IY and IZ
*   GYZ reference angle at the Gyz main inertia marker: ALPHA
*   characteristic distances, relative to the centre of gravity G of the section for maximum stress calculations: Y_MAX, Y_MIN, Z_MAX, Z_MIN and R_MAX.
  - In the global benchmark, in a point $P$ supplied by the user:
*   Y_P, Z_P: point for calculating inertia moments
*   IY_P, IZ_P, IYZ_P: moments of inertia in the PYZ marker
*   IY_P, IZ_P: moments of inertia in the Pyz marker.
- Mechanical characteristics:

  - $JX$ : Torsion constant
  - $EY$ : Position of the center of torsion / shear
  - $EZ$ : Position of the center of torsion / shear
  - $PCTY$: Torsion center is removed from $GYZ.$ on $Y$ axis
  - $PCTZ$: Torsion center is removed from $GYZ$ on $Z$ axis
  - $AY$ : shear coefficient
  - $AZ$ : shear coefficient
  - $JG$ : Gauching constant
