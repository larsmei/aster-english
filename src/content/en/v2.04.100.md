## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is obtained using elements finite SAMCEF calculation software for identical modellings but with consistent mass elemental matrices.

The analytical solution given in the SDLL09/89 sheet of the VPCS guide is recalled. The differential equation in bending of the beam considered, in Euler-Bernoulli theory is written (Euler-Bernoulli Theory):

$\frac{{\partial}^{2}(E{I}_{z}\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {x}^{2}})}{\partial {x}^{2}}=-\rho A\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {t}^{2}}$

where ${I}_{z}$ and $A$ vary with the apse.

The eigenfrequencies are then of the form:

${f}_{i}=\frac{1}{2\pi }{\lambda}_{i}(\alpha ,\beta )\frac{{h}_{1}}{{L}^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{E}{12\rho }}$

with $\alpha =\frac{{h}_{0}}{{h}_{1}}=4$ and $\beta =\frac{{b}_{0}}{{b}_{1}}=4$ .

For this value of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ , the first values of the $({\lambda}_{i})$ suite are:

|  | ${\lambda}_{1}$ | ${\lambda}_{2}$ | ${\lambda}_{3}$ | ${\lambda}_{4}$ | ${\lambda}_{5}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\beta =4$ | 23.289 | 73.9 | 165.23 | 299.7 | 478.1 |

## 5 Summary of the results

The differences between the results of the calculations *Code_Aster* and SAMCEF with consistent masses are less than 0.2%.

Differences between the results of *Code_Aster* calculations with diagonal masses and SAMCEF with consistent masses remain below 1%.

These results are consistent with what could be expected, and reliably validate the eigenfrequency calculations in *Code_Aster* by CALC_MODES and the CALC_MATR_ELEM operator in coherent masses as well as diagonal masses.
