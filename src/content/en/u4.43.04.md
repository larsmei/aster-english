# u4.43.04 Operator DEFI_TRC

## 1 Purpose

Define a reference TRC diagram (continuous cooling transformations) for metallurgical analyses.

The TRC diagram thus defined is necessary for the characterisation of a metallurgical constitutive law on cooling in the operator DEFI_MATERIAU (factor keyword META_ACIER).

For the definition of the metallurgical data and the modelling which is made of them, one will refer to the document [R4.04.01](doc:r4.04.01).

Produces a data structure of type table_TRC.

## 2 Syntax

```text
table = DEFI_TRC(
    ◆ HIST_EXP = _F(
         ◆ VALE = float,
      ),
    ◆ TEMP_MS = _F(
         ◆ SEUIL = float,
         ◆ AKM = float,
         ◆ BKM = float,
         ◆ TPLM = float,
      ),
    ◇ GRAIN_AUST = _F(
         ◇ DREF = float,
         ◇ A = float,
      ),
)

◆ : obligatoire
◇ : optionnel
⟐ : présent par défaut
& : ensemble
/ : un seul parmi
| : plusieurs choix possibles
```

## 3 Operands

### 3.1 Keyword HIST_EXP

```text
♦    HIST_EXP =
```

One occurrence of the factor keyword HIST_EXP makes it possible to define the evolutions of ferrite, pearlite and bainite associated with a thermal history on cooling and with given austenitisation conditions.

#### 3.1.1 Operand VALE

```text
♦    VALE = lval
```

List of values defining the austenitisation, the thermal history $T(t)$ and the evolutions of ferrite, pearlite and bainite.

The first value is the value of the derivative of the function $T(t)$ (that is to say the cooling rate) when $T$ is equal to $700°C$ .

The second value is the grain size (that is to say their diameter) resulting from the austenitisation conditions associated with the TRC.

The following 6 values define the thermal history between ${\mathrm{AR}}_{3}$ and $\mathrm{TMF}$ (temperature of the start of the decomposition of the austenite on "quasi-static" cooling and temperature of the end of the martensitic transformation, respectively). These values are the respective coefficients of the monomials of degree 0 to 5 such that the polynomial of order 5 thus constructed is the interpolation, between ${\mathrm{AR}}_{3}$ and $\mathrm{TMF}$, in the least-squares sense, of the function $F(T)$ deduced from the thermal history and such that:

> $F(T)=\ln(t(T))$

In the case where the experimental thermal history to be defined is an affine function of time (that is to say where the cooling rate is constant) one will fill in these six values as all equal to zero.

The following values (necessarily in groups of 4) define the respective proportions of ferrite, pearlite and bainite present at a given temperature for the experimental thermal history defined by the first 8 values.

The ferritic, pearlitic and bainitic transformations associated with a thermal history are defined by the set of the final proportions of each of the phases (${Z}_{1}$  final, ${Z}_{2}$ final, ${Z}_{3}$ final) and the corresponding temperatures, for each of the transformations, at:

- the temperature at which the transformation starts,
- the temperature at which 1% of new phase is formed,
- the temperature at which ${Z}_{\mathrm{final}}$ –1% of new phase is formed,
- the temperature at which the transformation is finished (with ${Z}_{\mathrm{final}}$ of phase formed).

The set of the "points" $({Z}_{1,}{Z}_{2,}{Z}_{3,}T)$ defining an experimental history is therefore presented as follows:

### 3.2 Keyword TEMP_MS

```text
♦    TEMP_MS
```

This factor keyword makes it possible to define the law of evolution of the temperature $\mathrm{Ms}$ as a function of the quantities of ferrite, pearlite and bainite already formed, according to the law:

$\begin{array}{ccc}\mathrm{Ms}=\mathrm{Ms0}& \text{si}& {Z}_{1}+{Z}_{2}+{Z}_{3}\le \text{SEUIL}\\ \mathrm{Ms}=\mathrm{Ms0}+\mathrm{AKM}({Z}_{1}+{Z}_{2}+{Z}_{3})+\mathrm{BKM}& \text{si}& {Z}_{1}+{Z}_{2}+{Z}_{3}>\text{SEUIL}\end{array}$

where $\mathrm{Ms0}$ is the "classical" temperature of the start of the martensitic transformation when the latter is total (it is defined under the factor keyword META_ACIER of DEFI_MATERIAU.

#### 3.2.1 Operand SEUIL

```text
♦    SEUIL =    zs,
```

${z}_{s}$ is the quantity of austenite transformed below which $\mathrm{Ms}$ is invariant.

#### 3.2.2 Operand AKM

```text
♦    AKM =        akm,
```

$\mathrm{akm}$ is the factor of proportionality between the decrease of the temperature $\mathrm{Ms}$ and the quantity of transformed austenite $({Z}_{1}+{Z}_{2}+{Z}_{3})$ .

#### 3.2.3 Operand BKM

```text
♦    BKM =        bkm,
```

$\mathrm{bkm}$ is the intercept of the affine equation relating the decrease of $\mathrm{Ms}$ to the quantity of transformed austenite.

#### 3.2.4 Operand TPLM

```text
♦    TPLM =        Vc,
```

$\mathrm{Vc}$ is the cooling rate at $700°C$ of the slowest experimental history which makes it possible to form a little martensite.

These four keywords define the values of the quantities $\mathrm{SEUIL},\mathrm{AKM},\mathrm{BKM}$ appearing in the law of evolution of $\mathrm{Ms}$, which one assumes to be independent of the grain size.

### 3.3 Keyword GRAIN_AUST

Makes it possible to define the influence of the grain size on the metallurgical transformations on cooling defined by the TRC diagram.

#### 3.3.1 Operand DREF

```text
◊    DREF    = do,
```

$\mathrm{do}$ is the grain size (that is to say its diameter) associated with the diagram defined under the factor keyword HIST_EXP.

#### 3.3.2 Operand A

```text
◊    A        = a    ,
```

$a$ is a material parameter which makes it possible to characterise the effect of the grain size on the TRC diagram of a steel (cf. [R4.04.01](doc:r4.04.01)).
