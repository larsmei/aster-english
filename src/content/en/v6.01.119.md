# v6.01.119 SSNA119 – Damage to AXIS-cut test piece

**Summary:**

This test represents a calculation of damage to a cut-out test piece. It allows the validation of GRAD_VARI Modelling to axisymmetric, for AXIS_GRAD_VARI modelling, allowing for damage calculations to be performed regularized by the damage gradient, taking into account degrees of freedom displacement and damage to nodes.

GRAD_VARI Modelling is based on a local resolution, with each Gauss point. The test validates two constitutive laws: ENDO_SCALAIRE and ENDO_FISS_EXP.

The different modellings and constitutive laws damaging are tested:

- **Model B**: GRAD_VARI Modelling with constitutive law ENDO_SCALAIRE
- **Model C**: GRAD_VARI Modelling with constitutive law ENDO_FISS_EXP

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A $10\mathit{dm}$ high-cut plate is considered. $3\mathit{dm}$ wide and $1\mathit{dm}$ nick radius.

![Figure](/figures/v6.01.119/10000000000001B00000027DE55D1F6471B68862.png)

**Figure 1:** **Promotion of the problem**

### 1.2 Common properties of the material

The material is assimilated to concrete (decipemetric scale is used):

Elastic characteristics:

> $E=3.E10\text{Pa}=3.E8{\text{N/dm}}^{2}$
> $\nu =0.2$

Tensile strength of the damage law:

> $\mathit{SY}=3.E4{\text{N/dm}}^{2}=3.E6\text{Pa}$

Energy restitution rate per crack surface (Griffith constant):

> ${G}_{f}=10\text{N/dm}=100{\text{J/m}}^{2}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

**Block**: $\mathrm{DX}=0$ on an node group located in the middle of the axis ($x=0.$ ), to ensure the maintenance of the structure.

**Charge**: Forced or controlled displacement (variety depending on model) in the traction direction.

## 2 reference solution

This test case is a case of non-regression. This is a particularly unstable study, which quickly changes from a weakly damaged state to a state of rupture (damage equal to 1).

A complete snap-back calculation is made with the elastic prediction steering technique for GRAD_VARI Modelling.

## 3 Modelling B

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

A model of damage GRAD_VARI, which is a mixed Lagrangian formulation of damage regulated by the gradient of damage, is considered. It takes into account in addition the degrees of freedom of displacement and damage to the nodes, the coeficients of Lagrange. This model accepts loading control by elastic prediction.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 1034 elements TRIA6 and 462 elements QUAD8. The mesh at the centre of the test piece is oriented out of symmetry.

### 3.3 Damage law: material ENDO_SCALAIRE

Standard concrete characteristics are defined above.

Characteristics related to the non-local damage law:

$c=1.875\text{N};p=1.5;m=10$ which corresponds to the damage area $\mathrm{1D}$ equal to $D=0.5\text{dm}$

The correspondence with physical parameters is as follows:

$c=3/8D{G}_{f};m=\frac{3E{G}_{f}}{2D\cdot {\mathit{SY}}^{2}};p=m/4-1;$

### 3.4 Boundary conditions and loads

** Charge**: The calculation is done by elastically predicting control until the test piece is completely broken, thanks to the projection on the predefined terminals.

### 3.5 Tested quantities and results

Non-regression test on point $\text{P\_HAUT}$ displacement, point $\text{P\_HAUT}$ nodal force and point Gauss 1 mesh cell $\mathit{M160}$ stress field.

## 4 Modelling C

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

A model of damage GRAD_VARI, which is a mixed Lagrangian formulation of damage regulated by the gradient of damage, is considered. It takes into account in addition the degrees of freedom of displacement and damage to the nodes, the coeficients of Lagrange. This model accepts loading control by elastic prediction.

### 4.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 1034 elements TRIA6 and 462 elements QUAD8. The mesh at the centre of the test piece is oriented out of symmetry.

### 4.3 Damage law: material ENDO_FISS_EXP

In addition to the concrete characteristics defined in §1.2, a compression limit of fc=30.E6 Pa, a shape parameter of the cohesive model p=1.5 and a half width of D=0.5 dm is introduced.

### 4.4 Boundary conditions and loads

** Charge**: The calculation is done by elastically predicting control until the test piece is completely broken, thanks to the projection on the predefined terminals.

### 4.5 Tested quantities and results

Non-regression test on point $\text{P\_HAUT}$ displacement, point $\text{P\_HAUT}$ nodal force and the overall stress field (absolute sum).

## 5 Summary of the results

This test allows you to check in non-regression:

- GRAD_VARI Modelling with constitutive law ENDO_SCALAIRE
- GRAD_VARI Modelling with constitutive law ENDO_FISS_EXP
