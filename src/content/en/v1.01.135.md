# v1.01.135 ZZZZ135 - Computer validation of SRM methods in CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the computer features of the CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command.

In this test case the stability of a dam in embankment with water infiltration is studied.
This test is a non-regression test to validate the use of the macro-command,
and therefore has no physical meaning or scientific reference solution.

There are 2 modellings available for this test case:

- **Model A**: Calculation of the upstream slope safety factor with Mohr-Coulomb law.
This Modelling validates the following features:

  - Using command variables in the input cam_mater.
  - Checking the constitutive law in the SRM area.
  - The use of Mohr-Coulomb law in SRM algorithm.
  - The GROUP_MA keyword for defining the SRM area by independent mesh cells groups.
  - The CHAM_DEFO key word for the glide surface.
  - The keyword METHODE = 'EXPONENTIELLE'
- **Model B**: Calculation of the safety factor of the entire model with Drucker-Prager law.
This Modelling validates the following features:

  - The use of Drucker-Prager law in SRM algorithm.
  - Le keyword METHODE = 'LINEAIRE'
  - Le keyword TOUT = 'OUI'

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The boundary conditions applied to the model are as follows:

- Bottom of the clamped foundation, and the vertical sides normally blocked.
- Hydraulic pressure from the upstream side of 77.19m (peak elevation = 94.2m).
- Hydraulic pressure defined from the level of the downstream table, which is 43m.

The following mechanical loads are applied to the model:

- The gravity with $g=9,81m/{s}^{2}$
- Hydrostatic pressure on the upstream surface of the dam.

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics

The safety factor for the upstream slope of the dam shall be calculated taking into account
the presence of the pore pressure field due to water infiltration into the dam.
The mechanical effect on the hydraulic field is neglected.
The hydromechanical problem is solved by chaining, and
introduced the pore pressure field in the mechanical calculation by the allocation of the
PTOT command variable in the cam_mater that entered into the CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command.

The dam is modeled in D_PLAN_HS for hydraulic calculation and D_PLAN for stability analysis.

## 3 Modelling B

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Model B re-uses the pore pressure field of model A,
but applies SRM algorithm to the entire model with Drucker-Prager law.
In addition, the law of linear variation of the increment of the safety factor is used,
with `INCR_INIT = 0,05`, `RESI_MAXI = 0,01`, and `ITER_RAFF_LINE= 1`.

The difference between initial increment and maximum residue being large, this configuration
The convergence parameters require that the algorithm converge after refinement of the increment.
