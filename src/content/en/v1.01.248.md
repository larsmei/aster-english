# v1.01.248 PLEXU02 – Multi-domain CALC_EUROPLEXUS command verification

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check the CALC_EUROPLEXUS command when it starts a multi-domain EUROPLEXUS calculation from a data set in Code_Aster and when the materials use the GLRC_DAMAGE law. It is verified that the results recovered by Code_Aster following the EUROPLEXUS calculation are those calculated by EUROPLEXUS alone.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A model structure is considered as a horizontal square slab with $1.3m$ thickness and $10m$ side, supported in four corners by springs. The mesh of the slab contains three subdomains gridded in triangles and quadrangles with three interfaces: a compliant interface (nodes coincides) between the subdomains $\mathit{DALQ1}$ and $\mathit{DALQ2}$ and two interfaces that do not conform between the subdomains $\mathit{DALT3}$ and $\mathit{DALQ1}$ and $\mathit{DALQ2}$.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.248/10000000000002D4000001AC7589B147924EE1FD.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The slab is made of reinforced concrete modelled by the GLRC_DAMAGE global law.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The slab is based on four springs with the stiffness specified. The slab is loaded by a uniform over-facial pressure whose time-likeness is given by a function.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution comes from the EUROPLEXUS calculation launched outside of Code_Aster.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The values of vertical displacement at node and of stress and moment at Gauss points, reread by Code_Aster, are tested at the final moment. They are compared with the values obtained from a single EUROPLEXUS calculation.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Q4GG Modelling is used for the slab.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh of the slab contains 64 elements of type QUAD4 and 64 elements of type TRIA3.

### 3.3 Material precision

The materials used in this modelling do not take nonlinear shear into account.
