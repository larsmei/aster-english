# v2.02.140 SDLL140 – Calculation of the proper modes of a beam with 3 discs, subject to gyroscopic effect.

**Summary:**

This problem is to validate the effect of the gyroscopic matrix on a beam supported at each end, on linear supports. The beam is full, circular cross section and has three discs.

Two calculations are made:

- Calculation A: zero rotation speed, no gyroscopic effect, calculation of eigenfrequencies,
- Calculation B: non-zero rotation speed, presence of gyroscopic effect, calculation of eigenfrequencies,

This problem allows us to test the effect of the gyroscopic matrix that was developed for an straight beam. The gyroscopic effect leads to the duplication of modes. The evolution of the frequencies of the own according to the speed of rotation allows to build the Campbell diagram.

The results obtained are in good agreement with those given in reference. The references are based on Timoshenko's beam theory.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one presented in Michel LALANNE and Guy FERRARIS's book.

The numerical results were obtained by a finished elements code, with elements beam type Timoshenko. The Modelling is done with 14 nodes (13 elements beams).

### 2.2 Baseline

- Top 10 frequencies specific to the shutdown ($\Omega =0$ ).
- Top 10 frequencies specific to $\Omega =25000\mathrm{tr}/\min$ rotation speed.
- With a slush type load, values of 7 maximas d'amplitude for $A$, $C$ and $E$ points, for a rotation speed ranging from $0$ to $30000\mathrm{tr}/\min$.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Less than 1%.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

Michel LALANNE and Guy FERRARIS, Rotordynamics, Prediction in Engineering, JOHN WILEY AND SONS (1990).
