## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Introduction

The test case study examines the hydraulic behaviour of a saturated porous medium consisting of a single fluid: water in its liquid phase. The constitutive law of the associated fluid is of the LIQU_SATU type. The Modelling is hydromechanical (HM). The fluid is incompressible and the medium is infinitely rigid. This is therefore a stationary problem with an analytical solution. The objective of this test is to validate the correct consideration of a hydraulic flow boundary condition in the case of an axisymmetric model.

### 1.2 Geometry

We represent a $L=\mathrm{1m}$ length bar. Its width l does not intervene in the analytical solution because the problem is purely 1D. We take here $l=0,2m$. The point N1 corresponds to the point coordinate $(0,0).$

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is one-dimensional because it depends only on vertical coordination (loading 1D). The water saturated system is simply solving the problem of mass conservation:

> $\frac{\partial (\phi {\rho}_{l})}{\partial t}-div({K}_{int}\frac{{\rho}_{l}}{{\mu}_{l}}\nabla {P}_{l})=q$

where q is the flow imposed on the edge.

- Liquid is incompressible: ${\rho}_{l}=c$
- The matrix is incompressible (infinite rigidity): the porosity remains constant $\phi =\mathit{cst}$ .

In this case, a stationary flow is obtained which is summed up by the writing of the flow:

$-div({K}_{int}\frac{{\rho}_{l}}{{\mu}_{l}}\nabla {P}_{l})=q$

If ${P}_{L}$ is noted the pressure in $Y=L$ , then after discretization:

> ${K}_{int}\frac{{\rho}_{l}}{{\mu}_{l}}\frac{{P}_{L}-{P}_{0}}{L}=q$

This gives with the data previously provided: ${P}_{L}=1.1{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$
