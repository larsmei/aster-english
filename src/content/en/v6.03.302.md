# v6.03.302 SSNP302 - element thermally charged - Appearance of parasitic stresses

**Summary:**

This quasi-static linear 2D mechanical test consists of charging a element plate in thermal mode at degree 1, applying a temperature field that varies linearly on the element and attaching a side of the element.

This element being of degree 1, the total mechanical deformation will be constant in the element. The thermal field imposing linear deformation in the element will require a dilation coefficient and a thermal gradient large enough to make the total mechanical deformation sensitive to the imposed thermal field.

The plate is modelled by a element plane (MECPQU4).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

analytical solution.

### 2.2 Baseline

Mechanical deformation is:

$\begin{array}{ccc}{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{mec}}& =& \varepsilon -{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{th}}\\ & =& \varepsilon -\alpha T\end{array}$

With a element at the degree one and a $2\times 2$ schema d'integration we will have:

$\begin{array}{ccc}{\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{mec}}& =& \frac{{u}_{\mathrm{xB}}-{u}_{\mathrm{xA}}}{a}-\alpha \left[\frac{1+\xi }{2}{T}_{\max}\right]\\ & =& \frac{{\sigma}_{d}}{E}+\frac{1}{2}\alpha {T}_{\max}-\alpha \left[\frac{1+\xi }{2}{T}_{\max}\right]\end{array}$

The constraint in the test will be:

$\sigma =E{\varepsilon}^{\mathit{mec}}$ with ${\varepsilon}^{\mathit{mec}}={10}^{-3}-\alpha \left[\frac{1+\xi }{2}{T}_{max}\right]$

### 2.3 Note

The thermal component of the stress dependent on the intrinsic coordinate, the solution is to consider an average temperature per element.
