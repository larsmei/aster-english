# v5.05.102 SDNX102 - Verification of Miss3D-code_aster Chaining for a rigid foundation beam

**Summary:**

This non-regression test implements the different options of Miss3D-code_aster chaining to deal with the linear problem of a beam placed on a rigid foundation with consideration of a soil-structure interaction (ISS) in dynamic analysis. L的ISS is then modelled through the frequency coupling from the CALC_MISS macro-command. Different modellings are presented:

- In **modelization A** CALC_MISS is used to use Miss3D and estimate the dynamic rigidity matrix of the interface
- In **modelization B** frequent coupling is tested via a single-support load using EXCIT_GENE option CALC_MISS
- In the **modelization C** frequency coupling is tested via a single-support load using EXCIT_MONO option CALC_MISS
- In **modelization D** frequency coupling is tested by loading equivalent seismic forces using CALC_MISS EXCIT_FORC option

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry taken into account is that of a beam with a concentrated head mass placed on a square rigid foundation on the $H=1m$ side. The characteristics of the beam are as follows:

- One length $L=12m$
- A square section on the $B=0.4m$ side

### 1.2 Material properties

This paragraph presents the material characteristics of the soil and structure.

#### 1.2.1 Soil properties

A homogeneous soil is considered with the following characteristics:

- Un Young's modulus de $E=200MPa$
- Un Poisson's ratio de $\nu=0.33$
- A density density of $\rho=1610kg/m^3$

#### 1.2.2 The properties of the beam

A beam with the following characteristics shall be considered:

- Un Young's modulus de $E=30GPa$
- Un Poisson's ratio de $\nu=0.2$
- A concentrated mass in the head of $m=2000kg$

The beam is modelled as a discrete element by providing the following stiffnesses:

- Horizontal Rigidity $K_h=12EI/L^3$
- Vertical Rigidity $K_h=EA/L$

where $I=B^4/12$ is the inertia of the beam and $A=B^2$ is the surface of the section.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and dynamic loadings

The foundation is placed on a macro-element of soil defined using CALC_MISS through the material properties of the soil. The imposed load is a Ricker wavelet with the following shape:

$$
a(t)=(1-2\pi f(t-t_0)^2)e^{-(\pi f(t-t_0))^2}
$$

where $t_0=1.24s$ is the time of phase of the wavelet and $f=2Hz$ is the main frequency.
