# v6.04.272 SSNV272 - Tensile test - homogeneous and confined compression, without viscosity

**Summary:**

This test modeled a homogeneous, confined tensile test followed by confined compression. No viscosity is activated. One first model (A) is in 3D, another (B) is in flat deformations. The test validates the ENDO_LOCA_TC law described in [R7.01.47](doc:r7.01.47) or in .

Validation is analytical.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a material point, processed by SIMU_POINT_MAT in 3D or D_PLAN depending on the Modelling.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material obeys the constitutive law elastic ENDO_LOCA_TC quasi-fragile. The macroscopic data correspond to:

It is a stress in a material point (SIMU_POINT_MAT). All deformation components are imposed; they are all zero except EPXX. ## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Input data and material parameters

| Name | Symbol | Value | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Poisson's ratio | $\nu$ | 0.2 |  |
| Tensile nutting coefficient | p | 1.5 | -- |
| Compression resistance | $f_{c}$ | 40 | MPa |
| Inter-crack distance | $L_{F}$ | 200 | mm |

Internal parameter values are calculated using the fib Model Code (fib-2010) using v6.04.272-tab-data-input. The equations are extracted from [R7.01.47](doc:r7.01.47).

$$
E = 21500*\left(\frac{f_{c}}{10}\right)^\left(1/3\right) \quad;\quad \nu = 0.2 \quad;\quad G_{F}=73*f_{c}^{0.18} \quad;\quad f_{t}=0.3*\left(f_{c}-8\right)^\left(2/3\right)
$$

These equations allow the Young, $f_{t}$ and $G_{F}$ module values to be retrieved. The $\sigma^{0}$ value is set to $f_c/3$ (see v6.04.272-tab-para).

DEFI_MATER_GC evaluates the last internal parameter of the law from the previous values, namely the standard energy consumed $\bar{\omega}$ defined by:

$$
\bar{\omega}=\frac{2E_{C}\,G_{F}}{L_{F}\,f_{t}^2}
$$

With the Young's modulus in contained traction defined by:

$$
E_{c}=\lambda+2\mu=\frac{\left(1-\nu\right)\,E}{\left(1-2\nu\right)\left(1+\nu\right)}
$$

### 2.2 Calculation method used for reference solution

In confined traction, the SIXX constraint follows the following relationship:

$$
\sigma_{t}=A\left(a\right)\,E_{c}\,\varepsilon_{t}
$$

with $A\left(a\right)$ the tensile stiffness defined by:

$$
A\left(a\right)=\frac{1-a}{e\left(a\right)} \quad;\quad e\left(a\right) = 1-a+\frac{\bar{\omega}}{2}\left[m_{0}a+\left(D_{1}-m_{0}\right)a^{r}\right]
$$

$m_{0}$, $D_{1}$ and r are expressed only with the ENDO_LOCA_TC law p parameter:

$$
m_{0}=\frac{3\pi}{2}\left(p+2\right)^{-3/2}, D_{1}=\frac{3\pi}{4}\sqrt{1+p}\quad;\quad r= \frac{2\left(D_{1}-1\right)-m_{0}}{2-m_{0}}
$$

These equations allow the deformation to be calculated at the tensile limit:

$$
\varepsilon_{e}=\frac{f_{t}}{E_{c}}
$$

Then the expected deformation for damage is 0.1:

$$
\varepsilon_{t}=\frac{\sigma_{t}}{A\left(a\right)\,E_{c}}
$$

knowing that the actual constraint is also defined by:

$$
\sigma_{t}=\left(1-a\right)\,f_{t}
$$

In the compression phase, damage has not changed. A ratio 15 is chosen between the final $\varepsilon_{c}$ compression deformation and the final $\varepsilon_{t}$ traction deformation. After an elastic discharge of stiffness $E_c\, A(a)$, the deformation becomes negative. The stress and damage then write:

$$
\sigma=C\left(c\right)\,E_c\,\varepsilon
$$

$$
c = max \left( 1 \, , \, - \, \frac{E_c \, \varepsilon}{\sigma^0} \right)
$$

where $C\left(c\right)$ is the standard residual compression stiffness with compression damage. It is written:

$$
C\left(c\right)=\frac{1}{c}\left[1+\left(\frac{f_{c}}{\sigma^{0}}-1\right)\tanh\left(\frac{c-1}{\frac{f_{c}}{\sigma^{0}}-1}\right)\right] \quad ;\quad f_{c}>\sigma^{0}
$$

These equations allow the evaluation of the stresses, deformations and internal variables of the model.

### 2.3 Post-processed and tested data

For the post-processing in traction, the strain energy corresponding to the traction elastic stresses called ENERTRAC follows the following equation:

$$
ENERTRAC = 1/2*E_{c}*\varepsilon_{t}^{2}
$$

HISTTRAC corresponds to a and ENDOTRAC corresponds to $1-A\left(a\right)$. For compression, ENDOCOMP refers to $1-C\left(c\right)$ and the energies are:

$$
ENERTRAC = 1/2*E_{c}*max\left(\varepsilon_{c},0\right)^{2}
$$

$$
ENERCOMP=1/2*E_{c}*min\left(\varepsilon_{c},0\right)^{2}
$$
