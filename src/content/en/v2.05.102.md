# v2.05.102 SDLX102 – Structure - ground interaction - parametric structure between two level buildings

**Summary:**

This test contributes to the validation of the Aster‐MISS3D chaining by the frequent coupling method. It represents a typical case of ground coupling between two buildings, i.e. a case of structural-soil-structure interaction. Two twin buildings each represented by a model-brochette 1D model, each positioned on a stratified floor and connected by an overfacial foundation of the same surface, are tested. A harmonic force, of constant module for each frequency between $0,1$ and $20\mathit{Hz}$, is imposed on the top of one of the buildings. To speed up the test, we will calculate only two frequencies: 7.3Hz and 10.4Hz.

The module of the direct response at the top of the excited building is tested, as well as the module of the coupled response at the top of the adjacent building for different modules of the intermediate foundation of restraint compared to the values obtained with the extreme conditions of restraint between the two buildings: completely decoupled or completely rigid foundations. It is shown that by dividing or multiplying by 1000 the constituent module of the structures to assign to the intermediate foundation of restraint with a soft foundation hypothesis, the two boundary cases of restraint between the two buildings are obtained with only the hypothesis of rigid foundation.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The Aster‐MISS3D software uses the frequent coupling method to account for the soil-structure interaction.
This method, based on dynamic substructure, consists of dividing the study field into three sub-domains:

- soil,
- the foundation,
- the building.

The problem treated exploits data from NUPEC experiments.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and mechanical loadings

Each of the 2 restraints between a 1D model and its foundation is achieved by a solid restraint condition between the
foundation and the node common to the building model. This node is blocked and a solid body movement is imposed on the building model.
Remove. To calculate the forced modes associated with the foundation of restraint, also block the degrees of freedom
translation of the foundation of restraint.

The top of the building model is excite in horizontal directions $X$ and $Y$ with a load
$F={F}_{o}\sin\omega t$ harmonic with ${F}_{o}$ force module with $10\mathit{kN}$
a pulse that varies from $0$ to $20\mathit{Hz}$ by step of $0,1\mathit{Hz}$. This returns in
*code_aster* to impose a load of type FORCE_NODALE and to transmit in IMPR_MISS_3D the assembled vector
corresponding with a multi-plicator function in frequency.

The excitation is applied as indicated on the v2.05.102-models-maillages-surfacial-foundations.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The characteristics used and the mesh are those derived from the v2.05.102-problem-reference data. To speed up the test, only two frequencies are to be calculated: 7.3Hz and 10.4Hz. The seismic impedances and forces calculated on the aster5 reference server are used, so these data are provided at the test input and the call to CALC_MISS used to calculate them is therefore commented.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh supplied to *Aster* contains mesh cells type SEG2 to model the building structure with elements beam and mesh cells types QUAD4, TRIA3 to model the foundation with elements DKT. It is important to have oriented the elements foundation surface with normal fit back into the ground. mesh cells side walls representing the sink are generated by the same vertical generator as MISS3D requires.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The tested values are the $\mu m$ module of the maximum direct response to the top of the excited building (point $\mathit{A1}$) and the $\mu m$ module of the maximum response coupled to the top of the neighbouring building (point $\mathit{B1}$).

## 4 Summary of the results

The module of the direct response at the top of the excited building and the module of the coupled response at the top of the adjacent building for various modules of the intermediate foundation of restraint are tested. They are compared with the values obtained with the extreme conditions of restraint between the two buildings: foundations completely decoupled or completely rigid. On the figures below, we show that by dividing or multiplying by 1000 the constituent module of the structures to assign to the intermediate foundation of restraint with the hypothesis of flexible foundation, we obtain the two boundary cases of restraint between the two buildings (the hypothesis of rigid foundation).
