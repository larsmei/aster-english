# v1.01.289 ZZZZ289 - Validation of the calculations of the elementary matrices of incompressible elements

**Summary:**

This test is used to validate the calculation of MASS_MECA and RIGI_MECA options of the following incompressible elements modellings: INCO_UPG and INCO_UP. Six modellings are used:

1. elements 3D: 3D_INCO_UPG and 3D_INCO_UP for a linear and quadratic mesh in small deformations,
2. elements D_PLAN: D_PLAN_INCO_UPG and D_PLAN_INCO_UP for a linear and quadratic mesh in small deformations,
3. elements AXIS: AXIS_INCO_UPG and AXIS_INCO_UP for a linear and quadratic mesh in small deformations,
4. elements 3D: 3D_INCO_UPG and 3D_INCO_UP in large deformations,
5. elements D_PLAN: D_PLAN_INCO_UPG and D_PLAN_INCO_UP, in large deformations
6. elements AXIS: AXIS_INCO_UPG and AXIS_INCO_UP in large deformations.

## 1 Test principle

CALC_MATR_ELEM operator is used to evaluate the mass and stiffness matrices of the different incompressible elements.

## 2 reference solution

For the verification of the mass matrix calculation, option MASS_MECA, the S_R property of the CALC_MATR_ELEM-calculated matrices is compared with that of the standard elements (3D, D_PLAN and AXIS) and is therefore of the AUTRE_ASTER type.

For stiffness matrices, the S_R property of the matrices calculated with the RIGI_MECA option is always used, except that in this case the solution is non-regression type.
