# v7.20.102 HSNA102 - Validation of drying laws on a concrete cylindrical test piece

**Summary:**

This test case is designed to validate the calculation of concrete drying, developed in the SECH_NON_LINE operator. Here we test the various available diffusion laws, namely SECH_GRANGER, SECH_MENSI, SECH_BAZANT, SECH_NAPPE and SECH_RFT. However, the possible dependence on the temperature of the models is not tested.

This is a test case axisymmetric where the water concentration is applied directly to the outer wall. The results are compared with a numerical resolution of the equations using Scilab except for SECH_RFT whose reference solution is given by a calculation with SECH_NAPPE to which an equivalent python formula is provided.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A cylindrical test piece of diameter $160\mathit{mm}$ is modelled.

![Figure](/figures/v7.20.102/1000224200003A8E000018DFB0C2A2A264AB8EC7.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

Each model allows validation of a $D$ diffusion coefficient, namely:

- Modelling A and E: Mensi law

$$
D(C) = A \exp(BC)
$$

- Modelling B: Granger's law

$$
D(C,T)=A\exp(\mathrm{BC})\frac{T}{{T}_{0}}\exp\left[-\frac{{Q}_{S}}{R}(\frac{1}{T}-\frac{1}{{T}_{0}})\right]
$$

- Modelling C: definition of $D$ as a tablecloth
- Modelling D: Bazant law

![Figure](/figures/v7.20.102/Object_4.svg)

- Modelling F: SECH_RFT law

The expression of $D\left(C,T\right)$ for this law is quite complex, it is presented in detail in R7.01.12.

The coefficients used are those recommended by Granger in his thesis [bib1]:

| SECH_MENSI: | $A=0.74{10}^{-13}{m}^{2}/s$$B=0.05$ |
| --- | --- |
| SECH_GRANGER | $A=0.74{10}^{-13}{m}^{2}/s$$B=0.05$ ${T}_{0}=293°K$ ${Q}_{s}/R=4700{K}^{-1}$ |
| SECH_NAPPE | The law of Mensi![Figure](/figures/v7.20.102/100047E8000036150000207ACBE9C75B4F76E784.svg) is in the form of a tablecloth |
| SECH_BAZANT | $\mathit{D1}=3.0{10}^{-10}{m}^{2}/s$$\alpha =0.04$ $n=6$![Figure](/figures/v7.20.102/Object_5.svg)with ${C}_{0}=128.8l/{m}^{3}$ and ${C}_{\mathit{eq}}=58.8l/{m}^{3}$ |
| SECH_RFT | Parameters are given in model F. |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The drying calculation is carried out over a period of 5 years

- the temperature remains uniform and is $20°C$
- apply to $\mathit{FE}$: ${C}_{\mathit{eq}}=58.8l/{m}^{3}$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The initial conditions are the initial temperature, which is taken at $20°C$, and the initial water concentration, which is $128.8l/m^3$.

## 12 Summary of the results

For modellings A to E, an difference is obtained between the SCILAB solution and the *Code_Aster* solution below $\text{1.5 \%}$, which allows the validation of the implementation of the different drying laws in the code. Let's just note that we observe a violation of the principle of maximum at the beginning of the simulation with Aster for the Mensi law. This can be explained (by analogy with thermal) by the important "shock" due to the way in which to impose the boundary conditions (imposed water concentration). This problem should be solved by the use of the elements fused in the same way as in thermal.

For F Modelling, it can be concluded that SECH_RFT law is equivalent to SECH_NAPPE law with the python formula provided.
