# v6.04.261 SSNV261 – Tensile ENDO_LOCA_EXP behavior – contained uniaxial compression

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the constitutive law ENDO_LOCA_EXP integration algorithm. The problem studied is a uniform straining and then pressure stression stress which can be obtained from analytical solution.

The problem is implemented:

- in 3D in Modelling A
- in D_PLAN in Modelling B
- COQUE_SOLIDE in Modelling C

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a hardware point, processed by SIMU_POINT_MAT in 3D or D_PLAN depending on the model or with a single element and STAT_NON_LINE for COQUE_SOLIDE.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material obeys the constitutive law fragile elastic ENDO_LOCA_EXP. The macroscopic data correspond to:

|  |  |

The internal parameters of the model are calculated by DEFI_MATER_GC macro-command.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

It is a stress in a material point (SIMU_POINT_MAT). All deformation components are imposed; they are all zero except EPXX. First, a single-axial tensile deformation is imposed and then a compression deformation is discharged to verify the effect of the stiffness restoration in compression.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

This problem admits an analytical solution. The calculation method is largely detailed in Lorentz-2020.

For the load phase, a target damage value $b=0.1$ is given. By noting ${E}_{c}$ the confined stiffness, ${\sigma}_{c}$ the confined tensile peak stress and $B(b)$ the stiffness function, the ${\sigma}^{(T)}$ stress can be calculated successively and then the ${\epsilon}^{(T)}$ deformation achieved for the target damage value:

> ${\sigma}^{(T)}=(1-b){\sigma}_{c}$         ${\epsilon}^{(T)}=\frac{{\sigma}^{(T)}}{{E}_{c}B(b)}$

The compression phase is conducted up to ${\epsilon}^{(C)}=-2{\epsilon}^{(T)}$ . By noting $S'$ the rigidity jump regularization function, the corresponding stress level is deduced:

> ${\sigma}^{(C)}={E}_{c}[B(b){\epsilon}^{(C)}+(1-B(b))\frac{S'({\epsilon}^{(C)})}{2}]$

Damage does not vary during discharge and compression.

### 2.2 Baseline

The internal parameters of the model corresponding to the selected set of values are as follows:

> ${E}_{c}=37921\text{MPa}$
> ${\sigma}_{c}=3.034\text{MPa}$
> ${w}_{c}=1.214\times {10}^{-4}\text{MPa}$
> $\kappa =5.842$
> ${m}_{0}=0.589$
> ${D}_{1}=4.081$
> $r=3.95$

It will be ensured that, when deformations are imposed, the model is well restored to the expected levels of damage and stress.

The calculation of the internal variables of post-processing is also tested, i.e. the $1-B(b)$ , the strain energy elastic $w(\epsilon ,b)$ and the energy consumed by damage. In this case, the latter simply writes, always with the ratings of Lorentz-2020:

> ${W}_{\mathit{cons}}=\kappa {w}_{c}\widehat{a}(b)$
