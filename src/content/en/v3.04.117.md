# v3.04.117 SSLV117 – Validation of the second 3D expansion gradient model

**Summary:**

This test validates the [R5.04.03](doc:r5.04.03) 3D second gradient Modelling from analytical solutions. It is an elastic ball of unit radius subject to imposed radial displacement and internal pressure.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.117/10000000000000F90000011B9305DA76C4C14512.png)

**Figure 1.1-a**

The geometry of the structure is a single radius ball for the analytical solution.

### 1.2 Material properties

In the context of the second gradient media, it is necessary to define material properties for the parts attached to the first and second gradients of the displacement field. The Material properties are not physically relevant but have been established to simplify analytical applications.

    - Young's modulus: $\text{E}=1\text{Pa}$ ;
    - Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0$ ;
    - Second gradient rigidity module: ${a}_{1}=0.25{\text{Pa.m}}^{2}$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

For reasons of numerical simplification, only one eighth of the ball is considered by symmetry:

- $\mathit{DX}=0$ on the GROUP_MA 'SYME_X'
- $\mathit{DY}=0$ on the GROUP_MA 'SYME_Y'
- $\mathit{DZ}=0$ on the GROUP_MA 'SYME_Z'

For reasons of numerical simplification, the ball is not modelled but the cube of this ball for the application of boundary conditions. This is applied in the $X=(x,y,z)$ marker:

- ${U}_{R}(X)=(\frac{{r}^{4}}{7}-\frac{1}{3})X$ for DX, DYet DZ components of GROUP_MA'BORD_EXT' where $r$ is the distance between the ball's origin and point coordinate $X$ .
- ${G}_{R}(X)=({r}^{4}-1)$ for GONF components of GROUP_MA'BORD_EXT'.

The following internal forces are applied in the $X=(x,y,z)$ marker:

${F}_{I}(X)=4(10-{r}^{2})X$ for FX, FYet FZ components of GROUP_MA'BORD_EXT'

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Quantities and reference results

The analytical solution is as follows:

$\nabla \chi (X)=4{r}^{2}X$ . where $\nabla \chi (X)$ are the DGONFX1, DGONFX2, DGONFX3 components of the SIEF_ELGA field.

${\varepsilon}_{xx}(X)=(\frac{{r}^{4}}{7}-\frac{1}{3})+\frac{4{r}^{2}}{7}x\mathrm{\ast }x$ . where $r$ means the distance between the ball origin and point coordinate $X=(x,y,z)$ (EPXX component of the EPSI_ELGA field).

${\varepsilon}_{yy}(X)=(\frac{{r}^{4}}{7}-\frac{1}{3})+\frac{4{r}^{2}}{7}y\mathrm{\ast }y$ . (EPYY component of the EPSI_ELGA field).

${\varepsilon}_{zz}(X)=(\frac{{r}^{4}}{7}-\frac{1}{3})+\frac{4{r}^{2}}{7}z\mathrm{\ast }z$ . (EPZZ component of the EPSI_ELGA field).

${\varepsilon}_{xy}(X)=\frac{4{r}^{2}}{7}x\mathrm{\ast }y$ . (EPXY component of the EPSI_ELGA field).

${\varepsilon}_{xz}(X)=\frac{4{r}^{2}}{7}x\mathrm{\ast }z$ . (EPXZ component of the EPSI_ELGA field).

${\varepsilon}_{yz}(X)=\frac{4{r}^{2}}{7}y\mathrm{\ast }z$ . (EPYZ component of the EPSI_ELGA field).
