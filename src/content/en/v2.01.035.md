## 1 Reference problem

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.035/schema.png)

*System of 3 masses and 3 springs in parallel*

Note $u_i$ absolute displacement to node i and $r_i$ anchor reaction.

System of equilibrium equations:

$$
\begin{cases}
k_1 (u_2 - u_1) + k_2 (u_3 - u_1) + k_3 (u_4 - u_1) + r_1 = 0 \\
-k_1 (u_2 - u_1)  = m_2 \ddot{u}_2 \\
-k_2 (u_3 - u_1) = m_3 \ddot{u}_3 \\
-k_3 (u_4 - u_1) = m_4 \ddot{u}_4
\end{cases}
$$

Note $\mathbf{U} = \left[ u_1 \, u_2 \, u_3 \, u_4 \right]^T$ the vector of absolute displacements
nodes. The equation sys_eq_equilibrium is brought back in the form:

$$
\mathbf{K} \, \mathbf{U} + \mathbf{M} \, \ddot{\mathbf{U}} = \mathbf{R}
$$

Rigidity matrix:

$$
\mathbf{K} =
\begin{bmatrix}
k_1+k_2+k_3 & -k_1 & -k_2 & -k_3 \\
-k_1 & k_1 & 0 & 0 \\
-k_2 & 0 & k_2 & 0 \\
-k_3 & 0 & 0 & k_3
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Mass matrix:

$$
\mathbf{M} =
\begin{bmatrix}
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & m_1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & m_2 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & m_3
\end{bmatrix}
$$

Reaction vector:

$$
\mathbf{R} = \begin{bmatrix} r_1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}^T
$$

Let's fix $\mathbf{K}$:

$$
\underline{\underline{K}}^p  = \left[
    \begin{array}{c c c |c}
     k_1 & 0 & 0 & -k_1 \\
    0 & k_2 & 0 & -k_2 \\
    0 & 0 & k_3 & -k_3 \\
    \hline
    -k_1 & -k_2 & -k_3 & k_1 + k_2 +k_3
    \end{array}
    \right]
$$

$$
\underline{\underline{K}}_{xx} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
    k_1 & 0 & 0 \\
    0 & k_2 & 0\\
    0 & 0 & k_3
    \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\underline{\underline{K}}_{xs} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
    - k_1 \\
    - k_2 \\
    - k_3
    \end{bmatrix} = \underline{\underline{K}}_{sx}^T
$$

$$
\underline{\underline{K}}_{ss} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
     k_1 + k_2 +k_3
    \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
\underline{\underline{M}}_{xx} =
    \begin{bmatrix}
    m_1 & 0 & 0 \\
    0 & m_2 & 0 \\
    0 & 0 & m_3
    \end{bmatrix}
$$

The masses are identical: $m_1 = m_2 = m_3 = m$.

We'll take:

$k_1 = k$, $k_2 = 1.21\,k$, $k_3 = 1.44\,k$

$\omega_i$ is the clean pulses and $\phi_{i,\, x}$ is the associated modes, we have:

$$
\left( \mathbf{K}_{xx} - \omega_i^2 \mathbf{M}_{xx} \right)\phi_{i,\, x} = 0
$$

$$
\Rightarrow \det \left( \mathbf{M}_{xx}^{-1}  \mathbf{K}_{xx}  - \omega_i^2 \mathbf{I} \right) = 0
$$

Since $\mathbf{K}_{xx}$ and $\mathbf{M}_{xx}$ are diagonal matrices, the eigenfrequencies are
obtained directly by:

$$
\omega_i = \sqrt{\frac{k_i}{m_i}}
$$

Eigenfrequencies:

$$
f_i = \frac{\omega_i}{2 \pi}
$$

The eigenmodes in the degrees of freedom base linked to the structure (freeddl) are:

$$
\phi_{1, x}=\left(\begin{array}{c}1\\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right) , \;
\phi_{2, x}=\left(\begin{array}{c}0\\ 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right) , \;
\phi_{3, x}=\left(\begin{array}{c}0\\0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right)
$$

Eigenmodes not standard $\{\phi_{i}\} \in \mathbb{R}^{4 \times 1}$ in the global benchmark:

$$
\phi_{1}=\left(\begin{array}{c}0 \\ 1\\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right) , \;
\phi_{2}=\left(\begin{array}{c}0\\0\\ 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right) , \;
\phi_{3}=\left(\begin{array}{c}0\\0\\0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right)
$$

Generalized modal mass:

${\mu }_{i} = \underline{\phi }_{i}^T \underline{\underline{M}} \, \underline{\phi }_{i}$

Eigenmodes standardised to the unit generalised modal mass
${\phi }_{\text{Ni}}$ :

**Modal reactions** ${\mathrm{F}}_{m,\,i}$:

From this we conclude:

$$
\begin{split}
        \Rightarrow \underline{r}_1 &=\underline{\underline{K}}_{sx} \underline{\phi}_{N1,\,x} = \underline{\underline{K}}_{sx} \frac{\underline{\phi}_{1,\, x}}{\sqrt{\mu_1}} \\
        &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{m}} \begin{bmatrix}
     -k & -1.21\, k & -1.44 \,k
    \end{bmatrix} \left(\begin{array}{c}1\\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right) = - \frac{k}{\sqrt{m}}
\end{split}
$$

$$
\begin{split}
        \Rightarrow \underline{r}_2 &=\underline{\underline{K}}_{sx} \underline{\phi}_{N2,\,x} = \underline{\underline{K}}_{sx} \frac{\underline{\phi}_{2,\, x}}{\sqrt{\mu_2}} \\
        &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{m}} \begin{bmatrix}
     -k & -1.21\, k & -1.44 \,k
    \end{bmatrix} \left(\begin{array}{c}0\\ 1 \\ 0 \end{array}\right) = - \frac{1.21\,k}{\sqrt{m}}
\end{split}
$$

$$
\begin{split}
        \Rightarrow \underline{r}_3 &=\underline{\underline{K}}_{sx} \underline{\phi}_{N3,\,x} = \underline{\underline{K}}_{sx} \frac{\underline{\phi}_{3,\, x}}{\sqrt{\mu_3}} \\
        &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{m}} \begin{bmatrix}
     -k & -1.21\, k & -1.44 \,k
    \end{bmatrix} \left(\begin{array}{c}0\\ 0 \\ 1 \end{array}\right) = - \frac{1.44\,k}{\sqrt{m}}
\end{split}
$$
