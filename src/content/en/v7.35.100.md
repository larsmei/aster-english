# v7.35.100 WSLP100 - Test for deaturation by ointment in an unsaturated porous sample

**Summary:**

This test validates hydraulic sprain conditions. The Modelling is done in 2D with an D_PLAN_HHMS model and a linear elastic constitutive law.

This test case contains only one model.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Description

The test consists of de-charging a porous permeable sample with boundary conditions initially impermeable on the edges (zero flowing water flow). At the moment $t=0$ , the right edge is imposed "an opening" of 50cm using the conditions of the sooting, as shown below. The water height gradually decreases until it stabilizes at the lower edge of the opening.

![Figure](/figures/v7.35.100/10000201000003D4000002C4BE6B87806D1344A7.png)

**Image** 1.1-a: Description of test case

### 1.2 Geometry

The dimensions of the rectangular sample are:

- height: $h=3m$
- width: $l=1m$

The length of the sprain section is: $e=50\mathit{cm}$. It is placed on the right edge, between a height of 1m and 1.5m.

### 1.3 Material properties

The elastic properties are:

- $E=515\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =2670\mathit{kg}.{m}^{-3}$

The constitutive law associated with the HH2M hydraulic model is LIQU_GAZ, so we consider 1 liquid phase without dissolved air and a gas phase without water vapour.

### 1.4 Boundary conditions and loads

The boundary conditions are:

- Vertical base displacement block: $\mathit{DY}=0$
- Horizontal displacement block on both edges: $\mathit{DX}=0$
- Zero water and air pressures on the upper edge: $\mathit{PRE}1=\mathit{PRE}2=0$
- Conditions for sooting on the section of the ‘opening': $\mathit{PRE}1\ge 0$

The load is the acceleration of the gravity.

The calculation is performed between $t=0$ and $t=0,2s$ by step from $0,001s$ to $t=0,02s$, then by step from $0,01s$ to $t=0,2s$.

### 1.5 Initial conditions

The initial effective stress in the sample is isotropic and geostatic, i.e. equal to:

$\sigma {'}_{\mathit{XX}}=\sigma {'}_{\mathit{YY}}=\sigma {'}_{\mathit{ZZ}}=(\rho -{10}^{3})gz$

Initial water pressure is geostatic: $\lbrace \begin{array}{c}\mathit{PRE}1={10}^{3}gz\\ \mathit{PRE}2=0\end{array}$

## 2 reference solution

A analytical solution with a flow problem with sooting conditions is not easy to calculate, and therefore a test case is proposed that is intuitive enough to allow certain expected intermediate and final values to be tested.

Thus the final values at $t=0,2s$ expected tested are:

- Base: $\mathit{PRE}1=-{10}^{3}g$
- Top hole: $\mathit{PRE}1=+{5.10}^{2}g$
- Top right edge: $\mathit{PRE}1=+{2.10}^{3}g$

At an intermediate moment when the water column height has not yet reached the opening:

$\mathit{PRE}1=0$

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Modelling D_PLAN_HHMS

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

- Number of nodes: 981
- Number of mesh cells: 80 SEG3 type and 300 QUAD8 type

The following post-processing nodes are defined:

- $\mathit{NBASE}$: at the base of the right edge
- $\mathit{NTROU}$: in the middle of the opening
- $\mathit{NSUP}$: at the top of the right edge

The boundary conditions on the move are:

- On the $\mathit{BAS}$ side: $\mathit{DY}=0$ and $\mathit{PRE}2=0$
- On the $\mathit{GAUCHE}$ and $\mathit{DROIT}$ faces: $\mathit{DX}=0$ and $\mathit{PRE}2=0$
- On the $\mathit{HAUT}$ side : $\mathit{PRE}1=0$
