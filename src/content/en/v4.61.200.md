# v4.61.200 MTLP200 – Multi-pass simulation of welding on line RIS

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the 2D welding simulation methodology with a massively parallel version. This is the modelling of the welding of two welding passes on the RIS line.

The calculation consists of chaining thermal calculations on several models (by successive addition of cords), then mechanical calculations with consideration of thermal loadings.

It validates several necessary features in the MECA_NON_LINE operator, such as the use of linear search and the restoration of nutwork. Calculation is done on 2 processors to validate the use of distributed mesh.

The results are compared with non-regression values.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A structure with a weld composed of 19 cords is considered.

The calculation is limited to the successive addition of the first 2 cords.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The thermomechanical calculation shall include the following steps in the calculation:

- Non-linear thermal calculation:

At the removal of each cord, a thermal calculation is performed where three loads are taken into account:

- A thermal source on the added cord. This source is defined by a function that depends on the following process parameters: Vs speed, Q power and $\eta$ efficiency. The equivalent heat source is defined by a triangle function on the cord surface.

Vs_1 = 1.53 mm/s Vs_2 = 1.19 mm/s

Q = 520 et $\eta =0.75$

- Radiation and convection on the outer surfaces with the parameters ${T}_{\mathit{ext}}=20°C$ , the emissivity ${\varepsilon}=0.2$ , the constant Boltzmann $\sigma =5.67e-14$ . The exchange coefficient for convection is $h=15e-06$ .,

The initial temperature is 20 °C over the entire model.

- Mechanical calculationNon-linear:

The mechanical calculation is done in two phases by applying thermal loading with dilation-stringing at the ends of the tube. Both phases correspond to the solidification phase (the temperature of the cord above the melting temperature ${T}_{\mathit{fusion}}=1450°C$ ), for the next phase, there is an adjustment of the position and deformation of the cord. The materials are modified between the two phases of calculation.

The application of cord 1 is done between 0. and 990.

The application of cord 2 is done between 1000. and 1990.
