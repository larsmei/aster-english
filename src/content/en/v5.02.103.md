# v5.02.103 SDNL103 - Dynamic of a portal modeled by elements beam in high rotation. Comparison with a small rotation analysis

**Summary:**

The response of a portal, clamped in feet and subjected to a dynamic force applied in the middle of its span and perpendicular to its plane is analyzed. The movements are small. Two modellings beams are compared: POU_D_T_GD and POU_D_T.

**Interest:** test element of MECA_POU_D_T_GD wide rotation beam and DYNA_NON_LINE command.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

This problem has no analytical solution. But since the movements are small, elements beam Modelling is taken as a reference.

### 2.2 Baseline

Moving from the mid-span in the $x$ direction at the moment: $0.14s$; $0.26s$; $0.36s$ and $0.47s$ .

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Characteristics of the span:

$A=2.24E–3{m}^{2}$ ; ${I}_{y}={I}_{z}=3.7E–6{m}^{4}$ ; ${J}_{x}=7.4E–6{m}^{4}$ ; ${A}_{y}={A}_{z}=1.2$

Characteristics of the poles:

$A=3.14E–2{m}^{2}$ ; ${I}_{y}={I}_{z}=4.5E–5{m}^{4}$ ; ${J}_{x}=9.0E–5{m}^{4}$ ; ${A}_{y}={A}_{z}=1.2$

The analysis is about $0.5s$ in equal 100 time step.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The span is modeled by 12 elements beam; each pole by 2 elements. All of these elements have $\mathrm{1m}$ length.

## 4 Summary of the results

The difference compared to reference solution is at the maximum of $\text{3,5\%}$ during the transient. As the reference solution is obtained with elements POU_D_T, in small movements, this difference is therefore explainable and remains low over time.
