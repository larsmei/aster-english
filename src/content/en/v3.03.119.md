# v3.03.119 SSLS119 - clamped Crochet Subjected to shear force at its End

**Summary:**

This test represents a static calculation of a clamped hook subjected to shearing effort, consisting of elastic material. This test allows to validate the following modellings elements finished:

- DST (QUAD4),
- DKT (QUAD4),
- COQUE_3D (QUAD9),
- COQUE_3D (TRIA7),
- 3D linear (HEXA8) and quadratic (HEXA20).

The study focuses on transverse shear blocking.

## 2 reference solution

This test allows testing the transverse shear lock and the effects of the rigidity of the rotation around the normal. It allows to validate the choice COEF_RIGI_DRZ = 1. E–05 , the default value of this coefficient. This multiplicative factor allows to affect a fictitious stiffness around the normal elements plate by multiplying the minimum stiffness in other directions by this coefficient so as to avoid singular stiffness matrices.

### 2.1 Baseline

The reference results are derived from a calculation by elements finite density:

A few percents depending on the refinement of the mesh.

## 15 Summary of the results

L-element DST with transverse shearing consideration does not seem to converge on this specific case. elements COQUE_3D triangles and quadrangles with transverse shearing consideration do not exhibit the same behavior and behave rather well on this test.
