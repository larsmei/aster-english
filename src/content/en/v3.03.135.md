# v3.03.135 SSLS135 - Ferry of a square tank according to the method of Capra and Maury

**Summary:**

This test is for the study of the square tank used as an example in the descriptive document of the Capra and Maury method. The aim is to calculate longitudinal and transverse ferrailage densities for elements Plate or Coque.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A square concrete tank of $L\times l\times h=10m\times 10m\times 5m$ (medium to medium sheet) and $0.4m$ thickness is considered.

### 1.2 Material properties

Isotropic linear elastic material:

Young's modulus: $E={3.10}^{4}\mathit{MPa}$ ,

Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.15$ ,

Density: $\mu =2500\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The density of stiffness soil applied under the tank is $50\mathit{kN}/{m}^{3}$.

The integral of this density on the basis of the tank is therefore $5{10}^{6}\mathit{kN}/m$ .

This amount is then distributed over the nodes of the base.

The load consists of:

- of the tank's own weight
- the water thrust of the filled tank (constant push on the bottom and gradual push on the edges)
- an overload spread over the contour at the top of the tank ($20\mathit{kN}/m$ )

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The density of longitudinal steels is calculated using the Capra and Maury method, for the $\text{X}$ and $\text{Y}$ directions of each element and on both sides, $\text{I}$ (Inferior) and $\text{S}$ (Superior), defined by their position following the normal $\text{Z}$ elemental. The cross-sectional steel density is also calculated as described in the article entitled "Automatic calculation of the optimum ironing of reinforced concrete plates or hulls" by Alain CAPRA and Jean-Francis MAURY.

### 2.2 Baseline

The different results are published in the article by Capra and Maury in the form of graphs (for a quarter model only) are:

- the deformed tank,
- $\text{Mxx}$ and $\text{Myy}$ iso-moments,
- Ferry densities in the $\text{X}$ and $\text{Y}$ directions above and below.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

We use DKT Modelling. We model only a quarter of the tank.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Elements are quadrangles. elements DKT are oriented to have their normal output.

### 3.3 Other parameters of the calculation

The acceleration of the gravity is $9.81m/{s}^{2}$ .

The distance between the steel axis and the surface of a element (coated) is $0.04m$ .

The equivalence coefficient is $15.0$ .

The maximum permissible stress of the steel is $2.8{10}^{8}\mathit{Pa}$ .

The maximum permissible stress of the concrete is $3.5{10}^{7}\mathrm{Pa}$ .

The calculation is performed at the Service Limit State (ELS).

The pivots are $\mathrm{PIVA}=1.0{10}^{3}$ and $\mathrm{PIVB}=3.5{10}^{-3}$ respectively

## 4 Summary of the results

This test shows the validity of the ELS ferrage density calculations. The results obtained are very similar to those in the method's reference document. However, validation is limited by the lack of precise data on certain parameters used (coating value, permissible stress for concrete, coefficient of equivalence) and the small amount of usable results provided by the original publication.
