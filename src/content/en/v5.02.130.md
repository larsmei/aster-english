# v5.02.130 SDNL130 - Seismic response of a reinforced concrete beam (rectangular section) with nonlinear behaviour

**Summary:**

The problem is to analyze the seismic response of a reinforced concrete beam. The $\mathit{BA}$ beam behaviour is nonlinear. Two modellings are studied:

- the beam is represented by [R3.08.08](doc:r3.08.08) multifibre beam Modelling. The constitutive law of the concrete is Mazars in its version 1D [R7.01.08](doc:r7.01.08) for Modelling A;
- the beam is represented by elements DKTG. The nonlinear constitutive law used is GLRC_DM[R7.01.32] for Modelling B.

## 1 General characteristics

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry is identical to that of SSNL119 and SDLL130 test cases except for longitudinal frames which are all identical here: these are four $\mathit{HA32}$ .

## 2 reference solution

The tests performed are only of non-regression type.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Longitudinal mesh of the beam:

It is composed of 17 nodes and 16 pairs d-elements POU_D_EM (16 elements for concrete and 16 for steel).

Cross section of the beam:

Concrete is modelled by an mesh composed of $2\times 20$ quadrilateral (40 fibres).

The steel is modelled by 4 point fibers.

The coefficients $\alpha$ and $\beta$ for depreciation shall be calculated using the following formula:

$\left[\begin{array}{c}\alpha \\ \beta \end{array}\right]=2\frac{{\omega}_{1}{\omega}_{2}}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}_{1}^{2}}\left[\begin{array}{cc}\frac{-1}{{\omega}_{2}}& \frac{1}{{\omega}_{1}}\\ {\omega}_{2}& -{\omega}_{1}\end{array}\right]\left[\begin{array}{c}{\xi}_{1}\\ {\xi}_{2}\end{array}\right]$

where ${\omega}_{1}$ and ${\omega}_{2}$ are the first two clean pulses $(\omega =2\pi f)$ and ${\xi}_{1}$ and ${\xi}_{2}$ are the desired dampings on the first two modes.

With ${f}_{1}=39.9\mathrm{Hz}$ and ${f}_{2}=157.6\mathrm{Hz}$ (calculated with *Code_Aster*), for $\text{5\%}$ modal depreciation, we find: $\alpha ={8.10}^{-5}$ and $\beta =20$.

The time step chosen is $0,01s$ .

The law for concrete behavior is Mazars in its version $\mathrm{1D}$.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

The beam is modeled by elements DKTG and GRILLE_EXCENTREE. 16 elements is used in the longitudinal direction $X$ and only one in the transverse direction $Z$.

The reinforced concrete is modelled by the constitutive law GLRC_DM. The constitutive law GLRC_DM parameters are obtained by DEFI_GLRC macro-command. The material data used is defined in 1.2 . For the GLRC model reinforcements, a $\mathit{OMX}=\mathit{OMY}=\mathrm{8,04E-4}\mathit{m²}/m$ reinforcement section is defined and $\mathit{RX}=\mathit{RY}=0,872$ ex-centre is defined. The PENTE =_F(

TRACTION='PLAS_ACIER',

FLEXION = 'UTIL',

KAPPA_FLEX = 3E-3,

),

The first two eigenfrequencies (calculated with *Code_Aster*) are ${f}_{1}=38.7\mathit{Hz}$ and ${f}_{2}=153.2\mathit{Hz}$ . For 5% modal depreciation, the damping coefficients $\alpha =8{.10}^{-5}$ and $\beta =20$ are obtained . These coefficients are provided to the DEFI_GLRC macro-command.
