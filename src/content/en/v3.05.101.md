# v3.05.101 SSLX101 - Straight pipe modeled in shell and beams

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the restraint beam hull. The hose is clamped at one end, and subjected to 4 successive forces (traction and 3 moments) on the other end. Half of the pipe is mesh in shell, the other is mesh in beams. The clamped support and the connection between the shell and the beam are made by an restraint beam shell, in particular allowing only the torsor to transmit to the shell the forces of the beam type, without causing any harmful stresses.

The reference solution is analytical (material resistance). the difference with the digital solution (3 to 5%) is explained by the fact that the mesh in shell is actually made up of element planes (facets). The geometry of the pipe is therefore approached. The solution obtained makes it possible to verify that the connection between the elements hull and the element beam is correct.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

$80m$ straight hose, modeled in inshells between $0$ and $\mathrm{40m}$, and in beams between $40$ and $\mathrm{80m}$.

Outside radius: $\mathrm{2m}$ , thickness: $\mathrm{0.1m}$ .

The hose axis is in the $\mathrm{Oxy}$ plane, inclined 30 degrees from $\mathrm{Ox}$.

![Figure](/figures/v3.05.101/Object_1.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

$E={2.10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$

$\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Clamped support "beam type" in $x=y=0$ , made by a restraint beam shell between the $\mathrm{C1}$ edge of the hull and an point $\mathrm{P1}$ (located in $O$ ). This point is blocked.

4 unit load cases applied to point $\mathit{P2}(80\times \mathit{cos30},80\times \mathit{sin30},0)$

$\mathrm{Fx1}=\mathrm{1N}$ Traction on $\mathrm{Ox1}$ axis, i.e. $\mathrm{Fx}=\mathrm{cos30.Fx}$ and $\mathrm{Fy}=\mathrm{sin30.Fx1}$

$\mathrm{Mx1}=\mathrm{1Nm}$ twisting moment around $\mathrm{Ox1}$, i.e. $\mathrm{Mx}=\mathrm{cos30.Mx1}$ and $\mathrm{My}=\mathrm{sin30.Mx1}$

Bending moment $\mathrm{My1}=\mathrm{1Nm}$ around $\mathrm{Oy1}$ i.e. $\mathrm{Mx}=-\mathrm{sin30.My1}$ and $\mathrm{My}=\mathrm{cos30.My1}$

Bending moment around $\mathrm{Oz}$ , i.e. $\mathrm{Mz}=\mathrm{1Nm}$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Analytical solution for each of the 4 loading cases:

In beam theory, in the context of the Euler-Bernouilli hypothesis, the solution of Reference problem is that of an straight beam subjected to effort and moments at one end and clamped at the other end:

**Traction**:

$$
\boldsymbol{u}_{x_1} = F_{x_1} \frac{x}{ES} \boldsymbol{e}_{x1}
$$

**Torsion**:

$$
\boldsymbol{\theta}_{x_1} = M_{x_1} \frac{x}{GJ} \boldsymbol{e}_{x1}
$$

**Flexion around** $O_{y1}$:

$$
\boldsymbol{u}_{z} & &= - M_{y} \frac{x^2}{2 EI_{y_1}} \boldsymbol{e}_{z} \\
\boldsymbol{\theta}_{y_1} & = (-\partial_x \boldsymbol{u}_{z} \cdot \boldsymbol{e}_{z} ) \boldsymbol{e}_{y_1} & = M_{y} \frac{x}{EI_{y_1}} \boldsymbol{e}_{y1} \\
$$

**Flexion around** $O_{z}$:

$$
\boldsymbol{u}_{y_1} & & = M_{z} \frac{x^2}{2 EI_{z}} \boldsymbol{e}_{y_1} \\
\boldsymbol{\theta}_{z} & = (\partial_x \boldsymbol{u}_{y_1} \cdot \boldsymbol{e}_{y_1} ) \boldsymbol{e}_{z} & = M_{z} \frac{x}{EI_{z}} \boldsymbol{e}_{z} \\
$$

No loading involves shear force, loading cases
bending being in bending **pure**.
The reference solutions are also valid for the cinematic of the
Timoshenko (in addition to Euler-Bernouilli's cinematic), as indicated in
[V3.01.102](doc:v3.01.102).

This implies that the same reference solutions can be used for
elements `POU_D_E` and `TUYAU_3M`.

### 2.2 Baseline

**Traction**:

$$
u_{x1}(P_2) = F_{x_1} \frac{L}{ES}
$$

So

$$
\begin{cases}
u_{x}(P_2) & = F_{x_1} \frac{L}{ES} \cos(30)\\
u_{y}(P_2) & = F_{x_1} \frac{L}{ES} \sin(30)\\
\end{cases}
$$

**Torsion**:

$$
\theta_{x1}(P_2) = M_{x_1} \frac{L}{GJ}
$$

So

$$
\begin{cases}
\theta_{x}(P_2) & = M_{x_1} \frac{L}{GJ} \cos(30)\\
\theta_{y}(P_2) & = M_{x_1} \frac{L}{GJ} \sin(30)\\
\end{cases}
$$

**Flexion around** $O_{y_1}$:

$$
\theta_{y_1}(P_2) = M_{y_1} \frac{L}{EI_{y_1}}
$$

So

$$
\begin{cases}
\theta_{x}(P_2) & = - M_{y_1} \frac{L}{EI_{y_1}} \sin(30)\\
\theta_{y}(P_2) & = M_{y_1} \frac{L}{EI_{y_1}} \cos(30)\\
u_z(P_2) & = - M_{y_1} \frac{L^2}{2EI_{y_1}}
\end{cases}
$$

**Flexion around** $O_{z}$:

$$
u_{y_1} = M_{z} \frac{L^2}{2 EI_{z}}
$$

So

$$
\begin{cases}
u_x(P_2) & = - M_{z} \frac{L^2}{2 EI_{z}} \sin(30) \\
u_y(P_2) & =   M_{z} \frac{L^2}{2 EI_{z}} \cos(30) \\
\theta_{z}(P_2) & = M_{z} \frac{L}{EI_{z}} \\
\end{cases}
$$

**Note:**

Using the restraint beam hulls for the clamped support and for the loadings allows you to stay within the framework of the hypothesis of Euler-Bernouilli (cf. [R3.03.06](doc:r3.03.06)). The previous analytical solution is therefore the reference solution of the problem.
