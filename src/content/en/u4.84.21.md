## 3 Operands

The command makes it possible to compute the complement of internal forces due to the non-linearities of the constitutive behaviour. This analysis concerns the constitutive laws in which the internal forces depend essentially on the kinematic fields (displacements, velocities, accelerations); it therefore concerns above all the localised non-linearities, for example penalised contact between discrete elements. The terms computed at the nodes are obtained by the difference between the integrated residual of internal forces (depending both on the kinematic fields and on the parameters of the constitutive law) and the internal force that would be obtained from the same kinematic fields by considering a linear constitutive behaviour for the structure.

**Note 1:**

*The name of the result concept created, of type* *dyna_trans, must be different from the name of the result concept of type* *dyna_trans used as input under the operand* *RESULTAT, and it constitutes an evolution of fields of type* ‘ *DEPL’. This new result of complementary nodal forces is used under the keyword* *EXCIT_RESU* *in the command* *DYNA_VIBRA* *with* *TYPE_RESU='HARM' (in this case after transformation by the operator* *REST_SPEC_TEMP*  [U4.63.34](doc:u4.63.34)) and *DYNA_VIBRA or* *DYNA_VIBRA and* *TYPE_RESU='TRAN'. An example is supplied in the test case SDLS119A.*

**Note 2:**

*In the specific case of the analysis with the relation* *‘DIS_CHOC’ assigned to discrete elements,* *it is then necessary always to orient the discrete contact element in the direction from the structure towards the stop. Whether with* *POI1 elements, as always by means of the keyword* *ORIENTATION of* *AFFE_CARA_ELEM, but also with* *SEG2 elements, where one must properly define the element in the sense from the structure that is a candidate for contact, associated with node 1, towards the stop, associated with node 2.*

### 3.1 Operands TOUT_ORDRE / NUME_ORDRE / LIST_ORDRE / NUME_MODE/ NOEUD_CMP / NOM_CAS / INST / LIST_INST / FREQ / LIST_FREQ / PRECISION / CRITERE

See [U4.71.00](doc:u4.71.00) for the description of these operands.

#### 3.1.1 Operand MODELE

♦    MODELE = mo,

Name of the model, which it is necessary to enter

#### 3.1.2 Operand CHAM_MATER

◊    CHAM_MATER = chmater,

Name of the material field in which the material characteristics of the elements are defined. This argument is necessary for the analysis because the constitutive laws defined in the keywords COMPORTEMENT always require a material field.

#### 3.1.3 Operand CARA_ELEM

◊    CARA_ELEM = carac,

The concept of the elementary characteristics of type cara_elem is necessary for the analysis if structural elements exist in the model.

#### 3.1.4 Operand COMPORTEMENT

◊    COMPORTEMENT = _F

Factor keyword making it possible to assign constitutive laws to mesh cells or to mesh-cell groups of the mesh.

One defines one assignment per occurrence of the keyword COMPORTEMENT.

##### 3.1.4.1 Operand RELATION

◊    RELATION,

Name of a constitutive-law relation in text format. The relations accepted are those in which the internal forces depend essentially on the kinematic fields (displacements, velocities, accelerations); besides the relation ‘ELAS’, the first relation provided for is the relation ‘DIS_CHOC’ assigned to discrete elements.

##### 3.1.4.2 Operand DEFORMATION

◊    DEFORMATION,

Name of the type of strain in text format, compatible with the type of the elements assigned the constitutive law.

##### 3.1.4.3 Operands TOUT/GROUP_MA/MAILLE

◊ TOUT = 'OUI' ,

The relation is assigned over the whole mesh.

> ◊ GROUP_MA = lgma ,

The relation is assigned on the mesh-cell groups contained in the list lgma.

> ◊ MAILLE = lma ,

The relation is assigned on the mesh cells contained in the list lma.
