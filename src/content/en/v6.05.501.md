# v6.05.501 SSNS501 - Large displacements of a cylindrical panel simply supported

**Summary:**

This test represents a stability calculation of a cylindrical panel simply supported under concentrated stress in its center. The behaviour of the panel changes completely and shows clearly points of return in charge and in motion "snap-through/snap-back". In this case a variable steering and a steering length of arc must be chosen.

It validates elements COQUE_3D finished Modelling with mesh cells TRIA7 and QUAD9 and DKT with mesh cells TRIA3 in the quasi-static, non-linear geometric field in the presence of strong instability.

The displacements and critical load are compared to a digital reference solution.

### 3.4 Remarks

The calculation strategy used consists of two steps:

- load calculation imposed up to $P=582.N$ corresponding to 97% of critical load,
- "Imposed displacement" calculation: Then, a forced displacement is imposed using the technique of the arc length imposed on the entire structure (option LONG_ARC in STAT_NON_LINE).

The use of the arc length technique makes it difficult to define the reference value to be inserted in the TEST_RESU command, since these values cannot be imposed. To define the reference values, we looked for the values of $\mathit{DZ}$ as close as possible to those listed in the table in [§2.2] and we carried forward the values of the control parameter that were to be obtained for the $\mathit{DZ}$ values in question.

## 6 Summary of the results

![Figure](/figures/v6.05.501/Object_35.png)

**Figure 7-a: Normal displacement at the top of the panel depending on the applied force.** **Broadening around the point limit 1**

![Figure](/figures/v6.05.501/10000201000003DB000002EB7C16E8F6808B70B5.png)

**Figure 7-b: Normal displacement at the top of the panel according to the force appliednormalized by its maximum value** (DKT TRIA3 in purple)

The COQUE_3D Modelling results for both the 1 and 4 boundary loads are correct. The maximum error is 2.5% for the mesh cell TRIA7 and 2.8% for the mesh cell QUAD9. However, the error on vertical displacement is greater. It is 5.6% for the mesh cell TRIA7 and 5.9% for the mesh cell QUAD9.

The results between the two boundary loads 1 and 4 are qualitatively correct. Limit points 2 and 3 are well detected. Quantitatively, the displacement values for these points are good at less than 1% for the point limit 2 and less than 5% for the point limit 3. At the level of the corresponding loads, the load at the point limit 2 is very much underestimated (about 70%) and the load at the point limit 3 is highly overestimated (about 20%).

Whatever the mesh cell, the pre-spoil behaviour is properly evaluated. Post-spoil L-ramp allows the correct determination of the displacements at the 2 and 3 limit points. The loads obtained are more distant from the reference solution. From the point limit 4, there is a good agreement between the reference and our solution.

The A_CIS transverse shear correction coefficient was set to $0.833$ , corresponding to the thick hulls. The value ($2500={10}^{6}\times H/L$ ) which should have been taken into account does not allow the calculations to be performed, due to poor conditioning of the stiffness matrices.

The same results are found with DKT (mesh cells TRIA3) Modelling as with COQUE_3D Modelling.

The same calculation is also tested with DKT Modelling for mesh cells QUAD4. However, it converges badly because of its poor description for the shell (curved).
