# v6.03.009 SSNP009 - Stability of a homogeneous slope with suction evaluated by the water retention curve

**Summary:**

This test case is part of the tutorials of the commercial software Geostudio SLOPE/W, designed with the aim of demonstrating and validating methods to take into account matrix suction in slope stability analysis in the presence of hydraulic pressure[1].

In this test case the SLOPE/W results are reproduced with the `CALC_STAB_PENTE` macro-command using the Simplified Bishop method. This test case allows you to validate the following features:

- Definition of hydraulic pressure field by the result of THM calculation.
- Consideration of matrix suction with pressure field defined via `LIGN_PHREA` and `CHAM_PRES`.
- Evaluation of suction resistance by the Van-Genuchten model or water retention curve in the keyword `THM_DIFFU`.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.009/ssnp009-geom.png)

*Geometry of the slope with horizontal waterline[1].*

The stability of a homogeneous slope with the horizontal water line at the foot of the slope is studied.
The slope geometry is shown in geom-ssnp009.

### 1.2 Material properties

The mechanical properties of the soil are as follows:

- Density: $2000kg/m^3$
- Friction angle: $20^{\circ}$
- Cohesion : $5kPa$

The water retention curve of the limousine clay forming the slope is recovered from the SLOPE/W software, which is shown in Fig-1.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.009/vwc_silty_clay.png)

* Water retention curve of the limousine clay.*

The saturated water saturation and the residual saturation are $\theta_s = 0.5$ and $\theta_r = 0.25$ respectively. Effective saturation is estimated by the formula:

$$
S_{we} = \frac{\theta - \theta_r}{\theta_s - \theta_r}
$$

This allows the Van-Genuchten model to be calibrated: $Pr = 19.79kPa$ and $n = 2.32$. However, as shown in Fig-2, the Van-Genuchten model cannot reproduce the negative effective saturation. So we define the same Van-Genuchten parameters in SLOPE/W to generate the reference results for the modellings A and C. In addition, when the water retention curve is defined via the `SATU_PRES` operand in `THM_DIFFU`, $\theta_s$ and $\theta_r$ automatically take the values of the first and last sample on the curve. So we slice the curve in Fig-1 to $p_c = 74kPa$ corresponding to $S_w = \theta_r = 0.25$ to generate the reference result in the B Modelling.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.009/s_effe_calib.png)

*Calibration of the Van-Genuchten model.*

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The following loads shall be applied:

- The gravity with $g=9,81m/{s}^{2}$

The hydraulic pressure field is defined by the horizontal water line corresponding to the 10m rating.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

Calculations with the same configuration in the different modellings of this test case are done with the software Geostudio SLOPE/W version 2024.1.0. Thus, we have available the external source type reference results.

### 2.2 Baseline

The safety factor results from SLOPE/W are listed in Table 1.

**Table 1**: Baseline

| **Method** | **Modelling** | **FS** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Van-Genuchten model | A, C | 1.250 |
| Curve defined via `SATU_PRES` | B | 1.227 |

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The parameters of the Van-Genuchten model are defined in `DEFI_MATERIAU/THM_DIFFU`.

To validate the FS resolution with suction resistance evaluated by the water retention curve, the apses of the ends of the breaking surface are fixed at 5.35m and 31.7m, and the horizontal tangential line $y = 9m$ is imposed. Thus, only one surface will be generated and analyzed. The mesh being sufficiently fine, the refinement of the mesh is deactivated. The slippery mass is divided into 15 slices of soil. The simplified Bishop method is adopted to solve the FS.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

The safety factor obtained is tested. The results are shown in Table 1.

**Table 1**: Modelling Reference Values A.

| **NUME_RAFF** | **Identification** | **Aster result** | **Reference value** | **Error** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | FS | 1.2467709213754843 | 1.250 | 0.26% |

### 3.3 Summary of the results

The result of the FS from the CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command gives the difference 0.26%
This demonstrates the relevance of the Bishop method to suction resistance evaluated by the Van-Genuchten model.
