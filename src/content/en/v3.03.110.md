# v3.03.110 SSLS110 - Stability of a compressed square plate

**Summary:**

A homogeneous isotropic square elastic plate simply leaning on its four sides is subjected to a linear compression force acting on two sides.

Critical loads leading to the elastic blazing of the plate are calculated. The geometric rigidity matrix used in solving the problem to the specific values is that due to the initial stresses.

- linear elastic mechanics,
- a shell flashing,
- test value: calculation of the geometric rigidity matrix of elements DKT, DKTG and COQUE_3D,
- 6 modellings.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Due to the geometric and physical symmetry of the problem, only one quarter of the plate is modelled. Taking into account the symmetry conditions, only symmetrical flaming modes can be captured.

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.110/10003486000069BB000066BC48DB072974BC2319.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

$E=2.1E5\mathrm{Mpa}$ .

$\nu =0.3$

The transverse shear coefficient for the plate is ${A}_{\mathrm{CIS}}=5/6$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

| boundary conditions : | $\mathrm{P2P3}$ : |  | $\mathrm{DZ}=0.$ |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | $\mathrm{P3P4}$ : |  | $\mathrm{DZ}=0.$ |  |  |
| Symmetry | $\mathrm{P1P2}$ : | $\mathrm{DY}=0.$ |  | $\mathrm{DRX}=\mathrm{0 }.$ | $\mathrm{DRZ}=0.$ |
|  | $\mathrm{P4P1}$ : | $\mathrm{DX}=0.$ |  | $\mathrm{DRY}=\mathrm{0 }.$ | $\mathrm{DRZ}=0.$ |

Loading:

$q$ compression linear force on $\mathrm{P2P3}$

### 1.4 Remarks

It is not possible to solve the compression deformation problem without introducing the symmetry conditions. Indeed, imposing boundary conditions symmetry for a quarter of a plate is to eliminate the rigid body modes for the complete plate.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 reference solution

- Critical loads

The analytical solution obtained with a isotropic homogeneous linear elasticity thin plate theory [bib1] without consideration of transverse shear energy determines the

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.110/Object_3.svg)

rd critical load:

${q}_{\mathit{cr}I}=\frac{D{\pi}^{2}}{{L}^{2}}{(i+\frac{1}{i})}^{2}$

with:

$D=\frac{E{h}^{3}}{12(1-{\nu}^{2})}$: the coefficient of bending rigidity of the hull

$h$: thickness

$L$: the length on the side of the square plate.

- Membrane deformation

The analytical expression of membrane deformation along the $X$ axis is as follows:

> ${e}_{xx}=\frac{q\times L}{(h\times L\times E)}$

The reference result was calculated with $q=1.N/\mathit{mm}$

### 2.2 Baseline

Some modes corresponding to the critical loads of the analytical solution are not symmetrical and cannot be captured with symmetry conditions for a quarter of a plate. The values of the critical loads obtained correspond to the first 3 symmetrical flaming modes:

- Mode 1 of the plate quarter = Mode 1 of the whole plate
- Mode 2 of the plate quarter = Mode 3 of the whole plate
- Mode 3 of the plate quarter = Mode 5 of the whole plate

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

The exact solution for a plate theory without transverse shear.

- Modellings DKT and DKTG: except for mode 1 (mesh cells TRIA3), are less good than COQUE_3D Modelling but are nevertheless satisfactory for both types d的elements, QUAD4 and TRIA3.
