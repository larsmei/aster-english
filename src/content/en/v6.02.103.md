# v6.02.103 SSNL103 - Cantilever beam in large rotations subject to one moment

**Summary:**

Calculation of the static distortion of one clamped beam at one end and subjected to an bending moment at the other end.

The beam is modeled by 5 elements MECA_POU_D_T_GD.

The advantage is to test MECA_POU_D_T_GD beam element and the large displacement algorithm installed in STAT_NON_LINE.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.02.103/1000000000000296000000E5629E76DDC1FF13C0.png)

Straight beam $\mathrm{AB}$ , $l=1m$ long , clamped in $A$ and submitted to $B$ at one time $M$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

Elastic behaviour: $E=1.0\mathrm{Pa}$ . The Poisson's ratio does not intervene in pure bending.

Characteristics of the section:

$A=1.0{m}^{2}$ ${I}_{y}={I}_{z}=2.0{m}^{4}$

${I}_{x}=4.0{m}^{4}$ (not involved) ${A}_{y}={A}_{z}=4.$ (not involved)

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Clamped support in $A$ . We are looking for balance under the loading of the moment: $M=4\pi \mathrm{N.m}$ in $B$ .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The curvature of a large rotation beam subjected to bending moment $M$ is: $\frac{1}{R}=\frac{M}{\mathrm{EI}}$

Since the moment is constant along the beam, the deformed is circular and its radius has the value, taking into account the data: $R=\frac{l}{2\pi }$ , the deformed is a complete circle.

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The beam is modeled by 5 elements MECA_POU_D_T_GD linears pressed on mesh cells SEG2: which remain straight. The deformed is therefore a pentagon.

The deformed beam modeled is a FERMÉ PENTAGONE. But the nodes, in a deformed situation, are outside the reference circle because elements beam MECA_POU_D_T_GD retain their length but remain straight instead of turning into arcs of circle.
