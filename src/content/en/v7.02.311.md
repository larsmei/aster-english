# v7.02.311 HPLP311 - Murakami 11.17 Central fissure of a rectangular thin plate that prevents uniform heat flow in isotropic medium

**Summary:**

This is a static isotropic linear thermo-elastic calculation.

It is a basic $\mathrm{2D}$ plane test for steady-state thermal analysis loading calculated by elements finished on the same mesh with an isotropic material in $\mathrm{II}$ mode.

Objective:

- $\mathrm{2D}$ basic test plan, for steady-state thermal analysis loading calculated by elements finished on the same mesh, with isotropic material, in $\mathrm{II}$ mode,
- validation of the calculation of ${K}_{\mathrm{II}}$ ,
- $G$ variability as a function of the topology (sectors, crowns) of the radiating mesh. Verification of the invariance of the results in the mechanical of the fracture, at one crack end, compared to the mesh of the other end of the same crack.

The calculation is tested on a complete mesh and half-mesh. The $L/W$ and $\mathrm{2A}/W$ parameters are fixed.

A relative difference is measured on ${K}_{\mathrm{II}}$, but the accuracy is not well defined.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

Two models are considered:

- the $x\ge 0$ half model
- the complete model

**boundary conditions mechanical:**

- half model

$\mathrm{UX}=0$ along the $X=0$ symmetry axis

$\mathrm{UY}=0$ au point $(W/2,0)$

- Full model

$\mathrm{UX}=0$ au point $(0,L/2)$

$\mathrm{UY}=0$ at $(–L/2,0)$ and $(L/2,0)$ points

**boundary conditions thermal:**

- half model

$T=100°C$ on the top edge $Y=L/2$

$T=–100°C$ on the lower edge $Y=–L/2$

zero flow on the symmetry axis, on the free edge $X=W/2$ and on the crack edge

- Full model

$T=100°C$ on the top edge $Y=L/2$

$T=–100°C$ on the lower edge $Y=–L/2$

zero flow on free edges $X=\pm W/2$ and on crack edge

### 4.2 Remarks

In the reference, the author assumes that $\mathrm{KI}=0$ , but he does not verify it a posteriori.

With regard to the $G$ energy return rate, if we assume that $\mathrm{KI}=0$ , we draw the reference value from the IRWIN formula in flat constraints:

${G}_{\mathrm{ref}}=(1/E)\ast {\mathrm{KII}}^{2}$
