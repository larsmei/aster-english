# v1.01.295 ZZZZ295 – Validation of the position of the sub-points of 2D plates

**Summary:**

This test validates the calculation of the position of the integration subpoints in the global benchmark for COQUE_AXIS Modelling. An elementary mechanical calculation is performed to allow the creation of a table with CREA_TABLE from the result. Only the coordinates of a few subpoints are tested in the table.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.295/10000000000001B40000011251F9A74FDB364D04.png)

Plate with infinite depth (modelling in 2D):

Length: $L=5.0m$

Thickness: $e=0.5m$

Coordinates of $A$ and $B$ points

${X}_{A}=0.0;{Y}_{A}=0.0$

${X}_{B}=4.0;{Y}_{B}=3.0$

### 1.2 Material properties

Concrete:

Young's modulus: $E=3{10}^{10}\mathrm{Pa}$

Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.0$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On the point $A$ you block the movements according to $X$ , $Y$ and the rotation around $Z$ :

${U}_{x}^{A}=0.0;{U}_{y}^{A}=0.0;{\mathit{DR}}_{z}^{A}=0.0$

On the point $B$ a load is applied according to $Y$:

${F}_{Y}=-100.0N$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The position of the nodes, integration points and integration subpoints is calculated from its coordinates in the local axes of the plate and the matrix of passage between the local axes and the global axes.

$T(\alpha )=\left[\begin{array}{c}\cos(\alpha )\\ \sin(\alpha )\end{array}\begin{array}{c}-\sin(\alpha )\\ \cos(\alpha )\end{array}\right]$

For any point of initial coordinates $(X,Y)$ it is possible to calculate its coordinates expressed in the global $(X',Y')$ mark after rotation with the following transformation:

$\left[\begin{array}{c}X'\\ Y'\end{array}\right]=T(\alpha )\left[\begin{array}{c}X\\ Y\end{array}\right]$

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The positions of the integration subpoints in the global benchmark, knowing their positions expressed in the local axes, are calculated.

Here we have: $\cos(\alpha )=\frac{4}{5}$ and $\sin(\alpha )=\frac{3}{5}$

For mesh cell SEG4 hose length $L=5m$ , the distance of the integration points from the first node are (see R3.01.01):

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Mesh characteristics

Mesh: The mesh is composed of a mesh cell type **SEG3** with three nodes ($\mathit{N001}$ , $\mathit{N002}$ and $\mathit{N003}$ ).

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.295/100002000000019F00000110455557434F44FB5B.png)

For each mesh cell, there are 4 $(1,2,3,4)$ integration points

- The node group $\mathit{ENC}$ is composed of the node $\mathit{N001}$
- The node group $\mathit{CHA}$ is composed of the node $\mathit{N002}$

### 3.2 Modelling characteristics

Modelling:

MO=AFFE_MODELE(

> mesh=MA,
> AFFE=_F(TOUT='OUI', PHENOMENE='MECANIQUE',

> MODELISATION=' **COQUE_AXIS** ',),

)

boundary conditions:

BLOCAGE=AFFE_CHAR_MECA( MODELE=MO,

> DDL_IMPO=_F(GROUP_NO='ENC', DX=0.0, DY=0.0, DRZ=0.0,),

)

Mechanical loading:

CHARGE=AFFE_CHAR_MECA(MODELE=MO,

> FORCE_NODALE=_F(GROUP_NO='CHA',FX = 0,FY = -100),

)

Assigning elements characteristics:

COQUE=AFFE_CARA_ELEM(MODELE=MO,

> COQUE=_F(GROUP_MA=('COQUE'), EPAIS = 0.5, COQUE_NCOU = 4,),

)
