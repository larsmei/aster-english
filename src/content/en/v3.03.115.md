# v3.03.115 SSLS115 - Square composite plate under uniform pressure

**Summary:**

The case of a three-layer square plate, simply supported and subjected to uniform pressure, is treated. The skins consist of homogeneous orthotropic material, as well as the heart (same axis of orthotropy).The $E$ and $G$ modules of the heart are ten times weaker than those of the skins.

The displacement at the centre and the stresses at the lower and upper interfaces of the skin are calculated.

The test consists of eight modellings: for the first four, the results obtained are compared for triangular and quadrangular superfacial mesh cells in two different user markers. The last four modellings allow the sensitivity of the results to the orientation of triangular mesh cells in both user markers.

## 12 Summary of the results

The results obtained show that:

- At mesh identical (type of mesh cells overfacial and mesh cells orientation), the change in user mark does not affect constraints;
- Due to the orthotropy of the problem, there is a significant sensitivity to the orientation of triangular superfacial mesh cells (the accuracy of the calculations is increased from 1 to 11% for stresses and from 0.17 to 0.95% for displacements). This sensitivity does not disappear by refining the mesh. This point is therefore to be taken into account when comparing triangle/quadrangle performance.
