# u4.84.44 Operator POST_LIQUEFACTION

## 1 Purpose

This command makes it possible to compute the value of a criterion ${r}_{u}$ evaluating the liquefaction zones in a geotechnical structure, modelled by a saturated porous medium. Four formulae are available to compute this criterion:

- DP_SIGV_REF: the criterion is based on the values, at every material point, of the pore pressure at the time instant considered, of the pore pressure at the reference time instant and of the component of the effective-stress field at the reference time instant, in the direction of gravity, associated with the weight of the soil. It is accepted that when the value of the criterion exceeds 0.8, one deduces from it a risk of local liquefaction.
- DP_SIGM_REF: the criterion is computed from the values, at every material point, of the pore pressure at the time instant considered, of the pore pressure at the reference time instant and of the mean effective stress at the reference time instant.
- DP: it is a difference of pore pressure between a time instant $t$ and a reference time instant.
- P_SIGM: the criterion is computed from the pore pressure and the mean effective stress at the time instant $t$ .

This post-processing analysis is independent of the mechanical constitutive model of the porous medium considered.

As input, one gives:

- the result concept EVOL_NOLI of the simulation of the structure at the time instant considered;
- the axis along which gravity is applied;
- For the criteria DP_SIGV_REF/DP_SIGM_REF/DP:

      - a “reference” result concept EVOL_NOLI of the simulation of the structure;
      - the reference time instant.

As output, one obtains a result concept EVOL_NOLI which contains, in a field at the Gauss points of type 'ELGA_SIEF_R', the value of the criterion, in the variable SIPXX or SIPYY or SIPZZ according to the direction of gravity in the mesh.

```text
evol_noli = POST_LIQUEFACTION(
```

## 3 Description

The command POST_LIQUEFACTION makes it possible to compute the value of a criterion evaluating the liquefaction zones in a geotechnical structure, modelled by a saturated porous medium. Four formulae are available to compute this criterion:

- DP_SIGV_REF: the criterion is based on the values, at every material point, of the pore pressure ${p}_{h}$ at the time instant $t$ , of the pore pressure at the reference time instant ${t}_{\mathrm{réf}}$ , of the component of the effective-stress field ${\sigma}_{v}^{\mathrm{eff}}$ at the reference time instant, in the direction of gravity, associated with the weight of the soil. It is accepted that when the value of the criterion exceeds 0.8, one deduces from it a risk of local liquefaction: consult for example the document DGPR, «Risque sismique et sécurité des ouvrages hydrauliques», final version of October 2014. Its expression is the following, at the time instant $t$ and at the point $x$ :

$$
{r}_{u}(t,\mathrm{x})=\frac{{p}_{h}(t,\mathrm{x})–{p}_{h}({t}_{\mathit{réf}},\mathrm{x})}{{\sigma}_{v}^{\mathit{eff}}({t}_{\mathit{réf}},\mathrm{x})}
$$

- DP_SIGM_REF: the criterion is computed from the values, at every material point, of the pore pressure ${p}_{h}$ at the time instant $t$ , of the pore pressure at the reference time instant ${t}_{\mathit{réf}}$ and of the mean effective stress ${\sigma}_{m}^{\mathit{eff}}$ at the reference time instant. Its expression is the following, at the time instant $t$ and at the point $x$ :

$$
{r}_{u}(t,\mathrm{x})=\frac{{p}_{h}(t,\mathrm{x})–{p}_{h}({t}_{\mathit{réf}},\mathrm{x})}{{\sigma}_{m}^{\mathit{eff}}({t}_{\mathit{réf}},\mathrm{x})}
$$

with the mean stress defined by

$$
{\sigma}_{m}^{\mathit{eff}}({t}_{\mathit{réf}},\mathrm{x})=\frac{({\sigma}_{xx}^{\mathit{eff}}({t}_{\mathit{réf}},\mathrm{x})+{\sigma}_{yy}^{\mathit{eff}}({t}_{\mathit{réf}},\mathrm{x})+{\sigma}_{zz}^{\mathit{eff}}({t}_{\mathit{réf}},\mathrm{x}))}{3}
$$

- DP: it is a difference of pore pressure ${p}_{h}$ between a time instant $t$ and a reference time instant ${t}_{\mathit{réf}}$ . Its expression is the following, at the time instant $t$ and at the point $\mathrm{x}$ :

$$
{r}_{u}(t,\mathrm{x})={p}_{h}(t,\mathrm{x})–{p}_{h}({t}_{\mathit{réf}},\mathrm{x})
$$

- P_SIGM: the criterion is computed from the pore pressure ${p}_{h}$ and the mean effective stress at the time instant $t$ . Its expression is the following, at the time instant $t$ and at the point $x$ :

$$
{r}_{u}(t,\mathrm{x})=\frac{{p}_{h}(t,\mathrm{x})}{{\sigma}_{m}^{\mathit{eff}}(t,\mathrm{x})}
         avec la contrainte moyenne définie par
$$

$$
{\sigma}_{m}^{\mathit{eff}}(t,\mathrm{x})=\frac{({\sigma}_{xx}^{\mathit{eff}}(t,\mathrm{x})+{\sigma}_{yy}^{\mathit{eff}}(t,\mathrm{x})+{\sigma}_{zz}^{\mathit{eff}}(t,\mathrm{x}))}{3}
$$

This post-processing analysis is independent of the mechanical constitutive model of the porous medium considered. Let us make it clear that only one formula may be associated with the command. If the user wishes to compute several criteria, he or she must use the command several times. The test case zzzz403a [V1.01.403](doc:v1.01.403) illustrates an implementation of the command on a fictitious case, and the documentation [U2.04.09](doc:u2.04.09) presents the liquefaction analyses.

## 4 Keywords

### 4.1 Concept created by the command

This command creates as output a concept of type resultat EVOL_NOLI, from which one will extract the field 'SIEF_ELGA', then the component SIPXX, or SIPYY or SIPZZ according to the direction of gravity in the mesh (see §4.4).

### 4.2 Keyword CRITERE

> This keyword makes it possible to choose the formula for computing the criterion.

```text
♦    CRITERE    =    'DP_SIGV_REF'ou'DP_SIGM_REF'ou'DP'ou'P_SIGM'
```

### 4.3 Keywords RESU_REF and RESULTAT

```text
♦    RESULTAT    =    resu
```

This mandatory keyword makes it possible to supply the name of the concept of type resultat, for which the analysis of the structure has been carried out, from which one recovers the pore pressure at the time instant considered, in the form of a field 'SIEF_ELGA'.

```text
Pour les critères 'DP_SIGV_REF'/'DP_SIGM_REF'/'DP':
```

```text
♦    RESU_REF    =    resu_ref
```

```text
Cet opérande obligatoire pour les critères 'DP_SIGV_REF'/'DP_SIGM_REF'/'DP'permet de renseigner le nom du concept de type resultat, pour lequel le calcul de l'ouvrage a été mené, dans lequel on récupère la pression interstitielle à l'instant de référence, sous forme d'un champ 'SIEF_ELGA'.
```

**Note:**

*Only a result structure of type* *evol_noli can be supplied in these* *keywords.*

### 4.4 Keyword INST_REF

```text
Pour les critères 'DP_SIGV_REF'/'DP_SIGM_REF'/'DP'.
```

```text
♦    INST_REF = inst_ref
```

This keyword is mandatory for the criteria 'DP_SIGV_REF'/'DP_SIGM_REF'/'DP'. It makes it possible to supply the reference time instant at which the value of the component of the effective-stress field ${\sigma}_{v}^{\mathrm{eff}}$ and of the pore pressure ${p}_{h}$ is taken.

### 4.5 Keyword AXE

```text
♦    AXE = /'X'
/'Y'
/'Z'
```

This mandatory keyword makes it possible to supply the direction $x$, $y$ or $z$ of the mesh along which gravity acts.
