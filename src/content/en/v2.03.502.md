# v2.03.502 SDLS502 - Square plate "solid" simply supported

**Summary:**

This test represents a calculation in dynamic modal analysis of a thick square plate simply supported. This test allows to validate:

- modellings elements finished DST, DKT, COQUE_3D with mesh cells QUAD4 and TRIA3, QUAD8 and TRIA6, and 3D with mesh cells HEXA20,
- account taken of the transverse shear stiffness.

The frequencies and modes obtained are compared to an reference solution, proposed by NAFEMS, obtained with a finite elements volume-type calculation.

## 10 Summary of the results

Given the nature of the numerical solution (elements finite density), the results obtained are satisfactory for:

- Modelling A and B (DST) the difference maximum is less than $\text{4\%}$ for the first 5 modes,
- Modelling E and F (COQUE_3D), the difference maximum is about $\text{5\%}$ for the first 5 modes,
- Modelling G (3D), the difference maximum is about $\text{5\%}$ for the first 5 modes,
- Reference modes 5 and 6 out of plane show symmetry different from those encountered in modellings E, F and G, but are equivalent because they are modal recombinations.

Modellings C and D (DKT) are less satisfactory with relative differences reaching $\text{10\%}$ in the out-of-plane mode 7 due to the non-recognition of transverse shear for this relatively thick plate.

In addition, bending and membrane modes are observed for all of these modellings, including 3D G-density modelling. When the meshes are sufficiently refined, this trend is confirmed and relative differences are reduced. 3D calculations also showed that below mesh $6\times 6$ in the plane ($\mathrm{XY}$), bending and membrane modes were not detected.
