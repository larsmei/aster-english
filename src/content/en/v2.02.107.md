# v2.02.107 SDLL107 - Transitional calculation of a beam under random excitation

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to calculate the temporal response of a beam under random excitation of power spectral density (DSP) and whose displacements are limited in several points by obstacles:

- The beam is subjected to random stresses,
- The obstacle is characterized by normal shock stiffness and friction coefficient.

Several quantities characterizing behaviour are identified at the level of obstacles:

- Average displacement,
- RMS value of normal force,
- Average value of the tangent force,
- Wear power

This test is performed on a beam made of elements SEG2 and circular section.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Reference calculation

A NON_REGRESSION reference is used to test the different amounts calculated at the obstacle level.

The calculation procedure is as follows:

- Calculate the modal base,
- Creates the interspectral matrix (or DSP) from complex functions,
- Generates random efforts,
- Calculates the transient dynamic response,
- Test of response values (movements and effort) at obstacle level.

### 2.2 Reference quantity

Components of the interspectral matrix obtained from complex functions.

| quantity | Component | Remarks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DEPL_X | MOYEN | Average displacement value following $X$ , at the impact point, in their local reference point, |
| DEPL_Y | ECART_TYPE | The difference-type value of the following $Y$ displacement at the impact point in their local reference point, |
| DEPL_RADIAL | RMS | RMS value on the impact time of the "radial displacement" to the impact point. |
| DEPL_ANGULAIRE | MAXI | Maximum value of "angular displacement" to the impact point. |
| FORCE_NORMALE | RMS_T_TOTAL | RMS value over the total time of the normal force at the point impact. |
| FORCE_TANG_1 | MOYEN | Mean value of the tangent force in the obstacle plane. |
| FORCE_TANG_2 | ECART_TYPE | The difference-type value of orthogonal tangent force at obstacle plane. |
| STAT_CHOC | T_CHOC_MOYEN | Average shock time |
| STAT_USURE | PUIS_USURE | Wear power calculated according to ARCHARD. |
