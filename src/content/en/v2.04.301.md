# v2.04.301 SDLV301 – Cyclical substructure: pump wheel

**Summary:**

The scope of this test is modal analysis of cyclic repeatability structures. The structure studied is a pump wheel and is modelled by elements hexahedral volumes at 20 nodes (3D Modelling).

This is an industrial test for which there is no analytical solution. The calculation method is the dynamic cyclic substructure of Craig-Bampton.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.04.301/10000000000004F700000342FFE76F787454ED2A.png)

Figure 1.1-1: Géométrie du problème

Figure 1.1 shows an overview of the structure: it has 21 sectors. The hub diameter is $60\mathit{mm}$ , the outer diameter is $480\mathit{mm}$ .

![Figure](/figures/v2.04.301/10000000000004EE0000030E2D066CC0E6327FC6.png)

Figure 1.1-2: Vue détaillée de la géométrie du problème

Figure 1.2 shows a detailed view of 8 sectors. The represented axis system is the structural $XYZ$ system. Each point can be identified in this system or, in an equivalent way, in a cylindrical axis system. The revolution axis of the cylindrical marker coincides with the $Z$ axis and the azimutal $q$ coordinate has its origin in the $XZ$ plane.

The right and left boundaries of the sector are defined by the points which have an angular difference in the cylindrical marker equal to the opening angle of the sector, $1/{21}^{\mathit{ème}}$ in $360°$. The points of the two borders go in pairs: the point of minimum $q$ angle belongs to the right border, the $q$ of maximum $q$ angle belongs to the left border.

## 5 Summary of the results

The A Modelling of this industrial test allows the MODE_ITER_CYCL command to be validated for a Craig Bampton interface on a structure of which $1/{21}^{\mathit{ème}}$ is represented by 160 elements. The results, compared to another calculation software, are satisfactory.

The Modelling B allows the MODE_ITER_CYCL command to be validated with stiffness centrifugal. The results obtained are satisfactory.
