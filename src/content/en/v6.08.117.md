# v6.08.117 SSND117 – Validation of DIS_ECRO_TRAC behavior

**Summary:**

The response of the nonlinear DIS_ECRO_TRAC behavior model, formulated on discrete elements for mesh cells SEG2 or POI1, is tested.

The STAT_NON_LINE operator is used for validation. Discrete d的elements response is analyzed for nonlinear constitutive law under a cyclic load.

The modellings and elements discreet tested are in $\mathrm{3D}$ with modellings DIS_T and DIS_TR on mesh cells POI1 and SEG2.

This behavior is also validated for dynamic stresses in test case SDND124 [V5.01.124](doc:v5.01.124) with DYNA_VIBRA and DYNA_NON_LINE operators.

### 1.4 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.4.1 Modelling A and E

When the discreet is a SEG2, one of the nodes is blocked, on the other a displacement condition is imposed. When the discreet is a POI1 the displacement condition is imposed on this node.

The condition on the move is a function of time:

${U}_{0}.\sin(2\pi .f.t)$ with $f=1\mathit{Hz}$ and ${U}_{0}=2.0\mathit{mm}$

This displacement is applied to the axial direction for Modelling A and to both directions (axial and tangential) for Modelling E.

#### 1.4.2 modellings B et C

When the discreet is a SEG2, one of the nodes is blocked, on the other a displacement condition is imposed. When the discreet is a POI1 the displacement condition is imposed on this node.

The conditions on the move are time functions:

$\mathit{Depl}={U}_{1.}\sin(2\pi .{f}_{1}.t)+{U}_{2.}\sin(2\pi .{f}_{2}.t)+{U}_{3.}\sin(2\pi .{f}_{3}.t)$

Following direction $Z$: $(u,f)=[(-\mathrm{0.20,0}.90),(\mathrm{0.15,2}.00),(-\mathrm{0.10,2}.80)]$

#### 1.4.3 Modelling D

Modelling D combines A and B.

When the discreet is a SEG2, one of the nodes is blocked, on the other a displacement condition is imposed. When the discreet is a POI1 the displacement condition is imposed on this node.
