# v7.22.126 HSNV126 - Thermo-metal-mechanical single-tension

**Summary:**

This test illustrates a mechanical calculation with non-linear nutring on a 16MND5 steel material undergoing metallurgical transformations.

In concrete terms, the CALC_META operator initially calculates the metallurgical evolution associated with a given thermal history. This metallurgical evolution is then provided to STAT_NON_LINE which will perform a mechanical calculation by taking into account the metallurgical phases (in addition to mechanical loadings). The mechanical calculation material is defined with ELAS_META_FO and META_TRACTION.
