## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

We're laying:

${k}_{\mathrm{An}}=\frac{{\mathrm{EI}}_{\mathrm{An}}}{{l}_{\mathrm{An}}}$

with $n=B,C,D$ or $E$

$\begin{array}{}K={k}_{\mathrm{AB}}+{k}_{\mathrm{AD}}+{k}_{\mathrm{AE}}+\frac{3}{4}{k}_{\mathrm{AC}}\\ {r}_{\mathrm{An}}=\frac{{k}_{\mathrm{An}}}{K}\end{array}$

with $n=B,C,D$ or $E$

${C}_{1}=+\frac{{\mathrm{Fl}}_{\mathrm{AD}}}{8}-\frac{{\mathrm{pl}}_{\mathrm{AB}}^{2}}{12}$

- $A$ rotation:

$\theta =\frac{{C}_{1}}{\mathrm{4K}}$

- $A$ Time:

$\begin{array}{}{M}_{\mathrm{AB}}=+\frac{{\mathrm{pl}}_{\mathrm{AB}}^{2}}{12}+{r}_{\mathrm{AB}}.{C}_{1}\\ {M}_{\mathrm{AD}}=-\frac{{\mathrm{Fl}}_{\mathrm{AD}}}{8}+{r}_{\mathrm{AD}}.{C}_{1}\\ {M}_{\mathrm{AE}}={r}_{\mathrm{AE}}.{C}_{1}\\ {M}_{\mathrm{AC}}={r}_{\mathrm{AC}}.{C}_{1}\end{array}$
