# v3.01.105 SSLL105 - Elastic flame of a L-shaped structure

**Summary:**

A $L$ structure consisting of two slender beams thin rectangular cross section is subjected to one end force, and is clamped at the other end. The critical loads of elastic flaming associated with positive and negative force values are sought. The field of testing is:

- Linear elastic mechanics,
- Flaming of beams and shells,
- The first 3 modellings are relative to (POU_D_E, POU_D_T, POU_D_TG).
- The 4th (POU_D_E) and 5th(POU_D_TGM) modellings test the criterion of flaming in the nonlinear static operator.
- The 5th (COQUE_3D: QUAD9) and 6th (COQUE_3D: TRIA7) modellings test the criterion of flaming in the nonlinear static operator.
- The 7th (DKT: QUAD4) and 8th (DKT: TRIA3) modellings test the criterion of flaming in the nonlinear static operator.

## 12 Summary of the results

**modellings beam:**

The results of the 5 modellings beams are very close to the reference solution which is an average of 8 code results. A small effect is observed due to the spin since the results of the modellings C and E (POU_D_TG/POU_D_TGM) are slightly different from the others, while remaining less than $\text{2\%}$ from the reference.

Although the last two modellings (D and E) implement the nonlinear CRIT_STAB flaming criterion in the nonlinear static operator, they do not use the same approach.

In Modelling D, it is the criterion d-Euler which is used because one is in small displacements (DEFORMATION=--PETIT) and the tangent matrix contains only the matrix of material rigidity.

In Modelling E, one has placed itself in large shifts (DEFORMATION=的REAC_GEOM, although this is not necessary to address this problem), the tangent matrix includes both material and geometric rigidities, thus illustrating the generalised nonlinear criterion in which the singularity of the tangent matrix is tested. Thus, the critical load is no longer predicted but detected (observing the change in sign of the smallest value of the tangent matrix, confir [R7.05.01](doc:r7.05.01)).

**modellings shell:**

In these modellings, it is the criterion d-Euler that is used because one is in small shifts (DEFORMATION=--PETIT) and the tangent matrix contains only the matrix of material rigidity.

The results of the modellings DKT (QUAD4, TRIA3) and COQUE_3D (QUAD9, TRIA7) are far removed from the reference solution, with a maximum difference of 15.5% for the first flashing mode. However, the results are satisfactory for the second mode, with an difference of 1.2%.
