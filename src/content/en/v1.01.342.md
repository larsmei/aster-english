# v1.01.342 ZZZZ342 - ELAS_HYPER validation in small disturbances

**Summary:**

This test validates the constitutive law ELAS_HYPER:

- relative to a linear elastic calculation (Hooke law) in the case of small disturbances (HPP);
- by checking, in compressible form, the equivalence of the formulation according to whether the Poisson's ratio $\nu$ or the $K$ compressibility module is provided;
- comparing the total (default) and incremental (formulation imposed by the presence of an initial state) formulation of the constitutive law.

A $1\mathit{mm}$ side cube is clamped at its base and is subjected to a uniform pressure load equal to $1\mathit{Mpa}$ at the other end. The material is Neo-Hookean ($\mathit{C10}=300\mathit{Mpa}$ , $\nu =0,25$ ).

The reference solution is of AUTRE_ASTER type and is obtained by a linear elastic calculation with the law of Hooke for an equivalent Young's modulus in the hypothesis of small disturbances ($E=1500\mathit{Mpa}$).

The results obtained in both displacement and stress are in very good agreement with the reference.

This test consists of three modellings:

- Modelling A: D_PLAN
- Modelling B: C_PLAN
- Modelling C: 3D

Modellings A and C are also the subject of GREEN_LAGRANGE kinematic calculations.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
