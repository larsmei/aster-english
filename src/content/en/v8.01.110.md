# v8.01.110 FDLV110 - Calculation of mass added on substructured modes

**Summary:**

This modal analysis and fluid-structure interaction test implements the mass calculation added on a structure consisting of three concentric cylinders separated by two fluid rings (water) which are assumed to be the behaviour governed by the potential theory (perfect fluid, incompressible at rest). The model is three-dimensional for water. The structure is represented by elements of the thin hull type in model A (the structure is rigid in the reference solution). This is characterized by two eigenmodes evaluated by dynamic substructure, with interface of type CRAIG-BAMPTON.

The value of the test lies in the use of the ‘NOEUD_DOUBLE' operator's ‘CALC_MATR_AJOU' feature. This feature allows the calculation of the added mass effects of a structure represented by an over-facial mesh (without thickness) that is bathed in a fluid. The fluids chosen in this test case are of different densities on either side of the intermediate cylinder (water at different temperatures).

## 4 Summary of the results

The mass calculation added on modes estimated by substructure is satisfactory. This allowed the ‘NOEUD_DOUBLE' option of the ‘CALC_MATR_AJOU' command to be validated. the difference observed on the second added mass coefficient is explained by the discretization of the second cylinder. The number d的elements is a little insufficient to accurately calculate the integral pressure field on the structure.
