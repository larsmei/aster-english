# v7.32.105 WTNP105 - Dispersal of dissolved air in water (plane HH2M)

**Summary:**

This is considered a problem at constant temperature and saturation. Appropriate boundary conditionss impose constant water pressure and vapour pressure. A gas pressure is imposed on one edge of the domain (zero flows on the other side). Only the pressures of dry air and dissolved air connected by Henry's law evolve. This problem is reduced to an equation for dry air pressure of the type "heat equation". The reference solution will then be a thermal calculation ASTER. It is exactly the same test case as the WTNP103 but in HH2M Modelling.
