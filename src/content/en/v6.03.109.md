# v6.03.109 SSNP109 - Prestress cable excreted in a concrete straight beam

**Summary**

A rectangular straight beam is considered to be concrete, crossed along its length by a steel prestress cable. The cable is straight, parallel to the average fibre of the beam, and passes halfway up the section of the beam, being out of the middle plane. The left section of the beam and the left end of the cable are fixed. The cable is pulled to its right end, in order to prestress the bending-compression beam. The tension losses along the cable are neglected.

The purpose of this test case is to validate the equilibrium state calculation method of a prestressed concrete structure compared to an analytical reference solution.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The concrete beam is straight, rectangular section.

Its dimensions are: $L\times h\times p=10m\times 0,4m\times 0,2m$ ($y=h/2$ ).

The cable crosses the beam parallel to the average beam fiber, at mid height. Its eccentricity compared to the average plane is $e=0,05m$ ($z=e$).

The right section of the cable is ${S}_{a}=1,5{.10}^{-4}{m}^{2}$.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.109/Object_1.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

| Concrete material constituting the beam: | Young's modulus ${E}_{b}={3.10}^{10}\mathit{Pa}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Steel material constituting the cable: | Young's modulus ${E}_{a}=2,1{.10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$ |

The Poisson's ratio is taken equal to 0 for both materials. So the Poisson effects are cancelled in the $y$ and $z$ directions. The displacements only have components in the $(x,z)$ plane.

The loss of tension in the cable is neglected, and the various parameters used for their estimation are set at 0.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The point $A$ located at the bottom of the left edge of the beam, of $(\mathrm{0 };–h/\mathrm{2 };0)$ coordinates, is blocked in translation in all three directions and in rotation around the $y$ axis.

The degree of freedom rotational $\mathit{DRY}$ lock involves a zero slope of the average fiber deformity to $x=0$.

The left end of the cable, $(\mathrm{0 };\mathrm{0 };e)$ coordinates, is blocked in translation in all three directions.

At the right end of the cable, $(L;\mathrm{0 };e)$ coordinates, a normal $({F}_{\mathrm{0 }};\mathrm{0 };0)$ traction force is applied where ${F}_{0}={2.10}^{5}N$.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The figure below gives a simplified representation of the mesh of the beam.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.109/10001BF4000069D500000D55AD49EC4615D37479.svg)

The concrete beam is represented by 20 elements of DKT type, supported by as many mesh cells quadrangles at 4 nodes.

$p=0,2m$ thickness is affected, as well as concrete material for which the ELAS (Young's modulus ${E}_{b}={3.10}^{10}\mathit{Pa}$) and BPEL_BETON behaviors are defined: the characteristic parameters of this relationship are set to 0 because the voltage losses along the prestress cable are neglected.

Degrees of freedom $\mathit{DX}$ , $\mathit{DY}$ , $\mathit{DZ}$ and $\mathit{DRY}$ of node $\mathit{NB001001}$ are blocked.

The cable is represented by 20 elements MECA_BARRE, supported by as many mesh cells segments as 2 nodes. The left and right ends are respectively nodes $\mathit{NC001001}$ and $\mathit{NC001021}$.

A straight section area ${S}_{a}=1,5{.10}^{-4}{m}^{2}$ is assigned to elements, as well as a steel material for which the ELAS (Young's modulus ${E}_{a}=2,1{.10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$) and BPEL_ACIER behaviors are defined: the characteristic parameters of this relationship are set to 0 (loss of neglected voltage), with the exception of the elastic limit stress for which the ${f}_{\mathit{prg}}=1,77{.10}^{9}\mathit{Pa}$ value is chosen.

Degrees of freedom $\mathit{DX}$ , $\mathit{DY}$ , and $\mathit{DZ}$ of node $\mathit{NC001001}$ are blocked.

The ${F}_{0}={2.10}^{5}N$ voltage is applied to node $\mathit{NC001021}$ . This voltage value is consistent with the section and elastic limit values for a prestressed toron cable.

The equilibrium state of the beam and cable assembly is calculated in one step, the behaviour being elastic. Two complementary calculations are then performed to determine the lower and upper skin stresses ($z=\pm p/2$) of the beam.

### 3.2 Calculation steps and tested features

The main steps in calculation correspond to the features that one wishes to validate:

- DEFI_MATERIAU operator: definition of BPEL_BETON and BPEL_ACIER behavior relationships, in the particular case where voltage losses along the prestress cable are neglected (default values of parameters);
- DEFI_CABLE_BP operator: determination of a constant voltage profile along the prestress cable, with losses neglected; calculation of the coefficients of kinematic relationships between degrees of freedom of the nodes of the cable and degrees of freedom of the nodes "neighbors" of the concrete beam, in the case of an eccentric cable;
- AFFE_CHAR_MECA operator: definition of a RELA_CINE_BP load;
- STAT_NON_LINE operator, option COMPORTEMENT: calculation of the equilibrium state taking into account the RELA_CINE_BP type loading.

CALC_CHAMP option SIGM_ELNO is used to calculate the lower skin and higher skin stresses of the beam.

### 3.4 Remarks

The calculated values correspond to those theoretically expected. A bending-compression state is obtained for the concrete beam.

## 4 Executive summary

The results obtained are validated by comparison with a reference analytical solution with very good accuracy.

The specific features tested are:

- DEFI_MATERIAU operator: definition of the characteristic parameters of steel and concrete materials for the calculation of voltage along the prestress cable, according to BPEL rules;
- DEFI_CABLE_BP operator: calculation of the voltage along the cable and the coefficients of kinematic relationships between degrees of freedom of the nodes cable and degrees of freedom of the nodes "neighbors" of the concrete beam;
- AFFE_CHAR_MECA operator: definition of a RELA_CINE_BP load;
- STAT_NON_LINE operator, option COMPORTEMENT: calculation of the equilibrium state taking into account the RELA_CINE_BP type loading.
