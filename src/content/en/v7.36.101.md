# v7.36.101 WSNP101– Modelling drying of a concrete sample with model HYDR_TABBAL

**Summary:**

This is a 2D model of drying a concrete sample with the THM unsaturated module and using the ‘HYDR_TABBAL' model developed as part of the work of [1]. This test is a lightened version of the test case wsnp100, made on a single time step. It is here a test of non-regression.

The calculation is made for a series of relative humiditys ranging from 80% to 35% with an analytical solution. The Modelling of this document shows a calculation of non-regression only for relative humidity (HR = 0.805).

## 1 Reference problem

The geometry of the problem, the assumptions of calculation, the boundary conditions and the analytical calculation method of the reference solution are detailed in the doc ‘V7.36.100'. The following are the characteristics and results of the non-regression Modelling.

## 2 Modelling

The objective of this Modelling is to model a calculation of non-regression for a single relative humidity (HR = 0.805).

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics

- Modelling in D_PLAN_THH2MS and D_PLAN_THH2MD.
- 700 elements $\mathit{QUAD}8$ .
- Time Discretization: 1 time step for a total calculation time of 3600s.

### 2.2 Modelling result

The stress field ‘SIEF_NOEU' is calculated to recover the actual stress according to direction X (‘SIXX') on a node (here the node N2) and for the final moment.

The values of ‘SIXX' are compared with the analytical value $-d{\sigma}_{p}$ for HR=0.805 in section 2.2 and are shown in the table below.

| HR tested | $-d{\sigma}_{p}$ (Mpa) (analytic) | SIXX (MPa) | $(\text{\%})$ offset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.805 | -7.950 | -7.258 | 8.7 |
