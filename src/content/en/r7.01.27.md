# r7.01.27 Constitutive law BETON_REGLE_PR

**Summary:**

This documentation presents the law BETON_REGLE_PR implemented by the company Sixense-NECS within the framework of the free code_aster community. It is a regulatory law [bib1], written by combining two 1D laws.

## 1 Presentation of the law BETON_REGLE_PR

The constitutive law is of the “twice 1D” type in the principal strain frame (coincident with the principal stress frame). Only a 1D behaviour is described, which is of non-linear elastic type.

In compression, it is the parabola-rectangle law defined by the regulations [bib1]; the stress is given by the following relations:

$$
\lbrace \begin{array}{c}\sigma ={\sigma}_{y}^{c}\left[1-{\left(1-\frac{\epsilon}{{\epsilon}_{0}}\right)}^{n}\right]\text{si}0<\epsilon \le {\epsilon}_{0}\text{(partie parabole)}\\ \sigma ={\sigma}_{y}^{c}\text{si}\epsilon >{\epsilon}_{0}\text{(partie rectangle)}\end{array}
$$

In tension the law is of triangular type [bib2]:

$$
\sigma =E\epsilon \text{si}\epsilon \le \frac{{\sigma}_{y}^{t}}{E}\text{avec}\sigma ={\sigma}_{y}^{t}+{E}_{T}\left[\epsilon -\frac{{\sigma}_{y}^{t}}{E}\right]
$$

The associated material parameters are as follows:

$E$ : Young's modulus;

${\sigma}_{y}^{t}$ : the peak stress in tension (${f}_{t}$ );

${E}_{T}$ : the tangent modulus (generally between $\frac{-E}{3}$ and $\frac{-E}{10}$ );

${\sigma}_{y}^{c}$ : the maximum stress in compression (${f}_{c}$ );

$n$ : the exponent of the hardening law in compression;

${\varepsilon}_{c}$ : the strain at which ${\sigma}_{y}^{c}$ is reached.

The stress-strain response is given in the following figure:

So that the slopes at the origin in tension and in compression are identical, the parameters can be chosen in such a way that:

$$
\frac{\partial \sigma }{\partial \epsilon }(\epsilon =0)=E={f}_{c}\frac{n}{{\epsilon}_{c}}
$$

## 2 Description of the internal variables

There is no internal variable for this model.

## 3 Elementary stiffness matrices

The tangent and secant stiffness matrices are available for this law. By default in code_aster, the tangent matrix is used. To use the secant matrix, or unloading matrix, the keyword PAS_MINI_ELAS must be added with a value greater than the interval between two analysis time instants, in the factor keyword NEWTON of the non-linear operators.

The secant matrix is computed from the secant modulus in each principal direction $i$ :

- ${E}_{i}^{s}={\sigma}_{i}/{\varepsilon}_{i}$ if ${\epsilon}_{i}\ne 0$
- ${E}_{i}^{s}=E$ if ${\epsilon}_{i}=0$

## 4 Limitations of the law

The law BETON_REGLE_PR has two important limitations:

- the model is elastic: unloading is therefore not taken into account in the formulation of the law. The loading and unloading paths are therefore identical. It is therefore not recommended to use this law for non-monotonic, cyclic or dynamic loading cases.
- the model does not represent the Poisson effect: the law is of the “twice 1D” type, in which the behaviour in the two principal directions of strain is treated independently.

## 5 Functionality and validation

### 5.1 Functionality

The law is available only in 2D and is accessible with the modellings D_PLAN, C_PLAN and DKT.

### 5.2 Verification & Validation

The law BETON_REGLE_PR is verified by the following test cases:

| SSNP129 | [V6.03.129](doc:v6.03.129) | Validation of the law BETON_REGLE_PR on a DKT element |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SSNS114 | [V6.05.114](doc:v6.05.114) | Degradation of a reinforced-concrete plate under various loadings with the law BETON_REGLE_PR |

The validation of the law was also carried out by comparison with the experimental results obtained on a reinforced-concrete slab subjected to its self-weight and to a pressure at the centre of the slab [bib1].
