### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The left side ($\mathit{OC}$ ) is clamped ($\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=0$ ).

In addition, the gravity applies (${g}_{y}=9810{\mathit{mm.s}}^{-2}$) and a compression effort is imposed in the $-x$ direction on the right side $\mathit{AB}$. The application of the pilot is done by controlling the displacement of the node $A$:

- a maximum $–1\mathit{mm}$ displacement following $y$ between each increment when imposed on the gravity
- a $50\mathit{mm}$ displacement following $x$ and $y$ between each increment when compression is imposed.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

To validate the LONG_ARC control, the solution obtained is compared with the reference solution resulting from the application of the same load i.e. (i) the gravity and then (ii) the unmanned compression. The second phase is also tested by combining the pilotage and linear search.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

To compare solutions, it is checked that:

- the difference between the 2 displacement field solutions gives a potential zero energy
- the difference between 2 consecutive moments in the phase where the gravity is imposed leads well to a node $A$ displacement following $y$ from $1\mathit{mm}$.

The solutions obtained with or without steering correspond perfectly. It is also possible to involve in addition linear research, even if in the present case this does not improve the convergence speed.

## 4 Summary of the results

This test allows the validation of the d-arc-length control for a 2D structure in flat constraints. It is also checked whether the steering can be combined with linear search even if, in the present case, convergence is not gained in speed.
