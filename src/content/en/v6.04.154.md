# v6.04.154 SSNV154 - Triaxial drained test with CJS (level 3)

**Summary**

This test validates the CJS model's level 3. This is a triaxial test in drained condition. The calculations are performed only on the solid part of the soil without hydromechanical coupling. The containment level is $400\mathit{KPa}$ .

Because of symmetry, one only cares about the eighth of a sample tested in a triaxial test. Two modellings are presented. Modelling A is axisymmetric. In Modelling B, $\mathrm{3D}$ , the tested sample is turned from an angle of $-\pi /6$ to the $x$ axis. Therefore, the $x,y,z$ directions are no longer the main directions. This allows validation of the numerical integration operations of the model that act on the non-diagonal terms of the strain and stress tensors.

This is a non-regression test. Nevertheless, the results obtained with *Code_Aster* for the CJS3 model are compared with those obtained with a private version of the FLAC-2D software.
