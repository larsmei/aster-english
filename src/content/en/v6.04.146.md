# v6.04.146 SSNV146 - Regularized limit analysis. Torispheric tank

**Summary**

This test enables operators to qualify for regularised limit analysis.

The limit load is calculated by a kinematic approach regulated by the Norton-Hoff-Friaâ method. Compared to the A index of this document, it should be noted that the calculation method has been modified in the Code_Aster which no longer uses Norton-Hoff material but uses the more general resolution with incompressible elements.

The Reference problem is the result of a European benchmark carried out in the framework of a Brite EuRam BE97-4547 project "LISA" in 1998.

A axisymmetric tank with a torispheric background is considered. The constituent material checks the vonMises criterion and the structure is subjected to internal pressure.

The structure is modelled by incompressible elements.

The resolution by the regularised method of Norton-Hoff-Friaâ is performed in the STAT_NON_LINE command. An post-processing in the POST_ELEM command allows the upper and lower terminal estimates of the limit load to be obtained.

The reference solution is digital and the results are in perfect agreement with the reference values. Some implementation details of this test case are presented in the [u2.05.04](doc:u2.05.04) Help Use document.

## 1 Reference problem

The test is the test case LA6 of a benchmark of the European project Brite EuRam BE97-4547 "LISA" studied by three bodies:

- EDF (former Norton-Hoff method with three values of the regularization parameter: Norton-Hoff $n$ coefficient);
- LTAS in Liège (the regularised kinematic method of the university software dedicated " **ELSA**";
- ForschungZentrum by Jülich (static method approached by elements finished on displacement, reduced representation of auto-contraint fields and algorithm of optimization of code **Permas**).

An axisymmetric tank with a torispheric background subject to internal pressure is considered.

The same mesh is used by the three participating organisms. It contains two elements Q8 in thickness, and in all there are 34 elements, and 141 nodes. The constituent material is homogeneous and checks the von Mises criterion with the ${\sigma}_{y}$ yield limit (${\sigma}_{y}$) for the threshold.

EDF LISA results: mesh initial and deformed Norton-Hoff coefficient *n* =31

The calculation of the limit load by the Norton-Hoff-Friaâ method is detailed in reference document [r7.07.01](doc:r7.07.01).

Let us briefly remember that the aim is to look for the ${\lambda}_{\lim}$ limit load, for which the structure, consisting of a material of the perfect elastoplastic type at the threshold of von Mises, can support ${\lambda}_{\lim}F$ surface loads and ${\lambda}_{\lim}f$ volume loads to which one can possibly add constant loads (${F}_{0}$ and ${f}_{0}$). Code_aster allows two parameters to be calculated for each moment of the calculation, i.e. for increasingly low regularizations:

- An estimate of an upper limit of the load:

$$
{\widehat{\lambda}}_{m}=\underset{\Omega}{\int}{\sigma}_{y}\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}\epsilon ({u}_{m})\cdot \epsilon ({u}_{m})}d\Omega -{L}_{0}({u}_{m})
$$

- and, in the absence of constant loading, an estimate of a lower terminal ${\underline{\lambda}}_{m}$:

$$
{\underline{\lambda}}_{m}=\underset{\Omega}{\int}\frac{A(m)}{m}\cdot {(\sqrt{\varepsilon ({u}_{m})\cdot \varepsilon ({u}_{m})})}^{m}d\Omega \cdot {(\underset{x\in \Omega }{\text{Sup}}(\sqrt{\frac{\frac{3}{2}{\sigma}^{D}({u}_{m})\cdot {\sigma}^{D}({u}_{m})}{{\sigma}_{y}}}))}^{-1}\le {\stackrel{ˆ}{\lambda}}_{m}
$$

If a constant load is present, the constant load power is provided in the speed field solution of the problem.

The stress deviator checks the behavioral relationship of Norton-Hoff:

$$
{\sigma}^{D}(u)=A(m).{(\sqrt{{\epsilon}^{D}(u).{\epsilon}^{D}(u)})}^{m-2}.{\epsilon}^{D}(u)
$$

with $\text{tr}\varepsilon (u)=0$, and $A(m)={k}^{1-m}{\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)}^{m/2}{{\sigma}_{y}}^{m}$.

## 2 Modelling A

The moment list is used to control the Norton-Hoff regularization method via an $t$ coefficient ($m=1+{10}^{1-t}$ ), not the loading evolution as in an ordinary calculation.

To perform a calculation in *code_aster* in limit analysis with the Norton-Hoff-Friaâ regularization method with the vonMises resistance criterion, it is necessary to:

- define the 2D (plane or axis) or 3D model with the elements near-incompressible finished, modellings 3D_INCO_UPG, D_PLAN_INCO_UPG, or AXIS_INCO_UPG;
- ensure the incompressibility condition: GONF=0 in AFFE_CHAR_MECA;
- define only the material characteristic ${s}_{y}$ , the limit load being independent of $E$ and $\nu$ ;
- define the permanent load and the one that is set by $\lambda$;
- define the time-based discretization (in practice between ${t}_{min}=1$ and ${t}_{max}=2$ to $5$);
- perform a nonlinear calculation with the NORTON_HOFF behavioral relationship with the STAT_NON_LINE [u4.51.03](doc:u4.51.03) command, and the ANA_LIM control. Linear search can be used in practice to improve convergence, and the time step subdivision;
- post-process calculation to obtain the limit load with POST_ELEM [u4.81.22](doc:u4.81.22) command.

## 3 Summary of the results

The mesh is the one used for the benchmark "LISA". The calculation was conducted up to the instant $t=2s$ . The regularised kinematic methods of EDF and the University of Liège give very similar results (difference less than 1% at $t=2s$ ). However, the lower terminal provided by FZJ is more important than the previous upper terminals.

**Note:** In this test case, the exception is the error ‘ConvergenceError'. Indeed, when the value of $m$ approaches 1, the law of Norton-Hoff tends towards the law of von Mises. Knowing that the analysis of the boundary charge with the law of von Mises is a singular problem that will cause non-convergence. Therefore, in case the calculation no longer converges with the growth of the instant $t$ (and therefore when $m$ tends to 1), the use of the exception allows to recover the values of $\lambda$ calculated before the convergence difficulty.
