# v2.02.023 SDLL23 - clamped-free earthquake beam (spectral response)

**Summary**

This unidirectional problem consists of performing a spectral seismic analysis of a clamped-free beam, equipped with two localized masses, subjected to a three-dimensional excitation provided as a pseudoacceleration oscillator spectrum.

Through this problem, COMB_SISM_MODAL operator's DPC, SRSS, CQC and DSC modal combinations are tested. The SRSS combination is tested with consideration of neglected modes.

In addition, CALC_MODES, NORM_MODE, MODE_STATIQUE, DEFI_FONCTION and DEFI_NAPPE operators are tested.

The results obtained for Modelling A, relating to Euler beams, are in agreement with the results of other codes, for cases without taking into account the pseudomode. The differences with the reference results in the case where the pseudomode is taken into account are explained by a different calculation hypothesis for the determination of this pseudomode.

The Modelling B, in Euler beams, is relative to a more refined mesh than that of Modelling A.

Model C has Timoshenko beams and is relative to an mesh identical to model B; the results show significant differences with Euler beam modelling and are highly sensitive to shear coefficient.

## 6 Summary of the results

Modelling A:

Case without pseudo-mode: Perfect agreement of results *Code* _*Aster* with validation sheet which indicates a tolerance of $\text{2\%}$ on reference values.

Case with pseudomode taken into account: significant differences with the validation sheet, up to $\text{160\%}$; they are explained by a different calculation hypothesis (in the reference, the pseudomode is calculated from the SRO value corresponding to the spectrum asymptote, while in *Code_Aster* the pseudomode is calculated from the SRO value corresponding to the last frequency of the modal base considered). Note that the values obtained by *Code_Aster* are conservative in relation to a non-truncated modal base calculation prior to the cut-off frequency.

Note that modellings B and C containing 21 elements resulted in a positive mode 4 participation factor, whereas it was negative with A Modelling.

The results of Modelling C (including nodal reactions) are highly sensitive to shear coefficient values.
