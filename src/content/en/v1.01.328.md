# v1.01.328 ZZZZ328 – Validation of CALC_CHAMP / ENEL_ELEM

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the elementary calculation of the elastic energy. For each model, one considers a unit element with all the degrees of freedom imposed, which allows to create homogeneous deformation and stress fields on the element. The results are compared to the analytical solution.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A element unit size 2 (quadrangle) or 3 (cube) is considered to be based on Modelling. The thickness of the element, when required, is $\mathrm{1m}$.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic, whose properties are:

- $E=1000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.2$

For Modelling E, the Poisson's ratio is zero.

The elastic energy is calculated by expressing the elemental volume $\mathit{dV}$ in the cylindrical marker for an infinitesimal $d\theta$ thickness:

${E}_{\mathit{elas}}={\int}_{V}\frac{1}{2}\sigma :\epsilon \mathit{dV}=\frac{1}{2}\sigma :\epsilon {\int}_{{R}_{1}}^{{R}_{2}}\mathit{rdr}{\int}_{{H}_{1}}^{{H}_{2}}\mathit{dz}$

The integral terminals are: ${R}_{1}=\mathrm{1m}$ , ${R}_{2}=\mathrm{2m}$ , ${H}_{1}=\mathrm{0m}$ and ${H}_{2}=\mathrm{1m}$ .
