# v2.07.301 SHLV301 – Harmonic response by substructure: two-wired beam

**Summary:**

The structure studied is a two-sided beam subjected to a distributed load that varies harmonically over time.

This beam is deformable to shear force. It is modelled by elements in hexahedral volume at 20 nodes (3D Modelling).

The harmonic response is calculated by the Mac-Neal dynamic substructure method.

The results are compared with analytically obtained values for a Timoshenko beam model deformable to shear force and taking into account the rotational inertia of the sections.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

It is permitted that bending in the $\mathit{XY}$ plane and extension along the $X$ axis. The boundary conditions model being volume-densal, the boundary conditions differ somewhat from the boundary conditions model that would be imposed on a beam model.

Movement required:

| En $X=0$ , $Y=h/2$ | $\mathit{DX}=0$ , $\mathit{DY}=0$ |
| --- | --- |
| En $X=L$ , $Y=h/2$ | $\mathit{DX}=0$ , $\mathit{DY}=0$ |
| En $Z=b/2$ | $\mathit{DZ}=0$ |

To the previous conditions, the planeity constraint of the $X=0$ and $X=L$ sections is added. This constraint can be expressed as follows. ${x}^{T}=(X,Y,Z)$ defines the vector of the coordinates and ${u}^{T}=(\mathit{DX},\mathit{DY},\mathit{DZ})$ the vector of the movements; the position of an point is identified by the vector $x{'}^{T}={x}^{T}+{u}^{T}=(X',Y',Z')$ . Soient $A$ , $B$ and $C$ three unaligned points of the section. Any point $P$ is required to:

$∣\begin{array}{cccc}X{'}_{P}& Y{'}_{P}& Z{'}_{P}& 1\\ X{'}_{A}& Y{'}_{A}& Z{'}_{A}& 1\\ X{'}_{B}& Y{'}_{B}& Z{'}_{B}& 1\\ X{'}_{C}& Y{'}_{C}& Z{'}_{C}& 1\end{array}∣=0$

Force imposed:

In $Y=h$ , for all $X$ , the loading is defined by:

$p(x,t)=p(x)\sin(\omega t)$

with $p(x)={p}_{0}=5.0\times {10}^{4}{\text{N.m}}^{-1}$ and $\omega =2000\pi {\text{rd.s}}^{-1}$ .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is obtained analytically for a Timoshenko beam, taking into account shear force deformation and rotational inertia of the sections.

The solution is developed in series of eigenmodes. The theoretical aspects are developed in the reference given in 2.4 .

#### 2.1.1 Modal base

Set the following adimensional quantities:

${\lambda}_{n}={k}_{n}L$ wavelengths

${\Omega}_{n}=\frac{\rho A{L}^{4}}{\mathit{EI}}{\omega}_{n}^{2}$ own values

$j=\frac{I}{A{L}^{2}}$ Rotary Inertiality

$g=\frac{\mathit{EI}}{k'AG{L}^{2}}$ shear coefficient

Each clean ${k}_{n}$ wave number mode is characterized by the following quantities:

The eigenfrequencies:

${\Omega}_{1,2}=\frac{\rho A{L}^{4}}{\mathit{EI}}{\omega}_{1,2}^{2}=\frac{(g+j){\lambda}_{n}^{2}+1\pm \sqrt{{(g-j)}^{2}{\lambda}_{n}^{4}+2(g+j){\lambda}_{n}^{2}+1}}{2gj}$

with

${\lambda}_{n}=n\pi$ , $n=1,2,3,\mathrm{...}$ ,

*(Indices 1 and 2 correspond to the signs + and – in front of the root).*

Generalised masses:

${\mu}_{1,2}=\rho A={\left({k}_{n}-\frac{{\omega}_{1,2}^{2}\rho }{{k}_{n}k'G}\right)}^{2}\rho I$ .

Critical depreciation percentages:

${ϵ}_{1,2}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\alpha {\omega}_{1,2}+\frac{\beta}{{\omega}_{1,2}}\right)$

#### 2.1.2 Harmonistic response

The amplitude and phase of the deflection W are given by

$W(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{+\infty }{p}_{n}\left[\sum_{m=1}^{2}\frac{1}{{\mu}_{m}({\omega}_{m}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}+2i{ϵ}_{m}{\omega}_{m}\omega )}\right]\sin({k}_{n}x)$

with

${p}_{n}=\frac{2{p}_{0}}{n\pi }\left[1-{(-1)}^{n}\right]$
