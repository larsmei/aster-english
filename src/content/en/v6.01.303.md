# v6.01.303 SSNA303 : Elastoplastic-tipped test piece in large deformations

**Summary:**

This test modeled a test piece cut into axi-symmetry. The behavior is elasto-plastic with linear isotropic hardening of the type von Mises (VMIS_ISOT_LINE).

A calculation of SIMO_MIEHE, hyper-elastic, is made, compared to GDEF_LOG, hypo-elastic.

The comparative data are the resulting stress and contraction of the ligament.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.01.303/1000E53C000069D50000E8A68F63AB50D36EDA9B.svg)

**Figure 1: Reference problem**

The test piece is axi-symmetric, and only half of the test piece is modeled by elements. The dimensions are given here in millimetres.

### 1.2 Material data

The material is elastoplastic with linear isotropic hardening of the type von Misès (VMIS_ISOT_LINE).

The material data used are as follows:

| Young's modulus: | $200000\mathrm{MPa}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Poisson's ratio | $0,3$ |
| Limit of elasticity | $200\mathrm{MPa}$ |
| Linear nutting module | $20000\mathrm{MPa}$ |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Due to symmetry, vertical displacements are blocked on the $\mathrm{P0}-\mathrm{P1}$ line and horizontal displacements are blocked on the $\mathrm{P0}-\mathrm{P9}$ axis; the load consists of a vertical displacement imposed on the $\mathrm{P8}-\mathrm{P9}$ side:

| $\mathrm{P0}-\mathrm{P9}$ axis | $\mathrm{DX}=0$ |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathrm{P0}-\mathrm{P1}$ axis | $\mathrm{DY}=0$ |
| $\mathrm{P8}-\mathrm{P9}$ axis | $\mathrm{DY}=6\mathrm{mm}$ |

The load is imposed in 50 increments of $0.12\mathrm{mm}$ .

## 2 Baseline

The reference results are non-regression. The contraction of the ligament, i.e. the following $x$ displacement of the node $\mathrm{P1}$ , and the resulting stress (REAC_NODA) on the $\mathrm{P8}-\mathrm{P9}$ face are compared.

The displacement values imposed $\mathit{Ud}=0,6\mathit{mm}$ , $\mathrm{Ud}=3\mathrm{mm}$ and $\mathrm{Ud}=\mathrm{6mm}$ are considered.

The resulting final deformations are at the maximum $70\text{\%}$ .
