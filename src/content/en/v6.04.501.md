# v6.04.501 SSNV501 – Stamping of a sheet metal by a hemispheric punch (Wagonner test)

**Summary:**

This test represents a calculation of the stamping of a sheet metal by a rigid hemispheric punch in the presence of large plastic deformations. This test is used extensively in the simulation of the forming of sheet metal.

The results analyzed are the vertical displacement of the punch according to the force imposed. They are compared to a numerical reference solution.

Three modellings axisymmetric are made. The contact flan/poin and flan/matrix is of the type node‐mesh cell.

1. Modelization A: the coefficient of friction, contact flan/poin and flan/matrix is zero.
2. Modelization B: the coefficient of friction, contact flan/poin and flan/matrix is 0.15.
3. Modelling C: Modelling similar to Modelling A with a finer mesh for flan.
