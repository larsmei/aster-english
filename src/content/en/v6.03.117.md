# v6.03.117 SSNP117 - 2D Sliding Model - DP

**Summary:**

This quasi-static non-linear mechanical test validates the $\mathrm{2D}$ flat deformations model of Rousselier for the following configurations: elastoplastic base model, germination model and viscoplastic model with theta-method for the integration of the constitutive law.

The Modelling is done with a quadratic element $\mathrm{2D}$, in flat deformation.

## 6 Modelling D

### 6.1 Modelling characteristics

The calculation with the viscoplastic model (ROUSS_VISC) is repeated in model A, adding viscous regularization (REGU_VISC).

Two parameters shall be determined for this viscoelastic regulation mechanism:

- The rigidity k: the apparent rigidity (k+ negative hardening) must always be positive. If k is not large enough, one can find oneself in the situation where there would be no solution, even with viscosity. In this Modelling, $k$ is chosen at the value of the Young's modulus $E=2.e5\mathit{MPa}$ .
- The characteristic time $\tau$: first, the deformation rate in each point is estimated in such a nice situation. At this rate of deformation, it takes some time to cross the elastic domain. $\tau$ must be small (1-10 %) in front of that time. More details will be in the internal note EDF R&D 6125-1724-2020-01606. In this Modelling, the 10 mm displacement is imposed on the 10 mm (Y direction) length side for 10 s. This takes about 1.35th-4 s to reach 10% of the elastic limit (27 MPa). The 1.0th-4 s is chosen for $\tau$.
