# v8.01.114 FDLV114 - Seismic response of a cylindrical tank

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the seismic response of a cylindrical tank.

In particular in model A, the impulsive contribution of the fluid contained in the reservoir is validated by an added mass method, the calculation of the participation factor and the actual modal masses of the wet modes.

The basic frequency is compared with the result given by NOVAX software. Similarly, the response of the filled tank to a soil spectrum is compared with the results given by NOVAX.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry, Materials and boundary conditions

It is a structure with simple geometry, thin cylinder type, filled to the edge and subjected to seismic excitation defined with stress spectra.

- geometrical parameters of the cylinder:
- radius *R* = 7 m,
- height *H* = 21 m,
- thickness of sheet metal *e* = 15 mm,
- material parameters:
- Young's modulus of the structure (steel) *E* = 1.95·1011 Pa,
- Poisson's ratio *ν*  = 0,3,
- density of the structure *ρ* = 7850 kg/m3,
- density of liquid (water) *ρL* = 1000 kg/m3,
- depreciation *c* = 4%,
- boundary conditions:
- base of the cylinder (lower surface) blocked following the three translation degrees of freedom (DDL),
- zero pressure on the free surface of the fluid.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

In model B, the same calculation is made as in model A, however, by decomposing it as a first part where the fluid potential associated with the tank structure modes is explicitly obtained.

### 4.2 Tested quantities and results

The added mass of the first mode is calculated by projection of the fluid displacement potential on itself. The result is compared with that given by the operator CALC_MATR_AJOU.

| Identification | Reference type | Reference value | Tolerance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Added mass of the first mode on itself | 'AUTRE_ASTER' | 96633 kg | 0,1% |

The pressure and speed potentials are then derived by a factor $\omega$ or ${\omega}^{2}$ , $\omega$ being the frequency of the mode in question.

## 5 Summary of the results

This validates the calculation and consideration of participation factors calculated on a generalized modal basis. They are calculated in the particular case of modes with added mass and used to determine the seismic response of a cylindrical tank filled with water.

The results are relatively far from the reference (difference 15% compared to other software) because to approach it requires the use of a modal base in extended air (up to 300 Hz). The calculation is feasible (less than one hour of calculation) but is no longer part of the basic verification.
