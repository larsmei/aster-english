# v3.02.303 SSLP303 - Faux plate loaded at its end

**Summary:**

The purpose of the test is to validate the keyword FORCE_CONTOUR, from a load applied to the end of a plate.The problem is treated in flat stresses.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Section inertia time: ${l}_{Y}=\frac{{h}^{3}l}{12}=1.042\times {10}^{-9}{m}^{4}$

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus : $E=2.1\times {10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$

Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- Clamped support of the $\mathit{AD}$ ($u=v=0$) edge.
- Result load $P=85N$ , applied to the $\mathit{BC}$ edge (constant linear load).

### 1.4 Boundary conditions and loads

Not applicable for static analysis.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The value of the $v$ displacement field at the free end of the plate ($\mathit{BC}$ edge) is given by:

${v}_{L}=\frac{{\mathit{PL}}^{3}}{{\mathrm{3EI}}_{y}}$ (negligible shear)

d’où ${v}_{L}=0.129m$

The ${\sigma}_{xx}$ bending stress field is given by:

${\sigma}_{xx}=\frac{\mathit{Ph}}{{\mathrm{2l}}_{y}}(L-x)$ on the $\mathit{AB}$ edge

either ${\sigma}_{xx}=2.04\times {10}^{8}(L-x)(\mathit{Pa})$

### 2.2 Baseline

- ${v}_{L}$ moving of nodes $B$ and $C$
- ${\sigma}_{xx}$ constraints on nodes $A$ and $B$ and $E$

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.

### 3.4 Remarks

The difference with the analytical solution, of the beam type or slender plate, is due to the Modelling used: the dimensions of the structure, very slender, do not allow to meet the conditions of flat stresses.

## 4 Summary of the results

This test, based on a slender plate solution, is processed in 2D (plane-contraints) to validate the onboard loading (keyword FORCE_CONTOUR). The solution obtained is close to the analytical solution (0.4% difference on the movements) and therefore validates this type of Modelling.
