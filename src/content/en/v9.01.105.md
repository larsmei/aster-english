# v9.01.105 SZLZ105 - Cycle count by RAINFLOW and damage calculation

**Summary:**

Nearly static elastic linear transient problem in structural mechanics.

Calculation of final damage in a element subjected to cyclic loading, with linear elastic behaviour.

A planar stress model and a 3D model.

This test validates the cycle counting method (RAINFLOW) installed in the CALC_FATIGUE operator and the method of calculating the stress damage imposed (Wöhler curve) or the strain imposed (Manson-Coffin curve). The reference solution is an analytical solution.

It also validates the calculation of equivalent stresses and deformations using the options SIEQ_ELGA, SIEQ_ELNO, EPEQ_ELGA, EPEQ_ELNO, EPMQ_ELGA and EPMQ_ELNO.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

analytical solution

- Calculation of stresses and deformations. For single-tension loading, a uniform uniaxial stress state is obtained in all point:

$\sigma =\left[\begin{array}{ccc}\sigma & 0& 0\\ 0& 0& 0\\ 0& 0& 0\end{array}\right]$ et $\varepsilon =\left[\begin{array}{ccc}\varepsilon & 0& 0\\ 0& \gamma & 0\\ 0& 0& \gamma \end{array}\right]$

the equivalent quantities are therefore $\lbrace \begin{array}{}{\sigma}_{\mathrm{VMIS}}=\mid \sigma \mid ={\sigma}_{\mathrm{TRESCA}}\\ {\sigma}_{\mathrm{VMIS}-\mathrm{SG}}=\sigma \end{array}$

et $\lbrace \begin{array}{}{\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{INVA}-2}=\frac{2}{3}\mid \varepsilon -\gamma \mid \\ {\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{INVA}-\mathrm{2SG}}=\frac{2}{3}\mid \varepsilon -\gamma \mid \ast \mathrm{sign}\left[\frac{\varepsilon +2\gamma }{3}\right]\end{array}$

- then manual calculation of the cycles by the RAINFLOW method, as well as loading amplitudes ($\frac{\Delta \sigma }{2}$ or $\frac{\Delta \varepsilon }{2}$).

| cycles | $\Delta \sigma /2$ | $\Delta {\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{INVA}-2}/2$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 2 3 4 | 1. 0.5 1. 3.5 | 0.8667 0.433315 0.8667 3.03335 |

- Finally, these values are carried over to the Wöhler or Manson-Coffin curves to estimate unit damage at each $i$ cycle, i.e. ${\mathrm{Du}}_{i}=\frac{1}{{N}_{i}}$ (${N}_{i}$ being the number of breaking cycles for a given amplitude), as well as the cumulative damage $D=\underset{i}{\Sigma}D{u}_{i}$ (linear cumulation rule of MINER).

**Note:**

*The equivalent stress* ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{VMIS}-\mathrm{SG}}$ *and equivalent deformation* ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{INVA}-\mathrm{2SG}}=\frac{2}{3}\mid \varepsilon -\gamma \mid \times \mathit{sign}\left[\frac{\varepsilon +2\gamma }{3}\right]$ * shall be used.*

### 2.2 Baseline

- Given the values of the loading parameters used, we simply obtain at the end of loading (increment 8) $\sigma =-3.$ $\varepsilon =-3.$ $\gamma =0.9$ ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{INVA}-2}=2.6$ .
- For the calculation of the damage, the following shall be obtained:

${D}_{\mathit{Wöhler}}=4,8133.{10}^{-3}=\underset{i=1}{\overset{4}{\Sigma}}D{u}_{i}$

${D}_{\mathit{Manson}}=4,67.{10}^{-3}=\underset{i=1}{\overset{4}{\Sigma}}D{u}_{i}$
