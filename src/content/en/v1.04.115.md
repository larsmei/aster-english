# v1.04.115 MUMPS05 - Validation of the MUMPS solver in parallel with a centralized matrix

**Summary:**

This test validates the MUMPS solver in parallel with a centralized matrix under different configurations:

- Change the value of keyword DISTRIBUTION/METHODE (CENTRALISEE, MAIL_DISPERSE, subdomains);
- Load balancing via CHARGE_PROC0_MA/SD keywords and IN_CORE/OUT_OF_CORE character;
- METIS/SCOTCH renumberers.

### 1.2 Material properties

- $E=1.0\mathit{E5}N/{m}^{2}$
- $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 boundary conditions

A force ${F}_{z}=1.0\mathit{E4}N$ is applied to the face defined by the group ' *COTE_H* '.

The travel requirements are:

- $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=0$ en A
- $\mathit{DY}=0$ en B
- $\mathit{DZ}=0$ on the face defined by the group *'COTE_B'*

## 2 Solution

The reference quantity used is the displacement according to the x axis and the z axis to the node D.

The point C: $\mathit{DX}=0,\mathit{DY}=0.1$ move
