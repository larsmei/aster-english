# v7.31.101 WTNV101 - Undrained triaxial test with Laigle model and hydraulic coupling

**Summary**

This test validates the Laigle model with hydraulic coupling. It is a triaxial test in undrained condition. The undrained aspect is modelled by a zero volume deformation of the skeleton and the hydraulic coupling is taken into account, the sample is fully saturated, the skeleton and fluid being assumed to be incompressible.

Because of symmetry, only one of the eighth samples tested is considered to be triaxial.

The containment level is $8\mathit{MPa}$ .
