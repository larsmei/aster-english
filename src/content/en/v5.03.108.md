# v5.03.108 SDNV108 – Density cylinder rotating around its axis, taking into account gyroscopy

**Summary:**

This test validates the calculation of the rotational modes of a volume model with and without gyroscopic stiffness.

This is a simple model of hollow cylinder, free-flowing around its axis. This example is taken from the reference [1].

The results of the calculations are compared with those obtained with ANSYS. The results coincide perfectly with the reference solution.

## 1 Reference problem

The objective of this test case is to validate the 3-D gyroscopic Modelling of a full cylinder (options MECA_GYRO and RIGI_ROTA) in Code_Aster.

The results obtained by modelling Code_Aster are compared with those obtained in ANSYS.

### 1.1 Geometry

A hollow cylinder of $L=0,254m$ length, $R=0,09525m$ radius and $\mathit{Ep}=0,03810m$ thickness is considered.

![Figure](/figures/v5.03.108/100002000000019F0000015BFCD94A64B62AA3DA.png)

Image 1.1-1: Hollow cylinder geometry

### 1.2 Material properties

The cylinder has a density of $\rho =7860\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$.

The Young's modulus is $E=207.{10}^{9}N{m}^{-2}$ and the Poisson's ratio is $\nu =0,28$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The cylinder is in free-flow configuration. It is stationary or rotating at 1000, 2000 and 3000 rpm.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is a 3D calculation performed with ANSYS V14.

1. ANSYS V14.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The rotor is modeled by linear elements density (modelling '3D', MECA_HEXA8).

CALC_MODES calculates the modes of shutdown (ie. without gyroscopic damping) and rotation, i.e. with gyroscopic damping (option MECA_GYRO), but taking into account or not the softening effect by the centrifugal stiffness (option RIGI_ROTA).
