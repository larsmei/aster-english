# v2.02.400 SDLL400 - Vibration beam with center of torsion excentrated

**Summary:**

This test is based on independent validation of version 4 of the beam models.

It allows testing the consideration of an eccentricity of the torsion center on the calculation of eigenfrequencies of an straight beam (modelling with elements POU_D_E, straight beam d-Euler).

### 1.3 boundary conditions

**boundary condition :**

Plan problem: $\mathrm{DZ}$ and $\mathrm{DRY}$ blocked.

Nodes $A$ and $B$ supported: $\mathrm{DX}$ and $\mathrm{DY}$ blocked

The eccentricity is taken into account using LIAISON_DDL operation AFFE_CHAR_MECA.

Degrees of freedom are still in $G$, and we take into account eccentricity by: $\mathrm{DY}(G)=\mathrm{DY}(C)+\mathrm{GC}\wedge {\Theta}_{x}$

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solutions

These are the eigenfrequencies solutions of the homogeneous problem without damping.

The resolution method, of the elements type, is partially given in [bib1]. However, a series of results is provided in the case where the effects of the spin twist are neglected, which brings the Modelling back to a POU_D_T.

Some confidence can be given to these results published in a peer-reviewed journal. However, uncertainties exist if these calculations are to be reproduced: ${J}_{x}$ torsion and ${k}_{y}$ shear constants are not provided in the article. They have had to be recalculated from the section geometry.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results are fairly close to the reference solution (digital). (difference $\text{< 5\%}$ ), for which some data were missing and therefore had to be estimated. However, they correspond very well to the CAILLOU results of the IAT (data identical to those of *Code_Aster*).

This allows the recognition of the centre of torsion excentering in mass and rigidity matrices to be validated.
