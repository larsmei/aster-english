# 1 **v6.04.131** SSNV131 –Functured right pipe in elastic-plasticity combined loading

**Summary:**

The problem is a straight cracked tube with semi-elliptic longitudinal defect in internal skin. The right tube is subjected to a combined load of pressure and bending moment. This test allows to calculate the rate of local energy restitution in crack bottom by the method G-theta (command CALC_G)The rate of local energy restitution is calculated according to the LINE interpolation. The integrating crowns are defined by the number of layers (NB_COUCHE_INF and NB_COUCHE_SUP).

The value of G at the bottom of the crack is compared with a note used for the validation of simplified methods in the mechanical of the fracture.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a semi-elliptic longitudinal crack that leads to a little internal dive into a straight pipe. Symmetrical planes are used to model only a quarter of the pipe.

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.131/100002010000032800000224DC7A72A08238C33C.png)

Figure 1.1-2: Géométrie du défaut

> The tube has for length 2000 mm, for thickness 60 mm, and for internal radius 270 mm.
> The defect is 7.5 mm deep and 22.5 mm long.

### 1.2 Material properties

> A rational traction curve of the Ramberg-Osgood type is used.

$\frac{ϵ}{{ϵ}_{0}}=\frac{\sigma}{{\sigma}_{0}}+\alpha .{(\frac{\sigma}{{\sigma}_{0}})}^{n}$ , with ${ϵ}_{0}=\frac{{\sigma}_{0}}{E}$

Où:

$n=6$

$\alpha =1$

${\sigma}_{0}=163\mathit{MPa}$

$E=174700\mathit{MPa}$

$\nu =0.3$

> The material constitutive law is elasto-plastic ( *‘VMIS_ISOT_TRAC

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Symmetrical to the 2 main plans:

${U}_{Y}=0.$ in the $Y=0$ plane.

${U}_{Z}=0.$ in $Z=0.$ plane out of crack

> Rigid mode on $X$ axis.
> ${U}_{X}$ average in nodes NODE_CENTRE_FISS_T and NODE_CENTRE_FISS_TM is null.

The loading conditions are applied in two stages:

1. A $P$ pressure load is applied progressively to the internal pipe wall.

> A traction load $\text{EFFET\_FOND}$ at the end of the pipe is also applied to model the background effect.
> $\text{EFFET\_FOND}=P\frac{{R}_{int}^{2}}{{R}_{\text{ext}}^{2}-{R}_{int}^{2}}$
> Until maximum pressure $P=36.97\mathit{MPa}$

1. By remaining with maximum pressure applied to the pipe, a next bending moment $M$ ($-Y$ ) requesting the semi-elliptic defect in opening is applied gradually up to $M=-5989.460\mathit{kNm}$.

> It is applied to the end of the tube via a linear distribution following $x$ of ${\sigma}_{zz}$ axial stresses

${\sigma}_{zz}=\frac{Mx}{I}$ in the $Z=2000\mathit{mm}$ plane

where $I$ is the quadratic bending moment. $I=\pi \frac{({R}_{\text{ext}}^{4}-{R}_{int}^{4})}{4}$
