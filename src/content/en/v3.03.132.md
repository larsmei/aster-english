# v3.03.132 SSLS132 – Reinforced concrete console plate under bending loading, elastic

**Summary**

This quasi-static test is part of the validation of elements GRILLE_EXCENTRE, GRILLE_MEMBRANE and MEMBRANE. A concrete plate (modified by COQUE if necessary) is covered with two rebar panels on its upper and lower sides, each excreted from the same quantity. The loads are of three types:

1. clamped edge and plate bending
2. effect of gravity and own weight
3. pre-deformations in both rebars to compress the plate

The results of the simulation are compared with analytical solutions.

The advantage of this test is to validate GRILLE_EXCENTRE, GRILLE_MEMBRANE and MEMBRANE Modelling under flexion loadings, gravity and by imposing pre-deformations.

$I$ Modelling tests IMPLEX algorithm in elasticity.

## 12 Conclusions

This test case various modellings validates the behaviour of a flex-loaded console plate, under the effect of gravity and by imposing pre-deformations on the rebar arrays. The modellings GRILLE_EXCENTRE,GRILLE_MEMBRANE and MEMBRANE are valid.

The simulation results are consistent with the analytical solution values.
