# v7.33.107 WTNA107 - axisymmetric heating Modelling of water saturated element

**Summary:**

This test represents the heating of a water saturated element with consideration of the mechanics. It is test case non-regression without physical reality. It allows testing thermo-hydro-mechanical coupled Modelling of type: AXIS_THM* in single-phase water (LIQU_SATU). It is the axisymmetric version of test case WTNL101.

Modelization A:

- Modelling AXIS_THM
- Matching: LIQU_SATU law

Modelling B:

- AXIS_THMD modelling (light)
- Matching: LIQU_SATU law

Modelization C:

- AXIS_THMS modelling (selective)
- Matching: LIQU_SATU law

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
