# v1.03.126 MFRON01 – code_aster-MFront interface test for elasto-visco-plastic laws

**Summary:**

This test is valid for standard constitutive laws elasto-plastic and visco-plastics (used especially for metals: Von Mises plasticity, Chaboche, ...) defined using *MFront* in comparison with similar behaviors of *code_aster*.

Each Modelling validates an constitutive law, usually by comparison with the equivalent law results of *code_aster* .

## 1 Modelling A

### 1.1 Modelling characteristics

- Tested behavior: Chaboche.mfront. Elasto-plastic law of two kinematic variables, comparable to VMIS_CIN2_CHAB (see [r5.03.04](doc:r5.03.04)).
- Modelling and data similar to SSNV101A.

### 1.2 Tested quantities and results

The reference solution is the one obtained by the VMIS_CIN2_CHAB behavior
