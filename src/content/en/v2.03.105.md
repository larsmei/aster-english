# v2.03.105 SDLS105 - Flat plate subjected to homogeneous turbulence

**Summary**

This test of the field of linear dynamics of hulls and plates implements the calculation of acceptance, a function intended to calculate the DSP of modal effort from a pressure DSP. This precise test implements a Modelling of plate type with elements of fluid coupling / structure to test the Corcos method whose correlation function is appropriate to established turbulent plane flows.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.105/100000000000047900000197F2D7825618F4A457.png)

$L=50m$ $l=5m$

fluid thickness $e=0.5m$

thickness of the plate $h=0.5m$

### 1.2 Material properties

Fluid: density $\rho =1000{\mathit{kg.m}}^{-3}$ (water).

> Structure: ${\rho}_{s}=7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$ , $E=2.1{10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$ , $\nu =0.3$ (steel).

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Fluid:

- In order to simulate the permanent flow, a normal speed of $–4m/s$ is imposed on the inlet surface of the fluid, with the rate of input ${\overrightarrow{V}}_{0}$ inverse to the normal input (analogous to thermal analysis, a normal heat flow equivalent to $–4$ is imposed),
- To calculate the fluid disturbance brought by the movement of the plate a Dirichlet boundary condition is imposed in a node of the fluid.
- $x=\frac{e}{2}$ is used to impose the ${\phi }_{1}={\phi }_{2}=0$ condition which corresponds to a zero flow through the upper fluid wall.

Structure:

- the plate is subject to a forced displacement corresponding to a first bending mode:

${X}_{1}=\sin\frac{\pi y}{L}\text{}$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

For the calculation of the added coefficients, the test case FDLV109 is referred to.

A depreciation calculation was made to the flow rate (${V}_{0}=4{\mathrm{m.s}}^{-1}$ ).

The added mass provided by the flow shall be:

$\begin{array}{}{M}_{11}^{a}=0.625{10}^{5}\text{kg},\\ {M}_{22}^{a}=0.625{10}^{5}\text{kg},\\ {M}_{12}^{a}=0.\end{array}$

The added depreciation is worth with ${V}_{0}=4{\mathrm{m.s}}^{-1}$:

$\begin{array}{}{C}_{11}^{a}=0,\\ {C}_{22}^{a}=0,\\ {C}_{12}^{a}=0.266{10}^{5}N.{m}^{-1}.\end{array}$

The stiffness added is valid for ${V}_{0}=4{\mathrm{m.s}}^{-1}$:

$\begin{array}{}{K}_{11}^{a}\text{=-}0.3943{10}^{4}N.{m}^{-1}.{\text{rad}}^{2},\\ {K}_{22}^{a}\text{=-}0.1577{10}^{5}N.{m}^{-1}.{\text{rad}}^{2},\\ {K}_{12}^{a}=0.\end{array}$

The test is useful here to calculate and test the modal effort autospectrum obtained from a characteristic pressure spectrum of established turbulent flows.

The spectrum chosen here is constant and then zero from a cutoff frequency:

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.105/10000200000006A9000005092E78E7DE07EF2274.png)

We got DSP pressure:

${S}_{{p}_{}}(\omega )={K}^{2}({\mathit{\rho U}}^{2}{)}^{2}{d}^{3}$ for $0,1<\frac{\omega d}{\mathrm{2pU}}<10$

The chosen coherence function for this parallel flow plate is derived from a Corcos model:

${r}^{(s)}(x-x\text{',}\omega )={e}^{-{k}_{L}(x-x')}{e}^{-{k}_{T}(y-y')}\cos(\omega (x-x')/{U}_{c}).$

The ${k}_{T}$ and ${k}_{L}$ parameters are called Bakewell parameters and are valid for the pulse:

${k}_{L}=0.1\frac{\omega}{{U}_{c}}$ et ${k}_{T}=0.55\frac{\omega}{{U}_{c}}$

The acceptance function, defined in general by

${J}_{{{A}_{ij}}_{}}^{2}(\omega )=\underset{A}{\int}\underset{A}{\int}r(x-x\text{',}\omega ){f}_{{i}_{\alpha}}(x){f}_{{j}_{\alpha '}}(x'){n}_{\alpha}(x){n}_{\alpha '}(x')\text{dA}\text{dA}'$

in our case:

$\begin{array}{c}{J}_{{{A}_{\text{nm}}}_{}}^{2}(\omega )=\underset{A}{\int}\underset{A}{\int}{e}^{-{k}_{T}\mid y-y'\mid }{e}^{-{k}_{L}\mid x-x'\mid }\cos(\frac{\omega (x-x')}{{U}_{c}})\sin(\frac{{k}_{n}x}{L})\sin(\frac{{k}_{m}x'}{L})\text{dxdy}\text{dx}'\text{dy}'\\ =\underset{-l/2}{\overset{l/2}{\int}}\underset{-l/2}{\overset{l/2}{\int}}{e}^{-{k}_{T}\mid y-y'\mid }\text{dydy}'´\underset{0}{\overset{L}{\int}}\underset{0}{\overset{L}{\int}}{e}^{-{k}_{L}\mid x-x'\mid }\cos(\frac{\omega (x-x')}{{U}_{c}})\sin(\frac{{k}_{n}x}{L})\sin(\frac{{k}_{m}x'}{L})\text{dxdx}'.\\ \end{array}$

The first factor integral has an analytical expression and is:

$\underset{-l/2}{\overset{l/2}{\int}}\underset{-l/2}{\overset{l/2}{\int}}{e}^{-{k}_{T}\mid y-y'\mid }\text{dydy}'=\frac{\mathrm{2l}}{{k}_{T}}-2(\frac{1-{e}^{-{k}_{T}l}}{{k}_{{}_{T}}^{2}}).$

The following table gives values of this integral:
