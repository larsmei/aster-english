# v2.02.009 SDLL09 - Vibration of a variable rectangular cross section slender beam (clamped-free)

**Summary:**

This plan problem consists of searching for vibration frequencies of a free clamped beam with rectangular variable cross section. This test includes only one model.

The variation in section of the beam is either homothetic or non-homothetic. The characteristics of the section of the beam are given according to the mesh cells in two different ways:

- section and inertia,
- height and width.

This problem allows testing the element of variable cross section beam for a prismatic structure and the calculation of vibration frequencies by inverse iterations. In addition, in the AFFE_CARA_ELEM operator, the remanence of some keywords is tested.

The results obtained are in good agreement with those given in the VPCS guide.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in the SDLL09/89 form of the VPCS guide which presents the calculation method as follows:

Accurate calculation by numerical integration of the differential equation of beam bending (Euler-Bernouilli Theory).

$\frac{{\partial}^{2}({\mathit{EI}}_{z}\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {x}^{2}})}{\partial {x}^{2}}=-\rho A\frac{{\partial}^{2}v}{\partial {t}^{2}}$

where ${I}_{z}$ and $A$ vary with the apse.

We get:

${f}_{i}=\frac{1}{2\pi }{\lambda}_{i}(\alpha ,\beta )\frac{{h}_{1}}{{L}^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{E}{12\rho }}$

with:

$\begin{array}{c}\alpha =\frac{{h}_{0}}{{h}_{1}}=4\\ \beta =\frac{{b}_{0}}{{b}_{1}}=4\mathit{ou}5\end{array}$

|  | ${\lambda}_{1}$ | ${\lambda}_{2}$ | ${\lambda}_{3}$ | ${\lambda}_{4}$ | ${\lambda}_{5}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\beta =4$ | 23.289 | 73.9 | 165.23 | 299.7 | 478.1 |
| $\beta =5$ | 24.308 | 75.56 | 167.21 | 301.9 | 480.4 |
