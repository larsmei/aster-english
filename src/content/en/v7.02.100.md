# v7.02.100 HPLP100 - Calculation of energy release rate of a thermo-elasticity cracked plate

**Summary**

This is a thermo-elasticity test for a two-dimensional problem. A cracked rectangular plate is considered and is placed in the hypothesis of flat deformations.

In **Modelling A** , the energy release rate is calculated in post-processing by two different methods:

- classical calculation by the theta method,
- calculation by the formula d-IRWIN from the stress intensity coefficient $\mathit{KI}$ .

These two calculations are performed on 4 different integration crowns. Their interest is to compare the values of $G$ , $G(\mathrm{IRWIN})$ and $\mathit{KI}$ with the reference solution and to test the invariance of the calculations with the different integration crowns.

In the Modelling method B**, the energy release rate is calculated in post-processing of a classical calculation by theta method. This calculation is performed for several different crowns of integrations, in a formalism of large transformations.

## 5 Summary of results

During the **first model**, the difference with reference values is 6 to 7%. Independent validation of the mechanical batch of the rupture should provide elements response on the validity of $G$ in thermo-elasticity.

When **second model** is not thermal, the difference with the reference solution below 2% allows the user of CALC_G operator to be validated in large transformations.
