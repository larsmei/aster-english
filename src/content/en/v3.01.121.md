# v3.01.121 SSLL121 – Pressures: Calculation of nodes SIPO_ELNO stress

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to verify the calculation of SIPO_ELNO stresses in the presence of specification of coefficients of flexibility and stress intensification on beams.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry studied is a straight cylindrical section of 10m long according to the Ox axis, of 500mm external radius and 100mm thickness.

### 1.2 Characteristics and materials

The Young's modulus of the beam is E=200 GPa.

The pipe segment has:

- two coefficients of flexibility cflex, depending on the Y axis and the Z axis,
- Two coefficients of intensification of the isigm stresses, according to the Y axis and the Z axis.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The section is anchored in (0.0), node group FIXA.

A force of 1e6 N is applied to the free end of the section, to the group of node TIPA. The force is either on the Oy axis or on the Oz axis.

### 1.4 Purpose

The correct calculation of SIPO_ELNO stresses is checked by taking into account flexibilities and stress intensification coefficients.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Inertia moment of tubular section

By defining:

- $d_{ext} = 2R$: outer diameter,
- $d_{int} = d_{ext} - 2e_p$: inner diameter,

the inertia moment of a hollow circular cross section is given by:

$$
I = \frac{\pi}{64} \left( d_{ext}^4 - d_{int}^4 \right) .
$$

### 2.2 Calculation of the deflection

The deflection at the end of a clamped tubular beam subjected to F-force is given by:

$$
\delta = \frac{F \cdot L^3}{3 \cdot E \cdot I}
$$

Note: in the presence of a Timoshenko beam, this solution is approximate, as the deflection depends not only on the bending moment but also on shearing effort.

In the presence of a ${c_{flex}}$ flexibility coefficient, the deflection is amplified as follows:

$$
I_{c_{flex}} = \frac{I}{c_{flex}},

\delta = \frac{F \cdot L^3}{3 \cdot E \cdot I_{c_{flex}}}.
$$

### 2.3 Calculation of SIPO constraints

SIPO constraints due to bending moment $M_f = -F \cdot L$ are defined by:

$$
\sigma = \frac{M_f}{I} \cdot R,
$$

With $R$ the outer radius of the tubular beam.

They are amplified where appropriate by the stress intensification coefficient $sif$.

$$
\sigma = sif \cdot \frac{M_f}{I} \cdot R
$$

Note: The stress calculation is done with the "real" inertia moment, not modified by the flexibility coefficient.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Mesh characteristics

The mesh consists of two mesh cells SEG2 of equal length.

### 3.2 Modelling characteristics

The beam is modelled by Timoshenko beams with elements POU_D_T.

### 3.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The beam is clamped at the node FIXA group with DDL_IMPO.

The force is applied at the other end of the beam at the node TIPA group with FORCE_NODALE.

### 3.4 Tested quantities and results

The values tested are SIPO_NOEU stresses at the anchor level (group of node FIXA) and deflections at the free end (group of node TIPA).

The analytical expressions are in the command file and are used to test all calculated values and all reference values in the TEST_RESU.

## 4 Summary of the results

For each model processed, the results are analytical solution.

Note that the accuracy in terms of deflection has been slightly relaxed, the deflection of the Timoshenko beams also depending on shearing efforts, neglected here.
