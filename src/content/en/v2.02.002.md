# v2.02.002 SDLL02 - slender beam, clamped-free, folded on itself

**Summary:**

This two-dimensional problem consists in searching for the frequencies and vibration modes of a mechanical structure, composed of a free slender beam, clamped and folded on itself.

The problem does not have physical significance, but it allows to validate the search for the own multiple bending frequencies and the search for double modes in a subspace of order 2.

In this test, three different modellingss are performed:

- in the first model, boundary conditions is imposed using Lagrange parameters (command AFFE_CHAR_MECA) and the own values and vectors are calculated by the Lanczos method (command CALC_MODES, METHODE='TRI_DIAG' under the factor keyword SOLVEUR_MODAL),
- in the second model, boundary conditions is imposed by removing degrees of freedom in the mass and stiffness matrices (command AFFE_CHAR_CINE) and the own values and vectors are calculated by the Bathe and Wilson method (command CALC_MODES, METHODE='JACOBI' under the factor keyword SOLVEUR_MODAL)

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in the SDLL02/89 form of the VPCS guide which presents the calculation method as follows:

By dynamic stiffness method, the folded beam is shown to allow double frequencies, a solution of:

$\begin{array}{ccc}\cos(\lambda )=0& \mathrm{\Rightarrow }& {\lambda}_{i}=(\mathrm{2i}-1)\frac{\pi}{2}\\ {f}_{i}=\frac{1}{2\pi }\frac{{\lambda}_{i}^{2}}{{L}^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{{\mathit{EI}}_{z}}{\rho A}}& & i=1,2,\mathrm{...}\end{array}$

For a rectangular section, we obtain:

${f}_{i}={(\mathrm{2i}-1)}^{2}\pi \frac{R}{{\mathrm{8L}}^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{E}{12\rho }}$ $i=1,2,\mathrm{...}$

This formulation neglects the deformations of shear force and rotation inertia (Euler-Bernoulli beam).

For the proper modes, the shapes are given in the VPCS guide. They are standardized to 1 or –1 to the point of greater amplitude. Only the results are given for modes 1, 2, 3, 4, 7 and 8. For example, the shapes of the first two modes are as follows:

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.002/10002354000032BF000009336FCB87CA75261F4A.svg)

Note:

In Code_Aster, when a value of its own is multiple, the modes associated with this value of its own, even if they are either standard and orthogonal two to two, are, a priori, unpredictable. We do not know, at the moment, how to test the shape of a multiple mode.

### 2.2 Baseline

The reference results are the first eight frequencies of its own.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

There is no uncertainty about the solution because it is analytical.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.002/100007BE00001B98000005CA196C84C62F6A248C.svg)

The beam was cut into 20 mesh cells SEG2 (10 for AB and 10 for $\mathrm{BC}$).

The model used for beams is Euler Bernoulli (POU_D_E).

We are looking for two-dimensional solutions. So we can block for all nodes moving $\mathrm{DZ}$ and $\mathrm{DRX}$ and $\mathrm{DRY}$ rotations.

The end of the beam (point $A$ ) is clamped from where in this point:

$\mathrm{DX}=\mathrm{DY}=0.$ $\mathrm{DRZ}=0.$

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 21 nodes and 20 mesh cells of the SEG2 type.

The Mesh characteristics points are as follows:

| Point $A=A$ | Point $B=B$ | Point $C=C$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
