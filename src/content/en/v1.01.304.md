# v1.01.304 ZZZZ304 – Projection of a field by elements ELNO discontinu

**Summary:**

This test shows how to project a elements field of discontinuous ELNO type over a border to preserve discontinuity.

Modelization A:

- Modelling THERMIQUE / PLAN

## 1 Test principle

The structure is 2D. It is a square formed of 2 $\mathit{CARR1}$ and $\mathit{CARR2}$ zones.

|  |
| --- |

This structure is meshed twice $\mathit{MA1}$ and $\mathit{MA2}$ . The meshes are different.

On $\mathit{MA1}$ , a field is created by elements ELNO hydration ($\mathit{CH1}$ ).

On $\mathit{CARR1}$: $\mathit{CH1}$ is $0.8$

On $\mathit{CARR2}$: $\mathit{CH1}$ is $0.2$

We want to project $\mathit{CH1}$ on $\mathit{MA2}$ to get $\mathit{CH2}$ so that $\mathit{CARR1}$ and $\mathit{CARR2}$ keep the same values.

The node $\mathit{N36}$ is on the mesh $\mathit{MA2}$ border.

It belongs to mesh cell $\mathit{M9}$ from $\mathit{CARR1}$

It belongs to mesh cell $\mathit{M10}$ from $\mathit{CARR2}$

## 2 Validation

On the node $\mathit{N36}$ , check that the field is $0.8$ on the mesh cell $\mathit{M9}$ and $0.2$ on the mesh cell $\mathit{M10}$ .
