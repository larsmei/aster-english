# v1.01.213 ZZZZ213 – Validation of AFFE_CHAR_MECA/AFFE_CHAR_CINE Operators with LDLT/MULT_FRONT/GCPC Solvers

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to validate the operator combinations:

- AFFE_CHAR_CINE and AFFE_CHAR_MECA with solvers:

  - LDLT
  - MULT_FRONT
  - GCPC
- COMB_MATR_ASSE and CALC_CHAR_CINE(REEL/SYMETRIQUE) with solvers:

  - LDLT
  - MULT_FRONT
  - GCPC

On the other hand, it is checked that the following combinations of operators are impossible:

- COMB_MATR_ASSE: Combine a LIGNE_DE_CIEL(LDLT) matrix with a MORSE (MULT_FRONT) matrix
- RESOUDRE with AFFE_CHAR_CINE forgetting the word "cle" CHAM_CINE,
- COMB_MATR_ASSE with 2 different AFFE_CHAR_CINE,
- RESOUDRE with AFFE_CHAR_CINE incompatible.

This test case includes two modellings.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
