# v3.02.323 SSLP323 - Propagation of radial crack leading to rotating disc

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to verify that the PROPA_FISS operator correctly treats the case of a crack propagation only caused by a rotating load.

The stress strength factor of the propagated cracks is verified to be analytical solution compliant.

In addition, these modellings are used to validate the use of the 'ERME_ELEM' mechanical residual error indicator on 2D modelling in the presence of rotations.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.323/10000000000003D3000002BDAEB8B49ABA737EEC.jpg)

**Figure** 1.1-a **: Geometry of cracked plate**

Geometrical dimensions of the cracked plate:

| Side square | $D=10m$ |
| --- | --- |

Initial crack length: ${a}_{0}=2m$ .

The crack leads to the middle of one side ($L/2$ ).

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus $E=206000\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.33$

Density $\rho =7800\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m³}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

In order to block the rigid body movement of the plate, the rotation axis in $\mathrm{DX}$ , $\mathrm{DY}$ and $\text{P\_SUP}$ in $\mathrm{DX}$ are blocked.

The load is only volume and induced by rotation, of $\omega =10$ pulse, normal axis in the plane of the structure and $(5;5)$ center.

Three propagations are calculated by imposing a maximum crack advance equal to $1\mathrm{mm}$ .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The stress intensity factors can be calculated using the following equations [bib1]:

> ${K}_{I}=\frac{1+\alpha }{16}\rho \omega \mathrm{²}\mathit{D²}\sqrt{\pi a}(F(a/D)-\frac{3\alpha -1}{1+\alpha }G(a/D))$
> with $F(a/D)=\frac{1,122+0,140(a/D)-0,545(a/D)\mathrm{²}+0,405(a/D)\mathrm{³}}{{(1-a/D)}^{3/2}}$
> $G(a/D)=\frac{0,187[6-9(a/D)+5(a/D)\mathrm{²}]-7,35(a/D)\mathrm{²}\cdot (1-A/D)\mathrm{⁴}\cdot (1-0,5(a/D))}{{(1-a/D)}^{3/2}}$

and $\alpha =\frac{1}{2}(\frac{1}{1-\nu })$ in flat constraints

### 2.2 Bibliographical references

[1] H. Tada, P.C. Paris, G.R. Irwin, "The Stress Analysis of Cracks Handbook -3rd Ed.", ASME Press 2000

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

SIMPLEXE is used by PROPA_FISS to solve crack propagation equations.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a mesh composed of 2500 elements QUAD4 (see ).

The crack is not meshed. It is modelled by the $\text{XFEM}$ method and initially defined by two level-sets:

$\mathit{LST}=y-{a}_{0}$

$\mathit{LSN}=x-D$

## 5 Summary of the results

The propagation of a radial crack under a rotational load is perfectly reproduced by $\text{XFEM}$ Modelling.

The 'ERME_ELEM' mechanical residual error indicator works correctly for modellings 2Ds containing rotations.
