# v6.04.210 SSNV210 – Drained shear test with Hujeux law

*Summary*

A *test* *pure mechanical shear* (equivalent to drained hydraulic conditions) is performed with *the Hujeux law* . Solutions are obtained with orthotropic elastic parameters Modelling isotropic behavior and the results are compared with a model with isotropic elastic parameters to validate the shear terms of the Hooke orthotropic tensor of the Hujeux law.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

For the A shear test Modelling is carried out on a single finite element square isoparametric QUAD8, mesh cell group named $\mathrm{BLOC}$. The length of each edge is $\mathrm{1m}$. The different sides of this square are mesh cell groups named $\mathrm{HAUT}$, $\mathrm{BAS}$, $\mathrm{DROIT}$ and $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$. The mesh-cell group $\mathrm{COTE}$ also contains the mesh cell $\mathrm{DROIT}$ and $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$ groups; the mesh cell $\mathrm{APPUI}$ group is the mesh cell $\mathrm{BAS}$ group.

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.210/Shape1.gif)

For the Modelling B l-test is performed on a single finite element isoparametric cubic form HEXA8. The length of each edge is 1. The different facets of this cube are mesh cells groups named $\mathrm{HAUT}$, $\mathrm{BAS}$, $\mathrm{DEVANT}$, $\mathrm{ARRIERE}$, $\mathrm{DROIT}$ and $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The shear test presented here is performed in model D_PLAN (modelization A) and model 3D (modelization B).

For model A, normal shifts in the design of studies are zero. Vertical displacement is imposed on the test piece while maintaining the constant lateral pressure in the design of study. In the model under consideration, the square element represents a quarter of the sample. The boundary conditions are therefore:

The symmetry conditions:

- ${u}_{y}=0.$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{BAS}$ group
- ${u}_{x}=0.$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$ group

Side pressure conditions:

- ${P}_{n}=100\mathit{kPa}$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{COTE}$ group

The loading conditions:

- ${P}_{n}=100\mathit{kPa}$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{HAUT}$ group
- ${u}_{x}=-1\mathit{mm}$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{HAUT}$ group

The load is carried out in two phases:

- An isotropic stress state, ${P}_{o}=100\mathrm{kPa}$ , is initially assigned to the mesh cell $\mathrm{BLOC}$;
- Hoizontal displacement is imposed on the mesh-cell group $\mathrm{HAUT}$ and varies between $t=0.$ and $t=10.$ from ${u}_{y}=0.$ to ${u}_{x}=-1\mathit{mm}$.

For Modelling B, the test piece is fitted with an clamped support on the $\mathrm{BAS}$ side and a horizontal X-movement on the $\mathrm{HAUT}$ side, while maintaining the constant lateral pressure in the side faces. The boundary conditions are therefore:

The condition d

- ${u}_{x}={u}_{y}={u}_{z}=0$ on mesh cell $\mathit{BAS}$ group

Side pressure conditions:

- ${P}_{n}=100\mathit{kPa}$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{DEVANT}$ , $\mathrm{ARRIERE}$ , $\mathrm{DROIT}$ and $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$ groups.

The loading conditions:

- ${P}_{n}=100\mathit{kPa}$ on the mesh cell $\mathrm{HAUT}$ group applied from the first moment of calculation and maintained thereafter
- ${u}_{x}=-0,2m$ on the mesh cell $\mathrm{HAUT}$ group applied progressively by a ramp function

The load is therefore carried out in two phases:

- A isotropic stress state, ${P}_{o}=100\mathrm{kPa}$ , is initially assigned to mesh cells $\mathrm{HAUT}$ , $\mathrm{DEVANT}$ , $\mathrm{ARRIERE}$ , $\mathrm{DROIT}$ and $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$ .
- Horizontal displacement according to X is imposed on the mesh-cell group $\mathrm{HAUT}$ and varies between $t=0.$ and $t=10.$ from ${u}_{x}=0.$ to ${u}_{x}=-0.2m$.

### 1.4 Results

The solutions are post-treated with point $C$ , in terms of ${\sigma}_{xy}$ stress , ${\varepsilon}_{v}$ total volume deformation and $\left({r}_{\text{ela}}^{\text{iso},m}+{r}_{\text{iso}}^{m}\right)$ isotropic and $\left({r}_{\text{ela}}^{d,m}+{r}_{\text{dev}}^{m}\right)$ deviatory nutring coefficients .
