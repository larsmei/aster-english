# v7.32.113 WTNP113 - Resaturation of a cell

**Summary:**

The test presented here is used to verify the correct functioning of the operators used for the resolution of the equations of unsaturated flow. This test corresponds to test 3.2 of the qualification plan of the ALLLIANCES [bib1] project.

It represents the evolution of the saturation of the medium around a storage cell. Two phases are taken into account, a deaturation phase during the operation of the underground structure and a phase of resaturation after the embankment of the gallery of the cells.

### 1.3 Initial conditions

The problem is two-phased:

- A first phase of 15 years of deaturation corresponding to the operation of the underground structure.
- A second phase of resaturation after the resaturation of the alveole corresponding to the holding (we initialize the saturation of the alveole at 0.7).

The initial conditions are as follows:

For Phase 1

- Alveole ${P}_{c}=9,4{.10}^{7}\mathit{Pa}$ ($S=0,49$ )
- Geological barrier ${P}_{c}={1.10}^{5}\mathit{Pa}$ ($S=0,999$ )

For phase 2 ($t>15\mathit{ans}$ )

- Alveole ${P}_{c}=3,015{.10}^{7}\mathit{Pa}$ ($S=0,7$ )

### 1.4 boundary conditions

They express themselves on the capillary pressure.

Phase 1:

On $[\mathit{AB}]$ ${P}_{c}={1.10}^{5}\mathit{Pa}$

On $[\mathit{CB}]$ No hydraulic flow

On $[\mathit{CD}]$ ${P}_{c}={1.10}^{5}\mathit{Pa}$

On $[\mathit{A01}]\cup [\mathrm{02D}]$ No hydraulic flow

On the whole of the ${P}_{c}=9,4{.10}^{7}\mathit{Pa}$ alveole ($S=0,49$ ).

Phase 2:

On $[\mathit{AB}]$ ${P}_{c}={1.10}^{5}\mathit{Pa}$

On $[\mathit{CB}]$ No hydraulic flow

On $[\mathit{CD}]$ ${P}_{c}={1.10}^{5}\mathit{Pa}$

On $[\mathit{AD}]$ No hydraulic flow
