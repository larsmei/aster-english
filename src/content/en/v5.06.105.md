# v5.06.105 SDNS105 – Simulation of a dynamic pull-out test with cohesive elements

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate elements with CZM_EXP_REG cohesive zone in nonlinear dynamics. A test of a square plate attached to a cohesive surface with thermal effects is simulated. This plate is subjected to a tensile force. The joint is heated beforehand and obeys a RUPT_FRAG_F0 fragile fracture law characteristic of the elements*.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The opening is modelled by a nodal force exerted on the point A $15.18750\mathit{E8}N$ .

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Initial acceleration and zero initial speed. Simulate the problem from 0 to 0.01s.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The test case is non-regression. It is used to perform dynamic CZM_EXP_REG functionality checks.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The internal variables VARI_NOEU are tested:

- V2: dissipation indicator (0 = discharge,1=dissipation)
- V4: damage indicator or percentage of dissipated surface energy (0 healthy joint, 1 broken joint, 0<V4<1 damaged joint)

Nodes tested on the seal opening line at the moment $0,00825s$:

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Non-regression tests.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

D_PLAN Modelling is used. A standard NEWMARK schema in DEPLACEMENT formulation is used.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 3810 elements type TRIA3 for the plate and 50 elements QUAD4 for the cohesive elements.
