# v1.01.294 ZZZZ294 – Validation of the position of sub-points of 3D plates

**Summary:**

This test validates the calculation of the position of the subpoints of integration in the global benchmark for modellings DKT, DST, COQUE_3D and GRILLE_EXCENTRE. An elementary mechanical calculation is performed to allow the creation of a table with CREA_TABLE from the result. Only the coordinates of a few subpoints are tested in the table.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The position of the nodes, integration points and integration subpoints is calculated from their coordinates in the local axes in the final position of the plate, and from the matrices of passage between the local axes and the global axes

The rotation around the $Z$ axis is made from the following matrix:

$\mathrm{Tz}(\alpha )=\left[\begin{array}{}\cos(\alpha )\\ \sin(\alpha )\\ 0\end{array}\begin{array}{}-\sin(\alpha )\\ \cos(\alpha )\\ 0\end{array}\begin{array}{}0\\ 0\\ 1\end{array}\right]$

The rotation around the $X'$ axis is made from the following matrix:

$\mathrm{Tx}'(\beta )=\left[\begin{array}{}1\\ 0\\ 0\end{array}\begin{array}{}0\\ \cos(\beta )\\ \sin(\beta )\end{array}\begin{array}{}0\\ -\sin(\beta )\\ \cos(\beta )\end{array}\right]$

For any point of the initial coordinates $(X,Y,Z)$, the coordinates expressed in the global $(X',Y',Z')$ reference mark can be calculated after rotation with the following transformation:

$\left[\begin{array}{}X'\\ Y'\\ Z'\end{array}\right]=\left[\mathrm{Tz}(\alpha )\right]\left[\mathrm{Tx}'(\beta )\right]\left[\begin{array}{}X\\ Y\\ Z\end{array}\right]$

## 7 Summary of the results

The main purpose of this test is to verify whether the positions of the sub-integration points of the DKT, DST, COQUE_3D and GRILLE_EXCENTRE Modelling are well calculated.

For this Modelling, the maximum error found is $3.0E-07\text{\%}$ .
