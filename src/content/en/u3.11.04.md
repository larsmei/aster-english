# u3.11.04 Modellings POU_D_TG, POU_D_TGM

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings POU_D_TG and POU_D_TGM:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear capabilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings

The POU_D_TG modelling corresponds to a formulation of Timoshenko beam elements taking into account a modelling of the warping of the section (cf. [R3.08.03](doc:r3.08.03)).

The POU_D_TGM modelling is based on the same formulation and makes it possible to take into account a non-linear constitutive behaviour of multi-fibre type.

They can be used for beam problems in isotropic linear mechanical analysis and in elastoplasticity.

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

The degrees of freedom are, at each node of the support mesh cell, the six displacement components (three translations and three rotations) plus one component (GRX) relating to the warping of the section with respect to the neutral fibre (cf. [R3.08.03](doc:r3.08.03)).

| **Finite element** | **Degrees of freedom (at each vertex node)** |  |  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| POU_D_TG | DX | DY | DZ | DRX | DRY | DRZ | GRX |

### 1.2 Support mesh cell of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the finite elements, in a displacement formulation, are two-node segments SEG2:

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Finite element** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| POU_D_TG | SEG2 | MECA_POU_D_TG |  |
| POU_D_TGM | SEG2 | MECA_POU_D_TGM |  |

## 2 Assignment of the characteristics

For these 1D structural elements, it is necessary to assign geometric characteristics which are complementary to the mesh data. The definition of these data is carried out with the command AFFE_CARA_ELEM associated with the following factor keywords:

**•** **POUTRE**

Makes it possible to define and to assign the characteristics of the cross-section.

Supported modellings: POU_D_TG, POU_D_TGM

**•** **ORIENTATION**

Makes it possible to define and to assign the orientation of the principal axes of inertia about the neutral fibre.

Supported modellings: POU_D_TG, POU_D_TGM

## 3 Supported loadings

The specific loadings, available in AFFE_CHAR_MECA, are the following:

**•** **CONTACT**

Makes it possible to define the zones subjected to contact conditions.

Supported modellings: POU_D_TG, POU_D_TGM

**•** **EPSI_INIT**

Makes it possible to apply an initial-strain loading.

Supported modelling: POU_D_TG

**•** **FORCE_POUTRE**

Makes it possible to apply line forces

Supported modellings: POU_D_TG, POU_D_TGM

**•** **PESANTEUR**

Makes it possible to apply a gravity-type loading.

Supported modellings: POU_D_TG, POU_D_TGM

## 4 Non-linear capabilities

### 4.1 Constitutive laws

The constitutive laws specific to these modellings, usable under COMPORTEMENT in STAT_NON_LINE and DYNA_NON_LINE, are the following (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)):

**/** **'VMIS_CINE_LINE'**

Supported modelling: POU_D_TGM

**/** **'VMIS_ISOT_LINE'**

Supported modelling: POU_D_TGM

**/** **'VMIS_ISOT_TRAC'**

Supported modelling: POU_D_TGM

**/** **'GRAN_IRRA_LOG'**

Supported modelling: POU_D_TGM

**Note:**

It is also possible for these modellings, which use a one-dimensional stress state, to use the 3D constitutive behaviours (thanks to the De Borst method [R5.03.03](doc:r5.03.03)).

### 4.2 Strains

The strains available, used in the constitutive relations under the keyword DEFORMATION for the operators STAT_NON_LINE and DYNA_NON_LINE, are (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)):

**/** **'PETIT'**

The strains used for the constitutive relation are the linearised strains.

**/** **'PETIT_REAC'**

The strain increments used in the incremental constitutive relation are the linearised strains of the displacement increment in the updated geometry.

**Note:**

*Warning: the computation of the strains by means of* *PETIT_REAC is only an approximation of the large-displacement assumption. It requires very small loading increments to be carried out. In order to take large displacements correctly into account, and above all large rotations, it is recommended to use the modelling* *POU_D_T_GD.*
