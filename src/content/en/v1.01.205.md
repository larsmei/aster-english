# v1.01.205 ZZZZ205 – Calculation of kinetic energy of a rectangular plate

**Summary:**

This test case is intended to validate the kinetic energy calculation for the massive modellings 2D.

Only one model is performed consisting of mesh cells QUAD4 and TRIA3.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.205/Shape1.gif)

### 1.2 Material properties

- Steel
- $E=\mathrm{2x1011}\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$

### 1.3 boundary conditions

Calculation of kinetic energy from speed ${E}_{c}=\frac{1}{2}{V}^{T}MV$:

- A uniform speed is imposed:
- $t=\mathrm{1s}$: $X$ $1.5m/s$
- $t=\mathrm{2s}$: $X$ $1.5m/s$ and $Y$ $2.5m/s$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The solution is analytical. Kinetic energy is derived from the speed:

${E}_{c}=\frac{1}{2}{V}^{T}MV$
