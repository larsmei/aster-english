# v3.01.120 SSLL120 – Internal force calculation beams at nodes: EFGE_ELNO

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check the calculation of the internal nodes forces of modellings: p_d_t, p_d_e, p_d_tg, p_d_em, p_d_tgm.

EFGE_ELNO are always expressed in the local elements marker.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry A and B

$A$ geometry is in the $\mathit{XY}$ plane and consists of 3 sections.

![Figure](/figures/v3.01.120/1000020100000519000001F1B76BE185D18C208D.png)

**Figure** 1.1-a **:** $A$ Geometry , in the $\mathit{XY}$ plane .

The coordinates of nodes, $A$ geometry, in the global reference:

| **node** | **X** | **Y** | **Z** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **B** | L | 0 | 0 |
| **C** | 2*L | L | 0 |
| **D** | 2*L | 3*L | 0 |

$B$ geometry corresponds to $A$ geometry, to which the following 3 successive rotations are applied:

- around the $Z$ axis , a rotation of $25.0°$ , which gives the new ${X}_{1},{Y}_{1},{Z}_{1}$ benchmark .
- around the ${Y}_{1}$ axis , a rotation of $-40.0°$ , which gives the new ${X}_{2},{Y}_{2},{Z}_{2}$ benchmark .
- around the ${X}_{2}$ axis , a rotation of $27.5°$ .

![Figure](/figures/v3.01.120/10000201000002B5000002B8BA94817B9EE07E42.png)

**Figure** 1.1-b **: Geometry B.**

### 1.2 Loading

#### 1.2.1 boundary conditions

In each case, the node $A$ is clamped and node $D$ is used to impose effort and moments.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Internal modelling efforts A

At node $D$ the load is the ${T}_{D}=({F}_{x},{F}_{y},{F}_{z},{M}_{x},{M}_{y},{M}_{z})$ stress torso

The internal stress torsors, expressed in the local elements marker, are:

- For beam 1 from node $A$ to node $B$:

${\mathit{Fa}}_{1}=[\mathit{Fx},\mathit{Fy},\mathit{Fz}]$

${\mathit{Ma}}_{1}=[{M}_{x}+3\ast {F}_{z}\ast L,{M}_{y}-2\ast {F}_{z}\ast L,{M}_{z}+2\ast {F}_{y}\ast L-3\ast {F}_{x}\ast L]$

${\mathit{Fb}}_{1}=[{F}_{x},{F}_{y},{F}_{z}]$

${\mathit{Mb}}_{1}=[{M}_{x}+3\ast {F}_{z}\ast L,{M}_{y}-{F}_{z}\ast L,{M}_{z}+{F}_{y}\ast L-3\ast {F}_{x}\ast L]$

- For the beam 2 from node $B$ to node $C$:

${\mathit{Fb}}_{2}=[({F}_{y}+{F}_{x})/\sqrt{(2)},({F}_{y}-{F}_{x})/\sqrt{(2)},{F}_{z}]$

${\mathit{Mb}}_{2}=[({M}_{y}+{M}_{x}+2\ast {F}_{z}\ast L)/\sqrt{(2)},({M}_{y}-{M}_{x}-4\ast {F}_{z}\ast L)/\sqrt{(2)},({M}_{z}+{F}_{y}\ast L-3\ast {F}_{x}\ast L)]$

${\mathit{Fc}}_{2}=[({F}_{y}+{F}_{x})/\sqrt{(2)},({F}_{y}-{F}_{x})/\sqrt{(2)},{F}_{z}]$

${\mathit{Mc}}_{2}=[({M}_{y}+{M}_{x}+2\ast {F}_{z}\ast L)/\sqrt{(2)},({M}_{y}-{M}_{x}-2\ast {F}_{z}\ast L)/\sqrt{(2)},{M}_{z}-2\ast {F}_{x}\ast L]$

- For the 3 beam from node $C$ to node $D$:

${\mathit{Fc}}_{3}=[{F}_{y},-{F}_{x},{F}_{z}]$

${\mathit{Mc}}_{3}=[{M}_{y},(-{M}_{x}-2\ast {F}_{z}\ast L),({M}_{z}-2\ast {F}_{x}\ast L)]$

${\mathit{Fd}}_{3}=[{F}_{y},-{F}_{x},{F}_{z}]$

${\mathit{Md}}_{3}=[{M}_{y},-{M}_{x},{M}_{z}]$

The elements local marker is the default: the $X$ local axis is carried by the SEG2, the $Z$ local axis is the $Z$ global.

## 3 Modelling A & B

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The modellings POU_D_E, POU_D_T, POU_D_TG, POU_D_EM and POU_D_TGM are rotated on the mesh.

The length is $L=1.5m$ .

The generalised forces depend only on the forces imposed, they are independent of the geometric characteristics of the section and the material. The selected section is circular of a radius of $0.10m$, of a thickness of $0.05m$.

For $A$ modelling , the stress torsor applied to the node $D$ , to the following components:

$\begin{array}{c}{F}_{x}=-1000N;{F}_{y}=5000N;{F}_{z}=10000N\\ {M}_{x}=830\mathit{Nm};{M}_{y}=1800\mathit{Nm};{M}_{z}=5300\mathit{Nm}\end{array}$

For $B$ modelling, the components of the stress torsor applied to node $D$ are derived from the formulae given in § 2.2. The formulae are written in the command file.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh consists of one mesh cell SEG2 per segment.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The values tested are the same regardless of beam Modelling and whatever geometry, as efge_elno are expressed in the local elements markers.

The 36 values of the torso components are tested.

The analytical expressions, as described in section 2.1 , are in the command file and are used to test all calculated values and all reference values in the TEST_RESU.

## 4 Summary of the results

For each model processed, the results are analytical solution.
