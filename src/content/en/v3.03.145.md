# v3.03.145 SSLS145 - Calculation of the securing of a flat plate loaded in its plane

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the COMBINAISON_FERRAILLAGE macro command which calculates the dimensioning ferrage density between several loading cases.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A rectangular plate of dimensions is considered:

- thickness $B=30\mathit{cm}$
- length $H=3m$
- height $L=12m$

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.145/100002010000032000000116E3EC5DBFAE1C86E2.png)

Figure 1. Géométrie de la plaque rectangulaire.

### 1.2 Material properties

The **beton** is isotropic elastic with the following material properties:

- $E=30000N/{\mathit{mm}}^{2}$
- $\nu =0.3$

For the calculation of the railing (at Eurocode 2), the following set of properties shall be considered:

- Lower and upper coating ${c}_{inf}={c}_{\sup}=20\mathit{mm}$
- Characteristic resistance to compression of ${f}_{\mathit{ck}}=35\mathit{MPa}$ concrete
- Nutring limit characteristic of steel ${f}_{\mathit{yk}}=450\mathit{MPa}$
- Type of diagram (ε-ε): ‘B1'
- Safety coefficient of steel with the ELU (Fondamental and Accidental) ${\gamma}_{s}=1,15$
- Concrete safety coefficient at the ELU (Fondamental and Accidental) ${\gamma}_{c}=1,5$
- Limiting stress of concrete to l-ELS Feature ${\sigma}_{\text{c,lim}}=1,0\times 35=35\mathit{MPa}$
- Limiting steel constraint to the ELS Feature ${\sigma}_{\text{s,lim}}=1,0\times 450=450\mathit{MPa}$
- Limiting stress of concrete to l-ELS Quasi Permanent ${\sigma}_{\text{c,lim,qp}}=1,0\times 35=35\mathit{MPa}$
- Coefficient of equivalence steel-beton to l-ELS ${\alpha}_{E}=15,0$
- Steel class: ‘B
- $\mathit{FERR}\text{\_}\mathit{SYME}='\mathit{NON}'$ / $\mathit{FERR}\text{\_}\mathit{COMP}='\mathit{OUI}'$ / $\mathit{EPURE}\text{\_}\mathit{CISA}='\mathit{NON}'$
- Volumetric mass of steel ${\rho}_{\mathit{acier}}=7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$
- Maximum crack opening permitted in front of lower than l-l-ELS QP ${w}_{\text{max,inf}}=0,25\mathit{mm}$
- Maximum crack opening permitted in the upper face of the ELS QP ${w}_{\text{max,sup}}=0,3\mathit{mm}$
- Load time coefficient for calculation at l-ELS QP ${K}_{T}=0,4$
- Diameter of the following bars ‘X' for calculation at l-l-ELS QP ${\varphi}_{X}=20,0\mathit{mm}$
- Diameter of the following bars ‘Y' for calculation at l-l-ELS QP ${\varphi}_{Y}=25,0\mathit{mm}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The plate is loaded in its plane by a ${F}_{Z}=-1,5\cdot {10}^{6}N$ shear force in the first load case (nodal loading "**shearLd1**" in the command file). This load case will be tested at the ELS QUASI PERMANENT.

A second load case (nodal loading " **shearLd2**" in the command file) is obtained by applying double the load, ${F}_{Z}=-3\cdot {10}^{6}N$. This load case will be tested at the ELS CARACTERISTIQUE.

A third load case (nodal loading "**shearLd3**" in the command file) is obtained by applying the triple of this force, i.e. ${F}_{Z}=-4,5\cdot {10}^{6}N$. This load case will be tested at l-l-ELU.

The plate is then loaded into its plane by a traction force ${F}_{Y}=1\cdot {10}^{6}N$ (nodal loading " **tractLd1**" in the control file). This load case will be tested at the ELS QUASI PERMANENT.

A second load case (nodal loading " **tractLd2**" in the command file) is obtained by applying double this force, ${F}_{Y}=2\cdot {10}^{6}N$ . This load case will be tested at the ELS CARACTERISTIQUE.

A third load case (nodal loading " **tractLd3**" in the command file) is obtained by applying the triple of this force, ${F}_{Y}=3\cdot {10}^{6}N$. This load case will be tested at l-ELU.

The plate is finally loaded into its plane by a compression force ${F}_{Y}=-1,5\cdot {10}^{6}N$ in a seventh load case (nodal loading " **comprLd1**" in the command file). This load case will be tested at the ELS CARACTERISTIQUE.

A last load case (nodal loading " **comprLd2**" in the command file) is obtained by applying double the load, ${F}_{Y}=-3\cdot {10}^{6}N$ . This load case will be tested at l-l-ELU.

The plate is clamped on the left side, at the opposite end of the point force application (); thus, all degrees of freedom are zeroed.

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.145/1000020100000227000000DC547B1B1F538A532A.png)

Figure 2. Blocage et chargement pour les cas ‘shearLd’

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The structure is initially at rest.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

For both loading cases, the railing is calculated using the Capra-Maury method (command CALC_FERRAILLAGE called by the COMBINAISON_FERRAILLAGE command).

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

This system is equivalent to an clamped beam and charged in flexion at the free end:

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.145/1000000000000320000000C26FCF64C70EE3EC59.png)

Figure 3. Représentation d'une poutre encastrée et chargée en flexion

An initial estimate of displacement and effort can therefore be obtained with a Modelling of Euler-Bernoulli:

- Deflection: $f=\frac{1}{3}\cdot \frac{F{L}^{3}}{EI}$ , such as $\mathit{EI}=30000\cdot {10}^{6}\times (\frac{{3,0}^{3}\cdot 0,3}{12})=20,25\cdot {10}^{9}N{m}^{2}$

Either:

$f=\frac{1}{3}\cdot (\frac{{12,0}^{3}}{20,25\cdot {10}^{9}})\times F=2,84\cdot {10}^{-8}\times F$

- Shearing: $V=F$
- bending moment à l'clamped support: $M=F\times L$

Thus, the following results are obtained:

|  | ShearLd1 | ShearLd2 | ShearLd3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F | $-1,5\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $-3\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $-4,5\cdot {10}^{6}N$ |
| V | $1,5\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $3\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $4,5\cdot {10}^{6}N$ |
| M | $-1,8\cdot {10}^{7}\mathit{Nm}$ | $-3,6\cdot {10}^{7}\mathit{Nm}$ | $-5,4\cdot {10}^{7}\mathit{Nm}$ |
| f | $42,6\mathit{mm}$ | $85,2\mathit{mm}$ | $127,8\mathit{mm}$ |

For cases with axial loading (tractLd and comprLd), the beam shrinkage/elongation shall be obtained analytically as follows:

- Deflection: $\Delta L=\frac{F\cdot L}{E\cdot S}$ , such as $\mathit{ES}=30000\cdot {10}^{6}\times 3\times 0,3=2,7\cdot {10}^{10}N$

Either:

$\Delta L=4,44\cdot {10}^{-10}\times F$

- Normal effort requesting beam: $N=-F$ (normal positive compression effort).

Thus, the following results are obtained:

|  | tractLd1 | tractLd2 | tractLd3 | comprLd1 | comprLd2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F | $+1,0\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $+2,0\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $+3,0\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $-1,5\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $-3,0\cdot {10}^{6}N$ |
| N | $-1,0\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $-2,0\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $-3,0\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $+1,5\cdot {10}^{6}N$ | $+3,0\cdot {10}^{6}N$ |
| f | $+0,44\mathit{mm}$ | $+0,89\mathit{mm}$ | $+1,33\mathit{mm}$ | $-0,67\mathit{mm}$ | $-1,33\mathit{mm}$ |

For the calculation of the railing using the Capra Maury method, analogue resonance is applied to the simple analytical cases proposed in the test case for this method, namely ssls134.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

The solution is analytical. The differences between analytical solution and reference solution in non-regression are due to the fact that the beam is not slender enough (high $H/L$ ratio) and therefore we are approaching the limits of the Euler-Bernoulli model.
