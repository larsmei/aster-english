# u3.12.02 Modellings COQUE_AXIS

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modelling COQUE_AXIS:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear possibilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings.

The thermo-elasto-plastic modelling COQUE_AXIS corresponds to a formulation derived from the 3D models with a shell kinematics [R3.07.02](doc:r3.07.02).

It can be used to model structures whose middle surface has a particular geometry:

- shells with rotational symmetry about the axis $\mathit{OY}$ ,

The thermomechanical analyses are chained from the thermal shell finite elements (see [U3.22.01](doc:u3.22.01)).

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

For the three shell modellings, the discretisation degrees of freedom are, at each node of the support mesh cell, the three displacement components (two translations and one rotation). The nodes are assumed to belong to the middle surface of the shell.

| **Finite element** | **Degrees of freedom (at each vertex node)** |
| --- | --- |

| MECXSE3 | DX | DY | DRZ |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

### 1.2 Support mesh cell of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the finite elements, in displacement formulation, are three-node segments:

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Finite element** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| COQUE_AXIS | SEG3 | MECXSE3 |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

### 1.3 Support mesh cell of the loadings

All the loadings applicable on the middle surface of the shell elements are treated by direct discretisation on the support mesh cell of the element in displacement formulation.

*No loading support mesh cell is therefore necessary for the faces of the shell elements.*

For the imposed displacements, the support mesh cells are mesh cells reduced to a point.

## 2 Assignment of the characteristics

For these 2D structural elements, it is necessary to assign geometrical characteristics which are complementary to the mesh data. The definition of these data is carried out with the command AFFE_CARA_ELEM associated with the following factor keyword:

- **COQUE**

Makes it possible to define and assign to the mesh cells the thickness, the shear coefficient, the eccentricity, ...

## 3 Supported loadings

The available loadings are the following:

- **'FORCE_COQUE'**

Makes it possible to apply surface forces.

- **'PRES_REP'**

Makes it possible to apply a pressure to a continuum domain.

- **'ROTATION'**

Makes it possible to define the rotation speed and the rotation vector.

- **'PESANTEUR'**

Makes it possible to apply a gravity-type loading.

- The application of a thermal expansion loading is carried out by defining the factor keyword AFFE_VARC under AFFE_MATERIAU[U4.43.03].

## 4 Non-linear possibilities

### 4.1 Constitutive laws

The constitutive laws specific to these modellings, usable under COMPORTEMENT in STAT_NON_LINE and DYNA_NON_LINE, are the plane-stress constitutive relations available with the modelling 'AXIS' (*cf.* [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).

### 4.2 Strains

Only the linearised strains, keyword 'PETIT' under DEFORMATION, are available in the constitutive relations (*cf.* [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).
