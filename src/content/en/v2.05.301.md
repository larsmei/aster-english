# v2.05.301 SDLX301 - Unsymmetrical floor-column building with horizontal excitation

**Summary:**

This is the three-dimensional study of a 3-floor building on 9 columns, clamped at the base of the columns, with excreted mass, subjected to horizontal seismic excitation while moving. The excreted distribution of the masses of the floors allows to break symmetry, to couple the main geometric directions and to generate a twist effect. The reference values are obtained with the code CASTEM2000 and SAMCEF, which have slightly different methods.

The columns are modelled by beams, and the floors by elements flat hulls. The first eight eigenmodes are kept for modal recombination calculations. Objective: to test the displacements, internal forces, and reactions to the clamped support column for CQC, SRSS, DSC modal recombination. Accuracy of the results: comparison between codes. Strong tolerances are allowed for some calculated fields with values of several orders of quantity lower.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

**boundary condition**

The columns are clamped at foundation level.

**Charge**

The earthquake is applied in the direction $x$ .

The oscillator response spectrum on the move is obtained by superposition of four displacement spectra. Each of these displacement spectra corresponds to the response of an oscillator to an degree of freedom to the sinusoidal excitations defined in the table below:

$\mathrm{SD}(f,\xi )=\underset{i=1}{\overset{4}{\Sigma}}\frac{{K}_{i}}{4{\pi}^{2}{f}^{2}\sqrt{{(1-\frac{{f}_{i}^{2}}{{f}^{2}})}^{2}+4{\xi}^{2}\frac{{f}_{i}^{2}}{{f}^{2}}}}$

In particular, the frequencies and depreciations used are close to the first four modes of the structure.

|  | **Frequency** ${f}_{i}(\mathrm{Hz})$ | **Amplitude** ${K}_{i}(m)$ | **Amortization** $\xi$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sinus 1 | 1.51 | 0.15 | 0.05 |
| sinus 2 | 2.05 | 0.25 | 0.05 |
| sinus 3 | 2.34 | 0.25 | 0.05 |
| sinus 4 | 4.86 | 0.30 | 0.05 |

The neglected modes are represented by a pseudo-mode.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics et du mesh

![Figure](/figures/v2.05.301/10000201000002C100000269B2B922DFB5CA4F0C.png)

The mesh of the model calculated with *code_aster* consists of 3357 nodes and 3387 mesh cells of which 135 straight beam elements of Timoshenko (including 12 SEG2 per column, i.e. 108 for columns) and 3072 elements DKT plate (1024 per floor). To ensure continuity of the degrees of freedom DRZ beams' own rotation with the rotation around the normal of the plates (not automatically provided by *code_aster* ), elements beams are added locally to the edge of the DKT plates, at the level of the 27 column-floor connections (mesh-cell group 'JONCTIONS' in the control file), to ensure the transmission of DRZ rotational rotations related to the plane of the rotating plate in the $(x,y)$ plane.

The mesh model calculated with CASTEM 2000 consists of 3765 nodes and 7368 elements including 108 elements straight beam from Timoshenko and 6960 elements from DKT hull.

The mesh model calculated with SAMCEF consists of 3360 nodes and 3180 elements including 108 elements straight beam from Mindlin and 3072 elements from Mindlin.

## 5 Summary of the results

**Comparison with CASTEM 2000:**

The differences on the eigenfrequencies calculated with CASTEM 2000 and Aster are less than $\text{1,4 \%}$ . The double mode was separated into two close modes (6 and 7) of which one is a dominant mode according to $y$ (mode 6) and the other according to $x$ (mode 7); the difference on the actual modal masses (in $\text{\%}$) very high according to $x$ for mode 6 and $y$ for mode 7 is irrelevant given the low weight of these directions in the modes considered.

The differences obtained on the calculation using the spectral method, for displacements, remain generally below $\text{8 \%}$ , the differences on the reactions to l-clamped support in columns B and E are generally below $\text{11 \%}$ (excluding the reaction moment according to z), and the differences on the generalized forces remain globally below $\text{7 \%}$ (without regard to the torsion moment).

Strong tolerances are allowed for some calculated fields with values of several orders of quantity lower.

**Comparison with SAMCEF:**

The resolution method adopted in SAMCEF is based on the node earth method. This method consists of binding all the nodes that are solidary to the foundation to a single node. This node is affected by a translation mass that is 1000 times the mass of the structure. The displacements carried over in the tables are not corrected for the residual mass effects which are also available results.

Differences on the eigenfrequencies calculated with SAMCEF and Aster are less than $\text{3,2 \%}$ . The type of hull used (deformable or not to the shear force) influences the result, the same is true of the fineness of the mesh of the floors. differences on eigenfrequencies up to $\text{10 \%}$ were observed by initially taking a rougher mesh for floors, consisting of 345 nodes and 516 elements including 108 straight beam elements of Timoshenko and 408 elements DKT hull. Modes 6 and 7 represent a double mode with an effective modal mass percentage not exceeding $\text{4 \%}$ in the direction $x$ and $\text{2 \%}$ in the direction $y$ .

Differences obtained on calculation with the spectral method, for displacements in the direction of excitation remain generally below $\text{10,5 \%}$. For $B$ column clamped support reactions, these differences are generally below $\text{30 \%}$. They reach $\text{80 \%}$ for the $E$ column, however for $x$ axis reaction and $y$ axis moment, they remain below $\text{18 \%}$. The torsion reaction of the columns is not zero. differences about the generalized excitation-direction forces remain globally below $\text{26 \%}$. However, a different coupling between the excitation directions introduces differences important on the forces in the transverse directions to excitation.

Strong tolerances are allowed for some calculated fields with values of several orders of quantity lower.

**Notes:**

- the shape of the function describing the spectrum on the move depends heavily on the ${f}_{i}$ eigenfrequencies for which the displacement peaks are given. Consequently, a offset of the calculated eigenfrequencies disturbs the seismic response at the input of the data and does not allow an effective comparison of the calculations,
- the results of generalized effort are expressed in the local beam marker and corrected for static effects.
