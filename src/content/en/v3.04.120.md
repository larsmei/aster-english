# v3.04.120 SSLV120 - Orthotropic parallelepipeda stretched under its own weight

**Summary:**

This structural mechanical test allows the evaluation of the displacements and stresses of a parallelepiped deforming under its own weight. The material is orthotropic linear elastic. The model is three-dimensional. The model is similar to the VPCS SSLV07 test (but in this case the material is isotropic) and the SSLV121 test (in this case the material isotropic transverse).

The differences of the results obtained by *Aster* are between 0.00 and 0.5% of the analytically calculated reference.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is derived from the SSLV07/89 plug in the VPCS guide (considering in addition an orthotropic elastic matrix). The analytical expression of the solution is as follows:

Travel:

$u=-\frac{{\nu}_{\text{NL}}\rho gxz}{{E}_{N}}$

$v=-\frac{{\nu}_{\text{NT}}\rho gyz}{{E}_{N}}$

$w=\frac{\rho g{z}^{2}}{2{E}_{N}}+\frac{\rho g}{2{E}_{N}}({\nu}_{\text{NL}}{x}^{2}+{\nu}_{\text{NT}}{y}^{2})-\frac{\rho g{L}^{2}}{2{E}_{N}}$

Constraints:

${\sigma}_{zz}=\rho gz$ ${\sigma}_{zz}={\sigma}_{yy}={\sigma}_{xy}={\sigma}_{yz}={\sigma}_{\mathrm{zx}}=0$

## 4 Summary of the results

The displacement and stress results are very similar to analytical solution with the adopted model (<0.2% for displacements, < 0.5% for stresses).

The elastic coefficients in the 3 orthotropy directions were chosen to obtain the same values of displacement at $B$ , $C$ , $D$ and $E$ as those calculated for an isotropic material (test SSLV007) or isotropic transverse (test SSLV121). Digitally, these values are very similar to those of these tests at the points considered (in the order of ${10}^{-6}$ ) the difference resulting from the construction mode of the stiffness matrices in the various cases. At point $X$, these values differ but correspond well to the reference solution.
