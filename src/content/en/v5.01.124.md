# v5.01.124 SDND124 – Seismic Excitation of a Discreet Affected Behaviour DIS_ECRO_TRAC

**Summary:**

The response of the DIS_ECRO_TRAC nonlinear behavior model, formulated on discrete elements for mesh cells SEG2, is tested.

DYNA_NON_LINE and DYNA_VIBRA operators are used for validation. Discrete d的elements response is analyzed for nonlinear constitutive law under seismic loading.

The modellings and elements discreet tested are in $\mathrm{3D}$ with DIS_T Modelling on a mesh cell SEG2.

This behavior is also validated for non-dynamic stresses in the test case SSND117 [V6.08.117](doc:v6.08.117) with the STAT_NON_LINE operator.

### 1.4 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.4.1 modellings A et B

The nodes N1, N2, N3 are blocked in the Y and Z directions.

For modellings A and B, nodes N1 and N3 are subject to $U(t)$ displacement in the X direction.

For Modelling B, nodes N1 and N3 are subjected to $U(t)$ displacement, $V(t)$ speed and $\gamma (t)$ acceleration in the X direction.

The condition on the move depends on the time:

$U(t)={U}_{0}.\sin(2\pi .\mathit{f.t})/(2\pi .f)$ with $f=0.5\mathit{Hz}$ and ${U}_{0}=\mathrm{6.0mm}$

The condition in speed depends on time: $V(t)\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}=\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\dot{U}(t)$

The condition in acceleration time function: $\gamma (t)\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}=\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\ddot{U}(t)$

#### 1.4.2 modellings C, D, E et F

The nodes N1, N2, N3 are blocked in the X direction.

For Modelling C, D, E and F, nodes N1 and N3 are subjected to $U(t)$ displacement in the Y and Z directions.

For Modelling B, nodes N1 and N3 are subjected to $U(t)$ displacement, $V(t)$ speed and $\gamma (t)$ acceleration in Y and Z directions.

The conditions on the move are time functions:

$\mathit{Depl}={U}_{1.}\sin(2\pi .{f}_{1}.t)+{U}_{2.}\sin(2\pi .{f}_{2}.t)+{U}_{3.}\sin(2\pi .{f}_{3}.t)$

Following direction $Y$: $(u,f)=[(\mathrm{0.20,0}.80),(\mathrm{0.15,1}.50),(\mathrm{0.10,3}.00)]$

Following direction $Z$: $(u,f)=[(-\mathrm{0.20,0}.90),(\mathrm{0.15,2}.00),(-\mathrm{0.10,2}.80)]$

The condition in speed depends on time: $V(t)=\dot{U}(t)$

The condition in acceleration time function: $\gamma (t)=\ddot{U}(t)$

## 9 Summary of the results

These tests allow to check the smooth operation of the discreet elements with the DIS_ECRO_TRAC behavior in the context of use with the DYNA_NON_LINE and DYNA_VIBRA command.

The behavior is tested:

- in the $x$ direction of the discreet, with isotropic hardening.
- in the y and z directions, plane tangent to the discreet, with isotropic and kinematic nutring.

The STAT_NON_LINE command uses an implicit algorithm and calculates 1281 time step, without any step adjustment.

The DYNA_VIBRA command uses an explicit algorithm and adapts the step for the resolution of the schema in time, which leads to the calculation of 42796 time step.

Although the number of time step in the DYNA_VIBRA study is 33 times higher than the number of STAT_NON_LINE studies, the DYNA_VIBRA command is 10 times faster in CPU time than the STAT_NON_LINE command.

*Note:* kinematic hardening is not available in the local direction x of the discreet (no request and need to date).
