# v6.03.111 SSNP111 - Switching from Gauss to nodes points to quadratic elements points

**Summary:**

It is a nonlinear static mechanical test.

The purpose is to test, in the CALC_CHAMP command, the matrices that allow the switch from integration points to the nodes peaks. The case dealt with concerns a flat plate submitted on one of its faces at a pressure varying linearly.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The cumulative plastic deformation $P$ is equal to:

$P=\frac{{\sigma}_{L}-{\sigma}^{y}}{C}$

with: ${\sigma}_{L}$: node stress

${\sigma}^{y}$: yield strength

$C$: slope of the traction curve

Constraints are given by:

${\sigma}_{xx}({N}_{i})=-{P}_{\mathit{res}}({N}_{i})$

The plastic deformation is given by:

$\mid {\varepsilon}_{xx}^{p}({N}_{i})=P({N}_{i})\mid$

### 2.2 Baseline

The nodes ${N}_{2}$ and ${N}_{3}$ are calculated with uniaxial stress, plastic deformation, and cumulative plastic deformation.
