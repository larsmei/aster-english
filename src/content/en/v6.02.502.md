# v6.02.502 SSNL502 - Torch beam

**Summary:**

This test represents a stability calculation of a console beam subjected to compression effort at one end. It allows to validate the modellings elements finished:

- COQUE_3D with mesh cells TRIA7 and QUAD9,
- DKT with mesh cells TRIA3 and QUAD4,
- DKTG with mesh cells TRIA3 and QUAD4,
- POU_D_T_GD and POU_D_TGM with mesh cells SEG2.

in the quasi-static non-linear field in large displacements and rotations in the presence of instability (Flambment d-Euler).

The movements and moments obtained are compared with an analytical reference solution.

The critical load is well detected. The first two results for $P/{P}_{\mathit{cr}}=1.015$ and $1.063$ loads are correct, the maximum error is 3.5% for mesh cell TRIA7 and 2.2% for mesh cell QUAD9. The mesh cell QUAD9 gives better results.

If the elements of hulls are further calculated, the mesh cell QUAD9 continues to give better results. In the area where the DZ displacements are the most important, the error on the load reaches 9% on the quadrangles and goes up to 30% on the triangles. Errors increase in this area due to the slopes of the curves.

The A_CIS transverse shear correction coefficient was set to 0.833, corresponding to thick hulls. The value ($9000={10}^{6}\times H/L$ ) which should have been taken into account does not allow calculations to be performed. It introduces a poor conditioning of stiffness matrices by increasing their disparities.

The code beam solution, whether POU_D_T_GD or POU_D_TGM Modelling, provides very good results compared to the analytical solution reference. However, the calculation times are different, the POU_D_T_GD Modelling that is geometrically accurate leads to a calculation time almost 40% lower than the POU_D_TGM Modelling.
