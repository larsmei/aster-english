# v6.02.143 SSNL143 – Validation of relaxation law for pre-stressed steel cables

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the constitutive law steel cable relaxation used in pre-stressed concrete. Validation is performed in relation to the BPEL expressions. Three modellings are performed:

  1. Simulation of the relaxation of a cable (class 1, $k1=6.0E-03$ , $k2=1.10$ ) over a 4000-hour period, with $\mu =0.75$ .

  1. Simulation of the relaxation of a cable (class 2, $k1=8.0E-03$ , $k2=1.25$ ) over a 4000-hour period, with $\mu =0.75$ .

  1. Simulation of a relaxation with variable deformation of a cable over a period of 6 years. On the imposed range [0,1an] deformation ${\epsilon}_{0}$ corresponding to $\mu =0.75$ , on the range ]1year,3years] deformation imposed of $1.025\ast {\epsilon}_{0}$ , on the range ]3years,6years] deformation imposed of $1.050\ast {\epsilon}_{0}$ .

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The formulas used are from BPEL 83. They are also found in NFEN1992-1-1 October 2005, AFCEN ETC-C2010, with some small variants.

${\sigma}_{p}\left(x\right)={\sigma}_{pi}\left(x\right)\left(1.0\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}-\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\Delta {\sigma}_{p}\left(\mu ,t\right)\right)$

$\Delta {\sigma}_{p}\left(\mu ,t\right)={k}_{1}.{\rho}_{1000}{\left(\frac{t}{1000}\right)}^{\frac{3}{4}\left(1-\mu \right)}.{e}^{\frac{10\mu -7.5}{{k}_{2}}}$ with $\mu =\frac{{\sigma}_{pi}\left(x\right)}{{f}_{\mathit{prg}}}$ , $t$ is in hours.

In order to apply these formulae the deformation must remain **constant**. ${\sigma}_{pi}$ is the initial stress which corresponds to the initial deformation.

The validation will be done on the modellings ‘a' and ‘b', with these formulas. The test case ‘c' will be an test case of non-regression because we simulate a cable re-voltage, so the deformation will vary over time.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The quantity tested will be the constraint in the cable, at different times.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

No uncertainty, reference solution is analytical.

## 5 Modelling C

### 5.1 Modelling characteristics

The Modelling is BARRE type.

This Modelling simulates the relaxation of a cable with a variable deformation over a period of 6 years. On the imposed ${\epsilon}_{0}$ range [0.1an] deformation corresponding to $\mu =0.75$, on the imposed $\left]1\mathit{an},3\mathit{ans}\right]$ range $1.025\ast {\epsilon}_{0}$ deformation, on the imposed $\left]3\mathit{ans},6\mathit{ans}\right]$ range $1.050\ast {\epsilon}_{0}$ deformation.
