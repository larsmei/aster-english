# v6.04.144 SSNV144 - Flexion Cute in Large Travels

**Summary:**

This test validates the modelling of the hull bending phenomena in large displacements in the elastic or elastoplastic field: a pipe elbow, extended by straight pipes is subjected to bending in its plane. The piping is thick (similar to the elbows of the primary circuits). The reference solution is numerical: it is obtained with *Code_Aster* using mesh $\mathrm{3D}$ of the elbow.

Modelling is done with elements COQUE_3D in large displacements or elastoplasticity.

## 1 Reference problem

The elbow has a radius of curvature: ${R}_{c}=\mathrm{1.25m}$

The tubular section has for medium $R=\mathrm{395.5mm}$ radius and for $e=\mathrm{77mm}$ thickness.

### 1.1 Material properties

The material is elastoplastic with linear isotropic nutring.

$E=2.E11\mathrm{Pa}$

$\nu =0.3$

Limit of elasticity $\mathrm{SIGY}={200.10}^{6}\mathrm{Pa}$

$D-\mathrm{SIGM}-\mathrm{EPSI}={2.10}^{10}\mathrm{Pa}$ Hardening Module

### 1.2 Boundary conditions and loads

Clamped support in $A$ (corresponding to the pipe section named $\mathrm{CERCLE1}$).

$\mathrm{MZ}$ time imposed in $D$ (corresponding to $\mathrm{CERCLE2}$ ) growing up to:

| Increment 1 | $\mathrm{Mz}=308670215.2\mathrm{Nm}$ |
| --- | --- |

In the case of large displacement calculation, $\mathrm{Mz}$ is achieved in 10 equal increments

### 1.3 Initial conditions

Not applicable.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

In linear elasticity, cf [V6.02.117](doc:v6.02.117) is compared to other numerical results obtained with *Code_Aster* on a mesh $\mathrm{3D}$ of the elbow and straight parts, connected to the ends to straight beams. This mesh $\mathrm{3D}$ has 1024 mesh cells HEXA20. COQUE_3D Modelling gives close results.

### 2.2 Baseline

For a time applied $\mathrm{Mz}$ in $D$ , the $\mathrm{DY}$ displacement of the same point $D$ is:

| **Moment** | $\mathit{DY}$ **point** $D$ **(** $m$ **) (** 3D **)** | $\mathit{DY}$ **point** $D$ **(** $m$ **) (** COQUE_3D **)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.08670D+08 | 1.09349D–02 | 1.08875D–02 |

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Because the reference solution is numerical, the accuracy of $\text{2\%}$ can be evaluated from [§2.2] by comparison with $\mathrm{3D}$ and COQUE_3D solutions.
