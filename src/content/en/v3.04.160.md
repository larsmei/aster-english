# v3.04.160 SSLV160 - Two-sided beam subjected to nodal force on its neutral fibre

**Summary:**

This test validates the connection between 1D and 3D models, in the Arlequin frame (3D_POU_ARLEQUIN) [1].

This is a model of slender beam on two supports, subjected to nodal force.

The results of the calculations are compared with those obtained by Code_aster with a 1D reference model and a 1D-3D mixed model connected with the 3D_POU option. The results coincide perfectly with the reference solutions.

## 1 Reference problem

The objective of this test case is to validate the Arlequin 3-D beam connection in Code_Aster.

The results obtained are compared with those obtained from two modellings in Code_Aster:

- 1D reference mono-modelization
- 1D-3D mixed reference model with 3D_POU connection

### 1.1 Geometry

A thin slender structure of 0.25 m in length on the X axis and circular section is considered, and is supported on both ends at positions -0.1 m (backrest 1) and 0.15 m (backrest 2).

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.160/100002000000043800000160B5065B2DDF76972C.png)

**Image** 1.1-1 **: Rotor Geometry**

### 1.2 Material properties

The two-wired beam has a density of $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$.

The Young's modulus is $E=2.{10}^{11}N{m}^{-2}$ and the Poisson's ratio is $\nu =0,3$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The beam rests on two infinitely rigid supports:

- DX = DY = DZ = 0 at support level 1
- DX = DY = DZ = 0 at support level 2

Also, the rigid rotational body movement on the beam axis is blocked (DRX = 0) over the entire structure.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is derived from a 1D calculation with Code_Aster (see Modelling C).

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The two-folded structure of 0.25 m length, extending between X = -0.1 m and X = 0.15 m, is connected by volume in the cover area, which extends between the positions 0.0 m and 0.05 m, to a 3D model by the 3D_POU_ARLEQUIN option of the keyword LIAISON_ELEM operator AFFE_CHAR_MECA.

It is modelled by elements of Timoshenko beam (POU_D_T) and elements linear density (PENTA6 and HEXA8).

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.160/10000200000004B5000001D8F3159758216CC258.png)

**Image** 3.1-1 **: mesh of 3D volumetric model**

DYNA_VIBRA calculates the dynamic response of the structure for 3 s due to a nodal force equal to 100 N on the node of the mesh 3D at X = 0.02 m.

## 6 Summary of the results

The test case implements in Code_Aster the 1D-3D volumetric connection in the Arlequin frame on the basis of a two-supported slender structure. The results of the resulting mixed model are compared with the results obtained with an equivalent 1D reference model and a mixed 1D-3D model connected with the 3D_POU option of keyword LIAISON_ELEM operator AFFE_CHAR_MECA.
