# v1.01.364 ZZZZ364 - Validation of large rotations

**Summary:**

This test allows you to check the correct consideration of large rotations by some modellings Code_Aster that can take them into account: '3D', 'COQUE_3D', 'POU_D_T_GD', 'POU_D_EM', 'POU_D_TGM'.

## 1 Test principle

The model (consisting of mesh cells 3D, or mesh cells hull or mesh cells beam) is subject to a shift imposed on a part of its edge (there is no load of "force" type).

Since the required displacement corresponds to a simple rotation of the edge, the complete solution is also the imposed rotation.

The structure does not deform, its state of constraint is zero.

The modellings tested are those that accept large rotations:

3D, COQUE_3D, POU_D_T_GD, POU_D_EM et POU_D_TGM

### 1.1 Baseline, validation

The displacement solution is known: it is the forced rotation. The stress solution is known: a zero stress state.

For displacement, the U - Uref field is checked for a maximum and a minimum of zero.

## 2 Observations

- It is difficult to converge this calculation for all modellings and for a large phi0 angle. That's why the phi0 angle and the time step cutting are chosen differently for each model.
- The modellings POU_D_EM and POU_D_TGM are frankly put into default on this test (error > 100%).
