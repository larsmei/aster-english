This test is essentially a computer test. It has no physical meaning: there is no mesh or model at the finished elements.

## 1 Modelling A

### 1.1 Modelling characteristics

This test case offers a number of analytical tests of the GENE_FONC_ALEA random signal generation operator. This operator generates achievements of a stationary Gaussian random process characterized by its power spectral density (DSP). A two-dimensional DSP with self-spectral ${S}_{1}$ and ${S}_{2}$ as shown in Figure 1 below is considered.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.180/10000000000004B100000384960B5731F68CBC1A.jpg)

**Figure 1: Autospectral ignition** ${S}_{1}$ **and** ${S}_{2}$

The ${S}_{12}(f)=\rho {({S}_{1}(f){S}_{2}(f))}^{0,5}{e}^{i2\pi fT}$ is the $T=0,025$ and ${\rho}^{2}=0,8$ are the correlation coefficients. The difference-types are ${\sigma}_{1}=\sqrt{(603.)}$ and ${\sigma}_{2}=\sqrt{(600.)}$ respectively.

DSP construction is also tested via POST_DYNA_ALEA, the operator for post-processing statistics.

- Random signals are drawn by the operator GENE_FONC_ALEA
- DSP is estimated with CALC_INTE_SPEC operator
- Post-processings statistical DSP is performed with the POST_DYNA_ALEA operator
- INFO_FONCTION is used to estimate the difference-type of a given signal
