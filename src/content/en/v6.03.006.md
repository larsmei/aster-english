# v6.03.006 SSNP006 – Stability analysis of a homogeneous slope using Bishop method

**Summary:**

This test case analyses the stability of a homogeneous slope in the embankment.
safety of the slope by the simplified Bishop method based on the theory of limit
d-balance using CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command.

The test case comes from the validation cases of the American geotechnical software Plaxis2D developed by
Geostudio.

By comparing the result with the reference value, the result of CALC_STAB_PENTE
is 0.84% error probably caused by the difference in the form of
the basis of the slices and the discretization of the space of the geometric variables
the fracture surface.

**Consistentness between CALC_STAB_PENTE, Plaxis2D and literature results allows
scientifically validate the Bishop method and associated optimization algorithm implemented
in CALC_STAB_PENTE.**

Stability is analyzed by gravity alone.

The following mechanical loads are applied to the model:

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Preliminary study indicates that the surface ends are determined
rupture and configure `X1_MINI = X1_MAXI = 3,0` and `X2_MINI = X2_MAXI =13,4`
In order to speed up the calculation. The search for the optimal radius with the terminals of
The search space automatically determined by the macro-command.

We take advantage of the CALC_STAB_PENTE mesh adaptation feature
to improve the accuracy of the FS. The refinement stops up to `NB_RAFF_MAXI = 4`
Since beyond the 4th refinement the FS does not evolve significantly.

At the exit of CALC_STAB_PENTE, the fracture surface shown in Figure4-modelA was obtained.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

The safety factor is tested at the last refinement of the mesh.
The results are shown in Table 1.

**Table 1**: FS Reference Values (Model A)

| **NUME_RAFF** | **Identification** | **Aster result** | **Reference value** | **Error** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | FS | 1.540807984559323 | 1.534 | 0,44 % |

CALC_STAB_PENTE error is of the same order of quantity as Plaxis2D error (0.4%)
This error is explained by:

- The different discretizations of space of geometric variables
the fracture surface.
- The different assumptions on the form of the base of the slices.
In CALC_STAB_PENTE the base is circular. L-angle d-tilt is measured by
the derivative of the circle equation at the centre of the base.
- Numerical error produced when calculating the own weight of the slices.

### 3.3 Summary of the results

The result of the FS from the CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command gives the difference 0.44%
in relation to the reference solution. This proves the relevance of the result of
the Bishop method and the optimization algorithm associated with CALC_STAB_PENTE.
