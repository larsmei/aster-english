# v7.33.111 WTNA111 - axisymmetric Modelling of a joint with hydro-mechanical coupling

**Summary:**

The test presented here allows to check the correct functioning of the elements joints with hydromechanical coupling in axisymmetric Modelling.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A axisymmetric rock massif is considered. It is separated into two parts by a vertical discontinuity $[\mathrm{EF}]$.

![Figure](/figures/v7.33.111/10000000000003620000034B4AF2642C88736111.jpg)

Point coordinates (in metres):

|  | $x$ | $y$ |  | $x$ | $y$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $A$ | 0 | 0 | $D$ | 0 | 1 |
| $B$ | 1 | 0 | $E$ | 0,35 | 0 |
| $C$ | 1 | 1 | $F$ | 0,35 | 1 |

### 1.2 Material properties

- Properties of interstitial fluid (liquid water):

| Density | $1000{\mathrm{kg.m}}^{-3}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Viscosity | ${1.10}^{-3}\mathrm{Pa.s}$ |
| Compressibility | ${3.10}^{9}\mathrm{Pa}$ |

- Properties of the massif :

The massif is elastic and has the following properties:

| Young's modulus | $200\mathrm{MPa}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Poisson's ratio | $0,25$ |
| Porosity | $0,4055$ |
| Intrinsic permeability | $1,688{.10}^{-17}{m}^{2}$ |

- Discontinuity properties:

The mechanical behavior of discontinuity is given the law of Bandis. Its expression is detailed in the reference documentation [R7.02.15](doc:r7.02.15).

The material parameters used are:

| Initial normal rigidity ${K}_{ni}$ | ${1.10}^{9}{\mathrm{Pa.m}}^{-1}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Asymptotic opening ${U}_{\max}$ | $5,0\mathrm{mm}$ |
| $\gamma$ coefficient | $2$ |
| Tangential Rigidity ${K}_{\text{t}}$ | ${1.10}^{12}{\mathrm{Pa.m}}^{-1}$ |

### 1.3 Initial conditions

The initial conditions are as follows:

- Initial opening of the ${\varepsilon}_{0}$ joint: $1,95{.10}^{-5}m$
- initial hydraulic pressure in the massif: $0,0\mathrm{MPa}$
- radial and orthoradial compression initial stress: $12,3\mathrm{MPa}$

### 1.4 boundary conditions

The mechanical and hydraulic boundary conditions are:

- On $[\mathrm{AB}]$: hydraulic pressure imposed by $1,0\mathrm{MPa}$
- On $[\mathrm{BC}]$: $12,3\mathrm{MPa}$ imposed mechanical pressure and zero hydraulic flow
- On $[\mathrm{CD}]$: $y$ blocked movements and zero hydraulic flow
- On $[\mathrm{DA}]$: $x$ blocked movements and zero hydraulic flow

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics

The Modelling is done in axisymmetric with 455 elements TRIA3 for the solid and 30 elements QUA4 for discontinuity.

Time Discretization:

- 25 time step for first 1000 seconds
- 25 time step for the next 3000 seconds.

### 2.2 Tested quantities and results

In the absence of reference solution, non-regression tests are performed only.

The pressure in the joint is tested in two points at two different moments.

## 3 Summary of the results

In the absence of reference solution, non-regression tests are simply performed. The results are consistent with what is expected physically.
