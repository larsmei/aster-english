# v2.01.002 SDLD02 - 8 degrees of freedom mass-spring system

**Summary:**

This two-dimensional problem consists in searching for the frequencies and vibration modes of a mechanical structure composed of masses and springs. This test case of Structures Mechanics corresponds to a dynamic analysis of a discrete model with linear behavior.

This test allows for a complete validation of the discrete (without elements finished) rigidity and mass modelling options offered by the AFFE_CARA_ELEM command. Four different modellings are offered: two modellings for the discrete elements in translation and two others for the discrete elements in translation/rotation. Furthermore, different features of the CALC_MODES commands (calculation of own values and vectors) and NORME_MODE (definition of the standard of a clean vector) are tested.

This test refers to an VPCS test, but it has been modified. Indeed, the *Code_Aster* test directs the mechanical system to an $\mathrm{3y}=\mathrm{4x}$ axis, which allows the data entry to be validated as a local reference.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in the SDLD02/89 form of the VPCS guide which presents the calculation method as follows:

The problem leads to the search for own values and vectors of:

$(K-M{\omega}_{i}){\Phi}_{i}=0$

$K=\left[\begin{array}{cccccc}k& -k& & & & \\ -k& \mathrm{2k}& -k& & & \\ & & \mathrm{..}& & & \\ & & & -k& \mathrm{2k}& -k\\ & & & & -k& k\end{array}\right]$ $M=\left[\begin{array}{ccccc}0& & & & \\ & m& & & \\ & & \mathrm{..}& & \\ & & & m& \\ & & & & 0\end{array}\right]$

d'où :

${f}_{i}=\frac{1}{\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}\cos(\frac{n+1-i}{(n+1)}\frac{\pi}{2})$

$i=1,2,\mathrm{...},n$

$n$ = number of masses

${\Phi}_{i}^{t}$ calculated by linear system resolution.

### 2.2 Baseline

8 first frequencies and the first and eighth standard own vectors such as:

The VPCS test provides standard modes to ${\Phi}^{t}M\Phi =10$ . Standard modes are presented:

- at the unit generalised mass: ${\Phi}^{t}M\Phi =1$; the reference components are divided by $\sqrt{10}$,
- to the generalized stiffness which amounts to dividing the previous components by

,

- the largest displacement component.
