# v1.01.329 ERREU12 – CONTACT_INIT validation with cutting

**Summary:**

This test, which includes only one model, checks the correct consideration of the initial contact (CONTACT_INIT) after cutting the first moment of calculation.

The principle is to limit the number of iterations of Newton to 2 in order to force the redistribution of the time step. After cutting it is checked that the calculation stops because of lack of iterations of Newton and not because of a singular matrix.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
