# v1.03.127 MFRON02 - Code_Aster-MFront interface test: for concrete and floor constitutive laws

**Summary:**

This test validates certain concrete or metal behaviors with damage defined using *MFront* compared to similar *Code_Aster* behaviors.

Modelling C: This Modelling validates the Burger concrete creep model with ageing.

Modelling G: This Modelling allows to validate the Drucker-Prager model.

Modelling H: This Modelling allows to validate the mtest call on a test similar to Modelling c.

Modelling J: This Modelling allows to validate the use of Iwan (MFront law) in an KIT_THM

Each model (except J) validates an constitutive law, usually in comparison with the equivalent law results of *Code_Aster*.

## 2 Modelling G

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics

- Tested behavior: DruckerPrager.mfront. Associated elasto-plastic law of Drucker-Prager [cf. R7.01.16], the criterion being defined by: $F(\sigma )={\sigma}_{\mathit{eq}}+a\mathit{tr}(\sigma )/3–k\le 0$

The coefficients a and k are defined from the material properties as follows:

$a=-3\frac{\tau –1}{\tau +1}$ et $k=2\frac{{\sigma}_{c}\tau }{\tau +1}$

- The Modelling is equivalent to that of a mtest (point hardware) file of Mfront:

a material point is subjected to an imposed deformation ${\epsilon}_{xx}=5{10}^{-3}t$

- the material properties are: $E=200\mathit{GPa},\nu =0,3,\tau =0.6,{\sigma}_{c}=150\mathit{MPa}$ ,

### 2.2 Tested quantities and results

The reference solution is the reference test Mfront:

| **Identification (t=1)* | **Reference** | **Tolerance** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ${\sigma}_{xx}(\mathit{MPa})$ | 90 | 0,1% |
