# v6.04.230 SSNV230 - Validation of the constitutive law of 300 steels under 3D irradiation

**Summary:**

These elementary tests are designed to validate constitutive law IRRAD3M of $\mathrm{3D}$-irradiated steels by activating irradiation creep and swelling.

It is a test case non-regression.
