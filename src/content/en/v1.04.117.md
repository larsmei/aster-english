# v1.04.117 PETSC02- Validation of PETS c with the distribution of the assembly matrix

**Summary:**

This test case allows the PETSC solver to be validated with the assembly matrix distribution. It has three modellings. Three of them are for the elimination of boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_CINE) and validate the use of ML, BOOMER and GAMG multigrill pre-conditioners. The C Modelling is also based on the dualization of boundary conditions (AFFE_CHAR_MECA) and is used for the validation of the LDLT_SP pre-conditioner. Modelling D applies a Neumann loading (pressure).

In addition, each model validates all the distribution modes of the elementary calculations (by groups of elements, by mesh cell).

## 2 Boundary conditions and loads

  - Clamped support of the cube base:

Lower side: $\mathit{DX}=0$ , $\mathit{DY}=0$ and $\mathit{DZ}=0$ ,

- On the upper side, two types of loading are applied:

  - Dutyed displacement (modellings A,B,C)

Upper side: $\mathit{DZ}=1$

- Pressure imposed (modelization D)

  - Upper side: $\mathit{PRES}=\mathrm{10⁸}$

## 3 reference solution

### 3.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is non-regression type.

### 3.2 Quantity and reference result

> The reference quantities used are:

- the average displacement following $z$ on the mesh-cell group Upper_Cote
- the average displacement following $x$ on the mesh-cell group Arete_superior_y
- the average displacement following $y$ on the mesh-cell group Arete_superior_x
- the following $z$ displacement to point $A$ .
