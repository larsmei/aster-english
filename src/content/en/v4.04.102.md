# v4.04.102 TPLV102 - Convection heat transport in a parallelepiped

**Summary:**

This feature was developed in the code to test nonsymmetric matrices.

Steady-state thermal analysis calculation is performed on elements quadrangle type at 4 nodes.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v4.04.102/1000000000000A1F0000058B33E289E31DB72F88.png)

The flat thermal problem of a square cavity (side equal to 1) where heat is spread is considered to be:

- by convection (i.e. the particles constituting the middle of the cavity move at a rate $u$ assumed here constant); the speed $u$ is assumed to make an angle of $67.5°$ with the axis $x$ ,
- by conduction.

### 1.2 Material properties

We take $\rho {C}_{P}=1.$ $\lambda ={10}^{-6}$

from which a diffused $\alpha =\frac{\lambda}{\rho {C}_{P}}={10}^{-6}$

and as we take $\parallel u\parallel =1$, we have the number of Peclet ${p}_{e}=\frac{\parallel u\parallel .L}{\alpha}={10}^{6}$ ($L$ is the characteristic length, here $L=1.$).

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On $\mathrm{AB}$ and $\mathrm{BC}$ segments, you will have a temperature $T=1.$

On the $\mathrm{AE}$ segment, you will set a temperature $T=0.$

On the other two sides, we have the default condition, namely, zero flow.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one advocated by Hughes and Brooks in their cited bibliographic reference [bib1].

The exact solution is the upstream temperature range of the border projected on the downstream border according to the direction of speed.

![Figure](/figures/v4.04.102/10000000000005FD0000059BA5A788C64D8F8840.png)

### 2.2 Baseline

Temperatures are tested on the boundary between $E$ and $D$ points.

analytical solution.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

![Figure](/figures/v4.04.102/10000000000007EC00000573386E34B82E70E3C0.png)

The Modelling is flat: the mesh consists of 100 elements QUAD4 squares of equal size, and 50 elements SEG2 on borders.

- the temperature of 0.0 is imposed on the GROUP_NO $\mathrm{d2}$ ,
- the temperature equal to 1.0 is imposed on the GROUP_NO $\mathrm{C1}$ and $\mathrm{C4}$ .
