# v6.08.118 SSND118 - constitutive law DIS_CONTACT, initial contact management

**Summary:**

This test validates the gradual contact of the discreet when using the DIS_CONTACT behavior.

## 1 Reference problems

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry represents a cell that makes up the fuel assemblies. The dimensions are similar to those used in the actual studies.

![Figure](/figures/v6.08.118/10000201000003EA00000326E3A91121EC9884D0.png)

### 1.2 Modelling A

This case models the gradual contact of the discrete, to take into account an initial contact and obtain the good contact effort taking into account the stiffness of the structure and the possible interactions between the discrete.

The discrete ones are SEG2 type with a DIS_CONTACT type behavior. elements finished type D_PLAN with elastic behavior.

![Figure](/figures/v6.08.118/10000000000002BC0000029EA6809F80B877F0FC.png)

#### 1.2.1 Material properties

Stiffness of the discreet elastic K_T_D_L, in the LOCAL marker: $(\mathit{kn},\mathit{kt})=(\mathrm{1.0E+07,}\mathrm{1.0E+09,})$ the friction coefficient of Coulomb is $0,5$, the game is a unit ramp between $-1,0$ and $0,0$ moments.

For the discreet the behavior is DIS_CONTACT, with material DIS_CONTACT

At rest the length of the discrete is $0.0045$ , with ${\mathit{dist}}_{1}=0.0046$ , the discreet is compressed with an initial displacement of $1.0E-04$ . L-effort in the discrete will depend on the stiffness of the structure.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Two cases are dealt with:

- in static, resolution with an STAT_NON_LINE. Taking into account the symmetry of the problem, the structure is maintained so as to keep this symmetry.
- in dynamic mode, resolution with DYNA_NON_LINE. In this case no blocking is necessary.

Both cases must produce the same results.
