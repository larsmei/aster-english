# v6.03.507 SSNP507 - Stability of an undrained clay slope on the low foundation

**Summary:**

This test analyses the stability of a slope composed of undrained clay
constructed on a weak foundation. The stability factor is calculated by the method
(in English)
CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command. Undrained behavior, characterized by
the significant volume compressibility of water, is nevertheless represented in this test by
Annulment of the friction angle of Mohr-Coulomb law in total stress.

Depending on the relationship between the cohesion of the slope and the foundation, one distinguishes
two break-up mechanisms and a transitional point,
corresponding to the different trends in the variation of the safety factor.
By comparing the results of all the break-up mechanisms written in the literature,
This test is used to validate the SRM method in the CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry and mesh

To simulate the undrained behaviour of clay, the angles of friction and dilatance
I'm not good.
So we find Tresca's criterion describing the soil's shear strength.
The foundation weakness is described by the $\lambda$ parameter defined as
the relationship between the foundation cohesions and the slope:

$\lambda =\dfrac{{c}_{u2}}{{c}_{u1}}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The boundary conditions applied to the model are:

- Bottom of foundation clamped (DX=0, DY=0), and the vertical sides normally blocked (DX=0).

The following mechanical loads are applied to the model:

- The gravity with $g=9,81m/{s}^{2}$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution comes from example 4 in
Griffiths article [1].

The safety factor was calculated by both the SRM method ("strength reduction method")
based on the elements method and traditional method
assuming the sliding circles [2].

The results of both methods are consistent.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The slope is modeled in D_PLAN.
The mesh of the base slope is shown in Fig4-modelA.

Mesh composed of quadratic triangular elements is refined
around the intersection between the slope and foundation and also on the inclined side.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.507/100002010000041D0000016AF3417D87D77DD0AB.png)

***mesh slope with foundation***

CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command is used with METHODE_STAB = 'SRM
for analysis of slope stability at 3 values of $\lambda$
representing both the break-up mechanism and the transition state.
The exponential method of refinement of the crement is used
to estimate the safety factor to within 0.01.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

The safety factor corresponding to the 3 values of $\lambda$ is tested
The reference values are shown in Table 2.

**Table 2**: FS Reference Values (Model A)

| **Identification** | **Aster result** | **Reference value** | **Error** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FS ($\lambda =1,0$ ) | 1,46 | 1.448 | 0,8 % |
| FS ($\lambda =1,5$ ) | 2,03 | 2,032 | 0,1% |
| FS ($\lambda =4,0$ ) | 2,05 | 2,032 | 0,9% |

### 3.3 Summary of the results

The result of the safety factors from the CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command
gives the difference maximum of 0.9% compared to reference solution.
This proves the relevance of the result and the applicability of the SRM method
implemented in code_aster to the 2D model consisting of a field of inhomogenous materials.
