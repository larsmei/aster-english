# v2.03.300 SDLS300 - Ground-excited air refrigerant

**Summary:**

The field of application of this test is seismic analysis. The structure studied is an aerofrigerant subjected to an excitation of ground training (spectrum).

The movements are calculated along a meridian in the $\mathit{xOz}$ plane.

The objective is to test axial, tangential and normal movements for $\mathrm{CQC}$ and $\mathrm{SRSS}$ spectral modal recombinations for 3D elements hulls.

Reference displacements are derived from results obtained by several calculation codes.

The calculation of the specific frequencies of this system is first considered. Reference values are obtained on a model of Fourier with the SAMCEF structure calculation software.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry of the mean surface of the revolution structure is defined by two d-hyperbol arcs. The dimensions are given by:

### 1.2 Material properties

Isotropic homogeneous elasticity $E=27600\mathit{MPa}$; $\nu =0.166$; $\rho =2244\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

**boundary conditions**

Clamped support of the ground-level refrigerant.

**Charge**

The aero-refrigerant is subject to a drive excitation in the direction $x$ "mono-support". The calculation is made from the speed response spectrum [Figure 1.3-a], producing the absolute pseudo-acceleration response spectrum [Figure 1.3-b]. The peak of the spectrum is $4,58077m/{s}^{2}$ to $3,88\mathit{Hz}$ . The PGA is from $1,95501m/{s}^{2}$ to $50\mathit{Hz}$ . The cut-off frequency considered is $33\mathit{Hz}$ .

## 5 Summary of the results

The first two hundred modes of the structure are interested: these are located in the $0–7\mathit{Hz}$ band.

These frequencies are well-received with *Code_Aster* with a maximum difference relative to reference solution's $\text{1.7\%}$.

Four modes provide an effective cumulative mass following $x$ and $y$ of $\text{83.2\%}$. The actual cumulative mass following $z$ is zero over the $0–7\mathrm{Hz}$ frequency range. These actual mass balances are identical to those obtained with SAMCEF.

For the spectral modal seismic calculation, the displacements obtained are far from the reference solution. However, some doubts can be expressed as to the validity of these reference results in spectral modal analysis, given the poor representation of the mass: indeed, for this reference calculation performed with Castem2000, the actual mass accumulated in the direction $x$ represents $\text{43\%}$ of the total mass. Therefore, this part of the calculation was commented on in the test case.
