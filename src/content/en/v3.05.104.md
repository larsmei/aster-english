# v3.05.104 SSLX104 - Discreet: expansion due to a temperature field

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check the thermal expansion for the discrete `K_TR_D_L` type. The results are compared with those of an `POU_D_E`.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry A

| **Nœud** | **X** | **Y** | **Z** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | $0.0$ | $0.0$ | $0.0$ |
| **B** | $L$ | $0.0$ | $0.0$ |
| **C** | $L.cos(\beta).cos(\alpha)$ | $L.cos(\beta).sin(\alpha)$ | $L.sin(\beta)$ |

The modelling consists of:

- `POUTA`: it is a `SEG2` between nodes $A$ and $B$ assigned to an `POU_D_E` model.
- `POUTB`: it is a `SEG2` between nodes $A$ and $C$ assigned to an `POU_D_E` model.
- `DISCA`: it is a `SEG2` between nodes $A$ and $B$ assigned to an `DIS_TR` model.
- `DISCB`: it is a `SEG2` between nodes $A$ and $C$ assigned to an `DIS_TR` model.

### 1.2 Loading

#### 1.2.1 boundary conditions

The node $A$ is clamped and nodes $B$ and $C$ are applied to the element axis.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Internal displacement and efforts

At node $A$ all moves are blocked.
At nodes $B$ and $C$ loading is an $F$ load following the axis of the element.
The temperature is assigned to nodes: $Temper$.

The displacement due to thermal expansion in the axis of the element is:

The stress displacement in the axis of the element is:

- For beams: $U_p =\frac{F L}{E S}$
- For the discreet: $U_d =\frac{F}{K_x}$

The stiffness of the discreet is chosen so that the movements of beams and discrete ones are identical: $K_x = \frac{E S}{L}$.

When loading is both temperature and effort:

- The total displacement is: $U_{total} = U_{th} + U_{d} = U_{th} + U_{p}$.
- The internal effort is: $F_{normal} = F$.
- The supportive reactions are:

  - For elements following $AB$: $F_x = F$
  - For elements following $AC$: $\left[ F_x, F_y, F_z \right] = -F \left[ \cos(\beta) cos(\alpha), \cos(\beta) sin(\alpha), \sin(\beta) \right]$

### 2.2 Uncertainties about solutions

None

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The modelling consists of:

- `POUTA`: a `SEG2` between nodes $A$ and $B$ assigned to an `POU_D_E` model.
- `POUTB`: a `SEG2` between nodes $A$ and $C$ assigned to an `POU_D_E` model.
- `DISCA`: a `SEG2` between nodes $A$ and $B$ assigned to an `DIS_TR` model.
- `DISCB`: a `SEG2` between nodes $A$ and $C$ assigned to an `DIS_TR` model.

The node $A$ is clamped.

For elements in the $AB$ direction, node $B$ effort in the global reference: $Fb_x = F$

For elements in the $AC$ direction, the node $C$ effort in the global reference:

$\left\lbrace  \begin{array}{l}Fc_x = F \cos(\beta)\cos(\alpha)\\Fc_y = F \cos(\beta)\sin(\alpha)\\Fc_z = F \sin(\beta)\end{array}\right.$

The temperature loading is a field at nodes:

| **Instants** | **Temperature[°c]** |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | $T_0 = T_{ref} = 20.0°C$ |
| 1 | $T_1 = 50°C$ |
| 2 | $T_2 = 75°C$ |
| 3 | $T_3 = 100°C$ |

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh consists of one mesh cell `SEG2` by element.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The theoretical solutions are written in the command file according to the parameters:

- related to mesh: $L, \alpha, \beta$
- related to beam geometry: $H_y, H_z$
- related to the characteristics of the discreet: $K_x$
- Material-related: $E, \alpha_{th}$

The quantities tested:

- nodes $B$ and $C$: moving, `DEPL` field.
- node $A$: Nodal reactions, `REAC_NODA` field.
- on beams and discrete: internal forces, `EFGE_ELNO` field.

## 4 Summary of the results

For all modeled elements, all results are analytical solution.
