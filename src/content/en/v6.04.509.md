# v6.04.509 SSNV509 – frictional contact chain with quadratic X- FEM

**Summary:**

This test is intended to validate the consideration of the contact on the lips of a curved crack, limiting itself to the case where the crack completely crosses the structure (interface). The continuous method adapted to the X-FEM method with resolution by increased Lagrangien. This test involves a parallelepipedic block in compression. The interface is represented by a circular *level set* cutting elements with variable angles. It involves elements X-FEM $\text{P2}$ on the move and $\text{P1}$ on the pressure that have degrees of freedom moving in each node and degrees of freedom contact-frost on the nodes peaks.

## 1 Reference problem en 2D

### 1.1 Geometry

The structure is a healthy square plate on the $L=10\text{m}$ side.

The interface is represented by a circular level set inserted into the command file using the DEFI_FISS_XFEM [U4.82.08](doc:u4.82.08) operator given in its analytical form: ${(x-5)}^{2}+{(y-11,77)}^{2}=64,27$. It was chosen to go through the structure completely but does not identify with an [V6.04.507](doc:v6.04.507) inclusion.

The position of the reference points is:

|  | $x$ | $y$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $A$ | 0 | 5,5 |
| $B$ | 5 | 3,75 |
| $C$ | 10 | 5,5 |

### 1.2 Material properties

Poisson's ratio: $0$

Young's modulus : $1.{10}^{6}N/{m}^{2}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The lower side is blocked by an clamped support and a ${u}_{y}=-{1.10}^{-6}m$ displacement is imposed on the upper side.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

Normal at the interface is noted $n$ and the tangential vector is noted $\tau$:

$n=(\begin{array}{}\cos\theta \\ \sin\theta \end{array})$ ,     $\tau =(\begin{array}{}-\sin\theta \\ \cos\theta \end{array})$             éq 2.1-1

with $\theta =\arctan\frac{y-11,77}{x-5}$ **eq 2.1-2**

In this Modelling, the hypothesis of flat stresses is considered (although the Poisson's ratio is zero here, there are no differences between flat stresses and deformations).

The increased Lagrangian method is used for contact/frotting treatment.

The interface has a slope that varies from end to end of the plate. However, where the inclination is highest, near the side edges, it may be slipped. To avoid this, we increase the adhesion via the coefficient of friction of Coulomb: we take $\mu =2$ .

The value of the contact pressure on the interface is a function of the normal $n$:

$\lambda =n.\sigma .n={n}_{y}{\sigma}_{yy}{n}_{y}$                 éq .2.1-3

- where ${n}_{y}$ is the following $y$ component of $n$
- where ${\sigma}_{yy}$ is the following $y$ constraint in the normal ${e}_{y}$ plane in the interfaceless structure: ${\sigma}_{yy}=E\frac{{u}_{y}}{{L}_{y}}$

The $\Lambda$ friction semi-multiplier is defined by:

${r}_{\tau}=\lambda \mu \Lambda$                     éq 2.1.1-4

With the tangential force density, which reads as follows:

${r}_{\tau}=(\tau .\sigma .n)\tau$                     éq 2.1-5

D’où:

$\Lambda =(\frac{1}{\mu}\frac{\tau .\sigma .n}{n.\sigma .n})\tau =(\frac{1}{\mu}\frac{{\tau}_{y}}{{n}_{y}})\tau$                 éq 2.1-6

With the numerical values previously introduced:

$\lambda (x,y)=\frac{-1}{10}{\sin}^{2}(\arctan\frac{11,77-y}{5-x})\mathrm{Pa}$ **éq 2.1-7**

$\Lambda (x,y)=\Lambda .\tau =\frac{x-5}{2(y-11,77)}$ **éq 2.1-8**

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

### 2.3 Uncertainties of the solution

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The contact/frost is handled with elements X-FEM quadratic $\text{P2}$ (moving) and $\text{P1}$ (pressing), i.e. carrying the degrees of freedom displacement on all nodes and the Lagrange contact/frost on the nodes peaks.

### 3.4 Remarks

This test is valid:

- Calculation of the stiffness matrix (the correct offset when filling the matrix because nodes do not carry the same number of degrees of freedom),
- calculation of contact matrices (integration on an SE3 at Gauss points),
- sub-cut (curved interface configurations and elements straight edge configurations),
- X-FEM post-processing of elements $\text{P2P1}$,
- the 3 version algorithm of the tie relationships on the cut edges for the small slide quadratic elementss, to satisfy the LBB.

The MUMPS solver detects a singularity in the matrix. This problem has already arisen on many other X-FEM test cases in contact. The normal level set readjustment improves the conditioning of the matrix but at the expense of too much error in discretizing the level set. Therefore, the option of deactivation of the singularity detection of the solver is retained before proposing a more satisfactory solution.

## 5 Summary of results

The objectives of this test are met.

- The aim was to demonstrate the feasibility of taking frictional contact into account on the lips of the chain using the continuous method adapted to the X-FEM frame. Only the case of an interface completely passing the structure was considered (interface).
- The method is validated in $\mathrm{2D}$ $\text{P2}$ (movement) $\text{P1}$ (pressure) on a quadrangle and triangle mesh.
- The method is validated with the increased Lagrangian method for the treatment of contact/frost.
