# v2.01.032 SDLD32 - Basic Schema Validation in Time HHT

**Summary:**

This test case validates the programming of the HHT time integration scheme (Hilber-Hughes-Taylor)
in `DYNA_NON_LINE` (`SCHEMA='HHT'` with `MODI_EQUI='OUI'`). It involves a simple problem,
i.e. a system with a degree of freedom.

Two schematic schemes are tested, namely the classic HHT schema initially introduced in [bib1]
(`SCHEMA='HHT'` with `MODI_EQUI='OUI'`), and the proposed HHT schema without overshooting
in [bib2] (`SCHEMA='NOHHT'`).

## 1 Reference problems

### 1.1 Geometry

A degree of freedom system consisting of a point mass $m$ is considered,
stiffness $k$ spring oriented to $x$ axis, and shock absorber. The displacement is made according to the $x$ axis.

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.032/sdof.jpg)

> Figure 1: Schéma du système masse-ressort-amortisseur.

### 1.2 Basic characteristics

The mass is assumed to be equal to $m=1 \ \text{kg}$. The system's own period is assumed to be equal
$T=1 \ \text{s}$, which corresponds to a clean pulse $\omega_0 = 2\pi/T$, and therefore to a
stiffness $k = m \omega_0^2 = 2\pi \ \text{N}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$.

$c = 2 \xi \omega_0$ depreciation is considered, where $\xi \in [0, 1[$ is the reduced depreciation
The system.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The spring base is clamped, so the only degree of freedom is the $x$ displacement of the $m$ point mass that is attached to the other end of the spring.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Initial $u_0 = 1.0 \ \text{m}$ displacement and initial $v_0 = 1.0 \ \text{m}\cdot\text{s}^{-1}$ speed are considered.
