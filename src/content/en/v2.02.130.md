# v2.02.130 SDLL130 - Seismic response of a reinforced concrete beam (rectangular section) with linear behavior

**Summary:**

The problem is to analyze the seismic response of a reinforced concrete beam through multifibre beam Modelling (POU_D_EM, model B).

The reference calculation (modelization A) is made using *Code_Aster* with "classical" elements of Euler Bernoulli beam (POU_D_E).

Model C is a copy of model A to validate the use of kinematic and non-homogeneous boundary conditionss with DYNA_VIBRA for transient calculations on a physical basis.

## 1 General characteristics

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a beam simply supported on both supports [Figure 1.1-a].

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.130/1007EACC000031320000313263846BFE75B4E5C2.svg)

**Figure 1.1-a: structure geometry**

### 1.2 Material properties

- Concrete : $E=37272\mathrm{MPa}$ , $\nu =0.2$ , $\rho =2400\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$
- steel : $E=200000\mathrm{MPa}$ , $\nu =0.33$ , $\rho =7800\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$
- damping: Rayleigh type ($\alpha K+\beta M$ ), with 5% on modes 1 and 2

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Simple support in $B$ : $\mathrm{dy}=0$

$A$ "double" support: $\mathrm{dx}=\mathrm{dy}=0$

To avoid the clean out-of-plane modes, the following degrees of freedom are blocked on the entire beam: $\mathrm{rx}=\mathrm{ry}=\mathrm{dz}=0$

Loading: earthquake *ac_s2_c_1* [Figure 1.3-a], in the $\mathrm{OY}$ axis applied to both supports (signal amplification factor = 137).

**NB:** transverse reinforcements are not included in the calculations

## 3 Modelling B (POU_D_EM)

Longitudinal mesh of the beam:

It is composed of 17 nodes and 16 pairs d-elements POU_D_EM (16 elements for concrete and 16 for steel).

Cross section of the beam:

Concrete is modelled by a mesh (DEFI_GEOM_FIBRE / SECT) composed of $2\times 20$ quadrilateral (40 fibres)

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.130/100024E000003D2F00002B190F04B7B20931898D.svg)

**Figure 3.1-a: Section discussion**

Steel is modeled by 4 point fibers (DEFI_GEOM_FIBRE / FIBRE)

The coefficients $\alpha$ and $\beta$ for depreciation are calculated using the following formula:

$\left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}\alpha \\ \beta \end{array}\right\rbrace =2\frac{{\omega}_{1}{\omega}_{2}}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}_{1}^{2}}\left\lbrace \begin{array}{cc}\frac{1}{{\omega}_{2}}& \frac{1}{{\omega}_{1}}\\ {\omega}_{2}& -{\omega}_{1}\end{array}\right\rbrace \left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\xi}_{1}\\ {\xi}_{2}\end{array}\right\rbrace$

where ${\omega}_{1}$ and ${\omega}_{2}$ are the first two clean pulses $(\omega =2\pi f)$ and ${\xi}_{1}$ and ${\xi}_{2}$ are the desired dampings on the first two modes.

With ${f}_{1}=37.8\mathrm{Hz}$ and ${f}_{2}=149.2\mathrm{Hz}$ (see paragraph [§4]), for $\text{5\%}$ modal depreciation, we find: $\alpha =8.5{10}^{-5}$ and $\beta =18.985$ .

For the calculation of the time response, the time step chosen is 1/100th of a second.

## 4 Modelling C

This Modelling is a computer test to validate the use of DYNA_VIBRA with kinematic (d-AFFE_CHAR_CINE) and non-homogeneous boundary conditions.

It is a copy of model A to which a non-nul displacement was imposed on one of the nodes involved in the boundary conditions.

The first calculation uses dualized boundary conditions (d-d-AFFE_CHAR_MECA) and is used as a reference for the second using kinematic boundary conditions.

## 5 Summary of the results

The results obtained by multifibre beam Modelling (POU_D_EM) are in good agreement with the classic Modelling of straight beam d-Euler (POU_D_E) of *Code_Aster*.
