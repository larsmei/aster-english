# v7.32.106 WTNP106 - Heating a desaturated porous medium with dissolved air

**Summary:**

A porous medium is heated with pores filled with a mixture of water (liquid and steam) and air (dry and dissolved in water). The initial saturation in liquid is 50%, the loading is a uniform thermal flux on the edges of the domain. The Modelling done by a single element corresponds to the modelling of a homogeneous problem in space.

The reference solution is an analytical solution approaching.
