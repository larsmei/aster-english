# v3.04.307 SSLV307 - Uniform axially charged oblique cylinder

**Summary:**

The test is designed to validate the various types of linear relationships defined by keywords LIAISON_DDL, LIAISON_OBLIQUE, LIAISON_GROUP.

It also allows testing the "cyclic symmetries" option from the Modelling of a cylinder sector.

The analysis is performed in 3D.

### 3.4 Remarks

- $\mathrm{ur}$ radial displacement is achieved with good precision.
- The symmetry conditions on the $\mathrm{AB}$ face ($v=0$ in local, i.e. $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}V–05W=0$) are verified at the $\mathrm{A2},A’2,G,\mathrm{B2},B’2,H,H’,I$ points considered.

Similarly, the symmetry conditions on the $\mathrm{EF}$ ($u=U=0$ ) face are verified at the $\mathrm{E2},E’2,\mathrm{F2},F’2,\mathrm{G1},\mathrm{H1},H’1,\mathrm{I1}$ points envisaged.

The keyword LIAISON_OBLIQUE is validated.

- The identification of nodes common to both zones by the keyword LIAISON_GROUP is also validated: $U,V,W$ moves are identical to $A’2,B’2,H’,E’2,F’2,H’1$ points compared to $\mathrm{A2},\mathrm{B2},H,\mathrm{E2},\mathrm{F2},\mathrm{H1}$ moves.
