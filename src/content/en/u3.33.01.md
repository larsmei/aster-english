# u3.33.01 Modellings 3D and PLAN of the phenomenon ACOUSTIQUE

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings 3D and PLAN:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the associated support mesh cells,
- the supported materials and loadings,
- the analysis options for the elementary matrices and the post-processings,
- the non-linear possibilities as well as the fracture-mechanics options if they exist.

The modellings 3D and PLAN (Phenomenon: ACOUSTIQUE) correspond to acoustic finite elements in a pressure formulation (cf. [R4.02.01](doc:r4.02.01)). The modelling 3D has volume support mesh cells, the modelling PLAN has plane support mesh cells.
