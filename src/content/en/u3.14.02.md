# u3.14.02 Modellings 3D_FLUIDE, FLUI_STRU, 2D_FLUI_PESA

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings 3D_FLUIDE, FLUI_STRU, 2D_FLUI_PESA:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the associated support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear capabilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings.

The modellings 3D_FLUIDE (volume elements) and FLUI_STRU (2D fluid-structure interaction elements) correspond to the linear formulation of the coupled problem allowing the study of the vibratory behaviour of a structure in the presence of an inviscid, compressible fluid [R4.04.01](doc:r4.04.01). The free surface is taken into account by the modelling 2D_FLUI_PESA (surface elements).

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

| **Finite element** | **Degrees of freedom (at each vertex node)** |
| --- | --- |

| MEFL_xxx (3D_FLUIDE) | PRES : hydrodynamic pressure ; PHI : fluid displacement potential |
| --- | --- |
| MEFL_FACE (3D_FLUIDE) | PHI : fluid displacement potential |
| MEFS_xxx (FLUI_STRU) | DX, DY, DZ : components of the structural displacement on the wall interfaced with the fluid ; PHI: fluid displacement potential |
| MEFP_FACE (2D_FLUI_PESA) | DH : deflection of the free surface (sloshing waves) ; PHI : fluid displacement potential |

**Note 1:**

*In general the free-surface elements (sloshing waves) are assigned on a boundary mesh-cell group of a domain on which fluid elements have been assigned. At the nodes there will therefore be continuity of the fluid displacement potential *PHI* on the surface and in the fluid volume.*

**Note 1:**

*Coupling with a structure meshed with elements* COQUE_3D* requires the fluid-structure interface to be meshed with elements* QUAD8 * (and not* QUAD9*). The bulk fluid domain is therefore meshed with* HEXA20* (and not* HEXA27*). Indeed, the fluid-structure coupling is carried out on the displacement degrees of freedom only, for the solid.*

> **Note 2:**

*It is imperative that the outward normal to the fluid domain always be oriented from the fluid towards the structure, for all fluid-structure interface modellings, in particular *FLUI_STRU *.*

## 2 Supported loadings

The available loadings are the following:

**PESANTEUR**

Makes it possible to apply a gravity-type loading.

Supported modelling: 2D_FLUI_PESA

**VITE_FACE**

Makes it possible to specify the vibratory velocity field imposed as a loading on boundary elements.

Supported modelling: 3D_FLUIDE

**IMPE_FACE**

Makes it possible to specify the impedance map imposed as a boundary condition on boundary elements.

Supported modelling: 3D_FLUIDE

**ONDE_FLUI**

Makes it possible to apply a pressure amplitude of a sinusoidal incident wave arriving normally to a face.

Supported modelling: 3D_FLUIDE

## 3 Non-linear capabilities

### 3.1 Constitutive laws

The only constitutive relation available in DYNA_NON_LINE, for the modelling FLUI_STRU, under COMPORTEMENT is the RELATION 'ELAS' (see [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).

### 3.2 Strains

Only the linearised strains, keyword 'PETIT' under DEFORMATION, are available in the constitutive relations (see [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).
