# v6.04.187 SSNV187 - Validation of ELAS_HYPER Law on a Cube

**Summary:**

This test validates the signorini type hyper-elastic behavior (material ELAS_HYPER with or without MFront).
A flat deformation and $\mathrm{3D}$ elemental test is used compared to an analytical reference.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.187/Shape1.gif)

A $\mathrm{1m}$ side cube is considered to be based on a plane (${x}_{2}=0$ on the lower side), subjected to $F$ pressure on the upper side and in a flat deformation situation following ${x}_{3}$ (${x}_{3}=0$ on the right and left sides), so the cube can only stretch along the ${x}_{1}$ axis.

### 1.2 Material properties

Three different materials are tested, corresponding to three common hyper-elastic models.

| Behaviour ELAS_HYPER | Mooney-Rivlin | New-Hookean | Signorini |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C10 | 0.709 | 1.2345 | 0.1234 |
| C01 | 2.3456 | 0 | 1.2345 |
| C20 | 0 | 0 | 0.456 |
| NU | 0.499 | 0.499 | 0.499 |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

| Lower face | : $\mathrm{DY}=0$ |
| --- | --- |
| Upper face | : $F=0.876\mathrm{Pa}$ |
| Left and right face | : $\mathrm{DZ}=0$ in 3D, nothing in D_PLAN |

The load is increasing from $F=0$ to $F=\mathrm{0.876Pa}$, in 20 increments.

## 2 reference solution

The result of [bib1] is the basis. The state of flat deformation allows to write the uniform field of displacement in the cube very easily:

$$
\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{u}_{1}={a}_{1}.{x}_{1}\\ {u}_{2}=w.{x}_{2}\\ {u}_{3}=0\end{array}
$$

with $w$ the vertical (negative) displacement of the upper face and ${a}_{1}$ an arbitrary constant.

The condition of incompressibility allows to write:

$$
{a}_{1}=\frac{-w}{1+w}
$$

And we find the relationship between the applied force $F$ and the $w$ displacement of the upper face:

$$
F=\mathrm{2S.}\frac{w.(2+w).(1+{(1+w)}^{2})}{{(1+w)}^{3}}.(\frac{\partial \Psi }{\partial {J}_{1}}+\frac{\partial \Psi }{\partial {J}_{2}})
$$

$S$ is the surface, $\Psi$ is the deformation potential and ${J}_{1}$ , ${J}_{2}$ are the invariants of the Green-Lagrange tensor. The deformation potential used by ELAS_HYPER is as follows:

$$
\Psi ={C}_{10}.({J}_{1}-3)+{C}_{01}.({J}_{2}-3)+{C}_{20}.{({J}_{1}-3)}^{2}+{\Psi}_{\text{vol}}
$$

${\Psi}_{\text{vol}}$ is the potential for incompressibility. It depends on the ${J}_{1}$ and ${J}_{2}$ invariants and ${C}_{10}$ , ${C}_{01}$ and ${C}_{20}$ which are the material characteristics. As more $S=1$ we get:

$$
F=2.\frac{w.(2+w).(1+{(1+w)}^{2})}{{(1+w)}^{3}}.\left[{C}_{10}+{C}_{01}+2.{C}_{20}.\frac{{w}^{2}.{\left(2+w\right)}^{2}}{{\left(1+w\right)}^{2}}\right]
$$

The resolution of this nonlinear equation to $w$ is done simply by dichotomy for $w<0$ .
