# v5.01.105 SDND105 – Shock from point material against a wall with plastic blazing

**Summary:**

This test validates the behaviour of a shock-type obstacle with possible flaming. Resolution was performed with the DYNA_VIBRA operator using the FLAMBAGE keyword (modellings A, B, C and D) and with the DYNA_NON_LINE operator using the CHOC_ENDO_PENA (modellings E and F) behavior.

The flashing start moment is calculated (this is the first moment when the impact force has passed the flashing threshold), the cumulative plastic deformation and the moment when it returns to the initial position.

The results obtained are consistent with the analytical results.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The problem is to analyze the response of a moving mass subjected to a non-zero initial speed and shocking against a wall following a constitutive law of the flaming type.

The equilibrium equation of the system reads:

$$
M\frac{{\partial}^{2}x}{\partial {t}^{2}}+\mathrm{Kx}={F}_{\mathrm{ext}}
$$

The resolution of the process takes place in several phases: before blazing the wall, loading after blazing, unloading after blazing and free flight.

#### 2.1.1 First phase

Before the blazing, the reaction force is: ${F}_{\mathrm{ext}}=-{K}_{1}x$

The solution of the equation is as follows: $x(t)={A}_{1}\sin{\omega}_{1}t+{B}_{1}\cos{\omega}_{1}t$

With: ${\omega}_{1}^{2}=\frac{K+{K}_{1}}{M}$

Taking into account the initial conditions: $x(0)=0$ and $\frac{\partial x}{\partial t}(0)={v}_{0}$

We identify: ${A}_{1}=\frac{{v}_{0}}{{\omega}_{1}}$ and ${B}_{1}=0$

Either: $x(t)=\frac{{v}_{0}}{{\omega}_{1}}\sin{\omega}_{1}t$

The ${F}_{\mathrm{fl}}$ flashing limit threshold is reached at the ${t}_{\mathrm{fl}}$ moment, such as: $x({t}_{\mathrm{fl}})={d}_{\mathrm{fl}}=\frac{{F}_{\mathrm{fl}}}{{K}_{1}}$.

D’où: ${t}_{\mathrm{fl}}=\frac{1}{{\omega}_{1}}\mathrm{Arc}\sin(\frac{{F}_{\mathrm{fl}}{\omega}_{1}}{{K}_{1}{v}_{0}})$

et $\frac{\partial x}{\partial t}({t}_{\mathrm{fl}})={v}_{\mathrm{fl}}$

#### 2.1.2 Second phase

The next phase is the loading phase after blazing; the reaction force is constant as long as the speed remains positive. This reaction force is: ${F}_{\mathrm{ext}}=-{F}_{\mathrm{seuil}}$ and the solution of the equation s.

With: ${\omega}_{0}^{2}=\frac{K}{M}$

Like ${\omega}_{0}^{2}\ll 1$ , a limited development of the trigonometric functions can be performed.

Either: $x(t)={A}_{21}{t}^{2}+{B}_{21}t+{C}_{21}$ .

Taking into account the initial conditions $x({t}_{\mathrm{fl}})={d}_{\mathrm{fl}}$ and $\frac{\partial x}{\partial t}({t}_{\mathrm{fl}})={v}_{\mathrm{fl}}$ , one obtains:

> ${A}_{21}=(\frac{{v}_{\mathrm{fl}}{t}_{\mathrm{fl}}}{2}-\frac{{d}_{\mathrm{fl}}}{2}-\frac{{F}_{\mathrm{seuil}}^{2}}{4M}){\omega}_{0}^{2}-\frac{{F}_{\mathrm{seuil}}}{2M}$
> ${B}_{21}={v}_{\mathrm{fl}}+\frac{{F}_{\mathrm{seuil}}{t}_{\mathrm{fl}}}{M}+({d}_{\mathrm{fl}}{t}_{\mathrm{fl}}-\frac{{v}_{\mathrm{fl}}{t}_{\mathrm{fl}}^{2}}{2}){\omega}_{0}^{2}$
> ${C}_{21}={d}_{\mathrm{fl}}-\frac{{F}_{\mathrm{seuil}}{t}_{\mathrm{fl}}^{2}}{2M}-{v}_{\mathrm{fl}}{t}_{\mathrm{fl}}-\frac{{d}_{\mathrm{fl}}{t}_{\mathrm{fl}}^{2}}{2}{\omega}_{0}^{2}$

The moment at which the speed is canceled is: ${t}_{m}=-\frac{{B}_{21}}{2{A}_{21}}$

By neglecting the terms in ${\omega}_{0}^{2}$, we deduce: ${t}_{m}={t}_{\mathrm{fl}}+\frac{M{v}_{\mathrm{fl}}}{{F}_{\mathrm{seuil}}}$,

and maximum displacement is: $x({t}_{m})={d}_{m}$

This allows to obtain the plastic deformation cumulated: ${d}_{p}={d}_{m}-\frac{{F}_{\mathrm{seuil}}}{{K}_{2}}$

#### 2.1.3 Third phase

This phase corresponds to the discharge, the reaction force is: ${F}_{\mathrm{ext}}=-{K}_{2}(x-{d}_{p})$

The solution of the equation ssss written: $x(t)={A}_{3}\sin{\omega}_{2}t+{B}_{3}\cos{\omega}_{2}t+\frac{{K}_{2}{d}_{p}}{K+{K}_{2}}$

With: ${\omega}_{2}^{2}=\frac{K+{K}_{2}}{M}$

Taking into account the initial conditions $x({t}_{m})={d}_{m}$ and $\frac{\partial x}{\partial t}({t}_{m})=0$ ,

we get: ${A}_{3}=({d}_{m}-\frac{{K}_{2}{d}_{p}}{K+{K}_{2}})\sin{\omega}_{2}{t}_{m}$ and ${B}_{3}=({d}_{m}-\frac{{K}_{2}{d}_{p}}{K+{K}_{2}})\cos{\omega}_{2}{t}_{m}$ .

The reaction force sssannules when the displacement of the material point reaches the value of the cumulative plastic deformation ${d}_{p}$ .

Like $K\ll {K}_{2}$, we do the following approximation: $\frac{{K}_{2}{d}_{p}}{K+{K}_{2}}\approx {d}_{p}$.

Thus, the ${t}_{d}$ moment that corresponds to the cancellation of the reaction force is such that:

$x({t}_{d})={d}_{p}={A}_{3}\sin{\omega}_{2}{t}_{d}+{B}_{3}\cos{\omega}_{2}{t}_{d}+{d}_{p}$

Either: $\sin{\omega}_{2}{t}_{m}\sin{\omega}_{2}{t}_{d}+\cos{\omega}_{2}{t}_{m}\cos{\omega}_{2}{t}_{d}=\cos{\omega}_{2}({t}_{d}-{t}_{m})=0$ .

D’où: ${t}_{d}={t}_{m}+\frac{\pi}{2{\omega}_{2}}$ .

#### 2.1.4 Fourth phase

The next phase corresponds to the free flight phase ${F}_{\mathrm{ext}}=0$.

The solution of the equation ssss written: $x(t)={A}_{4}\sin{\omega}_{0}t+{B}_{4}\cos{\omega}_{0}t$

The initial conditions are:

$x({t}_{d})={d}_{p}$ et $\frac{\partial x}{\partial t}({t}_{d})={v}_{d}={\omega}_{2}({x}_{m}-\frac{{K}_{2}{d}_{p}}{K+{K}_{2}})\sin{\omega}_{2}({t}_{m}-{t}_{d})$

This gives:

${A}_{4}={d}_{p}\sin{\omega}_{0}{t}_{d}+\frac{{v}_{d}}{{\omega}_{0}}\cos{\omega}_{0}{t}_{d}$

${B}_{4}={d}_{p}\cos{\omega}_{0}{t}_{d}-\frac{{v}_{d}}{{\omega}_{0}}\sin{\omega}_{0}{t}_{d}$

Like ${\omega}_{0}^{2}\ll 1$ , by performing a limited development of the trigonometric functions up to order 2, the solution is as follows:

$x(t)={A}_{41}{t}^{2}+{B}_{41}t+{C}_{41}$

With:

${A}_{41}=\frac{{\omega}_{0}^{2}}{2}[{v}_{d}{t}_{d}-{d}_{p}(1-\frac{{\omega}_{0}^{2}{t}_{d}^{2}}{2})]$

${B}_{41}={d}_{p}{t}_{d}{\omega}_{0}^{2}+{v}_{d}(1-\frac{{\omega}_{0}^{2}{t}_{d}^{2}}{2})$

${C}_{41}={d}_{p}(1-\frac{{\omega}_{0}^{2}{t}_{d}^{2}}{2})-{v}_{d}{t}_{d}$

By neglecting the terms in ${\omega}_{0}^{2}$, we get:

$x(t)={v}_{d}t+{d}_{p}-{v}_{d}{t}_{d}$ .

And we deduce the ${t}_{0}$ moment of transition to the initial position ($x=0$ ).

Either: ${t}_{0}={t}_{d}-\frac{{d}_{p}}{{v}_{d}}$ .

#### 2.1.5 Reference solution of Modelling C

The initial conditions selected allow to have a kinetic energy of $4J$ , which must lead to total compression of $6.5m$ and residual compression of $3.1m$ .
