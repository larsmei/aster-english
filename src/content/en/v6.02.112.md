# v6.02.112 SSNL112 - Bar subjected to a cyclic thermal charge

**Summary:**

This test case is part of the validation of the elements bar elastoplasticity behavior relationships for quasi-static structural mechanics.

A clamped bar at both ends undergoes a cyclic thermal loading that induces traction-compression forces.

Each model allows validation of one of the introduced nonlinear behavior relationships: linear isotropic hardening with von Mises criterion (modelization A), linear kinematic hardening with von Mises criterion (modelization B).

## 2 Reference solutions

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solutions

#### 2.1.1 Linear scrubbing

**Isotropic scrubbing**

For uniaxial traction, the plasticity criterion ssss:

$∣{\sigma}_{L}∣-R(p)\le 0$,

where $p$ is the cumulative plastic deformation. The expression of $R(p)$ is given by the relation:

$R(p)=R'p+{\sigma}^{y}$ et $R'=\frac{E{E}_{1}}{E-{E}_{1}}$.

The criterion then reads:

$∣{\sigma}_{L}∣-R'p-{\sigma}^{y}\le 0$.

The stress tensor is obtained by:

$\sigma =A.(\varepsilon (u)-{\varepsilon}^{p})-\mathrm{3K}\alpha (T-{T}^{\mathrm{ref}})\mathrm{Id}$.

The expression of ${\sigma}_{L}$ is derived from this:

${\sigma}_{L}=E(\varepsilon -\alpha T)-E{\varepsilon}^{p}$.                     $({T}^{\mathrm{ref}}=0)$.

In our case, $\varepsilon =0$ so:

${\sigma}_{L}=E{\varepsilon}_{L}-E{\varepsilon}^{p}$, with ${\varepsilon}_{L}=-\alpha T$.

So:

- Si $∣{\sigma}_{L}∣-R(p)<0$ : *p=0*  et ${\sigma}_{L}=E{\varepsilon}_{L}$,
- Si $∣{\sigma}_{L}∣-R(p)=0$ : $p=(\frac{∣\sigma ∣-{\sigma}^{y}}{R'})$ et ${\sigma}_{L}=E{\varepsilon}_{L}-E{\varepsilon}^{p}$.

**Application to the loading journey**

**Instant 1:**

$\sigma =E\varepsilon =\mathrm{200MPa}$ and $R(p)=R'p+{\sigma}^{y}=\mathrm{100MPa}$, because *p=0*.

We have ${\sigma}_{L}-R(p)\le 0$.

The criterion is not met, the evolution is elastic: ${\sigma}_{L}=\mathrm{100MPa}$ and $N=\mathrm{100kN}$.

**Instant 2:**

The criterion is met:

${\sigma}_{L}=\frac{E}{E+R'}(R'{\varepsilon}_{L}+{\sigma}^{y})=\frac{2\cdot {10}^{11}}{2\cdot {10}^{11}+2.02\cdot \mathrm{10⁹}}(2.02\cdot \mathrm{10⁹}\times 3.5\cdot {10}^{-3}+2\cdot \mathrm{10⁸})=\mathrm{205MPa}$.

With $N=\mathrm{102.5kN}$, and $p=2.475\cdot {10}^{-3}$.

**Instant 3:**

Elastically discharged:

${\sigma}_{L}=E{\varepsilon}_{L}-E{\varepsilon}^{p}=2\cdot {10}^{11}(1.5\cdot {10}^{-3}-2.475\cdot {10}^{-3})=-195\mathrm{MPa}$.

With $N=-97.5\mathrm{kN}$.

**Instant 4:**

We plasticize again:

The criterion s's written: $\mid \sigma \mid -R'p-{\sigma}^{y}=0$ with $p={p}_{1}+{p}_{2}$ where ${p}_{1}=2.475\cdot {10}^{-3}$

Thus, we obtain:

${p}_{2}=\frac{\mid \sigma \mid -{\sigma}^{y}}{R'}-{p}_{1}$,

$\sigma =-E{\varepsilon}^{p}=-E({p}_{1}-{p}_{2})$,

$\sigma =(\frac{R'}{R'+E})(-2E{p}_{1}-\frac{E{\sigma}^{y}}{R'})=-\mathrm{207.9MPa}$,

And so $N=-\mathrm{103.95kN}$.

**Instant 5:**

Elastically discharged:

${\sigma}_{L}=E{\varepsilon}_{L}-E{\varepsilon}^{p}=2\cdot {10}^{11}(2\cdot {10}^{-3}-1.0395\cdot {10}^{-3})=192.1\mathrm{MPa}$,

et $N=96.05\mathrm{kN}$.

**Instants 6 and 7:**

The reasoning is the same.

${N}_{(\mathrm{inst.6})}=\mathrm{105.87kN}$,

${N}_{(\mathrm{inst.7})}=-\mathrm{44.13kN}$.

**Ceninical collision**

The method of calculation is identical, but in this case the plasticity criterion reads:

$\sigma -{X}_{\mathrm{eq}}-{\sigma}^{y}\le 0$ with ${X}_{\mathrm{eq}}=C{({\varepsilon}^{p})}_{\mathrm{eq}}=\frac{3}{2}C{\varepsilon}^{p}=\frac{E{E}_{t}}{E-{E}_{t}}{\varepsilon}^{p}$.

With the previous notations, the criterion reads:

$∣{\sigma}_{L}-R'{\varepsilon}^{p}∣-{\sigma}^{y}\le 0$ where ${\sigma}_{L}=R'{\varepsilon}^{p}\pm {\sigma}^{y}$ (depending on the direction of flow).

**Application to the loading journey**

**Instant 1:**

The criterion is not met, the evolution is elastic: ${\sigma}_{L}=\mathrm{100MPa}$ and $N=\mathrm{100kN}$.

**Instant 2:**

The criterion is met: $\mid {\sigma}_{L}-R'{\varepsilon}^{p}\mid -{\sigma}^{y}=0$.

${\sigma}_{L}=R'{\varepsilon}^{p}+{\sigma}^{y}=2.02\cdot \mathrm{10⁹}\times 2.475\cdot {10}^{-3}+2\cdot \mathrm{10⁸}=\mathrm{205MPa}$.

**Instant 3:**

Elastically discharged:

${\sigma}_{L}=E{\varepsilon}_{L}-E{\varepsilon}^{p}=2\cdot {10}^{11}(1.5\cdot {10}^{-3}-2.475\cdot {10}^{-3})=-\mathrm{195MPa}$,

with $N=-\mathrm{97.5kN}$.

**Instant 4:**

$\mid \sigma -R'{\varepsilon}^{p}\mid -{\sigma}^{y}=0$, with ${p}_{1}=2.475\cdot {10}^{-3}$.

${\varepsilon}^{p}={p}_{1}-{p}_{2}$,

$\ {p}_{2}={p}_{1}-\frac{\mid \sigma +{\sigma}^{y}\mid }{R'}$,

$\sigma =-E{\varepsilon}^{p}=-E({p}_{1}-{p}_{2}) =-E(\frac{\mid \sigma +{\sigma}^{y}\mid }{R'})=-\mathrm{198MPa}$,

with $N=-\mathrm{99kN}$.

**Instant 5:**

Elastically discharged:

${\sigma}_{L}=E{\varepsilon}_{L}-E{\varepsilon}^{p}=2\cdot {10}^{11}(2\cdot {10}^{-3}-9.9\cdot {10}^{-4})=202\mathrm{MPa}$,

et $N=\mathrm{101kN}$.

**Instants 6 and 7:**

The reasoning is the same.

${N}_{(\mathrm{inst.6})}=\mathrm{103kN}$,

${N}_{(\mathrm{inst.7})}=-\mathrm{47kN}$.
