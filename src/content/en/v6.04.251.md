# v6.04.251 SSNV25 1 – Basic validation of GTN flat deformation damage law with simulations of a element volume in dual-axial traction e

**Summary:**

This test case allows the validation of the integration algorithm of the Gurson – Tvergaard – Needleman ($\mathit{GTN}$) damage law with the gradient and local elements standard under-integrated or mixed-in-large deformations. A element volume is modeled in two-axial traction.

The different modellings processed are:

- **Model A** ($2D$ ): D_PLAN_SI.
- **Modelling** **B** ($2D$ ): D_PLAN_GRAD_VARI.
- **Modelling** **C** ($2D$ ): D_PLAN_GRAD_INCO.
- **Modelling** **D** ($3D$ ): 3D_SI.
- **Modelling** **E** ($3D$ ): 3D_GRAD_VARI.
- **Modelling** **F** ($3D$ ): 3D_GRAD_INCO.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

$\mathrm{2D}$ (plane deformation) or $\mathrm{3D}$ Modelling means that a square or cube on the side of 0.05 $\text{mm}$ is considered (see Figure 1.1-1), respectively.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

For $2D$ modelling (plane deformation), horizontal displacements and vertical displacements of all nodes are controlled: ${u}_{x}=\text{}0.16x$ and ${u}_{y}=\text{}0.16y$ . (see Figure 1.1-1(a) for geometry).

For $3D$ Modelling, $X$ axis displacements and $Y$ axis displacements of all nodes are controlled: ${u}_{x}=\text{}0.16x$ and ${u}_{y}=\text{}0.16y$ . $Z$ displacements are blocked (see Figure 1.1-1(b) for geometry).

Boundary conditions and loads are imposed in this way so that the $2D$ and $3D$ problems are identical and homogeneous.

The load is imposed using 1000 time step identical. The pseudo-calculation time is 1.
