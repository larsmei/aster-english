# v6.02.111 SSNL111 - Three thermo-elastic bars Von Perfect bets

**Summary:**

This quasi-static test is part of the validation of elastoplastic behavioral relationships.

Three perfect, parallel thermo-elastoplastic bars, rotulated on a rigid support at one end and rotulated on a rigid bar at the other, undergo external thermal loading.

This application, where all fields are uniform in each bar, allows you to validate 2 types of digital modellings: elements solid finished ($\mathrm{2D}$ flat stresses), plates and bars.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.02.111/100010F20000170C000012808E18687B306C9DB4.svg)

The three bars have the same length $L=\mathrm{1m}$, and are spaced from $l=\mathrm{1m}$.

### 1.2 Material properties

Constitutive law thermo-elastoplastic perfect standard, with VonMises criterion. Plastic deformations are zero in the initial state.

![Figure](/figures/v6.02.111/1000066000001AAA00000968D17847C225C345D8.svg)

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The three bars have a blocked displacement following $\mathit{Oy}$ at the top ends, where they are articulated, and they are slit at the bottom points ${P}_{1,}{P}_{2,}{P}_{3}$ , which can be represented by a rigid frame that is required to move vertically, of $\mathrm{4l}$ length on which the three bars are articulated. The bars are free of mechanical effort.

The loading path is described by the temperature evolution, uniform in each bar (${T}^{max}=330°C$ ):

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

3 elements $\mathrm{1D}$ (SEG2). Modelling BARRE

In this Modelling, the second calculation was duplicated with STAT_NON_LINE to exit the elastic zone and thus be able to perform an anelastic deformation validation (EPSP_ELGA) in addition to the validation of the EPVC_ELGA and EPME_ELGA options on the elements BARRE on the elastic calculation.

To achieve this goal, the section of the bar has been modified and the nonlinearity parameters of the material have been changed. The section is moved to an area of $1.5$ and the nutring parameters are as follows:

D_SIGM_EPSI = $180$

SY = $100.$
