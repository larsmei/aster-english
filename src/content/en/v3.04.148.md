# v3.04.148 SSLV146 – Full Cube reinforced by triaxially loaded reinforcements

**Summary**

This $\mathrm{3D}$ test is part of the GRILLE_MEMBRANE formulation validation. It is a full cube of unit size. A steel rebar sheet is placed on each side to sweep all possible directions. The load consists of the displacements imposed on all nodes of the structure.

The main interest of this test is to test GRILLE_MEMBRANE Modelling for different orientations and for different elements (linear and quadratic). The results are compared to an analytical solution.

The units of all the numerical values are in SI.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.148/10004298000069F000004BA8A69FBB8DFD67FF3F.svg)

Six rebar arrays (one per side of the cube) are defined:

- $\mathrm{GEOX}$ (2 sheets): $\mathrm{NO1NO4NO8NO5}$ and $\mathrm{NO2NO6NO7NO3}$ faces
- $\mathrm{GEOY}$ (2 sheets): $\mathrm{NO1NO2NO6NO5}$ and $\mathrm{NO4NO3NO7NO8}$ faces
- $\mathrm{GEOZ}$ (2 sheets): $\mathrm{NO1NO2NO3NO4}$ and $\mathrm{NO5NO6NO7NO8}$ faces

### 1.2 Material properties

**For the full cube:**

| Modelling | $A$ | $B$ | $C$ | $D$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $E(\mathrm{Pa})$ | 2 | 2 | 2E14 | 2E14 |
| $\nu$ | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

**For reinforcing sheets (all modelling)**

$E=2E11\mathrm{Pa}$ , $\nu =0$

- Tablet $\mathrm{GEOX}$ : section per linear meter $0.01{m}^{2}/\mathrm{ml}$ , excentre 0, orientation (ANGL_REP) $(30;0)$
- Tablet $\mathrm{GEOY}$ : section per linear meter $0.02{m}^{2}/\mathrm{ml}$ , excentre 0, orientation (ANGL_REP) $(0;40)$
- Tablet $\mathrm{GEOZ}$ : section per linear meter $0.03{m}^{2}/\mathrm{ml}$ , excentre 0, orientation (ANGL_REP) $(\mathrm{15 };70)$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The boundary conditions are:

$\mathrm{DX}=0$ on the $\mathrm{NO2NO3NO7NO6}$ side

$\mathrm{DY}=0$ on the $\mathrm{NO1NO2NO6NO5}$ side

$\mathrm{DZ}=0$ on the $\mathrm{NO1NO2NO3NO4}$ side

The load is applied in an increment as follows (taxed displacements):

$\mathrm{DX}=1$ on the $\mathrm{NO1NO4NO8NO5}$ side

$\mathrm{DY}=2$ on the $\mathrm{NO4NO3NO7NO8}$ side

$\mathrm{DZ}=3$ on the $\mathrm{NO5NO6NO7NO8}$ side

**Modelization C:** Same Modelling as in A with quadratic elements

**Modelling D:** Same Modelling as in B with quadratic elements

## 6 Summary of the results and general remarks

The results obtained for these modellings are identical to the reference solutions. They validate GRILLE_MEMBRANE Modelling for four different types of d-elements in the case of linear mechanical calculation (MECA_STATIQUE and STAT_NON_LINE).
