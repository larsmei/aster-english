# v3.03.126 SSLS126 – Flexion of a reinforced concrete slab (model GLRC_DAMAGE) with two sides: elastic beam speed

**Summary:**

This test represents the calculation of a reinforced concrete slab, in bending, subjected to pressure. It allows to validate the DKTG Modelling with the GLRC_DAMAGE model for linear elastic behaviour and Q4GG Modelling with the ELAS model. The slab is in beam configuration: single supports on two opposite sides of the slab.

Four modellings are performed:

- Modelling A allows you to test the DKTG model with TRIA3,
- Modelling B allows you to test the DKTG model with QUAD4.
- Modelling C allows you to test the Q4GG model with TRIA3,
- Modelling D allows you to test the Q4GG model with QUAD4.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The square slab is $l=1.8m$ long, $h=0.12m$ thick, on single bilateral supports. The bending rail is parallel to the edges; it is identical on each side and in both directions ($\mathrm{dx}$ , $\mathrm{dy}$ being the spacings of the irons in the $x$ and $y$ directions). The coating of the longitudinal irons closest to the sides is $22\mathrm{mm}$ . The coating of the irons in relation to the lateral edges of the $2\mathrm{cm}$ slab is neglected. The following table summarizes the data of the ironing. The geometric percentage of steel $\mu$ is given for one side in a direction.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The elastic relationships, linking the $N$ and $M$ bending forces to the $\varepsilon$ membrane deformations and $\kappa$ curvatures, and taking into account two symmetrical grids, describe:

$N=(\frac{{E}_{b}h}{1-{\nu}_{b}^{2}}\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1& {\nu}_{b}& 0\\ {\nu}_{b}& 1& 0\\ 0& 0& \frac{1-{\nu}_{b}}{2}\end{array}\right]+2{E}_{a}\left[\begin{array}{ccc}{a}_{x}& 0& 0\\ 0& {a}_{y}& 0\\ 0& 0& 0\end{array}\right])\varepsilon$

$M=(\frac{{E}_{b}{h}^{3}}{12(1-{\nu}_{b}^{2})}\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1& {\nu}_{b}& 0\\ {\nu}_{b}& 1& 0\\ 0& 0& \frac{1-{\nu}_{b}}{2}\end{array}\right]+2{E}_{a}{e}_{z}^{2}\left[\begin{array}{ccc}{a}_{x}& 0& 0\\ 0& {a}_{y}& 0\\ 0& 0& 0\end{array}\right])\kappa$

As regards a beam configuration, a Poisson's ratio equal to 0 is assigned to concrete to cancel any bending in the perpendicular direction. Two opposite edges of the slab are simply pressed, two others remaining free:

| ![Figure](/figures/v3.03.126/100000000000031C00000202AE88E7C796C3E04E.png) | The equivalent stiffness bending which takes into account the steels is: ${(\mathit{EI})}_{\mathit{éq}}=\frac{{E}_{b}l{h}^{3}}{12}+2{E}_{a}{a}_{x}l{e}_{z}^{2}$ , i.e. here: ${(\mathit{EI})}_{\mathit{éq}}=10.111{\mathit{MNm}}^{2}$ . |
| --- | --- |

The elastic solution is calculated in beam theory for an equivalent pressure value $p'=\mathrm{pl}$ . The values of moments in "plate" configuration by division by the width of the slab $l$ .

| Quantity in the center | Expression |
| --- | --- |
| Deflection at the center under pressure on the surface | $w(l/2)=\frac{5p'{l}^{4}}{384{(\mathit{EI})}_{\mathit{éq}}}$ |
| Curvature | ${\kappa}_{xx}(l/2)=\frac{p'{l}^{2}}{8{(\mathit{EI})}_{\mathit{éq}}}$ |
| Deformation | ${\varepsilon}_{xx}={\kappa}_{xx}\frac{h}{2}$ |
| Overall time (in beam) | $M(l/2)=p'{l}^{2}/8$ |
| Global time (plate) | $M(l/2)=p{l}^{2}/8$ |

### 2.2 Baseline

For modellings A and B where GLRC_DAMA is valid with elements DKTG:

- Deflection at the center under pressure on the surface: $w=2,433.{10}^{-4}m$
- Curvature: $k=7,210.{10}^{-4}{m}^{-1}$
- Deformation: ${\epsilon}_{xx}=-0.4326.{10}^{-4}$ on lower skin
- Overall time (in beam): $M=7290\mathit{Nm}$
- Global time (plate): $M=4050\mathit{Nm}/\mathit{ml}$

For modellings C and D where ELAS is valid with elements Q4GG:

- Deflection at the center under pressure on the surface: $w=2,658.{10}^{-4}m$
- Curvature: $k=7,878.{10}^{-4}{m}^{-1}$
- Deformation: ${\epsilon}_{xx}=-0.47269.{10}^{-4}$ on lower skin
- Overall time (in beam): $M=7290\mathrm{Nm}$
- Global time (plate): $M=4050\mathrm{Nm}/\mathrm{ml}$

## 7 Summary of the results

The results of the four modellings are close to the analytical solution:

- DKTG: $6\text{\%}$ maximum for displacement, and $5\text{\%}$ for moment and curvature.
- Q4GG: $1\text{\%}$ maximum for displacement, and $0.5\text{\%}$ for moment and curvature.

These modellings validate:

- DKTG Modelling with GLRC_DAMAGE model in elastic behavior.
- Q4GG Modelling with ELAS model.
