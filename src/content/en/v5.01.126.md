# v5.01.126 SDND126 - Validation of DIS_ECRO_CINE in DYNA_VIBRA

**Summary:**

The DYNA_VIBRA operator has a keyword COMPORTEMENT to handle localized nonlinearities.
One of these non-linearities is to impose the constitutive law DIS_ECRO_CINE on a mesh cell with a discreet element.
The objective of this test is to validate this use by comparing the results with the DYNA_NON_LINE operator.
In this test, it is a mass-spring system subjected to sinusoidal loadings on the different degrees of freedom.
Il y a 4 modellings :

- Modelling A: DIS_TR/SEG2
- Modelling B: DIS_T/SEG2
- Modelling C: DIS_TR/POI1
- Modelling D: DIS_T/POI1

This documentation is deliberately succinct.

In the following we present only the displacement curves of the different degree of freedom by comparing the results obtained by DYNA_NON_LINE and DYNA_VIBRA. For information, we also trace the corresponding elastic behavior.
Note that the results from DYNA_VIBRA are ever closer to those from DYNA_NON_LINE as the time step is reduced.
