### 1.2 Material properties

Rating for thermoelastic properties:

$\left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\varepsilon}_{x}\\ {\varepsilon}_{y}\\ {\gamma}_{xy}\end{array}\right\rbrace =\left[\begin{array}{ccc}{S}_{11}& {S}_{12}& 0\\ {S}_{12}& {S}_{22}& 0\\ 0& 0& {S}_{66}\end{array}\right]\left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\sigma}_{x}\\ {\sigma}_{y}\\ {\tau}_{xy}\end{array}\right\rbrace +\left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\alpha}_{11}\\ {\alpha}_{22}\\ 0\end{array}\right\rbrace \cdot (T-{T}_{\mathit{ref}})$

$\begin{array}{c}{S}_{11}=1/{E}_{x}\\ {S}_{22}=1/{E}_{y}\\ {S}_{12}=-{\nu}_{x}/{E}_{x}=-{\nu}_{y}/{E}_{y}\\ {S}_{66}=1/{G}_{xy}\\ {\alpha}_{11}={\alpha}_{x}\\ {\alpha}_{22}={\alpha}_{y}\end{array}$

The isotropic material, both thermal and mechanical, is limited to:

${E}_{x}={E}_{y}=2.{10}^{5}\mathit{MPa}$

${\nu}_{x}={\nu}_{y}=0.3$

${\alpha}_{x}={\alpha}_{y}=1.2{10}^{-5}°{C}^{-1}$

${\lambda}_{x}={\lambda}_{y}=54.W/m°C$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

Two models are considered:

- the $x=0$ half model
- the complete model

**boundary conditions mechanical:**

- half model

$\mathrm{UX}=0$ along the $X=0$ symmetry axis

$\mathrm{UY}=0$ au point ($W/2.$ )

- Full model

$\mathrm{UX}=0$ au point $(0,L/2.)$

$\mathrm{UY}=0$ at $(-L/2.)$ and $(L/2.)$ points

**boundary conditions thermal:**

- half model

$T=100°C$ on the top edge $Y=L/2$ .

$T=-100°C$ on the lower edge $Y=-L/2$ .

zero flow on the symmetry axis, on the free edge $X=W/2.$ and on the crack edge

- Full model

$T=100°C$ on the top edge $Y=L/2$ .

$T=-100°C$ on the lower edge $Y=-L/2$ .

zero flow on free edges $X=\pm W/2.$ and on crack edge

### 3.4 Remarks

In the reference, the author assumes that ${K}_{I}=0$ , but he does not verify it a posteriori. At the sight of the distorted Code_Aster, the ${K}_{I}$ coefficient is different from zero, but it remains very low compared to ${K}_{\mathit{II}}$ (the crack slides more than it opens).

As for the $G$ energy return rate , if we assume that ${K}_{I}=0$ , we draw the reference value from the IRWIN formula in flat constraints:

${G}_{\mathit{ref}}=(1/E)\times {K}_{\mathit{II}}^{2}$

## 4 Summary of the results

The differences between reference solution and the results of *Code_Aster* do not exceed $\text{2\%}$ on stress intensity coefficients and $\text{4\%}$ on energy return rate. The results are checked for invariance with the different integration crowns.
