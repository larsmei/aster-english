# r7.01.18 Modified HOEK_BROWN constitutive law

**Summary:**

This document describes a constitutive law for rocks. The yield criterion is a parabolic criterion of Hoek-Brown type. It is written in principal stresses. The evolution of the plastic strains is of «non-associated» type, formulated from a Drucker-Prager criterion whose friction angle evolves with yielding.

This law can be used in pure mechanics as well as in coupled thermo-hydro-mechanical modelling.

In thermo-hydro-mechanical modelling, it can be used in effective stresses or in total stresses:

- In the first case, it is the effective stresses that are required to satisfy the Hoek and Brown criterion. The plastic strains are computed using the Drucker surface and the friction angle in the space of effective stresses
- In the second case, it is the total stresses that are required to satisfy the Hoek and Brown criterion. The plastic strains are computed using the Drucker surface and the friction angle in the space of total stresses

## 1 Introduction

This note presents a mechanical constitutive model for rocks. To represent the mechanical behaviour of rocks the user of Code_Aster can use the Drucker-Prager law DRUCK_PRAGER, the LAIGLE law or the HOEK_BROWN law presented here. The Drucker-Prager law is the simplest and the furthest from the real behaviour of rocks. The Laigle law is the most faithful to the physics of the phenomena. Thus the law presented in this note is intermediate between these two laws, in terms of complexity as well as in terms of representation of reality. It uses formulations that are fairly classical in the field of geomechanics.

This law can be used in modellings of mechanics alone or in modellings of THM type.

In thermo-hydro-mechanical modelling, it can be used in effective stresses or in total stresses:

- In the first case, it is the effective stresses that are required to satisfy the Hoek and Brown criterion. The plastic strains are computed using the Drucker surface and the friction angle in the space of effective stresses
- In the second case, it is the total stresses that are required to satisfy the Hoek and Brown criterion. The plastic strains are computed using the Drucker surface and the friction angle in the space of total stresses

One will find in reference [1] elements useful for the understanding of this law

The formulation is of non-associated plasticity type:

- The elastic domain is defined by a criterion of Hoek-Brown type, whose parameters evolve with the hardening parameter
- The hardening parameter is a combination of the plastic shear strain and of the plastic volumetric strain
- The plastic strains derive from a Drucker-Prager criterion whose friction angle evolves with yielding.

### 1.1 Characteristics of the model

The model simulates the short-term mechanical behaviour of rocks in 4 phases, described in reference [1]:

1. Elastic phase characterised by a constant Young's modulus and a constant Poisson's ratio
2. Elastoplastic phase with positive hardening which simulates the initiation of a form of damage and its progression towards failure of the rock. This phase is modelled by a plasticity criterion of Hoek-Brown type. This criterion evolves as a function of the major irreversible strain. The evolution of the irreversible strain is determined by a plastic flow potential expressed by a function of Drucker-Prager type.
3. Elastoplastic phase with negative hardening which represents the post-failure behaviour of rocks. The failure criterion is of Hoek-Brown type. The strain is determined by a non-associated plastic flow potential of Drucker-Prager type.
4. Residual strength phase characterised by a modified Hoek-Brown type function.

We describe here the model independently of whether it is used in mechanics alone, or in hydro-mechanical computations in total or effective stress. Thus the notation $\sigma$ used in the following paragraphs must be interpreted according to the use.

## 4 Incremental form

One places oneself here in the framework of finite increments. The index – denotes a component at the beginning of the loading step and the absence of an index a component at the end of the loading step. The operator $\Delta$ denotes the increment of a component.

$\begin{array}{}s={s}^{-}+2\mu (\Delta e-\Delta {e}^{p})={s}^{e}-2\mu \Delta {e}^{p}\text{}\begin{array}{cc}\text{où}& {s}^{e}={s}^{-}+2\mu \Delta e\end{array}\\ {I}_{1}={I}_{{1}^{-}}+\mathrm{3K}(\Delta {\varepsilon}_{\nu}-\Delta {\varepsilon}_{\nu}^{p})={I}_{1}^{e}-\mathrm{3K}\Delta {\varepsilon}_{\nu}^{p}\text{}\begin{array}{cc}\text{où}& {I}_{1}^{e}={I}_{1}^{-}+\mathrm{3K}\Delta {\varepsilon}_{\nu}\end{array}\end{array}$

## 8 Algorithm

### 8.1 Internal variables

The modified Hoek-Brown constitutive law is governed by the following three internal variables:

1. *the hardening parameter* $\gamma$ corresponding to the major irreversible strain.
2. *the cumulative plastic volumetric strain* ${\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{\nu  }}^{p}$ whose evolution law is given by $d{\varepsilon}_{\nu}^{p}=3\eta d\lambda =\frac{3\eta }{\eta +1}d\gamma$ .
3. *the state of yielding* ; it equals 0 if the Gauss point is in elastic loading or in unloading, and 1 if the Gauss point is in plastic loading.

### 8.2 Algorithm

One adopts an implicit formulation with respect to the criterion and to the flow direction. One places oneself at a material point and one assumes known at ${t}^{-}$ :

- The strain increment tensor $\Delta \varepsilon$ from which one deduces $\Delta e$ , $\Delta {\varepsilon}_{\nu}$
- The stresses at the beginning of the time step ${\sigma}^{-}$ from which one deduces ${s}^{-},{I}_{1}^{-}$
- The value of the internal variables ${\gamma}^{-}$ and ${\varepsilon}_{\nu}^{p-}$ at the beginning of the time step which give us

$(S{\sigma}_{c}^{2}{)}^{-},(m{\sigma}_{c}{)}^{-},{b}^{-},{\phi }^{-}$

The aim of the algorithm is then to compute:

- The stresses at the end of the time step $\sigma$
- The internal variables at the end of the time step
- The tangent behaviour at the end of the time step: $\frac{\partial \sigma }{\partial \varepsilon }$ if the law is in effective stresses
- The tangent behaviour at the end of the time step: $\frac{\partial \sigma }{\partial \varepsilon }$ and $\frac{\partial \sigma }{\partial {\sigma}_{p}}$ if the law is in total stresses.

**Algorithm:**

*Computation of the elastic solution* :

If the law is in effective stresses:

> $\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{s}^{e}={s}^{-}+2\mu \Delta e\\ {I}_{1}^{e}={I}_{1}^{-}+\mathrm{3K}\Delta {\varepsilon}_{\nu}\end{array}$

If the law is in total stresses:

> $\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{s}^{e}={s}^{-}+2\mu \Delta e\\ {I}_{1}^{e}={I}_{1}^{-}+\mathrm{3K}\Delta {\varepsilon}_{\nu}+3\Delta {\sigma}_{p}\end{array}$

*Computation of the elastic criterion* $F({\sigma}^{e},{\gamma}^{-})$ . If $S{\sigma}_{c}^{2}-{\sigma}_{3}^{e}.m{\sigma}_{c}<0$ , the function $F$ is not defined at the point $({\sigma}^{e},{\gamma}^{-})$ . One then considers that one is in the plastic case.

> *Solution* : computation of $\sigma ,\gamma$

> If $F({\sigma}^{e},{\gamma}^{-})\le 0$ , then $\Delta \varepsilon =\Delta {\varepsilon}^{e},{\gamma}^{p}={\gamma}^{{p}^{-}},\Delta \sigma =H\Delta \varepsilon$ .

Otherwise, one seeks $\sigma ,\gamma$ such that $F(\sigma ,\gamma )\le 0$ , which amounts to seeking $\Delta \gamma$ such that $F(\Delta \gamma )=\stackrel{ˉ}{F}(\Delta \gamma )=0$ . This problem is solved using a Newton method on $\stackrel{ˉ}{F}$ .

*Newton algorithm:*

> Initialisation: ${\mathrm{\Delta \gamma }}^{0}=0$

> After each iteration:

- if $\Delta {\gamma}^{n+1}\le 0$ , there has been no convergence: one subdivides the time step
- if $\Delta {\gamma}^{n+1}\le \frac{{\sigma}_{\text{eq}}^{e}}{3\mu [:ref:$eta (Delta {gamma}^{n+1})+1 <eta (Delta {gamma}^{n+1})+1>`]}` , there is no solution (see paragraph 4.2 ): one subdivides the time step

> *Update of the variables* : stresses, internal variables

*Computation of the consistent tangent matrix* $\frac{\partial \sigma }{\partial \varepsilon }$ *if the law is in effective stresses and* $\frac{\partial \sigma }{\partial \varepsilon }$ *and* $\frac{\partial \sigma }{\partial {\sigma}_{p}}$ *if the* *law is in total stresses for the option* RIGI_MECA_TANG or FULL_MECA.
