# v6.01.301 SSNA301 - Pressurized thick tank bottom

**Summary:**

This test consists of analyzing the torispheric bottom of a thick tank under internal pressure until the ruin, taking into account the elastoplastic behaviour of the material and the nonlinear behaviour of the structure.

Modelling is done with elements axisymmetric type MEAXQU8.

The study will test whether or not geometric nonlinearities are taken into account and whether or not one or two material hypotheses are used.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.01.301/1001A262000069D500006DC31EFA4D68D80F2E63.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

Elastoplastic material without nutring

$E=3.E+07\mathrm{MPa}$

$\nu =0.3$

${\sigma}_{y}=3.E+04\mathrm{MPa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Point $A$ : ${u}_{x}=0$

Point $B$ : ${u}_{y}=0.$

The following loading (= internal pressure) is applied:

$p=1.737{p}_{0}$ (near the ruin) with ${p}_{0}=658.257\mathrm{MPa}$ (end of linear speed)

The $p$ load is applied in 50 identical increments.

## 6 Summary of the results

The results provided by Aster for modellings A and C are close to the reference (difference $\text{< 0.6\%}$). Furthermore, the same results are found for modellings A and C; this is normal because the lower part remains elastic.

For Modelling B, the differences results between *Code_Aster* and reference (SAMCEF) reach $\text{3.3 \%}$ and come from the fact that the geometric nonlinearities are treated differently in *Code_Aster* (by PETIT_REAC command) and in SAMCEF.
