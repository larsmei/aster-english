# v6.08.115 SSND115 - constitutive law elasto-plastic with non-radiality effect

**Summary:**

The problem is quasi-static non-linear in structural mechanics. The tested law, VISC_MEMO_NRAD, is a constitutive law with non-linear kinematic hardening, isotropic hardening, and maximum hardening memory, as well as taking into account the non-radiality (or non-proportionality) of the load.

The response is analyzed in a material point on a cyclic tensile torsion test.

Model A validates memory and non-radiality effects in comparison to experimental results.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Point hardware, VISC_MEMO_NRAD behavior.

Each elementary cycle (a traction-compression for a given twist) is discretized in 48 steps. To complete a complete turn on the previous diagram it takes $360/15=24$ elementary cycles.

A total of 4 full spins are performed, i.e. $48\times 24\times 4=4608$ time step total.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results show the model's ability to account for over-hardening due to non-radiality of loading, as in the absence of non-radiality terms, the amplitudes obtained are much lower (about $100\mathit{Mpa}$ less). The difference between 5 and 6% with the experiment can be considered acceptable.
