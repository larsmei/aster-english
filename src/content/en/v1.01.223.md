# 1 **v1.01.223** ZZZZ223 - Validation of CREA_RESU/ASSE and AFFE_MATERIAU/AFFE_VARC/FONC_INST

**Summary:**

This test shows how to make a thermo-mechanical calculation with complex thermal loading, i.e. with several transients, repeated several times.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Contact details of $(m)$ points:

> $A:(0.,0.)$
> $B:(1.,0.)$
> $C:(1.,1.)$
> $D:(0.,1.)$

### 1.2 Material properties

- Mechanical

$E=1.N/{m}^{2}$

$\nu =0.$

$\alpha =1.°{C}^{-1}$

- Thermal

$\lambda =1.w/m/°C$

$\rho \mathit{cp}=1.J/{m}^{3}°C$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- Travel taxed:
- $A$ : $\mathrm{DX}=\mathrm{DY}=0.$
- $B$ : $\mathrm{DY}=0.$
- Thermal loading imposed:

Two homogeneous thermal evolutions are defined in space:

$\mathrm{ch1}$ is a temperature rise from 10 degrees to 17 degrees $[0.,0.7s]$

$\mathrm{ch2}$ is a temperature descent from 17 degrees to 14 degrees $[0.,0.3s]$

The $[\mathrm{ch1}+\mathrm{ch2}]$ cycle is shifted to form the $[1.5,2.5s]$ interval. This cycle is repeated periodically and the mechanical calculation is done on the $[0.5,4.5s]$ interval.

## 2 reference solution

This is a free expansion problem $(\alpha =1)$ of a square $(1\times 1)$.

$Y$ displacement of point $C$ is then identical to the temperature imposed.

This can be done by verifying that the displacement curve is a series of descent-rising cycles between temperatures 10 and 17.

We're testing:

- Au point $C$ : $\mathrm{DY}(t=\mathrm{1.0s})=\mathrm{DY}(t=\mathrm{2.0s})=\mathrm{DY}(t=\mathrm{4.0s})=\mathrm{15.m}$
- Au point $C$ : $\mathrm{DY}(t=\mathrm{1.3s})=\mathrm{DY}(t=\mathrm{2.3s})=\mathrm{DY}(t=\mathrm{4.3s})=\mathrm{16.m}$
