# v6.04.159 FORMA08 - Practical work of the "Creation mechanics" formation: circular crack in mixed mode

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the calculation of the energy return rate and stress intensity factors (SIF s) along the bottom of a mixed mode 3D crack in the linear elasticity.

This test involves a cube with a flat central circular crack, subjected to an inclined force relative to the crack plane.

For each model, SIF s are evaluated by CALC_G option G and K.

The numerical values are compared with the analytical values.

In Modelling A, the mesh used is a free mesh generated with ZCracks. In Modelling B, the mesh is set (raising around the crack front).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The crack is circular (penny shape crack) of $a=0,2m$ radius in the $\mathit{OXY}$ plane. The side of the cube is $L=2m=10a$ length. Thus, the crack is considered to be in an infinite medium.

### 1.2 Material properties

A homogeneous isotropic material with linear elasticity is considered to have the following characteristics:

- Young's modulus $E=200000\mathit{MPa}$ ,
- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0,3$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Given the symmetries of structure, crack and loadings, only half of the structure is modelled: half space such as $Y>0$. Therefore symmetry conditions are applied to the $Y=0$ face: on this face, the following $Y$ displacement is blocked.

The structure is subjected to an ${\sigma}_{xx}=1\mathit{MPa}$ constraint. The local $(x,y,z)$ marker is obtained by rotating an $\alpha =\pi /4$ angle around the $\mathit{OY}$ axis.

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.159/10000000000002B20000022EE428EC6E1D6BBC34.png)

**Figure** 1.3-1 **: Constraints in the global benchmark**

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.159/100000000000015B000000E8C71967D18D36A085.png)

**Figure** 1.3-2 **: Local benchmark**

Thus, we have:

on the upper side:

- ${\sigma}_{\mathit{ZZ}}=\sigma {\sin}^{2}(\alpha )$
- ${\sigma}_{\mathit{ZX}}=\sigma \cos(\alpha )\sin(\alpha )$

on the lower side:

- ${\sigma}_{\mathit{ZZ}}=-\sigma {\sin}^{2}(\alpha )$
- ${\sigma}_{\mathit{ZX}}=-\sigma \sin(\alpha )\cos(\alpha )$

on the right side side:

- ${\sigma}_{\mathit{XX}}=\sigma {\cos}^{2}(\alpha )$
- ${\sigma}_{\mathit{XZ}}=\sigma \cos(\alpha )\sin(\alpha )$

on the left side of the vehicle:

- ${\sigma}_{\mathit{XX}}=-\sigma {\cos}^{2}(\alpha )$
- ${\sigma}_{\mathit{XZ}}=-\sigma \cos(\alpha )\sin(\alpha )$

In order to block rigid body modes, the point $\mathit{D1}$ $(L/2,0,0)$ is blocked following $X$ and $Z$ and the point $\mathit{D2}$ $(-L/2,0,0)$ is blocked following $Z$.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is a analytical solution from 1, for a circular $a$ radius crack in an infinite medium, subjected to a uniform $\sigma$ surface force inclined from an $\alpha$ angle to the crack plane, the stress intensity factors for a point $A$ placed on the crack front are:

${K}_{I}=\frac{2}{\pi}\sigma ({\sin}^{2}\alpha )\sqrt{\pi a}$

${K}_{\mathit{II}}=\frac{4}{\pi (2-\nu )}\sigma (\sin\alpha \cos\alpha )\cos\omega \sqrt{\pi a}$

${K}_{\mathit{III}}=\frac{4(1-\nu )}{\pi (2-\nu )}\sigma (\sin\alpha \cos\alpha )\sin\omega \sqrt{\pi a}$

$\omega$ being the angle characterizing the position of the point $A$ on the circular background (see ).
