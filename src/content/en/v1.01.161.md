**Summary:**

This test case controls the MED format field writings and their ASTER rereading. It validates in particular the writing of undefined fields on the full domain or defined on several types of d的elements. It also controls the exchange of quantity fields.

It should be noted that mechanical calculation is only a pretext and has no ambition of validation per se.

### 3.3 Tested Features

This case tests the different options of writing in 'MED' format via the IMPR_RESU command. To do this we calculate various additional options of the single displacement and create a field of quantity equivalent to the field of moving to nodes. The impressions are successively these:

- the nodes displacement field and the Gauss point stress field, expressed throughout the mesh. This triggers the creation of a profile for displacement because it is not defined everywhere: the three nodes $M$ , $N$ and $O$ do not have any value,
- quantity field. It uses the same profile as the displacement field of the standard result,
- 'SIXY' and 'SIYY' components of the nodes stress field by element 'SIGM_ELNO',
- l , error indicator 'ERRE_ELGA_NORE' on mesh cells group $\mathit{MILIEU}$ . This triggers the creation of two profiles because the values written are not on all mesh cells. The first profile concerns the 4 triangles out of the 8 of the total; the second one concerns the 2 quadrangles out of the 4 of the total.

The rereading from the 'MED' format is that of the nodes field in the quantity field.
