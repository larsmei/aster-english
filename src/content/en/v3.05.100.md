# v3.05.100 SSLX100 - 3D Mix - Cover - Bend beam

**Summary:**

This test allows to validate for a linear elastic calculation:

- a mixture of different mechanical models: 3D model (element HEXA20), shell models (elements DKT, or elements COQUE_3D) and beam model (elements POU_D_E),
- linear relationships between degrees of freedom, built especially with [R3.03.03](doc:r3.03.03) and [R3.03.06](doc:r3.03.06) connections.

The test is based on the analytical solution **elastic** of a bending beam. The reduced number of elements for the different **models** leads to a poor solution, which is however significantly improved with the use of boundary conditions appropriate to the beam theory.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.05.100/100011E400002EB700001E480BBD016A6C22E4FD.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

$E=200000\mathrm{MPa}$

$\nu =0.3$

$\nu =0.0$ avoids the variation of orthogonal curvature induced by the effect of Poisson in the plates, which causes an difference between beams and plates theories, out of the average fiber.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- ${F}_{y}=-1$ force (load 1) or ${C}_{z}=1$ torque (load 2)
- defined or applied on neutral fibre
- clamped support section $x=0$
- continuity of translation displacements on $\mathrm{AB}$
- continuity of translation movements to $C$
- equal rotational displacements around $z$ on $\mathrm{C1}-\mathrm{C2}$
- for $M$ points in the section ($\mathrm{M1}$ $\mathrm{M2}$ $\mathrm{M4}$ ) the $u(M)$ translation moves are linearly dependent on ${\varphi}_{z}$ rotational displacement of $P$ points

$u(M)=–{\varphi}_{z}(P).y+\mathrm{dx}(P)$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Analytical solution, *isostatic structure* .

The elastic deflection, elastic axial stresses and deformations and the bending moment in all point d-abscisse $x$ are given by:

- Load #1: force ${F}_{y}=–1$

| ${M}_{y}(x)={F}_{y}.L(1-x/L)$ | (= $E.{I}_{z}.{u}_{y}''(x)$ in elasticity) |
| --- | --- |
| ${u}_{y}(x)={F}_{y}L.{x}^{2}.(3-x/L)/(6.{\mathrm{E.I}}_{z})$ | (elasticity) |
| ${\varepsilon}_{xx}(x,y)=–{F}_{y}.L(1-x/L).y/({\mathrm{E.I}}_{z})$ | (elasticity) |
| ${\sigma}_{xx}(x,y)=-{F}_{y}.L(1-x/L).y/{I}_{z}$ | (elasticity) |

- Load No. 2: ${C}_{z}=1$ torque or ${\mathrm{dr}}_{z}={C}_{\mathrm{z.L}}/({\mathrm{E.I}}_{z})$ rotation

| ${M}_{y}(x)={C}_{z}$ | (= $E.{I}_{z}.{u}_{y}''(x)$ in elasticity) |
| --- | --- |
| ${u}_{y}(x)={C}_{\mathrm{z.}}{x}^{2}/(2.E.{I}_{z})$ | (elasticity) |
| ${\sigma}_{xx}(x,y)=–{C}_{z}.y/{I}_{z}$ | (elasticity) |
| ${\varepsilon}_{xx}(x,y)=-{C}_{z}.y/(E.{I}_{z})$ | (elasticity) |

with:

$L=\mathrm{L1}+\mathrm{L2}+\mathrm{L3}=30\mathrm{mm}$

${I}_{z}=a.{h}^{3}/12=0.25{\mathrm{mm}}^{4}$

${\mathrm{dr}}_{z}=0.0006$

### 2.2 Baseline

Deflections, axial stresses and deformations and bending moments in 4 points of the beam axis.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.

## 9 Summary of the results

The cases studied are:

| Modelling | Restraints |
| --- | --- |
| A: 3D, DKT et POU_D_E | LIAISON_GROUP |
| B: 3D, DKT, POU_D_E et DIS_TR | LIAISON_GROUP; LIAISON_ELEM: 3D_POU, COQ_POU |
| C: 3D, COQUE_3D, POU_D_E et DIS_TR | LIAISON_GROUP; LIAISON_ELEM: 3D_POU, COQ_POU |
| D: C_PLAN , 2D_DIS_TR | LIAISON_ELEM: 2D_POU |
| E: C_PLAN , 2D_DIS_TR | LIAISON_ELEM: 2D_POU |
| F: C_PLAN , POU_D_E | LIAISON_ELEM: 2D_POU |

- The mesh is very coarse in elements 3D and plate. The test deserves finer modelling, since the results are influenced by how to describe the conditions d
- However, with the correct consideration of these conditions, the solution is much better (the $B$ Modelling leads to a maximum error of 3%).
- Comparisons of stresses and efforts give good results (modelling $B$). For the 1D hull element, the results are very good.
