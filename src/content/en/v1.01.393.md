# v1.01.393 ZZZZ393 - Pre-deformations by function for plates, grids and membranes

**Summary:**

This test validates the consideration of assigned pre-deformations subform function for elements DKT, DKTG, GRILLE_EXCENTRE, GRILLE_MEMBRANE and MEMBRANE.

It also validates the consideration of pre-deformations assigned per value for elements GRILLE_MEMBRANE and compared to elements GRILLE_EXCENTRE.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

#### 1.1.1 Mesh for validation on linear elements

The mesh consists of three plates oriented in the 3 directions of space:

| [Figure](images/10000000000001EF00000215B5D3F67028B774B0.png)Group COQ_X | [Figure](images/10000000000001CF00000213559EA4321FBEF8C6.png)Group COQ_Y | [Figure](images/10000000000001F90000023A02A50D03ED18B0DB.png)Group COQ_Z |
| --- | --- | --- |

These three plates can be used to validate the dependence of pre-deformation functions on space variables $X$ , $Y$ and $Z$ . They are all rectangular 3 meters by 1 meter.

For each mesh plate, several groups of mesh cells and nodes are defined:

- ENCX, ENCY and ENCZ: nodes groups consisting of both points at $X=0$, $Y=0$ and $Z=0$ corners.
- FLX, FLY and FLZ: nodes located in $X=3,Y=0$, $Y=3,X=0$ and $Z=3,Y=0$ respectively.
- G1X, G1Y and G1Z: Groups of three mesh cellss located between 0 and 1 depending on the length coordinate of the plate.
- G2X, G2Y and G2Z: Groups of three mesh cells located between 1 and 2 depending on the length coordinate of the plate.
- G3X, G3Y and G3Z: Groups of three mesh cells located between 2 and 3 depending on the length coordinate of the plate.

#### 1.1.2 Mesh for validation on elements quadratics

It is a mesh consisting of 27 elements 3D. The volume is 3 meters long, 1 meter wide and 0.5 metres thick.

The $X=0$-based face is the ENCAST group, the mesh cells-based surface is duplicated for the GRILLE group. The mesh-based surface is the same as the mesh-based surface area before the G1X, G1Y and G1Z groups. Finally, the node FLX-based surface is defined as the $(\mathrm{3,0,0})$-based surface.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.393/100000000000026D00000221B506055E6A33481D.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic linear.

Young's modulus $E=1E9\mathit{Pa}$

Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 Linear mesh

clamped support en ENCX, ENCY et ENCZ: $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DZ}=\mathit{DRX}=\mathit{DRY}=\mathit{DRZ}=0$ .

Pre-deformation loads will be detailed in each model. For function loads, they will depend on the following function each time:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.393/100002000000017E0000017888BB21BBC454B6B0.png)

Stairs function

#### 1.3.2 Quadratic mesh

clamped support en ENCAST: $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DZ}=0$ .

Pre-deformation loads will be detailed in each model. For function loads, they will depend on the stair function each time.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Baseline

In all calculations, reference results are obtained by performing the same calculation with pre-deformations affected by mesh cells group values per mesh cells group.

The nodes FLX, FLY and FLZ moves are compared.

### 2.2 Uncertainty about the solution

None. The results must be exactly the same as those of reference solution.
