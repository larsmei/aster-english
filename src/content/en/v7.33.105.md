# v7.33.105 WTNA105 – Injection of gas into a quasi-saturated clay-type material described by Van-Genuchten/Mualem laws

**Summary:**

This test represents the simulation of gas injection into a geological environment. It is a purely hydraulic calculation. The geometry represented corresponds to a radial cut around a storage package. The medium, consisting of a functional vacuum, a damaged area and a geological barrier of the Callovo-Oxfordian type (clay) is initially almost saturated. The medium is described by a model of Mualem Van-Genuchten. This test also allows us to test the hydraulic law called by HYDR_VGM.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and initials

On the left border a hydrogen flow and a water flow (corrosion modelling) are imposed:

${\mathrm{Flux}}_{\mathrm{H20}}=-2,13{.10}^{-10}\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{2}s$

${\mathrm{Flux}}_{\mathrm{H2}}=2,37{.10}^{-11}\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{2}s$

Initially, the initial capillary pressure is:

1. For the game $\mathrm{Pc}=5,18\mathrm{Mpa}$
2. For damaged area and Cox: $1\mathrm{atm}$

The gas pressure is initially $1\mathrm{atm}$ everywhere.

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics A

## 3 Summary of the results

This test case is well suited to one of its objectives: testing the functionality of HYDR_VGM. In addition, it allows you to have a classic problem of digital underground storage modelling: gas injection into a near-saturated medium. We do not have any reference solutions to compare with, however, the values and the appearance of the results are classic of this type of problem. So we make it a test case of non-regression.
