# v4.04.105 TPLV105 - Stationary nonlinear thermal in a moving marker: Varestrain test simulation

**Summary:**

This test presents the thermal simulation by elements finished from the Varestraint test. This weldability test is used to characterize the resistance to hot cracking of materials.

This test allows testing a nonlinear thermal problem formulated in a mobile marker under condition of stationarity.

In this test only one model is performed, this is a thermal model PLAN associated with the operator THER_NON_LINE_MO allowing to calculate the nonlinear stationary response with a mobile load.

The welding parameters are presented in the table below:

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

This torch is placed in the centre of the plate, $15\mathrm{mm}$ of the edge, and moves parallel to its length at constant speed ($\mathrm{14cm}/\min$) to $\mathrm{85.5mm}$ of the edge corresponding to the position of extinguishing the torch.
