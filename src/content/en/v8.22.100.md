# v8.22.100 AHLV100 - Anechoic output waveguide

**Summary:**

A straight waveguide with anechoic output, rigid walls, whose propagation medium is "normal" air, is excited by a vibrating piston harmonically.

The acoustic pressure field of the harmonic response for 13 different modellings is calculated by an acoustic formulation. The results are tested in 2 points at the inlet and 2 points at the outlet.

They enable the validation of rigidity, mass, damping (impedance), source vector (normal speed imposed) and post-processings operators.

The reference results are derived from an analytical calculation.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The general analytical solution for a waveguide is written:

- for pressure:

$$
p(x,y,z)=A\exp(\mathit{ikx})+B\exp(-\mathit{ikx})
$$

- for vibration speed:

$$
V=-\frac{1}{{\rho}_{0}{c}_{0}}\left(A\exp(\mathit{ikx})-\mathit{Bexp}(-\mathit{ikx})\right){e}_{x}
$$

- A and B are determined by boundary conditions:

$$
\text{En}x=0\to {V}_{n}={V}_{n0}
$$

$$
\text{En}x=L\to p(L,y,z)=ZV(L,y,z)\cdot {n}_{L}
$$

This gives:

$$
A=B\left(\frac{Z-{\rho}_{0}{c}_{0}}{Z+{\rho}_{0}{c}_{0}}\right)\exp(-2\mathit{ikL})
$$

Et:

$$
B=\frac{{\rho}_{0}{c}_{0}{V}_{n}}{\left(\frac{Z-{\rho}_{0}{c}_{0}}{Z+{\rho}_{0}{c}_{0}}\right)\exp(-2\mathit{ikL})-1}
$$

In the case studied, the output of the guide is anechoic , $Z={\rho}_{0}{c}_{0}$ and therefore:

$$
p(x,y,z)={\rho}_{0}{c}_{0}{V}_{n}\exp(-\mathit{ikx})
V(x,y,z)=-{V}_{n}\exp(-\mathit{ikx}){e}_{x}
$$

For acoustic intensity:

$$
I=\frac{1}{2}p{V}^{\text{*}}=\frac{1}{2}{\rho}_{0}{c}_{0}{V}_{n}^{2}{e}_{x}
$$

That is, the active acoustic intensity uniform throughout the guide and parallel to the axis. The eigenfrequencies are given for the guide closed at both ends by:

$$
{f}_{m,n,p}=\frac{{c}_{0}}{2}{\left(\frac{{m}^{2}}{{I}_{x}^{2}}+\frac{{n}^{2}}{{I}_{y}^{2}}+\frac{{p}^{2}}{{I}_{z}^{2}}\right)}^{\frac{1}{2}}
$$
