# v6.02.101 SSNL101 - Non-linear behavior of a line arming element

**Summary:**

This test considers 1 element discrete to 2 nodes subjected to a transverse stress in nonlinear static analysis.

The element has a behavior governed by a nonlinear relationship expressed in effort and unidirectional displacement in the $y$ transverse and local direction.

The test is useful in fully simulating the possible loading and discharge routes in each of the areas of the behavior relationship: elastic, plastic and ultimate.

The small size of the problem to an unknown (transverse displacement of the end) allows the solution of an algebraic expression exactly found by *Aster* .

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A discreet element of zero size at 2 nodes.

Local benchmark = global benchmark.

A K_TR_D_L rigidity matrix affected by default:

$1.6N/m$ in translation, $1.9N/m$ in rotation.

The stiffness characteristics of $y$ (here equal to the $Y$ global axis) are modified by a stress-moving ARME behaviour relationship introduced by a characteristic material.

### 1.2 Material properties

- ${d}_{e}$ limit displacement of the elastic domain,
- ${d}_{l}$ limit displacement of the plastic domain,
- ${K}_{\mathrm{el}}$ slope of elastic domain,
- ${K}_{\mathrm{pl}}$ slope of the plastic domain,
- ${K}_{G}$ ultimate slope,

### 2.2 Baseline

Direct calculations on the limit curve of the behavioral relationship:

${F}_{y}={k}_{\mathrm{el}}\cdot {U}_{y}$ si ${U}_{y}<{d}_{e}$

${F}_{y}={k}_{\mathrm{el}}\cdot {d}_{e}+{k}_{\mathrm{pl}}({U}_{y}-{d}_{e})$ si ${U}_{y}\in \left[{d}_{e},{d}_{l}\right]$

$\begin{array}{}\mathrm{Vari}={U}_{y}-{d}_{e}\\ \mathrm{Varimax}={d}_{l}-{d}_{e}\end{array}$

${F}_{y}={k}_{\mathrm{el}}\cdot {d}_{e}+{k}_{\mathrm{pl}}({d}_{l}-{d}_{e})+{k}_{G}({U}_{y}-{d}_{l})$     si $\mathrm{Vari}=\mathrm{Varimax}$
