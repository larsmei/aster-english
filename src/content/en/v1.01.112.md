# v1.01.112 ZZZZ112 - Cylinder under variable pressure. Validation of LIRE_PLEXUS

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the operation of the LIRE_PLEXUS command. This allows reading pressure fields calculated by PLEXUS on a wired mesh, and to apply these pressures to a mesh composed of wired hulls or d-elements.

This test has an analytical solution: it is a cylinder subjected to pressure that varies linearly according to its axis.

Three modellings are proposed:

- Modelization A: The cylinder is meshed in elements DKT / POU_D_E;
- Modelization B: The cylinder is meshed in elements COQUE_3D / POU_D_E;
- Modelization C: The cylinder is meshed in elements COQUE_3D / TUYAU_3M / POU_D_T.

The results differ from reference solution only because of the lack of refinement of the mesh hulls (especially for Modelling A).

This test therefore validates the LIRE_PLEXUS command.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

$X$ axis cylinder, $L$ length, $R$ medium radius, $e$ thickness.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.112/Object_1.svg)

This is $L=\mathrm{1m}$, $R=\mathrm{0.1m}$, $e=\mathrm{0.01m}$.

### 1.2 Material properties

Linear elasticity:

- Young's modulus : $E = 2 \cdot 10^{11} Pa$ ;
- Poisson's ratio : $\nu = 0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The mesh in shell is connected to elements beams in $A$ and $B$ . This allows to give boundary conditions compatible with beam kinematics. However, we are interested only in the solution of hulls subjected to internal pressure.

The point $D$ is clamped.

The pressure is read on a mesh "plexus" segment $\mathit{AB}$ , with 20 elements beams.

It varies as follows:

$$
P = 10 \cdot (1 - \frac{X}{L}) Pa
$$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is an analytical bib1.

If the pressure varies as:

$$
P = - P_0 (1 + \lambda_p - \frac{X}{L})
$$

then the circumferential component of the stress tensor is:

$$
\sigma_{\theta\theta} = \frac{P_0 R}{e} (1 + \lambda_p - \frac{X}{L})
$$

and the field of displacement is:

$$
u_x & = - \frac{\nu}{E} \frac{P_0 R}{e} X (1 + \lambda_p - \frac{X}{2 L}) \\
u_r & = - \frac{1}{E} \frac{P_0 R^2}{e} (1 + \lambda_p - \frac{X}{L})
$$

### 2.2 Reference results

This is ${\lambda}_{p}=0$.

| X | $u_r (m)$ | $u_x (m)$ | $\sigma_{\theta\theta} (Pa)$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | $5\cdot 10^{-11}$ | $0$ | $100$ |
| 1 | $0$ | $7.5\cdot 10^{-11}$ | $0$ |

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

PILKEY W.D.: "Formulas for stress, train and structural matrices." Wiley & Sons, New York, 1994.
