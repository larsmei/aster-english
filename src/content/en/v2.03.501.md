# v2.03.501 SDLS501 - Free vibrations of a corrugated sheet in its plane

**Summary:**

This test represents a dynamic modal analysis calculation of a corrugated sheet in its free-form design. It allows to validate the DKT and COQUE_SOLIDE plate models.

The frequencies obtained are compared with an reference solution obtained from an COQUE_3D model.

## 6 Summary of the results

This test case has been used to test the DKT and COQUE_SOLIDE models on a plate. The results obtained compared to a solution from COQUE_3D Modelling show that the error due to the less rich interpolation of DKT compared to COQUE_3D and the excellent performance of COQUE_SOLIDE.
