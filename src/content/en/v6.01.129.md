# v6.01.129 SSNA129 - Elastoplastic tubes subjected to internal/external pressures axisymmetric

**Summary:**

This test case allows a comparison of finished elements plates and hulls (cinematics, integration methods in thickness) and pipes to model homogenous elastoplastic cylindrical tubes subjected to static axisymmetric pressure loading paths, producing a solution independent of the axial direction of the tube.
Three tube geometries are processed:

- $\mathit{h}/\mathit{R} = \mathrm{0.05}= \mathrm{1/20}$,
- $\mathit{h}/\mathit{R} = \mathrm{0.10}= \mathrm{1/10}$,
- $\mathit{h}/\mathit{R} = \mathrm{0.20}= \mathrm{1/5}$

The axisymmetric pressure loading routes are:

- internal/external asynchronous pressures alternated to complete discharge of the isotropic hardening elastoplastic tube,
- Internal monotonous pressure increasing to the ruin limit, with radial steering of the elastoplastic tube without nutring.

The reference solutions are obtained by elements solid finished axisymmetric Modelling on a very refined mesh in thickness: see Modelling A. These solutions are also confronted with partial analytical results.

The elements finished plates and shells tested are:

- Modelling B: `DKT` [r3.07.03](doc:r3.07.03) plates,
- Modelling C: `COQUE_3D` [r3.07.04](doc:r3.07.04) density hulls.
- Modelling D: solid `COQUE_SOLIDE` [r3.07.10](doc:r3.07.10) hulls,
- Modelling E: `TUYAU_3M` [r3.08.06](doc:r3.08.06) pipes, with their ability to treat swelling (radial).

The following are compared respectively:

- radial displacement over time,
- axial displacement over time,
- circumferential and radial constraints in thickness at certain moments,
- the plastic deformations accumulated in thickness at certain moments.

## 1 Reference problem

The reference problem is the study presented in and .
The material of the tube is supposed to be elastoplastic with isotropic hardening and Von Mises criterion, described by a tensile curve with plastic tray.
A complicated pressure loading path is chosen, showing phases of the expansion or contraction of the tube (low pinch) and phases of the "rolling" type (strong pinching), such as entering the plastic domain. The residual state is also considered.

![Figure](/figures/v6.01.129/schema_castest.png)

*Schedema of the tube under internal/external pressure*

- Route 1: Asynchronous internal/external pressures alternated to complete discharge of the isotropic-nutted elastoplastic tube. This route is inspired by the tube's dudgeoning in a tube plate.
- path 2: Internal monotonous pressure increasing to the ruin limit, with radial steering of the isotropic hardening elastoplastic tube. The progression of the plastic flow area in the thickness is decisive to describe the fate of the ruin. The first plasticization appears, in any case, in the internal wall. This path is inspired by a test of tube burst pressure.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solutions for the three tube geometries are obtained by isotropic, Von-based elastoplastic Modelling with Code_Aster in elements solid finished axisymmetric on a very refined quadratic mesh: 100 mesh cells in thickness, cf. v6.01.129-Modelization_A. The solutions can also be compared with the results obtained in and with a mesh made with 10 elements finished axisymmetric quadratic and an elastoplastic calculation with isotropic and Von-based hardening.

The radial static equilibrium equation of the internal and external pressure tube independent of $z$, cf. e 3.3.1 of , leads to the expression of the mean circumferential stress in thickness, regardless of the behaviour of the material:

$\overline{\sigma_{\theta\theta}}=\frac{N_{\theta\theta}}{h}=\frac{1}{h}\int_{R_i}^{R_e}\sigma_{\theta\theta}\left(r\right)dr=\frac{R_i+R_e}{2h}\left(p_i-p_e\right)- \frac{p_i+p_e}{2}$

For routes 1 and 2, the elastoplastic material with isotropic hardening with the tensile curve with a perfect plastic tray is described, see section v6.01.129-Probleme_de_reference.

- For route 2 (monoton internal pressure to ruin)

The expression (obtained by R.Hill, cf. eq 8.7.9 de ) of the pure radial plastic discharge ruin pressure of a tube blocked under internal pressure with Von criterion is recalled.

$p_{ruine}= \frac{2 \sigma_{y}  \sqrt{3} }{3} ln \left( \frac{{R}_{e}}{{R}_{i}} \right)$

With the consideration of the nutrification, we also find the close expression of the pressure of ruin of a blocked tube which involves the average between the elastic limit (conventionally taken at 0.2% plastic deformation) and the plastic flow limit:

$p_{ruine}^{éc}=  \frac{ \left( \sigma _{y}+\sigma _{u}\right) \sqrt{3} } {3} ln \left( \frac{{R}_{e}}{{R}_{i}} \right)$

With the data provided in v6.01.129-Probleme_de_reference, the numerical values are obtained using the elements 2D axisymmetric finished digital simulation for an unblocked tube:

| 0.10 | 66.45 | 33.23 |
| 0.20 | 133.24 | 33.31 |

## 3 Modelling A

Model A is the reference calculation based on elastoplastic modelling (isotropic hardening model with two-linear tensile curve) in elements solid finished axisymmetric. The calculated quantities depend only on the radial direction. The three tubes (with their three different thickness/ray ratio) are calculated together.

### 3.1 Mesh characteristics

The quadratic mesh is very refined: 100 mesh cells QUAD8 in thickness, but one mesh cell according to the axis of the tube. There are 503 nodes.

### 3.2 Non-linear static Modelling characteristics and Loading Conditions

The elements solid finished are axisymmetric. The length of the modelled tubes is $10 \mathit{mm}$. The lower side is affected by a total blockage according to the axial direction; the upper side is affected by a uniform restraint according to the axial direction to simulate a kinematic identical to that of a tube of very long length.
The time step chosen is $0.5$, fixed for all non-linear calculation steps; however, a cut is allowed in the event of convergence failure, with the `RESI_GLOB_RELA` criterion fixed to $10^{-6}$. The Newton method uses the `MATRICE='TANGENTE'` option.
(For plotting curves, a calculation is used with a maximum time step of $0.1$.)

Pressures are applied to the inner and outer edges respectively.
For the calculation of the limit pressure (track 2), a load control by radial displacement of an node of the outer wall is used.

## 4 Modelling B

Modelling B is done with elements finished plates of the DKT family on a half-circumference of the tube.

### 4.1 Mesh characteristics

There are 36 mesh cells quadrangles on the $0,\pi$ interval, in the $Ox>0$ half-plane, but an mesh cell on the $Oz$ tube axis. The mesh is symmetrical with the $Oy = 0$ axis, so that it has a node exactly on this axis to compare with the mesh axisymmetric, cf. v6.01.129-Modelization_A.
`MODI_MAILLAGE (ORIE_NORM_COQUE)` is used to ensure proper orientation of normals to the hull.
Il y a 74 nodes.

![Figure](/figures/v6.01.129/maillage_B.png)

### 4.2 Modelling characteristics and Loading Conditions

There are 5 layers in the thickness, each with 3 subpoints for the calculation of stress fields and internal variables.

The lower edge is affected by a total blockage in $Oz$ axial direction; the upper edge is assigned a uniform restraint on the axial direction translation to simulate a kinematic identical to that of a tube of very long length.
The applied pressure values are corrected to meet the exact internal and external wall metrics, so that they are identical to those applied in model A. The factor keyword used in `AFFE_CHAR_MECA` is `FORCE_COQUE=_F(PRES=)`.

### 4.3 Non-linear static Modelling characteristics

The time step chosen is $0.5$, fixed for all nonlinear calculation steps; however, a cut is allowed in the event of convergence failure, the `RESI_GLOB_RELA` criterion being fixed to $10^{-6}$. The precision required for the fulfilment of the flat stress condition is: `RESI_CPLAN_RELA=1e-06`. The Newton method uses the `MATRICE='TANGENTE'` otion.
For the calculation of the limit pressure, a load control is used by radial displacement of an node from the outer wall.
(For plotting curves, a calculation is used with a maximum time step of $0.1$.)

### 4.4 post-processing

The post-processing of the radial displacement is made at any time in a node on the $Oy=0$ axis.
The post-processing of internal constraints and variables is done on an mesh cell and an point integration with its subpoint family, close to the $Ox$ axis.

## 7 Modelling E

The E Modelling is done with finished elements pipes on the tube axis.

### 7.1 Mesh characteristics

There is an mesh cell `SEG3` depending on the $Oz$ tube axis.

### 7.2 Modelling characteristics

The finite element is `TUYAU_3M`, with 3 hull modes on the circumference (Fourier). In this model, cf. [r3.08.06](doc:r3.08.06), kinematics represents axial, circumferential, tangential and transverse distortion deformations in the section plane, but not radial deformation or orthogonal transverse distortion in the section plane. The behavior relationship produces axial, circumferential, plane shears tangential and transverse stresses in the section plane.
The `MODI_METRIQUE = OUI` option is chosen to express gradients in thickness with the local metric (not the average metric as in the elements of ordinary cockurs).
There are 16 sectors on the circumference, each with 3 subpoints for the calculation of the stress fields and internal variables. There are 5 layers in the thickness, each with 3 subpoints for the calculation of the stress fields and internal variables. This makes $4 \times 2 + 3 = 11$ subpoints in the thickness and $15 \times 2 + 3 = 33$ subpoints in the circumference, hence $363$ subpoints in total.

### 7.3 Loading conditions

The lower edge is affected by a total blockage of all 6 degrees of freedom beam; the upper edge is affected by a total blockage of 5 degrees of freedom beam other than translational $Oz$ axial direction.
The degrees of freedom of Fourier "out-of-plane" modes: UO2, UO3, VO2, VO3, WO2, WO3 are also blocked on the lower and upper edges.

This simulates a kinematic identical to that of a tube of very long length.

The applied pressure values are corrected to meet the exact internal and external wall metrics, so that they are identical to those applied in model A. The factor keyword used in `AFFE_CHAR_MECA` is `FORCE_TUYAU=_F(PRES=)`.
Since this model only accepts pressures on the inner surface of the pipe, the application of external pressure is simulated by negative internal pressure, so that its effect on the normal circumferential stress is identical:
$p_i^{éq}=-p_e \times {R_e}/{R_i}$.

### 7.4 Non-linear static Modelling characteristics

The time step chosen is $0.5$, fixed for all non-linear calculation steps; however, a cut is allowed in the event of convergence failure, the `RESI_GLOB_RELA` criterion being fixed to $10^{-6}$. The precision required for the fulfilment of the flat stress condition is: `RESI_CPLAN_RELA=1e-06`. The Newton method uses the `MATRICE='TANGENTE'` otion.
For the calculation of the limit pressure, a load control is used by the radial displacement WO of the node of the lower edge.
(For plotting curves, a calculation is used with a maximum time step of $0.1$.)

### 7.5 post-processing

The post-processing of the radial displacement is made in a node on the $z=0.$ plane at any moment.
The post-processing of the axial displacement is made in a node on the $z=1.$ plane at any moment.
The generalized forces (beam theory) are produced at nodes of elements: N, VY, VZ, MT, MFY, MFZ.
The post-processing of the internal constraints and variables is done on an integration point sub-, close to the $z=0.$ plane.

## 8 Summary of the results

In conclusion, this comparative assessment of modellings of elastoplastic hulls and pipes, of various ratios of thickness over radius (from thin to thick hull), can be summarized as follows:

- for a complex loading journey in internal and external pressures:

  - `COQUE_SOLIDE` Modelling by clamping gives almost identical values to the 2D axisymmetric calculation; `DKT` Modelling with behavior model expressed in flat stresses deviates significantly from the 2D axisymmetric calculation in the case of plastic load. By introducing internal and external pressures with a corrected value that correctly represents the true area of the internal and external walls (metric correction according to thickness), the results are corrected significantly, but this is not enough to get close enough to the reference calculation; `COQUE_3D` Modelling produces results very similar to `DKT` Modelling, since these fine elements membranes, in a homogeneous situation, must give the same results as `DKT` with mesh cells planees; `TUYAU_3M` Modelling (`TUYAU_6M` Modelling would give the same result) provides very close results to `DKT` Modelling.
  - for stresses: The residual radial and circumferential stresses are very well evaluated by `COQUE_SOLIDE` modelling using the clamping in reference to the 2D axisymmetric calculation; `DKT` modelling with behavior model expressed in flat stresses deviates significantly from the 2D axisymmetric calculation even after internal and external pressures have been used with a corrected value. While in `DKT` and `COQUE_3D` modelling produce constant circumferential stresses in thickness, `TUYAU_3M` modelling produces variable stresses in thickness, but does not overlap the reference model values.
  - for internal variables: `COQUE_SOLIDE` Modelling using the clamping system is very good at evaluating the nutrification variable, while modellings in flat stresses (`DKT`, `COQUE_3D`, `TUYAU_3M`) are very disappointing.
- for a monotonous loading journey to the internal pressure ruin:

  - The `COQUE_SOLIDE` Modelling capacity is observed to correctly reproduce the evolution of plasticization in thickness and to provide the boundary load with high accuracy.
  - `DKT` Modelling with behavior model expressed in flat stresses deviates significantly from the 2D axisymmetric calculation, even after having corrected the internal pressure with the true area of the internal wall. It is apparent, as expected, that this Modelling without pinching leaves no margin between first plasticization and ruin of the structure.
  - The evolution of radial displacement during loading corroborates these observations.
  - However, in terms of plastic ruin limit load, modellings `COQUE_SOLIDE` , `COQUE_3D` , `DKT`, `TUYAU_3M` provide conservative values to within 10%.

For example, for these types of problems:

- The hypothesis of the flat constraints appearing in the writing of the constitutive law is too restrictive and enrichment in clamping constraints is decisive;
- The correction of metrics according to thickness is also necessary.

For this reason, `COQUE_SOLIDE` [r3.07.04](doc:r3.07.04) inshell modelling allows the reproduction of completely satisfactory results, with reference to the calculation with solid finished elements, cf. v6.01.129-Modelization_A. The other modellings in shell are too "poor".
