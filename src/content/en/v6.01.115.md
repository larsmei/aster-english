# v6.01.115 SSNA115 – Scratch of a rigid frame with cohesive elements

**Summary:**

This test case is designed to study the digital tearing of a clamped rigid frame in a hollow cylinder. The decohesion is described with two cohesive models.

**B** Modelling: from d的elements joint with CZM_LIN_REG cohesive law (see [R7.02.11](doc:r7.02.11) documentation) using AXIS_JOINT Modelling.

**C** Modelling: from d

To validate the results we will rely on the analytical solution developed in [bib3]. The interested reader will also be able to refer to it for a more in-depth study of this test case.

## 5 Summary of the results

The three types of d-elements allow for a good prediction of decohesion, as the latter develops in the same way throughout the height of the cylinder. Moreover, the numerical results are very close to the analytical solution. Furthermore, the models proposed allow the correct reproduction of the progressive evolution (in the case of modellings B and C) of the cracking according to the characteristic lengths of the structure and the material. The reader concerned can refer to [bib3] for more details.
