# u2.03.11 Recommendations for the modelling of “SCS”, Steel Concrete Structures

> **Summary.**

The aim of this document is to provide recommendations for the modelling of “Steel Concrete Structures”. This document addresses the modelling of walls, of floors and of the junctions between the various structural elements. Different approaches are possible and the aim of this document is not to take the place of the engineer's analysis, but to allow the engineer to choose the modelling options while warning of certain modellings which can lead to “unrealistic” results.

This document also addresses the constitutive laws and the post-processing.
