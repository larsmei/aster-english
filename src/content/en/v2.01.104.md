# v2.01.104 SDLD104 - Extrapolation of local measurements on a complete model (discreet)

**Summary:**

It is a discrete linear dynamic test.

The aim is to test the PROJ_MESU_MODAL command in the case of a discrete system. This command allows to project dynamic dynamic transient responses at a number of points on a numerical Modelling modal basis.

This test contains 2 modellings where the projection is made on a concept of type [mode_meca], the difference being given by the way this concept is made.

For the 2 modellings, the experimental measurements provided are identical and allow testing of nodes searches against nodes, taking into account local orientation and processing of sampling in constant or non-constant time for measurements on the move.

In both cases, the reference solution is determined analytically (by Maple); the projection is made in the favorable configuration where the number of modes is equal to the number of measurements.

The displacement responses obtained after projection are identical to the reference displacements provided in data.

The values of the speeds and accelerations derived from the movements obtained after projection are similar to those obtained analytically. The low differences observed are due to the approximation errors generated by the determination via a linear schematic in time of the speeds and accelerations.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Description of the system

We consider the system represented by the following diagram:

### 1.2 Masses and rigidity

## 2 Reference solutions

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The analytical solution of this problem is presented below.

- Vibration modes and frequencies:

The following system characterizes the dynamics of the masses:

> $\lbrace \begin{array}{}m\ddot{{x}_{1}}+\mathrm{2k}{x}_{1}-k{x}_{2}=0\\ m\ddot{{x}_{2}}+\mathrm{2k}{x}_{2}-k{x}_{1}=0\end{array}$                                     éq 2.1-1

This is equivalent to the following system:

> $\lbrace \begin{array}{}m(\ddot{{x}_{1}}+\ddot{{x}_{2}})+k({x}_{1}+{x}_{2})=0\\ m(\ddot{{x}_{1}}-\ddot{{x}_{2}})+\mathrm{3k}({x}_{1}-{x}_{2})=0\end{array}$                                 éq 2.1-2

The system's 2 frequencies are therefore given by:

${\omega}_{1}=\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$     et ${\omega}_{2}=\sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{3k}}{m}}$                     éq 2.1-3

and the associated modal deformations are:

${\Phi}_{1}=(\begin{array}{}1\\ 1\end{array})$     et ${\Phi}_{2}=(\begin{array}{}1\\ -1\end{array})$                         éq 2.1-4

The generalised matrices are:

$\stackrel{ˉ}{M}={\Phi}^{T}M\Phi =(\begin{array}{cc}1& 1\\ 1& -1\end{array})(\begin{array}{cc}m& 0\\ 0& m\end{array})(\begin{array}{cc}1& 1\\ 1& -1\end{array})=(\begin{array}{cc}\mathrm{2m}& 0\\ 0& \mathrm{2m}\end{array})$

$\stackrel{ˉ}{K}={\Phi}^{T}K\Phi =(\begin{array}{cc}1& 1\\ 1& -1\end{array})(\begin{array}{cc}\mathrm{2k}& -k\\ -k& \mathrm{2k}\end{array})(\begin{array}{cc}1& 1\\ 1& -1\end{array})=(\begin{array}{cc}\mathrm{2k}& 0\\ 0& \mathrm{6k}\end{array})$             éq 2.1-5

- Transitional response:

Sine stress is applied to the first mass: $F=(\begin{array}{}1\\ 0\end{array})\sin(\omega t)$

The dynamic system checked is as follows:

> $M\ddot{X}+KX=F$                                     éq 2.1-6

By projecting on the basis of the eigenmodes, we obtain:

> ${\Phi}^{T}M\Phi \ddot{\eta}+{\Phi}^{T}K\Phi \eta ={\Phi}^{T}F$                         éq 2.1-7

Either:

$(\begin{array}{cc}\mathrm{2m}& 0\\ 0& \mathrm{2m}\end{array})(\begin{array}{}\ddot{{\eta}_{1}}\\ \ddot{{\eta}_{2}}\end{array})+(\begin{array}{cc}\mathrm{2k}& 0\\ 0& \mathrm{6k}\end{array})(\begin{array}{}{\eta}_{1}\\ {\eta}_{2}\end{array})=(\begin{array}{cc}1& 1\\ 1& -1\end{array})(\begin{array}{}1\\ 0\end{array})\sin(\omega t)$             éq 2.1-8

We therefore end up with the following decoupled system:

$\lbrace \begin{array}{}m\ddot{{\eta}_{1}}+k{\eta}_{1}=\frac{1}{2}\sin(\omega t)\\ m\ddot{{\eta}_{2}}+3k{\eta}_{2}=\frac{1}{2}\sin(\omega t)\end{array}$                             éq 2.1-9

The solution of this system is given by:

$\lbrace \begin{array}{}{\eta}_{1}(t)={A}_{1}\cos({\omega}_{1}t)+{B}_{1}\sin({\omega}_{1}t)+\frac{\sin(\omega t)}{\mathrm{2m}({\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}^{2})}\\ {\eta}_{2}(t)={A}_{2}\cos({\omega}_{2}t)+{B}_{2}\sin({\omega}_{2}t)+\frac{\sin(\omega t)}{\mathrm{2m}({\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}^{2})}\end{array}$             éq 2.1-10

The movement in physical space is obtained by Ritz formula:

> $X=(\begin{array}{}{x}_{1}\\ {x}_{2}\end{array})=\Phi \eta =(\begin{array}{cc}1& 1\\ 1& -1\end{array})(\begin{array}{}{\eta}_{1}\\ {\eta}_{2}\end{array})=(\begin{array}{}{\eta}_{1}+{\eta}_{2}\\ {\eta}_{1}-{\eta}_{2}\end{array})$                 éq 2.1-11

The expressions of ${x}_{1}(t)$ and ${x}_{2}(t)$ are derived from this:

$\lbrace \begin{array}{}{x}_{1}(t)={A}_{1}\cos({\omega}_{1}t)+{B}_{1}\sin({\omega}_{1}t)+{A}_{2}\cos({\omega}_{2}t)+{B}_{2}\sin({\omega}_{2}t)+\frac{\sin(\omega t)}{\mathrm{2m}}(\frac{1}{{\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}+\frac{1}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}})\\ {x}_{2}(t)={A}_{1}\cos({\omega}_{1}t)+{B}_{1}\sin({\omega}_{1}t)-{A}_{2}\cos({\omega}_{2}t)+{B}_{2}\sin({\omega}_{2}t)+\frac{\sin(\omega t)}{\mathrm{2m}}(\frac{1}{{\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}-\frac{1}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}})\end{array}$

éq 2.1-12

At the initial moment, the system is at rest, where the final expressions of ${x}_{1}(t)$ and ${x}_{2}(t)$:

$\lbrace \begin{array}{}{x}_{1}(t)=\frac{1}{\mathrm{2m}}\left[\frac{\sin(\omega t)-\frac{\omega}{{\omega}_{1}}\sin({\omega}_{1}t)}{{\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}+\frac{\sin(\omega t)-\frac{\omega}{{\omega}_{2}}\sin({\omega}_{2}t)}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}\right]\\ {x}_{2}(t)=\frac{1}{\mathrm{2m}}\left[\frac{\sin(\omega t)-\frac{\omega}{{\omega}_{1}}\sin({\omega}_{1}t)}{{\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}-\frac{\sin(\omega t)-\frac{\omega}{{\omega}_{2}}\sin({\omega}_{2}t)}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}\right]\end{array}$             éq 2.1-13

The speeds of both masses are calculated by drifting the movements from time:

$\lbrace \begin{array}{}\dot{{x}_{1}}(t)=\frac{\omega}{\mathrm{2m}}\left[\frac{\cos(\omega t)-\cos({\omega}_{1}t)}{{\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}+\frac{\cos(\omega t)-\cos({\omega}_{2}t)}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}\right]\\ \dot{{x}_{2}}(t)=\frac{\omega}{\mathrm{2m}}\left[\frac{\cos(\omega t)-\cos({\omega}_{1}t)}{{\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}-\frac{\cos(\omega t)-\cos({\omega}_{2}t)}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}\right]\end{array}$                 éq 2.1-14

The accelerations of both masses are calculated by drifting the speeds from time:

$\lbrace \begin{array}{}\ddot{{x}_{1}}(t)=\frac{\omega}{\mathrm{2m}}\left[\frac{\omega \sin(\omega t)-{\omega}_{1}\sin({\omega}_{1}t)}{{\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}+\frac{\omega \sin(\omega t)-{\omega}_{2}\sin({\omega}_{2}t)}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}\right]\\ \ddot{{x}_{2}}(t)=\frac{\omega}{\mathrm{2m}}\left[\frac{\omega \sin(\omega t)-{\omega}_{1}\sin({\omega}_{1}t)}{{\omega}_{1}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}-\frac{\omega \sin(\omega t)-{\omega}_{2}\sin({\omega}_{2}t)}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}-{\omega}^{2}}\right]\end{array}$         éq 2.1-15

### 2.2 Baseline

The comparison of the results is based on the movements, speeds and accelerations along the axis of the two masses, at five different moments.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

The reference solution is accurate.

The discrete model is the perfect answer to the problem (the modal base is complete; therefore there is no approximation linked to a possible modal trunking). The number of modes of the modal projection base is equal to the number of measurements, so the solution of the inversion is accurate (as opposed to a solution approached by a generalised inverse problem). If the search for nodes vis-à-vis is good, the movements obtained after projection must be in perfect accordance with the experimental values. The speeds and accelerations are determined by bypassing the modal contributions identified via a linear approximation scheme in time, which can therefore generate some errors.

## 5 Summary of the results

For both modellings, the displacement responses obtained after projection are identical to the reference displacements calculated analytically with Maple and provided in data.

The values of the speeds and accelerations derived from the movements obtained after projection are similar to those obtained analytically. The low differences observed are due to the approximation errors generated by the determination of the speeds and accelerations by a linear time diagram.
