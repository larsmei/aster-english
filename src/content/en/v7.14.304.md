# v7.14.304 HSLV304 - Cylinder under thermal loading

**Summary:**

The purpose of the test is to validate a thermal load decomposable in 2 harmonics, applied to a structure axisymmetric (cylinder in this case).

The harmonies considered are harmonies 1 and 2.

The comparison is made in relation to a hull model, with finished elements software.

In $B$ modelling , a ${\varepsilon}_{0}$ deformation field defined by AFFE_CHAR_MECA is imposed, corresponding to the deformation field from the $A$ model . The aim is to compare the results with those obtained for the $A$ model .

$E=2.1\times {10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$

$\nu =0.3$

$\alpha =0.12\times {10}^{-4}/°C$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- $\mathit{AB}$ and $\mathit{CD}$ clamped Sections
- Temperature field $T=\cos\theta +\sin2\theta$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Not applicable for static analysis.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Comparison of results with elements CASTOR-SD finished software.

CASTOR's L-element used is an 8 nodes isoparametric shell element based on the d-Ahmad formulation. It is a three-dimensional degenerate element, whose application area is that of thick shells.

### 2.2 Baseline

- Move and stress to $E,F,G$ points for each harmonic.
- Move and stress to $E,F,G$ points for total loading.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Software comparison.
