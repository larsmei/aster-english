# v6.03.108 SSNP108 - element prestressed concrete in compression

**Summary:**

An elementary structure consisting of a square concrete plate crossed by a prestress cable whose neutral fiber is confused with the horizontal symmetry axis of the plate. The vertical left edge of the plate is fixed. The cable is pulled at both ends in order to prestress the plate in compression. The tension losses along the cable are neglected.

The purpose of this test case is to validate, on a simple configuration, the method of calculating the equilibrium state of a prestressed concrete structure. The results are validated by comparison with an analytical solution.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The concrete plate is square; the sides have the same length $L=H=2m$ .

The thickness of the plate is $e=0,6m$.

The cable crosses the plate horizontally, at mid-height, without eccentricity in thickness. L-section of the right section of the cable is ${S}_{a}=1,5{.10}^{-4}{m}^{2}$.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.108/Object_1.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

| Concrete material constituting the plate: | Young's modulus ${E}_{b}={3.10}^{10}\mathit{Pa}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Steel material constituting the cable: | Young's modulus ${E}_{a}=2,1{.10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$ |

The Poisson's ratio is taken equal to 0 for both materials, thus favouring the direction of application of the normal effort (direction $x$).

The loss of tension is neglected, and the various parameters used for their estimation are set at 0.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The bottom top of the left edge of the plate, i.e. the node origin $(0;0)$, is clamped: all translation and rotation degrees of freedom are blocked.

The top top of the same left edge, i.e. the node $(0;2)$, is supported bi-laterally: the blocked degrees of freedom translation is $\mathit{DX}$ and $\mathit{DZ}$.

At both ends of the cable (which are attached to the concrete in $A$ and $B$) a normal tensile force is applied: $(–{F}_{0};0)$ to node $A$ $(0;1)$ and $({F}_{0};0)$ to node $B$ $(2;1)$ , with ${F}_{0}={2.10}^{5}N$ .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Formal solution

The movements in the cable and concrete are continuous and homogeneous. Thus the horizontal displacement on the $[0;L]$ interval is $u(x)=\frac{{u}_{L}}{L}x$

The normal stresses in the concrete plate and the steel cable are described respectively (elasticity hypothesis):

$\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{\sigma}_{b}={E}_{b}\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}={E}_{b}\frac{{u}_{L}}{L}\\ {\sigma}_{a}={E}_{a}\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+{\sigma}_{0}={E}_{a}\frac{{u}_{L}}{L}+{\sigma}_{0}\end{array}$

where ${\sigma}_{0}=\frac{{F}_{0}}{{S}_{a}}$ is the initial prestress in the cable

and ${u}_{L}$ is the horizontal displacement to $L$ abscisse.

The balance of the plate and cable set writes: ${\sigma}_{b}{S}_{b}+{\sigma}_{a}{S}_{a}=0\mathrm{\Rightarrow }{u}_{L}=\frac{-L{F}_{0}}{{E}_{b}eH+{E}_{a}{S}_{a}}$

Normal stress in the cable is: ${N}_{a}={E}_{a}{S}_{a}\frac{{u}_{L}}{F}+{F}_{0}={F}_{0}\frac{{E}_{b}eH}{{E}_{b}eH+{E}_{a}{S}_{a}}$

The total effort on the vertical section of the concrete plate is:

${N}_{b}={E}_{b}eH\frac{{u}_{L}}{L}=-{F}_{0}\frac{{E}_{b}eH}{{E}_{b}eH+{E}_{a}{S}_{a}}$

The normal stress linear density is derived from the concrete plate

${N}_{xx}=-{F}_{0}\frac{{E}_{b}e}{{E}_{b}eH+{E}_{a}{S}_{a}}$

### 2.2 Reference numerical values

The numerical reference values are:

| --- |
| ${N}_{a}=1,99825153{.10}^{5}N$ |
| ${N}_{b}=–1,99825153{.10}^{5}N$ |

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The figure below gives a simplified representation of the mesh.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.108/Object_16.svg)

The concrete plate is represented by an element DKT, supported by a 4 nodes quadrangle mesh cell.

A thickness $e=0,6m$ is affected, as well as a concrete material for which the ELAS (Young's modulus ${E}_{b}={3.10}^{10}\mathit{Pa}$) and BPEL_BETON behaviors are defined: the characteristic parameters of this relationship are fixed to 0 because the voltage losses along the prestress cable are neglected.

The node $\mathit{NB001001}$ is clamped: DX, DY, DZ, DRX, DRY and DRZ are blocked. The node $\mathit{NB002001}$ is supported bilaterally: DX and DZ are blocked.

The cable is represented by 4 elements MECA_BARRE, supported by 4 mesh cells segments at 2 nodes.

A straight section area ${S}_{a}=1,5{.10}^{-4}{m}^{2}$ is affected, as well as a steel material for which the ELAS (Young's modulus ${E}_{a}=2,1{.10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$) and BPEL_ACIER behaviors are defined: the characteristic parameters of this relationship are set to 0 (loss of neglected voltage), with the exception of the elastic limit stress for which a null value is unlawful (${f}_{\mathit{prg}}=1,77{.10}^{9}\mathit{Pa}$).

The ${F}_{0}={2.10}^{5}N$ voltage is applied to nodes $\mathit{NC001001}$ and $\mathit{NC001005}$ . This voltage value is consistent with the section and elastic limit values for a prestressed toron cable.

The equilibrium state of the plate and cable assembly is calculated in one step, the behaviour being elastic.

### 3.2 Calculation steps and tested functionalities

The main steps in calculation correspond to the features that one wishes to validate:

- DEFI_MATERIAU operator: definition of BPEL_BETON and BPEL_ACIER behavior relationships, in the particular case where voltage losses along the prestress cable are neglected (default values of parameters);
- DEFI_CABLE_BP operator: determination of a constant voltage profile along the prestress cable, with losses neglected; calculation of the coefficients of kinematic relationships between degrees of freedom of the nodes of the cable and degrees of freedom of the nodes "neighbors" of the concrete plate;
- AFFE_CHAR_MECA operator: definition of a RELA_CINE_BP load;
- STAT_NON_LINE operator, option COMPORTEMENT: calculation of the equilibrium state taking into account the RELA_CINE_BP type loading.

## 7 Summary of the results

The results obtained are validated by comparison with a reference analytical solution with very good accuracy.

The specific features tested are:

- DEFI_MATERIAU operator: definition of the characteristic parameters of steel and concrete materials for the calculation of voltage along the prestress cable, according to BPEL rules;
- DEFI_CABLE_BP operator: calculation of the voltage along the cable and coefficients of kinematic relationships between degrees of freedom of the nodes cable and degrees of freedom of the nodes "neighbors" of the concrete plate;
- AFFE_CHAR_MECA operator: definition of a RELA_CINE_BP load;
- STAT_NON_LINE operator, option COMPORTEMENT: calculation of the equilibrium state taking into account the RELA_CINE_BP type loading.
