# v6.08.109 SSND109 - Cyclical loading on a single crystal

**Summary:**

Here we present a traction-compression test on a material point with the MONOCRISTAL behavior. The family of sliding systems is octahedral.

Model A validates the implicit and explicit integration (Runge-Kutta) in comparison with the solution obtained with Zmat.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Geometry is that of a material point.

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus : $E=190000\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio : $\nu =0.3$

**Model A:**

MONO_VISC1 : $N=10$ $K=10$ $C=100000$

MONO_ISOT1 : ${R}_{0}=40$ $b=5$ $Q=0$ $H=0$

MONO_CINE1 : $D=4150$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The load consists of a strain cycle imposed following $x$:

à $t=0$ , ${\varepsilon}_{xx}=0$

à $t=0.5$ , ${\varepsilon}_{xx}=0.002$ ,

à $t=1.5$ , ${\varepsilon}_{xx}=-0.002$

à $t=2.5$ , ${\varepsilon}_{xx}=0.002$
