# v1.01.367 ZZZZ367 - COMPORTEMENT POST_INCR Keyword Validation

**Summary:**

This test validates the operation of the `POST_INCR` directive in nonlinear mechanical calculation operators with the `REST_ECRO` (nut-restore) option in the case of a bi-material.

The objective is to check the possibility of enabling the nut-restore only on part of the mesh.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

We're considering a non-regression test.

### 2.2 Baseline

The ${\sigma}_{yy}$ stress values at the end of loading and the value of the $p$ cumulative plastic deformation at the end of the nut-restore or the booster tensor will be considered.

The objective is to verify that the calculation option for hardening restoration is applied only in the desired area.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Axi-symmetric 2D modelling

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.367/maillage.gif)

*mesh used*

The mechanical calculation is done with the constitutive law elasto-plastic by Von Bets with linear isotropic nutring (keyword `VMIS_ISOT_LINE`). The nut restoration is activated only on the mesh cell `M1` (keyword `POST_INCR = 'REST_ECRO'`).

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

| Number of nodes: | 6 |
| --- | --- |
| Number of mesh cells and type: | 2 `QUAD4` |

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The non-regression of the $p$ and ${\sigma}_{yy}$ values are tested at nodes N01 and N06 for 89s and 200s.
