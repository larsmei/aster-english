# v3.03.121 SSLS121 - Laminate plate subjected to elemental loading

**Summary:**

This test represents the quasi-static calculation of a laminated plate, composed of 3 layers of orthotropic material, subjected to 4 elementary loads.

The plate is modeled in elements finished DST (mesh cells QUAD4), it is located in a $\mathrm{XZ}$ plane and inclined 48.5 degrees from $X$ (to check for mark changes).

In this test, the flat stresses and transverse shear stresses are compared to an analytical reference solution.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.121/1000020100000416000001D41A116EAD1F866743.png)

Width $a=10\mathit{mm}$ , thickness $h=\mathrm{1mm}$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

The Material propertiess constituting each of the three layers of the plate are:

Orthotropic material:

| ${E}_{l}=25\mathrm{MPa}$ | ${E}_{t}=1\mathrm{MPa}$ |
| --- | --- |
| ${G}_{\text{lt}}={G}_{\text{lz}}=0.5\mathrm{MPa}$ | ${G}_{\mathrm{tz}}=0.2\mathrm{MPa}$ |
| ${\nu}_{\text{lt}}=0.25$ |  |

Stack:

| orientation: | $[0/90/0]$ |
| --- | --- |
| thickness: | $[h/4/h/2/h/4]$ |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The loads are applied in such a way as to obtain uniform states of stress in the plate:

- Load case 1: $\mathrm{Mxx}=1$ in plate
- Clamped support on $\mathrm{AD}$
- Time spread over $\mathrm{BC}$: $\mathrm{MX}=1$
- Load case 2: $\mathrm{Myy}=1$ in the plate
- Clamped support on $\mathrm{AB}$
- Time spread over $\mathrm{CD}$: $\mathrm{MY}=1$
- Load case 3: $\mathit{QY}=1$ in plate
- Clamped support on $\mathit{AB}$
- Effort spread over $\mathit{CD}$ : $\mathit{FY}=-1$
- Load case 4: $\mathit{QX}=1$ in plate
- Clamped support on $\mathit{AD}$
- Effort spread over $\mathit{BC}$ : $\mathit{FY}=1$
