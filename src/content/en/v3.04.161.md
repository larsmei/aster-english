# v3.04.161 RCCM13 – Pipe analysis with POST_RCCM in ZE200

**Summary:**

This test is an elementary validation test of the POST_RCCM command with the TYPE_RESU_MECA='ZE200 a' and 'ZE200 b'.

The analytical solution is simple, and allows testing the post-processing in the sense of the RCC-M. The constraints are not calculated but extracted from tables.

More specifically, Modelling A allows testing the SNet FATIGUE options for ZE200 a-type results with and without earthquake.

More specifically, the B Modelling allows testing the SN option for ZE200 b-type results with and without earthquake.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Material properties

The material properties and characteristics of the RCC-M calculation are as follows:

1. Young's modulus: $E=2.E+05\mathit{MPa}$ ;
2. material constants for $\mathrm{Ke}$ calculation: $n=0.2$ , $m=\mathrm{2}$ ;
3. Young's modulus reference: ${E}_{\mathit{REFE}}=2.E+05\mathit{MPa}$;
4. permissible constraint : $\mathit{Sm}=2000\mathit{MPa}$ .

The Wöhler curve is defined analytically: ${N}_{\mathrm{adm}}=\frac{{5.10}^{5}}{{S}_{\mathrm{alt}}}$

### 1.2 Changes in constraints

##### 2.1.1.2 Calculation of Sn with earthquake

The contribution of the earthquake to the quantity Sn is added as

${S}_{\mathit{nS}} = \begin{array}{c}{C}_{1} \frac{R}{e} |{P}_{A} - {P}_{B}| + {C}_{2} \frac{R}{I} \sqrt{({M}_{\mathit{xA}} - {M}_{\mathit{xB}} \pm 2{M}_{\mathit{xS}})^2 +({M}_{\mathit{yA}} - {M}_{\mathit{yB}} \pm 2{M}_{\mathit{yS}})^2 +({M}_{\mathit{zA}} - {M}_{\mathit{zB}} \pm 2{M}_{\mathit{zS}})^2} + \Vert \sigma_{\text{tran}}^{\text{lin}}(t_1) - \sigma_{\text{tran}}^{\text{lin}}(t_2) \Vert\end{array}$

*For situation 1 without transitional constraints,*

${S}_{\mathit{nS}}=\begin{array}{c}{C}_{1}\frac{R}{e}|{P}_{A}-{P}_{B}|+{C}_{2}\frac{R}{I}\sqrt{({({M}_{\mathit{xA}}-{M}_{\mathit{xB}}\pm 2{M}_{\mathit{xS}})}^{2})}\end{array}$

${\mathit{Sn}}_{S}=1\ast \frac{0,5}{1}|201-1|+2\ast \frac{0,5}{1}\sqrt{({(21-1\pm 2\ast 21)}^{2})}=100+62=162$ originally and at the end.

*For situation 2 without transitional constraints,*

${\mathit{Sn}}_{S}=1\ast \frac{0,5}{1}|0-0|+2\ast \frac{0,5}{1}\sqrt{({(1-61\pm 2\ast 21)}^{2})}=102$ originally and at the end.

##### 2.1.1.3 Fatigue calculation for situations 1 and 2 in the same group

The calculation is detailed for the combination of situations 1 and 2 only and *at the origin* .

The table of basic use factors is being completed.

First, the quantities is calculated by situation and then the combination.

*Situation 1*

For situation 1, it is recalled that with ${\mathit{Sn}}_{0}=210$ thermal stresses (see v3.04.161-ze200a). The quantity Sp is calculated at the origin.

$S_{p} = K_{1} C_{1} \frac{R}{e} |P_{A} - P_{B}| + K_{2} C_{2} \frac{R}{I} \sqrt{\big( (M_{xA} - M_{xB})^2 + (M_{yA} - M_{yB})^2 + (M_{zA} - M_{zB})^2 \big)} + \left\| \sigma_{\text{tran}}(t_{1}) - \sigma_{\text{tran}}(t_{2}) \right\|$

*Without thermal stresses* , for the situation 1 ${S}_{p}^{0}=120$ .

*With thermal stresses, the maximum amplitudes of thermal stresses are calculated, which are originally linearized:

| Instant 1 | Instant 2 | ${\mathit{Sp}}_{0}$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1, 5 | 2, 5 | 90 |
| 1, 5 | 3, 5 | 10 |

| 2, 5 | 3, 5 | **100** |

| 3, 5 | 4, 5 | **100** |

For situation 1, we have ${\mathit{Sp}}_{0}=120+100=220$.

For $\mathit{Sm}=2000\mathit{MPa}$ we have $\mathit{Ke}=1$ and ${\mathit{Salt}}_{0}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{{E}_{c}}{E}\mathit{Ke}{\mathit{Sp}}_{0}=110\mathit{MPa}$. Based on the Wöhler curve we have ${\mathit{Nadm}}_{0}=\frac{500000}{{\mathit{Salt}}_{0}}=4545$ or ${\mathit{FU}}_{0}=2,2{10}^{-4}$.

*Situation 2*

Similar for situation 2, we have ${\mathit{Sn}}_{0}=155$, ${\mathit{Sp}}_{0}=60+100=160$, either $\mathit{Ke}=1$ and ${\mathit{Salt}}_{0}=80\mathit{MPa}$ or ${\mathit{FU}}_{0}={\mathrm{1,6.10}}^{-4}$.

* Combination of situations 1 and 2*

For the combination of situations 1 and 2 we have without thermal

${S}_{n}^{0}=\begin{array}{c}{C}_{1}\frac{R}{e}|201-0|+{C}_{2}\frac{R}{I}\sqrt{({(21-61)}^{2})}\end{array}=100,5+40=140,5$

With the thermal we have ${\mathit{Sn}}_{0}=95+140,5=235,5$ for the moments 4,5 and 1. So $\mathit{Ke}=1$ .

Without ${\mathit{Sp}}_{0}^{1}=140,5$ thermal then for example by combining 2.5 and 3 ${\mathit{Sp}}_{0}^{1}=240,5$ moments is ${\mathit{FU}}_{0}^{1}=\mathrm{2,405.}{10}^{-4}$.

The second fictitious transient is calculated, without the thermal on the moments and the pressure one takes the additional states ${\mathit{Sp}}_{0}^{2}=\begin{array}{c}{C}_{1}\frac{R}{e}|1-0|+{C}_{2}\frac{R}{I}\sqrt{({(1-1)}^{2})}\end{array}=0,5$ . Then by combining the moments 3,5 and 2 ${\mathit{Sp}}_{0}^{2}=100+0,5=100,5$ is ${\mathit{FU}}_{0}^{2}=\mathrm{1,005.}{10}^{-4}$ .

The use factor for the combination of situations 1 and 2 is therefore $\mathit{FU}={\mathit{FU}}_{0}^{1}+{\mathit{FU}}_{0}^{2}=\mathrm{2,405.}{10}^{-4}+\mathrm{1,005.}{10}^{-4}=\mathrm{3,41.}{10}^{-4}$

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

No thermal or mechanical calculations are performed in this test: the stress survey tables are directly provided to the POST_RCCM operator. The ZE200 a results are analyzed for the SN and FATIGUE options.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

On this simple test case, all the tested results are in agreement with the reference solution with an accuracy of ${10}^{-4}$ %.

- for the calculation of Sn, Sp, Salt and the usability factor,
- for a pipe junction,
- with and without earthquake.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

No thermal or mechanical calculations are performed in this test: the stress reading tables are provided directly to the POST_RCCM operator. The ZE200 b results are analyzed for the SN option.

### 4.2 Tested quantities and results

On this simple test case, all the tested results are in agreement with the reference solution:

- for the calculation of Sn,
- for a pipe junction,
- with and without earthquake.

## 5 Summary of the results

The results are accurate and show that the POST_RCCM operator correctly selects the quantities to be processed and correctly calculates the integrals (means on segments) for both ZE200 a and ZE200 b results.
