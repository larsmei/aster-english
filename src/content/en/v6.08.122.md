# v6.08.122 SSND122 - DIS_CHOC, with contact managed in the global reference, in nonlinear static

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check the contact in the global benchmark for the discrete nonlinear static `K_T_D_L` and `K_TR_D_L` type.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry A

The geometry is shown in the figure below:

![Figure](/figures/v6.08.122/v6.08.122_schema_01.png)

*Geometry of Reference problem.*

The geometry consists of:

- Two unformable solids are represented by 4 $\color{red}{\blacklozenge}$ and 1 $\color{green}{\blacklozenge}$, which are connected with an `LIAISON_SOLIDE`. $\color{red}{\blacklozenge}$ are `DIS_T_D_N`, $\color{green}{\blacklozenge}$ are `DIS_TR_D_L`.
- a discrete `DIS_T_D_L` type to represent the discrete shock.

### 1.2 Loading

#### 1.2.1 boundary conditions

The centres of the 2 unformable solids are represented by an $\color{green}{\blacklozenge}$ affected by an `DIS_TR_D_L`, one of which is nodes, the other being connected to 4 $\color{red}{\blacklozenge}$.

At the center of one solid one is applied a displacement. This displacement must be found on the center of the other solid, at the $e_x$, $e_y$ games near, as soon as the contact is activated.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Travel

The centre of one of the solids is forced to move. Solids are considered to be unformable, so this movement must be found in the centre of the other solid at the nearest games, as soon as the contact is activated.

### 2.2 Uncertainties about solutions

None.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Dimensions of geometry:

- $L_x = L_y = 2.0~cm$.
- $e_x = e_y = 0.5~cm$.
- The nodes [1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 12] are $z=0$.
- The node 10 is $z=2.0{\times 10^{-05}}$, to test the `PRECISION` keyword.
- Nodes 2 and 8 which are at the same coordinates $x$ and $y$ as nodes 1 and 7, are at $z=-0.02~cm$.

The mesh is made up of discrete elements only:

- `DIS_T_D_N` are assigned to nodes 3, 4 , 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12.
- an `DIS_T_D_L` is assigned between the nodes (6, 10).
- `DIS_TR_D_L` are assigned between nodes (1, 2) and (7, 8).

### 3.3 Mechanical characteristics

The only features are the discrete elements. These features are not physical, they only serve to give some stiffness to the system.
