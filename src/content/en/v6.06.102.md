# v6.06.102 SSNX102 - element 3D_INTERF_POU Calculation Options

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the different calculation options of the 3D_INTERF_POU [R7.01.49](doc:r7.01.49) interaction element.

This test is purely numerical. It is a question of checking that the programming is correct. Movements are imposed in different nodes of the element. It is then checked that the nodal forces, as well as the linear forces of interest and relative displacement, at the integration points and nodes, are in line with expectations.

The different modellings check:

- the orientation of the reinforcement axis : MODI_MAILLAGE( ORIE_INTERF_POU )
- the orientation of the reinforcement section : AFFE_CARA_ELEM( ORIENTATION )
- the section type of reinforcement : AFFE_CARA_ELEM( POUTRE( _F(SECTION) ))
- the behavior type : STAT_NON_LINE( COMPORTEMENT( _F(RELATION) ))
- calculation options related to linear and nonlinear static resolution: MECA_STATIQUE, STAT_NON_LINE
- CALC_CHAMP post-processing calculation options: FORC_NODA, SIEF_ELGA, SIEF_ELNO, SIEF_NOEU, SAUT_ELGA, SAUT_ELNO
- other calculation options: COOR_ELGA

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

3D_INTERF_POU modeled the interaction between a beam element, representing a reinforcement section, with an element HEXA8, representing the surrounding matrix. The domain of interaction considered (l-interface) is a line confused with the field of beam. L-L-element 3D_INTERF_POU incorporates in its Gauss points a phenomenological law 1 returning the interaction efforts between matrix and reinforcement according to their relative displacement.

The element is carried by an mesh cell HEXA27, but only the first 8 nodes (corresponding to the HEXA8 of the matrix) and the nodes 23 and 25 (corresponding to the SEG2 of the reinforcement) are assigned to degrees of freedom (ddl). On the nodes of the matrix only the translation ddls are affected, while the nodes of the reinforcement carry translation and rotation ddls.

In this test case, the mesh cell is a parallelepiped $2 \times 3 \times 7 \, m$ ($X \times Y \times Z$) dimensional device.

The section of the beam is considered:

- rectangular, $h_y = 0,5 \, m$ and $h_z = 1,0 \, m$ sides
- or circular $r = 0,2 \, m$ radius

The beam is oriented as follows:

- $X$ axis
- or $Y$ axis

Its section is oriented according to a spin angle:

- $35^\circ$,
- ou $197^\circ$.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material characteristics are chosen arbitrarily and thus it remains linearly elastic for the applied loading levels:

- $K_N = 5 \, N/m^3$ ,
- $K_T = 3 \, N/m^3$ ,
- $F_{NY} = 10^2 K_N \, N/m^2$ ,
- $F_{TY} = 10^2 K_T \, N/m^2$ ,
- $G_N = 0 \, N/m^3$ ,
- $G_T = 0 \, N/m^3$ ,

The characteristics of the material chosen have no physical meaning.

## 4 Executive summary

The different features applied to the element 3D_INTERF_POU and tested in this test case are validated. As a reminder, the different modellings check:

- the orientation of the reinforcement axis : MODI_MAILLAGE( ORIE_INTERF_POU )
- the orientation of the reinforcement section : AFFE_CARA_ELEM( ORIENTATION )
- the section type of reinforcement : AFFE_CARA_ELEM( POUTRE( _F(SECTION) ))
- the behavior type : STAT_NON_LINE( COMPORTEMENT( _F(RELATION) ))
- calculation options related to linear and nonlinear static resolution: MECA_STATIQUE, STAT_NON_LINE
- post-processing-related calculation options by CALC_CHAMP: FORC_NODA, SIEF_ELGA, SIEF_ELNO, SIEF_NOEU, SAUT_ELGA, SAUT_ELNO
