# v6.04.135 SSNV135 - Triaxial drained test with CJS (level 1)

**Summary**

This test validates the CJS model's level 1. It is a triaxial test in drained condition. Three containment levels are simulated: $100$, $200$, and then $400\mathrm{kPa}$.

Because of symmetry, only one of the eighth samples tested is considered to be triaxial.

The results obtained with the CJS1 model are compared with the analytical solution.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Analytical solution development for CJS1

We have:

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.135/Object_10.svg)

**Elastic phase:**

Simply writing the elastic law, we have:

${\sigma}_{xx}^{0}={\sigma}_{xx}^{0}+\lambda {\varepsilon}_{zz}+(\lambda +2\mu ){\varepsilon}_{xx}+\lambda {\varepsilon}_{xx}$

${\sigma}_{zz}={\sigma}_{zz}^{0}+(\lambda +2\mu ){\varepsilon}_{zz}+2\lambda {\varepsilon}_{xx}$

where $\lambda$ and $\mu$ are the Lamé coefficients here.

By eliminating ${\varepsilon}_{xx}$ between these two equations, we find:

${\sigma}_{zz}={\sigma}_{zz}^{0}+\frac{\mu (3\lambda +2\mu )}{(\lambda +\mu )}{\varepsilon}_{zz}$

**Plastic phase:**

On a:

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.135/Object_18.svg)

où

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.135/Object_19.svg)

represents the containment pressure.

Deducted for deviator components

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.135/Object_20.svg)

:

${s}_{zz}=2\left[\frac{1}{3}{I}_{1}-{\sigma}_{xx}^{0}\right]$ et ${s}_{xx}={\sigma}_{xx}^{0}-\frac{1}{3}{I}_{1}$

either: ${s}_{\mathrm{II}}=\sqrt{6}\left[{\sigma}_{xx}^{0}-\frac{1}{3}{I}_{1}\right]$ and $\det(\underline{\underline{s}})=2{\left[\frac{1}{3}{I}_{1}-{\sigma}_{xx}^{0}\right]}^{3}$

Therefore: $h({\theta}_{S})={(1-\gamma )}^{1/6}$

Moreover, when the criterion of the deviatory mechanism is met: ${s}_{\mathrm{II}}h({\theta}_{S})+{R}_{m}{I}_{1}=0$

where the relationship:

${I}_{1}=\frac{\sqrt{6}{\sigma}_{xx}^{0}}{\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}-\frac{{R}_{m}}{{(1-\gamma )}^{1/6}}}$

And finally, we have for vertical stress:

${\sigma}_{zz}=\frac{\sqrt{6}{\sigma}_{xx}^{0}}{\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}-\frac{{R}_{m}}{{(1-\gamma )}^{1/6}}}-2{\sigma}_{xx}^{0}$

In addition, it can be calculated that the transition between the elastic and perfectly plastic states is made for an axial deformation equal to:

${\varepsilon}_{zz}=\frac{\mu (3\lambda +2\mu )}{\lambda +\mu }\left[\frac{\sqrt{6}{\sigma}_{xx}^{0}}{\sqrt{\frac{2}{3}}-\frac{{R}_{m}}{{(1-\gamma )}^{1/6}}}-2{\sigma}_{xx}^{0}\right]$
