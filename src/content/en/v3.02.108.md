# v3.02.108 SSLP108 - clamped Arche in elasticity

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check D_PLAN_MIX_STA Modelling
on clamped arch problem in elasticity. This problem allows to test
the application of symmetry conditions to the generalised displacement components
corresponding to deformations.

## 1 Reference problem

The problem is derived from article [1].

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry can be viewed in the figure below and will be used for all modellings.

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.108/ArcheEncastree.png)

Figure 1.1-1: Géométrie de la membrane

We're taking $t=1\mathit{mm}$ and $R=10\mathit{mm}$.
We represent only the right half of the ark, so to exploit the symmetries of the problem.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic.

- Elasticity module: $E=200\mathit{\;MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio: $\nu = 0,3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The segment is embedded at the bottom of the arch ($\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=0$ ).
Vertical surface force applied to the centre section such as $\mathit{FY}=-1 \mathit{kN}/\mathit{mm}\mathrm{²}$

The right half of the arch is modeled only. On the middle section, a symmetry condition ($\mathit{DX}=0$ and $\mathit{EPXY}=0$) is imposed.
Note that for modellings type MIX_STA, the symmetry condition also implies that the tangential deformation component (generalised displacement) is zero.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

D_PLAN_MIX_STA modelling is used.
The TAU_EPSI coefficient is fixed to 1/16 (16 elements in the characteristic length of the problem).

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh consists of mesh cells type QUAD4 (linear quadrangle), with a total of 2560 elements.
