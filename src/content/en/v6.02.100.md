# v6.02.100 SSNL100 - Laying of a double-gauge line township

**Summary:**

This test simulates the operation of a two-stranded cable. The cable is attached to one end of the cable, passes on a fixed pulley towards the other end and rests in its middle on a pulley at the bottom of a mobile suspension. The deflection of the ranges is adjusted by "giving" more or less cable at the level of the fixed pulley.

The interest is to test the elements cable and pulley cable and their operation in the STAT_NON_LINE operator.

### 1.2 Material properties

Linear weight of cable : $30N/m$

Axial rigidity of the cable (produced from Young's modulus by the area of the right section): $5.{10}^{7}N$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The $O$ , $C$ and ${P}_{2}$ points are fixed.

The cable, fixed in $O$ , is supported by two pulleys. The first is attached to the lower end of the ${P}_{1}$ suspension fixed in $C$ . The second is attached to ${P}_{2}$ .

The cable is subject to its weight and is given deflection by moving its right end from $\mathrm{10 }m$ from ${R}_{2}$ to ${R}_{2}'$.

The position of the ${Q}_{1}$ , ${R}_{1}$ and ${Q}_{2}$ points is not imposed, but it is necessary to ensure that during the installation the ${P}_{1}$ pulley remains on the ${Q}_{1}{R}_{1}$ cable segment.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference deflection is relative to an inextensible $105m$ cable over a range of $100m$. It is obtained by solving a transcendent equation [bib2].

### 2.2 Baseline

The deflection reference is $13.93m$ , equal for each range.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Semi-analytic solution.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of nodes : 25

Number of mesh cells and types: 20 mesh cells SEG2 and 2 mesh cells SEG3

### 3.3 Remarks

Starting from a horizontal straight line cable in weighting, gravity is applied while increasing the length of the cable between $O$ and ${P}_{2}$ from $10m$ by moving ${R}_{2}$ to ${R}_{2}'$ (${R}_{\mathrm{2 }}{R}_{2}'=10m$). Since straight untrenched cables do not have stiffness for transverse loads, the previous load case cannot be applied immediately as a system of singular equations would be achieved.

The calculation is therefore done in 2 stages:

- The cables are pre-voltageed by applying a voltage to the cable itself in ${R}_{2}$ and to the suspension in ${P}_{1}$ (it is suggested to take $10000N$ voltages).
- The gravity load will be declared SUIV type, because of elements MEPOULI with both parts of variable length.

## 4 Modelling B

Model B is identical to model A. It is just used to validate the fact that thermal loading is not taken into account. For this reason a constant temperature is imposed. The results are identical to model A.

## 5 Summary of the results

This test ensures that Code_Aster evolutions do not cause regressions for elements cable and pulley cable, as well as for the following loads of the STAT_NON_LINE command.
