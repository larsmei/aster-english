# v7.31.103 WTNV103 – 3D Modelling of unforced swelling with GonfElas

**Summary:**

This test represents the swelling of a cell filled with water. It uses the "GonfElas" model that was originally developed by Dashnor Hoxha. It is a nonlinear elastic model dependent on suction and describing the swelling behaviour of certain types of clay. This model is written according to the following couple of variables: net stress and suction (suction is the capillary pressure). It can be considered as the elastic part of the Barcelona model.

This test is a wtnv136 declination with free movement.

### 1.2 Material properties

*Elastic properties:*

$E+150.{10}^{6}\mathrm{Pa}$

$\nu =0.3$

*Model-specific parameters* *GonfElas:*

- ${\beta}_{m}=0.1142$
- Reference pressure $A=1.\mathrm{Mpa}$

These parameters $A$ and ${\beta}_{m}$ were calculated to recover a swelling pressure of $7\mathrm{MPa}$. Indeed, when saturation reaches 1 (or capillary pressure 0), the swelling pressure is given by the following formula

$\frac{{P}_{\mathrm{gf}}}{A}=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2\sqrt{{\beta}_{m}}}+\frac{1}{2{\beta}_{m}}$

Knowing that the overall swelling pressure is given by the following relationship (cf. doc R7.01.41):

${P}_{\mathit{gf}}({\mathit{Pc}}_{0})=\underset{{\mathit{Pc}}_{0}}{\overset{0}{\int}}b\left(1+\frac{\mathit{Pc}}{A}\right){e}^{-{\beta}_{m}{\left(\frac{s}{A}\right)}^{2}}\mathit{dPc}$

L-value of this pressure can be obtained by integrating and is represented by the following curve:

![Figure](/figures/v7.31.103/100002010000031B000001C4B5E4B96F36CB3087.png)

With these parameters, the swelling pressure stabilizes as soon as the capillary pressure exceeds 6,5 MPa.

### 1.3 Initial conditions

A $t=0$ :

$\mathrm{Pgaz}=\mathrm{1atm}$

$S=0,5$ (i.e. $\mathrm{Pc}=44,7\mathrm{Mpa}$ and $\mathrm{pw}=-44.6\mathrm{Mpa}$ )

Total constraint equal to - 1atm.

### 1.4 Boundary conditions and loads

Only BAS, DERRIERE, GAUCHE moves are blocked, the others are left free.

Gas pressures are imposed at 1 atm.

The initial saturation is $\text{50 \%}$ (Pc = 44.7 MPa): the capillary pressure is reduced on all the sample (which amounts to increasing saturation, so to hydrate) and it is checked that, at zero stress, the movements are positive and that there is swelling.

More precisely, a linear downward hair pressure load is imposed on the entire domain in $\mathrm{1s}$ between $44,7\mathrm{Mpa}$ and $-10\mathrm{Mpa}$ (see ).

The hair pressure becomes negative after 0.8 seconds.The temperatures are constant (insulated cases).
