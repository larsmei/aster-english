# v7.23.101 HSNS101 - Square plate in variable traction and temperature. Flat constraints integrated by the method of DE BORST and a direct method.

**Summary:**

This test, based on the one proposed by the IPSI for the Phi2As day of 30 March 2000 (test HSNV124), validates the De Borst method for treating the flat stress condition for any nonlinear behavior. In particular, the method for VMIS_CINE_LINE, VMIS_CINE_GC, VMIS_CIN1_CHAB and VMIS_CIN2_CHAB behaviors is tested here, compared to VMIS_ECMI_LINE, which uses a method for direct integration of flat stress. All the parameters of these different models are adjusted to reproduce the same behavior.

Four modellings enable the validation of plane stresses (Borst method).

The results are compared with those of the HSNV124 test (analytical reference).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Element volume materialized by a $\mathrm{ABCD}$ square plate on the $1\mathrm{mm}$ unit side:

![Figure](/figures/v7.23.101/Object_1.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

$E={2.10}^{5}\mathrm{MPa}$ , $\nu =0.3$ , $\alpha ={2.10}^{5}°{C}^{-1}$

The material is elastoplastic with linear kinematic nutring:

$\sigma =\pm {\sigma}_{y}(T)+C(T){\varepsilon}^{p}$

$\mathrm{SIGY}=200.-\mathrm{1.7.T}$ (en $\mathrm{Mpa}$ )

D_SIGM_EPSI hardening module = $C(T)=1000+\mathrm{2990.T}$ (in $\mathrm{MPa}$ )

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Such that the stress and deformation state is uniform in the volume element:

Point $A$ blocked in $x$ and $y$ .

$\mathrm{DY}=0$ on $\mathrm{AB}$

Force spread over $\mathrm{CD}$: $\mathrm{Fy}$

Uniform temperature $T$ on $\mathrm{ABCD}$ . Reference temperature is $0°C$ .

$\mathrm{Fy}$ and $T$ vary with time as follows:

| $t$ instant | 0 | $\mathrm{1s}$ | $\mathrm{2s}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mathrm{Fy}(t)$ | 0 | $210\mathrm{MPa}$ | $210\mathrm{MPa}$ |
| $T(t)$ | 0 | $0°C$ | $100°C$ |

For displacements (elements structure): Comparison with the displacements obtained with *Code_Aster* for Modelling A.

- using VMIS_CINE_LINE behavior, using:

D_SIGM_EPSI = $\mathrm{E.C}(T)/(E+C(T))$ and the Prager $\mathrm{PRAG}=2/3C(T)$ constant

## 8 Summary of the results

This test shows the correct consideration of the flat stresses for elasto-plastic behaviors VMIS_ISOT_TRAC, VMIS_CINE_LINE, VMIS_CIN1_CHAB, and VMIS_CIN2_CHAB.

The results are identical to the analytical solution. The $\mathrm{SIZZ}$ stresses that must be zero if the hypothesis of the plane stresses is verified are actually at convergence of the Newton iterations with good accuracy ($\mathrm{SIZZ}=\mathrm{2.8.E}-5\mathrm{MPa}$ at the most, compared to $\mathrm{SIYY}=210\mathrm{MPa}$), and this for a CPU time and a nearby number of iterations.
