# v3.03.138 SSLS138 – Request for a membrane

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the behaviour of an anisotropic elastic membrane for several modes of stress, as well as the calculation of deformations and stresses in the membrane. All available membrane elementss are validated.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A square of $1m$ is considered to be side, shown below:

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.138/Shape1.gif)

### 1.2 Material properties

The square has an anisotropic membrane behaviour, characterized by the following coefficients (the coefficients not mentioned are zero):

$\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{M}_{\mathit{LLLL}}=3\\ {M}_{\mathit{TTTT}}=3\\ {M}_{\mathit{LLTT}}=1\\ {M}_{\mathit{LTLT}}=2\end{array}$

These coefficients are defined in a 90° turning point around the $(\mathit{Oz})$ axis.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Two calculations are performed, corresponding to a tensile stress and a shear stress of the membrane. The corresponding limit conditions are indicated below:

- **Tenction investigation**

$\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{u}_{Z}=0\text{sur}\text{FACE}\\ {u}_{X}=0\text{sur}\text{X\_NEG}\\ {u}_{Y}=0\text{sur}\text{Y\_NEG}\\ {F}_{X}=1\text{sur}\text{X\_POS}\end{array}$

- **Shipling scissors**

$\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{u}_{Z}=0\text{sur}\text{FACE}\\ {u}_{Y}=0\text{sur}\text{X\_NEG}\\ {u}_{X}=0\text{sur}\text{Y\_NEG}\\ {u}_{X}={u}_{Y}\text{sur}\text{POINT}\\ {F}_{Y}=1\text{sur}\text{X\_POS}\\ {F}_{X}=1\text{sur}\text{Y\_POS}\end{array}$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

> In both modes of stress, the membrane is in a uniform state of stress, so the solution of this problem can be calculated analytically in both cases.

- **Tenction investigation**

In the case of a single traction charge, it is simply shown that in the overall reference:

$\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{XX}}={F}_{X}\frac{{M}_{\mathit{LLLL}}}{({M}_{\mathit{TTTT}}{M}_{\mathit{LLLL}}-{M}_{\mathit{LLTT}}^{2})}\\ {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{YY}}=-{F}_{X}\frac{{M}_{\mathit{LLTT}}}{({M}_{\mathit{TTTT}}{M}_{\mathit{LLLL}}-{M}_{\mathit{LLTT}}^{2})}\\ {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{XY}}=0\end{array}$

- **Shipling scissors**

In the case of shearing, it is shown that in the overall reference:

$\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{XX}}=0\\ {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{YY}}=0\\ {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{XY}}=\frac{{\sigma}_{\mathit{XY}}}{{M}_{\mathit{LTLT}}}\end{array}$

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results
