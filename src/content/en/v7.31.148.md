# v7.31.148 WTNV148 – Flowing into an interface within a porous mass: using the XFEM method

**Summary:**

This test checks the opening of a cohesive hydraulic XFEM interface under the action of afluid injection. The test includes two-dimensional modelling and three-dimensional modelling.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry of the problem

This block is a block of $\mathit{LZ}=10m$ height, $\mathit{LX}=10m$ length and $\mathit{LY}=2m$ width. This block has a continuous, cohesive interface type (unmesh interface that is introduced into the model via the level-set via the DEFI_FISS_XFEM operator). It is identified by the normal level-set of $\mathit{lsn}=Z-5$ equation and crosses the block entirely in the horizontal direction by dividing it into two identical sub-blocks. The points $A(0,0,5)$ , $A'(0,2,5)$ , $B(3,0,5)$ , and $B'(3,2,5)$ will be used for the taxation of the boundary conditions and the evaluation of the quantities tested.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The conditions of Dirichlet are:

- the following $x$ moves are blocked in the left edge of the domain,
- the following $y$ and $z$ moves are blocked on the lower and upper edges of the domain.

Also, a $Q=0.025\mathit{kg}.{m}^{-1}.{s}^{-1}$ point fluid stream is injected into the cohesive interface on the left edge of the domain during a $t=10s$ time.

## 2 Quantities and reference results

### 2.1 Quantities and reference results

Under the action of fluid injection into the cohesive interface on the left edge of the domain, the cohesive interface opens and hydraulic fracture develops. The fluid pressure value and vertical opening at $A$ , $A'$ , $B$ and $B'$ points are tested at the end of $t=10s$ injection.

### 2.2 Uncertainty about the solution

We're checking the non-regression of the calculation results.
