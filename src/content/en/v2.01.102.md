# v2.01.102 SDLD102 - Transitional Under Structure: 3 Mass-4 Spring System

**Summary:**

The scope of this test is for structural dynamics. It allows the validation of time step adaptive 'ADAPT_ORDRE2' and 'RUNGE_KUTTA_54' integration schemes of the modal-based transitional computing operator as well as the linear transient response calculation on a modal basis calculated by substructure (for the 5 integration schemas: 'EULER', 'DEVOGE', 'NEWMARK', 'RUNGE_KUTTA_32' and 'ADAPT_ORDRE2'). In particular, the case of application of reduced damping to dynamic modes of the substructure projection bases is treated.

The purpose is to determine the transient response of a system composed of 3 masses and 4 springs, clamped at its ends and subjected to constant force from the initial moment. Springs are modelled by elements type 'DIS_TR' and masses by elements type 'DIS_T'.

Six modellings are proposed. In the third model, the structure is depreciated. The transitional calculation methods by substructure with Craig-Bampton ('CRAIGB') and MacNeal ('MNEAL') interfaces are tested. The RELA_EFFO_DEPL option is also tested for dynamic substructure response calculations. The associated reference results are derived from an analytical calculation. In the third, a reduced $\text{1\%}$ damping is imposed on the dynamic modes of the substructure projection bases. The transient equation verified by the complete structure was obtained analytically. Its resolution, which acts as a reference, was performed by the Maple software.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

#### 2.1.1 Unamortized structure

In this case, the reference solution can be obtained analytically:

$m(\begin{array}{ccc}1& 0& 0\\ 0& 1& 0\\ 0& 0& 1\end{array})(\begin{array}{c}\ddot{{x}_{1}}\\ \ddot{{x}_{2}}\\ \ddot{{x}_{3}}\end{array})+k(\begin{array}{ccc}2& -1& 0\\ -1& 2& -1\\ 0& -1& 2\end{array})(\begin{array}{c}{x}_{1}\\ {x}_{2}\\ {x}_{3}\end{array})=(\begin{array}{c}1\\ 0\\ 0\end{array})$

The proper pulses of the mass-spring system are:

${\omega}_{1}^{2}=(2-\sqrt{2})\frac{k}{m}$ ${\omega}_{2}^{2}=2\frac{k}{m}$ ${\omega}_{3}^{2}=(2+\sqrt{2})\frac{k}{m}$

of the respective modal deformities:

${\phi }_{1}=(\begin{array}{c}\sqrt{2}\\ 2\\ \sqrt{2}\end{array})$ ${\phi }_{2}=(\begin{array}{}1\\ 0\\ -1\end{array})$ ${\phi }_{3}=(\begin{array}{c}-\sqrt{2}\\ 2\\ -\sqrt{2}\end{array})$

Projected on the basis of the eigenmodes, the transient equation becomes ${\eta}_{i}$ with generalised coordinates:

$m(\begin{array}{ccc}8& 0& 0\\ 0& 2& 0\\ 0& 0& 8\end{array})(\begin{array}{c}\ddot{{\eta}_{1}}\\ \ddot{{\eta}_{2}}\\ \ddot{{\eta}_{3}}\end{array})+\mathrm{4k}(\begin{array}{ccc}4-\sqrt{2}& 0& 0\\ 0& 1& 0\\ 0& 0& 4+2\sqrt{2}\end{array})(\begin{array}{c}{\eta}_{1}\\ {\eta}_{2}\\ {\eta}_{3}\end{array})=(\begin{array}{c}+\sqrt{2}\\ 2\\ -\sqrt{2}\end{array})$

The system can be solved analytically.

$\left\lbrace \eta (t)\right\rbrace =\frac{1}{\mathrm{2m}}(\begin{array}{c}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4{\omega}_{1}^{2}}(1-\cos{\omega}_{1}t)\\ \frac{1}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}}(1-\cos{\omega}_{2}t)\\ \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4{\omega}_{3}^{2}}(\cos{\omega}_{3}t-1)\end{array})$

The physical-based solution is obtained using the transformation of Ritz:

$x(t)=(\begin{array}{c}{x}_{1}\\ {x}_{2}\\ {x}_{3}\end{array})=\phi \eta =(\begin{array}{ccc}\sqrt{2}& 1& -\sqrt{2}\\ 2& 0& 2\\ \sqrt{2}& -1& \sqrt{2}\end{array})(\begin{array}{c}{\eta}_{1}\\ {\eta}_{2}\\ {\eta}_{3}\end{array})$

#### 2.1.2 Depreciated structure

The depreciation is applied to the proper modes of the projection bases of the clamped substructures (reduced depreciation), in which case the following transient equation in generalised coordinates is obtained (bib[1]):

$m(\begin{array}{ccc}8& 0& 0\\ 0& 2& 0\\ 0& 0& 8\end{array})(\begin{array}{c}\ddot{{\eta}_{1}}\\ \ddot{{\eta}_{2}}\\ \ddot{{\eta}_{3}}\end{array})+4\varepsilon \sqrt{\mathrm{2km}}(\begin{array}{ccc}3-2\sqrt{2}& 0& -1\\ 0& 1& 0\\ -1& 0& 3+2\sqrt{2}\end{array})(\begin{array}{c}\dot{{\eta}_{1}}\\ \dot{{\eta}_{2}}\\ \dot{{\eta}_{3}}\end{array})+\mathrm{4k}(\begin{array}{ccc}4-\sqrt{2}& 0& 0\\ 0& 1& 0\\ 0& 0& 4+2\sqrt{2}\end{array})(\begin{array}{c}{\eta}_{1}\\ {\eta}_{2}\\ {\eta}_{3}\end{array})=(\begin{array}{c}+\sqrt{2}\\ 1\\ -\sqrt{2}\end{array})$

This system was not decoupled and was solved using Maple software. $(\varepsilon =0.01)$ was obtained:

$\left\lbrace \eta (t)\right\rbrace =\frac{1}{\mathrm{2m}}(\begin{array}{c}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4{\omega}_{1}^{2}}(1-{e}^{-\frac{t}{{\tau}_{1}}}\cos{\omega}_{1}t)\\ \frac{1}{{\omega}_{2}^{2}}(1-{e}^{-\frac{t}{{\tau}_{2}}}\cos{\omega}_{2}t)\\ \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4{\omega}_{3}^{2}}({e}^{-\frac{t}{{\tau}_{3}}}\cos{\omega}_{3}t-1)\end{array})$

with ${\tau}_{1}=1.65{10}^{3}s$ , ${\tau}_{2}=\frac{1}{\varepsilon {\omega}_{2}}=\frac{100}{\sqrt{2}}$ and ${\tau}_{3}=4.85{10}^{1}s$

Thus, a formulation is obtained close to the unamortized case, but in which exponential terms are used which characterize depreciation.

The physical-based solution is obtained using the transformation of Ritz:

$x(t)=(\begin{array}{c}{x}_{1}\\ {x}_{2}\\ {x}_{3}\end{array})=\phi \eta =(\begin{array}{ccc}\sqrt{2}& 1& -\sqrt{2}\\ 2& 0& 2\\ \sqrt{2}& -1& \sqrt{2}\end{array})(\begin{array}{c}{\eta}_{1}\\ {\eta}_{2}\\ {\eta}_{3}\end{array})$

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The system is divided into 2 substructures:

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.102/10000000000004E200000097B6FED25CD7D50F2F.png)

In the situation, both substructures are connected to the level of the 2nd mass. The dynamic interface of the 1st substructure consists of a mass m at the node $\mathit{NO3}$ of the mesh and coincides with the dynamic interface of the 2nd substructure which has no mass and is simply blocked at the node $\mathit{NO1}$ .

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.102/1000000000000274000000D172FF9519019C87EA.png)

The proper modes of the complete system are calculated using the modal calculation method by substructure with interfaces of the type 'Craig-Bampton' (blocked interfaces). The bases of each substructure are composed of a dynamic mode and a constrained mode.

The transient system response shall be calculated on the modal basis calculated by substructure.

The time step used are equal to: ${10}^{-2}s$ in 'EULER', ${10}^{-2}s$ in 'NEWMARK', ${10}^{-2}s$ in 'DEVOGE', ${10}^{-1}s$ in 'ADAPT_ORDRE2' (for the latter, it is the initial time step in the algorithm and the maximum time step is fixed to the formula $0,15s$ and then to the formula $0,2s$ to optimize the calculation time).

## 9 Summary of the results

The accuracy of the node ${x}_{2}$'s displacement, speed and acceleration at the $t=\mathrm{80 }s$ moment is excellent (relative error $\text{< 1\%}$).

This test therefore validates the linear transient response calculation operators on a modal basis calculated by dynamic substructure (with and without damping), as well as the time step adaptive order 2 integration scheme of the transient calculation operator on a modal basis.
