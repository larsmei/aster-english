# v3.04.319 SSLV319 - Flat spread of semi-elliptic crack

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the flat propagation of a crack by observing the displacement of the crack bottom during propagation.

This test involves a paved stone with a flat semi-elliptic crack, subjected to a tensile force.

The crack is represented by the X-FEM method and the simulated propagation using the PROPA_FISS command.

We will compare the results with an experimental solution.

This test contains four modellings:

Modelling A uses mesh method from the PROPA_FISS operator.

Modelling B uses GEOMETRIQUE method from the PROPA_FISS operator.

Modelling C uses GEOMETRIQUE method of PROPA_FISS operator, with cohesive elements. Operation DETECT_COHESIF is used to determine front advance.

Modelling D uses SIMPLEXE method of the PROPA_FISS operator.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A three-dimensional bar shall be considered to have the following dimensions:

- height: $\mathit{LZ}$ = 4 mm,
- side: $\mathit{LX}$ = $\mathit{LY}$ = 1 mm.

The bar has a flat, semi-elliptic crack. The crack is located in the $\mathit{Oxy}$ plane. The crack characteristics are as follows:

- half-large axis: $a$ = 119 μm
- half-small axis: $b$ = 100 μm.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

$E=200000\mathit{MPa}$

$\nu =0,3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 Cyclic loading for fatigue study

The structure is subjected to a constant amplitude fatigue load: traction ${\sigma}_{max}=220\mathit{MPa}$ and a ratio $R=0,1$ . Temperature is the ambient temperature. The loading frequency is $40\mathit{Hz}$ . A load of 4000 cycles is applied.

The traction force is applied to the upper and lower surfaces.

Blocking of rigid modes shall be carried out as follows:

- the point $A$ is blocked in the $\mathit{Oy}$ and $\mathit{Oz}$ directions,
- the point $B$ is blocked in the $\mathit{Oy}$ and $\mathit{Oz}$ directions,
- point $C$ is blocked in $\mathit{Ox}$ and $\mathit{Oz}$ directions.

#### 1.3.2 Modelling with cohesive zones: monotonous loading

For this model, the load is monotonous instead of cyclic: the structure is subjected to ${\sigma}_{max}=220\mathit{MPa}$ traction. The tensile force is applied to the upper and lower faces.

Blocking of rigid modes shall be carried out as follows:

- the point $A$ is blocked in the $\mathit{Oy}$ and $\mathit{Oz}$ directions,
- the point $B$ is blocked in the $\mathit{Oy}$ and $\mathit{Oz}$ directions,
- point $C$ is blocked in $\mathit{Ox}$ and $\mathit{Oz}$ directions.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution was obtained by experimental method 4 . In this article, a numerical simulation is also performed.

### 2.2 Baseline

The Paris-type fatigue propagation law from the tests is as follows:

$\frac{\mathit{da}}{\mathit{dN}}=C{(\Delta K)}^{m}$ with $C={10}^{-9,2}$ and $m=3,5$. The Paris Law coefficient values are given for $\Delta K$ in $\mathit{MPa}.\sqrt{m}$ and $\frac{\mathit{da}}{\mathit{dN}}$ in $m/\mathit{cycle}$.

After 4000 cycles, the deepest point of the crack bottom reached the $y=173\mu m$ rib experimentally. It presents the experimental crack bottom and calculated numerically after 4000 cycles.

## 3 Modelling A: mesh method

In this Modelling, the crack is not meshed. X-FEM method and a representation of the crack by level set are used. Level set updates are performed by PROPA_FISS operator, mesh method.

The initial mesh of the structure is relatively coarse. It was made in SMESH module of Salomé, with Blsurf and GHS3D. The size defined for the mesh overfacial Blsurf is $2\mathit{mm}$.

## 5 Modelling C: DETECT_COHESIF validation

### 5.1 Modelling characteristics

In this Modelling, the initial crack is meshed. It is extended in its plane by a cohesive cracking zone, represented by level sets and whose discontinuity is described by the XFEM method. The CZM_LIN_MIX law is introduced into the XFEM model by the DEFI_CONTACT command.

A *monotone* traction charge is applied: the cohesive area opens, thus propagating the crack. At the end of the calculation, a detection of the new propagation front is performed, which determines the upstream limit of the cohesive area. It is performed by the operator PROPA_FISS, using the GEOMETRIQUE method, specifying the DETECT_COHESIF operation (OPERATION = 'DETECT_COHESIF').

### 5.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh is quite coarse, but refined at the initial crack tip. For more regularity of the front detected, it is a mesh radiating around the tip.

Number of nodes: 18167

Number of mesh cells and type: 15120 HEXA8 and 2440PENTA6
