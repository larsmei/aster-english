# v3.04.115 SSLV115 - element prestressed concrete compression and gravity

**Summary:**

This test allows simple verification of gravity calculations for elements concrete with prestress cables, in static linear structures mechanics.

Concrete L-element is volumetric, and prestress cable elements are elements BARRE or elements CABLE_GAINE with a adhering law.

The modellings $A$ , $B$ and $C$ test the application of gravity on elements BARRE or elements CABLE_GAINE, for two directions of the gravity.

The values tested are the result of the reactions on the supports equal to the total weight of the model.

This test case also validates the CALC_PRECONT command for different types of elements $\mathrm{3D}$ (parallelepiped, pyramid and tetrahedral). To do this, we check the values of the calculated stresses.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A rectangle parallelepiped Modelling concrete, and a line included in this volume Modelling the prestress cable:

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.115/Shape1.gif)

All dimensions are in meters. L-sections of the cable is $A=0.00015{m}^{2}$ The cable is parallel to the $x$ axis. Its intersection with the $(\mathrm{Oyz})$ plane is defined by the point $(1.,0.3)$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

$E=2.1{10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$ for cable, and $E={3.10}^{10}\mathrm{Pa}$ for concrete.

${\rho}_{c}=2{10}^{4}\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ for cable and concrete ${\rho}_{b}=3\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ (non-physical values to make the weight of the cable predominant)

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

$\mathrm{DY}=0$ to point $1001$ , $\mathrm{DZ}=0$ to all nodes to $z=0$ , and $\mathrm{DX}=0$ to all nodes to $x=0$ face .

Only one load is applied: the gravity, with $g=10m/{s}^{2}$, successively in the direction $–z$ and then $–x$.

There is also an initial voltage in the $N=2.{10}^{5}N$ cable.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

> The problem is solved in an analytical way.

The result of the effort (equal to the total weight) is:

- weight of concrete: $\mathrm{Pb}=V{\rho}_{b}g$
- Cable weight: $\mathrm{Pc}=AL{\rho}_{c}g$

in the direction where the gravity is applied.

The structure is isostatic. Prestressing forces are self-balanced.

${S}_{b}$ is the concrete area in a plane perpendicular to the ${S}_{b}=(2\times 0,6){m}^{2}$ cable, ${E}_{a}$ and ${E}_{b}$ are the modules of steel and concrete, ${N}_{a}$ the tension in the cable and ${\sigma}_{b}$ and the stress in the concrete after tensioning.

The balance of the concrete and cable assembly is written: ${N}_{a}+{\sigma}_{b}{S}_{b}=0$ so ${\sigma}_{b}=-\frac{{N}_{a}}{{S}_{b}}$

Since CALC_PRECONT is used, and there is no friction or loss in the cable, the tension in the cable is equal to the initial voltage, unlike the case where RELA_CINE_BP is used, which is subject to prestress losses due to shortening of the concrete (see SSNP108, [V6.03.108](doc:v6.03.108) test).

> The deformation of the concrete is: ${\varepsilon}_{b}=\frac{{\sigma}_{b}}{{E}_{b}}$

### 2.2 Baseline

- Resulting from the efforts: $R=132N$
- Constraint in concrete: ${\sigma}_{b}=-1,66666667{10}^{5}\mathit{Pa}$
- Normal effort in steel: ${N}_{a}=2{10}^{5}\mathit{Pa}$
- Deformation in concrete: ${\varepsilon}_{b}=-5,555555555{10}^{-6}$

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

This is a analytical solution.

The solution gives the average stress in the concrete. When there are several elements (modellings $B$ and $C$) you must make an average of the values of the mesh cells.

## 6 Summary of the results

This very simple test allows you to check the gravity in the elements for the correct operation of the elements, which allows you to model the prestress, i.e. BARRE and CABLE_GAINE, which is verified by the perfect coincidence of the results with the analytical solution. It was introduced following the discovery of an anomaly on the gravity in the bars, and allows you to validate the correction.

This test was enriched with two variants to test CALC_PRECONT in the case of different elements volumetrics.
