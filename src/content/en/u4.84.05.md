# u4.84.05 Operator POST_USURE

## 1 Purpose

Compute the worn volume and the wear depth from the wear power.

The wear power is given or computed by the operator DYNA_VIBRA [U4.53.03](doc:u4.53.03). A wear law, a contact geometry and a list of time instants must be supplied.

Produces a data structure of type table_sdaster.

## 3 Operands

Attention is drawn here to a few delicate points in the use of POST_USURE.

1. The result of POST_USURE does not depend on the final mechanical state of the analysis but on the whole history of the shocks. It is therefore very important to take into account **all**  the analysis time instants, that is, not to request selective archiving in DYNA_VIBRA.
2. The result of POST_USURE is very sensitive to the analysis parameters, in particular to the richness of the modal basis and to the time step. It is therefore strongly recommended to test different modal bases (increasingly rich) and different time steps (increasingly small). For example, for the time step, one may test different values spaced by a factor of 10, then of 2, in order to determine a range of time steps over which the result is stable.
3. In the case of a vibratory analysis, one may obtain a result which is not representative of the real wear if the experiment is not long enough. Likewise, in the case of randomly generated excitations, it is recommended to carry out several draws before drawing sizing conclusions.

### 3.1 Case PUIS_USURE or RESU_GENE

#### 3.1.1 Operand PUIS_USURE

```text
♦    PUIS_USURE = pu
```

The wear power is:

- taken from the result of a transient analysis by modal recombination, produced by the operator DYNA_VIBRA [U4.53.03](doc:u4.53.03) (following operands),
- or given by the user, who then uses the operand PUIS_USURE.

#### 3.1.2 Operand RESU_GENE

```text
♦     RESU_GENE = tg
```

Result of a transient analysis by modal recombination, produced by the operator DYNA_VIBRA [U4.53.03](doc:u4.53.03).

#### 3.1.3 Operand GROUP _ NO

```text
♦    GROUP _ NO = grnoeu,
```

Definition of the shock node to be post-processed.

#### 3.1.4 Operand INST_INIT

```text
◊    INST_INIT = t0
```

Start time instant of the averaging of the signals (cf. [§4]).

(t0 = 0. default value).

#### 3.1.5 Operand INST_FIN

```text
◊    INST_FIN = t1
```

End time instant of the averaging of the signals.

#### 3.1.6 Operand NB_BLOC

```text
◊    NB_BLOC = nb
```

Number of time blocks into which the interval [t0, t1] is split for the averaging of the signals (1 by default).

### 3.2 Case TUBE_NEUF

For the processing of the wear of the control-rod clusters, the user may take into account the replacement of a tube by a new tube by supplying the keyword TUBE_NEUF = 'OUI'

#### 3.2.1 Operand TUBE_NEUF

```text
◊    TUBE_NEUF = 'OUI'
```

If the user supplies this keyword, the operator modifies the wear values of the tube (V_USUR_TUBE, P_USUR_TUBE, V_USUR_TUBE_SECT, P_USUR_TUBE_SECT, V_USUR_TUBE_CUMU = 0) in the table produced by POST_USURE for the time instants later than the time instant at which the new tube is loaded.

#### 3.2.2 Operand TABL_USURE

```text
♦    TABL_USURE = tresu
```

If the user supplies the keyword TUBE_NEUF = 'OUI', the name of the table to be updated must be entered. This table is the same as the one which is the output of the operator. The time instant of the replacement of the tube by a new tube is entered in the keyword INST.

### 3.6 Operand CONTACT

```text
♦    CONTACT = géom
```

Definition of the contact geometry.

Depending on the type of contact, different geometrical relations between the worn volumes and the worn depths.

#### 3.6.1 Operand CONTACT = 'GRAPPE_ALESAGE'

The cluster is centred in a bore. The wear scar has a lune-shaped cross-section. The worn volume is reduced to a worn area in a cross-section.

#### 3.6.2 Operand CONTACT = 'GRAPPE_1_ENCO'

The cluster is centred with respect to the obstacle.

The guide card is formed of one notch. The worn volume is reduced to a worn area in a cross-section.

The coefficients are based both on the experimental results and on those from operating experience. They apply only to the control-rod clusters.

#### 3.6.3 Operand CONTACT = 'GRAPPE_2_ENCO'

The cluster is centred with respect to the obstacle.

The guide card is formed of two diametrically opposite notches. The worn volume is reduced to a worn area in a cross-section.

The coefficients are based both on the experimental results and on those from operating experience. They apply only to the control-rod clusters.

#### 3.6.4 Operand CONTACT = 'TUBE_BAV'

**Case 1:**

The tube is vertical, the bar impacts perpendicularly to the tube, and it is assumed that the bar does not wear.

**Case 2:**

The bar is inclined (operand ANGL_INCLI) with respect to the tube, the bar impacts perpendicularly to the tube, and it is assumed that the bar does not wear.

**Case 3:**

The tube is vertical, the bar impacts perpendicularly to the tube, and the wear of the bar is taken into account.

**Case 4:**

The bar is inclined (operand ANGL_INCLI) with respect to the tube, the bar impacts perpendicularly to the tube, and the wear of the bar is taken into account.

#### 3.6.5 Operand CONTACT = 'TUBE_ALESAGE'

**Case 1:**

The tube is perfectly centred in a bore animated by a pure orbital motion and wears uniformly over the whole periphery in contact with the obstacle.

**Case 2:**

The tube is centred in a bore animated by an elliptical impact-sliding motion which leads to the formation of cylindrical wear scars, diametrically opposite on the tube and having a lune-shaped cross-section.

**Case 3:**

The tube, animated by an impact-sliding motion, this time has an inclination with respect to the support (operand ANGL_INCLI). Two symmetrical V-shaped wear scars are obtained on the tube.

#### 3.6.6 Operand CONTACT = 'TUBE_3_ENCO'

**Case 1:**

The initial contact takes place against an edge of one of the isthmuses of a trefoil bore. The tube is assumed to be perfectly centred with respect to its obstacle. The wear scar does not extend over the whole isthmus. The wear of the obstacle is not taken into account.

**Case 2:**

Same assumptions as for case 1 except for the position of the tube with respect to the obstacle. It is assumed this time that the tube has an angle of inclination (operand ANGL_INCLI).

#### 3.6.7 Operand CONTACT = 'TUBE_4_ENCO'

**Case 1:**

The initial contact takes place against an edge of one of the isthmuses of the quatrefoil bore. The tube is assumed to be perfectly centred with respect to its obstacle. The wear scar does not extend over the whole isthmus. The wear of the obstacle is not taken into account.

**Case 2:**

Same assumptions as for case 1 except for the position of the tube with respect to the obstacle. It is assumed this time that the tube has an angle of inclination (operand ANGL_INCLI).

#### 3.6.8 Operand CONTACT = 'TUBE_TUBE'

Following the rupture of a plugged tube, there may be contact between this tube and one of its neighbours. The wear of the two tubes by accommodation of the surfaces in contact leads to the creation of two plane surfaces.

### 3.8 Definition of the analysis time instants

#### 3.8.1 Case PUIS_USURE or RESU_GENE


#### 3.8.2 Case TUBE_NEUF

If TUBE_NEUF = 'OUI', the loading time instant may be entered.

##### 3.8.2.1 Operand INST


The wear values of the tube (V_USUR_TUBE, P_USUR_TUBE, V_USUR_TUBE_SECT, P_USUR_TUBE_SECT, V_USUR_TUBE_CUMU) are set to 0 for the time instants later than the loading time instant of the new tube. By default, the wear values of the last time instant of the table are set to 0 if TUBE_NEUF = 'OUI'.

### 3.11 Table produced

The command POST_USURE generates a concept of type table, whose content is:

| INST: | time instants at which the user wishes to know the worn volume and the wear depth, |
| --- | --- |
| V_usur_tube: | worn volume at the tube (for each time instant specified by the user), |
| V_usur_obst: | worn volume at the obstacle (for each time instant specified by the user), |
| P_usur_tube: | wear depth at the tube (for each time instant specified by the user). |

The command IMPR_TABLE [U4.91.03](doc:u4.91.03) makes it possible to print the results.

## 4 Checking — Execution

### 4.1 Operand MATER_USURE

One checks that the pair of materials supplied by the user is present in the database.

### 4.2 Operands RESU_GENE / INST_INIT / INST_FIN / NB_BLOC

The value of INST_FIN is compared with the final time instant ${t}_{f}$ of the tran_gene result. The value of INST_FIN retained is $\min({t}_{f},{t}_{1})$ .

If the value of INST_INIT ${t}_{0}$ is greater than the value of INST_FIN, the analysis stops in error.
