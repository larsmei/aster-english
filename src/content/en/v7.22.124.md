# v7.22.124 HSNV124 - Voltage and temperature variable elements

**Summary:**

This test, proposed by the IPSI for the Phi2As day of 30 March 2000 on nonlinear behaviors, validates the correct consideration of the variation of coefficients with temperature for four models of behaviour (nonlinear isotropic hardening, linear kinematic hardening, and two types of nonlinear kinematic hardening) in $\mathrm{3D}$.

Four modellingss can validate each of these behaviours.

The reference solution is analytical for the first three behaviours, and the results will be compared with those of the other participants on the Phi2as day for the fourth.

## 7 Summary of the results

This test shows the effects of varying the coefficients of elastoplastic behaviour with temperature.

The results are identical to the analytical solution for linear kinematic hardening (where the solution does not depend on temporal discretization). For other behaviors, the accuracy is less good (difference less than $\text{1.5\%}$) because the solution depends heavily on the chosen temporal discretization.

This test thus validates the integration of the VMIS_ISOT_TRAC, VMIS_CINE_LINE, VMIS_ECMI_LINE, VMIS_CIN1_CHAB, and VMIS_CIN2_CHAB behaviors in relation to the temperature coefficient variation.
