# v6.03.147 SSNP147 – Modelling of crack initiation with ENDO_HETEROGENE

**Summary:**

This test represents the initiation of a crack in a plate made of heterogeneous material. This plate undergoes loading as a ramp of forced displacement. The booting is modelled by ENDO_HETEROGENE law using regulated constraints. The purpose of this test is to validate D_PLAN_GRAD_SIGM Modelling and ENDO_HETEROGENE law. It is a two-dimensional test case of non-regression made on a rectangular and triangular mesh.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

We represent a square on the $l=\mathrm{1m}$ side.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.147/10000200000000BC000000EDED7B21EA295799F1.png)

### 1.2 Illustration 1: Geometry of test case

### 1.3 Material properties

**Material:**

Elasticity parameters:

Young's modulus ${E}_{1}={20.10}^{9}\mathrm{MPa}$ , Poisson's ratio ${\nu}_{1}=0,25$

ENDO_HETEROGENE Law Parameters:

Elastic limit ${\sigma}_{y}=5\mathit{MPa}$

Weibull $m=6$ Module

Tenacity ${K}_{c}=1{\mathrm{MPa.m}}^{1/2}$

Sample thickness $\mathit{ep}=\mathrm{1m}$

$\mathrm{GR}=121$ seed

Non-local model parameter:

Characteristic length ${l}_{c}=0,2m$

### 1.4 Boundary conditions and loading

The lower edge is blocked in the vertical direction, the lateral edges are subjected to a displacement field which varies linearly in the height. Either:

Downstairs:

${u}_{y}(x,y=0)=0$

Right

${u}_{y}(x=l,y)={\mathrm{c.u}}_{d}(1-y/l)$

with ${u}_{d}=0,0001$

Right

${u}_{y}(x=0,y)=-{u}_{y}(x=l,y)$

$c$ is a loading ramp that varies between 0 and 1 over the simulation time (1s).

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.147/1000020000000147000000C397BCB8B820E6BC3D.png)

Illustration 2: boundary conditions diagrams

### 1.5 reference solution

There is no reference solution here and the test is non-regression type. It uses the ENDO_HETEROGENE coupling law, which is based on regulated constraints.

The result is therefore purely qualitative. Here, we must observe the initiation of a crack following lateral loadings.

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics

The entire domain is meshed in elements quadrangular to 8 nodes. The mesh has 225 rectangles and 30 segments.

1 time of $1s$ is modelled.

### 2.2 Results

The horizontal displacements DX and the damage criterion (internal variable V1) are plotted on the figures and respectively at the end of $\mathrm{1s}$.

A crack is seen that starts at the domain $2/3$ (when V1 is equal to 1, the material is broken).

## 4 Summary of the results

This test represents the modelling using the ENDO_HETEROGENE law of starting a crack in a plate made of heterogeneous material, which is loaded as a ramp of forced displacements. The location of the initiation is defined randomly (here determined by the choice of seed). The results obtained correspond well to the expected results.

Note: Due to the random generation of boot thresholds when using the ENDO_HETEROGENE law, the results of non-regression will be different depending on the number of processors used for execution.
