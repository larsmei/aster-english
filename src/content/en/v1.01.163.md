# v1.01.163 ZZZZ163 – Validation of POST_BEREMIN Macro-Command

**Summary**

This test validates the POST_BEREMIN [U4.81.08](doc:u4.81.08) command to calculate the breakability probability and stress of Weibull using a Beremin [R7.02.04](doc:r7.02.04) model. The functions of the control are validated on a simple uniaxial traction problem, it is a four-elements quadratic structure modelled in 2D.

The different modellings of this test have the following characteristics:

- Modelling A: D_PLAN Modelling, constitutive law elasto-plastic VMIS_ISOT_TRAC
- Modelling B: D_PLAN Modelling, constitutive law MFront
- Modelling C: D_PLAN Modelling, constitutive law elastic
- Modelling D: D_PLAN_GRAD_INCO Modelling, constitutive law VMIS_ISOT_NL Modelling
- Modelling E: D_PLAN_GRAD_VARI Modelling, constitutive law VMIS_ISOT_NL
- Modelling F: D_PLAN Modelling, with temperature dependence of threshold stress
- Modelling G: D_PLAN Modelling, with position-dependent threshold stress
- H Modelling: D_PLAN Modelling, Weibull stress calculation over several groups of mesh cells
- Modelling I: D_PLAN_INCO_UPG Modelling, constitutive law VMIS_ISOT_NL Modelling

## 13 Modelling F

We want to test the dependence of the threshold stress on temperature.
To do this, one affects a temperature field on the mesh and a temperature dependence on the SIGM_SEUIL threshold function such that SIGM_SEUIL is zero on both left and very high mesh cells (10000 MPa) on the two left meshes.
Therefore, the Weibull constraint will only be calculated where the maximum main stress will exceed the threshold, i.e., on half of the mesh (two mesh cells on the right).
The Weibull $\sigma_{W,F}$ constraint obtained will therefore be equal to:

$\sigma_{W,A}$ being the value obtained at Modelling A.
