# v3.03.139 SSLS139 – Flexion of a plate with simplified representation of the frames

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the representation of reinforcing sheets by a model of a grid or membrane homogenized in a reinforced concrete structure. Thus, a classic problem of plate bending is considered, and the solution obtained is compared to a three-dimensional reference solution.

It is also used to check the correct functioning of the membrane in large deformation, comparing its results with the result in small deformations.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A reinforced concrete plate, with rebars on the lower and upper sides, is considered. This plate is clamped at one end, and is subjected at one point to the other end.

## 5 Modelling C

### 5.1 Modelling characteristics

The membrane functions are compared in large and small deformations. The membrane in large deformations takes into account only isotropic behaviors, so the rebar arrays are modelled by isotropic membranes having the characteristics of the steel.

The constitutive law of Saint Venant Kirchhoff is used for the membrane in large deformations.

### 5.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 12,712 nodes, 8,018 quadratic tetrahedrons (TETRA10) and 500 quadratic triangles (TRIA6) to represent the rebar arrays.

### 5.3 Tested quantities and results

### 5.4 Remarks

The Modelling of the membrane in large deformations with the neo-Hookean constitutive law gives similar results.

The results are found to be in good agreement with reference solution, which validates the good behavior in small disturbances of the membrane element into large transformations.

## 6 Summary of the results

This test validates the principle of rebar table representation by a homogenized grid or membrane model. This validation is based on a comparison with a three-dimensional reference model, supplemented by several non-regression tests.

The homogenized membrane model gives excellent results in elastic regime, but requires prior identification of the coefficients of rigidity of the membrane via elementary homogenization calculations. This makes its use delicate in an industrial context.

The grid model gives acceptable results, although a little less precise. It is much simpler to use.

For the membrane in large deformations, the results are consistent, the behaviour of the element is validated.
