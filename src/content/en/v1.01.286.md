# v1.01.286 ZZZZ286 – Validation of the shift imposed on a edge

**Summary**

This test is intended to validate the assignment of a displacement-type load to a edge of a structure. It allows to qualify the keyword ARETE_IMPO operator as AFFE_CHAR_MECA.

The mesh nodes displacements are tested according to whether the structure has been subjected to a load produced by ARETE_IMPO or by an equivalent load.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 Loading on a vertical edge (supporting one of the axes of the marker)

The validation of keyword ARETE_IMPO shall be based on the equivalence of the following conditions:

- Conditions 1.3.1.1:

$\mathrm{DBC}$ face imposed: $\mathrm{DNOR}=10$

Blocking at $D,B,C$ points: $\mathrm{DX}=0,\mathrm{DY}=0,\mathrm{DZ}=0$

Move imposed on nodes on $\mathrm{DA}$ edge: $\mathrm{DZ}=0$

- Conditions 1.3.1.2:

$\mathrm{DBC}$ face imposed: $\mathrm{DNOR}=10$

Blocking at $D,B,C$ points: $\mathrm{DX}=0,\mathrm{DY}=0,\mathrm{DZ}=0$

$\mathrm{DA}$ edge imposed: $\mathrm{DTAN}=0$ except point $D$ .

The equivalence between these conditions will be validated by testing the node $A$ displacements.

#### 1.3.2 Loading on slashes

The validation of keyword ARETE_IMPO shall be based on the equivalence of the following conditions:

- Conditions 1.3.2.1.:

Nodes Blocking on the $\mathrm{ABC}$ Side: $\mathrm{DX}=0,\mathrm{DY}=0,\mathrm{DZ}=0$

Movement to nodes on the $\mathrm{DA}$ edge: $\mathrm{DZ}=-1$ (except $D$ and $A$ points)

Restraint oblique to nodes on the $\mathrm{DB}$ edge (except $D$ and $B$ points): $\mathrm{DX}=1$ , $\mathrm{ANGL}\text{\_}\mathrm{NAUT}=(0,45,0)$

Restraint oblique to nodes on the $\mathrm{DC}$ edge (except $D$ and $C$ points): $\mathrm{DX}=1$ , $\mathrm{ANGL}\text{\_}\mathrm{NAUT}=(45,45,0)$

- Conditions 1.3.2.2:

Nodes Blocking on the $\mathrm{ABC}$ Side: $\mathrm{DX}=0,\mathrm{DY}=0,\mathrm{DZ}=0$

$\mathrm{DA}$ edge imposed: $\mathrm{DTAN}=1$ except $D$ and $A$ points .

$\mathrm{DB}$ edge imposed: $\mathrm{DTAN}=1$ except $D$ and $B$ points .

$\mathrm{DC}$ edge imposed: $\mathrm{DTAN}=1$ except $D$ and $C$ points .

The equivalence between these conditions will be validated by testing the node $D$ displacements.

## 2 reference solution

The displacements obtained from loading conditions 1.3.1.1 (or 1.3.2.1) are the reference to the tests performed on the movements obtained from loading conditions 1.3.1.2 (or 1.3.2.2).
