# v1.03.133 MFRON07 – Code_Aster-MF interface test for elements interface and joint

**Summary:**

This test validates the implementation of a linear elastic CZM law resolved using MFront on the traction/compression of an oblique interface.

The modellings A and B consider two modellings 3D_INTERFACE and 3D_INTERFACE respectively.

The modellings C and D consider two modellings PLAN_INTERFACE and PLAN_JOINT respectively.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

#### 1.1.1 modellings A et B

It is a cube, $1000$ mm side and crossed by an interface
The cube is positioned on $0\leq x,y,z \leq 1000$ mm.

![Figure](/figures/v1.03.133/Cube.svg)

*Geometry of the cube studied. The interface is shown in yellow.*

#### 1.1.2 modellings C et D

It is a square, $1000$ mm side and crossed by an interface
The square is positioned on $0\leq x,y \leq 1000$ mm.

![Figure](/figures/v1.03.133/Carre.svg)

*Geometry of the square studied. The interface is shown in yellow.*

### 1.2 boundary conditions

#### 1.2.1 modellings A et B

- On the $x=0$ side, the displacement is blocked in $DX$.
- On the $y=0$ side, the displacement is blocked in $DY$.
- On the $z=0$ side, the displacement is blocked in $DZ$.
- On the $x=1000$ mm face, the displacement is driven in $DX$ from $0$ to $10$ mm and then to $-20$ mm.

#### 1.2.2 modellings C et D

- On the $x=0$ edge, the displacement is blocked in $DX$.
- On the $y=0$ edge, the displacement is blocked in $DY$.
- On the $x=1000$ mm edge, the displacement is driven in $DX$ from $0$ to $10$ mm and then to $-20$ mm.

### 1.3 Material properties

The characteristics used in the CZM linear elastic law are:

- Young's modulus $E = 30$ GPA
- Poisson's ratio $\nu = 0.2$
- Stiffness interface in normal direction $k_n = 20$ MPa/mm
- Stiffness of the interface in the tangential direction $k_t = 20$ MPa/mm

Furthermore, the increase coefficient of the Lagrangian used with the interface elements, without impact here because the solution is homogeneous, is fixed to $r=600$ MPa/mm.
