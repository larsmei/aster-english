# u4.66.11 Macro-command MACRO_MATR_AJOU

## 1 Purpose

Compute, in a more condensed way than with CALC_MATR_AJOU, added mass, damping or stiffness matrices [R4.07.03](doc:r4.07.03). The matrices are induced by a perfect, incompressible fluid, at rest or in potential flow, on a structure in 2D, axisymmetric 2D or 3D.

The terms of these matrices are computed on the modal basis of the structure in vacuum.

One can also compute, by launching CALC_FORC_AJOU [U4.66.03](doc:u4.66.03), the vectors of the added forces induced by a seismic entrainment motion, also computed on the modal basis of the structure in vacuum.

The advantage of this macro-command is essentially to hide the calls to the linear thermal operators which compute the hydrodynamic pressures caused by the vibration of the structure in the fluid. The user now only has to supply the characteristics of the fluid domain and the eigenmodes in air of the structure, the operator taking care of computing the added matrices.

The possibilities of the command retain those of the operator CALC_MATR_AJOU [U4.66.01](doc:u4.66.01).

Examples of use of the macro-command MACRO_MATR_AJOU are presented in the test case:

- FDLV101 [V8.01.101](doc:v8.01.101),
- FDLV102 [V8.01.102](doc:v8.01.102),
- FDLV106 [V8.01.106](doc:v8.01.106),
- FDLV108 [V8.01.108](doc:v8.01.108),
- FDLV109 [V8.01.109](doc:v8.01.109),
- FDLV112 [V8.01.112](doc:v8.01.112).

## 3 Operands

### 3.1 Operand MAILLAGE

Name of the mesh containing the fluid domain and the fluid/structure interface. This mesh may also include the structure.

### 3.2 Operand GROUP_MA_FLUIDE

```text
♦    GROUP_MA_FLUIDE = fluide
```
Mesh-cell groups (plane mesh cells in 2D and axisymmetric 2D, volume mesh cells in 3D) representing the fluid domain acting on the structure studied.

### 3.3 Operand GROUP_MA_INTERF

```text
♦    GROUP_MA_INTERF = interf
```
Mesh-cell groups representing the interface between the fluid and the structure (plane or line mesh cells). It must be emphasised that this mesh-cell group may be one of those used to model the surface of the structure, in particular if the latter is modelled by shell elements. It is not always necessary to create a specific interface mesh-cell group with mesh cells in their own right.

### 3.4 Operand MODELISATION

Type of modelling to assign to the fluid domain and to the fluid/structure interface. At present, the modellings 'PLAN' (2D fluid domain), '3D' (volumetric fluid domain) and 'AXIS' (axisymmetric fluid domain) are the only ones allowed.

### 3.5 Keyword FLUIDE

Factor keyword in which one assigns the characteristics of the fluid material. If the density of the fluid varies over the fluid domain, one must specify these different densities by several occurrences of the factor keyword FLUIDE.

#### 3.5.1 Operand RHO

```text
♦    RHO = rho
```
Value of the density of the fluid to be assigned on the topological entities defined below.

#### 3.5.2 Operands TOUT / GROUP_MA

Mesh-cell group or the whole of the fluid domain on which one assigns the density RHO.

### 3.6 Keyword DDL_IMPO

Factor keyword by which one specifies the boundary conditions of the fluid (of Dirichlet type).

#### 3.6.1 Operand GROUP_NO

Node groups on which one imposes the boundary conditions on the fluid domain.

#### 3.6.2 Operand PRES_FLUIDE

Keyword under which one specifies the value which one imposes on the hydrodynamic pressure (that is to say the pressure perturbation created by the vibration of the structure) on the topological entity determined above. This keyword must appear at least once because it makes it possible to compute the unsteady pressure fields which generate the added mechanical quantities (grandeurs).

#### 3.6.3 Operand PRES_SORTIE

Outlet pressure to be imposed on a steady flow. Keyword to be used (in an occurrence of the factor keyword DDL_IMPO different from the one in which PRES_FLUIDE has been used) in order to specify the boundary conditions on the steady quantities to be computed (such as the potential of the steady fluid velocities characterising a steady flow disturbed by the vibrations). This keyword is to be used only if one wants to compute the added damping and stiffness matrices, which require the analysis of a steady flow assumed to be potential.

### 3.7 Keyword ECOULEMENT

This factor keyword makes it possible to determine the boundary conditions of Neumann type when one wants to compute a steady flow assumed to be potential, in order to be able to compute the added damping and stiffness.

#### 3.7.1 Operands GROUP_MA_1 / GROUP_MA_2

Names of the mesh-cell groups, respectively of inlet and of outlet of the fluid domain, on which one imposes conditions of normal velocity of inlet or of outlet of the fluid.

#### 3.7.2 Operands VNOR_1 / VNOR_2

Real values of the normal velocities of the fluid, respectively at the inlet and at the outlet of the fluid domain.

#### 3.7.3 Operand POTENTIEL

Name given by the user to the potential of the steady fluid velocities if the user possibly wants to post-process it.

### 3.8 Operands MODE_MECA / DEPL_IMPO / MODELE_GENE

```text
♦    /    MODE_MECA = modes
```
Dynamic modes computed on the structural model. If one has several disconnected structures immersed in the same fluid, for which one wants to determine the added matrices including the coupling terms through the fluid, the structural model which one defines gathers all of the immersed structures. The modes used by the operator are the modes computed for the global structure.

```text
/    DEPL_IMPO = chamno
```
Nodal displacement fields assigned on node groups defining the fluid/structure interface. By assigning such a displacement field on the interface node group(s) by means of the operator CREA_CHAMP, one can for example easily simulate rigid-body modes of the structures. This operand is to be used if one wants to estimate the terms of the added and coupling matrices for a **plane geometry without first carrying out a modal analysis** of the structure in vacuum, and if one wants to assign these terms in discrete elements (modelling DIS_T). The operand INFO must necessarily be active in order to display the computed terms.

**Caution:**

*The use of this operand* **excludes** *that of the operand* *NUME_DDL_GENE. One therefore cannot carry out a modal, transient or harmonic analysis by using the operand* *CHAM_NO.*

*Indeed, in order to carry out an analysis of modes in water, one must have a generalised mass matrix and a generalised stiffness matrix of the structure. This supposes that one has carried out a modal analysis on the structure in vacuum (which is precisely what one wants to avoid).*

*Ultimately, this operand is used only to* **evaluate the added mass matrix**, *and the added stiffness and damping matrices, and to use them to couple beam-type structures by discrete mass elements, for example.*

```text
/    MODELE_GENE = modgen
```
Generalised model built by the operator DEFI_MODELE_GENE [U4.65.02](doc:u4.65.02). This keyword is to be used when one carries out an analysis by dynamic substructuring, and when one wants to compute the added mass matrix coupling all of the substructures. In this case, the substructures may be in different mesh files, these files themselves possibly being distinct from the fluid mesh file. The substructures which exhibit a repetitiveness within the fluid need be meshed only once, but one will take care, at the level of the fluid mesh, to mesh all the fluid/structure interfaces. Moreover, one must ensure that the fluid interface nodes coincide as well as possible with the structure interface nodes, in order to be able to copy, on the basis of a geometric proximity criterion, the values of the structural displacement field onto the fluid interface nodes (see operand DIST_REFE).

### 3.9 Operands MATR_MASS_AJOU / MATR_AMOR_AJOU / MATR_RIGI_AJOU

These keywords specify the user name of the added mass, stiffness or damping matrices which one wants to compute. At least one of these keywords is required in order to carry out the analysis. The 3 keywords can be used simultaneously, but in the case of the use of MATR_AMOR_AJOU or MATR_RIGI_AJOU, one must supply the keywords ECOULEMENT and DDL_IMPO with PRES_SORTIE.

### 3.10 Operand DIST_REFE

Reference distance to be supplied when one carries out an analysis of **added mass** on a generalised model. This distance is an absolute geometric proximity criterion intended for copying values of structural displacements into a fluid domain, in order to solve there the Laplace equation of the unsteady pressure field. By default, it is equal to 10–2.

### 3.11 Operand NOEUD_DOUBLE

```text
◊    NOEUD_DOUBLE = 'OUI'
```
This operand is to be used when one carries out an analysis of **added mass** from a generalised model which includes a substructure meshed by a wire or surface mesh (*i.e.* without thickness, such as a beam or a shell) and surrounded by two fluids. In this case one must, at the mesh level, duplicate the nodes of the fluid interfaces with respect to those of the structure, in order to be able to compute the jump in hydrodynamic pressure on either side of the structure (*cf.* figure below).

![Figure](/figures/u4.66.11/1000150400002461000010050A66C58130DE2C89.svg)

### 3.12 Operand AVEC_MODE_STAT

```text
◊    AVEC_MODE_STAT = 'NON'
```
This operand makes it possible to switch off the analysis of the added-mass terms on the static modes contained in the modal basis of the substructures in the case of an analysis with a generalised model (*cf.* [§3.8]).

### 3.13 Operand NUME_DDL_GENE

```text
◊    NUME_DDL_GENE = numgen
```
Generalised numbering based on the mechanical modes of the global structure. The presence of this operand makes it possible to compute an added mass matrix of type matr_asse_gene_R. It must necessarily be present if one subsequently wants to carry out a modal, harmonic or transient analysis.

### 3.14 Description of the seismic entrainment motion

#### 3.14.1 Operand MONO_APPUI

```text
♦    /    MONO_APPUI = 'OUI'
```
The structure is excited uniformly at all the supports (rigid-body entrainment motion).

#### 3.14.2 Multi-support excitation: operand MODE_STAT

In this case, the accelerations undergone by all the anchor points of the structure studied are not necessarily identical and in phase.

```text
/    ♦    MODE_STAT = mode
```
Static modes of the structure: concept of type mode_meca produced by the operator MODE_STATIQUE [U4.52.14](doc:u4.52.14) with the option DDL_IMPO. They correspond to the 3 or 6 * nb_supports static modes, where nb_supports is the number of different accelerograms undergone by the structure.

**Note:**

*If the structure is subjected only to translations, there are then 3 nb_supports static modes.*

### 3.15 Added forces due to the seismic entrainment motion: keyword FORC_AJOU

#### 3.15.1 Operand GROUP_NO

```text
♦    GROUP_NO    =    g_noeu
```
Node groups (g_noeu) of the structure subjected to the seismic excitation: these nodes carry the degrees of freedom of the supports of the structure to which the imposed motions are applied.

#### 3.15.2 Operand DIRECTION

```text
♦    DIRECTION    =    ( d1 , d2 , d3 , r1 , r2 , r3 )
```
Components of a vector giving the direction of the entrainment earthquake in the global frame. It is a list of three reals if the imposed accelerograms are only translations. If one also imposes rotational accelerations, a list of six reals is expected (valid for modellings with discrete elements).

#### 3.15.3 Operand VECTEUR

```text
♦    VECTEUR    = vecteur         [vect_asse_gene]

Nom du vecteur force ajoutée créé par le lancement de l'opérateur CALC_FORC_AJOU [:ref:`U4.66.03 <U4.66.03>`]. Il y autant de vecteurs créés que d'occurrences du mot clé FORC_AJOU.
```
### 3.16 Keyword SOLVEUR

Factor keyword specifying the technique for solving the linear system appearing here in the analysis of the unsteady pressure fields. See [U4.50.01](doc:u4.50.01).

### 3.17 Operand INFO

```text
◊    INFO =
```
Indicates the printout level of the results of the operator,

| 1: | no printout, |
| --- | --- |
| 2: | printout of the upper triangular part of the added mass, added damping or added stiffness matrices. |

Presentation in columns.

## 4 Execution phase

One checks that the storage of the added matrices is done on a **full** profile.

One checks that the added self-mass coefficients (diagonal terms of the matrix) are indeed all positive. Otherwise, an alarm message is issued so that the user checks the orientation of the normals.

In the case of an added-mass analysis on a generalised model, the user is informed about the copies of structural displacement fields into the fluid (number of values copied) and is told how to change the reference distance (DIST_REFE).

## 5 Example

### 5.1 Example 1: Analysis of the added mass matrix of a structure made up of two concentric cylinders separated by an incompressible fluid

The cylinders are each supported by springs. The added mass matrix is computed for the first two modes of the structure, namely the rigid-body modes of each of the cylinders in the Ox direction (*cf.* diagram). Only the stages of the command file will be presented here, because this example is inspired by the test case FDLV101 [V8.01.101](doc:v8.01.101). For the detail of the syntax of the other operators, the user may therefore refer to the command file of this test case.

![Figure](/figures/u4.66.11/10000F2600001DC4000017FA64ACE706587A5FD4.svg)

1. Assignment of the global structural model (comprising the two cylinders and their support springs).

1. Computation of the eigenmodes of the structure in vacuum
2. Generalised numbering based on the mechanical modes computed
3. Analysis of the added mass matrix of type matr_asse_gene_r (operand NUME_DDL_GENE present). As a result, one can then compute the eigenmodes of the "wet" structure

```text
MACRO_MATR_AJOU( MAILLAGE = maya ,

GROUP_MA_FLUIDE = 'grfluide' ,

GROUP_MA_INTERF = grinter,

MODELISATION = ’PLAN’,

FLUIDE = _F( RHO = rho ,

GROUP_MA = 'grtotal' ) ,

DDL_IMPO = _F( GROUP_NO = 'noflui' ,

PRES_FLUIDE = 0. ) ,

MATR_MASS_AJOU = massaj ,

MODE_MECA = modes ,

NUME_DDL_GENE= numgen ,

INFO =1 )
```
1. Analysis of the generalised mass and stiffness matrices on the generalised numbering based on the computed mechanical modes numgen. These matrices are of type [matr_asse_gene_r]
2. Analysis of the total mass matrix of the structure (generalised mass matrix plus added mass matrix)
3. Analysis of the modes of the immersed structure.
4. Recovery of the "wet" modes on the physical basis.

### 5.2 Example 2: cylinder subjected to an annular flow

This case (very close to the test case FDLV106 [V8.01.106](doc:v8.01.106)) implements the analysis of added mass, added damping, and added stiffness on a cylindrical structure subjected to an annular flow which one assumes to be potential. One computes, first, the mass and the damping added by the flow on the structure for different upstream velocities (4 m/s, 4.24 m/s and 6 m/s), this on a 3D model for the fluid and a shell model for the structure. The structure has a rotational displacement about a pivot located at the downstream end of the cylinder with respect to the flow.

_F( GROUP_NO = 'NOSORT', PRES_SORTIE = 0.)),

ECOULEMENT = _F( GROUP_MA_1 = 'ENTREE',

GROUP_MA_2 = 'SORTIE',

VNOR_2 = 4. ),
