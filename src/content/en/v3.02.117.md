# v3.02.117 SSLP117 – Square flexion plate – variable temperature gradient

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the calculation of displacements, moments and stresses in a square plate, simply supported, in bending subject to a variable temperature gradient.

modellings :

- $A$ Modelling: DKT with mesh cells TRIA3
- $B$ Modelling: DKT with mesh cells QUAD4

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.117/geometrie.jpg)

Thickness = $\mathrm{0.25cm}$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=3.\times {10}^{6}N/{\mathit{cm}}^{2}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\alpha =6.5\times {10}^{-6}/°C$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

boundary conditions :

- On the $\mathit{AB}$ edge: $\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DRX}=0$
- On the $\mathit{AD}$ edge: $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DRY}=0$
- On the $\mathit{BC}$ and $\mathit{CD}$ edges: $\mathit{DZ}=0$

Loading

- The load applied is a temperature load that varies as follows:

Upper skin: $T(x,y)=100-5∣x∣-5∣y∣+\frac{1}{2}∣xy∣$

Lower skin: $T(x,y)=-100+5∣x∣+5∣y∣-\frac{1}{2}∣xy∣$

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.117/Shape16.gif)

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution is a digital bib1 solution.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

- $\mathit{DZ}$ moving to the centre of the plate

Point $A:\mathit{DZ}=0.14712\mathit{cm}$

- ${M}_{\mathit{XX}}$ moment in the centre of the plate

Point $A:{M}_{\mathit{XX}}=103.80N$

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Digital solution

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

M.H. SADR-LAHIDJANI: "Modification and analysis of elastic thin plates and hulls subject to temperature fields", PhD UTC Thesis, 1984.
