# u3.14.01 Mechanical modellings 3D and 3D_SI

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the mechanical modellings 3D and 3D_SI:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the associated support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear capabilities,
- the test cases implementing the modelling.

The modellings 3D and 3D_SI (phenomenon MECANIQUE) correspond to finite elements whose support mesh cells are volume mesh cells.

The suffix _SI means Under-integrated: the integration of the terms related to the constitutive laws is carried out in a reduced manner (Gauss-point scheme of lower order than the full-integration modelling).

## 2 Supported loadings

The available loadings are the following:

- **'FORCE_ARETE'**

Makes it possible to apply line forces, on an edge of a volume element.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_SI

- **'FORCE_FACE'**

Makes it possible to apply surface forces on a face of a volume element.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_SI

- **'FORCE_INTERNE'**

Makes it possible to apply volume forces.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_SI

- **'PESANTEUR'**

Makes it possible to apply a gravity-type loading.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_SI

- **'PRES_REP'**

Makes it possible to apply a pressure to a continuum domain.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_SI

- **'PRE_EPSI'**

Makes it possible to apply a pre-strain field.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_SI

- **'ROTATION'**

Makes it possible to apply a rotational speed and a rotation vector.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_SI

- **'EFFE_FOND'**

Makes it possible to compute and apply the end-cap effect on a piping branch subjected to an internal pressure.

Supported modellings: 3D, 3D_SI
