# v1.01.320 PLEXU05 – Prestressed plate of reinforced concrete under uniform pressure with GLRC_DAMAGE law

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the connection of a voltage calculation of a prestress cable in *Code_Aster* with a fast dynamic calculation in Europlexus via the macro-command of *Code_Aster* CALC_EUROPLEXUS, and in the presence of the constitutive law GLRC_DAMAGE.

Specifically, it validates the following points:

- the correct transmission of information from the GLRC_DAMAGE law from *Code_Aster* to Europlexus via the CALC_EUROPLEXUS macro-command,
- the consideration of kinematic relationships from DEFI_CABLE_BP in the presence of GLRC_DAMAGE law.

## 1 Description

### 1.1 Geometry

The concrete plate is made up of a square of $L=0,9m$ edge length and $e=0,6m$ thickness. The four tops of the plate are named ${A}_{1}$ , ${A}_{2}$ , ${A}_{3}$ and ${A}_{4}$ .

A cable, located on the $[{A}_{3}{A}_{4}]$ segment, crosses the plate horizontally, without eccentricity in the thickness. L-section of the right section of the cable is ${S}_{a}={1.10}^{-4}{m}^{2}$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

$[{A}_{1}{A}_{2}]$ and $[{A}_{1}{A}_{3}]$ segments are blocked according to $Y$ direction and $X$ direction respectively. The $[{A}_{2}{A}_{4}]$ segment is blocked according to $Z$ direction and rotates around the three axes.

The load is applied in two steps. A first quasistatic calculation allows the cable to be prestressed at a $T=2,0{.10}^{5}N$ voltage. Then a uniform pressure is applied positively oriented in the $-Z$ direction on the entire surface of the plate. Its maximum amplitude is ${P}_{max}=0,15\mathit{MPa}$, and it is associated with a ramp ranging from $0$ to $1$ between ${t}_{\mathit{initial}}=\mathrm{0s}$ and ${t}_{\mathit{final}}=\mathrm{0.004s}$ moments.

## 2 reference solution

This is a non-regression test for the calculation performed with the DYNA_NON_LINE command. The DYNA_NON_LINE command is then used as a reference to the Europlexus calculation using the CALC_EUROPLEXUS macro-command.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The reinforced concrete plate uses Q4GG Modelling while the cable is in BARRE Modelling.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The reinforced concrete plate is composed of 72 elements T3GG, while the cable is modeled by 6 elements BARRE.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The $Z$ direction of the ${A}_{1}$ top is tested. A first calculation with DYNA_NON_LINE is used by a non-regression test, which is used as a reference for the Europlexus calculation using the CALC_EUROPLEXUS macro-command.

## 4 Executive summary

The calculation done with Europlexus via CALC_EUROPLEXUS took into account the different parameters of the GLRC_DAMAGE law.

The prestress calculation of the cable validated this functionality when the concrete is modelled by the constitutive law GLRC_DAMAGE.
