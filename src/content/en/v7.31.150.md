# v7.31.150 WTNV150 – Flowing into a interface junction within a porous mass: using the XFEM method

**Summary:**

This is a flow test in two cohesive XFEM hydraulic interfaces, the second being connected to the first. The opening of the cohesive zone is checked by the injection of a fluid and the development of a hydraulic fracture. This test consists of only one two-dimensional model.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry of the problem

This block is a square block on the $L=10m$ side. This block has two cohesive interface types of discontinuities (unmesh interface introduced into the model as a level curve (level-set) thanks to the DEFI_FISS_XFEM operator). The centre of the square and the origin of the $\mathit{Oxy}$ marker are confused. The first one is identified by the normal level-set of ${\mathit{lsn}}_{1}=Y-0.5X-0.2$ equation and crosses the block entirely in the horizontal direction. The second interface is identified by the normal level-set of ${\mathit{lsn}}_{2}=Y+0.5X+0.2$ equation. It connects to the lower lip of the first interface. The second interface therefore exists only in the part of the block such as ${\mathit{lsn}}_{1}<0$. The junction point between the two interfaces checks ${\mathit{lsn}}_{1}={\mathit{lsn}}_{2}=0$ and has $\lbrace \begin{array}{c}X=-0.4\\ Y=0\end{array}$ coordinates. The domain is thus cut into 3 blocks, a lower block, a upper block and an intermediate block between the two interfaces. The $A(5,2.7)$, $B(5,-2.7)$, $C(-1,-0,3)$, and $U(5,0)$ points will be used for the $U(5,0)$ assessment and the quantities evaluation will be tested.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The conditions of Dirichlet are:

- blocking of the $x$ movement on the right edge of the domain;
- blocking of the following $y$ movements on the lower and upper edges of the domain;
- block vertical displacements in the middle block;
- Blocking the movements following $y$ to point $U$ .

A $Q=0.04\mathit{kg}.{s}^{-1}$ point fluid stream is injected into the cohesive interfaces at $A$ and $B$ points for a duration of $t=50s$.

## 2 Quantities and reference results

### 2.1 Quantities and reference results

Under the action of fluid injection at $A$ and $B$ points, the cohesive interfaces open and hydraulic fractures develop. The value of fluid pressure and vertical opening at the two injection points $A$ and $B$ at the point $U$ are tested.
