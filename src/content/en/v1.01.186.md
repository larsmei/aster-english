# v1.01.186 ZZZZ186 - Validation of the IMPR_OAR Operator

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to validate the IMPR_OAR operator.

This operator allows to print a table of stresses from a mechanical or thermomechanical calculation in the d-format (Quick Analysis Tool).

## 1 Reference problem

A ASTER table is generated per load case.

The IMPR_OAR command reads the Aster tables and prints them in OAR software format.

The correct printing of the results is tested for the following cases:

- axisymmetric mechanical model,
- axisymmetric thermal model,
- 3D mechanical model,
- 3D thermal model.

### 1.1 Geometry

Axisymmetric thickness transition.

![geometry](/figures/v1.01.186/geom.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

#### 1.2.1 Mechanical properties

The material has elastic behavior with:

- Young's modulus E= 200,000 MPa,
- Poisson's ratio ν=0,3,
- $\alpha = 10.96\ 10^{-6}\ /°C$ thermal expansion coefficient.

#### 1.2.2 Thermal properties

The material has the following thermal properties:

- Conductivity λ = $54,6\ \mathrm{W \cdot m^{-1} \cdot K^{-1}}$
- Volumetric thermal capacity $\rho C_p = 3,71\ 10^{6}\ J/(m³·K)$.

## 2 reference solution

There is no reference solution. The tests presented are only regression tests.

## 3 Modelling A: thermal model axisymmetric

### 3.1 boundary conditions

The AB segment is blocked in direction Y.

The ED segment is uniformly deformed in the Y direction.

### 3.2 Loading

A thermal transient applied to the inner skin (line AGFE) is considered.

The transient is a ramp of 150°C to 50°C in 10s. The transient is cut in 6 moments.

### 3.3 Mesh

The mesh is in mm.

The mesh is a linear quadrangular mesh. It is converted to quadratic mesh before mechanical resolution.

It has 190 nodes.

### 3.4 Results

The thermal stresses are extracted from the calculation results by the MACR_LIGN_COUP operator.

The stress result of this calculation is printed for the FC segment by the IMPR_TABLE operator and by the IMPR_OAR operator.

## 4 Modelling B: Volumetric thermal model

### 4.1 boundary conditions

The AB side is blocked in the Y direction. In addition, on this side, a collinear azimuth with DZ on DX and a collinear point with DZ on DX is blocked.

The ED segment is uniformly deformed in the Y direction.

### 4.2 Loading

A thermal transient applied to the internal skin (surface AGFE) is considered.

The load is identical to the axisymmetric model.

### 4.3 Mesh

## 5 Modelling C: mechanical model axisymmetric

### 5.1 boundary conditions

The AB segment is clamped. The Y and Z directions are blocked on this segment.

### 5.2 Loading

*Pression:*

PX unit pressure is applied to the inner skin (line AGFE).

A $\mathit{PY}=\frac{R\stackrel{2}{i}}{R\stackrel{2}{e}-R\stackrel{2}{i}}$ background effect is applied at the end of the model to the ED segment.

*Flexion:*

One unit effort is applied to the skin mesh cells of the thin end: PY = 1

*Torsion:*

A unit effort is applied to the skin mesh cells of the thin end: PZ = 1

### 5.3 Mesh

The mesh is identical to the Modelling A. Before calculation, this linear mesh is converted to quadratic mesh.

### 5.4 Results

The mechanical stresses are extracted from the calculation result by the MACR_LIGN_COUP operator.

The stress result of this calculation is printed for the FC segment by the IMPR_TABLE operator and by the IMPR_OAR operator.

The two tables were verified to be the same at the 5th node of the cut (4.50526E-03 of the inner skin), with the difference in the conversion of Pa to MPa stresses.

| **Identification** | **Reference type** | **Value** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NB_VALE | REGRESSIONS | 380 |
| VALE_CALC | REGRESSIONS | 228.98662111199997 |

## 6 Modelling D: mechanical volumetric model

### 6.1 boundary conditions

The AB side is clamped.

The node centre of the ED side is bound to the ED side. The torsos are applied to this node.

### 6.2 Loading

*Pression:*

PX unit pressure is applied to the inner skin (line AGFE).

A $\mathit{PY}=\frac{R\stackrel{2}{i}}{R\stackrel{2}{e}-R\stackrel{2}{i}}$ background effect is applied at the end of the model to the ED segment.

Efforts *:*

A unitary effort of 104 N.m is applied to the node centre of the ED face for bending forces in both directions and torsion effort.

### 6.3 Mesh

The mesh is identical to the Modelling B. Before calculation, the linear mesh is converted to quadratic mesh.

### 6.4 Results

The mechanical stresses are extracted from the calculation result by the MACR_LIGN_COUP operator.
