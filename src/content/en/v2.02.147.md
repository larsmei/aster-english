# v2.02.147 SDLL147 – Single vertical beam mounted on a spring subject to earthquake

**Summary:**

This test contributes to the validation of the COMB_SISM_MODAL spectral seismic analysis operator of *code_aster*. This is an test case non-regression, relative to the Gupta method, which considers a dynamic and quasi-static contribution of eigenmodes in a frequency band to be defined beforehand.

The structure is a simple vertical beam mounted on a spring. The values tested are displacements, absolute accelerations and nodal reactions in 4 nodes.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a beam 40 metres high, the lower part (between altitudes 0 and 10 metres) of which is composed of a stiff material, while the upper part (over the 10 metre side) is more flexible; the whole is equipped with a spring at the lower end (see v2.02.147-fig-model-simplify).

## 4 Summary of the results

This test case non-regression validates the Gupta method for recombinant modal responses in spectral seismic analysis. The values tested are the movements, absolute accelerations and nodal reactions in 4 nodes of the beam mounted on a given spring.
