# v6.06.200 CONT200 - Projection-intersection for beam-launch pairing for linear 2D mesh cells

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to verify the projection-intersection procedure in the case of beam-launch matching (mortar approach).
Specifically, this test is limited to the case of linear mesh cells in 2D (so mesh cells skin type SEG2).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 General formalism adopted

First of all, we recall the definition of the operator.
***apparatus** (by **render** here).
$\Pi_{t}^{{\rm sm}}$, takes in an point surface
slave $\underline{x}^{{\rm s}}$, and makes it match a
point master surface $\widehat{\underline{x}}^{{\rm m}}$:

$$
\begin{array}{l|rcl}
\Pi_{t}^{{\rm sm}}: & \Gamma^{{\rm s}}_{{\rm C}, t} & \longrightarrow & \Gamma^{{\rm m}}_{{\rm C}, t} \\
    & \underline{x}^{{\rm s}} & \longmapsto & \widehat{\underline{x}}^{{\rm m}} \end{array}
$$

- $\underline{A}^{\rm s}$ and $\underline{B}^{\rm s}$ (resp. $\underline{A}^{\rm m}$ and $\underline{B}^{\rm m}$) the slave segment nodes (master resp.),
- $\Pi^{\rm sm} \underline{A}^{\rm s} = \widehat{\underline{A}}^{\rm m}$ and $\Pi^{\rm sm} \underline{B}^{\rm s} =\widehat{\underline{B}}^{\rm m}$ projections on the mesh cell master of the nodes slaves via a ray throw,
- $\widehat{\underline{a}}^{\rm m}$ and $\widehat{\underline{b}}^{\rm m}$ projections of $\widehat{\underline{A}}^{\rm m}$ and $\widehat{\underline{A}}^{\rm m}$ on the master mesh cell,
- $\left(\Pi^{\rm sm}\right)^{-1}\widehat{\underline{a}}^{\rm m} = \widehat{\underline{a}}^{\rm s}$ and $\left(\Pi^{\rm sm}\right)^{-1}\widehat{\underline{b}}^{\rm m} = \widehat{\underline{b}}^{\rm s}$: $\widehat{\underline{a}}^{\rm m}$ and $\widehat{\underline{b}}^{\rm m}$ projections on the slave mesh cell by the inverse of the matching operator.

### 2.2 Plans with rotation in 2d

$$
\underline{B}^{{\rm s}} - \underline{A}^{{\rm s}} = \begin{pmatrix}2\cos\theta \\ 2\sin\theta  \end{pmatrix} \qquad \Rightarrow \qquad \underline{n}^{{\rm s}} = \begin{pmatrix} -\sin\theta \\ \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}
$$

We therefore have the equation of the vector line
$\underline{n}^{{\rm s}} = \left(n_x^{{\rm s}}, n_y^{{\rm s}}\right)^\top$
via $\left(x_0, y_0\right)$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Leftrightarrow \quad n^{{\rm s}}_y x - n^{{\rm s}}_x y =n^{{\rm s}}_y x_0 - n^{{\rm s}}_x y_0 \\
&\Leftrightarrow \quad \cos\theta x + \sin\theta  y = \cos\theta x_0 + \sin\theta y_0
\end{aligned}
$$

We then obtain the different points of intersection in
Whereas the points of intersection between these lines passing through the
nodes of the mesh cell slave and the $y=0$ plan. More precisely,
we have:

$$
\left\{\begin{array}{rcl}
\left(\widehat{\underline{A}}^{{\rm m}}\right)_x&=& -\cos\theta + \tan\theta\left(h - \sin\theta\right) \\
\left(\widehat{\underline{B}}^{{\rm m}}\right)_x&=&  \cos\theta + \tan\theta\left(h + \sin\theta\right)\\
\end{array}\right.
$$

$\left(\widehat{\underline{A}}^{{\rm m}}\right)_x$ is the x-coordinated of the $\underline{A}^{{\rm s}}$ projection,
while $\left(\widehat{\underline{B}}^{{\rm m}}\right)_x$ is the x-coordinated of the $\underline{B}^{{\rm s}}$ projection.

By construction, the projection of the point $\underline{B}^{{\rm s}}$
always has a horizontal coordinate superior to the projection of the point
$\underline{A}^{{\rm s}}$. We deduct a condition
existence of point intersection:

$$
f_h\left(\theta\right) =  -\cos\theta + \tan\theta\left(h - \sin\theta\right) \leq 1
$$

To illustrate the different cases, a representation is provided
graph of this criterion according to several values
d-hyperparameters.

![Figure](/figures/v6.06.200/v6-06-200-figure-4.png)

** Function representing the condition of existence of an intersection**

We can then build a multitude of cases
to treat the existence or not of intersection between the two mesh cells.
In the adopted formalism, the projection of the point
$\underline{B}^{\rm s}$ is still outside the master segment,
The point $\underline{A}^{\rm s}$ projection is in
the value of the criterion, i.e.
We'll have $f_h\left(\theta\right) < 1$:

$$
\widehat{\underline{a}}^{\rm m} = \begin{pmatrix} -\cos\theta + \tan\theta\left(h - \sin\theta\right) \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} \qquad \text{et} \qquad \widehat{\underline{b}}^{\rm m} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0\end{pmatrix}
$$

The point $\widehat{\underline{b}}^{\rm m}$ belongs to
construction in the segment between $\widehat{\underline{A}}^{{\rm m}}$
and $\widehat{\underline{B}}^{{\rm m}}$. We can go through
parametric expression of this segment, which gives
$\widehat{\underline{b}}^{\rm m} = \widehat{\underline{A}}^{{\rm m}} + t_\star\left[ \widehat{\underline{B}}^{{\rm m}} -  \widehat{\underline{A}}^{{\rm m}}\right]$.
The parameter $t_\star$ can be obtained by equivalences
the following:

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\Leftrightarrow \quad 1 = \left(\widehat{\underline{A}}^{{\rm m}}\right)_x + t_\star \left[\left(\widehat{\underline{B}}^{{\rm m}}\right)_x - \left(\widehat{\underline{A}}^{{\rm m}}\right)_x\right] \\
&\Leftrightarrow \quad  t_\star = \frac{1 + \cos\theta - \tan\theta\left(h - \sin\theta\right)}{2\cos\theta + 2\tan\theta \sin\theta}
\end{aligned}
$$

This parameter is used to obtain the corresponding point on the
mesh cell slave (before projection):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\underline{A}^{{\rm s}} + t_\star\left[\underline{B}^{{\rm s}} - \underline{A}^{{\rm s}}\right] &= \begin{pmatrix} -\cos\theta \\ h - \sin\theta \end{pmatrix}+ 2t_\star \begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta  \\ \sin\theta \end{pmatrix}\\
&= \begin{pmatrix}
\left(-1 + 2t_\star\right)\cos \theta\\
h + \left(-1 + 2t_\star\right) \sin\theta
\end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

Finally, the intersection points on the slave mesh cell in
Actual coordinates are given by:

$$
\widehat{\underline{a}}^{\rm s} = \underline{A}^{\rm s} = \begin{pmatrix} -\cos\theta \\ h - \sin\theta \end{pmatrix} \qquad \text{et} \qquad \widehat{\underline{b}}^{\rm s} = \begin{pmatrix}
\left(-1 + 2t_\star\right)\cos \theta\\
h + \left(-1 + 2t_\star\right) \sin\theta
\end{pmatrix}
$$
