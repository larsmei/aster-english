# v3.04.157 SSLV157 – RBE3-type relationships between a cube and a discrete

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check the RBE3 relationship between a 3D element and a discrete.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A unit cube and a discrete element with the following coordinates are considered.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The node of the $(0,0,0)$ coordinate cube is blocked following $\mathit{DZ}$ .

The node of the $(1,0,0)$ coordinate cube is blocked following $\mathit{DX}$, $\mathit{DY}$, $\mathit{DZ}$.

The node of the $(1,1,0)$ coordinate cube is blocked following $\mathit{DX}$, $\mathit{DZ}$.

The node of the $(0,1,0)$ coordinate cube is subjected to nodal force $\mathit{FX}=-0.123456701636$ , $\mathit{FY}=-0.246913403273$ , $\mathit{FZ}=-0.370370090008$ .
