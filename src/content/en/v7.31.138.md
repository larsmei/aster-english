# v7.31.138 WTNV138 - Undrained triaxial test with VISC_DRUC_PRAG

**Summary**

This test validates the VISC_DRUC_PRAG model with hydraulic coupling. It is a triaxial test in undrained condition. The undrained aspect is modelled by a zero volume deformation of the skeleton and the hydraulic coupling is taken into account, the sample is fully saturated, the skeleton and fluid being assumed to be incompressible.

Because of symmetry, only one of the eighth samples tested is considered to be triaxial.

The containment level is $8\mathrm{MPa}$ .

### 1.3 Initial conditions, boundary conditions, and loading

**Phase 1:**

The sample is taken to a homogeneous state of effective stresses: ${\sigma}_{xx}^{0}={\sigma}_{yy}^{0}={\sigma}_{zz}^{0}$ , by imposing the corresponding total pressure on the front, right and upper side and by imposing zero water pressures everywhere. The movements are blocked on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$ ), left (${u}_{y}=0$ ) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$ ) sides.

**Phase 2:**

The movements are held on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$), left (${u}_{y}=0$) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$) sides. On all sides, the hydraulic flows are zero.

A displacement is applied to the upper face to obtain ${\varepsilon}_{zz}=-0,06$ deformation (counted from the beginning of phase 2). On the front and right side faces, boundary conditions in total stress is imposed:

$\sigma .n={\sigma}_{0}(=8\mathrm{MPa})$
