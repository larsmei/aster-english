# v2.01.110 SDLD110 –Set of dynamic stiffness at 2 degrees of freedom

**Summary:**

The problem considered is a dynamic system at 2 degrees of freedom, often used in the literature to illustrate the notion of stiff problem.

These are 3 unit masses connected by very different rigidity springs (several orders of quantity) with one end excited by sinusoidal displacement.

The test brings together several modellings, on a physical and modal basis with different time integration patterns.

### 1.2 Material properties

**Butterfly:**

| Rigidity | ${k}_{1}={10}^{7}$ et ${k}_{2}=1$ |
| --- | --- |
| Masses | ${m}_{1}={m}_{2}={m}_{3}=1$ |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The problem is unidirectional according to $x$ .

The displacement of the first mass is imposed ${u}_{1}=\sin({\omega}_{p}t)$ with ${\omega}_{p}=2.$ .

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The initial conditions are given by ${u}_{2}(0)={\dot{u}}_{2}(0)={u}_{3}(0)={\dot{u}}_{3}(0)=0$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The problem can be put into the following matrix form:

$\left[\begin{array}{cc}{m}_{2}& 0\\ 0& {m}_{3}\end{array}\right]\left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\dot{u}}_{2}\\ {\dot{u}}_{3}\end{array}\right\rbrace +\left[\begin{array}{cc}{k}_{1}{+k}_{2}& -{k}_{2}\\ -{k}_{2}& {k}_{2}\end{array}\right]\left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{u}_{2}\\ {u}_{3}\end{array}\right\rbrace =\left\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{k}_{1}\sin({\omega}_{p}t)\\ 0\end{array}\right\rbrace$

This problem admits an analytical solution [bib2]:

$\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{u}_{2}=\sin({\omega}_{p}t)\\ {u}_{3}=\frac{1}{3}\left({\omega}_{p}\sin\left(t\right)-\sin({\omega}_{p}t)\right)\end{array}$

### 2.2 Uncertainty about the solution

None (analytical solution).
