# v6.03.010 SSNP010 - Stability of a slope sustained at seismic inertial force

**Summary:**

This test case validates the application of seismic inertial force in CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command. The safety factor of a homogeneous slope is calculated and the result compared with that in the literature[1].

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.010/slope.png)

*mesh and homogeneous slope geometry.*

The stability of a homogeneous upstream slope with seismic inertial force is studied. The slope height is 15m. The slope angle is $36.87^{\circ}$.
The mesh of the slope is shown in geom-ssnp010.

### 1.2 Material properties

The mechanical properties of the soil are as follows:

- Density: $2000kg/m^3$
- Friction angle: $15^{\circ}$
- Cohesion : $35kPa$

The Mohr_Coulomb break-up criterion is adopted with the associated flow law.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The following loads shall be applied:

- The gravity with $g=9,81m/{s}^{2}$
- The horizontal seismic inertial force corresponding to the acceleration coefficient $k_a = 0.025$, which is the maximum acceleration coefficient of soil 0.1 factored by an equivalent coefficient 0.25.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

In the referenced literature, the safety factor is calculated by Morgenstern-Price procedure using the pseudostatic method according to GB 50330-2013 and SP117[1] recommendations.

### 2.2 Baseline

The reference safety factor is 1.32.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

In order to speed up the calculation, according to the preliminary study, the apses of the ends of the breaking surface are fixed at 50m and 76.8m. The slippery mass is divided into 5 slices of soil. The search space is discretized into 8 potential breakable surfaces. The simplified Bishop method is used to solve the FS. Four iterative refinements of the mesh are performed to obtain the exact solution of the FS.

In the referenced literature, the FS is solved by Morgenstern-Price method. However, since the material and the loads are all homogeneous, Bishop's method is supposed to give the same result.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

The safety factor obtained is tested. non-regression is also tested on intermediate results such as rotation center, radius and number of surfaces generated. The results are shown in Table 1.

The result of the FS from the CALC_STAB_PENTE macro-command gives the difference 0.28%
This proves the relevance of the Bishop method to seismic inertial force.
