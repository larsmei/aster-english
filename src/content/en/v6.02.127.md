# v6.02.127 SSNL127 - Tensile test with CORR_ACIER

**Summary:**

This quasi-static non-linear mechanical test validates the damaged CORR_ACIER elasto-plastic model.

6 modellings validates the behavior:

1. modellings A, B, C in $1D$ for elements BARRE, POU_D_EM, POU_D_TGM *;*
2. 3D Modelling D in the case of a axisymmetric test piece (homogeneous stress and deformation state) subjected to a single tensile test; 3D Modelling of the test piece is performed with elements CU20;
3. Modelling E in COQUE_3D in the case of a single-trail plate with an mesh cell QUAD9;
4. F Modelling valid for elements POU_D_EM, correct bending operation and consideration of thermal expansion.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry is chosen voluntarily simple, to translate a state of homogeneous stresses and deformations, as is the case in uniaxial traction. In the case of $1D$ and $\mathrm{3D}$, it is here a bar of $\varphi =6\mathit{mm}$ diameter and length $L=0.1m$. In $\mathrm{3D}$, one only mesh cell is one quarter of the bar. The traction is carried out at displacement imposed in $\mathrm{1D}$ and $\mathrm{3D}$ with the length of the test piece. In the case of COQUE_3D, the plate is square on the side , and $\pi \frac{{\varphi}^{2}}{4}$ thickness (to make the forces identical to the $\mathrm{1D}$ case).

For the case in bending with thermal expansion, 4 bars are used instead of one, symmetrically excreted in Y and Z from ${e}_{y}={e}_{z}=2.5\mathit{mm}$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 1D modelling

$\mathit{DX}$ $\mathit{DZ}$ and $\mathit{DY}$ moves blocked to point $A$

$\mathit{DX}$ and $\mathit{DZ}$ displacement imposed on point $B$

#### 1.3.2 3D modelling

$\mathit{DX}$ move prevented on ${\mathit{Surf}}_{int}$

$\mathit{DY}$ move prevented on ${\mathit{Surf}}_{1}$

$\mathit{DZ}$ move prevented on ${\mathit{Surf}}_{2}$

$\mathit{DX}$ displacement imposed on ${\mathit{Surf}}_{\mathit{ext}}$

Modelling COQUE_3D:

$\mathit{DX}$ move prevented on $\mathit{Li4}$

$\mathit{DY}$ move prevented on $\mathit{Li1}$

$\mathit{DX}$ displacement imposed on $\mathit{Li3}$

#### 1.3.3 Modelling in bending with thermal expansion

clamped support au point $A$ ,

$Y$ is applied an ${F}_{y}$ force to the point $B$ in such a way that it is placed at the elastic limit.

Knowing that in point $A$ one has ${M}_{z}={F}_{y}L$ on one hand, and ${M}_{z}=4{e}_{y}{\sigma}_{xx}\pi \frac{{\varphi}^{2}}{4}$ on the other hand, and that at the ${\sigma}_{xx}=\mathit{SY}$ elastic limit one then imposes ${F}_{z}=4{e}_{y}\mathit{SY}\pi \frac{{\varphi}^{2}}{4}\frac{1}{L}\simeq 1413.7167N$.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

No stress and deformation.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution is composed of the results of the tensile tests carried out on corroded bars (the initial diameter is $6\mathrm{mm}$). Following these tests, the plastic deformation with increased corrosion level is reduced.

### 2.2 Baseline

Evolution of plastic deformation with fracture of corroded bars as a function of corrosion level (reduction of bar diameter or flat reinforcement thickness from noncorroded bars) is presented in [Figure 2.2-a] and [Figure 2.2-b].

**Figure 2.2.b Evolution of plastic deformation with rupture as a function of corrosion rate**

#### 2.2.1 Modelling in bending with thermal expansion

By placing itself at the elastic limit the reference solution is analytical.

Au point $B$ on a:

${u}_{x}=\alpha ({T}_{\mathit{final}}-{T}_{\mathit{ini}})L$ ,

${u}_{y}=\frac{{F}_{y}{L}^{3}(4+{\phi }_{y})}{12E{I}_{z}}$ ,

${\theta}_{z}=\frac{{L}^{2}{F}_{y}}{2E{I}_{z}}$ ,

with ${\varphi}_{y}=0$ and ${I}_{z}=4{e}_{y}^{2}\pi \frac{{\varphi}^{2}}{4}$ .

Au point $A$ on a:

${V}_{y}={F}_{y}$ ,

${M}_{z}={F}_{y}L$

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Code accuracy.
