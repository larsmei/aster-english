# v4.01.007 TPLA07 - Orthotropic hollow cylinder

**Summary:**

This test is based on independent validation of version 3 in linear steady-state thermal analysis.

This is a 2D axisymmetric problem represented by two modellings, the first three-dimensional, the second 2D axisymmetric.

The advantage of this test case is to test an orthotropic material subjected to different boundary conditions (flux imposed, convection, linear variation of outside temperatures).

The results from this test case are compared with those provided by VPCS.

In addition, this test validates the calculation of thermal energy for an orthotropic material on the elements 2D. To this end, two calculations are made in addition to that with the orthotropic material:

- a calculation with isotropic material (keyword THER of DEFI_MATERIAU) as reference
- a calculation with isotropic material but using keyword THER_ORTH from DEFI_MATERIAU validated by the first calculation.

For the calculation actually orthotropic one will use a reference NON_REGRESSION.

- $\varphi =-500W/\mathrm{m²}$ flow density through the $z=0$ surface (outgoing flow),

- on the ${R}_{i}$ surface: ${T}_{i}^{e}=130°C$ in $z=0$; ${T}_{i}^{e}=135°C$ in $z=0.4$ $({T}_{i}^{e}=130+12.5z)$
- on the ${R}_{e}$ surface: ${T}_{e}^{e}=20°C$ in $z=0$; ${T}_{e}^{e}=25°C$ in $z=0.4$ $({T}_{e}^{e}=20+12.5z)$

## 4 Summary of the results

The results obtained are satisfactory in temperature and flow density, The maximum differences obtained are as follows:

- 0.02% temperature,
- 1.05% for the flow following the $z$ axis,
- 0.33% for the flow following the radius.
