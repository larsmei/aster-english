# v1.01.235 PLEXU01 – Basic CALC_EUROPLEXUS command validation

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the CALC_EUROPLEXUS command which initiates a EUROPLEXUS calculation (fast dynamic code) from a data set of the calculation in Code_Aster. It is verified that the results recovered by Code_Aster following the EUROPLEXUS calculation are those calculated by EUROPLEXUS alone.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A model structure consisting of a horizontal square slab $0.5m$ of thickness and side $3m$, four vertical poles of length $3m$ planted in four corners of the slab and four horizontal beams (of the same length) connecting the upper ends of the poles and thus forming a closed contour is considered. All posts and beams have a rectangular cross section $0.4m\times 0.6m$.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material of the slab is elastic isotropic corresponding to a concrete whose properties are:

- $E=30000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =2500\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$

The material of the poles and beams is elastic isotropic corresponding to a steel whose properties are:

- $E=200000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =7500\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The slab is based on a carpet of springs whose stiffness is calculated by the RIGI_PARASOL command from 6 specified values. A carpet of ground shock absorbers is also defined, of the A_T_D_N type, whose values are chosen voluntarily low in order to remain close to the reference solution, calculated, it, neglecting this damping.

The slab is loaded by a pressure overfacial whose time-likeness is given by a function.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution comes from the EUROPLEXUS calculation launched outside of Code_Aster.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The displacement, speed and acceleration values reread by Code_Aster are tested at the final moment and compared with the values from a single EUROPLEXUS calculation.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

None.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Q4GG Modelling is used for slab and POU_D_E for beams and poles.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh of the slab contains 36 elements of type QUAD4. Each beam and pole is discretized with 6 mesh cells SEG2.

The displacement, speed and acceleration values reread by Code_Aster are tested at the final moment and compared with the values from a single EUROPLEXUS calculation.
