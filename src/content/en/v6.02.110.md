# v6.02.110 SSNL110 - finite element from traction bar

This test checks elements finished `BARRE_2D_NL` and
`BARRE_3D_NL` in the case of a bar subject to elongation
uniform obtained by forced travel.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry is a slash in the $(Oxy)$ plane, the original coordinates of which are $(2,1,0)$ and $(4,2,0)$ end.

The bar has a $S = 5\ \mathrm{mm}^2$ section.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic, from Young's modulus
$E = 2 \times 10^5\ \mathrm{MPa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

All nodes on the bar are controlled while moving, with:

We choose $\varepsilon = 5\times 10^{-3}$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The displacement imposed leads to uniform $\varepsilon$ elongation deformation in the bar. Due to the elastic behaviour, the normal $N$ stress is also uniform and is:

$$
N = E S \varepsilon
$$

### 2.2 Baseline

Taking into account the values used for deformation and Young's modulus, the normal effort shall be:

$$
N = 5        \times 10^{3}\ \mathrm{N}
$$
