# v5.02.113 SDNL113 - Lyre-shaped pipework (ELSA tests) under seismic loading

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test case is to test the results of a structure, consisting of pipes (right or necked), rods and valves, subjected to seismic loading and whose nonlinearity is derived from the material behaviour in the elbows.

This is done by conducting a nonlinear direct transient analysis of the structure, including using TUYAU_3M modelling. The displacements, nodal reactions and cumulative plastic deformation at certain points in the structure are then tested.

In order to limit the calculation time, while having plasticity early enough during the transient, the elastic limit is divided by two.

This test also helps validate MOMENT_EQUIVALENT's POST_RCCM/B3600 option.

### 1.4 Initial conditions and calculation moment list

The system is initially at rest.

To limit the time CPU, the numerical simulation will only be conducted up to a final moment worth $0,17s$. At this moment, the level of loading reached is such that plasticity has already declared itself.

A list of optimized calculation moments is built: the time step is changing from $0,01s$ to 0.001 s to quickly go linear and reduce the time step just before the plasticization.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Reference calculation

This calculation is a test of non-regression. It therefore does not show any reference results.

Calculation procedure:

- Calculation of the rigidity and mass matrices (operator ASSEMBLAGE),
- Calculation of seismic loading,
- Nonlinear direct transient analysis in relative reference, single-support.

### 2.2 Quantities references

$\mathit{DX}$: represents, according to the quantity field:

- DEPL: the component of the relative displacement along the $x$ axis
- REAC_NODA: the component of the nodal reaction along the $x$ axis

$\mathit{V1}$: component of the VARI_ELNO field giving the cumulative plastic deformation.
