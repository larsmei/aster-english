# v2.02.128 SDLL128 - Tree line with bearing characteristics dependent on rotational speed

**Summary:**

This test validates the calculation of rotational modes of a shaft system rotating in the presence of bearings whose characteristics in stiffness and damping depend on the rotational speed.

In this test, a rotor model with three discs, supported by two hydrodynamic bearings, whose stiffness and damping matrices are nonsymmetric and dependent on the rotation speed. This example is taken from the ROTORINSA qualification manual, finished elements software designed to predict dynamic bending rotor behaviour.

## 1 Reference problem

A rotor model supported by 2 bearings (nodes $\mathrm{B1}$ and $\mathrm{B2}$ in the figure below), whose stiffness and damping matrices are non-symmetric. It consists of 3 discs and 4 shaft sections.

### 1.3 boundary conditions

To block rigid body-type movements in the $x$ direction, the degree of freedom $\mathrm{DX}$ is blocked at node $\mathrm{B1}$ bearing.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

Modal calculations were performed over the rotational speed range from $5000$ to $6000\mathrm{tr}/\min$ with a step of $250\mathrm{tr}/\min$. To validate the correct consideration of linear variation of bearing characteristics as a function of speed, a calculation of eigenfrequencies is also performed at the rotational speed $5750\mathrm{tr}/\min$, which corresponds to the centre of the speed range considered.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The results of Code-Aster give both the frequencies of bending, twisting and tensile/compression modes. The number of modes calculated is 12.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

The tests provide the non-regression of the code. They cover the first 12 frequencies, for the $5750\mathit{tr}/min$ rotation speed, for both calculation methods. They also test the reduced damping.

The frequencies obtained by direct calculation at the central rotation speed are in perfect alignment with those obtained by interpolation within the range of variation of the rotation speeds.

## 4 Summary of the results

This test case allows the validation of hydrodynamic bearings whose characteristics in stiffness and damping depend on the rotational speed.
