# v1.01.208 ZZZZ208 – Validation of Generalized Terms Extraction and Recovery Methods

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the EXTR_MATR_GENE, EXTR_VECT_GENE, RECU_MATR_GENE and RECU_VECT_GENE methods. General terms are extracted, a change is made in the python environment and a cross-validation is made by comparing the results obtained with the library nullsy and those obtained with the generic commands.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A slender structure of $0.25m$ length and $0.05m\ast 0.03m$ section is considered.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.208/10000000000004F200000251BEE5495EA3EDE7FB.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- Young's modulus $E=2.1{10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$
- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$
- Density $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/\mathit{m³}$
- Hysteretic damping $\eta =0.01$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

We place a zero displacement on the $y=0.25m$ abscisse face

For the calculation of the harmonic response, a vertical $(1+\mathrm{0.5j})/2$ amplitude point excitation is applied to the point coordinates $(0m,0.1375m,0.03m)$ .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

Two types of calculation are performed to test the methods for handling generalised quantities.

The first Modelling performs a modal calculation and a harmonic response calculation on the generalised quantities.

The second model performs a modal calculation using the dynamic substructure technique.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

Cross validation is performed by comparing results from standard operators and calculations with the library nully.

The obtained eigenfrequencies and the displacement field for the harmonic response calculation shall be checked.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Regression is considered to occur if the results obtained are not identical.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

Not applicable

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

A modal calculation and harmonic response calculation is performed on a basis composed of 10 modes.

To test the extraction and recovery of generalized quantities, a change in the generalised terms is made in the python environment, which consists of multiplying the generalized rigidity by two and dividing the generalised second member vector by two.

The generalised model's eigenmodes and harmonic response to $500\mathit{Hz}$ are then calculated.

For the calculation of the harmonic response, a point excitation is applied to point coordinates $(0m,0.1375m,0.03m)$ .

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.208/Object_4.png)

The results obtained by successively using diagonal storage and full storage with real matrices and complex matrices are tested.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 240 elements of type HEXA8.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

A modal calculation is performed using the dynamic substructure technique.

A first calculation is made using a projection base of the substructure composed solely of static modes. For the second calculation, a base consisting of normal modes and five interface modes is used.

The first substructure is from a mesh composed of 240 elements of the HEXA8 type.
