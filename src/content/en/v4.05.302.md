# v4.05.302 TPLP302 - Rectangular plate with temperature imposed

**Summary:**

This test is based on independent validation of version 3 in linear steady-state thermal analysis.

This is a 2D plan problem represented by a hull model.

The functions tested are as follows:

- element thermal hull,
- limit conditions: temperature imposed.

The results are compared with an analytical solution.

### 3.3 Remarks

The limit conditions, $T=100°C$ on $\mathrm{AB}$ , and $T=0°C$ on $\mathrm{AD}$ , are incompatible with the point A. *Code_Aster* applies an "overload law" which in this case consists of taking into account the last entry limit condition. The order of assignment of the imposed temperatures therefore has a great influence on the results obtained.

In the case treated, the temperature assigned to point $A$ is $0°C$ .
