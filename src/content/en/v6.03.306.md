# v6.03.306 SSNP306 - Validation of the criterion for flaming by selective search of own values

**Summary:**

This test allows the validation of the generalization of the flaming criterion (loss of solution uniqueness, presence of bifurcation) for mixed formulation models. The new criterion, which is written as a problem with the own sub-contraint values, is validated for the Modelling of non-local GRAD_VARI damage. The smallest modes of the GRAD_VARI formulation are compared with those of the local Modelling in the case of a homogeneous study.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Theoretical framework

The usual flashing criterion results in a sign check of the smallest value of $K\delta u=\mu \delta u$ tangent matrix. If $\mu \le 0$ the mechanical problem solution is no longer unique.

For mixed formulations, the modes of the Lagrange coefficients must be eliminated, which leads us back to a problem with the stress modes.

$K(\begin{array}{}\delta u\\ \delta \lambda \end{array})=\mu (\begin{array}{}\delta u\\ 0\end{array})$ is the new flashing criterion for mixed Modelling.

In the case of GRAD_VARI the criterion is even more restrictive, as the constitutive law must also be taken into account to obtain physical modes. This must be possible to generalize to other types of modellings. The criterion is written as a problem with generalised own values:

$K(\begin{array}{}\delta u\\ \delta \alpha \\ \delta \lambda \end{array})=\mu (\begin{array}{}\delta u\\ 0\\ 0\end{array})$ new flashing criterion for GRAD_VARI Modelling.

This gives us two constraints of equality in the search for the moving mode. The first allows us to check the material constitutive law and the second equals the disturbances of damage to the nodes with that calculated locally at Gauss points.

We validate the new flashing criterion by comparing the smallest modes of the GRAD_VARI formulation with those of the local Modelling in the case of a homogeneous study.

*Note:

Technically to achieve this, the new RIGI_GEOM and DDL_EXCLUS commands have been added to the CRIT_STAB operator. In this case, they are used as follows:

CRIT_STAB = _F( RIGI_GEOM = 'NON', # *Required to then call* DDL_EXCLUS

DDL_EXCLUS = ('VARI','LAG_GV'))

## 2 reference solution

The same study is done in local and non-local. The smallest tangential operator values are compared with the standard local blazing criterion (which will be taken as a reference) and with the new non-local criterion. D_PLAN modelling is used in local, D_PLAN_GRAD_VARI modelling is used in non-local. The loading is carried out over a period of 100 time step, so that the passage is visualized by a non-nul damage state of the structure. The test is considered to be successful, if relative the difference does not exceed 0.01%.
