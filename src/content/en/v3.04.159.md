# v3.04.159 SSLV159 - Cantilever beam subject to static deflection loading

**Summary:**

This test validates the connection between 1D and 3D models, in the Arlequin frame (3D_POU_ARLEQUIN) [1].

It is a cantilever beam model, clamped at one end and subjected to a static deflection loading at the other end.

The results of the calculations are compared with those obtained by Code_aster with a 1D reference model and a 1D-3D mixed model connected with the 3D_POU option. The results coincide perfectly with the reference solutions.

## 1 Reference problem

The objective of this test case is to validate the Arlequin 3-D beam connection in Code_Aster.

The results obtained are compared with those obtained from two modellings in Code_Aster:

- 1D reference mono-modelization
- 1D-3D mixed reference model with 3D_POU connection

### 1.1 Geometry

A beam of length 0.35 m on the X axis, clamped on the left end and charged with a concentrated force F at its right end. The cross section of the beam is a rectangle of width b and height h (in this case: b = h = 0.02 m).

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.159/10000200000002A00000006C876BE78652190138.png)

**Image** 1.1-1 **: Rotor Geometry**

### 1.2 Material properties

The cantilever beam has a density of $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$ .

The Young's modulus is $E=2.{10}^{11}N{m}^{-2}$ and the Poisson's ratio is $\nu =0,3$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The left end of the beam (X = 0.15 m) is fixed, and the right end (X = 0.5 m) is subjected to a concentrated load that is vertically directed downwards at 500 N.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is derived from a 1D calculation with Code_Aster (see Model B). The latter is itself validated in relation to the RDM solution for a cantilever beam. The analytical solution is equal to:

$w=\frac{F.{L}^{3}}{3.E.J}$

where F is the concentrated force applied, L is the length of the beam, E is the Young's modulus of the material, $J=\frac{b.{h}^{3}}{12}$ is the moment of inertia of the cross section of the beam.

  1. Ghanem, "Contribution to advanced modelling of rotating machines in transient dynamics in the Arlequin framework", thesis of the Lyon INSA.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The cantilever beam is 0.35 m long and extending between X = 0.15 m and X = 0.5 m. It is modeled in 3D near the clamped support (between 0 and 0.22 m) and continues with beam modelling. The 1D-3D connection is made in volume by the 3D_POU_ARLEQUIN option of keyword LIAISON_ELEM of AFFE_CHAR_MECA. The cover area extends between 0.2 and 0.22 m.

The structure is modelled by elements of Timoshenko beam (POU_D_T) and elements linear hexahedral (HEXA8).

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.159/100002000000050000000189E1D5642A1A838935.png)

DYNA_VIBRA (transitional calculation on physical basis) calculates the dynamic response of the structure due to a 500 N nodal force applied to the node end of the mesh 1D (located at X = 0.5 m).

## 6 Summary of the results

The test case implements in Code_Aster the 1D-3D volumetric connection in the Arlequin frame on the basis of a cantilever beam. The results of the resulting mixed model are compared with the results obtained with an equivalent 1D reference model and a 1D-3D mixed model connected with the 3D_POU option of keyword LIAISON_ELEM operator AFFE_CHAR_MECA.
