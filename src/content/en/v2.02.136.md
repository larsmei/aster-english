# v2.02.136 SDLL136 - Earthquake portal

**Summary:**

This test case of earthquake portal allows to check the modal combination method
CQC signed (`CQC_SIGNE`), developed in COMB_SISM_MODAL.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The structure studied is a simple portal of $L = 4\,\mathrm{m}$ width and $H = 2\,\mathrm{m}$ height (see
v2-01-136_fig_portic).

Both posts and the horizontal beam of the portal have a square cross section on the $a = 0.1\,\mathrm{m}$ side.

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.136/v2-01-136_geo_portique.png)

*Geometry of the portal studied.*

### 1.2 Material properties

The material constituting the gantry is a steel characterised by the following properties:

- Young's modulus : $E=2.1 \times {10}^{11}\, \mathrm{Pa}$
- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$
- Density: $\rho =7800 \, \mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 boundary conditions

$A$ and $D$ points are clamped $\left( DX = DY =DZ=0,\; DRX=DRY=DRZ =0\right)$.

#### 1.3.2 Loading

The structure is considered to be mono-support and subject to the seismic tri-directional loading with spectra
in figure v2-01-136_fig_sro_enter:

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.136/v2_01_136_input.png)

*Oscillator Response Spectrometer for Seismic Loadings*

The reduced depreciation rate is 2%.

## 2 reference solution

The reference results are calculated using the Civil Master tool.

In this tool, for each direction, the signs of the mode showing
the highest effective modal mass is retained for assignment to
the corresponding directional response.

As a result, the methods used for signing the results are:
- Direction X: mode 2
- Direction Y: mode 1
- Direction Z: mode 4

The results are compared with those obtained by Code_Aster.

The quantities of interest are the 24 components of the Newmark combination
of the response on the move in the middle of the portal (point E in figure
v2-01-136_fig_portic) in three directions X, Y, Z.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The portal is modelled using the straight beam elements Timoshenko (`POU_D_T`).

The mesh is composed of 40 elements linear `SEG2` type: 20 elements in the horizontal beam
and 10 elements in each vertical pole.

The characteristics of the material and the section are given in the
v2.01.136-problem_ref **Reference problem**.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

The 24 components of the Newmark combination
