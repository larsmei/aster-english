# v6.04.269 SSNV269 – Law validationRGI_BETON_BA: Cyclical loading of a concrete test piece

**Summary:**

This document presents a test to validate the capabilities of the constitutive law RGI_BETON_BA. It should be noted that this law allows to consider the phenomena of internal swelling reaction (RGI), creep, damage and distributed frames. The behaviors of a free and armed test piece uniformly under cyclic loading in single tensile are simulated.

## 1 Reference problem

As the validation of the distributed frames is the main object of this document, creep and RGI will not be considered here (Nulth of the swelling parameters, important values of characteristic creep times).

In order to be a case of validation, this study examines the stress of a concrete cube reinforced by a density of reinforcement of ${\rho}^{r}=1,0\text{\%}$. The cube is loaded in its armed direction (according to $\vec{x}$), first in tensile and then in compression. Furthermore, in order to define a complete validation process, the study is also restarted for a case using 5 density of reinforcement, the maximum density of reinforcement that can be considered. The latter is armed in the same quantity ( ${\rho}^{r}=1,0\text{\%}$) in the three orthogonal directions and holds in addition two prestressed densities ( ${\sigma}_{0}^{r}=0,1\mathit{MPa}$ ) in the vertical and horizontal direction (according to $\vec{z}$ and $\vec{x}$ ).
