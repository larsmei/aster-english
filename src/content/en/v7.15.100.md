# v7.15.100 FORMA02 - Practical work of the "Initiation" training: thermomechanical and dynamically-sold pipe

**Summary:**

This test corresponds to the practical work of the basic training when using *Code_Aster* . It is a bent pipe, consisting of a linear elastic material, subjected to various loads: internal pressure, thermal transient, transient force.

The modellings used are:

- Modelling A: 3D modelling, thermomechanical calculation,
- Modelling B: 3D modelling, dynamic calculation, modal and transient,
- Modelling C: elements DKT hulls, mechanical calculation under internal pressure loading,
- D Modelling: elements beams (POU_D_T), dynamic calculation.

Chapter 1 "Reference problem" presents the problem to be processed and the data common to all modellings; the statements of the Training Practical Work are included in this document:

- TP2: "3D thermo-elastic" see model A,
- TP2:"3D dynamic" see model B.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The study concerns a pipe with two straight pipes and one elbow [Figure 1.1-a].

The geometrical data of the problem are as follows:

- the ${L}_{G}$ length of both straight pipes is $3m$ ,
- the $\mathit{Rc}$ radius of the elbow is $0.6m$ ,
- $\theta$ angle of the elbow is $90°$ ,
- $\text{e}$ thickness of straight pipes and elbow is $0.02m$ ,
- and the $\text{Re}$ outer radius of the straight pipes and elbow is $0.2m$ .

![Figure](/figures/v7.15.100/Object_1.svg)

Figure 1.1-a

**Note:**

*The geometry of the problem shows a symmetry with the plane* $(A,X,Y)$ *.*

### 1.2 Material properties

For all modellings A, B, C and D:

Isotropic elastic material. Material properties are those of $\mathrm{A42}$ steel to $20°C$:

- le Young's modulus $E=204000.\times {10}^{+6}N/{m}^{2}$ ,
- le Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$ .

For thermo-elastic calculation (modelization A):

- the thermal expansion coefficient $\alpha =1.096\times {10}^{–5}/°C$ ,
- $\lambda =54.6W/m°C$ thermal conduction,
- $\rho \mathit{Cp}=3.71\times {10}^{6}J/{m}^{3}°C$ volumetric heat,

For dynamic calculation (modellings B, D):

- $\rho =7800\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ density ,
- The mode damping will be taken at $\text{5\%}$ for modes.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The boundary conditions for all modellings are as follows:

- clamped support at $A$ section level,
- when loading static clamped support at $A$ section and $B$ section .

For static calculations, the loads applied are of three types:

- internal pressure (on the inner side) $P={15.10}^{+6}N/{m}^{2}$ (shell Modelling or 3D),
- thermomechanical loading with a temperature transient imposed on the inner surface of the piping (mounting from $20°C$ to $70°C$ in $\mathrm{10s}$) and a zero exchange condition on the outer surface of the piping (calori-refuge) (modelling A only).

For dynamic calculation, the load applied is a transient force (in Newton):

$\mathit{FY}(t)=10000000.\sin(2\pi \mathit{Freq}1.t)$ directed on $Y$ axis and applied on $B$ section with $\mathit{Freq}1=20\mathit{Hz}$ .

### 3.4 Creation and launch of the calculation case (via AsterStudy)

Launch the AsterStudy module of the Salome-Meca platform.

Then in the left column, click on the Case View tab, right click CurrentCase to add a new internship where you set commands of the calculation case.

Note: add commands from the Commands→ Show All menu.

The study requires a first transient thermal analysis calculation followed by a mechanical calculation.

The main steps for creating and launching the calculation case are:

- Read mesh in MED format: LIRE_MAILLAGE command.
- Set the material: DEFI_MATERIAU($E,\nu ,\alpha$) command in ELAS, $\lambda ,\rho \mathit{Cp}$ in THER).

*Thermical calculus:*

- Set the finished elements used: AFFE_MODELE command to assign the THERMIQUE phenomenon and 3D Modelling to TOUT.
- Assign material to all: AFFE_MATERIAU command.
- Assign boundary conditions thermal and loading:

  - The temperature transient is imposed on the internal surfaceSurf of the piping. This rise from $20°C$ to $70°C$ to $\mathrm{10s}$ is defined with the DEFI_FONCTION command: set the data in VALE and NOM_PARA = INST.
  - This temperature transient is imposed with the AFFE_CHAR_THER_F command: option TEMP_IMPO, with Overfint as GROUP_MA, and the function defined as the temperature imposed TEMP)
  - The piping is considered to be calorifuged and no conditions are specified on the outside surface (which is a zero flow).
- Set the calculation moments, 0 s, 5 s, 10 s: DEFI_LIST_REEL command.
- Solve the linear thermal problem: THER_LINEAIRE control, by defining an initial uniform temperature state equal to $20°C$ (ETAT_INIT / VALE), and by affecting MODELE, CHAM_MATER (materials), EXCIT (loading), INCREMENT (score list).

* Thermomechanical calculus:*

- Set the finished elements used: AFFE_MODELE command to affect the MECANIQUE phenomenon and 3D Modelling.
- Assign material: AFFE_MATERIAU control. The result of the thermal calculation will be applied to all as variable controlling the behavior of the mechanical material by EVOL in the AFFE_VARC option with reference temperature NOM_VARC = TEMP at 20°.
- Mechanical boundary conditions Assigning and Loading: AFFE_CHAR_MECA Command:

  - The piping is clamped at both ends and has a symmetry plane: DDL_IMPO.
  - Ona an internal surface pressure of $15\mathit{MPa}$: PRES_REP (check the direction of normal to the internal surface in AsterStudy).
- Solve the static linear mechanical problem: MECA_STATIQUE control with the calculation of the above temperature field and the same list of moments as thermal calculation.
- Calculate field: CALC_CHAMP command with drill.

  - for the calculation of the stress field by elements extrapolated to nodes: CONTRAINTE / SIEF_ELNO.
  - for the calculation of the equivalent stress field of Von Bets: CRITERES/ SIEQ_ELNO.
- Print the maximum SIEQ_ELNO stress tensor values in Result format (RESULTAT) and print the temperature field, displacements, and stress fields in MED format: IMPR_RESU command.
- To start the calculation case, in the left column, click on the History View tab.

- Orient normals to elements: MODI_MAILLAGE / ORIE_NORM_COQUE command to steer all elements in the same way, with normal turning inside the pipe (given the pressure sign convention) to give a positive value to the pressure (use the surface group).
- Set Material: DEFI_MATERIAU Command: E, NU, ALPHA
- Assign material: AFFE_MATERIAU control. The mechanical characteristics are identical throughout the structure.
- Assign the features of the elements hulls: AFFE_CARA_ELEM / COQUE command to define the thickness.
- Assign mechanical boundary conditions and loads: AFFE_CHAR_MECA command:

  - There is a clamped support on the mesh-cell group $\mathrm{Base}$ and $\mathrm{Efond}$ , and symmetry conditions (normal displacement $\mathrm{DZ}$ null and $\mathrm{DRX}$ and $\mathrm{DRY}$ null rotations) on the mesh-cell group $\mathrm{Symetrie}$ : DDL_IMPO.
  - Internal pressure $P$: PRES_REP. The P pressure at the internal surface must be converted to the pressure at the average surface.
- Solve elastic problem: MECA_STATIQUE control: CARA_ELEM, CHAM_MATER, EXCIT, MODELE.
- Print displacements and constraints to MED format: IMPR_RESU command..
- To start the calculation case, in the left column, click on the History View tab.

## 6 Modelling D

### 6.1 Geometry

Geometry can be created by defining $A,C,D,B$ points, then the two $\mathrm{AC}$ and $\mathrm{DB}$ lines, and the $\mathrm{CD}$ circle arch. This can be done in several ways:

- By defining the coordinates of each point, including the point mid-arc $\mathit{CD}$ (Menu New Entity → Basic → ...);
- Simpler using the "Sketcher" tool (Menu New Entity → Basic→2D Sketch).

After building the entire line, you have to define the groups under this "Wire": it will be enough for these practical works to create the groups $A$ , $B$ , $\mathrm{AC}$ , $\mathrm{CD}$ , and $\mathrm{DB}$ ( Menu New Entity → Group → Create Group ).

### 6.2 Mesh

Launch the Salome-Meca Platform Mesh module.

The mesh is defined by the Mesh → Create Mesh menu. Select the geometry to be meshed, then the algorithm and the dimensional discretization hypothesis: 1D Wire Discretization with the 15 segment hypothesis per edge.

### 6.3 Creation and launch of the calculation case (via AsterStudy)

Launch the AsterStudy module of the Salome-Meca platform.

Then in the left column, click on the Case View tab.

The command file of the calculation case is defined.

Note: add commands from the Commands→ Show All menu.

The study requires a first transient thermal analysis calculation followed by a mechanical calculation.

The main steps for creating and launching the calculation case are:

- Read mesh in MED format: LIRE_MAILLAGE command.
- Set the finished elements used: AFFE_MODELE command.
- Set the material: DEFI_MATERIAU command.
- Assign Material: AFFE_MATERIAU command.
- Assign the features of elements beams: AFFE_CARA_ELEM command.
- Assign mechanical boundary conditions and loading: AFFE_CHAR_MECA/ Force DOF (DDL_IMPO) command. The point $A$ is clamped.
- Set the elastic problem matrices: HASSING/MASS_MECA and RIGI_MECA.
- Calculate the first 5 eigenmodes: CALC_MODES command.
- Print MED-specific modes: IMPR_RESU-command. The mesh and the modes will be printed.

To start the calculation case, in the left column, click on the History View tab.

*Transient analysis:*

- Construction of the point force

      - Assign the load "force to point $B$": Command AFFE_CHAR_MECA/ FORCE_NODALE.
      - Calculate the elementary force vectors: CALC_VECT_ELEM command.
      - Build a field at nodes by joining the force vector: ASSE_VECTEUR command.
      - Define the time evolution function: FORM CONTROL.

- Modal base transitory

1. Projection of the problem assembled on the basis of the eigenmodes: PROJ_BASE command.
2. Calculate the transient by modal recombination: DYNA_VIBRA command.
3. Extract $Y$ displacements in $B$: RECU_FONCTION command.
4. Print these functions in TABLEAU format: IMPR_FONCTION command.
