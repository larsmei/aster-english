**Summary:**

This test does not validate new Modelling. It is calibrated to operate on 1 (Modelling b) and 4 (Modelling a).

This is a computer and functional test of the various parallelism levels available in INFO_MODE and CALC_MODES+OPTION='BANDE' operators with subband cutting.

The sequential version (modelization b) serves as a reference.

## 1 Reference problem

This test is based on the first CALC_MODES+OPTION='BANDE' calculation of the test case fdlv112b ($N=5229$ degrees of freedom). 50 modes in 2, 3 or 4 subbands are searched for depending on the case of figures. Each modal search is pre-calibrated by calls to INFO_MODE parallel.

This test is dedicated to the computer and functional validation of parallel calculations diagrams of CALC_MODES+OPTION='BANDE' with subband cutting, and INFO_MODE.

The validity of many possible parallel configurations is tested by playing on:

- The activated parallelism level(s) (if Modelling a),
- MPI process distribution by sub-bands (if Modelling a),
- The Sturm post-verification criterion (if CALC_MODES),
- The combinatorium of numerical parameters (direct modal and/or linear solvers).

The calculation is launched on 4 processors (if Modelling a) and 1 processor (if Modelling b).
