# v1.01.269 PYNL02 - STAT_NON_LINE in split commands for elasto-plastic calculation

**Summary:**

This document is intended to validate the methodology for solving a nonlinear problem (here a problem of elasto-plasticity) in broken commands, without using the STAT_NON_LINE command.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

We consider a unitary cube.

### 1.2 Material properties

A material with a Von constitutive law Mixed-nuting (VMIS_ECMI_TRAC) bets is considered.

The elastic properties are as follows:

- Young's modulus: $E=221300\mathrm{MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0,3$

The Prager constant is $C=2200\mathrm{MPa}$ .

The traction curve considered is given on the .

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.269/1000000000000CE4000009F61350709DA581F139.png)

Figure 1.2-1: Courbe de traction

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The lower face (in the $z=0$ plane) is clamped.

The upper face (in the $z=1$ plane) is subjected to a vertical $\mathrm{dz}={10}^{-2}[m]$ displacement.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is obtained by numerical resolution of the problem using the STAT_NON_LINE command. This gives the constraint field and the field of internal variables.

The charge increment is discretized in 2 moments between 0 and 1.

An elastic prediction is made, and the tangential matrix is re-re-actualized with each iteration and increment.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

In this Modelling, the STAT_NON_LINE command is replaced by broken commands. boundary conditions are applied by dualization (Lagranges)

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh is composed of a single mesh cell HEXA8.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The difference between the stress field (respectively internal variables) calculated by STAT_NON_LINE and the CALCUL command, at last moment, is tested.

## 4 Summary of the results

This test validated the methodology for solving a nonlinear problem (here a problem of elasto-plasticity) in broken commands, without using the STAT_NON_LINE command.

In this test, the scope of the methodology is limited:

- no external forces (loading imposed via Lagranges)
- no temperature loading (control variables)
- no pilotage,
- no linear search,
- no contact,
- the NUME_DDL does not change during the time step (notably, the nodes whose displacement is imposed does not vary).
