# v6.01.106 SSNA106 - Hollow cylinder subjected to thermoviscoelastic behaviour

**Summary:**

This test case validates the LEMAITRE law implanted in *Code_Aster* in the case of linear thermoviscoelastic behavior. The results found are compared to an analytical solution.

### 1.1 Geometry

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus: $E=1\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.3$

Dilation coefficient: $\alpha =0.7$

Law of LEMAITRE:

$g(\sigma ,\lambda ,T)={(\frac{1}{K}\frac{\sigma}{{\lambda}^{\frac{1}{m}}})}^{n}$ with $\frac{1}{K}=1$ , $\frac{1}{m}=0$ , $n=1$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

**boundary conditions :**

The cylinder is blocked in $\mathit{DY}$ on the sides $[\mathit{AB}]$ and $[\mathit{CD}]$ .

**Charge:**

The cylinder is subjected to a temperature field $T(r,t)=t{r}^{2}$
