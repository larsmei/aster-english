# v2.04.132 SDLV132 - Substructured consideration of a generalized mass in a tree line modal calculation

**Summary:**

This test validates the substructured consideration of a generalized mass for tree line calculation.

In this test, a constant circular cross section rotor model is based on a massive through bearings considered infinitely rigid. This example is taken from the CADYRO Qualification Manual, a finished elements software designed to model dynamic rotor behaviour.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The structure consists of a rotor of L2 length and circular cross section, two infinitely rigid bearings and a mass of circular cross sections.

### 1.3 boundary conditions

The rotor bearings are based on the massif via restraints considered to be infinitely rigid. The four feet of the massif are clamped. The rotor and the massif are therefore perfectly coupled to the nodes bearings, according to the CRAIG-BAMPTON method.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The infinitely rigid bearings and the absence of shaft rotation allow direct calculation with *Code_Aster* of the first modes of the solid-ply-rotor structure which will be used as a reference for the understructured calculation in *Code_Aster*.

The validation of the substructured consideration of a generalized mass in a tree line calculation in *Code_Aster* will be based on a comparison of the eigenfrequencies obtained by direct calculation and by a substructured calculation ( Craig-Bampton-type substructure).

The validation of each of the rotor-paliers and massive substructures will also be pursued separately.

In addition to the comparison between the global calculation and the substructured calculation in *Code_Aster* , the results are also valid compared to those of CADYRO [1].

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

*Code_Aster* results give both mode frequencies and modal deformities. Only frequencies are actually tested.

## 5 Summary of the results

This test case allows numerical validation of the integration of a generalized tree line mass by a substructured calculation. The results obtained are in good agreement with the reference values, which are derived from the CADYRO tree line code qualification manual.
