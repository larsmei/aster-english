# v4.02.100 TPLL100 - steady-state thermal analysis anisotropic wall

**Summary:**

This test, which concerns the stationary and transient linear thermal, is intended to validate Cartesian anisotropy.

Two modellings are made:

- a first volume,
- A second one in the plan.

The results obtained are in perfect agreement with the analytical values.

### 1.2 Material properties

Anisotropic material, preferred direction according to the axes of the $({X}_{1,}{Y}_{1,}{Z}_{1})$ reference:

$\begin{array}{ccc}{\lambda}_{X}=1W/m°C& {\lambda}_{Y}=0.5W/m°C& {\lambda}_{L}=2W/m°C\\ \rho {C}_{P}=2J/{m}^{3}°C& & \end{array}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

$\mathrm{FEJK}$ face: $\mathrm{400 }W/{m}^{2}$ output stream.

$\mathrm{CDIH}$ face: Inward $\mathrm{400 }W/{m}^{2}$ flow.

$\mathrm{EDIJ}$ face: $\mathrm{1200 }W/\mathrm{m2}$ output stream.

$\mathrm{FCHK}$ face: $100°C$ temperature imposed.

Other side: condition of Neumann.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

To make this stationary calculation, a transitional calculation is made for which boundary conditions are constant over time. This allows testing the elementary calculations of mass and rigidity in the first member and the second member.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

analytical solution.

Temperature varying linearly according to $\mathrm{CD}$

Isotherms parallel to the $\mathrm{CHKF}$ and $\mathrm{DIJE}$ faces.

In the marker: $(\frac{\mathrm{CD}}{\parallel \mathrm{CD}\parallel },\frac{\mathrm{CH}}{\parallel \mathrm{CH}\parallel },\frac{\mathrm{CF}}{\parallel \mathrm{CF}\parallel },)$ , we have:

$\left[\begin{array}{}{\varphi}_{x}\\ {\varphi}_{y}\\ {\varphi}_{z}\end{array}\right]=\left[\begin{array}{}-({\lambda}_{X}{\cos}^{2}\alpha +{\lambda}_{Y}{\sin}^{2}\alpha )\frac{\partial T}{\partial x}\\ -({\lambda}_{X}-{\lambda}_{Y})\cos\alpha \sin\alpha \frac{\partial T}{\partial x}\\ 0\end{array}\right]$

with:

${\varphi}_{x}=1200{\varphi}_{y}=400\alpha =({X}_{1,}\mathrm{CD})$ $T(x)=\frac{-{\varphi}_{x}}{{\lambda}_{{X}_{1}}{\cos}^{2}\alpha +{\lambda}_{Y}{\sin}^{2}\alpha }x+T(A)$

either: $T(x)=-\mathrm{1600.x}+20.$

Si $\beta =(\mathrm{CD},{X}_{0})$ : $\begin{array}{cc}\varphi .{X}_{0}=\cos\beta .{\varphi}_{X}-\sin\beta {\varphi}_{y}& \text{soit}720\\ \varphi .{Y}_{0}=\sin\beta .{\varphi}_{X}+\cos\beta {\varphi}_{L}& \text{soit}720\end{array}$
