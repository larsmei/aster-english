# v2.01.033 SDLD33 - Spectral seismic response of a 2 masses system - 4 multi-support springs by 2 groups decorated with supports

**Summary:**

The problem is to calculate the spectral response of a 2 masses – 4 multi-supported springs system, subjected to multiple seismic excitation, considering the 3 supports as 2 groups decorated with supports.

The discrete traction element, the calculation of eigenmodes, static modes and spectral response by modal superposition are tested via the COMB_SISM_MODAL operator.

Modelling A: This model allows to validate the order of combinations to be considered in the case of the multi-support system i.e. intra-group cumulation and then inter-group cumulation.

Modelling B: This model allows to validate the rules of combinations to be considered in the cumulation of groups decorated with equal intra-group DDS support.

Modelling C: This Modelling allows the validation of spectral modal calculations output options in the case of the correlated multi-support.

The results obtained are in very good agreement with the reference analytical results.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The spectral response is calculated by modal superposition of a 2 masses – 4 springs system subjected to three distinct excitations. The displacement of the masses to the nodes $\mathrm{NO2}$ and $\mathrm{NO4}$ along the $x$ axis is determined.

The following are analytically calculated:

- ${f}_{i}$ eigenfrequencies,
- ${\phi }_{{N}_{i}}$-associated own vectors standardized to modal mass,
- ${\Psi}_{i}$ static support modes of the system,
- ${P}_{ij}$ modal participation factors for support,
- ${\mathrm{Rm}}_{ij}$ the maximum response of each mode from excitation spectra,
- ${R}_{ij}$ the contribution of the drive movement of each support from differential displacements,
