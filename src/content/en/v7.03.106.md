# v7.03.106 HPLV106 – Homogenized parameters of a glass resin-fibre composite with hexagonal cell

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to calculate the equivalent thermoelastic properties of a resin-fibre composite made of hexagonal-step glass. The basic materials of the components are isotropic.

This test is used as a check for the CALC_MATE_HOMO command by comparing the results with the values presented in the Amphi EDF Lab course by Professor Jean-Jaques MARIGO.

In order to use the CALC_MATE_HOMO command, the exercise is done to determine a square step pattern in the hexagonal step (to have a base cell in parallelepiped) and only the positive octant is modeled.

The 3 modellings are 3 different fiber/resin ratios (0.2; 0.4; 0.6).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A heterogeneous resin-fibre composite structure is considered to be replaced by an equivalent homogeneous structure.

The homogenization theory is well adapted in such a case because of the periodicity of glass fibres in the resin. To simplify the problem and thanks to the periodicity hypothesis, a square base cell is given in the hexagonal step () and three different geometries are considered where the ratio of fiber to resin evolves from 0.2 to 0.6.

### 1.2 Material properties

The basic cell consists of resin and fiberglass. The material parameters are as follows:

*Glass fibre:*

$\begin{array}{c}E=70000\mathit{MPa}\hfill \\ \nu =0.22\hfill \\ k=1.0W/(m.°C)\hfill \\ {C}_{p}=0J/(°C.{m}^{3})\hfill \end{array}$

*Resin:*

$\begin{array}{c}E=3500\mathit{MPa}\hfill \\ \nu =0.35\hfill \\ k=1.0W/(m.°C)\hfill \\ {C}_{p}=0J/(°C.{m}^{3})\hfill \end{array}$
