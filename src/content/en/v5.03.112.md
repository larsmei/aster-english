# v5.03.112 SDNV112 - Multi-support seismically-sustained civil engineering building

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the MACR_SPECTRE command in the case of a multi-supported three-dimensional structure.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Geometry is a simplified building with three sails, a floor, a sole and a radier. In addition, a part of the ground is represented.

The elements civil engineering have a thickness of $0,1m$ while the radier and sole have a thickness of $0.5m$ and $0.35m$ respectively. The post has a rectangular cross section of $0.2\times 0.2m$ and the beam has a cross section of $0.15\times 0.325m$. The point anchoring post is named $S$. This point is not in contact with the radier.

![Figure](/figures/v5.03.112/100000000000049400000258C06566DFF2493ABE.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=200000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =\mathrm{7800.Kg}/{m}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The sails of the building are connected to the radier. The latter has a rigid body movement. The node group $\mathit{CDG}$ belongs to the radier. These nodes have a different acceleration depending on the $t$ time in each direction.

The node group $S$ , the end of the post side radier. These nodes have a different acceleration as a function of the $t$ time in each direction.

| node group | Next acceleration x | Next acceleration y | Next acceleration z |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\mathit{CDG}$ | $\cos(60\pi t)$ | $\cos(90\pi t+\pi /3)$ | $20\cos(30\pi t+\pi /4)$ |
| $S$ | $\cos(90\pi t+\pi /3)$ | $20\cos(30\pi t+\pi /4)$ | $\cos(60\pi t)$ |
