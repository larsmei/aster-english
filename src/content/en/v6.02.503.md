# v6.02.503 SSNL503 - Elasto-plastic Ruin of a thin sewn pipe

**Summary:**

This test consists of calculating the elasto-plastic ruin of a thin, bent pipe subjected to bending in its plane and internal pressure with background effect. It allows to validate elements TUYAU_3M finished Modelling (SEG3 and SEG4) and TUYAU_6M (SEG3) in the quasi-static field in non-linear material.

The results obtained are compared with a numerical reference solution obtained with the ABAQUS calculation code.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution was obtained numerically with ABAQUS 5.5 bib1. The mesh used consists of elements ELBOW31 at 2 nodes with 6 modes of Fourier. The discretization used is as follows:

- Part $\mathit{AB}$: 24 elements,
- Part $\mathrm{BC}$: 8 elements,
- $\mathrm{CD}$ part: 12 elements.

Integration into the section is as follows:

- 7 layers in thickness,
- 18 sectors in the circumferential sense.

### 2.2 Baseline

Limit time = $253.4{10}^{3}\mathit{N.m}$ for rotation around $z$ from $0.22\mathit{rad}$ to point $D$ .

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Less than $\text{2\%}$

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

ABAQUS/ StandardVersion 5.5: Example Problems Manual Volume 2, pp 4.2.2-1.

## 6 Summary of the results

![Figure](/figures/v6.02.503/Object_11.svg)

The results obtained for TUYAU (SEG3 and SEG4) modelling are quite far from the reference solution ($\text{20\%}$ error), but they are better for TUYAU_6M ($\text{6\%}$ error).

The deformation of the transverse cross section in the elbow is better represented by TUYAU_6M Modelling, better suited for Modelling thin pipes. In this Modelling, the movements of the average surface of the pipe are broken down in series from Fourier to order 6, instead of 3 for TUYAU Modelling. The reference model uses a series decomposition of Fourier to order 6.
