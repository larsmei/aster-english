# v7.22.135 HSNV135 – Model META_LEMA_ANI: Pressure tube and variable temperature

**Summary:**

This test is a numerical validation of the mechanical META_LEMA_ANI behavior model with effect of metallurgical transformations developed for the material of the fuel pencil sheath, the Zircaloy. It is a tube subjected to internal pressure, with consideration of the background effect and at a uniform temperature and variable over time (so several phases in presence). This test case is identical to the HSNV134 model A, except that here, there is a phase change.

There is no analytical solution. The results are either compared to those obtained by the CALC_META operator or tested in non-regression.
