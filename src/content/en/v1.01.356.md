# v1.01.356 ZZZZ356 - Validation of the RAFF_GP command

**Summary**

This validation test allows you to present a refinement of mesh obtained from the RAFF_GP command.

The starting point is a coarse mesh of a cut-out structure. The finish is a mesh of the same cut-out structure, mesh refined in the calculation area of the mechanical parameter of ${G}_{p}$ breakage.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.

## 1 Reference problem

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.356/10000000000001C3000001BD45AB5C65E891043D.png)

Figure 1.1:

A two-dimensional plane structure is considered to have a cut-off, a square side plate 1 and a cut-off of 0.05 radius inclined at 30° from the horizontal.

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Mesh characteristics

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.356/100000000000052C0000034C282845AC72056625.png)

Figure 2.1: Maillage initial de la modélisation.

A coarse mesh containing 250 nodes is produced.

### 2.2 Operations carried out

The RAFF_GP command is used to refine the mesh downstream of the trait. Four refinement steps are performed.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.356/1000000000000522000003BF280BD1B1E81513EC.png)

Figure 2.2: Maillage obtenu après 4 raffinements.

The mesh final contains 8504 nodes.

### 2.3 Quantity tested and result

The nodes number of the final mesh is tested in three areas more or less close to the bottom of the entialle. This is a non-regression test.

The areas are:

zone 1: radius disk 0.04 centered in (0.58;0.55), very close to the base of the pitch

zone 2: radius disk 0.04 centered in (0.77;0.66), in the chip zone

zone 3: radius disk 0.04 centered in (0.22;0.76), outside the chip zone

The mesh final contains 8504 nodes.
