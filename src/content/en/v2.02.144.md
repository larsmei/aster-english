# v2.02.144 SDLL144 – Speed transparency of a rotating beam with a disc

**Summary:**

This test validates the temporal response to a rotating speed transient of a rotating shaft system. The rotor is full, circular cross section and has a disc. The speed rise laws considered here are linear (modelization A) and exponential (modelization B).

For this test case, the load of the bald type is installed on the disk. The comparison is based on the value of the resonance peaks of the disk movements.

This problem also validates the effect of the damping matrices and stiffness gyroscopic ones that were developed for the straight beams of Timoshenko and Euler.

The results obtained are in good agreement with those given in reference. The references are based on Timoshenko's beam theory.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solutions are those presented in Michel LALANNE and Guy FERRARIS and those obtained with CADYRO, finished elements software to predict dynamic rotor behavior.

The CADYRO numerical results were obtained with elements Timoshenko beam. Modelling is done with 4 nodes (3 elements beams).

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

With a slush load, the values tested are the maximas d'amplitude for the node corresponding to the disc, for two laws of rotation speed climb from 0 to $5000\mathit{tr}/min$ (resonance concept at critical speed).

The methods of access to the values of the generalized coordinates (movements,
(i) the speed and acceleration of the vehicle from a transitional calculation result on a generalised basis, i.e.
`getDisplacementValues`, `getVelocityValues` and `getAccelerationValues` methods. At the end of the transitional calculation, the extraction of values
generalised coordinates are tested indirectly, by comparing the coordinates
on a physical basis (movements, speeds and accelerations) obtained from the operator `REST_GENE_PHYS`
and those obtained from a physical refund entirely made in Python,
using the above-mentioned access methods.

The proper functioning of methods for defining generalized coordinate values, i.e. the
`setDisplacementValues`, `setVelocityValues` and `setAccelerationValues` methods are also tested.

## 5 Summary of the results

The simulation results with CADYRO and Code_Aster (relative errors below 3%) are well matched. However, the spatial basis used in the bibliography only takes into account the first mode of a single-buttoned beam. Code_Aster and CADYRO use a more precise modal basis. The modal recombination method on an incomplete basis is highly sensitive to the truncation effects that tend to stiffen the structure overall. This therefore increases resonance frequencies, which explains that peaks appear for higher rotation speeds and therefore later in time (up to speed here). Therefore, the damping matrices and gyroscopic stiffness are well implanted for the element beam, in the case of calculation of the temporal response on a modal basis for a rate-up transition.
