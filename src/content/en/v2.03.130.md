# v2.03.130 SDLS130 – Natural frequency of a laminated composite plate consisting of 8 folds

**Summary:**

This test validates the eigenfrequency solution of a composite plate composed of 8 folds. The solution obtained by code_aster is compared to a solution from an article.

MODELISATION A: eigenfrequency of a composite plate of 8 folds DKT.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

This is a reference test in the literature to validate the modal analysis of a laminate plate. The literature provides reference results from experimental data and results from other codes such as Nastran3D.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The eigenfrequency solution obtained by code_aster is compared in case there is no delamination.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

In the case where layer delamination does not occur, the reference results vary between [79,2; 82,1] Hz. Therefore, the first eigenfrequency is sought in this range.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

1. Jinho Oh a , Maenghyo Cho, Jun-Sik, Kim G., "Dynamic analysis of composite flat with multiple delaminations based on higher-order zigzag theory", International Journal of Solids and Structures 42 (2005) 6122–6140.
