# v6.04.136 SSNV136 - Triaxial drained test with CJS (level 2)

**Summary**

This test validates the CJS model's level 2. This is a triaxial test in drained condition. The calculations are performed only on the solid part of the soil, without hydromechanical coupling. The containment level is $100\mathrm{kPa}$ .

Because of symmetry, only one third of a 3D sample tested in triaxial testing is interested in the eighth.

This is a non-regression test. Nevertheless, the results obtained with *code_aster* for the CJS2 model are compared with those obtained with a private version of the FLAC -2D software.

### 1.3 Initial conditions, boundary conditions, and loading

**Phase 1:**

The sample is taken to a homogeneous state: $\sigma_{xx}^{0} = \sigma_{yy}^{0} = \sigma_{zz}^{0}$, by imposing the corresponding containment pressure on the front, right lateral and upper side. The movements are blocked on the rear ($u_{x} = 0$), left lateral ($u_{y} = 0$) and lower ($u_{z} = 0$) sides.

**Phase 2:**

The blocked displacements on the rear ($u_{x} = 0$), left ($u_{y} = 0$) and lower ($u_{z} = 0$) sides are maintained, as well as the containment pressure on the front and right side. A displacement is applied to the upper face: $u_{z}(t)$, so that $\varepsilon_{zz} = -20\%$ deformation is obtained (counted from the beginning of Phase 2).
