# 1 **v7.32.129** WTNP129 – HM Modelling of a compressed liquid saturated bar

**Summary:**

The solution is one-dimensional, given the symmetries of the problem being treated, the structure is subjected to hydraulic pressure imposed on its upper part. Its mechanical behaviour is elastic. The purpose of this test is to test the resolution by coupling (see documentation "Notice of use of the THM model" [U2.04.05](doc:u2.04.05))

So we have 3 modellings in this test:

- Modelization A: the physical problem is solved by means of the "classical" method, by global coupling
- Modelling C: This Modelling is identical to Modelling A but with the element sub-integrated HM_SI

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On $\mathrm{HAUT}$ , the $\sigma \cdot n=0$ and $p=3.E6$ Pa conditions are imposed.

On $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$ and $\mathrm{DROITE}$, the ${u}_{x}=0$ conditions and $\mathrm{M.n}=0$ null liquid flow are imposed.

On $\mathrm{BAS}$, the ${u}_{x}={u}_{y}=0$ conditions and $M\cdot n=0$ null liquid flow are imposed.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The initial fluid pressure is taken equal to 2 $\mathrm{MPa}$ . The initial porosity is taken equal to 0.5.

## 2 reference solution

The values of DY, PRE1 and SIYY in 5 nodes ($\mathrm{N4}$ , $\mathrm{N23}$ , $\mathrm{N27}$ , $\mathrm{N31}$ , $\mathrm{N1}$ ) are examined on the right edge of the bar at both $t=1$ dry and $t=10$ seconds.

The tests performed are non-regression tests for Modelling A.

For Modelling C, the tests performed are adhesion tests to Modelling A (type AUTRE_ASTER).
