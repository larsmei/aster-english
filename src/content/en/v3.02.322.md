# v3.02.322 SSLP322 - Propagation of a X-FEM crack in a 3-point bending plate with 3 holes

**Summary**

This test is intended to validate the crack propagation path with $\text{X-FEM}$ in $\mathrm{2D}$ , as part of the linear elasticity.

This test involves a rectangular plate with three holes with a crack opening, and subjected to a 3-point bending.

Modelling is considered:

- $A$ Modelling: mesh method

The values given by the mesh method are the reference values (tests of non-regression).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Geometry, dimensions and materials are taken identical to those of Bittencourt et al. [1] and Ventura et al. [2].

The $\mathrm{2D}$ structure is a rectangular plate ($20\mathrm{mm}\times 8\mathrm{mm}$ ) with 3 holes, with an opening crack (). The length of the initial crack is $a=1,5\mathrm{mm}$ .

The nodes rated $\mathrm{P1}$ , $\mathrm{P2}$ and $\mathrm{P3}$ on it are used to impose the boundary conditions, which are explained in paragraph [§ 1.3 ].

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.322/100000000000037B000001D1FEB09A073CEAB243.jpg)

Figure 1.1-1: géométrie de la plaque fissurée

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus:                $E=205000\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio:                $\nu =0,3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

In order to block rigid modes, the nodes $\mathrm{P1}$ and $\mathrm{P2}$ movements are blocked as follows:

    - ${\mathrm{DY}}^{\mathrm{P1}}={\mathrm{DY}}^{\mathrm{P2}}=0$ ;
    - ${\mathrm{DX}}^{\mathrm{P1}}=0.$

To simulate fatigue propagation, a unit nodal force is applied in $\mathrm{P3}$: $\mathit{FY}=-1$ . A loading cycle will correspond to: zero loading → max loading→ zero loading. Simulate 35 no propagation. At each propagation step, the crack advances by an imposed length of 0.1 m.

### 1.4 reference solution

Due to the lack of precision in the diagrams in Article [1] , it is not possible to derive precise numerical values from this . It is merely verified that cracking paths have the same appearance (see § 2.3 ).

For the test, the values of stress intensity factors ${K}_{I}$ and ${K}_{\mathrm{II}}$ calculated by the A model at the end of the propagation shall be used as a reference:

$\begin{array}{c}{K}_{I}^{\mathit{ref}}=1,142045{\mathit{MPa.mm}}^{0,5}\\ {K}_{\mathit{II}}^{\mathit{ref}}=-0,057097{\mathit{MPa.mm}}^{0,5}\end{array}$

### 1.5 Bibliographical references

  1. Ventura, J.X. Xu, T. Belytschko, A vector level set method and new discontinuity approximations for crack growth by EFG, *International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering* , vol. 54, pp. 923–944, 2002

## 2 Modelling A: mesh method

In this Modelling, mesh operator PROPA_FISS method is used for crack propagation.

### 2.1 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by 7551 TRIA3. The crack is not meshed.

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.322/1000000000000690000003EAA6ED1D434BC5C594.png)

Figure 2.1-1: maillage de la structure

The size of mesh cells in the refined area is about 0.05 mm.

### 2.2 Tested quantities and results

The ${K}_{I}$ and ${K}_{\mathit{II}}$ stress intensity factors are tested after the last propagation step, given by CALC_G. The integration crowns are R_INF = 0.1 mm and R_SUP = 0.2 mm.
