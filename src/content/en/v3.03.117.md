# v3.03.117 SSLS117 - Non-symmetric plate excentering

**Summary:**

This test validates the excentre of single plates not symmetrical with the plane of the mesh or plane of the depur (keyword EXCENTREMENT of the AFFE_CARA_ELEM command).

The reference is given by a first resolution in which a two-layered layer of two layers of different thicknesses and two materials is modelled.

It is used to validate a second calculation where two layers are modelled out of the mesh plane.

It differs from the SSLS111 test only in that the 2 layers are different thicknesses.

Four modellings implement the elements DKT, DKQ.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The $\mathit{A1A4}$ edge is clamped.

A distributed force ${F}_{z}=–1000N/m$ is applied to the $\mathit{A2A3}$ edge.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The calculation with the two-layer material (DEFI_COMPOSITE command) is used as a reference. The non-regression in relation to the results obtained by this first calculation is verified. The two plates of the second model are out of the middle plane of the two-layer.

### 2.2 Baseline

#### 2.2.1 modellings A et B

They consist of the $\mathrm{DZ}$ displacement field values at $\mathrm{A3}$ point and the generalised node $\mathrm{A1}$ forces. On the other hand, the 4 smallest frequencies of the structure are calculated.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

For modellings $A$ and $B$ , the reference solution is analytical, so there is no uncertainty.
