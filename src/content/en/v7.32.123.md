# v7.32.123 WTN123 - Phase Appearance/Disappearance in a Diphasic Flow: Gas Injection Around a Gallery in a Saturated Domain

**Summary:**

This test represents the simulation of gas injection into a geological environment. It involves Modelling and simulating the appearance and evolution of a diphasic water/hydrogen flow into a porous medium initially saturated with pure water. A situation $\mathrm{2D}$ is considered where the effects of gravity are neglected.

This is a purely miscible hydraulic calculation. The geometry represented corresponds to a square domain from which an area has been removed. The transfer terms are described by a model of Mualem Van-Genuchten. The problem is dealt with by the various diagrams available for the modelling of diphasic flows: the classic finished elementss and the Decented Arest Volumes.

This test case is an extension of $\mathrm{2D}$ to test case WTNP120.

The saturation and permeability curves follow the Mualem-Van-Genuchten (HYDR_VGM) model. The $n,\mathrm{Pr},\mathrm{Sr},\mathrm{Smax}$ parameters must therefore be defined in the materials.

It is recalled that these models are: ${S}_{le}=\frac{{S}_{l}-{S}_{\mathrm{lr}}}{1-{S}_{\mathrm{lr}}}$ and $m=1-\frac{1}{n}$

${S}_{\mathrm{we}}=\frac{1}{{\left[1+{(\frac{{P}_{c}}{{P}_{r}})}^{n}\right]}^{m}}$

Water permeability is expressed by integrating the prediction model proposed by Mualem (1976) into the Van Genuchten capillary model.

${k}_{r}^{l}=\sqrt{{S}_{le}}{(1-{(1-{S}_{le}^{\frac{1}{m}})}^{m})}^{2}$

Gas permeability is similarly formulated by Parker's law:

${k}_{r}^{g}=\sqrt{(1-{S}_{le})}{(1-{S}_{le}^{\frac{1}{m}})}^{2m}$

It is recalled that for $S>\mathrm{Smax}$ , these curves are interpolated by a polynomial of degree 2 $\mathrm{C1}$ in $\mathrm{Smax}$ .

For Modelling E, Parker's permeability law for gas is replaced by a cubic law (me 'HYDR_VGC'):

> ${k}_{r}^{g}={(1-{S}_{l})}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and initials

The limit conditions are as follows:

- Neumann conditions on the right and left of the domain:

$({F}_{l}^{w}+{F}_{g}^{w}).n=0$

$({F}_{l}^{c}+{F}_{g}^{c}).n=0$

- Neumann conditions in $\tau$ hole:

      - If $0<t<T$ then $({F}_{l}^{w}+{F}_{g}^{w}).n=0$

If $0<t<T$ then $({F}_{l}^{c}+{F}_{g}^{c}).n=Q$

If $T<t<T$ then $({F}_{l}^{c}+{F}_{g}^{c}).n=0$

- Dirichlet's condition on the upper right side of the estate:

      - ${P}_{l}(9\le x\le 10,y=10,t)={10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$

${P}_{g}(9\le x\le 10,y=10,t)=0\mathit{Pa}$

The initial conditions are as follows:

${P}_{l}(x,y,t=0)={10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$

${P}_{g}(x,y,t=0)=0\mathit{Pa}$

The hydrogen flow imposed on the left side, $Q$ , is:

$Q=0,44{.10}^{\text{-11}}{\mathrm{kg.m}}^{\text{-2}}.{s}^{\text{-1}}$

The injection time, $\mathrm{TINJ}$ is ${5.10}^{5}$ years and the simulation time is ${10}^{6}$ years.

### 2.3 Tested quantities and results

The gas pressure and capillary pressure profiles are drawn at the bottom of the domain at different times:

![Figure](/figures/v7.32.123/100002000000024D0000014A66C0D87C55A1FA1F.png)

The gas pressure is gradually increased by dissolution (when the medium is saturated, i.e. when the capillary pressures are negative) and then by gas as the deaturation begins after 50 years. The medium then deaturates over its entire length. After the injection time, the medium is resaturated and the gas pressure decreases. These results are in line with those expected.

This test case has no reference value, so it is a case of non-regression.

## 5 Summary of the results

This test case allows us to have a classic problem of digital underground storage modelling: gas injection into a $\mathrm{2D}$ saturated medium. We do not have any reference solutions to compare with, however, the values and the look of the results are classic of this type of problem. We therefore make it an test case of non-regression. This test is processed with the 2 numerical schemas available for diphasic flow modelling:

- the diagram of the finite volumes decentralized ridge.
- The classic elements finishes.

The results obtained are the same. In terms of performance and reliability, the final Arest-decented Volumes (* _HH2SUDA) are strongly preferred.
