# v6.02.126 SSNL126 - Elastoplastic flame of an straight beam

**Summary:**

A circular cross section slender straight beam is subjected to a compression force at one end, and is clamped at the other end. The material's behaviour is elastoplastic, with linear isotropic hardening. During the load climb, the critical loads of elastic flaming are calculated, then plastic.

Two modellingss test the elastoplasticity flaming criterion:

Modelization A: mesh volume, small deformations and small displacements.

Modelization B: mesh volume, large displacements and large deformations, but small rotations (GREEN).

Modelization C: mesh 1D, small deformations and small displacements.

Modelling D: mesh 1D, large displacements and large rotations, but small deformations (GROT_GDEP).

## 1 Reference problem

![Figure](/figures/v6.02.126/Shape1.gif)

### 1.1 Geometry

Straight beam, $L=\mathrm{1m}$ long.

$R=\mathrm{0.01m}$ circular section.

### 1.2 Material properties

Elastoplastic material with isotropic linear nutring:

Young's modulus : $E=210000\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio: $\nu = 0$ (an Euler-Bernoulli beam hypothesis)

Elasticity limit: ${\sigma}_{Y}=4\mathrm{MPa}$

Tangent module: ${E}_{T}=70000\mathrm{MPa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- Boundary conditions: clamped support on the whole base surface
- Overfaced force on the upper side: $t=\mathrm{1s}$ , $F=6.5\mathrm{MPa}$

## 6 Modelling D

### 6.1 Modelling characteristics

Mesh 1D with 'MULTIFIBRE' Modelling. 'GROT_GDEP' deformation. Large displacements and rotation (but small rotations).

Mesh 2D section, used by DEFI_GEOM_FIBRE.

The applied nodal force is $–6283.185{N}$ to $t=\mathrm{1s}$, which corresponds to $–20{MPa}$ on the section. This force allows the critical point to pass through the load evolution.

This load is applied in 100 time step equidistant.

Two complete calculations are performed: one with a purely elastic behavior, so that the result can be compared with the elastic reference solution, and the other with an elastoplastic behavior.

### 6.2 Mesh characteristics

Mesh 1D : Number of nodes : 201, Number of mesh cells and types : 200 SEG2

Mesh 2D section: nodes number: 53, mesh cells number and types: 80 TRIA3

## 7 Summary of the results

The modellings results in small shifts (A and C) are consistent with the analytical reference (less than 2% d的difference plasticity). Large shifts (modellings B and D) cannot be compared with an reference solution, but the change in critical coefficient sign is consistent with the expected solution.
