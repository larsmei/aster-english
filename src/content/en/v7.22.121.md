# v7.22.121 HSNV121 - Traction in plastic deformations of a bar under thermal loading

**Summary:**

This quasi-static thermomechanical test consists of uniformly heating a rectangular ($\mathrm{3D}$) or cylindrical ($\mathrm{2D}$ axisymmetric) crossbar and then tensile testing. This validates the kinematics of the large plastic deformations (command STAT_NON_LINE, keyword DEFORMATION: 'SIMO_MIEHE') for a behavioral relationship in large deformations with linear isotropic nutring (command STAT_NON_LINE, keyword RELATION: 'VMIS_ISOT_LINE' and 'VMIS_ISOT_TRAC') with thermomechanical loading.
For plates, large plastic deformations are not available, so the test is run in small deformations (DEFORMATION = 'PETIT').
With COQUE_3D Modelling, large plastic deformations are accessible with keyword DEFORMATION: 'GROT_GDEP' provided that rotations remain low.
However, this possibility is not satisfactory at present, so the calculation is also started in small deformations.

The bar is modelled by element volume (HEXA20, A Modelling) or quadrangular (QUAD4, axisymmetric Modelling, B Modelling) or by elements plate or hull (DKT for C Modelling and COQUE_3D for D Modelling).

The solution is analytical.

The reference results shall be the displacements, the stress of Cauchy $\sigma$ and the cumulative plastic deformation $p$ .

**On time** $t=\mathrm{2 }s$ ($\Delta T=100°C$ , traction $u$ )

$\tau =1500\mathit{Mpa}$ (à $T=120°C$ )

- $p=0,2475$
- ${G}^{p}=0,609$
- $\stackrel{ˉ}{F}=1,289$
- $F=1,303$
- $\tilde{u}=303\mathit{mm}$
- $\tilde{v}=–110\mathit{mm}$

## 9 Summary of the results

Results found with *Code_Aster* and DEFORMATION: 'SIMO_MIEHE' are very satisfactory with error percentages below $\text{0.4\%}$ on stress and $\text{1.2\%}$ on the nutrishing variable.
For elements plate and hull use of DEFORMATION: 'PETIT' gives results quite far from the reference values.
