# v2.02.311 SDLL311 - Transient dynamic response of a beam in traction under pressure

**Summary:**

This test problem corresponds to a linear transient analysis of a pressured pull bar by applying a forced displacement at one end, the other end being clamped. The time-dependent displacement is of the "Heaviside" type imposed from the initial moment.

The mid-beam results for a four-elements model are compared with the analytical solution of the four-elements-discretized problem by not taking into account the instantaneous peaks of speed and acceleration at the initial instant at the end where displacement is imposed.

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The discretized problem checks:

with $l$ index: degree of freedom free

$d$ index: degree of freedom imposed

${F}_{d}(t)$ external loads applied to nodes ends and leading to ${u}_{d}$ imposed displacements are unknown, so these equations are eliminated and we obtain:

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.311/Object_13.svg)

.

The only non-zero terms of the second member of this system are related to the kinematic variables relative to the node end where displacement is imposed. However, at $t=0$ , $\ddot{{u}_{\mathit{dC}}}$ and $\dot{{u}_{\mathit{dC}}}$ are not defined but to $t={0}^{-}$ and $t={0}^{+}$ , $\ddot{{u}_{\mathit{dC}}}$ and $\dot{{u}_{\mathit{dC}}}$ are null. All the complexity of the problem comes from this.

To obtain an reference solution, we considered $\ddot{{u}_{\mathit{dC}}}$ and $\dot{{u}_{\mathit{dC}}}$ uniformly zero, which amounts to considering only the internal elastic forces at the $C$ end. This is questionable for a physical point but, by adopting the same assumptions when Modelling the problem, the validation of *Code_Aster* can be completed.

The reference solution is calculated by addressing the following problem:

$[{M}_{ll}]\lbrace  \ddot{{u}_{l}}\rbrace  +[{C}_{ll}]\lbrace  \dot{{u}_{l}}\rbrace  +[{K}_{ll}]\lbrace  {u}_{l}\rbrace  =-[{K}_{\mathit{ld}}]\lbrace  {u}_{d}(t)\rbrace$ with $\lbrace  {u}_{l}(0)\rbrace  =0$ and $\lbrace  \dot{{u}_{l}}(0)\rbrace  =0$ .

To do this, the problem is carried to the modal base of the system which checks:

$[{M}_{ll}]\lbrace  \ddot{{u}_{l}}\rbrace  +[{K}_{ll}]\lbrace  {u}_{l}\rbrace  =0$ .

The damping being diagonal, we obtain the diagonal system:

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.311/Object_19.svg)

,

with $\lbrace  X(0)\rbrace  =0$ and $\lbrace  \dot{X}(0)\rbrace  =0$ .

In the modal space, three differential equations (3 degrees of freedom free) of the second order are solved and then returned to the physical space. The point mid displacement is obtained:

${u}_{B}(t)=\sum_{i+1}^{3}{e}^{-\lambda it}({a}_{i}\cos(\tilde{{\omega}_{i}}t)+{b}_{i}\sin(\tilde{{\omega}_{i}}t))$ ,

with $\tilde{{\omega}_{i}}$: $i$ pseudopulsation proper to the damped system.

### 2.2 Baseline

Moving, speed and acceleration of point mid $B$ of the beam.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Analytical solution of the problem discretized in four elements of equal length considering uniformly zero speed and acceleration at the point $C$ where displacement is imposed.

## 5 Summary of the results

The results given by *Code_Aster* are in perfect agreement with the results of the analytical model, whether the beam end displacement is imposed by an VECTEUR ASSEMBLE or by an CHARGE.

**Warning:** Dirichlet's questions for the physical-based transition calculation with DYNA_VIBRA are only compatible with the NEWMARK integration method.
