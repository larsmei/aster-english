# v1.01.147 ZZZZ147 – Validation of POST_COMBINAISON operation on simple geometry

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to validate the correct functioning of POST_COMBINAISON on a simple geometry incorporating different types of d-element. It validates a correction to the management of the lagranges.

## 1 Presentation of the test case

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry, realized under CIVIL_MASTER, is on 3 levels. It consists of 6 sails, 3 floors, 4 columns and an opening located in a sail. It is illustrated by the following figure:

### 1.3 Material & Geometric Properties

Concrete material is applied to the whole structure.

- The Young $E=30000\mathit{MPa}$ module
- Le Poisson's ratio $\nu =0,2$

The thicknesses of the sails and floors are $200\mathit{mm}$ and the beams have a square cross section on the side $200\mathit{mm}$.

### 1.4 Loading

The load is:

- The base is clamped.
- The weight is applied throughout the structure,
- $F=-10\mathit{kN}/m\mathrm{²}$ over-fact pressure is applied to the roof of the structure and to a lower sail (Fig 1).

Two load cases are defined and correspond respectively to Weight (Cas G) and Pressure (Cas Q). Then POST_COMBINAISON is used to sum up the loads.

### 1.5 Validation

The node displacement of the intermediate floor is extracted following the calculations of the load cases and then POST_COMBINAISON by ensuring that the sum of the load cases (Case G : $\mathit{DZ}=-0,0107m$ , Case Q $\mathit{DZ}=-0,0153m$ ) is the displacement from POST_COMBINAISON $\mathit{DZ}=-0,026m$ .
