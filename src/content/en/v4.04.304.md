# v4.04.304 TPLV304 - Temperature distribution in a square cross-section bar

**Summary:**

This test is based on independent validation of version 3 in linear steady-state thermal analysis.

The objective of the project is to validate the elements thermal volume under convection and temperature conditions.

The reference solution is based on an analytical approach.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v4.04.304/Object_1.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

$$
\lambda =43.2675W/\mathrm{m.}°C
$$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- temperature imposed on the $y=0$ ${T}_{w}=37.78°C$ face,
- $\varphi =0$ on the $y=L$ side,
- convection on other sides:

  - $h=5.678W/{m}^{2}°C$ ,
  - ${T}_{a}=-17.780°C$ .

Not applicable.

### 3.4 Remarks

Volumetric heat $\rho {C}_{P}$ is not involved in this test, but must be declared for *Code_Aster* . We take $\rho {C}_{P}=1.0J/{m}^{\mathrm{3 }}°C$ .

The $\varphi =0.$ boundary condition is implicit on the free edges.

The small differences that remain correspond to a temperature gradient in the observed section, which is consistent with the modelled physical phenomenon.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results obtained are very satisfactory, the difference maximum is $-0.166\text{\%}$. The main interest of this test is to check the mesh cell HEXA27.
