## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Modelling A

The analysis is to determine the CROSSLAND criterion and the DANG VAN-PAPADOPOULOS criterion in a point structure subject to radial periodic multiaxial loading.

**CROSSLAND criteria:**

${\tau}_{a}+a{P}_{max}-b\le 0$ où:

$\begin{array}{cc}{\tau}_{a}=\frac{1}{2}\underset{0\le {t}_{0}\le T}{\mathit{Max}}\underset{0\le {t}_{1}\le T}{\mathit{Max}}∥\tilde{s}({t}_{1})-\tilde{s}({t}_{0})∥=& \frac{1}{2}\underset{0\le {t}_{0}\le T}{\mathit{Max}}\underset{0\le {t}_{1}\le T}{\mathit{Max}}\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}({\tilde{s}}_{11}^{2}+{\tilde{s}}_{22}^{2}+{\tilde{s}}_{33}^{2}+2{\tilde{s}}_{12}^{2}+2{\tilde{s}}_{13}^{2}+2{\tilde{s}}_{23}^{2})}\\ \text{amplitude de scission}& \text{avec}\tilde{s}\text{déviateur du tenseur des contraintes}\sigma \end{array}$

${P}_{max}=\underset{0\le t\le T}{\mathit{Max}}(\frac{1}{3}\mathit{trace}(\sigma ))=$ maximum hydrostatic pressure

$a=({\tau}_{0}-\frac{{d}_{0}}{\sqrt{3}})/\frac{{d}_{0}}{3}$ et $b={\tau}_{0}$

with ${\tau}_{0}$ = stamina limit in pure alternating shear

${d}_{0}$ = stamina limit in pure alternating traction-compression

The criterion is: $\mathrm{Rcrit}={\tau}_{a}+a{P}_{\max}-b$

If $\mathrm{Rcrit}$ is negative or zero, there is no damage. If $\mathrm{Rcrit}$ is positive, there is a risk of damage.

**Criteria of DANG VAN-PAPADOPOULOS:**

${K}^{\text{*}}+a{P}_{max}-b\le 0$

where ${K}^{\text{*}}=R/\sqrt{2}$ where $R$ radius of the smallest sphere circumscribed to the loading path in space of $\tilde{s}$ stress deviators.

$R=\underset{0\le t\le T}{\mathit{Max}}\sqrt{(\tilde{s}(t)-{C}^{\text{*}}):(\tilde{s}(t)-{C}^{\text{*}})}$ where ${C}^{\text{*}}$ is the center of the hypersphere

${C}^{\text{*}}=\underset{C\epsilon K}{\mathit{Min}}\underset{0\le t\le T}{\mathit{Max}}\sqrt{(\tilde{s}(t)-C):(\tilde{s}(t)-C)}$

${P}_{max}=\underset{0\le t\le T}{\mathit{Max}}(\frac{1}{3}\mathit{trace}(\sigma ))=$ maximum hydrostatic pressure

$a=({\tau}_{0}-\frac{{d}_{0}}{\sqrt{3}})/(\frac{{d}_{0}}{3})$ et $b={\tau}_{0}$

with ${\tau}_{0}$ = stamina limit in pure alternating shear

${d}_{0}$ = stamina limit in pure alternating traction-compression

The criterion is: ${R}_{\mathit{crit}}={K}^{\text{*}}+a{P}_{max}-b$

If $\mathit{Rcrit}$ is negative or zero, there is no damage. If $\mathrm{Rcrit}$ is positive, there is a risk of damage.
