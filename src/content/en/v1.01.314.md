# v1.01.314 ZZZZ314 – DEFI_FOND_FISS IT Validation

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the DEFI_FOND_FISS operator in an elementary way through six modellingss, each with a mesh crack in a glued configuration (CONFIG_INIT='COLLEE'). This test does not necessarily have physical significance, it is essentially a computer test.

The test case consists of testing vectors from the local base to the crack bottom in different configurations of DEFI_FOND_FISS.

*A case of a straight crack bottom*:

Modelling A:

- mesh $\mathrm{2D}$ .

Modelling B:

- Mesh $\mathrm{2D}$ symmetrical.

Modelization C:

- mesh $\mathrm{3D}$

Modelling D:

- Mesh $\mathrm{3D}$ symmetrical .

*Cas of a curved crack bottom*:

Modelling E:

- $\mathrm{3D}$ Modelling with a quarter circle background, from the test case sslv134b .

Modelling F:

- $\mathrm{3D}$ Modelling with symmetry and a closed circular background, from the test case sslv134a .

### 1.3 Tests and references

The tests are based on the values of the components of normal vectors and vectors of direction of propagation to nodes of the crack bottom.

In the results tables, the components of the normal vector are (VNORX,VNORY,VNORZ) and the components of the propagation direction vector are (VDIRX,VDIRY,VDIRZ).

## 6 Modelling E

In **modelization E**, a 3D structure is studied with a geometry based on that of the test case sslv134b. DEFI_FOND_FISS is tested in the case of a crack with a curved bottom and both lips defined.

### 6.1 Mesh characteristics

> The mesh and geometry used are those of the test case sslv134b. The mesh is presented by the

Figure suivante:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.314/10000000000002EC000002429D32A376464251EB.png)

It is composed of 5527 nodes, 784 elements HEXA20 and 432 PENTA15. The bottom is a quarter of a circle in the $\mathrm{Oxy}$ plane composed of 17 nodes.

### 6.2 Tested quantities and results

The crack is a quarter of a circle having as its center the origin of the marker. Thus, the analytical values of the vectors of direction of propagation of the crack are the coordinates of the nodes of the standard bottom.

Node of $i$ background of $({\mathit{COORX}}_{i},{\mathit{COORY}}_{i},{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i})$ coordinates:

The direction vector for crack propagation is:

${\mathit{VDIRX}}_{i}=\frac{{\mathit{COORX}}_{i}}{\sqrt{({\mathit{COORX}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORY}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i}\mathrm{²})}}$

${\mathit{VDIRY}}_{i}=\frac{{\mathit{COORY}}_{i}}{\sqrt{({\mathit{COORX}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORY}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i}\mathrm{²})}}$

${\mathit{VDIRZ}}_{i}=\frac{{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i}}{\sqrt{({\mathit{COORX}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORY}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i}\mathrm{²})}}$

The normal vector at the crack plane for each nodes of the bottom is directed from the lower lip to the upper lip. It is $(0,0,1)$.

For all these values, 0.1% error between the analytical value and the calculated value is tolerated.

## 7 Modelling F

In **modelization F**, a 3D structure is studied with geometry based on that of the test case sslv134a. DEFI_FOND_FISS is tested for a circular closed bottom and a structure with a symmetry plane.

### 7.1 Mesh characteristics

> The mesh and geometry used are those of the test case sslv134a. The mesh is presented by the

Figure suivante:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.314/10000000000002EC00000242F33EBCD25DBBD74F.png)

It is composed of 10135 nodes, 1568 elements HEXA20 and 864 PENTA15. The circular closed bottom is composed of 65 nodes.

### 7.2 Tested quantities and results

The crack is a circle having as its center the origin of the marker. Thus, the analytical values of the vectors of direction of propagation of the crack are the coordinates of the nodes of the bottom, normed.

At node of the $i$ $({\mathit{COORX}}_{i},{\mathit{COORY}}_{i},{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i})$ coordinate background, the direction vector for crack propagation is:

${\mathit{VDIRX}}_{i}=\frac{{\mathit{COORX}}_{i}}{\sqrt{({\mathit{COORX}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORY}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i}\mathrm{²})}}$

${\mathit{VDIRY}}_{i}=\frac{{\mathit{COORY}}_{i}}{\sqrt{({\mathit{COORX}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORY}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i}\mathrm{²})}}$

${\mathit{VDIRZ}}_{i}=\frac{{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i}}{\sqrt{({\mathit{COORX}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORY}}_{i}\mathrm{²}+{\mathit{COORZ}}_{i}\mathrm{²})}}$

The normal vector at the crack plane for each nodes of the bottom is carried by the $z$ axis: $(0,0,-1)$ .

When the crack is defined with a symmetry plane, the propagation direction vector belongs to the lip plane. However, the defined lip is not completely flat. So, the accuracy of the results is altered according to the $Z$ axis for VDIR and as normal at the crack plane is calculated from VDIR, the accuracy of VNORX and/or VNORY is also calculated.

For VDIRZ, VNORX and VNORY the tolerance error percentage is 1.8% compared to 1% for the other components.

## 8 Summary of the results

The vector values from the local base to the nodes of the crack bottom are properly evaluated even when the bottom is curved.

However, the results are less precise when studying a structure with symmetry, since without symmetry, the final vectors of the base are calculated by averaging the vectors of the lower lip and the upper lip. With symmetry, the vectors are those of the only modeled lip.

In a node background, VDIR belongs to the lip plane and VNOR is calculated by making the vector product between VDIR and the vector carried by the segment of the crack bottom to which this node belongs.

Thus, the values of this base are highly dependent on the angle of the crack and are all the more accurate as the angle is small. To avoid having a false base, it is not calculated when the crack is in the off-set configuration (CONFIG_INIT='DECOLLEE').
