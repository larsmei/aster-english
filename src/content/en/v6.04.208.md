# v6.04.208 SSNV208 – Drained Biaxial Test with Hujeux Law

*Summary*

A *biaxial test in pure mechanics* (equivalent to drained hydraulic conditions) is performed with *the Hujeux law* . The calculated solutions are compared with results from elements GEFDYN finished code from the École Centrale Paris.

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.208/Shape1.gif)

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The biaxial test is performed on a single finite element square isoparametric QUAD8, mesh cell group named $\mathrm{BLOC}$. The length of each edge is $\mathrm{1m}$. The different sides of this square are mesh cell groups named $\mathrm{HAUT}$, $\mathrm{BAS}$, $\mathrm{DROIT}$ and $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$. The mesh-cell group $\mathrm{COTE}$ also contains the mesh cell $\mathrm{DROIT}$ and $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$ groups; the mesh cell $\mathrm{APPUI}$ group is the mesh cell $\mathrm{BAS}$ group.

### 1.2 Material properties of the sand of Hostun

The elastic properties are:

- isotropic compressibility module: $K=148\mathrm{MPa}$
- shear module: $\mu =68\mathrm{MPa}$

The anelastic properties (model of Hujeux) are derived from the thesis of K.Hamadi [2] and correspond to sand d-Hostun slightly dense:

- power of nonlinear elastic law: ${n}_{e}=0.$ (linear elastic)
- $\beta =30.$
- $d=2.5$
- $b=0.2$
- friction angle: $\phi =33°$
- characteristic angle: $\Psi =33°$
- critical pressure: ${P}_{\mathrm{CO}}=-400\mathrm{kPa}$
- Reference pressure: ${P}_{\mathrm{ref}}=-1000\mathrm{kPa}$
- isotropic mechanism elastic radius: ${r}_{\mathrm{ela}}^{s}={10}^{-4}$
- elastic radius of the deviatory mechanism: ${r}_{\mathrm{ela}}^{d}=0.01$
- ${a}_{\mathrm{mon}}=0.017$
- ${a}_{\mathrm{cyc}}=0.0001$
- ${c}_{\mathrm{mon}}=0.08$
- ${c}_{\mathrm{cyc}}=0.04$
- ${r}_{\mathrm{hys}}=0.05$
- ${r}_{\mathrm{mob}}=0.9$
- ${x}_{m}=1.$
- $\mathrm{dila}=1.$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The biaxial test presented here is carried out in D_PLAN Modelling. Normal shifts in the design of studies are therefore null. Vertical displacement is imposed on the test piece while maintaining the constant lateral pressure in the design of study. It can be drained (the fluid pore pressure does not vary during the test) or non-drained (the valve is closed: the fluid pore pressure evolves in the sample). Here, we are interested in the drained case, simpler, because not involving the influence of the fluid pore pressure. We then choose a model in pure mechanics.

In the model under consideration, the square element represents a quarter of the sample. The boundary conditions are therefore:

The symmetry conditions:

- ${u}_{y}=0.$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{BAS}$ group
- ${u}_{x}=0.$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{GAUCHE}$ group

Side pressure conditions:

- ${P}_{n}=1.$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{COTE}$ group

The loading conditions:

- ${P}_{n}=1.$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{HAUT}$ group
- ${u}_{z}=-1.$ on mesh cell $\mathrm{HAUT}$ group

The load is carried out in two phases:

- An isotropic stress state, ${P}_{o}=100\mathrm{kPa}$ , is initially assigned to the mesh cell $\mathrm{BLOC}$;
- Vertical displacement is imposed on the mesh-cell group $\mathrm{HAUT}$ and varies between $t=0.$ and $t=10.$ from ${u}_{y}=0.$ to ${u}_{y}=-0.2$ (total vertical deformation of $20\text{\%}$).

### 1.4 Results

The solutions are post-treated with point $C$ , in terms of ${\sigma}_{yy}$ stress , ${\varepsilon}_{v}$ total volume deformation and $({r}_{\text{ela}}^{\text{iso},m}+{r}_{\text{iso}}^{m})$ isotropic and $({r}_{\text{ela}}^{d,m}+{r}_{\text{dev}}^{m})$ deviatory nutring coefficients .

The validation is carried out by comparison with GEFDYN solutions provided by the Ecole Centrale Paris (
[http://www.mssmat.ecp.fr/-GEFDYN,016-](http://www.mssmat.ecp.fr/-GEFDYN,016-) ).

The PDIL_ELGA elemental option calculation for this softening problem is also performed. This calculation option allows the maximum value to be estimated for the A1 regularization parameter of the [R5.04.03](doc:r5.04.03) expansion second gradient media. This value is a function of the material parameters, the stress state and the values of the internal variables at the moment of calculation [1].

### 1.5 Bibliographical references

[1] Foucault A. "*Modulation of the cyclical behaviour of earth works incorporating regularization techniques*".* Thesis by Doctor, École Centrale Paris, Châtenay Malabry, France, 2010.

[2] **Hamadi K.** "*Modification of forklifts and instability in geomaterials*". Thesis by Doctor, École Centrale Paris, Châtenay Malabry, France, 2006

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics

The Modelling is two-dimensional with D_PLAN flat deformations and nonlinear static.

The vertical displacement imposed on the upper facet varies between $0$ and $-\mathrm{0.2m}$ in $280$ time step between $t=0.$ and $t=10$. The automatic subdivision of the time step is enabled to manage non-convergence situations of local integration.

The INDL_ELGA calculation option, which allows you to calculate the directions of the Rice tensor, is also enabled. The results provided by this option are tested in non-regression mode.

The PDIL_ELGA calculation option, which allows the calculation of the A1_LC2 parameter, is also enabled. The results provided by this option are tested in non-regression mode.

### 2.3 Remarks

The comparison between *Code_Aster* and GEFDYN solutions is particularly good, with usually less than $1\text{\%}$ d-error. Relative errors greater than $1\text{\%}$ appear for lower tested values.
