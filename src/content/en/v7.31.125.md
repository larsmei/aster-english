# v7.31.125 WTNV125 –Hair rebalancing calculus of a bi-material

**Summary:**

This test case corresponds to the simplified hydraulic study of a piece of land in a storage site. Two materials are considered: a working barrier ($\mathit{BO}$ ) and a geological barrier ($\mathit{BG}$ ). Initially the $\mathit{BO}$ is de-saturated and the $\mathit{BG}$ saturated. Here the capillary rebalancing of the whole is studied (which corresponds to the resaturation of the barrier that is worked by the geological barrier).

This test case is similar to the wtna100 case (in axisymmetric version).

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On all edges: No hydraulic flow

The only engine here is saturation of one medium by another.

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics A

Axisymmetry Modelling. The open barrier is meshed by 15 elements HEXA20 and the geological barrier by 59 elements HEXA20, progressively spread over the entire length.

This is a 3D_HHD model.
