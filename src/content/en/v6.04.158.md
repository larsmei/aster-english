# v6.04.158 SSNV158 - Triaxial drained test with the Laigle model

**Summary**

This test validates the Laigle model in rock mechanics (CIH formalism). It is a triaxial test in drained condition. Calculations are performed only on the solid part of the soil without hydromechanical coupling. Four containment levels ($8\mathit{MPa}$ – $4\mathit{MPa}$ – $2\mathit{MPa}$ – $1\mathit{MPa}$) are applied. Due to symmetry, one only cares about the eighth of a sample tested in triaxial. The Modelling is axisymmetric.

This is a non-regression test.

### 1.3 Initial conditions, boundary conditions, and loading

**Phase 1:**

The sample shall be taken into a homogeneous state:

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.158/Object_2.svg)

, by imposing the corresponding containment pressure on the front, right and upper side faces. The movements are blocked on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$ ), left (${u}_{y}=0$ ) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$ ) faces.

**Phase 2:**

The blocked movements on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$), left (${u}_{y}=0$) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$) sides are maintained, as well as the containment pressure on the front and right side faces. The displacement is applied to the upper side:

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.158/Object_9.svg)

, to obtain ${\varepsilon}_{zz}=-20\text{\%}$ deformation (counted from the beginning of phase 2).
