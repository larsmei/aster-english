# v6.04.155 SSNV155 - Triaxial drained test on a turned sample at an angle of –PI/6 compared to the x axis with the CJS model (level 2)

**Summary**

This test completes the validation of the CJS model level 2 already addressed in the test case SSNV136. It corresponds to the numerical simulation of the same test (triaxial drained test) on the same material but with a different geometry. The tested sample is thus turned from an angle of $–\pi /6$ to the $x$ axis. Therefore, the $x,y,z$ directions are no longer the main directions. This allows validation of the numerical integration operations of the model that act on the non-diagonal terms of the strain and stress tensors.

As with test case SSNV136, calculations are made only on the solid part of the soil, without hydro-mechanical coupling. Due to symmetry, one only cares about the eighth of a sample tested triaxially. The containment level is $400\mathit{kPa}$.

This is a non-regression test. The results obtained are compared with those provided by a *Aster* calculation with an mesh with symmetry axes as coordinates, which correspond exactly to the $–\pi /6$ angle rotation.

### 1.3 Initial conditions, boundary conditions, and loading

**Phase 1:**

The sample is taken to a homogeneous state, by imposing the corresponding containment pressure on the $\mathit{EFGH}$, $\mathit{CDHG}$ and $\mathit{BCGF}$ faces. Normal movements are blocked on the ABCD, $\mathit{ADHE}$ and $\mathit{ABFE}$ faces.

**Phase 2:**

The normal movements on the $\mathit{ABCD}$ , $\mathit{ADHE}$ and $\mathit{ABFE}$ faces are maintained; as well as the containment pressure on the $\mathit{CDHG}$ and $\mathit{BCGF}$ faces are maintained. A normal displacement is applied to the $\mathit{EFGH}$ face, so that the normal direction of deformation is equal to – $\text{20\%}$ (counted from the beginning of phase 2).
