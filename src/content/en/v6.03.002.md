# v6.03.002 SSNP02 - element plate in flat deformation and biaxial traction (law of Norton)

**Summary:**

This test case allows testing the NORTON law using the LEMAITRE behavioral relationship in the case of a flat plate in biaxial traction. Plastic deformations are considered.

## 1 Reference problem

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.002/Shape1.gif)

### 1.1 Geometry

Contact details of $(m)$ points:

- $A(0.,0.)$
- $B(1.,0.)$
- $C(1.,1.)$
- $D(0.,1.)$

### 1.2 Material properties

Elastic

- $E=178000\mathrm{MPa}$     Young's modulus
- $\nu =0.3$     Poisson's ratio

LEMAITRE

- $n=9.7$
- $K=1920.$
- $\frac{1}{m}=0.$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Forced displacement $(m)$:

- $\mathrm{AB}$     : $\mathrm{DY}=0$
- $\mathrm{AD}$     : $\mathrm{DX}=0$

Loading

- Uniform pressure on $\mathrm{BC}$: ${\sigma}_{1}$
- Uniform pressure on $\mathrm{CD}$: ${\sigma}_{2}$
- $\mathrm{OA},\mathrm{AB},\mathrm{CD},\mathrm{DE}$ and $\mathrm{EF}$ journeys lasting 30 seconds
- $\mathrm{BC}$ journey of 60 seconds duration
- Support time in $A,B$ and $D$ of 3600 seconds
