## 2 Test case objective, validation

The objective of the test case is to validate the following commands in particular:

- MAC_MODES: calculation of the MAC matrix between two modal bases,
- LIRE_RESU: experimental data readout in IDEAS format,
- MACRO_EXPANS: extension of data measured on a numerical model consisting of a "judicious" deformed database.

The test case is based on the gardener's benchmark ([www.garteur.eu](http://www.garteur.eu/), European Aviation Research Group).

The test case is trivial here: experimental data (FRF and eigenmodes) were first numerically calculated before being exported to IDEAS format. Their expansion on the same numerical model must therefore allow the exact data to be retrieved.

### 2.1 Test case operation

- Digital model:

  - Definition and calculation of the modal basis of the numerical model,
  - Extraction of modes according to their actual mass in the three directions (with EXTR_MODES)
  - Calculation of the correlation matrix between two modal bases with MAC_MODES: this calculation is done to verify the orthogonality of the MODESORT base.
- Experimental model:

  - Definition of the experimental model: the values of the mechanical characteristics are arbitrary, they are only used to create coherent data structures for reading experimental data,
  - Reading the experimental harmonic response,
  - Expansion of the experimental response on the numerical model (MACRO_EXPANS),
  - Reading experimental modes,
  - Expansion of experimental modes on the digital model.

The expansion of experimental data with MACRO_EXPANS consists of the succession of PROJ_MESU_MODAL and REST_GENE_PHYS commands (and PROJ_CHAMP, for verification). The objective is to find the best combination of the own vectors contained in the numerical database in front of the keyword BASE that correctly reproduce the behavior of the experimental data. Subject to the fact that the modelling correctly reflects the physics of the measured structure, the experimental data is interpolated with the deformed data contained in the numerical model.

- Modelling A: expansion of FRF defined on the experimental model on a basis consisting of dynamic modes of the structure, and expansion of the eigenmodes identified on the experimental model on the same basis of expansion,
- Modelling B: expansion of the eigenmodes identified on the experimental model using an expansion base consisting of static deformed defined on part of the DDL of the numerical structure.

### 2.2 Validation of results

The test results are by expansion/projection, so no external reference to the calculation has to be defined. For Modelling a, the following tests are performed:

- test of the orthogonality of the MODESY (5 modes) eigenmodes: the matrix obtained is the matrix unit,

|  | Component | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matrix obtained by MAC_MODES | SOMM | $5.0$ |

- FRF extended: differences test between experimental FRF and extended/reprojected FRF, on a three-nodes component: the following ratio is calculated:

$R=\frac{\mathit{RMS}(∣{\mathit{FRF}}_{\exp}-{\mathit{FRF}}_{\mathit{et}}∣)}{\mathit{RMS}({\mathit{FRF}}_{\exp})}$

- extended eigenmodes: test the orthogonality of the extended base, making the MAC of it by itself (MAC_ET),
- extended eigenmodes: test of diagonal components of the MAC matrix between the extended modes numerical modes (MAC_ETNX), for Modelling A, and between extended/reprojected modes and experimental modes for Modelling B (MAC_RDEX).

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Digital and experimental models

The modellings are identical to the A Modelling.

### 4.2 Base of expansion

The expansion base is obtained with the MODE_STATIQUE operator. The theoretically, the experimental degrees of freedom-associated deformities must be created, i.e., the nodess placed at the measurement point level.

Here, the measuring points do not match nodes defined on the digital model. Two solutions are proposed:

- Short nodes list: for each experimental node, a node of the nearest numerical model was selected; the correspondence between the nodes of the two models in the PROJ_MESU_MODAL calculation ratio,
- Similarly, a long list is defined, which contains all nodes located near the nodes measurement; however, the static modes from this list are redundant for the resolution of the reverse problem: in this case, the reverse problem is wrong, and it is necessary to regularize it, using the keywords EPS, REGUL and COEF_PONDER in MACRO_EXPANS; by default, the list is in comment.

### 4.3 Tested quantities and results

Some differences are noted between the results of Modelling A and B. The expansion base is the basis for static modes. It should be noted that these are defined (with the MECA_STATIQUE operator) on a matrix of stiffness for which the degrees of freedom corresponding to static readings are clamped. Therefore, it is not the same as for numerical modes: therefore, it is not possible to compare extended modes and numerical modes by MAC criterion (which is not even calculated). Therefore, a comparison of experimental modes compared to extended/reprojected modes is made.

The static expansion base is not of very good quality, the orthogonality of extended modes is not realized. It is not tested here.
