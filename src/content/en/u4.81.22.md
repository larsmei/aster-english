# u4.81.22 Operator POST_ELEM

## 1 Purpose

Calculate quantities on the whole or on part of the structure. The quantities calculated correspond to particular computation options of the assigned modelling.

The options currently available are:

- calculation of the mass, of the inertias and of the position of the centre of gravity,
- calculation of the potential energy,
- calculation of the kinetic energy,
- calculation of the dissipation energy (DKTG elements),
- calculation of the work of the external forces,
- calculation of the indicators of loss of proportionality of the loading in elastoplasticity,
- calculation of the limit load,
- calculation of the Weibull stress,
- calculation of the growth rate of a spherical cavity (Rice - Tracey),
- calculation of the elastic energy and of the total energy,
- calculation of the area of a hole in a 2D mesh,
- calculation of the integral and of the mean of a field,
- calculation of the surface/volume distribution of the value of a component of a field,
- calculation of the spatial extrema of a list of components of a field,
- calculation of the norm of a field.

The concept returned is a table.

## 3 Operands

The operands MODELE, CHAM_MATER and CARA_ELEM are optional if RESULTAT is supplied, and mandatory if it is CHAM_GD that is given.

### 3.1 Operand MODELE

```text
◊    MODELE = mo,
```

Name of the model on which the option is calculated. The name of the model is optional because it is contained in the resultat data structure.

### 3.2 Operand CHAM_MATER

```text
◊    CHAM_MATER = chmater,
```

Material field associated with the model mo, optional because it is contained in the resultat data structure.

### 3.3 Operand CARA_ELEM

```text
◊    CARA_ELEM = carac,
```

The elementary characteristics carac are necessary if structural elements (beam, plate, shell or discrete elements) exist in the model, optional because they are contained in the resultat data structure.

### 3.4 Operands NUME_COUCHE / NIVE_COUCHE

```text
◊    NUME_COUCHE = nume,
```

In the case of a multilayer material, integer value lying between 1 and the number of layers, necessary to specify the layer where one wishes to carry out the elementary computation. By convention, layer 1 is the **lower** layer (in the direction of the normal to the element) in the case of mechanical shell elements or thermal shell elements.

```text
◊    NIVE_COUCHE =
```

For the layer nume defined by NUME_COUCHE, makes it possible to specify the ordinate where one wishes to carry out the elementary computation:

| 'INF' lower ordinate of the layer | (inner skin), |
| --- | --- |
| 'SUP' upper ordinate of the layer | (outer skin), |
| 'MOY' mean ordinate of the layer | (mean ply by default). |

### 3.5 Operand MODE_FOURIER

```text
◊    MODE_FOURIER =
```

Number of the Fourier harmonic: positive or zero integer (default = 0).

### 3.6 Operand GEOMETRIE

♦    GEOMETRIE =    /    'INITIALE',          [DEFAUT]

```text
/    'DEFORMEE',
```

Indicates whether one is working on the initial geometry or on the deformed geometry. In the latter case, a displacement field must be supplied by CHAM_GD or RESULTAT.

### 3.7 Operands CHAM_GD / RESULTAT

The options ENER_POT and ENER_CIN are calculated from a field at the nodes or an element-wise field, existing or extracted from a resultat.

#### 3.7.1 Operand CHAM_GD

```text
♦    /    CHAM_GD = cham,
```

Name of a field (for the options ENER_POT and ENER_CIN).

For the option ENER_POT, a displacement field or a temperature field must be supplied (see [§3.9]).

For the option ENER_CIN, a velocity field must be supplied (without supplying a frequency), or else a displacement field and a frequency (see [§3.9]).

#### 3.7.2 Operand RESULTAT

```text
/    RESULTAT = resu,
```

Name of a result concept of type evol_elas, evol_ther, mode_meca, evol_noli, mult_elas, fourier_elas or dyna_trans.

| Option ENER_POT: | evol_elas, evol_ther, mode_meca, mult_elas, fourier_elas, evol_noli or dyna_trans. |
| --- | --- |
| Option ENER_CIN: | mode_meca, evol_elas, evol_ther, evol_noli, or dyna_trans. |
| Option ENER_ELAS and ENER_TOTALE: | evol_noli, evol_elas |

##### 3.7.2.1 Operands TOUT_ORDRE / NUME_ORDRE / NUME_MODE / LIST_ORDRE / NOEUD_CMP / FREQ / LIST_FREQ / INST / LIST_INST / PRECISION / CRITERE

See [U4.71.00](doc:u4.71.00).

### 3.8 Keyword MASS_INER

#### 3.8.1 Purpose

Factor keyword making it possible to calculate the mass, the inertias and the centre of gravity.

This option allows the computation, on each element, of the following characteristics:

($\rho$ denoting the density defined in DEFI_MATERIAU [U4.43.01](doc:u4.43.01) by ELAS or ELAS_FO).

Mass: $m=\underset{v}{\int}\rho \mathrm{dv}$

Centre of gravity: ${x}_{G}=\frac{1}{m}\underset{v}{\int}x\rho \mathit{dv};{y}_{G}=\frac{1}{m}\underset{v}{\int}y\rho \mathit{dv};{z}_{G}=\frac{1}{m}\underset{v}{\int}z\rho \mathit{dv}$

Inertia tensor at the centre of gravity $G$ in the global frame of description of the mesh:

### 3.11 Keyword ENER_ELAS

#### 3.11.1 Purpose

Makes it possible to calculate the elastic strain energy for each time instant $t$ after an elastic or elastoplastic analysis, on the topological selection chosen by the user.

The user may carry out this post-processing from a result concept of type evol_noli or evol_elas. In the case of an evol_noli concept, the computation carried out depends on the choice of strain formulation made during the analysis.

- In small strains (DEFORMATION = PETIT or DEFORMATION = PETIT_REAC), the computation is carried out from the Cauchy stress field SIEF_ELGA by Hooke's expression:

${E}^{e}(t)=\frac{1}{2}{\int}_{v}^{t}\sigma (t){D}^{-1}\sigma (t)\mathit{dv}$

### 3.14 Keyword WEIBULL

#### 3.14.1 Purpose

Keyword making it possible, for each defined time instant, to calculate the elementary field of the power

![Figure](/figures/u4.81.22/Object_25.svg)

of the Weibull stress, whose expression on the mesh cell $K$ is given, without taking the plastic strain into account, by:

${\sigma}_{w}^{m}(K)=\frac{1}{{V}_{\mathrm{ref}}}{\int}_{{K}_{p}}{\sigma}_{1}^{m}d{K}_{p}$

and, taking the plastic strain into account, by:

${\sigma}_{w}^{m}(K)=\frac{1}{{V}_{\mathrm{ref}}}{\int}_{{K}_{p}}{\sigma}_{1}^{m}\exp(\frac{-m}{2}{\varepsilon}_{1}^{p})d{K}_{p}$

${K}_{p}$ denotes the part of the mesh cell $K$ which has plastified, that is, the part of $K$ where the cumulative plastic strain exceeds a certain threshold; ${\sigma}_{1}$ represents the maximum principal stress and ${\varepsilon}_{1}^{p}$ represents the maximum principal plastic strain.

The material parameters $m$ , ${V}_{\mathrm{ref}}$ and the plasticity threshold are defined in DEFI_MATERIAU by the constitutive relation WEIBULL (cf. [R7.02.06](doc:r7.02.06)).

Once this elementary field has been determined, the option calculates by "summation" the Weibull stress of a domain $D$ for each defined time instant:

${\sigma}_{w}(D)={(C\underset{K\in D}{\Sigma}{\sigma}_{w}^{m}(K))}^{\frac{1}{m}}$

where

![Figure](/figures/u4.81.22/Object_37.svg)

is a coefficient intended to take into account the symmetries (two- and three-dimensional case) and the thickness (in the two-dimensional case) of the structure containing the domain

![Figure](/figures/u4.81.22/Object_38.svg)

(keyword COEF_MULT).

The failure probability of the domain $D$ is then calculated by:

${P}_{w}(D)=1-\exp(-\frac{{\sigma}_{w}^{m}}{{\sigma}_{u}^{m}})$

The parameter "cleavage stress" ${\sigma}_{\mu}$ is, likewise, defined in the constitutive relation WEIBULL.

Finally, the preceding expressions of the Weibull stress and of the failure probability are valid only in the case of a monotonic loading path. This type of post-processing may nevertheless also be applied to a more general loading path, including when the cleavage stress depends on the temperature (constitutive relation WEIBULL_FO). The expressions of the Weibull stress and of the failure probability are then different (cf [R7.02.06](doc:r7.02.06)).

$\frac{d}{\mathrm{dt}}\log(\frac{R}{{R}_{0}})=0.283\mathrm{Signe}(\frac{{\sigma}_{M}}{{\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}})\exp(∣\frac{2{\sigma}_{M}}{2{\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}}∣)\frac{d{\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{p}}{\mathrm{dt}}$

(${\sigma}_{M}=\frac{1}{3}\mathrm{Trace}(\sigma )$ ; ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}$ denotes the von Mises equivalent stress and ${\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{p}$ denotes the von Mises equivalent strain).

### 3.16 Keyword INDIC_ENER

#### 3.16.1 Purpose

Factor keyword making it possible to calculate a global indicator of loss of proportionality of the loading in elastoplasticity, based on the energy density. This indicator is described in detail in the document [R4.20.01](doc:r4.20.01).

Its function and its expression are recalled here. This indicator is intended to detect whether, in the course of the history of the structure and up to the current time instant t, and for a zone of the structure chosen by the modeller, there has been a loss of proportionality of the loading (i.e. it is a matter of having a global measure of the change in the principal directions of the stress tensor for each point of the zone defined by the user).

This indicator can be used only for models whose material exhibits isotropic hardening and whose elements are 2D or 3D isoparametric elements.

This indicator has the expression:

$I=\frac{1}{V}\underset{V}{\int}(1-\frac{\underline{\Psi}}{\Omega})\mathrm{dv}$

where:

- $V$ is the volume of the domain defined by the user,
- $\underline{\Psi}$ is the total elastic energy density associated with the tensile curve if one considered the material to be non-linear elastic.

More precisely, its expression is the following:

$\begin{array}{ccc}\underline{\Psi}=\frac{1}{2}K\cdot {\mathrm{tr}}^{2}(\varepsilon )+\frac{2\mu }{3}{\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{2}& \mathrm{si}& {\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}<R(p)\\ \underline{\Psi}=\frac{1}{2}K\cdot {\mathrm{tr}}^{2}(\varepsilon )+\frac{{R}^{2}(p)}{6\mu }+\underset{0}{\overset{p}{\int}}R(s)\mathrm{ds}& \mathrm{si}& {\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}=R(p)\end{array}$

where:

- $K$ is the bulk modulus,
- $\mu$ is the Lamé shear coefficient,
- $R(p)$ is the threshold of the tensile curve associated with the cumulative plastic strain $p$ ,
- $\Omega$ is the strain-energy density defined by:

$\Omega (t)=\underset{0}{\overset{t}{\int}}\sigma \cdot \dot{\varepsilon}d\tau$

one may decompose $\Omega (t)$ into an elastic part and a plastic part:

$\Omega (t)={\Omega}_{\mathrm{élas}}(t)+{\Omega}_{\mathrm{plas}}(t)$

with:

$\begin{array}{}{\Omega}_{\mathrm{élas}}(t)=\frac{1}{2}\sigma \cdot {\varepsilon}^{\mathrm{élas}}\\ {\Omega}_{\mathrm{plas}}(t)=\underset{0}{\overset{t}{\int}}R(p)\mathrm{dp}\end{array}$

**Note:**

*In the case where one has* $\Omega (t)=0$ *, one sets* $I=0$ *.*

### 3.17 Keyword INDIC_SEUIL

#### 3.17.1 Purpose

Factor keyword making it possible to calculate a global indicator of loss of proportionality of the loading in elastoplasticity.

This indicator makes it possible, on the one hand, to know, on average over the zone considered, whether the stress tensor and the plastic-strain tensor have the same directions and whether the plastic threshold is reached at the current time instant, and, on the other hand, whether in the course of the history the plastic strain has changed direction.

This indicator has the expression:

$I=\frac{1}{V}\underset{V}{\int}(1-\frac{\sigma \cdot {\varepsilon}^{p}}{R(p)\cdot p})\mathrm{dv}$

where:

- $V$ is the volume of the domain defined by the user,
- $\sigma$ is the stress tensor at the current time instant,
- ${\varepsilon}^{p}$ is the plastic-strain tensor at the current time instant,
- $R(p)$ is the hardening function (with $R(o)={\sigma}_{y}$ where ${\sigma}_{y}$ is the elastic limit).

i.e. it is the threshold of the tensile curve associated with the cumulative plastic strain $p$ .

- $p$ is the cumulative plastic strain.

**Note:**

*In the case where one has* $R(p)\cdot p=0$ *, one sets* $I=0$ *.*

The scalar product $\sigma \cdot {\varepsilon}^{p}$ is associated with the norm in the von Mises sense.

This indicator is normalised and has a value lying between 0 and 1.

It is zero if the loading has retained its proportional character at every point of $V$ throughout the elapsed history.

This indicator is described in detail in the document [R4.20.01](doc:r4.20.01).

### 3.18 Keyword CHAR_LIMITE

#### 3.18.1 Purpose

Post-processing of the limit-load computation [R7.07.01 §2.3].

This factor keyword makes it possible to calculate the limit load of a structure by a kinematic approach. Its use requires beforehand that a non-linear analysis has been carried out (see the operator STAT_NON_LINE [U4.51.03](doc:u4.51.03)), supplied under the keyword RESULTAT and whose characteristics are the following:

- constitutive law NORTON_HOFF,
- an increasing list of computation time instants corresponding to regularisation values of the NORTON_HOFF law which tend towards 1 (in practice, one recommends limiting oneself to time instants lying between 1 and 2, which do not lead to excessively long analyses while making it possible to obtain a sufficiently accurate upper bound of the limit load),
- a piloted (unit) loading corresponding to the loading with respect to which one seeks to estimate the limit load, the piloting method being TYPE='ANA_LIM',
- possibly a constant loading, whose existence must then imperatively be recalled by the keyword CHAR_CSTE ='OUI'.

The operator POST_ELEM then produces a table which gives, for each time instant of the analysis, that is for increasingly weak regularisations, an upper bound CHAR_LIMI_SUP of the limit load supported by the structure. Furthermore, in the absence of a constant loading, CHAR_CSTE = 'NON', the table also contains an estimate CHAR_LIMI_ESTIM of a lower bound of the limit load. On the other hand, if a constant loading is present, CHAR_CSTE = 'OUI', such an estimate of the lower bound is no longer available, but the table then contains the power PUIS_CHAR_CSTE of the constant loading in the velocity field which is the solution of the problem.

A detailed example of a limit-load computation is given in [U2.05.04](doc:u2.05.04).

### 3.22 Keyword TRAV_EXT

#### 3.22.1 Purpose

Factor keyword making it possible to calculate the real work of the external forces TRAV_REEL or the elastic work TRAV_ELAS as defined below:

$\text{TRAV\_REEL}=\underset{{t}_{0}}{\overset{t}{\int}}\underset{\Omega}{\int}\sigma \cdot \dot{\varepsilon}=\underset{{t}_{0}}{\overset{t}{\int}}{\mathrm{F}}_{int}\cdot \dot{\mathrm{U}}$                     eq 3.22.1-1

$\text{TRAV\_ELAS}=\frac{1}{2}\underset{\Omega}{\int}\sigma \cdot \varepsilon =\frac{1}{2}{F}_{int}\cdot U$

> eq 3.22.1-2

The computation is carried out on the basis of a result data structure, supplied under the keyword RESULTAT, for which the nodal forces, that is the internal forces, have previously been calculated by the operator CALC_CHAMP, option 'FORC_NODA' [U4.81.04](doc:u4.81.04). In the case of the real work, the initial time instant ${t}_{0}$ corresponds to the first time instant archived in the result data structure; the integration in time is carried out by a trapezoidal rule:

$\begin{array}{}\text{TRAV\_REEL}=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\underset{{t}_{i}}{\overset{{t}_{i+1}}{\int}}F(U({t}_{i})).U({t}_{i})\mathrm{dt}=\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}\underset{{U}_{i}}{\overset{{U}_{i+1}}{\int}}F(Z)\mathrm{dZ}\approx \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}({U}_{i+1}-{U}_{i})({F}_{i+1}+{F}_{i})\\ \approx \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}({U}_{i+1}.{F}_{i+1}+{U}_{i+1}.{F}_{i}-{U}_{i}.{F}_{i+1}-{U}_{i}.{F}_{i})\end{array}$

The two quantities TRAV_REEL and TRAV_ELAS are calculated for each time instant archived in the result data structure.

These quantities may be interpreted graphically on the force–displacement response curve of the structure (provided that the force is dual to the displacement, for example the pressure and the volume in the case of a pressurised cavity).

### 3.26 Operand VOLUMOGRAMME

#### 3.26.1 Purpose

Makes it possible to calculate the surface or volume distribution of the value of a component of a field at various time instants on a domain defined by the keywords TOUT or GROUP_MA.

One produces a table containing:

- the bounds of the intervals defined by the minimum and maximum values of the component and by the number of intervals desired,
- the percentage of the structure corresponding to each interval.

**Note**

*The number of intervals is defined by the user; the bounds are determined by Code_Aster in the following way:*

*Let* $n$ *be the number of intervals, and* $\mathrm{Vmin}$ *and* $\mathrm{Vmax}$ *the extreme values of the component.*

*Let* $p=(\mathrm{Vmax}-\mathrm{Vmin})/n$ *be the length of each interval.*

*The intervals are:*

$[\mathrm{Vmin},\mathrm{Vmin}+p],[\mathrm{Vmin}+p,\mathrm{Vmin}+\mathrm{2p}],\mathrm{...},[\mathrm{Vmin}+(n-1)p,\mathrm{Vmax}]$

#### 3.26.2 Syntax

```text
|    VOLUMOGRAMME =_F    (
♦    / TOUT         =    'OUI',
/ GROUP_MA    =    grma,            [gr_maille]

/'3D',
◊    NOM_CHAM        =     champ,            [Kn]

◊    NORME            =     /'RELATIF'    [defaut]
/'ABSOLU'
◊    |    NB_INTERV        = nb_int        [I]

```

## 4 Example

The example which follows applies to the calculation of global quantities on a dynamic modelling of a reactor building. The following are modelled: the outer containment, the inner containment, the internal structures, the reactor pit.

The modelling of a half-building is carried out with beam elements and discrete elements representing the ground connections, the added masses and the connections between nodes.

It is on this beam model that the operator POST_ELEM will calculate:

- the mass of the structure,
- the coordinates of the centre of gravity,
- the inertia tensor,
- the potential energy of certain modes and its distribution in the structure,
- the kinetic energy of certain modes and its distribution in the structure.

### 4.1 Calculation of the mass, of the centre of gravity and of the inertias

- for the whole structure (TOUT = 'OUI')
- for the mesh-cell group containing the beams ( GROUP_MA = 'pou_d_t' )
- for the mesh-cell group containing the ground connections ( GROUP_MA = 'liai_sol')
- for the mesh-cell group containing the added masses ( GROUP_MA = 'masses' )
- for the mesh-cell group containing the connections between nodes ( GROUP_MA = 'liai_noe' )

**Command**

```text
massestr = POST_ELEM (    MODELE    =    stickmod,
CHAM_MATER = chmater,
CARA_ELEM = caraelem,
MASS_INER = _F(GROUP_MA=(    'pou_d_t', 'liai_sol',
'masses', 'liai_noe' ),
TOUT= 'OUI',),
TITRE='masse, centre de gravite et inerties de la structure');
```

### 4.2 Calculation of the potential energy of modes 1, 2 and 7

- for the whole structure (TOUT = 'OUI')
- for the mesh-cell group containing the beams ( GROUP_MA = 'pou_d_t' )
- for the mesh-cell group containing the ground connections ( GROUP_MA = 'liai_sol')
- for the mesh-cell group containing the added masses ( GROUP_MA = 'masses' )
- for the mesh-cell group containing the connections between nodes ( GROUP_MA = 'liai_noe')

enerpot = POST_ELEM (    RESULTAT = modes,

> MODELE = stickmod,
> NUME_MODE = ( 1,2,7,),

> CHAM_MATER = chmater,

> CARA_ELEM = caraelem,
> ENER_POT = _F(    TOUT = 'OUI',

> GROUP_MA = (    'pou_d_t', 'liai_sol',

> 'masses', 'liai_noe'),

> ),

> TITRE= 'energies potentielles des modes 1, 2 et 7',

> )

- for the whole structure ( TOUT= 'OUI' )
