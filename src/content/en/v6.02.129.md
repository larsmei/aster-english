# v6.02.129 SSNL129 – Validation of VISC_ISOT_TRAC and VISC_ISOT_LINE laws on a traction test

**Summary:**

This test consists of applying a traction load to an elemental volume. Three loading speeds are modelled.

The modellings A, B, and C enable the constitutive law VISC_ISOT_TRAC (DEFORMATION=的SIMO_MIEHE) to be validated in 3D, D_PLAN, and AXIS. For this, the results obtained are compared with the ROUSS_VISC and DEFORMATION=的PETIT_REAC, degenerated so that the evolution of porosity is negligible. The modellings D, E, and F enable the VISC_ISOT_LINE (DEFORMATION=SIMO_MIEHE) law to be validated for integration into 3D, D_PLAN, and AXIS. For this, the results obtained are compared with those given by the VISC_ISOT_TRAC law for the same linear nutrification.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The purpose is to test the 2 constitutive laws viscoplastic VISC_ISOT_TRAC and VISC_ISOT_LINE on a $R$ representative Elemental Volume of $\mathrm{1mm}$ size, i.e. a $\mathrm{3D}$ cube, equivalent to a flat deformation bar or a axisymmetric cylinder.

## 2 Baseline

For the VISC_ISOT_TRAC model tested in modellings A, B and C, validation is done by comparison with the results obtained with the ROUSS_VISC model, the parameters of which were chosen to make the evolution of porosity negligible and thus to be reduced to the "classical" visco-plastic model.

For the VISC_ISOT_LINE model tested in modellings D, E and F, the solution obtained with VISC_ISOT_TRAC for which a linear hardening has been defined is compared (only the end points of the curve used to validate this model are retained).

## 9 Summary of the results

With less than $\text{1\%}$ difference between the ROUSS_VISC (degenerate) model and the VISC_ISOT_TRAC model regardless of the loading speed applied, it can be assumed that adding the viscous component to the VISC_ISOT_TRAC model is correct.

On the other hand, the very low differences (less than $\text{0.02\%}$) observed between the solutions obtained with VISC_ISOT_LINE and VISC_ISOT_TRAC for linear hardening, also enable the VISC_ISOT_LINE model to be validated.

In any case, less than 10 local iterations are required to achieve convergence (${10}^{-9}$ precision).
