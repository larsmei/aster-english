# v6.01.108 SSNA108 - Weibull, Bordet and Rice and Tracey models

**Summary:**

This quasi-static non-linear mechanical test validates the following three models:

- Weibull model (POST_ELEM and POST_BEREMIN controls)
- Rice Model - Tracey (POST_ELEM command)
- Bordet model (command CALC_BORDET)

in the case of a axisymmetric test piece subjected to a single tensile test. The Modelling of the test piece is carried out with elements 2D (QUA8).

In particular, this test proposes the use of plastic corrections for the calculation of the stress of Weibull [R7.02.04](doc:r7.02.04)

The modellings studied are the following (unless otherwise stated, all calculations are in HPP):

- Modelling A:

- Weibull stress calculation without plastic correction, SIGM_ELGA and SIGM_ELMOY options with POST_ELEM / WEIBULL ET POST_BEREMIN.
- calculation of Bordet stress with POST_BORDET and triaxiality rate with POST_ELEM / RICE_TRACEY

- Modelling B: Idem than Modelling A, but the calculation is done in parallel
- Modelling C: test of a plastic correction formulation for the calculation of the stress of Weibull, option SIGM_ELGA
- Modelling D: test of the same plastic correction formulation for the calculation of the stress of Weibull, option SIGM_ELMOY
- Modelling E: test of a second formulation of plastic correction for the calculation of the stress of Weibull, option SIGM_ELGA
- Modelization F: calculation of the stress of Weibull with the large deformation kinetics SIMO_MIEHE and GDEF_LOG.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The test reference values are derived from the following comparisons:

- the Weibull stress and triaxiality rate of the Rice and Tracey criterion by the POST_ELEM operator is validated by comparison with a numerical solution calculated by CASTEM2000 and Zebulon codes, and tested in non-regression;
- Weibull stress calculated by the POST_BEREMIN operator is compared to that calculated by the POST_ELEM operator and tested in non-regression;
- The values of the stress calculated with the Bordet model are tested only in non-regression.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

On different mesh cells and at different times, we calculate:

- Weibull constraint with the 2 POST_ELEM / WEIBULL and POST_BEREMIN operators;
- Bordet constraint with the POST_BORDET operator;
- the triaxiality rate with the operator POST_ELEM / RICE_TRACEY.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Code accuracy.

## 6 Modelling C

### 6.1 Modelling characteristics

This Modelling tests the calculation of the Weibull stress by POST_ELEM / WEIBULL and POST_BEREMIN operators, with the SIGM_ELGA option and with or without plastic correction.
The values from the POST_BEREMIN operator calculation are compared with those from POST_ELEM / WEIBULL.

For POST_BEREMIN, the corrected stress field given under keyword SIGM_CORR is as follows:

où :

- ${{\sigma}}_{I}$ is the maximum main stress (calculated from the stress field at Gauss SIGM_ELGA points)
- ${{\varepsilon}}_{I}$ is the maximum main total deformation.

It should be noted that this plastic correction differs from that implemented in POST_ELEM / WEIBULL in all respects, as the total Green-Lagrange deformation is considered in the direction of the maximum main stress (see [U4.81.22](doc:u4.81.22)).
In the configuration studied, the two implementations give similar results and the results of POST_BEREMIN will therefore still be compared with those of POST_ELEM / WEIBULL.

The Weibull stress calculated is compared to the last moment of calculation.
