# v8.01.115 FDLV115 - Harmonic response of a visco-elastic ring in fluid-structure coupling for model reduction

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check the DEFI_BASE_REDUITE model reduction operator in a case of IFS with viscoelastic material.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v8.01.115/Cadre2.gif)

Figure 1.1-1: Géométrie (pas à l'échelle)
A flat metal ring is considered to be immersed in a fluid medium with an external boundary for the latter (Figure ).

In the metal ring, a visco-elastic layer is modelled on a quarter of the circumference.

### 1.2 Material properties

For the steel part, an isotropic elastic material is considered:

- Elasticity module: ${E}_{a}=177\mathit{GPa}$
- Poisson's ratio: ${\nu}_{a}=0.3$
- Density: ${\rho}_{a}=7450\mathit{kg}.{m}^{-3}$

> For the fluid, it is water with the following characteristics:

- Celerity of the sound: $c=1500m.{s}^{-1}$
- Density: ${\rho}_{e}=1000\mathit{kg}.{m}^{-3}$

> For the visco-elastic part, a material of the fractional derivative (Zener model) model type is considered to be the shear module dependent on pulsation:

$$
{G}^{\text{*}}\left(\omega \right)=\frac{{G}_{0}+{G}_{\infty}{(\mathit{i\omega \tau })}^{\alpha}}{1+{(\mathit{i\omega \tau })}^{\alpha}}
$$

The characteristics are as follows:

- Poisson's ratio: ${\nu}_{v}=0.49$ (almost uncompressible material)
- Density: ${\rho}_{v}=1460\mathit{kg}.{m}^{-3}$
- Coefficients: ${G}_{0}=2,11\mathit{MPa}$ , ${G}_{\infty}=0,59\mathit{GPa}$ , $\tau =0,44\times {10}^{-6}$ and $\alpha =0,53$

Note: To improve the calculation of modes, all these quantities are measured in relation to water.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

For the structural part, a clamped support condition is imposed on the $\mathit{AB}$ segment and a horizontal $\mathit{Fx}=A,0\times {10}^{-5}N$ nodal force is imposed on the point $A$ .

A fluid-border interaction condition is achieved by using elements 2D_FLUI_STRU at the interface between the structure and the fluid. Furthermore, an impedance condition is defined on the external boundary of the fluid, by a load of type IMPE and the calculation of the impedance matrices attached to it.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

Empirical modes are calculated. Modes are tested by point values of non-regression at different points and ddl.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The following system is being sought:

$$
\left(-i{\omega}^{3}I(\mu )-{\omega}^{2}(\mu )M(\mu )+K(\mu )\right)u(\mu )=F(\mu )
$$

The $\mu$ coefficient represents the parameters of variation, $I$ the impedance matrix, $M$ the mass matrix, $K$ the rigidity matrix and $F$ the second member (applying nodal force). The variable parameters are: $\omega$ , ${E}_{a}$ , ${\rho}_{a}$ and ${\rho}_{v}$ .

The system is written as follows:

$$
{f}_{1}(\omega ,{E}_{a}).{K}_{v}+{f}_{2}(\omega ,{\rho}_{a}).{K}_{e}+{K}_{f}+{f}_{3}(\omega ,{\rho}_{v}).{M}_{e}-{\omega}^{2}{M}_{f}-j{\omega}^{3}.I=F
$$

The different contributions were separated: ${K}_{v}$ for stiffness coming from the viscoelastic part (calculated by RIGI_MECA_HYST), ${K}_{e}$ for stiffness coming from the elastic part, ${K}_{f}$ for stiffness coming from the fluid part, ${M}_{e}$ for mass coming from the elastic part, ${M}_{f}$ for mass coming from the fluid part and $I$ for impedance matrix.

And the functions:

$$
{f}_{1}(\omega ,{E}_{a})=2\times {E}_{a}(1.0+{\nu}_{v})\frac{\frac{{G}_{0}+{G}_{\infty}{(\mathit{jc}\omega \tau )}^{\alpha}}{1+{(\mathit{jc}\omega \tau )}^{\alpha}}}{{\rho}_{e}{c}^{2}}+2j{E}_{a}(1.0+{\nu}_{v})\frac{\frac{{G}_{0}+{G}_{\infty}{(\mathit{jc}\omega \tau )}^{\alpha}}{1+{(\mathit{jc}\omega \tau )}^{\alpha}}}{{\rho}_{e}{c}^{2}}\times \frac{\mathrm{\Im }\left(\frac{{G}_{0}+{G}_{\infty}{(\mathit{jc}\omega \tau )}^{\alpha}}{1+{(\mathit{jc}\omega \tau )}^{\alpha}}\right)}{\mathrm{\Re }\left(\frac{{G}_{0}+{G}_{\infty}{(\mathit{jc}\omega \tau )}^{\alpha}}{1+{(\mathit{jc}\omega \tau )}^{\alpha}}\right)}
$$

$$
{f}_{2}(\omega ,{\rho}_{a})=\frac{{\rho}_{a}{E}_{e}}{{\rho}_{e}{c}^{2}}
$$

$$
{f}_{3}(\omega ,{\rho}_{v})=\frac{-{\omega}^{2}{\rho}_{v}{\rho}_{a}}{{\rho}_{e}}
$$

For calculation, the four parameters are varied over ten values (the value of these parameters that were randomly drawn is not reproduced here, see command file).

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

The error on the solution depends on the degree of reduction (number of empirical modes).

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

2D_FLUIDE, D_PLAN and 2D_FLUI_STRU Modelling is used in coupled IFS linear dynamics ($(u,p,\varphi )$ formulation).

This Modelling tests the creation of empirical modes. Three modes are calculated.
