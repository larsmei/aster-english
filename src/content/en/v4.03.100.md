# v4.03.100 TPLS100 - Infinite plate subjected to antisymmetric fluxes

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to test the three-field linear thermal hull model (MODELISATION: ‘COQUE' or ‘COQUE_PLAN') in comparison to an analytical solution, for an infinite plate subjected to a stationary antisymmetric heat flow torque on its two half-sided, stationary-state.

The heat equation is solved by stationary, with linear, isotropic, homogeneous conduction.

Two modellings: A for elements overfacic shell finish (triangles) and B for elements linear (segments).

The elementary controls and the THER_LINEAIRE global control are tested simultaneously in each model.
