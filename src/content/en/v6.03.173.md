# v6.03.173 SSNP173 - Contact between two concentric spheres

**Summary:**

This problem corresponds to a quasi-static analysis of a mechanical problem with frictionless contact, of which the analytical solution is known. Here, we are particularly interested in integrating contact terms by the method *Mortar* *Local Average Contact* (LAC) on a curved surface.Meshings are incompatible.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A hollow sphere of $30\mathit{mm}$ external radius and $20\mathit{mm}$ internal radius is considered, included in a hollow sphere of $40\mathit{mm}$ external radius and of internal radius equal to the external radius of the included sphere.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.173/Object_4.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=2000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

$\mathit{DX}=0$ symmetry conditions on the $\mathit{AD}$ and $\mathit{DY}=0$ surface on the $\mathit{BC}$ surface are imposed to model only a quarter of the model.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.173/Object_13.png)

$P=25\mathit{MPa}$ pressure is imposed on the $\mathit{AB}$ surface, inside the first sphere. Both spheres are kept in contact at the interface.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

There is a known analytical solution to this problem, if ${R}_{1}$ is considered the inner radius and ${R}_{2}$ the outer radius, then the radial stress expressed in polar coordinates sss.

${\sigma}_{\mathit{rr}}(r)=\frac{{R}_{1}^{3}}{{R}_{2}^{3}-{R}_{1}^{3}}\cdot \frac{{R}_{2}^{3}-{r}^{3}}{{r}^{3}}\cdot P$ .

So we find ${\sigma}_{\mathit{rr}}({R}_{1})=P$ and ${\sigma}_{\mathit{rr}}({R}_{2})=0$ well.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The contact pressure on the interfaces, in $r=30\mathit{mm}$, on both sides of the discontinuity, is tested. With ${R}_{1}=20\mathit{mm}$ and ${R}_{2}=40\mathit{mm}$, we have:

${\sigma}_{\mathit{rr}}(r)=4.894179894\mathit{MPa}$ .

## 5 Summary of the results

This very simple example is designed to test a new technique for integrating contact terms based on sub-elements subdivision. This method is activated when the ALGO_CONT='LAC' is selected for a contact area in CONTINUE formulation. This is done in 2D for quadratic elements, in the case of curved surfaces.
