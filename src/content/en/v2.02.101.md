# v2.02.101 SDLL101 - Vibration of a beam with pre-stress

**Summary:**

This plan problem consists of searching for vibration frequencies of a mechanical structure composed of a slender beam, circular cross section, clamped-glider voltage. This Structure Mechanics test corresponds to a dynamic analysis of a linear model with linear behavior. This test consists of two modellings.

In the first model, the prestressed Timoshenko beam element is tested, geometric rigidity is calculated, and the eigenfrequencies are calculated by the Lanczos method. In the second model, the prestressed Euler-Bernouilli beam element is tested, geometric rigidity is calculated, and the eigenfrequencies are calculated by the Bathe and Wilson method.

The results obtained are in good agreement with the results of the VPCS guide. An upward shift in vibration frequencies is observed when the prestress in the beam increases.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The vibration equation of a prestressed beam is:

${\mathrm{EI}}_{z}\frac{{\partial}^{4}y}{\partial {x}^{4}}+P\frac{{\partial}^{2}y}{\partial {x}^{2}}=-\rho S\frac{{\partial}^{2}y}{\partial {x}^{2}}$

prestressing traction if $P>0$ , compression if $P<0$ , and leads to own bending frequencies ( Euler-Bernoulli hypothesis)

${f}_{i}=\frac{{i}^{2}\pi }{2{L}^{2}}{(1+\frac{{\mathit{PL}}^{2}}{{\mathit{EI}}_{z}{i}^{2}{\pi}^{2}})}^{1/2}{(\frac{{\mathit{EI}}_{z}}{\rho S})}^{1/2}$ , $i=1,2,3,\mathrm{...}$

### 2.2 Baseline

First five eigenfrequencies.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

Analytical solution (the Euler-Bernouilli beam hypothesis).
