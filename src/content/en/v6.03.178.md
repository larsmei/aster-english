# v6.03.178 SSNP178 – Cook Membrane in small plastic deformations

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to provide additional verification of modellings of incompressible type on the problem of Cook's membrane which is a very often used problem to test numerical methods in the literature.

## 1 Reference problem

The problem is the result of an article by J.C. Simo *and* *M* .S. Raifai [1] .

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry can be viewed on the figure and will be used for all modellings.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.178/10000201000002100000022AE3CEFA7E1D6B4F0C.png)

Figure 1.1-1: Géométrie de la membrane

Coordinate of node A(48.60) and node C (24.30).

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic almost incompressible, i.e. its Poisson's ratio tends towards $0,5$:

- Elasticity module: $E=70\mathit{MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0,499$

> The plasticity model is a Von Mises model with linear isotropic hardening

- Elastic limit: $\mathit{SY}=0,243\mathit{MPa}$
- Nutrient module: $\mathit{ET}=0,13474\mathit{MPa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The left side of the membrane ($\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=0$) is clamped and a vertical surface force applied to the right side such as $\mathit{FY}=0,1125\mathit{kN}/\mathit{mm}\mathrm{²}$

### 1.4 Extension to 3D

The extension to 3D is obtained by extruding in the $z$ direction (thickness $1\mathit{mm}$). Furthermore, the displacements according to DZ are blocked to return to the solution in flat deformations.
