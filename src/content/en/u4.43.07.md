# u4.43.07 Operator CALC_MATE_HOMO

## 1 Purpose

Command dedicated to the analyses of the homogenised linear thermal and thermoelastic properties for periodic heterogeneous structures with a rectangular pitch, with three-dimensional base cells symmetric with respect to the three planes of the Cartesian frame, cf. [R5.03.37](doc:r5.03.37).

Several situations can be treated:

- a three-dimensional homogenisation, for base cells periodic in the three spatial directions;
- a homogenisation within the framework of the theory of heterogeneous plates, in Love-Kirchhoff kinematics, in bending-membrane.
- a homogenisation within the framework of the theory of heterogeneous plates, in Mindlin kinematics with account taken of transverse shear.
- a homogenisation within the framework of the theory of heterogeneous plates, in Touratier kinematics with account taken of transverse shear.

For the homogenisation of the plates the base cells are periodic in the two spatial directions of the plane tangent to the plate or to the shell, the third spatial direction corresponding to the thickness of the latter.

The cases of periodic type with a "hexagonal pitch" can also be treated by defining a periodic and symmetric "sub-cell" with a square pitch.

The equivalent material computed by CALC_MATE_HOMO is orthotropic: the homogenised coefficients are given along the axes of the Cartesian frame of the mesh of the base cell.

The table produced contains: the homogenised orthotropic elasticity matrix and the orthotropic elasticity coefficients, the homogenised orthotropic expansion coefficients, an indicator of transverse isotropy where applicable, the homogenised density, the homogenised orthotropic conductivity matrix, the homogenised heat capacity.

The thermal and thermoelastic correctors produced are also accessible on output.

## 2 Syntax

```text
table = CALC_MATE_HOMO(
    ◆ MAILLAGE = maillage,
    ◆ AFFE = _F(
         ◆ / TOUT = "OUI" (ou non renseigné),
           / GROUP_MA = grma,
         ◆ MATER = mater,
      ),
    ◆ VARC = _F(
         ◆ NOM_VARC = / "TEMP",
                      / "IRRA",
         ◆ VALE = float,
      ),
    ◆ TYPE_HOMO = / "MASSIF",
                  / "PLAQUE",
    ◇ UNITE = unit,
    ◇ INFO = / 1 (par défaut),
             / 2,
    ◇ CORR_MECA = co,
    # Si: TYPE_HOMO == 'MASSIF'
        ◇ CORR_THER = co,
)

◆ : obligatoire
◇ : optionnel
⟐ : présent par défaut
& : ensemble
/ : un seul parmi
| : plusieurs choix possibles
```

## 3 Operands

### 3.1 Keyword MAILLAGE

Mesh of the base cell that one wants to use for the homogenisation analysis. It is supplied in the Cartesian frame $x, y, z$ . The straight boundaries of this base-cell mesh, parallel to the axes of the Cartesian frame $x, y, z$, are considered as planes of symmetry of this base cell.  These axes will be the axes of definition of the homogenised material.

One will be able to carry out the rotation of the frame with AFFE_CARA_ELEM (factor keyword MASSIF with TYPE_HOMO = 'MASSIF', or factor keyword COQUE with TYPE_HOMO = 'PLAQUE', cf. [u4.42.01](doc:u4.42.01)) in order to define directions of orthotropy according to the need of the study in the frame of the homogenised structure.

**Notes:**

- The mesh is that of an octant (1/8) of the base cell $\left(x\ge 0\phantom{\rule{0.5em}{0ex}};\phantom{\rule{0.5em}{0ex}}y\ge 0\phantom{\rule{0.5em}{0ex}};\phantom{\rule{0.5em}{0ex}}z\ge 0\right)$.
- The thickness of the mesh corresponds to half the thickness of the structure for a homogenisation with a view to an analysis with plate or shell elements in Love-Kirchhoff kinematics (operand TYPE_HOMO="PLAQUE"). The axis normal to the mean surface of the plate is necessarily Oz.
- The mesh is necessarily made with three-dimensional mesh cells.
- The mesh may contain cavities.
- Only the volume mesh-cell groups are used; it is not necessary to define any others.
- No group is necessary if one uses TOUT='OUI' in the factor keyword AFFE.

### 3.2 Factor keyword AFFE

> The factor keyword AFFE makes it possible to assign the various material concepts assigned to the mesh-cell groups constituting the base cell.

**Notes:**

- The assigned materials are obligatorily isotropic
- The materials are obligatorily functions
- The materials ELAS_FO, THER_FO or THER_NL are mandatory
- The intersection of the assignment groups must be empty
- The overloading rule applies

### 3.3 Factor keyword VARC

The factor keyword VARC makes it possible to define the command variable which will serve to extract the elastic and thermal parameters of the materials of the base cell, as well as the values of the command variable, uniform over the whole base cell: temperature or irradiation.

### 3.4 Operand TYPE_HOMO

Defines the type of approach on which one applies the homogenisation method:

- MASSIF for a three-dimensional homogenisation (thermoelastic: six elastic correctors, one expansion corrector, and thermal: three thermal correctors) with a view to a three-dimensional analysis in solid finite elements and
- PLAQUE for a homogenisation with a view to an elastic analysis with plate or shell elements in Love-Kirchhoff kinematics.
- PLAQUE_CT_MINDLIN for a homogenisation with a view to an elastic analysis with plate or shell elements with account taken of transverse shear in Mindlin kinematics.
- PLAQUE_CT_TOURATIER for a homogenisation with a view to an elastic analysis with plate or shell elements with account taken of transverse shear in Touratier kinematics.

### 3.5 Operands CORR_MECA and CORR_THER

The keywords CORR_MECA and CORR_THER (the latter only if: TYPE_HOMO == 'MASSIF') serve for the recovery of the correctors.

## 4 General points

This command serves to compute the equivalent thermal-conduction and thermoelastic properties for periodic structures by a homogenisation method.

In the general case, this type of operation cannot be automated, because the definition of the periodicities on the edges of the base cell is linked to the geometry to be treated, in particular its possible symmetries.

In order to arrive at an automated procedure it is necessary to restrict the use of CALC_MATE_HOMO to base cells with a parallelepiped pattern, so that the pairs of faces facing each other are trivial to identify. This has no impact on structures with heterogeneities of rectangular pitch, but on the other hand it obliges one to find a periodic pattern of rectangular pitch in the other situations (for a hexagonal pitch see the test cases hplv105c or hplv106)

Moreover, in order to take advantage of the symmetries, the use of CALC_MATE_HOMO is restricted to the base cells which are also symmetric with respect to the 3 axes: this leads to an equivalent orthotropic homogenised material. The mesh thus corresponds to the first octant (1/8) of the base cell $\left(x\ge 0\phantom{\rule{0.5em}{0ex}};\phantom{\rule{0.5em}{0ex}}y\ge 0\phantom{\rule{0.5em}{0ex}};\phantom{\rule{0.5em}{0ex}}z\ge 0\right)$. This choice leads to an orthotropic equivalent material, and to the absence of bending–membrane coupling for the homogenised plates.
