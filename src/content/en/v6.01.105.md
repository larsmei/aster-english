# v6.01.105 SSNA105 - Pressured hollow cylinder, linear viscoelasticity, contact

**Summary:**

This test case validates the LEMAITRE law implanted in *Code_Aster* in the case of linear viscoelastic behavior. The results found are compared to an analytical solution.

This test is based on the same model as the test case SSNA104A to which a cylinder (pastille) is added and the contact is processed.

### 1.2 Material properties

The tablet consists of elastic material, the sheath consists of viscoelastic material.

The elastic data coincide for both materials.

Young's modulus: $E=1\mathit{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.3$

Law of LEMAITRE:

$g(\sigma ,\lambda ,T)={(\frac{1}{K}\frac{\sigma}{{\lambda}^{\frac{1}{m}}})}^{n}$ with $\frac{1}{K}=1$ , $\frac{1}{m}=0$ , $n=1$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

**boundary conditions :**

The cylinder is blocked in $\mathit{DY}$ on the $[\mathit{AP},\mathit{BP}]$, $[\mathit{AG},\mathit{BG}]$ and $[\mathit{CP},\mathit{PD}][\mathit{CG},\mathit{PG}]$ sides.

**Charge:**

The cylinder is subjected to internal pressure on $[\mathit{DP},\mathit{AP}]$ , this pressure is calculated so that at the moment $t=0$ , the sheath has the same behavior as the cylinder modelled in the ssna104a test.

$$
p_1(t) =
\begin{cases}
\left[\dfrac{r_3}{r_2} - 1\right] E \left(\dfrac{r_2^2 - r_1^2}{2r_1^2(1-\nu)}\right) & \text{si } -1 \le t \le 0 \\\\
A\left[B(r_3 - r_2 + C(D + G e^{-Ekt} + Ht)) + K\right] & \text{si } 0 < t \le 5
\end{cases}
$$

with

$$
A = \frac{r_2^2 - r_1^2}{2r_2^2(1-\nu)}, \quad
B = \frac{E}{r_2(1+\nu)}, \quad
C = \frac{P_0 r_3^3}{r_4^2 - r_3^2}, \quad
P_0 = 1.E^{-3} \text{ MPa}
$$

$$
D = \frac{1}{E}\left[(1+\nu)\frac{r_4^2}{r_3^2} + \frac{3}{2}(1-2\nu)\right],
$$

$$
G = -\frac{(1-2\nu)^2}{2E}, \quad
H = \frac{3}{2}k\,\frac{r_4^2}{r_3^2}, \quad
K = \frac{P_0 r_2^2}{r_2^2 - r_1^2}
\left[1 - 2\nu + \frac{r_1^2}{r_2^2}\right]
$$

It is recalled that ${P}_{0}$ is the pressure of the ssna104a test. However, in this case, contact between the two cylinders (cylinder and sheath) is treated.

## 2 Reference solutions

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solutions

All of this demonstration can be read with more details in the [bib1] document.

**Contactless phase**

The ${p}_{1}(t)$ value to be applied to the inner wall of the tablet for which the contact takes place is to be found.

For the pastille, we find:

$\sigma =\left(\begin{array}{ccc}\gamma (1-\frac{{r}_{2}^{2}}{{r}^{2}})& 0& 0\\ 0& \gamma (1+\frac{{r}_{2}^{2}}{{r}^{2}})& 0\\ 0& 0& 2\nu \gamma \end{array}\right)$ où $\gamma =\frac{{p}_{1}(t){r}_{1}^{2}}{{r}_{2}^{2}-{r}_{1}^{2}}$

${\varepsilon}_{\theta}=\frac{1+\nu }{E}\gamma \left[1-2\nu +\frac{{r}_{2}^{2}}{{r}^{2}}\right]=\frac{w}{r}$ .

Contact condition writing: $w({r}_{3})-w({r}_{2})=0$ , we have ${r}_{3}-{r}_{2}={r}_{2}\frac{2(1+\nu )\gamma }{E}(1-\nu )$

D’où $\gamma =(\frac{{r}_{3}}{{r}_{2}}-1)\frac{E}{2{(1-\nu )}^{2}}$

${p}_{1}\lim=(\frac{{r}_{3}}{{r}_{2}}-1)\frac{E({r}_{2}^{2}-{r}_{1}^{2})}{2{r}_{1}^{2}{(1-\nu )}^{2}}$ .

**Contact phase**

We want from the moment $t=0$ , the sheath to have the same behavior as in the ssna104a test.

When there is contact, we have:

${w}_{P}({r}_{2})={W}_{G}({r}_{3})+{r}_{3}-{r}_{2}$ ,

So by recovering the value of the moves in the ssna104 test, we must obtain:

${w}_{P}({r}_{2})={r}_{3}-{r}_{2}+\frac{{p}_{0}{r}_{3}^{3}}{{r}_{4}^{2}-{r}_{3}^{2}}\left\lbrace \frac{1}{E}((1+\nu )\frac{{r}_{4}^{2}}{{r}_{3}^{2}}+\frac{1-2\nu }{2}(3-(1-2\nu ){e}^{-\mathit{Ekt}}))+\frac{3}{2}k\frac{{r}_{4}^{2}}{{r}_{3}^{2}}t\right\rbrace$ .

The stress field of the tablet is given by

$\sigma =\left(\begin{array}{ccc}{\gamma}_{1}(1-\frac{{r}_{2}^{2}}{{r}^{2}})-{\gamma}_{0}(1-\frac{{r}_{2}^{2}}{{r}^{2}})& 0& 0\\ 0& {\gamma}_{1}(1+\frac{{r}_{2}^{2}}{{r}^{2}})-{\gamma}_{0}(1+\frac{{r}_{2}^{2}}{{r}^{2}})& 0\\ 0& 0& {\sigma}_{Z}\end{array}\right)$

with ${\gamma}_{1}=\frac{{p}_{1}{r}_{1}^{2}}{{r}_{2}^{2}-{r}_{1}^{2}}$ and ${\gamma}_{0}=\frac{{p}_{0}{r}_{1}^{2}}{{r}_{2}^{2}-{r}_{1}^{2}}$ .

Like ${\varepsilon}_{Z}=\frac{1+\nu }{E}{\sigma}_{Z}-\frac{\nu}{E}(2({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{0})+{\sigma}_{Z})=0$ , we find: ${\sigma}_{Z}=2\nu ({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{0})$ .

So we have ${\varepsilon}_{\theta}=\frac{1+\nu }{E}{\sigma}_{\theta}-\frac{\nu}{E}({\sigma}_{r}+{\sigma}_{\theta}+{\sigma}_{Z})=\frac{1+\nu }{E}\left[(1-2\nu )({\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{0})+{\gamma}_{1}\frac{{r}_{2}^{2}}{{r}^{2}}-{\gamma}_{0}\frac{{r}_{1}^{2}}{{r}^{2}}\right]=\frac{w}{r}$

${w}_{P}({r}_{2})=\frac{1+\nu }{E}{r}_{2}\left[2(1-\nu ){\gamma}_{1}-{\gamma}_{0}(1-2\nu +\frac{{r}_{1}^{2}}{{r}_{2}^{2}})\right]$, we find ${p}_{1}(t)$ given by the formula a little higher.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The problem is modelled in axisymmetry. Contact is handled by the discrete formulation (`DEFI_CONTACT`, with `FORMULATION` = `DISCRETE`):

- the slave interface corresponds to the cylinder interface (inner block),
- The master interface corresponds to the interface of the sheath (outside block).

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

600 mesh cells `QUAD4`

160 mesh cells `SEG2`

## 6 Summary of the results

The results calculated by *Code_Aster* are consistent with the analytical solutions but depend very heavily on the refinement of the mesh. Both methods of contact consideration (discreet and continuous formulation) give the same results.
