# v2.06.103 SHLL103 – Harmonic response of a rotor with two non-symmetric discs and two bearings, subject to gyroscopic effect

**Summary:**

This test validates the calculation of the harmonic response of a rotating tree system with consideration of the gyroscopic effect and nonsymmetric bearings.

In this test, a rotor model with two discs, supported by two hydrodynamic bearings, is nonsymmetric, with stiffness and linearized damping matrices, and the corresponding reference results are taken from the ROTORINSA qualification manual, [bib2], a finite elements calculation software designed to predict dynamic bending rotor behaviour.

A good match is observed between the Code_Aster and reference solution results.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

To block rigid body-type movements in the $y$ direction, the degrees of freedom $\mathrm{DY}$ and $\mathrm{DRY}$ are blocked at node $\mathrm{B1}$ bearing.

The load is a harmonic force at a constant rotation speed.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution is the one provided by ROTORINSA code, finished elements code (Timoshenko beam type) to predict dynamic bending rotor behaviour.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

These are harmonic force response calculations with four different rotor rotation speeds: 0, 40000, 50000 and 60000 $\mathit{tr}/min$.

The frequency of the harmonic force is $1\mathit{Hz}$ , $25\mathit{Hz}$ and $250\mathit{Hz}$ successively.

At each rotor rotation speed, two calculations are performed:

- unit harmonic effort following $X$ applied to node disk $\mathit{D2}$
- unit harmonic effort following $Z$ applied to node disk $\mathit{D2}$

Each time, the maximums of amplitude and phase are recorded at node of the $\mathit{D2}$ disk.

### 2.3 Bibliographical references

1. ROTORINSA, finished elements software designed to predict dynamic bending rotor behavior, LaMCoS UMR5259, INSA-Lyon.
