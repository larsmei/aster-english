# v2.05.103 SDLX103 – Structure - ground interaction - structure between two buried buildings

**Summary:**

This test contributes to the validation of the *code_aster* -MISS3D chaining by the frequent coupling method. It represents a typical case of ground coupling between 2 buried buildings, i.e. a case of structural-soil-structure interaction. Two twin buildings each represented by a model-brochette model 1D, each embedded in a stratified floor, are tested. A harmonic force, of constant module for each frequency between $0,1\mathit{Hz}$ and $20\mathit{Hz}$, is imposed at the top of one of the buildings.

The module of the direct response at the top of the excited building and the module of the coupled response at the top of the adjacent building are tested against the values obtained during a test programme on structures representative of nuclear buildings subject to seismic risk: the NUPEC program. The correspondence between calculations and tests is very satisfactory.

## 1 Reference problem

MISS3D software uses the frequent coupling method to account for the soil-structure interaction.
This method, based on dynamic substructure, consists of dividing the field of study into three sub-domains:

- soil,
- the foundation,
- the building.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and mechanical loadings

Each of the 2 restraints between a 1D model and its foundation is achieved by a solid restraint condition between the
foundation and the node common to the building model. This node is blocked and a solid body movement is imposed on the building model.
Remove.

buildings, with a $F={F}_{o}\sin\alpha t$ harmonic loading with ${F}_{o}$ force module
$10\mathit{kN}$ with a pulse that varies from $0$ to $20\mathit{Hz}$ by step

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Baseline

The maximum of the module of the direct response at the top of the excited building and the maximum of the module of the coupled response at the top of the adjacent building are tested in relation to the values obtained in a test programme on structures representative of nuclear buildings subject to seismic risk: the NUPEC [bib1] programme.

Detailed study of the results of the calculations was carried out.

1. KITADA & al: "Models test on dynamic structure-structure interaction of nuclear power plant buildings. Nuclear Engineering and Design 192 (1999) 205-216.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The characteristics used and the mesh are those derived from the v2.05.103-problem-reference data.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh supplied to *code_aster* contains mesh cells type SEG2 to model the building structure with elements beam and mesh cells types QUAD4, TRIA3 to model the foundation with elements DKT. It is important to have oriented the elements foundation surface with normal grounding. The mesh cells side walls representing the sinking are generated by the same vertical generator as MISS3D requires.
