# v5.02.138 SDNL138 - Frequency and clean vibration modes of a self-contraning frame

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the calculation of the frequencies and vibration modes of a self-contraning frame. The forces in the structure are generated only by the tensioning of the cables. The principle of the test case is:

- perform a nonlinear calculation,
- recover the different fields at a given moment,
- calculate the stiffness and mass matrices,
- calculate the vibration frequencies and modes.

Several modellings are made:

- calculation with STAT_NON_LINE, use of CALC_MATR_ELEM, ASSE_MATRICE and COMB_MATR_ASSE operators for the assembly of matrices. Then calculation of the frequencies and vibration modes with CALC_MODES operator.
- calculation with DYNA_NON_LINE and MODE_VIBR option.
- STAT_NON_LINE calculation, stiffness matrix determination using CALCUL operator. Then calculation of the vibration frequencies and modes with CALC_MODES operator.

The results are compared to a theoretical solution.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution chosen is the one obtained by solving the vibrating string equation:

![Figure](/figures/v5.02.138/1000000000000207000001DBAF1FE05E64E73BB7.png)

The equation of vibrating strings is:

$\frac{{\partial}^{2}y}{\partial {t}^{2}}=\frac{{F}_{0}}{\rho S}\frac{{\partial}^{2}y}{\partial {x}^{2}}$

with:

${F}_{0}$: tension in the rope,

$\rho$: density,

$S$: section of the rope.

For a $L$ length rope, fixed to these 2 ends, the nth proper vibration frequency is:

$f=\frac{n}{2L}\sqrt{\frac{{F}_{0}}{\rho S}}$

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The quantities tested are the clean vibration frequencies of the cables for 2 calculation moments, which correspond to 2 different voltages.

| **Not** | **Cable Tension** | **Frequency** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | $6171.050459855N$ | $62.73227096292042\mathit{Hz}$ |
| 20 | $12345.2954376N$ | $88.72830898253936\mathit{Hz}$ |

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

The vibrating string equation solution is obtained under the following assumptions:

- the movements of the rope around its equilibrium position remain small.
- $\alpha$ angle variations remain small.
- The variation in tension of the moving rope remains small.

Under these assumptions, the reference solution is unsure.

### 5.4 Remarks

The first calculation is done with the STAT_NON_LINE operator. The CALCUL operator, which has the input fields of displacement, constraint, internal variables and an increment of displacements, allows you to know the stiffness matrix of the system. The calculation of the frequencies and vibration modes is done with the CALC_MODES operator using the stiffness matrix which comes from the CALCUL operator.

The CALCUL operator assumes that the fields are linear as a function of the increment of the movements. However, in the case of cables this hypothesis is not quite right. This displacement increment should allow to approximate the matrix the tangent, so it must be small. It is determined by a sensitivity study. That is why this Modelling is a test of non-regression.

## 6 Summary of the results

The results of modellings A, B and C are in good match with the reference solution.

The results of Modelling C (only for educational purposes) require a sensitivity study to find the displacement increment that allows to approximate the tangent matrix and to stow the reference solution. This is due to the linearity hypothesis of the CALCUL operator.
