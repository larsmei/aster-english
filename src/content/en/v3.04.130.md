# v3.04.130 SSLV130 - Hollow cylinder in incompressible

**Summary:**

This test allows the validation of elements near-incompressible static for a three-dimensional, axisymmetric or two-dimensional (plane deformations) problem. A hollow cylinder is considered to be subjected to internal pressure. The material has an Poisson's ratio equal to 0.4999 and the elements near-incompressible (modelling INCO_UPG and INCO_UP) is used. In all Modelling cases, the test is performed by imposing or not the perfect d-incompressibility condition if necessary (DDL_IMPO and GONF=0).

Five modellings are done for this problem.

INCO_UPG Modelling is tested in the following configurations:

- $A$ Modelling: 3D_INCO_UPG Modelling with HEXA20;
- $B$ Modelling: 3D_INCO_UPG Modelling with TETRA10;
- $E$ Modelling: 3D_INCO_UPG Modelling with PENTA15;
- $C$ Modelling: D_PLAN_INCO_UPG Modelling with a mixed mesh TRIA6/QUAD8;
- $D$ Modelling: AXIS_INCO_UPG Modelling with a mixed mesh TRIA6/QUAD8.

INCO_UP Modelling is tested in the following configurations:

- $A$ Modelling: 3D_INCO_UP Modelling with HEXA20;
- $B$ Modelling: 3D_INCO_UP Modelling with TETRA10 and TETRA4;
- $C$ Modelling: D_PLAN_INCO_UP Modelling with a mixed mesh TRIA6/QUAD8 and with TRIA3;
- $D$ Modelling: AXIS_INCO_UP Modelling with a mixed mesh TRIA6/QUAD8 and with TRIA3.

INCO_UPO Modelling is tested in the following configurations:

- $A$ Modelling: 3D_INCO_UPO Modelling with HEXA8;
- $B$ Modelling: 3D_INCO_UPO Modelling with TETRA4;
- $E$ Modelling: 3D_INCO_UPO Modelling with PENTA6;
- $C$ Modelling: D_PLAN_INCO_UPO Modelling with a mixed mesh TRIA6/QUAD8;
- $D$ Modelling: AXIS_INCO_UPO Modelling with a mixed mesh TRIA6/QUAD8.

HHO Modelling is tested in the following configurations:

- $G$ Modelling: AXIS_HHO Modelling with a mixed mesh TRIA6/QUAD8.

This test is similar to the SSLV100 test.

The numerical results are satisfactory for all modellings. The explicit imposition of the condition of incompressibility n- only has a very limited effect on the results. The linear elements for 2-field modelling is logically a little less precise than the quadratic elements.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The general solution on the move is as follows:

$\left\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{u}_{r}=\frac{{\mathit{Pa}}^{2}}{E({b}^{2}-{a}^{2})}(1+\nu )\left[(1-2\nu )r+\frac{{b}^{2}}{r}\right]\\ {u}_{\theta}={u}_{z}=0\end{array}\right.$

In deformations:

$\left\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{rr}}=\frac{{\mathit{Pa}}^{2}}{E({b}^{2}-{a}^{2})}(1+\nu )\left[(1-2\nu )-\frac{{b}^{2}}{r}\right]\\ {\varepsilon}_{\theta \theta }=\frac{{\mathit{Pa}}^{2}}{E({b}^{2}-{a}^{2})}(1+\nu )\left[(1-2\nu )+\frac{{b}^{2}}{r}\right]\\ {\varepsilon}_{r\theta }={\varepsilon}_{zz}=0\end{array}\right.$

Constraints:

$\left\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{\sigma}_{\mathit{rr}}=P\frac{{a}^{2}}{{b}^{2}-{a}^{2}}\left[1-\frac{{b}^{2}}{r}\right]\\ {\sigma}_{\theta \theta }=P\frac{{a}^{2}}{{b}^{2}-{a}^{2}}\left[1+\frac{{b}^{2}}{r}\right]\\ {\sigma}_{zz}=2\nu P\frac{{a}^{2}}{{b}^{2}-{a}^{2}}\\ {\sigma}_{r\theta }=0\end{array}\right.$

For a perfectly incompressible cylinder $(\nu =0.5)$:

|  | $r=0,1$ | $r=0,2$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ${u}_{r}$ | $6.{10}^{-5}\mathrm{mm}$ | $3.{10}^{-5}\mathrm{mm}$ |
| ${\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{rr}}$ | $-6.{10}^{-4}$ | $-1,5{10}^{-4}$ |
| ${\varepsilon}_{\theta \theta }$ | $-6.{10}^{-4}$ | $1,5{10}^{-4}$ |
| ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}$ | $-60\mathrm{MPa}$ | $0\mathrm{MPa}$ |
| ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }$ | $100\mathrm{MPa}$ | $40\mathrm{MPa}$ |
| ${\sigma}_{zz}$ | $20\mathrm{MPa}$ | $20\mathrm{MPa}$ |

The transition to the Cartesian system is made by the following relationships:

$\begin{array}{c}{\sigma}_{xx}={\sigma}_{\mathit{rr}}\cos{(\theta )}^{2}+{\sigma}_{\theta \theta }\sin{(\theta )}^{2}-2{\sigma}_{r\theta }\sin(\theta )\cos(\theta )\\ {\sigma}_{yy}={\sigma}_{\mathit{rr}}\sin{(\theta )}^{2}+{\sigma}_{\theta \theta }\cos{(\theta )}^{2}+2{\sigma}_{r\theta }\sin(\theta )\cos(\theta )\\ {\sigma}_{xy}={\sigma}_{\mathit{rr}}\sin(\theta )\cos(\theta )-{\sigma}_{\theta \theta }\cos(\theta )\sin(\theta )-2{\sigma}_{r\theta }(\cos{(\theta )}^{2}-\sin{(\theta )}^{2})\end{array}$

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The reference values are compared with:

- $(u,v)$ moves to $A$ and $F$ points,
- the deformations (${\varepsilon}_{xx}$ , ${\varepsilon}_{yy}$ , ${\varepsilon}_{xy}$ ) and the stresses (${\sigma}_{xx}$ , ${\sigma}_{yy}$ , ${\sigma}_{zz}$ , ${\sigma}_{xy}$ ) at the points $A$ and $F$ ,
- equivalent deformations and stresses equivalent to point $A$ .

Finally, to test the transition of quantities from Gauss points to nodes for nodes media, we also test the non-zero deformations and stresses in a mid-node structure.
