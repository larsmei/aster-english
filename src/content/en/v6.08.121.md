# v6.08.121 SSND121 – Validation of non-linear behaviourCHOC_ELAS_TRAC

**Summary:**

The response of the CHOC_ELAS_TRAC nonlinear behavior model, formulated on discrete elements for mesh cells SEG2, is tested.

The STAT_NON_LINE operator is used for validation. Discrete d的elements response is analyzed for nonlinear constitutive law under a cyclic load.

The modellings and elements discrete tested are in $3D$ with DIS_T Modelling on mesh cells SEG2.

This behavior is also validated for dynamic stresses in the test case SDND110[V5.01.110] with DYNA_VIBRA and DYNA_NON_LINE operators.
