# v1.01.319 ZZZZ319 – MACR_ADAP_MAIL IT Validation

**Summary:**

The test case aims to validate some of the options in the MACR_ADAP_MAIL command computerically.

## 1 Modelling A

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry, presented in the following figure, is composed of two squares on the $1m$ side.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/10000000000001CE000000ED8A3A8CDCA12341E3.png)

### 1.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh is 2D, quadratic, and is presented in the following figure:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000E70000007FD68A5210BBAB07FEE.png)

It consists of 14 nodes for an mesh cell QUAD8 component of the FACE_1 group and two mesh cells TRIA6 component of the FACE_2 group. The onboard segments are present as mesh cells SEG3 and are stored in BAS, HAUT, DROITE, GAUCHE groups.

### 1.3 Adaptations made and resulting meshes

#### 1.3.1 First series of adaptations

Two refinements are made from the initial mesh and from a field of internal variables, with a proportion of mesh cells to be refined by 50%. For the first component of the field, $\mathit{V1}$ , the $-1$ value is assigned to the nodes of the n°1 face and the $-2$ value is assigned to the nodes of the n°2 face. The keyword USAGE_CMP is equal to RELATIF for the first adaptation and ABSOLU for the second. The aim is to compare the results of the two options. Therefore, one expects to refine the n°1 face to the first adaptation and the n°2 face to the second. To test this, the field created by MACR_ADAP_MAIL with the ADD_CHAM=(_F(CHAM_CAT='DIAMETRE') option will be used. This field contains the value of the diameter of each mesh cell after refinement. For triangles resulting from the conformance refinement, it is the length of the largest side of which is the value of ADD_CHAM=(_F(CHAM_CAT='DIAMETRE'=X)

The following figure shows the mesh adapted with USAGE_CMP = RELATIF:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000D80000006DB6945C09DB9A82B75.png)

In accordance with our expectations, face one is being refined.

*Note:*

The validation, starting from the diameter value, will be performed on mesh cells 15 and 23.

The following figure shows the mesh adapted with USAGE_CMP = ABSOLU:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000D80000006DB54FBB0192F831189.png)

In accordance with our expectations, face 2 is being refined.

*Note:*

Validation, from the diameter value, will be performed on mesh cells 12 and 20

#### 1.3.2 Second series of adaptations

Two uniform refinement cycles - de-refining are made from the initial mesh. This time, the mesh output of the $n$ adaptation is used as mesh input of the $n+1$ adaptation. For refinements, a resulting field corresponding to the number of refinements per mesh cell is created via the keywords ADD_CHAM=(_F(CHAM_CAT='NIVEAU',)). For de-fining, this field is used as a criterion with the NIVE_MIN=1 option. We are looking to check that we will not de-finize at the end of the first cycle and that we will de-finize at the end of the second cycle.

The adapted mesh after the first cycle is shown in the following figure:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000D80000006DB844D9F6421D56612.png)

The refinement worked while the derefining was not achieved.

*Note:*

The validation, starting from the 'NIVEAU' field, will be performed on the mesh cell 17. At the end of the first cycle as the derefining has not been done, the value of this field must be equal to 1.

The adapted mesh after the second refinement is presented in the following figure:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/100000000000035B000001B1B9C0006C5CCB53DE.png)

The new refinement has been achieved.

*Note:*

The validation, starting from the 'NIVEAU' field, will be performed on the mesh cell 44. At the end of the refinement of the second cycle the value of this field must be equal to 2.

The adapted mesh after the second cycle is shown in the following figure:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000D80000006DB844D9F6421D56612.png)

The de-refining of the second cycle was therefore carried out.

*Note:*

The validation, starting from the 'NIVEAU' field, will be performed on the mesh cell 17. At the end of the second cycle as the derefining has worked, the value of this field must be again equal to 1.

## 2 Modelling B

### 2.1 Geometry

The geometry, presented in the following figure, is composed of a base triangle $\mathrm{1m}$ and height $\mathrm{1m}$.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000584000002CA52D4A07263A5345D.png)

### 2.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh is 2D, quadratic and presented by the following figure:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000584000002CA12798E944A172410.png)

It is composed of 6 nodes for a mesh cell TRIA6.

### 2.3 Adaptations made and resulting meshes

A nodes displacement field is created and the $\mathit{DX}$ component is initialized to zero for all nodes, except the node $A$ (responded to in the previous figure) for which $\mathit{DX}=1$ is used.

Two uniform refinements are made with the update of the displacement field. The first update is made with the CH_AUTO option and the second with the CH_ISOP2 option. The expected updated field should therefore not be the same at the end of the two adaptations.

The following figure shows the initial field:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000536000002CAD941B5FB4CFC1405.png)

*Note:*

The validation, from the updated field, will be performed on the nodes 2 and 4. The value of $\mathit{DX}$ to $\mathit{N2}$ must be equal to 1 while in $\mathit{N4}$ it is null.

The following figure shows the field resulting from the adaptation with the CH_AUTO option:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000536000002CA2FE42E9CB9FF3249.png)

It appears, as expected, that the interpolated field does not respect the extreme values of the initial field (confer U7.03.01, §4.15.5).

*Note:*

The validation, from the updated field, will be performed on nodes 2, 4 and 8. The values at nodes 2 and 4 must be identical to those in the initial field. For node 8, with the CH_AUTO option, the $\mathit{P2}$ , ${N}_{Ni}(i\in [1,6])$ (confer R3.01.01 §3.1) shape functions of the original mesh are used for interpolation. It is therefore expected to be found: ${\mathit{DX}}_{\mathit{N8}}=\sum_{1}^{6}{\mathit{DX}}_{Ni}.{N}_{Ni}({\xi}_{\mathit{N8}},{\eta}_{\mathit{N8}})$ for ${\xi}_{\mathit{N8}}=1/4$ , ${\eta}_{\mathit{N8}}=0$ , ${\mathit{DX}}_{\mathit{N2}}=1.$ and ${\mathit{DX}}_{Ni}=0.(i\mathrm{\ne }2)$ i.e.: ${\mathit{DX}}_{\mathit{N8}}=1.(-(1.-0.25).(1.-2.(1.-0.25)))=0.375$ .

The following figure shows the field resulting from the adaptation with the CH_ISOP2 option:

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.319/1000000000000536000002CA4B0FFCCEEA2A9CEF.png)

It appears, as expected, that with the CH_ISOP2 option, the interpolated field respects the extreme values of the initial field (confer U7.03.01, §4.15.5).

*Note:*

Nodes 2, 4 and 8 are identified as they are used for validation. The values at nodes 2 and 4 must be identical to those in the initial field. For node 8, with the CH_ISOP2 option, $\mathit{P1}$, ${N}_{Ni}(i\in [1,3])$ (confer R3.01.01 §3.1) functions expressed on the mesh cells sub-element of the original mesh are used for interpolation. Therefore, it is expected to be found: ${\mathit{DX}}_{\mathit{N8}}=\sum_{1}^{3}{\mathit{DX}}_{Ni}.{N}_{Ni}({\xi}_{\mathit{N8}},{\eta}_{\mathit{N8}})$ for ${\xi}_{\mathit{N8}}=1/2$, ${\eta}_{\mathit{N8}}=0$, ${\mathit{DX}}_{\mathit{N2}}=1.$ and ${\mathit{DX}}_{Ni}=0.(i\mathrm{\ne }2)$: ${\mathit{DX}}_{\mathit{N8}}=1.(1.-0.5)=0.5$.
