# v6.04.178 SSNV178 – Cylinder with pressure-reinforced frame

**Summary:**

A cylinder with pressure-rebar is considered. More precisely, a 3d hollow cylinder is completed on its outer face with a circumferential frame. This structure is charged by an internal pressure. The calculation is elastic linear.

The simulation is compared with an analytical solution to validate modellings of the grid_membrane-type frame structure.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.178/10000200000002960000014A1411D26ED09645B3.png)

The cylinder has an internal $a=10m$ radius, an external $b=20m$ radius, a height of $h=\mathrm{0.02m}$

The circumferential frame, positioned on the outer surface of the cylinder, has one section per unit length of $s=0.1{m}^{2}/\mathit{ml}$. The main direction of the frame is vertical.

Only one portion of the cylinder, corresponding to an $\theta ={0.1}^{°}$ angular cross section, is modelled.

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.178/100002000000013F000001217B0214EBCA47C525.png)

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.178/10000200000001AC0000015E2C3EB6ECC8F99DD0.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The material characteristics of the concrete forming the hollow cylinder are:

- $E=20000\mathrm{MPa}$  , $\nu =0.2$

The material characteristics of the frame are:

- $E=200000\mathrm{MPa}$ , $\nu =0.0$ , ${\sigma}_{y}=200000\mathrm{MPa}$ , ${E}_{\mathrm{cr}}=20000\mathrm{MPa}$

To model the reinforcement grids, RELATION='GRILLE_ISOT_LINE' is used in the stat_non_line command. To do this, the behavior assigned to the reinforcement grids must define the plastic characteristics. In all modellings the behaviour of the steels must remain in the elastic domain, ${\sigma}_{y}$ is given large enough to meet this condition.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The following boundary conditions are imposed on the modeled angular section:

- zero displacement on the lower surface of the cylinder
- a normal zero displacement on the side faces of the cylinder section

The load consists of a pressure imposed ${10}^{6}\mathrm{Pa}$ on the internal surface $\mathit{SI}$ of the cylinder section.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Initially, displacement and stress are zero everywhere.

## 2 reference solution

The analytical solution of the problem is presented in the test case SSNS107 [V6.05.107](doc:v6.05.107) documentation.

All behaviour of materials remains in the elastic domain.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

3D volume modelling for concrete is used and GRILLE_MEMBRANE model is used for reinforcements with mesh cells supports as quadrangles.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains:

- 100 mesh cells hexa20 for concrete
- 1 mesh cell QUAD8 for steel frame

### 3.3 Remarks

- All behaviour of materials remains in the elastic domain.
- The displacements are evaluated at $A$ and $B$ points described above.
- Deformities and stresses are evaluated in the mesh cell of the concrete adjacent to the internal surface and in the mesh cell of the concrete adjacent to the external surface.

## 5 Summary of the results

The different modellings of this test case validates the GRILLE_MEMBRANE behavior for a complete structure (cylinder with frame). The results are compared to an analytical solution.
