# v5.06.107 SDNS107 – Transitional Response of a reinforced concrete slab: model with GRILLE_EXCENTRE

**Summary:**

This test validates in linear transient dynamics the Modelling of square reinforced concrete plate using for concrete a model of plate DKT and for reinforcements elements of grid-membrane GRILLE_EXCENTRE. We check the frequencies of eigenmodes, temporal responses on the move, reactions, and kinetic energy, for a sinusoidal loading.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry used in this test case is a reinforced concrete plate of $e=0.1\text{m}$ thickness and $l=1\text{m}$ length.

![Figure](/figures/v5.06.107/Object_4.svg)

**Figure** 1.1-a **: Geometry studied**

The characteristics of the steel sheets of the reinforced concrete plate are:

- Upper tablecloth: section per linear metre $=0.05{m}^{\mathrm{²}}/\mathrm{ml}$; de-centrement compared to average sheet: $+0.03m$ ,
- Lower tablecloth: section per linear meter $=0.05{m}^{\mathrm{²}}/\mathrm{ml}$; excentered from average sheet: $–0.03m$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

The material characteristics for multilayer concrete modelling with steel reinforcements (DKT and GRILLE_EXCENTRE) are summarized in the following table.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On the $A$ ($\mathrm{B0X}$) side of the plate the ${u}_{x}={u}_{y}={u}_{z}=0$ moves are embedded, as well as ${\theta}_{x}={\theta}_{y}={\theta}_{z}=0$ rotations. During the modal calculation, the ${u}_{y}=0$ displacement is blocked everywhere on the plate. A linear force is applied to the $\mathrm{B1X}$ side (opposed to $\mathrm{B0X}$) in the $(0.0,0.0,1,0)$ direction and depends on ${F}_{0}={10}^{6}N$ value.

In the case of dynamic calculation, a sinusoidal linear force is applied to the $\mathrm{B1X}$ side in the $(0.0,0.0,1.0)$ direction. The frequency of the sinusoidal is $20\mathrm{Hz}$ . The duration of the stress is $0,1s$ .

### 1.4 Initial conditions

In the initial state, the movements and speeds are zero everywhere.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

It is possible to calculate the frequencies of the first and second modes of bending vibration of the plate as it functions as a beam-console.

The ${f}_{1}$ frequency of the first eigenmode is written:

${f}_{1}=\frac{3,5156}{2\pi {L}^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{EI}}{\rho}}$

with $L$ the length of the console (1 m here), $\mathrm{EI}$ *l* e produces bending inertia by the Young's modulus for the complete structure and $\rho$ the mass of the structure per unit of length.

Similarly, the ${f}_{2}$ frequency of the second mode ssss:

${f}_{2}=\frac{22,0336}{2\pi {L}^{2}}\sqrt{\frac{\mathrm{EI}}{\rho}}$

To calculate ${f}_{1}$ and ${f}_{2}$ , the concrete and reinforcing parts are broken down:

$(\mathrm{EI})={(\mathrm{EI})}_{\mathrm{beton}}+{(\mathrm{EI})}_{\mathrm{acier}}$

où

${(\mathrm{EI})}_{\mathrm{acier}}=2{E}_{\mathrm{acier}}({\mathrm{sL}}_{2}){e}_{\mathrm{exc}}^{2}$

with ${E}_{\mathrm{acier}}$ the Young's modulus of steel, $s$ the section of the frames per linear meter and ${e}_{\mathrm{exc}}$ the excentre of the rebar sheets in relation to the average sheet, and:

${(\mathrm{EI})}_{\mathrm{beton}}={E}_{\mathrm{beton}}{L}_{2}\frac{{e}^{3}}{12}$

where ${E}_{\mathrm{beton}}$ is the Young's modulus of the concrete.

For mass per unit length, the concrete mass and the steel mass shall be broken down.

Using the previous equations, it becomes possible to calculate the frequency of the modes considered. The results are:

| **Frequency** | **Reference** |
| --- | --- |
| First bending mode | $54,67\mathrm{Hz}$ |
| Second bending mode | $342,64\mathrm{Hz}$ |

The centre of gravity is located at the centre of the console, so its coordinates are: $(0.5,0.05,0)$ . L-inertia according to the y axis of the complete structure is:

${I}_{yy}(G)={\int}_{V}({(x-{x}_{G})}^{2}+{(z-{z}_{G})}^{2})\rho \mathrm{dv}$

$({x}_{G},{y}_{G},{z}_{G})$ the coordinates of the centre of gravity, $V$ the volume of the structure and $\rho$ its density. By decomposing the elements linked to concrete and those linked to steel, it is possible to calculate inertia analytically:

${I}_{yy}(G)=8,611{m}^{4}$

## 5 Summary of the results

The difference Modelling is a small difference between the solutions obtained by the two calculation codes. It is considered reasonable and the results are considered satisfactory. Although this test was designed to approach Code_Aster Modelling as closely as possible to that of Europlexus code, one is aware, among other things, of the difference in the calculation of the mass matrix. The mass matrix implemented in Europlexus follows the method proposed in [1], which is better suited for an explicit fast dynamic calculation software. Code_Aster uses a more standard approach, explained in [2]. The difference concerns, in particular, the calculation of inertias (components of the mass matrix corresponding to the degrees of freedom rotation), which are not important in the case of slow dynamics. However, in fast dynamics, they are the most influential influence of the critical time step. For example, neglecting them the mass matrix becomes singular and explicit integration becomes unconditionally unstable.

The various simulations proposed validate the use of GRILLE_EXCENTRE Modelling for modal calculations, explicit dynamics and implicit dynamics.

The values obtained are consistent with analytical solutions, when available.
