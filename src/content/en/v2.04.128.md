# v2.04.128 SDLV128 - Modal expansion on a 3D cylindrical tube from extensiometric measurements

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the expansion of modes identified with extensiometric gauges on a numerical model.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A cylindrical tube of length $2.17m$ , external diameter $10\mathit{mm}$ and thickness $1\mathit{mm}$ is considered

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=210000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/\mathit{m3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The tube is supported by multi-point supports as shown below.

The mechanical characteristics of these supports are:

- $\mathit{Kx}=\mathit{Kz}=\frac{1.8{10}^{6}}{4}N/m$
- $\mathit{Ky}=\frac{2.1{10}^{7}}{4}N/m$

To break the axisymmetry of the model that generates double modes, add a point mass of ${10}^{-4}\mathit{kg}$ , in a point of the lower support. This point also multiplies the stiffness following x by 1.05. In addition, to another point of the lower support, the stiffness following z by 0.95 is multiplied. This allows on the one hand to obtain a well defined order of modes according to the increasing frequency without overly disturbed the model, and on the other hand to have static deformations not too collinear (linearly independent expansion base) for Modelling a.

![Figure](/figures/v2.04.128/100000000000030B00000292E3B6E562647689B3.png)

The support point ratings are 0.3 m, 0.8 m, 1.29 m, 1.88 m and 2.09 m

To avoid overloading the illustration, the support (springs) in the xOy plan are not presented.

Gauges were glued inside the tube on two generators. Eight gauges were glued to each generator. The first generator is in the xOy plane and the second generator is in the yOz plane.

The measurements shall be given at 0.2 m, 0.4 m, 0.63 m, 0.92 m, 1.22 m, 1.51 m, 1.80 m and 2.09 m.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The aim here is to estimate the deformation field over the whole structure, from the deformations measured at a few points of the tube.

To do this, an expansion of the measurement is made using the modal expansion technique.

The experimental modes were "simulated" by projection of the numerical modes on the gauges, in the form of a deformation field at node.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The technique used is considered robust if the deformation field is found on all nodes of the original numerical model after expansion of the measurement.

The quantity reference is the scalar product between the extended field and the field calculated on the initial numerical model. This scalar product is obtained by performing a calculation of MAC.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

The solution obtained is considered correct if the scalar product between the extended field and the field calculated directly with the numerical model is close to 1.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

An expansion of the measurement is made using an expansion base consisting of static readings at the measuring points.

3D Modelling for the tube is used and DIS_T Modelling for the supports is used.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 8680 elements type HEXA8 and 20 elements type POI1.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The non-regression, for the first four modes of the tube, is tested for scalar product between the extended modal deformation field and the modal deformation field calculated with the initial numerical model.

The results obtained are considered correct here despite the fact that the expansion base consists solely of static readings.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

An expansion of the measurement is achieved using an expansion base consisting of the first six modes of measurement calculated on the initial numerical model.

3D Modelling for the tube is used and DIS_T Modelling for the supports is used.

### 4.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 8680 elements type HEXA8 and 20 elements type POI1.

### 4.3 Tested quantities and results

The scalar product between the extended modal deformation field and the modal deformation field calculated with the initial numerical model is tested for the first six modes of the tube.

## 5 Summary of the results

The results obtained show that the deformation field can be estimated from a base consisting of eigenmodes or static readings at the measuring points.

The results obtained with a base made up of eigenmodes are obviously better than those obtained by static measurements.
