# v5.03.110 SDNV110 – Rotor volume in rotation around its axis, taking into account gyroscopy

**Summary:**

This test validates the calculation of the rotational modes of a rotor volume with and without gyroscopic stiffness.

This is a rotor model, supported by two bearings and rotating around its axis. This example is drawn from references [1] and [2].

The results of the calculations are compared with those obtained with ANSYS. The results coincide perfectly with the reference solution.

## 1 Reference problem

The objective of this test case is to validate the 3D gyroscopic Modelling of a rotor (options MECA_GYRO and RIGI_ROTA) in Code_Aster.

The results obtained by modelling Code_Aster are compared with those obtained in ANSYS.

### 1.1 Geometry

The rotor shown in the diagram below is considered to be a shaft of 0.3454 m length and variable cross section, supported by two bearings at positions 0.1651 m (palar 1) and 0.287 m (palar 2) respectively. The rotor has an internal radius 0.0203 m disc and an external radius 0.0495 m (strip 0.028 m).

![Figure](/figures/v5.03.110/10000200000002510000017B245BEDA12AF2C3F7.png)

Image 1.1-1: Rotor Geometry

### 1.2 Material properties

The cylinder has a density of $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$.

The Young's modulus is $E=207.{10}^{9}N{m}^{-2}$ and the Poisson's ratio is $\nu =0,3$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The shaft is based on two bearings (Palier1 and Palier2) with the following stiffness characteristics:

- Configuration A: Kxx = Kyy = 4.378x107N/m, Kxy = Kyx = 0
- Configuration B: Kxx = Kyy = 3.503x107N/m, Kxy = Kyx = -8.756 x106

It is stationary or rotating at a speed of 100000 rpm.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is a 3D calculation performed with ANSYS V14.

2. H.D. Nelson and J.M. McVaugh, 的The dynamics of Rotor-Bearing System using Finish Elements, Journal of Engineering for industry, May 1976, pg: 593-600.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The rotor is modeled by quadratic elements density (PENTA15 and HEXA20) and is based on two bearings with the characteristics given by configuration A above.

![Figure](/figures/v5.03.110/10000200000001DE0000013E85B56F2576FB9E3A.png)

Image 3.1-1: mesh rotor

CALC_MODES calculates the modes of shutdown (ie. without gyroscopic damping) and at multiple rotation speeds, i.e. with gyroscopic damping (option MECA_GYRO), but without taking into account the softening effect by the centrifugal stiffness (option RIGI_ROTA).

## 5 Summary of the results

The test case implements the rotation of a tree pressed on two bearings around its axis. The 3D Modelling of the gyroscopy programmed in Code_Aster is thus validated in relation to the results obtained with the equivalent 3D model in ANSYS.
