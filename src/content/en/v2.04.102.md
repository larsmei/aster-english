# v2.04.102 SDLV102 –Lyre with IFS in dynamic substructure

**Summary:**

The problem of determining the proper modes of a pipe filled with water in the shape of a lyre with IFS taken into account is considered.

This is done through different approaches:
- a dynamic substructure methodology by interface modes imposed for modellings A and B (see U2.07.07 for more precision);
- a global approach for modellings C and D whose specificity lies in its massively parallel character. It relies on the SLEP library c.

The ability of these two approaches to represent the modal behaviour of the overall structure is shown.

## 2 reference solution

These test cases are non-regression test cases. The reference solution is the one obtained with an code_aster calculation.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

The lyre is cut into 2 substructures, gridded in the same way and therefore conform.

In addition, the problem is solved on a global mesh. This allows to compare the 2 approaches by calculating the criterion of MAC (Modal Assurance Criterion).

### 4.2 Mesh characteristics

Global lyre

Number of nodes: 12376

### 4.3 Tested quantities and results

The values of the MAC matrix are tested.

## 5 Modelling C

### 5.1 Modelling characteristics

The mesh is used for the overall lyre. boundary conditions is imposed by dualization (see [R3.03.01](doc:r3.03.01)). The simulation is run on 4 processes on which the mesh is cut and distributed.

### 5.2 Mesh characteristics

Global lyre

Number of nodes: 12376

Number of mesh cells and types: 1880 SEG2, 7450 QUAD4 and 11250 HEXA8

### 5.3 Tested quantities and results

Non-regression values of the calculated eigenfrequencies and associated modes are tested.
The results are consistent with the classistic approach.

## 6 Modelling D

### 6.1 Modelling characteristics

The overall mesh of the lyre is used. boundary conditions is imposed by removal (see [U2.01.02](doc:u2.01.02)). The simulation is run on 4 processes on which the mesh is cut and distributed.

### 6.2 Mesh characteristics

Global lyre

Number of nodes: 12376

Number of mesh cells and types: 1880 SEG2, 7450 QUAD4 and 11250 HEXA8

### 6.3 Tested quantities and results

Non-regression values of the calculated eigenfrequencies and associated modes are tested.
The results are consistent with the classistic approach.

## 7 Summary of the results

The calculated results demonstrate the ability of the dynamically substructured approaches to impose interface modes and massively parallel to represent the modal behavior of the overall structure.
