# v6.03.128 SSNP128 - Discontinuity validation of the element on a flat plate

**DOC A SUPPRIMER**

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to display an analytical solution to validate the quality of the discontinuity element (see [R7.02.12](doc:r7.02.12) documentation for details on this element). The objective of this test is to verify that this model leads to a good prediction of the value of the displacement jump along a crack. To do this, we are looking for an analytical solution with an unconstant jump along a discontinuity that is compared with the numerically obtained solution. Furthermore, when we are looking for a numerical method, it is preferable to ensure the uniqueness of the desired solution. We will see that this is the case for the analytical solution presented if a condition relating to the maximum size of the domain studied according to the parameters of the model is verified.

### 1.1 Geometry

Note ${\Gamma}_{0}=\left\lbrace 0\right\rbrace \times ]0,H[$ the left side of the domain and $\partial \Omega \mathrm{\setminus }{\Gamma}_{0}$ the additional part of the edge.

Choose a $\Phi (x,y)$ bi-harmonic function defined by:

$\Phi (x,y)=\beta \frac{{y}^{3}}{6}+(\alpha x+\gamma )\frac{{y}^{2}}{2}+\eta xy$

with $\alpha ,\beta ,\gamma$ and $\eta$ arbitrary real constants. From this the stress field is derived after [eq 2.1-1]:

$\lbrace \begin{array}{ccc}{\sigma}_{xx}& =& \alpha x+\beta y+\gamma \\ {\sigma}_{yy}& =& 0\\ {\sigma}_{xy}& =& -\alpha y-\eta \end{array}$                                 éq 2.1-2

By integrating elastic law, if $E$ is noted the Young's modulus and $\nu$ the Poisson's ratio (which is taken null), the displacement field is derived from it in $\Gamma$ checking balance:

$u=\left\lbrace \begin{array}{}u(x,y)\\ v(x,y)\end{array}\right\rbrace =\left\lbrace \begin{array}{}\frac{1}{E}(\alpha (\frac{{x}^{2}}{2}-{y}^{2})+x(\beta y+\gamma ))\\ -\frac{1}{E}(\beta \frac{{x}^{2}}{2}+2\eta x)\end{array}\right\rbrace$                     éq 2.1-3

Note, respectively, the ${U}_{0}$ and $U$ moves on ${\Gamma}_{0}$ and $\partial \Omega \setminus {\Gamma}_{0}$ given by [eq 2.1-3]. These are the boundary conditions leading to the stress field [eq 2.1-2]. From this data, it is easy to construct a displacement field with discontinuity on the edge ${\Gamma}_{0}$. Indeed, knowing the normal $\sigma n$ stress on ${\Gamma}_{0}$ that the $F(y)$ notes, one obtains the $\delta (y)$ displacement jump by inverting the exponential constitutive law Barenblatt: CZM_EXP type (see documentation on internal elements discontinuity and their behavior: [R7.02.12](doc:r7.02.12)):

$\delta (y)=-\frac{{G}_{c}F(y)}{{\sigma}_{c}\parallel F(y)\parallel }\ln(\frac{\parallel F(y)\parallel }{{\sigma}_{c}})$

For all $y$ in $\left[0,H\right]$ . Thus, the new displacement imposed on ${\Gamma}_{0}$ generating such a jump is equal to ${U}_{0}-\delta$ . So we built a analytical solution of the flat plate checking the equilibrium and compatibility equations with a ${\Gamma}_{0}$ discontinuity along which the $\delta$ displacement jump is not constant. Let's remember the boundary conditions problem:

$\lbrace \begin{array}{ccc}u=U(x,y)& \mathrm{sur}& \partial \Omega \setminus {\Gamma}_{0}\\ u={U}_{0}(y)-\delta (y)& \mathrm{sur}& {\Gamma}_{0}\end{array}$                                 éq 2.1-4

### 2.2 Uniqueness of the solution

After building a analytical solution it is important to ensure that it is unique in order to compare it with the digital solution. See [bib2], that the uniqueness is guaranteed as soon as the following condition, on the geometry of the domain as well as on the material parameters, is verified:

$L<2\mu \frac{{G}_{c}}{{\sigma}_{c}^{2}}$ .                                            éq 2.2-1

The dimensions of the plate and the material parameters given above verify this condition.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The idea is to perform a numerical simulation corresponding to the problem presented in the previous part and compare the results obtained. The discontinuity elements allows to represent the crack along ${\Gamma}_{0}$ . These have for PLAN_ELDI modelling and CZM_EXP behavior. The other elements of the mesh are elastic QUAD4 models in D_PLAN Modelling.

The d-Airy function parameters for the analytical solution construction are taken arbitrarily:

$\alpha =0{\text{MPa.mm}}^{-1},\beta =1/4\pi {\text{MPa.mm}}^{-1},\gamma =1/2\text{MPa}\text{et}\eta =0\text{MPa}$

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Mesh of the squared plate with 20 mesh cells in width and 50 in height is performed. elements with discontinuity along the ${\Gamma}_{0}$ side with the normal directed following $-X$ is available. This is done using CREA_FISS's CREA_MAILLAGE keyword (see [U4.23.02](doc:u4.23.02) documentation).

## 4 Summary of the results

These results allow us to conclude that the internal discontinuity of element leads to a good approximation of the analytical solution. Furthermore, a study of mesh dependence was performed in [bib2]. It is found that the error made on the displacement jump decreases when the mesh is refined. This concludes that, despite a constant jump by element, this model correctly reproduces a crack with a non-constant jump by refining the mesh.
