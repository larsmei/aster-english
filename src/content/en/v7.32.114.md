# v7.32.114 WTNP114 - test case reference for the calculation of mechanical deformations

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the post-processing of mechanical deformations in THM: EPSI_ELGA and EPSI_ELNO.

This two-dimensional problem is handled with $\mathrm{2D}$ Modelling.

The functions processed are, in particular:

- distributed pressure,
- deformations and stresses of nodes,

The reference solution is an analytical solution.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

It's a analytical solution.

Indeed, although the introduced law is a law of THM, only the mechanical part is active here because the liquid pressure is imposed zero everywhere on the domain.

In addition, the mechanical part of the KIT_THM ELAS was applied to an elastic law by choosing the KIT_THM ELAS as a mechanical law.

Thus, given the boundary conditions and the load, we have:

${\sigma}_{xx}=11\text{MPa}$ on the whole surface

${\sigma}_{yy}=15.4\text{MPa}$ on the whole surface

In addition, we are in flat deformations either ${\varepsilon}_{zz}=0$

Gold like $\varepsilon =\frac{1+\nu }{E}\sigma -\frac{\nu}{E}\text{Tr}(\sigma )I$

Either ${\varepsilon}_{zz}=0=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}}{E}-\frac{\nu}{E}{\sigma}_{xx}-\frac{\nu}{E}{\sigma}_{yy}$

D’où ${\sigma}_{zz}=\nu ({\sigma}_{xx}+{\sigma}_{yy})$

So ${\sigma}_{zz}=7.92\text{MPa}$

Therefore, we obtain the values of deformations by means of elastic law

$\begin{array}{}{\varepsilon}_{xx}=6.9034482759{10}^{-4}\\ {\varepsilon}_{yy}=1.67655172414{10}^{-3}\end{array}$

The other values of the strain tensor (and stresses) are zero.

The displacement of the structure is also calculated.

$\begin{array}{}{\varepsilon}_{xx}=\frac{\partial {u}_{x}}{\partial x}\\ {\varepsilon}_{yy}=\frac{\partial {u}_{y}}{\partial y}\end{array}$

$\begin{array}{}{u}_{x}(x,y)={\varepsilon}_{xx}x+{u}_{x}(0,0)\\ {u}_{y}(x,y)={\varepsilon}_{yy}y+{u}_{y}(0,0)\\ \end{array}$

But because of symmetry ${u}_{A}=u(-1,-1)=0$ , so

$\begin{array}{}{u}_{x}(0,0)={\varepsilon}_{xx}\\ {u}_{y}(0,0)={\varepsilon}_{yy}\end{array}$

we're interested in moving point C, $(1,1)$ coordinates

So we have:

$\begin{array}{}{u}_{x}(C)=2{\varepsilon}_{xx}=1.3806896551{10}^{-3}\\ {u}_{y}(C)=2{\varepsilon}_{yy}=3.35310344828{10}^{-3}\end{array}$
