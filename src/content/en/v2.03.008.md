# v2.03.008 SDLS08 - Eigenmodes of a square plate calculated on a reduced basis

**Summary:**

This test case is designed to test the calculation of the eigenmodes of a square plate with static condensation of the matrices assembled on the internal degrees of freedom and the physical restitution.

The test is performed with DKT Modelling, with only one node left free. A unit load is applied to this node.

In the first part, the construction of a modal base obtained from the assembly is tested two bases of dynamic eigenmodes.

Then the static mode base, on which the mass and rigidity matrices are projected, is calculated. The eigenmodes are then calculated on this basis. The results are returned to the physical base.

The final step is to perform a harmonic calculation based on reduced static modes. The results are also returned to physical basis.
