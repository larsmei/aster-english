# v6.01.302 SSNA302 - Circular plate simply pressed under pressure

**Summary:**

This test consists of applying a transverse pressure to a circular plate, simply supported and made up of elastic material. It is intended to study the consideration of geometric nonlinearities especially in the absence of initial curvature when the transverse stiffness is solely due to plate effect.

Modelling is done with elements volumetric HEXA20 and PENTA15 and elements surface QUAD8 and TRIA6 for pressure application.

The reference is SAMCEF software. The results from thin shell theory are presented for information.

Under keyword COMPORTEMENT, the GREEN option is compared to the PETIT_REAC option.

### 2.2 Analytical solution and reference results

The following formula gives the deflection ${w}_{0}$ at the centre of the plate:

$\frac{{w}_{0}}{h}+A{(\frac{{w}_{0}}{h})}^{3}=\frac{\mathrm{Bp}}{E}{(\frac{r}{h})}^{4}$ with $A=1.852$ and $B=0.696$

The mid-thickness stresses are:

$\begin{array}{ccc}{\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}& =& {\alpha}_{r}E\frac{{w}_{0}}{{r}^{2}}\\ {\sigma}_{\theta \theta }& =& {\alpha}_{t}E\frac{{w}_{0}}{{r}^{2}}\end{array}$

Lower skin stresses are valid

$\begin{array}{ccc}{\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}'& =& {\beta}_{r}E\frac{{w}_{0}h}{{r}^{2}}\\ {\sigma}_{\theta \theta }'& =& {\beta}_{t}E\frac{{w}_{0}h}{{r}^{2}}\end{array}$

The coefficients are:

In the centre of the plate:

${\alpha}_{r}={\alpha}_{t}=0.905$

${\beta}_{r}={\beta}_{t}=1.778$

On the edge of the plate:

${\alpha}_{r}=0.610$

${\alpha}_{t}=0.183$

${\beta}_{r}=0$

${\beta}_{t}=0.755$

For $222.72\mathrm{MPa}$ pressure, the deflection ${w}_{0}$ is $1.5\mathrm{mm}$ and the following constraints are obtained:

| Position | ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}$ $(\mathrm{MPa})$ | ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }$ $(\mathrm{MPa})$ | ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}'$ $(\mathrm{MPa})$ | ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }'$ $(\mathrm{MPa})$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Centre | 4072.5 | 4072.5 | 5334.0 | 5334.0 |

These results correspond to a thin hull hypothesis.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics A

![Figure](/figures/v6.01.302/10000000000001A900000166A633CE2175358E79.jpg)

**Figure 3.1-** 3.1-a

Only one quarter of a plate is modelled. Symmetric conditions are introduced on the two sides shown above.

In addition, all nodes of the edge at mid-thickness is fixed according to $x,y,z$.

To represent thin hull assumptions most accurately, linear stresses are introduced on the degrees of freedom of the edge nodes. These are:

for two nodes $i$ and $j$ on both sides of the average sheet:

${u}_{i}+{u}_{j}=0$

${v}_{i}+{v}_{j}=0$

where u and v denote the movements along the $x$ and $y$ axes.

The following pressure is applied linearly using 6 increments.

## 6 Summary of the results

The performance in time calculation and accuracy of the results is satisfactory using the keyword COMPORTEMENT option GREEN_LAGRANGE. However, the treatment of the geometric nonlinearities by the keyword COMPORTEMENT option PETIT_REAC provides results quite far from the reference solution by adopting a 6 time step discretization that leads to already significant computational costs.
