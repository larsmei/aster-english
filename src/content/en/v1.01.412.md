# v1.01.412 ZZZZ412 - Validation of DEFI_SOL_EQUI

**Summary:**

This test validates the use of the DEFI_SOL_EQUI command.

Modelization A: simulation of field-free deconvolution → rock with single-support, 1D approach – 3 components.

Modelling B: simulation of field-free deconvolution → rock with flat wave with 1D approach – 3 components.

Modelization C: simulation of rock-exposed convolution → free field in single support with 1D – 3 component approach.

Modelling D: simulation of rock-exposed convolution → free field in flat wave with 1D – 3 component approach.

Modelling E: simulation of field-free deconvolution → mono-support rock with 1D approach – 1 components with the consideration of water pressures in a simplified way by the Byrne model.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and mechanical loadings

#### 1.3.1 boundary condition

The boundary conditions applied to the column during the dynamic calculation phase are:

- *Column bottom*: Assignment of an absorbent element border.

If 1D calculation – 1 component:

- *Right and left sides of column*: Periodicity condition. This means that the nodes moves on the left and right sides opposite are equal.

If 1D calculation – 3 components:

- *Side edges*: stiffness penalty surrounding the column, to ensure a single response in horizontal directions (half-space hypothesis)

#### 1.3.2 Loading

Transitional acceleration in the soil given by LBNS functions. If field-free deconvolution calculation → outcrop rock, a coefficient of $1.5$ is applied. If the rock outcrop convolution calculation → free field, a coefficient of $0.3$ is applied.

## 8 Summary of the results

The results show the ability of the DEFI_SOL_EQUI macrocomande to perform a linear calculation Equivalent for different configurations: free field loading or outcropping rock, with single-support or flat wave modelling.

The results also show the macro-command's ability to use the Byrne model for the consideration of water pressures in a simplified manner in soil column calculations.
