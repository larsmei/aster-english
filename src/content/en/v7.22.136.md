# v7.22.136 HSNV136 - META_LEMA_ANI model degeneration in Norton law: single traction in large deformations

**Summary:**

This test consists of the application of a cylindrical bar with viscoplastic behaviour to a traction axis, Modelling it in two different but equivalent ways: either with the META_LEMA_ANI model that is "degenerated" into a Norton law by judiciously choosing coefficients (modelization A), or with a Norton law itself (modelization B).

The load is done on the required displacement and requires the re-upgrading of the geometry (large deformations, keyword PETIT_REAC). The same response is then obtained for both modellings A and B.

In addition, Modelling C is introduced to validate the large logarithmic deformations (keyword GDEF_LOG) for this behavior.

Finally, the D Modelling allows the SIMU_POINT_MAT command to validate the META_LEMA_ANI-based data.

The bar is modeled by a element quadrangular QUAD4, in axisymmetric.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v7.22.136/1000052400001B980000129BB7A905A498F51911.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

The material obeys a viscoplastic constitutive law of Norton (special case of the Lemaître law, where the UN_SUR_M parameter is null, confir [R5.03.08](doc:r5.03.08) ), whose parameters are:

Young's modulus : $E=80000\mathit{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio : $\nu =0.35$

$n=4.39$

$K=\frac{1}{0.003944}{\mathit{MPa.s}}^{\text{-1}}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The bar, of initial length ${I}_{0}$ , blocked in the direction $\mathrm{Ox}$ on the face $[1,2]$ is subjected to a uniform temperature $T$ and a mechanical displacement of traction ${u}^{\mathit{meca}}$ on the face $[3,4]$ . The loading sequences are as follows:

![Figure](/figures/v7.22.136/10000DDE00000E2900000B440A7CDEAB66C949B6.svg)

![Figure](/figures/v7.22.136/100000000000027A0000018DC524C792B7D31701.png)

Reference temperature: ${T}_{\mathrm{réf}}=700°C$ .

**Note:**

*The uniform temperature imposed on the element, constant over time and equal to the reference temperature, is used only to operate the META_LEMA_ANI* model. There is no thermal expansion.*

## 2 reference solution

The validation of the META_LEMA_ANI law is done by comparing the two modellings A and B. Each of the two modellings therefore constitutes one reference solution for the other.

Validation of this law into large logarithmic deformations is done by intercomparison of the two modellings A and C.

It is also tested that the beta phase proportion remains zero.
