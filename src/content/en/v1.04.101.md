# v1.04.101 GCPC001 - CTJ25 tester

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to ensure the non-regression of the linear elasticity problem resolution method with the GCPC solver with 2 LDLT_INC and LDLT_SP preconditioners.

The test consists of 1 3D model of a quarter of CTJ25 630 hexahedron test pieces for a load of displacement imposed.

**In previous versions this test was named SSLV101 and then YYYY108.**
