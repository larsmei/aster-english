# v2.05.201 SDLX201 - non-regression Test: eigenmodes

**Summary:**

This is a non-regression test to validate:

- The different GEP and QEP modal solvers with real symmetrical;
- Interoperability with direct linear solvers;
- Some pre and post-processing modal commands (INFO_MODE, NORM_MODE, ...).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

This is an assembly:

- a parallel pipe length $10.m$ , width $3.m$ and depth $1.m$ ,
- a rectangular plate of length $10.m$ , width $3.m$ and thickness $1.m$ ,
- Of a beam of length $10.m$ and rectangular cross section $3.mX1.m$ .

![Figure](/figures/v2.05.201/10000000000004C6000003102E48235BBB0D8C1C.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

- $E=2.0\mathit{E5}N/{m}^{2}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =8000.\mathit{Kg}/{m}^{3}$
- $\alpha =0.$

### 1.3 boundary conditions

The travel requirements are:

- on the group *'GRNO1'* DX = DY = DZ = 0
- on nodes N10, N11, N26, N23 DZ = 0
