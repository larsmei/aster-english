# v4.03.302 TPLS302 - Temperature distribution in thin plate

**Summary:**

A convection plate is considered applied to the lower and upper sides and to a edge of the plate (width). The temperature is imposed on the opposite edge of the plate.

The purpose of this test is to validate the element thermal hull in the plane conduction and in the [R3.11.01](doc:r3.11.01) and [U1.01.01](doc:u1.01.01) convection. It also allows the validation of the elements convection edge.

Due to the boundary conditions and the loads considered, the temperature distribution is uniform along the width. The results are compared with a solution based on a graphical estimate. Both types of approach give equivalent results.

This test is based on the independent validation of thermal version 3.

$h=85.169W/{m}^{2}°C$

$h'=2.163W/m°C$ corresponding to $h\times b$ for this test
