# v6.06.203 CONT203 - 2D mortar pairing for two squares in vis-à-vis slightly offset

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to verify the projection-intersection procedure in the case of beam-launch matching (mortar approach).
Specifically, this test is limited to the case of linear 3D mesh cells (so mesh cells skin type QUAD4 or TRIA3).

This test case allows to process a plan-plan match between two 2D solids.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometrics and meshes tested

A pairing between two flat surfaces is considered in the simplest case, i.e. two flat surfaces.
These two squares are slightly shifted (relative translation on x and y axis).

The upper square will be meshed more finely than the lower cube.

### 1.2 Variations of matching approaches for a given mesh

For each mesh, the various matching algorithms available are tested, including:

- the resolution algorithm by gross force
- the resolution algorithm says PANG (fast)

In addition, both solids do not have symmetrical mesh roles (mesh higher finer).
Therefore, two configurations are tested each time:

- the case where the superior solid is the solid slave
- the case where the lower solid is the solid slave

### 1.3 Understanding of data

To perform the matching data set, we provide the mesh cells groups that define the surface
slave and the master surface. In all the modellings presented here, the group associated with the interface
for the upper solid (lower resp.) is called *CONT_HAUT* (rep. *CONT_BAS*).

## 2 reference solution

For each model, details of the expected results are provided.
The latter are dependent on mesh and therefore differ for each model.
