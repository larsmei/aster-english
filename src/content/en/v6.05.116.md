# v6.05.116 SSNS116 – Soft membrane under clean weight

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the operation of element MEMBRANE in largedeformations for different types of mesh cells (linear, quadratic and bi-quadratic) by taking and adapting test case from the chain under clean weight (SSN l114).The results are compared with a semi-analytic solution.

The results of the gravity-subsidised membrane (gravity) are also compared with those of the membrane under non-following pressure (PRES_REP with TYPE_CHARGE='FIXE_CSTE'), amplitude and direction chosen, which are identical in result. The two functions are validated.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A $325m$ length and $10m$ width rectangle is considered in the $(0,X,Y)$ plane.

![Figure](/figures/v6.05.116/1000000000000375000000DF5ECDBD3794327099.png)

The thickness of the membrane is indicated in AFFE_CARA_ELEM via the keyword EPAIS and is $e={\mathrm{2,2783.10}}^{-5}m$. This thickness is chosen to obtain a mass equal to that used in the test case SSNL114, with the same density.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is almost incompressible isotropic hyperelastic with properties of:

- $E=57000\mathit{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.49$
- $\rho =2844,23\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$

We use the constitutive law of Saint Venant Kirchhoff.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On the $\mathit{AB}$ and $\mathit{CD}$ edges: $\mathit{DX}=0$ , $\mathit{DZ}=0$ .

On the $\mathit{AD}$ edge: $\mathit{DY}=0$ .

The gravity is applied to the entire model, it is $g=9,81m/{s}^{2}$ and is oriented according to (0.0,-1) in the $(X,Y,Z)$ marker.

The non-following pressure is applied to the entire model, it is $p\text{}=\text{}\rho \ast e\ast g\text{}\approx \text{}0,6357\mathit{Pa}$ and is oriented to $(\mathrm{0,0,}-1)$ in the $(X,Y,Z)$ marker.

Both loads are treated separately.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

An initial $10\mathit{MPa}$ voltage is reported in AFFE_CARA_ELEM via the N_INIT keyword. This voltage disappears after the first increment of Newton.

## 6 Summary of the results

This document validates element's MEMBRANE in large deformations for linear, quadratic and bi-quadratic elementss, as well as gravity and PRES_REP loading options (with TYPE_CHARGE='FIXE_CSTE') applied to membranes. This validation is based on a semi-analytic comparison and is supplemented by several non-regression tests.

The reference solution ($\text{}<0,05\text{\%}$) results are very similar in all tests, confirming that the model is close to the one of the heavy cable. Better results could probably have been obtained with a membrane of smaller width, in order to get closer to the cable hypothesis.

In addition, in this model, results converge much faster for linear elements without degrade the moving solution.
