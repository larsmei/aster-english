# v2.01.004 SDLD04 - Transitional response of a mass-spring system subject to an accelerated requirement

**Summary**

This test consists of calculating the undisturbed transient response of a linear clamped-free mass-spring system subject to an accelerated requirement.

The discrete traction-compression element, the calculation of eigenmodes, static modes, and the calculation of the transient response of a system subject to an accelerated requirement are tested. The direct calculation of the response is compared with its calculation by modal recombination.

This test case comes from the VPCS guide. The reference solution is an analytical calculation. Errors on the results obtained are normal given the time step chosen for digital integration.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The response of a linear system consisting of three masses and three springs is calculated to an acceleration imposed on the point sound of $(A)$ anchor:

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.004/1000108E00001CBB0000077142BB037A4805ADE5.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

- restraint stiffeners: $k={k}_{1}={k}_{2}={k}_{3}=1000N/m$;
- point masses: $m={m}_{1}={m}_{2}={m}_{3}=1\mathrm{kg}$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

**boundary conditions**

The only travels allowed are $x$-axis translations.

The point $A$ is clamped: $\mathrm{dx}=\mathrm{dy}=\mathrm{dz}=\mathrm{drx}=\mathrm{dry}=\mathrm{drz}=0$.

**Charge**

The point anchor $A$ is subject to an acceleration, a growing time function, according to the $x$ direction: $\gamma (t)=2.{10}^{5}.{t}^{2}$ ($t$ varies from $0$ to $0,1s$).

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The system is initially at rest: $t=0$ , $\mathrm{dx}(0)=0$ and $\mathrm{dx}/\mathrm{dt}(0)=0$ in all point.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

${f}_{i}$ eigenfrequencies and associated ${\phi }_{\text{Ni}}$ own vectors are first calculated, standardized to the mass matrix. The generalised response of the single-excise system is then calculated by the analytically-solvented integral of Duhamel [bib1]. Finally, the relative displacement of point $D$ is restored on the physical basis.

**Calculation of eigenfrequencies**

The mass and stiffness matrices are as follows:

$M=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}m& 0& 0\\ 0& m& 0\\ 0& 0& m\end{array}\right]$ , $K=k\left[\begin{array}{ccc}2& -1& 0\\ -1& 2& -1\\ 0& -1& 1\end{array}\right]$

$\omega$ eigenfrequencies are solution of the $\det[K-{\lambda}^{2}M]=0$ equation, i.e. ${\lambda}^{3}-5{\lambda}^{2}+6\lambda -1=0$ where $\lambda =\frac{\omega}{{\omega}_{0}}$ and $\omega =\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$.

**Calculation of the generalised response of the mono-excise system**

$\gamma (t)={\mathrm{a.t}}^{2}$ with $a={2.10}^{5}$ .

In the absolute benchmark, the fundamental equation of the undisturbed mass-spring system dynamics is written: $M\ddot{{X}_{a}}+K{X}_{a}=0$ .

The ${X}_{a}$ absolute displacement is broken down into a uniform drive displacement in ${X}_{e}$ translation and a relative ${X}_{r}$ displacement: ${X}_{a}={X}_{r}+{X}_{e}$.

The equation of motion in the relative marker is written: $M\ddot{{X}_{r}}+K{X}_{r}=-M\Psi \ddot{{X}_{s}}=Q$

with $\ddot{{X}_{s}}=\gamma (t)={\mathrm{a.t}}^{2}$ and $\Psi =\left[\begin{array}{}1\\ 1\\ 1\end{array}\right]$ and therefore $Q={\mathrm{a.t}}^{2}m\left[\begin{array}{}1\\ 1\\ 1\end{array}\right]$ .

The equation of the projected motion based on the dynamic modes standardized with the mass matrix sss written:

$\ddot{{\alpha}_{i}}(t)+{\omega}_{i}^{2}{\alpha}_{i}(t)=\frac{{\Phi}_{i}^{T}.M.\Psi }{{\Phi}_{i}^{T}.M.{\Phi}_{i}}\gamma (t)=-{p}_{i}(t)\gamma (t)$ .

The response of this linear system, at a moment $t$ is given by Duhamel's integral:

$\ddot{{\alpha}_{i}}(t)=\frac{1}{{\omega}_{i}}{\int}_{0}^{t}-{p}_{i}(t)\gamma (t).\sin{\omega}_{i}(t-\tau )d\tau =-\frac{{p}_{i}(t)}{{\omega}_{i}}{\int}_{0}^{t}{\mathrm{a.t}}^{2}\sin{\omega}_{i}(t-\tau )d\tau$ .

However, after [bib1], ${\int}_{0}^{t}a.{t}^{2}\sin{\omega}_{i}(t-\tau )d\tau =\frac{\alpha}{{\omega}_{i}}\left[{t}^{2}+\frac{2}{{\omega}_{i}}(\cos{\omega}_{i}t-1)\right]$ .

So ${X}_{r}={\Phi}_{i}\cdot {\alpha}_{i}=-\underset{i}{\Sigma}\frac{a.{p}_{i}(t).{\Phi}_{i}}{{\omega}_{i}^{2}}\left[{t}^{2}+\frac{2}{{\omega}_{i}}(\cos{\omega}_{i}t-1)\right]$.

### 2.2 Baseline

The reference results are the three specific frequencies of the system and the relative displacement ${x}_{r}$ to point $D$ , for different moments between $0$ and $0,1s$ .

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

None if Duhamel's integral is calculated analytically [bib1], [bib2].

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The springs and point weights are modelled by discrete elements at 3 degrees of freedom DIS_T:

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.004/100008280000159A00000668FDB858FB2B8DCEC3.svg)

The node $\mathit{NO1}$ is clamped and is subject to an acceleration imposed $\gamma (t)$ . The relative displacement of the node $\mathit{NO4}$ is calculated .

**Modal synthesis calculus**

The full basis of the eigenmodes is considered. Temporal integration is performed with the algorithms of Newmark, d的Euler and Devogelaere with an time step of $0,001s$. Calculations are archived all time step.

A reduced ${\xi}_{i}$ depreciation is considered to be zero for all calculated modes.

The load is taken into account as a projected vector on the EXCIT:(VECT_GENE) modal basis or as a EXCIT:(NUME_MODE) modal component or both.

**Direct calculations**

The time integration is performed either with the Newmark algorithm or with the explicit algorithm of the time step-centered differences. The calculations are archived every ten time step.

**Note:**

*As the diagram of the centered differences can only be used with a diagonal mass matrix, the elementary matrices are calculated with the option* *MASS_MECA_DIAG in the operator* *CALC_MATR_ELEM.*

** Account for an initial state**

In both types of calculation, the relative displacement obtained from a one-time calculation is verified to be identical to that obtained in several times, i.e. by considering as the initial state, the result of the last time step calculated:

```text
ETAT_INIT=_F (RESULTAT ...) pour un calcul par synthèse modale;

ETAT_INIT=_F (DEPL ...

VITE ..) pour un calcul direct.
```

**Considered of modes neglected by static correction:**

A modal base consisting of the first two eigenmodes is considered and is completed by a mode corresponding to the static response of the system studied to a unit load of force type imposed in the direction $–x$ (keywords MODE_CORR and CORR_STAT in the operator DYNA_VIBRA).
