# v5.06.106 SDNS106 – Transitional Response of a reinforced concrete slab: GLRC_DAMAGE and GLRC_DM models

**Summary:**

This test validates the GLRC_DAMAGE [R7.01.31](doc:r7.01.31) and GLRC_DM [R7.01.32](doc:r7.01.32) global models applied to the explicit dynamic (DYNA_NON_LINE(SCHEMA_TEMPS=DIFF_CENT)). It is a trapezoidal reinforced concrete plate, supported on two opposite sides and pressed in bending by pressure.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry used in this test case is a reinforced concrete plate of $e=0.1\text{m}$ thickness and trapezoidal shape.

![Figure](/figures/v5.06.106/Object_4.png)

**Figure** 1.1-a **: Geometry studied**

The characteristics of the reinforced concrete section are:

- Upper tablecloth: section per linear metre following $x$ and $y$ $=5.65{10}^{-4}{m}^{\mathrm{²}}/\mathrm{ml}$; ex-centre against the following average sheet $x$ and $y$: $+0.0475m$ (95% of thickness),
- Lower tablecloth: section per linear metre following $x$ and $y$ $=5.65{10}^{-4}{m}^{\mathrm{²}}/\mathrm{ml}$; ex-centre against the following average sheet $x$ and $y$: $-0.0475m$ (95% of thickness),
- Prestress cables: section per linear metre following $x=4.56{10}^{-3}{m}^{\mathrm{²}}/\mathrm{ml}$ and $y=1.32{10}^{-2}{m}^{\mathrm{²}}/\mathrm{ml}$; no ex-centre to average sheet; prestress following $x$ and $y$ $=-3\mathrm{MN}$ ,
- Liner: The liner thickness is $6\mathrm{mm}$ and is positioned on the bottom side.

![Figure](/figures/v5.06.106/10000000000004BC00000244BF10CC0BD09D7C5B.jpg)

**Figure** 1.1-b **: Reinforced concrete plate section**

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On the $\mathrm{A1}$ corner of the plate, ${u}_{x}={u}_{y}={u}_{z}=0$ moves are embedded, as well as ${\theta}_{x}={\theta}_{y}={\theta}_{z}=0$ rotations. The movements are blocked according to $x$ and $z$ on the $\mathrm{A1A3}$ *and* $\mathrm{A2A4}$ sides. Pressure is applied to the entire slab in the $(0.0,0.0,1,0)$ direction and is ${F}_{0}=20.{10}^{7}N$ for A Modelling. For modellings B and C, a nodal force is applied to the entire slab $1500N$ . This force is applied gradually following the multiplicative function shown on the following figure.

![Figure](/figures/v5.06.106/10000000000001E3000001234BCE459B0C8DE952.png)

Figure 1.3-a: Fonction multiplicatrice du chargement pour les modélisations B et C

### 1.4 Initial conditions

In the initial state, the movements and speeds are zero everywhere.
