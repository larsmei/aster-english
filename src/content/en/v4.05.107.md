# v4.05.107 TPLP107 – 2D thermal manufactured solutions method

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to verify 2D thermal modelling using the method of manufactured solutions [bib1].

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A square is considered on the $1m$ side.

![Figure](/figures/v4.05.107/Shape1.gif)

### 1.2 Material properties

$$
\lambda =15.W/\mathrm{m.}°C
$$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On the $\text{GAUCHE}$ , $\text{BAS}$ and $\text{HAUT}$ edges a temperature is imposed (see paragraph 2 ).

On the $\text{DROITE}$ edge a flow is imposed (see paragraph 2 ).

In the whole area, a source is imposed (see paragraph 2 ).

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution analytical is given by:

$$
T=100\times ({X}^{6}+{Y}^{6})
$$

The conditions of Dirichlet, Neumann and the source term are obtained by the method of manufactured solutions [bib1].

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

La valeur de l'écart entre solutions analytique et calculée sur le maillage : $\sum^{\text{noeuds}n}\mid {T}_{\text{n}}^{\text{calc}}-{T}_{\text{n}}^{\text{ref}}\mid$ .

In the case of modellings that perform convergence analysis with the fineness of mesh, the convergence speed with the fineness of mesh of the solution calculated to analytical solution in ${L}_{2}$ standard, the largest real $\alpha >0$ such as ${\mathrm{\parallel }{T}^{\text{calc}}-{T}^{\text{ref}}\mathrm{\parallel }}_{0,\Omega }<C\times {h}^{\alpha}$ where $C$ is independent of $h$.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

None

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

1. Document U2.08.08, Using the Manufacturing Solutions Method for Software Validation, Code_Aster U2 Documentation

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

PLAN modelling is used.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 56 elements of SEG3 and 392 elements of TRIA6.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The sum of the absolute values of the difference between the calculated solution and analytical solution is tested.

| **Identification** | **Reference type** | **Reference value** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $\sum^{\text{noeuds}n}\mid {T}_{\text{n}}^{\text{calc}}-{T}_{\text{n}}^{\text{ref}}\mid$ | 'NON_REGRESSION' | 0.59734630434863 |

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

PLAN modelling is used.

### 4.2 Mesh characteristics

A convergence study is conducted with the fineness of the mesh from the calculated solution to the analytical solution. A sequence of meshes obtained by uniform refinement using the MACR_ADAP_MAIL command is used:

- mesh 0 : 4 SEG3, 2 TRIA6
- mesh 1 : 8 SEG3, 8 TRIA6
- mesh 2 : 16 SEG3, 32 TRIA6
- mesh 3 : 32 SEG3, 128 TRIA6

### 4.3 Tested quantities and results

The convergence speed with the fineness of the mesh of the solution calculated to the analytical solution in ${L}_{2}$ standard is tested, the largest real $\alpha >0$ such as ${\mathrm{\parallel }{T}^{\text{calc}}-{T}^{\text{ref}}\mathrm{\parallel }}_{0,\Omega }<C\times {h}^{\alpha}$ where $C$ is independent of $h$.

The sum of the absolute values of the difference between the calculated solution and analytical solution is also tested.

| **Identification** | **Reference type** | **Reference value** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $\sum^{\text{noeuds}n}\mid {T}_{\text{n}}^{\text{calc}}-{T}_{\text{n}}^{\text{ref}}\mid$ | 'NON_REGRESSION' | 0.59734630434863 |
| $\alpha$ | 'ANALYTIQUE' | 3.0 |

## 5 Summary of the results

The results are in very good agreement with the theory.
