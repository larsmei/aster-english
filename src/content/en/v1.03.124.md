# v1.03.124 UMAT001 – Test of the Code_Aster-Umat interface in thermo-linear elasticity

**Summary:**

A comparison of *Code_Aster-* Umat and *Code-Aster* with ELAS behaviour is performed on a linear thermo-elastic problem. This test validates the Aster-Umat interface, especially the correct consideration of thermal expansions.

$A$ Modelling validates the AXIS interface.

$B$ Modelling validates the interface in large deformations (DEFORMATION ='GDEF_LOG')

The valid C Modelling validates the AXIS interface. with command variables.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a hollow cylinder, similar to that treated in SSNA106.

![Figure](/figures/v1.03.124/Shape1.gif)

| ${R}_{0}$ | $1m$ |
| --- | --- |
| ${R}_{1}$ | $2m$ |

### 1.2 Material properties

#### 1.2.1 Umat data

The elasticity of the Umat behavior in Code_Aster sources is expressed as an example by:

$\mathrm{C1}=\lambda =\frac{E\nu }{(1+\nu )(1-2\nu )}$

$\mathrm{C2}=\mu =\frac{E}{2(1+\nu )}$

$\mathrm{C3}=\mathrm{C4}=\mathrm{C5}=0$

#### 1.2.2 Aster data

Young's modulus: $E=1\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.3$

Dilation coefficient: $\alpha =0.7$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

**boundary conditions :**

The cylinder is blocked in $\mathrm{DY}$ on the sides $[\mathrm{AB}]$ and $[\mathrm{CD}]$ .

Loading:

The cylinder is subjected to a temperature field $T(r,t)=t{r}^{2}$

## 2 reference solution

The results obtained with the ELAS behavior are intercomparisoned with those obtained with the Umat behavior.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The problem is modelled in axisymmetry: AXIS.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh has 200 mesh cells of the QUAD4 type.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

The loads and material are identical to those of $A$ Modelling.

The only model changes the model of large deformations: SIMO_MIEHE is compared here with VMIS_ISOT_LINE behavior (simulating elastic behavior via a high yield strength) and UMAT behavior (also elastic) with GDEF_LOG.

### 4.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh is identical to the $A$ model: 200 elements of the QUAD4 type.

Comparison of the results obtained with SIMO_MIEHE law with those obtained with GDEF_LOG.

## 5 Modelling C

The loads and material are identical to those of $A$ Modelling.

Only changes the control variables: SECH is used here in addition to the temperature and half of the expansion coefficient used in model A is chosen. This value is also assigned to the expansion coefficient due to drying.

The "thermal" expansion will therefore consist of half pure thermal expansion and another half of deformation due to drying. The results must be identical to those of model A.

### 5.1 Modelling characteristics

The problem is modelled in axisymmetry: AXIS.

### 5.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh has 200 mesh cells of the QUAD4 type.

### 5.3 Tested quantities and results

Comparison of the results obtained with ELAS law with those obtained with UMAT law.

### 5.4 Remarks

The results from the two large deformation models (SIMO_MIEHE and GDEF_LOG) are also tested in NON_REGRESSION at $\text{0.1\%}$ .

## 6 Summary of the results

The results obtained are in perfect agreement between *Code_Aster* -Umat and *Code_Aster.*

The two large deformation models (SIMO_MIEHE and GDEF_LOG give different results (8 to $\text{9\%}$ difference) which is explained by the difference in elasticity formulation (one being hyper-elastic and the other hypo-elastic). This test shows the possibility of using UMAT in large deformations.
