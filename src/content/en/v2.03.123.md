# v2.03.123 SDLS123 – straight beam with Rayleigh damping (elastic behaviour)

**Summary:**

This test represents the dynamic calculation with Rayleigh damping of an straight beam, clamped at both ends and subjected to constant pressure.

Two modellings are performed:

1. Modelling A allows you to test the Q4GG model with QUAD4,
2. Modelling B allows you to test the Q4GG model with TRIA3.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.123/geometrie.png)

Thickness: $\mathrm{1m}$

### 1.2 Material properties

The mechanical properties are as follows:

- Young's modulus $E=3.5\times {10}^{10}\mathit{Pa}$
- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0,2$
- Density $\rho =2500\mathit{Kg}/{m}^{3}$
- $\alpha =\mathit{AMOR}\text{_}\mathit{ALPHA}=6.69\times {10}^{-5}$
- $\beta =\mathit{AMOR}\text{_}\mathit{BETA}=20.06$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- Clamped support on the $\mathit{AD}$ and $\mathit{BC}$ sides: $\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DZ}=\mathit{DRX}=\mathit{DRY}=\mathit{DRZ}=0$;
- The uniformly distributed pressure on the beam increases linearly to $0.1\mathit{ms}$ and then remains constant and equal to $p={10}^{5}\mathit{Pa}$ until the end of the calculation ($1.0\mathit{ms}$ ).

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The beam is initially at rest in a pristine state.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference results were obtained with Europlexus.

The meshes used by Europlexus and Code_Aster are the same.
