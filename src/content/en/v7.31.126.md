# v7.31.126 WTNV126 – Response to mixed saturation-consolidation paths with the Barcelona model

**Summary:**

This test validates the integration of the Barcelona [r7.01.17](doc:r7.01.17) model. This test is carried out in model A, in hydro-mechanical coupling (modelling HHM), and includes three loading routes:

- A saturation path.
- An isotropic compression path.
- A deaturation journey.

The Modelling is performed on a hexahedral element 3D.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The gas pressure remains constant at $\mathrm{PRE2}={1}$ Pa. In the first loading phase, a desaturation is performed, during which the capillary pressure decreases to zero from $\mathrm{PRE1}={100}$ kPa. In the second phase, an isotropic compression is performed, until the average stress $\sigma_m=-400$ kPa is reached. In the third and last phase, while maintaining the average stress, the capillary pressure is increased to $\mathrm{PRE1}={300}$ kPa.
