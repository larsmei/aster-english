# v2.03.001 SDLS01 - Slim square plate, free or clamped at the edge

**Summary:**

The application field of this test case is structural dynamics, specifically modal calculation and harmonic response calculation.

For modal calculation, the calculation of the proper bending modes of a thin square plate is carried out in two configurations:

- Clamped plate on one edge,
- Free plate.

The plate is meshed in triangular elements to which elements DKT are assigned.

Four different modellingss are tested:

- Modal calculation – Plate edges oriented according to the axes of the marker,
- Modal calculation – Any orientation of the plate and harmonic response for the plate clamped,
- Modal calculation by classical and cyclic dynamic substructure,
- Modal calculation following Guyan condensation.

The modal calculations are based on analytical calculations, which validate the tools for creating mass and stiffness matrices, as well as the classical and cyclic dynamic substructure operators implemented in *Code_Aster* . On the other hand, this test case validates the modal calculation following Guyan condensation (condensation of the mass matrix).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in the SDLS01/89 form of the VPCS guide which presents the calculation method as follows:

The formulation of M.V.BARTON, for a side plate $a$, leads to:

${f}_{i}=\frac{1}{2\pi {a}^{2}}{\lambda}_{i}^{2}\sqrt{\frac{{\mathrm{Et}}^{2}}{12\rho (1-{\nu}^{2})}}$ $i=1,2,\mathrm{...}$

with, for a Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$:

| 1°: clamped plate on one side | 2°: Free plate |
| --- | --- |

| $i$ | ${\lambda}_{i}^{2}$ |  | $i$ | ${\lambda}_{i}^{2}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3.492 |  | 1 à 6 |  |
| 2 | 8.525 |  | 7 | 13.49 |
| 3 | 21.43 |  | 8 | 19.79 |
| 4 | 27.33 |  | 9 | 24.43 |
| 5 | 31.11 |  | 10 | 35.02 |
| 6 | 54.44 |  | 11 | 35.02 |

(6 solid body modes at zero frequency).

This reference solution applies to thin plates such as: $t/a<0.1$

${\lambda}_{i}$ coefficients are established by limited development on the modal deformities of a cross beam network (clamped beam – free and free beam).

## 5 Modelling C

### 5.1 Modelling characteristics

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.001/10000E8C00000DBF00000E297B3FEB9B28C31402.svg)

In both cases, the plate is cut into 4 parts of equal size. Each substructure is meshed into triangles to which elements plate DKT are assigned.

**Case 1: clamped plate on one edge**

The structure is studied using the classic substructure method with CRAIG_BAMPTON interfaces. The modal base used for each substructure is composed of 25 eigenmodes and the forced modes associated with the interfaces.

The structure is studied using the cyclic substructure method with CRAIG_BAMPTONHARMONIQUE interfaces and taking into account the node specificity of the axis (point $G$). The modal base used for the base sector is composed of 25 eigenmodes and harmonic modes associated with the interfaces.

### 6.4 Remarks

The first 3 non-zero eigenfrequencies of the free plate problem on its edges are being calculated.

If the matrices are condensed on the nodes only:

$(A,B,C,D,G,H,I,J,K)$

The frequency accuracy is then only $\text{2\%}$ .

To achieve the desired results with the expected accuracy ($\text{1\%}$ ), add the $(L,M,N,O)$ points.

## 7 Summary of the results

- modellings A et B :

Accuracy on clean $\text{≤ 1\%}$ frequencies up to the sixth bending mode.

- Modelization C:

Under-structured results could be improved by using a finer substructure mesh.

- Modelling D:

To obtain $\text{1\%}$ accuracy on eigenfrequencies, it is necessary to condense also on 4 nodes media of $L$, $M$, $N$ and $O$ edges.
