# v3.01.119 SSLL119 – POWERS SUBMITTED AT A FIXED TIME

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the application of moments spread over beams.

Note: The moments on the beams are affected by AFFE_CHAR_MECA and AFFE_CHAR_MECA_F, FORCE_POUTRE operand, MX, MY, MZ, MT, MFY and MFZ key words. They are applicable to constant-character straight beams.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A $1m$ length beam is considered to be $X$-oriented or $Z$-oriented according to modellings.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Torsion time

A analytical solution for the twist moment is easily found by a Material Resistance calculation.

Either $\mathit{AB}$ beam length $L$, clamped in $A$, if you apply a $\mathit{mt}$ twist moment to point $C$ from $[\mathit{AB}]$ then the resulting $A$ moment is $\mathit{mt}$. The reaction depending on the moment is $-\mathit{mt}$.

Applying a linear twist moment, distributed over the beam, equal to ${\mathit{mt}}_{A}$ in $A$ and ${\mathit{mt}}_{B}$ in $B$, we obtain the ${M}_{A}$ reaction in $A$ at the moment:

${M}_{A}=-{\int}_{0}^{L}{\mathit{mt}}_{A}+\frac{({\mathit{mt}}_{B}-{\mathit{mt}}_{A})}{L}x\mathit{dx}$

${M}_{A}=-L\frac{({\mathit{mt}}_{A}+{\mathit{mt}}_{B})}{2}$

### 2.2 bending moment

Les formulaires de Résistance des Matériaux fournissent des résultats de référence pour un moment selon $Z$ appliqué au point $C$ d'une poutre $\mathit{AB}$ de longueur $L$ encastrée en $A$ et et en appui selon $Y$ en $B$ .

![Figure](/figures/v3.01.119/1000000000000248000000CF1D036B7855322E72.png)

${R}_{A}=-{R}_{B}=\frac{3\mathit{mfz}({L}^{2}-{b}^{2})}{{\mathrm{2L}}^{3}}$

${M}_{A}=\frac{\mathit{mfz}({L}^{2}-{\mathrm{3b}}^{2})}{{\mathrm{2L}}^{2}}$

where ${R}_{A}$ is the support reaction and ${M}_{A}$ the moment, in $A$.

Applying a linear bending moment, distributed over the beam, equal to ${\mathit{mf}}_{A}$ in $A$ and ${\mathit{mf}}_{B}$ in $B$, you get:

${R}_{A}=-{R}_{B}=\frac{3}{{\mathrm{2L}}^{3}}\underset{0}{\overset{L}{\int}}({\mathit{mf}}_{A}+\frac{({\mathit{mf}}_{B}-{\mathit{mf}}_{A})}{L}x)({L}^{2}-{(L-x)}^{2})\mathit{dx}$

${M}_{A}=\frac{1}{{\mathrm{2L}}^{2}}\underset{0}{\overset{L}{\int}}({\mathit{mf}}_{A}+\frac{({\mathit{mf}}_{B}-{\mathit{mf}}_{A})}{L}x)({L}^{2}-3{(L-x)}^{2})\mathit{dx}$

What gives after integration:

${R}_{A}=-{R}_{B}=\frac{3{\mathit{mf}}_{A}+5{\mathit{mf}}_{B}}{8}$

${M}_{A}=L\frac{{\mathit{mf}}_{B}-{\mathit{mf}}_{A}}{8}$

Note: If you pass in the $\mathit{XOZ}$ plane with the application of a moment according to $Y$, you need to multiply the reactions by $-1$.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

None.
