# u3.13.08 Modellings D_PLAN_HM, D_PLAN_HHM, D_PLAN_THM, D_PLAN_THH, D_PLAN_THHM, AXIS_HM, AXIS_HHM, AXIS_THM, AXIS_THH, AXIS_THHM

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings D_PLAN and AXIS in thermo-hydro-mechanics:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the associated support mesh cells,
- the supported materials and loadings,
- the computation options for the elementary matrices and the post-processing,
- the non-linear capabilities as well as the fracture-mechanics options if they exist.

The modellings D_PLAN_HM, D_PLAN_HHM, D_PLAN_THM, D_PLAN_THH, D_PLAN_THHM, D_PLAN_HM_DIL, D_PLAN_HHM_DIL, D_PLAN_THM_DIL, AXIS_HM, AXIS_HHM, AXIS_THM, AXIS_THH, AXIS_THHM (Phenomenon: MECANIQUE) correspond to finite elements whose support mesh cells are surface mesh cells.

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

DX, DY denote the displacement degrees of freedom.

PRE1 and PRE2 denote two pressure degrees of freedom, whose precise meaning depends on the constitutive laws used. TEMP denotes the temperature.

### 5.2 DYNA_NON_LINE

Non-linear analyses are allowed on these elements but there are not yet any tests in the database. Furthermore, the computation of Rayleigh damping is not yet available for these elements; the material parameters AMOR_ALPHA and AMOR_BETA must therefore be zero so that the analysis does not stop with an error.
