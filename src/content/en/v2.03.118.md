# v2.03.118 SDLS118 - Response of a rigid circular foundation to space-variable seismic excitation

**Summary:**

This test case validates the calculation of the response of a rigid surface foundation subjected to a spatially variable seismic movement via the DYNA_ISS_VARI macro. The reference transfer functions are derived from results obtained by Mita and Luco [bib2].

Modelling E is a computer validation of the accelerograms account in the three directions of space in one call to DYNA_ISS_VARI.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The MISS3D software uses the frequent coupling method to account for the soil-structure interaction. This method, based on dynamic substructure, consists of cutting the study domain into three subdomains which are the soil, foundation and structure. Here we deal with the case of a surface foundation alone (without structure). It is a circular foundation of $>$ radius. The geometry is that of the foundation treated in the reference [bib1] and represented in paragraph 3.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Seismic loading consists of a unit excitation in the frequency domain. This allows to determine directly the transfer functions (between seismic excitation and structural response). The foundation is considered rigid. This translates into a solid restraint of the GROUP_NO radier.

## 7 Modelling E

### 7.1 Modelling characteristics

This Modelling is identical to Modelling B.

### 7.2 Principle

A calculation is performed with DYNA_ISS_VARI by indicating a different signal in each direction (ACCE_X, ACCE_Y and ACCE_Z operands of EXCIT_SOL). Three other calculations are then started, for which only one sign is given (ACCE_X, ACCE_Y and then ACCE_Z). The results are rammed on a physical basis. The results of the unidirectional calculations are then combined using CREA_CHAMP and CREA_RESU. The result created is the reference for the result with signals in the three directions.

## 8 Summary of the results

This test case allows the DYNA_ISS_VARI command to be validated by calculating the response of a rigid surface foundation represented either by rigid body modes (MODE_INTERF='CORP_RIGI') or by any modes on spring mats determined from static ground impedances (MODE_INTERF='QUELCONQUE'). The results obtained with DYNA_ISS_VARI are in good agreement with the results of the Mita and Luco reference.
