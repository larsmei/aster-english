# v5.01.111 SDND111 - DIS_CHOC, with contact managed in the global reference, in nonlinear dynamics

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check the contact in the global reference for the discrete `K_T_D_L` and `K_TR_D_L` types in nonlinear dynamics.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry A

The geometry is shown in the figure below:

![Figure](/figures/v5.01.111/v5.01.111_schema_01.png)

*Geometry of Reference problem.*

The geometry consists of:

- 10 discrete `DIS_T_D_N` type. Four unformable solids are represented by 4 $\color{red}{\blacklozenge}$ and 1 $\color{green}{\blacklozenge}$, which are connected with an `LIAISON_SOLIDE`. $\color{red}{\blacklozenge}$ are `DIS_T_D_N`, $\color{green}{\blacklozenge}$ are `DIS_TR_D_L`.
- 2 discrete `DIS_T_D_L` type to represent $\color{blue}{━━━}$ shock discretes in the global marker.
- 8 `DIS_T_D_L` discretes to represent $\color{fuchsia}{━━━}$ shock discretes in their local landmark.

### 1.2 Loading

#### 1.2.1 boundary conditions

The centres of the 4 unformable solids represented by an $\color{green}{\blacklozenge}$ are affected by an `DIS_TR_D_L`, one of which is nodes, the other being connected to 4 $\color{red}{\blacklozenge}$.

The displacements are applied to the centre of the solid $A$.
