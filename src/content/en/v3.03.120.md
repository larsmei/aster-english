# v3.03.120 SSLS120 - Cylindrical thin shell under hydrostatic pressure

**Summary:**

This test represents a static calculation of thin cylindrical tank filled with water. It validates the correct consideration of the function pressures of geometry, as well as the orthotropic elastic materials. 6 modellings elements finished are used: AXIS, COQUE_3D with mesh cells QUAD9, COQUE_3D with mesh cells TRIA7, and DKT with mesh cells QUAD4, 3D with mesh cells HEXA20 and D_PLAN with mesh cells QUAD8. The displacements and stresses obtained are compared to an analytical reference solution.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

- *Isotropic material*: analytical solution bib1, obtained with the thin shell hypothesis:

${\sigma}_{zz}=0$

${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }=\mathrm{P0}R\frac{(L-z)}{Le}$

${u}_{r}=\frac{\mathrm{P0}{R}^{2}}{Ee}\left[1-\frac{z}{L}\right]$

${u}_{z}=\frac{-\mathrm{P0}Rvz}{Ee}\left[1-\frac{z}{\mathrm{2L}}\right]$

Radial displacement at cylinder base: ${u}_{r}(z=0)=\frac{\mathrm{P0}{R}^{2}}{Ee}$

Vertical displacement at the top of cylinder: ${u}_{z}(z=L)=\frac{-\mathrm{P0}RLv}{2Ee}$

Circumferential stress at the bottom of the cylinder ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }(z=0)=\frac{\mathrm{P0}R}{e}$

- *Orthotropic material*: The solution can be deduced from the previous one: the stresses are statically determined, it is enough to modify the constitutive law, and to integrate the deformations. In order to make the solution independent of the different notations (the value ${E}_{T}$ does not have the same meaning according to the d-orthotropy marker), one places itself as a cylindrical marker $(r,\theta ,z)$ .

Radial displacement at cylinder base: ${u}_{r}(z=0)=\frac{\mathrm{P0}{R}^{2}}{{E}_{\theta}e}$

Vertical displacement at the top of cylinder: ${u}_{z}(z=L)=\frac{-\mathrm{P0}RL{v}_{\theta z}}{2{E}_{\theta}e}$

Circumferential stress at the bottom of the cylinder ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }(z=0)=\frac{\mathrm{P0}R}{e}$

## 9 Summary of the results

The results of the five modellings are very close to the analytical solution: up to 0.4% difference for modellings COQUE_3D and DKT, and less than 2% d的difference for axisymmetric and 3D modelling, which is explained by the fact that the analytical solution is a thin hull solution.

This test therefore validates the pressure forces linearly varying with geometry, for thin shells, and the consideration of orthotropic elasticity.
