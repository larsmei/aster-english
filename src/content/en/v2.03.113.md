# v2.03.113 SDLS113 – Flat-plated deformation under harmonic pressure

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to validate the calculation of a flat deformation plate (D_PLAN) subjected to sinusoidal pressure using a harmonic calculation.

For this purpose, two calculations are performed on the same model, a harmonic calculation and a transient calculation. The transient calculation serves as a reference.

## 1 Reference problem

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.113/Shape1.gif)

### 1.1 Geometry

Contact details of points expressed in metres:

> $A:(0.0,0.0)$
> $B:(0.35,0.0)$
> $C:(0.35,0.6)$
> $D:(0.0,0.6)$

### 1.2 Elastic properties of the material

- $E=1.8\times {10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$     Young's modulus
- $\nu =0.3$     Poisson's ratio
- $\rho =7800.0{\mathrm{kg.m}}^{-3}$ Volumic mass
- $\alpha =3\times {10}^{-5}s$
- $\beta =0.001{s}^{-1}$

$\alpha$ and $\beta$ coefficients allow the construction of a viscous damping matrix proportional to the stiffness and mass $[C]=\alpha [K]+\beta [M]$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

  - Clamped support on the side $\mathit{AD}$

$\mathrm{DX}=0.0m$ , $\mathrm{DY}=0.0m$

    - The $\mathit{BC}$ side is subjected to an $p={10}^{5}\mathit{Pa}$ amplitude harmonic pressure at a $f=1500\mathrm{Hz}$ frequency

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The answer is calculated for a plate subjected to harmonic pressure on one side of the plate and clamped on the opposite side.

The reference solution is obtained by performing a transient response calculation with the DYNA_VIBRA operator using the Newmark integration scheme with the $alpha=0.25$ and $delta=0.5$ parameters.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The following quantitiess are proposed to be tested:

- Next $x$ shift to point coordinates
- Gauss point stress and deformation of an mesh cell containing node coordinates $(0.3383,0.39)$
- Node coordinate stress and deformation $(0.3383,0.39)$

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

The scheme is considered to be established after 90 periods. The reference values used are those recorded for the 98th and 99th periods of the transitional response.
