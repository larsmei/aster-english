# v6.03.107 SSNP107 - Traction-slicing plate: Lemaître viscoelasticity and isotropic hardening

**Summary:**

This non-linear quasi-static mechanical test consists of loading a square plate in a tensile-strain. This is the basis for the sequence of several calculations with alternatively a plastic constitutive law with isotropic nutring and a viscoelastic constitutive law of Lemaître, the value of internal variables (cumulative plastic deformation and load indicator (discharge) at the end of a calculation being resumed at the beginning of the next calculation. The load remains radial throughout the test.

The plate is modelled by a element volume (HEXA8).
