# v3.03.150 SSLS150 – Verification of the securing of reinforced concrete plates

**Summary:**

This test concerns the analytical verification of the calculation of the mechanical margin of the railing and the critical angle of a reinforced concrete plate with operator VERI_FERRAILLAGE.
the calculation of the equilibrium state parameters of the critical section with the operator POST_VERI_FERRAILLAGE.
Several cases of loadings are studied at ELU, ELS (Characteristics) and BAEL91.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A square plate of $1m$ and $h$ thickness is considered.

### 1.2 Material properties

None.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The mechanical resolution operator is not called, VERI_FERRAILLAGE input is given generalized (calculation and reference) and analytical ferrailment forces in the following configurations:

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

For each configuration and the corresponding loading cases, the analytical margin of the critical facet is calculated according to the formula (see `Veri_ferraillage` [r7.04.05](doc:r7.04.05)):

$$
𝑚=1−\frac{𝐶_0 𝐶}{𝐶_0 𝐶_{𝑅𝑑}}
$$

### 2.2 Uncertainties about the solution

None.
