# v1.03.134 MFRON08 – Code_Aster-MF interface test for modellings GRAD_VARI

**Summary:**

This test validates the implementation of a von Mises law with negative isotropic hardening regulated by the gradient of an internal variable. An answer with localization of the deformation field on a loaded bar in single traction is concerned.

Model A considers AXIS_GRAD_VARI and AXIS_GRAD_HHO modelling.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a bar of length $1$ m and width $0.1$ m defined by $0\leq y \leq 1$ m and $0\leq x \leq 0.1$ m.

### 1.2 boundary conditions

- On the $x=0$ edge, the displacement is blocked in $DX$.
- On the $y=0$ edge, the displacement is blocked in $DY$.
- On the $y=1$ m edge, the displacement is driven in $DY$ from $0$ to $25$ mm.

### 1.4 Material properties

The characteristics used in the non-local von Mises law are:

- Young's modulus $\youngModulus = 200$ MPa
- Poisson's ratio $\poissonCoef = 0.3$
- Initial yield strength $\sigma_0 = 1$ MPa
- $H = -20$ MPa isotropic nutring module
- $C = \sigma_0 (\delta/\pi)^2$ offshoring parameter with $\delta = 0.6$ m
- Penalization coefficient $r = -H$

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics et du mesh

This is AXIS_GRAD_VARI Modelling. The mesh is composed of 40 mesh cells QUAD8.

To trigger the field location of deformations in the softened plasticization regime, the initial $\sigma_0$ yield yield is reduced by $5\%$ on 2 elements in the bar length from the lower end.

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

Non-regression is tested on the result DY of the stress on the upper end of the bar at the end of loading at $U = 25$ mm.

| **Component** | **Reference type** | **Value** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DY | NON_REGRESSION | $953.85$ N |

**Note**

The previous result value is that of a section of a radian in the test piece, so it must be multiplied by $2\pi$ to recover the resulting stress on the entire axisymmetric test piece.

The following figure shows the stress response with the displacement applied and the evolution of the profile of the cumulative plastic deformation during loading. In agreement with a harmonic support profile $\delta = 0.6$ m.
