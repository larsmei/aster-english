# v3.02.119 SSLP119 – Calculation of the static safety factor of a homogeneous embankment with POST_NEWMARK

**Summary:**

This test compares the static safety factor estimates for a homogeneous embankment by POST_NEWMARK to other methods used by other geotechnical computing software.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A homogeneous embankment of 15 m high and slope 1:2 (26.6°) is considered. A sliding circle joining the top and bottom of the embankment is considered, the centre of which is positioned at (6.08, 49.6).

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and mechanical loadings

#### 1.3.1 boundary condition

The boundary conditions applied to the model are:

- *Bas* *e*: zero displacement in vertical direction (DY)
- *Right and left sides*: zero displacement according to horizontal direction (DX)

#### 1.3.2 Loading

Gravity type loading applied to the model.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The displacement is zero throughout the model at the initial moment.

### 1.5 Position of sliding circle

A mesh of the sliding circle is provided, the centre of which is positioned at $(\mathrm{6.08,}49.6)$, with a radius of $50m$.

## 2 reference solution

The POST_NEWMARK macrocommand result is compared with other safety factor estimation methods, based on the Potgieter results [1]:

- Band method by SLOPE/W software
- *Enhanced Limit Equilibrium* by SIGMA/W and SLOPE/W software
- C-phi reduction by PLAXIS 2D software.

The approach chosen by the POST_NEWMARK macro-command is similar to the *Enhanced Limit Equilibrium* method and the results must be as close to this method.

The reference values obtained by Potgieter [1] for these three methods are as follows:

- Band method: FS = 1.73
- *Enhanced Limit Equilibrium*: FS = 1.82
- Reduction c-phi: FS = 1.72

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Model A uses a behavior model for the linear type dam, the calculation is based on operator MECA_STATIQUE. The safety factor assessment is performed:

- from a 2D auxiliary mesh sufficiently refined sliding circle.
- from mesh of the sliding circle built directly by the macro-command

### 3.2 Tested quantities and results

The value of the overall static safety factor estimated by the Newmark method is tested, comparing it with the result of the *Enhanced Limit Equilibrium (ELE) method.*
