# v6.04.229 SSNV229 - Validation of ETCC formulas in DEFI_CABLE_BP and failure of a cable

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test case is to bring elements validation of the DEFI_CABLE_BP operator for the calculation of voltage profiles in prestress cables in concrete structures. This is:

- validate the ETCC formulae installed in Code_Aster that allow for the loss of tension by friction between cables and concrete, by recoiling at anchors, and by relaxation of steel, the material constituting the cables (modellings A and B);
- validate the procedure developed to model the breakage of a cable with re-anchoring (keyword factor MODI_CABLE_ETCC of the DEFI_CABLE_BP command) (modelization C).

The structure is a cylinder, containing ten prestressed cables in its thickness. The cables each describe a circle in a horizontal plane, and travel the structure along its length. All the cables are anchored on the same line.

Modelling A allows the direct consideration of steel relaxation to be tested (approximate method). Modelling B allows the further modelling of steel relaxation by performing a short-term calculation and a long-term calculation that can be combined with the modelling of the removal and creep in concrete. In both cases, the results obtained are validated by comparison with those theoretically expected.

Model C Modelling modeled the failure of one of the 10 prestress cables after the voltage setting. It was verified that the result obtained was in accordance with what was injected into the calculation.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Normal effort in cables according to the ETCC

The reference solution for modellings A and B was obtained by an Excel spreadsheet by GDS.

Both modellings correspond to two methods of calculating prestress loss due to steel relaxation.

- In model A, relaxation losses are considered by neglecting elastic losses (direct calculation with DEFI_CABLE_BP) either:

${F}_{i}(s)={F}_{0}{\exp}^{-\mu (\theta +ks)}-\text{recul d'ancrage}$

And finally:

$F(s)={F}_{i}(s)-0,66{\rho}_{1000}.{\exp}^{9,1{F}_{i}(s)/{F}_{\mathit{prg}}}.{(\frac{t}{1000})}^{0,75(1-{F}_{i}(s))/{F}_{\mathit{prg}}}.{10}^{-5}{F}_{i}(s)$

- In model B, the losses by relaxation of steels are calculated from the voltage taking into account the elastic losses obtained by a previous calculation, where the cables were energized into 2 groups, i.e.:

for Group 1 (unfair cables):

${F}_{i}^{1}(s)={F}_{0}{\exp}^{-\mu (\theta +ks)}-\text{recul d'ancrage}-\frac{{A}_{p}{E}_{p}\Delta {\sigma}_{c}(x)}{E}$

for Group 2 (peer cables):

${F}_{i}^{2}(s)={F}_{0}{\exp}^{-\mu (\theta +ks)}-\text{recul d'ancrage}$

et $F(s)={F}_{i}^{1,2}(s)-0,66{\rho}_{1000}.{\exp}^{9,1{F}_{i}^{1,2}(s)/{F}_{\mathit{prg}}}.{\left(\frac{t}{1000}\right)}^{0,75(1-{F}_{i}^{1,2}(s))/{F}_{\mathit{prg}}}.{10}^{-5}{F}_{i}^{1,2}(s)$

The reference value was actually obtained by considering that the same elastic losses were present on all $\Delta {F}_{\mathit{el}}(s)=\frac{{A}_{p}{E}_{p}\Delta {\sigma}_{c}(x)}{2E}$ cables.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics et mesh

The mesh is identical to Modelling A. On the other hand, the voltage calculation is performed in 2 steps.

- In the first, the voltage calculation is performed by taking into account only friction and anchoring losses. The tensioning is then carried out using CALC_PRECONT , in two steps allowing to recover elastic losses: the tensioning of odd cables in the first stage and the tensioning of even cables.
- In the second phase, the calculation of the tension of the cables is carried out taking into account the relaxation of the steels.

## 6 Summary of the results

The results obtained are validated by comparison with those theoretically expected with good accuracy.

The specific features tested are:

- DEFI_MATERIAU [u4.23.01](doc:u4.23.01) operator: definition of characteristic parameters of steel and concrete materials for voltage calculation along prestressing cables, in accordance with ETCC rules;
- DEFI_CABLE_BP [u4.23.06](doc:u4.23.06) operator: calculation of voltage along cables and in particular the key words factor MODI_CABLE_ETCC and MODI_CABLE_RUPT .
