# v3.01.402 SSLL402 - Dynamometric ring

**Summary:**

This test allowed the linear elasticity to check the calculation of the internal forces and stresses on a curved beam (POU_C_T), these elements have been removed. straight beams are now being used to treat the problem.

Modelling allows testing the elements of Timoshenko's straight beams (POU_D_T).

The reference solution is analytical and the results are of very good quality.

## 2 Reference solutions

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solutions: analytical

On the $(A,B)(0<\theta <\frac{\pi}{2})$ section we have:

$N=\frac{F}{2}\cos\theta$ , $\mathrm{Vy}=-\frac{F}{2}\sin\theta$ , ${M}_{z}=-\frac{\mathrm{FR}}{2}(1-\cos\theta )+\Gamma$ .

On the $(B,C)(\frac{\pi}{2}<\theta <\pi )$ section we have:

$N=-\frac{F}{2}\cos\theta$ , $\mathrm{Vy}=\frac{F}{2}\sin\theta$ , ${M}_{z}=-\frac{\mathrm{FR}}{2}(1+\cos\theta )+\Gamma$ .

By using the constitutive law connecting $M$ to normal rotation and considering that the normal rotation is zero in $A$ and $B$, we have:

${\int}_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}Md\theta =0$ ,

d’où: $\Gamma ={M}_{A}={M}_{C}=\frac{\pi -2}{2\pi }FR$

### 2.2 Baseline

### 3.4 Remarks

The symmetry with $(A,C)$ implies the nullity of the shear force $T$ in $A$ and $C$ . The following $\mathit{Oy}$ balance of the $(A,B,C)$ half-ring imposes in $A$ and $C$ a normal effort equal to $\frac{F}{2}$ . The symmetry with $(B,D)$ implies that the $A$ and $C$ moments are equal in absolute value and contrary meaning.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results are very close to the analytical solution (error less than 1.0E-05) except for two values (N to $\theta =90°$ and Vy to $\theta =0°$ ) for which the error is 2.0E-03. This error is observed to be slowly decreasing when the mesh is refined, but the accuracy of 1.0E-05 is still not reached with 1000 elements per quarter of circle.
