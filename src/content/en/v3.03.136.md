# v3.03.136 SSLS136 – RBE3-type relationships between a plate and a discrete

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check the RBE3 relationship between a DKT mesh hole plate and a discrete one.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A $10\mathrm{mm}$ side plate is considered to be pierced with a $1\mathrm{mm}$ hole in diameter.

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.136/10000200000000FD000000F1D333B8C1F5065F91.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

| $E=\mathrm{1MPa}$ | Young's modulus |
| --- | --- |
| $\nu =0.3$ | Poisson's ratio |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The plate is blocked on all its periphery following its degrees of freedom $\mathrm{DX}$, $\mathrm{DY}$, $\mathrm{DZ}$, $\mathrm{DRX}$, $\mathrm{DRY}$, $\mathrm{DRZ}$.

A discreet degrees of freedom $\mathrm{DX}$ , $\mathrm{DY}$ , $\mathrm{DZ}$ is placed in the centre of the hole. It is connected by an restraint $\mathrm{RBE3}$ type to the entire periphery of the circular hole.

The center of the circle is subjected to nodal force $\mathrm{FX}=-0.123456701636$, $\mathrm{FY}=-0.246913403273$, $\mathrm{FZ}=-0.370370090008$.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None
