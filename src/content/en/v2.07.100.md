# v2.07.100 SHLV100 - Harmonic response of a hollow cylinder to flat deformations

**Summary:**

This three-dimensional axisymmetric test validates calculations of stiffness, mass, and pressure vectors on all elements $\mathrm{3D}$ and $\mathrm{2D}$ flat deformations and axisymmetric (10 modellings).

Travel is required:

- either by degrees of freedom,
- or per face of element.

For the four modellings 3D, applied pressures are provided with the minus sign, as the faces of elements $\mathrm{3D}$ are poorly oriented in the mesh files used.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

![Figure](/figures/v2.07.100/Object_7.svg)

$A$ and $B$ constants are calculated by solving the linear system obtained by writing:

${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}(a)=-p$ ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}(b)=0$

We get:

| For $r=0.1$ | ${u}_{r}=7.3398{10}^{-3}$ | For $r=0.2$ | ${u}_{r}=4.6816{10}^{-3}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}=-1$ |  | ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}=0.$ |
|  | ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }=1.6685$ |  | ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }=0.66738$ |
|  | ${\sigma}_{zz}=0.20055$ |  | ${\sigma}_{zz}=0.20031$ |

Crossing into the Cartesian axis system:

$\begin{array}{ccc}{\sigma}_{xx}& =& {\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}{\cos}^{2}\theta +{\sigma}_{\theta \theta }{\sin}^{2}\theta -2{\sigma}_{r\theta }\sin\theta \cos\theta \\ {\sigma}_{yy}& =& {\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}{\sin}^{2}\theta +{\sigma}_{\theta \theta }{\cos}^{2}\theta +2{\sigma}_{r\theta }\sin\theta \cos\theta \\ {\sigma}_{xy}& =& {\sigma}_{\mathrm{rr}}\sin\theta \cos\theta -{\sigma}_{\theta \theta }\sin\theta \cos\theta -2{\sigma}_{r\theta }({\cos}^{2}\theta -{\sin}^{2}\theta )\end{array}$

with:

$\theta =0°$ at $A$ and $B$ points

$\theta =22.5°$ at $C$ and $D$ points

$\theta =45°$ at $E$ and $F$ points
