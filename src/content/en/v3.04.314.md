# v3.04.314 SSLV314 - Flat spreading of an opening crack with X-FEM

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to compare the simplex, mesh, upwind, geometric propagation methods of the PROPA_FISS operator for a $\mathrm{3D}$ crack requested in pure $I$ mode.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.314/10000000000001980000024F039F309296A63C31.jpg)

**Figure** 1.1-a **: geometry of cracked plate**

Geometrical dimensions of the cracked plate:

| width | $L=8m$ |
| --- | --- |
| thickness | $E=1m$ |
| height | $H=18m$ |

The crack is horizontal, the front being initially positioned in $(x,2,9)$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus $E=205000\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

boundary conditions:

- Point $P$ : $\Delta X=\Delta Y=\Delta Z=0$
- Points on the $\mathrm{AB}$ segment: $\Delta X=\Delta Z=0$
- Surface points $\text{INF}$ : $\Delta Z=0$

Loading:

- Surface pressure $\text{SUP}$: $P=-1\mathrm{MPa}$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

non-regression.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The results of Modelling B (mesh method) are taken as a reference.

For Modelling B and C, the non-regression of the code is checked against the position of the crack bottom.

For modellings A, D, E and F, the nodes closest to the trace of the crack bottom on the $(1,y,z)$ plane at the last moment of propagation shall be checked for level-sets very close to zero.

| **Instant of propagation** | **Nœud** | **Coordinate** ${y}_{i}$ | **Coordinate** ${z}_{i}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | $\mathit{N926}$ | 2.33 | 8.80 |
| $\mathit{N1028}$ | 2.33 | 9.00 |  |
| $\mathit{N1130}$ | 2.33 | 9.20 |  |

These nodes are those included in a capture radius of the largest edge of an element, centered on the trace of the crack bottom on the $(1,y,z)$ plane.

These nodes are identified in the .mess of model B and the value of their level-set is estimated in the modellings A, D , E and F .

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The **simplex** method is used by PROPA_FISS.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a mesh composed of 2040 elements HEXA8 (see ).

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.314/100002010000013B000003381CEECC25E0FB087B.png)

Figure 3.2-a: maillage de la structure

The mesh is very coarse to reduce the calculation time. A little smaller elements are used in the crack propagation area. The elements dimension is $0.17\times 0.33\times 0.2m$ in this area. The largest element used has a dimension equal to $0.17\times 0.8\times 1.6m$.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The normal ($\mathrm{LSN}$) and tangent ($\mathrm{LST}$) level set is extracted using the POST_RELEVE_T operator and the maximum and minimum values remain within the capture radius of the nodes tested around the crack front, i.e. 1/3:

| $\mathrm{Propag.}i$ | $\mathrm{Max}{\mathrm{LSN}}_{i}$ | $\mathrm{Min}{\mathrm{LSN}}_{i}$ | $\mathrm{Max}{\mathrm{LST}}_{i}$ | $\mathrm{Min}{\mathrm{LST}}_{i}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 0.2 | 0.2 | 0.267 | 0.267 |

The results show that the level-set remains below the radius in which the crack front was located, which means that the crack bottom was correctly located by the simplex method.
