# v6.04.133 SSNV133 - Uniaxial compression-traction. Mixed scrubbing

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the VMIS_ECMI_TRAC and VMIS_ECMI_LINE elastoplastic behaviors, which combine isotropic (linear or given by a tensile curve) hardening with linear kinematic hardening.

Geometric and mechanical data allow to be in a uniaxial situation (uniform constraints, a single non-zero component). The reference solution is simple, analytical. This test simply allows to verify that the integration of the behavior model is correct.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is obtained by analytical calculation.

The ${u}_{y}^{D}$ displacement is immediately used to provide ${\epsilon}_{yy}=\frac{{u}_{y}^{D}}{1}$ deformation. Only this component (corresponding to the non-zero component of the stress tensor) is of interest to us here.

On the other hand, to calculate the behavior, we must extract data from the $R(p)$ isotropic hardening function:

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.133/Object_7.svg)

$$
{\sigma}^{t}=F(\epsilon )={\sigma}_{y}+\frac{E.{E}_{T}}{E-{E}_{T}}p

R(p)={\sigma}_{y}+\left(\frac{E.{E}_{T}}{E-{E}_{T}}-\frac{3}{2}C\right)p
$$

#### 2.1.1 Elastic behaviour

The behavior is elastic up to $t=1$. For $t=1$, the ${\sigma}_{yy}=E{\epsilon}_{yy}=400\mathit{MPa}$ constraint, it just reaches the plasticity threshold.

#### 2.1.2 Elastoplastic charge

At $t=2$ , the plasticity criterion (laden or landfilled) is met:

$$
|\sigma -\frac{3}{2}C{\epsilon}^{p}|=R(p)

\textrm{et}

\sigma =E(\epsilon -{\epsilon}^{p})
$$

For the load, the following equation must therefore be solved:

$$
{\sigma}^{t}=F(\epsilon )={\sigma}_{y}+\frac{E.{E}_{T}}{E-{E}_{T}}p={\sigma}_{y}+\frac{E.{E}_{T}}{E-{E}_{T}}\left(\epsilon -\frac{{\sigma}^{t}}{E}\right)
$$

Which gives ${\sigma}^{t}={\sigma}_{y}+{E}_{T}\left(\epsilon -\frac{{\sigma}_{y}}{E}\right)$ back. Moves on the traction curve to point $A$ such as:

$$
{\sigma}_{A}^{t}=E\left({\epsilon}_{A}-{\epsilon}_{A}^{p}\right)
$$

#### 2.1.3 Elastic discharge

At $t=3$ , the discharge is elastic to point $A’$ :

$$
-{\sigma}^{A'}-\frac{3}{2}C{\epsilon}_{A}^{p}=R({p}_{A})

{\sigma}^{A'}=E\left({\epsilon}_{A'}-{\epsilon}_{A}^{p}\right)
$$

#### 2.1.4 Elastoplastic charge in compression

A $t=4$ :

$$
{\sigma}^{c}=\frac{3}{2}C{\epsilon}^{p}-R(p)
$$
