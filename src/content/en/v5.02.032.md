# v5.02.032 SDNL32 - Impact of an articulated beam on elastic support

**Summary:**

This problem is a transient modal superposition analysis of a nonlinear system consisting of a flexible beam rotulated at one of its ends. It is animated by a solid body rotation movement at the initial moment and encounters an elastic stop with unilateral contact. The problem has an reference solution proposed by the VPCS Commission.

There is a Modelling with elements POU_D_T. The vibratory dynamic operator is tested. differences with reference solution does not exceed $\text{0.8\%}$.

## 4 Summary of the results

The reference solution ($\text{1.2\%}$) is in good agreement despite the presence of a non-linearity of shock and the use of a modal recombination method with 1 modal base reduced to 10 modes. The greatest difference is observed for the steepest non-linear stop.
