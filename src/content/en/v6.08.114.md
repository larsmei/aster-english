# v6.08.114 SSND114 – constitutive law for flexion damageable elastoplastic junctions with discrete elements

**Summary:**

The response of the behavior model is tested to model a bending junction between a sail and a floor or radier, accessible by the keyword JONC_ENDO_PLAS, cf. [R5.03.17](doc:r5.03.17), formulated on discrete elements on mesh cells SEG2.

The behavior is tested first with the STAT_NON_LINE operator. The reference values are numerical and obtained by an independent method: the response is tested on rotation-moment cycles.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Description

The nonlinear rheological JONC_ENDO_PLAS junction model is evaluated by the response it provides in terms of rotation-moment curve, in the local reference:'MZ' obtained by 'DRZ' rotations, regardless of the orientation of the local reference and other directions that remain linear elastic.

### 1.2 modellings

The modellings tested are all with elements DIS_TR on mesh cells SEG2. The stiffness features of the discrete elements, which are used for prediction of the nonlinear algorithm, are therefore of the type: K_TR_D_L.

Note: The units of the parameters must be in accordance with the unit of forces and moments, length unit and time unit of the [R5.03.17](doc:r5.03.17) problem. For all modellings units are homogeneous to [N], [m], [s].

#### 1.2.1 Modelling A

This Modelling allows testing the static, nonlinear, cyclic behaviour of the JONC_ENDO_PLAS behavioral relation.

Elements 1 to 8 are each identified by mesh-cell group (L1 to L8) and nodes 1 to 16 by nodes (P1 to P16) groups.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The tested Modelling includes 8 DIS_TR elements on mesh cells SEG2. The stiffness features of the discrete elements, which are used for prediction of the nonlinear algorithm, are thus of the type: K_TR_D_L.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of nodes: 16, number of mesh cells SEG2: 8, elements DIS_TR: 8. See § 1.2.1 .

### 3.3 Boundary conditions and loads

See § 1.4 .

### 3.4 Time Discretion

The time step and the analysis time interval are:

$\Delta t=1,0{10}^{-2}\phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}s$ et $t\in [0s,24s]$

### 3.5 Tested quantities and results

The quantities tested are rotations, and moments. The values are those presented in § 2.2 .

Tolerances are the defaults.

## 4 Summary of the results

These tests allow to check the smooth operation of elements discrete $\mathrm{3D}$ with the JONC_ENDO_PLAS behavior as part of the use of STAT_NON_LINE and DYNA_NON_LINE.
