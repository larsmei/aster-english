# v4.61.102 MTLP102 - Metallurgical calculation for a steel with grain size consideration

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to observe the evolution of grain size as a function of temperature change in the case of steel.

The grain size is calculated at nodes $A$ , $B$ and $C$ by the CALC_META operator.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v4.61.102/10000000000001450000010544C605D9C3A7B20B.png)

Bar Section

It is an infinite bar with square cross-section $L=0.05m$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

The material properties are described by the following parameters:

(Salt 16MND5)

$\rho {C}_{P}=5260000{\mathrm{J.m}}^{-3}.°{C}^{-1}$

$\lambda =33.5{\mathrm{W.m}}^{-1}.°{C}^{-1}$

Metallurgy coefficients:

TRC

$\mathrm{AR3}=830°C$ , $alpha=-0.0306$

$\mathrm{MS0}=400°C$ , $\mathrm{AC1}=724°C$ , $\mathrm{AC3}=846°C$

${\tau}_{1}=0.034$ , ${\tau}_{3}=0.034$

${\lambda}_{0}=0.117$ , $\mathrm{qsr}=37500$ ,

$\mathrm{D10}=3.31$ , $\mathrm{wsr}=12860$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Temperature is imposed on the entire bar at the times $t=\mathrm{0s}$, $\mathrm{200s}$, $\mathrm{1100s}$ and $\mathrm{1900s}$.

$T(x,y,t=0)=700°C$

$T(x,y,t=200)=900°C$

$T(x,y,t=1100)=900°C$

$T(x,y,t=1900)=100°C$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The following variables are initialized to the following values:

$\mathrm{Zf}(x,y,0)=0.7$

$\mathrm{Zp}(x,y,0)=0.0$

$\mathrm{Zb}(x,y,0)=0.3$

$\mathrm{Zm}(x,y,0)=0.0$

$d(x,y,0)=0.0$
