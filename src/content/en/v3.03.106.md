# v3.03.106 SSLS106 - Pungged spherical shell with hole

**Summary:**

The case of the shell is treated with a hole, doubly pinched in linear elasticity. The values tested are the deflections at the application points of the forces.

Modelling is available:

- $A$ : elements COQUE_SOLIDE

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A hemisphere is considered cut into 4 pieces (by symmetry around the equatorial planes).

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.106/100002010000032D0000022BAD420090D7F3AB79.png)

Rayon of the sphere: $R=10m$

Sphere thickness: $t=0,04m$

The hole is open: 18°

### 1.2 Material properties

The sphere is made of aluminium. The material is elastic isotropic linear.

$E=6.825{10}^{7}\mathit{Pa}$ , $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On a quarter of the hemisphere symmetry conditions are applied on the three planes (which are therefore QUAD4):

- $(12)$ plane: $\mathit{DZ}=0$;
- $(13)$ plane: $\mathit{DY}=0$;
- $(23)$ plane: $\mathit{DX}=0$ .

In addition, a load in the form of a point load is applied to points A and B such as:

- Point A: $\mathit{FX}=0,5N$ ;
- Point B: $\mathit{FY}=-0,5N$ .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in [Bib1].

### 2.2 Baseline

Move the point $A$ following $x$ .
