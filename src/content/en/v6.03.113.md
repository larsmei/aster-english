# v6.03.113 SSNP113 - Rotation of main constraints (law of MAZARS)

**Summary:**

This test case of mechanics is based on Willam's work [bib1] and has been used in the EDF/R&D benchmark "Three-dimensional models of nonlinear behaviour of cracking concrete material" [bib2] to evaluate concrete-specific behaviour models. It is characterized by a specific loading path that creates a continuous rotation of the main stresses. It is used here to test the implantation of the Mazars model. Validation is performed by comparison with the results obtained with the CASTEM2000 code at LGCNSN (Central School of Nantes).

## 2 reference solution

This is a code-code comparison. The reference used is Castem2000 (version 2001). The results were obtained by the LGCNSN (Central School of Nantes) with the same material parameters and the same discretization in time. Unlike the calculation made with *Code_Aster*, the Castem calculation was performed in $\mathrm{2D}$ under the hypothesis of flat stresses.

## 5 Summary of the results

With differences very low in the order of $\text{0.05 \%}$ at the maximum on stresses in the most nonlinear phase and in the order of $\text{0.002 \%}$ after complete damage on a test where the loads are not radial, it can be considered that the implantation of the Mazars model is true to the original model.
