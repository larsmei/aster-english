# v1.01.400 ZZZZ400 - Validation of a field of internal variables with multiple behaviors in LIRE_RESU in MED format

**Summary:**

This test has no physical meaning, it is essentially a computer test.

The purpose of the manual is to test the correct functioning of the rereading of an internal variable field in LIRE_RESU in MED format and its re-use in a calculation. The initial calculation is performed with two nonlinear behaviors on subparts of the model with a number of different internal variables. The writing in MED format requires the addition of the fields to the maximum number of internal variables. In LIRE_RESU, the field must be rebuilt to the correct size taking into account the argument behavior map. The latter must be consistent with that used in the initial calculation.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
