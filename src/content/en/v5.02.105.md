# v5.02.105 SDNL105 - Non-linear transitional substructure: shock of 3 beams between them

**Summary:**

The scope of this test concerns structural dynamics, and in particular the calculation of nonlinear transient response by dynamic substructure.

The purpose is to calculate the nonlinear transient response of 3 bending beams with impact in the centre of the beams. The beams are modelled by elements type POU_D_E (Euler model).

The reference results are derived from a direct transient calculation by modal recombination. This test therefore allows the validation of the tools for calculating transient response by substructure, in the case of the consideration of non-linearity of the impact type between mobile structures.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v5.02.105/100009BA0000191D00001A9031085C6B3F5E32A3.svg)

The length of the beams is: $L=1m$

The beams are circularly sectioned:

- radius: $R=0.1m$
- thickness: $\mathrm{ep}=0.01m$

The game between beams is: $\mathrm{Jeu}=1{10}^{-3}m$

### 1.2 Material properties

$E=1.{10}^{10}\mathrm{Pa}$

$\nu =0.3$

$\rho =1.{10}^{8}\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$

The contact spring stiffness is: ${K}_{c}={1.10}^{8}N/m$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On the whole structure: $\mathrm{DX}=\mathrm{DZ}=\mathrm{DRX}=\mathrm{DRY}=0$ .

At the upper and lower ends of the beams: $\mathrm{DY}=\mathrm{DRZ}=0$ .

In the middle of the left beam: from the $t=0s$ moment, $\mathrm{Fy}=–1.{10}^{6}N$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Structure initially at rest.

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

![Figure](/figures/v5.02.105/1000055C00001C520000145CF5F8822F0EA268B5.svg)

Dynamic substructure allows the vibrational behaviour of the 3 beams to be calculated from the dynamic characteristics of a single beam. This beam is meshed into segments to which elements POU_D_E type are assigned.

The structure is studied using the "Craig-Bampton" (blocked interface) substructure method.

The basis of the first 15 modes of the complete structure is calculated by sub-structure. Then the transitional problem, projected on this basis, is solved by the transient computing operator by modal recombination.

### 4.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of nodes: 15

Number of mesh cells and types : 14 SEG2

## 5 Summary of the results

The accuracy of the nodes movement and speed of the 3 beams at the moment $t=1s$ is excellent (relative error $<\mathrm{0.01s}$).

This test therefore validates the nonlinear transient computing operators by dynamic substructure.
