# v3.03.505 SSLS505 – Composite plate subjected to purely thermal mechanical deformations

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the thermally-derived mechanical deformation of composite plates on a mesh made up of a 4 nodes quadrangle element.

MODELISATION A: DKT homogeneous plate with orthotropic thermo-elastic behavior via DEFI_COMPOSITE.

## 1 Reference problem

A square is considered on the $\mathrm{1mm}$ side.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is orthotropic elastic (defined via DEFI_COMPOSITE). Orthotropy is of thermal origin, i.e. the longitudinal, transverse and normal expansion properties are:

- ${E}_{L}={E}_{T}={E}_{N}=E=100E9;{\nu}_{LT}={\nu}_{LN}={\nu}_{TN}=\nu =0,3;{G}_{LT}={G}_{LN}={G}_{TN}=E/2(1+\nu )$
- ${\alpha}_{LL}=1.E-5;{\alpha}_{\mathit{TT}}=1.E-6;$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

*Blocking*:

The limits condtions are such that only the thermal membrane effect is allowed. Furthermore, the plate is blocked so that the flat shear effects are negligible in view of the effects of longitudinal and transverse deformations:

- The points N1,N2,N3,N4 are such that: DZ=0.0: which causes zero bending,
- N1,N2 points are blocked:DY=0.0 which makes the flat shear negligible,
- The N1 points are such that: DX=0 what totally recesses this point,
- The nodes N3, N4 are free of any movement in the plane.

* Charge*:

No mechanical loading is applied. The consequence is that the purely elastic deformations are zero. A loading differential is applied to the whole plate:

$T-{T}_{\mathit{ref}}=\Delta T=1°C$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

To determine the analytical solution, the equation of total deformation of a plate with thermal expansion taken into account is used:

$\epsilon \left(u\right)=\left(\begin{array}{c}{E}_{11}\\ {E}_{22}\\ {E}_{12}\end{array}\right)+{x}_{3}\left(\begin{array}{c}{K}_{11}\\ {K}_{22}\\ {K}_{12}\end{array}\right)+{\epsilon}^{\text{th}}\text{et}{\epsilon}^{\text{th}}=\left(\begin{array}{c}{d}_{11}\\ {d}_{22}\\ {d}_{12}\end{array}\right).\left(T\left({x}_{3}\right)-{T}^{\text{réf}}\right)$

With:

${E}_{ij}=\frac{\partial {U}_{i}}{\partial {X}_{j}};{K}_{ij}=\frac{\partial {\beta}_{i}}{\partial {X}_{j}};i,j=1,2$ ${U}_{i}$ being the displacement in direction i and ${X}_{i}$ the coordinate following i. ${E}_{ij}$ is the pure membrane deformation while ${K}_{ij}$ represents the curvature. boundary conditions are such that ${E}_{ij}={K}_{ij}=0$ .

et

${d}^{\left(m\right)}=\left(\begin{array}{c}{d}_{11}\\ {d}_{22}\\ {d}_{12}\end{array}\right)={P}^{{m}^{-1}}{\left(\begin{array}{c}{\alpha}_{\text{LL}}\\ {\alpha}_{\text{TT}}\\ 0\end{array}\right)}_{\left(L,T\right)}=\left(\begin{array}{cc}{C}^{2}& {S}^{2}\\ {S}^{2}& {C}^{2}\\ 2\text{CS}& -2\text{CS}\end{array}\right){\left(\begin{array}{c}{\alpha}_{LL}\\ {\alpha}_{\mathit{TT}}\end{array}\right)}_{\left(L,T\right)}$

${P}^{(m)}=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}{C}^{2}& {S}^{2}& 2\text{CS}\\ {S}^{2}& {C}^{2}& -2\text{CS}\\ -\text{CS}& \text{CS}& {C}^{2}-{S}^{2}\end{array}\right]$ with $\begin{array}{c}C=1\\ S=0\end{array}$

In the end, the expression of deformation is determined analytically:

$\epsilon \left(u\right)={\epsilon}^{\text{th}}=\left(\begin{array}{c}{\alpha}_{\text{LL}}\\ {\alpha}_{\text{TT}}\\ {0}_{\rbrace \end{array}\right).1$

## 4 Summary of the results

This test makes it possible to verify that the thermal expansion of an orthotropic plate modelled with the DKT is correctly calculated (with zero elastic deformation) via DEFI_COMPOSITE.

The expected analytical result is exactly what we are seeing.
