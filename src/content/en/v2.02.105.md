# v2.02.105 SDLL105 - Pipe subjected to sources of random fluid excitation

**Summary:**

A straight pipe clamped at one end in the wall of a tank and supporting one mass at the other end is subjected to fluid excitation.

Excitation is defined by its power spectral density as a "white noise".

It covers all source types implanted in the code:

- volume flow source,
- mass flow source,
- pressure source,
- source of force,
- The effort imposed.

The spectral power density of the response in a pressure degree of freedom on the node supporting the mass is considered.

The random dynamic response is given here in absolute motion.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.105/100008FA0000319C000011C76119886E1226E597.svg)

Circular section hose:

Outer diameter: $0.1m$

Thickness: $3\mathrm{mm}$

The gravity field is not taken into account.

### 1.2 Material properties

| Young's modulus of the hose: | $E=2.1E+11N$ |
| --- | --- |
| Clamp compressibility coefficient: | $\nu =0.3$ |
| Volumetric mass of the pipe: | $\rho =7800\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ |
| Volumetric mass of the fluid: | ${\rho}_{f}=8.3\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ |
| Fluid celerity: | $c=495m/s$ |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Degrees of freedom $\mathrm{dy},\mathrm{dz},\mathrm{drx},\mathrm{dry},\mathrm{drz}$ are blocked for the entire hose.

On the acoustic section dx is also blocked and the only free degrees of freedom are $\mathrm{PRES}$ and $\mathrm{PHI}$.

At the end of the tank side: $\mathrm{PRES}=0.$ $\mathrm{PHI}=0.$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

None reference solution. The values tested for non-regression are those obtained with version 3.02.17.

### 2.2 Baseline

Spectral density of node pressure at the right end of the tube at $10,12,14,36,38,40\mathrm{Hz}$ frequencies. These frequencies are close to the two eigenfrequencies taken into account ($12.38$ and $37.36\mathrm{Hz}$).

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

![Figure](/figures/v2.02.105/10000A90000026C200000BAEC4485C0DA09BDA5A.svg)

Elements for pipes: FLUI_STRU

Element used for $\mathrm{AM10}$ mass: DIS_T

In all cases of calculation, the excitatory spectral density is a level 1 white noise.

The volume flow and pressure flow sources are applied to node $\mathrm{AM1}$ .

The mass and force sources are applied between nodes $\mathrm{AM1}$ and $\mathrm{AM2}$ .

The last calculation case corresponds to a force imposed on the node $\mathrm{AM10}$ in the $\mathrm{dx}$ direction.

The eigenfrequency modes in the $[0,100\mathrm{Hz}]$ interval were taken into account in the calculation, i.e. the first two modes.

The damping is introduced in modal form into the dynamic response operator random. For all calculation cases, it is taken equal to $\text{1\%}$

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Number of nodes: 17

Number of mesh cells and types : 16 SEG2, 1 POI1

### 3.3 Remarks

The fluid source spectral densities are expressed in their physical units. For a volume flow source in ${({m}^{3}/s)}^{2}/\mathrm{Hz}$.
