# v1.01.231 ZZZZ231 – Validation of contact in initial geometry

**Summary:**

This test is an analytical test. Tests are made on movements only. The objective is to validate for discreet formulations of contact:

- the correct consideration of the initial geometry contact (REAC_GEOM='SANS') when calculating the time (reuse=)
- the correct consideration of the initial geometry contact (REAC_GEOM='SANS') with initial state (ETAT_INIT)
- the calculation of the game in method 'RESOLUTION='NON''

Modelization A:

- 'CONTRAINTE' method reference calculation, 'RESOLUTION='NON'' method calculation, discrete and continuous formulation

Modelling B:

- Calculation in initial geometry with resumption

Modelization C:

- Calculation in initial geometry with initial state

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
