# v3.05.201 SSLX201 – Validation of connection2D_POU

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the consideration of the 2D_POU (AFFE_CHAR_MECA) connection with different 1-level connector surfaces and also for elements AXIS. The principle of this test is to compare the effect of a load applied on a discrete element connected to the 2D volume model by the restraint 2D_POU to the equivalent load applied directly to the 2D volume model. For the three modellings, the node unconnected of the element is applied in turn to the discrete FX, FY and MZ components of the FORCE_NODALE load.

- Modelization A: Modelization C_PLAN with a surface connection of 0.5.
- Modelling B: AXIS Modelling with a 0.5 surface connection located on a vertical edge.
- Modelling C: AXIS Modelling with a surface connection of 5 located on a horizontal edge.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
