# v7.03.111 HPLV111 – Verification of homogenized parameters of a breakthrough plate

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check the parameters calculated by the CALC_MATE_HOMO command on a plate with the cross shearing option (TYPE_HOMO = "PLAQUE_CT_MINDLIN"). The reference problems are the result of the internal EDF note.

Modellings A and D calculate the properties of a thick plate whose representative elemental volume octant (VER) contains a quarter of a hole, with a holed surface ratio on a full surface equal to 0.51 and a side ratio of VER on the plate thickness equal to 1.

Model B calculates the properties of a fine plate with a circular quarter hole and a square quarter hole in the VER octant, with a full surface hole ratio of 0.50 and a side ratio of VER to plate thickness of 4.

Model C calculates the properties of a thick plate with an entire hole in the VER octant, with a holed surface ratio on a full surface equal to 0.67 and a side ratio of VER on the plate thickness equal to 0.48.
