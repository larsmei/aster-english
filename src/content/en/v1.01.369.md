# v1.01.369 ZZZZ369 – Validation of MASS_MECA option for elements MEMBRANE and GRILLE_MEMBRANE

**Summary**

This dynamic test is part of the validation of elements MEMBRANE and GRILLE_MEMBRANE. A concrete plate (modelled in COQUE) contains two rebar sheets combined on the medium sheet of the plate. The plate is subjected to a one-time effort in its center.

In Modelling A, grids are modeled by elements GRILLE_EXCENTRE. This Modelling is used as a reference. In Modelling B, grids are modeled by elements MEMBRANE. In Modelling C, grids are modeled by elements GRILLE_MEMBRANE.

This test is designed to validate the MASS_MECA and MASS_MECA_DIAG options of the elements MEMBRANE and GRILLE_MEMBRANE.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The concrete console is modeled by elements hull (DKT). The reinforcement sheets (not excreted) are modeled by modellings GRILLE_ENCENTRE for Modelling A, MEMBRANE for Modelling B and GRILLE_MEMBRANE for Modelling C.

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.369/1000020100000219000000FF53BC7E03349463E1.png)

Figure 1-1: définition de la géométrie

### 1.2 Material properties

**Concrete console:** $E=3.{10}^{10}\mathit{Pa}$ , $\nu =0$ , $\rho =2500\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$

> Console thickness: $0.2m$; ANGL_REP = (0;0)

**Steel rebars:** $E=2.{10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$ , $\nu =0$ , $\rho =7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$

> High-rebarn tablecloth: section per linear meter = $0.2{m}^{2}/\mathrm{ml}$; ANGL_REP = (0;0)
> Low rebar tablet: section per linear metre = $0.2{m}^{2}/\mathrm{ml}$; ANGL_REP = (0;0)

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Boundary conditions and loads are broken down as follows:

> $\mathrm{B0X}$ and $\mathrm{B1X}$ clamped edges
> Nodal force $\mathit{FX}={10}^{7}N$ to node $\mathit{FLECHE}$ (node in the centre of the plate), applied with the following $f$ multiplicative function:

| Time (s) | $f$ |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 |
| 0,001 | 1 |
| 0.01 | 1 |
