# u4.81.07 Operator POST_ERREUR

## 1 Purpose

Compute the relative error between a solution obtained by a finite-element analysis and a reference analytical solution in terms of a given norm, with the aim of assessing the convergence of the finite elements.

The norms currently available are:

- the energy norm,
- the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the displacement,
- the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the contact pressure.

The concept returned is a table.

## 2 Syntax

```text
table = POST_ERREUR(
    ◆ OPTION = / "DEPL_RELA",
               / "ENER_RELA",
               / "LAGR_RELA",
    ◆ MODELE = modele,
    ◆ GROUP_MA = grma,
    # Si: equal_to("OPTION", 'DEPL_RELA')
        ◇ CHAM_MATER = cham_mater,
        ◆ CHAM_GD = cham_no,
        ◇ DX = formule,
        ◇ DY = formule,
        ◇ DZ = formule,
    # Si: equal_to("OPTION", 'ENER_RELA')
        ◆ CHAM_MATER = cham_mater,
        ◆ DEFORMATION = "PETIT" (ou non renseigné),
        ◆ CHAM_GD = cham_elem,
        ◇ SIXX = formule,
        ◇ SIYY = formule,
        ◇ SIZZ = formule,
        ◇ SIXY = formule,
        ◇ SIXZ = formule,
        ◇ SIYZ = formule,
    # Si: equal_to("OPTION", 'LAGR_RELA')
        ◆ CHAM_GD = cham_no,
        ◇ LAGS_C = formule,
)

◆ : obligatoire
◇ : optionnel
⟐ : présent par défaut
& : ensemble
/ : un seul parmi
| : plusieurs choix possibles
```

## 3 Operands

### 3.1 General operands

#### 3.1.1 Keyword OPTION

> This keyword makes it possible to determine the type of norm to be used:

- 'ENER_RELA' for the energy norm (FEM and X-FEM).
- 'DEPL_RELA' for the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the displacement (FEM and X-FEM).
- 'LAGR_RELA' for the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the contact pressure (FEM only).

This keyword also determines the type of field corresponding to the keyword CHAM_GD:

- the stress field at the Gauss points in the case OPTION='ENER_RELA'.
- The displacement field at the nodes in the cases OPTION='DEPL_RELA' and OPTION='LAGR_RELA'.

#### 3.1.2 Operand MODELE

Name of the model on which the option is computed. It must be the same model as the one which was used to carry out the mechanical analysis from which the field given by the keyword CHAM_GD comes.

#### 3.1.3 Operand GROUP_MA

The operand GROUP_MA makes it possible to specify the mesh-cell groups for which the computations of energy or of ${L}^{2}$ norms will be carried out.

### 3.2 Operands for OPTION = 'ENER_RELA'

The option 'ENER_RELA' makes it possible to estimate the difference between the stress field obtained by the finite-element analysis ${\sigma}_{h}$ and the reference stress field $\sigma$ .

For each mesh-cell group of the list given by the operand GROUP_MA, the macro-command POST_ERREUR computes:

- The elastic energy of the difference field ${\sigma}_{h}-\sigma$

$\frac{1}{2}{\int}_{{\Omega}_{i}}({\sigma}_{h}-\sigma ):{D}^{-1}:({\sigma}_{h}-\sigma )\mathit{dV}$ ,

where ${\Omega}_{i}$ is the domain obtained by concatenating all the mesh cells of the mesh-cell group under consideration and $D$ is the Hooke tensor.

- The elastic energy of the reference stress field $\sigma$

$\frac{1}{2}{\int}_{{\Omega}_{i}}\sigma :{D}^{-1}:\sigma \mathit{dV}$ .

Finally, the relative error in terms of the energy norm is obtained by:

$e=\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i}\frac{1}{2}{\int}_{{\Omega}_{i}}({\sigma}_{h}-\sigma ):{D}^{-1}:({\sigma}_{h}-\sigma )\mathit{dV}}{\sum_{i}\frac{1}{2}{\int}_{{\Omega}_{i}}\sigma :{D}^{-1}:\sigma \mathit{dV}}}$ ,

where the sum is taken over all the mesh-cell groups.

#### 3.2.1 Operand CHAM_GD

The stress field ${\sigma}_{h}$ extracted from a finite-element analysis result.

#### 3.2.2 Operand CHAM_MATER

Name of the material field to be used for the energy computations. It is recommended that it be the same model as the one which was used to carry out the mechanical analysis from which the field given by the keyword CHAM_GD comes.

#### 3.2.3 Operand DEFORMATION

This keyword makes it possible to define the assumptions used for the computation of the strains *(cf.*  [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11), §4.5). The only permitted value is 'PETIT', which corresponds to small displacements and small strains.

#### 3.2.4 Operands SIXX, SIYY, SIZZ, SIXY, SIXZ and SIYZ

These keywords make it possible to define the components of the reference stress field $\sigma$ in the form of formula objects. These operands are optional because all the components which are not specified are set to zero.

The value of each keyword is a list of formulae to be matched with the list of mesh-cell groups specified by the operand GROUP_MA.

A frequent source of error is not to fill in SIZZ for a plane problem, forgetting that, in the general case ( *i.e.* non-zero Poisson's ratio), the component ${\sigma}_{zz}$ of the stress tensor $\sigma$ is zero only in the case of plane stresses (C_PLAN).

#### 3.2.5 Example

Computation of the error in terms of the energy norm for a crack opening in pure mode I, for a plane problem. It is to be noted that the component ${\sigma}_{zz}$ is zero for this problem and is therefore not specified.

```text
# extraction du champ de contraintes de la structure de données résultat

Scal=CREA_CHAMP(OPERATION='EXTR',

TYPE_CHAM='ELGA_SIEF_R',

RESULTAT=UTOT,

NOM_CHAM='SIEF_ELGA',

NUME_ORDRE=1)

# calcul de l'erreur en termes de la norme en énergie

tabNRJ=POST_ERREUR(OPTION='ENER_RELA',

CHAM_GD=Scal,

MODELE=MODELK,

DEFORMATION='PETIT',

CHAM_MATER=CHMA,

GROUP_MA='SURF',

SIXX=SXX,

SIYY=SYY,

SIXY=SXY,

)

)
```

#### 3.2.6 Table produced

> The table produced contains, for each mesh cell of the mesh-cell group, the energy of the difference field ${\sigma}_{h}-\sigma$ and the energy of the reference field $\sigma$ . It also contains the sum over all the mesh-cell groups of the energy of the difference field ${\sigma}_{h}-\sigma$ , the sum over all the mesh-cell groups of the energy of the reference field $\sigma$ and the relative error in terms of the energy norm.

| **GROUP_MA** | **DIFFERENCE** | **REFERENCE** | **ERREUR RELATIVE** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SURF | 1.53608E-09 | 3.50518E-06 |  |
| TOTAL | 1.53608E-09 | 3.50518E-06 | 2.09340E-02 |

### 3.3 Operands for OPTION = 'DEPL_RELA'

The option 'DEPL_RELA' makes it possible to estimate the difference between the displacement field obtained by the finite-element analysis ${u}_{h}$ and the reference displacement field $u$ .

For each mesh-cell group of the list given by the operand GROUP_MA, the macro-command POST_ERREUR computes:

- The ${L}^{2}$ norm of the difference field ${u}_{h}-u$

$\sqrt{{\int}_{{\Omega}_{i}}{\parallel {u}_{h}-u\parallel }^{2}\mathit{dV}}$ ,

where ${\Omega}_{i}$ is the domain obtained by concatenating all the mesh cells of the mesh-cell group under consideration.

- The ${L}^{2}$ norm of the reference displacement field $u$

$\sqrt{{\int}_{{\Omega}_{i}}{\parallel u\parallel }^{2}\mathit{dV}}$ .

Finally, the relative error in terms of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the displacement is obtained by:

$e=\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i}{\int}_{{\Omega}_{i}}{\parallel {u}_{h}-u\parallel }^{2}\mathit{dV}}{\sum_{i}{\int}_{{\Omega}_{i}}{\parallel u\parallel }^{2}\mathit{dV}}}$ ,

where the sum is taken over all the mesh-cell groups.

#### 3.3.1 Operand CHAM_GD

The displacement field ${u}_{h}$ extracted from a finite-element analysis result.

#### 3.3.2 Operands DX, DY and DZ

These keywords make it possible to define the components of the reference displacement field $u$ in the form of formula objects. These operands are optional because all the components which are not specified are set to zero.

The value of each keyword is a list of formulae to be matched with the list of mesh-cell groups specified by the operand GROUP_MA.

#### 3.3.3 Example

Computation of the error in terms of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the displacement for a crack opening in pure mode I, for a plane problem.

```text
# extraction du champ de déplacements de la structure de données résultat

Ucal=CREA_CHAMP(OPERATION='EXTR',

TYPE_CHAM='NOEU_DEPL_R',

RESULTAT=UTOT,

NOM_CHAM='DEPL',

NUME_ORDRE=1)

# calcul de l'erreur en termes de la norme L2 du déplacement

tabL2=POST_ERREUR(OPTION='DEPL_RELA',

CHAM_GD=Ucal,

MODELE=MODELK,

GROUP_MA='SURF',

DX=U1,

DY=U2)
```

#### 3.3.4 Table produced

> The table produced contains, for each mesh cell of the mesh-cell group, the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the difference field ${u}_{h}-u$ and the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the reference field $u$ . It also contains the sum over all the mesh-cell groups of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the difference field ${u}_{h}-u$ , the sum over all the mesh-cell groups of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the reference field $u$ and the relative error in terms of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the displacement.

| **GROUP_MA** | **DIFFERENCE** | **REFERENCE** | **ERREUR RELATIVE** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SURF | 1.14688E-09 | 7.60569E-06 |  |
| TOTAL | 1.14688E-09 | 7.60569E-06 | 1.50793E-04 |

### 3.4 Operands for OPTION = 'LAGR_RELA'

The option 'DEPL_RELA' makes it possible to estimate the difference between the contact pressure obtained by the finite-element analysis ${\lambda}_{h}$ and the reference pressure field $\lambda$ .

For each mesh-cell group of the list given by the operand GROUP_MA, the macro-command POST_ERREUR computes:

- The ${L}^{2}$ norm of the difference field ${\lambda}_{h}-\lambda$

$\sqrt{{\int}_{{\Gamma}_{i}}{({\lambda}_{h}-\lambda )}^{2}\mathit{dS}}$ ,

where ${\Gamma}_{i}$ is the domain obtained by concatenating all the mesh cells of the mesh-cell group under consideration.

- The ${L}^{2}$ norm of the reference displacement field $\lambda$

$\sqrt{{\int}_{{\Gamma}_{i}}{\lambda}^{2}\mathit{dS}}$ .

Finally, the relative error in terms of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the contact pressure is obtained by:

$e=\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i}{\int}_{{\Gamma}_{i}}{({\lambda}_{h}-\lambda )}^{2}\mathit{dS}}{\sum_{i}{\int}_{{\Gamma}_{i}}{\lambda}^{2}\mathit{dS}}}$ ,

where the sum is taken over all the mesh-cell groups.

#### 3.4.1 Operand CHAM_GD

The contact-pressure field ${\lambda}_{h}$ extracted from a finite-element analysis result.

#### 3.4.2 Operand LAGS_C

This keyword makes it possible to define the reference contact pressure $\lambda$ in the form of a formula object.

The value of the keyword is a list of formulae to be matched with the list of mesh-cell groups specified by the operand GROUP_MA.

#### 3.4.3 Example

Computation of the error in terms of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the pressure for the case of the inclusion of two crowns.

```text
# définition de la pression de contact analytique

PRES=FORMULE(NOM_PARA=('X','Y'),VALE='-pres_cont*EXP(0.1)')

# extraction du champ de déplacements de la structure de données résultat

Ucal=CREA_CHAMP(OPERATION='EXTR',

TYPE_CHAM='NOEU_DEPL_R',

RESULTAT=RESU1,

NOM_CHAM='DEPL',

NUME_ORDRE=1)

# calcul de l'erreur en termes de la norme L2 de la pression de contact
```

tabL2=POST_ERREUR(OPTION='LAGR_RELA',

CHAM_GD=Ucal,

MODELE=MO,

GROUP_MA='S2R2',

LAGS_C=PRES)

#### 3.4.4 Table produced

> The table produced contains, for each mesh cell of the mesh-cell group, the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the difference field ${\lambda}_{h}-\lambda$ and the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the reference field $\lambda$ . It also contains the sum over all the mesh-cell groups of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the difference field ${\lambda}_{h}-\lambda$ , the sum over all the mesh-cell groups of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the reference field $\lambda$ and the relative error in terms of the ${L}^{2}$ norm of the contact pressure.
