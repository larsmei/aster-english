# v1.01.119 ZZZZ119 - PROJ_CHAMP for 3D surfaces

**Summary:**

The case of a quarter of a cylinder (modeled by DKQ hulls) subjected to internal pressure on a first mesh $\mathit{ma1}$ is treated. The displacement field obtained $(\mathit{ch1})$ is assumed to be correct. This displacement field is projected on another mesh $(\mathit{ma2})$ of the same quarter of a cylinder. $\mathit{ch2}$ is obtained. $\mathit{ch2}$ is re-projected on $\mathit{ma1}$ , $\mathit{ch1bis}$ is obtained. The result $(\mathit{ch1bis})$ is close to $\mathit{ch1}$ (2% difference). This validates the projection method of one field of one surface over the other.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A cylindrical shell (1/4 cylinder) of $h=40$ height and $R=10$ radius and $e=0.2$ thickness is clamped on its middle line and subjected to internal pressure $p=2$.

She's mesh twice:

| $\mathit{ma1}$ : $7\times 14$ | QUAD4 |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathit{ma2}$ : $8\times 16\times 2$ | TRIA6 |

| --- | --- |
| $\mathit{ma1}$ | $\mathit{ma2}$ |

### 1.2 Material properties

$E=1.{10}^{6}$

$\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions, loading

The node group $\mathit{AB1}$ is clamped (three moves and three rotations are blocked).

$$
\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=\mathit{DZ}=0.
$$

The hull is subjected to internal pressure $p=2$ .

### 1.4 Validated Features

Validation is for the 'ELEM' method in the case of mesh cells surfactants dived into $\mathit{R3}$ space. The VIS_A_VIS keyword for the 'ELEM' method is also tested.

## 2 reference solution

For the $\mathit{ch1}$ field , calculated on the mesh $\mathit{ma1}$ , the reference solution is obtained with *Code_Aster* (version 5.02.07).

For the $\mathit{ch1bis}$ field obtained by a "return-round" projection ($\mathit{ma1}\to \mathit{ma2}\to \mathit{ma1}$), the reference solution is the $\mathit{ch1}$ field.

This is how the error due to the 2 successive field projections is measured: $\mathit{ch1}\to \mathit{ch2}\to \mathit{ch1bis}$
