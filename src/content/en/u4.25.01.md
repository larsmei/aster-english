# u4.25.01 Operator DEFI_FLUI_STRU

## 1 Purpose

Define the characteristics necessary for the dynamic study of a structure under flow.

By the choice of one of the factor keywords, the user specifies the type of "structure-flow" configuration studied: tube bundle under cross-flow or axial flow, control-rod cluster, coaxial shells. Depending on the type of configuration, the data supplied make it possible to carry out downstream:

- a fluid/structure coupling study: estimation of the fluid-elastic force coefficients and computation of the new modal parameters of the structure by means of the operator CALC_FLUI_STRU [U4.66.02](doc:u4.66.02),
- the projection onto the modal basis under flow of one or more turbulent excitations defined by their spectral density (densities): operators DEFI_SPEC_TURB [U4.44.31](doc:u4.44.31) and PROJ_SPEC_BASE [U4.63.14](doc:u4.63.14). One may then compute the response of the structure to the turbulent excitation, in time and in frequency.

The operator "DEFI_FLUI_STRU" produces a concept of type type_flui_stru intended to be used downstream by the operators FONC_FLUI_STRU [U4.35.02](doc:u4.35.02), CALC_FLUI_STRU [U4.66.02](doc:u4.66.02) or, indirectly, PROJ_SPEC_BASE [U4.63.14](doc:u4.63.14).

## 3 Operands

### 3.1 Keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS

```text
♦    /    FAISCEAU_TRANS
```
Factor keyword making it possible to characterise a configuration of the type "tube bundle under cross-flow". If the study is based on the definition of several excitation zones, there must be as many occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS as there are zones.

```text
◊    COUPLAGE = 'OUI' ou 'NON'
```
Text-type indicator specifying whether or not the fluid-elastic forces are taken into account. This operand must appear in at least one of the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS, and may be omitted in the others.

```text
◊    CARA_ELEM = cara
```
Concept of type [cara_elem] making it possible to supply all the data relating to the geometry of the elements of the structure: useful for the estimation of the hydraulic diameter. This operand must appear in at least one of the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS, and may be omitted in the others.

```text
♦    PROF_VITE_FLUI = profv
```
Concept of type [fonction] making it possible to supply the non-dimensional profile of transverse velocity along the tube. The parameter of the function is the curvilinear abscissa. This operand must appear in all the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS.

```text
◊     PROF_RHO_F_INT = profrhoi
```
Concept of type [fonction] making it possible to supply the density profile of the fluid internal to the tube, along the tube. The parameter of the function is the curvilinear abscissa. This operand must appear in at least one of the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS, and may be omitted in the others.

```text
◊     PROF_RHO_F_EXT = profrhoe
```
Concept of type [fonction] making it possible to supply the density profile of the fluid external to the tube, along the tube. The parameter of the function is the curvilinear abscissa. This operand must appear in at least one of the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS, and may be omitted in the others.

```text
◊    NOM_CMP = 'DX' ou 'DY' ou 'DZ'
```
Text-type indicator [TXM] specifying the direction along which the fluid-elastic forces and/or the turbulent forces act. This operand must appear in at least one of the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS, and may be omitted in the others.

◊    COEF_MASS_AJOU = cm

Value of the added-mass coefficient cm. This operand, if it is used, may be defined in only one of the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS, and may be omitted in the others.

```text
◊    CSTE_CONNORS            =            cste
```
List of two reals defining the interval of the Connors constants for the method of the same name (see R4.07.04).

```text
◊    RHO_TUBE                =            rho
```
Density of the tube for the Connors method

```text
◊    NB_CONNORS            =            nb ,        [I]
```
Number of discretisation points inside the interval defined by the keyword CSTE_CONNORS presented above.

```text
◊    /    ◊    TYPE_PAS = 'CARRE_LIGN' ou 'TRIA_LIGN'
```
Text-type indicator [TXM] making it possible to specify the type of pitch of the bundle, defined by the arrangement of the tubes with respect to one another and by the direction of the flow with respect to the bundle. This operand may appear in only one of the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS, and may be omitted in the others.

```text
'CARRE_LIGN'    =    pas carré ligne.

'TRIA_LIGN'    =    pas triangulaire ligne.
```

```text
♦    TYPE_RESEAU = tr
```
Integer indicator, strictly less than 1000 and positive, defining the experimental configuration for which the coupling coefficients used for the study were obtained [bib1]. This operand must appear in all the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS.

**Note:**

*"tr" must appear in two files «cd.70» and «ck.71» which make it possible to describe the evolution of the damping and of the stiffness added by the flow as a function of the reduced velocity* ${V}_{r}$ *, the latter being computed from the inter-tube velocity.*

*The files «cd.70» and «ck.71» are read on logical units 70 and 71. They both have the following structure:*

**Line 1 of the file**: *number of correlations present in the whole file (integer)*

**** then, for each of these correlations, a block composed of ****

**Line 1 of the block**: *type of pitch of the array associated with the correlation (1 if* TYPE_PAS=«CARRE_LIGN» *, 2 if* TYPE_PAS=«TRIA_LIGN»)

**Line 2 of the block**: *tr (integer supplied for the operand* «TYPE_RESEAU»)

**Line 3 of the block**: *nbplages (number of contiguous ranges of reduced velocity over which the added damping and stiffness have been interpolated by the user in polynomial form)*

**Line 4 of the block**: *nbplages reals, followed by two reals A and B; the first nbplages reals correspond to the reduced-velocity values — ordered by increasing order — of the lower bounds of the contiguous nbplages; the two reals* $A$ *and* $B$ *are respectively the smallest and the largest values of reduced velocity for which the added damping and stiffness have been determined experimentally on a test rig in thermohydraulic similarity; they therefore bound the range of reduced velocities inside which the correlation may be interpolated from the values* *identified on the test rig. In general, one therefore chooses the first of the nbplages reals equal to* $A$ *and the last of the nbplages reals equal to* $B$ .

**Following Nbplages lines**: *each line corresponds to the polynomial interpolation of the damping correlation or of the added stiffness in the range considered, the first range of reduced velocities extending between the first and the second of the nbplages reals. On each line, 11 coefficients are to be supplied. These coefficients are the* ${({\alpha}_{i})}_{1\le i\le 11}$ *defining the polynomial used for the interpolation in the range considered. Thus, for example,* ${C}_{d}$ *denoting the damping added by the flow. The expression which will be taken into account as a function of the reduced velocity* *is the following* ${V}_{r}$ *(the expression for the added stiffness is analogous):*

${C}_{d}({V}_{r})=\sum_{i=1}^{11}{\alpha}_{i}{V}_{r}^{(i-4)}$

*Following line: a line making it possible to delimit the blocks associated with each correlation, in general of the form:* « ********** »

**** end of the block****

*If there are other correlations, Line 1 of the block corresponding to the next correlation.*

*On the basis of a certain number of tests, EDF has developed and validated a set of fluid-elastic correlations making it possible to simulate the damping and the stiffness added to a structure by a flow. The supply of these correlations in the form of two files of a* *format consistent with that specified above will be studied case by case according to the request.*

```text
◊    UNITE_CA        =        iu1
```
Number of the logical unit in which the file «cd.70» of the dampings added by the flow is written.

```text
◊    UNITE_KA        =        iu2
```
Number of the logical unit in which the file «cd.71» of the stiffnesses added by the flow is written.

```text
◊    PAS = pas
```
Value of the reduced pitch of the bundle: ratio between, on the one hand, the centre-to-centre distance between 2 neighbouring tubes, and on the other hand the outside diameter of the tubes. This operand may appear in only one of the occurrences of the factor keyword FAISCEAU_TRANS, and may be omitted in the others.

### 3.2 Keyword GRAPPE

```text
♦    /    GRAPPE
```
Factor keyword making it possible to characterise a configuration of the type "control-rod cluster".

```text

Text-type indicator [TXM] specifying the taking into account of the fluid-elastic forces.

```
Four possible choices corresponding to the various experimental configurations for which the fluid-elastic force coefficients have been identified:

- AScending flow, control rod CENtred;
- AScending flow, control rod off-centre (EXCentred);
- DEScending flow, control rod CENtred;
- DEScending flow, control rod off-centre (EXCentred).

```text
♦    GROUP_NO = grno
```
Identifier of the node where the resultant force and the resultant moment representing the action of the fluid-elastic forces are applied.

```text
♦    CARA_ELEM = cara
```
Concept of type [cara_elem] supplying all the data relating to the geometry of the elements of the structure: useful for the estimation of the diameter of the control rod. This concept provides, among other things, the information relating to the orientations of the elements.

```text
♦    MODELE = modele
```
Concept of type [modele] supplying the information relating to the types of the elements of the structure.

```text
◊    COEF_MASS_AJOU = cm1
```
Value of the added-mass coefficient due to the local confinement of the control rod at the housing plate. If the modal characteristics in water at rest of the structure have been computed with the equivalent density.

${\rho}_{\mathrm{eq}}=\alpha \frac{\pi {D}^{2}}{\mathrm{4S}}{\rho}_{\mathrm{eau}}+{\rho}_{\mathrm{poutre}}$

The coefficient cm1 of added mass due to the local confinement at the housing plate is given by the relation:

$\text{cm1}=\frac{\pi (-\alpha )}{2}$

where $D$ denotes the outside diameter of the rod; $S$ is the area of the straight section of the tube and $H$ represents the thickness of the fluid film at the confinement.

**Note:**

*When the user does not supply the operand* *COEF_MASS_AJOU, cm1* *is estimated automatically by means of this expression with* $\alpha =1$ *.*

```text
♦     RHO_FLUI = rho_f
```
Value of the density of the fluid surrounding the structure.

### 3.3 Keyword FAISCEAU_AXIAL

```text
♦    /    FAISCEAU_AXIAL
```
Factor keyword making it possible to characterise a configuration of the type "tube bundle under axial flow" [bib2].

**Note:**

*In the case where the study is carried out by means of a representation of the complete bundle, only one occurrence is allowed for this factor keyword.*

*If the study is based on a simplified representation, there must be as many occurrences as there are tubes in the simplified bundle. Each tube of the simplified bundle defines an equivalence class for the tubes of the real bundle. The characteristics of the tubes of the real bundle for one and the same equivalence class (common radius, positions) are the subject of one occurrence of the factor keyword.*

*In order to be able to use a simplified representation of the bundle, the modal basis computed in air must be equivalent to the complete modal basis in air of the real bundle; each tube of the simplified bundle must therefore be a tube equivalent to each class of real tubes. For example, for a class of* $N$ *real tubes, of Young's modulus* $E$ *and of density* $\rho$ *, a possible equivalent tube is characterised by a Young's modulus* $\mathit{NE}$ *and a density* $N\rho$ *.*

## 4 Further information concerning configurations of the type "tube bundles under cross-flow"

The four operands TYPE_PAS, TYPE_RESEAU, PAS and COEF_MASS_AJOU are optional. No value is taken by default. When these characteristics are defined, three possibilities of use are offered:

- **TYPE_PAS** , **TYPE_RESEAU**  and **PAS**  are present. **COUPLAGE = 'OUI'**

The fluid-elastic forces will be taken into account during a dynamic analysis. The added-mass coefficient is computed as a function of the pitch and of the type of pitch.

- **TYPE_PAS** , **TYPE_RESEAU** , **PAS**  and **COEF_MASS_AJOU**  are present. **COUPLAGE = 'OUI'**

The fluid-elastic forces will be taken into account during a dynamic analysis. The added-mass coefficient is defined by the user.

- **COEF_MASS_AJOU**  is present. **COUPLAGE = 'NON'**

Only the added mass due to the fluid is taken into account (no coupling).
