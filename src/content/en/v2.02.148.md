## 2 Test case objective, validation

The objective of the test case is to validate the SPEC_CORR_CONV_3 functionality of the DEFI_SPEC_TURB operator, which allows you to define a spectrum using analytical functions of the space and frequency variable.

In model A, the defined spectrum is purely theoretical, since the functions are sinus. The calculation of the ${\int}_{\Omega}{\int}_{\Omega}{\Phi}_{i}(\underline{{x}_{1}}).{S}_{f}(\underline{{x}_{1}},\underline{{x}_{2}},\omega ).{\Phi}_{j}(\underline{{x}_{2}})d{\Omega}_{1}d{\Omega}_{2}$ integral double can therefore be solved analytically, assuming that the first 2 modes of the beam are also sinus functions. In model B, the defined spectrum is representative of a flow downstream of a mixing grid on a fuel pencil (Corcos model).

### 2.1 Test case operation

- Calculation of eigenmodes: the first two modes are calculated, they are of the form $\phi (y)=\sin(\pi y)$ and $\phi (y)=\sin(2\pi y)$,
- definition of turbulent spectrum functions:

  - Modelling A ${S}_{xx}=f.\sin(\pi {y}_{1}).\sin(\pi {y}_{2})$
  - Modelling B

${S}_{f}(\underline{{x}_{1}},\underline{{x}_{2}},\omega )=\lbrace  \begin{array}{c}{S}_{xx}=\exp(-\frac{\mid {y}_{2}-{y}_{1}\mid }{{\lambda}_{\text{cx}}(\omega )}).\exp(\mathit{j\omega }\frac{{y}_{2}-{y}_{1}}{{U}_{c}}){S}_{f}({y}_{1},{y}_{1},\omega )\\ {S}_{yy}=\exp(-\frac{\mid {y}_{2}-{y}_{1}\mid }{{\lambda}_{\text{cy}}(\omega )}).\exp(\mathit{j\omega }\frac{{y}_{2}-{y}_{1}}{{U}_{c}}){S}_{f}({y}_{1},{y}_{1},\omega )\end{array}$

(by adding terms of correlation between $x$ and $y$ forces with ${S}_{xy}$ and ${S}_{yx}$ functions).

- creating a table containing the functions associated with the directions,
- creating the interspectrum with DEFI_SPEC_TURB,
- interspectrum projection on both modes calculated with PROJ_SPEC_BASE; projection is made on mesh-cell group 'BAS' only.

**Note:**

*For Modelling B, an excitation is defined in both directions. However, the two calculated modes are in the direction* $X$ *(the direction* $\mathit{DZ}$ *is blocked). So the excitation according to* $Y$ *will not influence the result of the modal excitation.*

### 2.2 Validation of results

#### 2.2.1 Modelling A

For model A, validation is analytical, because the integrals to be calculated are:

- Autospectrum mode 1: ${\int}_{0}^{0.5}{\int}_{0}^{0.5}{\sin}^{2}(\pi {y}_{1}){\sin}^{2}(\pi {y}_{2}){\mathit{dy}}_{1}{\mathit{dy}}_{2}=\frac{1}{4}.\frac{1}{4}=0.0625$
- interspectre mode 1 – mode 2: ${\int}_{0}^{0.5}{\int}_{0}^{0.5}-{\sin}^{2}(\pi {y}_{1})\sin(\pi {y}_{2})\sin(2\pi {y}_{2}){\mathit{dy}}_{1}{\mathit{dy}}_{2}=-\frac{2}{3\pi }.\frac{1}{4}=-0.05305$
- Autospectrum mode 2: ${\int}_{0}^{0.5}{\int}_{0}^{0.5}\sin(\pi {y}_{1})\sin(2\pi {y}_{1})\sin(\pi {y}_{2})\sin(2\pi {y}_{2}){\mathit{dy}}_{1}{\mathit{dy}}_{2}=-\frac{2}{3\pi }.-\frac{2}{3\pi }=0.04503$

#### 2.2.2 Modelling B

For Modelling B, validation is done by non-regression.

- Autospectrum mode 1: $0.11$
- interspectre mode 1 – mode 2: $0.11+\mathrm{0.01534j}$
- Autospectrum mode 2: $0.11$ .
