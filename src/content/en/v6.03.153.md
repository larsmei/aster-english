# v6.03.153 SSNP153 - frictional contact deformable-deformable 2D in large deformations (shallow ironing)

**Summary:**

This test modeled the indentation of a massive by a punch. Both bodies are elastic, deformable and obey a kinematic of large deformations. This test checks the consideration of friction in 2D.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry described in the figure in which the dimensions in mm are:

${d}_{1}=0.2{d}_{2}=1.2{d}_{3}=10.6r=0.75{h}_{1}=0.95{h}_{2}=4{a}_{1}=0.3{a}_{2}=0.2$

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.153/10000000000004EB000002965C3F4B7AD475D58D.png)

Illustration 1: Geometry of the problem (image from [1])

### 1.2 Material properties

Both materials are elastic isotropic with properties:

- $E=68.96E8\mathrm{MPa}$         $\nu =0.32$
- $E\text{*}=6.896E8\mathrm{MPa}$         $\nu \text{*}=0.32$

Both are represented in large deformations according to the model of Simo and Miehe.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The bottom edge of the solid is clamped.

The upper edge of the punch is subject to a displacement imposed by:

$\begin{array}{ccc}t\in [0,1]& {u}_{x}=0& {u}_{y}=t\\ t\in [1,2]& {u}_{x}=10(t-1)& {u}_{y}=1\end{array}$

For contact, the punch is master and the solid slave. We consider a friction of Coulomb with a coefficient $\mu =0.3$ .

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None
