# v7.20.106 HSNA106 – Model META_LEMA_ANI: full single-trail cylinder with variable temperature

**Summary:**

This quasi-static thermomechanical test consists of uniformly heating a cylindrical bar ($\mathrm{2D}$ axisymmetric) and then tensile.

The bar is represented by a element axisymmetric quadrangular QUAD4. It is modelled in two different but equivalent ways: either with the META_LEMA_ANI model that is "degenerated" into a Norton law by judiciously choosing coefficients (modelization A), or with a Norton law itself (modelization B).

The META_LEMA_ANI model has two different thermal expansion coefficients for the cold a phase and the hot b phase. In the most general case, the expansion is calculated by a law of the mixtures. Here, one takes place in the case of 100% phase b and the calculation of the thermal expansion is tested.

The thermomechanical load is done on the required displacement and by imposing a rising temperature. The same response must then be obtained for both modellings A and B.

The material obeys a viscoplastic constitutive law of Norton (special case of Lemaître law, where the UN_SUR_M parameter is null, confir [R5.03.08](doc:r5.03.08)), with the parameters:

- $n=4.39$
- $K=253.5497\mathit{MPa}.{s}^{-1}$
- $\alpha =0.00004$

## 2 reference solution

The validation of the META_LEMA_ANI law is done by comparing the two modellings A and B.

Each of the two modellings is therefore one reference solution for the other.

## 5 Summary of the results

The results found with these two modellings are very close, with the relative error being less than $\text{0.02\%}$. For deformations, the differences are very small between the reference model B and model A. This allows the calculation of thermal deformations to be validated with the metallurgical model.
