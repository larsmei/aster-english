# r7.01.41 Constitutive law of porous media: GonfElas

**Summary:**

The GonfElas model is a model initially proposed by D. Hoxan and F. Colin [ 1 ] to describe the swelling behaviour of certain types of clay. It is a non-linear elastic model depending on suction (this model must therefore be used in a THHM or HHM environment only). This model is inspired by the Barcelona model BBM (cf. R7.01.17) and corresponds to its elastic part. It is particularly suited to the study of the behaviour of compacted clay plugs (bentonite). This model is developed within the MFront framework.

## 1 Presentation of the model and notation

This model is a constitutive law in rock mechanics making it possible to describe the behaviour of materials of the “swelling clay” type (bentonite). It is a non-linear elastic model relating the net stress to the swelling pressure, which itself depends on the suction (or capillary pressure). It can be used only within the framework of the THHM and HHM behaviours.

This model rests on the following relation:

$$
d\stackrel{~}{\sigma}={K}_{s}(\mathit{Pc})d{\epsilon}_{V}+b\left(1+\frac{\mathit{Pc}}{A}\right){e}^{-{\beta}_{m}{\left(\frac{s}{A}\right)}^{2}}\mathit{dPc}
$$

with $\tilde{\sigma}$ : net stress or effective stress. Here the net stress is chosen (cf. section 2.1).

In the version of the model available here, neither the non-linearity of ${K}_{s}(\mathit{Pc})$ nor its dependence with respect to suction is taken into account. In the end, one simply has ${K}_{s}(\mathit{Pc})={K}_{0}$ .

With the following notation:

${K}_{0}$ is the bulk modulus of the material

$b$ is the Biot coefficient

$A$ is a parameter with the dimension of a pressure

${\beta}_{m}$ is a dimensionless parameter

$\mathit{Pc}$ is the capillary pressure

The notion of swelling pressure ${P}_{\mathit{gf}}$ , which will be used later, is also defined here.

## 2 Implementation of the model

### 2.1 Effective stresses and net stresses

It is recalled that the THM formulation rests on the distinction between the effective stress $\sigma'$ and the total stress $\sigma$ , such that:

$$
d\sigma =d\sigma '+d\pi
$$

with $\pi$ the hydraulic stress such that $d\pi =-b(\mathit{dPg}-S\mathit{dPc})$

where $S$ is the liquid saturation and $Pg$ the gas pressure.

In the case of a net-stress formulation, one has:

$$
d\sigma =d\stackrel{~}{\sigma}+d\pi
$$

with $\pi$ the hydraulic stress, which is then defined such that $d\pi =-b(\mathit{dPg})$ .

It is this formulation that is retained for the law GonfElas, which differs from the other constitutive laws available in unsaturated THM. This allows this law to have notation consistent with that of the Barcelona law.

### 2.2 Programming of the law

The law is programmed incrementally on the mean stress (applied to the net stresses), which gives:

$$
\Delta \stackrel{~}{{\sigma}_{m}}={K}_{0}\Delta {\epsilon}_{V}+b\Delta \mathit{PG}
$$

With:

$$
\stackrel{~}{{\sigma}_{m}}=\frac{1}{3}\mathit{Tr}(\stackrel{~}{\sigma})
$$

and by introducing the swelling-pressure function in the saturated and unsaturated cases:

$$
\mathit{PG}(\mathit{Pc})=\lbrace \begin{array}{cc}A\left(\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2\sqrt{{\beta}_{m}}}\right)\mathit{Erf}\left(\frac{\mathit{Pc}}{A}\sqrt{{\beta}_{m}}\right)+\frac{1}{2{\beta}_{m}}\left(\begin{array}{c}1-{e}^{-{\beta}_{m}{\left(\frac{\mathit{Pc}}{A}\right)}^{2}}\\ \phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\end{array}\right)& \text{si}S<1\\ \mathit{Pc}\text{si}S=1\hfill & \phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\end{array}
$$

with $\mathit{Erf}(x)=\underset{0}{\overset{x}{\int}}{e}^{-{\mathrm{\chi }}^{2}}d\mathrm{\chi }$ .

### 2.3 Material data and identification

The material parameters specific to the law and to be entered in DEFI_MATERIAU are:

- BETAM: dimensionless material parameter corresponding to the ${\beta}_{m}$ of the law above.
- PREF: parameter with the dimension of a pressure corresponding to the $A$ of the law above.

The identification of ${\beta}_{m}$ is carried out by seeking the swelling pressure. Let ${P}_{\mathit{gf}}({\mathit{Pc}}_{0})$ be the swelling pressure found by the model when a sample is re-saturated in a blocked-strain test, starting from a suction ${\mathit{Pc}}_{0}$ . It is recalled that at saturation, $\mathit{Pc}=0$ , which implies that:

$$
{P}_{\mathit{gf}}({\mathit{Pc}}_{0})=\underset{{\mathit{Pc}}_{0}}{\overset{0}{\int}}b\left(1+\frac{\mathit{Pc}}{A}\right){e}^{-{\beta}_{m}{\left(\frac{s}{A}\right)}^{2}}\mathit{dPc}
$$

After integration one obtains:

$$
\frac{{P}_{\mathit{gf}}({\mathit{Pc}}_{0})}{A}=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2\sqrt{{\beta}_{m}}}\mathit{Erf}\left(\frac{{\mathit{Pc}}_{0}}{A}\sqrt{{\beta}_{m}}\right)+\frac{1}{2{\beta}_{m}}\left(\begin{array}{c}1-{e}^{-{\beta}_{m}{\left(\frac{{\mathit{Pc}}_{0}}{A}\right)}^{2}}\\ \phantom{\rule{2em}{0ex}}\end{array}\right)
$$

The expected swelling pressure corresponds to the re-saturation path between the dry state (${P}_{c}=\infty$ ) and the saturated state, that is ${P}_{\mathit{gf}}={P}_{\mathit{gf}}(\infty )$ . It is known that $\mathrm{Erf}(\infty )=1$ and therefore: $\frac{{P}_{\mathit{gf}}}{A}=\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2\sqrt{{\beta}_{m}}}+\frac{1}{2{\beta}_{m}}$

An identification of the coefficient ${\beta}_{m}$ is deduced from this.

### 2.4 Output internal variables

There are no output internal variables.
