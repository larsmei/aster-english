# v1.01.254 ZZZZ254 – Validation of ORIE_INTERF_POU

**Summary:**

This test has no physical meaning, it is essentially a computer test.

It validates the correct operation of the orientation of biquadratic hexahedral elements in the MODI_MAILLAGE command.

On mesh cells HEXA27 with initial connectivity, reorientation is applied and output connectivity is checked.

It is recalled that the purpose of the operation is to force the orientation of the first axis of the mesh cell in its parametric space relative to the acute angle formed with the VECT_OIRE vector given by the user. The verification performed in this test case at the end of the operation therefore only concerns the position, in the mesh cell connectivity table, of the two nodes bearing this axis.

We're checking the operation:

- operates on a mesh-cell group ('GR_DEUX') containing two different mesh cells,
- operates on a single mesh-cell group ('GR_OPPX') containing a single mesh cell with an orientation opposite to the desired final orientation,
- does nothing on a mesh-cell group ('GR_NOORI') containing a single mesh cell already correctly oriented.
