# v3.02.316 SSLP316 - Error estimate on a cracked plate with X-FEM

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the error estimator as a standard of energy based on the equilibrium residues in the X-FEM frame. Neumann and Dirichlet type conditions are treated.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A square on the $1m$ side is considered to be crossed by a horizontal crack:

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.316/1000000000000208000001B68A88A403A1F8B567.png)

The crack is defined by the normal "level set" ${l}_{n}(Y)=Y-0.5$ and tangent ${l}_{t}(X)=-X+0.5$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=100000\mathrm{MPa}$
- $\nu =0.0$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 Boundary conditions by Dirichlet

*For Modelling A*, Dirichlet boundary conditions is imposed on the entire contour of the square corresponding to the asymptotic opening mode in mode I [1] .

${u}_{X}=\frac{1}{2\mu }\sqrt{\frac{r}{2\pi }}{K}_{I}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}(\kappa -\cos\theta )$ et ${u}_{Y}=\frac{1}{2\mu }\sqrt{\frac{r}{2\pi }}{K}_{I}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}(\kappa -\cos\theta )$ ,

with:

$\mu =\frac{E}{2(1+\nu )}$ and $\kappa =3-4\nu$ in flat constraints,

$r$ and $\theta$ are the polar coordinates as defined in the following figure:

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.316/10000000000001B90000019B565D5099C07B41FD.png)

With $\nu =0.0$ and ${K}_{I}=1$, we finally impose:

${u}_{X}=\frac{1}{E}\sqrt{\frac{r}{2\pi }}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}(3-\cos\theta )$ et ${u}_{Y}=\frac{1}{E}\sqrt{\frac{r}{2\pi }}{K}_{I}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}(3-\cos\theta )$ .

#### 1.3.2 Boundary conditions by Neumann

*For Modelling* *B* , Neumann boundary conditions are imposed. A uniform compression $P=-20\mathit{MPa}$ is imposed on the right edge while the left edge is clamped.

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.316/10000000000001F0000001AC6D1621B47E7B64E9.png)

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

#### 2.1.1 Boundary conditions by Dirichlet

The analytical stress field corresponding to the problem of the infinite cracked plate submitted to mode I sssed [1]:

${\sigma}_{\mathit{XX}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi r}}{K}_{I}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}(1-\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\sin\frac{3\theta }{2})$ , ${\sigma}_{\mathit{YY}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi r}}{K}_{I}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}(1+\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\sin\frac{3\theta }{2})$ et ${\sigma}_{\mathit{XY}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi r}}{K}_{I}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{3\theta }{2}$ .

With ${K}_{I}=1.0$, we have:

${\sigma}_{\mathit{XX}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi r}}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}(1-\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\sin\frac{3\theta }{2})$ , ${\sigma}_{\mathit{YY}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi r}}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}(1+\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\sin\frac{3\theta }{2})$ et ${\sigma}_{\mathit{XY}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi r}}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{3\theta }{2}$ .

#### 2.1.2 Boundary conditions by Neumann

Knowing that $\nu =0.0$ and the pressure imposed is constant, the stress field is simply the pressure imposed:

${\sigma}_{\mathit{XX}}=-P=20\mathit{MPa}$ , ${\sigma}_{\mathit{YY}}=0\mathit{MPa}$ et ${\sigma}_{\mathit{XY}}=0\mathit{MPa}$ .

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The SIGM_ELNO stress field calculated with nodes by the CALC_ERREUR operator is compared and verified by non-regressions that they correspond well to the SIEF_ELNO field calculated by the CALC_CHAMP operator.

The stress field calculated at nodes of sub-elements X-FEM is also tested.

Finally, the field of estimation of the residual error is tested [2] .

#### 2.2.1 Boundary conditions by Dirichlet

Since the refinement of the mesh will be insufficient to capture the asymptotic stress variation in the crack bottom, the calculation of the indicatord-error based on these numerical variations is not able to make a good estimate of the error. It is therefore decided to apply non-regression tests.

#### 2.2.2 Boundary conditions by Neumann

The stresses are constant in the plate. The solution being P0, the numerical error must be zero. The estimated error must also be zero.

### 2.3 Bibliographical references

  1. IRWIN, "Analysis of stresses and strains near the end of a crack crossing a flat", Journal of Applied Mechanics 24, 361-364, 1957.

1. Document R4.10.02, Residual Error Estimator, *Code_Aster Reference Manual* .

### 3.4 Remarks

The stresses calculated at nodes of mesh by CALC_ERREUR and CALC_CHAMP are the same.

The field of constraints is solved at the points of Gauss:

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.316/10000000000002CD00000264394DB487D8B57D71.png)

The smoothing made by the calculation of stresses at nodes introduces errors that lead the CALC_ERREUR operator to underestimate the asymptotic field error at the bottom of the crack.

## 5 Summary of the results

The modellings A and B are used to cover the calculation operators of the mechanical residue error estimator in the X-FEM frame.

However, attention should be paid to the interpretation of these estimates in the background of the crack. The method of re-interpolation to nodes and nodes of the sub-elements d'integration does not seem convincing to estimate the error of the asymptotical fields.
