# v6.03.506 SSNP506 – Non-linear Excentering Verification

**Summary:**

This test case validates the functionality of DKT non-linear plate excentre. A hole plate with non-linear VMIS_ISOT_TRAC isotropic hardening elastic behaviour is considered to be quasi-static.

Modelling A: DKT + STAT_NON_LINE / VMIS_ISOT_TRAC

Modelling B: DKQ + STAT_NON_LINE / VMIS_ISOT_TRAC

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a rectangular plate of 1mm thickness, with a hole and modeled with DKT DKT. Only a quarter of the plate is modelled with symmetries. Dimensions are given in millimetres.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.506/Shape1.gif)

### 1.2 Boundary conditions and loads

**Conditions of symmetry**

The plate is blocked following $\mathrm{Ox}$ along the $\mathit{AG}:\mathit{DY}=0.0,\mathit{DZ}=0.0,\mathit{DRY}=0.0$ side.

It is blocked following $\mathrm{Oy}$ along the $\mathit{BD}:\mathit{DX}=0.0,\mathit{DZ}=0.0,\mathit{DRX}=0.0$ side.

**Conditional loading imposed**

It is subjected to $p(t)$ traction following $\mathrm{Oy}$ distributed on the $\mathrm{FG}$ side.

$p(t=0)=0$ , $p(t=1000)=1000$ .

We stop at the very beginning of plasticity at the edge of the hole, that is to say 110s.

### 1.3 Material properties

The behavior is elastoplastic of Von Mises, with isotropic hardening.

The elastic characteristics are:

- Young's modulus $E=200000\mathrm{MPa}$ ;
- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$ ;
- Elasticity limit: $200\mathrm{MPa}$;

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

To validate the nonlinear excenteration, the solution (in stresses and displacements) of a single plate is compared with two glued plates of demis-thickness and excreted from a quarter of this thickness.

### 2.2 Remarks

Because of the theoretical assumptions for calculating shear stresses on DKT, a homogeneous plate cannot be compared with its equivalent of two plates glued in thickness. Therefore, the SIXZ component of the stress is not tested in this case.
