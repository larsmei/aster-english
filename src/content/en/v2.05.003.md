# v2.05.003 SDLX03 - Assembly of thin rectangular plates

**Summary:**

This three-dimensional problem consists in searching for vibration frequencies in a mechanical structure composed of a plate assembly where a stiffening effect has been simulated. This Structure Mechanics test corresponds to a dynamic analysis of an assembly structure having linear behavior. It consists of two modellings.

Through this problem, DKT plate element is tested and the vibration frequency calculation by the Lanczos method is calculated with the detection of rigid body modes.

In the second model, the restraint between hulls is tested (keyword LIAISON_COQUE of the AFFE_CHAR_MECA command).

The results obtained are in good agreement with the results given in the VPCS guide (average of results obtained by different calculation codes).The six rigid body modes were detected.

A comparison with experimental results is also satisfactory.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is the one given in the SDLX03/89 plug in the VPCS guide.

The reference solution was obtained by experimental study of the frequencies and modes of the free structure on a model made with welded plate.

The structure suspended by flexible restraints is vibrationally driven by an electrodynamic exciter. The eigenfrequency reading is obtained from an accelerometer.

In addition, numerical simulations, performed by different calculation codes, have established "reference results" for the finished elements models.

### 2.2 Baseline

6 first non-zero eigenfrequencies.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

Elements plate DKT is used

![Figure](/figures/v2.05.003/100040CE000069D500002C8CDB573572E212921F.svg)

The cutting is done as follows:

| On a | 1 | element between ${P}_{1}$ and ${P}_{2}$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | 6 | elements between ${P}_{2}$ and ${P}_{3}$ |
|  | 1 | element between ${P}_{3}$ and ${P}_{4}$ |
|  | 1 | element between ${P}_{17}$ and ${P}_{20}$ |
|  | 10 | elements on sides parallel to $y$ (e.g. ${P}_{4}{P}_{5}$) |

We're going to go through two calculations:

- In the first calculation, solid body restraintss are established between the straights:
- ${P}_{2}{P}_{7}$ et ${P}_{17}{P}_{22}$
- ${P}_{3}{P}_{6}$ et ${P}_{18}{P}_{21}$
- ${P}_{10}{P}_{15}$ et ${P}_{20}{P}_{23}$
- ${P}_{11}{P}_{14}$ et ${P}_{19}{P}_{24}$

via factor keyword 'LIAISON_COQUE' of the AFFE_CHAR_MECA command.

- In the second calculation, solid body restraints is established between nodes in relation to the above-mentioned right-hand pairs via the factor keyword 'LIAISON_SOLIDE' of the AFFE_CHAR_MECA command.
