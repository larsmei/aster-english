# v7.22.122 HSNV122 - Thermoplasticity and metallurgy in large deformations in single tensile

**Summary:**

The mechanical evolution of a cylindrical bar subjected to known and uniform thermal and metallurgical evolutions (metallurgical transformation is of the bathitic type) and mechanical tensile loading is processed.

The behavior relationship is a plasticity model in large deformations (command STAT_NON_LINE, keyword DEFORMATION: 'SIMO_MIEHE') with linear isotropic hardening and plasticity transformation.

The elastic limit and slope of the tensile curve depend on temperature and metallurgical composition. The expansion coefficient depends on the metallurgical composition.

The bar is modeled by elements axisymmetric.

The mechanical loading applied is a follower pressure.

This test case is identical to the test case HSNV101 (modelization B, [V7.22.101](doc:v7.22.101)) in the sense that it is the same material, the same loading and the same thermal and metallurgical evolutions but in a version in large deformations.
