# v6.03.145 SSNP145 – PRED_ELAS plasticity piloting validation

**Summary:**

Using softening laws can lead to rough snapbacks that make it difficult to run the calculation. To track these instabilitys, two types of steering are available in Code_Aster: elastic prediction (PRED_ELAS), which depends on the constitutive law and generic deformation steering (DEFORMATION). In the case of modellings with non-damaging elasto-plastic behavior, it is possible to use elastic prediction steering. This adds the possibility of steering materials with a nut-set (a perfect plastic behavior) but also allows mixing plastic materials (e.g. reinforcements in concrete) with damaging materials.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry and boundary conditions

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.145/Shape1.gif)

**Figure** 1.1-a **: geometry of the structure studied**

The structure studied is a hollow sphere under pressure $p=\mathrm{1MPa}$ . The symmetry properties of the problem will be used.

The inner radius is ${R}_{int}=100\mathrm{mm}$ and the outer radius is ${R}_{\text{ext}}=200\mathrm{mm}$).

### 1.2 Material properties

| **constitutive law** | **Elastic behaviour** | **Plastic behaviour** | **Damaged behaviour** **(weakened area)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VMIS_ISOT_LINE | $E=200.000\mathit{MPa}$ $\nu =0,3$ | ${\sigma}_{y}=300\mathit{MPa}$ ${E}_{T}=0$ | ${\sigma}_{y}=5\mathit{MPa}$ ${E}_{T}=-10000\mathit{MPa}$ |

## 2 reference solution

The values tested are analytical values of a purely spherical problem under perfect plasticity. The pressure varies in the structure according to the $r$ radius according to the following formula:

$p(r)=2.{\sigma}_{y}.\log(\frac{r}{{R}_{int}})+(\frac{2}{3}).{\sigma}_{y}.\frac{{R}_{int}^{3}-{R}_{\text{ext}}^{3}}{{R}_{\text{ext}}^{3}}$
