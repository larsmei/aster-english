# v2.01.001 SDLD01 - degree of freedom mass-spring system

**Summary:**

This one-dimensional problem consists in making a transient dynamic calculation on a physical basis of a mechanical structure consisting of a mass and two springs subjected to multi-support loading.

This test allows for a complete validation of the options for calculating the displacement fields in the absolute marker. Three different modellings are proposed: one test the classic multi-support approach in the trained marker, and two other test the direct approach in the absolute marker. The results are compared to the analytical solution from an oscillator to an degree of freedom (1 ddl).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

In MassSpring_System, one mass and two spring Modelling is shown.

![Figure](/figures/v2.01.001/Systeme_Masse_Ressorts.svg)

*One-dimensional system with one mass and two springs*

### 1.2 Material properties

Both springs represent two beams with stiffness equal to $k=EA/L$, where:

- $E = 35 GPa$ is the Young's modulus
- $A = 0.016 m^2$ is the surface of the beam section
- $L = 4 m$ is the length of the beam

The concentrated mass is equivalent to $m = 4000 t$. A Rayleigh damping is re-applied around the basic frequency of the $\omega_0=\sqrt{k_{eq}/m}$ system, where $k_{eq}=2EA/L$. The reduced damping is taken equal to $\xi=0.05$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The following limit conditions are imposed:

- *DX = DY = DZ = 0* on nodes *A* and *B*
- *DY = DZ = 0* on the mass*

The following loads are required:

- On the node *A* you will require a $u_A = u_{0,A}sin(\omega_At + \alpha_A)$ displacement, where:

  - $u_{0,A} = 2 mm$
  - $\omega_A = \pi$
  - $\alpha_A = 0$
- On the node *B* you will require a $u_B = u_{0,B}sin(\omega_Bt + \alpha_B)$ displacement, where:

  - $u_{0,B} = 4.5 mm$
  - $\omega_B = 2\pi$
  - $\alpha_B = 0$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

In this test case, we propose two ways to impose the load:

- Through a multi-support load in trained frame, in this case there is no need to impose initial conditions
- Two shifts on the nodes *A* and *B* are required in this case to impose the initial speed and acceleration fields which are not zero

## 3 Modelling A

In this model, a multi-support load is tested in the **-trained marker**. The DEPL field at the end of this calculation is, of course, the field in the driven marker. The field in the absolute marker is stored in DEPL_ABSOLU. The field value at the moment $t=0.268 s$, which corresponds to the first peak of the displacement, is tested. The results are compared to the analytical solution.

## 4 Modelling B

In this Modelling, a multi-support load is tested in the absolute **mark** using the DYNA_VIBRA operator. The DEPL field at the end of this calculation is therefore the field in the absolute marker. The field value at the moment $t=0.268 s$ is tested, which corresponds to the first peak of displacement. The results are compared to the numerical solution of Modelling A.

## 5 Modelling C

In this Modelling, a multi-support load is tested in the absolute **mark** using the DYNA_NON_LINE operator. The DEPL field at the end of this calculation is therefore the field in the absolute marker. The field value at the moment $t=0.268 s$ is tested, which corresponds to the first peak of displacement. The results are compared to the numerical solution of Modelling A.
