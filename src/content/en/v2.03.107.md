# v2.03.107 SDLS107 – Turbulent Cylinder in axial ring flow

**Summary**

This test of the field of linear dynamics of hulls and plates implements the calculation of acceptance, a function intended to calculate the DSP of modal effort from a pressure DSP. This precise test implements a circular or 3D hull type Modelling to test the Au Yang method. Compatibility of the compatibility of the compatibility calculation with different types of interface models is tested: 3D Mechanical, 3D Thermal and 3D Fluide-Structure.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The test is useful here to calculate and test the modal effort autospectrum obtained from a characteristic pressure spectrum of established turbulent flows.

In this case, the mode of movement is imposed. Therefore, it is not necessary to perform a modal calculation for the structure.
The modal self-spectrum, calculated from the static deformation, is written:

$\mathit{DSP}(\omega )={S}_{p}(\omega ){J}_{{{A}_{ij}}_{}}^{2}(w)$

The spectrum chosen here is constant and then zero from a cutoff frequency:

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.107/10000200000006A9000005092E78E7DE07EF2274.png)

The pressure spectrum is:

${S}_{{p}_{}}(\omega )={K}^{2}({\mathit{\rho U}}^{2}{)}^{2}{d}^{3}$ for $0,1<\frac{\omega d}{2\pi U}<10$

With the selected parameters, ${S}_{{p}_{}}(\omega )\approx 1.0$

The chosen consistency function for this circular cylinder subjected to parallel flow is derived from a model of AU_YANG:

${r}^{(s)}(x-x\text{',}\omega )={e}^{-(x-x')/\lambda }\cos(\omega (x-x')/{U}_{c})$

${r}^{(s)}(\theta -\theta \text{',}\omega )={e}^{-R(\theta -\theta ')/\lambda '}$

The $\lambda$ and $\lambda '$ parameters are the correlation lengths according to the axis and the orthoradial direction respectively.

${U}_{c}$ is the axial convective speed of the vortices: it is equal to the product of the axial speed coefficient by the fluid speed.

The acceptance function is defined by:

${J}_{{{A}_{ij}}_{}}^{2}(\omega )=\underset{A}{\int}\underset{A}{\int}r(x-x\text{',}\omega ){f}_{{i}_{\alpha}}(x){f}_{{j}_{\alpha '}}(x'){n}_{\alpha}(x){n}_{\alpha '}(x')\text{dA}\text{dA}'$

> in our case:

${J}_{{{A}_{\text{nm}}}_{}}^{2}(\omega )=\underset{A}{\int}\underset{A}{\int}{e}^{-R\mid \theta -\theta '\mid /\lambda '}{e}^{-R\mid x-x'\mid /\lambda }\cos(\frac{\omega (x-x')}{{U}_{c}})x\cos\theta x'\cos\theta '\mathit{dxRd}\theta \mathit{dx}'\mathit{Rd}\theta '$

${J}_{{{A}_{\text{nm}}}_{}}^{2}(\omega )=\underset{0}{\overset{2\pi }{\int}}\underset{0}{\overset{2\pi }{\int}}{e}^{-R\mid \theta -\theta '\mid /\lambda '}\cos\theta \cos\theta '{R}^{2}d\theta d\theta '\underset{0}{\overset{H}{\int}}\underset{0}{\overset{H}{\int}}{e}^{-R\mid x-x'\mid /\lambda }\cos(\frac{\omega (x-x')}{{U}_{c}})xx'\text{dxdx}'$

> The following table gives values of this integral using Python:

| $\omega (\mathrm{rad}/s)$ | ${I}_{T}(\omega )$ |
| --- | --- |
| 0.06283 | 252.701 |
| 0.6283 | 249.663 |
