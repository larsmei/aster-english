# v7.22.101 HSNV101 - Single Tensile Decoupled Thermo-plasticity and Metallurgy

**Summary:**

The mechanical evolution of a cylindrical bar subjected to known and uniform $T(t)$ and $Z(t)$ thermal evolutions is determined (the metallurgical transformation is of the bathitic type).

The elements used are elements axisymmetric and the behavioral relationship is VonMises plasticity with linear isotropic hardening (for modelingB, also consideration is given to transformation plasticity).

The elastic limit and slope of the tensile curve depend on temperature and metallurgical composition.

The expansion coefficient $\alpha$ depends on the metallurgical composition.

Metallurgical transformations take place at ${\dot{\epsilon}}^{p}=0$ (this is in the sense that the **decouple** test is the plasticity of transformation of classical plasticity).

The results provided by *Code_Aster* are very satisfactory with errors below $\text{2 \%}$.

Modelling A, B, D uses meshes composed of two mesh cells QUAD8, Modelling C uses mesh cells TRIA6, and Modelling E validates metallurgy on point hardware.

Modellings A, C, and E use META_P_IL behavior, B Modelling validates META_P_IL_PT behavior, and D Modelling uses META_P_CL.

> **Metallurgical parameters:**

TRC to model a metallurgical evolution of the bathitic type, over the whole structure, of the shape:

${Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}=\lbrace \begin{array}{ccc}0.& \mathit{si}t\le {\tau}_{1}& {\tau}_{1}=60s\\ \frac{t-{\tau}_{1}}{{\tau}_{2}-{\tau}_{1}}& \mathit{si}{\tau}_{1}\le t<{\tau}_{2}& {\tau}_{2}=112s\\ 1.& \mathit{si}t\ge {\tau}_{2}& \end{array}$

**Thermal parameters:**

Heat capacity: $\rho {C}_{p}=2.{10}^{6}{\mathit{J.m}}^{-3}.°{C}^{-1}$

Conductivity: $\lambda =9999.9{\mathit{W.m}}^{-1}.°{C}^{-1}$

** Thermomechanical parameters:**

- Thermo-elastic parameters:

Young's modulus $E=200000{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$

Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$

Thermal expansion coefficients

${\alpha}_{\mathit{fbm}}=15.{10}^{-6}°{C}^{-1}$ ${\alpha}_{\mathit{aust}}=23.5{10}^{-6}°{C}^{-1}$

Definition temperature of the expansion coefficient: ${T}_{\mathit{ref}}=900°C$

Reference thermal deformation state: $\Delta {\epsilon}_{f\gamma }^{{T}_{\mathit{ref}}}=2.52{10}^{-3}$

Limit of elasticity:

${\sigma}_{y}^{\mathit{fbm}}={\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}+{s}^{\mathit{fbm}}(T-{T}^{0})$ with ${\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}=400.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$ and ${s}^{\mathit{fbm}}=0.5{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa.}°{C}^{-1}$

${\sigma}_{y}^{\mathit{aust}}={\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}+{s}^{\mathit{aust}}(T-{T}^{0})$ with ${\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}=530.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$ and ${s}^{\mathit{aust}}=0.5{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa.}°{C}^{-1}$

- Thermoplastic parameters (law with linear nutring)

Tangential modules: ${E}_{T}^{\mathit{fbm}}$ and ${E}_{T}^{\mathit{aust}}$ are selected as:

${H}^{\mathit{fbm}}={H}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}+{\lambda}^{\mathit{fbm}}(T-{T}^{0})$ with ${H}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}=-50.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$ and ${\lambda}^{\mathit{fbm}}=-5.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa.}°{C}^{-1}$

${H}^{\mathit{aust}}={H}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}+{\lambda}^{\mathit{aust}}(T-{T}^{0})$ with ${H}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}=1250.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa}$ and ${\lambda}^{\mathit{aust}}=-5.{10}^{6}\mathit{Pa.}°{C}^{-1}$

It's recalled that $H=\frac{{\mathit{EE}}_{T}}{E-{E}_{T}}$

- Parameters for transform plasticity:

Background:

| In the case of a metallurgical evolution of the bathitic type, the model of plasticity transformation is as follows: ${\dot{\epsilon}}^{\mathit{pt}}=\frac{3}{2}\stackrel{̃}{\sigma}{k}^{\mathit{fbm}}{F}^{'}({Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}){\dot{Z}}_{\mathit{fbm}}$ |
| --- |

Model settings: ${k}^{\mathit{fbm}}=1.{10}^{-10}{\mathit{Pa}}^{-1}$ and ${F}^{'}({Z}_{\mathit{fbm}})=2(1-{Z}_{\mathit{fbm}})$

- temperature imposed on the whole structure:

$T(t)={T}^{0}+\mu t$ with $\mu =-5°{\mathit{C.s}}^{-1}$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

**Before transformation** , thermo-elastic solution for $t<{\tau}_{1}^{'}$ .

$\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\epsilon}_{zz}(t)={\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)\\ {\sigma}_{zz}(t)={p}_{0}t\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)}{E}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)={\alpha}_{\mathit{aust}}(T-{T}^{0})\end{array}$

The elastic limit is reached for:

${\tau}_{1}^{'}=\frac{{\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}}{{p}_{0}-{s}^{\mathit{aust}}\mu }=47.06s$

**Before transformation** , thermo-elasto-plastic solution, ${\tau}_{1}^{'}\le t\le {\tau}_{1}$ , ${\tau}_{1}=\mathrm{60 }s$ .

$\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\epsilon}_{zz}(t)={\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}(t)\\ {\sigma}_{zz}(t)={p}_{0}t\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)}{E}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)={\alpha}_{\mathit{aust}}(T-{T}^{0})\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)-({\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}+{s}^{\mathit{aust}}\mu t)}{{H}_{0}^{\mathit{aust}}+{\lambda}^{\mathit{aust}}\mu t}\end{array}$

**During transformation** , thermo-elasto-metallurgical solution, ${\tau}_{1}<t<{\tau}_{2}$ , ${\tau}_{\mathrm{2 }}=\mathrm{112 }s$ .

$\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\epsilon}_{zz}(t)={\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}({\tau}_{1})+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{pt}}(t)\\ {\sigma}_{zz}(t)={p}_{0}{\tau}_{1}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)}{E}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)={Z}_{\mathit{aust}}{\alpha}_{\mathit{aust}}(T-{T}^{0})+{Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}\left({\alpha}_{\mathit{fbm}}(T-{T}^{0})+\Delta {\epsilon}_{f\gamma }^{{T}_{\mathit{ref}}}\right)\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{pt}}(t)={k}^{\mathit{fbm}}F({Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}){p}_{0}{\tau}_{1}\end{array}$

with $F({Z}_{\mathit{fbm}})={Z}_{\mathit{fbm}}(1-{Z}_{\mathit{fbm}})$

**After transformation** , thermo-elasto-plastic solution,

![Figure](/figures/v7.22.101/Object_28.svg)

, ${\tau}_{3}=\mathrm{176 }s$ .

$\lbrace \begin{array}{c}{\epsilon}_{zz}(t)={\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}(t)+{\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{pt}}({\tau}_{2})\\ {\sigma}_{zz}(t)={p}_{0}{\tau}_{1}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{e}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)}{E}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{\mathit{th}}(t)={\alpha}_{\mathit{fbm}}(T-{T}^{0})+\Delta {\epsilon}_{f\gamma }^{{T}_{\mathit{ref}}}\\ {\epsilon}_{zz}^{p}(t)=\frac{{\sigma}_{zz}(t)-({\sigma}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}+{s}^{\mathit{fbm}}\mu t)}{{H}_{0}^{\mathit{fbm}}+{\lambda}^{\mathit{fbm}}\mu t}\end{array}$
