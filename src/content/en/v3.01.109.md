# v3.01.109 SSLL109 - Balance in distorted configuration of a multifibre beam

**Summary:**

This test represents a deformed equilibrium calculation of a multifibre beam. This test validates the RIGI_GEOM=的OUI, available only for POU_D_TGM beams.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Integration of the equilibrium equation into distorted configuration by assuming no extension of the middle axis.

### 2.2 Baseline

The balance is described by:

$$
{w}_{x}''(z)=\frac{-M(z)}{{\mathit{EI}}_{y}}=\frac{-{F}_{z}({w}_{x}(z)-{w}_{x}(l))-{F}_{x}(z-l)}{{\mathit{EI}}_{y}}
$$

The ${w}_{x}(z)$ displacement is therefore:

$$
{w}_{x}(z)=\frac{{F}_{x}}{{F}_{z}}\left\lbrace -z+\frac{1}{\alpha}\left[\sin(\alpha z)+\tan(\alpha l)\left(1-\cos(\alpha z)\right)\right]\right\rbrace
$$

with $\alpha =\sqrt{\frac{{F}_{z}}{{\mathit{EI}}_{y}}}$ and $l\approx {l}_{0}$ . This last lever approximation is acceptable for moderate ${F}_{x}$ forces.

## 4 Summary of the results

The numerical results show a good agreement with the analytical solution. the difference in the example presented is less than 1%. This difference could be reduced by decreasing the ${F}_{x}$ effort due to the $l\approx {l}_{0}$ approximation.
