# v6.04.122 SSNV122 - Hyper-elastic tracker and traction of a bar

**Summary:**

This quasi-static mechanical test consists of turning a parallelepipedic bar 90° and subjecting it to a high tensile strength by means of following forces. This validates the kinematics of the large hyper-elastic deformations (command STAT_NON_LINE, keyword COMPORTEMENT), and therefore in particular the large rotations, for a linear elastic behavior relationship, as well as the consideration of following forces (command STAT_NON_LINE key word TYPE_CHARGE='SUIV').

The bar is modelled by a volume element (HEXA8, modelingA).

Model B, on the other hand, validates the following loads of function type depending on the initial geometry.

The results obtained by *Code_Aster* do not differ from the theoretical solution.

## 5 Summary of the results

It appears from this test (modelization A) that the numerical solution coincides remarkably with the analytical solution. However, it is noted that the strong nonlinearity due to large rotations requires relatively fine time discretization, without being penalising on accuracy since, unlike an incremental behavior relationship, errors do not cumulate from one time step to the other.

Model B validates the dependence of pressure on initial geometry.
