# v3.03.004 SSLS04 – Z-section beam

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the calculation of displacements, and stresses in a section beam in Z. square plate, subjected to shearing effort.

modellings :

- $A$ Modelling: DKT with mesh cells QUAD4/TRIA3
- $B$ Modelling: DST with mesh cells QUAD4/TRIA3
- $C$ Modelling: Q4G with mesh cells QUAD4/TRIA3
- $D$ Modelling: COQUE_3D with mesh cells QUAD4/TRIA3

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=2,1.\times {10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$
- $\nu =0.3$

The beam is clamped in $x=0$:

## 7 Summary of the results

**Movements**: Whatever mesh cell type is used (TRIA3, QUAD4) and the Modelling chosen the results obtained are far from the reference solution, there is a maximum difference of 19%. However, the 4 modellings (DKT, DST, Q4G and COQUE_3D) gives the same results.

**Contraints**: Whatever the Modelling, the results obtained are far from the reference solution. A maximum difference of 30.5 % for the SIXY stress and 18.5 % for the SIXX stress are observed. As with the displacements, the 4 modellings give the same results.
