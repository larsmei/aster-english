# v7.22.133 HSNV133 - Thermoplastic Traction in Large Deformities VMIS_ISOT_PUIS

**Summary:**

This quasi-static thermomechanical test consists of uniformly heating a rectangular section bar (strain and homogeneous deformation states) and then tensile.

Similarly, in the HSNV121 [V7.22.121](doc:v7.22.121) test, the kinematics of the large elasto-plasticity deformations are validated for a Von-type behavioral relationship. Isotropic hardening bets defined either by a given traction curve point by point (VMIS_ISOT_TRAC); or by a power law (VMIS_ISOT_PUIS).

The bar is modeled by a volume element.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material obeys a constitutive law in large plastic deformations with isotropic hardening defined by a tensile curve (point by point or power law).

The tensile curve is given in the logarithmic deformation plane - rational stress.

| $\sigma =\frac{F}{S}=\frac{F}{{S}_{o}}\cdot \frac{l}{{l}_{o}}$ | $R(p)={\sigma}_{y}+{\sigma}_{y}{(\frac{E}{a{\sigma}_{y}}p)}^{1/n}$ |
| --- | --- |
| ![Figure](/figures/v7.22.133/100000000000012A0000013200CD6E22C2675915.png) | $\begin{array}{cc}\nu & =0.3\\ \alpha & ={10}^{-4}{K}^{-1}\\ {\sigma}_{y}& =1000\mathrm{MPa}\\ E& =200000\mathrm{MPa}\\ n& =7\\ a& =1\end{array}$ |

${l}_{o}$ and $l$ are, respectively, the initial and current length of the useful part of the test piece.

${S}_{o}$ and $S$ are the initial and current surfaces, respectively. Between the $20°C$ and $120°C$ temperatures, the characteristics are interpolated linearly.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Baseline

Reference results will be taken from the displacements, the stress of Cauchy $\sigma$ and the cumulative plastic deformation $p$ obtained with the VMIS_ISOT_TRAC behavior (also valid with DEFORMATION=的SIMO_MIEHE and 的GDEF_LOG).

The solutions obtained at time $t=\mathrm{2 }s$ ($\Delta T=100°C$ , traction $u$ ) will be compared.

### 2.2 Uncertainty about the solution

Very low since it is a question of inter-comparison between two formally identical behaviors. However, the discretization of the law of hardening in power leads to uncertainty.

## 5 Summary of the results

The intervalidation of VMIS_ISOT_TRAC and VMIS_ISOT_PUIS behaviors performed here shows that isotropic hardening curves can be modelled in code_aster in large deformations, via 'SIMO_MIEHE' and 'GDEF_LOG' models.
