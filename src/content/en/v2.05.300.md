# v2.05.300 SDLX300 - Pipes subjected to excitation while moving, speed, acceleration

**Summary:**

Three-dimensional study of clamped-rotulated piping with localized stiffeners and mass subjected to excitation while moving, speed and acceleration.

The reference is obtained for 6 modes with CASTEM 2000.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 boundary conditions

Node 1: $\mathrm{dx}=\mathrm{dy}=\mathrm{dz}=0$ (rotula)

Au node 7: $\mathrm{dx}=\mathrm{dy}=\mathrm{dz}=\mathrm{drx}=\mathrm{dry}=\mathrm{drz}=0$ (clamped support)

Node 2, support in direction $z$

Node 3, support in $y$ direction

Node 5, support in $y$ direction

Node 6, support in direction $x$

The stiffeners brought by each support are:

${K}_{x}={K}_{y}={K}_{z}=80.E+3{\mathrm{N.m}}^{-1}$ ${K}_{\theta x}={K}_{\theta y}={K}_{\theta z}=J=1.2{\mathrm{N.m.deg}}^{-1}$

#### 1.3.2 Loading

**Calculation of static modes**

A first calculation validates the calculation of static modes.

The whole model is subjected to a uniform acceleration according to $x$ value $100\times g$ with $g=9.81{\mathrm{m.s}}^{-2}$

**Spectral response**

The line of piping shall be subjected to an excitation following $x$ , defined by a response spectrum such as:

- its moving value for frequencies between 1. and 10. $\mathrm{Hz}$ , i.e. $d=48.E-\mathrm{3m}$
- its speed value for frequencies between 10. and 63. $\mathrm{Hz}$ , i.e. $v={\mathrm{3.m.s}}^{-1}$
- its acceleration value for frequencies between 63. and 1000. $\mathrm{Hz}$ , i.e. $\gamma =120\ast g$

Below is represented the acceleration spectrum, determined from excitation for reduced damping $\xi =0$.

![Figure](/figures/v2.05.300/100002000000027E000001C2B82D53D1ED537DBB.png)

The characteristic values used are:

$\gamma (1\mathrm{Hz})=\mathrm{1.92m}/{s}^{2}$ $\gamma (10\mathrm{Hz})=192m/{s}^{2}$ $\gamma (63\mathit{Hz})=1000m/{s}^{2}$

$\gamma (1000\mathrm{Hz})=1000m/{s}^{2}$

#### 1.3.3 Flexibility of elbows

The flexibility coefficient, ${C}_{\mathrm{flex}}$ , of the elbows is given by the RCC-M regulation:

${C}_{\mathrm{flex}}=\frac{1.65}{h}$ with $h=\frac{\mathrm{ep}\ast {r}_{\mathrm{courb}}}{{r}_{m}^{2}}$

| $\mathrm{ep}$ | : | elbow thickness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ${r}_{\mathrm{courb}}$ | : | radius of curvature of the elbow |
| ${r}_{m}$ | : | mean radius of elbow |

The stress intensification index, ${I}_{\mathrm{sigm}}$ , is given by:

${I}_{\mathrm{sigm}}=\frac{0.9}{{h}^{0.666}}$

According to RCC-M regulations, the flexibility coefficient and stress intensification index are greater than or equal to one. This is not the case in this test case.

## 5 Summary of the results

** Modal characteristics**

Differences on the calculated frequencies remain below 0.4%.

Maximum the difference on the significant effective masses is 4.4 per cent (direction $z$). A 24% difference is recorded in the $y$ direction on the actual mass of mode 6 which represents 0.15% of the total mass.

**Static modes**

The differences are about 1% in the $y$ and $z$ directions, and less than 0.5% in the $x$ direction.

**Spectral response**

**Movements**

The differences are about 1%, with an difference of 4.6% for node 6 in the $y$ direction (but the displacement to node 6 is about 1.e-6 and is not significant compared to the other calculated values of 1.e-3).

**clamped support reactions and generalized efforts**

The difference average is about 2%. A difference of 12.8%, appears at the node 5 level for the $\mathrm{Fz}$ component of the generalized effort. There is an order of quantity between the force value to the left and right of the mass, the difference on the minimum value of the effort is 12.8%, 2.6% on the maximum value, and 5% on the average value.
