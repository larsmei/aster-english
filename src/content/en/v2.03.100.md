# v2.03.100 SDLS100 - Study of mesh sizes on a thin square plate

**Summary:**

This three-dimensional problem is to look for vibration frequencies of a mechanical structure composed of a clamped thin square plate on one side. The influence of mesh distortion on the results is studied. This Structures Mechanical test corresponds to a dynamic analysis of a superfacial model with linear behavior. It has three modellings.

This problem allows testing element plate in transverse bending and calculation of eigenfrequencies, either by the Lanczos method or by the Bathe and Wilson method. The first Modelling consists of finely and regularly knitting the plate by triangles. For the second Modelling, the mesh is coarser while for the third, it is coarse and distorted.

The first model serves as a reference result.

The results obtained are in good agreement with each other and with those of an NAFEMS plug. The distortion effect of the mesh does not appear on the first vibration frequencies.
