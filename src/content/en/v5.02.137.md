# v5.02.137 SDNL137 – Calculation of nonlinear modes of a curved tube with two nonlinearity ring contact type

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the nonlinear mode calculation with the MODE_NON_LINE operator.

The system is particularly valid with a nonlinear ring contact type.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The following structure is considered:

![Figure](/figures/v5.02.137/Shape1.gif)

The tube has an external ${R}_{\mathit{ext}}=11.11{10}^{-3}m$ radius and a thickness of $\mathit{ep}=1.27{10}^{-3}m$

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic with properties of:

- $E=2.056{10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$
- $\nu =0.3$
- $\rho =8357\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$
- Shock rigidity: ${K}_{\mathit{choc}}=4{10}^{6}N/m$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The game at the ring contact is $e=2.9{10}^{-4}m$ .

The ring contacts were removed from $0.1e$ of the contact nodes.

## 2 reference solution

The calculation of periodic solutions of the system characterizing the nonlinear mode is being studied.

### 2.1 Method of calculation

For system resolution, the EHMAN method [1] is used. The branch of periodic solutions is tried from the first mode of the underlying linear system.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The selected reference quantities are the frequency-energy torque and stability of the periodic solution obtained.

A periodic solution is found for frequency – energy torque such as:

$5.2\mathit{Hz}<f<5.5\mathit{Hz}$     et    $7{10}^{-5}J<E<8{10}^{-5}J$

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Non-regression solution.

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

    1. MOUSSI, Analysis of vibrating structures with non-linearities located at play using non-linear modes. PhD Thesis 2013.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

POU_D_E modelling is used.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh contains 10 elements of type SEG2.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The frequency-energy interpolation torque is tested from the table produced by MODE_NON_LINE. A solution of non-regression is proposed. The values obtained are presented in the table below.

| **Frequency (** $\mathit{Hz}$ **)** | **Energy (** $J$ **)** | **Stability of the periodic solution** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $5.25698$ | $7.83624{10}^{-5}$ | NON_EVALUE |

## 4 Summary of the results

This test case validates the nonlinear mode calculation operator (MODE_NON_LINE) on a system with a nonlinear ring contact type.

A solution of non-regression is proposed.
