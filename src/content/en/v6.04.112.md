# v6.04.112 SSNV112 - Incompressible hollow cylinder (large deformations)

**Summary:**

This test allows the validation of elements near-incompressible in large deformations, static for a three-dimensional, axisymmetric or two-dimensional problem (plane deformations). A hollow cylinder is considered to be subjected to internal radial displacement. The material has an Poisson's ratio equal to 0.4999 and is used elements near-incompressible `INCO_UPG` with `SIMO_MIEHE` and `GDEF_LOG` deformations and elements `INCO_UP` with `GDEF_LOG` deformations.
