# v6.03.151 SSNP151 – Compact Tension (CT) Test in 2D and 3D with CZM_TRA_MIX law

**Summary:**

This non-linear static mechanical test ensures that the non-regression of a *Code_Aster* functionality in breaking mechanics. The tested functionality is the ductile breakage law: CZM_TRA_MIX [R7.02.11](doc:r7.02.11).

A Compact Tension Test ($\mathit{CT}$ ) is used in tensile operation. The evolution of the force during the propagation of ductile rupture is calculated.

The Modelling of the test piece is done with elements $\mathrm{2D}$ (QUA8) or elements $\mathrm{3D}$ (HEXA20).

### 1.2 Material properties

The behaviour of the axisymmetric test piece material (densal material) is described by using an elastoplastic constitutive law with an isotropic hardening (law VMIS_ISOT_TRAC).

Take: $E=207\mathit{GPa}$ and $\nu =0.3$ and the selected nutring curve is given below:

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.151/10000200000001C100000134E1017A238A1EAC7A.png)

**Figure 2**: isotropic hardening curve of the volume material.

For interface elements the following parameters are used in the CZM_TRA_MIX:

${\sigma}_{c}=1800\mathit{MPa}$ , ${G}_{c}=150\mathit{MPa}\cdot \mathit{mm}$ , ${\delta}_{e}=0.01\mathit{mm}$ , ${\delta}_{p}=0.06\mathit{mm}$ , ${\delta}_{c}=0.117\mathit{mm}$

The resulting law is schematized below.

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.151/100002000000016900000108F387E7EDCA4FFB9E.png)

**Figure 3**: constitutive law of the elements interface.

NB: Only half of the crack is modelled due to the symmetry of the problem, the tenacity of the material is $2{G}_{c}$.

Finally, the rigid pin has elastic behavior (law ELAS) with: $E=1\times {10}^{9}\mathit{MPa}$ , $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

The boundary conditions imposed on the pin are:

- $X$ motion blocked,
- $l$ forced displacement following $Y$ direction.

The evolution of the $l$ displacement over time is given in the following table:

| Time $[s]$ | 0 | 0,4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moving $l$ $[\mathit{mm}]$ | 0 | 1,6 |

The cohesive area is represented by the elements interface on the ligament of the test piece. The boundary conditions on the elements interface are:

- $X$ displacement imposed identically on both lips of the cohesive area,
- $Y$ movement blocked on the lower lip.

## 3 Modelling A

Modelling in flat deformations.

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Modelling of ductile rupture is performed with PLAN_INTERFACE Modelling and CZM_TRA_MIX law. The volume elements are modeled with D_PLAN plane deformations.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The input mesh is linear. It is converted into a quadratic mesh by LINE_QUAD in CREA_MAILLAGE.

After transformation its characteristics are as follows:

Number of nodes: 2357

Elements number for $\mathrm{CT}$ test piece: 663 QUAD8 and 42 TRIA6

Number of elements for pin: 20 TRIA6.

Number of elements interface: 40 QUAD8.
