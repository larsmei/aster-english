# v9.01.111 SZLZ111 - Damage to Lemaître-Sermageen post-processing POST_FATIGUE

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to calculate the damage of Lemaître-Sermage "LEMAITRE" from any multiaxial loading history and the history of the cumulative plastic deformation.

The damage is calculated from the stress tensor data and the cumulative plastic deformation at all times ${t}_{i}$ (provided by the user).

The material characteristics $E$ (Young's modulus), $\nu$ (Poisson's ratio) and $S$ (material parameter) must depend on the temperature $T$. This temperature must therefore be provided by the user at the same times as $\sigma (t)$ and $p(t)$.

## 1 Reference problem

The damage, $D(t)$ , is calculated from the stress tensor data, $\sigma (t)$ , and the cumulative plastic deformation, $p(t)$ .

| $\dot{D}=\frac{1}{{(1-D)}^{\mathrm{2s}}}{\left[\frac{1}{\mathrm{3ES}}(1+\nu ){\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{2}+\frac{3}{\mathrm{2ES}}(1-2\nu ){\sigma}_{H}^{2}\right]}^{s}\dot{p}$ | si $p>{p}_{d}$ |
| --- | --- |
| $D=0$ | if not |

${\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}$ is the equivalent constraint of von Mises

${\sigma}_{H}$ is the hydrostatic stress

${p}_{d}$ represents the damage threshold

$S$ is a material characteristic ($\mathrm{MPa}$ )

$s$ is a material characteristic
