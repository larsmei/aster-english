# v2.03.120 SDLS120 – 2D cracked plate subjected to loading in Mode I. Validation of modal calculation with X-FEM

**Summary:**

This test case validates the modal calculation for a 2D plate with a crack introduced by the X-FEM method. Among other things, the RIGI_MECA, MASS_MECA and RIGI_GEOM calculation options newly developed for a X-FEM model are valid. The plate has a horizontal and straight opening crack, boundary conditions clamped support are applied to one edge and one pressure loading to the other to open the crack in $I$ mode.

The reference solution, which is the subject of Modelling A, is calculated by *Code_Aster* using the similar model with the gridded crack in a classic way. The first 8 eigenmodes are calculated and their eigenfrequencies are compared.

The operation of post-processing commands for viewing a mode_meca result calculated with a X-FEM model is also tested.

Modelling C is a copy of Modelling A with C_PLAN Modelling instead of D_PLAN. It is intended to test the RIGI_GEOM option with elements C_PLAN.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The structure is a 2D plate of dimensions $\mathrm{LX}=10m$ and $\mathrm{LY}=30m$ , with a straight and horizontal cracking $a=5m$ length , mid-height (see [Figure 1.1-a]).

To obtain the reference solution, the problem is treated by the conventional method of the finished elements and the crack is meshed. However, for the validation of the modal calculation with the X-FEM method, the crack is not meshed, and the geometry is actually a healthy plate without cracks. The crack will then be introduced by level set functions directly into the command file using the DEFI_FISS_XFEM [U4.82.08](doc:u4.82.08) operator.

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus: $E=205000\mathrm{MPa}$ (unless otherwise stated)

Poisson's ratio : $\nu =0.0$

Density: $\rho =7800\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The plate is clamped on its lower edge and subjected to a uniform $P=10\mathrm{Mpa}$ pressure on its upper edge to open the crack (see Figure3.2-a).

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is calculated by Modelling A (see next chapter) with the conventional method of elements finished by considering the mesh crack.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

In this Modelling, the frame of the flat deformations (D_PLAN) is considered, the crack is meshed, and the conventional method of the finished elements is used to perform the calculation. This Modelling will serve as a reference and will allow comparison with the X-FEM method.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a regular mesh composed of $30\times 50$ QUAD4, respectively, along the $x,y$ axes (see [Figure 3.2‐a]). The crack lips are generated by the two edges superimposed at mid-height of the plate (heighter stroke on the figure).

### 4.5 Remarks

As can be seen in the comparison of the results for this model, differences are obtained between the eigenfrequencies calculated with the X-FEM model and those calculated with the classical model. These differences are normal, since the mass, elastic rigidity and geometric contribution of rigidity are calculated differently for the elements X-FEM. These are partitioned in triangles on which 12 point integration patterns are considered. However, for the classic elements the 4-point diagram is used. For some of the eigenmodes (especially mode 5 here) the difference is even greater, because in this case the contribution of geometric rigidity plays a more important role. As for the reference solution the mesh is quite coarse around the crack tip, the stress field is not calculated with the same accuracy as in the X-FEM case, where special enrichment functions allow a more accurate assessment. For the modal distortions, there is (see Figure 4.5-a) a very good correlation between the results from the classical calculation and those from the X-X-X calculation.

## 5 Modelling C

Copying the A Modelling with elements C_PLAN to test the RIGI_GEOM option on these elements. Produces the same results as Modelling A.

## 6 Summary of the results

This test case allowed the validation of the modal calculation of a 2D structure with a crack introduced by the X-FEM method. The comparison of the results considered here, the eigenfrequencies of the first 8 modes, shows a good agreement between the "classical" calculation where the crack is meshed and the calculation with X-FEM.
