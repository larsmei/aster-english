# v3.01.017 SSLL17- Subtended beam

**Summary:**

The linear elasticity static test is used to validate straight beam elements POU_D_T and BARRE.

This test case concerns a set of beams and bars subject to distributed loading. The lower membrane bar is pre-voltageed by a device allowing elongation.

The axial effort, beam moment and displacement are tested by comparing simulation results with reference solution AFNOR (ref. [1]).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Loading

> The load is shown in the following figure:

![Figure](/figures/v3.01.017/1000000000000FA0000008CA4E9A31E1F8E89AD5.png)

**Figure** 1.1-1

The AB beam has a total length of 8m. AC, CD, CE, EF and EB bars work only in traction and compression. In the middle of the CE bar, a device allows a shortening between two points C and E, which is translated by elongation of the left and right parts of the CE bar of $\delta$ = 6.52mm. The A support is fixed while the B support is mobile.

### 1.2 Restraints

- Supports A (DX=DY=DZ=0, KRX=KRY=KRZ=0.0001 N.m/rad)
- B support (KY = 1 x 109 N/m)
