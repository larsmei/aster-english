# v2.03.116 SDLS116 – Transient loading plate, processed in explicit dynamic

**Summary:**

This test validates DKT Modelling for a transient calculation with an explicit numerical integration scheme by the DYNA_NON_LINE operator and with the diagonized mass matrix (MASS_MECA_DIAG). The square plate is required by a linear force on one side and linear in time. The results are compared with those obtained by the *EUROPLEXUS* software.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.116/10000E68000069D5000024612FE9DC384805F819.svg)

Square plate **:**

Length: $l=1.0m$

Thickness: $e=0.1m$

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus, $E=4.388{10}^{10}N/{m}^{2}$

Poisson's ratio, $\nu =0.0$

Density, $\rho =2500\mathrm{kg}/\mathrm{m3}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

On the $A$ side, the $x$ and $z$ moves are embedded, ${u}_{x}={u}_{z}=0.0$ , as well as the rotations, ${u}_{\mathrm{Rx}}={u}_{\mathrm{Ry}}={u}_{\mathrm{Ry}}=0.0$ . Then, the linear force is applied on the $B$ side in the $(1.0,0.0,1.0)$ direction , linear in time:

$F(t)={F}_{0}.t$

where ${F}_{0}$ is amplitude, ${F}_{0}=1.6166{10}^{5}N$.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Initially, the movements and speeds are zero everywhere.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is obtained by the software *EUROPLEXUS* (see the corresponding command file, *.epx* ).

## 4 Summary of the results

This test compares the results of a fast dynamic analysis (with the DYNA_NON_LINE operator) with those obtained with the software *EUROPLEXUS*. the difference can nevertheless reach $\text{6\%}$ on a very simple example (not much degrees of freedom and linear behavior), the test also shows a difference in the treatment for both codes.
The difference between the two codes has been expanded since the removal of the PETIT_REAC deformation for the elements DKT.
