# v7.31.121 WTNV121- Concrete Wetting with Damage Law

**Summary:**

This test validates the connection of ENDO_ISOT_BETON and MAZARS damage laws to modellings HHM. Intrinsic permeability under the keyword PERM_END is a user data in the form of a formula, a function of the damage variable. It is a test case of non-regression.

The mechanical boundary conditions are such that the displacements perpendicular to each of the facets are prevented. The anchorage consists of applying a capillary pressure on the upper side of the structure which decreases over time.

## 5 Summary of the results

This test case is an test case of non-regression whose goal is to test the connection of MAZARS and ENDO_ISOT_BETON damage laws to HHM Modelling. This test case has no ambition to compare the results of the two damaged models.
