# v7.31.127 WTNV127 – Desaturation of a porous medium without air (3D_THV Modelling)

**Summary:**

A porous medium is heated with pores filled with a mixture of water and water vapour. The initial liquid saturation is $\text{50\%}$ , the loading is a uniform thermal flux on the edges of the domain. The Modelling done by a single cubic element corresponds to the modelling of a homogeneous problem in space.

The reference solution is an analytical solution approaching.
