# v6.01.110 SSNA110 - Parameter matching with VISC_CIN2_CHAB

**Summary:**

This quasi-static non-linear mechanical test validates the parameter recalibration for VISC_CIN2_CHAB $\mathrm{2D}$ in the case of axisymmetric test piece (homogeneous stress and deformation state) subjected to a single tensile test.

Four tensile curves are used as reference curves (with different deformation speeds). The reference curves are derived from tests on $10\mathrm{CD}9-10$ steel at $545°C$ .

The 11 viscoplastic parameters of the model are simultaneously identified.

The Modelling of the test piece is done with a element $\mathrm{2D}$ (QUA4).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry is chosen voluntarily simple, to translate a state of homogeneous stresses and deformations, as is the case in uniaxial traction. It is here a volume element represented by a square on the side $\mathrm{0.01mm}$. The Modelling is axisymmetric, and the tensile is done with imposed deformation.

$\mathrm{DY}=0$ on the lower side

$\mathrm{DX}=0$ on left side

$\mathrm{DY}(t)=({\mathrm{EPS}}_{\mathrm{final}}\ast H)/\mathrm{tmax}\ast t$
