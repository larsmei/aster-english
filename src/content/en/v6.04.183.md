# v6.04.183 SSNV183 - Fluid test with VENDOCHAB

**Summary:**

The VENDOCHAB model, is based on a formulation proposed by Chaboche. It is a coupled formulation that covers an elasto-viscoplastic law with multiplicative isotropic hardening and isotropic damage kinetics. This law was originally developed to predict the life and cracking of turboreactors' blades and more generally to predict the ruin time of the structures being pressed at high temperatures.

This non-linear quasi-static mechanical test validates the VENDOCHAB $\mathrm{3D}$ model in the case of a test piece subjected to an isothermal uniaxial creep test. The stress and deformation states are homogeneous in the test piece. This test validates the explicit integration of this model. The equations of this coupled formulation are described in the [R5.03.15](doc:r5.03.15) reference booklet.

The Modelling of the test piece is done with an element $\mathrm{3D}$ at 8 nodes (HEXA8).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry is chosen voluntarily simple, to translate a homogeneous state of stress and deformation, as is the case in uniaxial creep. It is here a volume element represented by a side cube $3\mathrm{mm}$ . The Modelling is volume volume and the creep is done at stress imposed.

This corresponds to a uniaxial creep test under a constant load of $200\mathit{MPa}$.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

Analytical solution for the damage variable $D$:

$D(t)=1-{(1-(1+k){(\frac{{\sigma}_{0}}{A})}^{R}t)}^{\frac{1}{1+k}}$

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.183/Object_2.png)

Analytical solution for the viscoplastic isotropic hardening variable, $r$ , in the case of a zero ${\sigma}_{Y}$ threshold:

$r(t)={\left[\frac{(M+N)}{M(1+k-N)}{(\frac{{\sigma}_{0}}{A})}^{-R}{(\frac{{\sigma}_{0}}{K})}^{N}(1-{(1-(1+k){(\frac{{\sigma}_{0}}{A})}^{R}t)}^{\frac{1+k-N}{1+k}})\right]}^{\frac{M}{M+N}}$

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.183/Object_4.png)

In previous expressions, $D$ is the damage variable corresponding to the internal variable $\mathit{V9}$ and $r$ is the multiplicative viscoplastic hardening variable corresponding to the internal variable $\mathit{V8}$.

The following correspondence is also available in relation to the parameters of the keyword VENDOCHAB:

$N={N}_{\mathit{VP}}$

$M={M}_{\mathit{VP}}$

$K={K}_{\mathit{VP}}$

$A={A}_{D}$

$R={R}_{D}$

$k={K}_{D}$
