# u4.84.31 Command DEFI_SOL_EQUI

## 1 Purpose

Characterise the variation of the mechanical characteristics of layers of stratified soil in the course of an earthquake from their level of shear strain.

For this, one determines by an iterative procedure, for each of these soil layers, equivalent linear characteristics recalibrated from the curves of reduction of the shear modulus and of increase of the hysteretic damping as a function of the shear strain. This procedure generally relies on a model of a column of stratified soil with two different types of modelling:

- 2D in plane strain, meshed by horizontal layers, subjected to a driving acceleration with a periodicity condition between the left-hand face and the right-hand face and an absorbing condition at the base. This modelling also allows the simplified taking into account of the water pressures by the Byrne method.
- 3D, meshed by horizontal layers and subjected to a driving acceleration in the 3 directions X, Y and Z, with penalisation stiffnesses on the lateral edges in order to ensure the same dynamic behaviour in the two horizontal directions and an absorbing condition at the base.

The command DEFI_SOL_EQUI makes it possible to produce a table containing the information necessary for the description of a soil file for the software MISS3D.

## 3 Operands

### 3.1 Operand CHARGEMENT

```text
◊    CHARGEMENT =    /    'MONO_APPUI',    [DEFAUT]
                     /    'ONDE_PLANE',
```

Indicates the nature of the loading imposing the motion:

CHARGEMENT = 'MONO_APPUI': loading in the form of a single-support driving acceleration imposed on the soil column.

CHARGEMENT = 'ONDE_PLANE': loading in the form of a vertically propagating plane wave in velocity imposed on the soil column, a shear wave if the excitation motion is horizontal, a pressure wave if the excitation motion is vertical.

### 3.2 Operand FONC_SIGNAL

```text
♦     FONC_SIGNAL = foncs
```

Makes it possible to define a time function defining the acceleration signal for a 1D modelling – 1 component: a driving acceleration if the loading is single-support, or else a loading by a vertically propagating plane wave, imposed on the column model representative of the soil stratification. From this model one characterises the variation of the mechanical characteristics of layers of stratified soil as a function of their level of shear strain.

**Note 1**

It is necessary that the initial time instant of the signal be zero and that the time step $\mathit{dt}$ be constant. If one uses a number of time steps which is a power of two, then the final time will necessarily be equal to $({2}^{N}-1)\mathit{dt}$.

**Note 2**

In the case of a plane-wave loading, this option is an alternative to the supply of a table of the transient evolutions of the accelerations, and possibly of the velocities and displacements obtained by a previous call to DEFI_SOL_EQUI. This table is supplied by the keyword UNITE_TRAN_INIT.

### 3.3 Operands FONC_SIGNAL_X/FONC_SIGNAL_Y/FONC_SIGNAL_Z

```text
♦     FONC_SIGNAL _X = foncs

♦     FONC_SIGNAL _Y = foncs

♦     FONC_SIGNAL _Z = foncs
```

Makes it possible to define the time functions defining the acceleration signal for a 1D modelling – 3 components: a driving acceleration if the loading is single-support, or else a loading by a vertically propagating plane wave, imposed on the column model representative of the soil stratification.

**Note**

It is necessary that the initial time instant of the signal be zero and that the time step $\mathit{dt}$ be constant. If one uses a number of time steps which is a power of two, then the final time will necessarily be equal to $({2}^{N}-1)\mathit{dt}$.

### 3.4 Operand DSP

```text
◊    DSP =        dsp    [FONCTION]
```

The operand makes it possible to define a spectral density function (DSP) imposed on the column model representative of the soil stratification. This keyword is compatible only with a single-support loading.

If the keyword DSP is present, then one calls on the tools of the RVT (*Random Vibration Theory*) for the (de)convolution and the computation of the degraded soil profiles. The DSP equivalent to a spectrum (SRO) is obtained beforehand by CALC_FONCTION/SPEC_OSCI. This approach, which does not require a passage into the time domain for the computation of the maximum responses and of the response spectra, is also called spectrum transfer.

### 3.5 Operand LIEU_SIGNAL

```text
◊    LIEU_SIGNAL =        /    'AFFLEURANT',        [DEFAUT]
                          /    'CHAMP_LIBRE',
```

Indicates the place of imposition of the signal with respect to the soil column:

LIEU_SIGNAL = 'AFFLEURANT': signal imposed at the base of the column (at the outcropping bedrock).

LIEU_SIGNAL = 'CHAMP_LIBRE': signal imposed at the top of the column (in the free field).

### 3.6 Operand DUREE

```text
◊    DUREE =        T    [R]
```

This keyword is to be supplied only in the presence of the keyword DSP.

*Cf.*  [U4.32.04](doc:u4.32.04) for the meaning of this keyword.

### 3.7 Operand NOM_CMP

```text
◊    NOM_CMP =        /    'DX',        [DEFAUT]
                      /    'DY',
```

Indicates the direction of the signal imposed on the soil column:

NOM_CMP = 'DX': horizontal imposed signal. This is the usual case, in which one determines the variation of the mechanical characteristics of the layers of stratified soil in the course of the earthquake from their level of shear strain.

NOM_CMP = 'DY': vertical imposed signal. In this case, the mechanical characteristics of the layers of stratified soil do not vary in the course of the earthquake. The only point of interest is to supply as a result the transient evolutions and the acceleration spectra obtained at the bottom of each soil layer.

### 3.8 Operand FREQ_COUP

```text
◊    FREQ_COUP = freqc
```

Cut-off frequency making it possible to filter the input signal. By default, one takes a maximum value corresponding to half the inverse of the time step of the input signal.

### 3.9 Operand INTEGRATION

```text
◊    INTEGRATION =    /    'TEMPS',    [DEFAUT]
                      /    'FREQUENCE',
```

Makes it possible to choose the method of integration in post-processing of the motion signals in displacement or velocity. By default, one keeps the time method by the value 'TEMPS', which implements the call to the option INTEGRE of CALC_FONCTION. Otherwise one may choose the frequency method by the value 'FREQUENCE', which implements the call to the option INTEGRE_FREQ of CALC_FONCTION.

### 3.10 Operand FREQ_FILTRE

```text
◊    FREQ_FILTRE =     /    0.0 ,    [DEFAUT]
                       /    freqf
```

Frequency making it possible to filter the input signal from below if one has chosen the frequency integration method. By default, one takes a zero value, which cancels the filtering from below.

### 3.11 Operand MAILLAGE

```text
◊    MAILLAGE = ma
```

If the modelling is of type 1D – 1 component, makes it possible to define the name of the concept of the global surface mesh of the column representative of the soil stratification. A plane-strain model ('D_PLAN') will be assigned to it. If the operand is not supplied, one will fill, from the names of mesh-cell groups supplied in the operands GROUP_MA, the contents of a mesh file in 'ASTER' format printed in the file RESULTAT.

If the modelling is of type 1D – 3 components, makes it possible to define the name of the concept of the volume mesh of the column representative of the stratification of the soil, to which a 3D modelling will be assigned. In this case, this keyword is mandatory.

### 3.12 Operand GROUP_MA_COL

```text
♦    GROUP_MA_COL = gmco
```

Makes it possible to define, in the previous mesh (if it exists), the name of the surface mesh-cell group comprising the whole of the column representative of the soil stratification. If the modelling is 1D – 1 component and the previous mesh does not exist, this group will be created in the mesh file in 'ASTER' format filled by the operator.

### 3.13 Operands GROUP_MA_DROITE/GROUP_MA_GAUCHE

```text
♦    GROUP_MA_DROITE = gmda

♦    GROUP_MA_GAUCHE = gmga
```

For a 1D – 1 component simulation, make it possible to define respectively, in the previous mesh (if it exists), the names of the line mesh-cell groups to which a periodicity relation will be assigned between the right-hand edge and the left-hand edge of the column representative of the soil stratification. If the previous mesh does not exist, these groups will be created in the mesh file in 'ASTER' format filled by the operator.

For a 1D – 3 component simulation, make it possible to define respectively, in the previous mesh, the names of the surface mesh-cell groups on the facing faces of the column to which a periodicity relation will be assigned.

### 3.14 Operands GROUP_MA_ARRETE_1 and GROUP_MA_ARRETE_2

```text
♦    GROUP_MA_ARRETE_1= gmda

♦    GROUP_MA_ARRETE_2= gmda
```

Only for a 1D – 3 component simulation, makes it possible to define the name of the line mesh-cell groups defining the edges over the whole length of the column, both belonging to the same face defined either by GROUP_MA_GAUCHE or by GROUP_MA_DROITE, to which a periodicity relation will be assigned.

### 3.15 Operand GROUP_MA_SUBSTR

```text
♦    GROUP_MA_SUBSTR = gmdr
```

For a 1D – 1 component modelling, makes it possible to define, in the previous mesh (if it exists), the name of the line mesh-cell group to which an absorbing-boundary modelling will be assigned. If the previous mesh does not exist, this group will be created in the mesh file in 'ASTER' format filled by the operator.

For a 1D – 3 component modelling, makes it possible to define, in the previous mesh, the name of the surface mesh-cell group to which an absorbing-boundary modelling will be assigned.

### 3.16 Operand CORRECTION

```text
◊    CORRECTION =    /    'SANS',    [DEFAUT]
                     /    'BYRNE',
```

For a 1D – 1 component modelling, makes it possible to activate the taking into account of the water pressures in a simplified manner by the Byrne model. In this case, the evaluation of the degraded value of the shear modulus is corrected by a factor depending on the rise of effective water pressure computed.

### 3.17 Operand COEF_KSI

```text
◊     COEF_KSI         = / 0.666666667         [ DEFAUT ]
                         /coef_ksi,        [R]
```

Parameter making it possible to define the value of the weighting coefficient which makes it possible to modulate the impact of ${r}_{\mathit{umax}}$ on the effective strain for a 1D – 1 component modelling with the Byrne model.

### 3.18 Operand SURF

```text
◊    SURF =    /    'NON',    [DEFAUT]
               /    'OUI',
```

Indicates whether or not one wishes to have certain layers of the soil modelled in the column above an embedded foundation, which one finds in the soil table usable by MISS3D:

SURF = 'OUI': shallow foundation with respect to the soil layers.

SURF = 'NON': embedment of the foundation in the soil.

### 3.19 Operand NIVE_COUCH_ENFO

```text
◊    NIVE_COUCH_ENFO = ncoue
```

If SURF = 'NON', this keyword becomes mandatory: it then gives the number of soil layers above the embedded foundation.

### 3.20 Operand NB_RECEPTEUR

```text
◊    NB_RECEPTEUR =    /    2,    [DEFAUT]
                       /    4,
```

This keyword has a meaning only if SURF = 'NON': it makes it possible to define the number of receivers per layer of element embedded in the stratified soil: it also corresponds to the number of sub-layers generated for each layer of embedded element in the soil table produced.

### 3.21 Keyword MATERIAU

```text
♦     MATERIAU = _F (
```

Repeatable factor keyword allowing the description of the characteristics of families of non-linear materials, material by material, defined by curves of reduction of the shear modulus and of increase of the hysteretic damping as a function of the shear strain. These materials constitute groups of layers of the stratified soil. Each group is generally associated with a geological zone (clay, rock...).

#### 3.21.1 Operand GAMMA

```text
♦    GAMMA = lgamma
```

Makes it possible to define the ordered increasing list of reals of the abscissae of the shear strain for each family of non-linear material defined in the column.

#### 3.21.2 Operands G_GMAX/D

```text
♦    G_GMAX = lggma,

♦    D = lisd,
```

Make it possible to define respectively the lists of real values of reduction of the shear modulus and of increase of the internal damping D, half of the hysteretic damping, as a function of the shear strain for each current non-linear material. Each of the terms of these lists corresponds to a value of the list supplied by the operand GAMMA. All these lists must therefore have the same cardinality for the same material.

**Note 1**

It is necessary to define a non-linear material for the last soil layer corresponding to the substratum, which will have, for the operand G_GMAX, constant values of 1 (no reduction) and, for the operand D, constant values of the half-value of the hysteretic damping of the substratum.

**Note 2**

The functions thus created are extended to the left and to the right with the option 'CONSTANT' and interpolated with an option ('LOG','LIN').

**Note 3**

If the lists of the abscissae of the shear strain of the families of non-linear material differ from one another, one will use as a common reference list, in order to define by interpolation the description of the characteristics of the non-linear materials, material by material, the list of the non-linear material for the last soil layer corresponding to the substratum. One may nevertheless force the supply of the common reference list by the operand LIST_EPSI.

### 3.22 Operand LIST_EPSI

```text
◊    LIST_EPSI = lepsi
```

Makes it possible to force the supply of the common reference list of the abscissae of the shear strain for interpolation of the characteristics of the families of non-linear material.

### 3.23 Keyword COUCHE

```text
♦    COUCHE = _F (
```

Repeatable factor keyword allowing the geometrical description of the layers of a stratified soil, that of the characteristics of the materials constituting these layers, and the assignment, layer by layer, of the non-linear materials defined previously by the keyword MATERIAU.

#### 3.23.1 Operands EPAIS/E/NU/RHO/AMOR_HYST/N1

Make it possible to define respectively the real values of the thickness, of Young's modulus, of Poisson's ratio, of the density and of the initial hysteretic damping of each layer; as a reminder, twice the value of the internal damping D (to be supplied in absolute value and not as a percentage). For the modelling with the Byrne model, the user must also supply the value ${N1}_{60}$ of the SPT of the layer. This value is used to obtain the parameters of the Byrne model via the following expression:

${C}_{1}=\frac{8,7}{{({N}_{1})}_{60}^{1,25}}$

${C}_{2}=\frac{0,4}{{C}_{1}}$

**Note**

A special treatment is applied so that the value N1=0 supplies the same result as for a modelling CORRECTION = 'SANS' (cf. test case zzzz412e).

#### 3.23.2 Operand NUME_MATE

```text
♦    NUME_MATE = numat
```

Number of the non-linear material in the order of description of the occurrences of the keyword MATERIAU to be assigned to the current layer.

#### 3.23.3 Operand GROUP_MA

```text
♦    GROUP_MA = gmaco
```

Makes it possible to define the name of the surface mesh-cell group of the current layer. The last occurrence corresponds to a substratum layer. If the latter is not meshed in the column model, then one assigns to it the name of the line mesh-cell group of the substratum already supplied by the keyword GROUP_MA_SUBSTR. If the operand MAILLAGE is not supplied, this group will be created in the mesh file in 'ASTER' format filled by the operator.

### 3.24 Operand COEF_VARI_MATE

```text
◊    COEF_VARI_MATE =    /    1.0,        [DEFAUT]
                         /    coevm
```

Gives a global amplification factor of Young's moduli defined initially in the keyword COUCHE. Besides the default unit value, one also generally uses in sizing studies the values 1.5 and 0.67.

### 3.25 Operand COEF_AMPL_ACCE

```text
◊    COEF_AMPL_ACCE =    /    1.0,        [DEFAUT]
                         /    coeaa
```

Gives an amplification factor of the acceleration signal defined initially by the operand FONC_SIGNAL.

### 3.26 Operand COEF_GAMMA

```text
◊    COEF_GAMMA    =    /    0.65,    [DEFAUT]
                        /    coega
```

Gives a factor, less than 1, used to compute the effective strain from the maximum strain obtained in each of the soil layers during the signal. The value obtained will give the level from which one will determine the variation of the characteristics of the non-linear material constituting the layer, by reading on the curves given by the operands D and G_GAMMA of the keyword MATERIAU.

### 3.27 Operand CORR_AMOR

```text
◊    CORR_AMOR =    /    'NON',    [DEFAUT]
                    /    'OUI',
```

Indicates whether or not one wishes to correct the datum of the hysteretic damping given by material by introducing a new formulation of the shear modulus G* which corrects the real modulus G by the formulation of LYSMER, namely that G* is written:

${G}^{\text{*}}=G[(1-{2\xi }^{2})+2\xi j\sqrt{1-{\xi}^{2}}]$

This formulation makes it possible to obtain the equality between the real value of G and the modulus of the complex G*. This is done by adding the keyword CORR_AMOR equal to 'OUI'.

### 3.28 Operand LIST_FREQ

Allows the user to introduce a list of frequencies for the computation of the harmonic evolution launched by the operator. By default, the operator imposes one bounded by the cut-off frequency (operand FREQ_COUP).

### 3.29 Operand LIST_FREQ_SPEC_OSCI

Allows the user to introduce a list of frequencies different from the one used by default in the computation of the oscillator response spectra (or SRO) by the option SPEC_OSCI of CALC_FONCTION.

### 3.30 Operands NMAX_ITER/RESI_RELA

Give the parameters of the iterative process of variation of the characteristics of the materials constituting the layers. Respectively the maximum number of iterations and the stopping criterion on the maximum relative variation, between two successive iterations, of Young's modulus over the set of soil layers. “Iteration” here has the meaning “analysis stage”. At least some are needed in order to compute the possible degradations and to establish the stopping criterion with respect to the initial state.

Therefore NMAX_ITER is greater than or equal to 1.

One may then give directly tables of values resulting from an initial definition by this keyword. ### 3.33 Operand UNITE_TABLE_RESU

Supplies the logical unit for writing the table containing the result of the iterative process of DEFI_SOL_EQUI, including the evolution in the course of the analysis iterations of the characteristics of the materials constituting the stratified soil layers. One also prints, once convergence has been reached, the variation with depth, that is layer by layer, from top to bottom, of the maximum accelerations and strains in the course of time.

### 3.34 Operand SEPARATEUR

Makes it possible to define the separator character (blank by default) making it possible to space the columns of the results constituting the table entered by the operand UNITE_TABLE_RESU containing the result of the iterative process of DEFI_SOL_EQUI. This operand plays the same role as in the operators LIRE_TABLE or IMPR_TABLE.

### 3.35 Operand UNITE_RESU_TRAN

Supplies the logical unit for writing, in 'TABLEAU' format, the transient evolutions of the accelerations, then of the strains and then of the shear stresses, obtained at the end of the iterative process of DEFI_SOL_EQUI. These evolutions are printed, column by column, successively: in the free field, at the outcropping bedrock, then at the bottom of each layer of stratified soil, from top to bottom.

### 3.36 Operand UNITE_RESU_SPEC

Supplies the logical unit for writing, in 'TABLEAU' format, the frequency evolutions of the response spectra (SRO) in acceleration obtained at the end of the iterative process of DEFI_SOL_EQUI. The spectra are computed for a damping level of 5% and normalised by 9.81 m/s2. These evolutions are printed, column by column, successively: in the free field, at the outcropping bedrock, then at the bottom of each layer of stratified soil, from top to bottom.

### 3.37 Operand UNITE_RESU_DSP

Supplies the logical unit for writing, in 'TABLEAU' format, the spectral densities (DSP) in acceleration obtained at the end of the iterative process of DEFI_SOL_EQUI. These evolutions are printed, column by column, successively: in the free field, at the outcropping bedrock, then at the bottom of each layer of stratified soil, from top to bottom.

### 3.38 Operand TOUT_CHAM

Indicates whether or not one prints, in the file given by UNITE_RESU_*, the transient evolutions of the fields 'VITE' and 'DEPL' as well as the nodal-force fields 'FORC_NODA' at each of the levels of the column of stratified soil. This is necessary in particular for a later use by the operator DEFI_CHAR_SOL [U4.84.32](doc:u4.84.32).

**Note**

It is strongly advised, in successive calls to DEFI_SOL_EQUI, to distinguish the logical-unit numbers of UNITE_RESU_xxx because, by using the same numbers, one risks creating shifts in the table number to be read, especially when one has used the option TOUT_CHAM='OUI'.

### 3.39 Operand TOUT_ACCE

```text
◊    TOUT_ACCE =    /    'OUI',    [DEFAUT]
                    /    'NON',
```

Makes it possible to restrict the printout in the file given by UNITE_RESU_TRAN to the acceleration evolutions only, at the following points of the soil column: free field, outcropping bedrock, borehole if TOUT_ACCE='NON'. Use the keyword ACCE_LIST in order to obtain the acceleration signals at other positions in the soil column.

### 3.40 Operand ACCE_LIST

```text
◊    ACCE_LIST =    /    alist,
```

Together with the use of TOUT_ACCE='NON', makes it possible to supply the list of additional GROUP_MA for printout of the acceleration signals in the file UNITE_RESU_TRAN.

**Note 1**

The signals obtained for each element defined in the list alist are positioned at the lower nodes of the mesh-cell groups of the layers of the soil column (whose name has been defined either by the operand GROUP_MA of the keyword couche, or via column M of the table supplied as input via the keyword TABLE_MATER_ELAS).

**Note 2**

The command ignores the terms of the list which are not equal to a layer name.

### 3.41 Operand LIAISON

```text
◊    LIAISON =    /    'PERIODIQUE',
                  /    'SANS',
```

Indicates, in the case of a plane-wave loading, whether the periodicity relation between the right-hand edge and the left-hand edge of the column representative of the soil stratification is active. If the modelling is of type 1D – 3 components, one must imperatively set LIAISON ='PERIODIQUE'.

### 3.42 Operand LONG_CARA

Indicates, in the case of a plane-wave loading, the value of the characteristic length defined in DEFI_MATERIAU, keyword ELAS, making it possible to compute the added stiffness on the absorbing boundary constituted by the right-hand and left-hand edges of the soil column.

### 3.43 Operand UNITE_TRAN_INIT

In the case of a plane-wave loading and of a 1D – 1 component modelling, supplies the logical unit for writing, in 'TABLEAU' format, the transient evolutions at each of the levels of the column of stratified soil obtained during a previous call to DEFI_SOL_EQUI. During this previous call, the evolutions are created by means of the keyword UNITE_RESU_TRAN.

### 3.44 Operand MASS_PENA

Indicates, in the case of a plane-wave loading and of a supply of input signals by UNITE_TRAN_INIT, the value of penalised mass assigned at each point of the edges of the soil column in order to impose the acceleration evolutions at these points.

### 3.45 Operand TITRE

```text
◊    TITRE = titre
```

Allows the user to define a title for the soil table.

### 3.46 Operand INFO

```text
◊    INFO =    /   1,    [DEFAUT]
               /   2,
```

Indicates a printout level for information in the file "MESSAGE":

INFO = 1: no printout

INFO = 2: printout of the soil table
