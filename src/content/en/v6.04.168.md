# v6.04.168 SSNV168 – Triaxial drained test with softening DRUCK_PRAGER behavior

**Summary:**

This test case allows you to simulate a drained triaxial test on four different modellingss when calculating nonlinearly. This allows you to highlight the effect of the negative nut type in the case of AXIS or 3D models.

Modelling A:

- constitutive law "DRUCK_PRAGER" with linear negative nutring for $2\mathit{MPa}$ containment.
- AXIS model with mesh cells QUAD4.

Modelling B:

- constitutive law "DRUCK_PRAGER" with parabolic negative nutting for $2\mathrm{MPa}$ containment.
- AXIS Modelling with mesh cells QUAD4.

Modelization C:

- constitutive law "DRUCK_PRAGER" with linear negative nutting for $2\mathrm{MPa}$ containment.
- 3D Modelling with mesh cells HEXA20.

Modelling D:

- constitutive law "DRUCK_PRAGER" with parabolic negative nutting for $2\mathrm{MPa}$ containment.
- 3D Modelling with mesh cells HEXA20.

Modelling E:

- constitutive law "DRUCK_PRAG_N_A" with linear, parabolic and exponential nutting for $2\mathrm{MPa}$ containment
- AXIS model with mesh cells QUAD4.

Modelling F:

- constitutive law "DRUCK_PRAG_N_A" with linear, parabolic and exponential nutting for $2\mathrm{MPa}$ containment
- 3D model with mesh cells HEXA20.

Modelling G:

- constitutive law "DRUCK_PRAG_N_A" with linear, parabolic and exponential nutting for $2\mathrm{MPa}$ containment
- AXIS_HHO model with mesh cells QUAD4.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

#### 2.1.1 Moving $\mathrm{DY}$

The $\mathrm{DY}$ displacement reference to point $A$ , corresponds to the required displacement.

#### 2.1.2 Constraint $\mathrm{SIXX}$

The $\mathrm{SIXX}$ constraint is the load applied.

#### 2.1.3 $\mathit{SIYY}$ stress and cumulative plastic deformation $V1$

Comparison with an analytical solution.

Triaxial calculation in drained conditions with DRUCK_PRAGER or DRUCK_PRAG_N_A law. Calculation is performed for DRUCK_PRAG_N_A law (most general case) and solution can be extended to DRUCK_PRAGER case by taking $\beta (p)=\alpha =\mathit{constante}$ .

![Figure](/figures/v6.04.168/Shape4.gif)

${\sigma}_{\mathit{eq}}+\beta (p)\mathit{tr}(\sigma )-R(p)=0$

We're imposing

> ${\sigma}_{xx}={\sigma}_{yy}=-2\mathit{MPa}={\sigma}^{0}$
> ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{\frac{3}{2}}{S}_{\mathrm{II}}$
> $S=\left(\begin{array}{c}{\sigma}_{xx}-\frac{1}{3}\mathit{tr}\sigma \\ {\sigma}_{yy}-\frac{1}{3}\mathit{tr}\sigma \\ {\sigma}_{zz}-\frac{1}{3}\mathit{tr}\sigma \end{array}\right)$ with $\mathit{tr}\sigma ={\sigma}_{xx}+{\sigma}_{yy}+{\sigma}_{zz}={\sigma}_{zz}+2{\sigma}^{0}$
> $S=\frac{1}{3}\left(\begin{array}{c}-{\sigma}_{zz}+{\sigma}^{0}\\ -{\sigma}_{zz}+{\sigma}^{0}\\ 2{\sigma}_{zz}-2{\sigma}^{0}\end{array}\right)$
> ${S}_{\mathit{II}}=S.S=\frac{1}{3}\sqrt{2{({\sigma}_{zz}-{\sigma}^{0})}^{2}+{(2{\sigma}_{zz}-2{\sigma}^{0})}^{2}}$
> ${\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}=\sqrt{(\frac{3}{2})}{S}_{\mathrm{II}}$

which gives us ${\sigma}_{\mathit{eq}}=\left|({\sigma}_{zz}-{\sigma}^{0})\right|={\sigma}^{0}-{\sigma}_{zz}$

So, in plastic mode: ${\sigma}_{zz}=\frac{1}{\alpha -1}(R(p)-(1+2\alpha {\sigma}^{0}))$ (1)

We also have $\dot{{{\varepsilon}}^{P}}=\lambda \left(\frac{3}{2}\frac{S}{{\sigma}_{\mathit{eq}}}+\beta (p)I\right)=\dot{p}\left(\left(\begin{array}{c}1/2\\ 1/2\\ -1\end{array}\right)+\beta (p)I\right)$

By noting $B(p)$ the primitive of $\beta (p)$ sssannuating in 0, it comes:

${{\varepsilon}}^{P}=p\left(\begin{array}{c}1/2\\ 1/2\\ -1\end{array}\right)+B(p)I$

The stress-deformation relationship gives:

${{\varepsilon}}_{zz}^{e}=\frac{1}{E}{\sigma}_{zz}-\frac{2\nu }{E}{\sigma}^{0}-\frac{1-2\nu }{E}{\sigma}^{0}$

Using ${\sigma}_{zz}$ expression, we then obtain:

${{\varepsilon}}_{zz}=\frac{1}{E(a-1)}(R(p)-3a{\sigma}^{0})-p+B(p)$ (2)

The expressions (1) and (2) give a parameterization of the stress response deformation as a function of the $p$ hardening variable.

The following are the expressions of $B(p)$ ($\beta (p)$ primer ss-annuating in 0), depending on the type of hardening and constitutive law:

- DRUCK_PRAGER, for any nutting:

$B(p)=\alpha p$

- DRUCK_PRAG_N_A, linear and parabolic nutring:

$B(p)=\beta (1-\frac{p}{2{p}_{\mathit{ultm}}})p$

- DRUCK_PRAG_N_A, exponential hardening:

$B(p)=\beta {p}_{c}(1-\exp(\frac{-p}{{p}_{c}}))$
