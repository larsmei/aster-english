# v6.06.103 SSNX103 - SP_* behaviors for elements 3D_INTERF_POU

**Summary:**

This test implements a simulation of a load path in effort and relative displacements of the Gauss points of a element 3D_INTERF_POU [R7.01.49](doc:r7.01.49). The load is chosen so that it is identical at each of the Gauss points. The outputs of the digital integration are checked at the scale of the Gauss point. The effort and the tangent matrix returned by the constitutive law are checked. The law is only verified in 3D, because it is only compatible with the element 3D_INTERF_POU.

Two constitutive laws are tested. The first is the elasto-plastic law with linear kinematic nutring INTERF_POU_CINE [R7.01.50](doc:r7.01.50), and the second is its purely elastic INTERF_POU_ELAS version.

- Modelling A: This Modelling allows to validate the INTERF_POU_CINE model in 3D.
- Modelling B: This Modelling allows to validate the INTERF_POU_ELAS model in 3D.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Constitutive laws is incompatible with hardware point simulation as proposed by SIMU_POINT_MAT[U4.51.12] macro-command. Law verification requires finite element 3D_INTERF_POU support.

This element modeled the interaction between a beam element, representing a reinforcement section, with a element HEXA8, representing the surrounding matrix. The domain of interaction considered (l-interface) is a line confused with the field of beam. L-L-element 3D_INTERF_POU incorporates into its Gauss points the phenomenological law INTERF_POU_CINE returning the interaction efforts between matrix and reinforcement according to their relative displacement, carried by three components.

The element 3D_INTERF_POU is carried by an mesh cell HEXA27. In this test case, this mesh cell is cubic on the side 2. The section of the reinforcement is considered square on the side 1. The objective, in fact, is not to test the element 1, but the law.

The beam is oriented along the X axis and its cross section at an arbitrary $- \frac{\pi}{3}$ spindle angle to verify the correct definition of the load surface.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material characteristics are chosen arbitrarily:

- $K_N = 5 \, N/m^3$ ,
- $K_T = 3 \, N/m^3$ ,
- $F_{NY} = K_N \, N/m^2$ ,
- $F_{TY} = K_T \, N/m^2$ ,
- $G_N = \frac{K_N}{3} \, N/m^3$ ,
- $G_T = \frac{K_T}{3} \, N/m^3$ ,

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 Application of solicitations

The nodes of the matrix part is blocked in all directions. The nodes of the beam is blocked in all directions (including rotations) except on DY.

In this direction nodal forces are applied, the same for the two nodes of the beam.

#### 1.3.2 Evolution of loading

The load evolution follows a course on 4 segments [O-A-B-C-D] 2. The loading points correspond to different states of the material, beginning at rest (O):

1. reached the yield limit, then
2. hardening until a certain loading value is reached
3. Elastic discharge until the yield limit is reached in the opposite direction, then
4. hardening until another loading value is reached

The values of the load applied are described in the table below:

| Point loading | Nodal force FY applied | Commentary |

| B | $- 1,25 \times F_{NY}$ | kinematic hardening |

| D | $1,0 \times F_{NY}$ | hardening |

### 1.4 Initial conditions

No stress and deformation.

### 1.5 Quantities verified

The loading is uniform with regard to the Gauss points, so all of these are the same values.

The quantities verified are:

- relative displacement

### 2.1 General aspects

#### 2.1.1 Interaction pressure

$F_{nd}$ is the nodal force applied to the two nodes of the beam in the direction of interest, $\sigma$ is the interaction pressure returned by the constitutive law, $L = 2$ is the length of the interface, and $B = 1$ is the thickness in the direction of interest. The sum of the forces applied to the nodes of the beam in the direction of interest is therefore:

$$
\begin{align}
                            & \sum_{1}^{2} F_{nd}  = \int_{L} - B \sigma \, dL    \\
    \Leftrightarrow \quad   & 2 F_{nd}             = - L B \sigma                 \\
    \Leftrightarrow \quad   & \boxed{ F_{nd}       = - \sigma }
\end{align}
$$
