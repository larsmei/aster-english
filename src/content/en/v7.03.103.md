# v7.03.103 HPLV103 - Calculation of KI and 3D thermo-elastic G for circular cracking

**Summary**

This is a mechanical test of the break in thermo-mechanical for a three-dimensional problem. A circular crack plunged into a thermo-elastic medium is considered. A uniform temperature is imposed on the lips of the crack. This test allows to calculate the $G$ energy return rate and the stress intensity factor ${K}_{I}$ at different points of the crack bottom.

The interest of the test is the invariance of $G$ and ${K}_{I}$ in different crowns and comparison to a analytical solution.

### 3.4 Remarks

- The reference value is the local energy release rate value: ${G}_{\mathrm{réf}}=5.66{10}^{-8}J/{m}^{2}$ .
- The results of the local $G$ are given only for $A$ and $B$ points, respectively, on a symmetry plane and in the middle of the crack front. The results for the point $B$ (mid-front) show an difference of approximately $\text{3\%}$ relative to the reference result. The results for the point $A$ are less good (the difference is between $\text{3\%}$ and $\text{13.5\%}$), which is a common finding for the estimation of the local $G$ for points on a symmetry plane.

## 4 Summary of the results

- The discretization LEGENDRE leads to the most accurate results for the local $G$ values on this test case. For the calculation of the local $K$, it is recommended that the linear discretization be done.
- The accuracy of the local ${K}_{I}$ calculation is satisfactory, with the difference being limited to $\text{2.3\%}$.
