# v3.04.315 SSLV315 – Unflattering crack propagation with X-FEM

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to compare SIMPLEXE, mesh, UPWIND and GEOMETRIQUE propagation methods for $\mathrm{3D}$ cracks in mixed mode.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.315/10000000000001980000024F439DF0AEACD0BF2B.jpg)

**Figure** 1.1-a **: geometry of cracked plate**

Geometrical dimensions of the cracked plate:

| width | $L=8m$ |
| --- | --- |
| thickness | $E=1m$ |
| height | $H=18m$ |

The crack is initially oriented to $45°$ , the front being initially positioned in $(x,2,9)$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus $E=205000\mathrm{MPa}$

Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$

For modellings with cohesive elements, the material parameters are:

Critical stress ${\sigma}_{c}=2\mathit{MPa}$

Energy tenacity ${G}_{c}=17.75{\mathit{N.m}}^{-1}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

boundary conditions:

> Point $P$ : $\Delta X=\Delta Y=\Delta Z=0$
> Points on the $\mathrm{AB}$ segment: $\Delta X=\Delta Z=0$
> Surface points $\text{INF}$ : $\Delta Z=0$

Loading:

> Surface pressure $\text{SUP}$: $P=-1\mathrm{MPa}$

## 2 reference solution

The results of Modelling B (mesh method) are taken as a reference.

For modellings B, C, G and H, the non-regression of the code is checked against the position of the crack bottom.

For modellings A, D, E, F, I and J **,** it is verified that the nodes closest to the crack bottom trace on the $(1,y,z)$ plane at the last moment of propagation have their level-set very close to zero.

| **Instant of propagation** | **Nœud** | **Coordinate** ${y}_{i}$ | **Coordinate** ${z}_{i}$ |

These nodes are those included in a capture radius of the largest edge of an element, centered on the trace of the crack bottom on the $(1,x,y)$ plane.

These nodes are identified in the message file ( $.\mathrm{mess}$ ) of model B and the value of their level-sets is estimated in the modellings A, D, E, F, I and J.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

SIMPLEXE is used by PROPA_FISS.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a mesh composed of 6720 elements HEXA8 (see ).

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.315/10000201000001230000031A91CF748FC10B399D.png)

**Figure** 3.2-a **: mesh of the structure**

The mesh is not very refined to reduce the calculation time. The size of the elements is uniform and equal to $0.29\times 0.33\times 0.25m$.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The normal ($\mathrm{LSN}$) and tangent ($\mathrm{LST}$) level set is extracted using the POST_RELEVE_T operator and the maximum and minimum values remain within the capture circle of the nodes tested around the crack front, i.e. $2/3$:

| $\mathrm{Propag.}i$ | $\mathrm{Max}{\mathrm{LSN}}_{i}$ | $\mathrm{Min}{\mathrm{LSN}}_{i}$ | $\mathrm{Max}{\mathrm{LST}}_{i}$ | $\mathrm{Min}{\mathrm{LST}}_{i}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 0.233 | 0.40 | 0.33 | 0.28 |

The results show that the level-set remains below the radius in which the crack front was located, which means that the crack bottom was correctly located by the SIMPLEXE method.

## 8 Modelling G

### 8.1 Modelling characteristics

The purpose of this Modelling is to validate the entire propagation procedure *with cohesive elements* in a global manner. The GEOMETRIQUE method is therefore used by PROPA_FISS, alternatively with DETEC_COHESIF and PROPA_COHESIF operations, to update the tangent level-set (and hence the propagation front) and normal level-set (and thus the "possible" cracking surface), respectively. The bifurcation angle is determined by CALC_G (in FEM, mesh crack), and the cohesive elements are introduced into the model by the DEFI_CONTACT command.

### 8.2 Mesh characteristics

An unstructured mesh is used, with the initial crack meshed. It has 16632 elements of the TETRA4 type.

### 8.3 Tested quantities and results

Two steps of propagation are performed and the position of the propagation front is extracted after this operation. Validation consists of a non-regression test on the position of this front. To reduce the number of tests, the maximum and minimum $Y$ and $Z$ coordinates are tested along the front.

| $\mathit{Propag.}i$ | $\mathit{Max}(Y)$ | $\mathit{Min}(Y)$ | $\mathit{Max}(Z)$ | $\mathit{Min}(Z)$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 3.14 | 3.05 | 9.04 | 9.03 |

## 10 Modelling I

### 10.1 Modelling characteristics

GEOMETRIQUE is used by PROPA_FISS, with CRIT_ANGL_BIFURCATION = 'SITT_MAX_DEVER'. The connections and spill angles are calculated taking into account the value of KIII. The spill rotation is activated.

### 10.2 Mesh characteristics

The same mesh is used as model A.

### 10.3 Tested quantities and results

The normal ($\mathrm{LSN}$) and tangent ($\mathrm{LST}$) level set is extracted using the POST_RELEVE_T operator and the maximum and minimum values remain within the capture circle of the nodes tested around the crack front, i.e. $2/3$:

| $\mathrm{Propag.}i$ | $\mathrm{Max}{\mathrm{LSN}}_{i}$ | $\mathrm{Min}{\mathrm{LSN}}_{i}$ | $\mathrm{Max}{\mathrm{LST}}_{i}$ | $\mathrm{Min}{\mathrm{LST}}_{i}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 0.237 | 0.397 | 0.317 | 0.274 |

The results show that the level-set remains below the radius in which the crack front was located. This means that the crack bottom was correctly located by GEOMETRIQUE method with KIII and spill rotation.

## 12 Summary of the results

All propagation methods used (SIMPLEXE, mesh, UPWIND ET GEOMETRIQUE) by the PROPA_FISS operator were able to calculate the position of a crack propagating in mixed mode in a $\mathrm{3D}$ structure.
