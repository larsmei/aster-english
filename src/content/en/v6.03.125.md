# v6.03.125 SSNP125 - test case for the validation of the INDL_ELGA option

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the INDL_ELGA location indicator by comparing the DRUCK-PRAGER behaviors in its associated version (see R7.01.16 doc). The Modelling is D_PLAN type and takes into account a material with damage to a single mesh cell that results in a loss of cohesion of $\text{5\%}$ in the same mesh cell in question. This test is also used to validate the PDIL_ELGA elemental calculation useful for softening models associated with second gradient expansion media.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry is rectangular.

### 1.2 Material properties

For Modelling A, the mass is made of elasto-plastic material with linear negative hardening.

For Modelling B, the mass is made of elasto-plastic material with parabolic negative hardening.

The elastic parameters of the material are as follows:

- Young's modulus : $E=5800\mathrm{MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0,3$
- Actual constant density: $\rho =2764$
- Isotropic thermal expansion coefficient: $\alpha =0$

The nut-work characteristics are then given by:

- Pressure dependency coefficient: $\alpha =0,33$
- Ultimate cumulative plastic deformation: ${P}_{\mathrm{ULT}}=0,01$
- Elasticity limit: ${\sigma}_{y}=2.11{10}^{6}$
- Elasticity limit for the mesh cell concerned: ${\sigma}_{y}=2.\times {10}^{6}$

For modelling A:

- Nutrient module for the entire mesh: $H=-200.\times {10}^{6}$

For modelling B:

- Ultimate Constraint: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{yULT}}=0.47\times {10}^{6}$
- Ultimate constraint for the mesh cell concerned: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{yULT}}=0.44\times {10}^{6}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

A drained biaxial test (D_PLAN) is modelled, so normal shifts on the design of the study are zero. Vertical displacement is imposed on $[\mathit{DC}]$ while maintaining constant lateral pressure ($2\mathit{MPa}$) in the design of the study. The boundary conditions are therefore:

${u}_{y}=0$ on $[\mathit{AB}]$ (group of mesh cell $\mathit{BAS}$)

${u}_{x}=0$ on $[\mathit{AD}]$ (group of mesh cell $\mathit{GAUCHE}$)

${\sigma}_{n}=2.\times {10}^{6}$ on $[\mathit{BC}]$ (group of mesh cell $\mathit{EXTREM}$)

Vertical displacement is then imposed on $[\mathit{DC}]$ (group of mesh cell $\mathit{HAUT}$) to apply vertical deformation up to $\text{3\%}$.

### 1.4 Results

The solutions are post-processed to control the evolution of quantities from INDL_ELGA and PDIL_ELGA to non-regression.

## 4 Summary of the results

This test case shows the values obtained with the INDL_ELGA criterion on an example of a compression triaxial test described by a softening behavior of the Drucker-Prager type. These are values in non-regression. An additional calculation of the PDIL_ELGA option for the Drucker-Prager model is also added. The values obtained are also tested in non-regression.
