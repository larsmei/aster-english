# v6.01.109 SSNA109 - Tensile test with VISC_CIN2_CHAB

**Summary:**

This quasi-static non-linear test validates the VISC_CIN2_CHAB $\mathrm{2D}$ model in the case of a axisymmetric test piece (homogeneous stress and deformation state) subjected to a single tensile test. The particularity of the test (the VISC_CIN2_CHAB model being tested elsewhere) comes from the coefficients, which were identified on the $10\mathrm{CD}9-10$ steel at $545°C$ , and for which the initial plasticity threshold is very low.

The Modelling of the test piece is done with a element $\mathrm{2D}$ (QUA4).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry is chosen voluntarily simple, to translate a state of homogeneous stresses and deformations, as is the case in uniaxial traction. It is here a volume element represented by a square on the side $\mathrm{0.01mm}$. The Modelling is axisymmetric, and the tensile is done with imposed deformation.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

$\mathrm{DY}=0$ on the lower side

$\mathrm{DX}=0$ on left side

$\mathrm{DY}$ on top, such as:

$\mathrm{DY}(t)=({\mathrm{EPS}}_{\mathrm{final}}\ast H)/\mathrm{tmax}\ast t$

With ${\mathrm{EPS}}_{\mathrm{final}}=0.01$

$H=0.01\mathrm{mm}$

$\mathrm{Tmax}=\mathrm{10000s}$

This corresponds to an imposed deformation speed of $0.01/10000=1.E–6/s$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

No stress and deformation.
