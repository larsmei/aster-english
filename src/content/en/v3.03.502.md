# v3.03.502 SSLS502 - Orthotropic cylinder subjected to a charging line

**Summary:**

This test represents the quasi-static calculation, the orthotropic short cylinder and the long orthotropic cylinder subject to a charging line. At their ends, the cylinders are based on rigid diaphragms. This test case allows to validate elements DST finished Modelling with mesh cells TRIA3 and QUAD4, with homogeneous orthotropic material.

The displacements and forces obtained are compared with an experimental reference solution and an analytical solution.

## 7 Summary of the results

The results are generally satisfactory. The differences point-based, which is shown at the tested points, notably the point $D$, appears to be due to experimental uncertainty, probably reinforced by uncertainty about the graphical sampling.

On the other hand, the solutions proposed by Batoz in deep hull theory are well verified by the four modellings, with relative errors of less than 5% for the long cylinder.

It appears that:

- modellings TRIA3 and QUAD4 are substantially equivalent for this problem,
- the relative errors are much lower for the long cylinder (modellings C and D) than for the short cylinder (modellings A and B): at point $F$, the error is reduced by a factor 10 compared to the Batoz reference solution,
- mesh refinement does not significantly minimize relative differences, both with TRIA3 and QUAD4.

It is therefore found that the results degrade when the ratio of length to diameter decreases, in fact the geometric effects become important with this type of Modelling. It would be desirable to be able to perform a calculation in elements finished shells in orthotropic medium, in order to better take into account curvature, the plates constituting a case limit.
