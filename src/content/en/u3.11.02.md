# u3.11.02 Modellings DIS_T and DIS_TR

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings `DIS_T` and `DIS_TR`:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear capabilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings.

The two modellings `DIS_T` and `DIS_TR` make it possible to represent discrete elements in translation and in translation-rotation.

They can be used for three-dimensional problems in linear and non-linear mechanical analysis.

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

For the two modellings in three dimensions, the discretisation degrees of freedom are, at each node of the support mesh cell, the three translational displacement components or the six components (three translations and three rotations).

| **Finite element** | **Degrees of freedom (at the vertex nodes)** |  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DIS_T | DX | DY | DZ |  |  |  |
| DIS_TR | DX | DY | DZ | DRX | DRY | DRZ |

### 1.2 Support mesh cell of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the discrete elements, in a displacement formulation, are two-node segments SEG2 or point mesh cells POI1 coincident with a node:

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Finite element** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DIS_T | POI1 | MECA_DIS_T_N |
|  | SEG2 | MECA_DIS_T_L |
| DIS_TR | POI1 | MECA_DIS_TR_N |
|  | SEG2 | MECA_DIS_TR_L |

For the `POI1` mesh cells, the forces are computed from the differences of the degrees of freedom of the node of the mesh cell with the fixed frame, whereas for the `SEG2` mesh cells they are computed from the differences in degree of freedom between the two nodes.

## 2 Assignment of the characteristics

For these discrete elements, it is necessary to assign geometric characteristics which are complementary to the mesh data. The definition of these data is carried out with the command AFFE_CARA_ELEM associated with the following factor keywords:

- **DISCRET**. Makes it possible to define and to assign the values of the stiffness, mass or damping matrices. Supported modellings: `DIS_T`, `DIS_TR`
- **ORIENTATION**. Makes it possible to define and to assign a local frame. Supported modellings: `DIS_T`, `DIS_TR`

## 3 Supported loadings

- PESANTEUR. Makes it possible to apply a gravity-type loading. The supported modellings: `DIS_T`, `DIS_TR`

## 4 Linear and non-linear capabilities

### 4.1 Constitutive law

The constitutive laws specific to these modellings, usable under [COMPORTEMENT](doc:u4.51.11) in [STAT_NON_LINE](doc:u4.51.03) and [DYNA_NON_LINE](doc:u4.53.01), are documented in [R5.03.17](doc:r5.03.17) Constitutive relations of the discrete elements, except for the law ASSE_CORN [R5.03.32](doc:r5.03.32). The possible constitutive behaviours:

- **ELAS**. In the general case, it is not necessary to assign an elastic constitutive behaviour to the discrete element. The operator [STAT_NON_LINE](doc:u4.51.03) carries out this assignment by default on all the mesh cells present in the model. This assignment is then overloaded when the keyword [COMPORTEMENT](doc:u4.51.11) is processed.
  On the other hand, if a discrete element is assigned a temperature and one wishes to take thermal expansion into account (only for a `DIS_TR_L`), it is necessary to define the expansion coefficient $\alpha$ and this is done by defining an elastic material. One must therefore define $ALPHA=\alpha$; the keyword `E` is mandatory but its value will not be taken into account, see [R5.03.17](doc:r5.03.17).
  Taking the expansion of the discrete elements into account is not possible with the operator [MECA_STATIQUE](doc:u4.51.01) unless $ALPHA=0$.
- **ASSE_CORN**. See [R5.03.32](doc:r5.03.32)
- **ARME**
- **DIS_CHOC**
- **DIS_CONTACT**
- **ELAS**
- **DIS_VISC**
- **DIS_ECRO_CINE**
- **DIS_ECRO_TRAC**
- **DIS_BILI_ELAS**
- **DIS_GRICRA**
- **DIS_GOUJ2E**
- **CHOC_ENDO**
- **CHOC_ENDO_PENA**

In addition to the assignment of the characteristics [AFFE_CARA_ELEM](doc:u4.42.01), the use of the modellings `DIS_T` and `DIS_TR` with [STAT_NON_LINE](doc:u4.51.03), [DYNA_NON_LINE](doc:u4.53.01) implies defining material characteristics, via [DEFI_MATERIAU](doc:u4.43.01) and [AFFE_MATERIAU](doc:u4.43.03).

With the material `DIS_CONTACT` and the constitutive behaviours `DIS_CHOC` and `DIS_CONTACT`, the elastic matrix is computed with the stiffness characteristic defined in [AFFE_CARA_ELEM](doc:u4.42.01), whereas the tangent matrix is computed via the constitutive behaviour `DIS_CHOC` or `DIS_CONTACT`.

### 4.2 Strains

The only strain available, used in the constitutive relations under the keyword `DEFORMATION` for the operators [STAT_NON_LINE](doc:u4.51.03) and [DYNA_NON_LINE](doc:u4.53.01), is **'PETIT'** (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)):

The strains used for the constitutive relation are therefore the linearised strains computed on the initial geometry.

## 5 Examples of implementation: test cases

- **DIS_T**

  - Linear statics
    SSLL100B [V3.01.100](doc:v3.01.100): Linear static analysis of a structure made up of straight and curved beams subjected to a bending loading.
  - Non-linear statics
    SSNL118A [V6.02.118](doc:v6.02.118): Non-linear static analysis of a bar subjected to a wind-velocity field.
  - Linear dynamics
    SDLD02A [V2.01.002](doc:v2.01.002): Search for the frequencies and the vibration modes of a mechanical structure composed of masses and springs.
  - Non-linear dynamics
    SDND102B [V5.01.102](doc:v5.01.102): Seismic response of a non-linear multi-supported mass-spring system.
- **DIS_TR**

  - Linear statics
    SSLX100D [V3.05.100](doc:v3.05.100): Analysis of a beam in bending whose model is composed of a mixture of 3D, shell and beam modelling.
  - Non-linear statics
    SSNL102A [V6.02.102](doc:v6.02.102): Analysis of the non-linear behaviour of an assembly of angle sections subjected to a two-dimensional loading of tension and of moment.
  - Linear dynamics
    SDLD02C [V2.01.002](doc:v2.01.002): Search for the frequencies and the vibration modes of a mechanical structure composed of masses and springs.
  - Non-linear dynamics
    SDND102B [V5.01.102](doc:v5.01.102): Seismic response of a non-linear multi-supported mass-spring system.
