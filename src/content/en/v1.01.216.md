# v1.01.216 ZZZZ216 – Low 3D coupling code_saturne-code_aster

**Summary:**

This test validates the low 3D coupling *code_saturne*-*code_aster*.

The aim is to model a mechanical calculation with a following pressure coming from a fluid calculation.

Here is what the different modellingss test:

- A: purely mechanical reference calculation,
- B and C: coupling *code_aster*-*code_aster* with interface-compliant meshes on 2 and 4 MPI,
- D and E: coupling *code_aster*-*code_aster* with non-conform meshes at interface on 2 and 4 MPI,
- F: coupling *code_saturne*-*code_aster* with interface-compliant meshes on 4 MPI,
- G: coupling *code_saturne*-*code_aster* with non-conform meshes at the interface on 4 MPI,
- I: dynamic *code_aster*-*code_aster* coupling with interface-compliant meshes on 2 MPI,
- J: dynamic *code_aster*-*code_aster* coupling with non-conform meshes at the interface on 2 MPI,
- K: dynamic *code_saturne*-*code_aster* coupling with interface-compliant meshes on 2 MPI,
- L: dynamic *code_saturne*-*code_aster* coupling with non-conform meshes at interface on 2 MPI,
- M: like F but with nodal projection for forces.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry of the problem is that of a cube on the side 200
whose faces are called $\mathit{Face1}$, $\mathit{Face2}$,
$\mathit{Face3}$, $\mathit{Face4}$, $\mathit{Face5}$ et $\mathit{Face6}$.

### 1.2 Material properties

It is a linear isotropic elastic material of Young's modulus $1.$ and Poisson's ratio $0.3$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The cube is clamped on its $\mathit{Face1}$ face while all the others are subject to fluid pressure.

The fluid pressure is defined here as a pressure field on the fluid mesh depending on time and space depending on the function
$10^{-4} \times \mathrm{INST} \times (X_f+Y_f+Z_f)$, where $(X_f,Y_f,Z_f)$ is the coordinate on the distorted configuration.
For this purpose, fluid pressure is considered to be a follower.

### 1.4 Modelling characteristics

Solid calculation is performed at $0.2$, $0.4$, $0.6$, $0.8$ and $1$ moments.

For low coupling cases, a relative residue on the displacement increment of $10^{-6}$
is required to switch to the next time step.

### 1.5 Initial conditions

The initial conditions are blank of any displacement and constraint.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is the Modelling A where the calculation is mechanically and without coupling.

## 3 Summary of the results

The results are quite the expected results.
