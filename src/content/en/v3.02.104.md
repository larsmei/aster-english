# v3.02.104 SSLP104 - Cook Membrane in Elasticity

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to check 3D_MIX_STA and D_PLAN_MIX_STA models
on Cook's membrane, a benchmark problem often used in literature
to test and compare finished modellings elements.

## 1 Reference problem

The problem is derived from article [1].

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry can be viewed in the figure below and will be used for all modellings.

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.104/MembraneCook.png)

Figure 1.1-1: Géométrie de la membrane

Coordinate of node A (48.60) and node B (24.22).

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic.

- Elasticity module: $E=200\mathit{\;MPa}$
- Poisson's ratio : $\nu = 0,3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The left side of the membrane ($\mathit{DX}=\mathit{DY}=0$) is clamped and a vertical surface force applied to the right side such as $\mathit{FY}=1 \mathit{kN}/\mathit{mm}\mathrm{²}$

### 1.4 Extension to 3D

The extension to 3D is obtained by extruding in the $z$ direction (thickness $1\mathit{\;mm}$). Furthermore, the displacements according to DZ are blocked to return to the solution in flat deformations.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

D_PLAN_MIX_STA modelling is used.
The TAU_EPSI coefficient is fixed to 1/16 (16 elements in the characteristic length of the problem).

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The mesh is made up of mesh cells type QUAD4 (linear quadrangle), with a total of 256 elements.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The results of the modelling are tested against the numerical solution presented in reference.
