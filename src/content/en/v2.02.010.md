# v2.02.010 SDLL10 - Variable rectangular cross section beam (clamped-clamped)

**Summary:**

This plan problem consists in searching for the frequencies and vibration modes of a mechanical structure composed of a clamped-clamped beam whose area of the right section varies exponentially. This Structure Mechanics test corresponds to a dynamic analysis of a linear model with linear behavior. It includes only one model.

Through this problem, the variable section Timoshenko bending beam element is tested as well as the calculation of frequencies and vibration modes by the Lanczos method. The "normal to 1" functionality is also tested at the point maximum amplitude in translation of vibration modes.

Using fine spatial discretization, the results obtained are in good agreement with the analytical results given in the VPCS guide.

## 4 Summary of the results

Appropriate modelling (frequency and modes specific to less than $\text{2\%}$) with a fine mesh.

A calculation on a rough mesh (12 mesh cells) shows larger differences with the reference solution. This is mainly due to the way the modes are standardized.
