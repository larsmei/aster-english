# v3.04.304 SSLV304 – Cylinder under variable external pressure

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to validate the application of pressure to an axi-symmetric structure, from a Fourier mass decomposition of the load (AXIS_FOURIER Modelling).

The pressure applied is a function of the three coordinates of the $(r,\theta ,z)$ space.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

\mathit{AB}

| External pressure on side $\mathit{BC}$ | Radial component: $-{p}_{0}\frac{R}{L}\sin(\theta )$ (normal at circumference) Axial component: ${p}_{0}\frac{z}{L}\sin(\theta )$ (following $z$) |
| --- | --- |
| External pressure on side $\mathit{DC}$ | Radial component: ${p}_{0}\sin(\theta )$ (following $r$ ) Circumferential component: ${p}_{0}\cos(\theta )$ (following $\theta$ ) Axial component: $\frac{1-\nu }{\text{}\nu }\frac{{p}_{0}r}{L}\sin(\theta )$ (normal in section) |

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The deformation due to pressure alone is given by:

${u}_{r}=\frac{{p}_{0}}{\nu EL}\times \frac{1}{1-2\nu }\times \frac{{z}^{2}}{4}\sin(\theta )$ ; ${u}_{z}=\frac{-{p}_{0}}{\text{}2nuEL}\mathit{rz}\sin(\theta )$ ; ${u}_{\theta}=\frac{{p}_{0}}{\nu EL}\times \frac{1}{1-2\nu }\times \frac{{z}^{2}}{4}\cos(\theta )$ .

The field of constraints is:

${\sigma}_{\mathit{rr}}=-\frac{{p}_{0}r\sin(\theta )}{L}$ ; ${\sigma}_{zz}=-\frac{1-\nu }{\nu L}{p}_{0}r\sin(\theta )$ ; ${\sigma}_{\theta \theta }=-\frac{{p}_{0}r\sin(\theta )}{L}$ ;

${\sigma}_{\mathit{rz}}=\frac{{p}_{0}z\sin(\theta )}{L}$ ; ${\sigma}_{r\theta }=0$ ; ${\sigma}_{\theta z}=\frac{{p}_{0}z\cos(\theta )}{L}$ .

### 2.2 Baseline

Radial ($\mathit{DX}$ ), vertical ($\mathit{DY}$ ) and ortho-radical ($\mathit{DZ}$ ) displacements to $C$ and $D$ points for an $\theta =45°$ angle.

Constraints on $A$ and $B$ points.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

- analytical solution.
