# v7.23.102 HSNS102 - Reinforced concrete plate with thermal loading

**Summary:**

The main purpose of this test is to verify the Modelling of elements reinforced concrete under thermal loading using three techniques:

- Modelling A: Model shell: DKT + GRILLE_EXCENTREE
- Modelling B: Solid model: Volumic + GRILLE_MEMBRANE
- Modelling C: Solid model: Volumic + MEMBRANE

The objective is to check the mechanical response compared with an analytically obtained reference solution.

In addition, it tests MATRICE=ELASTIQUE functionality for STAT_NON_LINE GRILLE_MEMBRANE GRILLE_MEMBRANE EPVC_ELGA, EPVC_ELGA, EPME_ELGA and EPSP_ELGA in B Modelling.

In Modelling C, the calculation of EFGE_ELGA and EFGE_ELNO options is also tested on MEMBRANE elements (linear and nonlinear).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The total deformations in steel and concrete are:

> ${\epsilon}_{a}^{t}={\epsilon}_{a}^{m}+{\epsilon}_{a}^{\mathit{th}}$ and ${\epsilon}_{b}^{t}={\epsilon}_{b}^{m}$ with ${\epsilon}_{a}^{\mathit{th}}=\alpha \Delta T$

$\epsilon$ is the deformation of the average plane of the plate and $\chi$ the curvature of the plate, the two unknowns to be found. By respecting the kinematics (the sections remain flat), the steel being perfectly bound to the concrete, we have:

> ${\epsilon}_{a}^{t}=\epsilon -e\chi$     et    ${\epsilon}_{b}^{t}=\epsilon -y\chi$

The normal effort imposed on the plate is zero:

> $N={N}_{a}+{N}_{b}={E}_{a}{S}_{a}{\epsilon}_{a}^{m}+{E}_{b}\int{\epsilon}_{b}^{m}\text{}={E}_{a}{S}_{a}(\epsilon -e\chi -{\epsilon}_{a}^{\mathit{th}})+{E}_{b}{S}_{b}\epsilon =0$

Similarly, the bending moment imposed on the plate is zero:

> $M={M}_{a}+{M}_{b}=e{E}_{a}{S}_{a}{\epsilon}_{a}^{m}+{E}_{b}\inty{\epsilon}_{b}^{m}\text{}={E}_{a}{S}_{a}(e\epsilon -{e}^{2}\chi -e{\epsilon}_{a}^{\mathrm{th}})+{E}_{b}{I}_{b}\chi =0$

Two equations are obtained to determine the two unknowns:

> $\begin{array}{c}({E}_{a}{S}_{a}+{E}_{b}{S}_{b})\epsilon -{E}_{a}{S}_{a}e\chi ={E}_{a}{S}_{a}{\epsilon}_{a}^{\mathit{th}}\\ {E}_{a}{S}_{a}e\epsilon +({E}_{b}{I}_{b}-{E}_{a}{S}_{a}{e}^{2})\chi ={E}_{a}{S}_{a}e{\epsilon}_{a}^{\mathit{th}}\end{array}$

We get:

> $\epsilon =\frac{\alpha \Delta T}{A}$         et         $\chi =\frac{-\alpha \Delta T}{B}$

with:

> $A=1+\frac{{E}_{b}{S}_{b}}{{E}_{a}{S}_{a}}+\frac{{S}_{b}{e}^{2}}{{I}_{b}}$             et         $B=e+(\frac{1}{{E}_{a}{S}_{a}}+\frac{1}{{E}_{b}{S}_{b}})+\frac{{E}_{b}{I}_{b}}{e}$

From these values one can calculate:

- elongation of the plate: $\Delta L=\epsilon L$
- the rotation of the plate: ${R}_{y}=\chi L$
- deflection at the end of the plate: $f=-\chi \frac{{L}^{2}}{2}$ or in the middle of the plate $f=-\chi \frac{{L}^{2}}{8}$
- normal stress in steel: ${\sigma}_{a}={E}_{a}(\epsilon -e\chi -{\epsilon}_{a}^{\mathit{th}})$
- the normal stress in the concrete: ${N}_{b}={E}_{b}{S}_{b}\epsilon$

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The elongation and deflection of the plate (moving ${U}_{x}$ and ${U}_{z}$ of an node of the $C$ edge of the plate), rotation (${R}_{y}$ constant length), normal stress in the frame, normal stress in the ${N}_{b}$ concrete are calculated.

## 6 Summary of the results

This test compares the solutions obtained with three types of Modelling to an analytical solution in the case of a reinforced concrete plate subjected to thermal loading.

- Model hull: DKT + GRILLE_EXCENTREE
- Solid model: Volumic + GRILLE_MEMBRANE
- Solid model: Volumic + MEMBRANE

The comparison balance shows a negligible difference between the results. Only one finite element is sufficient to find the analytical solution in the case DKT + GRILLE_EXCENTREE.

In the case of GRILLE_MEMBRANE or MEMBRANE density, the error is less than $1\text{\%}$ for a sufficiently fine mesh (26 elements in length).
