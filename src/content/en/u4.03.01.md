# u4.03.01 Title and subtitle of a produced concept

## 1 Purpose

Define a title or a subtitle during the execution of an *Aster* command.

The title and the subtitle, when they are provided for by the command, are:

- either defined by default,
- or supplied by the user.

The title is attached to the data structure produced by an operator.

The titles of the modified concepts are stacked.

The choice of a title is possible as soon as the operator has a simple keyword TITRE.

The expected argument is a list of texts (l_Kn).

The text supplied by the user is used as it stands; in particular, there is no implicit lower-case/upper-case conversion.

The subtitle makes it possible to add comments on the occasion of a printout by a procedure.

The subtitle exists only for the duration of the procedure and must therefore be rebuilt at each call to the procedure, in particular if the latter is reused in POURSUITE. It is therefore not attached to a concept.

The choice of a subtitle is possible as soon as the procedure has the simple keyword SOUS_TITRE (possibly under a factor keyword). It is therefore not attached to a concept. The expected argument is a list of texts (l_Kn).
