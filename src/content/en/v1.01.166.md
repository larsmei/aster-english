# v1.01.166 ZZZZ166 - Calculation of the heat flow

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the thermal flow calculation options: FLUX_ELGA and FLUX_ELNO.

In addition, the use of unit thermal conductivity also validates the temperature gradient calculation, as the gradient is equal to the opposite of the flow.

The 6 modellings tested are:

- A 2D PLAN: degree 1 triangles, degree 1 and 2 quadrangles and 9 nodes quadrangles,
- B In 2D PLAN_DIAG: degree 1 and 2 triangles, degree 1 quadrangles and quadrangles at 9 nodes,
- C In 2D AXIS: degree 1 triangles, degree 1 and 2 quadrangles and 9 nodes quadrangles,
- D In 2D AXIS_DIAG: degree 1 and 2 triangles, degree 1 quadrangles and quadrangles at 9 nodes,
- E 3D: hexahedrons, pentahedrons, pyramids and tetrahedrons of degree 1 and 2, hexahedrons at 27 nodes,
- F In 3D_DIAG: hexahedrons, pentahedrons, pyramids and tetrahedrons of degree 1.

### 1.2 Material properties

The thermal properties applied to the model are:

$\lambda =1.0W/m°C$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Not defined.

### 1.4 Temperature field

The temperature field is directly assigned to the model from one function. For each model, the flow calculation is tested for a linear thermal field and a quadratic thermal field.

For modellings 2D PLAN and PLAN_DIAG (modellings A and B), the two successively assigned fields are:

- $T(X,Y)=\mathrm{2.X}+\mathrm{3.Y}$
- $T(X,Y)={\mathrm{2.X}}^{2}+{\mathrm{3.Y}}^{2}$

For modellings 2D AXIS and AXIS_DIAG (modellings C and D), the two successively assigned fields are:

- $T(X,Y,Z)=\mathrm{2.R}+\mathrm{3.Y}$ (with $R=\sqrt{{X}^{2}+{Z}^{2}}=X$ in the mesh $(\mathit{OXY})$ plane)
- $T(X,Y,Z)={\mathrm{2.R}}^{2}+{\mathrm{3.Y}}^{2}$

For modellings 3D and 3D_DIAG (modellings E and F), the two fields successively assigned are:

- $T(X,Y,Z)=\mathrm{2.X}+\mathrm{3.Y}+\mathrm{4.Z}$
- $T(X,Y,Z)={\mathrm{2.X}}^{2}+{\mathrm{3.Y}}^{2}+{\mathrm{4Z}}^{2}$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is analytical:

$$
\Phi_x = -\lambda \cdot \frac{\partial T}{\partial X}

\Phi_y = -\lambda \cdot \frac{\partial T}{\partial Y}

\Phi_z = -\lambda \cdot \frac{\partial T}{\partial Z} \quad \text{(en 3D uniquement)}
$$

Taking into account the value of $\lambda$ ($1$ ), the flow obtained for each configuration is therefore:

For modellings 2DPLAN and PLAN_DIAG (modellings A and B):

- $T(X,Y)=\mathrm{2.X}+\mathrm{3.Y}$ is ${\Phi}_{x}(X,Y)=–2$ and ${\Phi}_{y}(X,Y)=–3$
- $T(X,Y)={\mathrm{2.X}}^{2}+{\mathrm{3.Y}}^{2}$ is ${\Phi}_{x}(X,Y)=–\mathrm{4.X}$ and ${\Phi}_{y}(X,Y)=–\mathrm{6.Y}$

For modellings 2DAXIS and AXIS_DIAG (modellings C and D):

- $T(X,Y,Z)=\mathrm{2.R}+\mathrm{3.Y}$ ($R=X$ in $(\mathit{OXY})$ plane ) or ${\Phi}_{x}(X,Y,Z)=–2$ and ${\Phi}_{y}(X,Y,Z)=–3$
- $T(X,Y,Z)={\mathrm{2.R}}^{2}+{\mathrm{3.Y}}^{2}$ ($R=X$ in $(\mathit{OXY})$ plane ) or ${\Phi}_{x}(X,Y,Z)=–\mathrm{4.X}$ and ${\Phi}_{y}(X,Y,Z)=–\mathrm{6.Y}$

For modellings 3D and 3D_DIAG (modellings E and F):

- $T(X,Y,Z)=\mathrm{2.X}+\mathrm{3.Y}+\mathrm{4.Z}$ is ${\Phi}_{x}(X,Y,Z)=–2$ , ${\Phi}_{y}(X,Y,Z)=–3$ and ${\Phi}_{z}(X,Y,Z)=–4$
- $T(X,Y,Z)={\mathrm{2.X}}^{2}+{\mathrm{3.Y}}^{2}+{\mathrm{4Z}}^{2}$ is ${\Phi}_{x}(X,Y,Z)=–\mathrm{4.X}$ , ${\Phi}_{y}(X,Y,Z)=–\mathrm{6.Y}$ and ${\Phi}_{z}(X,Y,Z)=–\mathrm{8.Z}$

### 2.2 Baseline

For modellings 2D (PLAN, PLAN_DIAG, AXIS and AXIS_DIAG), the values tested are:

- With the linear temperature field: the temperature at nodes $A$ , $C$ , $J$ and $L$ , the following flow $X$ and $Y$ by element to nodes $A$ , $C$ , $J$ and $L$ and the first flow point from Gauss of the same elements,
- With the quadratic temperature field: the temperature at nodes $A$, $C$, $J$ and $L$, and the following flow $X$ and $Y$ by element to nodes $A$, $C$, $J$ and $L$.

For the 3D 3D 3D and 3D_DIAG modellings, the values tested are:

- With the linear temperature field: the temperature at nodes ${D}_{i}$ , the following flow $X$ and $Y$ by element at nodes ${D}_{i}$ and the first flow at point from Gauss of the same elements,
- With the quadratic temperature field: the temperature at nodes ${D}_{i}$, and the following flow $X$ and $Y$ by element at nodes ${D}_{i}$.

With $i=1$ to $9$ for 3D and $i=1$ to $4$ for 3D_DIAG modelling.
