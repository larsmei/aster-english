# v2.03.506 SDLS506 – Square plate in transient dynamic

**Summary:**

This test validates the element COQUE_SOLIDE for dynamic (linear and nonlinear) transient analysis. It deals with a thick plate of steel, prone to sinusoidal excitation on a vibrating table.

## 1 Introduction

### 1.1 Geometry

The study concerns a thick plate of steel. Its geometry is simple. It is a simple flattened cube:

length $2,0$ meters, width $2,0$ meters and thickness $0,03$ meters [].

The plate is firmly clamped on one of its sides (ENCAS) and fixed to a vibrating table.

The displacement of an point located on the top side in a plate corner is calculated, in contrast to the clamped support (point P) [].

### 1.2 Materials

Steel is considered to be a linear isotropic, elastic material for linear analysis:

- Young's modulus : $E=200000.{10}^{+6}\mathit{Pa}$ ,
- Poisson's ratio : $\nu =0,3$ ,
- density: $\rho =8000\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$

For nonlinear analysis, this is a VMIS_ISOT_LINE behavior with $\text{D\_SIGM\_EPSI}=2\mathit{GPa}$ (the Young's modulus divided by 100) and an $\mathit{SY}=200\mathit{MPa}$ elastic limit.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

The plate is clamped on one side.

The input signal is a simple $15\mathit{Hz}$ frequency sinus.

The amplitude is $\mathrm{30g}$ horizontally and $\mathrm{30g}$ vertically downwards ($g=10m/{s}^{2}$ ).

The signal duration is $0.5s$ .

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The calculations are performed on a 3D model with HEXA27. This reference corresponds to the forma12 test.

### 2.2 Baseline

The displacements are measured on the point P and at different times.
