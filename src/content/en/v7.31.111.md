# v7.31.111 WTNV111 – Heat flow over saturated porous medium

**Summary:**

A three-dimensional thermal problem is considered in saturated porous medium.

This test involves studying the effect of applying a temperature flux on the upper side of the model to the temperature distribution in the element. The first time step is studied only. Pressure and displacement are blocked.

The models studied are $\mathrm{2D}$ planes (DPQ8 and DPTR6) and $\mathrm{3D}$ density (HEXA20) with linear behavior.

The reference solution is then the solution for pure thermal calculation (THER_LINEAIRE) with *Code_Aster.*

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Introduction

The pure thermal behavior of a porous medium saturated by a single fluid is studied in this test case: water in its liquid phase. It is used in THM Modelling in *Code_Aster*. The constitutive law of the associated fluid is of the LIQU_SATU type.
