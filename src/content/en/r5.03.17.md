# r5.03.17 Constitutive relations of discrete elements

**Summary:**

This document describes the non-linear behaviours of the discrete elements that are called by the operators for the solution of non-linear problems [STAT_NON_LINE](doc:u4.51.03) or [DYNA_NON_LINE](doc:u4.53.01).
More precisely, the behaviours described in this document are:

- the thermoelastic behaviour ELAS,
- the Von Mises-type behaviour with isotropic hardening used for the modelling of threaded assemblies DIS_GOUJ2E, accessible via the keywords DIS_GOUJ2E_PLAS and DIS_GOUJ2E_ELAS of the factor keyword [COMPORTEMENT](doc:u4.51.11),
- the bilinear elastic behaviour, accessible via the keyword DIS_BILI_ELAS,
- the Von Mises-type behaviour with non-linear kinematic hardening, accessible via the keyword DIS_ECRO_CINE,
- the non-linear viscoelastic rheological behaviour, accessible via the keyword DIS_VISC,
- the non-linear elastoplastic behaviour, accessible via the keyword DIS_ECRO_TRAC,
- the contact-type behaviour with impact and Coulomb friction, in explicit integration with penalty stiffnesses, accessible via the keyword DIS_CHOC of the factor keyword [COMPORTEMENT](doc:u4.51.11),
- the non-linear elastic contact-type behaviour DIS_CHOC_TRAC, in explicit integration with a non-linear stiffness given by a function,
- the contact-type behaviour with impact of solids and Coulomb friction, in implicit integration, accessible via the keyword DIS_CONTACT,
- the behaviours CHOC_ENDO and CHOC_ENDO_PENA, which make it possible to model a behaviour that takes into account a threshold for the impact force, as well as a variation of the stiffness and of the damping as a function of the relative displacement of the nodes of the discrete element,
- the unilateral contact behaviour with Coulomb friction, used to model the translational and rotational behaviour of the set of grid-to-rod connection springs of the fuel assemblies, accessible via the keyword DIS_GRICRA.
- the line-fitting behaviour, accessible via the keyword ARME,
- the “Dashpot”-type behaviour, accessible via the keyword DASHPOT,
- the non-linear behaviour which makes it possible to model a bending connection between a wall and a floor or a raft, accessible via the keyword JONC_ENDO_PLAS.

The integration of the constitutive models mentioned above is detailed in this document. Other behaviours relating to discrete elements are available, but are not detailed here:

- non-linear assembly of pylon angles, relation ASSE_CORN [R5.03.32](doc:r5.03.32),
- the non-linear elastoplastic behaviour of a rectangular shallow foundation subjected to a static or seismic three-dimensional loading, relation FONDA_SUPERFI [R5.03.31](doc:r5.03.31).
