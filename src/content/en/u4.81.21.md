# u4.81.21 Operator POST_RELEVE_T

## 1 Purpose

Extract values of components of fields of quantities (grandeurs) and carry out computations of means and of invariants. The values are recorded on nodes, mesh cells, broken lines linking nodes. They can be the subject, by this same procedure:

- of computations of:

- means,
- resultants and moments of vector fields,
- invariants of tensor fields,
- directional trace of fields,
- extrema and arithmetic mean on nodes or mesh cells

- of expression in the frames GLOBAL, LOCAL, POLAIRE, UTILISATEUR or CYLINDRIQUE.

POST_RELEVE_T produces a concept of type table, which can be printed by means of IMPR_TABLE [U4.91.03](doc:u4.91.03).

## 3 Principles of use of POST_RELEVE_T: operand ACTION

The post-processing carried out by POST_RELEVE_T requires the data of three pieces of information:

- location,
- object,
- nature.

Each occurrence of the factor keyword ACTION defines this triplet.

The **location** of the post-processing designates a geometric figure linking the post-processing points. This location is defined by means of the keyword GROUP_NO.

The **object** of the post-processing is defined by the choice of a field of a quantity (grandeur), of components and possibly of associated quantities chosen by various keywords.

The fields of quantities (grandeurs) are chosen by one of the keywords:

RESULTAT and the keywords making it possible to choose the fields at the nodes or the fields on the elements of the data structure resultat.

CHAM_GD field of a quantity (grandeur) produced by an elementary operator, or extracted from a result concept by CREA_CHAMP [U4.72.04](doc:u4.72.04).

the components by:

TOUT_CMP

NOM_CMP

and the associated quantities by:

INVARIANT

ELEM_PRINCIPAUX

TRAC_NOR

TRAC_DIR and DIRECTION

RESULTANTE and, optionally, MOMENT and POINT

The **nature** of the post-processing corresponds to the operations: keyword OPERATION

- extraction of values: 'EXTRACTION'
- computation of means (in the sense of integration), of the minimum and of the maximum on an ordered group of nodes: 'MOYENNE'
- computation of the minimum and of the maximum on groups or entities: 'EXTREMA'
- computation of means in the arithmetic sense on groups or entities: 'MOYENNE_ARITH'

## 4 Choice of the location of the post-processing

### 4.1 OPERATION = 'EXTRACTION' or 'MOYENNE'

#### 4.1.1 Syntax

```text
♦    GROUP_NO = lgrno,                    [l_gr_noeud]
◊    /    TOUT= 'OUI',
/    GROUP_MA = lgrma,                [l_gr_maille]
```

#### 4.1.2 Operands GROUP_NO / TOUT / GROUP_MA

- The argument of GROUP_NO is a list of node groups. The nodes are processed in the order supplied by the user. To reorder the nodes of a list, the command DEFI_GROUP/OPTION =‘NOEUD_ORDO’ [U4.22.01](doc:u4.22.01) must be used
- The keyword GROUP_MA makes it possible to limit the post-processing to a list of mesh-cell groups.

TOUT= 'OUI' amounts to the standard case (all the mesh cells are considered). These keywords can be used only with GROUP_NO.

*Notes:*

It is not recommended to use GROUP_MA with the operation MOYENNE unless the elements are segments. Recall that the operation MOYENNE computes quantities along a length L. For further information, refer to the paragraph devoted to the keyword OPERATION where the quantities computed by means of this option are presented.

*The keyword* TOUT='OUI' *means “all the mesh cells of the mesh”. The nodal elements assigned directly on the nodes of the mesh (* AFFE_MODELE / NOEUD *), which are moreover not recommended, are ignored by the post-processing.*

#### 4.1.3 Notions of post-processing points and examples

The post-processing points are the points of $\Omega$ where the components (or derived quantities) are evaluated. The extraction operation carries out this evaluation.

#### 4.1.4 Example of post-processing points specified by the keyword GROUP_NO

![Figure](/figures/u4.81.21/10000A4C000014AC0000182F9F40293B2295C902.svg)

**Figure 4.3.3-a**

This figure visualises a post-processing location built from a list of nodes.

The post-processing points are the nodes shown, the post-processing location is the broken line linking them in the order supplied by the user, which makes it possible to define a curvilinear abscissa. The value extracted on these lines is interpolated linearly between the values recorded on the nodes. Thus it must be noted that if the post-processing operation is a computation of a mean, everything happens as if the segment $[{N}_{\mathrm{47 }}{N}_{61}]$ were contained entirely in $\Omega$ **.**

In this case, the curvilinear abscissa is computed from the path of the nodes, as defined in the list of nodes.

### 4.2 OPERATION = 'EXTREMA' or 'MOYENNE_ARITH'

#### 4.2.1 Syntax

```text
♦    |    TOUT=     'OUI',
|    GROUP_NO = lgrno,                    [l_gr_noeud]                                |    GROUP_MA =    lgrma,                    [l_gr_maille]
```

#### 4.2.2 Operands TOUT/ GROUP_NO / GROUP_MA

- The argument of GROUP_NO is a list of node groups. The nodes are not ordered.
- The keyword GROUP_MA makes it possible to limit the post-processing to a list of mesh-cell groups.
- TOUT= 'OUI' amounts to the standard case (all the mesh cells are considered).

#### 5.2.4 Operand REPERE

```text
/    REPERE
```

Makes it possible to choose a frame from among the following frames:

- GLOBAL frame: Cartesian frame of definition of the mesh,
- POLAIRE frame: standard polar frame of the plane $(\mathit{OXY})$ (order of the components: $(r,\theta )$ ,
- LOCAL frame: frame of the plane made up of the tangent and normal vectors (in that order) to the post-processing location. The normal vector is defined at each post-processing point as the mean of the normals to the right and to the left.

**Definition of the normal to the post-processing location.**

At each post-processing point the normal is defined as the mean of the normals to the right and to the left.

![Figure](/figures/u4.81.21/100004E200000DF400000ADAD353FDB2C062059D.svg)

**Figure 5.2.4-a**

The tangent vector is obtained by a rotation of $-\pi /2$ from the normal vector.

In the case of the LOCAL frame and of a 3D line, one will have to supply:

```text
VECT_Y = (oy1,oy2,oy3)
```

The coordinates of a vector whose projection onto the plane orthogonal to the directing axis of the line will be taken as the normal to the line. The order of the components in a local frame is $(t,n,k)$ .

**Example of use:**

O

![Figure](/figures/u4.81.21/10001198000069D500005B433BF929FCF819D56D.svg)

**Figure 5.2.4-b**

One wants to carry out an extraction on the line $\mathit{ABC}$ in the local frame defined above (local axis y in the global direction $\mathit{OX}$ ).

Here, one can find a vector constant at every point of the line in order to define the vector VECT_Y=(1.,0.,0.).

This is possible because at every point this vector is already in the plane orthogonal to the line.

![Figure](/figures/u4.81.21/1000159E000069D5000064C526B4F3F2762F9FE5.svg)

**Figure 5.2.4-c**

If, on the other hand, one wishes to have the local axis $z$ in the global direction $\mathit{OX}$ [Figure 5.2.4-c], the vector VECT_Y will depend on the point considered:

$(0.,0.,1.)$ is suitable except at $A$ (where $(0.,-1.,0.)$ is suitable)

$(0.,-\mathrm{1.0.})$ is suitable except at $C$ (where $(0.,0.,1.)$ is suitable)

One will therefore have in this case to cut the line into two pieces ($\mathit{AB}$ and $\mathit{BC}$ ) and to define a different VECT_Y on each piece.

- UTILISATEUR frame: defined by the data of 3 nautical angles (in degrees):

```text
ANGL_NAUT= (a, b, c)
```

- CYLINDRIQUE frame defined by:

| ORIGINE= (x,y,z) | the coordinates of the origin $O$ of the frame |
| --- | --- |
| AXE_Z=(oz1,oz2,oz3) | the coordinates of a vector defining the axis $\mathit{Oz}$ (axis of the cylinder). The order of the components in a cylindrical frame is $(r,z,\theta )$ . |

#### 5.2.5 Operand TRAC_NOR

```text
/    TRAC_NOR: uniquement pour les modélisations 2D et 3D.
```

Determination of the normal trace of a vector or of a tensor of order 2: this is the particular case of the directional trace obtained when the direction $u$ is identified with the normal $n$ to the post-processing location.

#### 5.2.6 Operands TRAC_DIR / DIRECTION

```text
/    TRAC_DIR : uniquement pour les modélisations 2D et 3D.
♦    DIRECTION
```

Determination of the directional trace of a vector $v=({v}_{i})$ or of a tensor of order 2 $\sigma =({\sigma}_{i})$ in the direction $u=({u}_{i})$ ; that is, of the scalar ${v}_{k}{u}_{k}$ or of the vector ${\sigma}_{\mathit{ik}}{u}_{k}$ .

The direction $u$ is defined by means of the keyword DIRECTION whose arguments are the components of the vector $u$ given in the order $X,Y,Z$ and evaluated in the global frame. If this list contains only two values then, by convention, the component along $Z$ of the vector $u$ is considered to be zero.

#### 5.2.7 Operand INVARIANT

Post-processing of a stress or strain tensor of order 2 associated with the principal directions of the tensor:

TRACE        $\mathrm{Tr}(\sigma )=\sum_{i=1}^{2\mathrm{ou}3}{\sigma}_{ii}$

VON_MIS    $\mathrm{VM}(\sigma )=\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{2\mathrm{ou}3}\frac{3}{2}{({\sigma}_{ij}-\frac{1}{3}\mathrm{Tr}(\sigma ){\delta}_{ij})}^{2}}$

TRESCA        $\mathrm{TR}(\sigma )=\max(∣{\lambda}_{i}-{\lambda}_{j}∣)$  with ${\lambda}_{i}$ eigenvalues of $\sigma$

DETER        $\mathrm{DET}(\sigma )=\mathrm{déterminant}\mathrm{de}\sigma$

#### 5.2.8 Operand ELEM_PRINCIPAUX

```text
/    ELEM_PRINCIPAUX
```

Determination of the principal values of a tensor $2\times 2$ or $3\times 3$ of order 2. They are arranged in increasing order of their values.

#### 5.2.9 Operands RESULTANTE / MOMENT / POINT

Determination of the resultant and of the moment of a torsor field on the post-processing location.

RESULTANTE can be used only if OPERATION='EXTRACTION'.

From the software point of view, these keywords can be applied to any field of a quantity (grandeur) but, for the results to have a physical meaning, one must restrict oneself to the fields of nodal forces and nodal reactions.

In this latter case, 2 possibilities arise:

- the user wants to compute the resultant of certain components of the field: behind the keyword RESULTANTE a list of components will be entered, to be taken from among $\lbrace$ 'DX', 'DY', 'PRE1', 'PRE2', 'TEMP' $\rbrace$ in 2D and $\lbrace$ 'DX', 'DY', 'DZ', 'PRE1', 'PRE2', 'TEMP' $\rbrace$ in 3D or for structural elements (the resultant of rotation components having no physical meaning),
- the user wants to compute the resultant and the moment of certain components of the field: behind the keywords RESULTANTE and MOMENT 2 lists of components of the same length will be entered, to be taken from among

| formula $\lbrace$ 'DX', 'DY', 'DZ' $\rbrace$ | behind the keyword RESULTANTE |
| --- | --- |
| formula $\lbrace$ 'DRX', 'DRY', 'DRZ' $\rbrace$ | behind the keyword MOMENT |

In addition, behind the keyword POINT the list of the coordinates of the point with respect to which the moment is evaluated will be introduced.

If one denotes by $P$ this point and by $M_{i}$ the post-processing points, the quantities evaluated will be:

- Resultant: $F=\sum_{i}{F}_{i}=\sum_{i}(FX_{M_i},FY_{M_i},FZ_{M_i}, PRE1_{M_i},PRE2_{M_i},TEMP_{M_i})$
- Moment: $m=\sum_{i}(\overrightarrow{PM}_i\wedge F_i)+\sum_{i}m_i^c$

where $m_i^c$ designates the list of the concentrated moments corresponding to the rotation components introduced by the keyword MOMENT, relevant only in the case of structural elements (beams, shells, discrete elements).

  1. In continuous media, one must not introduce behind MOMENT translation components which would be considered as concentrated moments and therefore summed with the true moments.

  1. The computation of RESULTANTE and/or of MOMENT is carried out by a sum over a set of nodes. This sum has a meaning only if all the nodal forces (or moments) are expressed in the same frame. This means that the keyword REPERE can take as value only 'GLOBAL' or 'UTILISATEUR'.

  1. The components 'PRE1', 'PRE2' and 'TEMP' of the keyword RESULTANTE are accessible only in thermo-hydro-mechanical modelling.

## 6 Nature of the post-processing

### 6.1 Operand OPERATION

```text
♦    OPERATION =
```

```text
|    'EXTRACTION'
```

The operation of extraction of a field of a quantity (grandeur) makes it possible to recover the values of one or several components or of quantities derived from these components at the points of the post-processing location.

In the case of an extraction on a cham_elem, the values of the components extracted from this field are computed as follows:

- If the post-processing location is determined by the keyword GROUP_NO, for each node the components are averaged over all the elements meeting at this node. In the case of the extraction of a field of type ELNO, the same values are obtained by giving the field of type NOEU.

**Note:**

*The averagings at the nodes of fields computed in local frames are licit only if the angles between these frames are small. Otherwise, they have no meaning.*

If the post-processing location is defined by GROUP_NO = $(\mathit{GN}1,\mathit{GN}2,\mathit{GN}3,\mathit{GN}4,\mathit{GN}5)$ , the values are averaged over all the elements of the mesh above.

If the post-processing location is defined as being the segment of origin $\mathit{N1}$ and of end $\mathit{N5}$ , the values will be averaged over the hatched elements.

In the case of quadratic elements (presence of mid-side nodes), the mean at the vertex nodes can lead to greater weights of certain elements (depending on the subdivision) with respect to the mid-side nodes which average over 2 elements (hence of the same weight). One can therefore find oneself in the presence of oscillations between the values at the vertices and at the mid-sides.

```text
|    'MOYENNE'
```

This operation is limited to 6 field components at a time. Given a scalar field $U$ (typically a component of a quantity (grandeur)), the operation 'MOYENNE' computes the following quantities ($L$ designating the length of the post-processing location $C$ considered):

MOMENT_0        =    $\frac{1}{L}{\int}_{c}U(s)\mathrm{ds}$

MOMENT_1        =    $\frac{12}{{L}^{2}}{\int}_{c}U(s)(s-\frac{L}{2})\mathrm{ds}$

MINIMUM        =    $\underset{c}{\mathrm{Min}}U$

MAXIMUM        =    $\underset{c}{\mathrm{Max}}U$

MOYE_INT        =    MOMENT_0 - ½ MOMENT_1

MOYE_EXT        =    MOMENT_0 + ½ MOMENT_1

It is important that the post-processing location be traversed in one direction. If a node group is used, care will be taken to reorder the nodes, by using the command DEFI_GROUP option 'NOEUD_ORDO', [U4.22.01](doc:u4.22.01). Thus, the curvilinear abscissa is defined from the origin node of the group, following the broken line made up of the nodes.

The integrals above are evaluated by assuming $U$ linear between two nodes. Thus, denoting by ${U}_{i}$ the values of the field at the nodes (numbered by $i=1,\mathrm{...},N$ ) of abscissa ${s}_{i}$ , one has:

$\text{MOMENT\_0}=\frac{1}{2({s}_{N}-{s}_{1})}\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}({s}_{i+1}-{s}_{i})({U}_{i}+{U}_{i+1})$

$\begin{array}{}\text{MOMENT\_1}=\frac{2}{{({s}_{N}-{s}_{1})}^{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}({s}_{i+1}-{s}_{i})({U}_{i}({s}_{i+1}+{\mathrm{2s}}_{i})+{U}_{i+1}({\mathrm{2s}}_{i+1}+{s}_{i}))\\ -\frac{3}{({s}_{N}-{s}_{1})}\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}({s}_{i+1}-{s}_{i})({U}_{i}+{U}_{i+1})\end{array}$

```text
|    'EXTREMA'

calcule les MIN, MAX, MINI_ABS, MAXI_ABSd'un champ réduit éventuellement sur une liste de nœuds ou de mailles, sur toutes les composantes ou une liste de composantes.
```

### 6.2 Operand MOYE_NOEUD

Keyword making it possible to choose a detailed or averaged printout at a point. This keyword is significant only for quantities (grandeurs) of type cham_elem and for the operation EXTRACTION.

```text
MOYE_NOEUD = 'OUI'
```

For each post-processing point, the displayed value of a component or of a deduced quantity is obtained as the mean of the values given by each mesh cell meeting at this point. The way of taking the mean is the same as for the fields XXXX_NOEU computed by CALC_CHAMP [U4.81.04](doc:u4.81.04).

```text
MOYE_NOEUD = 'NON'
```

The list of the values obtained for each mesh cell meeting at the post-processing point is displayed.

## 8 Verification phase

One checks that the headings are all different.

### 8.1 During execution

This phase checks the consistency of the arguments with one another. It concerns all the occurrences of the factor keyword ACTION. Two groups of checks are distinguished.

The **first** group is common to the arguments of the keywords CHAM_GD and RESULTAT (which are mutually exclusive) and reduces to the following checks:

- acceptability of the components:

one checks that the components requested are indeed present in the catalogue of description of the quantity (grandeur) to be processed,

The **second** group is specific to the keyword RESULTAT, and reduces to the checks:

- check of the acceptability of the symbolic field:

one makes sure that the symbolic field argument of the keyword NOM_CHAM does indeed exist for the type of concept resultat argument of the keyword RESULTAT,

- existence of at least one data structure for the symbolic field to be processed.

At the end of the verification phase, in the execution phase the following alternative arises:

- all the occurrences of ACTION are correct and the operations are launched,
- at least one occurrence of ACTION is incorrect, then a fatal error message is produced with a stop of the command. Information is given on the nature of the errors and the means of correcting them.

### 8.2 During processing

A new verification filter arises but never causes the command to stop. This filter reduces to the following checks:

- existence of the data structure designated by CHAM_GD or RESULTAT and one of the keywords allowing access in a RESULTAT data structure.

In the event of failure a message is issued and the following occurrence is processed,

- in the case of a computation of tensor invariants, one checks that the object of the processing does indeed designate a tensor of order 2,
- in the case of a computation of a mean or of a request for extraction of values, one checks that the components to be processed have indeed been computed on the post-processing location. In the event of failure of this check, a message details the components not available as a function of the mesh cells or nodes of the post-processing location.

In the case where this last filter detects no impossibility of computation, the post-processing is launched and the results are inserted in the table.
