# v5.06.109 PLEXU04: Cylinder with internal pressure prestress cables in transient dynamics

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate all the features implemented as part of the Code_Aster/Europlexus linkage methodology for the use of prestress in Europlexus, on a semi-industrial model (these features have been tested, together and/or separately, only on elementary tests).

To do this, the CALC_EUROPLEXUS macro-command results are compared with those from the DYNA_NON_LINE operator using the explicit pattern of finite differences.

## 1 Description

### 1.1 Geometry

The design design is a cylindrical casing of medium radius $R=21.9m$ , height $H=49.6m$ and thickness $t=0.6m$ . The anchors for the cables on the sides of the cylinder are for $L=1.5m$ length and width $l=0.5m$ . As for the cables, they are positioned on the outer skin of the cylinder . They are then eccentricated from a distance $e=0.25m$ . The model is composed of $20$ horizontal cables and $20$ vertical cables. ### 1.4 Boundary conditions and loads

The cylinder and anchors are clamped at the top and bottom.

The nodes at the ends of vertical and horizontal cables are considered "active" and the nodes is used to provide a voltage in the cables equal to $3.75\times {10}^{6}N$.

In a second stage, the cylinder is subjected to an internal pressure that increases over time (from ${P}_{i}=0\mathit{Pa}$ to ${P}_{f}=1\mathit{kbar}$ to $\Delta t=1\mathit{ms}$). This load is shown on the following chart.

### 1.5 Key steps in testing

DEFI_CABLE_BP macro-command is used to obtain kinematic relationships between the cylinder and cables as well as voltage-related loading in cables.

The CALC_PRECONT macro-command is then launched to prestress the structure from the voltages of the data cables.

The result of this prestressing is given in the initial state to the CALC_EUROPLEXUS macro-command to calculate the mechanical response of the prestressed cylinder to the internal pressure loading.

To validate the results from CALC_EUROPLEXUS, the same calculation is done with the DYNA_NON_LINE operator.

From the two concepts and results obtained, we extract:

- The evolution of the displacement time on the reference point at the middle height of the cylinder, i.e. $(0,R,H/2)$ , noted ${N}_{\mathit{ref}}^{\mathit{cyl}}$ .
- the resulting $\mathit{Nxx}$ and $\mathit{Nyy}$ membrane forces, at three different moments in a cylinder mesh cell having a node in common with the cylinder reference node, noted ${M}_{\mathit{ref}}^{\mathit{cyl}}$.
- The normal effort in an element of the nearest localized horizontal cable of the cylinder mid-height and the furthest from the anchors, noted ${\mathit{EL}}_{\mathit{ref}}^{\mathit{câb}}$.

## 4 Executive summary

Tests performed on the most important quantities in the model show that the values calculated with CALC_EUROPLEXUS are very similar to those obtained with DYNA_NON_LINE.

Based on the $\mathit{DY}$ shift pattern of the reference point of the cylinder as a function of time, it is first observed that the prestress was taken into account, as the initial displacement is negative, and then the two curves overlap.

It is concluded that vertical and horizontal cables and kinematic relationships were correctly defined and taken into account in the dynamic calculation with the CALC_EUROPLEXUS control, since this control is capable of correctly restoring the value of displacements, generalized forces in the hulls and stresses in the cables after applying dynamic loading.
