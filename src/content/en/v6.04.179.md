# v6.04.179 SSNV179 - Fluid Cube via LEMA_SEUIL Law

**Summary:**

This test is intended to validate the LEMA_SEUIL law derived from the classic LEMAITRE law. In particular, we will focus on the activation of the threshold specific to this law. So we perform a creep test on a simple geometry that is here a cube.

3D modelling with elements HEXA8 is currently available.

## 4 Summary of the results

The objective of this test case is completely fulfilled since the activation of creep via threshold detection is very precise (in the order of ${10}^{-8}\text{\%}$ error). Of course, since it is a viscous phenomenon, time discretization plays a very important role especially in the vicinity of threshold activation. In this test case a special care was taken to surround the time when the threshold is reached precisely to achieve satisfactory results.
