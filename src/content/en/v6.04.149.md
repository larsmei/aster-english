## 5 Summary of the results

The objective of Modelling A is to show the re-closure of cracks in compression after a damaging tensile phase. Modelling B shows the effects of the parameter ${\sigma}_{\mathit{seuil}}^{C}$ (threshold stress in a simple compression test). It should be noted that the threshold stress does not correspond in the calculation exactly to the stress of initiation of compression damage, this is due to the fact that the criterion is evaluated from the deformations at the previous time step and not to the current time step. The observed error therefore depends on the size of the time step.
