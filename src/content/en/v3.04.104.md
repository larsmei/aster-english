# v3.04.104 SSLV104 - Rotating beam

**Summary:**

This test validates the linear elastic calculation of an slender beam that rotates at one of its ends. Four modellings are tested: elements 3D (HEXA20) , elements COQUE_3D (QUAD9 and TRIA7) and elements D_PLAN (QUAD8). This tests the rotation inertia forces, without taking into account the basic terms of centrifugal stiffening (see [V3.04.105](doc:v3.04.105)).

The reference solution is analytical (1D). The results coincide perfectly with the reference solution.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Slender beam oriented in space by the $(1,1,1)$ vector axis.

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.104/10002210000069D500003CE0DE35EC54BD6A5F6F.svg)

Square section of area: $4.0{10}^{-4}{m}^{2}$

Length of beam: $0.5m$

### 1.2 Material properties

$E=2.{10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$

$\nu =0$

$\rho =7800\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$

$\text{A\_CIS}=0.8333$ (transverse shear correction factor equal to $5/6$ for a Reissner thin hull theory)

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Clamped beam, free in rotation around an axis perpendicular to its largest dimension and passing through the centre of the clamped face.

Component of the rotation vector: $(1,0,–1)$ .

Rotation speed: $\omega =3000\mathrm{rd}/s$ .

The important value of the rotation speed is nothing physical.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

In the local beam marker:

$\frac{{\partial}^{2}{U}_{x}}{\partial {x}^{2}}+\frac{\rho}{E}{\omega}^{2}x=0$ with $\begin{array}{}{U}_{x}(0)=0\\ \frac{\partial {U}_{x}}{\partial x}(L)={\sigma}_{xx}(L)=0\end{array}$

By integrating the previous differential equation, the beam marker obtains:

${U}_{x}(x)=\frac{\rho {\omega}^{2}}{2E}(x{L}^{2}-\frac{{x}^{3}}{3})$ ${U}_{y}={U}_{z}=0$

The movements of all points of the beam are thus written in the global reference :

$\begin{array}{}{U}_{x}(X,Y,Z)=\frac{\rho {\omega}^{2}}{2\sqrt{3}E}(r{L}^{2}-\frac{{r}^{3}}{3})\\ {U}_{y}(X,Y,Z)=\frac{\rho {\omega}^{2}}{2\sqrt{3}E}(r{L}^{2}-\frac{{r}^{3}}{3})\\ {U}_{z}(X,Y,Z)=\frac{\rho {\omega}^{2}}{2\sqrt{3}E}(r{L}^{2}-\frac{{r}^{3}}{3})\end{array}$

with $r=\sqrt{{X}^{2}+{Y}^{2}+{Z}^{2}}$

### 2.2 Baseline

Values of the three movements in the centre of the section furthest from the axis of rotation.

## 7 Summary of the results

The coincidence of the results with the analytical solution allows the load to be validated due to the inertial forces of rotation.

COQUE_3D Modelling with MEC3QU9H gives the solution with very few elements.

The SSLV105 [V3.04.105](doc:v3.04.105) test will be used to evaluate the effect of centrifugal stiffening on the element 3D, HEXA20.
