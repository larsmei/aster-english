# v2.02.133 SDLL133 - Calculation of own prestress modes of a bagged wheel

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to calculate the prestressed modes of a bagged wheel. The prestress is obtained by applying a rotation to the wheel and blocking one of the fins.

Two modellingss are performed:

- Modelling A: Modelling with elements 3D wheel as a whole,
- Modelling B: The elements 3D model is only a wing on which cyclic symmetry conditions are applied.

For each of the two modellings, a first nonlinear static calculation is performed to find the stresses, then a modal analysis to obtain the prestressed modes.

## 5 Summary of the results

The results obtained are satisfactory. A good correspondence is observed between the "global" calculation and the calculation on a sector with a condition of cyclic symmetry of double diameter.

Of course, there is no B Modelling, no first mode of its own, which has no modal diameter, nor the following two modes of its own, which correspond to modes with a modal diameter.
