# v1.01.110 ZZZZ110 - Validation of PROJ_CHAMP / ELEM command (in 2D and wired)

**Summary:**

This test case allows you to validate the PROJ_CHAMP/ELEM command in the following cases:

Projection of a temperature field (modellings $A$ and $B$ ) :

- Projection of a mesh $\mathrm{2D}$ on another mesh $\mathrm{2D}$
- Projection of a mesh $\mathrm{2D}$ on a mesh wired $\mathrm{1D}$
- Projection of a mesh $\mathrm{1D}$ wired on a mesh $\mathrm{2D}$
- Projection of a mesh $\mathrm{2D}\mathrm{axis}$ on a solid $\mathrm{3D}$

Projection of a stress field (modellings $A$ and $B$ ):

- Projection of a mesh $\mathrm{2D}\mathrm{axis}$ on a solid $\mathrm{3D}$
- Projection of a mesh $\mathrm{3D}$ on a solid $\mathrm{3D}$

### 1.2 reference solution

#### 1.2.1 Method of calculation

** Reference scale for the temperature field**

The field projected from one model to another is an analytical field of temperature, the evolution of which is as follows:

> $T=3.+X+Y$

The reference solution is identical to the projected analytical field.

**Calculation of the reference for the stress field**

The objective is to perform a change of reference, after having made a projection of a known stress field on a mesh to a mesh 3D.

> Switching from a cylindrical marker ($\mathrm{XOY}$ ) to a Cartesian marker $\mathrm{3D}$ ($\mathrm{XYZ}$ ).

The $(N/{m}^{2})$ constraint field in the axisymmetric marker ($\mathrm{OY}$ axis) is as follows:

- $\mathrm{SIXX}=2$
- $\mathrm{SIYY}=y$
- $\mathrm{SIZZ}=1$
- $\mathrm{SIXY}=0.$

The stress field in the Cartesian marker ($\mathrm{3D}$ ) is obtained by performing:

- A projection of the stress tensor evaluated on the mesh $\mathrm{2D}\mathrm{axis}$ on the mesh 3D.
- Change of the $[{\sigma}_{\mathrm{3D}}]=[P][{\sigma}_{\mathrm{cyl}}]{[P]}^{T}$ or $[P]$ stress tensor marker is the reference change matrix.
