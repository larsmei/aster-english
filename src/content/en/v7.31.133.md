# v7.31.133 WTNV133 – Undrained Triaxial with Hujeux Law

**Summary**

This test validates the implantation of monotonous deviatory mechanisms and the mechanism of cyclic consolidation of the Hujeux law. This is a triaxial test performed in undrained condition. The hydraulic coupling is taken into account, the sample is fully saturated, the skeleton and fluid are assumed to be incompressible.

The containment levels applied are $50\mathrm{kPa}$ and $200\mathrm{kPa}$ .

The results obtained with Hujeux's law are compared with results from elements GEFDYN finished code from the École Centrale Paris.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a sample of cubic form in $1/8$ representation using element $\text{HEXA20}$.

![Figure](/figures/v7.31.133/Object_1.svg)

### 1.2 Material properties

The elastic properties are:

- isotropic compressibility module: $K=516200\mathrm{kPa}$;
- Shear module: $\mu =238200\mathrm{kPa}$;
- density 1: ${\rho}_{s}=2500\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$ .

The anelastic properties of the cyclic Hujeux law are:

- power of nonlinear elastic law: ${n}_{e}=0,4$;
- $\beta =24$ ;
- $d=2,5$ ;
- $b=0,2$ ;
- friction angle: $\varphi =33°$;
- Dilatance angle: $\psi =33°$;
- critical pressure: ${P}_{\mathrm{c0}}=-1\mathrm{MPa}$;
- Reference pressure: ${P}_{\mathrm{ref}}=-1\mathrm{MPa}$;
- isotropic mechanism elastic radius: ${r}_{\mathrm{éla}}^{s}=0,001$;
- elastic radius of deviatory mechanisms: ${r}_{\mathrm{éla}}^{d}=0,005$;
- ${a}_{\mathrm{mon}}=0,008$ ;
- ${a}_{\mathrm{cyc}}=0,0001$ ;
- ${c}_{\mathrm{mon}}=0,2$ ;
- ${c}_{\mathrm{cyc}}=0,1$ ;
- ${r}_{\mathrm{hys}}=0,05$ ;
- ${r}_{\mathrm{mob}}=0,9$ ;
- ${x}_{m}=1$ ;
- $\mathrm{dila}=1$ ;

The hydraulic properties are:

- Biot coefficient: $B=1$;
- the density of water: ${\rho}_{e}=1000\mathrm{kg}/{m}^{3}$;
- viscosity: $\nu =0,001$;
- Intrinsic permeability: ${K}^{int}=1{E}^{-8}{m}^{3}/\mathrm{kg}/s$;
- Water compressibility module: ${K}_{e}=1{E}^{+12}\mathrm{Pa}$ (compressibility coefficient $1/{K}_{e}=1{E}^{-12}{\mathrm{Pa}}^{-1}$ )

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 boundary conditions

These are the symmetry conditions on the element, which represents $1/8$ of the sample. The movements are blocked on the front (${u}_{y}=0$), left (${u}_{x}=0$) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$) sides.

#### 1.3.2 Loading

**Phase 1: consolidation of sample to containment pressure** ${p}_{0}$

The sample is brought to a homogeneous state of isostatic *effective* stress ${\sigma}_{xx}^{0}={\sigma}_{yy}^{0}={\sigma}_{zz}^{0}={\sigma}_{0}$ , by placing the pressure ${\sigma}_{0}$ on the rear, right lateral and upper faces of the element, and by maintaining zero $\mathrm{PRE1}$ water pressures everywhere.

**Phase 2: Undrained triaxial loading**

To obtain undrained conditions, zero hydraulic fluxes are imposed on all surfaces.

By holding a pressure equal to ${\sigma}_{0}$ on the rear and right side faces , a load of $\Delta u$ shall be applied while moving equal to $0,02m$ on the upper side, in order to obtain a homogeneous deformation of the $2\text{\%}$ sample.

### 1.4 Results

The solutions are post-treated with point $C$ , in terms of equivalent stress of Von Mises $Q$ ($=\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}({\sigma}^{d}:{\sigma}^{d})}$ ), effective isotropic pressure $P$ ($=\frac{\text{trace}(\sigma ')}{3}$ ), plastic density deformation ${\varepsilon}_{v}^{p}$ and $({r}_{\text{iso}}^{m}+{r}_{\text{ela}}^{\text{iso},m})$ and $({r}_{\text{iso}}^{c}+{r}_{\text{ela}}^{\text{iso},c})$ isotropic nutring coefficients and $({r}_{d}^{m}+{r}_{\text{ela}}^{d,m})$ deviatory.

The validation is performed by comparison with GEFDYN solutions provided by the Ecole Centrale Paris.

In the absence of gravity, the density weights of soil and water are not involved in the problem.
