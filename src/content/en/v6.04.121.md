# v6.04.121 SSNV121 - Hyper-elastic rotation and traction of a bar

**Summary:**

This quasi-static mechanical test consists of turning $90°$ a parallelepipedic bar, subjecting it to a significant tensile force and finally allowing it to return to an unloaded state. This validates the kinematics of the large hyper-elastic deformations (command STAT_NON_LINE, keyword COMPORTEMENT), and therefore in particular the large rotations, for a relation of linear elastic behavior.

The bar is modelled by a volume element (HEXA8, modelingA) or plane QUAD4 (flat deformation hypothesis, modelingB).

The results obtained by *Code_Aster* do not differ from the theoretical solution.

## 5 Summary of the results

It appears from this test that the numerical solution coincides remarkably with the analytical solution. However, it will be noted that the strong nonlinearity due to large rotations requires relatively fine time discretization, without being penalising on accuracy since, unlike an incremental constitutive law, errors do not cumulate from one time step to the other.
