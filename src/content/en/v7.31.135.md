# v7.31.135 WTNV135 - Drained triaxial test: LETK, LKR and NLH_CSRM models

**Summary**

This test validates the LETK, LKR, and NLH_CSRM models as part of hydromechanical modelling. This is a triaxial test in drained condition.

Because of symmetry, only one of the eighth samples tested is considered to be triaxial.

The containment level is $5\mathit{MPa}$ .

Modelling A: Integration of the model by an explicit schema, with classical Modelling: model LETK.

Modelling B: Integration of the model by implicit schematic whose jacobian matrix is obtained by disturbance, with classical Modelling: model LETK.

Modelling C: Integration of the model by implicit schematic whose jacobian matrix is obtained analytically, with classical Modelling: model LETK.

Modelling D: Integration of the model by an explicit schema, with the sub-integrated Modelling: model LETK.

Modelling F: Integration of the model by implicit schematic with a jacobian matrix obtained by disturbance, increasing temperature 0°C-100°C (AFFE_CHAR_MECA): model LKR.

Modelling G: Integration of the model by implicit schematic with an analytically obtained jacobian matrix, increasing temperature 0°C-100°C (AFFE_CHAR_MECA): model LKR.

Modelling H: Validation of model NLH_CSRM, ambient temperature, international unit system.

### 1.5 Initial conditions, boundary conditions, and loading

**Phase 1:**

The sample is taken to a homogeneous state: ${\sigma}_{xx}^{0}={\sigma}_{yy}^{0}={\sigma}_{zz}^{0}$ , by imposing the corresponding containment pressure on the front, right side and upper side. The movements are blocked on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$ ), left side (${u}_{y}=0$ ) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$ ) faces.

**Phase 2:**

The blocked movements are maintained on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$), left (${u}_{y}=0$) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$) sides. On all sides, the water pressure is zero.

A displacement is applied to the upper face to obtain a ${\epsilon}_{zz}=-6\text{\%}$ deformation (counted from the beginning of phase 2). On the front and right side faces, a stress of $5\mathit{MPa}$ is imposed.

For modellings E, F and G, increasing temperature is imposed via the AFFE_CHAR_MECA control between 0°C and 50°C in phase 1 and 50°C and 100°C in phase 2.

For H Modelling, the deformation at the end of the test is 100 times higher than that obtained at the end of the containment phase: --0.00028571428571428416 * 100.
