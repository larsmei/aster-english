# v6.04.184 SSNV184 - Triaxial test with modified Hoek-Brown model

**Summary**

This test validates the elasto-plastic constitutive law from Hoek-Brown, which is modified to rock mechanics. It is a triaxial test for which calculations are performed in pure mechanics. Three containment levels are applied: $5\mathrm{MPa}$ , $12\mathrm{MPa}$ and $25\mathrm{MPa}$ . For symmetry reasons, one only cares about the eighth of a sample tested in triaxial testing.

This is a non-regression test.

### 1.3 Initial conditions, at limits and loading

The test is divided into two phases:

|  | In the first step, the sample is taken to a homogeneous state![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_13.svg). For this, the corresponding containment pressure is imposed on the front (![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_14.svg)), the right side (![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_15.svg)) and the upper side (![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_16.svg)), while the displacements are taken null on the rear (![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_17.svg)), the left side (![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_18.svg)) and the lower side (![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_19.svg)). |
| --- | --- |
|  | Once the homogeneous state is obtained, the movements are held on the rear, left lateral and lower faces and the containment pressure is always imposed on the front and right lateral faces. A displacement is imposed on the upper face (![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_20.svg)) so as to obtain a deformation![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_21.svg) equal to –25% from the beginning of the second phase (at t=2). The deformation increment is taken constant:![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_22.svg) for the modellings A and B, and![Figure](/figures/v6.04.184/Object_23.svg) for the C Modelling. |
