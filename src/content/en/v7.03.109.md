# v7.03.109 HPLV109 – Calculation of local fields of a plate pierced from a homogenized model

## 1 Purpose

This test case is designed to validate the reconstruction of local fields (*movement and temperature*) of a breakthrough plate, obtained from a homogenized global solution and correction fields generated by the `CALC_MATE_HOMO` command.

The reconstruction of local fields is carried out by means of the `REST_HOMO_LOCAL` command, which is applicable only in the context of homogenization based on an equivalent **MASSIF**.

## 2 Problem studied

The problem studied is a **thermoelastic** calculation of a steam generator tube plate.
A cell representative of the periodic pattern of the plate is meshed to determine, through `CALC_MATE_HOMO`, the correction fields and the properties of the equivalent **MASSIF**.
These are then used to establish a homogenized global solution.

## 3 Modellings considered

Four uses of the command are tested:

- **Modelization A**: Relocation of local fields (*movement and temperature*) of a thermoelastic calculation.
- **Model B**: Relocation of the local field (*movement*) of an elastic calculation.
- **Modelization C**: Local field relocation (*temperature*) of a thermal calculation.
- **Modelling D**: Relocation of local fields (*movement and temperature*) from a distributed thermoelastic calculation.

Each Modelling simultaneously tests the reconstruction of the field on the geometry of the **VER** (corresponding to 1/8 of the structure) and on the complete geometry of the **VER**.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The local mesh represents a square step pierced plate, with a period of **27.43 mm** and a bore radius of **9.7 mm**.

The overall mesh corresponds to that of a steam generator bottom, only certain values at specific points are imported for the implementation of this test.

Local and global grids use the same coordinate marker.

The representative elemental volume (VER)** is positioned on the point global $P_0(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ with the following coordinates: $x_0 = 221.005, \quad y_0 = -68.575, \quad z_0 = 595.0$

### 1.2 Properties of Materials

The plate consists of steel **16MND5**.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and Loadings

The simulated transient is fictitious and consists of a **pressure and temperature** of the primary bowl, followed by a **pressure and temperature** of the secondary bowl of the steam generator.
