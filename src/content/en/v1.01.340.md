# v1.01.340 ZZZZ340 – keyword TEMP_CONTINUE validation for AFFE_CHAR_THER

**Summary**

This test validates the TEMP_CONTINUE keyword of factor keyword ECHANGE_PAROI operators AFFE_CHAR_THER and AFFE_CHAR_THER_F [U4.44.02](doc:u4.44.02). This keyword only applies to X-FEM models, and results in the cancellation of all enriched degrees of freedom ("Heaviside" and "crack-tip") corresponding to the presence of the cracks given by the user under the keyword FISSURE. The zeroing of these degrees of freedom results in the solution of the heat equation on a domain without these cracks.

This function is validated on a simple problem of stationary linear thermal in size 2. A square plate with a temperature imposed, with a straight crack opening in the orthogonal direction at the temperature gradient is considered. The use of the keyword TEMP_CONTINUE allows you to ignore the discontinuity of the temperature field through this crack and to reduce to the solution of the "healthy" problem, the latter admitting an analytical solution.

The calculation of the temperature gradient at Gauss points of the elements X-FEM in the THER_LINEAIRE operator (field TEMP_ELGA) is also valid in this test.

Only one model is considered:

- $A$ Modelling: $\text{X-FEM}\mathrm{2D}$ (crack in the middle of elements)

The $\mathrm{2d}$ structure is a unit square: $\mathit{LX}=1m$ and $\mathrm{LY}=1m$ (Figure ).

For the test case, the same domain is also considered in the presence of a fissure, straight and right-hand, at mid-height (Figure ). ${P}^{+}$ is noted the point coordinates $\left(\mathit{LX},{\mathit{LY}}^{+}/2\right)$ (located on the upper lip), ${P}^{-}$ the point coordinates $\left(\mathit{LX},{\mathit{LY}}^{-}/2\right)$ (located on the lower lip), and $Q$ the point coordinates $\left(\mathit{LX}/2,\mathit{LY}/2\right)$ (located on the crack tip).

$\text{}\stackrel{̄}{T}{\rbrace ^{\inf}=10°C$ temperature is imposed on nodes segment $\mathit{AB}$ and $\text{}\stackrel{̄}{T}{\rbrace ^{\sup}=20°C$ segment nodes segment $\mathit{CD}$ (see Figure ).

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None (the problem is stationary)

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

This is an analytical solution. In this configuration the problem is one-dimensional and the heat equation is reduced to the following differential equation:

> $\frac{{d}^{2}T}{{\mathit{dy}}^{2}}=0$ with $T(y=0)=\text{}\stackrel{ˉ}{T}{\rbrace ^{\inf}$ and $T(y=\mathit{LY})=\text{}\stackrel{ˉ}{T}{\rbrace ^{\sup}$ conditions

This equation allows the following solution:

> $T(y)=(\text{}\stackrel{ˉ}{T}{\rbrace ^{\sup}-\text{}\stackrel{ˉ}{T}{\rbrace ^{\inf})\frac{y}{\mathit{LY}}+\text{}\stackrel{ˉ}{T}{\rbrace ^{\inf}$

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The temperature is tested at ${P}^{+}$ , ${P}^{-}$ and $Q$ points (see Figure ) :

> $T(\frac{\mathit{LY}}{2})=\frac{(\text{}\stackrel{̄}{T}{\rbrace ^{\sup}+\text{}\stackrel{̄}{T}{\rbrace ^{\inf})}{2}$ , Digital Application: $T(\frac{\mathit{LY}}{2})=15°C$

The two components (space constants) of the temperature gradient are also tested:

> ${\partial}_{x}T=0$ , Digital Application: ${\partial}_{x}T=0°{\mathit{C.m}}^{-1}$
> ${\partial}_{y}T=\frac{(\text{}\stackrel{̄}{T}{\rbrace ^{\sup}-\text{}\stackrel{̄}{T}{\rbrace ^{\inf})}{\mathit{LY}}$ , Digital Application: ${\partial}_{y}T=10°{\mathit{C.m}}^{-1}$

## 3 Modelization A: dimension 2 non-grinded crack

The crack is not meshed, so the exchange condition between the crack lips is applied using keyword FISSURE of factor keyword ECHANGE_PAROI operator AFFE_CHAR_THER [U4.44.02](doc:u4.44.02).

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

PLAN modelling of the THERMIQUE phenomenon is used. The extended elements finite method (X-FEM) is used.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

The structure is modelled by a regular mesh composed of $101\times 101$ QUAD4, respectively, along the $x$ , $y$ axes. The crack is not meshed.

### 3.3 Tested quantities and results

The quantities below are first tested in case the load that allows to impose the continuity of the temperature field (keyword TEMP_CONTINUE) is the result of the AFFE_CHAR_THER operator, then tested when the AFFE_CHAR_THER_F operator results.

The temperature is tested at ${P}^{+}$ , ${P}^{-}$ and $Q$ points (see Figure ). This is done by testing the temperature field after using POST_MAIL_XFEM and POST_CHAM_XFEM operators.

The two components of the temperature gradient stored in the TEMP_ELGA field are then tested in the result produced by the THER_LINEAIRE operator. These components are tested:

- in a Gauss point of a "Heaviside" element
- in a Gauss point of a "crack-tip" element
- in a Gauss point of an element of the type Heaviside/crack-tip

## 4 Summary of results

The objectives of this test are achieved:

- validate the TEMP_CONTINUE factor keyword ECHANGE_PAROI key word for X-FEM models from AFFE_CHAR_THER and AFFE_CHAR_THER_F operators.
- validate the calculation of the temperature gradient at the Gauss points of elements X-FEM in the THER_LINEAIRE operator
