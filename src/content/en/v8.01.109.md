# v8.01.109 FDLV109 - Calculation of coefficients added in flat flow

**Summary:**

This fluid/structure domain test implements the calculation of mass, stiffness and damping added on a flat structure subject to a confined flow that is assumed to be potential. These added coefficients are calculated for an upstream speed of $4{\mathrm{m.s}}^{-1}$ , on a 3D model for the fluid and hull for the structure. The structure is subjected to a forced bending displacement.

## 4 Summary of the results

The coefficient calculation tool added under flow (potential hypothesis) was validated on the first two bending modes of a flat structure. However, it should be noted [bib1] that the very good concordance between the proposed semi-analytical model for comparison and the numerical calculation is only obtained if the plate is sufficiently long, the semi-analytical model being in fact only a solution close to the problem.
