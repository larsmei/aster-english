# v7.31.137 WTNV137 - Drained triaxial test with VISC_DRUC_PRAG

**Summary**

This test validates the VISC_DRUC_PRAG model as part of a hydromechanical Modelling process. It is a triaxial test under drained conditions.

Because of symmetry, only one of the eighth samples tested is considered to be triaxial.

The containment level is $5\mathrm{MPa}$ .

### 1.3 Initial conditions, boundary conditions, and loading

**Phase 1:**

The sample is taken to a homogeneous state: ${\sigma}_{xx}^{0}={\sigma}_{yy}^{0}={\sigma}_{zz}^{0}$ , by imposing the corresponding containment pressure on the front, right side and upper side. The movements are blocked on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$ ), left side (${u}_{y}=0$ ) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$ ) faces.

**Phase 2:**

The blocked movements are maintained on the rear (${u}_{x}=0$), left (${u}_{y}=0$) and lower (${u}_{z}=0$) sides. On all sides, the water pressure is zero.

A displacement is applied to the upper face to obtain a ${\varepsilon}_{zz}=-0.06$ deformation (counted from the beginning of phase 2). On the front and right side faces, a stress of $5\mathrm{MPa}$ is imposed.
