# v3.02.304 SSLP304 - Square orthotropic plate in uniaxial traction out of the axes d-orthotropy

**Summary:**

This test represents the static calculation of a square plate, of orthotropic elastic material, with the axis d-orthotropy inclined 30 degrees from the base edge, subjected to uniaxial traction. It validates the correct consideration of orthotropic elastic materials and the associated change of reference. 4 modellings are used: C_PLAN with mesh cells QUAD8 and TRIA6, in a first marker, C_PLAN in a second marker, COQUE_3D with mesh cells QUAD9 and TRIA7, in small displacements and COQUE_3D in large displacements. The displacements and stresses obtained are compared to an analytical reference solution.

The first two modellingss of this test are from the independent validation of version 3 of *Code_Aster* (linear static lot).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A square plate consisting of an orthotropic material inclined 30 degrees from the $\mathrm{AB}$ edge.

![Figure](/figures/v3.02.304/Shape1.gif)

With $b=1m$ , any thickness (planes) angle of orthotropy: $\theta$ = 30 degrees.

### 1.2 Material properties

The properties of the materials constituting the plate are:

orthotropic elastic:

${E}_{L}=4.E10\mathrm{Pa}$

${E}_{T}=1.E10\mathrm{Pa}$

${G}_{LT}=0.45E10\mathrm{Pa}$

${G}_{\mathrm{TN}}=0.35E10\mathrm{Pa}$

${\mathit{NU}}_{LT}=0.3$

The $L$ axis is inclined 30 degrees from $\mathrm{AB}$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

- Au point $A$ : $\mathrm{DX}=0$ , $\mathrm{DY}=0$
- Au point $B$ : $\mathrm{DX}=0$ ,
- Linear load distributed: $\mathrm{Fx}={10}^{4}\mathrm{Pa}$ on $\mathrm{BC}$
- Linear load distributed: $\mathrm{Fx}=-{10}^{4}\mathrm{Pa}$ on $\mathrm{DA}$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

Not applicable.

## 7 Summary of the results

The results of the four modellings are very close to the analytical solution: up to 0.015% d

This test therefore validates the consideration of orthotropic elasticity.
