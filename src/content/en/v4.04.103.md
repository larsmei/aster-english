# v4.04.103 TPLV103 - Infinite Cylinder steady-state thermal analysis Anisotropic

**Summary:**

This test, which concerns the stationary and transient linear thermal, is intended to test cylindrical anisotropy.

Two modellings are made:

- a first volume,
- a second in 2D plan.

The results obtained are in perfect agreement with the analytical values.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v4.04.103/10000A9600002285000014427CFC1AE12E2BB98B.svg)

In the reference point (X0, Y0, Z0), the coordinates are:

| $C(0;2;1)$ | $D(2;0;0)$ | $E(0;2;0)$ | $F(1;0;1)$ | $O(0;0;0)$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $A(2;0;1)$ | $B(\sqrt{2};\sqrt{2};1)$ | $G(0;1;1)$ | $H(1;0;0)$ | $I(0;1;0)$ |

### 1.2 Material properties

Anisotropic material, preferred direction according to the axes of the cylindrical marker $({u}_{r},{u}_{\theta},{u}_{z})$

${\lambda}_{r}=1.$ ${\lambda}_{\theta}=0.5$ ${\lambda}_{z}=3.W/m°C$ $\rho {C}_{P}=2J/{m}^{3}°C$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

| $\mathrm{AFHD}$ side: | Temperature imposed on $100°C$ |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathrm{CGIE}$ side: | Temperature at $0°C$ |
| Other side: | Neumann |

### 1.4 Initial conditions

To do this stationary calculation, a transient calculation is made for which the boundary conditions are constant over time. This allows testing the elementary calculations of mass and rigidity in the 1st member and the 2nd.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

analytical solution.

Temperature varying linearly in $\theta$ .

in $(r,\theta ,z)$

$T(\theta )=[T(C)-T(A)].\frac{2}{\pi}.\theta +T(A)$

$\phi (A).Y=-{\lambda}_{}\theta .\frac{1}{r}.\frac{\partial T}{\partial \theta }=-{\lambda}_{\theta}.\frac{1}{r(A)}[T(C)-T(A)].\frac{2}{\pi}$

### 2.2 Baseline

Temperatures at $A$ and $B$ points, following $Y$ to point $A$.

$T(A)=100.$ $T(B)=50.$ $\phi (A).Y=\frac{100.}{2\pi }\approx 15.915$

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

$\theta$ of the time scheme imposed on $1$ to test the calculation of the second member in transition.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Set to 250 HEXA8 (5 elements on $\mathrm{HD}$ and $\mathrm{DM}$ edges, 10 elements on $\mathrm{DF}$ by IDEAS.

### 3.3 Test values

| **Identification** | **Reference** |
| --- | --- |
| T(A) * N1 | 100 |
| T(B) N133 | 50 |
| $\phi (A).Y$ | 15.9155 |

- : temperature imposed

### 3.4 Remarks

The symmetry of the mesh makes the $T$ solution to the nodes of the mesh accurate, but in the elements the extrapolated solution is not accurate.

The flow is calculated by *Aster* at the elements integration points and then reported to nodes by extrapolation. Since the flow is not uniform, this extrapolation results in a difference between calculation and reference.
