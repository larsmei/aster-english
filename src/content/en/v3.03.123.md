## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The node radial displacement of the outer pressure sphere is given by:

${U}_{r}=B.r+\frac{C}{{r}^{2}}$

With

$B=\frac{1-2\nu }{E}.\frac{{r}_{e}^{3}}{{r}_{i}^{3}-{r}_{e}^{3}}.P$ et $C=\frac{1+\nu }{2E}.\frac{{r}_{i}^{3}{r}_{e}^{3}}{{r}_{i}^{3}-{r}_{e}^{3}}.P$

où ${r}_{i}=R-\frac{t}{2}$ et ${r}_{e}=R+\frac{t}{2}$

### 2.2 Baseline

Moving the point $A$ following $x$ , moving the point $B$ following $y$ , moving the point $C$ following $z$ .

## 4 Summary of the results

The results are consistent with the reference solution.

One might expect to find exactly the same displacement at the three points $A$ , $B$ and $C$ . The difference to point $C$ comes from the non-symmetry of the mesh. The mesh is slightly more distorted around this point, which explains the decrease in accuracy, which nevertheless remains very good.
