# v1.01.156 ZZZZ156 – REAC_NODA verification after DYNA_NON_LINE

**Summary:**

The modellings are made for `POUTRE` and validate the calculation of `REAC_NODA` after an [DYNA_NON_LINE](doc:u4.53.01).
'POU_D_E', 'POU_D_T', 'POU_D_TG', 'POU_D_EM', 'POU_D_TGM' beams are tested for `DDL`: 'DX','DY','DZ','DRX','DRY','DRZ'.

The symmetry of the problem (even intertwined in Y and Z, ...) results in identical or very similar values depending on the `DDL` treated with beam modelling.

Other base tests check the calculations with physical values.

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
