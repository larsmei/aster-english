# v2.01.022 SDLD22 - Transient of a mass-spring system with 8 degrees of freedom with viscous shock absorber

**Summary:**

The mechanical structure is composed of a linear unidirectional set of spring masses with viscous shock absorbers and subject to a niche transient excitation.

Two modellings are developed. The first retains only the degree of freedom in axial translation of the masses, the second considers axial translation and rotation.

This problem allows testing:

- Discrete elements (masses, springs, shock absorbers) in translation-rotation,
- the definition of a transient point excitation force,
- the calculation of the transient response by modal recombination and the resumption with initial conditions (modelization A),
- the direct transient response calculation with the adaptive time step scheme (modellings B and C).

The results obtained (movement field, speeds) are in good agreement with the results of the VPCS guide, taken for reference solution.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution comes from the VPCS guide.

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The numerical integration chosen to achieve this solution is based on a finite difference integration scheme, of the $\beta$ -Newmark improved method type, with time step of $\mathrm{0.001s}$ [bib2].

$\left[\frac{1}{\Delta {t}^{2}}M+\frac{1}{2\Delta t}C+\frac{1}{3}K\right]{u}_{n+2}=\frac{1}{3}({F}_{n+2}+{F}_{n+1}+{F}_{n})+\left[\frac{2}{\Delta {t}^{2}}M-\frac{1}{3}K\right]{u}_{n+1}+\left[\frac{1}{\Delta {t}^{2}}M+\frac{1}{2\Delta t}C-\frac{1}{3}K\right]{u}_{n}$

## 4 modellings B et C

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

The modellings B and C are the same except that the B Modelling uses elements DIS_TR while the C Modelling uses elements 2D_DIS_TR.

This Modelling allows, in addition to a new use of modal recombination, the validation of direct adaptive step integration.

**element discrete rigidity in translation and rotation**

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
|  | and rigidity matrices | K_TR_D_L | K_TR_L |
|  | and damping matrices | A_TR_D_L | A_TR_L |

Boundary conditions and blocked directions:

| in all nodes | DDL_IMPO: | ( TOUT:’OUI’ DY: 0. , DZ: 0. ) ( TOUT:’OUI’ DRX: 0. DRY: 0 DRZ: 0) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nodes ends |  | ( GROUP_NO: AB DX: 0. ) |

- Integration by direct integration with the algorithmADAPT_ORDRE2, time step maximum ${10}^{-3}s$ .
