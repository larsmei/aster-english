# v3.04.305 SSLV305 - Calculation of pressure of an earthquake tank according to Eurocode 8

**Summary:**

This test case is intended to validate the DEFI_PRES_EC8 macro-command.

## 1 Modelling A

### 1.1 Purpose of modelling

This Modelling is valid for the calculation of the various components of the total pressure of the DEFI_PRES_EC8 macro-command at points at different altitudes and at azimuth 0, compared with Framatome shared results.
It also validates:
- calculation of the total pressure (in the two available Newmark combinations "PC+" and PC-) from the values of the different components
- calculation of the different pressure components for different azimuths of 0 and the total pressure associated
- calculation of the different components on the bottom of the container (except the rigid impulsive pressure, validated in model B)
- taking into account different thicknesses according to the points assessed

In the next section, we only detail the comparison test with the Framatome references, the other validations being computer validations whose description is not of interest.

### 1.2 Parameters

The container is defined by:

- Z_FOND = 0
- RAYON = 4.820
- HAUT_EAU = 9.250
- RHO_EAU = 1000

The gravity $g$ is 9.81. The density of the tank walls is 7800 and the thickness is 0.006.

Accelerations are as follows:

- ACCE_SP_H = 0.241 $g$
- ACCE_FLEX_H_N = 0.51252007 $g$
- ACCE_FLEX_V = 0.33572503 $g$
- ACCE_SP_V = 0.16067 $g$
- ACCE_CONV_H = 0.085 $g$

## 2 Modelling B

### 2.1 Purpose of modelling

The purpose of this model is to validate the calculation of the rigid impulsive pressure on the bottom of the tank, i.e. for rays smaller than the radius of the tank.

### 2.2 Baseline

To validate this, we have abacums from Eurocode 8 that give three pressure curves depending on the $r/R$ ratio.
The 3 curves correspond to three different values of $\gamma = H/R$ with $H$ the water height and $R$ the tank radius.

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.305/abaques_pi.png)

In abscisses, this is the impulsive pressure normalized to $\rho_l R ZPA_ h$, with $\rho_l$ the density of the water and $ZPA_ h$ the rigid horizontal impulsive acceleration (opérande ACCE_SP_H).
These curves were digitized to provide reference values.

| $r/R$ | $P_i^{adim} ,\gamma=3$ | $P_i^{adim}, \gamma=1$ | $P_i^{adim}, \gamma=0.5$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 0.1 | 0.10176 | 0.06342 | 0.01769 |
| 0.2 | 0.19911 | 0.12684 | 0.03687 |
| 0.3 | 0.29941 | 0.19321 | 0.05899 |
| 0.4 | 0.3997 | 0.25958 | 0.08554 |
| 0.5 | 0.5 | 0.33038 | 0.11504 |
| 0.6 | 0.60029 | 0.40265 | 0.15339 |
| 0.7 | 0.70058 | 0.48082 | 0.20058 |
| 0.8 | 0.80235 | 0.56342 | 0.25958 |
| 0.9 | 0.90412 | 0.65191 | 0.33038 |
| 1 | 1 | 0.75073 | 0.42477 |

### 2.3 Parameters

The parameters of the Modelling are retained Exepted the radius fixed to $R=H/\gamma$ for the three values of $\gamma$ to be tested.

### 2.4 Tested quantities and results

The normalized pressure value for the 11 $r/R$ ratios in the table above and the 3 $\gamma$ values shall be tested.
Because values are not of interest, the figure comparing the results with the references is preferred.

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.305/figure_pir_norma.png)

### 2.5 Remarks

The results are very close to the reference, which validates the calculation of the rigid impulsive pressure on the bottom of the container.

## 3 Modelling C

### 3.1 Purpose of modelling

This Modelling is intended to validate the use of the DEFI_PRES_EC8 formula with FORCE_COQUE_FO from AFFE_CHAR_MECA.

### 3.2 Parameters

The parameters of model A are retained.

### 3.3 Details

The mesh is a cylinder with a elements DKT modeled bottom (and ceiling).
The bottom of the tank is clamped.
The pressure from DEFI_FONC_EC8 is applied. The calculation is elastic.

### 3.4 Tested quantities and results

After checking that the deformed was in the overall form expected, non-regression tests were added at two points in the middle of the water height, the first of azimuth 0 and the second of azimuth 180°.
The values don't matter here because they're non-regression tests.
