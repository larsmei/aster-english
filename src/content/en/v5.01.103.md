# v5.01.103 SDND103 - Axial dynamically-sustained pole

**Summary**

The answer is calculated for a post subjected to any seismic loading. The post is modelled by an unabated mass-spring system, its restraint with the ground by an effort-displacement nonlinearity.

The discrete element in traction-compression, the calculation of eigenmodes and the calculation of the transient response by modal recombination with consideration of non-linearity of the force-displacement type are tested. The initial speed is taken not zero and the load is of the acceleration type imposed on the ground.

The results obtained are in very good agreement with the reference results, which are analytical results.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The system consists of a ground-based pole subjected to seismic stress. It is modelled by a mass, its restraint with the ground by a ${k}_{0}$ spring whose behavioral relationship reflects an effort-displacement nonlinearity.

![Figure](/figures/v5.01.103/100005CC0000082A000011E125935A3D1510106A.svg)

Characteristics of the pole:

Length: $L=\mathrm{2 }m$;

section: $S=0,3{m}^{2}$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

Pole mass: $m=\mathrm{450 }\mathrm{kg}$ .

spring stiffness de restraint: ${k}_{\mathrm{0 }}={10}^{5}N/m$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

**boundary conditions**

The only travels allowed are $X$ axis translation: $\mathrm{dy}=\mathrm{dz}=0$.

The corrective force ${F}_{c}$ due to nonlinearity of the soil is defined by the following relationship:

${F}_{c}(x)=\frac{f({x}_{\mathrm{seuil}})}{{x}_{\mathrm{seuil}}}-f(x)$ with, if $x>{x}_{\mathit{seuil}}$ , $f(x)={k}_{0}\left[1–\frac{\mid x\mid }{{x}_{0}}\right]x$

We take ${x}_{\mathrm{seuil}}={10}^{-6}m$, ${k}_{0}={10}^{5}N/m$ and ${x}_{0}=0,1m$.

Therefore, the RELA_EFFO_DEPL operator's key word is used to specify the function: ${F}_{c}(x)=\frac{{k}_{0}}{{x}_{0}}\mathit{x.}[\mid x\mid -{x}_{\mathit{seuil}}]$ if $∣x∣>{x}_{\mathit{seuil}}$

> ${F}_{c}(x)=0$                     si $∣x∣\le {x}_{\mathit{seuil}}$

**Charge**

The ground is subjected to an acceleration $\gamma (t)$ in the direction $x$ , built so that the displacement of the mass-spring system is sinusoidal $x=\mathrm{a.sin}(\omega t)$ with $a=0,01$ and $\omega =\pi /4$ .

### 1.4 Initial conditions

In the initial state, the system is released from its equilibrium position with a speed ${v}_{\mathrm{0 }}$: at $t=0$ , $\mathrm{dx}(0)=0$ , ${v}_{0}=\mathrm{dx}/\mathrm{dt}(0)=a.\omega$ *.*

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

This test is further developed in the reference [bib1].

The fundamental equation of the dynamics, in relative movement of the mass-spring system relative to the ground is written: $\ddot{x}+\frac{k(x)}{m}x=\gamma (t)$ .

For a displacement of the form $x=\mathrm{a.sin}(\omega t)$ and $\ddot{x}=-a{\omega}^{2}\sin(\omega t)$ , the accelerogram form is obtained from the equation of the movement:

$\gamma (t)=a\sin(\omega t)\left[-{\omega}^{2}+\frac{{k}_{0}}{m}(1-\frac{\mid a\sin(\omega t)\mid }{{x}_{0}})\right]$

The basic frequency ${f}_{0}$ of the unamortized oscillator is ${f}_{0}=\frac{1}{2\pi }\sqrt{\frac{{k}_{0}}{m}}$.

### 2.2 Baseline

Basic frequency ${f}_{0}$ of unamortized oscillator.

Travels related to 2, 6, 10, 14 and 18 seconds.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

None if Duhamel's integral is calculated analytically [bib2].
