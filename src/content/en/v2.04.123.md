# v2.04.123 SDLV123 - Calculation of Elastodynamic G in infinite medium for a flat crack of finite length

**Summary**

This is a mechanical problem of rupture in a medium in a state of flat deformation in transient elastodynamic regime. A crack of length $\mathrm{2a}$ is considered to be immersed in an infinite supposed medium. A uniform pressure is imposed on the lips of the crack, which reaches a bearing in a time of 1 micro-second (shock). This test allows to calculate the rate of $G$ energy restitution over time, taking into account the terms of inertia.

The value of the test is the stability of $G$ in different crowns and the comparison to an exact analytical solution up to the time $t=\mathrm{2a}/{V}_{C}$ , where ${V}_{C}$ represents the speed of longitudinal waves.

This test contains a flat deformation model and a three-dimensional model. Absorbent boundary conditionss on the solid boundaries help to avoid wave feedback and thus simulate an infinite medium.

Differences of $G$ calculation on different crowns compared to reference solution does not exceed 1.4%.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry and modelling

It is a flat $a=\mathrm{20m}$ half-length crack.

The supposed infinite medium is modelled by a square side of $\mathrm{200m}$. The planes $xz$ and $yz$ being symmetry planes, one represents only a quarter of the structure (square of $100m$ on $100m$), and a half lip of crack length $a=20m$.

An absorbing border is present on the border1 and border2 groups to model the infinite medium.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The reference solution is from THAU and LU [bib1] and the book of L.B. FREUND [bib2]. The following figure represents the infinite medium and not the geometry of the test.

![Figure](/figures/v2.04.123/Object_3.png)

${K}_{I}^{D}(t)=\mathrm{2P}H(t)\frac{\sqrt{1-2\nu }}{(1-\nu )}\sqrt{\frac{{c}_{d}t}{\pi}},0<t<\mathrm{2a}/{c}_{d}$

The expression of energy release rate is as follows:

$G(t)=\frac{{K}_{I}^{{D}^{2}}(t)}{E}(1-{\nu}^{2})={\mathrm{4P}}^{2}H(t)\frac{(1-2\nu )(1+\nu )}{(1-\nu )E}\frac{{c}_{d}t}{\pi},0<t<\mathrm{2a}/{c}_{d}$

$G=\frac{(1-{\nu}^{2})}{E}{K}_{1}^{2}$ with ${K}_{1}=\frac{\alpha E}{P(1-\nu )}{T}_{0}\sqrt{\mathit{Pa}}$

either: $G=\frac{(1-{\nu}^{2})}{P{(1-\nu )}^{2}}{\alpha}^{2}{\mathit{ET}}_{0}^{2}a$

### 2.2 Baseline

The reference result is therefore: $G=5.9115{10}^{-7}N/\mathit{mm}$
