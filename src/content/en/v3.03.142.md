## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

For each configuration, 8 verification points (N,M) at ELU and 6 points at ELS are calculated manually by imposing the section equilibrium equations at normal effort and at the moment (see `Veri_ferraillage` [r7.04.05](doc:r7.04.05)).
The points chosen are:

#### 2.1.1 Reference points to l-ELU

- point A - Pivot A: uniform traction;
- point B - Pivots A and B: partially compressed section + positive moment;
- point C - Pivot B: partially compressed section + elastic deformation lower frame limit;
- point D - Pivot B: fully compressed section + zero deformation of the lower fibre of the concrete;
- point E - pivot C: uniform compression;

For the part of the diagram corresponding to the negative moment:

- point D' - Pivot B: fully compressed section + zero deformation of the top fibre of the concrete;
- point C' - pivot B: partially compressed section + elastic deformation upper frame limit;
- point B' - Pivots A and B: partially compressed section + negative moment.

#### 2.1.2 Reference points to l-ELS

- point A - Pivot A: uniform traction;
- point B - Pivots A and B: partially compressed section + positive moment;
- point C - Pivot B: fully compressed section + zero stress in the lower fibre of concrete;
- point D - Pivot C: uniform compression;

For the part of the diagram corresponding to the negative moment:

- point C' - Pivot B: fully compressed section + zero stress of the top fibre of the concrete;
- point B' - Pivots A and B: partially compressed section + negative moment.

Given the direction of effort, the critical facet is obvious.

### 2.2 Details of manual verification calculation

See `Veri_ferraillage` [r7.04.05](doc:r7.04.05) for more details on equilibrium equations and pivots.

#### 2.2.1 Calculations at l-ELU

Details of the calculation for the default case (configuration 1) will be given, with similar calculations for the other configurations.
The geometric properties of the reinforced concrete section, and the mechanical properties of the materials are reported in part v3.03.142_concrete properties.
the following quantities:

- Compression strength of concrete: $f_{cd} = f_{ck}/{\gamma}_{c}$;
- Ultimate deformation of compressed concrete: ${\varepsilon}_{cu} = 0.0035$;
- Compressed concrete deformation limit for the rectangle domain: ${\varepsilon}_{c2} = 0.002$;
- Steel design strength: $f_{yd} = f_{yk}/{\gamma}_{s}$;
- ultimate deformation of steel calculation: ${\varepsilon}_{ud} = 0.045$;
- Elastic deformation limit of ${\varepsilon}_{yd} = f_{yd}/E_{s}$ steel.

##### 2.2.1.1 Pivot A: uniform traction

The upper and lower reinforcements are stretched to the ultimate ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{ud}}$ deformation limit of the steel and the concrete does not resume traction.

**Resistant normal effort:**

${N}_{\mathit{Rd}}=-({A}_{s,inf} + {A}_{s,sup})\times {f}_{\mathit{yd}}$

**Resistant time:**

${M}_{\mathit{Rd}}={A}_{s,inf}\times {f}_{\mathit{yd}}\times (h/2 - {c}_{inf}) - {A}_{s,sup}\times {f}_{\mathit{yd}}\times (h/2 - {c}_{sup})$

##### 2.2.1.2 Pivots A and B: partially compressed section + positive moment;

The lower reinforcements are stretched to the ultimate deformation limit of ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{ud}}$ steel and the top fibre of the concrete is compressed to the ultimate deformation limit of ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}}$ concrete.

The quantities of interest are:

- useful height: ${h} = d -  {c}_{\mathit{inf}}$;
- depth of neutral axis relative to the top fibre of concrete: ${x} = d\times{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}}/({\varepsilon}_{\mathit{ud}} + {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}})$;
- deformation of the upper reinforcements: ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,sup}}= {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}}\times(1 - {c}_{sup}/x)$;
- stress in the upper frame: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}}= min(E_s\times\lvert{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,sup}}\rvert, f_{yd})\times sgn({\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,sup}})$;
- concrete fibre depth with deformation equal to ${\varepsilon}_{c2}$: $x_{c2} = x\times {\varepsilon}_{c2}/{\varepsilon}_{cu}$;
- compression force in concrete: $N_{b} = \int_{0}^{x_{c2}} (f_{cd}\times (1-y/x\times \varepsilon_{cu}/\varepsilon_{c2})^2) \, \mathrm{d}y + f_{cd}\times (x - x_{c2})$
- moment in concrete: $M_{b} = \int_{0}^{x_{c2}} (f_{cd}\times (1-y/x\times \varepsilon_{cu}/\varepsilon_{c2})^2\times(y + h/2 -x)) \, \mathrm{d}y + f_{cd}\times (x - x_{c2})\times (h/2 -(x-x_{c2})/2)$

**Resistant normal effort:**

${N}_{\mathit{Rd}}=-{A}_{s,inf}\times f_{yd} + {A}_{s,sup}\times{\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}} + N_b$

**Resistant time:**

${M}_{\mathit{Rd}}={A}_{s,inf}\times {f}_{\mathit{yd}}\times (h/2 - {c}_{inf}) + {A}_{s,sup}\times \sigma_{s,sup}\times (h/2 - {c}_{sup}) + M_b$

##### 2.2.1.3 Pivot B: partially compressed section + elastic deformation lower frame limit

The lower reinforcements are stretched to the elastic deformation limit of ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{yd}}$ steel and the top fibre of the concrete is compressed to the ultimate deformation limit of ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}}$ concrete.

The quantities of interest are:

- useful height: ${h} = d -  {c}_{\mathit{inf}}$;
- depth of neutral axis relative to the top fibre of concrete: ${x} = d\times{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}}/({\varepsilon}_{\mathit{yd}} + {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}})$;
- deformation of the upper reinforcements: ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,sup}}= {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}}\times(1 - {c}_{sup}/x)$;
- stress in the upper frame: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}}= min(E_s\times\lvert{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,sup}}\rvert, f_{yd})\times sgn({\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,sup}})$;
- concrete fibre depth with deformation equal to ${\varepsilon}_{c2}$: $x_{c2} = x\times {\varepsilon}_{c2}/{\varepsilon}_{cu}$;
- compression force in concrete: $N_{b} = \int_{0}^{x_{c2}} (f_{cd}\times (1-y/x\times \varepsilon_{cu}/\varepsilon_{c2})^2) \, \mathrm{d}y + f_{cd}\times (x - x_{c2})$
- moment in concrete: $M_{b} = \int_{0}^{x_{c2}} (f_{cd}\times (1-y/x\times \varepsilon_{cu}/\varepsilon_{c2})^2\times(y + h/2 -x)) \, \mathrm{d}y + f_{cd}\times (x - x_{c2})\times (h/2 -(x-x_{c2})/2)$

**Resistant normal effort:**

${N}_{\mathit{Rd}}=-{A}_{s,inf}\times f_{yd} + {A}_{s,sup}\times{\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}} + N_b$

**Resistant time:**

${M}_{\mathit{Rd}}={A}_{s,inf}\times {f}_{\mathit{yd}}\times (h/2 - {c}_{inf}) + {A}_{s,sup}\times \sigma_{s,sup}\times (h/2 - {c}_{sup}) + M_b$

##### 2.2.1.4 Pivot B: fully compressed section + zero deformation of the lower fibre of concrete

The top fibre of the concrete is compressed to the ultimate deformation limit of the ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}}$ concrete and the depth of the neutral axis is $x=h$.

The quantities of interest are:

- useful height: ${h} = d -  {c}_{\mathit{inf}}$;
- deformation of the upper reinforcements: ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,sup}}= {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}}\times(1 - {c}_{sup}/x)$;
- deformation of the lower reinforcements: ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,inf}}= {\varepsilon}_{\mathit{cu}}\times{c}_{inf}/x$;
- stress in the upper frame: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}}= min(E_s\times\lvert{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,sup}}\rvert, f_{yd})\times sgn({\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,sup}})$;
- stress in lower frame: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{s,inf}}= min(E_s\times\lvert{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,inf}}\rvert, f_{yd})\times sgn({\varepsilon}_{\mathit{s,inf}})$;
- concrete fibre depth with deformation equal to ${\varepsilon}_{c2}$: $x_{c2} = x\times {\varepsilon}_{c2}/{\varepsilon}_{cu}$;
- compression force in concrete: $N_{b} = \int_{0}^{x_{c2}} (f_{cd}\times (1-y/x\times \varepsilon_{cu}/\varepsilon_{c2})^2) \, \mathrm{d}y + f_{cd}\times (x - x_{c2})$
- moment in concrete: $M_{b} = \int_{0}^{x_{c2}} (f_{cd}\times (1-y/x\times \varepsilon_{cu}/\varepsilon_{c2})^2\times(y + h/2 -x)) \, \mathrm{d}y + f_{cd}\times (x - x_{c2})\times (h/2 -(x-x_{c2})/2)$

**Resistant normal effort:**

${N}_{\mathit{Rd}}={A}_{s,inf}\times{\sigma}_{\mathit{s,inf}} + {A}_{s,sup}\times{\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}} + N_b$

**Resistant time:**

${M}_{\mathit{Rd}}=-{A}_{s,inf}\times \sigma_{s,inf}\times (h/2 - {c}_{inf}) + {A}_{s,sup}\times \sigma_{s,sup}\times (h/2 - {c}_{sup}) + M_b$

##### 2.2.1.5 Pivot C: uniform compression

The section is subjected to a uniform compression deformation equal to ${\varepsilon}_{\mathit{c2}}$. The quantities of interest are:

- stress in the upper frame: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}}= min(E_s\times{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{c2}}, f_{yd})$;
- stress in lower frame: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{s,inf}}= min(E_s\times{\varepsilon}_{\mathit{c2}}, f_{yd})$;

**Resistant normal effort:**

${N}_{\mathit{Rd}}={A}_{s,inf}\times{\sigma}_{\mathit{s,inf}} + {A}_{s,sup}\times{\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}} + f_{cd}\times h$

**Resistant time:**

${M}_{\mathit{Rd}}=-{A}_{s,inf}\times \sigma_{s,inf}\times (h/2 - {c}_{inf}) + {A}_{s,sup}\times \sigma_{s,sup}\times (h/2 - {c}_{sup})$

##### 2.2.2.1 Pivot A: uniform traction

The tensile stress in the lower reinforcements is equal to ${\sigma}_{\mathit{s,max}}$ and the compression stress in the upper fibre of the concrete is equal to ${\sigma}_{\mathit{c,max}}$.

- useful height: ${h} = d -  {c}_{\mathit{inf}}$;

- useful height: ${h} = d -  {c}_{\mathit{inf}}$;

- stress in lower frame: ${\sigma}_{\mathit{s,inf}}= n\times{\sigma}_{\mathit{c,max}} \times(c_{inf})/x$

**Resistant normal effort:**

${N}_{\mathit{Rd}}={A}_{s,inf}\times {\sigma}_{s,inf} + {A}_{s,sup}\times{\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}} + x\times {\sigma_{c,max}}/2$

**Resistant time:**

${M}_{\mathit{Rd}}=-{A}_{s,inf}\times {\sigma}_{s,inf}\times (h/2-c_{inf}) + {A}_{s,sup}\times{\sigma}_{\mathit{s,sup}}\times (h/2-c_{sup}) + x\times {\sigma_{c,max}}/2\times (h/2-x/3)$

##### 2.2.2.4 Pivot C: uniform compression
