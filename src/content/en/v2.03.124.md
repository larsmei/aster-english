# v2.03.124 SDLS124 – Exhaust plate in flexion under dynamic loading

**Summary:**

This test is intended to compare the consideration of the excentre of the hulls in Code_Aster and Europlexus. It is also used to provide two Europlexus test cases with reference results for the validation of the excipted hulls.

This comparison is made on an excreted plate, clamped on one side, subject to dynamic loading leading to bending. Comparisons are made for a quadrangle mesh and a triangle mesh.

The corresponding Europlexus tests are *bm_str_q4gs_exce* for QUAD4 and *bm_str_t3gs_exce* for TRIA3.

## 1 Description

### 1.1 Geometry

The design of the study is a square plate on the side $c=\mathrm{1m}$ and $\mathit{ep}=\mathrm{0.05m}$ thickness. This plate is excreted from $d=\mathrm{0.05m}$.

![Figure](/figures/v2.03.124/10000000000001D1000000C7144816A2AA7CA7CF.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The properties of the steel for the plate are given in the following table.

| **Material** | **Storage** |
| --- | --- |
| **Young's modulus** | $2\times {10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$ |
| **Poisson's ratio** | $0.2$ |
| **Volume size** | $7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$ |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

#### 1.3.1 boundary conditions

The P1P4 segment of the plate is blocked at 0 for $\mathit{DX}$, $\mathit{DZ}$ and $\mathit{DRY}$ components, and the $\mathit{DY}$ component is blocked at $\mathit{P1}$.

#### 1.3.2 Loading:

The load consists of applying a linear force on $Z$ on the $\mathit{P2P3}$ segment, going from $0$ to $50000N/m$ in $0.01$ second and then maintained constant.

## 2 reference solution

The reference solution is given by the results obtained for the same calculation with Europlexus.

It is specified that the corresponding Europlexus tests will use the Code_Aster results as a reference.

## 7 Executive summary

When looking at the curves, we see that the results of the two calculation codes (code_aster and europlexus) are very similar. In Modelling A, we can see a slight phase shift on the displacement curves. In Modelling B, there is not this phase shift but the $\mathit{DZ}$ error at the second peak is approaching 1%. modellings with homogenized plates are also in agreement.

It can be concluded that the treatment of the excentre in Code_Aster corresponds to what is done in Europlexus.
