# v7.33.109 WTNA109 - Unsaturation of airless porous medium on unit cell

**Summary:**

This test represents the desaturation of a porous airless medium (only liquid water and steam) by heating. It is a test case non-regression. It is the axisymmetric version of the test case WTNP115.

Modelization A:

- AXIS_THVD modelling (light)
- Matching: LIQU_VAPE law

Modelling B:

- AXIS_THVS modelling (selective)
- Matching: LIQU_VAPE law

This documentation is deliberately succinct.
