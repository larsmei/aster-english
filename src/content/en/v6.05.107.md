# v6.05.107 SSNS107 – Cylinder with pressure-rebar

**Summary:**

A cylinder with pressure-reinforced frames is considered. More precisely, a hollow cylinder, 3D, is completed on its outer face with a circumferential frame (skin 2D). This structure is charged with an internal pressure. The calculation is elastic linear.

The simulation is compared with an analytical solution to validate modellings GRILLE_MEMBRANE reinforcements on a structure.

All numeric values are given in SI unit.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v6.05.107/Object_1.svg)

![Figure](/figures/v6.05.107/Object_2.svg)

The cylinder has an internal ${R}_{1}=10m$ radius, an external ${R}_{2}=20m$ radius, a height $h=1m$. The circumferential frame has a section per unit length of $e=1{m}^{2}/\mathrm{ml}$.

### 1.2 Material properties

The concrete (drain cylinder) is $E=2E+10\mathrm{Pa}$ and $\nu =0.2$; the steel (circumferential frame) $E=2E+11\mathrm{Pa}$ , $\nu =0$ , ${E}_{t}=2.E+09$ (dangerous module), ${\sigma}_{y}=2.E+11\mathrm{Pa}$ (very large plasticity constraint to stay in the elastic range).

The rebar is in its vertical main direction and a cross section per linear meter of $1m$.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

Only one section of the cylinder is interested.

The following conditions are imposed:

1. a normal zero displacement on the side faces of the cylinder angular section ($\mathrm{SG}$ and $\mathrm{SD}$ on the schematic figure) (zero ortho-radial displacement)
2. a zero vertical displacement on the lower side of the cylinder section (single support)
3. a pressure imposed by ${10}^{6}\mathrm{Pa}$ on the inner side of the cylinder section ($\mathrm{SI}$ ) on the schematic figure (internal pressure)

## 3 Modelling A

Modelling only concerns one angular sector of the cylinder, one element in the section, one element in the height, 100 elements between ${R}_{1}$ and ${R}_{2}$

In this Modelling, the local circumferential frame marker is defined in four different ways in AFFE_CARA_ELEM: VECT_1/ VECT_2/ ANGL_REP_1 and ANGL_REP_2.
