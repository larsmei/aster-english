# v4.01.301 TPLA301 - Temperature distribution in a short cylinder

**Summary:**

This test is based on independent validation of version 3 in linear steady-state thermal analysis.

The 2D axisymmetric problem is designed to validate elements thermal axisymmetric under temperature imposed in the case of a short cylinder with radial and axial behavior.

It has a single model (axisymmetric).

The results are compared with a solution based on a graphical estimate.

### 3.3 Remarks

The $\rho {C}_{P}$ volumetric heat does not interfere in this test, but must be declared. $\rho {C}_{P}=2.0J/{m}^{3}\text{°}C$ is taken.

The $\varphi =0.$ boundary condition is implicit on the free edges.

The limit conditions, $T=-17.778\text{°}C$ on KL, and $T=4.444\text{°}C$ on LE, are incompatible with point L (noudn25).

*Code_Aster* applies an "overloading law" which in this case consists of taking into account the last entry limit condition. The order of assignment of the temperatures imposed has therefore a great influence on the results obtained.

In the case treated, the temperature on the upper side (LE) is affected after that on the cylinder flan (KL).

## 4 Summary of the results

The Modelling results (out of 10) exceed the tolerance initially set $(5\text{\%})$. Maximum the difference obtained is $-6.10\text{\%}$ , it is located on the smallest reference value.

In this test, *Code_Aster* applies a "overloading law" which in this case consists of taking into account the last limit condition entered. The order of assignment of the temperatures imposed, therefore has a great influence on the results obtained.

The calculations were made in $\text{°}C$ . The determination of the difference, considering the temperatures in $\text{°}F$ , gives a maximum value very different from that obtained in $\text{°}C$ .

A calculation with NISA software gives the same results as Code_Aster (verified in case the temperature imposed on point L is $4.44\text{°}C$).

The quality of the results could be improved by making a finer mesh, the problem of overload would still be present, but the zone of influence of temperature imposed on the point L would be lower. The results are considered acceptable given the Modelling performed (mesh and unit system, overload law).
