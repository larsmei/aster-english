### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loading

The model will be limited to the part of the thick disk located in the $Y\ge 0$ half space, the plane of the vertical crack being a plane of symmetry.

In the absence of nodes on the revolution axis, a rigid mode will be blocked by a linear relationship between degrees of freedom.

| Either the points: | $A({R}_{1},0,t)$ | $B(-{R}_{1},0,t)$ |
| --- | --- | --- |

Translation block to $X$: $\mathit{UX}(A)+\mathit{UX}(B)=0$

Blocking of translation into $Y$: $\mathrm{UY}=0$ in $\mathrm{XOZ}$ plane, except for crack lips.

Translation block to $Z$: $\mathrm{UZ}(A)=0$

Blocking rotation around $\mathrm{OX}$: provided by the boundary condition symmetry in the $\mathrm{XOZ}$ plane

Rotation block around $\mathrm{OY}$: $\mathrm{UZ}(B)=0$

Blocking rotation around $\mathrm{OZ}$: provided by the boundary condition symmetry in the $\mathrm{XOZ}$ plane

Loading: stationary angular speed $\omega =500\mathrm{rad}/s$

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

In [bib1], an reference solution is given, based on an integral boundary equation method. The value of the stress intensity factor in mode I is then:

${K}_{I}=\frac{3+\nu }{4}\cdot \rho {\omega}^{2}({R}_{2}^{2}+\frac{1-\nu }{3+\nu }{R}_{1}^{2})\cdot \sqrt{\pi b}\cdot {F}_{I}$ where the geometric correction factor is given, depending on the parametric angle of the $\theta$ ellipse, in the figure below.

![Figure](/figures/v3.04.311/10000000000007E0000005684CB762FE86C059B2.png)

The $a/t$ ratio chosen corresponds to the top curve (squares).

Maximum the difference between marked points and the curve being $\text{2\%}$ , the reading error on the curve is less than the maximum announced error ($\text{5\%}$ ).

However, we do not use this reference because it seems wrong.

We use numerical results from calculations with ANSYS software as a reference.
