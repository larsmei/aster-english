# v6.02.118 SSNL118 - Wind speed field bar

**Summary:**

This test is for the validation of the application of wind loads on linear elements. The load is described by wind speed fields.

This problem allows testing:

- Linear finished elements (bars, cables, beams (except curved beams)) with following loads of a kind
- Loads representing wind speeds:
- reading wind field data,
- projection of the wind fields attached to the dot cloud on the distorted mesh of the structure,
- calculation of the relative speed,
- the function giving the force distributed according to the relative speed of the structure,
- the updating of geometry to take account of large movements and large rotations.

Modelling A tests elements BARRE.

Modelling B tests elements CABLE.

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

Movements of $\mathit{A1}$ and $\mathit{B1}$ points at moments: $1.s$ , $1.05s$ and $2.s$ . These moments correspond respectively to wind speeds of $10$ , $15$ and $\mathrm{20m}/s$

The resolution of the 3 equilibrium equations, $\mathit{Fa}+\mathit{Fb}+\mathit{Fvent}=0$ projection, is done by iterations. The 3 unknowns of the problem are the position of the centre of gravity of the $G$ bar: $(x,y)$ and the angle variation: $\theta$.

In *Code_Aster* , the wind effect is taken into account by a force distributed along the linear element. The expression of the module of this distributed force is as follows:

${\mathit{Fcx}}_{(v)}=\frac{1}{2}.\rho .{V}^{2}.\mathit{Cx}(v).{D}_{h}$

| où | ${\mathit{Fcx}}_{(v)}$: is the distributed force module along the $N/m$ cable, depending on the speed. |
| --- | --- |
|  | $\rho$: is the density of the air in $\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$. $V$: is the relative speed of the cable in $m/s$. $\mathit{Cx}(v)$: is the drag coefficient of the cable, depending on the relative speed. ${D}_{h}$: is the hydraulic diameter of the cable in $m$. |

To obtain a simple reference solution analytics, the ${\mathit{Fcx}}_{(\mathit{Vp})}$ function is taken equal to $\mathrm{\parallel }{V}_{p}\mathrm{\parallel }$. In the command file of *Code_Aster* the function of the $\mathit{Fcxv}$ is therefore set as follows:

```text
FCXV=DEFI_FONCTION(

NOM_PARA='VITE',

VALE=( 0.0 , 0.0,

10.0 , 10.0 ),

PROL_GAUCHE='LINEAIRE',

PROL_DROITE='LINEAIRE',

)
```

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

None. The balance equation is solved by iterations with an error of less than $1.0E-09$.
