# v6.03.131 SSNP131 - Energy parameter identification Gp in 2D and 3D

**Summary**

This non-linear quasi-static mechanical test allows the calculation of the ${G}_{p}$ parameter from the energy approach of the elastoplastic rupture and the identification of critical values corresponding to the given experimental tenacity values. It requires the representation of the crack by a cut and the finely knitting of the vicinity of the bottom of the dent. It also requires the calculation of elastic energy on the virtual propagation zone of the dentaille, cut into "cops".

Modelling A is done with quadratic elements $\mathrm{2D}$, in flat deformation. The mesh represents the so-called chip areas; the calculation of the parameter is done using the properties of the mesh (POST_GP and CALC_GP) or by automatic creation of these zones (CALC_GP).

Model B is identical to model A, but uses any mesh; only the automatic definition of chips is used (CALC_GP).

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution in 2D

The energy method of elastoplastic rupture based on the parameter $\mathrm{Gp}$ [1], [2] is used.

The bottom of the core is formed by a half circle of $R$ radius. The $\mathrm{Ze}$ zone of length $\Delta l$ corresponds to the virtual propagation of the core and is cut into "cops".

![Figure](/figures/v6.03.131/10001A1C000069BB0000140D95B54754556914A7.svg)

It determines at each moment the evolution of the quantity $\mathrm{Gp}(\Delta l)$ defined by:

$\mathit{Gp}(\Delta l)=2[{W}_{\mathit{elas}}^{\mathit{traction}}(\Delta l)]/\Delta l$

where ${W}_{\mathit{elas}}^{\mathit{traction}}(\Delta l)$ is the elastic traction energy calculated on the $\mathrm{Ze}$ area. The maximum of this quantity shall then be calculated in relation to $\Delta l$, which is called "$\mathrm{Gp}$".

$\mathrm{Gp}=\underset{\Delta l}{\mathrm{Max}}\lbrace \mathrm{Gp}(\Delta l)\rbrace$

The critical moment when the propagation of the defect will start is then the moment where the tenacity $\mathrm{Kj}={\mathrm{Kj}}_{\mathrm{crit}}$. It is said then that $\mathrm{Gp}$ reaches the critical value "$\mathrm{Gpc}$".

### 2.2 Bibliographical references

1. WADIER Y.: "Snapshot of the energy approach to elastoplastic rupture applied to cleavage rupture", Note EDF R&D HT-64/03/001/A, January 2003.
2. WADIER Y., LORENTZ E.: "Mechanics of rupture in the presence of plasticity: Modelling crack by a cut". C.R.A.S. t. 332, Series IIb, 2004.

The differences between the different calculation possibilities are due to the choice of the free elastic energy used either in the case of an explicit mesh of the chips or in the case of the free mesh, respectively ${W}_{\mathit{elas}}^{\mathit{traction}}(\Delta S)$ and ${W}_{\mathit{elas}}(\Delta S)$. In the case of the explicit mesh of the chips (${W}_{\mathit{elas}}^{\mathit{traction}}(\Delta S)$), where the participation of spherical compression and compression in each specific deformation direction is removed, the elastic energy will be modified into an elastic energy of traction and by definition ${W}_{\mathit{elas}}^{\mathit{traction}}(\Delta S)<{W}_{\mathit{elas}}(\Delta S)$.

## 5 Summary of the results

The tests are validated with an difference below 2nd-4%.

There is also a high consistency of the $\mathrm{2D}$ results.
