# v7.32.130 WTNP130 – Calculation of hydraulic and thermal nodal forces on a bar

**Summary:**

This test case models several hydraulic and thermal flows in a bar. The main objective is to validate the calculation of the resultant nodal forces on the edge at which pressure and temperature are imposed.

Modelling A is done in D_PLAN_THMS.

Modelling B is done in AXIS_THMS.

Modelling C is done in D_PLAN_HHMS.

Modelling D is done in AXIS_HHMS.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A bar of length $L = 5$ m and width $W = 1$ m is considered.

#### 1.2.2 Boundary conditions and initials

The reference values for liquid pressure and temperature are $0$ and $273$ K, respectively.

- On the $x=0$ and $x=5$ m edges, the displacement is blocked in $DX$.
- On the $y=0$ and $y=1$ m edges, the displacement is blocked in $DY$.
- On the $x=5$ m edge, the change in liquid pressure from its reference value is imposed on $PRE1 = 100$ kPa.
- On the $x=5$ m edge, the temperature change from its reference value is imposed on $TEMP = 100$ K.

The simulation duration is one year. It is discretized in two phases: first, 10 time step evenly distributed up to one day; then, 10 time step additional evenly distributed between one day and one year.

**Note**

The van Genuchten parameters are only used to connect saturation to capillary pressure. They are not used to define the relative permeability of the liquid and gas in the porous medium. These are constant ([r7.01.11](doc:r7.01.11)).

#### 1.3.2 Boundary conditions and initials

The reference values for capillary pressure, gas pressure and temperature are $0$, $100$ kPa and $273$ K, respectively.

- On the $x=0$ and $x=5$ m edges, the displacement is blocked in $DX$.
- On the $y=0$ and $y=1$ m edges, the displacement is blocked in $DY$.
- On the $x=5$ m edge, the variations in capillary pressure and gas pressure are zero, i.e. $PRE1 = 0$ and $PRE2 = 0$.
- The initial saturation of the test piece is $S_{lq}^0=0.5$.

The simulation duration is one year. It is discretized in two phases: first, 10 time step evenly distributed up to one day; then, 10 time step additional evenly distributed between one day and one year.

## 2 Modelling A

### 2.1 Modelling characteristics et du mesh

This is a D_PLAN_THMS model. The mesh is composed of 200 mesh cells QUAD8 and 206 mesh cells SEG3.

### 2.2 Tested quantities and results

A non-regression test is performed on the hydraulic and thermal fluxes on the $x=5$ m edge one day after the simulation begins. These fluxes are recovered by the PRE1 and TEMP components of the RESULTANTE key word for the POST_RELEVE_T operator. It should be noted that these components are negative, as the fluxes are taken positive in the direction of normal exit to the bar.

| **Component** | **Reference type** | **Value** |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  |  |  |
| PRE1 | NON_REGRESSION | $-0.09630722$ kg/s |
| TEMP | NON_REGRESSION | $-6893720649$ J/s |

The opposite of the integral of these flows is shown below. These are the mass of liquid and entropy inputs throughout the bar. These converge towards two asymptotic values that approach:

with $\Delta p_{lq} = 100$ kPa, $\Delta T = 100$ K, and $|\Omega_0|=L\times W\times = 5$ m3.

In fact, the relationships are correct in the hypothesis of small disturbances for the fluid and skeleton. In the present case, the following figure shows that the test case conditions are only very little distant from it.
