# v6.04.233 SSNV233 – Torsion test with Mohr-Coulomb law

**Summary:**

A twist test is performed with Mohr-Coulomb law. The objective of the test is to check the correct convergence of the model in the presence of rotation of the main stresses. The calculated tangent matrix is compared to that obtained by disturbance.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

A torsion test is repeated on a hollow cylinder subjected to ${P}_{i}$ internal and external containment pressures, ${P}_{e}$ vertical force, and $M$ twist moment. This test is schematized by a cubic sample (a material point) subjected to ${\sigma}_{z}$, ${\sigma}_{r}$ and ${\sigma}_{\theta}$ containment stresses, and ${\epsilon}_{z\theta }=M(t)$ shear deformation ( **Figure**).

If ${P}_{i}={P}_{e}={\sigma}_{0}$ and $F=\frac{2{\sigma}_{0}}{\pi}$ , the following containment values shall be obtained:

- ${\sigma}_{\theta}={\sigma}_{r}={\sigma}_{0}$
- ${\sigma}_{z}=3{\sigma}_{0}$

The following values of the main constraints are then used:

- ${\sigma}_{1}=2{\sigma}_{0}+\mid {\sigma}_{z\theta }\mid$
- ${\sigma}_{2}={\sigma}_{0}$
- ${\sigma}_{3}=2{\sigma}_{0}-\mid {\sigma}_{z\theta }\mid$

But above all, the main axes rotate at an $\alpha$ angle equal to [:ref:` **1**  < **1** >`]:

$\alpha (t)=\frac{1}{2}\arctan\left(\frac{2{\sigma}_{z\theta }(t)}{{\sigma}_{z}-{\sigma}_{\theta}}\right)=\frac{1}{2}\arctan\left(\frac{{\sigma}_{z\theta }(t)}{{\sigma}_{0}}\right)$

### 1.2 Sample properties

The elastic parameters are:

- isotropic compressibility module: $K=516,2\mathit{MPa}$
- shear module: $\mu =238,2\mathit{MPa}$

The Mohr-Coulomb Law parameters are:

- friction angle: $\phi =33°$
- characteristic angle: $\Psi =27°$
- Cohesion: ${c}_{0}=1\mathit{kPa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The torsion test presented here is performed on a material point with the SIMU_POINT_MAT command.

The following constraints are imposed:

- ${\sigma}_{xx}={\sigma}_{yy}=-50\mathit{kPa}$
- ${\sigma}_{zz}=-150\mathit{kPa}$

The shear deformation imposed ${\epsilon}_{yz}$ varies linearly between $t=0$ and $100\mathit{sec}$ from $0$ to $0,01\text{\%}$ to $N=10$ time step.

We're checking that ${N}^{\mathit{num}}<{N}^{\mathit{per}}$ is on every time step.

The calculated tangent matrix is therefore better than that obtained by disturbance, as shown in paragraph § 4, by better taking into account the multiple mechanisms of constitutive law.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The Modelling is performed on a hardware point with SIMU_POINT_MAT.

Redistribution of the time step is active.

The convergence criterion is RESI_GLOB_RELA = ${10}^{-10}$ .
