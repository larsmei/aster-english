# v6.05.108 SSNS108 – Simulation of SAFE Test by Progressive Push

**Summary:**

This test represents a simplified Modelling of a SAFE (Slowly-Raised Armed Structure) study under static monotonous progressive pushover loading. The objective of this test is to validate the DEBORST calculation option on NEWTON.

This test is delicate because it considers a problem not posed, without one-sided solution, due to the softening material related to the damage.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry studied is that of the T5 structure of the SAFE [bib1] program. The geometrical characteristics of reinforced concrete parts are illustrated by the []. They are composed of a veil and two return walls (or recasts). The structure is also equipped with metal parts related to its loading. These parts will not be modelled in this study.

The model's railing consists of horizontal and vertical reinforcements on each side of the central wall, as well as in the returns []. In both horizontal and vertical directions, the corrosion rates ${r}_{h}$ and ${r}_{v}$ (the rebar quantity per linear metre of the sail) are identical and equal to $\text{0,8\%}$ , i.e.:

$\frac{{S}_{\mathrm{armatures}\mathrm{horizontales}}}{\mathrm{ml}(\mathrm{vertical})}={r}_{h}e=\frac{0,8}{100}.20\mathrm{cm}=16{\mathrm{cm}}^{2}/\mathrm{ml}$

$\frac{{S}_{\mathrm{armatures}\mathrm{verticales}}}{\mathrm{ml}(\mathrm{horizontale})}={r}_{v}e=\frac{0,8}{100}.20\mathrm{cm}=16{\mathrm{cm}}^{2}/\mathrm{ml}$

And this for all the two tables facing North and South.

Either $8\mathit{cm²}/\mathit{ml}$ per tablecloth and direction (horizontal and vertical, or $\mathrm{2x8}=16\mathit{cm²}/\mathit{ml}$).

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

*restraint at base:*

The restraint model with the low sill was considered stiff enough to be modeled by a perfect anchor. Thus, all nodes of the model base are blocked according to all degrees of freedom.

*High sill movements:*

The presence of the high sill is intended to keep the upper edge of the wall at the horizontal by preventing rotations around the $Y$ axis.

* Charge:*

The loads taken into account are the own weight of the structure and a displacement imposed at the top of the structure.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

The concrete is modelled using elements DKT (a multilayer flat-stressed hull). The railing is simplified by neglecting the seam reinforcements between the different layers. As for the rebars of the sails, they are modelled by elements planes of type GRILLE_MEMBRANE (positioned at the exact coast inside the concrete to account for the concrete of the coating). The restraint between the mesh cells of the central wall and the return walls is done by sharing the nodes at the mid-walls.

## 4 Summary of the results

Although the results obtained between the two modellings can give important differences (more than $\text{50\%}$), the test case has shown the possibility of taking DEBORST calculation option into account on NEWTON.
