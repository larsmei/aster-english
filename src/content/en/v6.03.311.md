# v6.03.311 SSNP311 - Biblio_131. Fissure in mode II of an elastoplastic test piece

**Summary:**

This test is based on the independent validation of version 3 in the mechanical of the breakage.

It is a two-dimensional static test that aims to validate the calculation of $G$ , and its non-dependence on the crown screw, in elastoplastic regime in an incremental calculation, on a nontrivial geometry. The constitutive law used is an elastoplastic law of Von Isotropic Hardening Bets.

This test case includes a single $\mathrm{2D}$ plane Modelling in which the influence of an incremental charge is studied.

The results obtained with *Code_Aster* are compared with calculations using ADINA.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Elements calculation finished with ADINA. stiffness matrix update tangent by BFGS method (BROYDEN, FLETCHER, GOLDFARB and SHAMNO). $J$ calculation per Rice integral in which strain energy density is evaluated based on Hencky plasticity theory (nonlinear reversible elastic model equivalent to incremental plasticity theory for a monotone radial loading increasing in the space of main stresses)

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

The difference between experimental measurements and calculation does not exceed $\text{7\%}$ , with respect to the load/movement response curve.

The accuracy of the $J$ calculation is unknown; the error appears to increase with the load level, as shown by the increasing dependence of $J$ on the contour, which reaches a margin of variation of 12% at step 64.

## 4 Summary of the results

The results for the energy return rate give 1 difference maximum $\text{9,7 \%}$ compared to the reference solution on the last crown, for an announced accuracy of $\text{12\%}$. The results are excellent given the nonlinear character of the test piece.
