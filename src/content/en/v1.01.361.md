# v1.01.361 ZZZZ361 - Degenerate cutting case for elements XFEM 3D quadratic

**Summary:**

In this test case, it is proposed to check the smoothness of the specific volume cutting cases in the quadratic XFEM frame. In three-dimensional XFEM, the finished elements "parents" through the crack are cut into "children" tetrahedrons which are then re-cut into sub-tetrahedras to be consistent with discontinuity.

There are in total five configurations of the "children" tetrahedrons cut into subtetrahedras ( *cf.* [R7.02.12](doc:r7.02.12)), distinguished by the number of nodes apex of the child tetrahedron for which the "lsn* cancels (npts) and the number of intersection points between the surface *lsn* =0 and the edges of the child tetrahedron. However, degenerate "scratch" configurations exist. These degenerate configurations are easily resembled to the classical cutting configurations.

The six degenerate configurations (modellings A to F) for which the surface *lsn=0* shaves the surface of the child tetrahedron are examined and which occur only in quadratic XFEM. These situations occur when one or more edges of the child tetrahedron have exactly 2 points such as *lsn=0* (necessarily one end and the mid-node based on the adjustment of the *level set* in the quadratic case). Finally, in model G, the case of undegenerated cutting for which the surface *lsn=0* shaves one of the edges of the tetrahedron is studied.
