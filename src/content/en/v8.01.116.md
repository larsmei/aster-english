The objective of this test is to compare two different modellings fluid elements for the dynamic response of a dam with fluid-structure coupling.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

There are two types of boundary conditions (see ):

- At the side edges of the ground absorbent boundaries are affected;
- At the left edge of the fluid part a fluid absorbent boundary is affected.

There are also two fluid-structure interfaces defined between the soil and the fluid part, as well as between the dam and the fluid part. In addition, for the 3D modellings (C and E), a periodicity condition is added in the direction of the flat deformation.

The imposed dynamic loading is a Ricker wavelet which is presented in . The load is imposed for a total time of **1s** . This wavelet has a 20° inclined propagation direction relative to the vertical. This wavelet is injected both as P wave and S wave.
