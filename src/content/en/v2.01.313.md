# v2.01.313 SDLD313 - Spring mass system with 2 degrees of freedom hysteretic damping

**Summary:**

This unidirectional problem consists of a harmonic analysis of a mechanical structure composed of a set of spring masses with hysteretic damping and subjected to sinusoidal excitation. This mechanical test of the structures corresponds to a dynamic analysis of a discrete model with linear behavior. It consists of three modellings.

The elements discrete translation (mass, spring) options AMOR_HYST are tested through Modelling A.

Through Modelling B, elements beam (POU_D_T) and AMOR_HYST options are tested,

Through Modelling C, the complex modal calculation (CALC_MODES) is tested.

The results obtained for the first two modellings (moving field for different excitation frequencies) are in good agreement with the results of the VPCS guide. The results obtained for the third model are in good agreement with the semi-analytic results.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The system of differential equations of the second coupled order is of the form:

$M\ddot{u}+Ku=F$

with $M=\left[\begin{array}{ccc}0& 0& 0\\ 0& 10& 0\\ 0& 0& 5\end{array}\right]$ and $K=28000\left[\begin{array}{ccc}1+0.1j& -1-0.1j& 0\\ -1-0.1j& 2+0.1j& -1\\ 0& -1& 1\end{array}\right]$

$\omega$ solution with $\mathrm{F}={\mathrm{F}}_{0}{e}^{j\omega t}({j}^{2}=-1)$ harmonic excitation is of the $u={u}_{0}{e}^{j\omega t}$ form, which leads to: $(\mathrm{K}-\mathrm{M}{\omega}^{2}){u}_{0}={\mathrm{F}}_{0}$

This system is solved for all $\omega$ .

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

$x$ point $C$ displacement for certain frequencies.

Eigenfrequencies and depreciation reduced.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

Semi-analytic solution.

## 5 Modelling C

### 5.1 Modelling characteristics

**element discrete translation rigidity**

Computer test of a complex GEP modal calculation (via hysteretic damping in the matrix)
K). Comparison of analytical/value values provided by rumpy (reference values) and those produced by two CALC_MODES modal solvers: SORENSEN and QZ. For each of these modal methods, 20 calculations are performed, varying:

- 5 different depreciation values: 0.1, -0.1, 10.0, 1.0e-6, 0.0;
- 4 different considerations of the boundary conditions: DDL_IMPO alone, MECA_IMPO alone or 2 possible variegations of both.

For each of these 40 calculations, the frequency, reduced damping and generalized rigidity and mass are tested in non-regression. Only the first two parameters are also compared to analytical and numpy values.
