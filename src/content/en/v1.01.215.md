# v1.01.215 ZZZZ215 – 3D chaining code_saturne-code_aster

**Summary:**

This test validates the 3D link *code_saturne*-code_aster*. Different features are affected by this validation:

- reading a pressure field in a MED file
- creating a data structure EVOL_CHAR result
- the spatial projection of one field of one mesh on another
- the temporal interpolation of the load induced by fluid pressure from fluid to solid time discretization

For example, various types of input fields are used

- a pressure field produced by *code_saturne*
- pressure fields artificially created by *code_aster*

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry of the problem is that of a 200-side cube with faces called $\mathit{Face1}$, $\mathit{Face2}$, $\mathit{Face3}$, $\mathit{Face4}$, $\mathit{Face5}$ and $\mathit{Face6}$.

### 1.2 Material properties

It is a linear isotropic elastic material of Young's modulus $1.$ and Poisson's ratio $0.3$ .

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The cube is clamped on its $\mathit{Face1}$ face while all the others are subject to fluid pressure.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

The initial conditions are blank of any displacement and constraint.

## 2 reference solution

Not applicable.

## 3 Modelling A

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Here we define 2 pressure fields on the fluid mesh which is associated with 2 moments, $0$ and $1$. Then we assemble them in a data structure of type loading.

To apply this load to the solid mesh, the loading data structure is projected on the solid mesh.

The solid calculation is performed at $0.2$ , $0.4$ , $0.6$ , $0.8$ and $1$ moments which validates the time interpolation of the load.

### 3.2 Mesh characteristics

Not important

## 4 Modelling B

### 4.1 Modelling characteristics

A pressure field is defined on the time- and space-dependent fluid mesh depending on the $1.E-4\times \mathit{INST}\times (X+Y+Z)$ function. It is then associated with the $0$ , $1$ moment list in a load-type data structure.

To apply this load to the solid mesh, the loading data structure is projected on the solid mesh.

The solid calculation is performed at $0.2$ , $0.4$ , $0.6$ , $0.8$ and $1$ moments which validates the time interpolation of the load.

### 4.2 Mesh characteristics

Not important

## 5 Modelling C

### 5.1 Modelling characteristics

Here we read a true *Code_Saturn* constant pressure result by element at $0.25$, $0.5$ , $0.75$ and $1$ moments, which is defined as a load-type data structure.

To apply this load to the solid mesh, the loading data structure is projected on the solid mesh.

The solid calculation is performed at $0.333$ , $0.6666$ and $0.9999$ moments which validates the time interpolation of the load.

### 5.2 Mesh characteristics

Not important

## 6 Modelling D

This test is very close to Modelling B. It allows testing the code coupling in MPI
replacing the fluid-mechanical chaining with a low coupling (without pressure dependence on displacement).

Note: this test would be close to a coupling to make fluid-strucutre interaction (IFS) if the pressure had a dependence
The main reason for this is that the system is not yet fully integrated into the system.

### 6.1 Modelling characteristics

A pressure field on the fluid mesh is defined here depending on time and space
depending on the $1.E-4\times \mathit{INST}\times (X+Y+Z)$ function.

This pressure field will be interpolated on the solid mesh to serve as an input load.

Solid calculation is performed at $0.2$, $0.4$, $0.6$, $0.8$ and $1$ moments.
