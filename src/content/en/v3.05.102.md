# v3.05.102 SSLX102 - bended bending hose

**Summary:**

This test validates the Modelling of the ovalization phenomena in the pipes: an elbow, extended by straight pipes, is subjected to an bending moment. This causes in the elbow an ovalization that spreads and simmers in the straight parts, and which alters the rigidity of the entire pipe. To verify the accuracy of the results, the flexibility of the entire pipe (value of displacement at the end for a moment imposed) and elements hulls DKT is tested.

The test case has six modellings:

- For Modelling A, the elbow is mesh in shell (modelling DKT), the pipe Modelling is used for straight parts, the restraint is provided by an COQUE_TUYAU connection.
- For modellings B and C, TUYAU Modelling is assigned to the entire piping: TUYAU_3M for B and TUYAU_6M for C ($M$ = Fourier modes).
- In Modelling D, the piping is mesh in shell (elements COQUE_3D), the ends are gridded in beams to apply the loads.
- Modelling E: TUYAU Modelling (3 modes of Fourier) is assigned to the entire piping.
- For Modelling F, the elbow is meshed in mesh cells density, the straight parts in elements TUYAU and the restraint is provided by a 3D_TUYAU connection.

Modelling A validates the correct transfer of the ovalization (mode 2) between elements TUYAU, and validates the COQUE_TUYAU connection, both modellings B and C validate the elements TUYAU (with 3 and 6 modes of Fourier) in linear elasticity, and the latest model validates the correct transmission of the ovalization (mode 2) between elements TUYAU, and validates the 3D_TUYAU connection.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Coved pipework in the $\mathrm{XY}$ plane . The straight parts are $L=1200\mathrm{mm}$ length .

## 9 Summary of the results

Since this test does not have analytical reference solutions, but is numerical, the differences found (less than 2% except one value) can be considered reasonable.

More specifically, for Modelling A (DKT and straight beams in TUYAU inshell mesh neck) it can be estimated that the solution obtained (2.7% d-difference in flat bending, and 0.4% in off-plane bending, compared to the reference: mesh in all-inshell Modelling D) validates the correct operation of the hull_pipe connection.

For Modelling B (elements TUYAU, 3 modes of Fourier), the difference important on flat bending (6.8%) is due to the fact that the piping is relatively thin, so that the ovalization in the elbow shows Fourier modes of greater order than 3.

In fact, Modelling C (TUYAU, 6 modes of Fourier) is very close to the reference (0.01% in flat bending, and 2% in off-plane bending). L-element hose is therefore validated in elasticity for these loads, compared to a solution in shell (modelization D).

Modelling E (elements TUYAU at 4 nodes) gives identical results to Modelling B, for a lower cost of calculation due to a lower mesh.
