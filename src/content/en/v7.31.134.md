# v7.31.134 WTNV134 – Undrained triaxial cyclic with Hujeux law

**Summary**

This test validates the implantation of cyclic deviatory mechanisms and the mechanism of cyclic consolidation of Hujeux's law. This is a triaxial cyclic test performed in undrained condition. The hydraulic coupling is taken into account, the sample is fully saturated, the skeleton and fluid are assumed to be incompressible.

The containment level is $30\mathrm{kPa}$ .

#### 1.3.2 Loading

**Phase 1: consolidation of sample to containment pressure p0**

The sample is taken to a homogeneous state of ${\sigma}_{xx}^{0}={\sigma}_{yy}^{0}={\sigma}_{zz}^{0}={\sigma}^{0}=-30\mathrm{kPa}$ isostatic *effective* stresses, by placing the ${\sigma}_{0}$ pressure on the rear, right lateral and upper faces of the element, while maintaining zero $\text{PRE1}$ water pressures in the sample.

**Phase 2: Undrained triaxial loading**

To obtain undrained conditions, zero hydraulic fluxes are imposed on all surfaces.

By holding a pressure equal to ${\sigma}_{0}$ on the rear and right side faces, an alternative load equal to $\Delta \sigma$ in the upper face shall be applied at the $\Delta \sigma$ amplitude pressure, so as to obtain a variation of the vertical stress ${\sigma}_{zz}$ in the sample in the $-45\mathit{kPa}\le {\sigma}_{zz}\le -15\mathit{kPa}$ interval (with the minus sign convention for compression).

### 1.4 Results

The solutions are post-treated with point $C$ , in terms of effective isotropic pressure $P$ ($=\text{tr}(\sigma ')/3$ ), ${\varepsilon}_{v}^{p}$ plastic density deformation and $({r}_{\mathrm{iso}}^{m}+{r}_{\mathrm{ela}}^{\mathrm{iso}})$ isotropic nutring coefficients and $({r}_{\mathit{iso}}^{c}+{r}_{\mathit{ela}}^{\mathit{iso}})$ and $({r}_{d}^{m}+{r}_{\mathrm{ela}}^{d})$ and $({r}_{d}^{c}+{r}_{\mathrm{ela}}^{d})$ deviatories .

The validation is performed by comparison with GEFDYN solutions provided by the Ecole Centrale Paris.

In the absence of gravity, the density weights of soil and water are not involved in the problem.
