# v1.01.125 ZZZZ125 - POST_FM IT Validation

**Summary:**

The objective is to validate the macro-command of calculation of POST_FM margin factors computerically.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

The geometry is restricted to the crack bottom tip noted $B$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elasto-plastic. It is not necessary to provide traction curves, as the test does not make elements calculation finished. quantities such as temperature and energy restitution rates are directly extracted from tables. Only material parameters useful to post-processing are provided:

- Poisson's ratio $\nu =0.3$
- Moduled

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.125/1000020100000640000004B0207721657B4355AE.png)

- ${K}_{\mathit{IC}}$ tenacity given as a temperature-dependent curve under the RUPT_FM key word of the DEFI_MATERIAU command

![Figure](/figures/v1.01.125/1000020100000640000004B0C02E7C32602F996B.png)

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The approach consists of chaining the following calculations:

- the mechanical calculation parameters are temperature dependent, a thermal calculation allows the $\mathit{TEMP}$ control variable to be determined
- a mechanical calculation in linear elasticity
- an incremental elasto-plastic mechanical calculation
- calculation of the ${G}_{\mathit{ELAS}}$ energy return rate from the linear mechanical result, in $B$ crack background
- calculation of the ${G}_{\mathit{PLAS}}$ energy return rate from the nonlinear mechanical result, $B$ crack bottom

Then the margin factors are obtained by post-processing via user python of the results of the calculation of the ${G}_{\mathit{ELAS}}$ and ${G}_{\mathit{PLAS}}$ energy restitution rates, and of the $\mathit{TEMP}$ temperature at point $B$.

The following quantities are retained:

1. $\mathit{KELAS}$: elastic stress intensity factor
2. $\mathit{KPLAS}$: plastic stress intensity factor
3. $\mathit{FM}\text{\_}\mathit{ASN}$: Regulatory Margin Factor

These results are provided and validation is of the type "AUTRE_ASTER".

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The output of the POST_FM operator, i.e. the following quantities at the bottom of the crack, is tested:

1. $\mathit{TEMP}$: crack bottom temperature
2. $\mathit{KIC}$: crack-back tenacity
3. $\mathit{KELAS}$: elastic stress intensity factor
4. $\mathit{KPLAS}$: plastic stress intensity factor
5. $\mathit{KCP}$: adjusted stress intensity factor
6. $\mathit{FM}\text{\_}\mathit{ASN}$: margin factor (case DSR)
7. $\mathit{FM}\text{\_}\mathit{PLAS}$: Regulatory Margin Factor (case DDR)
