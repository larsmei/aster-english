# v5.02.301 SDNL301 – Vibration of a beam with multi-point impact

**Summary:**

The objective of this test case is to simulate the dynamic response with damping of a beam with impact-multi-points:

- The beam is subjected to sinusoidal stresses.
- Impacts (obstacles) have normal stiffness and normal damping.
- Only one POU_D_T model is performed

The RMS value of the normal force at the free end shall be determined with or without consideration of static modes.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Quantity and reference result

The $\mathrm{RMS}$ in non-regression is tested over the total time of the normal force at the free end of the beam.

Two types of analyses were performed:

- The first analysis is to calculate the transient dynamic response on a modal basis consisting of the first 30 modes of its own.
- The second analysis is to calculate the transient dynamic response on a modal basis consisting of the first 30 modes, to which static modes are added.

The calculation procedure is as follows:

- the first 30 eigenfrequencies (up to $\mathrm{4800Hz}$) and the associated eigenmodes are calculated,
- The rigidity, mass and damping matrices are projected on the modal basis,
- The efforts are projected on a modal basis.
- the transient dynamic response is calculated on the modal basis
- RMS is calculated on the total time of the normal force at node $\mathrm{N2}$

There is no RMS reference value for this problem, so the two results are presented independently.

### 2.2 Reference quantity

- RMS_T_TOTAL: RMS value over the total normal force time (FORCE_NORMALE) at node $\mathrm{N2}$.
