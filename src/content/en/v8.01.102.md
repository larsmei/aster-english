# v8.01.102 FDLV102 - Weight added calculated on a generalized model

**Summary:**

This test belongs to the field of fluid/structure interaction, in its inertial coupling aspect: it is to calculate an added mass matrix, from a generalized model derived from a dynamic substructure calculation. Modal analysis on the fluid/structure coupled system is performed from a substructure calculation, and the result is compared with a modal calculation in direct fluid. Thus, for a two-dimensional fluid problem, the possibility of calculating the terms of self-added mass and added mass of coupling between substructures derived between them by rotation and translation (these "deducted" substructures are not gridded).

There is currently only one model, which is to assign to the fluid mesh of the elements thermal planes.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

Direct modal calculation (without dynamic substructure)

**Calculation of clean air modes:**

The CALC_MODES operator's 'BANDE' option calculates the first 4 eigenfrequencies of the air system (mass-spring system):

| Mode 1: | vibration of both cylinders in phase according to $\mathrm{Ox}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Mode 2: | Cylinder vibration No.2 according to $\mathrm{Oy}$ (right) |
| Mode 3: | vibration of both cylinders in phase opposition according to $\mathrm{Ox}$ |
| Mode 4: | Vibration of cylinder No. 1 according to $\mathrm{Oy}$ (left) |

These modes can be determined analytically [bib1].

The Code_Aster calculation provides for clean air frequencies:

| Mode 1: | ${f}_{1}=17.3555\mathrm{Hz}$ | Mode 2: | ${f}_{2}=18.2034\mathrm{Hz}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mode 3: | ${f}_{3}=42.6760\mathrm{Hz}$ | Mode 4: | ${f}_{4}=57.5418\mathrm{Hz}$ |

**Calculation of the mass matrix added on modal basis:**

On this modal basis, the order 4 added mass matrix is calculated with the CALC_MATR_AJOU [U4.55.10](doc:u4.55.10) option 'MASS_AJOU' keyword MODE_MECA (lower triangular terms):

| $\mathrm{m11}=300.67\mathrm{kg}/m$ | $\mathrm{m12}=0.001\mathrm{kg}/m$ |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathrm{m13}=269.98\mathrm{kg}/m$ | $\mathrm{m14}=0.009\mathrm{kg}/m$ |
| $\mathrm{m22}=269.98\mathrm{kg}/m$ | $\mathrm{m23}=0.009\mathrm{kg}/m$ |
| $\mathrm{m24}=31.05\mathrm{kg}/m$ | $\mathrm{m33}=301.71\mathrm{kg}/m$ |
| $\mathrm{m34}=–0.011\mathrm{kg}/m$ | $\mathrm{m44}=269.86\mathrm{kg}/m$ |

**Add this matrix to the generalized mass matrix:**

The matrix thus determined is added to the generalised mass matrix (operator COMB_MATR_ASSE [U4.53.01](doc:u4.53.01)) and then the proper frequencies of the submerged structure are calculated with the CALC_MODES operator option 'PLUS_PETITE' [U4.52.02](doc:u4.52.02).

The calculation finds the following eigenfrequencies:

| Mode 1: | $f{'}_{1}=15.8782\mathrm{Hz}$ | Mode 2: | $f{'}_{2}=16.7811\mathrm{Hz}$ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 4 Background

**Module-added mass calculation procedure**

- Calculation of the eigenmodes of substructure 1 (mesh left cylinder) with interfaces blocked by CALC_MODES
- Definition of two dynamic interfaces type CRAIG-BAMPTON (unit displacement required):

'EST': corresponds to point $\mathrm{PBLOC2}=L$

'SUD': corresponds to point $\mathrm{PBLOC4}=G$

- Definitions of 2 modal bases associated with these interfaces: DEFI_INTERF_DYNA [U4.55.03](doc:u4.55.03) operator:

| $\mathrm{BAMO1}$ : | two dynamic modes and one constrained mode: unit displacement on $\mathrm{PBLOC2}=L$ |
| --- | --- |
| $\mathrm{BAMO2}$ : | two dynamic modes and one constrained mode: unit displacement on $\mathrm{PBLOC4}=G$ |

- Definitions of 2 macro-elements associated with these modal bases: operator MACR_ELEM_DYNA [U4.55.05](doc:u4.55.05)
- Definition of the generalized model: operator DEFI_MODELE_GENE [U4.55.06](doc:u4.55.06):

Sub_structure_1: $\mathrm{CYLINDR0}$: corresponds to the left cylinder (mesh)

Sub_structure_2: $\mathrm{CYLINDR1}$: corresponds to the right cylinder (not meshed)

This second substructure is derived from the first by rotation of -90°.

ANGL_NAUT : (-90.,0.,0.)

restraint : $\mathrm{EST}$ et $\mathrm{SUD}$

This definition of the two substructures allows DEFI_MODELE_GENE to calculate the translation between the two substructures.

- Creating a full skyline profile from the generalised model defined: operator NUME_DDL_GENE [U4.55.07](doc:u4.55.07)
- Assembly of stiffness and Generalized Mass Matrices: ASSE_MATR_GENE [U4.55.08](doc:u4.55.08) Operator
- Calculation of the mass matrix added from the Generalized Model defined:

The modal bases attached to each of the two substructures define fields to the nodes displacement at the location of the 1st substructure in the mesh. The CALC_MATR_AJOU [U4.55.10](doc:u4.55.10) operator transports the field to the nodes corresponding to the modal base of the second substructure via the translation and rotation defined above to assign it to the location of the second substructure in the mesh. The calculation of the added mass is therefore made following this field shift to the nodes: the added mass on the 1st substructure, the added mass on the second substructure and the coupling term between the two substructures, taking into account the fluid environment of each substructure, can be calculated.

- Sommation of mass matrix assembled to mass matrix added: COMB_MATR_ASSE [U4.53.01](doc:u4.53.01)
- Calculation of the mode of the submerged global structure: CALC_MODES [U4.52.02](doc:u4.52.02).
