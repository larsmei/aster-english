# 1 **u3.23.02** Thermal modelling AXIS_FOURIER

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the thermal modelling AXIS_FOURIER:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the associated support mesh cells,
- the supported materials and loadings,
- the non-linear possibilities,
- the test cases implementing the modelling.

The modelling AXIS_FOURIER (Phenomenon: THERMIQUE) corresponds to finite elements whose support mesh cells are plane and make it possible to model the longitudinal section of parts of revolution about the OZ axis (in cylindrical coordinates) subjected to boundary conditions whose spatial distribution is decomposed into a Fourier series about this axis.

## 2 Supported loadings

The loadings available are the following:

- **SOURCE**

Makes it possible to apply volumetric sources.

- **FLUX_REP**

Makes it possible to apply normal fluxes to faces of elements.

- **ECHANGE**

Makes it possible to apply exchange conditions with an external temperature to faces of elements.

## 3 Non-linear possibilities

None.
