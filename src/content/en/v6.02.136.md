# v6.02.136 SSNL136 - Large displacement of the arc at angle of opening 45°

**Summary**

This test validates element multifibre beam POU_D_TGM in the geometric nonlinear field of large displacements and large rotations. Additional Modelling with POU_D_T_GD (geometrically accurate model of large displacement beams) allows comparison of the two elements.

A $45°$ clamped aperture plane is studied at one end and subjected to bending force perpendicular to its plane at the other end. This very severe test gives results in good agreement with the results already obtained by other researchers in the literature.

2. The free end of the point $B$ is required, with a vertical load (perpendicular to the arc plane) increasing to $\mathrm{600N}$:

> $\mathit{FZ}=600\times t$"

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method used for the calculation of the reference solution

The first reference results for this problem are the results obtained by Bathe and Bolourchi bib1 in 1979 with beam modelling. Bathe obtained substantially the same results in 1990 with 3D modelling, thus completely different from the beam model, which is why its results are widely accepted by the scientific community as a reference value.

Since then, several other researchers have addressed this problem, so we choose to compare our results to an average of 8 codes.

### 3.4 Remarks

L-element POU_D_TGM has an additional degree of freedom compared to a classic element beam, the spindle in the axis of the section. It is chosen not to model here by imposing $\mathit{JG}=0$ (zero-thickening constant) on one part because it is negligible (full square section) and on the other hand because the available reference results do not take account of it.

## 5 Summary of the results

The results obtained are in very good agreement with the reference, regardless of the beam's element used. It can be noted that the element multi-fibre has better tolerances than the element beam in large rotations, however the latter requires in practice to perform much less loading.

In addition, despite its arched structure, discretized in only 8 elements and undergoing large rotations, the continuity of degrees of freedom to nodes on elements is well ensured for both modellings.
