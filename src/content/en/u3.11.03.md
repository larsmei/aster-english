# u3.11.03 Modellings CABLE and CABLE_POULIE

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings CABLE and CABLE_POULIE:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the related support mesh cells,
- the supported loadings,
- the non-linear capabilities,
- the test cases implementing the modellings.

The modellings CABLE and CABLE_POULIE correspond to bar elements written specifically in order to take large displacements into account (cf. [R3.08.02](doc:r3.08.02) and [R3.08.04](doc:r3.08.04)).

They cannot be used for problems of linear mechanical analysis.

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

For these two modellings, the discretisation degrees of freedom are, at each node of the support mesh cell, the three translational displacement components.

| **Finite element** | **Degrees of freedom (at each vertex node)** |  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CABLE | DX | DY | DZ |  |  |  |
| CABLE_POULIE | DX | DY | DZ |  |  |  |

### 1.2 Support mesh cell of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the finite elements, in a displacement formulation, are two-node segments SEG2:

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Finite element** | **Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CABLE | SEG2 | MECABL2 |  |
| CABLE_POULIE | SEG3 | MEPOUL1 |  |

## 2 Assignment of the characteristics

For these modellings it is necessary to assign geometric characteristics which are complementary to the mesh data. The definition of these data is carried out with the command AFFE_CARA_ELEM associated with the factor keyword CABLE, which makes it possible to define and to assign a constant section.

## 3 Supported loadings

The loadings available are the following:

- **'CONTACT'**

Makes it possible to define the zones subjected to contact conditions.

Supported modelling: CABLE

- **'FORCE_POUTRE'**

Makes it possible to apply line forces

Supported modelling: CABLE

- **'INTE_ELEC'**

Makes it possible to apply the LAPLACE force acting on a principal conductor, due to the presence of a secondary conductor which is not necessarily straight with respect to this principal conductor.

Supported modelling: CABLE.

- **'PESANTEUR'**

Makes it possible to apply a gravity-type loading.

Supported modellings: CABLE, CABLE_POULIE

**Note:**

- *Follower load possible in the case of a loading of type* *FORCE_POUTRE.*
- Contact possible between a beam and a surface [R5.03.50](doc:r5.03.50).

### 4.2 Strains

No linear analysis is possible with these modellings (the analyses are necessarily carried out in large displacements).

The strains used in the constitutive relation are the GREEN_LAGRANGE strains: keyword 'GREEN' under DEFORMATION (Cf. [U4.51.11](doc:u4.51.11)).

## 5 Examples of implementation: test cases

- **CABLE**
- Non-linear statics
- SSNL100A [V6.02.100](doc:v6.02.100): This test simulates the laying of a cable with two spans. The cable is fixed at one of its ends, passes over a fixed pulley towards the other end and rests at its middle on a pulley placed at the bottom of a movable suspension.
- Non-linear dynamics
- SDNL100A [V5.02.100](doc:v5.02.100): This test simulates the motion of a heavy bar hinged to a fixed point by one of its ends, free elsewhere and oscillating with large amplitude in the vertical plane.
- **CABLE_POULIE**
- Non-linear statics
- SSNL100A [V6.02.100](doc:v6.02.100): This test simulates the laying of a cable with two spans. The cable is fixed at one of its ends, passes over a fixed pulley towards the other end and rests at its middle on a pulley placed at the bottom of a movable suspension.
