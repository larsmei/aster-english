# v1.04.113 MUMPS03 - Validation of the MUMPS solver for complex matrices

**Summary:**

This test case allows the MUMPS solver to be validated when using complex matrices for the modelling of an acoustic phenomenon with broken commands by modulating:

- the taking into account of Dirichlet's boundary conditions,
- the use of different renumberers.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a parallelepiped $1.m$ length following $x$ , $0.2m$ width following $z$ and $0.1m$ depth following $y$ .

### 1.2 Material properties

- $\rho =1.3\mathit{Kg}/\mathit{m³}$
- $c=343.+0.jm/s$

### 1.3 boundary conditions

The speed imposed on the 'ENTREE' group is $0.014+2.jm/s$ .

The impedance imposed on the 'SORTIE' group is $445.9+3.jNs/m$ *.*

The pressure imposed on node 'NO600' is $1.+3.jN/{m}^{2}$ *.*

The pressure imposed on node 'NO500' is $1.+3.jN/{m}^{2}$ *.*

## 2 Solution

The reference quantity used is the pressure calculated at node 'NO1'.
