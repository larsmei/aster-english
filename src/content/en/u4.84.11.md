# u4.84.11 Operator POST_DECOLLEMENT

**Summary:**

This post-processing operator computes the contact area or the lifted-off area between the raft and the soil of a soil–structure interaction analysis (ISS), carried out with the operator DYNA_NON_LINE.

This operator produces a table of type table_sdaster containing the percentage of lift-off at each time instant.

## 2 Operands

### 2.1 Operand RESULTAT

Simple keyword making it possible to retrieve the result data structure of type evol_noli which contains, among other things, the displacement field on the surface of the raft at the various time instants.

### 2.2 Operand NOM_CHAM

Simple keyword making it possible to collect the name of the field. By default it is 'DEPL', the displacement field.

### 2.3 Operand NOM_CMP

Simple keyword making it possible to supply the name of the component of the displacement field which shows the lift-off. By default, it is 'DZ'.

### 2.4 Operand GROUP_MA

Simple keyword making it possible to retrieve the group of surface mesh cells of the raft.

### 2.5 Operand INFO

```text
◊    INFO =     /1,                                 [DEFAUT]
/2,
```

Level of messages in the file 'MESSAGE'.

> If INFO = 2, then the table produced by this operator is printed in the file 'MESSAGE'.

## 3 Principle of the macro-command

The operator POST_DECOLLEMENT carries out the following actions:

- computes the total area of the raft: it calls the command POST_ELEM/INTEGRALE to determine the area of the mesh-cell group supplied to the operand GROUP_MA. This analysis requires the creation of a 2D model reduced to the mesh-cell group GROUP_MA and the creation of a unit field at the nodes of this group before it is integrated.
- loops over the time instants of the result data structure supplied to the operand RESULTAT in order to:
- extract from it the component NOM_CMP of the displacement field NOM_CHAM at the current time instant,
- define a nodal field whose values are 0 at the negative values of NOM_CMP of the field NOM_CHAM, and 1 at the strictly positive values,
- determine the area of the raft for which the values of the previous field are equal to 1,
- compute the ratio of the areas in order to obtain the raft/soil lift-off percentage.

Although this operator was developed to compute the lift-off area of a raft on the soil in soil–structure interaction analyses, it may be used for other purposes, on fields other than a displacement field.
