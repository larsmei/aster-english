# v6.03.126 SSNP126 - Validation of constitutive law JOINT_BA (restraint steel- concrete) in 2D plan

**Summary:**

Validation of constitutive law JOINT_BA of restraint steel – concrete using $\mathrm{2D}$ joint $\mathrm{2D}$ plane. This element joint is clamped in two nodes and bound to a element cube with the characteristics of any elastic material. Applying a monotonous loading slipping onto the element cube, we check the degradation of the interface, as well as the passage of small deformations at the beginning of the experiment, to the large slips observable from the peak of the resistance of the restraint. In this test case, the parameters used do not correspond to the data of a particular experimental case. However, the validation is performed by comparison with results obtained with the FEAP code of Professor Taylor, of Berkeley, software in which this formulation was implanted.

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Zero forced displacements on left side of the joint element.

Mechanical loading is imposed in the form of displacements imposed on the right side of the cube in increments of $0.01\times U$ to each time step, from 0 to 300.

### 1.4 Note

The constitutive law of the element joint is locally given (scale $(n,t)$), the system calculations are performed in the global $(x,y)$ benchmark. The base change was taken into account in the calculations. The test case was developed with a rotation of $30°$ to validate this basic change.

## 5 Summary of the results

Comparison of the results from *Code_Aster* and those obtained numerically by Professor Taylor's code FEAP from Berkeley are satisfactory (maximum the difference is $\text{2.18\%}$ on constraints).

The objective of the Modelling is to test the stability of the constitutive law using the already existing elements joints in the *Code_Aster*: it can be considered that the law implementation is correct. However, since the maximum resistance of the restraint between the steel frame and the concrete is reached beyond the frame of the small deformations, attention must be paid to the choice of time increments, especially when JOINT_BA is used in combination with other non-linear constitutive laws (e.g. MAZARS law).
