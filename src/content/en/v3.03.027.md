# v3.03.027 SSLS27 - Thin, twisted or bent plate

**Summary:**

The test, taken from the VPCS guide, allows to check the behaviour of a flat plate clamped subjected to its free end with two nodal forces of the same (flexion) or opposite (torsion) sign.

The first load is an extension of the initial test for which an reference solution is given in [bib3].

5 modellings are made: DKT, DST, DKQ, DSQ and Q4G.

### 1.2 Material properties

The elastic properties of the material are as follows:

Young's modulus : $E=1.{10}^{11}\mathrm{Pa}$

Poisson's ratio: $\nu =0.25$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Side $\mathrm{AD}$ clamped:

all point $P$ such as ${x}_{P}=0$ ($u=v=w=0$ ${\theta}_{x}={\theta}_{y}={\theta}_{z}=0$ )

Loading: 2 load cases

| $B$ and $C$: opposite forces parallel to $Z$ axis | ${F}_{{z}_{B}}=–1N$ | ${F}_{{z}_{C}}=+1N$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $B$ and $C$: forces of the same direction parallel to the $Z$ axis | ${F}_{{z}_{B}}=+1N$ | ${F}_{{z}_{C}}=+1N$ |

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

- Opposite forces perpendicular to plate [bib1], [bib2]

The reference solution is the one given in the SSLS27/89 form of the VPCS guide:

Moving point $C$ : $w=35.37{10}^{-7}m$

Resulting from the clamped support ($\mathit{AD}$) effort following $\mathit{DZ}$: ${\mathit{RESULT}}_{Z}=0$

Moment associated with the result of clamped support forces in the middle of $\mathit{AD}$:

${\mathit{MOMENT}}_{X}=-1$

${\mathit{MOMENT}}_{Y}=0$

- Forces of the same direction perpendicular to the plate [bib3]

Euler beam formulation gives an approach solution for a different Poisson's ratio $\nu$.

Move all nodess to the $\mathrm{BC}$ side:

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.027/Object_1.svg)

Resulting from the clamped support ($\mathit{AD}$) effort following $\mathit{DZ}$: ${\mathit{RESULT}}_{Z}=-2$

Moment associated with the result of clamped support forces in the middle of $\mathit{AD}$:

${\mathit{MOMENT}}_{X}=0$

${\mathit{MOMENT}}_{Y}=24$

### 2.2 Baseline

Moving $B$, $C$ and $E$ points. Resulting at clamped support along $\mathit{AD}$, moment at clamped support compared to $X$ and $Y$ axes in the middle of $\mathit{AD}$.

### 2.3 Uncertainty about the solution

analytical solution.
