# v6.04.217 SSNV217 - Single Traction Cube and Compression with ENDO_ORTH_BETON Law

**Summary:**

The $A$ Modelling of this test allows you to test different possible damage configurations of the ENDO_ORTH_BETON law in four steps and check the proper management of the different mechanisms:

- progressive traction damage in the direction $X$ until it is blocked
- progressive traction damage in the direction $Y$ until it is blocked, in the presence of a direction of damage blocked (according to $X$ )
- progressive traction damage in the $Z$ direction , in the presence of two blocked damage directions (according to $X$ and according to $Y$ )
- discharge phase and progressive compression damage according to $Z$

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 XZGLADCBEHFYG eometry

The cube is in $[0.,1.]\times [0.,1.]\times [0.,1.]$ space.

Contact details of $(m)$ points:

> $A:(0.,0.,0.)$
> $G:(1.,1.,1.)$

Geometry of the cube

> $L=\mathrm{1m}$

### 1.2 Material properties

Isotropic elastic:

$E=32\mathrm{GPa}$ : Young's modulus

$\nu =0.2$     : Poisson's ratio

Law ENDO_ORTH_BETON

${k}_{0}=300\mathrm{Pa}$ ; ${k}_{1}=10,5\mathrm{MPa}$ ; ${k}_{2}=7\times {10}^{-4}$ ; $\alpha =0,9$ ; ${\gamma}_{b}=1000\mathrm{Pa}$ ; ${\gamma}_{d}=60000\mathrm{Pa}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

They consist of forced displacements on each side of the cube, and are expressed in metres.

### 1.4 reference solution

This test is a non-regression test.
