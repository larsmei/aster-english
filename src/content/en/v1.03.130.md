# v1.03.130 MFRON05 – Code_Aster-MFront Interface Test: for laws with damage

**Summary:**

This test validates certain behaviors (e.g. metals) with damage defined using *MFront* compared with similar behaviors of *Code_Aster*.

Modelling A: This Modelling allows the elasto-visco-plastic model to be validated with Hayhurst damage and implicit integration, compared to HAYHURST model of the SSNV225C test on a hardware point.

Modelling B: This Modelling allows the validation of the elasto-visco-plastic model with Hayhurst damage and explicit integration, compared to the HAYHURST model of the SSNV225A test on a hardware point.

Modelling C: This Modelling allows to validate the model with damage from Gurson.

Modelling D: This model allows to validate the Tvergaard cohesive zone model.

## 3 Modelling C

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

- Tested behavior: GussonTvergaardNeedlemanPlasticFlow_NumericJacobian.mfront

Elasto-plastic law with damage to Guson (model GTN in the literature).

Ref: «Analysis of the cup cone fracture in a round tensile bar».

V.Tvergaard, A.Needleman, Acat Metallurgica 32 (1984) 157-169

the criterion is of the form(f is porosity)

$F(\sigma ,f)={(\frac{{\sigma}_{\mathit{eq}}}{{\sigma}_{0}})}^{2}+2{q}_{1}f\cosh(\frac{3}{2}{q}_{2}\frac{{\sigma}_{h}}{{\sigma}_{0}})-1-{({q}_{1}f)}^{2}\le 0$

The isotope hardening is modified as follows(

$R(p)=\mathit{R0}+{Q}_{1}(1–{e}^{-{b}_{1}p})+{Q}_{2}(1–{e}^{-{b}_{2}p})$

- Modelling: point material subject to required deformations:

${\epsilon}_{xx}=0,02t$ , ${\epsilon}_{yy}=0,1t$     ${\epsilon}_{zz}=0$
