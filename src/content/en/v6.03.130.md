# v6.03.130 SSNP130 - Detection of singularities in cracked plate

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to test the detection of singularities in a cracked plate as well as the calculation of a size card (for a given target error).

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

Geometry is a cracked plate with only the upper part modeled.

### 1.2 Material properties

The massif consists of a linear isotropic elastic material:

- Young's modulus isotropic elasticity: $E=200000\mathrm{Pa}$
- Poisson's ratio : $\nu =0,3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Unit pressure is applied to the top of the plate.

For symmetry reasons, the crack ligament is blocked on the $Y$ axis and the node at the end of the ligament is blocked on the $X$ axis.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

When the exact solution of the problem studied has singularities, the convergence order of the elements solution is changed. Consider, for example, a problem of flat elasticity discretized with elements triangular $p$ degree.

If the exact ${U}_{\mathrm{ex}}$ solution is regular, it is known that ([bib1]):

${\parallel u-{u}_{h}\parallel }_{\Omega}={\parallel e\parallel }_{\Omega}\le C{h}^{p}$                                     éq 2.1-1

With $C$ a constant, $h$ the size of elements.

Where ${\parallel e\parallel }_{\Omega}\le C{h}^{p}$ is the contribution to energy error, i.e.:

${\parallel e\parallel }_{\Omega}\le \frac{1}{2}\underset{\Omega}{\int}\varepsilon ({e}_{h})K\varepsilon ({e}_{h})d\Omega$                                 éq 2.1-2

However, if the exact solution has a singularity, for example if, locally near a point ${M}_{0}$ , the displacement field is of the shape (with $r$ and $\theta$ polar coordinates near the point ${M}_{0}$ ):

${U}_{\mathrm{ex}}={r}^{\alpha}V(\theta )+W$ with $0<\alpha <1$ eq 2.1-3

With $V$ a function of $\theta$ and $W$ a constant.

So we show that [bib1]:

${\parallel {e}_{h}\parallel }_{\Omega}\le C{h}^{\alpha}$                                     éq 2.1-4

As a result, the rate of convergence of the overall energy error becomes independent of the $p$ degree of the finished elements used and the same is true of the measurement of the error (for example, if $p=1$ or $p=2$ then $\alpha =1/2$ for a crack).

Thus, at crack point the order of singularity will be 0.5, and away from singularity (where the element solution is regular the order of singularity is $p$ (1 for linear elements, 2 for quadratic elements)

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

The value of the singularity in crack point (analytical solution), in its vicinity (non-regression) and away from the crack (analytical solution) will be tested.

The size ratio to be applied to the mesh for a target error (non-regression) and the new size of the elements (non-regression) is also tested.

It is noted that for Modelling A, the target error is the error (in amount of interest) on average displacement on the structure. For the other modellings, the error used is the energy standard error.
