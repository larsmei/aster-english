# v7.11.001 HSLS01 - Slim square plate subjected to thermal gradient in thickness

**Summary**

The purpose of this test is to validate thermal expansion in the elements plate, where the temperature is variable in thickness.

Two modellingss are used to test modellings DKT, DST, Q4G on mesh cells TRIA3 and QUAD4 and COQUE_3D on mesh cells TRIA7 and QUAD9.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v7.11.001/Shape1.gif)

### 1.2 Material properties

Young's modulus : $E={2.10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$

Poisson's ratio : $\nu =0.3$

Dilation coefficient: $\alpha ={1.10}^{-5}°{C}^{-1}$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

The $\mathrm{AB}$, $\mathrm{BC}$, $\mathrm{CD}$ and $\mathrm{DA}$ sides are clamped. The temperature is constant on the upper side and is equal to ${T}_{s}=100°C$.

The temperature is constant on the lower side and equals ${T}_{i}=0°C$; the temperature gradient is assumed linear in thickness.
