# v6.04.102 SSNV102 - Tensile shear test with TAHERI model

**Summary:**

The problem is quasi-static non-linear in structural mechanics.

The response of a volume element to a tensile load is analyzed in such a way that it imposes a uniform stress-deformation state in the element.

There are 2 modellings: one in $\mathrm{3D}$ volumetric and one in $\mathrm{2D}$ flat stress.

This test validates the numerical integration of the elasto-plastic behavior model of Said Taheri.
