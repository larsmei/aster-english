# v2.02.106 SDLL106 - A beam subjected to a random, distributed excitation

**Summary:**

A bi-clamped beam is subjected to a distributed force over its entire length. The force distribution profile is identical to all frequencies.

The random movement of this beam is evaluated by a stochastic approach: the spectral power density of the displacement at different points of the beam is determined.

The two options are tested:

- spatial function of the forces applied with interspectre unit (method 1),
- direct interspectrum for excited ddl (method 2).

This test is an illustration of the response of a structure subjected to Eolian excitement.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

The direct calculation defines an assembled vector of spatial load distribution and applies the ${G}_{\mathrm{FF}}(\omega )$ stress spectral density on this distribution (method 1).

The decomposed calculation defines excitation as an interspectral matrix of dimension 3 (equal to the number of nodes excited) and applies, in force imposed on nodes, the following interspectral matrix (method 2):

$\left[\begin{array}{ccc}\frac{1}{4}& \frac{1}{2}& \frac{1}{4}\\ \frac{1}{2}& 1& \frac{1}{2}\\ \frac{1}{4}& \frac{1}{2}& \frac{1}{4}\end{array}\right]{G}_{\mathrm{FF}}(\omega )$

Both results must be identical without any approximation.

### 2.2 Baseline

Spectral density of displacement power of node $\mathrm{P3}$ at frequencies: $4.,6.,8.,10.$ and $12\mathrm{Hz}$.

### 3.1 Modelling characteristics

Elements beam: POU_D_T

The excitatory spectral density is a white level 1 noise.

The first two modes of the calculation were taken into account.

The damping is introduced as a modal damping in the random dynamic response operator. For all calculation cases, it is taken equal to $\text{5\%}$

### 3.3 Remarks

Spectral densities are expressed in their physical unit. For a force it will be in ${N}^{2}/\mathrm{Hz}$ .

## 4 Summary of the results

Method 1 (space distribution of effort) and indirect method (by decomposition on the three excited nodes) provide the same result.

This verification ensures that both methods are consistent and that their programming is high.
