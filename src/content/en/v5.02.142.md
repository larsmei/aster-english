# v5.02.142 SDNL142 – Submerged cable subject to wave field

**Summary:**

This test modeled the response of a submerged cable, subjected to Morison's hydraulic forces (trailer effort only) in the presence of a regular wavefield.

- Modelling A: In this Modelling, the calculation of drag forces for a cable subjected to a regular wave field is tested.

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry and boundary conditions

The figure below shows the Reference problem: a cylindrical cable immersed under the free surface in a column of water of height $h=30m$. The ends of the beam sss press on two springs, themselves clamped at their ends.

The hydraulic section (seen by the fluid) is noted to be transverse to the beam.

![Figure](/figures/v5.02.142/100002010000041D0000037838E06D7DA834D231.png)

### 1.2 Material properties

The material has an isotropic elastic behavior:

| Young's modulus | $200\mathit{GPa}$ |
| --- | --- |
| Poisson's ratio | $0.3$ |
| Cable density $\rho$ | $7800\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$ |
| Volumetric mass ${\rho}_{w}$ | $1000\mathit{kg}/{m}^{3}$ |
| Spring rigidity following x, y and z | $50.0N/m$ |
| Train coefficient ${C}_{d}$ | $1.0$ |

### 1.3 Swell Cinematic

A single-directional, single-directional, steady wave is considered to be directed along the $+x$ axis, order 1 (d-Airy head), $H$ height, $T$ period and $\lambda$ wavelength, see diagram below.

![Figure](/figures/v5.02.142/10000201000002F50000020D0162112424016B88.png)

The $\eta$ free surface as a function of $x$ space and $t$ time is expressed as follows:

$$
\eta (x,t)=\frac{H}{2}\cos(kx-\omega t)
$$

with $k=\frac{2\pi }{\lambda}$ the number of wave and $\omega =\frac{2\pi }{T}$ the pulse.

The speed of the ${V}_{w}=(u,v,w)$ fluid is as follows:

$$
\begin{array}{c}u(x,z,t)=0\\ v(x,z,t)=\frac{H}{2}\omega \frac{\sinh(k(h+z))}{\sinh(\mathit{kh})}\sin(k.x-\omega t)\\ w(x,z,t)=\frac{H}{2}\omega \frac{\sinh(k(h+z))}{\sinh(kh)}\sin(k.x-\omega t)\end{array}
$$

The acceleration of the fluid is noted ${a}_{w}=\frac{\partial {v}_{w}}{\partial t}$.

### 1.4 Loading

The cable is subjected to drag forces induced by regular wave fields of height $H=3m$ and $T=12s$ period.

Note that Archimedes' weight and thrust are not taken into account in this problem.

**Model A**

The cable is subject to Morison's efforts (trailer term only):

$$
{F}_{\text{mor}}=\frac{1}{2}{C}_{d}{\rho}_{w}{S}_{w}({v}_{w}^{\text{ortho}}-{v}_{s}^{\text{ortho}})({v}_{w}^{\text{ortho}}-{v}_{s}^{\text{ortho}})
$$

where ${v}_{w}$ and ${v}_{s}$ refer respectively to the fluid speed and solid speed (inlet parameter of mechanical load FORCE_POUTRE . The symbol $\text{ortho}$ represents the projection in the normal direction at the neutral fibre of beam 1
It is noted that these are linear forces which are applied in a distributed way on the beam.

*Note: Morison's forces depend on the position of the beam, they must be mentioned as follow-up loads in the calculations.*

This projection also requires switching to the input parameter of FORCE_POUTRE l-beam orientation.
