# u3.14.07 Modellings 3D_HM, 3D_HHM, 3D_THM, 3D_THH, 3D_THHM

**Summary:**

This document describes, for the modellings 3D_HM, 3D_HHM, 3D_THM, 3D_THH, 3D_THHM, 3D_HM_DIL, 3D_HHM_DIL, 3D_THM_DIL:

- the degrees of freedom carried by the finite elements which support the modelling,
- the associated support mesh cells,
- the supported materials and loadings,
- the analysis options for the elementary matrices and the post-processings,
- the non-linear possibilities as well as the fracture-mechanics options if they exist.

The modellings 3D_HM, 3D_HHM, 3D_THM, 3D_THH, 3D_THHM, 3D_HM_DIL, 3D_HHM_DIL, 3D_THM_DIL: (Phenomenon: MECANIQUE) correspond to finite elements whose support mesh cells are volume mesh cells.

## 1 Discretisation

### 1.1 Degrees of freedom

DX, DY and DZ denote the displacement degrees of freedom.

PRE1 and PRE2 denote two pressure degrees of freedom, whose precise meaning depends on the constitutive laws used. TEMP denotes the temperature.

| **Finite element** | **Degrees of freedom** |
| --- | --- |

| 3D_HM | DX, DY, DZ, PRE1 |
| --- | --- |
| 3D_HHM | DX, DY, DZ, PRE1, PRE2 |
| 3D_THM | DX, DY, DZ, PRE1, TEMP |
| 3D_THH | PRE1, PRE2, TEMP |
| 3D_THHM | DX, DY, DZ, PRE1, PRE2, TEMP |
| 3D_HM_DIL | DX, DY, DZ, PRES, PRE1, TEMP, GONF |
| 3D_THM_DIL | DX, DY, DZ, PRES, PRE1, TEMP, GONF |
| 3D_HHM_DIL | DX, DY, DZ, PRES, PRE1, PRE2, GONF |

### 1.2 Support mesh cell of the stiffness matrices

The support mesh cells of the finite elements can only be hexahedra. The elements are isoparametric.

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Interpolation** | **Remarks** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 3D_HM | HEXA20 | Serendip 20 nodes in displacement, trilinear on 8 nodes in pressure | The pressure of a mid-side node is the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3D_HHM | HEXA20 | Serendip 20 nodes in displacement, trilinear on 8 nodes in pressure | The pressures of a mid-side node are the averages of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| 3D_THM | HEXA20 | Serendip 20 nodes in displacement, trilinear on 8 nodes in pressure and temperature | The pressure and the temperature of a mid-side node are the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| 3D_THH | HEXA20 | Trilinear on 8 nodes in pressure and temperature | The pressures and the temperature of a mid-side node are the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| 3D_THHM | HEXA20 | Serendip 20 nodes in displacement, trilinear on 8 nodes in pressure and temperature | The pressures and the temperature of a mid-side node are the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| 3D_HM_DIL | HEXA20 | Serendip 20 nodes in displacement, trilinear on 8 nodes in pressure, in volumetric strain and Lagrange multiplier | The pressure of a mid-side node is the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| 3D_HHM_DIL | HEXA20 | Serendip 20 nodes in displacement, trilinear on 8 nodes in pressure, in volumetric strain and Lagrange multiplier | The pressures of a mid-side node are the averages of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| 3D_THM_DIL | HEXA20 | Serendip 20 nodes in displacement, trilinear on 8 nodes in pressure, temperature, in volumetric strain and Lagrange multiplier | The pressure and the temperature of a mid-side node are the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |

### 1.3 Support mesh cell of the loadings

| **Modelling** | **Mesh cell** | **Interpolation** | **Remarks** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 3D_HM | QUAD8 | Serendip 8 nodes in displacement, bilinear on 4 nodes in pressure and temperature | The pressure of a mid-side node is the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3D_HHM | QUAD8 | Serendip 8 nodes in displacement, bilinear on 4 nodes in pressure | The pressures of a mid-side node are the averages of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| 3D_THM | QUAD8 | Quadratic in displacement, linear in pressure and temperature | The pressure and the temperature of a mid-side node are the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| 3D_THH | QUAD8 | Bilinear on 4 nodes in pressure and temperature | The pressures and the temperature of a mid-side node are the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |
| 3D_THHM | QUAD8 | Serendip 8 nodes in displacement, bilinear on 4 nodes in pressure and temperature | The pressures and the temperature of a mid-side node are the average of the vertex nodes of the segment |

The support mesh cells of the loadings of the _DIL modellings are identical to those of the corresponding HM, THM or HHM modellings.

### 5.2 DYNA_NON_LINE

Non-linear analyses are authorised on these elements but there are not yet any tests in the base. Moreover, the computation of the Rayleigh damping is not yet available for these elements; the material parameters AMOR_ALPHA and AMOR_BETA must therefore be zero so that the analysis does not stop in error.
