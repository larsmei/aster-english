# v6.04.262 SSNV262 – Validation of viscous regularization REGU_VISC by viscoelastic simulation

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to validate the REGU_VISC_ELAS behavior model mobilized by REGU_VISC viscous regularization of the STAT_NON_LINE operator. The problem admits an reference solution in the viscoelastic case.

The problem is implemented:

- in 3D in Modelling A
- in D_PLAN in Modelling B

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

It is a material point, processed by SIMU_POINT_MAT in 3D or D_PLAN depending on the Modelling.

### 1.2 Material properties

The material obeys the elastic constitutive law, which is superimposed on the viscous regularization.The parameters of the behaviorvalent:

| $E=200000\text{MPa}$ | Young's modulus |
| --- | --- |
| $\nu =0.3$ | Poisson's ratio |
| $k=100000\text{MPa}$ | Rigidity of viscous regularisation |
| $\tau =10\text{s}$ | Typical viscous adjustment time |

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

This is a material point (SIMU_POINT_MAT) load. All deformation components are imposed; all are zero except EPXX. A ${\dot{\epsilon}}_{xx}={10}^{-3}{\text{s}}^{-1}$ deformation rate is prescribed for 20 s, until a deformation of 2% is achieved.

### 1.4 Initial conditions

It is natural state, all fields are void.

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Calculation method used for reference solution

This problem admits an analytical solution. We are very much inspired by the results of [Lorentz, Drouet, Hamon, 2020].

In the case of confined uniaxial loading, the elastic behaviour relationship is reduced to:

${\sigma}^{e}={E}_{c}\epsilon ;{E}_{c}=\lambda +2\mu ;\lambda =\frac{E\nu }{(1+\nu )(1-2\nu )};2\mu =\frac{E}{1+\nu }$

where $xx$ indices are now omitted. The viscous stress is governed by the following differential equation:

$\dot{{\sigma}^{v}}+\frac{1}{\tau}{\sigma}^{v}=k\tau \dot{\epsilon};{\sigma}^{v}(0)=0$

Since the second member is constant, she accepts the following solution:

${\sigma}^{v}(t)=k\tau \dot{\epsilon}\left[1-\exp\left(-\frac{t}{\tau}\right)\right]$

And therefore the constraint taking into account viscous regularization is:

$\sigma ={\sigma}^{e}+{\sigma}^{v}={E}_{c}\dot{\epsilon}t+k\tau \dot{\epsilon}\left[1-\exp\left(-\frac{t}{\tau}\right)\right]$

In the transverse direction, the viscous stress is zero, so that the stress is reduced to the elastic stress:

${\sigma}_{yy}=\lambda \epsilon$

The energy stored in the viscoelastic branch can easily be inferred from this:

$\mathit{VISCELAS}=\frac{1}{2k}{{\sigma}^{v}}^{2}$

As well as the energy dissipated by the viscoelastic branch by integrating dissipation:

$\mathit{VISCDISS}=k{\tau}^{2}{\dot{\epsilon}}^{2}\left[\frac{t}{\tau}+2\exp(-\frac{t}{\tau})-\frac{1}{2}\exp(-2\frac{t}{\tau})-\frac{3}{2}\right]$

### 2.2 Baseline

It will be ensured that at $t=20\text{s}$ , the axial and transverse stress as well as the viscous stress, stored energy and dissipated energy in the viscous branch correspond well to the analytical results.

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

None.

Lorentz E., Drouet G. Hamon F. (2020) . CIWAP3 / lot SA – Digital stabilization of a model of softening behaviour by viscoelastic regularization. Internal note EDF R&D 6125-1724-2020-01606-EN.
