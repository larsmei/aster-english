# v6.04.106 SSNV106 - 2 piece screw assembly with frictional contact HPC

**Summary**

This test describes the assembly of 2 parts by a screw with frictional contact in HPC. It involves linear relationships between degrees of freedom for Modelling the clamping as well as frictional contact relationships between the 2 parts as well as between the reinforcement and the screw. A variable temperature field is also applied to the system.

### 1.2 Material property

$E=2{10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$ $\nu =0,3$ $\alpha =1,6{10}^{-5}$

### 1.3 Initial conditions, boundary conditions, and loading

A temperature field is imposed that varies with time.

The head of the screw is "hanged" in the reinforcement by linear relationships and then a relative displacement is imposed on it to represent the clamping.
