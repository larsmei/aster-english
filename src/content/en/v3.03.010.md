# v3.03.010 SSLS10 – Tore under uniform internal pressure

**Summary:**

The objective of this test is to validate the calculation of displacements, and stresses in tore subjected to a uniform internal pressure.

modellings :

- $A$ Modelling: DKT with mesh cells QUAD4/TRIA3
- $B$ Modelling: DST with mesh cells QUAD4/TRIA3
- $C$ Modelling: Q4G with mesh cells QUAD4/TRIA3
- $D$ Modelling: COQUE_3D with mesh cells QUAD9/TRIA7

## 1 Reference problem

### 1.1 Geometry

![Figure](/figures/v3.03.010/1000000000000263000001F416CD1B56C5036A3D.png)

Thickness: $h=0,02m$

### 1.2 Material properties

The material is elastic isotropic:

- $E=2,1.\times {10}^{11}\mathit{Pa}$
- $\nu =0.3$

### 1.3 Boundary conditions and loads

Free conditions

Loading:

- Internal pressure: $p={10}^{4}\mathit{Pa}$

### 1.4 Initial conditions

None

## 2 reference solution

### 2.1 Method of calculation

The reference solution is a digital bib1 solution.

Si $a-b\le r<a+b$

- Travel: ${\delta}_{r}=\frac{\mathit{pb}}{\mathrm{2Eh}}(r-\nu (r+a))$
- Constraints: ${\sigma}_{11}=\frac{\mathit{pb}}{\mathrm{2h}}\times \frac{r+a}{r}$ ${\sigma}_{22}=\frac{\mathit{pb}}{\mathrm{2h}}$

These formulae are only applicable to thin tors, such as $\frac{b}{h}>10$ and radius of curvature such as $r\times \pi <100\sqrt{\frac{{I}_{x}}{A}}$ with ${I}_{x}$ = inertial moment and $A$ = area of geometrical section of the torus

### 2.2 Quantities and reference results

- Travel

| Point | $\mathit{DX}(m)$ |
| --- | --- |
| $r=a-b$ | ${\delta}_{r}=1.19\times {10}^{-7}$ |
| $r=a+b$ | ${\delta}_{r}=1.79\times {10}^{-6}$ |

- Constraints

| Point | Constraints (Pa) |
| --- | --- |
| $\forall r$ | ${\sigma}_{22}=2,5\times {10}^{5}$ |
| $r=a-b$ | ${\sigma}_{11}=7.5\times {10}^{5}$ |
| $r=a+b$ | ${\sigma}_{11}=4.17\times {10}^{5}$ |

### 2.3 Uncertainties about the solution

analytical solution

### 2.4 Bibliographical references

Guide VPCS - 1990 edition.

## 7 Summary of the results

**Movements**: Whatever mesh cell type is used (TRIA3, QUAD4) , the results obtained for the 4 modellings (DKT, DST, Q4G and COQUE_3D) are satisfactory. Compared to the analytical solution, the maximum difference is 5%.

**Contraints**:

- Modellings DKT, DST and Q4G: Whatever type of mesh cell used (TRIA3, QUAD4), the results obtained are correct. Compared to the analytical solution, there is a maximum difference of 12%. These modellings use faceted elements, refining the mesh should achieve better results.
- COQUE_3D Modelling: mesh cell QUAD9 gives very good results, the difference observed is 0.1%. the difference is important (46%) for mesh cell TRIA7.
