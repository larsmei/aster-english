# v6.04.189 SSNV189 - Validation of ELAS_HYPER law on a tab

**Summary:**

This test validates the Signorini-type hyper elastic behavior (ELAS_HYPER material) on an test case that performs a tab extension, compared to ANSYS.

## 1 Reference problem
