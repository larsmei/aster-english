# v9.01.109 SZLZ109 - Lemaitre damage to post-processing

**Summary:**

The purpose of this test is to calculate the damage of LEMAITRE from any multiaxial loading history and the history of the cumulative plastic deformation.

The $D(t)$ damage is calculated from the $\sigma (t)$ stress tensor data and the $p(t)$ cumulative plastic formation at all times ${t}_{i}$ (provided by the user). In addition, the total $D=\underset{i=1}{\overset{N}{\Sigma}}D({t}_{i})$ damage is calculated.

The material characteristics $E$ (Young's modulus), $\nu$ (Poisson's ratio) and $S$ (material parameter) must be dependent on the temperature $T$ ($T(t)$ must therefore be supplied by the user at the same times as $\sigma (t)$ and $p(t)$).

## 1 Reference problem

The $D(t)$ damage is calculated from the $\sigma (t)$ stress tensor data and the $p(t)$ cumulative plastic deformation.

| $\dot{D}=\frac{1}{{(1-D)}^{2}}\left[\frac{1}{\mathrm{3ES}}(1+\nu ){\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}^{2}+\frac{3}{\mathrm{2ES}}(1-2\nu ){\sigma}_{H}^{2}\right]\dot{p}$ | si $p>{p}_{d}$ |
| --- | --- |
| $D=0$ | if not |

${\sigma}_{\mathrm{eq}}$ is the equivalent constraint of von Mises

${\sigma}_{H}$ is the hydrostatic stress

${p}_{d}$ represents the damage threshold

$S$ is a material characteristic ($\mathrm{MPa}$ )

The total damage $D=\underset{i=1}{\overset{N}{\Sigma}}D({t}_{i})$ is also calculated.
