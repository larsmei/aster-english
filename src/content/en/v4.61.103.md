# v4.61.103 MTLP103 - Trempe of a 16MND5 steel cylindrical bar

**Summary:**

This test case is a validation of the metallurgical transformation models at cooling in steels. It is the tempering of a 16MND5 steel cylindrical bar, for which experimental data of final hardness measurements are available. The nonlinear thermal calculation of the cooling phase is first performed from the identification of a thermal exchange coefficient (when the bar is in the air), then from a time-dependent surface flux density on the outer boundary (at the dip), which boundary conditions was identified by thermocouple temperature measurements performed during the test. The metallurgical evolution is then calculated in post-processing of the thermal calculation. The calculated final hardness values (depending on the formed phase proportions) are compared with measurements made at different points in the bar section.
