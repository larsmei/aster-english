# v7.31.130 WTNV130 - Heating of a desaturated porous medium with dissolved air (3D)

**Summary:**

A porous medium with pores filled with a mixture of water (liquid and steam) and air (dry and dissolved in water) is heated. The initial liquid saturation is $\text{50\%}$ , the loading is a uniform thermal flux on the edges of the domain. The Modelling done by a single element corresponds to the modelling of a homogeneous problem in space.

The reference solution is an analytical solution approach. This test case is the $\mathrm{3D}$ extension of the test case WTNP106.
